## Why

Tenant staff need one dependable place to handle incoming requests, collaborate, use permitted CRM context and follow through without losing messages, exposing private information or confusing a reply with completion of an owned business action. Phase26 formalizes the forty fully ratified Support Hub decisions and their amendments against the current partial implementation, after explicit confirmation of the testing boundaries.

## What Changes

- Complete one canonical Support conversation and original-source model with explicit recipients, four work meanings, qualified assignment/coverage, recovery and immutable communication history.
- Complete ordinary email continuation and contextual Help without a mandatory requester archive, CRM record creation or identity verification merely to converse; protected actions retain their owning domain's assurance.
- Qualify receiving routes, durable intake, held review, source custody and optional exact sender/receiving-inbox holding before activation; never treat retention, denial or failed processing as successful admission.
- Complete shared governed reply authoring, exact P17 preparation/P6 delivery, private Reply and Note drafts, deliberate quotation, safe files, Compact/Full reading and truthful composing/read cues.
- Complete bounded collaboration, own-note history, guides, saved wording/shortcuts, views/search/labels, timely review, sampled feedback and focused reporting with their accepted scope and authorization.
- Preserve native CRM, finance, document, care, publication, communication and workflow owners across surfaces. Support assignment, source linking or conversation membership grants no additional domain authority.
- **BREAKING:** fence incompatible legacy Support writers and overbroad mutation/projection behavior before enabling the qualified contracts. Migrate only verified data, preserving historical provenance and accepted effects; unknown mappings remain explicitly restricted/read-only. Do not convert labels, old counters, current addresses or broad defaults into new approved state.
- Carry every ratified edge case, failure outcome, numerical boundary, custody rule, security requirement, operating control and proof obligation into independently verifiable stories and acceptance scenarios. Add no feature-parity scope and execute no product implementation in this planning change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `support-hub`: tenant Support workflows, ordinary requester continuation, qualified conversation/identity/CRM boundaries, collaboration, source content and privacy, receiving/review, authoring/delivery seams, staff interaction, recovery, reporting and release proof.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing platform, identity/access and workflow requirements remain controlling. CRM's native-Postgres correction already exists in the accepted retirement change; this change consumes it and expressly rejects the stale Twenty backing/synchronization wording rather than creating a competing CRM redesign. P6/P17 and other owning-domain requirements are referenced and qualified at their existing boundaries, not copied into Support as new authorities.

## Impact

Mission Control's Support surface and permitted inline CRM context; selected donor/missionary/public Help entry; shared business APIs, authorized database projections, Supabase/Auth/Storage, source assets, shared Base UI/base-maia and Tiptap authoring/read profiles; canonical inbound and durable execution; Email Studio/P17 content/preparation and P6 actual local/provider communication; source/privacy/records and operational recovery.

The confirmed primary test seam is real authenticated commands and authorized projections, including canonical intake/job entries on disposable local Supabase, with focused database-boundary/concurrency checks and real Playwright journeys over the same backend. Actual provider qualification and intended-user/accessibility evidence remain separate obligations. Current smoke, in-memory adapters and historical probes do not discharge those obligations.

Publication is a specification record in the existing GitHub issue tracker, not implementation-ticket breakdown, a production cutover or permission to send real messages. The local OpenSpec change remains active with implementation tasks unchecked until actual accepted implementation evidence exists.

A kill switch stops unsafe optional admission/effects while preserving source restrictions, accepted state, held review and uncertain-delivery recovery; it never restores broad legacy writes or repeats an unknown provider effect.
