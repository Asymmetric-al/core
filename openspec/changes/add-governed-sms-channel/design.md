## Context and source authority

This active change implements the adopted Phase 43 intent when its source prerequisites and explicit qualification gates are satisfied; it is not evidence those prerequisites exist today. The complete [specification](../../../docs/prds/governed-sms/specification.md) supplies detailed product meaning, [qualification register](../../../docs/prds/governed-sms/qualification.md) identifies exact owner gates, and [delivery map](../../../docs/prds/governed-sms/delivery.json) preserves work-package, scenario and dependency identities. The OpenSpec requirements below trace every original acceptance case exactly once; no source case is discarded when several packages consume it.

## Ownership and safety

Business data and consequential operations remain behind the established API boundary. Tenant/environment scope and same-scope references, current source authorization, immutable accepted identity, revision/epoch/claim fences, receipts and bounded recovery apply to every new profile. Browser controls cannot become permission or evidence authority. Credentials and protected payloads remain server-side and outside generic logs, models and orchestration envelopes.

The specific predecessor and successor boundaries, supported starting profile direction and unresolved decisions are enumerated in the qualification register. Missing proof keeps the affected path unavailable. Do not resolve an authority gap by adding an ownerless service role, widening anonymous access, trusting provider identity labels or deleting a predecessor negative test.

## Dependencies and compatibility

Use the acyclic internal package graph and exact external dependency clauses in `delivery.json`. A shared capability may qualify before an entire checkpoint completes; a consumer cannot become a backward prerequisite of its source. The minimum Phase 31 connection foundation remains independently deliverable. All three named checkpoints and selected mandatory profiles are required for full Phase 43 completion.

## Data, migration and recovery

Census actual source code, current accepted records and retained bytes before choosing storage or changing writers. Logical record names in the supplied roadmap are semantics, not assertions that a physical table/API already exists. Reuse qualified existing owners, extend or migrate explicitly, deploy compatible readers before new writers and retain exact provenance, current restrictions and legal/source retention. Separate local acceptance from external provider work; external creation/submission uncertainty must reconcile the original operation without fresh identity or permission.

## Qualification and rollout

Use red-green-refactor for substantive implementation and real database/source-adapter/browser proof at each slice. Validate provider interoperability, selected markets/protocols, privacy, revocation, concurrency, unknown outcomes, accessible use, quantitative workload and source-specific recovery independently of unit mocks. Preserve every original case plus applicable IG-AT/EXT-INT cross-product cases. Local planning validation proves trace coverage and graph structure only.

Release by exact qualified profile with a controlled pilot and attributable evidence. Freeze favorable effects during restore or unresolved authority and preserve safe negative handling, history and reconciliation under the qualified source policy. Do not archive until all in-scope implementation and acceptance have merged and become accepted repository reality.
