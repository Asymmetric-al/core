# Phase 27 scoped owner amendments

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Phase 27 role-qualified staff assignment identity

For the staff-assignment family only, active logical identity SHALL distinguish Tenant, exact staff/Party endpoints and role-qualified responsibility. Multiple roles per person/Party and multiple assignees per role remain permitted; duplicate active copies of the same role-qualified edge are forbidden. Preserve shared one-primary behavior independently and historical lineage. Portfolios remain derived. P27 policy consumes P9 authoritative transfer commands; manual/unassigned/unknown provenance stays protected and transfer grants no access.

#### Scenario: Distinct legitimate roles

- **GIVEN** the same staff and Party hold two different legitimate roles.
- **WHEN** one role is deliberately transferred.
- **THEN** the other role remains unchanged, the exact transfer is recorded once, and current access is not expanded.

#### Scenario: Concurrent primary and manual choice

- **GIVEN** a manual protection or primary change races an automatic transfer.
- **WHEN** both commands reach the owner.
- **THEN** the serialized source outcome preserves protected intent and valid primary cardinality without dropping independently owned work.

### Requirement: Phase 27 new shared partnership interaction source

P9 SHALL own one newly authored sharing-eligible interaction source for an exact partnership; P27 owns development meaning. The general missionary exclusion for private/general notes, scores and care remains. Only qualified current audience/fields/purpose and explicit historical access allow the narrow D26 projection. No protected-content migration or shared-copy workaround is permitted; contribution is voluntary and creates no cultivation admission, compulsory tasks or coaching.

#### Scenario: Historical access is not assignment

- **GIVEN** a new representative or collaborator is assigned without an historical-access grant.
- **WHEN** earlier shared entries or protected notes are requested.
- **THEN** assignment alone reveals neither and an explicit source/history-qualified grant is required for earlier eligible entries.

#### Scenario: Independent update and follow-up

- **GIVEN** a missionary chooses to share a new eligible update.
- **WHEN** the source accepts the update.
- **THEN** one attributed source record exists and no mandatory report, task, stage change or staff status is created.
