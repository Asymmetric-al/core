# Phase 27 scoped owner amendments

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Phase 27 P15 reported gift intake and independent completion

P15 SHALL own a bounded operational actual-receipt report with trusted Tenant/recorder/time, attributed handling assertion/time precision, reported holder or explicit unknown, current accountable next work/owned exception and exact lineage.

Every clause in `openspec/contracts/contribution-operations.md#contract-1fe25b586ff24315db30` MUST apply without exception.

#### Scenario: Truthful incomplete report

- **GIVEN** staff actually receive a gift but do not know donor, amount or intended entity.
- **WHEN** a qualified reporter records the occurrence.
- **THEN** the same P15 source retains known facts and owned unresolved handling without fake donor, currency, Legal Entity, money or finance access.

#### Scenario: Posting precedes handling

- **GIVEN** qualified finance posts the exact intake item while physical transfer remains outstanding.
- **WHEN** the appeal refreshes.
- **THEN** recording is shown as source-confirmed for covered work and physical handling remains actionable without duplicate posting.

#### Scenario: Handling precedes posting

- **GIVEN** physical handling is resolved but financial entry is incomplete.
- **WHEN** the source projection refreshes.
- **THEN** only handling ends and recording remains owned and visible.

#### Scenario: Lost posting response

- **GIVEN** posting committed but its reply or projection failed.
- **WHEN** reconciliation retries.
- **THEN** the exact source result is recovered without another financial contribution or erasure of history.
