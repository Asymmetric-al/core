# Add Imports And Migration

Publication issue: [AL-2543](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/issues/2543). Specification: [Phase 30 — Imports & Migration](../../../docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-30-imports-migration.md).

## Why

Ministries cannot leave SiteStacker, or any other system, for Asym without losing history, consent evidence, recurring continuity or trust. Asym has no importer, no Data Tools area, no import permissions and no safe custody for real exports. Ad hoc imports would risk duplicate people, guessed money facts, double receipts, unwanted sends and leaks of restricted people. A founder-led discovery ratified 21 decisions (Q1–Q21) that make one planned Switch per ministry, careful everyday imports and one import reversal path safe. This change turns those decisions into one verifiable capability without moving any owner's business rule into Phase 30.

## What Changes

- **Switch model (Q1).** One planned Switch per Tenant and source installation, with a recorded Switch Boundary, Minimum Switch Kit scheduling gates, practice runs, go/no-go with measured pauses, one final Source Snapshot Manifest, two-level reconciliation, a bounded Legacy Recurring Tail with Legacy Tail Backfills, Legacy Surface Dispositions and a decommission checklist. Side-by-side and area-by-area switching are not offered.
- **Evidence and custody (Q2).** Staged evidence classes; no real donor data until every Q2 gate holds: a signed agreement with data-processing terms, the ministry's own Tenant with the single-installation checks done, an Approved environment, a qualified Phase 29 staff-migration custody profile, restricted-by-default classification, a complete retained-state census (D13) and a fresh readiness census. Source profile status moves Unavailable → Pilot-qualified → Supported only on representative real evidence.
- **Source coverage (Q3).** Direct support for SiteStacker only; any other system through per-Tenant Import templates (Reviewed transformation); no vendor claims; never-imported families are Visible exclusions.
- **Tax documents (Q4).** One issuer per gift, fixed at first recording. Switch history and Legacy Tail Backfill gifts carry the immutable Recorded outside Asym fact and never receive Asym tax documents; Gift-file gifts are ordinary current gifts first recorded in Asym through a Phase 15 batch and are documented under Phase 7/14 rules.
- **Who runs a Switch (Q5).** The ministry decides and can complete a whole Switch itself; a Switch lead is accountable but holds nothing. Asym's default help is coaching on the ministry's own screen, which the ministry drives, with Hide values on and no recording, transcription, AI notetaker, remote control or file exchange outside the product. Hands-on help comes only through ministry-approved, time-limited Asym help access that never decides, commits, uploads or downloads.
- **Practice (Q6).** Practice runs write nothing live; dress rehearsals commit into a short-lived Practice copy in a separate Approved Practice environment.
- **Legacy tail (Q7, Q8).** Tail gifts stay silent to donors and honest to staff; tail donors are invited to Move and are ended only with notice by one End date; no double charge and no catch-up.
- **Consent (Q9).** Imported restrictions commit first and only tighten; each email list moves, stays or retires; Adopted email permissions are created only through a moved list's published Consent adoption disposition, need evidence that meets the rule for where the person lives, keep that evidence and start on Adoption hold.
- **People and households (Q10).** People first; households only from evidence; one holder per email; one legal donor per gift; no automatic merge.
- **Mapping (Q11).** Rule-based matching, Remembered mappings per Tenant and source, a Value-mapping table; no AI and no outside importer.
- **Everyday imports (Q12).** People imports with per-column Write modes (Fill empty only by default) and Gift files that open draft Phase 15 gift batches. Everyday imports grant nothing and send nothing: Gift-file rows are treated as ordinary current gifts, not the held import kind, and Phase 15 issues their receipts and thank-yous only once a person posts the batch.
- **Giving history (Q13).** Gift by gift through Phase 13's adoption command; complete Fixed gift facts or a Waiting row; no defaults; a Reader readiness check before the first commit.
- **Other record families (Q14).** Each family only into its owner's protected home, tiered by source category, never by reading content; one File safety check with Asym's own malware scan.
- **Retention and size (Q15).** Time-boxed Retention classes, Verify your copy, Legal hold (placed or released by a Run the Switch holder or by Asym's named legal role, which never reads content), erasure inside held files, a Size profile and streamed downloads.
- **Custom fields (Q16).** Field drafts created by a person from a Switch's mapping step through Phase 11's batch command, and new options added deliberately in a Switch only, while everyday imports use existing fields and options only; sensitive identifiers refused.
- **Website and old links (Q17).** Content on its own ADR-0173 track; one owner outcome per old link; Kept giving links served in place; a Staged domain move in which the main website moves only after a Domain readiness check.
- **Import reversal (Q18).** One Prepare reversal page with three groups: Can be reversed now, Needs its owner's correction and Stays as it is. Asym itself reverses only People results that pass Q18's checks (through Phase 9) and, through Phase 23's plan, website drafts; owners correct money, giving history, notes, files and custom fields; People results that fail a check, restrictions, opt-outs, suppression evidence and Retired designations stay as they are. Each import is reversed at most once, within its 30-day Import reversal window (from commit, or from go-live for a Switch run).
- **Data Tools (Q19).** One Mission Control area with Imports, Switch and Remembered mappings tabs, shortcuts where staff already work, one status vocabulary and a derived Switch task list.
- **Permissions (Q20).** One Phase 12 Data Tools row with View, Manage and Admin levels; Held for review; per-batch authority re-checks; Hide values; no interim access.
- **Governance (Q21).** The Phase 28 D17 engineering defaults are self-import profile configuration only; every imported file gets Asym's own malware scan; only the founder relaxes a ratified rule.

## Non-goals

- Partial or side-by-side switching, named vendor profiles, API pulls from other systems (Phase 31), TntConnect files and MPDX money copies, child sponsorship, and AI-assisted mapping.
- Any processor coordination, vault transfer, card or bank number handling, or proof that a legacy executor stopped; those stay with the Phase 13 and 16 owners.
- Creating collections, active funds or consent from mapping; bulk deletes, merges or gift changes by import; pledge uploads; automatic change-of-address services.
- Publishing website content, activating routes or domains, issuing Giving addresses, or porting text-to-give numbers.
- Changing any owner's business rule. Owner requirements are requests to those owners; Phase 30 never acquires their authority.

## Rollout And Turning Kinds Off

Data Tools stays switched off and default-deny until Phase 12 can grant the Data Tools permissions (O102). Each import kind (People import, Gift file, Switch) has its own kill switch; the area appears only when at least one kind is on for a ministry. No Switch is offered until the Practice environment is Approved and Phase 17 staff notices exist (O96): a Switch's Start action appears only to holders of Run the Switch while the Switch kind is on for the ministry and Asym's Start preconditions in the design hold. A started Switch cannot be scheduled until the further scheduling gates in the design hold, and no Switch is offered to a ministry with recurring giving until O12, O31 and O36–O40 are live.

Turning a kind off stops new starts of that kind and hides its entry points; its running imports stop after the current batch as Needs attention and can be resumed once the kind is on again, and every import record stays readable. Prepare reversal stays available within its window while a kind is off; import reversal prepare and confirm have their own off switch, per ministry or globally: while it is off, no import reversal page can be prepared or confirmed, and a running import reversal stops after its current batch and stays resumable for its remaining items. Turning a kind or import reversal off never deletes committed history, Source Record Bindings, receipts, restrictions or audit evidence, and recovery proceeds forward through owner corrections.

## Authorization Boundary

This change records intended behavior only. It authorizes no implementation, no tracker backlog, no tracker label change and no activation in any environment. Implementation tickets require a separate `/to-ticket` authorization.
