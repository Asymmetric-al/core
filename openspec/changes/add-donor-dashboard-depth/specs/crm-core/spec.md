## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Phase25 Narrow Personal Contact And Recognition Projections

CRM SHALL expose only the explicitly admitted personal contact mutation and own-employee/DAF-advisor reads through the existing owners and current projection floor. P13 retains immutable contribution legal-donor facts; P14 recognition/matching never takes ownership of them. A record relationship does not confer private access.

#### Scenario: Contact clear and result

- **WHEN** a permitted contact clear commits but later page refresh fails
- **THEN** the committed result remains authoritative and old fallback text cannot resurrect the value

#### Scenario: Matching with no origin gift

- **WHEN** an own-employee record is permitted but no original legal gift is visible
- **THEN** the independent matching read remains usable without fake financial records, payer receipt or coworker disclosure

#### Scenario: Rare projection absent

- **WHEN** no relevant rare record is admitted or presence is cold unknown
- **THEN** ordinary UI has no rare-feature artifacts while an explicitly attempted route retains truthful safe state

### Requirement: Phase25 Newsletter Requests Do Not Enroll External Subscribers

Before the Q25 consumer is dispatched, the P32 owner SHALL implement and qualify the exact request-only exclusion already required by EX11–EX16. The request occurrence and supplied contact details SHALL NOT cause automatic external contact/list insertion, subscribe/resubscribe, suppression reset, consent creation or consent/export admission. P23 retains the request occurrence, P17 its finite message purpose and local presentation, and P6 its transport evidence. None of those facts establishes external enrollment or verification. Any later external action requires its own independently qualified owner authority; this extension does not authorize or require the entire future newsletter-synchronization feature.

#### Scenario: A newsletter request is accepted and handed off

- **WHEN** a guest or signed-in visitor submits the qualified request and its provider or local handoff completes
- **THEN** the request and handoff facts remain distinct from external subscription and consent state
- **AND** no automatic external list/contact insertion, subscription or suppression reset occurs

#### Scenario: A request is revisited, corrected or replayed

- **WHEN** provider events, Read, Archive, Copy, correction-as-new-request or duplicate/out-of-order delivery occur
- **THEN** they do not establish enrollment, create unrelated contact records or expand consent/export rights
- **AND** source, message, delivery and engagement truth retain their original owner and lifetime boundaries
