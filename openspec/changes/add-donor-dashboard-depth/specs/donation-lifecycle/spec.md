## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Phase25 Donor Records Commands And Documents Preserve Source Meanings

The donor portal SHALL compose canonical financial, recurring, credential and document owners. Original Gift amount, requested amount, effective monetary giving, recognition, expectations and document coverage remain distinct. New-gift preference is not a provider billing default. Replacement never implies removal; Remove requires current all-live-dependency qualification and same-operation provider reconciliation.

#### Scenario: Annual History download

- **WHEN** a donor downloads a qualified annual History view
- **THEN** the complete reviewed subject/issuer/currency/civil-period/measure/filter descriptor is preserved, with original and current-giving amounts independently labeled

#### Scenario: Uncertain credential removal

- **WHEN** a provider response is lost after an accepted removal
- **THEN** the original operation and new-use fence remain until exact source proof; no fresh detach or default promotion is inferred

#### Scenario: DAF and IRA documents

- **WHEN** received DAF attribution and an IRA gift are both admitted
- **THEN** DAF awareness remains outside personal legal giving and ordinary advisor statements; IRA money and its separately qualified acknowledgment retain their own source purpose and case
