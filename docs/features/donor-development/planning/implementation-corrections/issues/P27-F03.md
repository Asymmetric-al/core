## What to build

Record actual presented terms and occurrence precision separately from trusted record time; one deliberate save may record an earlier presentation plus reply. Distinguish no answer, partial/conditional, deferred, declined and staff withdrawal; bind speaker/represented organization/known capacity without promoting the latest contact reply to definitive organizational agreement. Keep independently answerable invitations separate and coherent requests together; installments/designations never manufacture extra requests. Ship moderated comprehension and full real-browser accessibility/retry cases.
