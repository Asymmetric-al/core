## What to build

Implement the qualified P9-owned update/encounter source and bounded P12 create/read/history/correct/restrict capability consumption as one vertical behavior, with P27 development meaning. Render a clearly shared composer and saved entry in the existing staff context and narrow missionary projection. Keep a qualified private-note destination separate and truthfully labeled. General updates require no invented encounter; two contributions about one actual encounter retain separate words and one typed occurrence. Enforce exact tenant, partnership, source, fields, ongoing audience and independently granted earlier-history authority at commands, direct database paths and current result recovery. Save state/history/audit/receipt and required local intent atomically, derive attribution server-side, and preserve context-bound drafts after slow/lost results without persistent sensitive offline drafts.

## Context and contracts

Existing admin notes service is native but uses general standard/restricted notes; it is not D26 shared history. The missionary activity command still inserts donor_activities after donor ownership checks and stamps now. Current memory/mocked unit tests do not prove transactional audience/history authorization. Existing P9 timeline #617 and projection #622 are OPEN; their old bodies do not themselves supply D26 eligibility. This slice extends the owner source; it must not build a second diary or convert private notes.

Current authority, tenant/field/history-safe read and write behavior, atomic source history/receipt/required intent, truthful lost-response recovery and applicable two-session concurrency proof accompany this slice. Base UI/base-maia/Zinc, keyboard/screen-reader/mobile/zoom behavior and slow/failing network recovery accompany every reached screen. These are in-slice delivery obligations under REQ27-Q01–Q14, not deferred to final qualification. Terminal Q tickets prove composition and release evidence.

## Scope and non-goals

- Migrating private/imported/care messages into shared history
- Full Phase28 workspace, personal journal, new ACL/team engine, attachments or all-collaborator broadcasting
- Exchange-recency calculation, ratings, compulsory follow-up or coaching

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC27-C01-01: A missionary can decline to write and continue ordinary permitted use without penalty, required stage/task, activity report or coaching enrollment; staff cannot configure such coercion through this lane.
- [ ] AC27-C01-02: Saving or reading an update leaves managed-work admission, pause/closure, source obligations and staff status unchanged; a gift handoff cannot be completed by a note.
- [ ] AC27-C01-03: An independently selected personal follow-up is created once through its source. Its failure or cancellation neither fails nor duplicates an already accepted update.
- [ ] AC27-C02-01: Existing private/imported/internal/care/location-sensitive/financial fixtures remain protected after activation, import, refresh and shared-history migration.
- [ ] AC27-C02-02: The private destination discloses its actual reader scope, and its absence leaves a clear unavailable/cancel option with no shared save.
- [ ] AC27-C02-03: A newly authored eligible update can be saved directly in its qualified shared context without a repeated sharing checkbox or supervisory approval.
- [ ] AC27-C03-01: Two ministries sharing one church and two tenants cannot see or write each other’s updates or protected audience metadata.
- [ ] AC27-C03-02: A new assignee gains no earlier history from assignment alone; an independently history-authorized collaborator sees only the qualified earlier slice with the audience explanation.
- [ ] AC27-C03-03: Revocation before acceptance blocks the write; revocation after acceptance blocks subsequent unauthorized body/result disclosure while preserving accepted history. Both serialized orders are demonstrated.
- [ ] AC27-C03-04: Users can identify the exact partnership and permitted audience before saving; a changed or unavailable audience does not silently broaden the destination or disclose restricted names.
- [ ] AC27-C04-01: Staff and missionary projections resolve the same permitted source update; two accounts of one meeting preserve distinct authorship and a single encounter, without copied editable transcripts.
- [ ] AC27-C04-02: Caller-forged tenant/actor/author and cross-tenant/context/source references are rejected without mutation or protected diagnostic disclosure.
- [ ] AC27-C04-03: Actual database roles cannot retarget origin or author through direct mutation, view, RPC or service paths; authorized source correction remains attributable, and destructive relationship removal does not erase history.
- [ ] AC27-C04-04: Concurrent edit or policy narrowing is revalidated at acceptance; stale intent cannot overwrite accepted history or publish beyond current authority.
- [ ] AC27-C05-01: Failure of required audit/history/outcome persistence leaves no accepted update; an identical retry accepts one effect, and changed payload/context under that identity conflicts.
- [ ] AC27-C05-02: A lost reply recovers the accepted update once; a retry after read revocation returns no protected stored body or event-existence detail.
- [ ] AC27-C05-03: An accepted update with delayed projection remains truthfully accepted/pending, and replay repairs the permitted derivative once without another update, encounter, notification or permission grant.
- [ ] AC27-C05-04: Save-versus-revoke/narrow and competing edits are exercised in both applicable orders using actual source/database boundaries.
- [ ] AC27-C06-01: An ordinary eligible update takes one deliberate save; the user can identify audience and correction/follow-through options without another form or approval.
- [ ] AC27-C06-02: A draft for A cannot submit into B after a record/role switch; a late A response cannot erase B’s draft or text entered after A’s submission.
- [ ] AC27-C06-03: Slow/lost saves, session loss and unavailable audience/history show distinct states and permit safe recovery without false Saved feedback or duplicate content.
- [ ] AC27-C06-04: No automatic read receipt, all-collaborator broadcast, pressure to acknowledge, offline publication or newly exposed donor/public field follows from saving.
