## What to build

Use exact source next-work applicability and a qualified shared task/owned exception for outstanding Request obligations. Close or withdraw only the chosen Request; keep other invitations, promised support and independent hosting/finance work intact. Owner departure narrows disclosure and yields eligible reassignment or a discoverable source exception; genuinely finished records need no artificial task or replacement historical owner. Permit actual late facts without reopening partnership lifecycle, reviving ended effects or inferring access from assignment. Expose complete bounded outstanding-source readiness to partnership/appeal close.

## Context and contracts
