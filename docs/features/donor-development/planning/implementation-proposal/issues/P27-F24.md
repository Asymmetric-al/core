## What to build

Present recording, physical handling/deposit and bank clearance as separate facts. Support finance-before-transfer and transfer-before-recording without forced order. Record lost/returned/disputed/rejected/mistaken dispositions with exact remaining next work; generic task completion or exception label cannot establish Recorded by finance. Preserve independent handling through appeal closure/member removal, staff departure/anonymization and batch archive/carry-forward; genuinely resolved exceptions end applicable work. P13 reversals/NSF change current finance projection without recreating old custody or replacement gift. Expose qualified same-source responsibility recovery and bounded projection retries with clear accepted-versus-delayed status. Restore against later posting/correction/access/disposal before resuming; prove real staff distinguish all dimensions.

## Context and contracts

Use current report/route/financial authorities independently, race recipient loss against capture/reassignment/completion/result reads, and reassign through source commands rather than a copied appeal queue. C-01 remains an external gate for financial-correction execution, not ordinary handling.

Current authority, tenant/field/history-safe read and write behavior, atomic source history/receipt/required intent, truthful lost-response recovery and applicable two-session concurrency proof accompany this slice. Base UI/base-maia/Zinc, keyboard/screen-reader/mobile/zoom behavior and slow/failing network recovery accompany every reached screen. These are in-slice delivery obligations under REQ27-Q01–Q14, not deferred to final qualification. Terminal Q tickets prove composition and release evidence.
