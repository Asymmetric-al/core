## What to build

Qualify actual conversations, real meeting exchanges and relevant human replies in the exact partnership; expose manual occurrence capture and existing qualified communication references without copying messages. Count short declines and inbound replies without prior outbound. Never count call launch, voicemail, calendar metadata, passive opens or generic saves. Calculate over complete permitted source history rather than a display page, rederive backward on correction/restriction/dedupe, retain unknown/date-only facts and show Latest dated exchange when undated evidence prevents a definitive latest assertion. Present source type, date precision and next permitted action in both native views, without performance judgment or automatic contact.

## Context and contracts

Current CRM model maps lastTouchAt to donor.updated_at; detail activity history is capped at 50 with a created-at/epoch fallback; missionary record activity uses save time. P27 must introduce its exact named metric without silently redefining generic lastTouchAt. Current activity-cent formatting was already fixed in merged #1991 at origin/develop and is not new work here.

Current authority, tenant/field/history-safe read and write behavior, atomic source history/receipt/required intent, truthful lost-response recovery and applicable two-session concurrency proof accompany this slice. Base UI/base-maia/Zinc, keyboard/screen-reader/mobile/zoom behavior and slow/failing network recovery accompany every reached screen. These are in-slice delivery obligations under REQ27-Q01–Q14, not deferred to final qualification. Terminal Q tickets prove composition and release evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC27-C10-01: A sent personal email updates outreach only; a source-qualified human response updates exchange recency using occurrence evidence, including a brief decline and an inbound without recorded outbound.
- [ ] AC27-C10-02: Thank-you outreach needing no answer generates no pending-response/overdue warning; a short/negative response is not disqualified.
- [ ] AC27-C10-03: Call attempts, voicemail, scheduled/no-show meetings, passive attendance, delivery/open signals, automated/uncertain replies and generic completed activities do not create exchange evidence.
- [ ] AC27-C10-04: One truthful manually recorded conversation is sufficient without transcript, extra approval, meaningfulness checkbox or compulsory next task.
- [ ] AC27-C11-01: The qualifying latest exchange beyond the first 50 display rows is found; restricting/correcting the newest event produces the truthful earlier or uncertain result.
- [ ] AC27-C11-02: Historical entry, date-only travel, leap/date boundaries and unknown dates retain precision; no created-at/epoch fallback, negative age or future plan appears as completed exchange.
- [ ] AC27-C11-03: An undated qualified event is retained and prevents an unsupported definitive latest-date/aging claim while independently known dated facts remain usable.
- [ ] AC27-C11-04: Empty, filtered, incomplete, loading, unavailable and undated states are distinguishable without disclosing hidden history or using Never contacted.
- [ ] AC27-C12-01: The same permitted source event yields consistent exchange meaning in staff and missionary views; two narratives/retries/imports count one encounter.
- [ ] AC27-C12-02: Cross-tenant/other-ministry/church-affiliation/selected-participant-only paths cannot update another partnership or disclose its date, source or count.
- [ ] AC27-C12-03: Body expiry retains only independently permitted body-free evidence; source revocation protects all unauthorized derivatives and recovered outputs.
- [ ] AC27-C12-04: Old recency, absent reply and a new reply to closed work create no automatic solicitation, stage/lifecycle change or missionary penalty and add no public/donor input.
- [ ] AC27-P33-01: **Given** a personal email or thank-you is sent without a reply being required, **when** delivery, opening and a later genuine human exchange are recorded, **then** outreach updates independently; only qualified exchange evidence updates exchange recency, and no inferred pending-response/overdue warning is created.
- [ ] AC27-P33-02: **Given** a relevant human inbound arrives without a stored prior outbound and may be a brief decline, **when** its source outcome is qualified, **then** it can count as an exchange without message pairing, length, sentiment or financial threshold; automated or uncertain reply evidence alone cannot count.
- [ ] AC27-P33-03: **Given** call attempts, voicemail, booked/cancelled/no-show meetings, passive attendance and an actual manually recorded conversation exist, **when** exchange recency is computed, **then** only actual qualified exchange evidence contributes; calendar/task completion alone creates none.
- [ ] AC27-P33-04: **Given** two narratives or repeated transport/import/share/edit refer to one underlying meeting, **when** staff and missionary permitted views display it, **then** one actual encounter remains with distinct attributed contributions and no duplicated logging step or event count.
- [ ] AC27-P33-05: **Given** history includes backdated, date-only, unknown-date, future-plan and invalid future-completed values across timezone travel, **when** source dates and recency are displayed, **then** actual precision is retained; unknown never becomes save time/epoch or reliable aging and future planned/untrue completion does not become actual exchange.
- [ ] AC27-P33-06: **Given** the latest qualifying exchange lies outside the first fifty display rows, **when** the latest source fact is found then corrected/restricted/merged, **then** source-complete recency finds it and subsequently derives an earlier or unknown truthful result rather than a stale permanent maximum.
- [ ] AC27-P33-07: **Given** a user supplies another Tenant, church-affiliation-only link, different ministry purpose or merely selected participant, **when** a contribution or projection is requested, **then** no other partnership is updated and protected date/type/source/count information is not disclosed.
- [ ] AC27-P33-08: **Given** a source body expires or source authority is revoked, **when** recency, summaries, source links and exports are requested, **then** only separately qualified permitted body-free facts remain; copied bodies or church access cannot bypass source restrictions.
- [ ] AC27-P33-09: **Given** a missionary chooses not to log or an actual reply arrives during paused/closed work, **when** the ordinary workspace and relationship are evaluated, **then** normal use remains available without penalty, score or invented task; lifecycle and actual consent/obligations remain unchanged.
- [ ] AC27-P33-10: **Given** the user encounters empty, filtered, incomplete, undated, loading or unavailable history, **when** the compact relationship context is displayed, **then** each admitted state is understandable without Never contacted claims or revealing private event existence.
- [ ] AC27-P33-11: **Given** a contribution save is slow, loses its response or races a scope change, **when** the user recovers it, **then** permitted draft context and one accepted source intent remain, with no silent offline publication, duplicate encounter or false success.
- [ ] AC27-P33-12: **Given** keyboard/screen-reader/touch/mobile/RTL/long-name/low-bandwidth users complete the flow, **when** they record, explain and correct an exchange, **then** instructions, source links, focus, contrast, reflow, dates and status remain usable without address forms, transcripts, duplicate logs or meaningfulness checkboxes.
- [ ] AC27-P33-13: **Given** representative staff and missionaries inspect sent outreach, real exchange, unknown coverage and agreed follow-up examples, **when** they interpret the UI without coaching, **then** they distinguish all four meanings; any unsafe confusion blocks rollout expansion until corrected and retested.
- [ ] AC27-P33-14: **Given** donor checkout, donor account or public content is used, **when** internal exchange recency changes or becomes old, **then** those surfaces gain no field/step/internal disclosure and no new contact is triggered merely by age or missing reply.
