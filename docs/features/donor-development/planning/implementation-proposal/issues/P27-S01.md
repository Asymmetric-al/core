## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A ministry with zero current participants exists and is selectable under current permitted exact Tenant/Legal Entity scope without a Page, gift, email, support binding or login.
- [ ] One Party may participate in several ministries, several Parties may participate in one ministry, and changing spouse/team/leadership/login names does not replace ministry identity.
- [ ] Prospective add/end/correct records exact half-open membership interval and immutable source evidence; overlapping concurrent membership intervals in one exact scope cannot both commit, adjacent intervals behave correctly.
- [ ] Current principal/field/history/purpose authority applies before list/count/detail/result and at acceptance; real cross-Tenant/entity, caller-supplied actor/scope, raw grants/RLS/RPC and privileged-service attacks cannot add forbidden facts or disclose restricted participants.
- [ ] Membership alone creates no responsibility, task, notification, Page display/edit, Support Workspace/supporter identity or finance grant. Former member with independent current grant and member without grant receive the correct independent policy outcome.
- [ ] Retirement or owner-certified identity correction preserves source identity/history and exposes current eligibility without retargeting existing released or accepted downstream facts; actual source changes can be consumed prospectively.
- [ ] Lost response/retry/ABA/revocation race leaves one intended mutation and original actor/time; before-commit failures leave no partial source/history/receipt.
- [ ] Evidence-based migration and rollback preserve restrictions and history; ambiguous profile-to-many-ministry cases remain unresolved, no inferred membership or money/attention effect.
- [ ] Keyboard/screen-reader/single-pointer/320px/400% zoom/RTL/international-name workflows allow staff to distinguish Associated with ministry from all independently granted rights; restricted alias never falls back to legal name/location.
