## What to build

Implement deliberate pause and reschedule in source/detail/shared-work flow with one current review occurrence and stable source task identity. Save date-only plus authoritative Tenant IANA zone, due instant and conversion provenance; today valid, new past/nonexistent date rejected, absent zone requires qualified organization setup. Due at first valid instant of saved local date and overdue next local date, independent of DST length/travel/later zone change. Controlled timezone-rule reconciliation preserves authoritative meaning. No routine one-hour offset, default cadence or automatic lifecycle transitions.

## Context and contracts

Staff can pause and reschedule work without losing obligations or inventing review completion or donor duties.

Current authority, tenant/field/history-safe read and write behavior, atomic source history/receipt/required intent, truthful lost-response recovery and applicable two-session concurrency proof accompany this slice. Base UI/base-maia/Zinc, keyboard/screen-reader/mobile/zoom behavior and slow/failing network recovery accompany every reached screen. These are in-slice delivery obligations under REQ27-Q01–Q14, not deferred to final qualification. Terminal Q tickets prove composition and release evidence.

## Scope and non-goals

- No new CRM identity, authorization engine, task/notification platform or shadow finance ledger.
- No duplicate implementation of already owned request, encounter, Party merge or finance command.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Repeated pause creates one review occurrence; changed meaning under same key conflicts.
- [ ] Reschedule changes date but creates no completed review; held work retains independent promises and history.
- [ ] Today/DST 23h/25h/nonexistent date/missing zone/travel/zone-change cases preserve stated date-only meaning.
- [ ] Reading attention, gift receipt, elapsed time or task expiry neither resumes/closes work nor claims supporter nonperformance.
- [ ] Stage/list edits while paused are allowed by their qualified commands and do not satisfy review.

### AC27-P02-04

**Given** two commands use different labels or command IDs for the same ordinary meaning, **when** they race with one another or with a pause, **then** one authoritative ordinary meaning remains and permitted reuse does not reset the existing work.

### AC27-P08-01

**Given** authorized staff choose a date and pause current work, **when** the same save is repeated, **then** one current review occurrence and at most one source task identity are retained; changed meaning under the same key conflicts.

### AC27-P08-03

**Given** staff need a different date without conducting the review, **when** they reschedule the current occurrence, **then** the schedule changes with attributable history and no completed-review fact is created.

### AC27-P08-06

**Given** staff intend meaningful future work but do not choose a date, **when** they attempt the dated-pause action, **then** a clear date requirement is shown without fabricating a cadence, donor action or completed review.

### AC27-P09-01

**Given** the Tenant has a valid authoritative work timezone and today is selected, **when** staff save a pause, **then** the visible chosen date/zone and stored due interpretation agree and today is accepted.

### AC27-P09-02

**Given** a new ordinary review date is in the past or the selected local date does not exist, **when** staff save, **then** a linked understandable error retains permitted input and no substitute date or review is accepted.

### AC27-P09-03

**Given** the organization zone is missing or invalid, **when** staff choose a review date, **then** the flow requires the qualified setup/repair path and never silently uses browser timezone.

### AC27-P09-04

**Given** a review spans a twenty-three-hour or twenty-five-hour day or crosses hemispheres, **when** due and overdue views are evaluated, **then** due begins at the first valid instant of the saved local date and overdue at the following local date, without UTC-midnight or one-hour drift.

### AC27-P09-05

**Given** staff travel, the assignee changes or the Tenant changes its work timezone after acceptance, **when** the review is viewed and processed, **then** its saved date/zone meaning remains unchanged unless a deliberate qualified schedule correction occurs.

### AC27-P09-06

**Given** timezone rules affecting an accepted future date change, **when** the controlled reconciliation runs, **then** the authoritative interpretation and provenance are reconciled consistently and reader disagreement cannot silently trigger duplicate attention.
