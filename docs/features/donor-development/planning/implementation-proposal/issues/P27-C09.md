## What to build

Use the shared partnership model with organization Party identity and existing typed relationships/contact capacities. Present pastor, committee, finance/signatory, representative and missionary distinctions and sending vs supporting facts. Preserve old response/encounter attribution when contacts change; permitted current assignments/Requests may change deliberately. Use existing source context plus deliberate shared tasks for known budget, visit, renewal and leadership facts. Compose church legal giving, member remittance attribution and personal member gifts through qualified independent readers without conflating the totals or granting contact/privacy rights. Narrow foundation proposal context remains ordinary Requests/documents/dates.

## Context and contracts

Current relationship service reads donor/missionary/profile/pledge/member-care activity collections and builds a mapped view; it is not proof of the formal P9 organization graph/contact capacity or D26 audience. Open #625 owns relationship management and #736 member-remittance credit fan-out; consume their exact contracts instead of recreating them in a church-only CRM.

Current authority, tenant/field/history-safe read and write behavior, atomic source history/receipt/required intent, truthful lost-response recovery and applicable two-session concurrency proof accompany this slice. Base UI/base-maia/Zinc, keyboard/screen-reader/mobile/zoom behavior and slow/failing network recovery accompany every reached screen. These are in-slice delivery obligations under REQ27-Q01–Q14, not deferred to final qualification. Terminal Q tickets prove composition and release evidence.

## Scope and non-goals

- Separate church CRM, annual recurrence generator or event booking
- Combining church and member gifts
- Grant management, signing or planned giving

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC27-C33-01: Church, staff, missionaries and contact capacities remain distinct identities; sending does not imply financial support or personal donor access.
- [ ] AC27-C33-02: Changing a pastor/committee representative preserves the actual historical participants and old response attribution, while current work can be deliberately updated.
- [ ] AC27-C33-03: Church gift, member remittance and personal member gift do not merge into one giving or recognition total; shared mailbox/committee access grants no member solicitation.
- [ ] AC27-C33-04: Known budget/renewal/visit context and nonfinancial Requests can guide deliberate next work without automatic annual reminders, event booking or a separate church CRM.
