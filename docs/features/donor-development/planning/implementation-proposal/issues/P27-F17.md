## What to build

Consume owner-qualified Party identity repair. Coalesce compatible current memberships and retain both origins; incompatible currently applicable Include/Remove/Exclude becomes bounded member conflict with authorized source resolution. Do not read superseded historical removal as perpetual exclusion. Unmerge, source-shell purge and later Return/access/disposal reconcile current restrictions before actionable use. Show a clear safe repair route with no hidden-membership existence leak, duplicate workaround Party or whole-appeal freeze.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC27-F40-03: Given two source Parties with compatible or conflicting current appeal intentions, when performing an owner-qualified merge, then Qualified Party merge of duplicate compatible membership avoids double counting and retains both origins; conflicting current inclusion/exclusion/removal becomes a bounded member conflict, not actionable survivor-wins or whole-appeal freeze.
- [ ] AC27-F41-03: Given merged membership with later Return, exclusion, access change or disposal, when unmerging or purging an allowed source shell, then Unmerge after later Return/exclusion/access change and source-shell purge safeguards preserve current restrictions/history. An old removal superseded by valid Return is not misread as an eternal exclusion.
- [ ] AC27-F47-01: Given a backup older than exclusion, revocation, merge and disposal, when restoring and attempting reads or outward worker admission, then Restore a backup before later exclusions, revocation, merge or disposal. Affected readers/workers remain fenced until reconciled; no erased content or ended outreach returns. Roll-forward preserves newly accepted data.
