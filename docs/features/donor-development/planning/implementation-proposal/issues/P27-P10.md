## What to build

Register finite P27 due-review producer/recipient profile with Phase17 presentation.source_actionable_then_recent_90d@1. Use exact occurrence/schedule/responsibility generation/applicability/recipient-role-surface dedupe; re-prove eligibility at release and fence obsolete prepared envelopes. Staff profile activates as complete task/context/action/attention bundle; missionary profile remains reserved until exact P28 bundle is qualified, no copied staff task/route. ## Context and contracts

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Same staff occurrence has one admitted recipient-role-surface row across tasks/context/attention, safe authenticated link and no duplicate task.
- [ ] Read cannot hide unresolved review; source end controls90-day authorized Recent with no fictitious read/recall.
- [ ] Two workers versus reschedule/resume/end/revocation in both orders emit no obsolete active item or retargeted prepared envelope.
- [ ] Protected pause reasons, identities and locations never copy into preview/count/export/log or donor/public surface.
- [ ] Zero/indeterminate recipient yields source repair; no email/SMS/push/default escalation/snooze introduced.
- [ ] More than15minutes after later eligible due/current schedule-recipient boundary signals named platform owner without retry-age reset; Reserved-to-Live emits no historical attention.

### AC27-P08-04

**Given** the due date arrives or a notification is read/archived or a gift is received, **when** the external or engagement event is processed, **then** cultivation remains paused, the review remains unresolved, and no automatic donor contact or source completion occurs.

### AC27-P10-01

**Given** the lead, creator, Party representative and finance owner are different people, **when** review responsibility and due attention resolve, **then** only the qualified current lead or authorized explicit review assignee receives the appropriate source-backed work; other identities receive nothing without independent qualification.

### AC27-P10-02

**Given** a currently eligible explicit review assignee exists, **when** the partnership lead changes, **then** the explicit review responsibility remains until a deliberate qualified handoff changes it; no duplicate recipient is inferred.

### AC27-P10-03

**Given** an enabled staff or missionary recipient profile is used, **when** the user opens the task and attention, **then** each route resolves the same source review and allowed action with a working role-safe authenticated link; incomplete or reserved profiles cannot emit substitute attention.

### AC27-P10-04

**Given** a user reads an actionable review notification, **when** they revisit work or try to archive it, **then** the unresolved work remains actionable and archive is unavailable; after source ending it is visible only within the authorized 90-day Recent ceiling.

### AC27-P10-05

**Given** zero eligible recipients, an indeterminate resolver or revocation immediately before release occurs, **when** the worker processes the review, **then** no unqualified recipient receives attention and an authorized source repair exception remains; restored access does not revive an ended engagement.

### AC27-P10-07

**Given** a protected reason, restricted identity or worker location is associated with the source, **when** task previews, notification payloads, logs, counts, exports and links are examined under each admitted role, **then** only independently allowed minimal metadata appears; public and donor surfaces receive no review context.

### AC27-P10-08

**Given** the optional email provider or Studio is unavailable, **when** a qualified internal-only review becomes due, **then** native source work and permitted in-product attention remain functional without a substitute email or duplicate Studio effect.

### AC27-P32-06

**Given** backfill or a recipient/surface profile changes from Reserved to Live, **when** activation runs, **then** no historical attention is emitted; any eligible legacy-work reminder proves a distinct prospective qualified source transition.

### AC27-P32-08

**Given** an eligible due review lacks required local attention, **when** the delay passes 15 minutes from the later eligible due/schedule-recipient boundary, **then** the recorded platform-operations signal fires without retry-resetting age, substitute email or marking review complete.

### AC27-P32-09

**Given** a local materialization reaches typed terminal failure or a qualified responsibility resolver is zero/indeterminate, **when** operating recovery is evaluated, **then** the named owner receives an authorized source repair route and the original identity is reconciled without broadening access or routine SQL edits.
