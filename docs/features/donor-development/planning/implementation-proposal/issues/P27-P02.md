Add the bounded separate-purpose entry to the existing start/reuse flow. Collect concise actual outcome and why existing source records are insufficient, without routine approval, essay, purpose hierarchy or custom workflow engine. Invalid justifications provide the appropriate existing-work/request/task route; business purpose remains distinct from P12 processing purpose.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Changing staff, year, currency, stage list, deadline, label or privacy preference alone cannot create a second ordinary journey.
- [ ] A verified intended-user distinct continuing outcome can be recorded with concise reason and exact scope.
- [ ] Prayer and financial participation and several requests coexist without an automatic extra purpose, success stage or closure.
- [ ] Concurrent differently labelled submissions cannot evade canonical ordinary identity; hidden existing work never prompts a duplicate-purpose workaround.

### AC27-P02-02

**Given** a proposed additional enrollment differs only by staff, deadline, label, year, currency, confidentiality or stage list, **when** the proposal is evaluated, **then** it cannot create an independent journey on that basis; the permitted request/task/responsibility or existing-work path is explained.

### AC27-P02-03

**Given** an actual intended-user case requires a distinct continuing outcome that simpler source records cannot represent, **when** authorized staff deliberately admit that exception, **then** its separate purpose and concise reason are recorded without constructing a general purpose hierarchy or routine approval queue.
