## What to build

Deliver one settings→qualified facts→staff-only detail/list behavior. New/legacy tenants stay ratings Off and automatic review Off. Present editable scope/period and exactly three numeric bands per axis with two validated cutpoints; raw facts remain when unconfigured. Enabling analysis never creates tasks. Atomically accept immutable policy revisions/current activation generation/history/receipt with optimistic conflicts and current authority. Fence calculation admission, publication, reads, query predicates, exports and optional consumers at the authoritative generation; Off must succeed without a working preview/calculation service. Late responses cannot restore values. Disabled/unauthorized saved predicates explicitly require repair and cannot be dropped silently. Configuration/source changes establish fresh comparable baselines without manufactured drops; histories remain clearly historical and source-governed.
