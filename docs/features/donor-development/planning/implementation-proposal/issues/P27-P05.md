## What to build

Add expected-version source correction for a mistaken reference and consume source-certified identity repair separately. Record original reference/revision, trusted actor and reason. Presented asks, encounters, gifts, supporter intent and prior effects remain attributable to original owner context. Detect ordinary-identity collisions; use existing Party/source repair disposition without permission union, fake distinct purpose or generic Undo. Clearly separate mistaken reference from genuinely different future ministry/purpose/scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Correcting a permitted draft updates current reference while retaining original actor/source/reason/history.
- [ ] Accepted asks, encounters and finance facts do not change meaning; downstream correction uses their owners.
- [ ] Same-name ministries and cross-Tenant/entity proposals cannot retarget historic evidence.
- [ ] Ordinary collision retains both histories for qualified disposition, with no automatic permission union or fabricated purpose.
- [ ] Concurrent source/current-revision change rejects stale correction with usable nonrevealing recovery.

### AC27-P02-05

**Given** a Party correction would cause two ordinary enrollments to collide, **when** the existing source owner processes the correction, **then** no history, grants or accepted effects are silently unioned and no fake purpose is created; unresolved meaning remains explicit for qualified correction.

### AC27-P06-03

**Given** a draft relationship points to the wrong source and authorized correction is qualified, **when** staff correct it with the expected revision, **then** the current association is corrected with original reference, trusted actor and reason preserved.

### AC27-P06-04

**Given** an ask has already been presented or an encounter/gift accepted, **when** staff correct the enrollment reference, **then** those accepted source facts remain attributable to their original context; required downstream corrections use their owners.

### AC27-P06-05

**Given** two distinct same-name ministries or different Tenant/Legal Entity scopes are involved, **when** a move or source repair is proposed, **then** it cannot silently merge or retarget history; genuinely different future work uses explicit permitted disposition.
