## What to build

Build a P27 consumer projection over qualified financial owners, not a ledger fold duplicate. Bind subject/lens/scope/entity/original currency/period/cutoff/coverage tuple and show it. Compute count, effective monetary sum and period-specific recency from distinct eligible source gift roots; apply exact calendar-month boundaries and explicit as-of cutoff. Preserve source finalized return/date corrections, fee-cover treatment and precision, with all pending/promised/noncash/liquidation substitutes excluded. Known empty has F=0/M=0 and nonnumeric recency; incomplete or undated evidence invalidates only affected axes. Keep nonfinancial participation useful and broader organizational giving clearly broader than ministry-specific support.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC27-C15-01: One gift with three designations and replayed delivery counts one eligible occurrence and only its selected-scope amount; two genuine identical gifts remain two.
- [ ] AC27-C15-02: Legal giving and recognition—including DAF advisor, employer, household and overlapping roles—remain separate qualified lenses with no double-credit scalar.
- [ ] AC27-C15-03: Entity-wide giving is labeled correctly within a ministry partnership; unsupported purpose/campaign/recognition readers stay unavailable rather than guessed.
- [ ] AC27-C15-04: Changing screen, current household membership, name, staff assignment or an unrelated donor cannot alter authoritative attribution or reveal another scope.
- [ ] AC27-C16-01: USD, JPY and supported three-decimal amounts retain exact source units; processor costs, settlement conversion and tax benefits cannot move the original-currency band.
- [ ] AC27-C16-02: Pending handoffs, unposted ACH, promises, match expectancies, monthly equivalents, securities valuations and liquidation do not add monetary occurrences/amounts; an admitted posted provisional check follows source truth.
- [ ] AC27-C16-03: Prayer/hosting/introduction and recognition-only participation do not become a low-value relationship or adverse review solely from zero direct giving.
- [ ] AC27-C16-04: A source-finance fact change is consumed through its owner; rating commands cannot post money, issue receipts, fulfill commitments or mutate payment execution.
- [ ] AC27-C17-01: Known empty history produces frequency 0, amount 0 and nonnumeric no-qualifying-gift recency; incomplete/denied data does not produce those known-zero claims.
- [ ] AC27-C17-02: For qualified roots in the period, independently computed exact root count, eligible monetary sum and calendar-day recency agree, including split/replay cases.
- [ ] AC27-C17-03: A known older out-of-period gift is shown separately and never included in the period’s count/amount/recency.
- [ ] AC27-C17-04: An unresolved eligible gift date/amount invalidates every affected axis without erasing independently complete facts or pretending full coverage.
- [ ] AC27-C18-01: Leap-day/month-end fixtures produce the declared clamped lower boundary, exclude it, include the evaluation date and display matching inclusive dates.
- [ ] AC27-C18-02: Future-dated evidence cannot create a completed gift or negative recency; an open fiscal period is labeled to-date and excludes later dates.
- [ ] AC27-C18-03: Timezone travel and locale display do not shift source gift dates or fiscal boundaries; trailing months, fixed year, fiscal year and year-to-date stay distinct.
- [ ] AC27-C20-01: Partial/full return, void and date correction produce the exact new count/amount/date while retaining history and explicit correction cause; unresolved refunds do not reduce posted money.
- [ ] AC27-C20-02: Incomplete imports, permission loss, asynchronous recognition and source outages cannot become a zero/low band; independently complete axes remain accurate.
- [ ] AC27-C20-03: Deduplication or identity correction does not double count roots, overwrite financial provenance or manufacture an adverse review episode.
- [ ] AC27-C20-04: Known no gifts, inapplicable nonfinancial participation and missing source history are visibly distinct and independently testable.
