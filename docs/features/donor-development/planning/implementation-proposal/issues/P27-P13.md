## What to build

Compose current/closed partnership list/detail/report projections over authoritative enrollment and owner references. Apply permission before counts/ranking/filter/export and declare complete/partial/unavailable/known-empty honestly beyond page caps. Keep latest exchange in C03 owner, finance projections in financial reader owner, stage/current absence in stage owner. Reconciliation must move backward or unknown after source correction/redaction/merger. Preserve money currency/period/recognition and no shadow ledger or silentFX/unknownzero. Add real largest-Tenant/many-small fairness measurements, indexed source-complete pagination, export noninterference and current-disposition restore qualification for these views.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Relevant source fact beyond displayed cap is included or coverage is explicit; loaded page never pretends complete portfolio total.
- [ ] Requested/expected/committed/received/recognized/staff credit remain distinct; different currencies/periods are never silently summed; unavailable is not0.
- [ ] One gift/encounter source shown in several views counts once for its actual aggregate meaning.
- [ ] Correction/restriction/erasure rebuild may move recency backward or unknown; hidden facts do not affect permitted output/rank/export.
- [ ] Restore predating revocation/erasure/consumed effects reconciles source disposition before read/worker activation.
- [ ] Measure stated Tenant distributions and approve numeric budgets; no tenant starvation, capped completeness lie or uncontrolled permission-per-row scan.

### AC27-P31-01

**Given** the latest qualifying source fact or relevant obligation lies beyond all current display caps, **when** the complete projection is computed, **then** the admitted total or latest fact includes that evidence or explicitly reports incomplete coverage, never a falsely complete capped result.

### AC27-P31-02

**Given** finance source coverage is absent or amounts use different currencies/periods and recognition meanings, **when** a cultivation summary is displayed, **then** unknown is not zero and incomparable values are not summed, converted or represented as received cash.

### AC27-P31-03

**Given** one source gift or encounter appears in multiple allowed views, **when** organization totals and source identity are reconciled, **then** the source fact is counted once for its admitted aggregate meaning and remains a reference rather than another financial or encounter record.

### AC27-P31-04

**Given** the newest source fact is corrected, restricted, merged or erased, **when** derived views rebuild, **then** the result may move backward or become unknown; stale cached maxima and forbidden derivatives do not remain authoritative.

### AC27-P31-05

**Given** a protected fact differs between otherwise equivalent fixtures, **when** authorized summaries, ranks, filters and export contents are compared, **then** the restricted fact does not change admitted output unless an independently qualified aggregate disclosure exists.

### AC27-P31-06

**Given** staff change a ministry target, stage, lifecycle or relationship purpose, **when** source owners are reconciled, **then** gifts, commitments, Designations, consent, recurring support and publishing authority do not change by implication.

### AC27-P31-07

**Given** a reader fails or a claimed-complete projection disagrees with source evidence, **when** the authorized user opens the affected view, **then** it exposes incomplete/unavailable or pending reconciliation truth and a permitted owner resolution route instead of false zero or healthy status.

### AC27-P32-05

**Given** a restored snapshot predates revocation, erasure, schedule change, completed review or consumed delivery, **when** reads and workers resume, **then** current owner dispositions are reconciled first; forbidden content, revoked recipients, completed work and consumed effects cannot revive.

### AC27-P32-07

**Given** the declared largest Tenant, many small Tenants and concurrent interactive/batch work run together, **when** query plans, source scans and bounded recovery are measured, **then** approved numeric budgets and honest coverage are met and one overloaded Tenant cannot starve the others.

### AC27-Q06-01

- **GIVEN** two viewers have different source permissions and share a cached filter.
- **WHEN** both open list, counts, brief, export and history.
- **THEN** each receives only their current permitted facts and no cache or aggregate expands disclosure.

### AC27-Q06-02

- **GIVEN** only one page is loaded or financial coverage is missing.
- **WHEN** a total or coverage label renders.
- **THEN** the complete qualified total is used or the limitation is explicit; it never reports the page sum as complete or unknown as zero.
