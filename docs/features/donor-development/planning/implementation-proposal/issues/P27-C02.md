## What to build

Add source-owned attributed correction and restriction paths to the shared history journey, with actual/date-only/unknown occurrence precision separate from recorded time and departed author identity retained lawfully. Keep source facts usable on paused/closed work without reopening. Propagate current restriction to all reached metadata/derivatives and explain limits for already-read/exported material. Provide bounded indexed pagination, loading/unavailable/known-empty states, writer census and non-sharing backfill posture, stop-new-writing with qualified read/correction/restriction continuing, plus compatible reader/writer and restore proof. Implement the specified monitors through source recovery, not direct database repair.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC27-C07-01: Backdated, date-only and unknown occurrences remain distinguishable from recorded time across timezone changes; current owners do not replace departed authors.
- [ ] AC27-C07-02: Saving, sharing, viewing or correcting one meeting cannot add another encounter, change stage, complete work, send a message or count money.
- [ ] AC27-C07-03: Paused/closed contexts accept only source-permitted factual history/correction without reopening or reviving cancelled work; an actual current reply retains its own truthful date.
- [ ] AC27-C07-04: An authorized correction preserves prior lawful provenance and cannot silently rewrite another contributor’s account.
- [ ] AC27-C08-01: Protected source facts and their existence do not leak through enabled consumers, result recovery, cache entries, sort/count or diagnostic output after revocation.
- [ ] AC27-C08-02: A mistaken disclosure can be restricted/corrected through a qualified source action; downstream access stops, lawful audit remains, and delivered-copy limits are explained honestly.
- [ ] AC27-C08-03: Restoring backup data reapplies source restriction/erasure/expiry before readability and does not republish private content.
- [ ] AC27-C08-04: Technical logs and security audit retain required identifiers/outcomes without copying private bodies or becoming a second diary.
- [ ] AC27-C09-01: Native shared history operates with optional workflow/external-sync services unavailable and without a second diary or blanket shared-account permission.
- [ ] AC27-C09-02: Private retained fixtures stay private under old/new clients, activation, stop, roll-forward and restore; the stop action preserves authorized corrective recovery.
- [ ] AC27-C09-03: Multiple tenants, a long-history tenant, maximum admitted payload/page and concurrent permission changes meet declared numeric budgets with permission-safe bounded queries.
- [ ] AC27-C09-04: An unavailable or failed history query is never presented as an empty, healthy history.
