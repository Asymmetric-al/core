## What to build

Conduct and record candidate-bound integrated acceptance and bounded intended-user pilot for the complete approved Phase 27 experience. Reuse per-slice source/security/race and UX evidence, then prove the composed assignment→admission→Request→response→follow-up flow, deliberate lifecycle/stage/list changes, appeal/finance distinction, voluntary shared history, financial band Off/review meaning and bounded Auto choices. Include finance handlers and missionary contributors in their actual surfaces, unfamiliar users, assistive technology and mobile/slow-network contexts. Preserve the full stable requirement/story/AC/monitor trace into exact tests and release evidence; do not collapse unrun or gated work into a pass. Complete the explicit cross-source AC27-F61-06 qualification after the real intake-handling and appeal-lifecycle producers are available. Each producer retains its own safety and continuity tests; this composed proof must not gate ordinary nonfinancial appeal closure on full financial posting/clearance. Primary-qualify the exact full cross-journey cases AC27-P30-01, P10-06, P11-05, P12-02 and P12-06 using the real start/pause/review/end/reopen/stage/attention and admitted owner event producers. Earlier domain slices retain their complete reached-source implementations and local proofs; this allocation prevents fake future-state fixtures or circular producer dependencies.

## Context and contracts

This is cross-journey evidence and participant observation, not late implementation of a11y/security or a generic finish-everything ticket. Every producer already proves its source behavior and composed local controls. Q16 defines the pilot stop criterion: any unsafe interpretation blocks expansion until fixed and retested. C35/C36 require clear shared context/recency/rating meaning and current audience; A14/A18 require staff understanding of Apply/Keep/Use rules again/Off with the complete D30 trace. Planning publication and pure tests do not prove implementation or activation.

Current authority, tenant/field/history-safe read and write behavior, atomic source history/receipt/required intent, truthful lost-response recovery and applicable two-session concurrency proof accompany this slice. Base UI/base-maia/Zinc, keyboard/screen-reader/mobile/zoom behavior and slow/failing network recovery accompany every reached screen. These are in-slice delivery obligations under REQ27-Q01–Q14, not deferred to final qualification. Terminal Q tickets prove composition and release evidence.

## Scope and non-goals

- Redesign settled decisions or introduce new mandatory confirmations, reporting, quotas or donor-worth scores.
- Call synthetic walkthroughs user feedback or an axe pass WCAG conformance.
- Activate production, contact actual donors, or clear an upstream owner gate without authorization.
- Close missing source implementation/acceptance by administrative checklist.
