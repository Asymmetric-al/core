Research date: 2026-10-09. Decision under review: ordinary missionary-support cultivation attaches to the identified ministry, using the Phase-9-owned Ministry Assignment identity, rather than following whichever missionary person currently participates. This report covers authorization/tenant/entity/privacy/RLS/source disclosure/caches/revocation and their interactions with that choice; other review agents cover other categories. It does not ratify a staff–missionary sharing policy, implement a change, or prove a live vulnerability.

## Verdict and answer effect

**Retain D2.** A stable organization-owned ministry identity is safer than using a spouse, staff profile, mutable financial binding, or public Page as relationship ownership. The identity can outlive personnel while access changes independently. No inspected inherited contract requires abandoning that choice.

**Record a necessary clarification:** “The enrollment references the exact existing Ministry Assignment within its Tenant and Legal Entity. That reference, enrollment responsibility, participant membership, related church, Page contribution, financial support binding, and cultivation stage grant no new access. Every person’s current permitted view and action remains independently decided by Phases 3/10/12 and each referenced source owner.” This preserves already-ratified constraints; it is not a new product permission restriction.

**Do not treat D2 as approval of shared notes or historical access.** The precise staff–missionary shared-interaction policy is genuinely open. There is a real unreconciled textual conflict between P9’s blanket missionary exclusion of Party notes/activity and the adopted roadmap’s shared permission-scoped interaction direction. Resolve the exact exception with the owner before enabling sharing, not by silently assuming all ministry participants can see its history.

Classification: these are **durable patterns/accepted owner intent**. They are permanent constraints to consume, not optional implementation habits. Their ratification does not establish that current code fulfills them.

## The shared-interaction conflict remains open

- P9 `:901–908` says, verbatim, “party notes/activity never appear in any missionary-workspace projection.” P9 `:897–898` also structurally excludes member-care relations/private notes from the general timeline. It separately says missionary enumeration is limited to the permitted slice.
- `docs/prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md:128–138` requires one interaction log shared with staff under permission-scoped visibility and preserves supporter/referral limits. `docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:4169, 4181` requires one log and names P9 as interaction owner.
- `roadmap.md` Phase 27 Open decisions explicitly leaves shared interaction visibility to grooming. The immutable supplied v3 text `docs/prds/program-roadmap/source-2026-09-22/supplied-roadmap.md:880` has the same open decision. No inspected explicit amendment changes P9’s absolute exclusion to a defined shared subset.
- This is a **cross-owner requirement conflict**, not proof of a current leak and not resolved by D2. Later source text preserves direction, but gives no exact field/history/editor permission rules permitting blanket sharing.

**Proposed product amendment for a later explicit decision (not ratified here):** keep staff-only CRM notes, restricted notes, care and Support-private material excluded. Permit only an independently classified source-owned development encounter and its approved missionary projection under current P3/P10/P12 authorization for the exact relationship/purpose. Distinct purpose notes remain with their authoring source; a reference neither copies a transcript nor widens it. P9 owns encounter identity/authoritative history; P27 owns cultivation meaning; P28 only consumes the authorized projection. Decide explicit shared fields, who may edit/correct, historical window and withdrawal behavior before this exception is formalized.

That amendment changes disclosure and owner acceptance; it must not be hidden in an “engineering safeguard” or inferred from a membership row. Until decided, D2 can be adopted with sharing unresolved and fail-closed.

## Engineering acceptance proof required before affected behavior activates

These are safeguards implementing inherited intent, not new founder policy decisions. They must be owned in existing P3/P9/P10/P12/P21/P26 contracts and reused by P27.

1. **Reference integrity and caller scope:** two Tenants, two entities within one Tenant, same visible labels, distinct auth-user/profile/Party/assignment IDs. Reject forged actor/Tenant/entity/context/membership, cross-scope source references and stale target source revisions. Confirm exact current authorized result, not merely HTTP error. Validate composite FK/unique/check constraints under direct SQL as well as API.
2. **Participation is not authorization:** spouse/team member, Page contributor, church finance contact, Support Assignment participant, designated task assignee and representative each get zero extra Party/financial/note access absent the separate exact grant. Approved narrow access reveals only those fields and history, with withheld identities not recoverable through aggregate/filter/result existence.
3. **Both SQL row states:** inspect catalog grants and all policies; test SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, UPSERT/ON CONFLICT, RETURNING and qualified RPC/definer paths as anon, authenticated ordinary worker, scoped staff and denied staff. Reject attempts to change target Tenant/entity/source/actor even when old row is visible. Service-only commands must prove actor context independently; FORCE RLS does not restrict a BYPASSRLS service role.
4. **Revocation concurrency:** independent DB connections/commit barriers for (a) write commits before revocation, (b) revoke commits before write admission, (c) preparation before revoke but dispatch after, (d) new content after revoke while old read waits, and (e) reassignment simultaneous with revoke. Prior accepted historical receipt remains true; no stale access authorizes new effect. No permanent sleep timing test as substitute for deterministic barriers.
5. **Enumeration and aggregate negatives:** denied rows removed before counts, pagination, grouping, sums, rank, RFM, meaningful-contact/stage-aging classifications, filter choices, exports, search and brief compilation. Identical authorized dataset plus extra hidden records must produce the same observable authorized result except explicitly permitted coverage semantics. Unknown source coverage cannot become zero.
6. **Task brief custody:** initiating staff can read a sensitive source while chosen task recipient cannot. Recipient still gets a useful permitted action or assignment is rejected/needs eligible assignment; no private source transcript/notes/metadata leaks. Revocation before notification/view blocks that derivative; task existence does not certify financial action or cultivation touch.
7. **Same-user context switch and late response:** switch Tenant/active assignment/entity/ministry without signing out; old request resolves afterward; no old data appears or clears a new draft. Permission epoch changes while detail/kanban/brief is open; current source read refuses, cache clears appropriately and context-bound cursor does not continue old scope.
8. **Departure and history:** end ministry participation and/or staff assignment without deleting ministry identity or completed history. Reassign pending work deliberately to independently eligible current person/queue, preserving original actor and task/source-purpose identity. New person gets no automatic history widening; money/binding/Page release stays unchanged.
9. **Sharing exception only after ratification:** current prohibition remains until exact approved source projection exists. Test staff-private CRM note, care activity and Support note all absent from missionary DTO/search/export/cache. Shared encounter presents exactly approved fields and only currently authorized edit/correction operations; one source identity, no cloned editable log.
10. **Restore and old writer containment:** imported/restored grants or stale queued jobs cannot revive access, notifications or contact permission after recorded adverse revision. Mixed old/new clients cannot call a weaker legacy writer; unqualified projection remains unavailable, not a person-profile fallback. Retain existing real task/history identity.
11. **Privacy and safety:** restricted legal identity never leaks via ministry name, brief title, URL, source chip, task text, notification, diagnostic, export or fallback. Retention expiry and redaction stop new retrieval and clear qualified derivatives; separately retained audit is identifiers-only and independently authorized.
12. **Qualified real environment:** actual authenticated route/session and source services against disposable local Postgres/Supabase, meaningful catalog and resulting-row tests, then Playwright negative and positive journeys. No demo/auth bypass, intercepted success, broad service-role-only assertions or skipped required fixtures counted as qualification.

## Official sources and technical corrections

- PostgreSQL 17, [CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html), fetched 2026-10-09. USING governs old rows; WITH CHECK governs proposed rows; UPDATE may also require SELECT visibility; with no explicit WITH CHECK, USING is used for both where applicable. **Do not claim that omitted WITH CHECK automatically permits ownership reassignment.** Inspect effective policies and operations. Current Support UPDATE already spells both out.
- Supabase, [Row Level Security](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security), fetched 2026-10-09. Grants and RLS are separate gates; service role bypasses RLS; security-invoker views respect underlying RLS; raw_user_meta_data is user-editable and unsuited to authorization; even app_metadata in JWT can be stale until refresh. These support live owner context/epoch rather than client claims.

## What requires founder choice versus engineering work

**Explicit later product/owner decision:** exact shared encounter fields/audience/edit/history window and explicit P9 missionary-projection exception; whether/how a departing missionary continues to receive any bounded history; whether the new representative needs a deliberate broader history grant. D2 alone settles none. A rejected permission increase must not cause abandoned obligations: authorized staff/queue can retain them.

**Already binding engineering safeguards:** current trusted context, same-scope constraints, no membership-as-access, role-free capability enforcement, strictest field/classification floor, permission-before-counts, source refs, revalidation at commit, revocation/late-response fencing, no consent from enrollment, alias-safe egress, useful authorized task briefs, immutable historical actor/source identity, real DB concurrency tests. These should be recorded as conditions of faithful implementation without presenting the founder a new general security questionnaire.

**No needed D2 reversal:** enrollment attaches to the ministry’s enduring organizational identity; current humans receive only independently authorized participation in that work. This keeps donor care continuous without carrying private permissions along with the relationship.
