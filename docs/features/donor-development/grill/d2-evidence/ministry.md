2026-10-09. Research and founder-grooming evidence only; no specification, implementation, activation, data changes, or donor contact. The founder selected the organization's identified ministry as the journey's purpose target. This review tests that direction without ratifying further permissions, lifecycle, succession, or private-prospect policy.

## Assessment and strongest objection

**Keep the ministry target, narrowly understood.** A relationship journey concerns a supporter Party's partnership for an identified ministry. The existing Ministry Assignment is a plausible stable reference for that purpose, whether its participants are one worker, a couple, or a team. It avoids making one login, one marriage, or the current employee the identity of an organization-run ministry.

**The strongest objection is real:** many supporters trust and support particular people across changing jobs, places, and life seasons. A ministry identifier cannot declare that trust transferable to whoever replaces them. In an OMF-published case, a church continued supporting named workers across changes of country, role, and semi-retirement [S7]. That is concrete evidence against interpreting the ministry as an interchangeable staffing slot.

The strongest person-centered alternative would anchor cultivation to the supporter–missionary-person relationship, with ministry purpose attached as context. It makes a worker's personal network and a worker-following relationship easier to recognize, particularly where spouses serve separately or a supporter intends to follow one worker through transitions. Its cost is that shared couple/team work needs coordination across person journeys, creating risks of duplicate asks, ambiguous responsibility, and split interaction history. A person anchor still cannot determine gift destination, consent, export rights, or access. Neither alternative solves privacy by itself.

**Preferred synthesis, as judgment rather than vendor fact:** retain the identified ministry as the development purpose, retain actual people as Parties and relationship/interaction actors, and show the current relationship lead and introduction provenance. Do not create a second person-based cultivation authority merely to preserve who knows whom. When the actual supported purpose changes, treat continuity as a product decision rather than blindly following either the person's identity or the ministry identifier.

## Whole-product and implementation consequences, without a design prescription

- **Missionary and staff experience:** show the known ministry/people names and responsible person clearly. Users should recognize whose partnership they are working on without learning internal identity terminology. A shared call must be recorded once with actual participants, while each consumer receives permitted context.
- **Finance and donor care:** the target is a reason for cultivation, not who legally gave, a payment instruction, a financial account, or proof of fulfillment. Successor ministry choices cannot silently move received gifts or recurring instructions. A decline may finish one ask while permitted care continues.
- **Public and donor surfaces:** public presentation remains source-approved; private prospects, stage, internal commentary, and staff judgment do not become page content or donor self-service facts through this target reference.
- **Records and APIs:** reference existing P9 ministry identity and existing Party identity. Do not introduce another independently writable ministry or portfolio authority. Commands and history must distinguish the target, actual actor, current responsible person, and separately authorized viewer. Exact fields/uniqueness are not decided here.
- **Notifications and workflows:** participant or login change is not permission to dispatch. Pending effects must remain subject to suppression, revocation, and source-owned handoff. Native work stays usable without workflow products.
- **Long-term maintenance:** a stable purpose avoids yearly or staffing-driven identity churn. Its cost is an explicit, understandable distinction between preserving historical identity and deciding whether the live relationship still concerns the same purpose. Ignoring that distinction would create expensive future migration and trust problems.

## Existing Core fit and precedent classification

[Phase 9 D19](/workspace/core/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-09-full-crm-depth-relationship-graph.md:1277) deliberately defines the stable organization-owned Ministry Assignment, separate participants, Tenant/Legal Entity scope, and independently owned support binding and permissions. **Durable pattern:** aligned with the selected purpose target, provided its existence is not confused with implementation qualification or supporter agreement.

[Phase 28 roadmap](/workspace/core/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:4163) consumes shared development records through authorized support-raising views. **Durable direction:** one relationship source, qualified surfaces. It does not decide all access, purpose-transition, or private-prospect rules.

MPDX team-account continuity and Tnt participant history are **useful precedents**. Their full-account sharing, irreversible list merges, weak desktop passwords, and options to rewrite historical actor attribution are **conflicts with Core first principles** if imported. They are not unavoidable costs of supporting missionary couples or teams.

MPDX's donor-couple separation workarounds are **useful failure evidence** about identity conflation, not a recommended migration path. OMF and Reliant are **practice evidence**, not domain owners for Asym or universal policy authorities.

## Minimum empirical evidence before calling the ordinary experience proven

Do not invent a success percentage, sample size, or time limit from vendor marketing. Agree appropriate measures with participating organizations after establishing their current workflow. A small formative pilot should at least cover the following roles and situations, possibly with people fulfilling several roles:

- A missionary working alone; a couple working jointly; spouses with genuinely separate ministries; a team leader or development administrator who has managed a handoff; someone responsible for church relationships; and a finance/privacy reviewer. Include a restricted-worker case using safe fabricated data.
- Have participants choose the intended ministry and record a new referred supporter, an existing church's second ministry relationship, a completed conversation and next action, and a special appeal alongside recurring support. Observe whether they can explain the target, responsible person, and visibility without coaching.
- Walk through marriage, team departure, home assignment, transfer, retirement, and a private friend prospect with synthetic records. Ask what they expect to remain, end, become inaccessible, or need approval. Compare expectations among workers, agency staff, and finance instead of assuming agreement.
- Use an authorized demonstration or sanitized historical example of a real ministry transition to test whether preserved history would accurately explain what the supporter intended and what happened. Do not contact donors or import real sensitive data as part of this grooming.
- Record misclassification, duplicate records/actions, unexpected disclosure expectations, abandoned steps, extra spreadsheets needed, and confusion between ministry purpose and donation account. Measure task effort against the organization's actual baseline; do not set arbitrary thresholds in this note.
- Before implementation readiness, require owner contract and permission tests for the chosen scenarios, plus native prospect-to-ask-to-follow-up usability. A positive interview cannot prove financial correctness, safe authorization, or working source services.

## Recommendation for synthesis

Accept only the narrow D2 target choice after presenting the material caveat: ministry continuity does not mean a supporter's personal loyalty automatically transfers. Preserve person-level relationship provenance without adding another cultivation source. Explicitly leave purpose-change/closure policy, entry of private prospects, detailed sharing, and same-purpose concurrency unresolved. None of the evidence warrants abandoning the selected target, but claiming it solves those questions would exceed both the founder's answer and the available evidence.
