# Phase 27 Q28 — facts or calculated giving-pattern ratings

2026-10-11, Asia/Bangkok. **Amended D27 explicitly ratified; Q28 A+B selected.
D28 explicitly ratified; review disposition: Accept with required amendments.**
This is the next substantive product-direction question, not another contact-date,
logging, tooltip or reminder-interval vote. The founder selected a combination:
retain clear facts and optional giving ratings, let each tenant turn ratings off,
and preserve donor dignity. The original alternatives below are historical framing;
the combined reviewed decision is recorded at the end.

## Why this is the next frontier

D27 fixes actual-exchange recency and distinguishes useful outreach and existing
follow-up. It does not select a giving score. The
[roadmap](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:4095) and
[retained P27 owner requirements](../../../prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md:28)
currently require transparent recency/frequency/monetary (RFM) bands, scheduled
recomputation and score-drop attention, including internal tasks. The
[open-decision clause](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:4133) leaves
inputs and completeness unresolved. RFM here concerns giving patterns; it is not
D27's actual-conversation recency or a measure of ministry relationship quality.

Choosing to replace mandatory native RFM ratings is a material owner change.
D21 delegates routine UX and safeguards, not this scope reduction. D26/D27 already
forbid missionary surveillance, compulsory logging, false contact inference and
old-exchange dates alone creating work; those decisions are not being re-asked.

## Founder question — A+B selected

**Q28 — How should Asym help staff identify partnerships that need attention?**

For example, one church may offer prayer and personal support without giving
money. Another may give annually and already have a future follow-up agreed.
These are illustrative possibilities, not observed tenant workflows. The product
must make each situation understandable without calling either relationship weak.

**A — Show clear facts and reasons for attention (recommended).** Keep recorded
exchanges, recent outreach, agreed follow-up and relevant giving information
separate. Explain what needs review and why, without calculating an engagement
rating or creating a task solely because such a rating fell. Staff retain normal
filters, useful financial facts and all genuine source-owned obligations.

**B — Also calculate giving-pattern ratings.** Add explained ratings based on how
recently, how often and how much someone gave, helping staff compare financial
patterns across their portfolio. Keep the facts visible. Continue the roadmap's
internal review tasks for qualifying rating drops, with clear source evidence and
an authorized responsible person. A review task is not an instruction to contact
the supporter.

**Original recommendation, superseded by the combined selection: A.** The direct reason is easier to interpret across financial
and nonfinancial partnerships and avoids a separate scoring/task policy. B is a
credible alternative for organizations wanting systematic financial segmentation
and required review of qualified changes. Neither choice proves usability or
fundraising results; the recommendation is product judgment, not a universal
industry standard.

A would deliberately replace the roadmap's native RFM-band/score-drop-task
approach. It would not remove financial history, reporting, staff responsibility,
agreed follow-up, pause reviews or independently required financial work.

## Safeguards already applicable to both options

- Financial and nonfinancial partnerships remain first-class. No missing or
  nonapplicable giving data becomes a zero/low relationship rating. Annual giving,
  planned future follow-up and approved quiet periods remain visible context.
- Display advice as advice; Due/Overdue refers to an actual source obligation.
  [ADR0183](../../../adr/0183-source-owned-work-projects-into-one-shared-tasks-hub.md:24)
  requires a qualified concrete action predicate before shared task projection.
  A task can require internal review without requiring another solicitation.
- Neither choice dismisses actual follow-up, D6 pause review, P15 gift handling,
  P16 commitment/occurrence work or a separately qualified P28 gift-event purpose.
  A missing financial gift is not a breached commitment unless its owner proves it.
- No automatic contact, stage movement, reopening, missionary performance score
  or logging quota. Do not label a person or church lapsed. Keep D26's voluntary
  participation and D27's actual-exchange meaning intact.
- Current tenant, source, field and purpose authority protects facts, dates,
  counts, rankings, sorts, exports and task previews. An internal rating grants
  no missionary, donor or public disclosure rights. Unknown, private, incomplete,
  stale and unavailable data must not become fabricated certainty.
- Preserve exact money/currency/Legal Entity meaning, one authoritative source,
  correction/replay behavior and evidence dates. A computed rating or task never
  owns consent, money, source completion or financial truth.
- Reuse clear shared record/list controls and accessible source explanations.
  No new dashboard, configuration engine or extra donor checkout step is implied.
  Routine labels, mobile layout, error states and thresholds within an accepted
  policy remain design/engineering work under D21, not micro-questions.

## Research and current-source check

Fresh primary pages were retrieved 2026-10-11 UTC:

- [Blackbaud RFM Giving Analysis](https://webfiles-sc1.blackbaud.com/files/support/helpfiles/rex/content/en-gb/content/bb-rfm-analysis.html),
  undated current help page, defines RFM in terms of giving recency, frequency
  and amount. It is evidence that B is an established fundraising comparison,
  not that giving measures a prayer partnership or that its likelihood claims
  have been validated for Asym tenants. Its caution against over-asking or
  neglecting people supports treating segmentation with care.
- [Bloomerang engagement calculation](https://help.bloomerang.com/en/articles/12632614-how-is-engagement-level-calculated-and-what-does-it-tell-me),
  dated 2026-01-28, describes a proprietary composite using financial and other
  activities, including email tracking and communication preferences. This shows
  a different vendor convention, not a model to copy. Email opens do not prove
  D27 exchanges, and a preference for fewer emails is not evidence of relationship
  failure. Published vendor descriptions are not comparative usability studies.

Repository facts remain separate from intended behavior:

- [P9's accepted planning clause](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-09-full-crm-depth-relationship-graph.md:430)
  treats `donors.score` as a labeled placeholder until P27. It is not an accepted
  real engagement calculation or a reason to retain a weak design.
- [Missionary donor normalization](../../../../packages/api/src/missionary-portal/donors.ts:372)
  and the [related client model](../../../../packages/database/hooks/missionary-donors-model.ts:374)
  both substitute zero for absent `donor.score`. This cannot prove low engagement,
  authorized disclosure or source-qualified scoring. No deployed behavior was
  tested in this continuation.
- [Earlier financial research](phase27-financial-research.md:118) explicitly leaves
  financial lens/subject grain open and distinguishes unknown from zero.
  [D27](phase27-d27-contact-recency-review.md) already rejects record-update and
  capped-timeline shortcuts for actual exchange recency.

Two independent read-only owner/frontier reviews agree that this is a genuine
material fork and that compulsory timed recontact is not an open alternative.
No runtime experiment or previous probe was rerun; no interview, pilot or measured
preference is claimed. Existing D27 research and validation stay unchanged.
