# D6 evidence — ownership, shared work and internal attention

Research readback: 2026-10-10, checkout
`225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`. D1–D4 are ratified; D5 A is
selected. The founder selected D6 A: normally choose an internal date to review
deliberately paused cultivation. The safeguards below are review proposals,
not additional ratified decisions, a formal specification or implementation.
The consolidated D6 review controls the final interpretation.

## Finding

A fits the accepted architecture. P27 owns the pause and the review's meaning;
shared work makes the required action findable; the Phase 17/6 notification seam
draws the qualified person's attention to it. These are separate projections
of the same source work. A reminder, read state, generic task checkbox or timer
does not resume cultivation, contact the supporter, change an ask, or stop money.

The bounded recommendation is one source-owned review obligation, reusing
appropriate shared work, with qualified in-product attention when due. It does
not require a new reminder product, coordinator policy, routing engine, cadence
builder, or communication channel. The precise D6 producer/recipient/end binding
still needs qualification; neither a stored task nor the current demo bell
proves that binding exists.

## What is and is not currently promised

- **Staff destination:** accepted intent is the existing Mission Control shared
  Tasks Hub/CRM work surfaces, including the Party's Tasks tab, plus the shared
  staff notification bell/inbox for the qualified D6 attention contract. A typed
  authenticated link should open the source-authorized review context. D6 does
  not choose a new route, standalone pause inbox or whole-Party access grant.
- **Missionary destination:** the same authorized work can appear in Missionary
  Workspace under the exact P28/P12 projection; contextual notification views
  are allowed when the contract is Live. A complete missionary notification
  center is not proved or designed by D6. One person using two allowed surfaces
  must not produce two independent review tasks or duplicate effects.
- **Recovery destination:** independently authorized staff can find the source
  work when personal routing is absent. Reuse the qualified source recovery
  surface/owned-exception path. Do not invent an always-authorized administrator,
  new coordinator roster, automatic broadcast or queue to satisfy “right place.”
- **Channel:** in-product is a real catalog channel, separate from task storage
  and from external email. Choosing a date does not authorize email, SMS, push,
  Slack, digest, repeated reminders or escalation. Their future use needs exact
  source/channel contracts. ADR-0028's SMS reservation is not transport authority;
  Phase 43's later qualification is likewise not implied by D6.
- **Current versus intended:** Phase 17's PRD at 12–15 and roadmap at 1082–1084
  state planning-only/blocked posture. ADR-0027 at 548–558 identifies demo bell
  and domain-local contribution/Support notification migration inputs, not the
  shared contract. The sibling runtime review supplies current-code findings;
  this appendix makes no deployment claim. Existing source checks did not prove
  a delivered D6 source, task binding, message key or qualified recipient route.

## Material concerns and proposed exact safeguards

Likelihood below is conditional on the described implementation mistake, not
a measured frequency or an assertion of an existing production defect.

### O1 — The reminder arrives in a private new silo or is mistaken for the task

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** high if existing task-shaped prototypes or a
bell mock are treated as the final contract. **Evidence:** ADR-0183:24–55;
ADR-0027:18–65; platform surfaces:157–165. **Effect on A:** retain A, qualify the
common source/task/attention binding rather than add another task product.

**Proposed exact requirement:** “The chosen review belongs to P27 and reuses
appropriate shared work. Its due attention uses Core's qualified in-product
notification contract and links to the authorized review context. Task and
notification are distinct projections of one causal review; neither is a second
review obligation.” **Proof:** navigate staff record, staff work list, staff
bell/inbox and authorized missionary context; all lead to the same current
review, with no duplicate task from a retry or an additional surface.

### O2 — ‘Lead’ silently means Party primary, original creator or everyone related

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** medium when routing takes shortcuts through
existing profile/relationship joins. **Evidence:** D5:163–195;
P9:484–494; P12:1552–1556,2832–2849. **Effect:** retain the partnership-lead default
without automatically reassigning existing independent work.

**Proposed exact requirement:** “New pause-review work defaults to the
partnership's eligible responsible lead. Respect any separately authorized
review responsibility and subsequent source-owned handoff. Recipient resolution
uses the exact current source responsibility and authorization; overall Party
primary, creator, ministry membership, email address, role label or task
possession alone never selects or authorizes a recipient.” **Proof:** a church
has one overall representative, two ministry leads and a separately assigned
reviewer; only the qualified responsible person receives each admitted review.

### O3 — Departure, access loss or ambiguity abandons work or creates a guessed fallback

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** high over long pauses unless explicitly
handled. **Evidence:** D2:55–60; ADR-0183:57–62,102–126; P12:1552–1556.
**Effect:** preserve A; adopt the existing source-owned exception boundary.

**Proposed exact requirement:** “If personal routing cannot prove a current
eligible recipient, release no protected personal attention and preserve the
review as visible source work/needs-assignment for independently authorized
operations. Use another person or queue only through an existing qualified
source contract and authorized handoff. Access loss removes protected
presentation; it does not complete or erase the review.” **Proof:** lead leaves,
loses only one entity grant, or resolution becomes ambiguous before release;
no stale send, broad-admin fallback or hidden disappearance occurs. Recovering
an executor's authority does not by itself reassign the partnership.

### O4 — Reading, task completion or a due timer changes the partnership

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** medium if generic task completion is wired
to pause state. **Evidence:** ADR-0183:71–100; ADR-0027:39–65;
Q6:55–77. **Effect:** retain A and keep the review's source meaning explicit.

**Proposed exact requirement:** “Due time creates internal attention only.
Reading, archiving where allowed, notification delivery, or a generic task
checkbox does not decide stay-on-hold, resume or end. Any accepted review
disposition or change to the scheduled review uses P27's authorized source
operation and truthful source receipt. Required-work attention remains visible
after read while the source is actionable.” **Proof:** read/unread, generic
complete, retry and timer cannot alter cultivation; a real source disposition
ends only the appropriate review projection. A later legitimate review is a
new source occurrence, not resurrection of ended recipient engagement.

### O5 — A changed date or stale worker creates wrong-time or duplicate attention

**Severity:** medium/high. **Likelihood:** high if executor schedule time is
treated as truth. **Evidence:** ADR-0026:17–20; orchestration spec:25–32,76–109;
Studio runtime:45–67. **Effect:** retain one chosen date, not a cadence engine.

**Proposed exact requirement:** “P27 owns the chosen review date and its
explicit human time interpretation, current revision and source applicability.
Wake-ups recheck that current source, recipient and authority before releasing
the one intended review attention. A changed date, accepted disposition or
superseding pause fences obsolete work; retries and optional Studio work reuse
the source-purpose identity. Preserve truthful unresolved work during outage;
do not issue a catch-up flood or silently declare it reviewed.” **Proof:** local
date/time-zone interpretation, date-change versus wake race, duplicate wake,
complete-before-notify and recovery after downtime. D6 does not silently choose
an hour, grace period, review frequency or Studio/Website timing profile.

### O6 — Internal reminder is interpreted as permission for email or sensitive detail

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** medium with domain-local defaults or free
text copied into notifications. **Evidence:** ADR-0026:17–61;
outbound OpenSpec:24–71; ADR-0183:64–69; P17 PRD:295–313.
**Effect:** keep the native internal reminder; do not widen channel or audience.

**Proposed exact requirement:** “Use a Live, meaning-specific, code-owned
in-product contract with safe source facts and an authenticated source link.
Sensitive pause reasons and protected source context remain at the source.
Unknown/Reserved bindings create no catalog item. No Support assignment-email
preference, generic profile address or donor contact permission substitutes for
this contract. External channels are not implied.” **Proof:** reserved/unknown
key creates no delivery; source work stays available and is not mislabeled as
sent. No private pause reason appears in list, search, notification, event,
email, cache or telemetry without its exact permitted projection.

### O7 — Native review depends on optional Studio, or a different domain's policy is copied

**Severity:** medium/high. **Likelihood:** medium when a generic workflow recipe
looks like an available reminder system. **Evidence:** roadmap:4091,4103,4127;
Studio owner bindings:24–28; ADR-0026:129–160; ADR-0027:111–128.
**Effect:** retain native A with exact shared owner seams.

**Proposed exact requirement:** “The review works natively without optional
Workflow Studio or Web Studio. Reuse shared execution only as an executor.
P27 does not inherit access-review coordinator membership, elapsed-hour timing
profiles, late-usefulness cutoff, no-task reminder decision, Website return
codes, email family policy, or Studio reminder cadence. Qualify only the exact
task, permission and local-attention producer seams needed by this operation.”
**Proof:** disable optional Studios; review work remains visible and the native
qualified reminder remains functional. Delivery cannot depend on an executor's
own clock, stored access snapshot or an unrelated domain's settings.

### O8 — Pausing one ask suppresses stewardship, consent restrictions or unrelated obligations

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** medium if one global paused flag is used as
an all-effects gate. **Evidence:** Q6:63–80; roadmap:4101,4119,4163–4167;
D3 continuing-partnership boundary. **Effect:** no change to A; keep the scope
that was actually selected.

**Proposed exact requirement:** “This review concerns deliberately paused
cultivation for its exact purpose. A deferred ask alone does not pause the
whole partnership. Existing promised follow-up, source-required service and
independent money/consent states retain their owners. The review date is not a
contact appointment or permission to resume.” **Proof:** January ask deferral
with a promised thank-you; paused cultivation with ongoing recurring support;
Never Ask restriction; true ending. None silently changes another owner's fact
or becomes a mandatory repeated ‘try again’ chore.

## Limits and remaining choices

No new formal owner amendment, capability, route, message key, database object,
queue or lifecycle is specified here. D6 does not settle all handoff powers,
indefinite pauses, review outcome UX, repeated reminders, date defaults, stage
aging, ending/reopening or the complete missionary sharing policy. Those open
details do not justify losing the accepted review obligation or bypassing the
already-settled source/permission/notification boundaries. A local-only
notification contract need not wait for email setup or the entire Phase 17/34
program, but it must pass its exact local proof before being called available.
