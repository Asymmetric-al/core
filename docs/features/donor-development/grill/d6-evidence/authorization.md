# D6 evidence — authorization, privacy and reminder routing

2026-10-10. Research for the founder-selected **A: choose an internal review date when cultivation is deliberately put on hold**. This is an adversarial evidence note, not an adopted specification, implementation or live-system audit. Repository snapshot: `225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`; uncommitted grooming notes are separate from that snapshot. The Q6 question note still says unanswered; the current interview answer supplies the selected direction.

## Decision effect and evidence limits

D6 is viable. Its strongest requirement is that **a review obligation survives departure or loss of eligibility, while access and notification authority do not**. The due date prompts an authorized review; it is neither a contact instruction nor permission to resume cultivation. Responsibility, source access, a task brief, notification eligibility and source-transition authority are separate decisions.

The task-table and route inspections below are current committed code, not proof that these paths are deployed, reachable with a particular production role, or vulnerable. No deployed catalog, real user records, Supabase project settings, provider delivery or live revocation was inspected. No application tests were run for this research-only change. Text assertions and mocked ownership tests cannot qualify the eventual D6 contract. Likelihood below describes a plausible adoption error if current seams are reused without qualification, not measured incident probability.

## Graded concerns and exact safeguards

### A1 — Wrong default recipient or abandoned review after departure

**High impact; likely reuse trap. Answer effect: keep A; clarify its eligible-recipient boundary.** Actor/Finance defaults and historical creator IDs are available today, whereas the qualified partnership lead is not established by those seams. Using creator, former lead, ministry participant, Party primary representative or missionary profile interchangeably could disclose work or orphan it. Replacing one leader does not silently transfer every ask or task.

**Proposed language:** “The review belongs to the partnership work. Core routes it through the partnership's current, independently eligible responsible lead and the qualified shared-work owner. Creator identity and former responsibility are historical evidence, not continuing recipient authority. If that route becomes unavailable, the outstanding review remains owned and recoverable through an already authorized source-owner path; Core does not invent a lead, widen access, discard the review or declare it completed.”

This does not create a universal management queue or decide a new handoff policy. If the source owner has no eligible recovery destination, that bounded capability must be qualified before activation; an inaccessible silent task is not a completed review route.

**Proof:** creator departs; lead loses tenant membership; lead retains independent read but loses responsibility; a new eligible lead is deliberately admitted; ministry closes; eligible staff recovery exists/does not exist. Retain the one source obligation and history, do not notify an ineligible person, and do not transfer unrelated work.

### A2 — Assignment mistaken for source visibility or a sharing grant

**Critical impact; plausible. Answer effect: inherited no-auto-disclosure condition, not a new grant.** Ministry/team membership and a task assignment cannot unlock donor identity, sensitive location, Party notes, Support-private history, member-care content or finance details. Conversely, requiring full transcript/history access merely to receive a legitimate released task brief would unnecessarily block work. A stale deep link grants neither read nor edit.

**Proposed language:** “Responsibility authorizes neither disclosure nor a source transition. A review task may expose only a useful brief and references independently authorized for that recipient, purpose and destination. Original records remain subject to their current permissions. Shared ministry context and a review date create no new staff, missionary, supporter or church visibility.”

**Proof:** eligible task recipient without source-body rights receives only an expressly released brief; source visibility alone cannot cause a release; missionary recipient gets no P9-excluded notes/activity; donor/portal/CC/household person receives no task by association. An unresolved P9/P28 interaction-sharing contract cannot be bypassed by relabeling its text a reminder.

### A3 — Revalidation accidentally retargets immutable old messages

**High impact; plausible. Answer effect: implementation safeguard; no channel selected.** “Send to the current lead” must not mean editing the concrete recipient of an old notification or retrying an indeterminate email to a successor. Current qualification narrows delivery/presentation; it does not mutate historical recipient identity. There can be one review obligation and, only where source-authorized, a separate notification occurrence for a newly eligible recipient.

**Proposed language:** “Before new notification admission and unstarted delivery, re-prove the exact recipient's current eligibility and source applicability. Do not retarget an existing concrete recipient occurrence or prepared message when responsibility changes. Preserve and reconcile its real outcome; any later notice to another recipient requires its own source-authorized occurrence. Notification engagement or delivery never completes the review.”

**Proof:** handoff before admission, after local availability, after preparation, and after submission may have begun; old access revision ends; later authority returns. No old feed resurrection, email readdressing, false recall, duplicate semantic occurrence or automatic alert to a successor without the source's applicable notification rule. D6 does not select email, SMS, escalation, a new contract key or repeated reminders.

### A4 — Date processing or a task click silently changes source state

**High impact; likely if Support snooze semantics or generic task completion are copied. Answer effect: explicitly preserve A's meaning.** P26 reminders return Support work to Open; that owner-specific state transition conflicts with D6 if copied. Generic task completion/deletion also does not prove someone deliberately reviewed the pause or was authorized to resume/end cultivation.

**Proposed language:** “Reaching or displaying the review date makes review due; it does not resume or end cultivation, contact anyone or change an ask, gift, commitment or contact restriction. Reading/dismissing a notice, opening a link and completing a generic task cannot substitute for the authorized current-source review outcome.”

Exact stay-paused/date-renewal, end, reopen and task-outcome semantics remain separate product decisions. A GET, mail scanner or link preview is inert. Do not silently select a recurrence interval by completing the first review.

**Proof:** due callback while paused, early callback, stale date callback, task marked done/deleted, notification read/archived, email opened, concurrent review; only an independently authorized current-version source action changes source meaning. Distinguish delivered, reviewed and resolved in both history and metrics.

### A5 — Authorization is frozen at scheduling or borrowed from the assignee

**Critical impact; plausible. Answer effect: inherited P12 invariant.** The human scheduling context, ongoing system authority and eventual viewer/recipient are different principals. Neither an old JWT, task's `assignee_profile_id`, cookie, client tenant/source ID nor service-role key is a substitute. Current eligibility must govern scheduling/replacement, deferred work, disclosure and consequential review; state and authority can change during the delay.

**Proposed language:** “Each consequential command, due-work execution, notification boundary and result projection proves the current applicable principal/context and exact tenant, legal entity, source, purpose, capability, field/history floor and authorization epoch. System work is system-attributed under its own qualified scope. It never borrows a departed human's identity or treats task assignment as authorization.”

**Proof:** forged tenant/entity/actor/owner/source refs, stale capability/epoch, service worker with wrong scope, cross-tenant profile or task link, human-to-service context mismatch, revocation concurrent with final write/dispatch. Use a source/version/authority fence that prevents a stale authorized read from becoming a later unauthorized effect; a worker lease alone is insufficient. Preserve accepted historical evidence without granting current access or promising recall of already sent bytes.

### A6 — Privacy is lost before the recipient clicks

**Critical impact; plausible for sensitive missionary contexts. Answer effect: inherited projection floor.** A benign “review pause” subject can still disclose a church, donor, ministry assignment, real name, exact location, pause reason or protected existence. The task title, OS/email preview, badge/count, grouping, search snippet, link unfurl, export, log and error path are all disclosures. An authenticated destination does not protect the already emitted preview.

**Proposed language:** “The permitted review summary uses the current authorized identity/alias and geographic precision and excludes unreleased source detail. Apply the same current row, field, purpose and history floors before lists, counts, groups, search, notification previews, exports and links are disclosed. A hidden source is not signaled through dedupe, reminder totals, error details or Realtime.”

**Proof:** alias change, protected-person floor, precise-country restriction, missionary limited slice, mixed-permission group, hidden source vs absent source, role switch in an open browser, link unfurl, failure logs and downloaded task export. The existing 100-row attention helper is not a complete review census; permission filtering cannot occur only after counting/grouping or pagination. Query predicates and summary fields require the same authorization.

### A7 — Hidden-task reuse or deduplication becomes an existence oracle

**High impact; plausible. Answer effect: retain D3/D4 admission rule; no new coordination capability.** “Prefer an appropriate existing shared task” requires authority to observe the permitted minimum identity and reuse effect. A hidden-task conflict, task ID, count or success/failure distinction can leak existence. Silently reassigning a hidden task is also unauthorized.

**Proposed language:** “Reuse existing review work only within an independently authorized observable scope and when its source meaning and selected effect match. Dedupe grants no access, reveals no hidden task and changes no unrelated ownership. A context without the necessary observation/reuse authority cannot acquire it through attempting creation.”

**Proof:** otherwise identical worlds with hidden task present/absent; retry after revocation; appropriate visible task vs unrelated follow-up; existing task separately owed for another reason. Deny/defer equivalently where minimum authority is absent; do not rely on a generic unique-constraint error as the privacy boundary. Do not introduce a new approval queue for ordinary creation.

### A8 — Partial writes, stale callbacks or cancellation lose owed work

**High impact; likely without a canonical source command. Answer effect: inherited durable-work safeguard; exact representation remains open.** Separate task/link/event inserts and reminder `pending/sent/cancelled` do not prove atomic source acceptance. Replacing a date, ending cultivation or losing a lead must not cancel unrelated promised work, nor can an old timer reinstate a prior date or recipient. The same shared task may serve multiple reasons.

**Proposed language:** “The accepted source change and its required local review obligation, history and any dispatch intent commit together, or the source retains an owned actionable exception. Date changes and cancellations act only on the exact current review generation and selected consequence. They do not cancel separately owed tasks, asks or commitments. Stale, replaced and already-processed callbacks cannot revive work or broaden recipients.”

**Proof:** crash after each local write, duplicate callback, early callback, two date edits, review-versus-resume/end, handoff-versus-due, source correction after scheduling, cancellation of a review link to a multi-purpose task. Retry returns the same admitted outcome only after current result authorization; source state and historical admitted outcome remain distinct. An outbox alone is not the review obligation. No distributed lock service or full event-sourcing rewrite is implied.

### A9 — RLS/grants or a privileged side door bypass resulting-state checks

**Critical impact; plausible adoption risk, no demonstrated current exploit. Answer effect: engineering acceptance floor.** The current raw-table grants are revoked, which is a useful boundary. Copying finance/profile routes or reopening raw browser writes to make the old TaskDialog work would not qualify P27. Actor/source/tenant fields and both old-row authority and resulting-row scope must survive every admitted writer.

**Proposed language:** “Keep canonical source mutation behind the qualified server command. Inventory actual grants, policies, RPC execution, views, service jobs and maintenance paths. Enforce exact same-tenant/entity source relationships and immutable actor/source provenance on the resulting state. If direct client mutation is admitted, its effective old-row and new-row policies must provide equivalent protection; service-role paths perform the same source authorization and fences.”

**PostgreSQL precision:** `USING` governs eligible existing rows; `WITH CHECK` governs proposed inserted/updated rows. Where permitted, omitted `WITH CHECK` inherits `USING`; omission alone is not a vulnerability. Permissive policies combine with OR, so one permissive alternate may reopen a path. Table privileges and RLS are independent. Owner bypass normally applies unless forced; superuser/BYPASSRLS remains privileged. `SECURITY DEFINER` runs with the owner's privileges and is not intrinsically an unconditional bypass. Safe search path, EXECUTE/schema grants and actual effective role matter. Unique/FK checks can disclose existence even under RLS. Avoid cross-row/current-time `CHECK` expressions as substitutes for transactional authorization and generations.

**Proof:** actual-catalog assertions plus real-role SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/UPSERT/RETURNING and RPC/view tests; cross-tenant new values on an authorized old row; alternate permissive policy; service-role direct writer; wrong-scope relationship; caller-selected actor; restricted read result. Revoke the browser path rather than restoring broad grants just for compatibility. Requalify the route before adoption instead of claiming every existing source must be rewritten for D6.

### A10 — Retention, restore or cache revives old authority and private content

**High impact; plausible. Answer effect: inherited owner controls.** Keeping review history does not authorize keeping every reason/body/name forever. Snapshot restoration can revive a superseded reminder, former lead, erased pause reason or expired feed projection. A task deletion cascade can instead destroy required structural evidence. These are separate failure directions.

**Proposed language:** “Keep the permitted structural evidence of the review and actual outcomes under source-owner retention; do not promise permanent private content. Reclassification, erasure, access loss and expiry apply to task briefs, history projections, notification previews, exports, caches and restored material. Restore re-applies current restrictions and consumed/cancelled source generations before any reader or worker activates; it never replays old review notices or revives erased content.”

**Proof:** restore from before revocation, alias change, redaction, completed/cancelled review and recipient handoff; expired cache/feed content; anonymized creator; source removal with an outstanding obligation; retention-authorized deletion vs ordinary archive. Preserve minimal valid lineage without recovering forbidden text from audit/logs or treating an old recipient projection as current. Staff notification preferences may suppress an eligible optional channel; they cannot erase source-owned pending review or create authority to contact the church.

## Product boundaries that this research does not silently select

- Exact exceptions to the normal review-date default, repeated-review cadence and indefinite pauses.
- The pause/end/reopen lifecycle, review-outcome vocabulary and treatment of each open ask or promise.
- A channel, notification key, global missionary notification center, additional escalation recipient or automatic notification on every handoff.
- New task/source capabilities, an always-eligible universal queue, missionary supporter access, church-wide access or a resolution of P9/P28 interaction sharing.
- Physical tables, indexes, scheduler, a generic workflow platform or completion/retention durations copied from P26/P17.

These remain explicit source qualification/product dependencies where relevant. The selected meaning can be retained now; paths depending on unresolved material choices cannot be represented as delivered or activated. The simplest faithful route is an existing qualified dated shared-work obligation, with a narrowly source-owned review relationship and authorized presentation, rather than a second reminders/permissions/history platform.
