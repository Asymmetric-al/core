# Phase 27 D9 — keep the last recorded stage on reopening

2026-10-10. Founder: **“A — Keep the last recorded stage; let staff update it
when ready.”** **Disposition: Accept.** No material amendment or repeat
ratification is required. This records the option and boundaries presented in
[Q9](phase27-q9-progress-on-return.md), not a formal specification or implementation.

## Recorded decision

Reopening the same partnership work preserves its authoritative current stage
state, clearly presented as the previously recorded understanding or known absence.
D13/D19 clarify that no-current-stage remains absent even when older assessments
exist in history; reopening does not restore a historical non-null value. Staff may update it
when ready; there is no compulsory stage review or confirmation at reopening.
Reopening itself is not evidence of a new assessment, stage transition, meaningful
contact or changed relationship progress.

Keep the source's authoritative current stage or known absence, not a stale value copied
from the reopening screen. A concurrent authorized stage edit must not be
overwritten by the separate reopening action.

Preserve the recorded stage's identity, historical meaning and authoritative
provenance. Do not reset its recorded date to the reopening date or substitute a
generic record-modification date; unknown historical dates remain unknown. This
does not require a second stage value, fresh-confirmation flag, mandatory reason,
new task, timer, approval step or reminder cadence.

Current responsibility and the existing due-task-or-owned-actionable-exception
rule still apply. Stage never grants permission to contact a supporter, implies a
gift/commitment/ask outcome or revives ended work. A retired or changed stage
definition must retain its historical meaning and use the applicable preset
owner's qualified resolution, not a silent remapping. Exact preset evolution,
stage transitions and reporting-duration definitions remain separate questions.

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

Old information could look newly confirmed, reports could count a fabricated
stage transition, or reopening could overwrite a newer edit or restart stage-based
effects. Keep the
recorded meaning and provenance; reopening changes lifecycle, not the stage or
its evidence. Current source contracts control any separately required effect.
Do not cure stale information by inventing a new assessment or reviving old work.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

The last stage is known and remains interpretable. An import may lack its date,
or a changed preset may retire the value. Show missing provenance honestly and
preserve historical meaning instead of guessing. This choice does not establish
that the old assessment is accurate today, that someone has contacted the church,
or that every stage has the same meaning across staff and missionary presets.

### How does this affect the whole product?

P27 owns the recorded stage; P9 owns Party lifecycle and shared work; authority,
finance and communications remain with their existing owners. Lists, history and
reporting must distinguish last recorded progress from a freshly verified fact.
Neither D9 nor reopening changes financial totals, meaningful-contact recency,
task outcomes, stage permissions or donor/public disclosure.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Staff can return to work without making an assessment before they have enough
information. The source view explains what was last recorded and lets authorized
staff update it in context. No extra reopening form or confirmation is required.
The existing accountability rule still keeps current work actionable; optional
stage reassessment is not permission for unattended or unowned work.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

The previously verified CRM/practice sources support keeping context and history
and distinguishing relationships, individual asks and performed actions. They do
not prove that compulsory stage confirmation improves ministry outcomes. The
choice is a reasonable low-friction product judgment, with honest provenance
doing the work a forced guess could not. No fresh user research, vendor execution
or measured outcome improvement is claimed.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes. D7 preserves observed stage independently from closure; D8 reopens the same
journey without fabricating new stage knowledge. P27 requires stage history and
current next-action accountability. The original request separates stage from
Party/task/ask/financial state and forbids silent advancement from gifts or scores.
Generic CRM updated_at/lastTouchAt behavior is not proof of a stage assessment.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

No material amendment is needed. Accept A with the already stated provenance,
history, retired-value and source-authority safeguards. Do not ask the founder to
ratify the same answer again. Continue to the unresolved stage-preset product
choices without freezing schema, labels, customization or transition rules here.
