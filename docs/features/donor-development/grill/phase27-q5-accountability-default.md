## Why this question follows

D4 makes new work deliberate, but does not say whose existing responsibility
should be preferred when starting it. The original request already requires
distinguishing overall relationship responsibility from responsibility for a
journey, ask or task. Therefore Q5 asks about the **default lead**, not whether
those responsibilities may differ at all.

The next-task-or-owned-actionable-exception rule is also settled. A lead need
not perform every task, and a task assignee is not automatically the continuing
journey lead. This choice helps resolve initial accountable work before exact
assignment transitions and lifecycle behavior. It does not decide those later
rules or grant access.

**Product judgment:** prefer responsibility for the actual ministry partnership
when it is established, appropriate and currently eligible. This fits the

## Proposed single founder question

**When starting a journey, whose responsibility should Core prefer as its lead?**

Illustrative case, not an asserted agency practice: a church supports two
ministries while one church-relations representative handles its overall agency
relationship. Someone must keep each ministry partnership's work moving.

**A — The lead for that ministry partnership (recommended).** Prefer an
established, eligible responsibility matching that partnership when it is clear;
otherwise ask for an authorized choice. Keep the Party's overall representative
distinct. Other people can own particular asks and tasks, and the same person
may lead several journeys. This need not add an extra step when context is clear.

**B — The supporter's overall representative by default.** Prefer the Party's
eligible primary representative to coordinate the journey, with an authorized
different lead where needed. This favors centralized coordination while allowing
others to handle specific work.

Both options retain a visible, reviewable choice and currently authorized
assignment. Neither means a different lead is compulsory for every journey,
that the creator automatically owns it, or that ministry participation proves
responsibility. Missing or ambiguous responsibility must not be guessed from
personal friendship, role labels, login identity or the order of participants.

## Boundaries after the answer

- The distinction among overall representative, journey lead and task/ask owner
  remains. Exact assignment representation must use the existing owners and a
  narrowly qualified P9/P27 contract; P9's Party edge alone does not prove that
  contract exists. No shadow owner or writable portfolio-membership store.
- Defaulting is a proposed starting choice, not standing automatic reassignment.
  A later change of the Party's primary representative does not silently move
  journeys, rewrite history or discard open work. Handoffs need their own rule.
- Do not invent a hierarchy among existing responsibility roles, a new
  missionary role/capability, an always-eligible queue or a general delegation
  engine. An existing qualified person/queue and D2's owned-exception path retain
  their actual source limits.
- Responsibility grants no visibility, authority over another person's work,
  financial instruction or permission to contact. P28 projection/sharing remains
  separately governed; primary representative is not a universal private reader.
- Initial stage, task requirements beyond the settled invariant, exact access
  grants, eligibility precedence, handoff and lifecycle remain their own choices.
  This note does not choose a physical owner column, FK or unique index.
