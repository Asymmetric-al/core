## Ratification and decision frontier

The founder instructed, “Yes, ratify this amended D18,” and asked to continue.
The exact [D18 capability and safeguards](phase27-d18-adversarial-review.md) are

At the time Q19 was asked, D18 left the current-stage outcome unresolved and blocked exposure of
saved-list changes until that policy is settled. A stage belongs to its own list;
the old assessment cannot simply be carried into a different list as a valid current
stage. The genuine choice is whether a current stage on the destination list is
optional or required when saving this change.

## Founder question — answered A

**Q19 — When changing to another stage list, may staff save before they know which
stage fits on the new list?**

**A — Allow “Not yet assessed” (recommended).** Choose an appropriate stage from
the new list when ready; otherwise save without a current stage on that list.
The intended outcome must be clear before the same deliberate save.

**B — Require a stage before saving.** Choose an appropriate stage from the new
list before changing lists; otherwise keep the current list for now.

In both options, earlier list choices and assessments remain in history. Here,
“Not yet assessed” would describe the current assessment on the destination list;
it would not claim that the partnership had never been assessed. The destination
may be a previously used list, not a newly created one. Equal stage names or display
positions do not establish equivalence between lists.

**Recommendation:** A. It lets staff make an appropriate process change while
recording uncertainty honestly and preserving earlier understanding. B's strongest
benefit is immediate stage categorization on the destination list, with fewer
unassessed current records; it can delay an otherwise appropriate process change.
This is a product judgment, not a measured agency preference or error-rate claim.

## Consequences to review after the answer

- If A is selected, distinguish an intentional, clearly shown absence of current
  assessment on the destination list from an unresolved placeholder or silent
  reset. Preserve earlier assessment history, including a return to a previously
  used list. Do not imply that no earlier assessment ever existed.
- If B is selected, make the required eligible target stage and the option to
  retain the current list clear. Do not guess a first stage merely to permit save.
- Neither option requires a second confirmation, reason narrative or acknowledgment
  ritual. An already-unassessed source must not trigger pointless repeated input;
  the clear final outcome and ordinary deliberate save remain the interaction.
- The selected policy applies to an actual change to another list. It does not
  automatically authorize clearing a saved stage while staying on the same list,
  historical backdating/remapping, automatic mapping or restoring an old assessment.
- Initial target-stage suggestions/defaults are not inferred from optionality or
  requiredness. Handle supported outcomes and material changes explicitly, preserving
  D18's concurrent-edit, current-authority and uncertain-result safeguards.
- Qualify history/event and reported time-in-stage meaning without fabricating
  progress. Normal next work and its owner remain intact; no new assessment queue,
  deadline, donor contact or workflow activation follows from this answer alone.
- Paused/closed operation, bulk change and cross-scope behavior remain separately
  reserved. Both options must become precise source/UI acceptance criteria before
  any affected flow is released.
