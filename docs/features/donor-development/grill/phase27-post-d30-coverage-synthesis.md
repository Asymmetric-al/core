## Delegated design treatment carried forward

These are coherent applications of existing decisions under D21, not new founder
scope selections, schema declarations or hidden formal requirements.

**Correction and collision.** Distinguish correction of an erroneous event or
reference from genuine new work. Preserve the recorded event and attributable
correction; do not rewrite prior Requests, encounters or financial facts. Returning
to work uses D8's same continuing context and current authority. Ended child work
stays ended unless its own owner admits a new action. An unchanged repeated command
does not create another occurrence.

A same-intent admission or return reuses an existing context only when current
source authority permits it. A Party merge that brings two ordinary journeys
together needs the existing source's qualified collision resolution. Do not
concatenate histories, choose the first row, erase child work or reveal a hidden
record through a duplicate error. A permitted surviving continuing context requires
preserved provenance and explicit dispositions for affected references and work.
If this cannot be done safely, block that unsafe resolution and provide the
qualified repair path. No new Ministry merge or universal merge engine follows.
Physical lifetime uniqueness must follow these outcomes, not an `active`-only
shortcut that lets a pause create a competing ordinary journey.

**Unfinished and undated work.** D7 permits closure when no meaningful development
work remains; lack of a date by itself does not establish that fact. No new undated
paused state is selected here. Existing active work may use the already-required
owned actionable exception when a truthful next task cannot yet be established.
Resolve missing responsibility/readiness visibly rather than inventing a date,
action or recipient, closing real obligations, or declaring the record healthy.

**Native follow-up and health views.** Carry the next-task-or-owned-exception
invariant through admission, completion, cancellation and transfer. A blocked or
cancelled task is not healthy future work. Retain D6's exact internal dated-review
and time-zone profile for pause reviews. Bind other required native due-work
attention to its own qualified source purpose through the existing task and
notification owners; D6 and ADR0027 do not mean every task edit gets a notification.
Overdue work remains discoverable and can be deliberately rescheduled or resolved.
No extra email, SMS, manager escalation, repeated cadence or business-hour date
shifting is introduced by synthesis. Existing source-specific P16/P28 reminder
policies remain distinct. Quiet periods and contact restrictions stop prohibited
outward effects without hiding lawful internal or financial obligations.

Show factual elapsed stage time, recorded exchange recency, recent outreach and
chosen due work separately. No universal age threshold or unverified portfolio
performance statistic becomes policy. Sparse voluntary history does not prove
neglect, require missionary logging or automatically create solicitation work.

**Church work and narrow proposals.** A church, its pastor/committee/finance
contact and its missionaries remain distinct identities and relationships. Budget
cycles, leadership changes and annual discussions use source context plus chosen
next work. Preserve actual historical participants; do not retarget old responses
or contacts merely because leadership changed. A foundation Request may reference
qualified proposal documents and dates without becoming a grant-management,
planned-giving, signing, event-booking or accounting system.

**Briefs and documents.** The baseline brief is a complete on-screen structured
composition of authorized source facts: partnership context, giving/recognition
and commitments in their correct lenses, current Requests, interactions and next
work. Dates, source links and unknown/incomplete coverage remain visible. No AI
narrative, donor rating or public publishing path is needed to make it useful.
D28 Off and D26 current audience continue to govern the composition.

Link existing documents only through their owners' current access and retention.
Requested printable/exported artifacts use qualified P18/P12 and source rules;
no new private upload store, public share link or disclosure grant is created.
Export is never permission to contact the exported population. Complete the useful
screen independently of broad future document-product delivery. One encounter is
referenced through its source rather than copied as multiple editable transcripts.

**Native measures and wider analytics.** P27's ordinary views retain owner/stage/
Request totals and forecast-versus-qualified-outcome comparison. Same entity,
currency, period, attribution basis and coverage remain explicit. P33 enhances
reporting; it cannot back-gate these native views or authorize a local money
substitute. Choose indexes, caching and materialization against measured query
and authorization costs, preserving reconstructibility and current privacy.
