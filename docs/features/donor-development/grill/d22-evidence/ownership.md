# D22 — one bounded request capability, distinct source-owned outcomes

2026-10-10 UTC. Founder selected **A: cover funding and significant nonfinancial
partnership requests**, with responsible person, response and follow-through;
financial fields appear only where relevant. Research against Core HEAD
`225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3` and current working-tree decisions. This
appendix changes no formal specification, issue, implementation or live state.

**Recommendation: Accept the selected scope with the boundaries below.** One
request/response core is compatible with the owners. It must not become one
universal task, promise, participation or fulfillment aggregate. Routine product
design is delegated; exact states/fields need coherent design and proof, not another
sequence of founder micro-votes. Genuine changes to owner contracts remain explicit.

## Graded concerns and exact clauses

### O1 — A common core becomes a universal work or promise engine

**High impact; plausible if all records called “requests” are unified.** E1/E3/E4/E8.
**Clause:** “P27 owns the particular invitation, its presented meaning, response
and development follow-through. Keep the continuing journey, individual request,
shared action, participation and source-owned commitment/fulfillment distinct.
Record one request without creating a new journey, financial account or generic
commitment object; routine calls and to-dos remain ordinary work.”

**Proof:** A church can have a funding request and a prayer invitation on the same
partnership. Calling to arrange discussion does not require another proposal.
No active-journey prerequisite, automatic enrollment or artificial ceremony is
inferred for every possible request from D22 alone; qualify the actual contextual
creation/linking path under D4 and the source's admitted scope.

### O2 — Mixed responses are flattened or the design grows a bundle engine

**High impact; plausible with one blanket Accepted status.** E1/E2/E3.
**Clause:** “Preserve what was actually requested and answered. Acceptance of
prayer or hosting cannot accept or fund a different monetary request. Retain
actual presented/revised terms and response provenance; later edits cannot rewrite
what the supporter was asked or to which proposal a response applied.”

**Lean delegated design judgment:** one independently answerable request per record,
with several on the same partnership/encounter, avoids a bundle-item engine. This
is a proposed product shape, not a previously mandated owner invariant. A single
combined invitation with one response may remain descriptive. Do not split every
installment, designation or funding-fulfillment measure into another ask, and do
not confuse alternative proposals with simultaneous independent promises.

**Proof:** “Yes to prayer, no to monthly support” produces separate truthful outcomes.
A revision from $100/month to $50/month preserves the presented request rather than
retroactively improving forecast accuracy. Source financial allocations remain
with their owner, not duplicated as request components.

### O3 — A positive response manufactures commitment, money or participation

**High impact; likely if response and fulfillment share one status.** E2/E4/E6/E7.
**Clause:** “A response records the evidenced answer to the request. It is not
received money, collection authority, a P16 commitment, an enrolled participant,
completed introduction, reserved event place or verified prayer. Any supported
handoff invokes the exact source command with its own identity, intent and rights.”

**Proof:** A financial yes can support a deliberate P16 handoff but cannot charge a
card. A prayer yes means agreement was reported, not that prayer was measured.
A host's yes does not allocate a seat or record attendance. Existing independently
qualified evidence may support a completion claim; absent proof remains absent.
Recording bounded reported follow-through does not create a universal fulfillment
service or impose future P37 installation merely to record a hosting invitation.

### O4 — Nonmonetary requests require fake amounts or contaminate reports

**High impact; plausible with reused fundraising forms and sum queries.** E1/E4/E8.
**Clause:** “Show and validate monetary fields only for their actual monetary meaning.
Nonmonetary requests need no zero amount, guessed currency or fake valuation. This
does not remove Tenant/entity/source authorization. Preserve amount, currency,
period, requested/expected/source-confirmed distinctions wherever money is used.”

**Proof:** A requested introduction contributes no zero-dollar gift or financial
forecast. A noncash donation intention is not automatically a nonfinancial favor;
finance retains its valuation, acceptance and receipt meaning. Material changes to
the request preserve earlier terms and source links, rather than reinterpreting
them through a type toggle. Request counts, responses and money rollups declare
their different grains; prayer acceptance is not monetary conversion or donor value.

### O5 — Every request creates another task, or task completion implies a response

**High impact; plausible with owner-plus-next-date templates.** E3/E5.
**Clause:** “Use existing shared work for actual follow-through. One appropriate
action may cover several requests only where the qualified source scope proves
that coverage. Do not mandate a duplicate task per request or infer coverage from
matching Party/date. Task completion does not itself accept or fulfill the request.”

**Proof:** One real call can discuss funding and hosting, while preserving each
response and any still-open follow-up. Completing that call cannot silently resolve
an unanswered request. Preserve the journey's due-work-or-owned-exception invariant
and independent source dispositions; no generic multi-source task graph, duplicate
inbox, reminder cadence or coordinator queue is invented.

### O6 — Requests bypass communication, representation or private context

**High impact; plausible for churches, referrals and prayer content.** E2/E5/E7/E8.
**Clause:** “The responsible staff member, addressee, responder, represented Party,
financial authorizer and actual participant remain distinct. Recording or accepting
a request grants none of the others' powers. Every read, response, reference,
export and contact action uses its own current scope/purpose and disclosure rules.”

**Proof:** A pastor's reply is not automatic organization mandate or member consent.
An introduction does not grant access to the introduced person's records or permit
solicitation. Prayer wording cannot copy sealed care context into ordinary CRM.
Use qualified encounter/message references instead of copied transcripts. Delivery,
opening an email or completing a task proves neither presentation understood nor
agreement; donor objections take effect through the communication owner.

### O7 — Existing P25/P28/Support records become duplicate development sources

**High impact; plausible because these products also use “request” or “supporter.”** E6/E7.
**Clause:** “Reuse authorized source references without auto-promoting newsletter
interest, Update acknowledgement, giving, Support intake or a public-form result
into a P27 ask, journey or participation fact. Missionary views consume the same
P27 request and response, subject to independent permissions.”

**Proof:** A newsletter submission retains its P23 occurrence and narrow P25/P28
handoff; staff Read/Archive is not development completion. ‘I prayed’ remains its
exact acknowledgement, not prayer-partnership enrollment. One call is recorded
once through P9/communication owners. P27 may deliberately record its distinct
development purpose without a second encounter or broader missionary disclosure.
A P16 pledge-change service request is not a partnership ask merely because both
are called requests. Preserve source retention: do not clone expiring intake bodies
into durable development evidence or use a reference as a new access grant.

### O8 — Optional automation or later domains become universal prerequisites

**Medium/high impact; plausible with package dependency lists.** E2/E5/E7/E8.
**Clause:** “Native request preparation, response and follow-through work without
optional Studio. Qualify each actually offered handoff and effect against its owner;
do not require an entire finance, event, volunteer, application or care product for
every nonfinancial request. Unsupported execution is not falsely reported complete.”

**Proof:** An internal hosting request can be recorded without claiming event
registration. DON-15 does not activate automatically or import a universal approval
chain, fixed clock or fallback queue. Genuine request-review policy remains P27's
product decision; finance correction approvals do not answer it. Current effects
share source-purpose identities across native and optional execution.

## Permanent minimal direction and qualification

The strongest alternative keeps formal records fundraising-focused and uses tasks/
interactions for other invitations. That reduces scope but loses a consistent,
visible request/response story for significant nonfinancial partnership work. The
selected broader core does not need four fixed types, custom field semantics,
tenant workflow taxonomies or an all-purpose fulfillment engine.

Carry these boundaries into an ordinary end-to-end request design: clear request,
responsible person, actual presentation/response, revision history, appropriate
follow-through and exact optional owner handoffs. Honor the founder's delegated
judgment for routine states and interaction details. Qualify material review policy,
source/context permissions, financial association, mixed responses, correction,
concurrency, migration, current role-safe projections and reporting before those
paths ship. No phase-ready claim follows from accepting D22, and no new ADR is
needed merely to restate source ownership.
