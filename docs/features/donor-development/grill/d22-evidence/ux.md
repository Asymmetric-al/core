# D22 UX evidence — one clear request and response workflow

2026-10-10 UTC / 2026-10-11 Asia/Bangkok. Research and design review only. The founder selected A: common financial and significant nonfinancial Requests. Proposed clauses below exercise the delegated ordinary design judgment; they are not implemented behavior or a formal UI specification. Source inspected at `225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`.

## Recommendation and scope

Keep A. Use **Requests** within the continuing partnership. Each records a specific invitation, its owner, the reported response and relevant follow-through. A short title describing its purpose, with details when needed, is the common core. Prayer, introductions, hosting and other descriptive nonfinancial invitations need no compulsory taxonomy, monetary equivalent, significance score or new approval step. “Track a request when you need to remember the invitation and its answer; use a task for the next thing to do” is sufficient introductory guidance.

Separate independently answerable invitations only when their terms, responses or outcomes need separate tracking. “Pray with us and consider monthly support” can yield a prayer request and a funding request within the same partnership and encounter. One coherent hosting invitation may describe its supporting details without a bundle/item editor. Do not duplicate the partnership, interaction or activity merely to connect the two. This follows [Q22's boundary](../phase27-q22-partnership-request-scope.md), as subsequently answered A, and [owner constraints:22–27,45–49](../../../../prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md).

The strongest B alternative is a focused fundraising proposal tool with nonfinancial follow-through handled through shared tasks and interactions. It reduces structured scope and may be sufficient for occasional practical requests. Its cost is that independently answerable nonfinancial invitations lose a consistent response record. A is preferable provided its common core stays small and the interface does not become an event, volunteer, application or pastoral-care system. This is product judgment, not measured ministry preference.

## Concrete interaction clauses

**UX01 — Start in context, with one ordinary save.** From the partnership, **Add request** carries the visible supporter/ministry context and existing scope; do not make staff choose the partnership again. Show what is requested, responsible owner and relevant details. Money-related details appear only for a monetary invitation. A short nonfinancial description can stand alone; “Other” must not mean unsupported. Required owner/scope rules remain governed, but no new justification field or mandatory review screen is introduced.

**UX02 — Record reality without manufacturing a sequence.** Staff may prepare a request before presentation or directly record an existing invitation and response. The latter asks what was requested, who responded, what they said, and relevant time/context together; it does not require saving a dummy draft, marking it presented, then reopening it. Preserve unknown presentation/response time honestly instead of using today as a factual default. System “recorded at” remains distinct. Associate an existing authorized encounter where available without retyping it. These are capabilities, not a frozen workflow enum or wizard.

**UX03 — Recording presentation is not sending.** For a prepared invitation, a contextual action such as **Record presentation** records the actual terms, date/context and permitted evidence. Saving a request or marking presentation sends no communication. Displaying a contact does not authorize outreach. Any existing mandatory source review remains applicable; D22 adds no blanket review or approval. Once presented, material revisions preserve the earlier terms and the response they received; an edit cannot silently rewrite what the supporter was asked.

**UX04 — Make the next useful action obvious.** A compact detail view gives the title, supporter, owner and readable current summary, followed by **Request**, **Response** and **Follow-through** information. This is an information hierarchy, not a requirement for three tabs/cards. Example: “Host the October visit”; “Response: willing, subject to dates”; “Next task: agree dates with Maria.” Offer the applicable action near its facts: **Record response**, **Update response**, or open/add the existing shared follow-up task. Use text and normal spacing, not a rainbow of competing badges. Completed tasks do not automatically accept or fulfill the request. Do not create a parallel task queue or require a new task for every request when the journey's existing next action is sufficient.

**UX05 — Responses preserve meaning and attribution.** A positive, negative, pending, conditional, partial or unknown answer must be expressible without pretending they are equivalent. Do not require a narrative on every routine answer, but retain qualifications when they matter. “Maria said the committee will consider USD 150/month” is a reported response, not an accepted church commitment. Show the respondent and represented party/scope when known and permitted; a linked organizational contact alone proves no decision-making authority. Multiple or corrected responses preserve their chronology and uncertainty rather than replacing the original with a single misleading badge. A refusal ends neither the relationship nor unrelated requests automatically.

**UX06 — Preserve monetary terms losslessly.** Distinguish requested/presented terms, staff expected forecast, reported answer, source-owned commitment and source-confirmed received funds. A USD 200/month request and a USD 150/month offer for six months must not become a single unlabeled “Amount 150” or “Funded.” Show currency with amounts and cadence/term/horizon where relevant. Do not invent annual totals or indefinite commitment values. Legal Entity and applicable campaign/designation context must follow the monetary owner contract; do not force a campaign onto every nonfinancial invitation or invent one where the owning monetary context does not require it. Keep rollups within permitted same-currency/entity lanes. A source integration/read failure is **Unavailable**, not zero received.

Keep unknown monetary values explicitly unknown when allowed by the operation; incomplete preparation is not permission to save an invalid presented proposal. Nonmonetary requests have no amount rather than amount zero. Preserve decimal input without silent rounding, reject unsupported precision with a field-specific repair message, and make locale parsing unambiguous. A selected currency must not silently change after a late default/refetch. Changing the kind of an unsaved request must visibly reconcile entered relevant details, not serialize hidden money or discard it unnoticed. Changing a presented request uses deliberate correction/revision and preserves its financial history; it never alters the related gift or commitment. Narrow noncash intentions must not force a cash valuation or receipt claim.

**UX07 — Fulfillment has its own evidence.** Financial actuals come from their financial owner; agreement does not create a charge, payment authorization, recurring agreement or gift. Nonfinancial follow-through can record reported facts and applicable evidence without fabricating a booking, volunteer registration or other source-owned execution. “Introduction promised” differs from “Introduction made”; “visit hosted” differs from ongoing prayer participation. Show partial completion and unresolved parts without claiming a perpetual relationship is 100% fulfilled. Do not add a generic progress percentage or participation engine. Where an owning record exists, link its permitted view rather than duplicate its editable truth.

**UX08 — Preserve journey lifecycle while recording facts.** Recommended D22 behavior: authorized historical request/response/fulfillment recording remains possible for paused/closed journeys, with that lifecycle visible and unchanged. Do not silently resume/reopen, change a pause review date, clear the stage, or generate future tasks from fact capture. Continuing cultivation uses the established explicit resume/reopen action and its owner rules. D21 already allows stage/list updates while paused; it does not by itself grant all Request mutations. Existing suppression/contact policies still govern suggestions and outbound follow-up. These boundaries need command-level proof, not only a banner.

**UX09 — Honest recovery is part of the flow.** Keep the editable draft and entered terms on validation/network failure within the open interaction. Show field errors beside the affected controls plus a navigable summary when needed. A pending save says **Saving…** and prevents duplicate submission; Cancel/close must not imply an accepted server operation was undone. A lost reply says saving could not be confirmed and offers status checking/safe retry of the same submitted operation. Never suggest “Create another request” as the first recovery. A stale revision requires reconciling the current permitted record with the preserved proposal; a late response cannot overwrite newer visible facts. If request save succeeded but a separately requested task failed, identify both outcomes and retry only the failed work. Atomic boundaries follow the source contract, not the visual grouping.

## Accessibility, mobile and visual acceptance

Use shared Maia geometry, semantic tokens, clear typography and a restrained hierarchy. The form must work with keyboard and touch, persist labels outside placeholders, and associate required/optional guidance and errors. Conditional money fields enter the reading/tab order only when applicable; changes must not unexpectedly move focus or submit. Preserve visible focus and restore it sensibly after dialogs. A toast may supplement durable save/error feedback, not replace it.

At 320 CSS pixels and equivalent zoom, essential Request text/actions reflow without page-wide horizontal scrolling. Long names and translated labels wrap; full amounts, currency and consequential qualifications are available without hover. Use logical layout for RTL, explicit localized date presentation and bidi-safe money/context text. A historical date stays the stated calendar date, not a date silently shifted by device timezone. Core's touch guidance targets 44px hit areas; WCAG 2.5.8's AA criterion is 24px or a qualifying exception, not a claim that all AA targets must be 44px. A sticky footer/mobile keyboard cannot hide the focused field or required error. On a slow connection, retain input and distinguish pending, failed and confirmed saving; do not claim offline persistence from an in-memory form.

Acceptance on the actual implementation must demonstrate:

1. Create a plain nonfinancial request and a monetary recurring request; monetary fields/validation/rollups never contaminate the first, and currency, exact amount, term and scope survive the second.
2. Record an existing response directly with unknown original presentation date, no invented draft/presentation events or default-now claim, and one retained encounter context.
3. Record prayer acceptance plus funding still under consideration in the same partnership. No aggregate accepted/funded result, duplicated journey or compulsory bundle editor appears.
4. Record the six-month reduced monthly offer, a conditional hosting answer and a reported organizational contact response. Each preserves its terms; none creates payment, organizational authority or execution.
5. Show unknown, zero, unavailable and permission-restricted financial states separately and without exposing protected values. Test nonfinancial kind changes and presented-term corrections for lost/hidden money.
6. Complete a follow-up task and record partial nonfinancial fulfillment. The unrelated response, commitment, received funds and journey lifecycle remain correct.
7. Save facts while paused/closed under applicable authority. Review date, stage and lifecycle remain unchanged; future-work actions use the established explicit owner path.
8. Exercise invalid precision, stale revision, dropped success reply, retry, denied refresh and separately failed task creation. Retain the permitted draft, produce no duplicate accepted effect, and show the correct recovery action.
9. Complete these flows by keyboard and touch, screen-reader-check labels/errors/status, and test 320px, zoom, RTL, long translated text and slow responses in light/dark themes. Automated accessibility checks alone cannot pass this criterion.
10. In a moderated pilot, staff must identify what was asked, who answered, what is actually confirmed and what to do next without facilitator explanation. Include ordinary-task creation to detect added bureaucracy. Record observed mistakes and corrections; no speed, satisfaction or aesthetic result has been measured yet.
