## Initial substantive findings

### IR1 — Follow-through must not become perpetual work on closed requests

**Medium impact; plausible implementation consequence.** The initial exact decision
and R03 require each request to have next work or an owned actionable exception,
without distinguishing outstanding work from a request genuinely finished. That
conflicts with the supported close/withdraw and late-fact cases: a historical closed
request could acquire another artificial task solely to satisfy the invariant.

**Correction:** “Requests with outstanding source-owned work retain appropriate
follow-through or an owned actionable exception. A genuinely finished request
retains its disposition and lawful history without requiring new work. Independent
remaining obligations cannot be erased by closing the request.”

**Proof:** close a genuinely completed request with no remaining work; record a
late factual response/payment projection without reopening or generating future
work. Separately close one request while another request or source-owned obligation
remains due: that work remains accountable. The active-journey invariant is intact.

### IR2 — Separate decisions without forcing fragmentation

**Medium impact; plausible UX/domain restriction.** The exact decision initially
says not to split an installment/designation into another request, while R02 more
accurately says those dimensions **alone** do not justify another request. The
absolute wording could prohibit genuinely separate requested decisions merely
because they concern different designations. Conversely, a coherent hosting
invitation should not need separate records for every descriptive detail.

**Correction:** retain one coherent invitation when it can be answered together;
use separate request records when independently attributable responses or work
need separate tracking. Do not split **solely because** of installments,
designations or reporting dimensions. No automatic splitting, mandatory bundle
editor, extra journey or duplicated encounter follows.

**Proof:** prayer yes/funding undecided remain separate; one coherent hosting
invitation remains one record; independently requested funding decisions can be
separate even when their destinations differ. An ordinary installment is not
another ask merely because its payment date differs.

### IR3 — Disposition must distinguish safeguards from a new founder amendment

**Medium process impact; likely confusion if left ambiguous.** The initial draft
calls separation of request, response and execution a required amendment to A.
Those distinctions already appear in Q22's boundaries and the original brief.
Ordinary workflow detail is explicitly delegated in D21. None of the five evidence
appendices identifies an owner-contract change that requires another approval.

**Correction:** use **Accept, with required safeguards and delegated design
clarifications** after correcting actual overrestrictions. Do not claim a later
ratification; do not create a new ratification loop for inherited requirements.
If a genuinely new scope/authority conflict is found, name that conflict separately.

**Proof:** Q22, main disposition and session log agree that A is selected/accepted;
none calls routine clarification a new founder selection or already ratified change.

### IR4 — A late answer may address an older presented version

**High historical-integrity impact; plausible implementation interpretation.**
AC09 initially requires a conflict for a stale response against replaced terms.
That is sound for an unseen concurrent edit, but must not prohibit a fresh,
authorized recording of a late answer that explicitly addresses an earlier
presented proposal. R05/R06 already require truthful terms and late evidence.

**Correction:** “A fresh authorized recording that explicitly identifies older
presented terms retains that historical target rather than attaching the response
to current revised terms. Stale source revisions still conflict or reconcile.”

**Proof:** after proposal version B exists, record a newly received answer expressly
about presented version A. Preserve its occurrence/recording evidence and target
version; do not accept B, rewrite A or manufacture downstream financial authority.
