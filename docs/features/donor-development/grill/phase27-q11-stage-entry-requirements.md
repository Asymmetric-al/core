## Original single founder question — answered A

**When staff change a partnership's stage, should an organization be able to
require certain information before the change can be saved?**

For example, an organization might want a brief explanation of what changed
before staff select a particular stage. This is an illustration, not a prescribed
missions workflow, stage label or new required field.

**A — Guidance, with staff judgment (recommended).** Organizations explain what
each stage means. Authorized staff choose the appropriate stage without additional
organization-configured information requirements blocking the change.

**B — Allow required information for selected stages.** Organizations may specify
supported information that must be recorded before certain stage changes can be
saved. This provides more consistent documentation but can add work or block an
otherwise accurate update when information is unavailable.

Existing permission, eligible-stage, history and next-action safeguards remain
mandatory under either answer. A is not an unrestricted-write option; B is not
permission for arbitrary rules, mandatory manager approval or automated outreach.

**Recommendation:** A keeps routine relationship updates straightforward and
avoids encouraging staff to enter filler just to record their actual understanding.
B is a credible choice where the organization needs consistent documented evidence
at particular stages. This recommendation is a product judgment about proportionate
friction, not a measured Asym user-study result. Tenant flexibility in the stage
list remains ratified under either answer.

## Boundaries and consequences to review after the answer

- Keep information requirements separate from forced stage sequence, approval,
  consent, financial prerequisites and automatic work. Do not infer these from B.
- Under B, supported information types, truth/evidence ownership, legitimate unknown
  values, inaccessible required information, exceptions and current-policy acceptance
  need explicit bounded treatment. Do not offer an arbitrary expression language
  or assume filling a field proves a conversation or donor agreement occurred.
- Neither answer retrospectively invalidates old stages or compels information
  collection on D9 reopening. Configuration policy changes cannot silently rewrite
  historical assessments, restore retired choices or block unrelated work.
- Information required by an existing source contract stays required under A;
  tenant settings cannot waive authorization, privacy, consent or next-action rules.
- Policy enforcement must be source-owned across admitted commands/imports/jobs,
  not a browser-only checkbox. That is an implementation safeguard after the
  product scope is selected, not an invitation to create another rule engine now.
