## Founder question — answered A

**Q24 — Should an appeal keep a recorded working list of supporters, or should
that list automatically follow a saved filter as records change?**

For example, staff begin with 40 churches. Later, some churches enter or leave
the saved filter. This choice determines whether the appeal's planned list also
changes automatically; it never erases whom staff actually asked or their responses.

**A — Keep a recorded list, with deliberate changes (recommended).** Staff select
the starting churches or people, then deliberately add, exclude or remove entries
as needed. Preserve the changes and actual request/response history. Saved filters
can help staff find candidates without silently changing the appeal's working list.

**B — Let the working list follow a saved filter.** The current planned list changes
as matching records change, without staff accepting every membership change.
Preserve actual asks, responses and historical participation even when someone
stops matching. This favors continuously refreshed planning, with extra care to
explain changing membership and counts.

**Recommendation:** A. It makes intended outreach and remaining work easier to
understand while allowing deliberate changes throughout the appeal. It does not
mean freezing the list permanently or adding an approval ceremony. B is credible
for teams that prefer a continuously refreshed working list; its tradeoff is that
plan membership can change without a staff decision specific to the appeal.
This is product judgment, not a measured missions-staff preference.

## Boundaries in both options

- Being listed never means a supporter has been asked, agreed, committed or given.
  Source-backed facts retain their meanings; a kanban move cannot create money.
- Historical inclusion never grants current contact permission, source visibility
  or access. Never-ask/contact restrictions and current actor/field/purpose scope
  apply to candidate suggestions, actionable inclusion and each outward effect.
  Retained historical entries can remain lawfully visible while ineligible for
  further contact; show that distinction instead of erasing their history.
- An organization Party in the plan is not automatically a set of message
  recipients. Recipient identity and eligibility are separately qualified at the
  actual communication boundary. Neither option sends or enrolls anyone merely
  because list membership changes.
- Existing Request review, eligible responsibility and appropriate next-work
  requirements remain. Planning membership alone does not create every possible
  task or revive completed work; exact actionable source predicates govern tasks.
- P13 owns giving/source attribution, P16 commitments and financial fulfillment,
  and P6/P17 communication authority and delivery; P32 campaign delivery is consumed
  only where qualified. The appeal owns its development meaning, not another
  financial ledger, email editor, consent system or workflow designer.
- No automatic giving inference, double counting, default disclosure expansion,
  live-only history loss or resurrection of excluded work is authorized by either
  answer. The [D24 review](phase27-d24-adversarial-review.md) now resolves exact
  membership and correction behavior coherently under D21's delegation.

## Answer recorded and reviewed

The founder chose **A — Keep a recorded list, with deliberate changes.** Staff
may add, exclude or remove churches and people as needed; changing the filter
does not silently change the appeal's plan.

**Disposition: Accept.** [D24](phase27-d24-adversarial-review.md) records the exact
decision, all 22 review categories, R01–R18, AC01–AC29 and the complete shared UI
flow. Contextual Remove and Exclude retain their distinct intent and lawful history;
only deliberate current-authorized Return restores either, and ordinary Add/import/
retry cannot. Recorded membership, current eligibility and actual requests/responses
stay separate. Exact membership-dependent solicitation uses the communication
owner's current producer fence; independent obligations and in-flight truth remain.
Group changes preserve finite intent and per-item results. No extra approval, new
list engine, automatic journey or per-entry task is introduced.

These safeguards refine A within the stated boundaries and delegated design
judgment. The founder subsequently instructed, “Yes, ratify this amended D24.”
The exact reviewed decision and safeguards are explicitly ratified without
substantive change. No further micro-vote is required.
