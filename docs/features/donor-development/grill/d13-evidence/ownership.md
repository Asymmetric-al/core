## Concerns, exact safeguards and proof

Severity is the prospective consequence. Likelihood is conditional engineering
judgment, not an observed incident rate. All proofs below are future acceptance
obligations, not claimed test results.

### O1 — Missing assessment becomes a false catalog or lifecycle fact

**High; likely if a required enum is filled with its first value.** E1/E3/E5/E6.
**Requirement:** “An otherwise valid deliberate start may have no recorded
cultivation stage. Represent that source absence clearly; do not create a
mandatory tenant stage, infer the first stage, or interpret absence as paused,
closed, never contacted, unresponsive, uninterested or financially unsupported.”
An organization may still supply an accurate introductory stage; absence is
not mandatory. P28's mixed legacy labels need their already-required owner
reconciliation, not automatic mapping to D13.

**Proof/effect:** start an unassessed church/prayer partnership with valid work;
neither consent nor participation changes. A known suitable introductory stage
remains selectable. A supporter giving to another ministry supplies no assessment
for this exact ministry/purpose. Retains A without a new enum or data model.

### O2 — Tenant flexibility silently reinstates B

**High; plausible with generic required-field settings.** E1/E3/E4.
**Requirement:** “Tenant stage configuration and advisory guidance must not make
an initial stage, explanation or assessment approval compulsory for an otherwise
valid unassessed start.” Mandatory identity, purpose, scope, current authority and
work requirements remain mandatory. Tenant flexibility still includes accepted
stage definitions/order/guidance; it does not mean every platform safeguard or
founder decision becomes a tenant switch.

**Proof/effect:** the permitted unassessed path remains available after ordinary
preset customization. D10's no-zero-selectable-stage rule still holds; allowing
absence does not make an unusable catalog valid. No hidden force-B switch. This
preserves A rather than adding a new policy.

### O3 — Optional assessment erases accountability or creates administrative debt

**High; plausible if an unassessed journey is excluded from health queries or
receives a compulsory assessment task.** E2/E5/E7.
**Requirement:** “Unassessed active work retains an appropriate dated next task
or owned actionable exception. Missing assessment alone creates no compulsory
assessment task, review deadline, grace period, escalation or notification.”
Use ordinary source-owned follow-through; do not require duplicate work merely
to populate a stage. Conversely, “Not yet assessed” is not itself the actionable
exception that satisfies missing work or responsibility.

**Proof/effect:** preserve a valid introductory appointment without an extra
assessment chore; missing next work remains actionable under its source contract.
Stage absence alone produces no bell/email. Retains accountability without new
policy machinery; exact task-purpose qualification remains separately required.

### O4 — Unassessed records disappear or unavailable data looks unassessed

**High; likely with inner joins or a board containing only stage columns.** E1/E5/E9.
**Requirement:** “Authorized ordinary work views must account for admitted
unassessed journeys rather than silently dropping them. Distinguish known source
absence from loading, failed/stale data, insufficient history coverage or a
protected/unavailable value without revealing hidden information.”

**Proof/effect:** lists, boards, counts and exports reconcile for the same declared
scope; missing stage does not erase active work or its responsible person.
An explicit stage-only filter/report may exclude unassessed records when its
meaning makes that exclusion clear. This does not require every chart to show
every category, a universal column layout, new reports or a second health engine.

### O5 — The first real stage manufactures history or time in stage

**High; likely if stage-entry time is initialized from enrollment creation.** E2/E4/E9.
**Requirement:** “Preserve admission without assessment as that fact. The first
accepted recorded stage has its own actual source provenance; no prior catalog
stage, assessment, intermediate visit or stage-entry date may be manufactured.
Do not backdate that selection to enrollment creation.”

**Proof/effect:** admit without a stage, later directly select any eligible stage;
history distinguishes both facts and retains the unassessed interval. No phantom
‘Unknown → Identification’ sequence, gift, contact, completed task or forecast.
Repeated retries preserve the same accepted result. D12's direct choice applies;
this does not choose a historical-correction or backdating feature.

### O6 — Admission omission clears a stage or overwrites concurrent understanding

**High; plausible with blind upsert, bulk defaults or stale replay.** E2/E4/E6.
**Requirement:** “An unassessed start request is not authority to clear an existing
stage. Safe reuse preserves current recorded understanding and later changes.
First assessment follows current authorization, applicable target eligibility,
source revision and durable retry rules.”

**Proof/effect:** race an unassessed start with an assessed admission for the same
ordinary journey; reuse creates no duplicate and erases no accepted stage. Race
two first assessments and target retirement; recover one authoritative result
without phantom history. A reviewed group preserves finite per-member intent and
truthful partial outcomes, without an extra mandatory ceremony for each member.
Null stage does not relax tenant/ministry/purpose/preset reference integrity.

### O7 — Optional automation treats valid absence as failed admission

**High; plausible if DON-14's generic evidence text is overread.** E7/E8.
**Requirement:** “Accepted enrollment without a stage is an enrollment occurrence,
not a fabricated stage transition or uncertain acceptance. Native admission
remains independent of optional automation. Each qualified downstream operation
uses its actual required source facts and current authority.”

**Proof/effect:** a stage-dependent optional step can be inapplicable or unable to
proceed without invalidating already valid admission or inventing facts. Do not
turn ‘require relevant information’ into a backdoor mandatory initial assessment.
DON-14's binding remains unqualified until owner reconciliation; D13 neither
activates it nor makes all unknown facts safe to ignore.

### O8 — Reporting converts absence into failure, zero or donor worth

**High; plausible with ordinal funnels or incomplete denominators.** E5/E6/E9.
**Requirement:** “Each exposed stage metric must identify its population,
unassessed/coverage treatment and clock. No stage assessment is not evidence of
zero relationship progress, no contact, missing giving, poor donor quality or a
completed assessment. P27 supplies facts; P33 owns qualified report meaning.”

**Proof/effect:** financial/prayer facts remain usable from their own authorities;
unknown stage contributes no invented stage age or ordinal probability. Do not
silently improve conversion by omitting unassessed journeys from a declared
all-journey denominator. Exact duration formulas, cutoff thresholds and extra
reports stay open; no compulsory reporting taxonomy or analytics store follows.

## Alternative, scope and unresolved product choices

B's strongest benefit is immediate classification using an approved stage that
accurately covers early exploration. It remains usable voluntarily under A.
Making that assessment mandatory can force inaccurate data or block deliberate
useful work; no inspected owner contract requires that tradeoff. A has genuine
costs: missing-stage presentation and honest report coverage must be designed.
Those bounded costs do not justify a configurable admission-policy engine.

Still separate: initial suggestions/defaults, preset assignment/switching,
correction/clearing/backdating, detailed stage-dependent work, and exact reporting
metrics. No maximum unassessed age, mandatory explanatory note, donor-facing
status, bulk reassessment feature or automatic first assessment is selected.
Any such policy addition would need explicit treatment rather than being
presented as an inherited D13 safeguard. The implementation must preserve existing
source context and qualify these separate dependencies before using them.
