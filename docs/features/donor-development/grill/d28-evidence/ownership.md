## Minimum proposed owner amendment

> P27 always provides permitted source-qualified giving facts, actual exchanges,
> outreach, agreed follow-up and explainable attention. Giving-pattern ratings are
> an optional tenant capability, Off for new tenants, and never an overall measure
> of relationship value or missionary performance. Enabling ratings initially
> enables analysis only. Automatic internal review of qualified changes requires
> a separate deliberate tenant choice within the same bounded settings. Native
> analysis/review needs no Workflow Studio installation. Disabling either feature
> immediately ends its continuing source eligibility without erasing history or
> canceling independent obligations. Re-enabling establishes a fresh qualified
> baseline; it does not replay historical drops or revive old work.

Three understandable behaviors suffice: **Off**, **Ratings only**, and **Ratings
with internal review**. This describes policy, not a required three-position
widget, database enum, second rule builder or independent permission system.
Review cannot remain operational while ratings are Off. On re-enable, review
does not silently return; an ordinary clearly disclosed choice can enable both.

Reconcile owner-constraints:32–34, roadmap:4095 and P9:430–431 at /to-prd. The
score socket becomes optional qualified analysis rather than a promise that
every tenant must replace its placeholder with a rating. P28 consumes only
already-permitted facts; no missionary/donor/public rating publication is added.
P33/P34 consume the same policy/definition instead of recreating a disabled
native rating under another label. Independent lawful financial reporting remains.

## Material concerns, exact safeguards and effects

### O1 — Off merely hides a card

**High severity; likely with existing generic score fields or caches.**

**Required clause:** “The current tenant policy governs rating computation,
publication and consumption, not just layout. Off stops new rating calculation
and publication, live rating queries/filters/orderings/exports, scheduled rating
delivery, AI consumption and source review generation. Reject a rating-dependent
saved query with an actionable explanation; never drop its predicate or replace
the rating with zero. Independent giving/history queries remain available.”

Retain necessary source/audit evidence under its owner and retention policy.
Already delivered lawful files cannot be recalled. Existing immutable historical
report artifacts retain their own access/retention contract; Off does not rewrite
them or invent a new document-revocation regime. They must remain clearly
historical and cannot become a backdoor current-rating cache or regeneration
source. No fresh scheduled distribution of disabled rating output.

### O2 — Disable loses a race to a worker or export

**High severity; plausible whenever computation/delivery is deferred.**

**Required clause:** “Commit a revisioned source-policy change with attributable
history. Re-prove the current compatible policy and authorization at publication
and consequential release boundaries. A pre-Off job or stale client cannot
publish/release a rating or automatic review after Off; re-enable cannot make
that old operation current again.”

Use existing owner transactions, expected revisions and durable execution, not
a new scheduler or all-tenant transaction. Source applicability changes at the
authoritative boundary; bulk cache/projection cleanup may converge afterwards
but cannot serve disabled results meanwhile. Already-crossed external delivery
remains truthful and cannot be recalled. Distinguish explicit Off from missing,
ambiguous or failed policy reads: uncertainty releases no new effect and must
not fabricate successful disablement or source completion.

### O3 — Disabling ratings erases promised follow-up or leaves ghost tasks

**High severity; plausible if all Party tasks are canceled or none are ended.**

**Required clause:** “When review is disabled, end only automatic review action
occurrences whose continuing predicate depends on that option. Their task and
attention projections become No longer required under the source, with truthful
history, not Completed. A deliberately created independent follow-up, D6 review,
P15 gift handling, P16 obligation or qualified P28 gift task survives.”

A task does not become independent merely because someone opened or started it.
Conversely citing a rating does not make an ordinary deliberate task cancelable
by this feature. Use its actual owner predicate and provenance; ambiguous legacy
work must not be deleted or falsely closed. Current-work counts and notifications
follow authoritative applicability immediately, without waiting for everyone to
read them. Preserve authorized historical context without active/unread debt.

### O4 — Activation or a new formula creates a catch-up flood

**High severity; likely if every recomputation is treated as a drop event.**

**Required clause:** “Initial activation, re-enable and newly incompatible
definition/coverage establish a new qualified baseline, not a deterioration
event. Review-on also establishes a current comparable baseline rather than
enrolling old low ratings. Subsequent review requires an actual qualified change
under a comparable source/policy basis and its current concrete action rule.”

Retain definition/version, financial subject/scope, time/coverage basis and
activation provenance sufficient to explain comparability. A new version,
source correction, backfill, missing cohort or changed coverage is not itself
proof of worse giving. No old task or notification is reopened. Do not impose an
extra human approval ceremony for ordinary settings; separate existing P12 or
projection-widening controls still apply where their exact trigger applies.

### O5 — A rating difference becomes a task, duplicate notification or contact

**High severity; plausible with generic score-change listeners.**

**Required clause:** “The optional native source defines one concrete internal
review occurrence for a genuinely actionable qualified change, with current
responsibility, permitted evidence, applicability/end rules and semantic identity.
Repeated computation or native/Studio observation of the same occurrence does
not create another task, alert or unread item. Recurrence needs a new source
occurrence; it never reopens its predecessor.”

A review is not a solicitation instruction, debt, stage change or workflow
enrollment. Current recipient resolution requires actual eligible authority;
zero recipients produces owner-visible Needs assignment, never an invented
queue, missionary recipient or broad staff blast. Indeterminate resolution
releases nobody. Tasks and notifications retain distinct engagement under the
same causal source occurrence. This small native option is not arbitrary
tenant-authored automation; advanced recipes remain P34's vocabulary.

### O6 — Optional ratings become false certainty or surveillance

**High severity; plausible with placeholder defaults and incomplete imports.**

**Required clause:** “Distinguish Off, calculating, current, stale, unavailable,
incomplete and not applicable. These are honest presentation conditions, not
mandatory new stored states. Unknown input is not zero, a low band, a drop or a
breach. A stale previously permitted result may be shown as historical with its
basis while enabled, but cannot serve as current proof for a new review.”

A failed refresh is not an authoritative recovery or reason to close a previously
valid review; its current source predicate determines applicability. Give a
safe unavailable state where that predicate cannot be proved. No financial
rating incorporates missing voluntary logs, D27 exchange recency, prayer/visit
participation or ministry-update likes. P3's score floor remains binding across
screens, counts, filters, exports, tasks and AI; enabling the capability grants
no access. Do not equate small gifts with weak relationships or infer capacity.

### O7 — Legacy data silently opts tenants into the new policy

**High severity; confirmed source seam, unknown retained population.**
[Missionary roster:372](../../../../../packages/api/src/missionary-portal/donors.ts:372)
currently emits `score: donor.score ?? 0`. This is not evidence of a qualified
financial lens, tenant opt-in or permission to publish D28 ratings. P9 already
calls the field a placeholder. No runtime census was performed.

**Required clause:** “Inventory existing score producers, fields, displays,
exports, filters, jobs and score-related tasks before rollout. Preserve necessary
history and source distinctions; never promote old numeric values or mere
feature usage into enabled policy, a current baseline or review consent. Qualify
all reached readers together. Migration and imports create no review flood.”

## Acceptance proof required

1. New tenant Off still has useful facts, follow-up and ordinary reports; enabling
   ratings alone creates no review tasks. Invalid/denied config grants nothing.
2. Disable races with computation, cache read, report generation and effect
   release cannot publish disabled current results; Off→On rejects old work.
3. Solely automatic rating reviews end No longer required; independent tasks and
   obligations survive; task/notification history does not falsely show success.
4. Re-enable/review-on/new incompatible policy starts a baseline without catch-up
   drops, resurrected unread items or duplicate native/workflow effects.
5. Saved queries cannot silently discard disabled rating conditions; lawful
   historical artifacts remain distinguishable from current operational output.
6. Unknown, stale, nonfinancial, hidden and incomplete histories never become
   zero/low or leaked rankings. No missionary performance/logging consequence.
7. Legacy scores do not opt in a tenant or populate current ratings; census and
   source-qualified migration evidence precede activation.

These are proposed qualification obligations, not executed tests. Exact financial
bands, comparable subject/period/coverage and action predicates must be settled
in the consolidated D28 review before implementation; this appendix introduces
no new scoring, rule, notification, task or reporting engine.
