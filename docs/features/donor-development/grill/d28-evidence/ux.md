# D28 UX evidence — facts first, optional giving-pattern ratings

2026-10-11 UTC. The user selected A plus tenant-disableable B, with tasteful, inclusive presentation and no money-bag treatment of supporters. This appendix proposes the minimal experience and proof criteria; it is not an implemented rating, tested prototype or efficacy claim. Inspected source HEAD: `225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`.

## Daily experience and configuration

Keep facts as the permanent foundation: **Last recorded exchange**, **Recent outreach**, **Next follow-up**, and relevant qualified giving facts. A prayer partnership, annual giver and frequent giver must each be understandable without a total relationship score. Where an authorized tenant enables ratings, show a secondary **Giving pattern** explanation, not an avatar overlay, money-bag icon, prestige badge, leaderboard, star rating or red heat meter. The rating describes a bounded financial pattern, never the person's worth, generosity, faithfulness, missionary performance or value to the ministry.

An illustrative record can show “Last recorded exchange — 3 September,” “Next follow-up — 15 December, as agreed,” and applicable giving facts. An optional Giving pattern line follows those facts, with **How this is calculated** leading to the financial period, scope, inputs, coverage and policy explanation. The example selects no thresholds or algorithm. A list can offer a secondary rating column only while enabled and permitted; the default work surface should remain useful without it. Do not require a new dashboard, recurring chooser or extra donor step.

The smallest tenant settings proposal is **Giving-pattern ratings — Off** initially, plus a separately explicit **Create internal review tasks for qualifying rating changes — Off**. The latter depends on a qualified enabled rating policy; enabling display/analysis alone does not silently create work. Both controls name their effect and current saved state. No workflow designer, wealth-data provider, global “engagement” setting or per-person override is needed. Show a concise preview of the proposed policy and task impact in the same settings surface; do not add a ceremonial approval wizard.

This combination preserves A's clarity and B's optional financial comparison. Pure A is the strongest simpler alternative because it has fewer policies and failure modes. Optional B is justified by the user's requested flexibility, not a claim that scoring improves fundraising. [Q28](../phase27-q28-relationship-attention.md):33–44,77–109 requires financial scope/coverage and protects nonfinancial relationships. [D26](../phase27-d26-adversarial-review.md):21–72 and [D27](../phase27-d27-contact-recency-review.md):12–35 keep participation voluntary and distinguish exchanges from giving.

## Exact proposed requirements

**UX01 — Facts remain first-class everywhere.** In record detail, list and permitted summaries, preserve exchanges, outreach, agreed plans, true obligations and giving as distinct facts. Show the specific reason for attention and its source; Due/Overdue refers to a real source obligation. A rating is supplemental analysis, not the only way to locate or understand a record. No giving history is not a failed prayer/hosting partnership. Do not require another name, address, logging step or donor response to make these views work.

**UX02 — Explain the financial lens without overwhelming the row.** The optional band has a neutral textual name and accessible explanation of subject/scope, observation period, gift recency/frequency/amount inputs, currency basis, coverage, calculation date and policy version. Provide permitted source links and actual reasons for changes. Do not present an RFM code or mysterious 0–100 number alone. Keep the list concise and put detail one action away; neither a hover-only tooltip nor color alone carries essential meaning. Do not combine wealth estimates, email opens, personal-message content or D27 exchange recency into this giving-pattern rating.

**UX03 — Missing and nonapplicable are not low.** Distinguish **Not applicable**, **Not enough information**, **Updating**, **Unavailable**, and tenant **Off** from an actual calculated band. Use those terms only when the qualified source establishes them; do not expose protected-data existence through a reason. A genuine zero within a complete defined period is different from missing access/imports or no qualified money scope. Annual giving and one-time gifts retain their actual pattern; no universal monthly expectation or breach of commitment is inferred. Ordinary source obligations remain visible regardless of rating applicability.

**UX04 — Two effects, two clear opt-ins.** Only an authorized policy editor changes tenant ratings or rating-generated review work. Start Off; show saved state separately from edits/pending changes. Explain that ratings describe giving and that optional tasks request internal review, not solicitation. A useful preview includes permitted representative cases—annual giving, no applicable financial relationship, incomplete coverage and currencies that cannot be compared—plus the task policy's effect. Use anonymized/illustrative samples when actual-data access is absent. Label them as examples; do not promise predictive accuracy or infer tenant-wide coverage from a loaded page. Formula/threshold/subject decisions need source qualification but not a generic configuration engine.

**UX05 — Disabling is operational, not cosmetic.** Before applying Off, explain briefly that current ratings and their use in rating-based ranking/filtering/new task generation stop; underlying facts and independent work remain. After acceptance, remove current rating badges/columns/ranks from ordinary consumers, including permitted exports/briefs and stale client projections. Do not merely stop recomputation while showing last values as current. Preserve only owner-qualified historical/audit records, clearly historical where accessible. Existing rating-only tasks follow their exact source predicate and traceable disposition; do not falsely mark them completed or erase unrelated tasks. Never promise recall of copies already lawfully exported or delivered.

**UX06 — Restore deliberately and recalculate honestly.** Re-enabling ratings returns through the same current-authority/policy checks and shows Updating until a qualified result exists. Do not reuse old values as a fresh baseline or manufacture a drop from Off, unknown or changed policy. Do not silently enable the separate task option, backfill a flood of review tasks or revive ended tasks. If a retained setting is offered for restoration, show the proposed effect clearly. Resetting personal columns or opening a saved view cannot restore tenant ratings.

**UX07 — Preserve saved-view intent.** If a saved view only included a now-disabled rating column, remove the unavailable display and explain the change without deleting the saved preference. If its filter or ordering depended on the rating, do not silently return a broader or differently prioritized list under the same view name. Show that the view needs adjustment with a direct way to use a facts-only view or explicitly remove unavailable criteria; do not interpret it as zero matches. Preserve other filters. No export/bulk action may accidentally operate on the broadened population. Re-enabling still requires fresh current policy/data before the criteria can apply.

**UX08 — Review is internal and source-owned.** A qualified review task states the changed financial facts, observation period and reason, plus the authorized responsible person and actual due date if one exists. Its action is **Review giving pattern**, not Contact donor. Use the existing Tasks Hub identity and source-controlled outcome; do not create one task per refresh or three tasks for recency/frequency/amount bands. Known annual cadence, agreed quiet periods and corrections inform review. Paused/closed cultivation and no-contact remain intact. Task activation/disposition must follow the accepted source policy; the UX must not invent deadlines, automatic solicitation or a rating-specific task engine.

**UX09 — Failures preserve truthful state.** Loading a setting is not Off; an unavailable result is not low. A pending switch/save shows an intelligible action/result, retains permitted edits and prevents duplicate activation. Permission changes or concurrent configuration edits produce a specific refresh/reconcile path, never silent overwriting. Recover a lost reply against the same submitted change; do not claim Off succeeded while old policy is still effective. If a rating is retained during authorized refresh, label its actual date/staleness and never use it to imply a newly qualified task trigger. Source corrections update facts and their derived analysis without editing money from the rating UI.

**UX10 — Inclusive presentation and international facts.** Keep readable names, plain labels, restrained semantic emphasis and ordinary source links. No money bags, trophies, value-ranked portraits, red heat meters or person-level “lapsed” labels. Format money with explicit currency and locale; do not mix currencies or legal/recognition lenses into an unexplained total/comparison. Date ranges must disclose their calendar/fiscal and timezone meaning where relevant; calendar dates do not shift with travel. At 320 CSS pixels/equivalent zoom, long names/translations and RTL, facts remain readable and rating explanations/settings usable. Keep keyboard focus visible, labels persistent and async status perceivable; do not announce every background recomputation. This adds no public/donor display right or checkout step.

## Material concerns and targeted product acceptance

These grades are prospective severity/likelihood judgments, not measured rates or shipped D28 vulnerabilities.

| Concern                                                            | Grade                                         | Required evidence                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Optional financial rating becomes moral/relationship/person score  | High / plausible                              | Prayer-only, annual and frequent giving cases remain understandable and respected; facts stay primary, no wealth/open-tracking blend or missionary metric. UX01–03,10.          |
| Off leaves hidden ranking/tasks active                             | High / plausible                              | Disable with open tabs, saved views, exports and queued work; no current rating-dependent effect survives the accepted boundary, while independent obligations remain. UX05–08. |
| Unknown currency/coverage becomes low band or false precision      | High / plausible; legacy zero fallback exists | Exercise missing/restricted/import-partial data, real zero, multicurrency and different financial scopes. Explain qualified exclusions without leakage. UX02–04.                |
| Policy change/retry/restoration creates false drops or work floods | High / plausible                              | Lost replies, competing edits, corrected gifts and restoration yield current versioned results and exact source work once. UX06,08–09.                                          |
| Dense labels/color/pending switches mislead users                  | Medium / plausible                            | Actual keyboard/screen-reader/mobile tests and comprehension pilot; shared controls alone are insufficient. UX02,04,09–10.                                                      |

Actual-product acceptance must demonstrate:

1. With ratings Off, staff can understand relevant exchanges/outreach/follow-up/giving and resolve genuine work; missionaries can contribute nothing without a low score or reporting penalty. Donors gain no new required step.
2. Enable ratings while keeping review tasks Off. Show explained secondary analysis and no generated tasks. Enable the separately qualified task option and show a source-backed internal review without outreach, stage change or duplicate work.
3. Use annual, nonfinancial, zero-in-period, insufficient-history, protected-data and incompatible-currency cases. Verify accurate labels, facts, coverage and no person-value interpretation.
4. Disable during recomputation and task delivery, including another open client, export and derived summary. Confirm current effects stop at the qualified boundary; preserved historical evidence and independent obligations have truthful dispositions.
5. Open rating-filtered/sorted and column-only saved views after disabling. No silent broadening, empty-result fiction, unexpected bulk action or tenant re-enable occurs. Restore current results only after fresh qualification.
6. Drop accepted settings replies, revoke editor authority, make concurrent changes and restore an older configuration. Recover exact intent and current saved state without false Off/On, fabricated drops or replayed task bursts.
7. Test screen reader, keyboard, touch, 320 CSS-pixel reflow/equivalent zoom, mobile keyboard, long translations, RTL, explicit currencies/date boundaries and both themes. Labels/explanations/actions remain readable, focus visible and status changes comprehensible without color dependence.
8. In a real staff/missionary pilot, participants distinguish giving analysis from relationship value, explain why a case is unscored, and predict the two controls' effects. Record confusion and revise; no aesthetic, usability, fundraising or predictive success has been measured.

## Fresh primary comparison and limitations

Retrieved 2026-10-11 UTC:

- [Blackbaud RFM Giving Analysis](https://webfiles-sc1.blackbaud.com/files/support/helpfiles/rex/content/en-gb/content/bb-rfm-analysis.html) documents giving recency/frequency/amount and cautions against over-asking or neglecting lower-ranked supporters. Its vendor likelihood claims are not efficacy evidence for Core. [Lifecycle help](https://webfiles-sc1.blackbaud.com/files/support/helpfiles/rex/content/bb-donor-lifecycle.html) advises checking whether infrequent giving is normal; its person labels, thresholds and outreach prescriptions are not adopted here.
- [HubSpot score management](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/scoring/manage-lead-scores), updated August 25, 2026, explicitly leaves last-known property values after turning a score off. This is a useful counterexample: Core must specify its own disable semantics rather than assume a familiar switch guarantees removal from consumers.
- [W3C use of color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html), [form instructions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/instructions/) and [status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html) support textual meaning, persistent guidance and accessible asynchronous feedback. [Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) and [focus not obscured](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html) guide actual responsive/focus checks.
- [Base UI Switch](https://base-ui.com/react/components/switch) documents naming and controlled state. Its generic default is not the tenant's saved policy, and its current documentation does not qualify persistence, authorization or the composed feature.

No new runtime experiment, product UI, automated source test, browser run, live data operation or prototype was performed. Existing adjacent code is evidence of reuse constraints, not a delivered rating/task contract.
