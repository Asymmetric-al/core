# D28 independent challenge — combined facts and optional giving bands

2026-10-11 UTC. Independent review of the
[consolidated D28 review](../phase27-d28-adversarial-review.md), all five evidence
appendices, the selected Q28 A+B direction, ratified D26/D27 and the relevant
financial/work/permission contracts. This is a documentation and scenario review,
not a second product implementation, database experiment or user study.

## Disposition and scope

**Accept with required amendments.** The combined direction preserves useful
facts for every tenant and offers the optional financial analysis the founder
requested. It does not silently adopt the earlier A-only recommendation or treat
tenant enablement as permission to disclose scores. Default Off, absolute bands
and separate internal-review activation are explicitly identified product
judgments requiring the recorded amendment, not alleged prior ratification or
universal nonprofit practice.

The main review resolves the initial independent concerns about financial scope,
current activation fences, source-owned withdrawal, independent obligations,
nonfinancial participation and truthful configuration/recovery. Two integrated
details were raised and addressed in the
[precise calculation and episode contract](../phase27-d28-adversarial-review.md#financial-calculation-and-review-episodes-made-precise):

1. **Define the actual review crossing and recurrence.** “Qualifying change” and
   “one episode” alone leave mandatory work underspecified. A finite rule should
   identify a supported financial dimension and a configured review range, and
   observe a comparable transition into that condition. One continuing condition
   must not regenerate a task every poll or after a reviewer records No change
   needed. A fresh episode needs an independently qualified re-arm/transition;
   baseline creation, restored coverage, changed rules and retries cannot supply
   it. Multiple active reasons share the one source review instead of one task
   per axis. **Resolved:** the main contract uses a finite selected-band/known-empty
   OR predicate, one outside-to-inside episode after baseline, no re-arm while
   still inside, and a proved later exit before fresh entry. Unknown is not a
   proved exit. This is bounded native policy, not an arbitrary workflow engine.

2. **Make the exact monetary formulas operative and guard the as-of date.** The
   [financial appendix](financial.md) F01–F07 defines effective-positive distinct
   gift roots, one source lens, scoped amount, return treatment and in-period
   recency. The main requirements and acceptance criteria must consume that
   contract expressly. With proved complete empty input, frequency and amount
   can be zero while recency has no numeric value. Unknown input is different.
   A gift after the qualified evaluation as-of date must not produce negative
   recency or future received-giving inference. Reject unsupported evidence or
   keep it outside that evaluation according to the financial owner contract.
   **Resolved:** the main contract states the positive effective gift set and exact
   F/M/R arithmetic, empty-period condition, return treatment and explicit calendar
   interval. Its as-of boundary prevents negative or future-gift recency. Numeric
   bands retain units and explicit inclusivity; their values are tenant policy,
   not invented universal currency tiers.

These corrections narrow implementation and testability, not the founder's combined
product choice. No unresolved material correction remains from this independent
review, and no further founder micro-question is needed.

vendor benefit claims and product judgments. Blackbaud, Salesforce, DonorPerfect
and Bloomerang are useful contrary comparisons, not evidence of a universal

this independent review, and no measured fundraising or usability benefit is
