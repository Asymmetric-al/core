# D1 — Continuing purpose-specific cultivation, missions fit

2026-10-09. Research and decision note only. Founder answer:
“A. but make sure this best and most appropriate for Christian missions
organizations that have missionaries.”

**Accepted scope:** A continuing, purpose-specific relationship journey is the
default unit of cultivation. Successive asks, annual renewals and stewardship
can belong to that journey. The founder's missions qualification reinforces
Asym's existing sending-organization/support-raising operating model.

**Assessment:** retain A. Official MPDX/TntConnect workflows and current-published
agency practice support continuing partnership beyond an initial gift or
fundraising season. This is a reasoned fit for Asym's target organizations,
not a claim that one model is empirically best for every Christian ministry.
See [source rechecks and scenarios](phase27-q1-ministry-check.md).

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A continuing journey could become perpetual solicitation, an unattended open
record, or duplicated outreach by agency and missionary. Continuing partnership
does not mean every ask stays open, every task requires donor contact, or every
staff/participant change starts a fresh journey. Existing suppression and shared
effect boundaries remain; pause/end/exception rules still need decisions.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

We are designing for sending organizations whose missionaries raise personal
support, as already established in platform intent. A missionary person, couple,
team, ministry and financial account are not interchangeable. The exact ministry
target is a next decision; D1 does not imply spouse access, automatic supporter
ownership, one financial methodology or one agency's required outreach cadence.

### How does this affect the whole product?

Phase 27 retains cultivation meaning and Phase 28 uses the same source model
through its authorized scope. Church agency partnership and support for specific
ministries may remain distinct purposes. Finance retains gifts/commitments;
Support retains service work; public publishing receives no internal context.
Departure preserves history and requires source-owned handling of obligations
and access, rather than automatic transfer of a support network.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Initial support raising, ongoing support and home assignment can preserve
relationship continuity. Annual asks do not require re-entering the partner or
treating them as a fresh prospect. Prayer and other nonfinancial partnership
remain legitimate purposes. The routine screen should show the current purpose,
responsibility and appropriate next action; detailed rules remain to be groomed.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

MPDX/TntConnect distinguish ongoing financial/special/prayer partnership from
special appeals. OMF describes home assignment as continuing church/supporter
ministry; Reliant documents sustaining support development after an initial goal.
Source dates and limitations are retained in the linked note. These support the
default without importing contact-global stages, irreversible account merges,
agency quotas, wealth screening or automatic consent.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes: platform intent, Phase 9 A12, roadmap v3 Phases 27–28, and the accepted
Ministry Assignment identity support a shared missions model. Source contracts
do not yet settle the exact enrollment target or uniqueness. Existing donor-only
readers and demo task collections remain transitional implementation evidence.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Retain A and record the missions-specific interpretation above. No new authority
or scope amendment is needed to accept this default. Exact ministry target,
creation, sharing, stage/lifecycle and handoff rules remain open; none is silently
ratified by this decision.

## Next frontier — Q2, unanswered

For an ordinary ongoing support-raising purpose served by an individual,
missionary couple or team, should the cultivation journey attach to the
organization's identified ministry, or separately to each missionary person?

Recommend the existing **Ministry Assignment** as that ministry reference. One
person can serve alone; a couple/team shares a journey only when the organization
has actually identified one ministry. Distinct ministries remain distinct. This
is a recommendation to discuss, not accepted schema or permission policy.

The donor/church remains a Party. Staff responsibility, ministry participation,
financial Support Assignment, public Page and permitted access remain separate.
Choosing a ministry reference would not automatically enroll its supporters,
create an agency-level parent enrollment, or share private records.
