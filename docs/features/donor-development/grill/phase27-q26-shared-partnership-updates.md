## Why this question is next

[D25](phase27-d25-adversarial-review.md) now settles the source-owned gift handoff.
The next meaningful frontier is **how staff and missionaries see routine updates
about partnership work they share**. It affects daily coordination, trust and
subsequent briefs/attention, rather than another button or financial status.

One underlying interaction history is already required by the
[retained owner obligations](../../../prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md:129)
and [Phase 28 roadmap](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:4169).
Neither option below introduces separate logs or requires entering the same call

explicitly reserve staff/missionary visibility, also preserved in log E5/E9 and
the original brief's shared-context section. Shared source identity does not settle
who can see each part of the context.

## Founder question — A selected

**Q26 — When staff and a missionary work together with a church, how should
routine partnership updates be shared?**

For example, the missionary meets the church's missions committee and agrees to
follow up after its budget meeting. Agency staff are preparing their own church
conversation. This illustrates the choice; it is not claimed as an observed
workflow or a requirement that all church relationships have this structure.

**A — Shared working history (recommended).** Routine updates recorded for that
shared partnership are visible by default to the staff and missionary already
authorized to see them. Show the audience clearly before saving; keep private
and sensitive notes separate.

**B — Share individual updates deliberately.** An update stays within its original
permitted audience until an authorized person deliberately shares eligible content
with the other collaborators. This gives authors more control but adds a sharing
step and makes coordination depend on remembering it.

**Recommendation:** A for newly recorded, eligible routine partnership context,
with a clearly identified audience and separate private-note treatment. This is a
product judgment favoring coordination, not a claim of measured user preference.
Neither choice shares existing private CRM notes or grants access merely because
someone is involved in a partnership. The founder subsequently selected A and
explicitly rejected treating missionaries as staff to be controlled.

## Boundaries in either option

- Share only a specifically qualified development-interaction projection, within
  current Tenant, source, field, purpose and audience authority. "Authorized
  collaborators" is descriptive, not a new role, permission group or team engine.
  Staff do not automatically see everything a missionary records, and missionaries
  do not gain all staff CRM history.
- Limit the fork to newly authored sharing-eligible routine context. Existing
  private notes, restricted finance, Support/care bodies, imported history and
  unrelated relationships are not automatically shared. A routine-looking title
  does not downgrade protected content or authorize its summary.
- Make **who can see this** understandable before save and in later detail. A
  private entry must not become shared merely by opening a shared view, changing
  an assignment, adding an appeal link or switching an acting role. No silent
  fallback to a broader audience when the intended audience is unavailable.
- Reference the one actual encounter and source-owned content. A shared history
  does not mean one universally readable payload, duplicated transcripts or a new
  universal encounter service. Distinct development-purpose notes may refer to
  that encounter without becoming another authority for what happened.
- Neither option creates a donor message, consent, meaningful-contact evidence,
  stage change, task completion or financial outcome from visibility alone. A
  private event cannot leak through shared counts, scores, briefs, notifications,
  exports or cached summaries. Revocation applies to all those projections.
- Asym's shared record shell and UI remain the presentation foundation. Exact
  controls, routine corrections, truthful save feedback and mobile/accessibility
  behavior are delegated design work, not additional founder micro-questions.
- A full future Missionary Workspace is not a blanket prerequisite for native
  staff development. Qualify the exact shared source and consumer before enabling
  that surface; do not work around missing contracts by copying private notes.
