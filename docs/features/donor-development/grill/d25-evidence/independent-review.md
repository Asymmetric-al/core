Reviewed 2026-10-10 UTC; final targeted readback 19:04 UTC. Core HEAD

does not claim another executed experiment, a live permission audit, product
implementation, user research or subsequent founder ratification. Only this

**Disposition: Accept with the explicit required owner amendments recorded in the
synthesis.** The useful selected capability is appeal-side visibility and action
on the same finance intake. It requires truthful receipt evidence, accountability
and exact continuation/resolution in P15; an arbitrary draft link does not provide
those facts. The amendment is an additive owner requirement for future `/to-prd`
reconciliation, not a claim that this source capability is already implemented.
It does not grant finance permissions or require a second gift ledger, custody
platform, compulsory precursor form or further founder microdecision.

## Initial findings and verified resolutions

Grades describe the consequence of an unqualified implementation and conditional
likelihood, not measured incidents. Early framing findings were sent before the
integrated main draft; their inclusion here does not imply the final main retained
them. The last finding arose from the integrated acceptance-matrix readback.

### IR1 — A draft is not a receipt report, but incomplete staging is already allowed

**High / plausible.** P15's draft fields, received date and receiving-staff field do
not themselves establish current physical possession or accountable next handling.
Conversely, P15 already permits incomplete donor/amount fields in valid staged
rows. Describing every unknown amount or donor as requiring a new precursor would
misstate the owner and add unnecessary intake work. Unknown intended Legal Entity
also cannot be solved by weakening a financial root's mandatory scope.

**Required correction:** identify the additive P15 action/evidence/projection and
owned unresolved-intake path, reuse an existing admissible source intake, and allow
one ordinary authorized staff report without mandatory image, signature or second
approver. Reporting, source viewing, handling assignment, financial entry/posting
and receipt/fulfillment actions retain distinct qualified authority. An unresolved
operational report is not a nullable financial-root escape hatch.

**Resolution verified:** the opening, exact decision, eight owner-extension items
and explanation of the operational “envelope” now say this explicitly. “Envelope”
does not prescribe another table or form. R01–R06, AC01–AC04, AC13 and AC23 make the
observable boundaries testable. The amendment remains necessary owner work rather
than silent reuse of the retired 501 writer. This resolves the concern without
requiring the whole future finance/storage/Studio program before the narrow lane.

### IR2 — Financial recording cannot settle independent physical handling

**High / plausible where one status drives all completion.** P15 allows entry and
deposit independently. Finance may record a check while the original still needs
delivery or deposit; the original may reach finance before entry. Reporter,
reported holder, next-action owner and posting actor can also differ. Neither task
reassignment nor posting demonstrates physical movement. An initial UX clause
coupled “Recorded” to completion of the handoff obligation.

**Required correction:** resolve only the exact source-owned action. Show the
financial recording fact from its exact source evidence and preserve applicable
handling separately, reusing existing source tasks. Do not add a perpetual custody
certification task. A settled exception must be able to end its real obligation
without fabricating a contribution.

**Resolution verified:** [UX03/UX06](ux.md), the exact decision, transition table,
R07–R10 and AC08–AC11 separate the dimensions. UX06 now explicitly permits
**Recorded by finance** with remaining handling. The main preserves actual handling
through archive, appeal closure, staff departure and lawful exception resolution,
without making physical transfer or bank clearance a posting gate.

### IR3 — A source revision is not a reason to forget an accepted gift

**High / plausible if a generic version counter is treated as business meaning.**
The experiment's revision guard models a material source/coverage change. Copying
it indiscriminately could turn a note, display correction or reassignment into
“unrecorded” work, despite valid accepted financial lineage.

**Required correction:** distinguish incompatible material source or coverage
changes from incidental edits. Preserve the historical receipt and accepted
financial linkage; derive any new reconciliation or handling from actual current
source facts. Same-intent transport replay cannot overwrite a later disposition.

**Resolution verified:** the database contract, R12 and AC17 now state this boundary
directly. The probe's version guard is described as synthetic mechanics, not a
universal product revision schema. No prior passing experiment needed to be rerun
to correct the product language.

### IR4 — An exception does not erase a previously valid recording

**High / plausible if the original acceptance criterion were implemented literally.**
Initial AC09 said lost, returned, disputed and rejected items “cannot be marked
Recorded by finance.” A check already recorded may subsequently be lost or returned.
The wording contradicted the otherwise correct independent dimensions and AC11's
preserved financial history.

**Exact correction requested:** an exception disposition or generic task action
cannot _itself establish_ recording or erase a previously source-confirmed
recording; preserve current owner-qualified financial status/history and any
remaining handling.

**Resolution verified:** final AC09 now contains that qualification. It permits the
honest recorded-then-lost/returned case while still rejecting “mark the exception
complete” as evidence of posting. It agrees with R07–R10 and AC08/AC11. This was the
only additional material contradiction found in the completed integrated draft.

## Independent scope and alternative checks

- **The no-build alternative remains credible.** Existing finance intake and
  ordinary notes/tasks can coordinate a small team's handoff without an appeal
  projection. They do not independently supply qualified financial lineage or
  automatic resolution. A is proportionate only as a view/action on the same source
  item. No evidence establishes universal demand, improved completion rates or a
  preferred label; those remain actual pilot questions.
- **No financial or access shortcut is selected.** Report-only visibility is a
  separately qualified possibility, not a blanket missionary/staff grant. Current
  source restrictions govern even limited completion facts, counts, exports and
  stale replies. Money remains P13/P15-owned and fulfillment P16-owned. Reported
  possession is an attributed assertion, not independently verified continuous
  custody, legal acceptance, tax-date authority or permission to contact a donor.
- **Identity and cardinality remain appropriately bounded.** Separate reports of
  one item require qualified duplicate reconciliation; idempotent transport alone
  cannot solve that problem. Multiple appeals/designations must not multiply money
  or intake. Distinct checks remain distinct. The source must resolve coverage,
  including partial cases, without choosing a universal one-handoff/one-header
  schema or matching by donor, amount, date or check number.
- **The interface need not become another finance workbench.** The compact report,
  status and recovery flow uses existing shared components and task identity.
  AC22 now explicitly preserves P15's full-grid mobile boundary while requiring
  the small reporting flow to work on phones. No image picker, extra universal
  approval, second appeal queue or universal online-gift stage is introduced.
- **All 22 review categories have material concern, grade, evidence qualification,
  effect and R/AC references.** R01–R19 and AC01–AC32 cover owner reconciliation,
  permissions, exact financial linkage, independent handling, recovery, lifecycle,
  privacy, accessibility, rollout and meaningful operational proof. Monitoring is
  supplementary; proposed five-minute/window thresholds are not claimed measured
  performance or substitutes for preventive qualification. No material category
  omission or unresolved ownership contradiction was found after the corrections.

## Proof assessment and remaining implementation obligations

Read the [experiment README](handoff-probe/README.md), setup/mechanics and retained
results/metadata. The artifacts support **7/7 cases on the first runtime attempt**,
PostgreSQL **17.11**, read committed, 2026-10-10 18:51:57–18:52:04 UTC, with **three
observed separate-session lock schedules** and exact container cleanup. This is
source-confirmation consumption against independently seeded finance facts. It
demonstrates local consumer rollback/recovery, exact coverage, ordered material
revision and historical/idempotent replay in that fixture.

It does **not** demonstrate initial reporting, P15 posting atomicity, physical
receipt veracity, real P12/RLS authority, duplicate physical-item identification,
current source revocation races, actual tasks/outbox behavior, browser usability,
retention or production scale. The two source references and lab bounds do not
choose final product cardinality or workload limits. The main now preserves those
limits and does not count earlier experiments again.

The future owner reconciliation and real release qualification remain substantive
work. Their existence does not require another founder vote on routine safeguards,
and this review does not claim that either implementation or formal reconciliation
has occurred. Final readback found no remaining material contradiction requiring a
different D25 disposition.
