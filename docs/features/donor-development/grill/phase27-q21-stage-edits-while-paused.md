## Founder question — answered A

**Q21 — While partnership work is paused, may staff update its stage or stage list
without resuming the work?**

**A — Allow updates while paused (recommended).** Authorized staff can keep the
record accurate or choose a better-fitting approved list. The work remains paused,
and its review date and obligations stay governed by the existing pause rules.

**B — Wait until work resumes.** Keep the recorded stage and list unchanged while
paused; staff can update them when the work genuinely resumes.

**Recommendation:** A. New information or a mistaken classification can be dealt
with while preserving the deliberate pause. B's strongest benefit is a clear freeze
on the stage/process record until work resumes. Do not present B as a reason to
resume temporarily and pause again merely to edit. These are product judgments,
not measured staff preferences or claimed agency workflows.
