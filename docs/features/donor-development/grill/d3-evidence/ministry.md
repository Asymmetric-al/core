# D3 ministry-practice adversarial review

> Research input, 2026-10-09. The [consolidated D3 review](../phase27-d3-adversarial-review.md)
> reconciles these recommendations and controls decision status. This is
> grooming evidence, not an approved schema, formal specification or proof
> of application/production qualification.

2026-10-09. Research/grooming only. Founder selected one ordinary partnership journey, with extra journeys only for genuinely independent continuing purposes. D1 and amended D2 remain settled. The amendments below are recommendations for the requested review, not new ratified decisions. No repository, production, financial, communication or permission change was made.

## Finding in brief

Retain the ordinary partnership default. **The evidence reviewed does not establish a concrete need for generic additional-purpose machinery for the exact same supporter and identified ministry.** It establishes multiple ways of partnering, particular appeals, formal sending relationships, and other operational responsibilities. Those are not interchangeable with independently staged P27 journeys.

That is a bounded research result, not proof that no mission agency ever needs an additional continuing development purpose. Preserve the possibility without manufacturing a “typical” example or building an arbitrary purpose catalog. The phrase “independent progress, follow-through and ending” is insufficient by itself: an ordinary ask also has all three.

## Current official evidence and authority limits

All URLs below were retrieved or rechecked on **2026-10-09**. Current-published documentation is not a verification of a running product or a current universal agency policy. Word-for-word quotations are avoided; observations are concise paraphrases.

1. **OMF International, Support an OMF Worker** — undated public practice page. <https://omf.org/get-involved/support-a-worker/>. Prayer, practical help and financial provision are presented as ways of partnership with a worker; practical examples include accommodation/storage and help during return visits. **Supports:** multifaceted ordinary partnership. **Does not establish:** OMF's CRM enrollment model, one universal workflow, or a need for distinct journeys per participation mode.

2. **OMF Germany practice reported on the OMF Australia site**, “Partnering with Sending Churches and the Global Influence of the Local Church,” **published 2018-12-17**. <https://omf.org/au/partnering-with-sending-churches-and-the-global-influence-of-the-local-church/>. Describes a cooperation agreement allocating agency, missionary and sending-church responsibilities; churches provide spiritual/personal support and may contribute money. Joint home-assignment conversations evaluate the partnership. **Supports:** sending involves responsibilities beyond giving. **Limits:** older regional practice account, not a global current policy; its quotas/cadence must not become Core defaults. A cooperation agreement is not evidence of two same-target development journeys.

3. **Reliant International Churches & Ministries** — undated official program page. <https://reliant.org/partner/partners/international>. Describes church/ministry partnerships encompassing assessment, training, employment, field support, transitions and crisis support. **Supports:** “partnership” can name operational relationships substantially broader than fundraising. **Limits:** Reliant's international program; descriptive page, not a P27 domain specification or proof that every duty belongs in donor development.

4. **MPDX web help, MPDX Status, MPD Phase**, **updated 2023-09-18**. <https://help.mpdx.org/article/162-mpdx-status-mpd-phase>. Financial, special and prayer statuses exist; only one contact status is supported and tags are suggested when more than one description applies. **Supports:** modes overlap enough to create a documented representation problem. **Limits:** do not copy the exclusive contact-global status or assume tags are Asym's source model. This documents a limitation, not an empirical preference for either D3 option.

5. **MPDX web help, Setup an Appeal**, **updated 2022-10-13**. <https://help.mpdx.org/article/190-set-up-appeal>. Distinguishes special financial appeals from monthly goals and supports an appeal-specific selected audience/exclusions. **Supports:** a particular special need is not inherently a new continuing partnership. **Limits:** not evidence of Core communication permission, fulfillment, or exact ask/enrollment cardinality.

## Material concerns, exact amendments and falsifiable checks

Likelihood ratings are qualitative design judgments, not measured prevalence. These are implementation risks, not demonstrated defects in live P27 software.

### M1 — “Independent” can authorize arbitrary parallel pipelines

**Severity: high. Likelihood: medium-high if arbitrary labels are allowed.** A second ask, annual review, different representative, private note set or reporting segment can each be described as needing separate progress and follow-through. The adopted next-action requirement would then multiply real obligations and potential outreach without a genuinely different relationship purpose.

**Exact proposed amendment:** “A second concurrent journey for the same supporter and ministry requires a separately admitted, distinct continuing development purpose. Independence is necessary but is not established merely by separate status, tasks, responsible staff, permissions or an ask. Identify the relationship outcome that remains meaningful beyond completion of a particular request and why an existing ask, task, role or authoritative relationship cannot express it. D3 does not approve a free-form purpose engine or a catalog of speculative purpose types.”

This is a product boundary, not a new manager-approval workflow, score, mandatory essay or generic validation framework. Precise admission behavior remains a later founder decision. No source reviewed justifies prebuilding generic extra-purpose machinery before a concrete case is groomed.

**Check:** monthly support plus prayer plus equipment request plus annual budget conversation produces the ordinary journey with appropriate existing work, not four journeys. Renaming “annual ask” to “annual partnership” cannot by itself qualify an exception. Ask staff for an actual additional-purpose case; explain why its outcome cannot be handled by the existing lower-level objects before admitting it.

### M2 — One journey can accidentally become one exclusive participation status

**Severity: high. Likelihood: high if familiar MPDX/Tnt status labels are copied directly.** “Prayer,” “financial,” and “special” can become mutually exclusive rungs, making prayer a preliminary financial stage or marking an engaged person as a failed/lost partner when giving changes.

**Exact proposed amendment:** “One ordinary journey does not make participation modes mutually exclusive or rank their spiritual or relationship value. Financial evidence, recorded nonfinancial participation, communication preferences, ask responses and journey progression retain separate meanings. A financial change alone neither proves prayer participation nor ends, restarts or advances the partnership journey.”

No new participation-table schema is implied. The evidence and authoring rules for nonfinancial participation remain to be decided; do not infer actual prayer from receipt/opening of an update.

**Checks:** a documented prayer partner makes one gift without becoming a recurring financial partner or losing prayer context; a supporter stops recurring giving but independently records continued prayer engagement without a forced second journey or “downgrade”; a recurring giver does not acquire a prayer-partner assertion automatically. A financial decline is recorded accurately without rejecting the whole relationship, and any broader no-contact instruction still takes effect.

### M3 — Sending-church responsibility can smuggle care and deployment into cultivation

**Severity: high. Likelihood: medium.** Sending churches have documented responsibilities beyond funding. That makes them the strongest real-world challenge to oversimplification, but it does not establish an additional P27 source for pastoral care, ministry assessment, deployment or ecclesial accountability. Agency partnership may also have a genuinely different target from ordinary ministry support.

**Exact proposed amendment:** “Sending and financial support remain distinct authoritative relationships; one ordinary journey does not collapse them. A sending-church label alone does not create another cultivation journey. P27 may reference authorized development-relevant work, while relationship facts, deployment/application decisions, protected care and other operational obligations remain with their owners. A separately proposed agency-level development purpose must use its own admitted target and scope, not a fabricated duplicate of the ministry.”

**Checks:** a church's financial support change does not remove its sending relationship; a pastoral-care discussion is not copied into a development brief; an annual partnership review can produce appropriate tasks without inventing a new enrollment. Church remittances, member personal gifts and church-owned giving remain distinct source facts; no church role opens member records.

### M4 — Confidentiality must neither split nor flatten the relationship

**Severity: high. Likelihood: medium.** Staff may create a “private purpose” solely to hide notes, or assume one journey means all participants may see all prayer/financial/context data. Both misuse purpose as authorization. Real prayer and sending contexts can be sensitive even when money is absent.

**Exact proposed amendment:** “A difference in visibility is not itself a distinct cultivation purpose. One journey does not unify permissions. Each source reference, note, financial fact and action retains independently authorized visibility and communication eligibility. Participation, church office or a new purpose grants no access; a hidden existing journey must not be disclosed through duplicate warnings, counts or labels.”

**Checks:** a missionary can work with an independently permitted brief without receiving staff-private context; the same encounter is not copied into a second history to support a private/public split; creating or discovering a proposed second purpose does not reveal restricted existence. This carries D2 and owner boundaries forward; it does not ratify the still-open sharing amendment.

### M5 — A simplifying default can become an unsupported “one forever” restriction

**Severity: medium-high. Likelihood: medium.** Current evidence is insufficient for arbitrary extras, but absence of a documented case is not proof of impossibility. A permanent same-pair uniqueness rule could block a later qualified purpose or confuse a renewed relationship with a historical record. Conversely, reopening and duplication could be treated as interchangeable.

**Exact proposed amendment:** “D3 establishes an ordinary concurrent-purpose boundary, not one enrollment per supporter/ministry for all time. It does not settle pause, closure, reopening, historical import or corrective consolidation. Preserve legitimate distinct admitted purposes and source history; do not choose physical uniqueness or merge behavior from this decision alone.”

**Check:** a returning partner's old history remains interpretable; the yet-to-be-groomed lifecycle rule—not a label, donation import or new year's date—determines reuse/reopening/new work. No Supabase constraint or migration is authorized by this research note.

## Strongest alternative and what would change the assessment

The strongest practical alternative is a real agency that manages a distinct, continuing church-development outcome independently from the same church's ordinary support of the very same ministry. For example, formal sending responsibilities make such a hypothesis worth investigating. **That is a hypothesis, not a documented requirement for two journeys.** Many resulting obligations may already be tasks, Party relationships, agency-level partnership or another product's work.

Before committing additional-purpose machinery, obtain a de-identified real case from intended mission users. Establish the exact supporter and ministry, the two continuing development outcomes, what can end independently, and the concrete failure caused by one journey plus ordinary asks/tasks/roles. Check whether the alleged second purpose actually changes target or owner domain. Review with the missionary and the relevant church-relations/development user; include an authorized privacy reviewer if context differs. Do not invent sample-size, timing or success-rate claims.

A useful ordinary-case usability check is whether those users can keep prayer engagement, financial requests and a sending relationship understandable without creating duplicate journeys or erasing a distinction. Record observed confusion and workarounds; source articles alone cannot establish this UX outcome.

## Disposition

**Keep A; bound the exception and preserve independent meanings.** Evidence supports the unified ordinary partnership and separate asks, not a universal exclusive status or a generic purpose engine. The highest-value amendment is M1. M2–M4 prevent oversimplification from erasing participation, ownership and confidentiality. M5 prevents absence of evidence from becoming an irreversible overconstraint. No new founder decision is silently accepted here.
