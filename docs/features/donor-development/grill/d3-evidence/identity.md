## Conclusion and bounded permanent decision

Retain A. It reduces routine duplication without erasing the distinction between relationship work, particular asks, task obligations, source financial facts, and permissions. The dangerous shortcut is turning this product boundary into forever-global Party+ministry uniqueness or allowing an arbitrary purpose label to evade it.

Proposed recording language:

> For a supporter Party and one identified Ministry Assignment in its exact Tenant and Legal Entity, ordinary financial and nonfinancial partnership is managed together in one continuing journey. Do not run duplicative concurrent journeys for that same ordinary partnership. Particular asks, annual renewals and special appeals belong to it when they serve that partnership. An additional concurrent journey requires an explicitly identified, genuinely independent relationship purpose needing its own cultivation/stewardship progression, accountable follow-through and ability to end independently. A different representative, role, stage preset, year, currency, channel or label is not by itself a new relationship purpose. Distinct purposes need not have different responsible people.
>
> “Continuing” does not mean perpetual solicitation or prevent a legitimate purpose from ending. Preserve independent partner instructions, participation facts and ask outcomes inside the shared journey; financial participation is not the measure of prayer partnership or a prerequisite for appropriate stewardship. The relationship purpose is an operational reason for managing work, not authorization, consent, a financial restriction or a Designation.
>
> Preserve D2's history and correction boundary. Changing a label or stage does not create new work; an admitted mistake correction retains its prior meaning and evidence; genuinely different purpose or ministry requires explicit future disposition. Duplicate attempts at the same intended work resolve once without silently collapsing distinct histories or creating duplicate downstream effects. D3 does not settle entry, pause/end/reopen behavior, stage mapping, exact correction eligibility, sharing, a purpose catalog, or a database uniqueness key.

This is the smallest durable product statement. One bounded P27 source remains responsible for admission and change; no purpose registry, configurable workflow platform, new merge engine, or generalized succession graph follows. Database constraints can enforce qualified scope and declared identities; they cannot determine that two arbitrary prose descriptions represent independent real-world purposes. Exact persistence choices belong after the remaining product decisions and implementation qualification.

## Material concerns and exact prevention

Likelihood is conditional qualitative judgment, not measured incidence. Scenarios below are falsifiable design tests; none is claimed to be a reported production event.

### I1 — Forever-global uniqueness erases legitimate future work

**High severity; high likelihood if “one journey” is implemented as unqualified `Party + ministry` uniqueness.** E1–E3 expressly preserve genuine purposes and unresolved lifecycle; E6 permits one person in multiple ministries.

**Effect:** retain A, narrow its interpretation. A supporter can have distinct admitted purposes for one ministry and independently scoped relationships with other ministries. Historical ended journeys cannot be overwritten to satisfy a database slot.

**Exact correction:** “One ordinary partnership prevents duplicate concurrent ordinary work; it is not a lifetime one-row-per-Party/ministry rule. Preserve ended history and admitted distinct purposes. Select exact collision and reopen rules before choosing persistence constraints.”

**Proof:** same Party/same ministry same ordinary intent collides; same Party/same ministry genuinely distinct admitted purpose remains possible; same Party/different ministry remains distinct; completed historical work remains attributable when later work is admitted. No global Party stage or cross-ministry roster union.

### I2 — Active-only uniqueness makes pause/reopen a duplicate loophole

**High; high under pause/create/resume races if only the currently active row is considered.** E2 preserves no-replay and current eligibility; E4 active-next-action obligation; #607's active-edge rule is not P27 lifecycle.

**Effect:** the need to handle all competing current/retained work is a safeguard; whether a paused journey reserves its purpose, when reopening is allowed, and whether ended work resumes or gets linked future work remain product choices.

**Exact correction:** “Pausing, ending, reopening or receiving a late event must not manufacture a second instance of the same intended work or erase unfinished obligations. Revalidate conflicts, source eligibility, current responsibility and restrictions. Do not infer enrollment lifecycle from Party lifecycle, assignment end dates, ask outcomes or ministry participant membership.”

**Proof:** pause → attempt another ordinary journey → resume; concurrent end/create/reopen; end with an outstanding ask/task; delayed event after close; retired ministry plus reopen attempt. The chosen policy later must yield one admitted intended work or a visible authorized unresolved state, with history intact. This review does not invent state enums or demand that paused equals active.

### I3 — A purpose label is not either business distinctness or authority

**High; high if free text or a new UUID is treated as sufficient independence.** E3's test is a genuinely separate relationship purpose; E8's processing-purpose authorization is a separate owner fact; E10 deduplicates effects across producers.

**Effect:** retain a small operational admission rule. Different strings, aliases, staff, years or currencies cannot make the same ordinary support purpose different. A database cannot establish semantic independence from prose alone.

**Exact correction:** “Record the actual relationship purpose and why separate cultivation/stewardship responsibility is needed; do not derive independence from a new label, identifier, preset, actor or channel. This is an attributable source admission, not a new mandatory manager-approval workflow or configurable purpose taxonomy. Relationship purpose grants no data access, consent or financial designation.”

**Proof:** ‘2027 support’, ‘annual partner’ and ‘financial support’ for the same ordinary work cannot bypass the boundary merely through labels; a real independent purpose can be explained without a different owner. Unauthorized create/duplicate lookup cannot disclose hidden journeys. Pure unique-key tests are insufficient proof of this product rule.

### I4 — Stage presets or staff roles create parallel truth

**High; medium-high if staff/missionary views each demand their own pipeline.** E4 says one primitive with staff and P28 presets; E5 assignments are responsibility, not access or enrollment; E8 labels are not powers.

**Effect:** preset/role changes may change presentation or governed progression under a future stage decision, but do not establish a new purpose. A donor representative and missionary coordinating ordinary support must not create two source journeys just to see different columns.

**Exact correction:** “Staff role, responsible person, surface and stage preset do not by themselves justify another journey. Preserve one underlying source and history. Decide exact preset mapping/change semantics separately; do not derive a conversion table, dual writable stage or automatic stage reset from D3.”

**Proof:** concurrent staff and missionary operation references one ordinary journey; authorized responsibility change preserves asks and source effects; changing a preset retains attributable historical stage meaning; no role grants access. P9 role cardinality conflicts are resolved at P9, not by extra journeys or stored portfolios.

### I5 — Simplifying the journey collapses prayer participation and ask outcomes

**High; medium if one relationship stage is treated as one universal engagement/financial state.** E1 recognizes prayer and nonfinancial partnership; E3 forbids treating prayer as a preliminary financial stage; E4 keeps asks and money distinct.

**Effect:** A combines relationship management, not every fact or task. A declined equipment request does not close a church partnership; a financial gift does not prove prayer participation; stopping financial support does not establish loss of the entire relationship. A prayer-only partner need not be advanced into solicitation.

**Exact correction:** “Preserve independently meaningful participation, partner instructions and individual ask responses within the journey. The shared journey does not force one financial progression or equate financial response with relationship health. A next action may be permitted internal stewardship/review or an explicit actionable exception.”

**Proof:** prayer-only partnership with never-ask restriction; regular support plus declined special ask; financial support stops while permitted nonfinancial partnership continues; participation is unknown. No fabricated prayer evidence, extra financial reminder or forced financial stage. Exact participation fields and stage names remain unchosen.

### I6 — Wrong-purpose correction becomes silent repurposing

**High; medium under ordinary data-entry errors and evolving relationship plans.** E2's ratified correction rule already settles preservation; E7 permits only admitted mutable repair.

**Effect:** retain correction without either an absolute immutable-reference ban or an unrestricted edit. Distinguish typo/label, mistaken same-intent association, and genuinely changed relationship purpose. Accepted ask/encounter/source financial facts retain original meaning even if a current reference is repaired.

**Exact correction:** “Correct a mistaken association only through the qualified owner path with previous value, reason, actor, revision and affected history retained. An actual new purpose is explicit future work, not a correction. Relabeling does not change the underlying purpose. A correction cannot rewrite accepted effects or make a new financial/contact instruction.”

**Proof:** correction before any ask; proposed correction after an ask was presented or a task completed; correction to a currently retired/inaccessible/cross-entity ministry; stale concurrent correction. The exact allowed correction matrix must be decided later. Do not import P22's pre-release/post-release Page rule or invent a universal successor engine.

### I7 — Party merge silently merges journeys and revives effects

**Critical for permission leakage; high for operational integrity. Medium likelihood if existing merge repoint is extended mechanically.** E5/E7/E8; fresh #512/#610/#675. Two duplicate Party records can already each have ordinary journeys for the same ministry before merge.

**Effect:** P27 must join the existing merge reference inventory through an exact owner contract. Canonical Party repair is not permission union, journey-history merge, money reattribution or proof that every cultivation purpose is identical. Graph-edge collision rules cannot dispose of business asks/tasks.

**Exact correction:** “A Party merge preserves original Party/actor/history and only source-approved current-reference repair. Any newly colliding cultivation work receives an explicit P27 disposition through the existing merge integration; no automatic stage winner, destructive history collapse, grant union or duplicated follow-up. Until a collision is safely resolved, preserve an actionable authorized exception and prevent duplicate consequences.”

**Proof:** merge two ordinary journeys with different stages, owners, restrictions and pending asks; merge genuinely distinct purposes; strictest-tier read during repair; retry merge; unmerge after new actions/restrictions. Unmerge restores only admitted recorded repairs under current policy; post-merge work is not cloned or blindly reassigned. Do not build a second merge engine. The safe collision policy is still an owner/specification prerequisite, not already implemented by #512.

### I8 — Cross-ministry, entity or agency retargeting hides a real transfer

**Critical; medium if “one relationship” is generalized beyond D2's exact scope.** E2/E5/E6 fix ministry identity and Tenant/Legal Entity; E7 Party merge is within Tenant only. Ministry identity and Support Assignment remain different.

**Effect:** the same supporter can support several ministries; one missionary can participate in several. A staff move, new financial binding, team split or ministry retirement does not transfer the relationship or supporter assent. A church-wide agency partnership is not necessarily ordinary support of one ministry.

**Exact correction:** “Apply D3 only within the admitted supporter, purpose and exact ministry scope. A genuinely different ministry/Tenant/Legal Entity uses explicit future disposition under D2; source-certified same-real-world identity repair remains a distinct owner-approved case. Do not clone private histories, transfer gifts or automatically create agency-parent journeys.”

**Proof:** one person/two ministries, same church/two ministries, same name across entities, replacement Support Binding, ministry split/retirement and real agency transfer. Preserve originals and source-owned outcomes; no hidden grants or financial succession.

### I9 — Converging journeys accidentally coalesces distinct legitimate effects

**High; medium if journey ID is used as a universal task/message/financial deduplication key.** E4 and E10 require once per intended source effect, while separately authorized intentional messages remain possible. One ordinary journey may contain successive asks and several independently necessary tasks.

**Effect:** journey admission, ask identity, next-action obligations and task/message effect identity are related but different. One journey is not one lifetime email/task/ask. A rename, merge or correction cannot regenerate accepted effect keys and replay delivery.

**Exact correction:** “Deduplicate each intended business occurrence/effect at its owner. Preserve distinct asks and independently necessary follow-through inside one journey. Retry, alternate surface/workflow, label change or canonical identity repair is not a new occurrence; a legitimately new source-qualified ask or stewardship action may be.”

**Proof:** two annual asks in one continuing journey remain distinct; native/Studio request the same current task once; two separately authorized different messages stay distinct; replay after Party repair or purpose-label edit produces no repeat. Conflict on the same admitted effect with different payload remains explicit, not last-write-wins.

### I10 — “Keep it simple” either removes safeguards or builds a premature generic engine

**High if source safeguards are omitted; medium if generalized tooling delays useful delivery. Conditional likelihood medium.** E4 native completion, E5 shared-owner reuse, E6 minimal separate facts, E9 source boundaries. Fresh #1284 proves an intended dependency, not a delivered P9 service.

**Effect:** choose a small P27 owner operation over the established relational/source model, using shared tasks and existing authorization/merge owners. Qualify only the consumed P9/P12/task seams; neither full P21/P22/P28 nor Workflow Studio is a general blocker.

**Exact correction:** “No purpose registry, universal transition graph, parallel authorization store, enrollment-as-workflow, copied task engine or broad all-phase dependency is required. Preserve exact source identities, attributable history, current authority and concurrency-safe admission in the existing architecture. Missing required source behavior stays an explicit qualification gate, not a reason to use profile IDs, raw browser writes or demo records as authority.”

**Proof:** one native same-ministry ordinary partnership is created and managed end-to-end without optional workflow, public Page or financial Support Binding; concurrent admission, retained history, restrictive visibility and conflicting source change are tested at real owner seams. This review does not certify any Supabase deployment, migration, API or performance benchmark; that evidence belongs to implementation qualification.

## Dependencies and unresolved product choices

1. **Ratification wording:** accept A with the narrow distinctions above; the parent should expose only material amendments for explicit acceptance, without treating this appendix as a new decision bundle.
2. **Next product choices:** intentional entry versus qualified automatic entry; pause/end/reopen meaning and disposition of open obligations; stage presets/progression and changes; exact staff/missionary projections. Correctness constraints already apply, but this review does not choose those policies.
3. **Before specification:** translate purpose admission into a bounded check with examples and negative examples; define current/historical collision policy; define permitted correction consequences; explicitly extend the existing Party merge owner inventory. Only then fix uniqueness, allowed states or command shape.
4. **Before affected implementation:** qualify P9 exact ministry identity/lifecycle, P12 current authorization, shared tasks and only the finance/communication readers actually consumed. P27 source/effect identifiers must work natively and be reused later by DON-14/15/16.
5. **Permanent proof:** independent concurrent create/resume/merge/correction/retire/revoke barriers; late events and lost response after commit; old versus current source references; distinct admitted purposes; explicit retained-history and no-new-grants assertions. SQL shape alone is not evidence that product intent, visibility or external effects are correct.

Source-supported scenarios: multiple ministries per person, zero participants, personal life events, retirement/binding succession, deliberate Party merge/unmerge, explicit role separation, distinct asks and current source-purpose effects. Proposed pressure-test hypotheses: particular concurrent pause/resume collisions, two old duplicate journeys, purpose-label bypass, stage-preset duplication and post-merge new work. Their plausibility justifies proof requirements; this note makes no claim they occurred in production.
