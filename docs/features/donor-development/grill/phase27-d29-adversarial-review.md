## Exact corrected decision

> **D29 — An organization-level development appeal may coordinate financial,
> significant nonfinancial or mixed invitations around one shared ministry
> purpose.** Reuse D24's deliberately recorded working list and D22's Requests;
> do not create a second audience, request, task, donation or campaign authority.
> One appeal can contain different invitations and different responses from the
> same person or organization. Independently answerable invitations retain their
> own Requests and responses. A coherent invitation answered together may remain
> one Request; do not manufacture records for each installment, designation or
> reporting measure.
>
> A meaningful purpose is sufficient: a monetary or numerical goal is optional.
> Show financial goals and facts only where relevant and currently authorized.
> Show nonfinancial invitations and observed outcomes in their own terms, without
> invented money, a combined success score, forced participation targets or a
> measure of prayer, faithfulness or relationship worth. Agreement, actual
> participation, a financial commitment and received money are different facts.
> Interest or a conditional response does not become confirmed hosting, a booking,
> recurring giving or a financial promise. A recorded agreement to pray does not
> create indefinite reporting or monitoring work.
>
> The ordinary appeal view shows the shared purpose, responsible staff, recorded
> supporters, their distinct Requests/responses and appropriate next work. Staff
> can prepare an invitation or record an already-made request and response without
> fictional intermediate steps. They can open, revise, correct or follow through
> on the relevant Request using familiar shared controls. Financial columns or
> cards do not dominate a nonfinancial appeal. No single Asked/Committed/Received/
> Given column may stand for the whole person or church in a mixed appeal.
>
> Keep planning membership, presentation, response, actual outcome and work status
> separate. Every count states what it counts and its scope. Count a source Request
> once in an appeal; count distinct supporters separately. Multi-purpose and
> multi-appeal views may overlap and must be labelled accordingly. Combined totals
> deduplicate authoritative source identities; legal money and recognition are not
> added, and currencies and Legal Entities are never silently combined.
>
> An existing Request may be deliberately referenced by multiple relevant appeals
> without copying its terms, responses or work. Each reference requires current
> access and coherent purpose. A link grants no access, creates no financial
> allocation, adds nobody to planned outreach, and changes no Request ownership.
> Unlinking ends that coordination reference prospectively and preserves lawful
> history. Each outward action retains its exact producer purpose and applicable
> appeal membership/exclusion checks; another appeal cannot become a bypass.
>
> Closing an appeal ends its new planning and appeal-originated solicitation. It
> preserves Requests, responses, gifts, commitments and independent obligations.
> Before claiming closure, account for affected appeal-owned work: lawfully end it
> through its owner or deliberately preserve/hand off genuine continuing work with
> eligible responsibility and a visible next action or owned exception. Do not
> require unrelated financial or participation work to finish first, silently drop
> it, or mark it completed. Late facts and corrections remain recordable by
> authorized staff without reopening outreach. Reopening the same appeal is
> deliberate and never resurrects ended task or message occurrences.
>
> Apply current tenant, purpose, field and history permissions to records and all
> derived counts, filters, exports, briefs and notifications. Nonfinancial does not
> mean nonsensitive. Appeal access does not expose private prayer, member-care,
> hosting/location or financial details, and does not expand missionary, donor or
> public access. P6/P17 communications, P13 money, P14 recognition, P15 intake,
> P16 commitments, P9 identity/activity and shared work retain their authority.
> Preserve D23 review, D26 voluntary collaboration, D27 actual exchanges and D28
> genuine rating optionality. Ordinary work remains usable without optional Studio,
> public publishing, event management or scoring capabilities.

## Evidence, alternatives and confidence

The five research appendices inspect separate aspects of the same decision:
[practice](d29-evidence/practice.md), [ownership](d29-evidence/ownership.md),
[authorization](d29-evidence/authorization.md), [financial](d29-evidence/financial.md)
and [UX](d29-evidence/ux.md). The [independent challenge](d29-evidence/independent-review.md)
reviews the synthesis. Each distinguishes inspected source from proposed behavior.

**Repository facts.** [D22](phase27-d22-adversarial-review.md#exact-decision-recorded)
already admits financial/nonfinancial Requests and separate independently
answerable decisions. [D24](phase27-d24-adversarial-review.md#exact-decision-recorded)
owns planning membership, not response or money. [D25](phase27-d25-adversarial-review.md)
owns the qualified gift-intake handoff. The
[roadmap](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md#phase-27-what-it-covers) and
[retained requirements](../../../prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md#phase-27)
still describe financially oriented appeal goals/buckets. They do not explicitly
prohibit nonfinancial appeals. D29 qualifies that previously unresolved scope;
it does not reverse an earlier financial-only founder ruling.

**Current behavior.** The inspected CRM services and UI do not establish an
implemented, qualified D29 appeal capability. Local workspace stages, generic
activity, capped legacy donation reports and table selection are reuse seams,
not proof of this model. In particular, money reports limited to 5,000 legacy rows
without currency in the selected fields cannot supply complete appeal funding.
The appendix records exact code anchors. These are reuse/qualification findings,
not claims of an exploited production appeal vulnerability.

**External facts.** [OMF](https://omf.org/get-involved/support-a-worker/) describes
financial, prayer and practical support including hosting travelling workers.
[CiviCRM](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/campaign/what-is-civicampaign/)
coordinates activities and contributions within a programmatic effort; its
[everyday-tasks guidance](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/campaign/everyday-tasks/) makes
revenue goals conditional. [MPDX](https://help.mpdx.org/article/190-set-up-appeal)
explicitly scopes appeals to financial campaigns outside monthly goals. These
official sources support real categories and credible alternatives, not a
universal CRM model. Current availability does not make MPDX's 2022 guidance a
newly researched usability finding. CiviCRM's broader meaning of campaign must
not replace Core's Giving Campaign or introduce surveys/events into D29.

**Strongest alternative.** Keep appeals financial and use D22 Requests plus saved
views/tasks for other support. It is simpler when no coordinated nonfinancial
effort needs shared oversight. It fragments a mixed effort across contexts when
staff do need that overview. The chosen small extension reuses the existing
audience and request primitives. Ordinary individual reminders still belong in
Tasks; not every invitation requires an appeal.

**Product judgments.** One mixed-purpose context, a list-first daily view,
purpose-appropriate detail, optional numerical goals and explicit source
references are the least complex coherent implementation direction. The review
also makes multi-appeal reference and appeal closure behavior explicit; neither
was settled merely by selecting A. These do not grant a new financial or
communications authority.

**Unknowns.** Actual tenant demand/frequency, retained-data shape, deployment
policies, source adapters, peak workloads and measured composed usability have
not been verified. No practitioner interviews or customer tests are invented.
Implementation/release gates below identify how to prove them; documents alone
cannot establish a perfect UI or safe live system.

## Clear daily experience

Use the existing Mission Control record shell and `@asym/ui` Base UI/base-maia
components with Zinc semantic tokens. Clear hierarchy, space, restrained status
styling and predictable controls matter more than a decorative dashboard. The
[UX appendix](d29-evidence/ux.md) binds the current source seams and accessibility
evidence. This is a composed interaction contract, not a new component system.

1. **Create the effort.** Ask for a clear name, purpose and eligible responsible
   staff. Reuse existing defaults visibly. Allow an optional goal description and
   relevant existing financial goal details. Do not demand an appeal-type wizard,
   dollar amount, numerical participation target or configured taxonomy first.
2. **Build the working list.** Use D24's deliberate individual or finite group
   choices. Show exactly what was selected, exclusions and permitted restrictions.
   A church is not automatically all its contacts. “Select this page” and “Select
   all matching results” must not be interchangeable. Adding a supporter sends
   nothing and creates no Request or task by itself.
3. **Prepare or connect Requests.** Show the supporter and partnership context.
   Use existing D22 Requests; search/link is permission-scoped. One deliberate
   save can record an already-presented invitation, response and appropriate next
   work. No copying an existing request to obtain another view. Linking historical
   participation need not put the supporter back in the working list.
4. **Read what happened.** Each supporter row summarizes its distinct Requests:
   “Hosting — agreed; Funding — declined; Prayer partnership — awaiting response.”
   Partial/conditional terms remain obvious, with detail available without
   hiding essential meaning in a tooltip. If there are several pending actions,
   show that fact; one earliest task is not the whole work state. Search, filters
   and request detail keep the experience useful when many invitations exist.
5. **Take the next action.** Open the relevant Request, record its actual response,
   or open its current shared work. Distinguish recorded agreement from source-
   confirmed execution. Disabled actions explain the permitted reason and a useful
   recovery path; do not expose a hidden owner or restricted source to explain it.
6. **Understand results.** Summary labels name their grain, period and coverage:
   supporters in the working list; Requests awaiting a response; current recorded
   agreements; and qualified money in its own panel. No combined progress meter.
   A narrative nonfinancial aim is valid. This decision does not add an arbitrary
   metric/formula engine. A numerical progress claim requires a defined,
   source-supported quantity and denominator, never title matching or an invented
   conversion of activities to ministry impact.
7. **Correct, finish or return.** Ordinary editing is direct and reversible with
   history. Deliberate link removal, material scope changes and closure explain
   their actual consequences once. Closing shows what ends and what continues;
   the UI cannot claim closure until authoritative closure is saved. Late replies
   and source corrections remain truthful without reopening outreach.

Illustrative content, not an actual tenant record:

```text
Missionary visit · Open
Purpose: Invite churches to support the visit
Responsible: [eligible staff person]                       Change

Working list       Requests       History

Grace Church
Host a gathering       Agreed — dates still to arrange
Financial support      Declined
Prayer partnership     No response recorded

Next work: Arrange dates · [owner] · [due date]            Open task
View requests                                            Record response
```

“Agreed — dates still to arrange” is a response, not a booking. The Record response
action identifies which Request before saving; an obvious single Request can open
directly. An eligible nonfinancial-only user sees no financial amount or count
shadow. An unavailable source is not “No gifts” or “Nobody responded.”

The ordinary combined view is a readable list. Preserve roadmap list/kanban
support by making any board a **Request-level lens on one stated response/work
dimension**, never a supporter-wide sales funnel. A financial board shows only
financially relevant authorized facts. Received money and fulfillment are
read-only source facts: dragging cannot manufacture them. Equivalent explicit
controls must work without dragging, pointer precision or color interpretation.

On mobile, retain supporter, Request, response and next action in reading order;
secondary columns become accessible details rather than disappearing facts. Long
international names, Unicode and translated text wrap without requiring a surname
or changing canonical identity. Display dates in the user's locale with the
relevant business timezone/date precision; preserve unknown historical dates.
No new address collection is needed for D29. Hosting addresses and travel details
belong in protected operational detail when genuinely needed, not list cards.

Loading, no supporters, no Requests, no response, no eligible action, no permission,
source unavailable, stale data, save pending, save failed and conflicting edit
are different states. Keep authorized text in the active editor after a failure;
provide retry/status reconciliation without promising durable offline storage of
sensitive notes. A lost response must not cause a second Request. After session
expiry or tenant switch, prevent cross-tenant draft replay and clear disallowed
data. Do not announce success or dismiss the editor before authoritative success.
Content and ordinary actions must remain usable with optional financial panels,
ratings or large charts unavailable.
D29 adds no donor checkout field, portal confirmation step or public publishing
workflow. Donor-facing simplicity is preserved by keeping staff coordination in
its own authorized surface, rather than asking donors to classify the internal
appeal. Existing public/missionary projections require their own qualified scope.

## Authoritative facts and permanent safeguards

The following R clauses are the exact review requirements to carry forward.
Physical table names, index names and component count are intentionally not frozen.

**R01 — Scope and composition.** P27 SHALL own one appeal coordination identity
and purpose. It SHALL reuse D24 membership and D22 Requests for financial,
nonfinancial and mixed support. It SHALL NOT require money or a numerical goal,
create a second request subtype per surface, or add an event, prayer-content,
volunteer, generic workflow or campaign-management engine.

**R02 — Independent meanings.** Planning membership, current contact eligibility,
presentation, response, actual participation, financial facts and human work SHALL
remain independent. Independently answerable requests SHALL preserve their terms,
speaker/capacity, qualifications and history. No response, checkbox, board move,
task completion or numerical goal attainment SHALL imply another domain outcome.
A statement from a pastor is recorded with known capacity; the title alone does
not prove organizational approval or financial instruction.
Conflicting accounts SHALL preserve their evidence and show that the response
needs clarification under the source's rules; the last saved contact reply SHALL
NOT automatically become the organization's authoritative answer.

**R03 — Exact relationships.** A same-tenant appeal–Request reference SHALL identify
one canonical Request and compatible purpose, checked under current permissions.
An appeal SHALL reference that Request at most once currently. Multiple relevant
appeals MAY reference the same Request without cloning facts or work; combined
views SHALL deduplicate its identity. Link/unlink SHALL be deliberate and audited,
shall not alter Request ownership, and shall not silently mutate the working list.
Historical links and unlink time SHALL remain interpretable under source retention.
No cascade delete SHALL erase Requests, responses or financial facts. Linking an
old Request SHALL NOT imply it was presented through the new appeal or backdate
that appeal's results. A newly distinct invitation needs its actual D22 terms and
response, not a borrowed acceptance.

**R04 — Honest measures.** Each measure SHALL state its grain, scope, period,
current-versus-historical meaning and coverage. A working-list Party count SHALL
exclude historical-only participation; a current Request-response count SHALL
count distinct Requests using the source-qualified current response to identified
terms. Contradictory or unresolved accounts remain clarification-required and
SHALL NOT count as confirmed agreement.
A supporter with several response types MAY occur in each corresponding distinct-
supporter set: those sets SHALL NOT be summed as disjoint. Event counts SHALL be
explicitly labelled; repeat edits or replies SHALL NOT inflate current agreements.
Unavailable, withheld, incomplete, nonapplicable and known zero SHALL remain
different. Hidden rows SHALL be excluded before counting; a permission-safe view
must not reveal their existence through a total, difference, rank or small group.

A response rate SHALL use an explicit compatible invitation population, never
today's smaller working list after removals. Zero/absent target, denied denominator
and incomplete coverage SHALL not produce a percentage. Over-target actuals remain
visible rather than clipped to the goal. Goal revisions preserve their earlier
versions; current adjusted results and a dated closure snapshot are distinct views.

**R05 — Financial truth.** P13 effective contribution lines, P14 qualified legal/
recognition lenses, P15 intake and P16 commitment/fulfillment SHALL remain the
authorities. A reference SHALL NOT allocate money or establish fulfillment. Every
amount SHALL retain Legal Entity, currency, amount basis, source identity, period
and freshness. Distinct source money SHALL be counted once within a financial
measure; overlapping appeal totals SHALL not be added as disjoint. No sum of legal
and recognition lenses, silent FX, invented valuation or gift inference from
names/dates/amounts/source code is allowed. Exact received-toward-appeal coverage
requires a qualified association adapter; otherwise label it unavailable while
retaining permitted source facts and ordinary nonfinancial work. Corrections,
returns and refunds SHALL change current derived outcomes through source truth,
without rewriting prior responses or adding another money writer.
Requested amounts, staff forecasts, supporter responses, commitments and received
money SHALL not be added together. Apply only admitted line/amount coverage, not
the full gift header because one portion is related. Preserve the qualified source
monetary basis, including admitted charitable fee cover and finalized corrections;
no processor-fee subtraction, expected gift, noncash valuation or liquidation
proceeds substitution. Readers SHALL use a coherent source/association/coverage
revision, not a maximum of unrelated per-record cursors.

**R06 — Authority at every boundary.** The server SHALL derive tenant and actor
from trusted context; prove proposed owner, subject, source and purpose eligibility;
and reject caller-authored audit, approval, presentation or financial authority.
Appeal administration SHALL NOT grant finance, source Request, care, location,
missionary or historical access. Commands SHALL revalidate current authority and
expected revisions at their mutation boundary. Imports, jobs, RPCs, privileged
service paths, caches, search, exports, generated documents, briefs/AI and
notifications SHALL preserve the same field/purpose restrictions. D26's narrow
shared-update grant SHALL NOT become an appeal-audience grant.

**R07 — Database safety.** Persisted identities/references SHALL have nonnull tenant
scope and tenant-aware referential integrity, protected audit attribution and
version/concurrency information. Optional money SHALL be genuinely absent for
nonfinancial work, not zero defaults; money uses exact currency-aware minor units
and supported bounds. Source monetary facts SHALL NOT be stored in independently
editable appeal totals. Enforce uniqueness of current membership/reference and
local valid-state/nullability constraints in the database where expressible;
cross-source compatibility and conservation belong to the qualified atomic owner
command. Check indexes for tenant/appeal/request lookup and every relevant FK.
Stable primary keys and required identity references SHALL be nonnull. Ordinary
commands SHALL not rewrite earlier meaningful response/terms versions; corrections
preserve prior meaning with attributed new history. History retention/deletion
SHALL follow source class, not blanket infinite storage.

Grants and policies SHALL prove both old-row eligibility (`USING`) and permitted
new state (`WITH CHECK` or its effective inherited equivalent), including tenant/
scope/owner/audience reassignment. Inspect permissive-policy combination, views,
function execution grants/search paths, service-role bypass and storage. PostgreSQL
can inherit `USING` as `WITH CHECK`; its absence in text is not alone a vulnerability.
RLS is not field authorization, and FK/unique errors can reveal hidden existence.
Use governed command access and safe conflict responses, not a UI-only check.

**R08 — Private-by-scope participation.** Nonfinancial content SHALL follow its
classification and purpose. Do not collect prayer details, home addresses,
itineraries or private member-care notes just to coordinate an invitation.
Store only necessary source references and permitted summaries. General logs,
metrics, email notifications and broadly readable appeal headers SHALL NOT copy
that content. New attachments are not required by this decision; any retained
attachment reference uses its source's current access and retention, including
download links. Authorized correction/redaction and privacy deletion SHALL preserve
only lawful minimal provenance, not undeletable sensitive narratives.

**R09 — Real work and source effects.** Reuse [ADR0183](../../../adr/0183-source-owned-work-projects-into-one-shared-tasks-hub.md)
and [ADR0027](../../../adr/0027-one-notification-presentation-and-engagement-model.md).
Create work only for a proved actionable source occurrence, with eligible
responsibility and defined end semantics. Appeal membership, a new link, a response
or an ongoing agreement to pray SHALL NOT automatically create one task per person
or perpetual check-ins. Native and optional workflows SHALL use one durable
source-purpose identity; linking the source into another appeal SHALL not create
another task, notification or delivery. Independent actual obligations remain.

**R10 — Lifecycle and closure.** Distinguish preparation, active coordination and
closed coordination, with cancellation recorded as an honest closing disposition;
archive is a view/retention operation, not source deletion. Close SHALL atomically
fence new appeal-originated planning/solicitation and record lawful treatment or
durable owner handoff for affected work. Do not claim downstream completion before
it occurs. If authority or required disposition is missing, preserve current state
and expose an owned repair path. Late facts and corrections remain recordable
against prior terms; closure does not authorize new outreach or silently reopen.
Reopen SHALL preserve closure history and require fresh, deliberate new work.
Removing the last member or reaching a goal SHALL NOT automatically close anything.

**R11 — Correctness under concurrency.** A mutation SHALL atomically persist its
authoritative intent, lawful history and required durable effect intent, or none.
Use semantic idempotency tied to the actual operation, not only an HTTP retry.
Identical retries return the same lawful result; stale competing edits conflict
without overwriting newer terms or responses. Link/unlink, close/reopen, merge,
suppression/revocation and dispatch races SHALL use current revisions/fences with
defined ordering. Cancellation of a finite bulk operation retains committed members
and truthfully identifies unattempted/failed ones. Lost responses and ambiguous
external success reconcile through the existing owner before resending. No unproved
exactly-once claim across external providers.

**R12 — Temporal and correction meaning.** Preserve occurred-at precision separately
from server recorded-at, presented terms, current effective response and source
correction history. Do not fabricate dates or backdate permissions. A late response
or imported event SHALL not create historical outreach or replay old tasks. Purpose,
goal, financial applicability and reference edits SHALL preserve prior versions;
hiding a financial section SHALL not delete gifts or silently submit hidden values.
Source-qualified Party merge SHALL preserve conflicting planning/response facts
and financial donor history; unresolved collisions remain visible to authorized
repair staff without revealing hidden records or creating duplicates.

**R13 — Coherent accessible interface.** The daily experience above SHALL preserve
shared design-system ownership, visible context, clear next actions, explicit
selection scope and truthful save/error/conflict feedback. Require WCAG 2.2 AA for
the composed flow, including keyboard/focus operation, text status announcements,
contrast, reflow and target size; do not mistake a shared primitive or axe pass for
complete accessibility. No drag-only financial/status mutation, compulsory wizard,
new dashboard language or donor-value rating. Current scope and relevant options
must be understandable without documentation or facilitator correction.

**R14 — Bounded reads and integrations.** Page/virtualize authorized lists with
stable server ordering; compute aggregates from the qualified complete source set,
not loaded pages or capped timelines. Avoid per-row source fan-out and loading all
financial/private children for an overview. Bounded bulk work uses durable checkpoints
and per-member outcomes. Optional Studio, scoring, event/publishing tools and provider
outages SHALL NOT block ordinary authorized nonfinancial work. Source-dependent
features fail visibly without a shadow substitute. Any optimization is a rebuildable
read model with its source cursor/coverage, never a second writer.

**R15 — Safe delivery and rollout.** Census retained records and active readers/
writers first; do not reinterpret legacy zero-valued gifts, campaign rows, task
completion or member statuses as nonfinancial outcomes. Preserve unknown historical
meaning. Introduce qualified readers/constraints before enabling writers and reject
incompatible old-client mutations. Backfills SHALL be resumable/idempotent and
produce no outward effects. Activate by bounded tenant/cohort with kill switches
for new writes/effects; rollback SHALL retain newly recorded history and favor
compatible reads/roll-forward over destructive downgrade. Reconcile formal owner
clauses and trace their tests before activation.

**R16 — Evidence and operations.** Business history SHALL show who changed which
source terms, reference, response or disposition and when. Security audit tracks
authorized actions/access; technical logs track operation/correlation/source-version
IDs and failures without sensitive content. A saved authoritative response SHALL not
be lost because a projection failed; expose pending update and replay durable effects.
Release proof SHALL include real source services, real role/grant/RLS tests,
concurrent schedules, mixed-version migration and observed staff UX. Unknown workload
or usability cannot be replaced by a fabricated performance number or claim of
perfection. Every monitored residual has an owner, threshold and response below.
