## Original single founder question — answered A

Illustrative situation, not a claimed agency workflow: staff decide there is
nothing further to pursue in developing this church's partnership with this
ministry for now. There is no outstanding development follow-up and no useful
date to review the situation again.

**Should staff close this current work, or keep it on hold without a date?**

**A — Close this work, keeping its history (recommended).** It leaves the current
development workload. Staff can deliberately take up work again if circumstances
change. If there is a useful reason/date to review instead, D6's ordinary dated
pause remains available.

**B — Keep it on hold without a date.** Retain a responsible lead and visibly held
status, with no scheduled review. Staff deliberately return when circumstances
change. This avoids artificial dates for waits that depend on an outside event,
but adds another form of open work to keep track of.

**Recommendation:** A keeps the workload honest without a special indefinite
holding state. This is a product judgment balancing simplicity against B's valid
event-dependent-wait case, not a claim that all ministries operate alike.
