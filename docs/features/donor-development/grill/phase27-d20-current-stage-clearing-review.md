## Recorded decision

An authorized person may deliberately change a partnership's current stage to
**Not yet assessed** while keeping the same partnership and stage list. Preserve
earlier assessments in lawful history and let staff choose an appropriate eligible
stage when ready. No current stage means no assessment currently recorded on this
binding; it does not mean that the partnership has never been assessed.

Show the current stage and proposed **No current stage — Not yet assessed** outcome
clearly before the ordinary Save. After acceptance, offer **Set stage** where
authorized. A real tenant-defined stage with the same label remains distinguishable
from the system absence outcome. This adds no required narrative, separate approval,
second acknowledgment, assessment task or deadline.

Keep the current list, source scope, supporter, identified ministry, continuing
purpose and responsibility intact. Retaining the same list is not a new attachment;
an existing list withdrawn from new use remains governed by D15. Clearing the
assessment does not reopen, pause, close or restart work, complete a pause review,
prove a supporter action, grant permission, contact a donor or change giving.
Normal next work and its actual owner remain authoritative.

Accept only intentional current-state changes under current authority, with the
required history/result and concurrency safeguards. Never infer clearing from
omitted input, failed or denied reads, an old assessment date, a retired stage,
automation, a handover or an unmatched control value. The ordinary outcome must
be supported explicitly by the source operation; a generic nullable patch is not
permission. Retrying an earlier clear cannot erase a later assessment. Selecting
absence when it is already the authoritative current state creates no new stage
change or assessment occurrence; current authorization and stale-intent checks
still apply.

This is a prospective change recorded now, not deletion or backdating of earlier
history. A later assessment records its actual choice and acceptance time. D9
preserves this current absence on reopening rather than restoring the latest
historical non-null stage. Permission to edit while paused or closed, bulk actions,
cross-scope changes and historical corrections remains separately unresolved.

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A generic Clear icon or omitted nullable field could erase an assessment without
meaningful intent, or a stale editor could clear newly recorded knowledge. Keep the
current and proposed state visible, use the authorized source command, preserve
history/result atomically and validate current source revision. An already-absent
state is a no-op, not another progress event.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

Staff sometimes need to stop asserting a current classification before knowing
its replacement. The founder has selected that flexibility; its frequency and
usability have not been measured. An old stage may remain valid, so age, handover
or retirement alone cannot trigger automatic clearing. Existing shared primitives
are not proof of correct form serialization or source semantics.

### How does this affect the whole product?

D20 extends deliberate absence to same-list updates without changing ownership.
Staff and authorized missionary surfaces share the same source meaning. Reports,
history, imports and effects must distinguish intentional current absence from
unknown facts and older assessments. Contact restrictions, consent, giving and
source-owned work remain separate. A current absent stage is not missing list or
ministry identity.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Use **Change stage** to show the permitted no-current-stage option alongside actual
eligible stages, with brief context that earlier assessments remain in history.
After acceptance, show **Not yet assessed** and **Set stage**. Staff can keep the
list and later record a new assessment without a duplicate partnership, placeholder
stage, reason form or extra confirmation. Hidden data never becomes an empty-state
shortcut; failures and conflicts explain the permitted next action.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

It follows the source-owned, explicit-intent, recoverable and accessible optional
value practices already researched in D19. That evidence includes nonprofit systems
which permit uncertain classification, and systems that require stage for different
financial objects. Neither establishes a universal ministry rule. No new external
search or runtime test was needed for this focused continuation, and none is claimed.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes: preserve P27 source truth, P9 shared work, P12 permission boundaries, D15 list
retention and the existing exact `@asym/ui`/Base UI/base-maia design language. D20
adds no assessedness flag, catalogue sentinel, new permission hierarchy, deadline
engine or general nullable-field framework. Current P27 implementation remains
unqualified; the decision is intended behavior.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Accept A with the offered and inherited safeguards. No material new product
amendment or repeat ratification is required. [Q21](phase27-q21-stage-edits-while-paused.md)
subsequently selected A, recorded in D21; its paused-editing policy is now settled.
Other routine details will be resolved under the founder's later delegation,
without inferring permission to violate history, access or financial boundaries.
