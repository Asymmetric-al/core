# D4 evidence — ministry practice, entry and existing support teams

2026-10-09. Research and adversarial-review appendix only. D1, amended D2 and tightened D3 remain ratified. The founder chose deliberate human start, individually or in a reviewed group; the wording refinements here are proposed for the full D4 review, not silently accepted additions. No specification, issue, application, production, automation or donor-contact change is made by this note.

## Assessment

**Retain A, without turning it into an enrollment ceremony.** Ministry tools document deliberate referral entry, batch contact entry, automatic financial synchronization and migration of existing relationship information. These practices support easy contextual selection and challenge repeated per-person setup. They do not prove that arrival of data should accept Core's accountable cultivation work.

The most important distinction is between **the organization's decision to begin development work** and **the supporter's decision to partner**. The former can precede the latter. A journey must not become evidence of donor agreement, a financial commitment or communication permission. Conversely, importing an established support team must not turn real continuing relationships into fresh prospects merely because Core is new.

This recommendation is product judgment for Core. No user study, live vendor test, agency-wide process audit or measured performance comparison was conducted. The sources do not establish a universally superior admission policy.

## Material concerns and exact proposed wording

Severity expresses consequence; likelihood is a qualitative judgment conditional on the shortcut, not measured incidence. These are risks in the emerging design, not demonstrated production defects. Several are already bounded by the proposed Q4 wording and must survive its consolidation.

### M1 — Internal acceptance can look like the supporter agreed

**High severity; medium-high likelihood if UI says “accepted partner” or copies a vendor's partner status.** A referral can legitimately enter development before a decision (S1/S6). A staff action cannot truthfully record the prospect's response. Prayer engagement, a receipt or a gift also cannot establish every other participation or contact preference.

**Exact wording:** “Starting a journey records an authorized person's decision to undertake internal development work for the identified Party/ministry/purpose. It does not record the supporter's agreement, a partnership response, financial commitment, prayer participation or permission to communicate. Initial progression and actual responses remain separately evidenced.”

**Proof:** start permitted internal work for a not-yet-contacted referral without marking that person a partner or generating outreach. Record actual prayer partnership without requiring a donation. Later gift arrival neither fabricates prayer evidence nor changes the journey's stage automatically. No donor-side approval or signup ceremony is required for the internal record.

### M2 — “Deliberate” can create a second queue or repeated setup

**Medium-high severity; high likelihood if implemented as separate contact, candidate, approval and enrollment forms.** S2/S3 document quick multi-contact work; forcing users to reselect each intentionally entered referral adds work without adding meaning. An obligatory manager or second confirmation step has no evidence here.

**Exact wording:** “One clear, authorized contextual action may both record a referral or ask and deliberately start or reuse its journey. Merely saving a contact or referral does not imply that choice. Do not require a separate candidate store, mandatory review queue, enrollment wizard, manager approval or repeated per-person confirmation.”

**Proof:** an authorized user can intentionally add selected referrals and begin the relevant work together; another user can record an introduction without beginning cultivation. Both preserve the same referral provenance. Existing permitted ordinary work is reused under D3; a new form does not restart it. The exact button label/interaction is a later usability choice, not a new product engine.

### M3 — Human-only new enrollment can accidentally block useful source automation

**High severity; medium likelihood if “nothing automatic” is applied beyond entry.** MPDX's donation synchronization and TntConnect organization imports reduce re-entry (S4/S5). Finance, contact restrictions and already accepted follow-up still need their existing source behavior. A gift's arrival is insufficient for new cultivation, but that is not a reason to withhold an authorized receipt or conceal the gift pending development review.

**Exact wording:** “D4 restricts automatic acceptance of new cultivation work. It does not disable authorized synchronization, financial recording, source-owned receipts, suppression, or execution of already accepted follow-up under their owners. Those effects do not require a new journey merely to continue operating. New cultivation still requires the deliberate authorized selection.”

**Proof:** a qualified gift syncs and its independently authorized receipt process remains functional without creating a journey; changed contact restrictions apply immediately without waiting for staff selection. An already accepted task reminder remains operational. A raw financial or referral event creates neither fresh accepted cultivation nor an inferred donor response.

### M4 — Existing support teams can be reset or blindly adopted during migration

**High severity; high likelihood during onboarding if all imports share one path.** Sources document both bulk transfer and meaningful losses/mappings (S4/S7/S8). An imported contact alone is not evidence of an accepted ongoing relationship; conversely, a trustworthy existing relationship should not be forced through a fresh-prospect sequence. “Manual only” and “import everything as active” are both excessive interpretations.

**Exact wording:** “Distinguish importing source facts, preserving evidenced existing work and deliberately accepting new cultivation. Preserve supported historical meaning, original dates and permitted context through owner-qualified migration; do not manufacture agreement, stage, assignment, complete history or a new outreach obligation. D4 does not require established relationships to restart. Ambiguous imports remain explicit for authorized resolution.”

**Proof:** migration distinguishes an existing continuing partner, an unused address-book entry and an unresolved mapping. The first does not lose its evidenced history; the others do not become accepted work automatically. Retrying import does not duplicate work or replay communication. A missing recurring-task schedule is reported as missing rather than claimed preserved. The migration policy still needs its own qualification; this wording does not create an automatic-entry loophole.

### M5 — Church, church contact, member and household can be conflated at entry

**High severity; medium likelihood under household-centric vendor imports.** A missions-committee contact and the church are different subjects. A named person's introduction or office does not prove authority to commit the church. OMF documents churches and individuals supporting missionaries, and TntConnect at least distinguishes organization and contact-person fields (S9/S11); neither supplies Core's complete authority graph.

**Exact wording:** “Select the actual supporter Party and admitted ministry/purpose. Preserve the referrer, church contact, organizational role and relationship through their authoritative references; do not substitute a pastor, spouse, household or shared mailbox for the organization, or enroll linked people because the church was selected. Internal entry does not prove who may agree on the church's behalf.”

**Proof:** a church journey can be worked through its permitted contact without turning that person's personal gifts or members into church cultivation. Leadership change does not create a new ordinary journey. Referral-source privacy and source restrictions survive bulk entry. Sending relationships and protected care remain with their owners; D4 does not add ecclesial or deployment authority.

### M6 — Nonfinancial or early work can be blocked by fundraising-shaped prerequisites

**High severity; medium likelihood if enrollment requires money, email or an existing financial account.** Prayer and practical partnership are real (S6/S10). Contact gathering can also precede knowing the exact ministry. D2 already forbids fake ministries and leaves ordinary Party/referral/task work available.

**Exact wording:** “Deliberate entry does not require a gift, financial promise, giving amount, published page, financial support binding or email address. Use the actual required Party/ministry/purpose and accountable work under existing authority. Where that ministry or authority is not established, retain permitted Party/referral/task work without fabricating a ministry, journey or completed admission.”

**Proof:** a nonfinancial partner with no gift history can enter appropriate work; an early introduction can be recorded before ministry selection; a user does not invent zero money, consent or a fictitious ministry to proceed. Different participation modes do not produce extra journeys contrary to D3.

### M7 — Reviewed groups can become silent evergreen enrollment

**High severity; medium likelihood if a saved filter is treated as standing permission.** Batch entry is documented (S2/S3), but none of those pages proves authorization to enroll every future matching contact. “Reviewed” must identify what the person actually selected, without requiring a click on every row or pretending a loaded page is the full scope.

**Exact wording:** “A reviewed group is a deliberate, bounded selection whose intended Party/ministry/purpose scope is understandable to the authorized person. It is not standing permission for future matches. Execution must retain that selected intent and current source checks; later arrivals need their own deliberate acceptance. Do not hide changed, unaccepted or unresolved outcomes behind a blanket success message.”

**Proof:** a new gift/referral matching yesterday's saved view does not inherit yesterday's acceptance. The user can review a meaningful group without repeated individual forms. Permission changes and existing journeys yield honest permitted results, not duplicate work or a hidden-record hint. Precise bulk result/transaction design belongs to the implementation review, not this ministry note.

## Counterargument and practical validation

The strongest challenge to A is repeated selection when an agency has a well-defined, high-confidence incoming stream. Automatic internal entry could avoid that work without itself sending anything. S4/S5 establish the value of automated data arrival, **not** that staff prefer automatic accountable enrollment. D4's selected default remains coherent if the ordinary action and reviewed-group path are efficient. No alternative automatic enrollment policy is accepted by this appendix.

Before release, observe intended mission users doing a few representative tasks using de-identified records: record an introduction only; deliberately begin referral development in context; start nonfinancial work; act for a church through a named contact; bring over an existing support team with an ambiguous entry; and review a bounded group. Include a newer support raiser and someone maintaining an established team, with church-relations staff where applicable. Record duplicate entry, mistaken subject/assent, assistance and lost context. No invented sample size, completion-time target or improvement percentage is claimed.

Useful disconfirming evidence would be users repeatedly creating shadow spreadsheets because acceptance is cumbersome, wrongly believing the donor agreed, or being unable to preserve existing work during migration. These would require correcting the entry experience or revisiting a specific assumption with the founder; they would not justify silently switching to automatic enrollment.

## Proposed concise carry-forward

“An authorized person deliberately starts new cultivation, individually or for a bounded reviewed group. The choice may be part of one contextual action and records internal work intent, not supporter agreement. Gifts, referrals and imported facts do not start new work merely by arriving. Preserve authorized source synchronization, independently owned service/financial effects and evidenced existing work without resetting relationships or generating outreach. Keep actual Parties, ministry, responsibility, participation and current authority distinct.”

This wording carries the selected A and existing owners forward. Initial stage, precise bulk outcomes, lifecycle/reopening and field-level sharing still need their own decisions. It does not change D1–D3 or supply a formal state machine, source schema or approval process.
