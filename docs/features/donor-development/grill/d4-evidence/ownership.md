# D4 evidence — deliberate admission, execution and source ownership

Reviewed 2026-10-09 against Core checkout `225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`. D1, amended D2 and tightened D3 remain ratified. The founder selected Q4 A: an authorized human deliberately starts ordinary cultivation work, individually or in a reviewed group. This appendix is research for the requested adversarial review. Its proposed clarifications require the parent's decision-status treatment; it is not an implementation, formal specification, new issue, authority grant or activation claim.

Only this evidence file was written. Root `AGENTS.md`, `docs/ai/rules/general.md`, `docs/ai/document-authority.md`, the OpenSpec index and relevant owner contracts were read. No nearer documentation `AGENTS.md` applies. The explicit user boundary against issues/specification controls over the general issue-first development workflow.

## Conclusion

Retain A. **Human selection of new work and mechanical execution of that selection are different things.** A worker may finish/retry an explicitly selected finite operation without turning it into standing automatic admission. A changing saved-view query, future gift/referral event, timer or reopened import cannot silently select additional people under the old human intent.

The essential boundary is attributable, finite admitted intent through the P27 source, with current execution authority and truthful outcomes. It does not require one browser request, a live human session throughout a job, an all-or-nothing tenant-wide transaction, per-person confirmation, manager approval or optional Workflow Studio. It also does not create an exemption from P12 fire-time checks.

P34 remains the shared configurable event/condition/action vocabulary. Fixed native product operations are a separate accepted architectural category, but no automatic P27 admission policy has been ratified. Neither a second P27 rule builder nor a fixed native “start on every gift” toggle may bypass the selected A policy. P42's fixed web automation is not an alternate cultivation admission owner.

## Concerns, effects and precise safeguards

Severity reflects potential impact; likelihood is qualitative and conditional on the named shortcut, not measured production frequency. The scenarios are pressure tests rather than claims of observed agency incidents. These findings cover ownership, hidden coupling, failure, temporal correctness, integrity, authorization, operational burden, dependencies, migration and proof categories; the consolidated review owns all 22 categories.

### O1 — Confusing deliberate admission with synchronous execution

**High severity; high likelihood if “human starts” is interpreted as “human must wait/click every item.”** P27's native workflow and the orchestration OpenSpec distinguish source meaning from execution. A network retry or worker finishing the person's choice does not independently choose new cultivation work.

**Change to recommendation:** retain A; explicitly allow bounded execution without adding a scheduling product or standing automatic policy.

**Exact requirement:** “A currently authorized human deliberately chooses a finite admission operation. Existing durable execution may finish and retry that same operation without another per-item human click, preserving the chosen scope, original initiator, current source checks and intended effect identity. Queueing is not itself proof that each journey was admitted.”

**Proof:** review 20 specific permitted records, disconnect after acceptance, then recover only those selected intents. Lost response cannot produce 40 journeys. A queued operation reports pending until source outcomes exist. This permits asynchronous execution; it does not independently approve future-dated admission scheduling or bypass review of materially changed meaning.

### O2 — “Reviewed group” becomes an expanding subscription

**High; high if a worker reruns a mutable saved-view predicate without a defined selection boundary.** D3 requires one admitted intended work; Q4's reviewed group is finite human selection, not a continuing rule. A new matching gift tomorrow was never among today's selected people.

**Change:** clarify finite reviewed scope; do not force per-person clicks or choose a specific batch serialization format.

**Exact requirement:** “Bind the human action to one explicit, reviewable finite membership and its admission meaning at a defined selection boundary. Later matches, changed filters and future events do not enlarge it. Recheck current eligibility, authority and source revisions for selected work; a newly ineligible item is not replaced by an unselected person.”

**Proof:** add/remove matching records after selection, change a filter, change ministry/purpose on a selected row, paginate beyond a loaded page, and retry after a partial result. The selected set never silently grows. Exact all-matching-current selection UX and capture mechanism remain to be designed; this is not a new candidate-list authority.

### O3 — The initiating human's past approval becomes permanent worker authority

**Critical; medium-high if a queue item carries a cached role, token or service key.** P12 `:1556,1587`, fresh #669, and WS-INT-01 require current context. The CMS appointment survival rule is explicitly source-specific.

**Change:** retain A; separate human intent provenance from actual execution authority and from already committed source facts.

**Exact requirement:** “Record the original human initiator and the actual executing context separately. A deferred membership-backed action re-resolves its validated Active Tenant Assignment; an admitted NHI/operator path re-proves its own current qualified context, capabilities, entity/purpose scope and applicable owner ceiling. Do not switch to a broader principal to defeat denial or infer perpetual delegation from the initial click.”

Already committed journeys/history/effects remain real after revocation. Still-unadmitted work must not borrow their past authorization. Any source-approved transfer/resumption of blocked work requires current authority and attributable disposition; it is not automatic actor substitution. Do not demand a fabricated human membership for legitimate service work.

**Proof:** revoke the initiator before a queued member action fires; reduce an NHI human-owner ceiling; revoke entity scope between selection and per-item admission; leave already committed records intact. Test a source-qualified recovery case separately from unadmitted new work. Exact continuity rights are an owner qualification question, not a blanket D4 departure policy.

### O4 — Either forbidding all automation or recreating it inside P27

**High; high if “A” is translated into manual-only execution or “small rules do not count as automation.”** P34's three customization envelopes distinguish configured definitions from protected native behavior; native fixed Support policies demonstrate that automatic behavior does not always imply Studio. However, D4 selected deliberate admission, not a new P27 automatic policy.

**Change:** retain A; make the strongest alternatives honest without implementing them.

**Exact requirement:** “A governs selection of new cultivation work. Deterministic native follow-up, retries, reconciliation and execution of explicit finite human selections remain possible through existing owners. No gift/referral/timer/score arrival alone starts new cultivation under D4. Tenant-authored event/condition/action admission rules belong to the existing qualified P34 vocabulary and require an explicit later product change; no second P27 builder is created.”

A fixed native policy such as “on this one qualified event, start ordinary work” is architecturally possible only after separately ratifying its exact P27 business rule and qualifying the source. It is not automatically illegal because it runs outside Studio, and it is not authorized by calling it a bounded setting. P42 is not an alternative owner. Native reviewed bulk does not become configurable automation, or require `automation:manage` solely because a worker executes it; exact business capabilities still require qualification.

**Strongest alternative:** a small source-owned automatic policy could reduce repetitive selection with less configuration than a generic rule, while P34 configured rules allow greater tenant variation. Neither is currently selected, and no measured unmet workload establishes a need to overturn A. Revisit only with actual use cases; do not reopen D4 merely to advertise a framework.

**Proof:** manual/contextual/group admission and native follow-up work with P34/P42 disabled; background execution cannot enroll later event arrivals. Optional Studio cannot invoke an automatic admission path forbidden by current P27 policy merely because an action is in its catalog.

### O5 — Recording historical facts is confused with creating new work

**High; high during legacy imports if raw presence is treated as approval.** D3 migration safeguards and P34 historical-dark rules preserve existing evidence without inventing current meaning.

**Change:** retain A, distinguish three operations rather than force every import through fresh admission.

**Exact requirement:** “Importing a Party, gift, referral or label is not starting cultivation. Migration may preserve genuinely evidenced existing cultivation and obligations under its owner-approved mapping and provenance without pretending each is a newly selected prospect. A human-reviewed finite set of imported candidates may separately start new work through D4. Ambiguous historical meaning remains unresolved rather than inferred.”

No stage reset, new ask, duplicate task, consent, outreach or retrospective declaration of intent follows merely from import. Preserving an existing task or stage history is not the same as inventing a new task or transition. Do not require retyping established relationships or contacting supporters again to satisfy A.

**Proof:** migrate evidenced existing partnership versus a raw gift-only record; imported prayer/financial tags; unknown ministry mapping; retry a migration; import after a journey already exists. Historical effects remain dark, source truth remains attributable and duplicates/conflicts follow the D3/D2 owner boundary.

### O6 — One contextual action silently performs an unrelated business decision

**High; medium if “no enrollment ceremony” becomes implicit enrollment on every click.** Q4 A allows a combined action where authorized; D3 says creating/reusing a journey is an admitted source operation, not a side effect of a label or read.

**Change:** preserve minimal UI while making the human consequence meaningful.

**Exact requirement:** “The ordinary contextual action clearly expresses starting or reusing cultivation when it performs that consequence. One action may record an ask/referral and admit the journey where the actor holds each required power and exact source scope is known. Reading, typing a note, saving unrelated data, entering a gift or recording a referral without that expressed consequence does not silently start a journey.”

An existing hidden journey supplies neither access nor permission to create a workaround duplicate. The create/reuse result must respect D3's independently qualified metadata/content/action boundaries. This does not prescribe a new confirmation dialog, manager review, form, initial stage or universal one-button transaction across owners.

**Proof:** authorized combined action; same record operation without starting intent; permitted metadata but forbidden private history; unknown ministry; hidden existing journey; source permission lost between preview and commit. Missing required scope leaves useful existing Party/referral/task work available rather than inventing a ministry.

### O7 — Human confirmation is mistaken for atomic admission or whole-batch success

**High; high under separate writes, large groups and lost responses.** Ratified D3 requires source mutation/history/required local obligation or owned exception to commit consistently. The orchestration OpenSpec supplies durable claims/dispatch, not business-state atomicity. P26 `REQ26-D07-R11` demonstrates truthful per-item batch outcomes; its exact policy is not copied into P27.

**Change:** require truthful source outcomes, without deciding whole-batch atomicity or adding a batch framework.

**Exact requirement:** “Acceptance of the human request, acceptance of an individual cultivation admission, and completion of required secondary work are distinguishable facts. Each admitted source change preserves the required local history/receipt and next-work invariant atomically, including necessary outbox intent. If items commit independently, preserve exact per-item pending/accepted/reused/refused/unknown outcomes and retry only the same still-eligible unresolved intents; never present partial or queued work as complete.”

Do not require one long database transaction across every selected person or any provider. If a local task cannot yet materialize, an admitted owned pending/actionable exception must be truthful, visible and recoverable; it cannot silently weaken the active-journey next-action requirement. Reusing appropriate existing work avoids administrative duplicate tasks.

**Proof:** child write fails; operation accepted but no item admitted yet; first items commit then permission/source eligibility changes; timeout after commit; cancel/stop before versus after item admission. Completed records survive cancellation; unknown outcomes are reconciled before retry. Specific batch statuses, cancel UX, limits and atomicity policy remain later decisions.

### O8 — Deliberate internal enrollment is confused with donor assent, consent or money authority

**Critical for disclosure/unauthorized effects; high for donor trust. Medium likelihood if “approved” is used generically.** P27 roadmap `:4101,4119`, D2 and D3 retain source owners. The human accepts cultivation responsibility, not a donor's promise or subscription.

**Change:** retain source boundaries and native independent service.

**Exact requirement:** “Admission is an internal development decision. It establishes no donor assent, contact permission, public visibility, financial instruction, pledge, received amount or shared history. Source-native receipts, essential notices, existing support work and already authorized follow-up do not become conditional on a P27 journey. Current restrictions still govern every actual later effect.”

Neither a gift nor referral is consent by itself. A never-ask restriction forbids prohibited solicitation, not necessarily all permitted internal stewardship. Source-limited newsletter request payloads, recognition-only members or protected care facts cannot be mined into general prospects merely because a human clicked.

**Proof:** an un-enrolled donor still receives source-required receipting; prayer-only/never-ask relationship gets no solicitation; enrollment writes no money or consent; a source-limited newsletter request is not repurposed; a direct refusal takes effect without waiting for admission review.

### O9 — Extending admission accidentally creates new responsibility, identity or lifecycle owners

**High; medium-high if a batch or exception is used as a new portfolio/assignment store.** P9 owns assignments/shared tasks, P12 powers, P27 meaning; ministry and support binding stay distinct. Current owner seams require qualification, and #1284 does not prove executable P9 ministry services.

**Change:** retain one bounded P27 source operation and exact consumed dependencies.

**Exact requirement:** “Admission references qualified Party/ministry/responsibility/task facts through their owners. Do not create a stored portfolio, private candidate CRM, approval engine, universal successor graph, P27 permission service or second task queue. Missing required ministry/authority/task seams block the affected admission path; they do not justify legacy profiles or direct database edits as fallback.”

An existing eligible person/queue may receive permitted work; if none exists, retain an authorized visible needs-assignment/actionable exception. Do not invent queue eligibility or treat membership as responsibility. D4 does not decide who may create versus reuse, assignment precedence, initial stage, pause/end/reopen, Party-merge collision disposition or additional-purpose admission beyond D3.

**Proof:** source-unavailable case; no eligible responsible person/queue; several assignees; same person in multiple ministries; concurrent create/reuse; ministry retirement and Party repair during a selected operation. Preserved scope/history and honest exceptions must hold without waiting for all P21/P22/P28/P34/P42 delivery.

## Proposed bounded recording language

> Ordinary cultivation starts when a currently authorized human deliberately accepts that work, individually or for an explicit reviewed finite group. Gifts, referrals, scores, imports and other record arrivals do not by themselves start a new journey. A clear contextual action may record the relevant referral/ask and start or reuse its journey without a separate enrollment ceremony, where every consequence is authorized and the source meaning is known.
>
> Existing durable execution may complete or retry the same selected intent; it does not select future matches, enlarge the group or replace current authorization. Preserve the human initiator and actual execution context, distinguish requested/queued work from admitted source outcomes, and retain truthful per-item recovery where work commits separately. Admission/reuse keeps D2/D3 source scope, purpose, hidden-record protection, duplicate protection and history boundaries. Each active journey has appropriate existing due work or an owned actionable exception; no redundant administrative task is required.
>
> This internal decision is not donor assent, communication consent, a gift/commitment or shared access. Migration may preserve evidenced existing work separately from starting new work. Native execution/follow-up stays independent of optional Studio; D4 adopts neither standing configurable admission rules nor a new fixed native automatic admission policy. Initial stage, detailed lifecycle, exact batch behavior and any new authority remain separately governed.

This wording is a proposed clarification for the consolidated review, not a claim that all details are accepted or implemented. The strongest material additions to the bare A answer are finite human scope versus future automatic selection, and past human intent versus current worker authority.

## Required proof versus unresolved choices

**Already required safeguards:** exact source scope, current authority, attributable human/executing context, no hidden-record oracle, same-intent deduplication, preserved history/accepted effects, no source-purpose widening, required work/exception, durable recovery and honest outcomes. These are not optional future monitoring tasks.

**Product decisions still open:** precise initial stages, user-visible finite-group selection/capture, per-item versus all-or-nothing behavior within qualified size bounds, cancel/resume experience, permitted source correction after material facts change, responsibility defaults and delegation/continuation authority, pause/end/reopen and merge collisions, and P27/P28 interaction projections. D4 should not invent these as worker implementation details.

**Qualification gates:** the exact P27 source command and result projection; P9 ministry/lifecycle and shared task/assignment commands consumed; P12 principal/current capability/context/metadata-result decision; any qualified executor and receipt/recovery path. Real database/API/browser tests must cover concurrent admission, changes after selection, revocation, partial failure, lost response, retry and migration. Current documentation and the earlier PostgreSQL mechanics experiment do not certify that integrated path.
