## Recommendation and proposed exact language

**Accept A with the following qualifications.** Human choice supplies deliberate business intent. It does not substitute for current authorization, create source identity, grant access to existing work, or legalize reuse of a purpose-limited payload.

> An authorized human deliberately admits new cultivation, for one identified relationship or an explicitly reviewed finite group. The selection binds the intended Party, ministry/purpose and reviewed work; it does not authorize a later changing query, hidden additional people, a different Tenant/Legal Entity or a substituted representative. Ordinary technical execution and retries may finish that exact accepted operation through existing owners; they cannot invent new enrollment intent.
>
> Resolve the actor and exact Tenant Authorization Context on the server. Re-prove the applicable current authority, source eligibility and expected revisions at selection, source admission, any later unstarted execution, and result disclosure. Review evidence is not a frozen permission token. Keep the original human decision and the actual executor attributable; do not require the original browser session to remain open or use the user's later active Tenant to retarget accepted work. An accepted source fact remains historical truth; later revocation blocks new unauthorized actions and reads, not the fact that authorized work already committed.
>
> Starting or reusing work requires independently permitted admission metadata and actions. A caller without the minimum authority must not learn whether a hidden existing journey occupies the ordinary slot, including through bulk totals, error details or returned IDs. Deduplication grants no access and does not justify a duplicate. This introduces no new coordinator role, routine approval queue or blanket history grant.
>
> Display only currently permitted selection details, progress and results. Source outcomes remain truthful even when some result details are no longer visible; do not claim failure, success, rollback or absence from a withheld response. New enrollment neither enrolls anyone in communications nor sends a donor message. Newsletter requests, Support context, referrals, giving and prayer participation keep their source purpose and disclosure boundaries; a human click cannot turn limited-purpose access into general cultivation authority.

This wording preserves A without choosing a schema, whole-batch versus per-item atomicity, partial-result UX, initial stage, close/reopen policy, exact action grants or extra sharing. Those later contracts must obey these floors. “Authorized human” is a principal/action condition, not an invented staff-only role, mandatory manager approval, creator-as-owner rule or perpetual live browser session.

## Findings, exact safeguards and required proof

Severity is potential impact; likelihood is conditional on the stated shortcut, not measured occurrence. These are design/reuse concerns, not demonstrated vulnerabilities in an existing D4 implementation.

### A1 — human intent is mistaken for permanent authorization

**Critical; medium-high under long-running groups. Effect: inherited guardrail.** At preview the human is allowed; before actual admission the assignment, entity scope, ministry eligibility or permission changes. A worker trusts `humanReviewed: true`, an old JWT/serialized projection, or a service credential and creates work anyway.

**Exact safeguard:** “Server-verify the original human decision and its exact context/selection; re-resolve applicable current owner authority and revisions at actual source admission. Deferred processing receives references to exact approved intent, never caller-supplied proof. Current worker/NHI authority and owner ceiling are separately necessary; they cannot manufacture a human decision or silently substitute a different source scope.” A live browser session is not the permission mechanism. Ending a session, revoking authority and ending a ministry participant are distinct events; apply their actual owner contracts, not blanket equivalence.

**Proof:** preview allowed then submit denied; submitted manifest but item not admitted then access revoked; original browser changes active Tenant; user signs out but relevant source authority remains valid; executor credential revoked or owner leaves. Original context is never swapped for a more powerful one. Test both causal commit orders: admitted source work remains true, while revoke-before-new-admission denies. Define the actual acceptance boundary before claiming the batch was accepted.

### A2 — reviewed group silently changes or includes hidden people

**High; high if existing report/export semantics are copied. Effect: D4 clarification, no new selection platform.** The user reviews loaded rows but implementation re-runs all matching rows later; another gift or status change adds people, selected IDs are replaced with a saved query, or a capped report is labeled complete. A protected filter dimension discloses sensitive cohort membership.

**Exact safeguard:** “Bind the reviewed finite membership and material action scope through the existing source command. Material changes cannot widen or substitute targets without a newly authorized human choice. Current narrowing/denial may stop affected unstarted work under the eventual partial-result contract; it cannot add others. Apply source/field authorization before selection enumeration, options, counts, sorting and pagination; complete coverage must be qualified.” No mandated separate per-person clicks or second review screen; a contextual action can express the exact finite choice.

**Proof:** page versus all-result selection, beyond-cap data, new matching rows after review, hidden member, changed query defaults/saved view, membership removals, mixed legal entities, revised target purpose and late responses. Exact originally reviewed target set and material intent are inspectable to an authorized operator; no new target appears from replay/retry.

### A3 — actor, creator, responsible person and executor collapse

**Critical; medium if payload IDs are treated as authority. Effect: inherited owner separation.** Client posts Tenant/actor/owner fields; selected assignee has no eligible responsibility scope; creator becomes primary automatically; NHI overwrites original human as actor; equal IDs across sources are assumed interchangeable.

**Exact safeguard:** “Derive acting human, exact Tenant Context and attribution on the server. Requested owner/assignee/ministry IDs are desired targets, not grants. Resolve source-owned responsibility and assignment eligibility independently; keep original human decision, actual executor and resulting responsible owner distinguishable. Required exact scope/keys have no caller-controlled default.” One author cannot confer unrelated private source/history access on a chosen task recipient.

**Proof:** forged `actorId`, `tenantId`, `acceptedBy`, source kind, entity and assignee; auth-user/profile/Party/assignment IDs differ; same human has multiple hats; a hidden/expired assignee or zero eligible owner. Existing authorized person/queue or owned exception preserves obligation, without inventing a coordinating role or granting access merely to complete a handoff.

### A4 — individual or aggregate create/reuse results disclose hidden existing work

**High; high under naive insert-or-return. Effect: inherited D3 no-disclosure floor; exact admitted observable result still needs owner qualification.** RLS SELECT returns none; uniqueness conflicts with a hidden journey; a bulk report says “7 existing,” enumerates denied IDs, or gives a reason that identifies the private purpose. Generic 23505 text still differs from successful creation when absent.

**Exact safeguard:** “Independently authorize source admission and the minimum existence/reuse metadata its outcome discloses before exposing that distinction. A context lacking this authority gets the same bounded outcome whether hidden work exists or not. Reuse does not imply content/history/edit authority; no duplicate workaround.” Metadata-permitted/content-denied users may get precisely approved safe reuse results. This does not create an approval queue or grant metadata visibility by implication.

**Proof:** paired absent/hidden ordinary-journey fixtures at single and group level; identical unauthorized observations across result body, summary counts, downloadable result, redirect, notifications, stream and errors, with P12's required uniformity/timing qualification. Test authorized existence-only result without revealing stage/purpose/history. Catalog uniqueness still holds while unauthorized existence is not disclosed. Trusted internal policy lookup is allowed to obtain protected evidence; the prohibition is on unauthorized disclosure, not internal reads.

### A5 — batch status becomes a permanent backdoor after revocation

**Critical for sensitive people; medium. Effect: inherited read/retention floor.** Original submitter retains a full stored selection/result CSV forever; polling endpoint or progress push lists now-denied names/locations/reasons. Old result response races a Tenant/context change. A withheld result is mislabeled “failed,” encouraging duplicate submission.

**Exact safeguard:** “Re-authorize current status/detail/result/export independently. Source receipts retain truthful accepted outcomes under owner retention, but original creation does not grant perpetual receipt payload/target access. Return only current permitted operation facts; withheld or unresolved detail does not prove absence, failure or rollback. Cache keys and late-response guards bind exact context/generation; restricted progress is not a row-existence broadcast.” Neither a guessed batch ID nor creator field is sufficient authorization.

**Proof:** submit then lose target access/result authority; privileged run opened by read-only colleague; signed-in same human switches Tenant; response arrives late; restricted target deleted/redacted; result-download URL shared; guessed operation IDs. No hidden names/count partitions or old content via logs/caches/export/notifications. Retry reconciles the original operation despite unavailable result details rather than silently creating new work.

### A6 — source admission and raw Supabase paths enforce different rules

**Critical; medium under quick CRUD adoption. Effect: inherited engineering safeguard.** UI is human-reviewed, but REST/GraphQL/RPC/raw collection/legacy writer/background import posts straight to tables; a permitted row is updated into another Tenant/entity/purpose; server-role UPSERT updates a hidden ordinary journey. Old permissive policy keeps a route open after new narrower policy is added.

**Exact safeguard:** “Qualify all admitted doors through existing source commands/P12. Use exact same-scope foreign keys and protected attribution; enforce old-row and proposed-row rules. Inspect real grants, every applicable policy, function owner/search path/EXECUTE, view security, service-role commands, Storage/exports and worker paths; revoke or fence weaker legacy paths.” RLS remains coarse Tenant defense, not a second capability/relationship engine. Optional link absence must not null a shared required Tenant key.

**Proof:** real disposable PostgreSQL/Supabase catalog and resulting-row tests for SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/UPSERT/RETURNING, two Tenants and two same-Tenant entities, ordinary/extra conversion, scope/actor replacement and forged reviewed intent. API error alone is insufficient: inspect no forbidden row or side effect. Missing authenticated grants on intentionally service-owned tables are not a bug to “fix” by granting CRUD.

### A7 — ministry location and group selection become an export channel

**Critical; medium-high if current CRM columns/exports are reused. Effect: inherited P10/P12 floor.** A bulk picker exposes exact country, legal identity, restricted ministry label or cohort counts; a “download failures” file becomes a restricted supporter roster. Field-safe individual reads are mistaken for permission to export the whole restricted set.

**Exact safeguard:** “Use current permitted aliases and geographic precision, including disambiguation and accessible labels. Source/field clearance, restricted-set action gates, read budgets, audit and residency apply to selection/results/export, not only detail. No real-name/location fallback, raw filter reason in telemetry, or unrestricted result attachment.” P12 may distinguish internal group admission from prohibited bulk contact/export; D4 itself must neither ban every legitimate internal group nor grant a restricted roster export.

**Proof:** same aliases, forbidden exact country used only as filter, mixed ordinary/restricted members, result-title/error/URL leak, exported aggregate, progress notification, backup/replica/AI diagnostic placement. Confirm both permitted narrowly scoped internal work and denied restricted-set export through the existing owner rules.

### A8 — human review repurposes newsletter, Support or referral data

**High, critical where private context is involved; medium-high if “manual means permitted” is assumed. Effect: inherited source-purpose boundary.** A missionary sees a short-lived newsletter request or staff sees a Support transcript and clicks start, thereby persisting private payload as contact/cultivation evidence. Referrer consent, a church committee address or giving history is taken as supporter consent or authority.

**Exact safeguard:** “Human choice does not expand source use. Require independently sufficient permitted Party/ministry/purpose evidence for cultivation; newsletter request access remains its exact external-handoff purpose and retention, and Support/private-care sources keep their own release and joint-read rules. Use authorized references/minimal briefs, not copied bodies. Contactability is checked at communication-owner boundaries; internal enrollment is neither consent nor a donor message.” Do not categorically prohibit an independently lawful cultivation relationship merely because the person also made a newsletter or Support request.

**Proof:** newsletter-only transient payload cannot become a prospect via group selection or copied free-text reason; expired request body never reappears in journey history; staff with Support read but no release permission cannot copy transcript; separately permitted relationship can proceed on its independent source. Do-not-contact blocks prohibited outreach but does not erase history or blanket-deny lawful internal/own-record access.

### A9 — selection/admission/dispatch races ignore current owner truth

**Critical; medium under normal concurrency. Effect: inherited causal-revocation and integrity safeguard.** Two humans select same ordinary target; one correction/revoke/retirement happens between validation and write; task responsibility changes during bulk execution; cached policy subquery authorizes new content after revoke.

**Exact safeguard:** “Use the existing guarded owner transaction and expected material revisions, with a defined causal order for admission versus scope/revocation/retirement/correction. Distinguish reviewed request, per-target acceptance and later effect execution. Preserve accepted effects and current obligation; no stale permission, new retry key or changed creator bypasses source dedupe.” Do not mandate a tenant-wide lock or per-member permission-update fan-out; retain P12's epoch substrate and narrow source locks/CAS as qualified by implementation.

**Proof:** independent connections with barriers, not sleep: duplicate create, revoke-before-admission, accept-before-revoke, retirement, same-key/different-intent, merge/correction, source updates and stage/owner changes. Paused/ended collision semantics still await their proper decision. No speculative claim of full-batch atomicity; whichever model is later accepted must expose truthful current permitted outcomes and preserve active task/owned-exception invariants.

### A10 — rollback/deletion/restore erases accountability or revives private data

**High; medium. Effect: inherited custody and recovery safeguard.** Cancelling a bulk request cascades deleting journeys that already have independent asks; worker/profile removal deletes source evidence; old backup restores reviewed selection, erased payload, permission or already executed task and retries it as new.

**Exact safeguard:** “Cancellation stops only still-unadmitted work under the source contract and is not universal Undo. Preserve admitted facts and independently useful obligations; correct through qualified source commands. Restore reconciles current retention/erasure/redaction, authorization/suppression, source revisions and effect receipts before affected reads or new actions resume. Unknown disposition remains unavailable; retain only owner-permitted minimal evidence.” An old human review never reauthorizes a deleted/expired payload. No universal retention period or new recovery platform follows.

**Proof:** cancel before/after mixed acceptance, creator/assignee departure, record disposal under owner policy, backup before revoke/redaction with still-authorized viewer, expired newsletter payload and duplicate worker after restore. No re-enrollment, repeated effect, erased accepted history or resurrected content. Actual source retention can lawfully remove content while retaining minimum non-content receipts; “preserve history” is not indefinite plaintext retention.

## PostgreSQL/Supabase accuracy and evidence limits

The [PostgreSQL17 row-security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html), [CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html), [INSERT](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-insert.html) and [CREATE FUNCTION](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createfunction.html) primary pages were fetched during this same review session's D2/D3 work and remain the relevant mechanics. They establish:

- UNIQUE/PK/FK checks bypass RLS for integrity and can disclose existence. Permission-filtered preflight is not atomic duplicate prevention; a generic error does not by itself remove the oracle.
- USING governs existing rows; WITH CHECK governs proposed rows; an omitted explicit WITH CHECK can inherit USING. Missing syntax alone is not an ownership-reassignment defect. Multiple permissive policies OR. The current Support UPDATE migration explicitly uses both.
- SECURITY DEFINER uses the owner's privileges; it is not inherently unconditional RLS bypass for every owner. Inspect actual owner/BYPASSRLS/FORCE/search_path/EXECUTE and schema grants. FORCE does not constrain a BYPASSRLS role.
- ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE is an update with relevant privileges/policies/RETURNING; it is not a free “get or create authorized journey.” A single-statement fallback read may miss a concurrent winner under its snapshot. The earlier synthetic D3 experiment is mechanics evidence only.
- Policy permission subqueries can race under READ COMMITTED; causal epoch/source admission needs qualified commit ordering, not a claim that RLS alone solves revocation.

Supabase skill guidance was read earlier in this continuous review; simplified omitted-WITH-CHECK/definer claims were subordinated to the primary docs. The [Apr28 Data API change](https://supabase.com/changelog/45329-breaking-change-tables-not-exposed-to-data-and-graphql-api-automatically) changes default exposure for future objects; existing objects retain their grants. Core's current opt-in/deployed catalog was not checked. No new grants, DB configuration or deployment upgrade is authorized by D4.

## Product decisions versus implementation proof

**D4 can settle:** human-origin intent for new work; single/group convenience; no automatic enrollment from mere arrival; contextual start/reuse without a manager ceremony.

**Existing binding safeguards:** trusted exact context, source purpose and scope, independently authorized admission metadata, current permission at consequential boundaries, field/egress/export restrictions, no grants from participation, immutable accepted-effect identity, safe retained/replayed results and owner-governed correction/disposal.

**Still genuine choices, not defaults to smuggle into D4:** precise enrollment admission capability/metadata projection; reviewed group outcome/partial handling and acceptance boundary; initial work/state; close/reopen/merge collisions; exact later P28 sharing. Implementing human-reviewed background execution does not require an always-open user session or authorize unrestricted future auto-starts. No new access grant or routine approval queue is justified by the security review.
