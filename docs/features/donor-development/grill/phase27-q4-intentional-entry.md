## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

Separate-purpose labels could conceal duplicate ordinary work; a blanket
lifetime uniqueness constraint could prevent legitimate later work. Preserve
the ratified independent-outcome test and settle lifecycle/collisions separately.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

The exception needs a real intended-user case before release, not an invented
ministry workflow. Ratification and the isolated PostgreSQL mechanics checks do
not establish a working Supabase/Core admission or authorization path.

### How does this affect the whole product?

P27 owns one development source for staff and later authorized P28 views. Party,
ministry, responsibility, money, communication and disclosure retain their
existing owners; neither joining nor creating a journey grants their powers.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Prayer and financial partnership can coexist without duplicate administration.
Separate asks and real follow-up remain visible; declining one request does not
silently end the whole relationship. No new manager gate or donor setup follows.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

The researched ministry and nonprofit products distinguish particular requests
and tasks from enduring relationships. Their procedures support that separation,
not a universal claim that every agency needs Core's exceptional-purpose model.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes. It preserves ADR-0136, P9/P12 ownership and the adopted P27/P28 source
boundary, using ordinary PostgreSQL mechanisms. Exact lifecycle uniqueness and
complete Supabase enforcement remain implementation proof obligations.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

No further D3 amendment is needed. Record ratification and ask how new work is
accepted; do not silently decide entry, sharing, initial stage or reopening.

## Why this is the next question

D1–D3 establish what the journey represents and when existing ordinary work
should be reused. The next decision is whether an authorized person selects new
work, individually or in a reviewed group, or whether configured rules may also
accept new work without a person reviewing each selection.

This is an internal accountability decision. It is not donor opt-in, permission
to send, a new Party identity or evidence that a prospect has agreed to partner.
Ordinary Party/referral/task work can exist before the identified ministry is
known. A ministry must not be fabricated to satisfy enrollment.

## Proposed single founder question

**What should start a new ordinary partnership journey?**

**A — An authorized person deliberately starts it, individually or in a reviewed
group (recommended).** Gifts, referrals and other records can inform the choice
in existing work views; arrival alone does not start new cultivation. One clear
contextual action can both record a referral or ask and start/reuse its journey
where authorized. No extra enrollment ceremony, mandatory candidate queue or
manager approval is implied.

**B — Also allow organization-configured automatic starts.** In addition to A,
an organization may enable qualified rules through the existing automation
capability to start/reuse internal journeys, with explicit ministry, purpose,
responsibility and follow-through. This reduces repeated selection but adds
configuration, source qualification and correction/recovery needs. It does not
create a second rule system or make optional automation a dependency of ordinary
native cultivation.

Under either option, D3's source authority, current permissions, permitted
create/reuse result, duplicate protection and hidden-record protections remain.
An existing hidden journey does not grant access or justify a duplicate. This
choice does not grant a new role, invent a coordinating queue or decide who can
read private history. It sends nothing and grants no communication consent.

Reviewed bulk means an explicitly selected, reviewable scope, not silently all
matching people or merely a loaded page presented as a complete set. It need not
require a separate click on every person. Exact partial-result handling belongs
to the later interaction design; no unqualified bulk implementation is claimed.

Migration of evidenced existing work is separate from automated admission of
new work. Preserve original context and outward-effect-dark import rules; neither
option requires existing supporters to be approached again or placed in a new
prospect stage. Exact initial stage, lifecycle and collision outcomes remain
separate decisions rather than hidden defaults of this question.
