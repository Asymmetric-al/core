# Phase 27 ministry-practice research notes

Research only; no specification, implementation, activation, or donor contact. Retrieved 2026-10-09. These notes describe published official documentation, not independently tested product behavior. Sources' old update dates are retained even when their official pages are currently accessible. No portfolio-performance statistics are adopted.

## Highest-leverage opening decision

**Recommendation/inference:** Ask what an enrollment represents over time: an ongoing, purpose-specific relationship with successive asks and annual reviews, or a bounded development initiative that closes on its outcome. Multiple legitimate purpose enrollments and the prohibition on one Party-wide stage are already settled by the user; do not reopen them.

Concrete scenario: a church maintains an ongoing sending/prayer/support partnership, reviews its annual support budget, and considers a separate special project appeal. Does the church-partnership enrollment continue through annual asks and stewardship, with the special initiative represented separately, or is each annual renewal a fresh enrollment that closes? Either can support nonfinancial outcomes. The answer determines stage aging, completion, follow-through, histories, and what P28 reuses.

Evidence supports the distinction but does not settle Core's choice: MPDX separates ongoing monthly support from special appeals; TntConnect distinguishes team-partnership phase, pledge amount/frequency, and typically one-time campaigns; CiviCRM campaigns may have an end date or continue indefinitely. None establishes an exact equivalent to Core's proposed enrollment. My preference is an ongoing purpose-specific enrollment for ongoing partnership, with time-bounded asks/appeal actions under or linked to it, because financial response alone should not finish church relationship care. This remains a founder decision, not a ratified design.

## MPDX

Edition: MPDX Web help unless marked Mobile. No current deployed version/build number asserted.

### Stage and ongoing partnership

- **Fact:** [MPDX Status, MPD Phase](https://help.mpdx.org/article/162-mpdx-status-mpd-phase), updated 2023-09-18, describes active statuses spanning first contact, cultivation, appointment, decision, financial/special/prayer partnership. Hidden statuses include not interested, unresponsive, never ask, research abandoned, expired referral. A contact can have only one status; tags are suggested for additional categorization. Partnership status also affects monthly progress reporting; one-time gifts do not appear in that monthly bar.
- **Inference for Core:** Borrow recognizable ministry vocabulary, not the contact-global status architecture. A person/church can have a nonfinancial partnership and a distinct financial ask. Stage, contact restriction, frequency, commitment receipt, and financial attention are different facts.

### Appeals and physical custody

- **Fact:** [Setup an Appeal](https://help.mpdx.org/article/190-set-up-appeal), updated 2022-10-13, defines appeals as specific financial campaigns outside monthly goals. Audience is selected using statuses/tags, with exclusions such as Send Appeals = No. Goals can include costs and can be edited. This does not prove how permission/suppression is rechecked at export/send time.
- **Fact:** [Flows View for Appeals](https://help.mpdx.org/article/774-flows-view-for-appeals), updated 2022-10-13, explicitly distinguishes **Received** (gift physically in hand, not processed by donation system) from **Given** (received by donation processing system). Dragging to Committed prompts expected amount/date. Dragging to Given may prompt choosing among recent gifts, such as regular monthly support versus a special gift. Assigning a donation to an appeal also moves the contact to Given.
- **Fact:** [Track an Appeal](https://help.mpdx.org/article/191-track-an-appeal), updated 2021-11-02, documents Excluded, Asked, Committed, Received, Given. All added contacts start in Asked. Exclusion retains visible membership; deleting membership does not delete the contact. Processing an associated gift advances it to Given.
- **Fact:** [List View for Appeals](https://help.mpdx.org/article/1013-list-view-for-appeals), updated 2021-11-02, collects amount, expected date, status, currency for an appeal commitment. Deleting a commitment moves membership back to Asked.
- **Inference for Core:** These buckets combine audience inclusion, solicitation, promise, custody, and financial evidence. In particular, MPDX's Asked does not by itself prove an ask was presented. Core should not inherit that equivalence. Whether Core has a meaningful pre-processing custody event depends on P13's source contract and actual staff practice. If P13 has no separately authorized custody fact, do not invent one by dragging an appeal card. Preserve explicit gift association and separate source evidence; do not infer that a monthly gift fulfills an appeal.

### Referrals, identity, and follow-through

- **Fact:** [Add Referrals to Contacts](https://help.mpdx.org/article/194-add-referrals-to-contacts), updated 2023-06-08, records who introduced a new contact and the referral date. It reports recent connections separately from contacts still in early phases. The article notes MPD practice increasingly calls these connections.
- **Fact:** [A Contact's Page](https://help.mpdx.org/article/1251-mpdx-quick-reference-guide-a-contacts-page), updated 2023-06-08, allows multiple people within a contact, identifies a primary contact person, and treats preferred contact method separately. It offers individual newsletter opt-out and locks donation-service-provided contact fields against edit/delete.
- **Fact:** [Contacts and Individuals](https://help.mpdx.org/article/145-contacts-and-individuals), page date not captured, treats an individual contact as a household, with people under it. Individual newsletter opt-out removes that person from the corresponding exports/MailChimp sync while permitting other household people to remain subscribed.
- **Fact:** [Tackle MPDX Mobile Tasks](https://help.mpdx.org/article/500-tackle-mpdx-mobile-tasks), Mobile help, updated 2021-11-29, shows overdue tasks first and prompts for a next-action task after completion. This establishes a prompt pattern, not a universal invariant that every active relationship always has a next task.
- **Inference for Core:** A referral records provenance, not contact consent or a data-access grant. The person who represents a church and the church itself need separate Party identities and relationship roles. A next-action prompt is useful, but prohibited contact can only lead to an internal review/service action or explicit exception.

## TntConnect

Edition: currently accessible official Exploring TntConnect help. Most topic pages do not publish update dates/version pinning. Some naming and screenshots descend from TntMPD/TntConnect 3.x documentation; treat those as current-published guidance with legacy limitations, not proof of the latest binary's exact behavior. Do not transplant personal-desktop bookkeeping into Core financial authority.

- **Fact:** [TNT/MPD Phase](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/mpd-phase.aspx), undated, separates pre-decision progression from financial, special, and prayer partners. It distinguishes the partner declining from staff deciding never to ask, and permits skipping stages.
- **Fact:** [TNT Phase technical topic](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/contacts_mpdphase.aspx), undated, distinguishes no response from a known refusal and describes abandoned research/expired referrals. This supports preserving outcome reasons, not treating every closed attempt as a donor rejection.
- **Fact:** [TNT Phase Auto-change](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/taskhistory_mpdphasechg.aspx), undated, describes prompting for a phase change after some pre-decision task completions. Financial partnership requires manual pledge amount/frequency entry. The documented interaction is a confirmation prompt, not silent stage advancement.
- **Fact:** [Using Campaigns and Promises](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/campaignsoverview.aspx), undated, calls campaigns typically one-time projects, also allowing regular-giving increase initiatives. Gifts can be matched to campaigns; promise tracking is separately enabled. This page says campaign gifts by default do not also fulfill regular pledges; another older [General Tab](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/options-general.aspx) topic describes long-frequency appeal gifts counting toward pledges and a setting to change that. Treat default behavior as documentation-version ambiguous; the substantive lesson is that appeal/regular-pledge overlap is an explicit policy choice.
- **Fact:** [Add Referrals](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/contacts-addreferrals.aspx), undated, adds basic information under the chosen referrer and can look up new or all referrals. [TNT Tab Overview](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/contacts_mpdtabtutorial.aspx), undated, permits multiple referrers and permits a referral source to be a non-contact or event.
- **Fact:** [TntConnect 3.0](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/whatsnew_3_0.aspx), historical release July 2013, describes an organization contact-person field for churches/businesses and unscheduled visits as a task type. It is evidence for separating church and representative, not an adequate multi-role church relationship model.
- **Fact:** [Log History](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/taskhistory-loghistory.aspx), undated, records task type, result (attempted/done/received), date/time, description, and optional appeal link. [Task Types](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/taskhistory_tasktypes.aspx), undated, distinguishes a casual church encounter from an appointment and allows internal data maintenance in To Do.
- **Fact:** [Edit/Delete Gifts](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/gifts_edit.aspx), undated, discusses duplicates created by manually entering a gift before organization processing, later sync, and downloaded gifts reappearing if locally deleted. Its proposed auto-delete window differs from [Dealing with Gifts](https://www.tntware.com/tntconnect/help/en/pages/gifts-dealingwith.aspx). Do not rely on either as a current exact retention contract.
- **Inference for Core:** Keep completed conversation, attempted contact, internal update, and financial event qualified. Keep referrals and historical roles. Reject local negative-gift/date-edit workarounds, automatic amount-match fulfillment, inferred giving-capacity features, and desktop-sync semantics wherever they conflict with P13/P14/P16 and the user's settled rules.

## CiviCRM

Edition: official User Guide `en/latest`, accessed 2026-10-09; no exact installed CiviCRM release or article update dates claimed. This is a configurable product with optional components/extensions and CMS-dependent permissions.

- **Fact:** [Campaign everyday tasks](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/campaign/everyday-tasks/) treats a campaign as an initiative with type, goals, dates, target groups, and references from mailings, events, and contributions. Without an end date it is ongoing. Contributions are deliberately assigned to a campaign; the campaign is not itself the contribution or email.
- **Fact:** [Activities](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/organising-your-data/activities/) describes dated interactions with subjects, creator, assignee, status, and optional recurrence. Activities also represent system-generated contributions/event participation. Attempted outcomes include left message/unreachable. Therefore presence in activity history is not evidence of a meaningful personal conversation.
- **Fact:** [Relationships](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/organising-your-data/relationships/) links people, organizations, and households, with type and optional start/end dates. Its relationship permissions feature can grant access in Profiles. **Core difference:** preserve P9 relationship facts but never import that access-granting feature into P27.
- **Fact:** [Groups and tags](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/organising-your-data/groups-and-tags/) distinguishes manually maintained regular groups from automatically populated smart groups; groups support coherent audiences, while tags generally classify. Some group types affect mailing/access functions. **Core inference:** saved-view lens, historical appeal population, and eligible communication recipients require distinct meanings, even where competitors use one group abstraction.
- **Fact:** [Soft credits](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/contributions/soft-credits/) separates actual contributor from others receiving credit, including solicitors and donor advisers. It permits partial credits and illustrates a foundation as donor with two different people credited for different roles. **Core inference:** consume P14 recognition; do not add credits to legal revenue totals or collapse them into ask fulfillment.
- **Fact:** [Contacts](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/organising-your-data/contacts/) distinguishes individuals, organizations, and households. Channel restrictions, no bulk email, and no sharing are distinct. Certain functions enforce relevant restrictions. Notes may be author-only subject to privileged access. **Core inference:** no church membership, shared address, referral, assignment, or appeal inclusion implies solicitation permission or personal donor visibility.

## AFP

- **Fact:** [Donor Bill of Rights](https://afpglobal.org/donor-bill-rights), current official publication without displayed amendment date, emphasizes informed voluntary giving, fidelity to purpose, suitable acknowledgment, confidentiality, professional conduct, and truthful answers. It also covers removal from shared mailing lists. These are practice principles, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific law or Core disclosure contracts.
- **Fact:** [Code of Ethical Standards](https://afpglobal.org/ethics/code-ethical-standards) states its December 2023 board-approved revision is enforceable. It addresses accurate communications/results, donor intent and restrictions, timely stewardship, explicit consent before changing restricted gift conditions, confidentiality, and requests to omit personal information from future organizational use. It rejects percentage-of-funds compensation and finder fees. AFP scope is its members/professional guidance; do not imply the code is universally binding law.
- **Inference for Core:** Relationship care must remain useful for prayer/nonfinancial partnership. Clear attribution, unambiguous financial meanings, suppression-safe follow-up, visible uncertainty, and source-linked meeting briefs serve donor autonomy and honest stewardship. No source supports turning low financial recency/amount into low human value. Linking/paraphrasing is appropriate here; do not reproduce AFP's complete copyrighted code/bill in a product or public page.
