# Phase 27 Q1 — continuing relationships in a missionary sending organization

Research/grooming only. Checked 2026-10-09. Founder chose A (continuing purpose-specific relationship) and asked that it be appropriate for Christian missions organizations with missionaries. This note stress-tests that choice; it does not ratify additional design decisions.

## Assessment

**Inference:** A continuing purpose-specific relationship is a strong fit for the stated support-raising sending-agency model. A first gift, completed initial fundraising season, annual renewal, or special appeal does not normally exhaust partnership care. It accommodates recurring support, prayer, encouragement, visits, introductions, and changing seasons without treating an established partner as a new prospect each year.

The evidence does **not** prove a universally best model for every Christian agency or settle the enrollment's exact subject, scope, access, stages, or closure rules. Continuing must not mean perpetual solicitation, permanent personal ownership of supporter records, or an enrollment that can never end. It also must not make every missionary repeat a staff workflow: P28 projects the shared source with its own permitted work and stage preset.

## Ministry scenario pressure tests

The following are **Asym implications to test**, not claims that vendors implement them or additional founder decisions.

| Scenario                                                                         | Why a continuing relationship fits                                                                                                                                      | Boundary that still needs care                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A church partners with the agency broadly and supports two missionary ministries | Agency partnership can continue while specific ministries have different asks, people, timing, or progress.                                                             | A general agency relationship and support for one missionary ministry are not automatically the same cultivation purpose. Church members, pastor, finance contact, and church Party remain separate.                                                     |
| Support for a single worker, married couple, or team                             | Partnership can outlast a login change, participant change, or annual budget cycle.                                                                                     | Use the accepted organization-owned ministry context; do not reduce it to the current user, a household, or one staff assignment. Whether each supported ministry warrants a distinct enrollment is still a product-scope decision.                      |
| A missionary reaches initial support or an annual ask succeeds                   | Ongoing reporting, thanks, prayer, and later review remain useful; the successful ask can finish without resetting the relationship.                                    | Commitment, gift receipt, and stage are separate. One annual renewal outcome must not silently rewrite recurring financial truth.                                                                                                                        |
| Home assignment/furlough                                                         | Existing partnerships continue; appropriate next work may become visits, church reporting, listening, or a quieter care cadence.                                        | Do not automatically terminate or pause all support because the worker is away from the field. Do not import one agency's furlough/compensation policy.                                                                                                  |
| Transfer to another ministry or agency; retirement                               | Historical partnership and prior stewardship obligations remain intelligible. Some relationships may continue under an explicitly revised purpose; others may conclude. | Continuing does not authorize automatic transfer of supporters, permissions, commitments, restricted gifts, balances, or private notes. Ask whether the original purpose genuinely continues; use explicit owner dispositions for anything that changes. |
| Prayer-only, hospitality, introductions, or a church's nonfinancial partnership  | These are legitimate forms of participation, not incomplete financial conversion or failed giving scores.                                                               | Do not require an amount or financial ask just to retain an active partnership. A prayer partner is not automatically consenting to appeals.                                                                                                             |
| Partner declines an ask or requests no solicitation                              | An individual ask may finish while permitted relationship/service care continues.                                                                                       | Never use relationship continuity or the next-action invariant to pressure prohibited contact. Restrictions may require internal review or closure instead.                                                                                              |

## Existing Core authority that governs the adaptation

- [Platform product intent](/workspace/core/openspec/specs/platform-product-intent/spec.md:31) already commits to Christian missions sending organizations with support-raising missionaries as users within an organizational tenant. The founder's qualification reinforces this settled intent.
- [Phase 9 D19 source amendment](/workspace/core/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-09-full-crm-depth-relationship-graph.md:1277), dated 2026-08-06, defines a stable, organization-owned Ministry Assignment in one Tenant/Legal Entity, with separately identified prospective participants. It survives spouse, household, team, leadership, login, and employment changes. It is distinct from Party, Ministry Project, Support Assignment, Designation, Campaign, page, and financial account.
- That amendment also says participation and the optional finance-authorized support binding confer no automatic grants, responsibility, supporter identity, or money movement. Transfers, retirement, or participant changes need explicit owner-domain dispositions and preserve historical identity.
- [Roadmap Phase 28](/workspace/core/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:4163) consumes Phase 27's enrollment primitive with a support-raising preset, multiple commitments, distinct currencies, qualified gift-driven tasks, referrals, and one interaction history. Its coaching goals cannot impose one agency's methodology on every tenant. These are effective planning contracts, not evidence all behavior is implemented.
- Staff cultivation responsibility, missionary participation, coach visibility, public display, newsletter eligibility, and financial support context remain independently owned facts. A continuing enrollment must not silently become an access container or missionary-owned donor database.

## Suggested next genuine product question

Resolve **the enduring ministry context of an ordinary missionary support enrollment**, now that its continuity is chosen:

> For ordinary missionary support, should the continuing enrollment be anchored to the ministry being supported—represented by one existing Ministry Assignment that may involve an individual, couple, or team—or to the individual missionary personally?

This asks about the enduring relationship purpose, not whether multiple purposes are allowed (already settled), or whether responsibility grants access (it does not). An assignment anchor would let the ministry relationship survive separately authorized participant changes; a personal anchor would require distinct treatment of shared couple/team work and succession. This is a founder question, not a ratified answer. It does not decide staff/missionary/coaching visibility, which remains separately unresolved. The exact Ministry Assignment is already a source-owned identity; do not assume a finance Support Assignment, worker login, or household is interchangeable.
