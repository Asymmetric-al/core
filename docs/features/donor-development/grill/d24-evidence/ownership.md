## Smallest coherent membership behavior — proposed product judgment

An appeal has an explicit working population of source-qualified Parties, with
attributed membership changes. A filter discovers candidates; selecting a finite
set records intent. Later filter, assignment, giving or stage changes do not add
or remove members. Repeated addition of the same current canonical Party is not
another member or another unit in its planning count. A person, household and
church are distinct Parties, not duplicates merely because they share an address
or representative. A church member row does not expand into every church contact.

**Exclude** keeps a deliberate appeal-specific decision visible while removing
the Party from planned outreach. **Remove** takes the entry out of the current
working list. Both preserve prior inclusion, dispositions and independently owned
requests/outcomes. Neither means decline, global opt-out, cancellation of every
request or deletion. Deliberate restoration/re-addition can restore current
planning only after current eligibility checks; it does not erase exclusions or
revive earlier messages, tasks or admissions. This is bounded membership history,
not a general lifecycle/workflow engine or a new literal database enum mandate.

Current consent, contactability, identity and access restrictions are separate
derived eligibility. Preserve a lawfully visible historical member while showing
that further outreach is blocked; do not rewrite an organization restriction into
a staff-entered appeal exclusion. Removing a restriction does not create new
membership, revive ended work or supply fresh outreach authorization. Unknown
eligibility permits no affected outward action and is not a silent “eligible.”

For outreach deliberately originated from the appeal, **current included and
not-excluded membership is a continuing producer eligibility condition**. Removal
or exclusion must invalidate affected unstarted appeal-originated outreach through
the qualified owner fence. The already-reviewed communication population can only
lose eligibility; it never gains replacement members or later additions. Re-addition
cannot reactivate its ended intent. The joint P27/P32/P6 adapter must prove this
before that integration activates; a warning or manual stop workaround is not
the permanent substitute. This is compatible with E4–E5 and does not amend their
ownership. The final owner boundary, not physical wire transmission, decides
whether prevention remains possible: in-flight/accepted/indeterminate outcomes
stay truthful and cannot be recalled by changing the list. Unrelated communication
continues under its own source.
