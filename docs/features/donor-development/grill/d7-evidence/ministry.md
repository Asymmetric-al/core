# D7 evidence — closing current development work without ending the relationship

Research and retrieval date: **2026-10-10 UTC**. The founder selected Q7 A:
close the current cultivation effort, preserving history, when there is no
meaningful further development step or useful internal review date. This note
reviews that selection; proposed amendments are not independently ratified.
No product, donor record, code, formal specification or external system was
changed. No vendor application or agency-user pilot was executed.

## Finding and strongest alternative

Keep A within its actual premise: staff deliberately conclude that this current
development effort has no further useful work. It is a disposition of staff work,
not a declaration that a church, friendship, missionary partnership, prayer
commitment or financial relationship has ended. A later start remains deliberate;
same-record reopening versus a linked successor is still a separate question.

The strongest alternative is an explicitly owned undated hold for work waiting
on an external change. It conveys continuing intent without repeated arbitrary
dates. Its drawback is another form of open work whose return depends on someone
remembering or recognizing the change. A's advantage is a clearer current
workload without fictitious review dates. These are product judgments, not
measured ministry-user preferences. The official sources below establish neither
a universal ban on undated holds nor a universal requirement to use them.

## Material concerns and exact amendments

Severity concerns consequence; likelihood is a qualitative design-risk judgment,
not a measured occurrence rate. Scenarios below are illustrative pressure tests.

### M1 — Staff closure is mistaken for donor rejection or an ended relationship

**High severity; plausible likelihood.** Labels such as End partnership, Lost,
Not Interested, Inactive donor or Never Ask can assert facts that staff do not
know or change a broader relationship than the intended effort.

**Exact amendment:** “Close current development work records staff's decision
about this effort only. It does not assert that the supporter declined, stopped
partnering, stopped praying or giving, or may never be contacted. The human,
church and ministry relationships retain their independently recorded meanings.”

Use language about this work in the action and history. Final copy remains to
be tested; avoid treating a vendor's Archive/Hide/Closed Lost label as authority.

### M2 — No financial request is confused with no useful development work

**High severity; plausible likelihood.** A supporter may participate through
prayer, practical help or ongoing stewardship. A declined equipment request or
quiet giving period does not establish that all meaningful work is over.

**Exact amendment:** “Apply D7 to the whole identified current development
effort only after considering its remaining meaningful work, including
nonfinancial partnership and stewardship. A declined ask, absent gift or lack
of recent contact alone does not establish that premise.”

This is not a mandatory new checklist, obligation to invent a task, or rule
that every prayer update requires an active cultivation effort. Where continuing
work belongs to another source, it retains that ownership and eligibility.

### M3 — Closing work cancels promises or conceals unfinished action

**High severity; plausible likelihood.** A pending acknowledgement, agreed call,
financial source operation or other valid responsibility can outlive an effort.
Conversely, a D6 reminder must not continue asking staff to review a hold after
the effort has validly ended and the review is no longer required.

**Exact amendment:** “Closing this effort must explicitly account for its
affected open work. Preserve, hand off or end each affected obligation only
through its owner's permitted behavior; do not blanket-complete or delete linked
tasks. End a no-longer-required review's applicability truthfully, without
claiming that the review was performed. An actual authorized review retains its
own truthful outcome.”

Other-source obligations must not be falsely completed merely to permit closure.
Neither an ongoing recurring gift nor an unrelated receipt task alone forces
this cultivation effort to remain open forever. Exact collision and task/ask
disposition rules must be settled before the close command ships.

### M4 — Removing workload becomes deleting history or hiding the supporter

**High severity; plausible likelihood.** A staff member seeking old context or
another ministry working with the same church must not interpret disappearance
as no previous relationship, or lose access they otherwise have to the Party.

**Exact amendment:** “Remove the closed effort from current development work
while preserving its authorized history and clear closed-work status. Do not
delete, globally hide or inactivate the supporter, Ministry Assignment, or other
efforts. Existing privacy and retention rules still govern historical access.”

Discoverability is not unlimited retention, broadened missionary access, or a
new archive product. Reuse the appropriate existing record/history views.

### M5 — Simplicity creates repetitive dates or compulsory outreach

**Medium severity; plausible likelihood.** Without a clear Close current work
option, users may choose arbitrary recurring dates, phone a supporter merely to
justify a review, or manufacture a reason narrative to clear a task.

**Exact amendment:** “A genuine decision that no useful current development
work remains may close the effort without inventing another date or contacting
the supporter. D7 adds no required donor confirmation, manager approval, reason
essay, outreach quota or monetary success criterion.”

A neutral internal disposition/history can evidence the action without copying
medical, family, pastoral or restricted-ministry details. Do not infer an approved
reason taxonomy or mandatory review form from this recommendation.

### M6 — Later activity silently restarts the effort or erases the break

**High severity; plausible likelihood.** A gift, church update, referral or
import is evidence of its own event, not proof that staff intend to resume this
particular work, or that the prior responsible person still has access.

**Exact amendment:** “Later development work requires a deliberate currently
authorized decision and current eligible responsibility. A gift, referral,
import, message or elapsed time cannot restart closed work automatically. Keep
the prior ending and history truthful; D7 does not choose same-record reopening
versus a linked successor.”

Ordinary financial synchronization, required receipts and permitted independent
communications continue through their owners. They do not need a new cultivation
effort merely to operate.

## Focused proof and usability checks

- A church declines one special request but continues ordinary financial or
  prayer partnership: no automatic whole-effort closure or negative donor label.
- Staff have no further meaningful development step or useful review date and
  choose closure: no invented date, supporter contact or compulsory narrative;
  current workload changes while authorized history remains findable.
- The same church supports another ministry: that separate effort, its lead,
  tasks and permissions remain unaffected. The church Party remains available
  through its ordinary authorized record.
- A prayer-only supporter has promised follow-through: closing one financial
  ask cannot conceal that work. Separately owned updates may remain valid without
  falsely keeping every cultivation effort active.
- Close with an outstanding promise and a queued D6 notification: preserve or
  explicitly dispose of the real obligation through its owner, prevent stale
  attention, and distinguish No longer required from an actually completed review.
- A gift or new referral arrives after closure: source processing continues;
  cultivation remains closed until deliberate authorized action. Prior history
  is neither rewritten nor exposed to a newly involved person by implication.
- Ask actual agency staff and support-raising missionaries to explain what the
  proposed close action ends and what it preserves. Include a church, a prayer
  partner and a supporter of multiple ministries. Check whether users can find
  history, avoid artificial dates, and distinguish a staff decision from donor
  refusal. These are proposed pilot checks; no preference, sample size, measured
  benefit or completed pilot is claimed.

The next dependent founder choice remains the identity/history behavior of a
deliberate later start. D7 should not resolve that physical lifetime question
through an apparently simple Close label.
