# D7 evidence — closing current cultivation without closing its source relationships

Research readback: 2026-10-10 at
`225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`. D1–D4 and amended D6 are ratified;
D5 A is selected. The founder selected D7 A: close current cultivation work,
preserving history, when there is no useful next step or review date. Proposed
safeguards below remain review recommendations until ratified. This is research,
not a PRD, OpenSpec amendment, implementation, issue or activation. The parent
review owns the consolidated interpretation.

## Finding and bounded recommendation

Retain A. Closing is an explicit P27 disposition of the exact current
cultivation effort. It removes that effort from current cultivation workload
without deleting history or changing the supporter Party, Ministry Assignment,
staff-assignment relationships, giving, commitments, contact restrictions or
other independently applicable work. Closing a review and closing an enrollment
are different facts, even when one authorized source action records both.

Qualify the dependencies of the exact closing effort, not an all-Core obligation
engine. A remaining promise, ask follow-through or stewardship obligation cannot
be hidden by closure. Its owner must retain it with legitimate accountability or
accept its explicit disposition; a caller's filtered task list cannot prove that
nothing remains. Conversely, unrelated financial/service work is not a reason to
keep cultivation artificially open forever.

The strongest alternative remains an explicitly owned undated hold for a real
external-change dependency. D7 A rejects that extra ordinary holding state for
the presented no-work/no-review-date case. It does not define every reason a
journey may end or decide whether later deliberate work reopens this enrollment
or creates a linked successor. Avoid adding an event watcher or policy engine to
recover B after A was selected.

## Concerns, grading and exact proposed safeguards

Grades describe consequence and conditional likelihood if a design mistake is
adopted. They are not measured frequencies or claims of existing live defects.

### O1 — Closing cultivation archives the Party or rewrites its stage/response

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** high if a convenient existing archive/status
command is reused. **Evidence:** P9:408–435,484–494,741–748; roadmap:4087–4101;
D3:20–44. **Effect:** preserve A, require an exact P27 source disposition.

**Exact proposed requirement:** “Closing affects only this current cultivation
effort. Preserve observed stage history and independently recorded ask responses;
do not infer Won, Lost, Not Interested, Never Ask, deceased, inactive, archived,
financial failure or success. Do not end Party/ministry/relationship/assignment
records or remove independently derived portfolio membership.”

**Proof:** a church has two ministries, an active overall representative and an
independent purpose; closing one effort changes only its current-work projection.
Giving, other ministry work, Party status and staff-assignment edges are identical.

### O2 — ‘Nothing remains’ means nothing visible in the caller's current list

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** plausible with paginated, filtered or
permission-limited views. **Evidence:** Q7:98–128; D2:35–60; D3:38–44;
ADR-0183:57–90; P12:2837–2849. **Effect:** qualify a bounded closure precondition,
without an all-platform obligations scanner or broad visibility grant.

**Exact proposed requirement:** “The source checks the complete, exact work whose
applicability/accountability this closure would change. A remaining promise,
accepted follow-through or stewardship obligation must be fulfilled or receive
its own authorized disposition, preserving any continuing accountability and
visibility. Merely assigning the same continuing cultivation work to another
person does not establish that this effort has no remaining work. Unknown
closure impact is not an empty set. Do not infer clearance
from the caller's visible task count; provide a permission-safe unresolved result
when the source cannot prove the required disposition.”

**Proof:** a hidden promise, later page of tasks, independent unresolved ask and
concurrent newly accepted obligation each prevent false clean closure. A valid
independent finance task or another ministry's work is not canceled or made a
blanket blocker. No hidden identity/count leaks through the result.

### O3 — A blanket cascade completes every linked task or invents a performed review

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** high if one generic task-status update is
used. **Evidence:** ADR-0183:71–100; D6:214–223; Q7:121–128. **Effect:** preserve
A and use existing truthful task outcomes at exact source scope.

**Exact proposed requirement:** “A performed pause review that explicitly decides
End may record a completed review. Direct closure that merely makes a scheduled
review unnecessary records No longer required, not Completed or Reviewed.
Only the source's accepted action/receipt changes the affected task. Do not
complete, delete or cancel all tasks linked to the Party, ministry or journey;
independently applicable work keeps its own owner and history.”

**Proof:** compare Review → End, direct Close before the review date, and Close
with an unrelated promised call. Review/task history distinguishes all three;
generic Complete, drag-to-Done, API, bulk, AI and notification read cannot imitate
the source decision.

### O4 — Accepted closure leaves stale due attention, or fabricates a new closing message

**Severity:** medium/high. **Likelihood:** plausible under queued work or a
separate notification store. **Evidence:** ADR-0027:23–65; D6:187–196,235–249;
orchestration spec:25–32. **Effect:** retain A, apply D6's existing source fence.

**Exact proposed requirement:** “Accepted closure invalidates only future
attention whose registered review/source applicability ended. An unreleased
obsolete item is not admitted; already available attention ends truthfully and
retains only currently authorized Recent history under its existing policy.
Never fabricate read, archive, completion or unread debt. D7 adds no donor
message, closure notification, digest or new channel.”

**Proof:** execute both orders of closure versus reminder admission, unread versus
already-read items, late worker recovery and access loss. Remaining unrelated
work stays visible; a stale click loads current authorized state and cannot
revive the closed review.

### O5 — A child-task no-reopen rule silently determines enrollment lifetime

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** plausible because terminology is shared.
**Evidence:** ADR-0183:93–100; ADR-0027:50–55; D3:55–59; Q7:129–134.
**Effect:** explicitly preserve the next founder decision.

**Exact proposed requirement:** “D7 does not select same-enrollment reopening
versus a linked new enrollment for later legitimate work. Preserve closure and
accepted historical facts under either future policy. Ended review-task and
notification occurrences never reopen; any newly required review/attention uses
its own qualified source occurrence. This child-work rule is not a mandate for
a new parent cultivation enrollment.”

**Proof:** schema/design review rejects lifetime Party/ministry uniqueness,
automatic new parent creation and automatic parent resurrection inferred from
D7 alone. Both future parent-lifetime alternatives can preserve old closure and
child outcomes until explicitly selected.

### O6 — Closing effort becomes a global stop switch, or a later gift restarts it

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** plausible with a shared `closed` filter or
event-driven admission shortcut. **Evidence:** D4:23–35,63–70; roadmap:4101,
4119,4163–4185. **Effect:** keep closure and native source service independent.

**Exact proposed requirement:** “Closure neither suppresses existing qualified
receipts/service work nor changes giving, commitments, consent or public state.
A later gift, referral, import, score or relationship event does not reopen or
admit cultivation. Its source may still require an independently authorized
receipt, thank-you task or other native follow-up without pretending that the
closed cultivation effort has restarted.”

**Proof:** a later recurring gift records finance/receipt truth and any qualified
native task while the effort remains closed. A donor's contact restriction still
blocks prohibited contact; closure creates no new consent or financial authority.

### O7 — A stale or insufficiently authorized actor closes newer work

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** plausible with independent close/edit/task
commands. **Evidence:** P12:1552–1556,2837–2849; D6:225–273;
orchestration spec:76–109. **Effect:** use existing source command, receipt and
current authorization boundaries, not a new approval workflow.

**Exact proposed requirement:** “An authorized explicit source command checks
current closure eligibility and the exact source/work revisions at acceptance.
Atomically persist closure, lawful attribution/history, disposition receipts for
the local effects it owns, and durable projection intent. Concurrent obligations,
review changes and conflicting future work cannot slip past a stale check.
Replay returns the prior permitted result without closing later work; current
authorization governs result disclosure and all deferred effects.”

**Proof:** closure versus accepted task/ask, review reschedule, authority loss,
second close, lost response, retry after later work and failed projection. Exactly
one truthful source result survives; post-commit projection failure is repaired
without reopening work or fabricating successful cleanup.

### O8 — Preserved history means perpetual visibility, or closure deletes the evidence

**Severity:** high. **Likelihood:** plausible with soft-delete filters, cascading
deletes or broad historical views. **Evidence:** D2:27–61; ADR-0183:64–69,93–100;
ADR-0027:39–55; P12:1552–1556. **Effect:** preserve lawful source history without
conflating it with access or notification retention.

**Exact proposed requirement:** “Keep closure, actor/source references and past
accepted outcomes under their existing retention and correction rules. Closing
does not hard-delete or silently rewrite them. Historical list/detail, export,
audit and missionary projections still require current authority. Notification
Recent expiry is not deletion of source history, and retained source history
does not extend notification visibility or preserve a former worker's access.”

**Proof:** access revoked after closure; authorized historical read; privacy
anonymization; corrected Party identity; expired notification preview; restored
backup. No invented actor, broadened history floor, stale recipient or revived
attention appears.
