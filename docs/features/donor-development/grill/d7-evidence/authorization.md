# D7 evidence — closure, private obligations and retained history

2026-10-10. Research-only authorization/privacy review for **close the current development work while retaining its history**, the selected direction supplied to this review. At the start of this appendix Q7 still recorded the pre-answer prompt; the parent review now records A selected and material amendments pending. This appendix does not itself ratify an amended decision. Source checkout: `225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`. D6 is now explicitly ratified. No application code, migration, production setting, remote record, formal specification or permission grant is changed here.

**Conclusion:** retain the closure direction. Its central guard is that permission to end cultivation is neither permission to inspect every private obligation nor permission to dispose of another owner's work. A complete closure check must have a separately qualified, minimized result projection. “Keep history” means retain lawful evidence under its owner, not perpetual access or permanent private bodies.

## Material concerns and proposed exact safeguards

Severity grades the consequence of an incorrect implementation; likelihood is a qualitative adoption risk, not an observed production incident rate. Requirements below are proposed product wording or inherited safeguards, not a declaration that Core already provides them.

### A1 — Incomplete closure check or a private-obligation oracle

**Critical privacy / High integrity; plausible. Effect: retain A with an explicit source-admission condition.** A filtered personal task list can report no remaining work while hidden obligations remain. A privileged full scan can then fail the close and reveal that hidden work exists. Omitting details or returning a generic error alone does not hide a success-versus-failure distinction. Conversely, treating every independent financial/service obligation as a blocker confuses cultivation closure with ending the human relationship.

**Proposed clause:** “The canonical close command proves complete treatment of the obligations relevant to this exact development-work closure, using their owning source contracts rather than the caller's visible list. The actor must be independently authorized to observe the minimized closure-readiness and close-result projection; that projection gives no right to private obligation detail, counts, owners or reasons. Without that minimal result authority, do not admit a hidden-present/hidden-absent closure probe. Preserve the current work and give the same safe non-admission outcome through the existing authorized source-owner path.”

“Complete treatment” must be defined by the source: an obligation can be satisfied, explicitly disposed of by its authorized owner, or continue in a proved authorized home where the accepted closure contract allows it. It is not a fabricated count of zero across the Party, and no P27 actor receives cancellation/transfer power over another owner. A timeout, partially paginated scan, missing source adapter or indeterminate owner result is not proof of closure readiness.

**Proof:** two otherwise identical worlds with hidden closure-relevant work present/absent and an actor lacking readiness-result authority produce indistinguishable non-admission; an actor with explicitly permitted readiness authority can receive only that exact minimal outcome, never hidden task detail. Test visible-empty/private-outstanding, independent finance/service work that remains valid, a lost source response, a new obligation admitted during closure and partial page failure. No hidden count or source type leaks through UI, HTTP, timing, logs, CSV or retry.

**Owner/product boundary:** defining the exact minimized P27 readiness/result projection and which owner dispositions permit close is necessary source qualification. It is not inherited merely from the word Close or P21's precedent. Do not introduce an approval queue for every close, a general read-all capability or a universal obligation service.

### A2 — Permission to close becomes permission to mutate another source

**Critical authority / High integrity; likely if a generic task or Party status is reused. Effect: preserve the narrow decision.** Closing a journey is not Party archive/deceased/inactive, Ministry Assignment retirement, cancellation of every linked task, revocation of all access, a financial stop or a contact restriction. An independent task can remain owed even when its visible link points to closed cultivation.

**Proposed clause:** “Only the qualified P27 source command closes the selected current development work. It neither changes Party or ministry lifecycle nor cancels, transfers, satisfies or suppresses separately owned asks, tasks, commitments, service, money or contact-permission facts. Any deliberately included consequence requires its own current owner authority and accepted outcome. Surviving obligations retain a useful, authorized current home and responsibility.”

**Proof:** closer with no finance/task-reassignment/care authority; exact source task versus task shared with another obligation; active promise or receipt work linked to closure; unchanged Party/ministry/Support Binding; invalid new owner or different entity. No all-Party task sweep or hidden cascade. A task that ends because it is no longer required is not recorded as completed human work.

### A3 — Stale close, combined retarget or receipt replay bypasses current authority

**Critical; plausible under ordinary concurrent edits. Effect: inherited P12/current-source safeguard.** A valid preview can be followed by another person's new promise, handoff, grant revocation or source correction. A permitted old row must not be moved into a different tenant/entity/purpose/owner while closing. Replaying a once-valid receipt can disclose history after access loss or overwrite the UI with old state.

**Proposed clause:** “Derive actor, Tenant and applicable human/service/operator context from trusted authority. Validate exact journey/purpose/ministry scope, current capability, door-declared processing purpose, classification/residency, field/history floor and authorization epoch at each consequential boundary. Close against the reviewed current source/obligation/responsibility versions; fence stale authority and concurrent obligation admission. Atomically preserve source outcome, permitted history, receipt and required local effects. A replay reveals its original outcome only through current result authorization and separately returns current authorized source state; it never reruns an old close.”

Do not invent a new Legal Entity column on the tenant-owned Party. Enforce entity isolation where the source is entity-bearing; explicit multi-entity authority inside one active Tenant remains valid. Deferred execution preserves and revalidates the applicable originating human action context or its expressly admitted service authority; it cannot borrow the current lead's identity or treat a departed initiator's session as perpetual delegation.

**Proof:** close versus new obligation, handoff, reschedule, revoke, source correction and duplicate close, in both commit orders; unrelated grants change without incorrectly retargeting the command; forged tenant/actor; old-row allowed/new-row forbidden; response loss and replay after revocation. Failure leaves truthful source work recoverable, not partially closed. UI-disabled controls are not the boundary.

### A4 — History retention is mistaken for continuing disclosure

**Critical privacy; plausible. Effect: clarify “keeping history.”** The former lead or creator may lose current source access. A new lead may have metadata but not old private content. Ending active responsibility may change a source-bound grant, but independent grants do not automatically disappear. A historical export can leak even when active work correctly vanishes.

**Proposed clause:** “Closure preserves lawful source history without granting or extending access. Every historical read, list/count, search, deep link, export, audit view, generated document, cache/refetch and AI-derived projection applies its current principal/context, purpose, field/history floor and source permissions. Former responsibility, authorship, a closed label, receipt possession or a new assignment does not confer historical access. Ending a source-bound grant follows its owning contract; independent current grants remain independently evaluated.”

**Proof:** former lead with revoked access; former lead with independently surviving read; new lead with a prospective history floor; dual staff/missionary context; source metadata allowed but private notes withheld; changed alias/location classification; source closure followed by export/cache/realtime refetch. Count/filter/group/search predicates must not reveal hidden rows. No broad donor, church or missionary visibility is created and the P9/P28 interaction-sharing question remains separate.

### A5 — “Keep history” becomes permanent sensitive text or destructive cascade

**High; likely reuse trap with generic audit/cascade patterns. Effect: inherited retention boundary, not a new retention duration.** Closure evidence needs truthful cause/order/outcome, but private reasons and complete obligation snapshots should not become immortal logs. Conversely, deleting a task or actor can erase the only record of what was accepted. Closure is neither erasure nor a lawful-retention hold.

**Proposed clause:** “Preserve the minimum permitted structural evidence of the source close, actual actor/cause, accepted outcome and surviving-obligation references under owner retention. Do not copy private bodies, names, care details or readiness manifests into unrestricted/permanent audit. Ordinary close/archive/delete cannot silently cascade away required evidence. Authorized redaction, anonymization, erasure and retention still apply to protected content, historical projections, exports, indexes, caches and backups.”

**Proof:** actor anonymized; deleted Party/task; private reason redacted; restricted-ministry name/location changes; old error/debug/audit/export retains only allowed data; relational evidence remains valid where lawful without preserving unnecessary identifying labels. Test ordinary close separately from an explicitly authorized retention operation. Neither append-only evidence nor checksum storage is a substitute for lawful content disposal.

### A6 — Closure races with stale review attention or rewrites delivery truth

**High; plausible. Effect: inherit D6/P6/P17, no new channel.** A close can occur while a due worker is preparing or publishing review attention. Task disappearance alone does not cancel the source's notification applicability. Blanket cancellation of every message associated with the Party can wrongly stop independent service. For later external channels or unrelated existing communications, already submitted bytes cannot be recalled or retargeted.

**Proposed clause:** “The accepted close ends only the task/review/notification applicability belonging to that exact source scope and generation. Fence unadmitted or definitely unstarted obsolete attention; preserve truthful source-ended history under current permission and retention. Do not mark it read or reviewed, transfer engagement, retarget an immutable recipient or promise recall. Independently owed communication continues under its owner. If an external request may already have crossed its submission boundary, preserve and reconcile its frozen outcome under P6/P17 rather than replaying or falsely cancelling it.”

D6 delivery remains internal-only. An inert old link can show current authorized source state; it cannot close/reopen/reassign via GET, and notification opening never performs the source transition. A closed review's P17 Recent window is not an authorization extension or P27 history-retention policy.

**Proof:** close before due release, release before close, lease expiry with old worker alive, reschedule plus close, stale notification link, current authorization loss before render, already accepted/indeterminate external source event unrelated to D6. Verify one exact closure and no new obsolete active attention, no false delivery recall, no cancellation of independent service and no resurrection on restored access.

### A7 — RLS, raw grants or privileged maintenance bypass source closure

**Critical; plausible adoption risk, not an observed deployed exploit. Effect: engineering acceptance floor.** Browser grants are already revoked on the inspected task tables, but the service role can still issue generic status/delete writes. The current CRM action label, route role or task ownership alone cannot establish P27 close permission or complete owner-authorized obligation treatment.

**Proposed clause:** “Expose close only through the qualified source command and enforce its resulting-state invariants on every admitted writer: API, RPC, job, bulk/import, AI, repair and maintenance. Keep raw grants least-privilege; prove exact tenant/source relationships, immutable ownership/provenance, source revision and current authorization. If client mutations remain admitted, their effective old-row and resulting-row policies must provide equivalent scope protection. Service-role and definer paths independently enforce the same source authority.”

**Database precision:** `USING` governs old-row visibility/eligibility and `WITH CHECK` proposed rows; applicable policies can inherit `USING` when `WITH CHECK` is omitted. Omission alone is not a vulnerability. Permissive policies OR together; grants are separate from RLS; table owners normally bypass unless forced and superuser/BYPASSRLS remains privileged. `SECURITY DEFINER` uses owner authority, not an unconditional bypass by definition. Check safe search path, actual EXECUTE/schema grants and effective role. Unique/FK checks can reveal existence, so raw constraint errors do not establish a nonrevealing readiness result. Cross-row/current-permission conditions belong in qualified transactional execution, not mutable cross-row `CHECK` assumptions.

**Proof:** actual-catalog plus real-role old/new row tests for SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/UPSERT/RETURNING/RPC/view, a bypassing generic task mutation, wrong-tenant/entity reference, alternate permissive policy, hostile actor field and service repair. Do not restore browser grants merely to make a legacy UI work. No global schema redesign or second permission engine is required.

### A8 — Restore or reopening revives closed effects and obsolete access

**High integrity / Critical privacy; plausible. Effect: inherited restore safeguard; later-work identity remains open.** A backup predating closure can make old reminders/current workload appear again; one predating revocation/redaction can restore prohibited history. A future deliberate restart might then revive old engagement, grant lineage or consumed effects if implemented as snapshot rollback.

**Proposed clause:** “Before restored readers or workers activate, reconcile authoritative close/consumed-effect evidence and current revocation, redaction, expiry and source revisions. If current fences cannot be recovered, hold affected presentation/execution; do not guess from the old snapshot. A later deliberate return follows its separately accepted source command and current authority, preserves earlier actual outcomes and cannot revive old permissions, erased content or consumed notification effects.”

**Proof:** restore from before close, permission loss, redaction, task/review completion and accepted notification; N/N-1 workers after rollback; delayed pre-close event; duplicate import. Demonstrate no auto-restart or backfilled unread history and lawful historical evidence remains readable only where currently permitted. This clause chooses neither same-enrollment reopening nor a linked successor, and does not decide concurrent-purpose uniqueness.

## Recommended bounded amendment and remaining source qualification

“Close this current development work only through its authorized source action, after complete owner-qualified treatment of closure-relevant obligations. Give the actor only the independently permitted closure-readiness/result projection, never private obligation detail by implication. Closing removes this work from the current development workload and ends its own applicable review attention; it does not dispose of independent obligations, alter identity/money/contact permissions or grant history access. Retain lawful source history under current permission and retention, with fenced retries and restoration. Later work requires a separately authorized deliberate action under the lifetime rule still to be decided.”

The minimal readiness/result projection and allowed obligation dispositions need explicit P27/P12/source-owner qualification before activation; they are not a hidden read-all grant. Use existing authoritative source commands and shared-work/notification contracts. There is no need for a new generic approval queue, an all-domain obligation database, a new role or a second audit/notification platform. The later same-enrollment versus successor question remains open.
