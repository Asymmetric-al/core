## Concrete concerns and inherited requirements

Likelihood is conditional engineering judgment, not observed production frequency.

**O1 — “No blockers” becomes unrestricted mutation. Critical; plausible if the
UI decision is mistaken for authority.** Preserve exact P12 action, source scope,
field/history/result authority and current eligible target. Configuration power
does not imply stage-edit power. **Clause:** “A removes additional tenant-defined
information gates only; every accepted source safeguard still applies.”
**Proof:** authorized ordinary update succeeds without optional explanation;
unauthorized, wrong-scope and retired fresh targets fail with permitted results.

**O2 — Required information reappears through another form. High; likely if a
whole Party editor is reused.** P11 E3 already forbids untouched required fields
blocking unrelated saves. **Clause:** “Do not make a P27 stage update submit or
validate unrelated custom-field/profile data. Separately requested source writes
retain their own validation.” **Proof:** missing unrelated required field does
not block stage-only work; an actually edited governed field still receives its
proper validation. No forged filler, default or unknown-as-complete value.

**O3 — Next-action discipline becomes optional or generates duplicate chores.
High; plausible in either direction.** P27:4091 and D3:42–44 control.
**Clause:** “Active work continues to have appropriate dated shared work or an
owned actionable exception; applicable existing work may satisfy that obligation.
A stage edit does not automatically require a new task or erase other due work.”
**Proof:** preserve an appropriate existing task; expose missing accountability;
do not represent a missing task as healthy or create a second task merely to
complete the form. D11 does not decide every stage-transition/work-remediation
transaction or authorize generic task completion.

**O4 — Guidance becomes executable policy. High; plausible if text or metadata
is interpreted as validation.** D10 R01/R05 and Q11 select explanatory guidance.
**Clause:** “Guidance cannot become a blocking checklist, required acknowledgment,
mandatory explanation or inferred permission rule.” **Proof:** blank optional
explanation does not prevent an otherwise valid change; words such as “must” in
guidance do not execute rules. Do not relabel tenant preferences as platform safety
checks to rebuild B. A real mandatory requirement must identify its accepted owner.

**O5 — Reopening is misclassified as fresh stage entry. High; likely with generic
form validation.** D9/D10 explicitly distinguish retention from selection.
**Clause:** “Unchanged historical/retired stage on an unrelated permitted edit or
return requires no fresh information, assessment or replacement.” **Proof:** D8
return retains the latest authoritative stage and unknown date; another actor's
newer edit is not overwritten. Fresh retired selection still fails. Historical
correction/import needs its separately qualified source operation.

**O6 — Easier updates weaken history or concurrent acceptance. High; plausible
with direct board writes.** Original request:109 and D10 R09 preserve concurrency.
**Clause:** “Use the source's expected-version update and current target eligibility;
accepted history is attributable and conflicts are truthful.” **Proof:** competing
stage edits, retirement versus selection and a lost response cannot silently
overwrite newer truth or duplicate an accepted effect. Ordinary config edits and
reopening cannot fabricate assessment or contact timestamps. Exact physical
transaction design remains implementation work, not a new policy engine.

**O7 — Stage implies another owner's outcome. High/Critical; plausible with legacy
MPD labels.** D1–D10 retain source separation. **Clause:** “Selecting a stage does
not itself establish participation, donor agreement, gift, consent, task completion
or closure.” **Proof:** “Financial Partner,” “Never Ask” or “Closed” labels do not
mutate those facts. Existing contact restrictions apply to relevant contact/ask
actions; they are not a blanket prerequisite for recording internal understanding.

## DON-14: apparent conflict resolved

[DON-14:1651–1677](../../../../prds/workflow-studio/07-core-workflow-catalog.md:1651)
triggers when the development owner **accepts** enrollment or stage transition.
“Require relevant information” is a subsequent coordination step; editable
parameters are allowed preset, cadence and owner. Activation is disabled and
the exact source binding unresolved. The recipe neither defines a native
tenant-configured pre-save requirement nor disappears under D11 A.

**O8 — Optional Studio reverses source acceptance. High; plausible if an unbound
catalog phrase is treated as a live gate.** The generated
[Studio OpenSpec:19–27](../../../../../openspec/changes/add-workflow-studio-program-contracts/specs/workflow-studio/spec.md:19)
permits only registered source-authorized operations; [runtime authority:79–87](../../../../../openspec/changes/add-workflow-studio-program-contracts/specs/workflow-studio/spec.md:79)
remains current. [Owner qualification:39–47](../../../../prds/workflow-studio/12-owner-bindings-and-decisions.md:39)
blocks an unqualified capability, not unrelated native work.

**Clause:** “DON-14 may coordinate separately qualified information work after
source acceptance; it cannot introduce tenant-configured blocking information
requirements into the native P27 stage-change command or retrospectively invalidate
an accepted stage.” **Proof:** native updates work with Studio absent; missing
information affects only the exact qualified downstream operation; retries retain
source acceptance and one intended effect. T-DON-14-01/02/03 remain
[specified, not executed](../../../../prds/workflow-studio/10-acceptance-scenarios.md:1295).

## Scope, alternative and remaining proof

B remains the strongest alternative for standardized documentation but was
explicitly rejected. Do not preserve a dormant gate builder, per-stage ACL,
waiver queue or forced checklist “for flexibility.” Human-readable guidance,
existing source commands and honest feedback are sufficient for A.

A does not decide arbitrary skip/backward movement, correction mechanics,
initial/default stage, preset switching, automatic transitions or ask completion.
Those must not be silently approved or prohibited through this review. D2's
qualified identity correction and D7's closure safeguards also remain intact.

This review checks governing intent and evidence boundaries. Actual P27 command,
permission, race, accessibility and representative ministry-UX proof remains
required before release; none is claimed here. Root's consolidated review should
record **Accept A**, with inherited safeguards, unless another reviewer establishes
a genuinely new product amendment requiring founder confirmation.
