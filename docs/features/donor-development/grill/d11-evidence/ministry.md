# D11 evidence — stage guidance and staff judgment

Research/retrieval: **2026-10-10 UTC**. The founder selected A: organization
guidance, without additional organization-configured information requirements
blocking a stage change. Existing source safeguards remain mandatory. This is
research and proposed clarification, not new stage definitions, permission,
automation, implementation or ratification. No vendor application, agency-user
study or live record was exercised.

## Recommendation and strongest alternative

Keep A. Stage guidance should help an authorized person record an honest current
understanding without inventing a meeting date, donor statement or explanatory
essay to satisfy a form. This is particularly relevant to inherited records and
shared work whose complete history may not be available to the current actor.
That relevance is product reasoning, not a measured prevalence claim.

The strongest B alternative is consistent documented context for selected stages,
helping another worker understand an assessment and reducing ambiguous handoffs.
Its weakness is that a nonempty field establishes neither accuracy nor permission
to collect its contents. No source reviewed demonstrates that compulsory
stage-entry fields improve Christian missions outcomes. A still needs clear
definitions, truthful provenance, understandable history and existing accountability;
it does not mean that every stage choice is equally accurate.

## Concerns, exact clauses and acceptance checks

Severity is consequence; likelihood is qualitative design risk, not a measured
failure rate. Examples are illustrative, not claimed missionary workflows.

### M1 — A selected stage becomes false evidence

**High severity; plausible.** A label or guidance sentence can be mistaken for
proof of a conversation, prayer commitment, financial agreement or permission.

**Clause:** “Guidance and a stage selection record staff's understanding. They
do not attest that contact occurred, the supporter agreed, a gift is committed,
or contact consent exists. Existing source facts remain independently required
by their owners.”

**AC:** Change a stage with no new encounter, consent or gift evidence. None of
those facts, their dates, financial totals or task outcomes changes. Unauthorized
contact remains unauthorized. Stage history still records the accepted edit.

### M2 — Incomplete history encourages fabricated certainty

**High severity; plausible.** An incoming worker may know enough to correct a
stage without knowing when an earlier conversation occurred. Retained values
may be old, imported or retired; requiring reconstruction produces false detail.

**Clause:** “Do not require invented historical facts, an arbitrary explanation
or a fresh confirmation to save an otherwise permitted stage update. Preserve
unknown provenance as unknown. Reopening under D9 does not become stage entry or
evidence of a new assessment.”

**AC:** Reopen with an old retired stage and unknown historical date; preserve
both meanings. A later permitted edit records its actual provenance without
backfilling contact dates or rewriting the earlier assessment. This selects no
new Unknown stage, confidence score or mandatory reason field.

### M3 — Prayer partners face a financial checklist

**High severity; plausible.** Sales-oriented guidance may suggest that meaningful
progress requires an ask amount, pledged frequency or conversion to giving.

**Clause:** “Ordinary partnership guidance must support nonfinancial partnership
without compulsory financial solicitation or invented financial values. A stage
does not replace separately recorded participation or measure supporter worth.”

**AC:** Record an appropriate stage for a prayer-only partner without amount,
financial ask or giving commitment; create none implicitly. A church supporting
multiple ministries retains each effort's distinct context and authority.

### M4 — Helpful guidance becomes a disguised blocking rule

**Medium severity; plausible.** A compulsory acknowledgement, required optional
note, mandatory task, warning loop or manager sign-off would restore B indirectly.
Conversely, invisible guidance would make A ineffective for newer staff.

**Clause:** “Present concise stage meaning where staff make the choice, with
additional help available without interrupting the ordinary action. Guidance
alone creates no required field, acknowledgement, task, approval or transition
rule. A missing optional note must not block an otherwise valid save.”

**AC:** New and experienced staff can find the definition and save through an
ordinary accessible control without checking a ceremonial box. Keyboard/mobile
paths offer the same guidance. Network failure shows truthful save status rather
than prompting fabricated duplicate documentation; offline support is not assumed.

### M5 — Staff judgment is mistaken for private rules or broader access

**High severity; plausible.** Coaching, handoff or ownership could be treated as
authority to change stages, see restricted notes or redefine organization meaning.

**Clause:** “Staff judgment operates within the approved stage's meaning and
current P12 authority. Configuration, stage editing, responsibility and protected
context access remain distinct. No sensitive narrative is required to explain
a stage to people who cannot access that narrative's source.”

**AC:** An eligible replacement can understand permitted stage history without
receiving private pastoral/support notes. A coach or lead without stage-edit
authority cannot save. Guidance changes do not rewrite existing historical meaning
or invalidate earlier assessments retroactively.

## Focused empirical gap

Test the proposed guidance with actual agency staff and personally support-raising
missionaries before finalizing copy. Include a handoff with incomplete history,
a prayer partner and a church relationship. Ask users to explain what the stage
claims, what remains unknown, where an optional useful explanation belongs, and
whether a task or ask would represent the situation better. Check whether two
authorized users understand the same definition consistently and can correct an
assessment without unnecessary contact or sensitive disclosure.

These are proposed checks, not completed findings. No sample size, benefit,
error rate or universal missions practice has been established. D11 supplies no
reason to add a sales checklist, new workflow engine or empirical claim.
