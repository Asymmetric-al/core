## Highest-value findings

1. Phase 26 is ratified, and its later implementation graph exists. `docs/features/support-hub/phase26-spec-publication.md:3–17` records the Sep 15 original publication and says that task created no implementation-ticket graph. Current issue #1656 is OPEN, last updated 2026-09-15; its body explicitly ratifies D1–D40 and says implementation/runtime/provider/accessibility evidence remains required. But #1662–#1860 were subsequently published Sep 16; inspected relevant tickets remain OPEN. Do not turn the original publication sentence into a claim that no P26 implementation issues exist now.
2. A shared encounter is referenced, not copied. P26 explicitly refuses to promote `donor_activities`, generic timeline rows, `contact_ref` JSON, or polymorphic free-form IDs into a new universal interaction authority. P26 owns the Support need/brief/work; an actual relationship encounter remains with its owning source. That same encounter can lead to several independent Support needs. P27 must determine development encounter semantics and source ownership deliberately rather than treating Support intake as cultivation or making P28 a prerequisite.
3. Context is not permission or engagement. Linking a Party, adding a task, noting a support need, internal notes, changes to handlers, and provider callbacks do not become actual correspondence or last meaningful contact. Joint source permissions apply before joins/counts/paging; a copied brief cannot preserve material after source access expires.
4. The delivered shared task owner is the starting seam. Current P26 #1704 explicitly says #618/#1046 are separate read/source consumers, not missing general task prerequisites. Extend shared tasks with bounded source-purpose meaning; do not build another Support/P27 task engine.

## Open product decisions / conflicts to keep visible

1. **Enrollment purpose/lifetime (recommended first question):** the unit of planned development is a Party plus a named organizational purpose/relationship, not a global Party stage. Exactly what makes a second concurrent enrollment legitimate, who can open it, and when it ends/reopens are user decisions. Privacy sharing depends on those boundaries.
2. **Staff–missionary sharing:** no default decided. Determine which development context is collaborative source content and which remains staff-private; what the missionary can author/edit; shared encounter versus distinct purpose note; permitted summary/brief/attachment subsets; and source revocation/retention behavior. P3 already rules out general staff notes/scores on missionary surfaces. Any proposed widening must be an explicit P3/P10/P12 owner amendment, not a P27 convenience or a relationship grant.
3. **Meaningful interaction:** define completed conversation/meeting/two-way communication versus attempted call, sent message, automated receipt, record edit, Support request and private note. P26's fixed exclusions must remain; P27 can define its qualified development-purpose criterion and references without changing underlying message facts.
4. **Interaction owner gap:** a source-typed encounter contract is required where an actual development meeting/call is recorded. P26 does not authorize a universal generic interaction engine or establish one through legacy donor_activities. Inspect P9 exact communication/activity and manual-interaction reservations together with P27/P28 intended write model before deciding placement. Do not silently make Support the encounter owner.
5. **P10 old coarse roles vs P12:** use later owning P12 single-context/capabilities/strict floors. Assignment can be a qualifying scope input only under independently granted authority, never grant private notes/financial detail itself.
6. **Never-ask source seam:** user requirement is binding; this bounded source search did not establish an implemented canonical never-ask service. Before adding a P27 flag, trace accepted P9/P3 purpose/contact restrictions and P6 dispatch owner; distinguish no solicitation from authorized internal review/service. Missing evidence is unresolved, not permission to infer consent.
7. **No dependency trap:** do not gate native P27 on full P26 or P28. Consume exact qualified Support read/reference/task contracts when exposed; otherwise preserve owner-unavailable states and native development work, never copied fallback notes or hidden zero.

Recommendation for opening: use enrollment-purpose/lifetime decision first, then exact visibility and meaningful interaction. A donor representative and missionary may share one real meeting while retaining different responsibilities and authorized purpose notes. This supports one encounter and shared source semantics without assuming mutual disclosure.
