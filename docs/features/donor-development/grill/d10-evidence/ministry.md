# D10 evidence — flexible organization-approved stage lists

Research/retrieval: **2026-10-10 UTC**. Scope: the founder selected B, allowing
authorized Tenant configuration to add, retire and reorder stages. This appendix
assesses missions suitability and proposes safeguards; it does not ratify new
labels, required steps, transitions, automation or permissions. No vendor was
executed, agency user interviewed, or live configuration changed.

## Recommendation and strongest alternative

Honor B as bounded configuration of organization-approved presets on the existing
shared work model. An organization can need a meaningful distinction that renaming
an existing step cannot express. However, we have not empirically established
which extra distinctions Asym's missions customers need. Examples below are
illustrative tests, not claimed agency workflows or an approved stage catalog.

The strongest A alternative was supplied structures with editable wording and
guidance. It reduces configuration, training and reporting variation, and may be
enough where different terminology describes the same work. It is insufficient
when an organization genuinely needs to distinguish or combine different parts
of relationship development. B enables that choice; it does not oblige each
organization to customize or give every missionary a private pipeline.

## Material concerns and exact amendments

Severity describes consequence; likelihood is qualitative design risk, not an
observed frequency. D1–D9 and existing source ownership remain binding.

### M1 — Flexible stages become competing authorities

**High severity; plausible.** Legacy MPD vocabulary can turn a stage into a
financial pledge, contact restriction, completed call or assumed donor response.

**Amendment:** “A configured stage describes recorded development progress only.
Adding, selecting or reordering one cannot create participation, an ask outcome,
a performed task, consent, financial truth, access, closure or reopening. Those
facts retain their existing owners.”

Enforce effect boundaries, not a simplistic forbidden-word list. Guidance must
explain what a stage means; descriptive text is not executable business rules.
Reconcile legacy preset meanings before implementation rather than silently
adopting every existing MPD label as a mutually exclusive stage.

### M2 — Prayer partnership becomes an inferior financial stage

**High severity; plausible.** Placing prayer before financial support can imply
that a prayer-only partner is unfinished, less valuable or expected to give.
Sending-church responsibilities also need not coincide with a gift decision.

**Amendment:** “An ordinary partnership preset must permit meaningful nonfinancial
partnership without requiring a financial ask, gift or commitment to progress.
Stage position is not a ranking of supporters or evidence of their participation.”

Do not solve this by automatically creating a second purpose or stage list for
every participation type. D3's ordinary partnership boundary still applies.

### M3 — Presets fragment one relationship across staff and missionary views

**High severity; plausible.** Staff, missionary and church-oriented presets
serve different work contexts; they are not independent private copies of the
same effort. A church can simultaneously support several ministries and have
separate sending responsibilities.

**Amendment:** “Configuration applies to the appropriate organization-approved
preset and its source records. Authorized staff and missionary views of the same
effort show the same recorded stage meaning. A viewer's role or a Party being a
church does not silently switch its preset, rewrite history or duplicate work.”

Exact preset applicability, selection and new-preset creation remain separate
decisions. A single global church/donor status is not a shortcut.

### M4 — Retirement blocks D9 reopening or rewrites old meaning

**High severity; likely without explicit treatment.** Removing a stage from
selection can make existing work disappear from boards or force a fresh assessment
when an old effort reopens. Reusing a name or identifier can falsify history.

**Amendment:** “Retirement removes a stage from future selection under its
qualified preset policy, while retaining the meaning and visibility of existing
references. D9 reopening preserves the last recorded stage without forced
remapping or confirmation, including a retired historical value. A different
meaning cannot silently replace that historical meaning.”

Distinguish displaying a retained value from choosing it anew. No stage-change
event, refreshed assessment date or compulsory task follows from retirement,
renaming or reopening. Definition migration mechanics remain to be qualified.

### M5 — Configuration rights become implied missionary or manager authority

**High severity; plausible.** Owning partnership work, coaching a missionary or
holding a vendor-style Admin title does not establish Core configuration rights.
Conversely, qualified delegation should not require a newly invented hierarchy.

**Amendment:** “Use P12's current scoped authority for organization configuration.
Reading or using a preset and editing it are distinct operations. Responsibility,
missionary status, church relationship and configuration access grant no further
data access or stage-edit authority by implication.”

No new permission role, approval board, headquarters-only rule or blanket right
for every missionary is established by B.

### M6 — Additional steps or ordering imply mandatory workflow and comparable scores

**Medium severity; plausible.** A board order can be mistaken for required
sequential passage, probability of giving, or a shared measure across unrelated
presets. An appointment/task can be promoted into a redundant stage.

**Amendment:** “D10 permits list configuration, not mandatory passage through
every step, transition gates, stage timers, financial probabilities or automatic
actions. Reordering is not progress. Reports must not equate differently defined
stages by label or position.”

For a proposed addition, establish the distinct development understanding it
expresses and why an existing ask, task or participation fact is insufficient.
This is a design/usability criterion, not a new case-by-case approval ceremony.

## Illustrative acceptance and pilot cases

- One organization uses “Exploring partnership” for a meaning another calls
  “Getting acquainted”: wording changes preserve meaning/history. These labels
  are examples, not supplied defaults.
- A church-oriented preset distinguishes an initial discussion from considering
  sustained collaboration: test whether that distinction helps staff understand
  the relationship, while a scheduled meeting remains a task and a particular
  budget request remains an ask. No published source establishes these stages.
- A prayer-only supporter has an active, meaningful partnership: no financial
  gate, downgrade, fabricated commitment or duplicate purpose results.
- Retire and reorder a stage used by active and closed work; then reopen the
  closed effort under D9. Both remain findable and interpretable, without a
  reassessment, silent migration, changed recorded date or triggered outreach.
- Staff and an authorized missionary view the same effort; a church supports
  two ministries. Each source record keeps its own correct preset/meaning and
  permissions; changing one definition does not grant access or alter the other.
- Exercise an authorized delegated configuration edit and an unauthorized
  missionary/lead attempt through the same source boundary. Preserve legitimate
  delegation without granting new authority from responsibility.
- Test supplied presets and a justified customization with actual agency staff
  and support-raising missionaries. Ask them to distinguish stage from next task,
  ask, prayer participation and contact permission. Include nonfinancial and
  church relationships. No preference, sample size, success threshold or measured
  outcome has been established; final labels and extra steps remain unselected.
