## Why this is the next question

D1 settled a continuing purpose-specific journey. D2 settled the identified
ministry context for ordinary missionary support. Neither decides whether
financial partnership, prayer partnership and a special ask are distinct
continuing purposes or aspects of the same partnership.

This precedes enrollment entry: both deliberate human entry and any separately
ratified source-qualified rule need to know whether to join existing work or
start another journey. Internal enrollment and communication permission are
separate; an eventual enrollment rule cannot turn gifts/referrals into consent.

The adopted roadmap requires a next shared task or owned actionable exception
for every active enrollment (`roadmap.md:4091`). Duplicate journeys therefore
create real obligations. Native/workflow effects must also converge on one
intended source-purpose effect (`roadmap.md:4103`; WS-AT-044). More records do not
by themselves provide better donor care.

## Proposed single founder question

**When the same supporter already has a journey with the same ministry, what
should justify another concurrent journey?**

Illustrative case: a church prays for the ministry, gives regularly and is asked
about a one-time equipment need.

**A — one ordinary partnership journey; separate only genuinely independent
purposes (recommended).** Prayer and financial participation can coexist in that
journey; the equipment request is a particular ask. A second journey requires a
distinct continuing relationship purpose that needs independently managed
progress, follow-through and ending. Another ask, annual renewal, appeal,
representative or currency alone is not enough. Different responsible people are
not required for legitimate distinct purposes.

**B — normally separate prayer and financial partnership journeys.** Each has
its own progression and accountable next action, even for the same supporter
and ministry. This provides more independent tracking when an organization
intentionally manages those relationships separately, at the cost of more
records and coordination of conversations and follow-up. It still cannot grant
access, infer consent or duplicate the same intended external effect.

The recommendation is product judgment: A better preserves the ordinary
relationship, while retaining D1's legitimate distinct purposes. It does not
make an independently tracked ask into a second journey merely because the ask
has its own status and tasks. Nor does it use prayer partnership as a preliminary
financial stage or a reason to keep asking for money.

## Boundaries to preserve after the answer

- Do not reopen D1 as one journey per annual ask or D2 as a person/profile target.
- Financial and nonfinancial participation must not imply consent, spiritual
  ranking, automatic stage advancement, or personal worth.
- Keep source facts and permitted human context independently meaningful;
  do not invent prayer-participation evidence from giving or communications.
- Different staff alone do not prove a different purpose; distinct purposes may
  also share the same responsible person.
- No new manager-approval bureaucracy, configurable purpose engine or prescribed
  schema follows from A. It supplies a practical product boundary whose exact
  admission and duplicate-handling behavior must be groomed next.
- A/B concerns ordinary support of the same identified ministry. Other agency,
  church or project relationships keep their separately admitted sources and
  purposes; this question does not force them into that ministry record.
- The founder selected A, received the full adversarial review and explicitly
  ratified the tightened D3. This question note retains the original alternatives
  as decision provenance; the ratified review supplies the final wording.
