# D12 evidence — direct stage selection in continuing ministry partnerships

Research/retrieval: **2026-10-10 UTC**. Founder selected A: authorized staff may
choose an appropriate currently available stage directly, including skipping or
returning to an earlier displayed position. Earlier decisions remain binding.
This appendix supplies evidence and proposed safeguards, not final stage labels,
reporting formulas, automation, implementation or new ratification. No vendor
application or agency-user study was executed.

## Finding and strongest alternative

Keep A. A continuing ministry partnership is not necessarily a one-way sequence
of staff actions. Direct selection lets a record reflect staff's present
understanding without pretending that intermediate stages were visited. This is
product reasoning supported by the distinctions below, not evidence that every
missionary or agency prefers unrestricted stage movement.

B's strongest case is consistent process: an organization can prevent a move
that bypasses work it considers necessary. However, a stage restriction cannot
prove that the work happened, and can prevent a truthful assessment or encourage
ceremonial intermediate changes. Core's existing source requirements protect
actual obligations independently; D11 guidance supports shared interpretation.
Do not reintroduce B through mandatory explanations, approvals, intermediate
tasks or a ban on moving against display order.

## Material concerns and precise clauses

Severity concerns consequence; likelihood is qualitative design risk, not an
observed rate. These clauses preserve A rather than create a transition engine.

### M1 — A direct move fabricates intermediate history

**High severity; plausible.** A board or report may infer that skipping displayed
steps means staff passed through them or completed their associated activities.

**Clause:** “Record only the actual accepted source-to-target stage change.
Do not manufacture intermediate visits, timestamps, tasks, conversations or
supporter decisions. D10's presentation order is not a required route.”

Preserve the previous assessment as history. Updating today's understanding is
not permission to backdate or correct what was recorded about the past; that
separate correction policy remains unresolved by D12.

### M2 — Returning to a stage is reported as regression or new acquisition

**High severity; plausible.** Freely revisitable stages break assumptions behind
linear funnels. A return can reflect new understanding, rather than poor staff
performance or a weaker supporter relationship.

**Clause:** “A stage revisit is another recorded assessment, not a new partner,
gift, completed activity or repeated acquisition unless that separately owned
fact exists. Display position alone cannot imply progress, regression or value.”

Reports must distinguish actual visits and disclose the basis for any duration
or conversion measure. Do not silently combine a current stay with earlier
stays, infer a visit to skipped stages, or treat a lifecycle reopening as stage
re-entry when D9 retained the same assessment. Exact paused/closed-time handling,
re-entry denominators and duration formulas remain separate reporting decisions.

### M3 — Financial/prayer changes trigger automatic stage movement

**High severity; plausible.** A special appeal, financial interruption or renewed
gift may be mistaken for an entire partnership beginning, failing or restarting.

**Clause:** “A stage move neither changes nor substitutes for participation,
giving, commitments, ask outcomes, contact eligibility or lifecycle. Those facts
do not automatically select a stage under D12. Ordinary nonfinancial partnership
does not require a financial step or carry an inferior rank.”

D12 does not authorize new outreach, workflow effects or cancellation of existing
promises. Any affected work retains its owner's disposition requirements.

### M4 — One person's context overwrites a church or team's separate work

**High severity; plausible.** A church can be involved with several ministries;
a team member or accountable lead may change without the supporter changing.

**Clause:** “Apply the deliberate stage edit to its exact authorized effort and
approved preset. Staff and missionary views consume the same source assessment.
Changing a church contact, ministry participant or lead does not itself reset,
advance or copy stages across efforts.”

D2's affected-work review rules and current P12 authority remain binding. Direct
selection grants no wider source visibility or right to record others' judgments.

### M5 — Direct selection becomes forced reassessment or unavailable choice

**Medium severity; plausible.** Retired values, changed presets and imperfect
history can tempt a forced reset, even though D9 permits retaining the old stage.

**Clause:** “Offer currently eligible targets through the existing stage command.
Preserve a retained historical stage when reopening; do not force an edit merely
because it is retired or old. A later deliberate change keeps unknown historical
provenance unknown and does not overwrite a concurrent accepted assessment.”

A does not permit choosing any Tenant's stage, selecting an ineligible retired
value anew, or switching presets implicitly. Initial-stage selection, preset
switching and backdated correction remain outside D12.

## Illustrative pressure tests and acceptance criteria

These are invented test situations, clearly separated from the published cases.

- An authorized worker discovers that an existing church record understates
  the relationship. Choose the appropriate available stage directly; history
  contains one accepted change and no fabricated calls or intermediate visits.
- A later discussion causes staff to reassess an effort at an earlier displayed
  stage. Preserve the earlier assessment; do not label the supporter a failure,
  demand a special approval or erase ongoing commitments.
- An established partner receives a special equipment ask. Its ask state can
  change without resetting the ongoing partnership. A declined ask does not
  automatically move the relationship backward.
- Financial giving pauses while documented prayer partnership continues. Do not
  infer declining commitment, stop permitted updates or force a financial stage.
- A church supports two ministries, one staffed by a team. Edit one effort;
  preserve the other's stage and permissions. Team membership change alone emits
  no stage change and does not transfer personal trust.
- Revisit a previously used stage, then reopen later without a stage edit.
  Reports distinguish the real revisit from unchanged reopening and do not count
  either as a new donor or gift. Unknown earlier dates remain unknown.
- Retire/reorder a stage during editing and return from a slow save: recheck the
  eligible current target and source version; return truthful conflict/success
  without silent remapping, repeated events or compulsory extra explanation.

Before qualifying the final product copy and interaction for release, test these situations with actual agency staff and
support-raising missionaries, including church and prayer partnerships. Ask what
the move asserts and whether a task, ask or participation fact would be more
accurate. No practitioner feedback, adoption rate, financial effect or validated
preference for A is claimed by this research.
