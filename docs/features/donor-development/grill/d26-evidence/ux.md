# D26 UX evidence — voluntary shared partnership history

2026-10-10 UTC / 2026-10-11 Asia/Bangkok. D26 A is selected, with the user's explicit distinction that missionaries should not be controlled as staff. This is source inspection and proposed UX, not an implemented projection, an observed ministry workflow or a validated prototype. Source HEAD: `225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`.

## Recommendation and concrete experience

Keep A as a helpful shared memory that people choose to contribute to. **Shared by default describes a new eligible update deliberately written in the shared-partnership composer; it does not mean compulsory logging, automatic publication of personal notes or staff supervision of missionary activity.** Do not require a note to view a partner, receive ordinary support, change an otherwise permitted stage or finish unrelated work. No activity quotas, streaks, missing-log warnings, compliance board or performance judgment based on silence belongs to this choice.

Illustrative flow: in a particular church/ministry partnership, a missionary chooses **Add partnership update**, sees the partnership name and an audience explanation before typing, writes “The committee will discuss next year's support in November,” and saves once. A permitted staff collaborator sees the same qualified update with its author and date before preparing their conversation. There is no second share step, duplicated call log, required next task, staff approval or donor message. A missionary who does not add an update is not marked noncompliant.

One calm composer needs a persistent **Partnership update** label, short audience line and normal **Save update** action. For example, “Shared with people authorized for this partnership,” with **Who can see this?** exposing understandable current scope and permitted names/context. The wording must make staff/missionary sharing apparent without promising an immutable roster. A long recipient selector or mandatory audience acknowledgment on every save is unnecessary. Display a more specific audience explanation where the qualified source can support it. These are proposed labels, not a mandated widget or new permission model.

The strongest B alternative keeps each eligible update within its original audience until deliberately shared. It gives authors a stronger moment of disclosure control but adds a step and can leave collaborators without useful context. A is preferable only in this explicitly shared context with private/sensitive content kept separate. That is product judgment, not measured ministry preference. [Q26](../phase27-q26-shared-partnership-updates.md):33–77 bounds the new-content decision; :81–96 identifies the owner reconciliation that still precedes activation.

## Exact proposed interaction clauses

**UX01 — Voluntary collaboration, consistent safety.** Offer the shared history where it helps an authorized partnership conversation. Let missionaries decide whether and what eligible context to contribute. Absence means no visible update recorded, not no relationship work or failure to meet a quota. Do not create overdue “log your meeting” tasks, manager review, required stages or individual activity rankings from this choice. Staff's independently governed work and everyone's source/privacy constraints remain intact; voluntary contribution does not waive permission checks.

**UX02 — Audience is visible before disclosure.** The new shared composer identifies the exact partnership and clearly says it is shared with the qualified staff/missionary audience before the user enters or submits content. Associate that explanation with the input for assistive technology and keep it visible while typing. Later detail shows the current permitted audience meaning too. Display allowed names when useful, with readable distinguishing context for similar names; do not leak a restricted collaborator directory or rely on raw IDs. Explain that access follows current partnership permissions, so today's named preview is not a promise that readers can never change. Do not fall back to all staff, the tenant or another partnership when audience resolution fails.

**UX03 — Private work stays separate without a new journaling system.** The shared composer is not the generic personal note box. Existing private notes, imported history, Support/care bodies, restricted finance and unrelated ministry relationships remain outside it. Where a qualified private-note action exists, make that separate destination easy to identify, with its actual audience; never promise “Only you” merely because a field is called Internal Notes. If no such action is qualified, let the user cancel without losing control of unsent text; do not invent an insecure personal notebook or save under a different scope. A private-to-shared move needs an explicit authorized disclosure action and content review, not opening a shared tab or attaching an appeal. Do not add a tenant toggle that turns old notes into shared history.

**UX04 — Small form, truthful event facts.** A useful text update does not need a separate title, compulsory activity type, exact meeting time, stage, outcome, next-step date or supervisor acknowledgment. Retain optional source-supported encounter context without forcing a made-up time. Show **Added on** separately from **Interaction on** when they differ; a later recollection must not claim the interaction happened at save time. Calendar-only dates keep their calendar meaning across travel; actual instants display with clear locale/timezone context where relevant. Do not split or reorder people's names on a Western first/last-name assumption. The illustrative committee update is neither a donor commitment nor consent, meaningful-contact qualification or organizational approval.

**UX05 — Share useful context, not protected transcripts.** Brief guidance can say “Add context useful to this partnership; keep private or sensitive details in the appropriate private record.” Do not demand a privacy checkbox or invent an automatic sensitive-content classifier. A routine meeting can include health, pastoral, security, household or financial details; a routine-looking title does not make the body shareable. A limited development-purpose summary may refer to the actual encounter only where its content is itself authorized. It must not copy a restricted transcript or imply that the system proved the summary safe. No automatic attachment/email import, AI summary, donor send, task or financial change is created by saving an update.

**UX06 — Read one history without surveillance cues.** Present a readable chronological list with the qualified author, relevant date, short body and direct access to full permitted content. It may identify source-owned stage/task/Request events where independently allowed; do not require retyping them as notes. Separate automated operational events from person-authored updates so a save/receipt cannot masquerade as a conversation. Use an ordinary filter only if needed to make history usable; no activity score, red inactivity badge or shared-note quota. No-update empty copy can be “No shared updates yet” with an optional Add action, not “You haven't completed your reporting.” Never expose hidden entries through counts, blank redaction rows, hints or a “three private notes” badge unless that exact metadata is authorized.

**UX07 — Correct clearly; do not promise recall.** A permitted author/editor can reach the applicable source correction action from the entry. A normal typo correction need not become a new approval ceremony. Preserve qualified provenance and make a material correction intelligible to permitted readers; the source owns revision visibility and retention. An accidental overshare needs a direct source-qualified restriction/redaction/help path, with truthful pending/result feedback and no requirement to paste the sensitive body into a support ticket. Do not claim “unshared,” “deleted everywhere” or “never seen” before the source result, and never promise to retract already delivered email, exports, screenshots or memory. Wording and authority for erasure/retention come from the owner; this review creates no legal deletion guarantee. Restrictions must apply to future authorized projections, notifications, search, summaries and caches, without retaining a public correction that repeats the protected content.

**UX08 — New or departed collaborators are not authorship changes.** Joining a partnership exposes only history allowed by the current exact grant; the UI cannot imply automatic access to all past staff/missionary records. A departing missionary loses future access under source policy; historical authorship does not transfer to a current assignee. Display a permitted historical name/alias or neutral unavailable-identity label where required. Being able to read an entry does not itself grant editing, audience widening or access to other relationships. An already open page and its summaries must respond correctly to revocation; missing access must not be described as an empty history or proof that the author deleted it.

**UX09 — Drafts and uncertain saves remain under control.** Bind a draft to its partnership and intended audience. Switching record, tenant or acting role must not silently carry the text into a newly broader context. Preserve permitted unsent edits on ordinary errors; if discarding is necessary, explain the specific loss at that point, without an extra acknowledgment on every normal save. Do not upload keystrokes as shared content or silently persist sensitive drafts to shared browser storage. Pending submission keeps a stable text action such as **Saving update…**, stops duplicate activation and allows safe navigation/recovery without pretending closing the overlay canceled an accepted save. A lost reply is **Save outcome unknown** with recovery of the same submitted intent, not an invitation to produce another update. A late success changes only its own draft/result. A current audience/permission conflict preserves only what the user is still permitted to retain and offers refresh/correction; it must never retry into a broader audience.

**UX10 — Saved is different from delivered or read.** Confirm the accepted source update and show it in the permitted history or link to it if a filter hides it. Do not say “Everyone has been notified” from a save receipt. Notification failure must not cause another note to be created. Do not add default all-collaborator broadcasts, read receipts, mandatory reactions or reminders to acknowledge each update. Those would add communication/monitoring policy that A did not select.

## Presentation conditions and recovery

These are interface conditions, not a new workflow enum.

| Condition                              | Required meaning and next action                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eligible new shared draft              | Visible partnership/audience, one ordinary Save update, optional cancellation.                                                                |
| Existing private or restricted entry   | Retains qualified private treatment; no automatic sharing because it appears near the shared history.                                         |
| History is loading / fails             | Explicit loading / safe retry; neither means no shared history. Preserve already permitted context without exposing revoked content.          |
| No visible updates / no filter matches | Neutral empty history / change filter. No implication that ministry work did not occur.                                                       |
| Intended sharing context unavailable   | Explain unavailable shared action; no substitute tenant audience or personal-note write.                                                      |
| Save accepted / outcome unknown        | Permitted saved result / recover same operation. Neither asserts delivery or reading.                                                         |
| Entry corrected or restricted          | Current permitted version/outcome and qualified provenance; no disclosure through stale snippets or change summaries.                         |
| Access changes during reading/editing  | Refresh effective access, remove no-longer-permitted content and prevent stale submission; do not recast denial as deletion or zero activity. |

The actual implementation must pass these falsifiable checks:

1. **Voluntary contribution:** staff and missionary complete the illustrative coordination flow with one source update, no duplicate logging, forced acknowledgment, next-task requirement or compliance metric. Repeat with no update; ordinary permitted use remains available.
2. **Audience comprehension:** before saving, participants can identify the partnership, shared audience and private-note distinction without coaching. A permitted new reader and a departed reader get exactly current source/history access; today's named preview is never represented as permanent.
3. **No widening:** exercise private/imported/care/finance content, role changes, additional appeal links and identical supporter names across ministries. No automatic publication or unauthorized metadata appears in either app, notifications, search, exports, briefs or cached summaries.
4. **Context and concurrency:** test A→B draft switching, lost reply/retry, overlapping edits, late responses, new input during an earlier save, audience revocation and permission loss. One accepted intent has one result; newer drafts and provenance survive without last-response overwrites.
5. **Correction/recovery:** correct a typo, a material meeting fact and an accidental sensitive disclosure. The qualified source controls visibility/history; no blank-check edit authority, duplicate encounter or promised recall. Verify already delivered limits and future read/derivative suppression separately.
6. **Truthful dates/attribution:** show past, calendar-only and unknown encounter dates separately from save time; test travel across timezone/day boundaries, long/multiscript names, RTL and departed actors. A new assignee is never substituted as historical author.
7. **Accessible composed flow:** at 320 CSS pixels/equivalent zoom, light/dark themes, keyboard, touch and a screen reader, reach and understand audience help, body, Save/Cancel, correction and result. Persistent labels/error relationships work; focus is visible and restored; a mobile keyboard does not cover actions. Pending/success announcements are intelligible without announcing every timeline row.
8. **Low bandwidth and honesty:** delayed/failed audience/history loads differ from empty results; typing is not silently shared, and failed saves retain permitted edits. Refresh and retry recover the source receipt safely. No offline-posting guarantee or sensitive browser persistence is implied.
9. **Actual pilot:** observe missionaries and staff adding, declining to add, locating and correcting an update. Record confusion about audience, coercion, next action and exposure recovery. No satisfaction, beauty, speed or compliance result is yet measured; revise the real composition from observed failures.
