# D26 ownership — shared context without missionary supervision

2026-10-10 UTC. Research against Core HEAD
`225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`. The founder selected Q26 A,
requested the full review, and expressly rejected treating missionaries as staff
to be controlled. These are selected product directions, not proof of a deployed
shared-history capability. This appendix proposes narrow owner reconciliation;
it changes no PRD, ADR, OpenSpec, issue, permission or runtime.

**Recommendation: accept A with the explicit privacy/source and autonomy
qualification below.** Whether the consolidated wording needs fresh ratification
depends on its additional product commitments, not on calling inherited access
checks an amendment. The founder's autonomy instruction is itself authority to
amend conflicting retained target/reporting assumptions. The alternative of
sharing each update deliberately preserves more author control and uses the same
single log. It adds a sharing step and can leave useful context unshared; a
universal collaboration, diary or encounter platform is unnecessary.

## Material concerns and minimum exact clauses

### O1 — Privacy widening disguised as a routine note

**High severity; likely if existing CRM notes are reused without qualification.**
E1–E3/E8 conflict unless the owner exclusion is explicitly narrowed. **Effect:**
A remains sound; enablement requires a bounded new source/projection contract.

**Required clause:** “P9 owns the exact development encounter/update source;
P27 supplies its partnership-purpose reference and P27/P28 consume authorized
projections. Newly recorded eligible routine shared context may be visible under
that classified projection. The general exclusion of staff/private CRM notes,
scores, care/Support bodies, restricted financial context and unrelated activity
remains. Existing/imported notes are not reclassified or shared by migration,
relationship linkage or a routine-looking title.”

Reconcile P9:905, P3:90 and their acceptance cases through the later formal
owner workflow. Register exact read/create/correct uses in P12; do not create a
P27 ACL system. Policy widening follows ADR0211. Saving a permitted routine
update under already-qualified policy needs no separate manager or checker.

### O2 — Shared history becomes missionary compliance monitoring

**High severity; plausible given E4's retained targets/self-report language.**
**Effect:** the user's new autonomy direction requires explicit P28 constraint
clarification, not merely a friendly interface over compulsory controls.

**Required clause:** “Shared history supports voluntary relationship coordination.
Reading, recording or sharing an update does not enroll or reopen managed work,
assign a goal/coach, require a report, create a task, or make absent activity
noncompliance. A missionary need not record every conversation, satisfy an
activity quota or obtain supervisory approval to use ordinary authorized
partnership functions. Weekly activity targets and qualitative check-ins are
optional invited coaching aids, never imposed by shared-history participation or
prerequisites for ordinary use. Organization configuration may offer these aids;
it does not convert them into compulsory missionary reporting or unlock raw
supporter history.”

Apply this qualification specifically to owner-constraints:111–119 and roadmap
4165/4173; do not remove useful voluntary goal/coaching capabilities or change
finance-owned obligations. Missing/incomplete voluntary records must not appear
as zero actual ministry activity or a performance failure. Separately admitted
work retains D4/D6/D7's source requirements: choose appropriate next work, review
a genuine hold, or validly close. Autonomy is not false completion, loss of
physical gift responsibility or authority to ignore consent.

### O3 — “Who can see this” freezes today's recipients or promises secrecy

**High severity; plausible during personnel changes.** E1/E5 require current
access, not a frozen recipient ACL. **Effect:** qualify the meaning of A's
audience without creating a new participant/team engine.

**Required clause:** “The shared update belongs to the exact source partnership
context. The visible audience explanation covers currently authorized readers
and future readers who independently obtain the applicable source and historical
access. A current collaborator list is explanatory, not an access grant or
permanent recipient roster. Adding a lead, spouse, coach or teammate does not
grant history; losing access removes future protected reads without rewriting
lawful authorship/provenance. No unavailable audience falls back to broader
staff, ministry or tenant visibility.”

Re-evaluate read/correct authority and the source history floor on each access.
Do not invent a new universal history-start date. A replacement worker can read
only history their actual grant permits; historical authorship does not preserve
access after departure. A draft cannot silently retarget to a new ministry,
Tenant, acting context or broader source audience.

### O4 — Corrections, merges and links widen history

**High severity; conditional on transfer, correction or deduplication.**
[ADR0216:17–40](../../../../adr/0216-non-destructive-source-governed-replayable-donor-merge.md:17)
permits only source-approved current identity repairs and restriction-safe Undo.
E5/E6 prohibit permissions through association. **Effect:** inherited source
safeguards, not a new successor engine.

**Required clause:** “Preserve the actual author, source identity, recording
occurrence, reported encounter precision and attributable correction history.
Reference repair is not ownership transfer or sharing permission. Ministry
succession, Party merge/Undo, new appeal linkage or staff reassignment never
copies private bodies, unions audiences, reparents history across scope or
turns an old private note into new shared context. Later restrictions survive
repair and Undo.”

An incorrect shared entry uses the owner's qualified correction/restriction
path with current authority and revision checks. Reclassification must not erase
the fact that permitted disclosure already occurred; narrowing protects future
access, not a promise to recall prior lawful views/exports. Do not introduce a
generic approval gate or immutable transcript requirement for ordinary edits.

### O5 — One log is mistaken for one universally readable body

**High severity; likely with cross-purpose briefs or summaries.** E6 and
[P3:238](../../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-03-minimum-permission-role-scoped-projection-foundation.md:238)
prevent laundering protected inputs through derived fields. **Effect:** retain
one actual encounter with source-owned, independently permitted context.

**Required clause:** “Reuse exact encounter identity where known; shared context
and separately authorized purpose notes do not become editable transcript
twins. A Support/care reference does not reveal its title/body/attachments.
Filters, counts, search, notifications, exports, briefs, AI context, caches and
streams use the same current permitted source slice. Denied material contributes
neither excerpts nor existence hints. Source unavailable/expired is not an empty
history or permission to retrieve a copied body.”

A private-note path must explain its real permitted audience. D26 does not
invent a universal missionary private journal or promise that an existing staff
note marked `standard` is author-only. Show the shared audience in the ordinary
composer and detail; use separately qualified private/source actions when
needed, not an extra sharing wizard or per-update approval ceremony.

### O6 — Visibility manufactures work, contact or money evidence

**High severity for financial/contact effects; plausible integration mistake.**
E1/E7 and D22–D25 already distinguish requests, responses, work and finance.
**Required clause:** “Creating/viewing/sharing/correcting an update alone does
not send a message, change consent, complete a task, move a stage, accept an ask,
recognize a gift, fulfill a commitment, or create meaningful-contact evidence.
An explicitly requested consequential action uses its own source contract.
An actual recorded encounter may later qualify for a separately defined contact
measure; sharing status itself never supplies that qualification.”

Do not invent automatic mentions, all-collaborator notifications, mandatory
follow-up tasks or a reporting cadence to make the log feel active. Exact
accepted source attention remains governed by its existing owner.

## Acceptance and release evidence required

1. **AC01:** one authorized missionary records an eligible update once; qualified
   staff see the same source; unrelated staff/missionaries and donors cannot
   enumerate it. No staff-role conversion or second transcript is required.
2. **AC02:** an old standard/restricted/imported note remains outside the new
   shared projection. A sensitive link/summary cannot downgrade its inputs.
3. **AC03:** reading or saving creates no enrollment, task, goal, report demand,
   coaching grant or compliance score; optional coaching does not gate use.
4. **AC04:** current exact-history grants govern a new collaborator; removal,
   restriction and author departure fence every protected projection, while
   source provenance survives. No frozen-recipient or all-staff fallback.
5. **AC05:** cross-Tenant/entity/ministry references, stale drafts, Party merge/
   Undo, succession and simultaneous audience changes cannot widen disclosure
   or silently retarget source context. Qualified corrections retain evidence.
6. **AC06:** recorded-time/report-time, no-contact, unanswered attempt and private
   note remain distinct. Duplicate retries do not create a second encounter or
   consequential task/send/financial effect.
7. **AC07:** an actual admitted work obligation still has truthful next action,
   hold or closure; merely declining optional coaching changes none of these.
8. **AC08:** phone-sized and keyboard flows clearly show shared audience, save
   state and source destination without a staff management console. Permission
   loss exposes no forbidden draft/content or hidden-count hint.

These are proposed proof obligations, not tests run or feature completion. The
exact P9 source, P3 classification, P12 purpose/history grants and consuming
surface must be qualified before sharing activates. No broader phase, generic
engine or implementation-ready claim follows from this review.
