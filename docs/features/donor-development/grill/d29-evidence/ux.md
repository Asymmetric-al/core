# D29 UX evidence — one appeal, several honest forms of support

2026-10-11 UTC. The user selected mixed financial/nonfinancial appeals and emphasized exceptional usability. This is a proposed experience grounded in current source and primary documentation, not a working prototype, measured ministry practice or implemented appeal. Source inspected at `225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`.

## Recommended daily experience

Use **one recorded working list**, with each supporter's relevant **Requests and responses** visible together. A church may agree to pray, decline funding and still be considering hosting. Those are three intelligible facts about independently answerable invitations, not one church moving through exclusive financial columns. Selecting the relevant Request opens its terms, response, actual outcome and permitted next action in the shared record shell.

Illustrative example, not an observed ministry workflow:

```text
Autumn ministry visit
Purpose: Coordinate prayer, introductions, hosting and applicable funding.

Working list                                         Add supporters
Hope Church
Prayer partnership      Agreed
Travel support          Declined
Host a gathering        Decision not yet recorded
Next work               Confirm proposed hosting date · Maria
                                                   View requests
```

The next-work line appears only if the source actually requires that action; it is not inferred from silence. A row with no Request says **No Request recorded** only after a successful permitted read. Adding a church means planned inclusion, not presentation, agreement, contact permission, a new journey or a task.

The appeal header conveys name/purpose and useful context; goals are optional and purpose-appropriate. A prayer-only appeal should open to useful people, invitations and follow-through, without a `$0 raised` tile, donation goal or disabled finance form. A mixed appeal can show qualified financial progress separately from nonfinancial responses/outcomes. Preserve the roadmap's list and kanban support while letting staff choose the useful daily view. The board is a clearly identified Request-level lens over one explicit workflow dimension; it cannot force a Party into a single overall answer. Source-owned financial facts remain read-only projections: moving a card cannot create a commitment, record money or resolve gift handling.

The strongest B alternative keeps fundraising appeals narrower and leaves other Requests at partnership level. It reduces appeal reporting complexity, but splits a mixed effort across views. A better fits the selected coordinated purpose if it reuses the same Requests and owner facts. This is product judgment, not evidence of superior fundraising results or user preference. [Q29](../phase27-q29-appeal-purpose-scope.md):33–83 defines the scope choice and explicit boundaries.

## Accepted contracts that shape the interface

- [D22](../phase27-d22-adversarial-review.md#exact-decision-recorded) keeps independently answerable Requests separate but coherent invitations together; preserves original/presented terms, response and actual fulfillment; allows direct recording of a past request/response without dummy steps. Ordinary to-dos remain tasks.
- [D24](../phase27-d24-adversarial-review.md#exact-decision-recorded) makes the list deliberate finite planning intent. Remove/Exclude/Return preserve actual history; membership does not choose a church's human contact or grant permission.
- [D25](../phase27-d25-adversarial-review.md#exact-corrected-decision) keeps Awaiting finance as source-owned operational work, separate from recorded money and remaining physical handling. The appeal continues the same intake rather than entering another gift.
- [D26](../phase27-d26-adversarial-review.md#exact-corrected-decision) preserves voluntary missionary updates and the exact sharing audience. A coordinated appeal does not make private notes or all church relationships shared.
- [D28](../phase27-d28-adversarial-review.md#exact-corrected-decision) keeps facts primary and giving ratings optional, staff-scoped and financial-only. Neither a mixed appeal nor nonfinancial participation creates a composite relationship score.

## Exact proposed UX clauses

**UX01 — Start with purpose, not an amount.** Appeal creation should make its name and intended support understandable, using existing owner-required scope. It need not require a financial goal, numerical target, new campaign hierarchy or Financial/Nonfinancial/Mixed wizard before useful work. Offer applicable monetary goals/details only where intended and qualified. A financial field becoming relevant must not discard prior nonfinancial terms; a purpose edit must not rewrite existing presented Requests, responses or financial attribution. No general category/metrics builder is added.

**UX02 — One working list, clear selection.** **Add supporters** reuses authorized discovery and D24's finite reviewed single/group admission. Distinguish selected page/loaded rows from a separately supported complete matching set, show the actual intended count, and preserve deliberate exclusions/removals. Ordinary Add does not reinstate them. Show per-item accepted/already-included/skipped/failed outcomes with permitted recovery, not one ambiguous success toast. A new query or retry cannot change the accepted group. Membership alone creates no Request, message, gift or task.

**UX03 — Several Requests do not become several churches.** Keep one qualified Party entry with concise Request summaries, preserving individual purpose and outcome. Within that context, **Add request** or **Open existing request** should retain known appeal/partnership/supporter facts rather than require another name/address search or journey. Split only independently answerable invitations, as D22 requires; one coherent invitation needs no artificial item editor. Filter by a particular Request condition with clear meaning—for example, churches with at least one hosting Request awaiting a recorded decision—and identify the matching Request. Do not label the whole church unanswered or hide its other permitted responses.

**UX04 — Show what was asked, answered and done.** Request detail places the actual invitation/presented terms next to the relevant response and actual outcome. Use contextual **Record response**, **Correct response** or **Continue follow-up**, not a vague Move to complete. Record an already-made invitation and answer directly; do not force Draft→Presented clicks for historical facts. An agreement to host is not a booked event or proof it happened. A prayer agreement is not a measurement of prayer. A funding yes is not a P16 commitment or P13 received gift. Preserve who said what, when and with what uncertainty; a human church contact's reply is not automatically binding organizational authority.

**UX05 — Partial and changing answers stay understandable.** Funding partly agreed, a proposed hosting date awaiting confirmation, an introduction offered with conditions and a later withdrawal each retain their actual terms/history. Multiple church contacts can provide conflicting information: show the qualified need for clarification, not a last-response-wins church status. Do not force a numerical percentage onto a qualitative answer or treat silence as decline. A decline may legitimately need no further action. Revisions/corrections update the source Request and its projections without another encounter, duplicate Request or erased earlier answer.

**UX06 — Outcomes have honest units and denominators.** Nonfinancial appeals can be useful with no numerical goal. When showing counts, name the object and fact: **Churches on working list**, **Requests with a recorded response**, or **Hosting arrangements reported completed**, only if each source meaning is qualified. Church count, Request count, agreement and actual participation must not be summed into one success total. A church with three Requests is not three churches. If an optional goal is used, state the unit/period and compare like facts; do not count prayer hours, spiritual impact or generic engagement unless independently established by an appropriate source contract. Do not add those measurement products through D29.

**UX07 — Money stays applicable and source-owned.** Display requested/expected/responded/committed/recorded-money facts separately where relevant, with explicit currency, Legal Entity/lens and period. Do not add prayer/hosting as zero-value gifts or estimated cash equivalents. No overall progress meter combines money with participation; no cross-currency total without an owner-qualified basis. Exact source references and attribution govern financial totals, not the number of Requests or appeal links. **Awaiting finance** links to the same intake and preserves unresolved handling after financial recording; a manual response or card move cannot post money. The shared [money formatter](../../../../../packages/api/src/money/index.ts):56–82 supports exact units and explicit locale, but cannot supply missing financial meaning.

**UX08 — Follow-through is specific, not pressure.** Show the existing eligible responsible person and source-backed next action for genuinely outstanding work. Avoid requiring a new task for each list member or repeated tasks for the same occurrence. Do not automatically ask again because funding was declined, prayer is the only support or one Request remains unanswered. Existing review, communications, consent, closed/paused work, gift handling and commitments retain their owners. Adding an appeal link or changing an invitation to prayer cannot bypass an appeal exclusion or wider restriction. Closing/removing appeal planning preserves actual responses and unresolved independent obligations.

**UX09 — Permission and empty states tell the truth.** A permitted empty working list offers Add supporters; no Requests offers Add/Open request; no filter matches offers change filters. These differ from loading, unavailable source and lost access. Counts and summary states must use the current authorized projection, never reveal private bodies/identities through aggregate hints. A mixed appeal can remain useful when a viewer may see nonfinancial work but not financial details; do not expose protected totals or replace hidden money with zero. Public pages, donor portals, mailing, event booking and prayer publishing do not appear merely because the appeal exists. Sharing continues under D26's exact audience.

**UX10 — Ordinary save, direct recovery.** Keep relevant known context visible, optional fields genuinely optional and source-required validation specific. One normal save suffices; preserve permitted input after validation/transport failure. Bind drafts and operations to the exact appeal/Party/Request. A delayed reply cannot overwrite another row's draft; lost acceptance is recovered against the same intent instead of creating a duplicate. Explain partial group results and conflict recovery. If a saved response leaves a filter, announce the result and offer the permitted Request/history link rather than leaving the user to wonder where the row went. Do not imply that closing a dialog canceled an accepted business operation or recalled a sent message.

**UX11 — Readable and operable in the real work setting.** Prioritize identity/purpose, then Request outcomes and next action; keep financial detail secondary where applicable. Prefer a few readable text lines over many tiny badges or icons. Preserve long/international names and addresses, locale-aware money, honest calendar/event dates and RTL reading order. At 320 CSS pixels/equivalent zoom, the working list becomes a useful compact presentation without losing Request distinctions or next actions. Use shared controls, visible focus and descriptive names. All actions work by keyboard and by click/tap without dragging; keyboard equivalence alone is insufficient for a touch user. Test mobile keyboard/overlay scrolling and focus restoration. Slow loading never resets displayed unknowns to failure or clears a user's draft.

## Key states and wording

These are presentation examples, not a frozen lifecycle enum.

| Condition                                          | Clear outcome/action                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Included, no permitted Request recorded            | **On working list** / **Add request**. Not Asked.                                                       |
| Prayer yes, funding no, hosting undecided          | Each Request's own response; no single Complete/Declined church badge.                                  |
| Presented invitation, no known response            | **No response recorded** within known coverage; no automatic decline or recontact obligation.           |
| Supporter agreed, activity not yet evidenced       | Agreement plus the actual next source action; not fulfilled or event booked.                            |
| Some participation completed, another Request open | Preserve both facts and the remaining specific work.                                                    |
| Gift reported in hand, not recorded by finance     | **Awaiting finance** with exact intake action; no received-money credit.                                |
| Removed/excluded but earlier responses exist       | Planned inclusion ends; permitted Requests/history remain reachable. Return is a new authorized action. |
| Source unavailable or permission changed           | Safe unavailable/recovery state; no invented zero, no hidden details or retry into broader access.      |

## Risks and falsifiable acceptance

Grades are prospective severity/likelihood judgments, not observed incident rates or claims of a shipped D29 defect.

| Concern                                                                     | Grade                                              | Prevention and proof                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One church/status erases independent answers                                | High / likely if financial flow columns are copied | UX03–05: prayer yes/funding no/hosting pending remain simultaneously readable and independently correctable.                                      |
| Nonfinancial work becomes dollars, empty money tiles or an engagement score | High / plausible                                   | UX01,06–07: prayer-only/no-goal appeal works; counts have exact units, no dollar equivalents or composite percentage.                             |
| Selection/membership is confused with asked/sent/consented                  | High / plausible; current selection seams above    | UX02,08–09: finite reviewed list, explicit source actions and current eligibility; adding/removing neither fabricates nor erases actual outcomes. |
| Similar rows/late saves corrupt another Request                             | High / plausible                                   | UX03,10: source identity, exact replay, context-bound draft, partial result and conflict tests.                                                   |
| A polished list hides restricted facts or inaccessible actions              | High / plausible                                   | UX09–11: current-role/source tests plus actual keyboard, touch, screen-reader and responsive proof.                                               |

The actual product must demonstrate:

1. Create a nonfinancial appeal with no financial or numeric goal, add one church and record a prayer invitation/response without money fields, dummy stages, extra journey or task creation from membership.
2. Add a finite reviewed group spanning pages, then change the search while processing. Exact selected Parties and per-item results remain truthful; excluded/removed entries stay out unless deliberately returned.
3. On one church, record prayer agreed, funding declined and hosting conditional. A participant can identify each answer and the real next action without opening three separate church records or interpreting a global status.
4. Record an already-presented Request and response once, then a partial/later changed answer and contradictory contact information. Preserve actual terms, attribution and history; no duplicate encounter or invented organization authority.
5. Show qualified source-confirmed money, another gift awaiting finance and nonfinancial agreement/completion together. Verify source attribution, currencies and count units; no duplicate gift from multiple links and no prayer valuation.
6. Remove/exclude the church, close planning and later return it. Current eligibility is respected, affected pending outreach stops through its owner, actual history/independent handling remains, and old tasks/messages do not revive merely from Return.
7. Filter by a Request condition, save a response that no longer matches, drop the accepted reply and retry. Explain the disappearing row, recover the same result and preserve other Request/draft state. Cover concurrent edits and permission loss without an accidental broader save or export.
8. At 320 CSS pixels/equivalent zoom, with keyboard, screen reader, touch, mobile keyboard, long translated text, RTL and explicit money/date locales, complete Add/Open request→Record response→Recover. Exercise both list and Request-level board views without forcing board use; preserve the selected workflow dimension and read-only financial facts. No drag-only operation, clipped essential distinction, hidden focus or toast-only result.
9. Run a real authorized-staff pilot: ask participants to identify what was planned, asked, answered, actually completed and still owned in these scenarios. Missionary participants evaluate only their independently qualified D26/P28 surface or denied-access scenarios; this review grants no organization-wide appeal access or additional financial/nonfinancial projection. Record mistaken interpretations, duplicate entry and recovery failures; revise the actual composition. No aesthetic, speed, fundraising or satisfaction result has yet been measured.
