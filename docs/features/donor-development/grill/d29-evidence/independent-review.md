## Material finding and verified resolution

**IR29-01 — A chronological reply must not become the authoritative response.**
The reviewed R04 initially instructed current-response counts to use the “latest
applicable response.” That was weaker than financial F02, UX05, R02 and AC06,
which preserve actual terms, speaker capacity and contradictory accounts. A
later saved reply from a different church contact could consequently be treated
as a confirmed organizational agreement and inflate appeal results. Severity is
high for truthfulness and downstream decisions; likelihood is plausible if the
summary query simply chooses the newest response. This is a design ambiguity,
not an observed production defect.

The permanent correction is the single source-qualified current response under
the Request owner's rules. It narrows counting semantics without changing the
selected mixed-purpose appeal scope or adding a new approval workflow.

The revised **R04** was reread and now says:

> A current Request-response count SHALL count distinct Requests using the
> source-qualified current response to identified terms. Contradictory or
> unresolved accounts remain clarification-required and SHALL NOT count as
> confirmed agreement.

**Resolved.** R02 preserves conflicting evidence and prohibits the last saved
contact reply from automatically becoming the organization's answer; AC06 requires
source-qualified clarification. This correction belongs in the eventual count,
detail, board, export and reporting implementations through their common source
contract. No implementation or runtime test of that contract was performed here.

## Independent challenges that did not reveal another material contradiction

- **Scope and necessity:** D22 already admits independently answerable financial
  and nonfinancial Requests; D24 owns deliberate working membership. D29 adds
  coordinated mixed appeal scope using those records. The financially narrower
  alternative remains accurately described. Published ministry support categories
  establish relevant forms of support, not demand for this particular interface.
- **New policy versus inherited requirements:** Multiple-appeal Request references
  and precise appeal closure are explicitly identified as proposed product
  judgments. They are not misrepresented as consequences already ratified by
  D22/D24 or hidden inside a security rule. D21's delegated routine design is
  respected without adding another founder micro-question.
- **Cardinality, links and purpose:** R03 permits one current link per exact
  appeal/Request pair, preserves a canonical Request across relevant appeals and
  does not backdate presentation or clone a response. R02/R06/R10–R12 preserve
  actual terms, current purpose authorization, producer eligibility, revisions
  and lawful history through edit, unlink, closure and reopen. A new link cannot
  grant source access or bypass another appeal's applicable exclusion. An extra
  generalized link-state or compatibility workflow is not justified here.
- **Money and counts:** R04–R05 and financial F02–F08 distinguish supporters,
  Requests, responses, actual participation and financial coverage. Overlapping
  views are not disjoint totals; the admitted line/amount scope governs money.
  Entity, currency, recognition, requested amounts, commitment and received money
  remain separate. A generic association does not settle the existing P13
  attribution conflict. Unsupported financial progress stays unavailable while
  independently safe nonfinancial work continues.
- **Closure and delivery races:** Closure fences new appeal-originated planning
  and solicitation, accounts for affected work and preserves independently owned
  Requests, promises, handling and source financial obligations. Late actual facts
  do not restore membership or outreach. Reopen preserves history and does not
  revive an old send/task occurrence. R10–R11 and AC20–AC23 require durable source
  intent, current admission fences and truthful ambiguous-delivery recovery.
- **Authority and dignity:** No appeal ownership, membership, hosting/prayer
  agreement or task assignment becomes a private-source grant. D26 voluntary
  missionary contribution, D27 actual exchanges and D28 optional financial ratings
  remain intact. Nonfinancial participation does not become a money equivalent,
  relationship score or continuing proof-of-prayer obligation.
- **Usability and proportion:** The list/Request-level board distinction avoids
  forcing a church with several answers into one exclusive financial stage.
  Purpose-first creation, optional goals, direct historical recording and honest
  loading/permission/empty states support ordinary work without a new wizard,
  engine or duplicate record. Shared Core components and actual composed mobile,
  keyboard, touch and screen-reader proof remain required; the draft claims no
  demonstrated beauty, satisfaction or speed.
- **Review completeness and release gates:** All 22 requested categories receive
  an explicit material-concern disposition. Concerns map to exact R clauses and
  falsifiable ACs rather than generic cautions. Monitoring has signals, thresholds,
  accountable roles and responses; a named operator must be assigned before
  rollout. Real numeric load/freshness limits belong in the release manifest and
  must be qualified before activation, not invented as measured present capacity.

affected product behavior. The review supports recording the amended decision;
