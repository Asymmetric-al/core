## Required language and material concerns

### O1 — One financial column loses independent responses

**High severity; likely with the inherited bucket wording.**

**Required owner amendment:** “A P27 appeal coordinates financial, nonfinancial
or mixed support for one shared purpose using recorded working entries and
canonical Requests. Numerical goals are optional. Display planning disposition,
request presentation/response, observed participation, financial commitment,
gift recording/handling and received money as distinct source-qualified facts.
Financial states apply only where meaningful; they are not an exclusive overall
supporter stage or mandatory funnel.”

Keep D24's In working list/Excluded/Removed separate from D22's no answer,
positive/partial/conditional/declined/deferred responses. A church may accept
hosting, defer prayer and decline funding concurrently. A summary must preserve
that combination, not choose whichever outcome looks most successful. Do not
revive Received/Given as two names for the same fact or treat a kanban drag as a
response, participation event or gift.

### O2 — Goals become invented money or a participation scoring engine

**High severity; plausible when one progress bar is reused for mixed support.**

**Required clause:** “An appeal without a numerical goal remains fully usable.
Any quantitative goal states its unit, source fact, scope and observation basis.
Money goals keep exact Legal Entity/currency meaning. Nonfinancial targets use
only supported, explainable request/participation facts; no arbitrary formula,
weighted success score, monetary equivalence or universal goal engine.”

For example, “churches agreeing to host” counts a qualified response, not visits
actually held. “Introductions completed” needs its distinct actual outcome.
“Agreed to pray” does not establish continued prayer activity, spiritual diligence
or permission for ongoing messages. A descriptive prayer intention needs no
target or recurring reporting chore. Show actual/requested/target independently;
exceeding a target is valid evidence, not a reason to clip the actual count.
Goal revisions preserve their prior basis rather than rewriting past success.

### O3 — Reused Requests become copied records or inflated totals

**High severity; plausible for overlapping initiatives.** D24 permits a Party in
several appeals, but this does not itself authorize several copies of one Request.

**Proposed minimum product qualification:** “Authorized staff may reference the
same canonical Request from more than one relevant appeal. The relationship is
explicit, scope-qualified and attributable; it provides coordination context,
not new authorship, responsibility, visibility, financial allocation or response.
Ending a reference preserves the Request and lawful association history.”

Within one appeal, count the same Request once regardless of its references or
staff viewers. Distinct people/organizations, requests, responses and actual
outcomes are different named measures. A combined organizational view deduplicates
the appropriate source identities; it does not add overlapping appeal totals.
Financial aggregation still uses the qualified money-owner lens and conservation
rules, not Request membership. Do not require a Request clone, a new journey or
another task to appear in another legitimate view.

This is a proposed explicit cardinality choice, not an inherited requirement.
Reject a universal group/link catalog: only bounded appeal-to-Request references
are needed. Linking an old Request is not proof it was presented through the new
appeal and cannot backdate campaign effectiveness. A newly distinct invitation
retains its own D22 terms/response rather than borrowing an old acceptance.

### O4 — Shared context bypasses exclusions or source disclosure

**Critical severity; plausible with multiple appeals, mixed purposes or workers.**

**Required clause:** “Every association/read/count/action re-proves current
Tenant, exact source/field/purpose and applicable entity authority. Appeal access
does not reveal all linked Requests, private notes or financial facts. Membership
and prayer/hosting agreement grant no access, consent or communication audience.”

An outward action retains its actual producer appeal/purpose. It cannot switch
to another linked appeal, ministry or human contact to bypass the applicable
restriction. D24's source-owned subtractive recipient gate remains binding at
the communication owner's release boundary; inclusion is not actual presentation.
Changing a financial invitation's label to “prayer” cannot evade contact rules.
Legitimately unrelated requests retain their own source eligibility.

### O5 — Closing coordination falsely completes independent work

**High severity; plausible when an appeal is treated as parent owner of all work.**

**Proposed minimum product qualification:** “An authorized person may end the
appeal's active coordination deliberately. Closure ends new appeal-originated
planning/outreach under its qualified producer predicate and preserves history.
Before accepting closure, account for the bounded work whose applicability this
action changes. Independent Requests, commitments, gifts and actual promised
follow-through retain their own owner, work and history; closure does not mark
them fulfilled, canceled, declined or financially settled.”

Where the appeal-ending predicate genuinely ends an automatic source action,
its task/attention ends No longer required, not Completed. Do not cancel all
linked tasks or block closure until every multi-year financial promise is paid.
No numerical target must be achieved to close; no date or achieved goal closes
automatically. An empty visible task list is not complete obligation proof.

Late responses, gifts, participation evidence and qualified corrections remain
recordable in their original scope without resuming solicitation or re-adding
working entries. Any deliberately exposed resume/reopen operation must preserve
the closure history and re-prove current eligibility; it cannot revive old send,
task or unread occurrences. Do not import D6's cultivation-pause review cadence
as a new mandatory appeal chore. These exact appeal lifecycle effects are proposed
here, not already decided by the founder's scope answer.

### O6 — Nonfinancial support quietly creates another platform domain

**High severity; plausible scope expansion.**

**Required clause:** “P27 records the invitation, response and qualified observed
outcome. Booking, volunteer scheduling, public content, subscriptions, care,
referral identity, commitments and payments stay with their qualified owners.
An agreement creates none of those effects automatically. Native coordination
remains useful without pretending an unavailable owner has executed the work.”

Reuse one permitted encounter/source reference, with occurrence/recording clocks
and current disclosure, under D26/D27. A ministry-page prayer click is not an
appeal acceptance or fulfillment feed without a separately qualified contract.
Keep the form about partnership support; do not turn prayer invitations into
care dossiers. P28's permitted shared view does not expose the organization-wide
appeal population or require missionary activity reporting. D28 ratings remain
optional financial analysis and cannot score nonfinancial participation.

## Acceptance evidence required

- **AC01:** create and use nonfinancial and mixed appeals without fake money,
  currency, gift, designation or numerical goal; retain the ordinary working list.
- **AC02:** the same church independently accepts hosting, defers prayer and
  declines funding; the view preserves all three and creates no implied booking,
  subscription, gift or stage change.
- **AC03:** one Request referenced by two appeals remains one source record and
  one outcome; removing either link changes no response or independent task.
  Per-appeal and combined measures use explicit units and deduplicate correctly.
- **AC04:** goal-free, partial, over-target, revised and late outcomes remain
  truthful; agreement is not actual delivery and unlike units never form one score.
- **AC05:** closure versus dispatch/late-response races preserve exact source
  effects, required history and independent obligations; reopening revives no old
  task/send. No request or financial outcome is manufactured by closure.
- **AC06:** exclusion, hidden linked records, scope changes, Party repair and
  different human representatives cannot bypass current read/contact restrictions
  or leak denied records through totals, exports, summaries or caches.
- **AC07:** a missionary can contribute permitted context without quotas, extra
  journeys, reporting demands, automatic ratings or broadened organization access.

These are proposed proof obligations, not executed acceptance tests. Exact source
commands, financial association reporting, P12 projections and communication
fences must be qualified before their affected paths ship. No generic campaign,
participation, goal, task or workflow engine is required by this decision.
