## Questions challenged and their disposition

**Does “exchange” accidentally require a recorded message pair?** No. The decision,
R01 and AC03 explicitly accept a relevant substantive human inbound without a
preceding Asym outbound. Ordinary attributed manual evidence of a conversation is
also allowed without a transcript or provider verification. Short personal replies
and declines may qualify; no minimum length, favorable sentiment or financial
outcome is selected. This avoids both rejecting legitimate off-platform history
and inventing a previous outbound interaction.

**Does any event labeled Call, Meeting or Reply now count?** No. The decision and
AC01–AC04 distinguish the actual outcome from launching an app, a calendar record,
passive attendance, a task completion, automated response or transport metadata.
Ambiguous evidence remains unqualified. The proposed small outcome choice is used
only when the owning source does not already establish it; no separate
meaningfulness checkbox, semantic classifier, compulsory review queue or duplicate
log is added. An ordinary shared note can remain useful context without becoming
an encounter.

**Does the narrower metric devalue one-way care or control missionaries?** No.
Recent outreach remains visible, including a thank-you that needs no answer.
The draft does not equate a missing reply with a failed relationship or create an
awaiting-response warning, task or recontact requirement. R08 and AC10 preserve
D26's voluntary contribution, existing paused/closed work, actual obligations and
contact restrictions. There is no missionary diligence score, logging quota,
aging threshold or mandatory monitoring product. A real current reply can be
recorded without implicitly reopening the partnership.

**Can a private event leak through an apparently harmless date?** The main treats
the date, source descriptor, counts, ordering and links as protected projections,
not just the body. R04 and AC08–AC09 retain current source/partnership/history
authority. A church link or selected participant cannot refresh every ministry
relationship. Where one real encounter concerns multiple partnerships, the
appendices require each actual qualified connection while preserving the one
encounter. The no-duplicate rule does not prohibit legitimate joint context.

**Does expired content make factual history unusable, or does a copied flag evade
revocation?** Neither shortcut is selected. ADR0031 separates durable body-free
communication facts from expiring recent sent copies. The decision permits only
separately retained, currently authorized facts to support the measure. It does
not claim that every durable provider fact proves human exchange, that every
message body must remain readable, or that a stored qualification flag survives
loss of governing authority. The actual source adapters still require proof.

**Does the newest visible timestamp necessarily mean the last exchange?** No.
R03 and AC06–AC07 reject save-time/epoch fallbacks, a maximum over a capped display
page, and an irreversible “latest” value. The draft explicitly covers a correction
or restriction moving the result backward. An undated qualifying encounter stays
useful history without becoming today or a reliable age; “Latest dated exchange”
can describe the known subset without asserting complete chronology. Any hint
about undated/private evidence must itself be authorized. Empty, incomplete and
failed retrieval remain distinct.

**Is the proposed interface proportionate and honest?** Yes as a proposal. One
compact summary, existing history, source links where permitted and the reused
D26 entry/correction flow are sufficient. Known identity data is reused; no new
name/address form, transcript, mandatory task or donor-facing step is introduced.
The persistent explanation distinguishes exchange from outreach without using an
unexplained judgment about “meaningfulness.” Actual keyboard/mobile/screen-reader
and comprehension checks remain necessary; shared components do not establish
that the composition is clear or accessible. The draft claims no measured beauty,
speed, adoption or conversion improvement.

## Evidence quality and remaining proof

The current source anchors support the reported implementation gaps:
`lastTouchAt` reads `donor.updated_at`; the detail query caps legacy activities at
50; its presentation falls back from activity date to creation time and epoch;
the missionary activity writer records save time. These are adjacent source facts,
not evidence that a delivered D27 feature is wrong. Preserving the broader P9
field and qualifying a separately named derived measure is the correct boundary.

The external research distinguishes publication/retrieval dates and editions.
HubSpot's broader Last contacted convention is treated as a credible alternative,
not dismissed as incorrect. CiviCRM, Blackbaud and MPDX support distinguishing
activity types/outcomes but do not prove Core's selected rule or present runtime
behavior. Apple/W3C and general usability guidance support specific evidence and
presentation safeguards rather than a universal ministry workflow. The source
facts are separated from the proposed labels and product judgment.

R01–R08 and AC01–AC16 cover the focused choice and its important negative cases.
The stated next work is actual source/permission qualification and testing the
small composed flow with representative staff and missionaries. No runtime probe,
generic event engine, additional approval product or broad monitoring program is
needed to complete this research decision. No tests were executed by this reviewer;
document link/whitespace checks do not establish implementation safety.
