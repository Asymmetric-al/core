# D27 UX evidence — recorded exchanges and outreach

2026-10-11 UTC. Q27 A is selected. This focused review proposes interaction and display safeguards; it is not a delivered contact measure, tested prototype or observed ministry workflow. Current source inspected at `225c02e9499bd6c0d493223cd4b1a4120ff787e3`.

## Recommendation: two clear facts, no score

Keep A. Show the partnership's **Last recorded exchange**, with a plain source descriptor such as **Phone conversation** or **Personal reply**, alongside relevant **Recent outreach**. Brief help can explain that an actual conversation or substantive personal message received qualifies, while sending a message alone does not. The labels are proposed product wording: test comprehension in the real surface rather than freezing a widget or adding “meaningful” as an unexplained judgment. Bare **Last conversation** is too narrow if the value can come from a written reply. **Last contacted** can misleadingly suggest that sending alone counts.

An illustrative compact summary:

```text
Church / ministry partnership
Last recorded exchange       3 September · Phone conversation
Recent outreach              8 October · Personal thank-you sent
                             View history
```

Both lines are neutral facts. The thank-you is valuable relationship care even though it does not reset the exchange date. It need not receive a reply, so the interface should not automatically mark it waiting or overdue. The older date must not create a task, a red relationship score or a missionary performance judgment. [Q27](../phase27-q27-meaningful-contact.md):31–75 establishes the distinction and exclusions; [ratified D26](../phase27-d26-adversarial-review.md):21–72 preserves voluntary participation, exact source/audience context and one encounter identity.

The strongest B alternative gives personal outreach its own contact-recency credit and better recognizes one-way stewardship. Keep that information visible as outreach under A instead of hiding it or calling it unsuccessful. A favors preparing a conversation with truthful exchange evidence; it is a product choice, not an industry standard.

## Exact proposed UX clauses

**UX01 — Explain the value from its source.** A reader can open the permitted source fact behind the date and understand what happened, when, who recorded it and which partnership it concerns. Display a useful channel/outcome descriptor, not an internal event ID. Keep exchange recency separate from outreach, record maintenance, gift recency and next-action health. Never derive it from an edit, completed generic task, automatic receipt, email-open signal or arbitrary latest timeline row.

**UX02 — Minimum recording, no duplicate entry.** A person who chooses to record an actual conversation supplies only what the source needs to establish the exact partnership, what happened and its truthful occurrence date/precision. Retain known context; ask for a call/meeting outcome only when the existing source does not establish it. Do not require re-entering a known name/address, resolving a duplicate supporter search or supplying missing phone/email just to record an actual encounter. Do not require a transcript, a second CRM note, a title, exact minute, follow-up task, stage change or approval solely to update the date. An ordinary D26 update can remain context without being an encounter. If a qualified source already records the exchange, project it once; do not ask staff and missionary to log it again or count each contributor's note separately. A manual record is attributed evidence, not a claim of provider verification.

**UX03 — Distinguish evidence with small, contextual choices.** If a call outcome is unknown, do not preselect Conversation completed from a Call action. If a meeting exists on a calendar, do not convert its start/end time or attendance flag into an exchange. Let the qualified source report the actual outcome once. Avoid a universal long outcome taxonomy or tenant rule designer. Imported/provider events without sufficient outcome evidence stay unqualified; the UI must not infer substance from a title, message length, sentiment, open tracking or AI guess.

**UX04 — Preserve useful one-way care and personal responses.** Sent personal messages remain visible as outreach. A personalized thank-you or update with no need for an answer does not become a failed/overdue exchange. When useful and supported, use **No reply recorded** rather than **No reply** or **Ignored**, and do not claim complete coverage of calls, personal inboxes or other channels. A genuine short answer to a partnership question can be substantive; a personal decline is not a failed conversation and creates no automatic follow-up obligation. An unsolicited substantive personal inbound message can evidence contact without fabricating an earlier outbound message or forcing a reply. Preserve that clarification of A explicitly; “exchange” must not require unnecessary back-and-forth merely to make a date move. This internal context measure adds no donor-facing acknowledgment, form, reply demand or donation step; do not chase an answer merely to improve the displayed date.

**UX05 — Scope to the exact partnership.** A conversation with a church's representative about ministry A must not refresh every ministry relationship with that church, the representative's personal partnership or unrelated work in the tenant. Where one actual encounter legitimately concerns multiple partnerships, each projection needs its qualified relation/authority while retaining the one encounter; do not duplicate the conversation to inflate activity. A personal reply from a contact does not by itself establish an organizational funding decision or signing authority. The displayed church/ministry context and permitted participant identity should make this understandable without raw IDs or another recurring chooser.

**UX06 — Be honest about date and coverage.** Use occurrence time, not time entered, imported, reshared, edited or refreshed. An old conversation recorded today stays old. Preserve a known calendar day without inventing midnight UTC or an exact time; show an instant in the intended locale/timezone. Do not coerce an unknown date to today or the epoch. An undated eligible exchange can remain useful history without supplying an orderable recency timestamp. If known and unknown dates coexist, do not claim the known date is definitely the last exchange: use a qualified description such as **Latest dated exchange**, with an authorized explanation of incomplete dates where appropriate. Never expose the existence of an undated or private entry through that explanation without metadata authority. Future scheduled events are plans; reject or correct an invalid future completed-event date without silently changing it to now.

**UX07 — Absence, loading and restricted data are different.** **No recorded exchange** is appropriate only for an authorized, successfully evaluated scope with no qualifying recorded evidence; it does not mean Never contacted. Where the source only promises a visible/permitted subset, the wording must say so and must not claim complete history. Source failure or unavailable qualification is an unavailable state with safe retry, not zero activity. Do not advertise hidden/private events with badges, unexplained newer dates or counts. Display only a currently authorized source projection; removing a private fact may change the visible value without declaring that the real-world encounter was erased.

**UX08 — Context informs judgment, not automatic pressure.** No universal aging threshold, red/green diligence score, missionary quota, automatic reminder or contact campaign follows from A. Existing paused/closed work, agreed waiting dates, supporter preferences and no-contact restrictions retain their own meaning. A decline may explain why no next action is wanted. A source correction may move the displayed date earlier or remove it; do not call that regression, undo a lifecycle decision or recreate an ended task. If the user wants follow-through, it is the separately deliberate, currently permitted source action already governed elsewhere.

**UX09 — Source correction and recovery stay simple.** Correct the underlying encounter/outcome/date once and refresh its permitted projections. Do not offer an editable recency date independent of the evidence. An exact retry of a saved encounter or later contributor note must not create another contact event. Bind draft and result to the intended partnership; a late response cannot overwrite another partner's draft or replace a newer corrected summary. Distinguish saved evidence from a still-refreshing derived summary when applicable; never silently substitute record-update time while waiting. Preserve permitted input on validation failure and recover a lost success reply without claiming failure or encouraging a duplicate record.

**UX10 — Legible without ceremony.** One compact summary and the ordinary history are enough. Important explanations stay available as text, with labels/errors programmatically associated; no color-only health badge or tooltip-only definition. Use locale-aware dates, full names and text that wraps, with sensible RTL ordering. At 320 CSS pixels/equivalent zoom, the source link, occurrence date, outcome and any permitted uncertainty explanation remain usable. If a dialog is used, preserve shared focus/scroll behavior and keep the mobile keyboard from covering Save/Cancel. Pending/result feedback has an intelligible accessible name and does not announce every timeline refresh as a new conversation.

## Edge-case decision examples

These illustrate qualification and display requirements, not an exhaustive source enum or another product-choice gate.

| Recorded fact                                                                    | Exchange date and useful presentation                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal outbound follow-up, no response recorded                                | Does not advance. Show outreach; do not assert the person read it or ignored it.                                                 |
| Personal thank-you with no reply needed                                          | Does not advance from sending alone. Show the care without an automatic awaiting-response state or task.                         |
| Provider accepts/delivers email; tracking image loads                            | Does not advance. These signals do not establish a person read or responded.                                                     |
| Automated reply/out-of-office/receipt                                            | Does not advance. Distinguish automation from a personal response.                                                               |
| Real personal reply resolves the partnership question, including a brief decline | Qualifies under A if the exact source/context is established. No minimum word count, positivity requirement or forced follow-up. |
| Substantive personal inbound with no earlier outbound                            | Qualify the actual personal message without inventing a preceding conversation or requiring a reply to it.                       |
| Generic courtesy/reaction with no established substantive content                | Do not automatically promote from a message/reaction event. Preserve the source fact; qualification must not be guessed.         |
| Attempted call / left outbound voicemail                                         | Does not establish a conversation. Retain the actual attempt outcome.                                                            |
| Meeting booked, canceled, no-show or attended without any actual exchange        | Does not advance merely from booking, elapsed time or passive attendance. Actual relevant interaction can qualify separately.    |
| Actual conversation recorded days later                                          | Qualifies at its truthful occurrence date, not save date.                                                                        |
| Note, gift, task edit or correction to existing encounter                        | Does not create a new exchange; a correction can legitimately change the derived date.                                           |
| Same church, different ministry partnership                                      | No automatic cross-partnership refresh.                                                                                          |
| Private/restricted conversation                                                  | No disclosure through date, count, tooltip, sort order or derived summary outside the qualified audience.                        |

## Material concerns and acceptance proof

Severity/likelihood are prospective judgments, not incident rates. None requires abandoning A.

| Concern                                                                     | Grade                                              | Decisive prevention/proof                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ambiguous “meaningful/last touch” label hides sent-vs-exchanged distinction | High / likely if current fields are copied         | UX01–04: show source facts; editing a profile, sending a thank-you and an automated open cannot advance exchange recency.                                  |
| Source type/default timestamp creates false conversation evidence           | High / plausible; adjacent code demonstrated above | UX02–03,06: attempted/canceled/passive cases, late entry and unknown dates retain truthful meaning without invented evidence.                              |
| Global church metric or private recency leaks another relationship          | High / plausible                                   | UX05,07: same Party in two ministries plus scoped/private history yields exactly permitted independent projections.                                        |
| Old date becomes neglect/coercion or unwanted recontact                     | High / plausible                                   | UX04,08: voluntary missionary use, thank-you, decline, pause, closed work and no-contact all retain their independent meaning and no automatic obligation. |
| Correction/retry and incomplete timelines lie about latest event            | High / plausible                                   | UX06–09: authoritative scoped projection rather than loaded-page maximum; one event identity, current correction and honest unknown/unavailable states.    |
| Small/mobile or assistive UI hides the explanation                          | Medium / plausible                                 | UX10 and actual browser/pilot gates; primitive adoption alone is insufficient.                                                                             |

The real feature must demonstrate:

1. A participant can explain why an October thank-you does not replace a September conversation, while still finding the outreach. They do not interpret the date as diligence, neglect or a requirement to call again.
2. Exercise every edge table row with qualified source fixtures, including a brief personal decline, unrelated inbound, automation, no-show and passive attendance. Verify the exact inclusion rules and the source explanation; no text-length or sentiment heuristic substitutes for them.
3. Add one actual encounter once, record another contributor's permitted note, retry after losing the reply, and correct the outcome/date. Exactly one encounter contributes; the derived result changes only with its qualified evidence, not each note or save.
4. Record a past calendar date while traveling across a day boundary, a true timestamp, an unknown date and a future scheduled meeting. Display/sorting never invent precision or present an arbitrary known date as definitively latest when permitted undated evidence prevents that conclusion.
5. Test the same church/contact across two partnerships and current/revoked audiences. No date/count/order/source link leaks protected evidence or refreshes unrelated work. Failed/partial source retrieval does not become a false empty state or older authoritative maximum.
6. Test authoritative history beyond the current 50-row legacy cap, a later correction that moves the date backward, stale results and a permission change during save/read. Do not calculate the measure from whichever timeline page happened to load.
7. With paused/closed work, an agreed quiet period, no-contact restrictions and a missionary who records nothing, verify no auto-reopen, task, reminder, quota, penalty or outreach authorization is created.
8. In actual browser tests, cover keyboard, screen reader, touch/mobile keyboard, 320 CSS-pixel reflow/equivalent zoom, long multilingual names/labels, RTL and light/dark themes. Reach the definition/source and recovery actions; distinguish pending, error, no evidence and unknown-date outcomes without relying on color or a disappearing toast.
9. Run a focused staff/missionary comprehension pilot using these scenarios; record mistaken interpretations and change the actual labels/composition. No measured usability, beauty, speed or adoption result is yet claimed.
