## Ratified decision and amendments

**Q57-C01 — Selected capability and owner.** Add optional Report a problem to the actual P28 organization-resource reader journey, with real handling by currently qualified resource curators in existing resource management. The first reader cohort remains the governed missionary audience; general staff-reference readership remains a separate explicit coverage item. P28 owns each report, its exact publication context, handling and source history; P4/P12 owns identity and current permission, P29 the underlying bytes/custody, and the existing resource owner retains publication/review/withdrawal. Reporting creates no resource-authoring, ordinary-file-comment, access-request, personal-support conversation or public-feedback power. Preserve qualified Help for personal assistance and cases outside this purpose. The stronger simpler alternative is existing capable Help alone; A deliberately adds contextual reporting because it preserves the subject and maintenance responsibility. No measured ministry demand, time saving or cost reduction is claimed.

**Q57-C02 — One short deliberate form.** Place Report a problem beside the permitted resource's existing information/actions without interrupting ordinary Open/Download. It is available when safe source metadata and current reporting authority identify the publication even if its body/download cannot open; a successful preview, download or proof of reading is not required. Use one labeled bounded plain-text field, What is wrong with this resource?, with useful example and purpose/privacy help. Identify the actual handling audience and explain that reporting is not anonymous; do not request known identity/contact details again or reveal a staff directory. One useful sentence can suffice. Reject blank/whitespace-only, invalid or over-limit input with an associated error; do not silently truncate. No category questionnaire, attachment, screenshot, rating, rich editor, URL fetch, mention delivery, AI quality judge or routine second confirmation is required. Send report is deliberate; typing, blur, IME composition, selection or opening a resource does not submit. Cancel before submission creates no report. No mandatory field implies that Core can verify the truth of a reader's allegation.

**Q57-C03 — Exact target and new-admission boundary.** A new report binds an exact currently reportable organization/resource/publication and its relevant version/language/purpose basis, with trusted actor and actual source occurrence time. The server resolves the proposed target; caller titles, links, resource IDs and timestamps are not authority. Require the relevant publication/report basis to remain eligible at admission after waits. If it changed, preserve lawful draft text, show only current permitted context and obtain deliberate refreshed intent; never silently attach the observation to a successor. A stale or denied link cannot enumerate hidden resources or create a historical-report right. This strict rule applies to NEW reports, not recovery of a committed original or separately authorized maintenance of an already received report. An external-resource report identifies the approved reference actually held and the reader's stated observation, not an immutable snapshot or verification of the remote website. No arbitrary fetch, screenshot or document-body snapshot is added.

**Q57-C04 — Coherent receipt, responsibility and three states.** Use only Open, Handled and Withdrawn as the business handling states; privacy restrictions, retention, loading and unknown outcome remain independent facts. The initial state is Open. Reader presentation may say Awaiting review after a genuine Report received confirmation; never confuse that with an in-flight send or Q55's resource-review status. Source admission atomically records the exact report/body revision, original actor/time/target, Open state and revision, a qualified durable handling route, mandatory business history and the original operation result. A source-owned route need not identify an online individual, but it must be capable of current authorized handling and stewardship takeover. An unowned/unqualified route cannot yield a false successful receipt. Show pending reports in existing resource management with permitted context and useful filters. This is a projection of the authoritative report, not a separately mutable task or copied handling state; no new outbox is required merely to read committed reports. No new general inbox, assignment engine, personal-owner lock, task per report or mandatory In progress state. Reading a report does not claim it, lock reader edits or manufacture a handling event. Curator absence or departure uses supported source stewardship with current authorization; it never grants every staff member the report body.

**Q57-C05 — A complete permitted return journey.** Provide a small Your reports view inside Resources, plus contextual access to the reader's own reports when appropriate. It contains bounded current-authorized report records, not a global inbox, unread obligation, conversation, subscription or archive of resource bytes. The owning source explicitly admits the reporter's minimal receipt/status projection and separately admits report text, title, outcome and history fields. A reader may retain a permitted minimal receipt/status after resource replacement or library withdrawal only while current independent report-purpose rights allow it; identity equality or retention alone grants nothing. Lack of current source context, membership/assignment where required, or a restrictive rule denies the affected projection. Where allowed, a neutral Resource unavailable label replaces a hidden title/link; report content/outcome may be withheld independently. No receipt link restores historical resource access or internal notes. Show actual original submission and relevant edited/handled/restored timestamps with localized display and clear meaning. Current report state is distinct from content safety, freshness, resource availability and proof a curator read it. Preserve known receipt when a secondary refresh fails.

**Q57-C06 — Correct an open report without rewriting history.** Permit the original reporter, under current independent report-edit authority, to edit their own Open report's text against the exact current report revision. Keep tenant, organization, publication target, original author and original receipt time immutable. The source retains attributable correction history and a new content/state basis; ordinary projections show only currently permitted text, not every prior private body. Editing remains possible after resource replacement/withdrawal only where current report-purpose rights permit it; it grants no source-byte access. A true canonical no-op, checked after original-result recovery and current authority/basis, creates no revision or business event. An intervening state/content change, including an Open→Handled→Open cycle, makes a stale edit conflict even when values look equal. A curator's stale handling form must refresh after a real edit. Staff cannot impersonate the reporter or silently rewrite their allegation. Wrong target uses deliberate withdrawal plus a new currently eligible report; changing a report's target is not an edit. New information after Handled is a new deliberate report on a currently eligible publication, with no automatic copying of protected old text or new chat thread.

**Q57-C07 — Withdrawal and recovery of an accidental withdrawal.** Permit the original reporter to deliberately withdraw their own Open report under current report-withdraw authority, with concise consequence copy. No mandatory reason essay or timed toast-only Undo. Open→Withdrawn removes ordinary pending handling but preserves the original report, actor/time and lawful history/obligations; it does not delete text, retract delivered copies, alter the resource or stop independently required privacy/safety/incident work. Handled is not an ordinary reader-withdraw state. Provide Restore report for an eligible Withdrawn report: current independent restoration authority, lawful retained latest text, permitted handling audience and a capable route must be re-proved. The reporter must be able to lawfully review and authorize the latest retained text being restored; unknown or withheld text is not treated as empty or silently restored. Withdrawn reports leave the ordinary pending-work view; any separate retained-record/body access still requires its actual current purpose. Restore creates a new attributed Open/state revision while preserving the original receipt/target and withdrawal history; it does not claim a fresh original submission, roll back newer facts, reopen resource access or restore redacted/disposed material. The original resource need not still be published when this existing report-maintenance purpose remains lawful. A source restriction or missing retained body can block restoration, with an actual supported source/privacy repair or fresh-report path where permitted. Privacy erasure and ordinary withdrawal are separate acts.

**Q57-C08 — Deliberate handling with an understandable outcome.** A currently qualified curator can Mark handled only for Open work, against the exact current report/content/state basis and current report-purpose authority. Require one concise Outcome for the reporter, explicitly labeled with its intended audience, containing a truthful safe disposition; no routine internal essay, taxonomy or extra approver. Examples can describe a corrected link, an issue not reproduced or no change needed, without requiring a particular outcome. The source atomically records the actual handler/time, assessed report revision, current handled outcome, state revision, required history and operation result. Current staff authority, not original authorship or last publisher identity, determines who may act. Seeing or opening a report is not handling. Mark handled does not publish, withdraw, reschedule, verify or Record review of the resource. Actual resource work uses its owner commands; if that work fails, do not claim it succeeded. A multi-command UI never silently reports both effects as atomic. The outcome is an attributed human statement, not automatically proven by a text classifier, and current disclosure still governs its delivery. No automatic per-report email or conversation is introduced.

**Q57-C09 — Correct mistaken handling without a workflow maze.** Provide two current-curator remedies. Correct outcome on Handled creates an attributable outcome correction against the exact current handled/report basis, with the correction attributable to its trusted action/context; require an additional short reason only when actual source policy calls for it, without inventing a reason or requiring a second field for an ordinary typo correction. Preserve the original handling fact, actor/time and old outcome under lawful history, and record the correction's actual actor/time. It does not create a second original handling or reset the report's receipt. Reopen on Handled is for a mistaken handling decision or work that actually remains: require a concise reason and current authority, lawful retained body, permitted handling audience and capable route, then record Open with a new revision. The earlier outcome remains historical and is no longer the current resolution. Neither operation silently restores resource availability or bypasses a privacy restriction; unknown outcome recovery never replays over a newer edit/withdrawal/reopen. Withdrawn cannot be handled or curator-reopened as an ordinary shortcut around the reporter's withdrawal; use its qualified Restore or independently required source incident remedy. No immutable irreversible Closed state, automatic close-on-publication, bulk resolution, approval chain or configurable workflow builder is required.

**Q57-C10 — Current Supabase Auth and one authorization owner.** Derive verified native principal, selected source-appropriate Tenant Authorization Context, tenant/environment, organization, actor and current action scope through trusted server context and P12. Require active assignment where that context is membership-backed, not a fabricated Party/member record for every principal. Session/token validity is separate from current capability, source eligibility, suspension and the actual revocation contract. Do not authorize from user_metadata, profile role, email, default/demo tenant, stale JWT membership, client-held report IDs or a pure capability resolver supplied with unproved snapshots. The application and database must agree on unforgeable scope; absent/mismatched context fails closed and pooled context is reset. Recheck current rights at protected reads and mutation admission after relevant waits. P12 remains the single fine-grained policy decision owner; use its prescribed coarse Tenant RLS rather than a second per-row membership/capability engine. Preserve R6's governed-read budget of at most one logical governance-row SELECT and one logical ledger UPDATE, including its declared physical-sharding carveout; this is not a one-query cap on all business data. Administrative/service/worker/import paths need their actual scoped authority and truthful initiator attribution, never possession of a secret key alone.

**Q57-C11 — Structural integrity and all database doors.** Use the source-owned packages/api command boundary for report, state, outcome and history mutations. These are trusted multi-fact effects, not direct browser status patches. Register and classify report fields at ADR-0209's shared static field-policy boundary; one conditional server resolver may only subtract from that projection. Do not add per-value provenance visibility, surface-local authority or a runtime policy engine. Preserve ADR-0210's stable code-owned capability meaning while qualifying the actual P12 grant/source integration; prepared metadata is not live authority. Enforce non-null admitted tenant/source/organization/publication/reporter/receipt references; same-tenant/source relations; valid Open/Handled/Withdrawn combinations; unique scoped operation effect; monotonic expected revision or equally proved ABA protection; and exact references from handling to assessed content. A current Handled state requires its qualified outcome/handler/time/basis; historical handled outcomes may remain after reopen without appearing current. Use appropriate composite keys/foreign keys, uniqueness/check constraints and restricted deletes where expressible; dynamic authority cannot be proved by static CHECK alone. Actual occurrence timestamps use timezone-aware instants; localized formatting does not change them. No money or floating-point money fields are introduced. Preserve immutable attribution/target and lawful history; deleting an Auth user, publication or parent must not silently cascade required report evidence or widen access. Lawful anonymization/erasure follows C13.

Inspect schema/table/column/sequence grants, explicit/default/PUBLIC/inherited EXECUTE, overloads/owners/search_path, views, triggers, SELECT/RETURNING and all insert/update/delete/upsert/MERGE old-row USING/new-row WITH CHECK behavior. **Q57-C12 — One original effect, exact concurrency and truthful recovery.** Bind a stable source-recognized semantic operation to actor/scope, action, target, expected relevant revision and exact intent. Different HTTP/callback correlation keys for that same intended act cannot multiply it. Recover a matching previously committed result under current disclosure before new eligibility, no-op, rate admission or fresh-write logic; changed intent under that identity conflicts. A returned original receipt describes that original act; a separately authorized current projection may differ. Fence late replay responses so they cannot overwrite newer visible report state. A lost response is unknown outcome, not permission to make a new report or send the same concern through another channel as a retry. Distinct deliberate reports remain possible even with equal text or the same reader/resource; do not globally deduplicate on content, person/resource or Handled state. Atomically commit each source effect with required history/result and handling references; multiple SDK calls are not assumed transactional. Serialize or compare-and-swap relevant report/content/state/outcome revisions and source restrictions, without tenant-wide locks or network work inside database locks. Relevant A→B→A transitions invalidate stale commands; unrelated resource metadata or curator presence does not globally stale work. Maintain minimum lawful uniqueness/result-or-reject evidence beyond ordinary logs/readable history, or safely reject expired/unverifiable original intents. Pruning, privacy repair or restore cannot make an old retry new work. Canonical no-ops create no fake history; real restores/reopens are new attributed state acts, never no-ops inferred from matching text.

**Q57-C13 — Privacy, retention and capable permanent repair.** A report can contain sensitive allegations or personal information even when the resource is broadly readable. Classify its body, identity, outcome and history for the actual report purpose, including applicable origin restrictions; resource readership does not grant other-reader report access, and reporter identity alone does not override later restrictions. Give no anonymity, unrestricted staff-directory, historical-byte or permanent-access promise. Support immediate authorized restriction of affected report fields/projections and a source/privacy/records correction or redaction path for mistakenly included sensitive content, including Handled and Withdrawn reports. Preserve only the lawful minimum event/actor/correction evidence; append-only history is not an excuse to keep exposed sensitive body versions forever. Redaction/erasure must cover current and retained revisions, outcomes, projections, caches, exports and applicable backups under actual policy. Restore/Reopen cannot recreate erased text from history or an old request. Retention/holds are source-owned and do not grant ordinary reading. Ordinary reader Edit/Withdraw is not physical deletion, and no generic admin role bypasses privacy authority. Keep raw bodies, private titles/names, tokens, typed links and sensitive search/filter values out of general logs, URLs, analytics/session replay and unqualified persistent browser stores. Authorized protected carriers retain their existing narrow contracts. Required source/privacy/incident work remains independent of report disposition, with a capable scoped product/operator path rather than routine SQL repair.
