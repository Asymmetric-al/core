# ADR0067 — Authorized recording of supported external events

Date: 2026-10-29. Status: **Q67 A and all required amendments fully ratified by Conrad.** Files and Documents / Phase 29 grooming. No actual occurrence, policy approval or native activation is claimed.

## Context and rationale

Q62 and Q66 govern records-policy selection/configuration, trustworthy trigger facts and correction. They do not themselves supply a new initial human producer for an event that happens outside software. Conrad selected optional authorized staff recording of supported external events. Existing-producer-only is a strong alternative when all real facts already have qualified sources; otherwise it creates unnecessary integration work or leaves the class unsupported.

Use one qualified contextual source command, with true provenance, exact occurrence identity, accurate time and current authority. Preserve cross-producer reconciliation, correction/retraction, current records impact and historical-dark behavior. These facts become durable inputs to future records decisions, so admission and recovery are costly to reverse and warrant an ADR. No new generic event registry or glossary entity is necessary.

## Decision

The following clauses are the fully ratified normative Q67 contract. They intentionally specify outcomes and boundaries without prescribing a new universal table, workflow engine, event registry or permission system.

**Q67-C01 — Optional source-owned capture.** Allow currently authorized tenant staff to record that an already-defined, supported external event actually occurred for its exact source subject and purpose, through the owning source's deliberate command. Reuse a suitable existing native human command or authoritative fact when available; keep qualified native and integration producers. This adds an input route, not arbitrary event definitions, policy-form date overrides or a generic status editor. It cannot mint contract termination, financial settlement, receipt, acceptance, Q22 fulfillment or Q65 reference End facts owned elsewhere.

**Q67-C02 — Tenant flexibility and real authority.** Tenants may use their existing qualified roles/delegations and supported source evidence rules to permit or restrict this route; no all-staff/admin grant, new mandatory configuration wizard, Asym support ticket or universal second approver is required. Initial recording, correction/retraction, policy editing, reviewing history, body access and disposition remain distinct actual source/P12 capabilities. Use a single actor when the true source permits. A policy editor, record viewer, uploader, workflow assignee or actor with a service credential is not thereby an event authority. No new public, donor, missionary or anonymous write route is granted.

**Q67-C03 — Truthful source admission.** The owning source validates the exact event meaning, subject, purpose, currently permitted evidence and accepted assertion at commitment. Preserve human/native/integration provenance and any supported evidence qualification distinctly. A staff report accepted for its declared records purpose is not proof of independent verification, legal validity or unrelated business completion. Source-required approval is honored; a successful save, attachment, checkbox, filename or signature-looking image cannot replace it. Relevant credible source conflict requires scoped resolution; arbitrary caller allegations cannot place a global hold or manufacture a conflict.

**Q67-C04 — Exact subject and honest impact.** Bind the typed source/event version and exact stable subject/occurrence identity within trusted tenant/environment scope; an empty or unknown scope never means all records with the same label. Use friendly names for display without making them identities. Show the permitted event/date/evidence and material current consequences before commitment, including that a late occurrence can make a policy already due. Explain automatic/manual mode where material without revealing hidden records/counts/hold reasons. Revalidate changed authority, subject, source basis and consequential impact; require renewed deliberate review when the submitted intent or material consequence changed. A read/preview is not authorization.

**Q67-C05 — Actual date and recorded time.** Preserve source-defined occurrence precision, calendar and timezone separately from trusted server-recorded time and actor. Date-only values remain dates rather than fabricated midnight instants; instant-based events use the source's required offset/zone semantics and reject unresolved ambiguous/nonexistent local times. Accept truthful late reporting when allowed. A future planned event is not an occurrence; compare under the declared source time basis, not an arbitrary UTC date or browser clock. Unknown/partial dates use only supported source semantics and truthful unresolved handling. Never default a missing occurrence to today, upload/edit time, an invented boundary or a convenient earlier date.

**Q67-C06 — Proportionate evidence and small UX.** Start in the relevant authorized source record with the subject and supported event known. Ask “When did [event] happen?” and only for missing evidence/reference the source actually needs; use an existing sufficient reference when allowed. Show a concise consequence summary and one specific “Record [event]” action. Preserve entered values for recoverable errors within current access, offer clear correction and cancel before submission, and avoid mandatory attachments, duplicate context, essays, second approval, global dashboards or blanket confirmation modals. Give truthful source-safe results: recorded; recorded with required processing pending; not recorded; or outcome unknown. Do not show final success before authoritative acceptance or “nothing will be deleted” when qualified automatic disposal may follow.

**Q67-C07 — Coherent commit and bounded follow-through.** At one real source transaction or proved equivalent fence, coherently accept the occurrence/current interpretation, necessary original outcome, attributed history and current safety/control effects, with durable required follow-through. Establish any necessary restrictive current-impact fence before old permissive downstream actions can proceed. Background impact may be bounded and partial; it cannot postpone a safety-critical restriction or pretend every record was processed. Keep short database locks out of external IO; a sequence of SDK calls, an authenticated wrapper or a dispatch ledger alone does not prove this boundary. Preserve current duty resolution and capable recovery when dependencies fail.

**Q67-C08 — Business identity, concurrency and retry.** Distinguish the actual business occurrence from caller operation identity and delivery attempt. Scope operation keys to trusted context/principal/action and complete semantic intent; a changed-intent retry conflicts. Recover an admitted matching original under current disclosure before reevaluating it as new work. Cross-actor/manual/native/integration reports of the same source occurrence reconcile to that fact without a duplicate clock or falsely attributing another actor's recording to the caller. A real repeated event remains possible under source-defined identity; subject/type/date alone is not a universal dedupe key, and a contradictory date is not automatically a new occurrence. Preserve provenance and source-defined precedence/equivalence; no last-arrival-wins, human-always-wins or new-key bypass. Expired unverifiable intent cannot be silently reaccepted. New commands, including apparent no-ops, require current authority and the relevant expected source basis; stale change-away-and-back intent does not become current because values match. A true complete semantic no-op creates no occurrence revision, new recording time or impact. New corroboration or changed evidence can be material despite the same date, without becoming a new occurrence. Original committed acceptance remains the original operation result; any current correction/retraction is a separately qualified current-state projection, never a rewritten original receipt or revival.

**Q67-C09 — Correction, retraction and mistaken retraction.** Provide real source-authorized correction and retraction of an exact admitted recording/current basis, with concise source-required reason/evidence, current impact and preserved original history. Correcting a date/evidence for the same occurrence preserves that identity. Retracting a recording removes reliance on that input; it does not assert the real event never happened, erase independent valid source evidence or automatically reset a clock to not-yet. Recompute current interpretation from the actual source evidence; disputed/unknown is distinct from false. A mistaken retraction can receive fresh current source acceptance for the same occurrence, linked to that history, without a new duplicate occurrence or replayed old command. Wrong subject/type requires correction of the mistaken scope and separate authorized admission to the correct scope, not reparenting immutable history. Required safe effects on both scopes must be accounted for. Retraction and required protective effects on the mistaken scope remain independently usable when unrelated correction or correct-target preparation is invalid, unavailable or unauthorized; do not require a successful replacement to stop mistaken reliance. Use sufficient permitted source basis without requiring unrelated body access or an extra upload. No generic Delete event/Undo or claimed restoration of lost bytes.

**Q67-C10 — Late facts, later records and historical workflows.** A truthful past occurrence can change current records applicability/eligibility through Q62/Q66 and Q63 safeguards. It must not silently enroll or replay historical workflows, send old notices, renew access, repeat collection or create unrelated outward effects. Preserve Workflow's explicit historical classification and separately authorized bounded backfill for such effects. Later matching records/uses resolve the existing real occurrence through their own current qualified applicability path; do not create a duplicate event to start them or blindly inherit a deadline from another class/use. Unknown event coverage is not proof of absence.

**Q67-C11 — Disposition and independent duties.** Event recording, correction, retraction, pending impact and verified physical disposition are separate facts. A reached deadline grants no disposal/hold-release authority. Preserve all Q62/Q63/Q66 current-policy, complete-use/copy/hold coverage, Stop, finite dispatch, possible-effect, recovery and restore rules, including races with already possible external deletion. An affected restriction must take effect at the controlling admission boundary; timeout or a new event revision cannot clear an unresolved possible effect. No universal grace period, blanket retain-forever or new per-file routine release is introduced. Missing producers, disputed facts and lawful privacy duties require capable resolution without inventing dates or bypassing obligations.

**Q67-C12 — Trusted Auth, scope and disclosure.** Derive actor/author/recorded time and trusted tenant/environment/source context from verified native identity and current P12/source authorization; permitted caller IDs are validated selectors. Check relevant current session/authority at consequential acts and after waits. A valid JWT, stale app metadata, user-editable metadata, UI role or demo profile does not prove current capability. Preserve qualified no-Party and NHI paths without inventing memberships; automation is not granted by this human route. Request-bound server clients and transaction-scoped database context must agree and reset on success/error/cancel/retry. The same decision controls reads, results, history, counts, exports, evidence, workers and recovery. Origin/CSRF protections apply to cookie-authenticated commands.

**Q67-C13 — Structural database and every-door safety.** Reuse the actual owning schema and enforce typed same-scope event/subject/evidence/current-interpretation/history/operation relationships, non-null required scope and null-complete composite references. Use tenant-aware referenced uniqueness/FKs or a proved equivalent where the actual placement requires it; arbitrary polymorphic IDs do not establish integrity. Enforce valid date-precision/state/revision combinations and required provenance. Original accepted facts/attribution are append-only for application actors; supported corrections change current interpretation through the owner. Prevent allowed updates from swapping tenant/subject/type, falsifying actor/approval, reopening stale state or clearing controls. Choose deliberate delete behavior so deleting an actor, policy or subject cannot cascade away required evidence/control history; lawful minimized disposition remains possible. Preserve the one P12/source decision layer and prescribed coarse Tenant RLS; missing, forged or mismatched application/database Tenant context fails closed. Qualify effective named/PUBLIC/default/inherited schema/table/column/sequence grants, column writes, policy composition, old `USING` and resulting `WITH CHECK`, SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/RETURNING/UPSERT/MERGE, trigger transformations, views/materializations, function owners/search paths/EXECUTE inheritance and overloads, service/BYPASS roles, REST/GraphQL/RPC, Storage, imports, workers and operator doors. No service key or function keyword is user authority. PostgreSQL's inherited predicate semantics apply; explicit-clause omission alone is not proof of a bypass. Zero affected rows is not accepted business success.

**Q67-C14 — Private evidence and projections.** Use existing source/P12 static classification and subtract-only projections for event names, source identities, dates, evidence/reasons, conflict details, impact, history and results. Recording permission does not grant unrestricted document access. Use only source-qualified evidence references; this feature does not add arbitrary URL fetching or private-body ingestion. Sanitize rendered input and allow only qualified link handling. Keep bodies, tokens, sensitive reasons, private URLs and locations out of technical telemetry. Prevent cross-tenant unions, public-cache fallback, cached authorization and late-response exposure after account/tenant/source changes. No persistent restricted offline command queue is introduced. Business history, source evidence, audit and technical logs have distinct lawful retention/minimization; append-only does not mean keep personal data forever. Search/export/backup/restore preserve the same restrictions and current controls.

**Q67-C15 — Accessible and resilient interaction.** Use shared Base UI/base-maia, specific visible labels and concise inline errors. Support keyboard/AT, visible unobscured focus, touch, 320-CSS-pixel ordinary reflow, zoom, Unicode/RTL/long names, localized unambiguous dates and low-bandwidth operation. Present source-required date precision/timezone clearly without unnecessary fields. Provide non-calendar entry for historical dates and announce meaningful recording/error/result status without focus theft or relying on color/hover. Passive render, GET, prefetch, blur, selection and IME composition do not submit; deliberate focused Enter/Space remains usable. On lost response recover the original operation; preserve safe input on validation/network errors, clear restricted data after loss of authority, and never blindly resubmit after sign-in or tenant switching. Closing the form after submission does not prove the command was canceled; preserve a safe route to the original result and reconcile a possibly accepted operation before new work. Canceling unsent input differs from correcting an accepted recording.

**Q67-C16 — Operations and finite scale.** Keep one owning source workflow and existing capable operator/source work surfaces. Required unresolved conflicts, partial impact, lost responses, missing capabilities and departed actors have authorized resolution/reassignment, with bounded durable processing and safe retry/reconciliation. Protect tenant fairness and source-qualified batch/lock/timeout/retry limits; use tenant/subject/operation indexes, bounded setwise authorization and stable paginated projections. Do not scan every file in a synchronous request or add a global queue/ledger when the qualified owner can supply the effect. Require measured finite input/evidence, impact/fanout, replay/control retention and recovery profiles before activation; no unmeasured vendor cap or “fast/scalable” claim. Preserve P12's logical/physical/restricted-tier placement distinctions and its governed-read R6 bound: at most one governance-row SELECT plus one ledger UPDATE logically, with the documented physical-shard carve-out. Restricted hard-deny budgets remain unstriped, conditional and enforced before egress; zero allowance denies and uniformity/safety takes precedence. This is not a cap on all mutation SQL. Current per-page/chunk projection is stable, unique and setwise.

**Q67-C17 — Migration, evolution and rollback.** Inventory existing authoritative facts and all readers/writers/consumers before enabling this route. Version event meaning/precision and source command schemas; labels and renamed fields do not define identity. Do not backfill occurrences from upload/edit time, task status, filenames, policy labels or missing rows. Qualify known legacy facts with provenance; unknown/conflicting legacy data remains explicit. Install compatible constraints/guards/readers before new writers; validate old-code/new-schema and new-code/old-schema behavior, unsupported versions and paused/retracted facts. Feature disable stops new capture while preserving safe reads, correction/reconciliation and required current restrictions. Rollback cannot delete accepted history, reopen old writers or revive historical effects; roll forward where the old runtime cannot interpret new accepted facts. Restored snapshots replay durable current controls before affected effects resume.

**Q67-C18 — Proof and traceability.** Require the positive/negative/boundary/authorization/concurrency/migration/accessibility and production-shaped proof below for the actual source profile, including one authentic staff journey and restricted-role denial. Analytical traces, static source reads and documentation checks are distinct from executable database, hosted/provider and observed-user proof. Trace Q67 clauses to its ADR, source-owned OpenSpec/design/task amendments and acceptance IDs during explicit publication, then actual ticket/implementation/test/release evidence; preserve source ownership and generated-view maintenance. No fake ticket ID, checked implementation task or legal/native qualification is supplied by this review. All mandatory gates remain gates, not monitoring substitutes.

**Q67-C19 — Scope, glossary and recording stage.** Q67 A and this complete 19-clause required-amendment package are fully ratified, including the complete category review, journeys, analytical traces, proof obligations and operating controls. Preserve all Q01–Q66 and the Q66 consequential amendments. ADR0067 is warranted by durable source admission, provenance/retraction and current-effect boundaries; it is adopted by this full ratification. No new glossary term is necessary: “source business occurrence” and “recording” are explained distinctly here; Workflow's existing Occurrence remains a node activation and is not renamed or reused as a new Files entity. Preserve both glossaries. No prior governing clause requires supersession: Q62/Q66 require trustworthy facts and correction without forbidding this additional qualified producer. Formal source/OpenSpec/design/tasks publication and tickets retain their explicit /to-prd and /to-issues stages. Full ratification authorizes recording this package and researching one Q68 question without selecting its answer. No actual GF policy/event, new legal period, native activation, implementation, hosted mutation, outreach, deployment or commit/push is inferred.
