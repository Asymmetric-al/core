## Ratified decision and amendments

Prove the original lawful admission and ordinary unfinished expiry while the
source remains genuinely unfinished. Make a new finite prospective source
decision for the same intended recipient and continuing original purpose;
preserve old ends, receipts, acceptance and adverse history. Unknown status,
proof failure, binding shortening, revocation, purpose expiry or an independent
intake prohibition cannot be relabeled ordinary expiry. Removed adverse causes
do not automatically restore rights. Apply holds and temporary restrictions at
their real scope without inventing universal access or denial.

Require the exact current source issuance capability, P12 administrative scope/
live assignable ceiling and actual continuing dependencies at the owning mutation
boundary. Generic staff/read/reviewer/support powers, old sender status and
service credentials are insufficient. Staff administration need not disclose
private original contents, and the old expired recipient grant need not pass
its former current-read check to authorize this new administrative decision.

Bound reviewed actions by original admitted requirement/purpose lineage and
current eligibility. Eligible pending requirements can admit first submissions
or exact immutable Q03 correction successors. Accepted/frozen writes, new
requirements/purposes/recipients and historical bytes stay excluded. Current
own-file reading may include accepted files where independently permitted.
Declare completed-read scope separately; neither writes nor expired grants
confer additional reading or widen a narrow reviewed subset.

Under the explicit prospective Q10 successor, actual eligible normal fulfillment
while fresh authority exists and is valid can admit the declared completed view
from eligible current own-submissions. Stop ordinary writes and resolve the end
at no later than fulfillment plus 720 elapsed hours under genuine caps/current
restrictions. Stage-only ends are not overall caps; early completion can yield
an earlier final read end. Lawful atomic admission/fulfillment can qualify with
proved causal eligibility; timestamp equality alone is insufficient either
way. Gap/completion-first cases receive no retroactive eligibility. Recover
timely durable outcomes from original evidence without observer-time resets.
Q08 further fresh-read eligibility is not automatically broadened.

Preserve stable deliberate effect identity, bound reviewed material input and
immutable first-admission outputs. Serialize the owners' relevant state changes
and recheck current source/issuer/recipient/time after waits. Coherently preserve
source effect, required durable audit and follow-up intent. Lost responses
recover the same effect under current disclosure; providers run outside locks.
An old unadmitted upload/correction/command cannot borrow new authority. A new
authorized deliberate submission may reuse exact qualified custody only under
its source contract, with truthful receipt and review lineage. An ambiguous
finalization response alone does not justify deleting potentially admitted bytes.

Use effective tenant-aware constraints, RLS/grant/service-path qualification
and Q04/Q05 current protection on every private metadata/byte door. Cache
invalidation is not a permission fence; a general best-effort log is not required
durable admission evidence. Current proof, representations, delivery and source
authority remain separate, as do independent source processing and retention.

Keep the existing accessible Base UI/base-maia request context, one meaningful
staff review/action and source-owned help. Explain actual received work, next
actor and permitted actions. Commit alone does not mean usable access, a resolved
end or notification; show actual timing once resolved, qualified terms/known caps
otherwise. No arbitrary picker, routine extra confirmation/approver, essay,
forced account/download, unnecessary reupload, automatic message or general drive follows.

Fresh duration/anchor/activation/unused bounds and repetition/overlap require
separate founder decisions before activation. Do not import another sixty days,
unlimited repeats or a one-use lifetime cap. Explicitly integrate the native
participant/source/P12 and Q10 successor, preserve specialized owners and adopt
prospectively. Qualify real roles, SQL/races, private serving, mixed versions,
restore and intended-user/browser/AT journeys with numeric operational budgets.
No new permission engine, scheduler, retention scheme or clone workaround.
