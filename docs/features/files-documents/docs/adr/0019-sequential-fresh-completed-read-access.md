## Ratified decision and amendments

Allow the exact currently source-authorized staff actor to approve another
fresh completed-read period after eligible ordinary expiry of the relevant
preceding period for the same intended recipient and lawful Q16 completed
admission. Preserve original completed proof, relevant predecessor/current
lineage, actual ending causes and current purpose/authority. An ancient expired
root, a copied request, missing history or no active-row result cannot create
eligibility or bypass later adverse facts. Restrictions retain their real scope.

Each new deliberate admission uses Q17/Q18's fixed source-issued period of
up to 720 elapsed hours, with the actual earlier-limited end, including unused
access. There is no preset lifetime grant-count or cumulative-time ceiling,
mandatory waiting gap, automatic renewal, pre-expiry extension or future
reservation. No overlapping fresh read periods may exist in the same tenant/
environment/source/request/recipient/original-completed-admission context,
including disjoint subsets. Other recipients and independent rights retain
their owners. Do not terminate a valid period merely to make room for another.
Legitimate revocation/remediation remains source-owned; its adverse ending
cannot be relabeled ordinary expiry. No uninterrupted access or later approval
is promised.

Each freshly reviewed subset is the original Q16 completed member/submitted-
original-version/action/Q05-context ceiling intersected with current eligibility
and this review. Do not union previous grants or silently ratchet the ceiling
to the last discretionary narrow subset. Genuine binding limits remain.
The request stays completed and read-only; fulfillment, receipts, accepted/
frozen evidence, correction lineage, immutable originals, retention/holds/
disposal and independent purposes remain unchanged. Retained files alone are
not a sufficient reason for another admission or a personal archive.

Recover a matching committed intent on its exact original lineage and terms,
under current disclosure, before applying new-admission rules. A later grant
does not replace its historical result. A failed response after admission is
still a committed effect; unknown commitment requires reconciliation. Only a
confirmed pre-admission failure/rollback establishes no effect. Changed payloads,
new transport keys and uncertainty cannot manufacture another approval.

For a genuinely unadmitted intent, revalidate material reviewed predecessor/
source state and exact current issuer capability, administrative scope and
live assignable ceiling at the existing source/P12 mutation fence. Atomically
enforce the same-context nonoverlap rule across different staff keys, subsets
and nonexistent-next-row cases. A stale intent cannot silently advance after
an intervening period ends. Do not park an ineligible command for automatic
later issuance. Qualified checks after ordinary waits remain; no new browser-
click clock or artificial cooldown is introduced. Preserve coherent required
effect/audit/follow-up evidence and keep provider work outside source locks.

Protect trusted relationships, required finite state, semantic uniqueness,
historical outputs and actor attribution across effective database grants,
USING/WITH CHECK, policy composition and direct/view/RPC/definer/service/job/
storage paths. An allowed update/upsert/parent change cannot retarget, widen
or reset a period. Each use requires current recipient proof, purpose and
scope plus the exact mapped continuing grantor/delegation dependency; do not
invent blanket survival or revocation on every issuer role change. An old
expired/revoked credential cannot become sufficient by resolving to a newer
grant. Compatible proof reuse remains allowed only under its owner contract.

Reuse the existing **Grant read access** action and completed context. Staff
review the safe eligible purpose/subset and standard timing terms once, with
estimates labeled before confirmed issuance and actual result/end afterward.
Material changes require meaningful refresh; ordinary time passing alone does
not. Distinguish new issuance, current use, proof, representation, delivery and
unknown outcomes. Keep useful safe help, partial/status-only access, localized
actual dates/zones, shared Base UI/base-maia and accessible mobile/low-bandwidth
recovery. Preserve neutral unqualified responses and protected diagnostics.

Flexibility adds no arbitrary quota, forced account, mandatory essay, routine
second approval, date editor, new request queue, renewal dashboard or automatic
notification. Existing genuine source requirements remain. Repair still-valid
access or perform independently permitted staff work without another grant.
Do not clone/reopen a request, force reupload or use an attachment fallback to
evade the source. Discretionary later access does not replace applicable
accessibility requirements for the current interaction.

Use bounded indexed source-owned current/predecessor/intent proof and authorized
paginated history, retaining P12 budgets and documented exceptions. A coherent
current relation is not another write authority. Required minimal history
follows its owner retention policy; no indefinite private payload log or full
chain reconstruction is needed. Mixed versions, import/export, deletion,
compaction and restore must preserve original effects/ends, fixed ceiling and
later restrictions. No invented root/date backfill or broad fallback. Existing
controls can stop unsafe new issuance while preserving history and current
serving authority; no rollback may erase a later grant or adverse fact.
