## Ratified decision and amendments

Allow the exact currently source-authorized staff actor to consider another
fresh unfinished admission after the relevant prior period's eligible ordinary
expiry, while the original request remains genuinely unfinished and current
recipient access serves its original purpose. Preserve original lawful
admission proof, relevant predecessor/current lineage and actual ending causes.
An ancient expired row, unchecked unfinished status, technical failure or
missing history is insufficient. Adverse endings, genuine source-specific
limits and independent prohibitions retain their actual scope and history.

Each new deliberate source effect uses Q14's up-to-720-hour fixed source-issued
period, including unused access. No overlapping unfinished fresh admissions
may exist within the same tenant/request/intended-recipient/original-admission
lineage, even for different narrow subsets. Other participants and independent
rights retain their existing rules. There is no preset count/cumulative-day
ceiling, automatic renewal, pre-expiry extension, reserved future grant or
mandatory waiting gap. Another approval or uninterrupted access is not promised.

Every new reviewed subset stays within original purpose/requirements and
current eligibility. Do not union expired rights or treat a previously narrow
grant as an invented permanent global ceiling. Actual binding restrictions
remain. Accepted/frozen writes, unrelated requirements/purposes/recipients
and historical-byte access remain excluded. Q13/Q07 causal completion,
qualified atomic admission/fulfillment and original-outcome recovery are
unchanged. Completion ends this unfinished lane; Q08 is not broadened.

Recover an already committed matching deliberate intent on its exact original
lineage and terms, subject to current safe disclosure, even when newer grants
exist. Do not substitute the latest grant or present historical success as
current access. For a genuinely unadmitted intent, revalidate its reviewed
predecessor/material source state, current authority and absence of overlapping
rights at the owning source/P12 boundary. A stale intent cannot silently
advance through an intervening grant's eventual expiry. Relevant races must
have a coherent valid result, including distinct keys and no live-row queries.
Use existing source fences and durable evidence/recovery; no lock framework
or separate renewal engine is prescribed.

Keep one meaningful **Grant access to continue** action in the existing request.
Show safe relevant prior outcome, actual current need and subset, actual end
and next actor. Keep grant result, present usability, proof and notification
distinct. Staff can finish independently authorized review without regranting
recipient access; legitimate readback need does not require a fabricated upload.
Preserve receipts and usable material without unnecessary reupload. Use shared
Base UI/base-maia, accessible proof/focus/status and mobile/low-bandwidth recovery.
No quota editor, extra account, mandatory essay, routine second approval,
renewal dashboard, automatic reminder or general file-drive behavior follows.

Retain trusted relationships, immutable history, required audit and effective
old/new-row and privileged-path checks under Q13. Use bounded indexed current
source/intent access and separately authorized paginated history; a coherent
source-maintained current relation is not an independent write authority.
Missing required evidence cannot become an empty/new lineage. Retention,
holds, received facts and real source outcomes do not reset on each grant.
Prospective rollout/restore must preserve later adverse facts and reject old
code that cannot interpret the new repeated history safely.
