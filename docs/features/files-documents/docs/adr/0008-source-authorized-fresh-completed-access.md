**Status:** Ratified by Conrad, 2026-10-20: Q08 A, the exact corrected
decision, Q08-C01–C22, Q08-P01–P28 and all eight operational controls.
The reviewed disposition was Accept with required amendments; those amendments
are adopted grooming intent. Q01–Q07 remain ratified. No fresh duration/anchor,
repetition/overlap rule, notification, implementation or activation is selected.

Conrad selected fresh staff-approved read access after ordinary completed-access
expiry. The ratified refined capability is a deliberate new source decision
within the original request's permitted scope, preserving the old grant and

provides governed assistance without new issuance; A offers a controlled
in-product response to a legitimate later need at the cost of a consequential
authorization command and its qualification.

## Ratified decision and amendments

Prove the original ordinary admission and its eligible ordinary expiry.
Evaluate current eligibility to issue new access under a newly qualified
source policy; do not require the expired grant to pass its old read check.
The issuer needs P12's exact current administrative scope/assignable ceiling
and the source's issuance capability, distinct from file-reading or generic
staff permission. Apply the exact continuing issuer/delegation dependency;
do not guess survival or revocation on every staff role change.

Each fresh admission is for the same intended recipient and currently
permitted request purpose. Its reviewed scope is bounded by the original
ordinary completed-view admission, current eligible own-submission members,
actions and Q05-permitted context. It cannot widen through recent grants,
later files, mailbox matching or access logs. A narrower individual fresh
grant alone does not impose a new global restriction.

Old grants/ends, source fulfillment, correction lineage and history remain
unchanged. Do not bypass genuine binding shortening, revoked authority,
purpose/content-use expiry, disposal or other current restrictions. Fresh
access does not reopen intake, change acceptance, admit historical bytes,
create membership or grant resharing. Qualified repair of a representation
of the same authorized original remains separate.

Use the existing source context for one meaningful review and explicit
Grant read access action. Review source-authorized recipient/scope/actions,
qualified timing terms and controlling limits; show an exact end where already
determinable and when the selected rule resolves it. Material changes require
refreshed review, while ordinary time advancing under unchanged terms does not.
Administrative review need not expose original contents.

At the governing source/P12 mutation boundary, revalidate current issuer,
recipient, source scope, policy and relevant grant history after waits.
Preserve one stable deliberate issuance identity with immutable reviewed
payload; changed payload conflicts instead of inventing a replacement intent.
Retries and lost responses recover the same effect. Separate intentional
repetitions require the adopted atomic repetition/overlap checks. Required
source/audit/effect intent must remain durable; private replay results still
require current disclosure authority.

Keep issuance, current access, recipient proof, representation readiness and
any separately authorized notification distinct. A timeout is not proof of
failure; closing after dispatch is not cancellation. Wrong issuance needs
source-authorized revocation/remediation with preserved history and current
delivery fences, without a claim to recall disclosed bytes.

Recipients use Q01-qualified current entry to the same completed read-only
context. Keep an actual source-owned help/handoff route and accurate
pending/unknown/committed/blocked states. Preserve shared Base UI/base-maia,
accessible names/focus/status, localized timing and low-bandwidth behavior.
No arbitrary recipient/date picker, new portal, mandatory essay, duplicate
confirmation, universal extra approver, raw attachment fallback or automatic
email is selected.

Before activation, explicitly resolve fresh timing/activation/unused-admission
bounds, repetition/overlap policy, exact capability/type mapping and source
lifecycle dependencies. Q07's original 30 days is not an automatic new period.
Prove real-role RLS/service paths, source races, exact private serving,
recipient handoff, browser/AT behavior and safe prospective rollout/restore.
Retention/holds/disposal and independent source rights remain separate.
