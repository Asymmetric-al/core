## Ratified decision and amendments

For an eligible issued unfinished ordinary Private Document Request, the requesting source SHALL permit currently authorized staff to deliberately publish a prospective revision of participant-facing instructions for the same outstanding, not-currently-accepted Document Request Item and continuing document need. Keep its intended recipient, legitimate purpose, requested document type, requiredness and independent technical/specialist constraints unchanged. The source qualifies what is the same need; arbitrary prose, a client label or an automatic semantic classifier cannot authorize a different demand, waiver, schema/form edit or stronger assertion. Q30 explanation and Q23 feedback cannot substitute for the actual revision command. Generic reviewer, task, file-read, assignment, administration and Q36 addition powers alone are insufficient.

Preserve three distinct facts: the immutable published instruction basis associated with an earlier receipt or review, the currently authoritative instruction revision, and any later deliberate review against its exact current basis. Publish attributable successor content rather than overwriting the earlier instructions or relabeling submissions. Drafting is inert; it does not alter participant demand, current review selection or fulfillment. Publishing exactly unchanged current material creates no new business revision. Deliberately correcting back to older wording, when currently permitted, is a new prospective act on the current basis, not restoration of old scope, decisions or clocks.

Existing durably received evidence remains received under its actual submitted-under basis. Revision creates no new bytes, receipt, Submission Correction, participant affirmation, Request changes decision, rejection or acceptance. Preserve Q03 current-candidate and corrected-predecessor fences and independently applicable review concerns. Retained current evidence may be deliberately reassessed under the explicit new requirement basis through Q21/Q23 without fabricated reupload; ordinary file evidence still requires qualified staff acceptance. Instruction publication supersedes the instruction criterion only under its source-authoring authority; it does not change historical Q21/Q23 decisions or grant review-resolution power. Determine current guidance applicability through qualified source rules, preserving independent concerns without retaining a concern whose only binding criterion has lawfully ended as a hidden blocker. Any required human resolution or reassessment remains a distinct current act by an authorized reviewer; no automatic concern classifier or new per-concern platform is required. Show the real next work and actor, including staff reassessment when needed, rather than resetting every item status or blaming the recipient by default.

Coherently establish the revision, its actual qualified handling route, relevant review coverage, prospective scope/currentness consequence, required history and durable reevaluation/recovery intent. For required participant work, apply an explicit source/P12/Q01 instruction-scope successor within the valid original or fresh period, retaining its exact end, reviewed subsets, earlier caps and current restrictions. Reconcile Q13/Q15/Q36 lineage rules explicitly for this same-item instruction successor; Q36 does not itself grant revision authority. After ordinary expiry, required participant work needs separately authorized actual fresh admission for the revised basis under existing eligibility and nonoverlap rules. No uncommitted promise of a later grant or hidden inaccessible demand is publication of usable work.

A qualified participant route does not require the recipient to be online, acknowledge the revision, finish proof exchange, receive email or successfully render a page when staff publish. A qualified route means actual admitted authority and a proved capable source handling path, not continuous presentation/provider availability or prior evidence acceptance. A recoverable technical delay may retain known publication and owned reassessment/recovery with truthful availability; missing or unknown required authority or a fundamentally absent route cannot be relabeled a delay. Required Q01 proof/current source authority still gates each protected use, with qualified proof reuse where permitted. Independently lawful current staff evidence/review may proceed without recipient renewal where no participant act or assertion is required. Show actual responsibility and participant availability, preserving independently valid old rights; if participant action later becomes necessary, establish its actual authorized route or capable source remedy before demanding it. Reuse valid Q24 coverage and qualify only the revised declared work under Q25; do not rerun all original issuance or require a roster, presence check or extra approver.

Order publication against relevant current instructions, evidence/correction, prepared review, Q30 context, Q33 removal, source restrictions and actual Q22/Q34 terminal acts through one qualified source boundary or equivalent version/fence protocol. Acceptance, removal, freeze or closure first denies an ineligible ordinary revision; publication first fences materially affected stale decisions and operations for deliberate current review. Not-currently-accepted does not mean never historically accepted, but this action cannot clear acceptance to create eligibility. A transfer or open form is not durable receipt; preserve receipt-first facts, revalidate affected unadmitted work and never retarget an old command to the new revision. Preserve unrelated lawful work. Drafts create no completion hold, optional nonblocking work remains nonblocking, and relevant publication/reassessment durably arranges complete-predicate reevaluation without another routine Complete action.

A revision does not reset any requested-by target or access clock, erase legitimate earlier lateness, fabricate pre-revision fault or create an overlapping/fresh period automatically. Record actual publication after relevant waits. Normal completed access requires actual timely qualified fulfillment and its declared member/version/action/context scope; later Q08/Q16/Q19 recovery remains within that actual fulfillment-created ceiling and current restrictions, never today's instructions or a union of historic grants. An individual narrow admission stays narrow without becoming an invented permanent global ceiling for later separately authorized admissions within their real source universe. Independent source processing after recipient expiry may finish work without reviving participant access.

Use one clear Update instructions flow in the existing source/item UI. Let staff inspect permitted current wording, review the proposed text and relevant consequence, then deliberately publish. Show truthful published, unchanged, uncommitted, conflict or outcome-unknown states and capable recovery; preserve permitted input/reading context/focus and discard stale cross-user/tenant/request results. No mandatory essay, read receipt, reply, second approval, per-file ritual, account, download, new diff engine or private offline archive follows. Updating a page does not prove awareness or understanding, and this decision selects no new email/default/reminder/channel or fake existing source event.

Retain original instruction content and references under their actual source retention, hold and privacy rules; retention is not perpetual participant disclosure. ## Ratified glossary definition

Full Q37 ratification adds this exact term to the feature CONTEXT.md, retaining
the23 existing definitions including Q36's exact Fresh Unfinished refinement.
It names a distinct business fact, not a new table or versioning platform.

**Document Request Item Instruction Revision**:
An attributable, source-published version of the participant-facing instructions
for one Document Request Item. It distinguishes current instructions from the
basis of an earlier submission or review and is distinct from a submitted file
version, Submission Correction or review decision.
_Avoid_: latest file, corrected submission, review approval, overwritten instructions

## Consequences and qualification

The ordinary flow is one reviewable Update instructions action in the current
item context. Staff can correct a same-need omission or excessive detail while
keeping earlier requests and receipts true. Existing qualified proof, team
coverage and evidence can be reused. Current participant instructions and the
submitted-under basis remain understandable without a generalized diff engine,
forced acknowledgment, essay or extra approval. Drafts do not hold fulfillment.

Unaccepted file evidence still needs Q21 acceptance, even if revised instructions
now make existing bytes sufficient. Current qualified staff reassessment can
use those bytes without fabricated reupload. Independent concerns and corrected-
predecessor fences remain; revision itself neither rejects nor accepts evidence,
creates a Request changes event nor enrolls recipients in a notice purpose.
An actual independently qualified source event retains its own exact meaning
and controls, including when it occurs alongside publication.

The cost is real source/access/currentness/provenance qualification and usable
changed expectations. Current-not-accepted is not never-historically-accepted,
but this action cannot unaccept an item to create eligibility. A mistaken
revision is corrected through a new currently eligible act; later stages need
capable source/privacy remediation, not universal Undo or old-right restoration.
Retention and historic-body access remain distinct. Mutable linked resources
do not become immutable snapshots merely because their URLs were saved.

P01–P30 require real SQL/effective roles/all protected doors, controlled races,
faults/replays, source reassessment, finite production-shaped budgets, mixed
versions/restore and composed user/AT evidence. Prospective legacy baselines
must prove current authority and truthful capture, leaving unknown prior basis
unknown. Bind actual operating owners before activation; stop new revisions
without erasing admitted work. No entire future Studio, generic engine,
automatic classifier, notification platform or new dependency is required.
