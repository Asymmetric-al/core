## Context and evidence

The [researched question](../../grill/phase29-q21-question.md) compared staff
acceptance for ordinary native file-evidence items with adding an upfront,
source-declared collection-only mode. Conrad selected staff acceptance and
requested excellent UX/UI without overengineering.

The founder brief, Q03, Q06/Q10 and Workflow Forms and Evidence already separate
receipt, technical safety, review, source satisfaction and fulfillment. GEN-06
is an adopted reviewer-route precedent, not a qualified native implementation.
Comparable products support contextual review and separate statuses, while
optional-approval/collection-first examples remain legitimate counterevidence
to a universal human gate. A is selected for the ordinary native cohort; no
Asym workload or usability benefit has been measured.

## Ratified decision and amendments

For ordinary native Document Request Items whose stated requirement is file
evidence, require a deliberate acceptance by currently authorized staff before
that evidence can satisfy the item. The source records the exact current
item/requirement/purpose and source-relevant submitted evidence set with trusted
attribution. This is necessary, not sufficient to bypass required technical
qualification or the source's remaining criteria. Acceptance is not legal
authenticity, application/financial approval, public release, access authority
or whole-request completion.

Use the existing meaningful request review and one clear acceptance action.
An item can contain several supporting files; do not impose approval per file,
preview clicks/dwell, forced download, mandatory essay, routine second
confirmation, extra reviewer or new approval engine. Existing exact applicable
staff acceptance for the same source/item/requirement/evidence/purpose can be
recognized without duplicate work or effects. Equal bytes, another request or
another use cannot transfer approval. Existing authorized non-file/waived/
not-required outcomes and specialist policies remain unchanged.

The reviewed basis includes actual source-relevant instructions and evidence
dependencies. Required safety/admissibility must hold. Quarantined or unknown
required-check material cannot become accepted to bypass checks. Unsupported
preview is not automatically business rejection: a separately qualified,
currently authorized inspection path may support review if its coverage is
sufficient. Partial rendition, OCR/AI, filename or open/download telemetry
cannot establish that omitted content was reviewed. No automatic original or
external-viewer/provider URL fallback.

Use the exact P12 source-review capability, not staff label, task assignment,
read permission or recipient-grant administration. Trusted server context owns
tenant/environment, actor, accepted-by, source time and audit attribution.
Same-scope relationships, immutable decision fields, valid states and permanent
effect identity need real keys/constraints and effective grants/RLS USING and
WITH CHECK, views/RPC/storage and privileged-path proof. Existing source-specific
separation of duties remains without a universal new second-actor rule.

Acceptance shares Q03's authoritative boundary with correction and relevant
source/authority changes. Correction-first immediately fences the predecessor;
acceptance-first prevents ordinary recipient correction from superseding it.
No stale decision silently rebases to new bytes. Preserve independent items
unless a real aggregate dependency requires coordination. Coherently commit
the decision, authoritative state and required durable history/follow-up;
provider work and expensive processing remain outside locks.

Recover a matching committed intent before treating its new item state as a
failed attempt, under current disclosure. Changed material conflicts. Two
reviewers or transport keys cannot create duplicate current acceptance or
effects. The original actor/time remain intact when another caller reads or
recovers the result; a retry or reuse must not impersonate a reviewer or rerun
downstream work. A successful acceptance with a failed secondary refresh is
still a known source success; unknown commitment requires reconciliation.

Historical acceptance, current applicability, current visibility and byte
availability are separate facts. Later reviewer departure/read-permission
loss alone does not erase lawful prior acceptance; actual source-defined
continuing dependencies, safety findings or requirement changes still govern
current use/remediation. Preserve later adverse evidence and original history.
No silent unaccept, predecessor revival, rewritten completion or reopened work.

Whole-request fulfillment uses its source's actual remaining predicate and
durable occurrence. This decision selects neither an extra Complete control
nor automatic whole-request completion. Preserve actual acceptance/fulfillment
times after relevant waits, never backdate to upload/scan/browser time or a stale
transaction-start timestamp. Independently authorized staff work can continue
after participant expiry without extending or reviving recipient access.

Keep receipt/checking/awaiting-staff/accepted/remaining-work messages accurate.
Timely recipients are not missing or overdue because staff is still reviewing.
Preserve reading position, notes and focus when evidence changes; do not silently
swap content. Use accessible persistent outcomes, clear names, current safe
help and supported mobile/AT/localized/low-bandwidth recovery. No unapproved
offline acceptance/private device cache or new name/address/account burden. Preserve
available document text/semantics and qualify the actual inspection path for
supported reviewers; accessible viewer controls alone are insufficient. Disclose
inaccessible/incomplete content and use only an existing sufficient qualified
alternative, without promising new OCR, translation or format support.

## Ownership, scope and qualification

P29 custody owns technical byte facts; the requesting source owns item acceptance,
satisfaction, remediation and fulfillment. P9 tasks and P6/P17 messages project
or follow separately authorized source effects. Q20 fresh-access email does not
select review-result mail. No collection-only profile, waiver power, rejection
taxonomy, reviewer assignment/pool, bulk acceptance, global policy editor or
whole future Workflow Studio prerequisite is added.

At authorized publication add the explicit ordinary-native prerequisite atop
Q03-C05/C10/C11/C16 and Q06/Q10, reconcile source/P12/P9 and applicable GEN-06
bindings, then update declared generated projections. Preserve specialist
owners and Q01–Q20. Record **Document Request Item Acceptance** as one domain
term, distinct from receipt, technical safety, current satisfaction and whole
fulfillment; the prior seventeen definitions remain unchanged.

Before activation require Q21-P01–P18's real authorization/database/concurrency/
replay/inspection/recovery/restore/workload/browser/AT/user outcomes and the
necessary adjacent source bindings. Legacy upload/scan/task flags cannot
backfill human acceptance. Mixed versions/restore preserve decisions and later
restrictions; scoped stopping and roll-forward cannot erase downstream truth.
Use bounded indexed current-proof checks and owned repair, not unbounded
history scans, provider-console intervention or direct database edits. Keep
minimal decision evidence and sensitive content under their actual retention
owners; history is not indefinite storage permission.
