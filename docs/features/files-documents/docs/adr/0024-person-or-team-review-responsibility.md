visibility does not secure underlying records. These are product comparisons,
not measured Asym user preference or proof of faster review.

## Ratified decision and amendments

For ordinary native document review, the requesting source may designate an
eligible person or an explicitly qualified team route as accountable for the
specified pending review work. A qualified team may remain responsible while
a currently authorized human performs the applicable Q21 acceptance or Q23
Request changes action, without a mandatory personal claim or nominal transfer.
Every substantive decision retains its actual human actor and exact current
source/evidence/review basis. Specialist individual-review and separation-of-
duties requirements remain authoritative.

Responsibility, membership, task assignment, participant contact and review
permission are distinct. Responsibility grants no access by itself, and a
responsibility change does not universally revoke independently valid access.
The source/P12 boundary checks current administration authority, destination
qualification and actual reviewer authority separately. Real membership,
grant, clearance or source-policy changes affect subsequent actions at their
applicable scope; they do not rewrite lawful historical decisions.

Reuse valid source responsibility and qualified defaults. Preserve deliberate
handoffs against stale defaults and projections; apply actual source-defined
policy changes through the qualified source transition. No creator/first-row/
all-admin fallback, new automatic routing policy or universal snapshot-versus-
dynamic rule is selected. If responsibility or needed eligibility is missing,
ambiguous, invalid or cannot be established, represent the actual condition,
deny only dependent protected actions and provide a capable source-owned
recovery route. A team name or task row alone is insufficient. Temporary
absence and lookup failure do not prove an empty team.

Preserve one coherent source responsibility and logical work identity,
attributable changes, received evidence, prior human decisions, correction
relationships and fulfillment history. Order material handoff/review races
through the source; durable retry and delayed projection must not restore
obsolete responsibility or authority. A coordination-only update must not
force unnecessary reupload, reinspection or review confirmation.

Use the existing request/My Work experience, with clear person/team identity,
truthful current status, predictable accessible changes and safe recovery.
Keep participant help distinct from private routing, rosters and review
history. Preserve all existing source completion rules, specialist boundaries,
recipient rights and clocks. Q24 adds no global issuance/intake/completion gate,
permission grant, extra reviewer, mandatory claim, exclusive lock, notification
default, per-member task copies or general routing/availability engine.

The full review's eight clauses are part of this decision in full:

- **C01:** qualify the exact source work and accountable person/team destination,
  real work/recovery route and specialist constraints without freezing a new
  Team aggregate, universal cardinality or per-item assignment designer.
- **C02:** separate responsibility administration, target qualification and
  actual human review authority under trusted context/current P12. Preserve
  independently valid paths and actual revocation. Any coordination NHI retains
  its own current human owner/ceiling; the team is not an NHI or reviewer account.
- **C03:** reuse valid source defaults and deliberate handoffs with actual
  source precedence. Distinguish missing, invalid, no eligible actor, unknown
  lookup and temporary absence. Fail closed only where required proof is absent,
  retain known work and provide capable source recovery without arbitrary fallback.
- **C04:** perform source-owned changes with relevant expected heads,
  attributable old/new/actor/reason/consequences and durable effect identity.
  Required facts/history/handoff are coherent and co-located source/outbox writes
  atomic. Keep providers outside locks. Recover lost responses, protect later
  successors, and preserve one logical source work identity without personal
  task fanout or exclusive view/review locks.
- **C05:** retain earlier actors, evidence, correction lineage, review outcomes,
  fulfillment and all recipient clocks. Staffing loss is not an extra completion
  predicate after actual conditions are satisfied. No automatic re-review,
  hidden intake gate, renewed access or new assignment/review-result email policy.
- **C06:** use existing request/My Work with clear responsibility/reviewer/contact
  meanings, predictable accessible changes, truthful status and real recovery.
  Preserve authorized context and known success; reject late private results
  across context changes. No repeated picker/claim/confirmation ceremony.
- **C07:** enforce tenant-aware stable relationships, valid current state,
  trusted immutable original acts/effect identity, effective grants/RLS and all
  privileged paths. Preserve identity through rename without reusing it after
  deletion/recreation. Govern retention and safe metadata/disclosure; bound
  current resolution/history/recovery and qualify finite operating budgets.
- **C08:** prospectively qualify native source/P12/P9 and applicable Workflow
  paths, real roles/races/faults/restore/mixed versions/workload and composed
  user/AT journeys. No legacy finance flags as proof, unsafe writer compatibility,
  restored obsolete effects, new permission/routing engine or whole-Studio gate.

## Consequences and proof

A retains the simple named-person case and adds qualified shared responsibility
without a nominal personal claim. B remains credible for sources whose policy
requires a particular individual; A does not override those policies. The
permanent path is source-owned responsibility and current authority, not a
private-looking queue that silently shares files or a generic task field that
becomes another system of record.

P01–P20 in the full review are required positive/negative/boundary/authorization/
concurrency/failure/migration/accessibility/workload/traceability outcomes before
activation. Document checks alone cannot satisfy them. Operational controls have
named owner roles, explicit zero-tolerance defects and finite budget thresholds
that must be measured and bound to named operators before activation.

One glossary term, **Document Review Responsibility**, distinguishes the source's
designation from permission, actual reviewer and participant help. It does not
create a schema or Team/Queue/Claim taxonomy. Preserve nineteen existing
definitions; the new twentieth definition is fully ratified with this package.

At authorized publication reconcile the ordinary source, P12/P9 and applicable
Workflow/GEN-06 bindings, canonical requirements/design/tasks, full incorporated
OpenSpec projections and later authorized tickets/tests/release evidence.
No new default-routing algorithm, bulk mode, presence policy, issuance gate or
notification policy is silently selected.
