## Ratified decision and amendments

Allow authorized staff to mark an outstanding ordinary Document Request Item
that is not currently accepted No longer required when it was mistakenly
requested or is no longer applicable
to the legitimate purpose of an eligible unfinished request. Record this as a
source-owned change to that exact demand, with trusted attribution and current
requirement/context basis. It is not evidence acceptance, file deletion,
whole-request cancellation, a participant self-waiver or permission to bypass
a still-binding specialist requirement.

Qualify requirement-change authority independently of reviewing, assignment,
generic staff/admin access and recipient-grant administration. Reuse the
source's authoritative command; add a bounded ordinary-source capability where
needed, without a parallel File Manager or task writer. The source retains a
concise cause/basis from trusted context or staff input where otherwise missing;
no universal free-text essay, second reviewer or policy-builder is required.

Order the decision with current source authority, requirement revisions,
evidence admission/review, relevant clarification and request closure. A stale
affected human decision cannot silently adopt new facts. Preserve prior receipt,
evidence, safety/retention duties and independently lawful reading/clarification.
Stop the ended ordinary evidence demand and new submission/correction for it;
do not erase already admitted work or close every conversation or access grant.
End only review concerns whose obligation depended on that superseded demand;
keep their history and every independently required concern.

Reevaluate Q22's complete current source predicate. A lawful last-item change
may fulfill; a filtered, missing or merely zero-item result cannot. An explicit
eligible complete empty/non-file predicate can be valid. An ended whole-request
purpose cannot be turned into successful fulfillment by removing its items.
Preserve actual fulfillment time, existing access boundaries and known item
success while any required aggregate follow-through recovers.

Make mistakes recoverable through source-owned correction. While still eligible
and unfinished, a new deliberate decision may supersede the exact mistaken
removal after rechecking current authority, requirement and evidence; preserve
the original act and make any renewed demand on that same item/requirement
lineage clear. Reassess retained evidence rather than restoring an old snapshot.
This is not restoration of
an old grant, review demand, deadline or queued effect. Once fulfillment/closure
wins, do not invert or reopen that record. Record the mistake and use a capable
authorized source remedy. For ordinary evidence still legitimately needed,
this may be a separately reviewed new request through independently qualified
normal original issuance, linked to the earlier mistake and subject to Q25 and
Q01–Q19. Preserve independently permitted Q01 proof reuse and Q28 exact evidence
reuse through their owners, without copying grants or evidence acceptance.
Q33 grants no generic Reissue or copied access. Qualify that route or
another effective existing domain remedy before activating the affected journey.

Persist source result, mandatory history and durable follow-through coherently.
Recover the same semantic effect after retry or lost response; changed material
conflicts and old replays cannot override a later correction. Show a deliberate
named action and its currently qualified consequence before commit, then a
persistent accessible No longer required result, distinct from Accepted and
Changes requested. Preserve useful context only while disclosure remains lawful.
Keep internal reasons separate from participant-safe explanations. No new
notification purpose, access period, bulk-waiver engine, inbox, routine final
completion checkoff or whole future Studio dependency is selected.

## Consequences and qualification

Staff can correct an unnecessary ordinary demand where the work is visible.
Participants see No longer required and what remains; the status does not mean
their file was accepted. Received evidence/history and independent duties stay
with their owners. A last-item decision can complete only under Q22's complete
eligible rule, and the consequence is explained before commit.

The principal cost is a genuine source requirement-change/correction capability,
not a boolean flag. Mistakes need a lasting route, including a proven terminal
remedy that preserves completion. The selected amendment supplies that policy
without inventing an inverse for fulfilled work or another workflow platform.
The actual command, normal new-request issuance where used, current permissions,
composed UX and recovery must pass native proof before the affected activation.

Do not add mandatory essays, routine extra approvals, universal Undo, new
notification purposes, bulk waiver, private offline cache or full future Studio
dependency. Reuse existing source/history/work/custody infrastructure where
qualified. All C/P obligations and six operator controls remain binding.
