## Context and decision

Re-adding a removed family or re-offering a withdrawn publication is a fresh deliberate currently admissible source act; no old retry, restore, stale draft, expired token or reversed UI checkbox revives the prior grant. Applicable source safety/privacy/P12 operators may urgently narrow affected access under their own real authority independently of ordinary full-scope editing or successor readiness. Being a narrowing is not itself permission. Recovery may prepare and re-publish still-lawful retained content without forced reupload, but must not overwrite newer saved or unsaved candidate work or automatically restore former audiences. Ordinary publication cannot clear a source protection, hold or restriction. If a narrower curator cannot safely change the shared publication, an actual qualified maintainer/operator remedy must exist; a powerless Help link or routine raw SQL repair is insufficient.

**Organization-managed missionary resource library** (Phase 28 D12):
A collection of useful materials prepared and published by authorized organization
curators for missionaries who are permitted to use them. The initial ordinary
missionary experience is to find and use resources, without submitting material
for publication. Managing the library is a separate responsibility; publishing
internally does not make a resource public, and removing it from the library is

**Organization-managed resource library** (Phase 28 D12; Phase 29 Q58):
A collection of useful materials prepared and published by authorized organization curators for staff or missionaries who are currently permitted to use them. _Avoid_: every staff member as publisher, upload as publication, internal as public, library withdrawal as deletion, resource use as completed training
