# ADR0063 — Expressly policy-authorized automatic ordinary disposal

Date: 2026-10-28. Status: **Fully ratified: Q63 A and all required amendments.** Reviewed disposition: Accept with required amendments. Context: Files and Documents / Phase29 grooming. No actual disposal, formal publication, implementation or activation is claimed.

## Context

Qualified ordinary source policies may determine routine disposition without a fresh operator release for every occurrence. Requiring release can help a person identify missing context but also creates recurring work and delay; neither a human click nor a timer proves complete current use/hold/copy coverage. Q63 selects the explicitly approved automatic path while preserving source-required human decisions. Source policy remains separate from shared custody, actual execution identity and verified physical outcome.

The consequential tradeoff is irreversible: an early claim is not final destructive permission, a database authorization cannot atomically recall a later storage call, and a stopped queue item can be rediscovered unless the source stop cause remains effective. The decision therefore requires exact scoped dispatch admission, durable possible-attempt fences, effective stop/reconsideration and truthful physical recovery, while keeping the operator UI inside existing source/status/work contexts. It does not generalize the P23 Media manual rule or copy Eve's specialized expiry protocol.

## Decision

**Q63-C01 — Bounded adoption.** Allow expressly policy-authorized automatic disposal of an eligible ordinary retained-record cohort without a new routine staff release for each already-authorized occurrence. The source policy must actually permit that mode and the complete path must be qualified. Keep all source-required human decisions and exact owner controls. Preserve P23/D27 explicit review/no-auto-purge, other specialist owners and independently qualified transient ZIP/preview/staging cleanup. No blanket age-based purge, Empty Trash, generalized drive delete, legal period, new consent duty or new glossary term is selected.

**Q63-C02 — Policy authority, flexibility and activation.** The responsible source/records/privacy owner supplies approved purpose/family, material applicability, version/effectivity, actual automatic/manual mode, typed event/date precision, preservation/privacy bounds, holds/conflicts, covered records/copies and correction/disposition/recovery obligations. Q66 permits currently authorized tenant actors to author and revise supported ordinary policy definitions, business values and disposition mode through its qualified lifecycle. Actual source semantics, independent obligations and current approval remain the boundary; choices are not limited to Asym-authored presets. Enabling or changing automation requires the actual source-authorized configuration/impact path with understandable affected scope; it cannot silently sweep existing or unmapped legacy records into disposal. Q62's historical assignments/current successors remain authoritative. A missing policy, unknown mode, shorter period, source closure or UI default is not permission. Policy approval, activation, execution and current exceptions are distinct facts. No arbitrary tenant DSL, free-form timer or universal grace is added.

**Q63-C03 — Discovery is inert; exact authority is real.** Candidate discovery, a preview count, a work claim and a worker lease do not authorize destruction. Discover only bounded permitted candidates; missing/stale source coverage is not proof of no use. Establish actual source authorization for each bounded operation under the approved policy or required human decision. Record the real policy/version/basis and execution mode; automatic authority is not fabricated fresh human approval. Details, GET/render/prefetch, filters and candidate review do not dispatch deletion. A required manual decision remains deliberate over sufficient permitted evidence; no timeout auto-approval or new routine approval ceremony is inferred.

**Q63-C04 — Finite exact targets and groups.** Bind each operation to immutable exact Tenant/environment/source record-use and physical-copy identities plus relevant policy/basis. A batch has finite fixed membership; discovery results or a broad prefix are never delete instructions. Newly eligible records cannot join an older authorization. Re-prove each independently actionable target/group; a blocked target does not falsely complete the whole set or necessarily block unrelated qualified targets. Preserve source-declared indivisible authorization groups and shared-copy constraints, while reporting actual physical partial outcomes rather than promising distributed all-or-none deletion. No caller-supplied locator or mutable filename identifies the target authoritatively. Provider target identity is resolved from trusted custody facts and the qualified adapter.

**Q63-C05 — Current actor, Tenant and execution context.** Use current source/P12 authority with trusted native Tenant/environment/source context. Distinguish policy administration, activation, automatic execution, required human release, stop/cause resolution, hold authority, view/body access and provider operation. A worker must have its actual qualified system/NHI authority for the exact operation; a service credential, queued message, generic admin label or the policy author's old session grants none. Attribute historical human approval and actual automated execution separately. A person's departure neither invents perpetual delegated access nor silently cancels a valid independently governed policy; current source delegation/authority decides. Application/database context must agree and reset safely across pooled success/error/cancel/retry. Caller IDs are validated selectors, not actor/tenant/approval facts.

**Q63-C06 — Complete current obligations.** Before destructive admission, prove current source policy, required preservation/privacy handling, complete registered owner/version/use/hold/processor/copy coverage and closed/fenced writers for the actual target. Include still-valid signed grants, multipart/resumable uploads, processors and restore writes; credential expiry alone is not proof that admitted in-flight writes cannot later complete. Include live and retained source uses, exact delivery/recovery references, drafts/schedules/shares, upload finalization, rendition/assembly promotion and applicable backups/exports. Unknown adapter coverage, stale counts or an empty permitted-use view blocks the destructive conclusion and reaches its capable owner. Retention floor/hold release, withdrawn submission, completed/canceled request, expired access and an unlinked view are not sufficient by themselves. No operator override, automatic fallback or longer/shorter blanket winner may substitute for the source's real conflict decision.

**Q63-C07 — Final dispatch admission and durable evidence.** Qualify one authoritative source/custody dispatch-admission boundary for each exact target or admitted group. After waits, order the relevant current policy, stop, hold, reference and writer facts at this boundary and coherently persist the immutable attempt, target/basis, trusted actor/time, current fences and recoverable result/follow-through before external IO. A claim made earlier cannot skip this step. Admission must prevent new conflicting references, reuses, finalizations or promotions while the destructive outcome is pending. Every participating use/hold/reference/write/promotion/restore path must honor the same effective fence or proved equivalent ordering; a row lock or SKIP LOCKED candidate query alone proves neither complete coverage nor external exclusion. Use an actual short transaction or proved equivalent fence; multiple SDK calls are not one transaction. Do not keep a SQL transaction open across provider IO to pretend there is global atomicity. Only the exact admitted qualified attempt may dispatch within its finite qualified authorization/handoff validity and current worker generation. An expired or superseded permit/generation cannot authorize a fresh late call; it requires fresh current admission. That expiry never proves an earlier possible provider effect stopped or releases its protective fence.

**Q63-C08 — Honest irreversible boundary.** A conflicting hold/use/stop committed before final dispatch admission wins and prevents that admission. After that boundary, a request may already be capable of reaching the provider even if an acknowledgment or physical result is absent. Stop known not-yet-started/preventable remaining work where the qualified path can actually do so; treat affected possible attempts as may-have-started until reconciled. Do not promise a later hold always beats the network call or restores that copy. Preserve any still-protectable copies, record actual scope/timing/outcomes and route incident/source repair where required. If a source demands a stronger guarantee, that provider/path stays unqualified until it can prove it; this clause does not weaken the source rule or create a policy grace interval.

**Q63-C09 — Provider-specific execution and verified results.** Use the qualified Storage/provider API against the exact admitted immutable copy/generation, including a version token only where the actual installed protocol supports and qualifies it. Never delete via Storage metadata SQL, Empty bucket, wildcard/prefix or an inferred hash/filename. Parse each target outcome; one envelope success, object disappearance from a UI or generic404/empty response does not prove the intended copies were removed. Distinguish qualified verified absence, confirmed removal, failure, unknown and still-required copies, with truthful attribution. A not-found due to access, wrong scope or wrong locator is not deletion success. Provider acknowledgments are evidence under a proved verification profile, not blanket proof of backups, archives, caches or every external copy. Record qualified observed physical facts under actual reconciliation authority even if a later hold/policy forbids more deletion; do not suppress true loss because a finalizer now rejects further destructive work, or let late stale evidence overwrite a newer verified fact. Retired keys/locators must not be reused where a late old request could affect new bytes.

**Q63-C10 — Idempotency, leases and unknown outcomes.** Tie original authorization, exact target/generation, attempts, per-copy results and required follow-through to the durable semantic effect. Duplicate transport or competing workers cannot create another destructive effect or substitute today's broader policy. Recover matching committed original results under current disclosure before new-action eligibility; changed intent under the same key conflicts. Lease expiry, cancellation, timeout, API error or deleted readable history does not prove an old provider request cannot finish. Keep the possible-attempt/write fence until qualified reconciliation or authoritative negative evidence establishes the outcome and excludes unsafe late effects. A new worker may take over reconciliation without reopening source admission or blindly clearing the old attempt. Evidence-only reconciliation is distinct from a retry capable of another destructive submission: every such new submission needs fresh current dispatch admission for the exact original target. A repeated transport key does not make it read-only if the first request never arrived. A later hold/stop can forbid additional calls while original possibly-started effects still require reconciliation. After lawful control/replay evidence expires, reject unverifiable stale intent rather than accepting it as new. No-op handling compares complete intended effect/current basis, not merely equal status text.

**Q63-C11 — Effective stop and safe reconsideration.** Provide a narrow actual source-authorized **Stop remaining work** action for the exact affected not-yet-irreversible scope, with a truthful source cause or concise necessary input and trusted attribution. It must establish a cause-owned intervention barrier that prevents rediscovery/re-admission of the same stopped targets through another scan, key, worker or manual route while unresolved; canceling one queue item is insufficient. This is operational containment, not a new legal hold, indefinite extension or authority to violate a required deadline. Use actual current source/records resolution and escalation. Distinguish reconciling possibly-started original attempts from reconsidering safely unstarted remaining targets. An authorized resolution may permit fresh current automatic authorization if the policy still allows; it never revives the stopped intent, drops independent holds or rekeys an uncertain prior effect. No universal Resume/Undo, arbitrary pause duration or new broad policy engine is created.

**Q63-C12 — Source-use end, copies and recoverability.** End only the source-use/record consequences the actual owner authorizes. A source use can end while bytes remain required by another use, class or hold; do not call that physical deletion. Originals, versions, selected assembly inputs/recipe/output, previews, request/history/audit, messages, staging, replay evidence, backups and external copies retain their own obligations. Show verified completion only for the stated target/copy scope. Policy correction or release-record correction is not restored bytes. For mistaken disposal, stop still-preventable work, preserve actual evidence and use only independently lawful qualified recovery; otherwise record actual loss and route capable incident/source remediation. Do not force recipient reupload, restore old access/acceptance or claim recovery from DB metadata alone. Restored bytes must meet current source admission, restrictions and adverse-history rules before serving.

**Q63-C13 — Required human cases and lawful timing.** Where the actual source requires human release, provide a finite reviewable proposal, sufficient permitted evidence and one deliberate qualified decision. Body-read permission is separate; no forced preview when safe metadata suffices and no blind approval when mandatory evidence is unavailable. Route genuine missing authority/evidence to a capable owner. Both automatic and manual paths need accountable coverage and source-compliant deadlines for holds/conflicts/required disposition/repair; an unserviceable queue or repeated retry does not authorize indefinite retention. Escalation resolves the actual source conflict, never chooses a blanket longest/shortest rule or approval by silence. No new reminder/email/approval hierarchy is selected; existing independently qualified P9/P6/P17 behavior retains its contract.

**Q63-C14 — Small truthful UX/UI.** Use the existing source Records/status context and a qualified existing source/P9 work view only for actual manual decisions or exceptions. Show safe policy/mode, exact affected scope, current known condition, next effective action and result history. Distinguish candidate/awaiting authorization, blocked/unknown eligibility, admitted or may-have-started work, stopped remaining scope, partial/unknown physical result and verified scoped completion. These are independent facts/views, not a mandatory giant status enum. Automatic routine work requires no per-file checkbox, routine modal, reason essay or new console. Stop/release/cause-resolution must be deliberate with understandable consequence, meaningful disabled states and current authority. Preserve lawful selection/focus and original-result recovery; no false optimistic Deleted or fictitious Undo. Base UI/base-maia controls must support keyboard/AT/touch,320 CSS pixel ordinary vertical reflow, zoom, RTL/long names, actual localized source dates and low-bandwidth status. Focused-button Enter/Space works; IME confirmation, select/blur, navigation or prefetch never initiates destruction, Stop or cause-resolution. Essential meaning is readable without hover/color alone. Keep keyboard focus visible and unobscured by authored sticky overlays; announce meaningful pending/partial/error/result changes to assistive technology without stealing focus or announcing every refresh. Existing command-origin/CSRF protections govern human cookie-authenticated commands.

**Q63-C15 — Private projections and current disclosure.** Register relevant policy, targets, use/copy history, stop causes and outcomes through the existing shared static field classification/subtract-only projection. Source/P12 purpose and current actor rights govern labels, counts, comparisons, errors, body access, operator views, original-result recovery, search/export and logs. Disposal permission does not grant another source's evidence or legal/hold details. Reject late responses and invalidate unsafe caches after actor/Tenant/environment/source changes; cached lists are never admission proof. No cross-Tenant bulk union, public fallback, persistent restricted device queue or telemetry with bodies/tokens/paths/sensitive reasons. Participants and donors receive only their independently authorized truthful availability/history; this decision adds no consent task or disposition notification. Actual lawful minimized history/tombstone/control retention is separate from retaining full private content forever.

**Q63-C16 — Structural database and every-door safety.** Preserve typed same-scope source/use/record/copy/policy/attempt/result/stop relations, null-complete composite references, required state/time checks, exact current-generation/semantic-effect uniqueness and deliberate delete behavior. Platform-global capability and specialist-source definitions may remain with their owners. Q66 ordinary tenant business-policy definitions and versions are tenant-owned; all assignments and execution remain scoped. Protect immutable original attribution and append-only correction/outcome history under lawful privacy repair; prevent authorized updates from reparenting, forging policy approval, clearing independent stops/holds, swapping a locator or converting an allowed row into forbidden scope. Keep one source/P12 decision layer and coarse Tenant RLS. Qualify effective schema/table/column/sequence, named/PUBLIC/default/inherited EXECUTE, overload/search_path/owner/FORCE/BYPASS/service composition, old USING and resulting WITH CHECK, trigger transformations, SELECT/RETURNING/UPSERT/MERGE/bulk, views/materializations/REST/GraphQL/RPC, Storage, workers/imports/legacy/admin/operator paths and actual pooled context. Unsupported bypass doors must close. Zero affected rows, a service credential or a function keyword is not business success; explicit literal WITH CHECK omission alone is not a vulnerability where PostgreSQL inherits the predicate.

**Q63-C17 — Minimal capable operations and audit.** Reuse narrow source/custody commands, existing durable-work and actual P9 owner routes instead of creating a global deletion scheduler, approval platform or separate incident queue. Required eligibility exceptions, unknown outcomes, intervention causes and impact work must have a capable owner, durable result and supported retry/reconciliation path; routine recovery cannot require direct production SQL or provider-console edits. Distinguish durable business history and policy/actor evidence from security audit and technical logs. Record real automation/human attribution, exact intent/target lineage and known/unknown outcome without private snapshots in general telemetry. No new communication purpose, blanket subscriber, per-request task or borrowed recipient reminder follows. Auditable operational privilege and provider-account controls must support the same boundary; UI-only safety does not constrain privileged tools.

**Q63-C18 — Finite scale and dependency qualification.** Qualify finite discovery/option/history pages, exact operation targets and source groups, copy counts, query/lock/attempt/retry/reconciliation and control-evidence budgets with real workload units and tenant skew. Use indexed bounded set-wise reads, stable unique paging/current per-page rights, bounded durable continuation and fair per-Tenant/source work; no full-store cleanup per review or unbounded tenant-wide destructive query. Respect the smaller actual source budget and qualified provider ceiling; documented Supabase1000/remove and Eve1–500/default100 are not ordinary defaults or capacity promises. Preserve P12 R6's applicable logical governance bound, physical-shard carve-out and restricted unstriped pre-egress/uniformity rules. Verify installed SDK/protocol, error/per-item/version semantics, current provider limits, identity/region/copy obligations and real byte recovery. No vendor's S3 label, lifecycle feature, ETag, TTL or native SDK example supplies Core policy or a stronger atomic guarantee.

**Q63-C19 — Migration, activation and restoration.** Inventory exact retained families/copies, policy/trigger evidence, every writer/reader/job/operator and unknown in-flight effects before adoption. No legacy row, object age, folder, current default or backfill may fabricate historical automatic approval. Introduce compatible source/custody representations and enforcement before writers; activate only an explicitly supported cohort after policy, operators, all-door authorization, boundary/race/fault, copy/restore and actual user proof. A safe stop/kill switch fences affected new admissions while retaining lawful custody, current restrictions and original reconciliation. Rollback cannot restore bypassing old writers, erase unresolved attempts or reinterpret admitted data as ungoverned; keep compatible protections or roll forward. Restore adverse disposal/revocation/correction/stop/control facts before serving or restarting workers, and qualify real object bytes separately from metadata. No activation-induced catch-up purge of unqualified existing records.

ADR0063 records the ordinary automatic-disposition tradeoff; it does not own another source's retention. Preserve Q01–Q62, all61 prior feature ADRs and both glossaries unchanged; no new term is needed. At explicit formal publication reconcile actual source owners and declared OpenSpec projections/design/tasks, then exact later tickets/tests/release evidence; do not author generated views independently. R-POL-ordinary/R-LEGACY and actual provider/source qualifications remain exact affected-cohort prerequisites. Full ratification authorizes recording this package and researching one Q64 question without selecting its answer. ## Alternatives and consequences

The strongest alternative requires a fresh qualified human release of each finite ordinary proposal, with bounded batches permitted. It can catch missing context but does not prove hidden references or stop a request already capable of reaching storage. Selected A removes that routine step only where the actual approved source expressly allows it and the complete path is qualified. Human-required cases, actual source deadlines and independent specialist modes remain unchanged.

The source-authorized Stop remaining work intervention and current cause-resolution path are fully ratified amendments. They prevent rediscovery of still-preventable stopped targets without inventing a legal hold or permanent extension; resolving the cause permits fresh current authorization, not revival of stopped or uncertain work. Qualified provider evidence determines actual copy outcomes. A later hold or correction cannot be promised to recover lost bytes.

Q66 adds narrow tenant ordinary policy authoring and lifecycle controls, with its Document Records Policy term. It does not add a global scheduler, generic approval console, new field/permission engine, universal source period, provider promise or participant duty. Actual R-POL-ordinary, R-LEGACY, native custody/authorization/fault/restore and user qualification remain prerequisites. Core should reuse capable existing seams while correcting or rejecting incompatible implementation precedents.
