# ADR-0009: Requested-by targets preserve otherwise-authorized intake

**Status:** Ratified by Conrad, 2026-10-20: Q09 A, the exact corrected
decision, Q09-C01–C22, Q09-P01–P28 and all eight operational controls.
The reviewed disposition was Accept with required amendments; those amendments
are adopted grooming intent. Q01–Q08 remain ratified. No active-access duration,
date-edit capability, mandatory-date rule, grace, reminder or implementation
is selected.

For the ordinary native Private Document Request, Conrad chose to allow
otherwise-authorized late submissions and pending corrections. A requested-by
target coordinates work without making staff unlock every late file. The
strongest alternative makes the date a hard intake cutoff; that remains
appropriate where an existing source purpose actually requires it.

## Ratified decision and amendments

Target passage alone does not close intake, end reading, grant permission,
approve evidence or fulfill the request. Current source/tenant/recipient/
purpose/item/revision authority, finite access, binding intake limits,
acceptance/freeze/completion and custody gates remain effective. Q08 fresh
completed access is read-only.

Preserve exact durable receipt, trusted source admission time and available
immutable target-applicability evidence. For a source-resolved instant,
strictly later is late; equality is not after it. Qualify date-only, precision,
zone/calendar and absent/unknown interpretation through the actual source.
Do not borrow a browser clock, scan completion, mutable task date or generic
Workflow expiry default as proof.

Keep current outstanding recipient work separate from historical receipt and
staff processing. Timely material awaiting checks/review is not recipient
overdue solely because the source has not fulfilled the request. Receipt does
not establish safe or sufficient evidence. New work and corrections use their
actually applicable terms without unrelated retroactive lateness or a new
interval. Q03's admission-time predecessor fence and no-revival rule remain.

Authorized target edits preserve earlier receipt/target evidence. Erroneous
facts need governed attributed correction with prior evidence retained.
Target-only outages can leave independently authorized work usable with
explicitly unresolved timing. Required source/receipt provenance must still
be durable. Recover historical applicable terms only; if they remain
unprovable, retain unknown timing. Unproved authority denies the action.

Serialize admission with relevant source changes, including review,
completion, real hard limits and revocation; check actual time after waits.
One immutable submitted intent survives transport retries. Qualified
compatible target-only changes may resolve at first admission without
reupload or a new intent; once admitted, retries preserve the original
receipt/time and captured terms or unresolved timing. Unknown responses must
be reconciled before another submission or destructive custody cleanup.

Keep the existing accessible request journey: Requested by, actual current
action, exact correction selection, one deliberate send, persistent receipt
and truthful checking/review/recovery. No late-only essay, approval queue,
repeated confirmation, new account, drive or scheduler is selected.
Shared Base UI/base-maia and current private read/history rules remain.

Use source-owned commands, custody, shared tasks and existing Workflow/
communication owners. No target timer may invent closure, approval or
notification. Adopt through qualified ordinary cohorts, safe schema/version
boundaries and real authorization/concurrency/provider/browser proof.
Active finite-access and unresolved Q08 timing policies remain activation
gates, independent of this requested-target choice.
