# ADR-0002: Files for Asym work and exact submitted-file readback

**Status:** Ratified by Conrad, 2026-10-20: Q02 A, the no-general-drive product
boundary and the complete required amendment package, including Q02-C01–C20
and Q02-P01–P20. The adversarial disposition was Accept with required
amendments; those amendments are now adopted as grooming intent. Q01 remains
unchanged. This is not a formal PRD or implementation/release authority.

## Context

Conrad selected submission confirmation, status and **View my submitted file**,
then clarified: “We also don't want Asym to be the user's new dropbox or google
drive. Most of this file management is for relevant files and media that is
needed to be used on Asym or produced by Asym.”

Receipt/status alone is simpler but cannot show which document the server
actually received. A general drive is unnecessary for that task. Conversely,
requiring an already-live placement would prevent valid reusable-media/resource
preparation, import staging, historical evidence and recovery. Current owners
already distinguish those purposes and their retention.

## Ratified decision

Provide the ordinary new private-document request experience with a currently
source-authorized submission confirmation, participant-visible status and a
deliberate **View my submitted file** action for the exact received submission
version when its qualified reading rendition is available.

Asym manages files/media for its admitted product work and the outputs or
obligations of that work. Each admitted use has a known source owner and purpose.
Shared bytes can support multiple independently admitted typed uses without
merging their meaning, rights, currentness or retention. Purpose is not a
filename, folder, free-text assertion, classifier result or permission.

Valid drafts, reusable catalog/resource intake, generation, import staging,
retention, holds and recovery do not require an already-live placement. Normal
contextual entry supplies the source; an otherwise ambiguous File Manager entry
offers only a short set of admitted source destinations. Do not fabricate a
business record or add a governance form merely to upload a file.

Keep the promised staff File Manager, source-owned libraries, folders, search,
views, bulk handling and embedded controls within their source boundaries. Do
not create a general-purpose personal/organizational drive, desktop sync,
arbitrary backup/mirroring or unrelated anyone-link service. Qualified public
publication and authorized external document delivery retain their owners.

Submission confirmation, safe-preview readiness, source review and business
acceptance remain separate. Own-submission readback binds the admitted
actor/recipient/request/occurrence/version; an uploader or email match is not a
global read grant. Original download, mutation, earlier-version history,
post-closure access, other participants and staff material require their own
source decisions. Existing source rights are not removed by this new default.

Bind the preview to immutable input and qualified profile/output lineage, with
meaningful limitations disclosed. Preserve available native text, semantics and
exact values. Unsupported, unsafe, incomplete or inaccessible contents have an
honest state and source-approved recovery/accessible path; do not fall back
automatically to original download, public URLs or an external viewer. Qualified
controls alone do not prove the contents accessible.

Current source authority controls every protected metadata/byte representation.
Independently admitted processing uses its current source/worker authority rather
than requiring an indefinitely open browser session. Revocation blocks new
affected reads; source withdrawal/expiry fences promotion as required. No visible
use, ended request or expired rendition authorizes deletion of original evidence
or overrides another use, hold or owner retention.

## Required amendments and tradeoffs

- Apply the exact Q02-C01–C20 rules and Q02-P01–P20 proof in the linked review,
  preserving the complete inherited D32 preview obligations where consumed.
  Its format/resource limits are preview-only and are not measured capacity.
- Reuse shared preview/custody mechanics with a new exact request-source and
  recipient projection. Support staff-reader authority does not transfer.
  Phase18 official-document semantics, Phase21 evidence, Phase23 Media and
  Phase28 resource publication remain separate.
- Purpose admission precedes durable allocation and expensive processing.
  An additional general-resource family needs a narrow named source contract
  before activation; neither an ownerless General folder nor removal of the
  requested resource outcome resolves that responsibility.
- Reconcile legacy public/mutable/unknown-origin objects without certifying or
  deleting them by inference. Preserve all source identities and obligations;
  source-qualified disposition and byte backup/restore remain required.
- Preview adds processing, fidelity, accessibility and ongoing read-authorization
  costs. Those costs are justified by the selected content-checking journey,
  not by an ambition to replace users' storage services. No new provider,
  editor, general search/OCR/AI service or permission engine is selected.

## Rejected interpretations

- General My Drive: broader than the requested Asym work and introduces unrelated
  storage/sharing expectations.
- Every file already attached to a live record: conflicts with admitted catalogs,
  preparation, staging, retention and recovery.
- Unused or out-of-product means delete: incomplete usage and source obligations
  forbid that inference; a new product boundary is not cleanup authorization.
- Uploaded by me means readable forever: current exact source permission governs.
- A preview or a file generated by Asym is official/current/accepted: its source
  alone decides that meaning.
