## Consequences and qualification

Staff can publish a lawful change in the existing source flow with a clear
optional email choice, actual safe destination and0/1/2 requested notices when
the narrow compound action applies. They can choose neither, either or both
eligible purposes without a second ceremony. Tenant administrators can disable
the new purpose or choose a quieter initial default. Missing input never sends.

Recipients get a minimal useful cue about the actual request, not private
instruction text, an access grant or a demand to reupload. Existing source
guidance remains authoritative and usable on authorized return. Staff-only
evidence/reassessment does not become participant work. Portal expiry alone is
not a blanket factual-email rule, while every protected action qualifies anew.

An update can be published successfully even when its email is declined, blocked,
stopped, failed or unknown. If work is accepted, removed, superseded or closed
before dispatch, its old cue stops rather than being sent merely because a fact
once existed. This intentionally favors current useful awareness over notifying
every historical update. In-flight effects cannot be recalled; evidence-only
reconciliation remains available. No digest or generic deduplication is needed.

Require native actual-role/RLS/all-door authorization, complete source/release
fault and replay tests, Disabled/utility/provider-call races, material erasure,
mixed-version/restore, finite workload/resource proof and composed intended-user
comprehension before activation. Adjacent send/ledger/financial helpers and
mocked email tests do not supply those proofs. No exactly-once physical delivery,
measured benefit, free operation or implementation completion is claimed.
