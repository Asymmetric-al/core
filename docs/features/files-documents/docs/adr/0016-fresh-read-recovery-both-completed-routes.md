## Ratified decision and amendments

Allow Q08 consideration after eligible ordinary expiry of either the original
ordinary completed admission or a completed admission actually established at
lawful normal fulfillment under valid Q13 fresh unfinished authority, including
an eligible Q15 period. Prove the exact source/request/intended-recipient
lineage, actual causal permission, historical policy/scope/end and ending cause.
A generic completed task, origin flag, current file, first-observed timestamp
or what today's policy would have permitted cannot create historical authority.

For either path, bound each new reviewed scope by the actual fulfillment-created
completed permission's member/original-version/action/Q05-context ceiling,
current eligibility and reviewed subset. Do not use the wider original
unfinished requirements, old write rights, later files, retained folders or
unions of grants. The narrow completed ceiling is binding; a later narrower
Q08 subset alone does not rewrite it as another permanent global limit.
Current source facts can be projected through permitted context without
freezing old display text or adding new members/actions.

Historical admission does not require a prior visit, download or successfully
rendered preview. Deliberately admitted status/context-only scope can qualify
only for those actions; it grants no file rights and cannot become a silent
success fallback for a failed file promise. Qualified rendition repair of the
same authorized original may produce a new qualified rendition revision, but
cannot add a member, substitute/add an authorized submitted-original version
or grant a new read action.
Unknown root/expiry/current authority closes the affected action safely, while
unrelated failed subviews do not invalidate independently proved eligible work.

Preserve original fulfillment, ends, received facts, correction/acceptance
lineage and adverse history. Gap/no-admission cases cannot receive a first
completed view retrospectively. Qualified recovery of a real timely outcome
keeps its original facts and end. Genuine revocation, binding shortening,
purpose/content-use limits and disposal remain effective as scoped, even when
they coincide with nominal expiry or a cause later clears. Current proof or
representation failure under still-valid authority calls for its own recovery.

Reuse Q08's exact current source/P12 administrative capability, live assignable
ceiling, grant-type dependencies, current recipient proof, material review,
semantic effect/audit/recovery and every-door private serving. The expired
recipient grant need not authorize staff consideration; administrative rights
do not require illicit file-body reading or establish recipient usability.
Bind the exact historical admission and terms to each deliberate intent;
current scope/authority must be rechecked after waits and before protected use.
Replay recovers the original effect under current disclosure, not another
grant or a widened baseline. Unknown commitment is not permission to reissue.

Use the intended **Grant read access** action and existing completed-request
context for both origins. Show authorized scope, adopted finite terms and
actual state/end when the selected rule resolves it. Keep operation, current
usability, proof, notification and representation distinct. Preserve shared
Base UI/base-maia, accessible focus/status/proof, localized dates and useful
mobile/low-bandwidth recovery. No route picker, new account, repeated approval,
mandatory essay, reupload, intake/correction controls or automatic message follows.

Protect trusted same-scope root/member/effect relationships and immutable
historical meaning with actual effective data/privileged-path enforcement.
Use bounded indexed proof and authorized pagination, not a full chain scan,
broad legacy fallback or second authority. Migrations/restores must prove
historical eligibility and preserve later restrictions; no automatic outward
backfill or retention/hold reset is authorized. A new grant type, portal or
permission framework is unnecessary.

## Explicit specialization and remaining gates

Specialize Q08-C01/C04/P02/P05 for the additional lawful completed-admission
origin and close only Q13-C10/Q13–Q15's corresponding Q08 non-expansion
reservation. Preserve Q13/Q07 view-formation and all other applicable Q08
controls and proof. Keep dated records as provenance; later authorized
owner/OpenSpec integration must amend conflicting current clauses and their
declared projections together.

Q08-C06 still requires complete finite fresh duration/anchor, activation/unused
bounds, repetition/cumulative/overlap policy and exact authority qualification
before issuance is activated. Q07/Q14's thirty days and Q15's unfinished repeat
rule are not defaults. Missing policy is not a valid grant with terms invented
later. Q16 chooses eligibility only, without a new timing, count, notification,
file membership, recipient replacement or retention rule.
