# ADR-0049: Guided linked ordinary request replacement

Date: 2026-10-25 (Asia/Bangkok)

Status: **Fully ratified by Conrad on 2026-10-25, Asia/Bangkok: Q49 A with all required amendments and the Document Request Replacement glossary term.** Reviewed disposition: **Accept with required amendments**; the complete package is adopted.

## Context

The founder requires recipient-change recovery and selected a guided linked replacement request over a same-request shortcut for untouched cases. Q01 already supplies correction ownership, not this ordinary continuity shape. Q34 supports real terminal cancellation and a qualified new-request remedy; Q25 gates original issuance; Q39 does not grant cloning an issued instance. A must preserve old evidence and reduce staff effort while making a complete, truthful new request—not merely cancellation plus a draft.

## Ratified decision and amendments

For an eligible ordinary Private Document Request that needs a different intended person for continuing legitimate source work, provide a guided, linked replacement request. Preserve the original request identity and history; issue a distinct request for the new recipient. Use this same correction shape even when the predecessor has no response, rather than adding a pristine-request retarget shortcut. An unfinished predecessor may be canceled through Q34 at the handoff. A predecessor already lawfully canceled may support Q34's independently authorized remedy when its actual cancellation and current replacement purpose are proved; a canceled status alone is insufficient. Actual fulfillment, a specialist source or an unrelated purpose does not enter this ordinary replacement path. Same-person mailbox correction/lost-inbox recovery, representation, collaborator addition, ownership transfer and source-specific remediation retain their own qualified paths; neither email/name similarity nor a staff checkbox proves those cases.

Separate the current source/P12 powers to prepare/read/edit a replacement draft, reuse origin definitions, administer responsibility if changed, cancel or restrict the predecessor, issue the new request and repair a blocked result. Generic staff/admin status, reviewer assignment, file Read, possession of a link and the fact that someone created the draft confer none of those powers. Tenants allocate these actual capabilities through existing qualified source/P12 controls and preserve their legitimate narrower policies; staff can prepare now, save and resume, discard preparation, stop old access independently when authorized, or complete the qualified handoff. Add no new universal tenant settings panel, routine second approver, mandatory reason essay or policy bypass. Require a concise source basis from trusted facts or only the necessary staff input where the source needs it.

Keep at most one active source-owned replacement preparation per predecessor, using the existing ordinary draft facilities and a narrow protected origin relationship. Repeated Start returns the existing permitted preparation or a safe actionable result; it does not create another draft or reveal hidden draft contents. Other currently authorized staff may continue it without impersonating its creator, gaining their rights or requiring their live session. Preparation, autosave, opening the form and merely selecting another person have no cancellation, recipient authority, notification or reminder effect. A draft does not reserve future issuance, hold fulfillment open or block the predecessor's safety or normal source operations. Deliberately discarding an unissued preparation leaves an active predecessor unchanged and never reverses an earlier committed stop; a later genuine preparation gets its own truthful identity.

Offer source-qualified reuse of selected current item definitions, not cloning an issued request. This is an explicit narrow amendment beyond Q39's template-to-new-draft capability. The reusable field set consists only of currently compatible ordinary definition content such as the item label, requested type, instructions and source-qualified requiredness, with fresh item identities and recorded origin/version provenance. Apply origin definition-read and destination authoring/disclosure authority. Review free text for prior-person or case-specific facts; an allowlist, matching tenant or AI redaction promise cannot make text safe. Do not automatically copy received files, evidence links, attachments, answers, comments, participant assertions, acceptance, source outcomes, proofs/grants, recipient bindings, reviewer instances, date/access clocks, notification material, reminder plans or personal preferences. Any new item or supporting material must enter through its independently qualified authoring/source path.

Use current source meaning to propose genuinely remaining work. Previously accepted or otherwise source-sufficient material is not automatically recollected from the new person, and optional nonblocking work does not become a new completion hold by copying it. Person-specific material may nevertheless be genuinely required for the new person; old acceptance is neither automatic new satisfaction nor a universal prohibition on a new legitimate requirement. Staff review which legitimate requirements apply now and can adjust permitted ordinary definitions before issuance without rewriting the predecessor's requirements. Old acceptance never becomes new-item acceptance, and the new recipient is not required to attest that they supplied someone else's material. Independently qualified Q28 reuse remains a separate attributable evidence act with its own origin/target authority; it is not an automatic copy or prerequisite for replacement. If no legitimate new recipient work remains, do not manufacture a replacement merely to repair a label or obtain another notification allowance; use the actual source outcome/remedy.

Keep the preparation bound to its actual predecessor, same tenant/environment/requesting source and continuing legitimate purpose, its chosen new recipient and current draft revision. A permitted draft edit may change the proposed new recipient or definitions before issuance, but it is not an update to the predecessor's recipient or global Auth/CRM/contact identity. Use expected relevant basis and conflict-safe draft saves; preserve lawful user edits rather than silently refreshing them from a changed source or template. Re-evaluate relevant source work, disclosure and readiness at final review and commitment. Changes that affect the reviewed recipient, definitions, timing, responsibility or notification choice require review of the actual new proposal; unrelated source changes must not cause pointless universal reconfirmation. Caller-controlled IDs, flags or normalized address guesses cannot provide authority.

Before a normal final handoff, show a concise editable review of the old and new recipient contexts the actor may see, selected safe work, actual review responsibility, requested-by meaning, the new admission's actual end rules and the intended communication/reminder consequences. Reuse qualified Q24 responsibility when it covers the new declared work, but satisfy Q25's actual first-issuance gate; no future-file read, entire-roster disclosure, online presence or mandatory re-selection is required. Verify the new person's legitimate source role, current source-approved address/purpose, usable recipient route and required assurance. A narrower actor may prepare but cannot complete an effect requiring powers they lack; provide capable authorized continuation without promoting them to a reviewer or administrator.

For the normal path, one authoritative source transaction SHALL co-commit the actual eligible Q34 cancellation and parent authority fence, the new request's Q25-qualified original issuance, the unique issued predecessor-to-successor relationship, mandatory business/audit history, durable semantic result and required local follow-through/outbox work. Keep providers, remote calls and long processing outside that transaction. Use the relevant source aggregate ordering or equivalent proved database isolation/fences across the affected local facts; multiple browser/API/RPC calls are not a transaction. A definite rejection or rollback commits neither new issuance nor the attempted old cancellation. A lost response after commit recovers the original result. Do not present Canceled plus an unissued draft as completed replacement. If the source cannot qualify this coherent ordinary boundary, that replacement path cannot activate; do not substitute a broad distributed saga or compensating reopening.

Urgent containment remains independent. Currently authorized staff may use actual source/P12 restriction or Q34 cancellation without waiting for a replacement draft, new recipient readiness or delivery. A narrower access revocation is not automatically request cancellation. When a real Q34 cancellation was committed earlier, preserve it permanently and show the actual canceled predecessor with a prepared, blocked or not-yet-prepared replacement; later deliberate issuance must prove that exact source remedy, current authority/readiness and the still-unconsumed successor relationship, then atomically commit its new issuance/result/history/follow-through. Do not re-cancel, re-notify the cancellation, restore old access or require cancellation power to perform a separately authorized new issuance solely because an earlier actor performed the stop. Repairing readiness does not automatically issue. If preparation is discarded or no valid replacement can be issued, keep the old stop truthful and provide the actual source remedy; do not call it successful replacement or leave a powerless help link as the only path.

Order replacement against actual fulfillment, cancellation, new evidence/clarification, instruction/requirement changes and relevant authority changes. Fulfillment first prevents this unfinished-request cancellation and stale replacement; a mere review click or queued evaluation is not fulfillment. Evidence or a clarification admitted first remains an original fact and requires current relevant source review of the proposed remaining work; it does not transfer into the successor. Cancellation/handoff first fences new ordinary predecessor actions, while already admitted independent duties retain their own authority. A prior independent cancellation may continue only through its expressly qualified remedy branch, not by treating all terminal states alike. Current revocation, wrong context, missing required history or unresolved authority fails closed for the affected act without erasing independently known outcomes.

Enforce at most one ever-issued direct replacement successor per predecessor and at most one predecessor for that replacement, in the same tenant/environment/source. The relationship cannot point to itself, form a cycle, reparent either issued request or be cleared by later cancellation, retention cleanup or restoration to make its slot reusable. Any issuance of a bound replacement draft through a generic Issue action, import, service or alternate writer must enforce the same handoff or exact earlier-cancellation continuation; no unlinking or relabeling a bound draft bypasses it. A subsequent mistake after issuance is addressed through the current successor's qualified source remedy and, when appropriate, its own new replacement; the old predecessor is never revived or reused to issue a sibling replacement. Distinct genuinely independent source needs keep their ordinary authoring paths and cannot use this operation to manufacture duplicate obligations. Linkage is historical fact, not a separate status owner or an access grant.

Use permanent semantic effect identity for preparation creation, draft changes, independent stop and final issuance/handoff, with explicit material user intent separated from resolved qualification witnesses and generated IDs/times. Recover a matching committed effect under current safe disclosure before applying fresh eligibility; return its original request/terms plus truthful current status rather than replaying its writes or substituting the latest successor. Changed material intent conflicts. Canonical no-op saves change no business revision, event, clock or count; stale expected basis still conflicts. Concurrent different transport keys cannot issue two successors. Retry serialization failures as the complete intended transaction with current source checks; reconcile uncertain commitment rather than inventing a fresh operation key. Providers' temporary idempotency windows, client button disabling and worker leases are not permanent effect identity.

Preserve the predecessor's Q34 cancellation meaning in full: no new ordinary predecessor intake, correction, review decision, item change or clarification; no unacceptance, deletion, waived requirement or fabricated fulfillment. Preserve durable receipts, accepted evidence, conversation, actor attribution, independently lawful safety/review follow-through, retention, holds and other uses. Its optional finite canceled-outcome response may use only the exact then-valid unfinished admission and unchanged end/caps/current restrictions under Q34; no files, transcript, internal reason or new person's identity is disclosed, and no later root or canceled renewal is created. Otherwise show neutral unavailable/help. Existing independent shares/rights retain their actual source scope and any applicable restriction, not a blanket global revoke or transfer. Already delivered copies cannot be recalled by this operation.

The successor is a new original request with fresh item identities, its own real issuance occurrence, Q11/Q12 original finite access terms, Q25 responsibility and Q01 recipient proof/current-authority requirements. It is not Q13/Q15 fresh continuation or inherited Q06 completed reading. Resolve and review its Requested by target under Q09/Q46 rather than blindly carrying forward old terms or backdating new work; a still-appropriate source-valid value, including a permitted past date, may be deliberately reused with new current applicability. Receiving history and source-sufficient facts keep their original meaning; no universal rule that the target must precede the access end, and no silent access extension, is introduced. Do not copy old ends, auto-renew old grants, promise a reset through resend, force a full donor/missionary account or create a fake Party. Qualified existing proof for the new principal/address/request purpose may be reused only under Q01's real rules. Source issuance, notification delivery, proof exchange, receipt, safety, acceptance and fulfillment remain distinct.

For Q48, end old recipient-work reminder eligibility through the actual predecessor cancellation/source fence. Preserve all old plans, captured settings, stops, used counts, reservations, original recipient/material pins and possible-send/unknown history; do not reroute, refund or revive old occurrences. A genuinely different source-authorized person may receive a genuinely new original request and its own Q48 plan/count under the tenant's current enabled policy and permitted staff choice, with fresh issuance timing; this is a new request, not a reset of the old one. Display that new enrollment choice at review. Do not automatically copy the former person's Stop/allow or pause state to another person, or clear restrictions whose actual scope still covers the new action. Source/P3 eligibility must reject nominal identity changes, recreation or contact aliases used to evade an existing person's stop, limit or contact restriction. A shared mailbox proves neither new identity nor new permission. No Q48 event or numerical bound is otherwise amended.

Reuse exact existing communication purposes: one actual Q34 cancellation may produce its Q35 occurrence under current tenant/default/staff choice, recipient/contact/privacy and usefulness rules; a cancellation already committed earlier produces no second cancellation occurrence on replacement issuance. Do not notify a known-wrong former recipient. A formerly legitimate recipient whose responsibility changed may still receive a factual Q35 notice when its current independent disclosure/contact qualification holds; impose no blanket cancellation-email ban. The successor's actual original invitation/follow-through uses its qualified source/P17/P6 contract, with required local handoff durable at issuance; provider completion is not a prerequisite for source success. No new generic recipient-changed notice, extra channel, automatic staff broadcast or copied old message is introduced. Keep the exact closed P17 binding, complete all-before-any message-plan release, P6 sealed original recipient/material/provider identity and original recovery/retry windows; no direct provider fallback, partial plan release, fresh-key resend or false recall. Keep source relationships, historical facts and serving boundaries consistent across staff, recipient, search, lists, counts, activity, export, notifications, result recovery, caches and retained copies. ## Ratified glossary addition

**Document Request Replacement:** A source-authorized new request issued for a different intended recipient in place of an eligible prior request, with an explicit relationship preserving each request's identity and history. It is distinct from draft preparation, an address edit, reopening, or transfer of earlier evidence or access.

This exact definition is adopted as the 30th canonical glossary term; all 29 earlier definitions are preserved. It describes an issued replacement outcome, not an inert draft, new identity system or evidence-transfer permission.

## Consequences and alternatives

A gives one contextual preparation and meaningful handoff for a different person, preserves each recipient's history and uses only reviewed safe definitions. Its cost is a distinct request and protected linkage with actual failure recovery. The strongest B would preserve the same reference only in a proved pristine cohort; that branch adds historical eligibility and clock/control complexity, so it is not selected. Same-person contact recovery remains separate and qualified.
