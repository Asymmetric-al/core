# ADR-0010: Separate finite unfinished and completed request access

**Status:** Ratified by Conrad, 2026-10-20: Q10 A, the exact corrected
decision, Q10-C01–C22, Q10-P01–P30 and all eight operational controls.
The reviewed disposition was Accept with required amendments; those amendments
are adopted grooming intent. Q01–Q09 remain ratified. No initial duration/anchor,
extension/recovery power, reminder or implementation is selected.

Conrad chose separate finite unfinished and completed stages for the new
ordinary Private Document Request. A prospective conditional policy preserves
useful Q06/Q07 follow-up after eligible normal fulfillment rather than only
the remainder of a deadline intended for unfinished work. The strongest
alternative is one original overall ceiling: simpler and potentially shorter
exposure, but review near that end can leave very little completed reading.

## Ratified decision and amendments

Declare the unfinished-stage deadline's scope before initial source admission.
It governs recipient access while the request remains unfinished; it does not
also cap a valid completed stage through that old stage role. Existing,
specialized, unknown or genuinely overall limits are not relabeled.
The complete finite initial clock and exact source mapping remain activation
gates; missing values or pending review cannot mean forever.

Only the exact durable normal source fulfillment while unfinished authority
is valid, with proved historical policy, scope and recipient eligibility,
admits the completed stage. Receipt, scan, generic task status, a click before
expiry or a backdated timestamp cannot substitute. At or after the exclusive
unfinished end, actual later fulfillment cannot revive recipient authority.
Independently authorized source completion remains possible without a
recipient admission.

Resolve the original completed end from that source occurrence using Q07's
720 elapsed hours and all genuinely applicable independent earlier caps.
Keep true cross-stage shortening and current restrictions. Do not reset an
end through retry, return, remediation, new work or complete/reopen cycles.
Ordinary submission/correction stops; Q03 exact review lineage and Q04/Q05
private current-file scope remain intact.

Serialize fulfillment/admission against relevant evidence-set, correction,
recipient and adverse-authority changes. Preserve required facts, historical
eligibility, exact outcome/end and durable follow-up intent under the source's
coherent transaction/recovery contract. Qualify time and current state after
waits; no provider call under source locks or timestamp-only authorization.

Delayed consumers may recover the original timely eligible transition, even
after the old unfinished deadline, while honoring current authority and the
actual completed end. They cannot manufacture old eligibility or use repair
time plus 30 days. Stale unfinished-expiry jobs/caches must not erase valid
completed authority. Proven derived errors need attributable repair without
bypassing genuine revocation or guessing unknown history.

Keep business completion, recipient admission, current reading, proof/session,
file readiness and notification independent. The transition neither extends
technical credentials nor requires an online recipient, extra enrollment,
timer-start confirmation or new grant approval. Use current qualified
re-entry or compatible proof reuse.

Use the same accessible request context with truthful receipt/review states
and actual relevant ends. Do not blame a timely recipient for staff review
or imply that upload triggers completion. If review outlasts unfinished access,
preserve source work and provide real owned help without an unselected
extension or Q08 promise. Partial history/preview failure preserves other
independently authorized actions.

Keep current private serving, scoped database/RLS/service boundaries, records
retention, independent source uses and communication ownership. Qualify
prospective cohorts, old/new worker compatibility, restore/rollback,
real-role races, actual recipient/reviewer/help journeys and useful initial
terms before activation. No second permission engine or general drive follows.
