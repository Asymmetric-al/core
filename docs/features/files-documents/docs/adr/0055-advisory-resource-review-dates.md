# ADR-0055: Advisory resource review dates and deliberate review evidence

2026-10-26, Asia/Bangkok. **Status: Fully ratified by Conrad: Q55 A with all required amendments.** Reviewed disposition: **Accept with required amendments**; the complete amendment package is adopted. Q01–Q55 are ratified; all31 glossary definitions remain unchanged. The approval mentions a glossary refinement, but this reviewed package requires none; no term edit is invented.

## Context and alternatives

Conrad chose to keep otherwise qualified resources available with clear review-due status. A review target is maintenance planning, not an automatic source-access expiry. The strongest alternative automatically withholds after the target, but can interrupt useful reference access. Existing independent safety/rights/withdrawal/access deadlines still bind. A simple current-target projection is preferred to an unselected sticky overdue cycle; optional ordinary next dates and deliberate review/correction are explicit amendments, not claims of existing native capability.

## Ratified decision and amendments

**Q55-C01 — Selected availability rule and scope.** Keep an otherwise published, currently authorized and source-qualified P28 resource available after its review target passes, with honest Review due status. Review due is advisory maintenance information, not a publication, approval, safety, access, retention or deletion state. Every independent source restriction, withdrawal, rights limit and actual hard validity boundary still governs. Known prohibited or unsafe content cannot continue merely because this rule is advisory. The first cohort is the existing P28 resource publication, missionary reader audience, authorized staff curation and already-qualified guide references. This does not make ordinary evidence, public Media, official documents, donor records or every held file into a resource, add a general staff catalog, or reopen P28 ownership. A broader staff-reference purpose remains an explicit coverage item requiring its actual owner, not an implied entitlement.

**Q55-C02 — Authoritative facts and exact basis.** P28 owns the resource publication, actual resource-review facts, review target and their corrections; P4/P12 owns identity and current authority, and P29 owns held bytes/version/integrity/safety/retention. Store a review against the exact admitted publication and its relevant review basis, including content version, audience, purpose and applicable source conditions; a familiar path or file name is insufficient. A newer draft/upload is not current and cannot inherit a previous basis's review. For an external resource, the fact describes review of the exact approved reference and its declared scope at that time, not permanent control or verification of mutable remote bytes. Derived Last reviewed and due labels never become mutation authority. The most recent still-applicable, noninvalidated actual review supplies Last reviewed; absence of such proof is not proof that nobody ever reviewed it.

**Q55-C03 — Minimal explicit planning profile.** For the ordinary resource purpose, the next review target is optional, with a known intentional absence represented as No review scheduled. This is an explicit proposed clarification of founder-required review metadata, not a claim that P28 already selected optionality. It creates no automatic interval, default future date or recurring obligation. Any independently applicable source requirement for a target remains binding and prevents Clear; no new general policy builder is required. A set target is a finite civil date with its source-declared IANA time-zone basis, initially drawn from the qualified organization setting and shown to the curator. If that basis is unknown, obtain a valid source-owned choice before setting a target; never guess from the browser. Review becomes due when the trusted current calendar date in that basis is later than the target, after the full chosen day. Equality is not overdue. Display locale does not change meaning. Tenant/time-zone setting changes do not silently reinterpret an existing target; an explicit attributed target/zone change is required. New plans use today or a future source-local date; imports and proved corrections preserve their actual evidence rather than fabricating a new plan. Reject malformed/infinite dates and invalid zones. Preserve ambiguous legacy dates as unknown until qualified, without silently converting instants to dates or applying retroactive expiry.

**Q55-C04 — Honest reader presentation.** A permitted resource remains in normal eligible results and can be opened/downloaded without a due-date acknowledgment, modal or forced preview. Show Review due with the meaningful date context when due. Otherwise show neutral Review planned for [date] or known No review scheduled; never Fresh, Verified, Safe or Guaranteed current merely because a target is future or absent. Show Last reviewed only from actual applicable review evidence and within its disclosure scope. A proved complete result with no applicable noninvalidated review record says No review recorded for this version; it does not claim nobody ever reviewed the material. Unknown/incomplete review metadata says Review information unavailable. Both are distinct from intentional no schedule for the next target. Actual publication revision and Updated are separate facts. A status-fetch failure must not invent clean freshness or itself withdraw an independently authorized resource; unknown permission still fails closed. A protected or withdrawn resource uses its existing neutral unavailable state, without leaking titles, status or successor details. The optional guide follows C44: independent safe advice can remain, unsafe dependent advice is withheld, and no blanket guide shutdown or new missionary task follows merely from review due.

**Q55-C05 — Deliberately record an actual review.** Provide one source-owned Record review action for a currently qualified curator who actually assessed the intended current publication/basis. The action records trusted actor and occurrence time at authoritative admission after relevant waits, not caller-authored identity, arbitrary backdating, browser time, read-click counts, preview duration or a scan result. It atomically records the actual review and the curator's explicit next-target choice: today/future in the declared source zone, or known no schedule where permitted. A valid existing future target may remain when shown and confirmed; an old past target is not silently rolled forward or presented as the next review. Explain that the actual review completes that prior target and show the proposed next state before saving. Unchanged content needs no re-upload or fake edit, no minimum essay and no routine second moderator. Publish/correct/replace may compose this act in one source command only when explicit actual-review intent and the exact reviewed basis are captured; generic Publish, Save, upload, restore or a newer version never automatically stamps Reviewed. If content needs correction or withdrawal, use the actual source route rather than recording a false successful review. A withdrawn historical publication is not made available by Record review; preparing/re-offering uses its real source publication command.

**Q55-C06 — Flexible date changes without fabricated review.** Allow currently authorized Set, Change or Clear review date where the source permits it, independently of an actual review. A deliberate reschedule changes the CURRENT due projection, including after a genuinely missed target; preserve the prior date/zone, actor, time and fact of when it changed in durable source history. Last reviewed remains unchanged, and the UI states Changing this date does not record a review. Do not create a sticky missed-review cycle, second planning date, automatic recurrence or new obligation merely to retain prior history. A future reschedule displays Review planned for [date], not Reviewed/Fresh; authorized history remains available to explain repeated deferral. A same-target save is a current-authorized, expected-basis no-op with no new target/history fact, business revision or review event, even if that unchanged target is now past; independently required security auditing remains. Today/future validation applies to a genuinely new plan; it must not reject recovery of a previously committed operation or manufacture a new target. Clearing is deliberate and says No review scheduled, never Never expires, and cannot override an independently required target.

**Q55-C07 — Supported factual correction.** A source-authorized correction of a demonstrably misrecorded target or erroneous review is distinct from ordinary rescheduling and from performing a new review. Require a concise factual reason and the actual source correction authority; preserve the original fact and actor/time, linking the attributed correction rather than overwriting/deleting history or impersonating its author. Correction of an erroneous review stops that fact from supplying current Last reviewed, recomputes from remaining applicable facts, shows No review recorded for this version when their absence is proved complete, or shows Review information unavailable when history is incomplete/unknown, and atomically confirms the CURRENT target choice. It never restores an old target or publication/access state by blind rollback, defeats newer facts, or fabricates another review. A proved target correction may legitimately yield a past target and Review due; ordinary prospective planning cannot use that route as an undocumented override. Later genuine reviews of the same unchanged basis remain possible. Normal correction must have a capable scoped product path; direct database repair or a powerless Contact admin instruction is not the permanent solution. Any required erasure/anonymization follows its actual owner while preserving only lawful history.

**Q55-C08 — Trusted current authorization.** Derive tenant/environment, organization, authenticated native principal and attribution from verified server context and current source/P12 scope. A request body, selected organization, URL, owner field, user-editable metadata, email, profile role, default/demo tenant or old JWT membership claim cannot establish curator authority. Auth token validity is distinct from current tenant membership, assignment, source action capability, restrictions and the qualified session-revocation contract. Reconcile the selected tenant with actual current scope and recheck at protected reads and authoritative mutation admission after relevant waits; current source restrictions cannot be defeated by stale token/context/cache. Qualified native staff need not be given a fabricated Party; neither the presence nor absence of a Party grants curation. Reader, curator, steward, support and system/service actions retain separate admitted powers and real attribution. A generic staff/admin helper or service-role bypass is not sufficient proof. Reader status, history, reviewer identity, counts, filters, links and bytes each require their actual disclosure scope.

**Q55-C09 — Database and RLS invariants.** Use the existing source-owned server command boundary in packages/api for review/date/correction/history mutations, with trusted validated inputs and one authoritative transaction for the business effect, mandatory history and durable operation result. Preserve P12's single fine-grained policy decision point and prescribed coarse Tenant RLS defense, not another capability/membership resolver in row-policy joins. Thread the validated Tenant Authorization Context through the qualified database context and prove application/RLS agreement, unforgeability, fail-closed missing/mismatched context and pooled-connection reset; current JWT helpers and generic wrappers do not establish that intended contract. Current source commands/projections and restricted alternate doors enforce finer purpose/capability scope. Set-wise governed reads retain R6: at most one logical governance-row SELECT plus one logical ledger UPDATE, with only its already-declared qualified physical-sharding carve-out; this is not a limit on all business queries. Do not permit direct browser patches to trusted review, actor, tenant, publication, correction or audit fields. Enforce same-tenant and actual organization/source/version relationships with appropriate composite keys/foreign keys or an equally proved authoritative boundary; source keys must be non-null for admitted facts, and a target date/zone must form a valid pair. Known-none and unknown remain distinguishable. Use date for civil targets and timestamp-with-time-zone for actual instants, stable identifiers and exact foreign references; no new money fields or floating-point money behavior is involved. Prevent duplicate effect per scoped operation identity, correction of a foreign fact and cascade deletion of required history; do not impose permanent uniqueness per publication/content basis because later real reviews are valid. Narrow grants and all effective policies must enforce both old-row USING and new-row WITH CHECK behavior, including SELECT/RETURNING, inserts, upserts and combined permissive policies. Row membership alone cannot protect immutable columns or permitted state transitions. PostgreSQL can reuse USING when WITH CHECK is omitted; inspect effective behavior rather than call omission alone a vulnerability. Exposed views must preserve caller authorization or have access revoked; privileged functions require deliberate role/EXECUTE/search_path/owner controls and explicit source checks. Definer privilege and RLS bypass depend on actual role configuration, not the keyword alone. Service-role, RPC, Data API, collection, export, Storage and internal-job paths must preserve the same business boundary.

**Q55-C10 — Atomicity, ordering and recovery.** Bind an operation identity to trusted actor/scope, exact immutable intent and the relevant expected source/review-plan basis. Recover a matching previously accepted result under current disclosure before treating its retry as a new write; do not reapply an old review/target over newer changes. Reuse of an identity with different intent conflicts. For a new act, recheck current action authority and relevant basis, classify a true plan no-op, then validate and atomically commit its effect/history/result. Record review is an actual attributed act, not a value-only no-op; a deliberate later review may create a new fact. Serialize or compare-and-swap the relevant source facts so replacement, withdrawal, correction, date changes and permission restrictions cannot jointly produce an invalid result. A changed reviewed content/audience/purpose basis requires refreshed review; preserve safe draft input without auto-submission. Check the relevant expected revision/generation, not only equal displayed values: stale A-to-B-to-A intent is not a no-op. Unrelated technical/display changes do not invalidate an actual review unless its source-defined meaning-bearing basis changed. Do not hold broad tenant locks or a transaction over remote network inspection. Lost response is an unknown outcome, not a proved failure; retries recover the original. Permission loss may limit result detail without authorizing another act or disclosing now-hidden data. No universal Undo, automatic alternate mutation or resurrection from stale retry/restore follows.

**Q55-C11 — Complete private serving and current projections.** Apply source permission before search results, due filters/counts, titles, dates, thumbnails, accessible labels, reviewer/history serialization and successor links; post-filtering a broad response is insufficient. Cache keys and invalidation bind tenant, organization, principal/scope and relevant publication/review basis; no shared private response cache, leaked prefetch or unqualified durable browser storage. Query results/realtime/late responses do not grant continuing permission. At source-day changes, navigation/refocus/reconnect and successful changes, refresh status through bounded shared behavior; do not poll each card. If a retained view cannot validate current status, display uncertainty instead of a silently fresh-looking target. Byte delivery, old URLs, previews, range/conditional requests and guide consumers keep P28-C40's current access contract. Supabase private Storage and short signed URLs alone do not prove immediate source revocation; qualify the actual delivery path. Due status adds no new byte endpoint, download tracking or public accessibility. External reference checks use the established destination policy; this feature creates no server URL-fetcher or SSRF-capable crawler.

**Q55-C12 — Historical integrity and independent ownership.** Preserve exact published/version and earlier delivery/review facts. A new version, audience or purpose does not inherit an old review as if it covered the new basis. A failed replacement leaves old content available only while independently eligible, with its own honest review state. Date/review/correction events neither change independent evidence acceptance, request fulfillment, Q48 reminders, consent, coaching, financial facts, other lawful source uses, holds nor retention clocks. Publication withdrawal stops affected new library releases without recalling downloaded copies, changing external websites or deleting underlying originals. Business history, security audit and technical telemetry remain distinct. Finite readable-history retention must preserve the minimum operation-uniqueness/result-or-reject evidence required for the supported retry contract; expiration or restoration cannot make an old semantic operation new again. If original identity/intent can no longer be safely verified, reject the unverifiable stale operation rather than execute it anew. Redaction of result detail does not change its real prior outcome. Keep sensitive names, internal reasons, raw content and protected identifiers out of generic logs, URLs, analytics and session replay; authorized history/export/backup follows actual minimization, retention, erasure and access policies, not an invented keep-forever rule.

**Q55-C13 — Small complete curator and reader journeys.** Use the existing resource management surface for due/unscheduled/unknown filters, current owner, exact content/basis and current target/history; do not build a second inbox/CMS. The curator can Record review, Change review date, prepare a correction/successor, Withdraw or use the qualified factual-correction route according to actual capability. A departed/unavailable curator can be replaced through existing source stewardship under current authority without impersonation, dead ownership or universal staff power. The ordinary reader sees a focused status/date and independently permitted Open/Download/Back/Retry or actual successor; internal reviewer identity, reasons and staff controls do not leak into that surface. Distinguish no resources, no filter matches, unknown review metadata, load failure and unavailable old link. Preserve safe list position, filter and input on return/conflict/retry while reauthorizing. Show Saving/Checking result and successful actual server outcomes honestly; do not optimistically announce a completed review before the transaction succeeds.

**Q55-C14 — Accessible shared UI and localization.** Preserve exact base-maia, Base UI and shared packages/ui semantics/tokens. Status must have text/programmatic meaning, not color, hover, toast or icon alone. Use plain Review due, Last reviewed, Review planned for, No review scheduled and Review information unavailable with localized dates and explicit source-zone help where relevant. Date entry supports keyboard and a clearly labeled accessible picker without making either the sole route; field errors and corrective hints remain associated and persistent. Preserve logical focus, screen-reader announcements for meaningful save/error outcomes, nonmodal ordinary status and safe focus return. Qualify 320 CSS-pixel reflow, 200% text, 400% zoom, long international names, RTL/IME, touch and low-bandwidth behavior. Core's 44px touch convention is not a claim that WCAG's separate 24px minimum is 44px. Existing accessible source-content requirements still apply; good chrome cannot repair an unreadable PDF. No unnecessary animation, mandatory essay, forced whole-file fetch or second approval is introduced.

**Q55-C15 — Bounded work and no new automation system.** Derive current due state from authorized source facts and trusted time; the passage of a date needs no business-state write, worker, event fan-out, email, task or recurring job. No sticky review-cycle table, AI verification, second search index/provider, per-resource polling or general policy/notification matrix is required. Use finite paginated source-scoped queries, stable continuation with a unique tie-breaker, deterministic ordering and measured indexes for actual tenant/organization/status/date/review lookups; store no time-varying generated/index predicate based on now() as if immutable. Avoid downloading file bytes to compute metadata or duplicating large history/content into each card. Apply finite input/page/history/time/resource budgets justified by the real cohort and EXPLAIN/production-shaped tests before activation; user-visible limits and safe recovery must agree. No new quantitative latency, capacity, zero-bill or cost-saving claim is made here. Preserve applicable existing P28 C49 operating thresholds and P12 R6 exactly; new measured budgets may not weaken them. The applicable resource target-read alert remains server p95 greater than one second for fifteen minutes at the approved tested workload, not a measured production baseline or a new review-date scheduler SLA. Tenant/staff control is through their current source powers and deliberate scheduling/clearing, not new compulsory cadence. Independently qualified source effects remain intact, including C44 maintenance for truly invalid references; due alone triggers none.

**Q55-C16 — Compatible rollout and repair.** Add the smallest source review/target/history support with compatible reads/writes, explicit unknown/known-none handling and no fabricated historical reviews. Backfill only facts actually established; a known published/authorized legacy resource does not disappear solely because review metadata is missing, while unknown publication/access/classification stays unadmitted under C40. Do not auto-enable a deadline, cadence or review badge for existing tenants. Expand/qualify/read-compatible first, then enable new commands for a bounded admitted cohort; fence old writers that can bypass new invariants. New commands can be disabled while retaining safe readable history and actual withdrawal/access controls. Roll forward corrections; rollback cannot drop committed evidence or revive invalidated reviews/restrictions. Restore/export/provider upgrade qualification reconciles current source facts, references, policy and lawful history before reopening serving. Use supported source remediation, not recurring manual SQL cleanup. Existing delivery/Auth/DB dependencies need real version/environment proof; no new vendor is chosen.

**Q55-C17 — Traceability, proof and operations.** Map every clause to the P28 C38–C40/C49 owner amendment and C44 consumer effects, with P12 current-authority and P29 custody acceptance retained. Formal owner/OpenSpec/design/task publication occurs together at the session's /to-prd stage; tickets at /to-issues. Generated projections are not independent authors and no implementation/ticket/release evidence is invented. Require native grants/RLS/RPC/service/Storage, real staff/reader roles, exact no-Party cases where admitted, revocation/row-transformation negatives, clock boundaries, races/recovery/correction, migration/restore, accessibility and production-shaped performance proof before activation. Use the operating controls below with a named role, signal, threshold and response; monitor cannot substitute for an unsatisfied safety invariant. No new Files/Document glossary term is needed because resource review stays P28-owned and is explicitly distinguished from ordinary evidence acceptance/comments. Preserve all 31 definitions and prior ratified decisions. This amendment package is fully ratified; research and one Q56 question are authorized without inferring its answer or runtime activation.
