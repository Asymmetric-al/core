# ADR-0012: Sixty elapsed days for ordinary unfinished request access

**Status:** Ratified by Conrad, 2026-10-21: Q12 A, the exact corrected
decision, Q12-C01–C22, Q12-P01–P30 and all eight operational controls.
The reviewed disposition was Accept with required amendments; those amendments
are adopted grooming intent. Q01–Q12 are ratified. No implementation,
qualification or formal publication is implied.

Conrad selected up to sixty elapsed days from original source issuance for
the new ordinary unfinished-request profile. This gives more opportunity for
delivery, recipient work and source review than the strongest tighter
alternative of thirty days, while allowing a longer potential access window.
Sixty is a product judgment, not a measured ministry optimum or vendor standard.

## Ratified decision and amendments

The nominal original unfinished end is Q11's qualified original source issuance
plus exactly 1,440 elapsed hours: 5,184,000 seconds in the qualified source
instant model. Resolve and preserve the admitted result and calculation/limit
provenance. No calendar-month/day, business-day, midnight, requested-target
buffer, first-visit or retry-time substitution.

“Up to” retains genuine current restrictions and correctly scoped earlier
limits; it does not create sender or tenant discretion from zero to sixty.
Do not minimize every date on the record. A hard intake-only cutoff can stop
submission/correction while independently authorized reading and review remain.
Per-file, proof/session, worker and retention ends retain their own meanings.
True overall limits retain their cross-stage scope.

Preserve Q10's eligible transition and Q07's at-most-720-hour completed period.
Early fulfillment may make the completed end earlier than the old unfinished
end; later timely fulfillment can make it later. Do not use a maximum of the
two dates, retain ordinary writes or promise a minimum sixty-day entitlement.
The derived original lifecycle bound of strictly less than ninety elapsed days
is explanatory, not a new cap, guarantee, storage rule or Q08 fresh-access limit.

Preserve exact original source identity/results, current capability and
recipient proof, causal historical eligibility, after-wait time/state checks,
durable same-effect recovery and qualified occurrence/commit semantics.
Actual late fulfillment cannot revive ended authority. No pause, grace or reset
follows from delivery failure, proof renewal, activity, correction or review.

Qualify supported timestamp precision, ranges, offsets and exact units at every
boundary. Invalid/nonfinite/overflowed values cannot fall back to now or a new
default. A single sixty-day Node timeout exceeds its supported delay and is
not a durable expiry mechanism. Source ends/current checks remain authoritative;
bounded durable wakes in existing infrastructure are permitted and must be
stage-aware. Preserve owner-required fences and avoid new global serialization.

Keep P12's sole capability resolver, exact native participant successor, coarse
tenant RLS, mutation boundary, current egress/stream checks and existing read
budgets. The sixty-day source period does not change cookies, credentials,
provider URLs or other technical lifetimes. No new permission engine or timer
platform is warranted.

Adopt prospectively for the exact ordinary source cohort. Explicitly map
Workflow's suggested fourteen-day wording: if it governs a credential, preserve
that shorter mechanism and qualified re-entry; if a binding makes it this
ordinary source's expiry, reconcile the exact owner clause during authorized
publication. Do not silently relabel old grants, extend existing ends, import
reauthorization power or replace specialized source policies.

Show the actual applicable date/time/timezone and next actor in the existing
Base UI/base-maia request context. Keep Requested by separate and explain that
completed access has its own end. A later target is a possible timing mismatch,
not a universal target-only rejection or implicit extension. Preserve received
work and partial authorized actions; staff review is not recipient lateness.
Proof/observer uncertainty fails closed without false expiry/deletion claims.
No extra confirmation, forced download, reminder cadence or duration editor.

Access expiry does not delete evidence, release holds, alter independent uses
or recall downloaded copies. New work, identity changes, active-expiry recovery
and Q08 fresh access retain their explicit owner procedures. Qualify native
source/database/race/provider/browser/AT behavior and useful actual cohorts
before activation; no serial cloning or manual date repair as the operating
model.
