## Ratified decision and amendments

For the ordinary native Document Request Item, use **Request changes** as the
recoverable negative-review outcome on exact eligible unaccepted submitted
evidence. The source records the authorized decision, current requirement/
evidence/review basis and recipient-safe actionable guidance. It remains an
unsatisfied review outcome; it is not acceptance, waiver, file deletion,
technical failure, a new submission or terminal item rejection.

Existing Q03 self-correction remains independent of staff initiation. The
recipient acts only under current source authority, with the same exact-item
immutable correction and predecessor fence. Preserve valid supporting members,
independent accepted items and received history. New bytes still require actual
technical qualification and Q21 review. A new receipt is not evidence that
all concerns are solved or that the item is accepted.

Require exact source authority for the stronger outcome and its scope; generic
staff/read/assignment/grant-administration labels are insufficient. Do not
mandate a new permission atom or assume acceptance authority automatically
covers every negative source action. Trusted context owns tenant/environment,
actor/author/time, with validated source/item/recipient selectors and current
disclosure on replay.

Expose the narrow current source-approved guidance to the exact eligible
participant for this request purpose. Make the item/member, what needs attention
and the permitted next step understandable. Concise safe text or existing
sufficient instructions may satisfy this; no compulsory essay or new reason-code
editor follows. Bare ambiguous rejection is insufficient. Where details cannot
be disclosed, give approved neutral wording and an actually owned next route.
Q05 receipt history is not old-review-note or raw staff-audit access. Protect
internal care/location/third-party data and sealed requirements.

Feedback cannot silently change instructions, requiredness, purpose or requested
data. Real source changes use their existing qualified command/version and
consequences. Record the relevant current review-state revision as well as the
evidence/requirement basis so a stale decision cannot ignore a newer return.
Preserve unaffected work and coordinate only actual dependencies.

If staff later establish that the same current evidence meets the current
requirement, permit a fresh authorized exact Q21 acceptance that records how the
earlier requested change is resolved. Preserve the earlier decision and its
attribution/history. Do not demand fabricated new bytes or use a generic Undo,
Mark resolved, byte-equality shortcut or silent unaccept/reopen. This does not
add a recipient text-only response channel or permit staff to edit submitted
evidence. A successor submission requires new current review; it neither
inherits old adverse findings automatically nor erases applicable unresolved work.

Order return, guidance revision, acceptance, correction, relevant source changes
and Q22 closure through the qualified source boundary. Acceptance/closure first
prevents an ordinary return from undoing it. Return first changes the relevant
review basis so an earlier acceptance attempt cannot ignore it; later deliberate
reassessment remains possible. Correction first fences predecessor review. Return
first does not itself change submitted bytes. Concurrent reviewers preserve
history and coherent current guidance, without silent last-writer loss.

Coherently persist the decision, mandatory history/current outcome and durable
handoff. Co-located source facts/outbox commit atomically; providers stay outside
locks. Durable business identity survives transport keys and runner leases.
Known source success recovers under current disclosure; changed intent conflicts,
and unknown commitment reconciles. Genuine new review decisions are not duplicates
merely because their evidence bytes match. Old replay or delayed projections
cannot reinstate Changes requested after correction, resolution, acceptance or
fulfillment or create stale outward work.

Unresolved required concerns remain unsatisfied under Q22. They are not dropped
from the predicate as waived/canceled. Genuine nonblocking optional feedback does
not hold a completed request open; actual dependencies still count. Closure ends
ordinary correction promises. Q06–Q22 clocks, actual fulfillment occurrence,
current serving and fresh-access rules remain unchanged. Staff may act under
independent current authority after participant expiry without reviving access.

Keep technical unavailable/unsafe/checking states separate from business review.
Use technical recovery or an existing sufficient qualified inspection path;
never fabricate a business return or require upload merely to repair a viewer,
task or notification failure. Preserve independently known source results and
permitted actions while unknown required authority fails closed.

Use the same staff/participant request context, one meaningful action, truthful
status and current guidance beside the affected item. Preserve permitted notes,
reading position and focus, and discard stale responses across user/tenant/
request/purpose changes. Qualify actual document inspection and controls,
keyboard/AT/mobile/reflow/touch/localized/low-bandwidth journeys. Staff delay is
not recipient error. No mandatory long reason, second reviewer/confirmation,
per-file click ritual, automatic download or private offline cache.

Each enabled source needs an accountable qualified route that can resolve
non-remediable or mistaken requests. A contact label without an owner able to
act is insufficient. Do not trap people in repeated demands, impose arbitrary
attempt caps or silently auto-reject/waive. Existing source alternatives and
final dispositions remain authoritative; Q23 adds no generic terminal item,
cancel/reopen/waiver or broader application decision.
