## Ratified decision and amendments

Fulfillment is an exact durable source outcome with relevant scope, evidence,
revision and trusted attribution. Upload/scan/task/technical success cannot
substitute for it. Completion and correction share the relevant source race
boundary, preserving Q03's admission-time predecessor fence and durable effects.
Later invalidation/remediation follows explicit source authority without
rewriting historical completion, silently reopening predecessor work or
expanding an old grant to new files/recipients.

Read-only closes ungranted business mutations, not all technical writes.
Narrowly qualified preview preparation, delivery recovery, audit/metering and
source help may continue under their appropriate current authority. They
cannot change evidence, reset clocks or reopen ordinary intake.

Protected recipient operations require a resolved finite source access policy
and effective end before activation, plus current Q01/P4/P12 authority and
independent representation eligibility. No duration or anchor is invented;
missing policy cannot mean forever. Completion/login/return/retry/help does not
incidentally renew or revive access. New reads at/after the applicable end
are denied; inherited stream expiry and feasible termination remain binding.

Independently authorized source work may complete or reconcile admitted work
after recipient access ends. Preserve that outcome without renewing the
recipient grant or exposing status/history through expired access. Worker,
session, representation, source-access and retention lifetimes stay distinct.

Keep the same accessible task context with truthful completion/current status,
applicable access-end wording, current-file actions and Q05 history. Distinguish
temporary outage, unknown authority, expired session, unavailable file and
ended source access. No permanent private metadata fallback or automatic
renewal follows a bookmark or successful sign-in. Qualify applicable WCAG
timing behavior; a security label or reauthentication alone is not conformance.

Preserve Q05's superseded-content restriction, Q04 exact private reads, current
source restrictions, specialist rights, retention/holds and accountable help.
No archive, new account/portal, automatic email, restore command, source-role
union or new execution platform is selected.
