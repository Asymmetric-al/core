# ADR-0047: Deliberate ordinary submission withdrawal

Date: 2026-10-25 (Asia/Bangkok)

Status: **Fully ratified by Conrad on 2026-10-25, Asia/Bangkok: Q47 A with all required amendments and the Submission Withdrawal glossary term.** Reviewed disposition: **Accept with required amendments**; the complete package is adopted.

## Context

Q03 allows an exact correction when a successor is supplied. Q28's removal applies only to staff-added contributions. Conrad selected deliberate recipient withdrawal without requiring replacement and requested tenant/staff flexibility. The ordinary source therefore needs an explicit new current-consideration action with retained receipt truth and practical mistake recovery. A generic Delete/Restore or staff impersonation would violate the existing ownership model.

## Ratified decision and amendments

Adopt Q47 A: permit deliberate Submission Withdrawal of an exact current participant-origin contribution from an eligible ordinary Document Request Item, without requiring replacement bytes. The requesting source owns the action and actual contribution unit; an item may contain several independent supporting contributions or a genuinely indivisible multi-file contribution. Preserve siblings, other items and independent uses of equal bytes. Historical uploader identity, filename, digest, folder, preview access, generic task status or an unfinished label alone does not confer authority. The ordinary recipient acts only within their current source-proved recipient/action context; this is an explicit new ordinary source/P12 mutation, not an interpretation of Q03 correction or Q28 staff-use removal.

Provide a bounded tenant/source control for Recipient self-service withdrawal, Enabled in the qualified ordinary starting profile. Authorized tenant administrators may require staff assistance by disabling this specific action through existing supported configuration; no arbitrary policy language, general permission builder or per-file exception engine is added. Configuration only narrows availability and never grants source authority or weakens specialist rules. A known qualified source default may resolve the effective policy without a redundant per-tenant override record. Unresolved or unqualified effective policy is not Enabled and cannot authorize a dependent new withdrawal. Apply the current policy and relevant policy generation at command admission, including after waits: Off-first blocks a new participant withdrawal; a committed earlier act remains true. Off does not disable independently permitted Q03 correction, reading, receipt/result recovery or recipient re-offer/resupply. Re-enabling does not silently execute an old uncommitted intent across an intervening policy change. Record attributable configuration changes; do not import Q27 notification suppression semantics into this source action.

Explicitly add narrowly source-authorized staff-assisted withdrawal of the same eligible participant contribution for tenants and legitimate assistance cases. Use the same exact contribution, current evidence-basis, acceptance/freeze, custody and source constraints, with the actual staff actor and occurrence. Record the legitimate assistance/remediation basis from trusted source context or concise staff input under its actual audience and retention rules; no routine essay is required. Do not impersonate the recipient, claim the recipient requested or performed the act without evidence, require the original staff author, or infer this power from staff rank, review assignment, read permission, policy administration or Q28. Participant self-service Off neither grants nor removes an independently qualified staff power. Staff may act after participant access expires only where their own current source authority permits it; no recipient grant, renewed access or credential borrowing is manufactured. This is a bounded new staff source capability, not general deletion, unacceptance or historical rewriting.

Qualify withdrawal against the actual current source/item/requirement/evidence basis, not a new per-file approval flag. A currently accepted, frozen, fulfilled, canceled, no-longer-required or otherwise source-ineligible target cannot be withdrawn through this ordinary action. Staff viewing or preparing a decision is not itself an acceptance lock. A received contribution may still be Checking, quarantined or preview-unavailable: these facts do not by themselves require body viewing or make it unreceived, and a lawful withdrawal can proceed when exact safe identification and source action authority are known. It cannot bypass independent incident/records duties. All-received work awaiting staff review may remain eligible; do not import Q46's outstanding-recipient-work condition. Unknown receipt/current target/authority must be resolved or safely blocked, never guessed from an empty or filtered list.

At durable withdrawal admission, remove only that contribution from current consideration, record the attributable source act and advance the genuinely affected evidence/review basis. Preserve the original receipt, exact immutable version, original participant attribution, applicable instructions/timing, prior decisions and withdrawal history. A withdrawn contribution cannot subsequently be accepted as current through stale review, a late processor result or an old projection. The underlying requirement and independently sufficient other evidence retain their actual source meaning; withdrawal is not rejection, waiver, No longer required, whole-request cancellation, fulfillment, a deadline change or an automatic refusal. Withdrawing a correction never silently revives its predecessor. No local selection, dialog opening, canceled transfer or uncommitted attempt is reported as a received withdrawal.

Use one authoritative source-aware server command per deliberate withdrawal or re-offer, deriving Tenant/environment, actor kind and identity, actual acting assignment or recipient context, occurrence time and immutable source scope from trusted context. Bind the exact reviewed target, canonical intended effect, relevant expected evidence/source/policy basis and durable semantic operation identity. Recheck current authority and time-sensitive limits at actual admission after relevant waits. Coherently commit current membership, attributable act/lineage, required receipt/history evidence, durable outcome and genuinely required existing follow-through. Keep external delivery, expensive processing and user interaction outside short source locks. A shared helper or several individually guarded RPC calls does not make a multi-record operation one transaction; use a source protocol that actually proves coherence.

Reconcile an already committed matching semantic operation under current result-disclosure rules before applying new-action eligibility, target-state or policy/no-op logic. Recover its original actor, occurrence and effect without withdrawing or re-offering newer material. Changed intent under the same identity conflicts. For a genuinely new command, enforce current authority and the expected relevant basis before an honest already-withdrawn, already-offered or unchanged result; no duplicate act, fabricated history, new work occurrence or effect follows a no-op. Equal-looking state after an intervening change is not the reviewed basis. Bound each withdrawal's allowed re-offer effect to its actual source lineage so concurrent clicks, new transport keys or later retries cannot create duplicate current contributions. Keep minimal justified operation evidence or reject unverifiable stale operations after readable history or provider keys expire. A recovered older outcome must not overwrite today's client projection.

Order withdrawal and re-offer with genuinely relevant correction/admission, acceptance and Request changes, source requirement/instruction changes, removal/closure/fulfillment, policy/permission/recipient changes and custody restriction/disposition. Test both source orders. Withdrawal-first fences affected stale acceptance; acceptance-first preserves the accepted basis and blocks ordinary withdrawal. Correction-first cannot silently retarget withdrawal from its reviewed predecessor to the successor. Once withdrawal changes the reviewed current target, a pending correction must not silently become a new re-offer or replacement intent; show current state and use a newly reviewed qualified admission. Preserve lawful selected input without repeating unnecessary transfer. Late technical results can update their own facts but cannot revive candidacy. Revalidate only structurally affected uncommitted review; do not reset unrelated accepted evidence, all reviews, Q25 original-issuance readiness or recipient proof merely because one contribution changed.

Withdrawal changes source consideration, not custody disposal or universal serving rights. Preserve each source's applicable retention, holds, incident/safety processing and independent uses without creating new indefinite storage. It does not delete originals/renditions/indexes, recall a download, prove nobody saw the file, automatically cancel necessary scanning or release reference/hold protection needed by retained history. This is an explicit narrow new resupply admission, not snapshot Restore, reversal of the withdrawal or revival of an earlier superseded predecessor. Preserve Q09/Q46's applicable target interpretation and honest unresolved historical terms. Only post-admission processing permitted by the actual source profile may remain Checking. Existing sufficient technical evidence may be reused only under the qualified current profile; known malicious or otherwise prohibited material cannot be made eligible by re-offering it. If changed instructions, missing required participant statements, restrictions or unavailable bytes prevent this route, show the exact permitted next step through actual new supply/source remediation rather than silently dropping requirements or offering a powerless help link. Supported ordinary recovery must be qualified before activation.

A re-offer cannot silently displace a later correction or another current member, widen its original scope, or impose a one-file-per-item rule. Resolve actual source-defined lineage/cardinality and relevant current basis; preserve independent supporting siblings. <a id="proposed-glossary-addition"></a>

## Ratified glossary addition

**Submission Withdrawal:** A source-owned act that removes an exact received contribution from current consideration for a Document Request Item while preserving its received history and applicable obligations. It is distinct from file deletion, requirement waiver, request cancellation and reversal of acceptance.

The source act is distinct from Submission Correction, which supplies a successor, and from physical deletion or requirement/request cancellation. Re-offer needs no second term. The exact definition is adopted in CONTEXT.md as the 28th glossary term; all 27 earlier definitions remain unchanged.

## Consequences

Recipients gain a deliberate narrow action; tenants can disable self-service withdrawal without disabling independently permitted correction/recovery. Staff assistance gains an explicit scoped capability and truthful attribution. Mistaken withdrawal is recoverable through a new current recipient re-offer of lawful retained bytes, with existing Q28 staff supply where independently qualified. No old approval, history, access or predecessor is restored.

Current source/receipt/custody/authorization/communication facts remain separately owned. Native all-role source/SQL/race/serving/processing/retention/restore/browser/accessibility and actual intended-user/workload proof is required before activation. This ADR records ratified grooming intent, not implementation, deployed qualification, bill neutrality or formal source/OpenSpec/ticket publication. Research and one Q48 question are authorized; its answer remains unselected.
