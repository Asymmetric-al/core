# ADR-0018: Thirty elapsed days for fresh completed-read access

**Status:** Ratified by Conrad, 2026-10-21 (Asia/Bangkok): Q18 A and
the full required amendment package. The reviewed disposition was
**Accept with required amendments**. The corrected decision, Q18-C01–C08,
Q18-P01–P16, eight journeys and six operational controls are adopted grooming
intent. Q01–Q18 are ratified. No runtime implementation, formal publication
or activation is implied.

Conrad selected up to thirty elapsed days from fresh source issuance,
requesting excellent UX/UI without overengineering. The strongest alternative
is fourteen elapsed days under the same existing controls. Thirty provides
sixteen additional days of potential opportunity when no earlier cap applies,
but also a longer opportunity for access. Comparable documented defaults
support its practicality, not an empirically proven Asym or nonprofit optimum.

## Ratified decision and amendments

For the standard Q08 Fresh Completed Request Access profile across both Q16
eligible origins, the nominal read end is Q17's qualified new source issuance
occurrence plus exactly **720 elapsed hours (2,592,000 seconds)**. One elapsed
day means twenty-four hours. Preserve the actual admitted end and material
policy/limit provenance as original source outputs. The same end applies when
access is never used. No calendar month, civil-day increment, local midnight,
business day, first-entry interval or staff date menu substitutes for this rule.

Apply genuine earlier limits at their actual scope. “Up to” does not guarantee
a minimum usable period or authorize arbitrary duration selection. Grant-wide
limits can shorten the period; member/action-only restrictions cannot end
unrelated permitted actions. Ordinary predecessor expiry is historical, not
a fresh cap. Continuing purpose/overall prohibitions and later real shortening
remain binding; clearing a shortening cause cannot silently extend this same
admission. Technical proof or representation failure is not itself permanent
shortening. One grant end does not promise that every file remains available.

Recover a matching committed semantic source effect before calculating another
end. Preserve its original occurrence, end and material provenance through
retries, changed defaults, new transport identifiers, late observations,
delivery retries, proof/session renewal and representation repair. Missing or
unreadable required timing calls for safe source reconciliation, not
now-plus-thirty, guessed nonexistence or activation pending. A historical
expired result remains history under current disclosure; it creates no usable
period. Conflicting material intent cannot manufacture a replacement effect.

Reuse Q17 source/P12 currentness and causal ordering, including relevant waits,
races and operations that cross an exclusive end. Validate trusted required
finite instants, units, supported range and precision. Whole-second duration
units do not permit truncating the source occurrence or silently changing the
admitted end. Effective constraints, tenant-aware relationships, attribution,
semantic uniqueness, old/resulting-row RLS and privileged paths must preserve
exact scope and immutable original results. No allowed update, upsert or
parent mutation can widen or retarget this permission.

Apply current authority and the actual end at every protected metadata and
byte-serving boundary. Correctness must survive process restarts, delayed
workers and failed invalidation; it cannot rely on a browser clock, provider
URL lifetime, cleanup sweep or one thirty-day Node timer. Reuse bounded indexed
checks and existing recovery mechanisms. Starting a download before expiry
does not authorize unlimited future serving; bytes already received cannot
be recalled by online expiry.

Keep the existing **Grant read access** action and completed read-only context.
Staff review the standard and material known limits once; label estimates
until the source returns the actual result. Then show **Read-only access ends
[date, time and zone]**, with current permitted actions and material earlier
scoped limits where disclosure is allowed. Late arrival gets the real remainder;
unused access shares the same end. Distinguish source expiry, proof/session
failure, unavailable content, delivery failure and unknown issuance. Provide
clear recovery in the same context, retaining shared Base UI/base-maia,
accessible status/focus/authentication and mobile/low-bandwidth behavior.
Shorter actual grant, proof and session timers require their own accessibility
qualification. Add no extra account, duration picker, routine confirmation,
forced download/reupload, automatic message or promise of another grant.

Apply this profile prospectively to its exact qualified source cohort. Mixed
versions, policy changes, migration and restore must preserve prior effects,
ends and later restrictions. No invented date backfill or broad fallback is
allowed. Preserve Q16's exact completed member/submitted-original-version/
action/context ceiling, original fulfillment/receipts/corrections, immutable
originals, retention/holds/disposal and independent domain ownership. Equal
thirty-day values in other policies do not make them the same policy or create
another completed stage. Asym remains a contextual use/production system for
relevant files, not a personal drive.
