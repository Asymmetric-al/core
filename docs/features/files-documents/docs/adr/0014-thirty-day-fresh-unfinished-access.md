Conrad selected up to thirty elapsed days from fresh source issuance and
explicitly required excellent UX/UI without overengineering. Fourteen days

## Ratified decision and amendments

For the standard Q13 Fresh Unfinished Request Access profile, the qualified
fresh source issuance occurrence establishes one nominal unfinished end at
exactly **720 elapsed hours** later. The source effect must durably commit and
preserve the actual finite end and calculation/limit provenance. Earlier
applicable limits retain their real scope; “up to” does not introduce a
sender/tenant menu. A click, preliminary approval, email, credential, first
visit, original issuance or later observation does not supply the anchor.

The same end bounds unused access. No separate enter-by stage, first-entry
working clock, pause, grace, rolling deadline or resend extension is selected.
Recover the original outcome of the same deliberate source act before any
new calculation. Preserve earlier admissions, receipts and adverse history.
Missing or unreadable terms require safe reconciliation, not a default period.

Q13's source-qualified completion remains distinct. Actual eligible normal
fulfillment while fresh authority is valid stops ordinary writes and can admit
only the declared completed-read scope, with an end no later than fulfillment
plus Q07's separate 720 hours under genuine caps/current restrictions. Do not
cap that view with the old unfinished-only end, guarantee a minimum, widen
scope or backfill eligibility after actual expiry. Timely durable effects may
be observed later and recovered with their original terms.

For uncapped equal thirty-day intervals, completion on fresh day five can
produce a completed end on day thirty-five; day twenty-nine can produce day
fifty-nine. Qualified atomic issuance/fulfillment can yield equal nominal
ends. Actual narrower read restrictions may shorten access. The derived
less-than-sixty-day bound for one admission is explanatory, not another
stored cap, original-request lifetime, retention or repeated-grant policy.

Keep one meaningful **Grant access to continue** action in the existing source
context. Show the actual localized end in main content, current task status
and next actor, with Requested by separately labeled. Preserve currently
authorized files and receipts without unnecessary reupload. Distinguish
grant success, proof/usability, delivery, receipt, review and unknown outcome.
Use existing Base UI/base-maia and qualified accessibility/recovery patterns;
add no date picker, activation setup, extra account/confirmation, policy
dashboard, notification program or general file-drive capability.

Reuse the owning source command, current permission/data/serving boundaries
and existing durable follow-up. Correctness cannot depend on a browser,
cache refresh, punctual worker or one thirty-day resident timer. Use exact
elapsed units, supported precision/range and current checks after relevant
waits. Apply this profile prospectively through the exact source binding;
preserve old admitted ends and specialized policies. Map Workflow's suggested
fourteen-day rule to its actual credential/source layer instead of globally
replacing it.

## Explicit specialization of Q13

Q14 resolves Q13-C09's standard duration/anchor/activation/unused reservation.
It specializes Q13-C19: **successful fresh source issuance resolves its end;
commit still does not establish current recipient usability, proof or delivery.**
Q13-P13 requires no first-entry activation substage for this profile. Q13-P19's
shortening case uses actual narrower completed restrictions here; equal
nominal thirty-day intervals alone do not produce an earlier end.

All other applicable Q13 authorization, lineage, custody, audit, serving,
retention and rollout safeguards remain binding. Preserve the dated Q13
record and trace this specialization rather than duplicating its architecture.
Repetition/overlap, cumulative policy and Q08 timing/eligibility remain
unselected. Neither unlimited renewal nor a one-use lifetime rule is inferred.
