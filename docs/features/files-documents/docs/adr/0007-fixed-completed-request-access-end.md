## Ratified decision and amendments

Bind the policy prospectively to the exact ordinary source cohort. At eligible
fulfillment, resolve and preserve the admitted end from the one authoritative
effect, its source policy, recipient scope and then-binding earlier access
ends. Use 720 elapsed hours, not a calendar month/local end-of-day. No
caller-supplied business date, mutable task timestamp, consumer processing,
email, first visit or retry may supply or reset the anchor.

The end is an exclusive ceiling, not permission or a guaranteed full interval.
Later genuine source-authority shortening must remain binding; removing or
relaxing its cause cannot restore a later end. Returning, reauthentication,
policy relaxation, replay, help and resends cannot lengthen or revive access.
Temporary representation failure or an explicitly reversible owner restriction
may recover only within unchanged, still-valid authority and its existing end.

Delayed consumers must prove the policy, recipient eligibility and binding
limits at fulfillment, then satisfy current authority before disclosure.
Missing recipient admission must not prevent independently authorized source
completion. Record that outcome without an invented grant; later repair uses
the original proved effect/end, never repair-time plus 30 days.

Preserve Q01/P4/P12 current exact scope, Q03 correction fences, Q04 independent
preview/original qualification and streaming expiry, Q05 participant-safe
history and Q06 business read-only mode. Protect every metadata and byte path,
including private idempotent response replay, conditional/range/resume reads,
cache and provider paths. Qualify the source clock after relevant waits and
state checks; database transaction-start time is not physical commit time.

Keep the same shared Base UI/base-maia request experience, showing the actual
effective date/time/timezone with sufficient precision. Formatting cannot
change an admitted instant. Distinguish expired proof/session, ended source
access, unknown authority and temporary file failure; preserve safe re-entry,
accessible timing and real source help. No automatic renewal, reminder,
membership, private metadata fallback or unrequested download follows.

Source access, intake, session/proof, technical processing/delivery and
retention/holds remain independent. A rendition may remain under legitimate
custody after recipient access ends without being served through that ended
authority. No disposal or copy recall is implied.

Use existing source/PDP/service ownership, bounded queries and durable effect
history rather than a general policy builder. Prospectivity, real-role and
provider/browser proof, mixed-version compatibility, restore barriers and a
scoped kill switch are activation requirements. A source cohort whose earlier
limits prevent any completed readback must be identified before claiming
Q06/Q07 delivery; the limit cannot be silently relaxed to make the claim true.
