## Ratified decision and amendments

For the standard Q08 profile across both Q16 eligible origins, qualified new
source issuance must durably resolve and preserve one finite read-access end
under the adopted calculation policy and actual applicable source limits.
The same end bounds unused access. There is no first-entry activation or
second reading interval. The source event is this new Q08 admission, not the
original request issuance/fulfillment, staff click, preliminary approval,
credential, message/provider event, visit or later observation.

The event/end and calculation/limit provenance are original effect outputs.
Keep them distinct from immutable reviewed material input and recover a
matching committed intent before a new calculation. Visits, proof/session
renewal, transport retries, resends, defaults changes and representation repair
cannot reset time. Conflicting material input does not manufacture another
intent. Historical success can describe an already-ended grant only under
current disclosure; it does not establish current usable access or delivery.

Missing, invalid or nonfinite proposed terms cannot create live permission.
Missing/unreadable required timing after reported issuance needs safe source
reconciliation, not an open-ended success, guessed end or first-entry-pending
state. A historical expired result remains recoverable as history. Qualify
source occurrence, current predicates after relevant waits, boundary-spanning
operations and coherent durable effects together; no clock function alone is
physical-commit or causal-authorization proof. Keep provider calls outside the
existing source/P12 mutation fence.

One fixed grant end does not guarantee every member/action until that date.
Preserve genuine earlier limitations at their real scope without globally
ending unrelated permitted work. The predecessor's ordinary expired period is
historical, not a fresh cap; genuine continuing overall/purpose restrictions
still bind. Technical proof/representation failure is not alone permanent
shortening; clearing a genuine shortening cause cannot silently extend this
admission. Preserve Q16's exact completed ceiling, source completion, receipts,
correction lineage, custody/retention/disposal and already-delivered copies.

Use Q08's existing **Grant read access** action and completed context. Staff
review source-authorized stable terms and known limits, with estimates labeled
before the actual source result exists. Show the actual end after confirmed
issuance under current disclosure, plus relevant scoped earlier limits. Keep
issuance, usability, proof, representation, notification and uncertainty
distinct. Late arrival gets only the remainder; unsupported handoff cannot be
called ready. Preserve shared Base UI/base-maia, accessible status/focus/proof,
localized date/time/zone and mobile/low-bandwidth recovery. No timer selector,
extra account, routine second confirmation, reupload, automatic message or
promise of another grant follows.

Reuse exact source/P12 authority and all-path current serving. Protect trusted
relationships, required finite state, semantic uniqueness, history/attribution,
effective old/new-row RLS and privileged mutations. A permitted update cannot
retarget, widen or extend access. Use bounded indexed current proof and
authorized pagination; expiry cannot depend on a browser, signed URL, resident
timer or punctual sweep. Prospective compatible rollout and restore must keep
actual old ends and later restrictions. No task/email/default-derived backfill
or second permission/timer framework is authorized.
