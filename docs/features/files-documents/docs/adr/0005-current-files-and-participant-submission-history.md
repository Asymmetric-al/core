## Ratified decision and amendments

Superseded means the exact predecessor in a correction lineage, not every
older supporting file. Preserve unrelated current members, Q03's admission-time
fence/no revival and Q04's current-file reads. Finalized-byte admission and its
receipt/history can precede hygiene or preview readiness.

Submission history is a minimized participant projection of authoritative
source facts. Current source/PDP permission applies before metadata, ordering,
counts, continuation and serialization. Do not union staff/audit purposes into
the recipient view or infer history from mailbox, filename, digest or time.
Preserve admitted request/item meaning and trusted attribution.

Enforce the ordinary superseded-content restriction through all reader,
preview/thumbnail/extraction/search/export and byte paths, not just hidden
buttons. Retained originals and existing specialized staff/evidence rights
keep their owning contracts. Current restrictions and governed disposition
still apply to historical metadata.

Keep current status, receipt, errors and next actions visible. Show short
history directly; use shared disclosure or bounded navigation only when
useful. History failure is not No submissions, does not undo receipt and must
not block independently authorized current work. Unknown current authority
still fails closed. Preserve accessible semantics, focus and truthful dates.

Provide an accountable source-approved help route before activation. It can
clarify prior submissions and next steps; it creates no automatic old-file
delivery, staff authority, restore or guaranteed release. Any exceptional
content disclosure retains its independent source/records permission.

No new archive, retention/access duration, bulk export, acknowledgement,
restore command, event platform or general permission builder is selected.
One current authorized projection and existing shared UI are the permanent
path. Actual source/role/browser/recovery and migration proof remain required.
