## Ratified decision and amendments

Allow currently authorized source staff to cancel an issued, eligible unfinished ordinary
Private Document Request when it was issued in error or its collection purpose
has legitimately ended. Record one source-owned Document Request Cancellation,
distinct from fulfillment, item acceptance, deletion, access expiry and any
enclosing application or financial decision. Partial received or accepted items
do not by themselves prevent cancellation; retain their true history and independent duties.

Require actual current source/P12 cancellation authority and the exact relevant
request/context basis, independently of reviewer assignment, generic staff/admin
status, file access or grant management. Preserve concise source justification,
using trusted context or concise staff input where needed, without a mandatory
essay, routine second reviewer or new permission/workflow designer.
Sufficient independent source facts can justify cancellation without file
inspection; unavailable optional preview cannot block that decision.

Commit cancellation through the same authoritative request boundary that orders
Q22 fulfillment and relevant evidence, clarification, requirement and authority
changes. Actual durable fulfillment first prevents cancellation through this
unfinished-request action; cancellation first prevents later ordinary fulfillment.
A review click or queued/deferred evaluation is not itself fulfillment. Record
the real source occurrence, never a browser or pre-wait timestamp.

Cancellation immediately fences new ordinary intake, corrections, review decisions,
Q33 item changes and ordinary Q30 clarification from either side through its
parent source state. Secondary task/index cleanup may recover asynchronously;
it cannot delay the fence. Preserve already admitted receipts/messages/decisions,
independent lawful staff follow-through, safety, retention, holds and other uses.
Retire only obligations dependent on the ended request, preserving their history.

As an explicit Q34 outcome-only amendment, narrow any then-current independently
valid finite unfinished participant admission, original or already-qualified
fresh, to Canceled plus safe source help only. Keep that exact admission's existing
end and every earlier cap/current restriction. If no such admission exists at
cancellation, create no outcome allowance. Optional status/proof readiness must
not delay cancellation; unresolved original eligibility denies protected status,
and later recovery may prove only the admission actually valid at that cancellation,
never select a later root. Q01 proof may be reused or re-established
only for the same existing recipient/root within that unchanged bound; it cannot
renew canceled access. Deny protected status on independent revocation, identity/
recipient restriction, expiry, wrong recipient or uncertain proof and show neutral
unavailable/help instead. No files, transcript, internal reason, new period or
normal completed-request view is granted.

Keep the canceled request terminal. A mistake is recorded through a lasting
source remedy, not Undo/reopen or changing the old outcome to Fulfilled. If the
ordinary need remains legitimate, authorized staff may deliberately prepare and issue a
separate new request through independently qualified normal original issuance,
linked to the mistake and subject to Q25/Q01–Q19. Preserve permitted Q01 proof
and Q28 exact evidence reuse through their owners, without copied grants or
acceptance. Another effective qualified domain remedy may apply. The affected
journey cannot activate with only a decorative contact or direct-SQL repair path.

Persist the decision, mandatory history and durable follow-through coherently;
retries recover the original effect under current disclosure and cannot reopen,
recancel or start a replacement request. Show a deliberate Cancel request action,
reviewable safe request/consequence before commit, then a persistent accessible
result. Preserve existing source-specific delivery identities and current-purpose
checks without a blanket all-mail stop, recall promise or new cancellation notice.
Add no bulk cancellation, participant self-cancel, pause/resume, archive, generalized
reopen, new settings platform or whole future Studio dependency.

## Consequences and qualification

Authorized staff can end an issued-in-error or no-longer-needed whole request
without fake successful completion or erasing already received/accepted work.
Participants with a qualifying retained admission receive only minimal outcome
and safe help within the old bound; others receive neutral unavailable/help.
Cancellation does not grant completed history or revive expired authority.

The principal cost is an actual source terminal command, immediate parent fence,
safe outcome-only action and capable remedy, not a cosmetic task/archive flag.
Known cancellation must survive delayed cleanup and response loss. A mistaken
cancellation is recorded and remedied through independent source authority;
the old record never silently becomes active or fulfilled.

Keep one deliberate reviewable action, concise source basis, persistent status
and lasting recovery. No automatic proof/cancellation email, universal Undo,
extra reviewer, mandatory essay, new permission/cancellation engine or whole
future Studio prerequisite. Existing current-purpose communications may narrow
without a blanket stop of independently lawful staff awareness.

Native P01–P26, composed user/AT journeys, actual-role and privileged-path proofs,
controlled races/fault/restore, current serving and finite workload/recovery
budgets must pass before prospective activation. Actual operators must own the
six controls. Documentation or adjacent mock tests do not supply that evidence.
