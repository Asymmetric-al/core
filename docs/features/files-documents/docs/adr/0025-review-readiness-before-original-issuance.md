## Context and evidence

Conrad selected **A — Resolve review responsibility before first issuance**,
asking for modern practice, flexibility and excellent UX/UI without
overengineering. The [historical question](../../grill/phase29-q25-question.md)
compared this with collecting under a qualified accountable source process
while instance-level review responsibility remained unresolved.

Q24 already allows person/team responsibility and explicitly reserved issuance
timing. Q11 owns original source admission and permanent effect identity; Q12
owns its finite bound. Source activation qualification is distinct from current
per-request readiness. Q21/Q23 actual human review and Q22 fulfillment remain.
This decision explicitly specializes the ordinary source's first admission;
it does not retroactively reinterpret Q24 as a continuing intake restriction.

External collection tools demonstrate default ownership and separation of
drafting from publication. Submittable's staged review supplies a credible B
counterexample. These manuals do not establish a universal best practice or
Asym staffing preference. A is a proportionate ordinary-product choice because
qualified shared responsibility can be reused before recipient effort and the
finite access period start. Its cost is delaying new issuance when required
readiness cannot be established, including a required-check outage.

## Ratified decision and amendments

Before the first original source issuance of recipient access for an ordinary
native Private Document Request, the source must resolve a current qualified
person or team review responsibility and a usable review path for the request's
declared review work and purpose, including applicable declared specialist
restrictions. Reuse a valid source-derived responsibility without mandatory
reselection, personal claiming, online presence, another reviewer or a separate
approval. Different qualified humans may cover distinct declared scopes under
the same team; this never combines their individual permissions.

Prove this through the existing authoritative source admission and current P12
rules, separately checking issuance authority, responsibility administration
when changed, target qualification and actual later review authority. A team
label, task row, stale preflight or caller-supplied readiness flag is insufficient.
Readiness is for the declared source work; it does not require access to
nonexistent future file rows, predict unknown future evidence restrictions,
prove future availability, or constitute evidence acceptance or an access grant.

If the new admission cannot establish the required readiness, do not issue
recipient authority. Preserve independently authorized preparation and provide
truthful, accessible, capable source-owned recovery. Distinguish an invalid or
unresolved destination, insufficient eligible coverage, an unavailable check
and lack of permission to repair. The issuer need not receive a roster, private
evidence access or administrative powers merely to understand the permitted
outcome. Repairing responsibility is not itself issuance or an automatic send.

Recover an authorized same original committed issuance before evaluating
readiness as a prerequisite for a new issuance. Preserve its original identity,
occurrence, deadline, actual admitted terms and minimal qualification provenance;
current disclosure and any new protected action remain separately authorized.
Resolved eligibility witnesses and generated admission outputs are not newly
invented retry intent. Changed explicit material intent conflicts; unknown
commitment is reconciled. No duplicate issuance, timer reset or false Draft
status follows a lost response, later staffing change or delivery failure.

Apply the gate prospectively to first original ordinary issuance through every
supported entry path. Drafts, credentials, email, resends, already-issued intake
and existing fresh-access operations retain their own contracts. Check at actual
source issuance: preparation for later issuance is different from issuing now
and delivering a message later. Later staffing loss uses Q24's qualified source
recovery and actual current restrictions; it does not retroactively erase a
lawful admission, add a completion condition, renew access or demand new bytes.
No new scheduling, routing, staffing, approval, notification or permission
system is selected.

The full review's C01–C08 are incorporated in full. Their essential obligations
are as follows; these summaries do not replace the exact clauses or P01–P20.

- **C01:** apply the prospective predicate to first original ordinary source
  issuance, including unissued existing drafts. Cover actual declared review
  scopes without requiring one person to cover everything, redundant staffing
  or per-item assignment UI. Do not silently gate existing intake or fresh access.
- **C02:** reuse source resolution and separate issuer, administration, target
  and later reviewer rights. A source may provide minimal eligible team-path
  evidence without copying/disclosing a roster or impersonating a reviewer.
  A witness change within a still-qualified team is not personal reassignment.
  Preserve deliberate named targets and all existing supported NHI owner/ceiling
  rules; introduce no automation lane, future-file grant or online/SLA requirement.
- **C03:** enforce through the actual source command across supported paths,
  using current relevant facts after waits. Preserve coherent source admission,
  mandatory history, minimal basis and durable handoff, with atomic co-located
  facts/outbox and providers outside locks. Prove material races without global
  roster locking or pointless re-confirmation. Genuine authorized source edits
  retain their own versions/consequences and recompute actual declared coverage;
  no silent lowering of protected requirements to bypass readiness.
- **C04:** recover original committed success under current disclosure before
  applying a new-issuance gate. Keep material user intent distinct from resolved
  witness and generated occurrence/end. Reconcile unknown effects, reject real
  intent conflicts, prevent concurrent duplicate originals and stale projection
  regression, and retain known source success through ancillary failure.
- **C05:** make the valid default path require no extra step. Give actionable,
  audience-safe repair/service/permission/unknown-result states, preserve actual
  authorized saved work and focus, and provide capable help when the issuer
  cannot repair. Fixing readiness is not a standing auto-issue subscription.
  Qualify the composed Core keyboard/AT/mobile/locale/slow-network journey.
- **C06:** preserve actual original timing. Schedule-only preparation checks at
  later admission; issue-now/deliver-later retains its admitted clock. Existing
  scheduling contracts determine which occurred. Later staffing loss does not
  undo lawful admission, automatically stop independent intake, reset deadlines,
  unaccept evidence, add a completion hold or create renewed/fresh access.
- **C07:** prove stable tenant-aware relationships, original-effect uniqueness,
  valid states, trusted immutable admitted outputs, effective grants/RLS and
  all direct/privileged paths. Dynamic eligibility uses the qualified currentness
  boundary, not a portable ready flag. Minimize private provenance/logs and keep
  bounded indexed coverage/history/recovery under measured finite budgets and
  existing applicable P12 limits. No financial data/precision change follows.
- **C08:** qualify actual native roles, source/custody/task bindings, races,
  faults, replay, workload, composed journeys, mixed versions and restore.
  Preserve genuine old admissions without fabricated historical readiness;
  apply the gate to old drafts when newly issued after activation. Gate unsafe
  old writers, preserve later acts during stop/rollback and reconcile restored
  outward effects before resumption. Trace canonical owners and projections at
  the authorized publication stage. No readiness aggregate or whole-Studio gate.

## Consequences, proof and rollout

The intended common journey stays small: use a valid responsibility and issue
through the ordinary action. If readiness is genuinely missing, retain permitted
preparation and solve the actual cause. The issuer need not inspect a private
roster, become a reviewer or gain administrative rights. A qualified team can
change its current eligible witness without another nominal personal handoff.

The rule covers declared source work; actual future evidence still requires
current classification, safety, inspection and review. A definite rejected
admission grants no usable recipient authority or success-contingent invitation.
An unknown result cannot be treated as a definitely unissued draft. Historical
readiness is never a credential for current use.

The full review specifies all 22 category outcomes, exact clauses, twenty
falsifiable proof groups, twelve journeys/states and six controls. Test actual
user/domain outcomes, including current authority, prospective scope, lost reply
after staffing loss, material races, changed intent, delayed execution/delivery,
real staff repair and restoration. Existing financial sequencing or the scanner
database fixture is not native qualification. Monitoring has explicit signals,
threshold rules, accountable roles and responses; finite measured budgets and
named operators are required before activation.

No new glossary term is necessary. All twenty definitions remain unchanged;
Document Request Access Issuance and Document Review Responsibility already
express the distinction. Readiness is a predicate, not another domain object.

At authorized publication reconcile the ordinary source's exact admission
predicate and qualification with P12/P9/custody/communication and applicable
Workflow owners, full incorporated OpenSpec sections, design/tasks and later
authorized tickets/tests/release evidence. Broader source policies and File
Manager/custody/library/format/disposition work are not silently decided here.
