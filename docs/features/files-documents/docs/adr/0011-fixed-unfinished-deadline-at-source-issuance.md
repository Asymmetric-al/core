## Ratified decision and amendments

Document Request Access Issuance is the source-owned act first making original,
purpose-bound access available to the intended recipient under approved terms.
It is distinct from request/draft/task creation, credentials, communication,
recipient proof and fulfillment, even where an owner lawfully combines effects
in one operation. Required confirmation and current permission still govern use.

Resolve and preserve the finite original unfinished end and its source-approved
policy/calculation/limit provenance. A selects when the end is established,
not a particular elapsed-days formula or a staff date editor. Missing, invalid,
nonfinite or already-ended live terms cannot silently become now-plus-default.
Pre-issuance estimates and material command preconditions must be clear; show
the actual admitted result without repeated timer-start confirmation.

Separate original source admission identity from credentials, prepared messages
and provider attempts. Look up the original semantic effect before computing
new timing. Issuance time/end are outputs of first admission, not regenerated
intent fields on retry. Same intent recovers the original result; conflicting
source/recipient/material terms fail. New transport keys, resends, proof renewal,
visits, corrections and changed defaults cannot restart the original period.

Prove the exact source issuer capability, current tenant/environment, recipient
revision, purpose, resource, host, policy and relevant continuing dependencies.
Preserve Q01's exact native participant successor and P12's sole capability
resolver, coarse tenant RLS, mutation boundary, egress audit and governed-read
budgets. Current broad role compatibility is not qualification of this lane.

Keep issuance, required history and durable follow-up coherent. Qualify source
occurrence and current checks after relevant waits, rollback, boundary-spanning
commit and unknown outcomes. A timestamp is not physical-commit or causal
authorization proof. Provider calls stay outside source locks. Unknown success
must be reconciled before new effects or destructive compensation.

A known unsupported entry path cannot be called ready. Once actual issuance
commits, delivery/proof/representation failures retain the original act/end and
need safe owned recovery. They do not prove source rollback, permanent denial
or expiry. Protected use remains closed until current proof/authority is
established. New credentials follow their required new message identity while
preserving source terms and the exact tenant-host doorway.

“Fixed” does not override genuine revocation/shortening or current restrictions.
Removing a true shortening cause cannot restore a longer end. Preserve Q10's
unfinished-only scope: timely eligible fulfillment can enter the separately
bounded completed stage. Original admission and historical eligibility must
exist in the causal source order at fulfillment. First issuing after earlier
completion cannot backfill that eligibility; qualified atomic/pre-visit
fulfillment is not excluded by equal timestamp precision or an offline browser.

Keep one accessible request journey with distinct requested-by and access ends,
truthful issued/delivery/proof/receipt/review outcomes, current own-file actions,
and accountable help. Do not blame recipients for staff review or create a
duplicate-issue recovery flow, forced download, private attachment workaround,
new account or general drive. Expiry does not determine retention or disposal.

Activate only after the initial finite policy, exact native mapping, real-role
SQL/RLS/service enforcement, concurrency/recovery, provider/browser/AT journeys
and compatible migration/restore paths pass qualification. No new deadline
editing, active-expiry recovery, Q08 fresh timing or notification power follows.
