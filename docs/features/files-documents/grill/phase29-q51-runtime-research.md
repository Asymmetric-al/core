## Proposed fork and recommendation

**A — Allow deliberate directed attention from an exact comment to specifically selected, currently qualified staff colleagues** is the recommended question option. The concrete outcome is a protected cue in Mission Control's **already-governed Phase17 staff notification center**, leading through a typed current-authorized destination to that exact source/comment. It is not a new inbox, a task assignment, a permission grant or a mark that the comment has been answered.

**B — Keep comments unaddressed**, using actual existing qualified review/task/source coordination or manual communication when a colleague needs attention. B retains all Q45 commenting and optional locations. It is a credible lower-complexity choice where the current responsibility/work route already brings comments to the right people, though staff must coordinate deliberate handoffs separately. Do not invent a generic finance queue, assume a reviewer roster is actively monitored, or call an unqualified link/email a safe route.

A makes it possible to bring a specific observation to a colleague's attention without creating a task for each comment or sending a broad notification. Its cost is real producer/recipient/destination qualification and recovery, not just an @-mention widget. B avoids that new directed effect but leaves the coordination step outside the comment. No measured Asym frequency, missed-comment rate or comparative success supports a numerical benefit. A is a bounded product judgment, not a claim that every comment should notify somebody.

A should not be described as already implemented. The source has an owner for the destination and existing UI primitives, but the current visible bell is demonstrative. A must adopt a real narrowly typed Q45 producer and recipient/destination binding into the governed shared presentation before activation. It need not build every future notification producer or the whole Workflow Studio. If that actual end-to-end slice cannot be qualified, A is not honestly available merely because an @ chip appears.

## Minimum consequences of A, without a new platform

1. **A deliberate source act, not typed decoration.** Bind attention to an actually admitted exact Q45 comment/use/version and a finite explicitly selected recipient basis. Typing @text, selecting a suggestion, editing a local draft, blur, preview and IME confirmation have no business attention effect. The selected-answer review must define the actual Post/Send boundary and recovery; an attractive chip is insufficient. No rich editor, Reply tree, follower/subscriber system or all-staff fanout is required.

2. **Existing comment and recipient authority remain independent.** The actor needs actual source power to direct attention; the selected colleague must currently qualify for the exact comment/purpose. Being named, assigned or in the same tenant grants no read, reply, review or archive access. A person's identity/address/assignment must come from its actual source/P12 branch rather than a raw string or generic Support profile. Do not retarget to a replacement teammate silently.

3. **A real shared destination, explicitly prospective.** Adopt a narrow compatible producer/recipient/destination step into P17's already-governed staff center. Show a safe source cue and a typed exact-comment action under current permissions. The current hardcoded bell and finance/Eve helpers prove neither admission nor visibility. Do not add a second inbox, task per comment, compulsory source task, read receipt, deadline or completion gate to compensate for absent wiring.

4. **Channel controls need their real contract.** A establishes directed in-product attention, not an implicit email, SMS or Discord default. Optional external channels, if adopted, need their exact source/P17/P6 meaning, compatible identity/contact/material and actual controls; none is activated by a generic preference or by copying Q27/Q32/Eve/finance defaults. The question is not a new channel-matrix designer. Existing independently qualified effects remain governed rather than globally suppressed.

5. **Commit and recovery must tell the truth.** The source must durably bind the admitted comment/attention intent and complete recipient outcome at its qualified boundary, with permanent semantic identity and a capable recovery path. Do not claim a recipient was notified because a client callback or queue call returned. If the exact directed choice becomes invalid before admission, preserve lawful input/choice and explain a deliberate valid path rather than silently downgrade or redirect. A valid comment's later projection failure must not force reposting the comment or fabricate a second source occurrence. Exact zero recipients and unresolved recipient qualification differ.

6. **Attention is not work completion.** Seeing/read/archive/delivery/open does not accept evidence, change Q24 responsibility, resolve a Q45 comment, answer it or satisfy Q22. A source-authorized comment may remain meaningful after participant expiry or business-status changes under Q45; do not inherit another notice's pending-work predicate. Exact current comment withdrawal/correction, recipient access change and original attention meaning must govern presentation rather than a generic “unread” or “request unfinished” test.

7. **Edit/withdraw/restore is not automatic re-alert.** Q45 owns comment successor revisions and restoration; attention pins its actual original source/recipient occurrence. The selected review must resolve material edit/supersession, removed targets, withdrawal, restore and a later deliberate attention request without silently mutating old evidence, replaying alerts or copying current roster membership. No target is inferred by reparsing old body text. The purpose needs a simple finite deduplication/currentness rule, not a follower engine.

8. **Current field privacy on every surface.** Safe actor/target/comment reference, badge/count, search, preview, history, errors and links all need current source/role authority. The cue is not a license to copy private comment text, Q50 staff names, filenames or geometry into an external payload. Use minimal authorized presentation and recheck the actual destination; no signed private URL, automatic download or external viewer fallback.

9. **Small operations and bounded release.** Use compatible source/API/P12/P6/P17/UI mechanics with finite recipient selection, list/picker/presentation/recovery work and actual source history retention. Preserve real effective grants/RLS/privileged-path checks and restore restrictions. Do not infer readiness from flags or introduce a general notification engine, new provider, mandatory Realtime or full Workflow Studio prerequisite. Actual current workload/cost and intended-user/native proofs remain required; this research supplies none of those measurements.
