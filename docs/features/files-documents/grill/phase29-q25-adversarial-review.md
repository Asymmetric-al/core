# Phase 29 Q25 — Resolve review responsibility before first issuance

**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

**Status:** Conrad selected Q25 A and fully ratified its complete required
amendment package on 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok, preserving modern practice,
tenant/user flexibility and excellent UX/UI without overengineering.
Q01–Q25 are ratified grooming intent. This preserves the adopted decision and
proof obligations; Q26's answer is not inferred. No implementation, activation
or formal owner/OpenSpec publication follows.

Keep A as a narrow source prerequisite: before first issuing original recipient
access, resolve a usable qualified person/team review responsibility for the
declared work. Reuse a valid default without another picker or approval. If
readiness is genuinely missing, keep authorized preparation and provide a real
way to resolve it before issuing access.

This is a defensible ordinary-product policy, **not a universal industry best
practice**. Staged grant/application systems can legitimately collect first and
assign reviewers later. Core's selected ordinary request starts a finite access
period at issuance and already permits shared-team responsibility. A is therefore
proportionate if it adds a reliable source check and helpful recovery, rather
than a new staffing system or a ritual for every sender.

The most important amendments are precise: readiness covers the declared review
work, not future unknown bytes; a team witness is not a personal assignment;
source issuance is not email; committed retries recover their original result
before applying this gate to a new issuance; and later staffing loss does not
retroactively turn a lawful issuance into a failure.

**Strongest B:** permit otherwise-authorized collection while instance-level
review assignment remains unresolved, inside a qualified source process with
accountable intake/recovery. It is useful for intentional collect-now/review-later
programs. It still requires current issuer/recipient/purpose/custody authority
and later qualified human review; it is not an unsafe “send anyway” loophole.
Its cost is knowingly running the original access period before the review path
is ready. A instead risks delaying new requests when its required lookup or
staffing resolution is unavailable. Neither choice guarantees future capacity.

**Simpler/no-build alternative:** use a valid existing source responsibility and
the existing original-admission command, adding the missing source predicate and
its honest recovery only where needed. Do not introduce a separate readiness
entity, certificate, approval queue, staffing calendar or per-request policy
toggle. Team membership can provide different qualified people for different
declared scopes without requiring one person to cover everything or unioning
their permissions into a shared account.

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A cosmetic assigned-team field could pass while nobody can handle part of the
declared work. A strict but stale UI check could block legitimate issuance or
be bypassed by an API. A lost response could be retried as new access after
staffing changed. The fix is one current source admission predicate, minimal
qualified coverage evidence and exact original-effect recovery.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

We do not know actual staff workload, response times or tenant preferences.
Source-owned reviewer eligibility must be qualified for this native use. A team
label, logged-in sender or configured source binding does not establish it.
Conversely, readiness cannot require examining files that do not exist yet or
predicting future membership. The source proves declared scope, not omniscience.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adds a prerequisite to first original ordinary issuance, preserving exact
source identity, clocks, participant confirmation, actual later review and
fulfillment. It is not a new giving-checkout, missionary enrollment, public CMS
publication or grant-program approval gate. Shared contracts stay reusable;
specialist owners keep their own prerequisites.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Valid defaults resolve quietly. A genuine gap is understandable and recoverable
without losing authorized preparation. The sender need not become a reviewer or
permission administrator. Participants do not receive staffing details or new
tasks merely because staff change responsibility. Unknown commit and delayed
delivery remain truthful states rather than misleading success/failure.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

It follows established separation of preparation, responsibility, source
authorization and accessible recovery. It is not an industry-mandated sequence:
nonprofit systems also document later reviewer assignment. A's value here comes
from the selected ordinary source model and finite clock, not vendor imitation.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes, when implemented through Q11's existing source admission and Q24's person/
team qualification, with P12 authorization and P9 projection. A would conflict
if it became an email-only UI gate, a team grant, a new clock, a whole-Studio
dependency, an unqualified financial callback reuse or a retroactive revoke.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Retain A with precise scope and proof. Make the common path automatic, use
current source-owned minimal coverage rather than a whole roster snapshot,
recover prior issuance before applying a new gate, and keep repair distinct from
silently issuing. Those amendments improve reliability and flexibility without
adding a new product subsystem.

## Ratified corrected decision

> Before the first original source issuance of recipient access for an ordinary
> native Private Document Request, the source must resolve a current qualified
> person or team review responsibility and a usable review path for the request's
> declared review work and purpose, including applicable declared specialist
> restrictions. Reuse a valid source-derived responsibility without mandatory
> reselection, personal claiming, online presence, another reviewer or a separate
> approval. Different qualified humans may cover distinct declared scopes under
> the same team; this never combines their individual permissions.
>
> Prove this through the existing authoritative source admission and current P12
> rules, separately checking issuance authority, responsibility administration
> when changed, target qualification and actual later review authority. A team
> label, task row, stale preflight or caller-supplied readiness flag is insufficient.
> Readiness is for the declared source work; it does not require access to
> nonexistent future file rows, predict unknown future evidence restrictions,
> prove future availability, or constitute evidence acceptance or an access grant.
>
> If the new admission cannot establish the required readiness, do not issue
> recipient authority. Preserve independently authorized preparation and provide
> truthful, accessible, capable source-owned recovery. Distinguish an invalid or
> unresolved destination, insufficient eligible coverage, an unavailable check
> and lack of permission to repair. The issuer need not receive a roster, private
> evidence access or administrative powers merely to understand the permitted
> outcome. Repairing responsibility is not itself issuance or an automatic send.
>
> Recover an authorized same original committed issuance before evaluating
> readiness as a prerequisite for a new issuance. Preserve its original identity,
> occurrence, deadline, actual admitted terms and minimal qualification provenance;
> current disclosure and any new protected action remain separately authorized.
> Resolved eligibility witnesses and generated admission outputs are not newly
> invented retry intent. Changed explicit material intent conflicts; unknown
> commitment is reconciled. No duplicate issuance, timer reset or false Draft
> status follows a lost response, later staffing change or delivery failure.
>
> Apply the gate prospectively to first original ordinary issuance through every
> supported entry path. Drafts, credentials, email, resends, already-issued intake
> and existing fresh-access operations retain their own contracts. Check at actual
> source issuance: preparation for later issuance is different from issuing now
> and delivering a message later. Later staffing loss uses Q24's qualified source
> recovery and actual current restrictions; it does not retroactively erase a
> lawful admission, add a completion condition, renew access or demand new bytes.
> No new scheduling, routing, staffing, approval, notification or permission
> system is selected.

## Ownership, invariants and lifecycle

These are meaningful conditions for a truthful journey, not a mandatory new enum,
certificate store or state-machine package. The source decides actual admission;
only permitted projections display it.

| Condition                              | Authoritative meaning                                                                    | Required behavior                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Unissued preparation                   | Authorized draft/preparation exists; no original recipient authority yet.                | May remain unresolved and be saved under its own rights; no access clock starts merely from preparation.   |
| Ready in preflight                     | Current source information suggests usable declared-scope coverage.                      | Useful explanation, not a durable permission token; actual admission validates current relevant facts.     |
| Known invalid or insufficient coverage | Missing/invalid destination or declared required scope lacks a usable review path.       | Do not newly issue; retain permitted preparation and use capable source repair.                            |
| Unknown qualification                  | Required current facts cannot be established.                                            | Do not newly issue or guess empty/ready; show a service/qualification problem and permitted next step.     |
| Admission in progress                  | New source operation is evaluating and committing its exact intent.                      | Serialize actual dependencies; UI timing and early timestamps are not authority.                           |
| Definitely no admission                | Source confirms new admission did not commit.                                            | Explain cause, retain separately saved preparation; fixing cause is not automatic issuance.                |
| Commitment unknown                     | Response loss or partial failure prevents determining original effect.                   | Reconcile durable identity; do not call it Draft/not issued, delete possible winning work or restart time. |
| Issued original effect                 | Source admission/history/terms are durably committed.                                    | Recover actual I/end/basis under current disclosure; later staffing does not make original success false.  |
| Schedule-only preparation              | An existing qualified operation plans to issue later, but authority is not yet admitted. | Recheck when actual admission occurs; no new scheduling product is selected.                               |
| Issued with delivery pending           | Authority already exists; invitation/provider or task projection is delayed.             | Preserve original time/result, independently recover delivery/projection; never reissue to fix it.         |
| Later staffing/authority change        | Current eligibility differs from admitted historical basis.                              | Enforce actual affected source rules and Q24 recovery while preserving independent valid work/history.     |
| Fulfillment, expiry or fresh access    | Existing Q10–Q22 source predicates/admissions govern.                                    | No new completion hold, reopened authority, fresh-period gate or clock change follows from Q25.            |

Historical readiness proves the admission's basis, not current permission.
Material scope, recipient or explicit target changes use their source rules;
they are not silently substituted into an old effect. Different still-qualified
members may witness the same team coverage during a genuinely new admission
without changing the sender's unchanged team choice.
