## Recommended frontier

The strongest next question is whether an eligible source may give a named outside recipient limited, confirmed access to read a private document **without requiring full Asym enrollment**, or whether ordinary outbound sharing uses only an already-qualified signed-in/account context with actual source rights.

Recommend A, narrowly. A partner who needs to read one source-authorized document should not have to become a donor, missionary, staff member or organization member merely for that act. This is a product judgment, not measured Asym demand or a promise of lower drop-off. It needs an exact outbound source purpose, recipient proof and finite admission through the shared owners. It must not turn the File Manager into a general drive or create authority from a filename, folder, uploaded bytes, staff rank or a copied link.
