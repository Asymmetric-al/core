## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A completed view could become an indefinite read grant, accept a late correction from an old tab, expose new files under an old purpose, or display stale success after a source restriction. An overbroad read-only guard could instead break legitimate preview preparation, download recovery and access auditing. A polished completion banner does not settle any of those boundaries.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

Normal fulfillment is not upload receipt, clean scanning, a task checkbox, canceled work or security revocation. Read-only describes permitted business actions, not an HTTP-method ban. Browser-session expiry, source access end, rendition lifetime and retention are separate. Completion evidence can remain historically true while current eligibility changes. A visible date is not a guarantee of delivery through an earlier restriction, and a saved link is not continuing authority.

### How does this affect the whole product?

The ordinary native request receives one bounded completed-recipient mode through the existing source, identity/PDP and custody owners. It does not create another portal, permanent member role, global archive or independent completion engine. Other source-specific documents and their history remain unchanged. Later remediation follows the owning source and must not silently reopen predecessor work or expand an old grant.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Keep the same request context. Explain the actual outcome, show the applicable access end and offer eligible current-file View/Download plus concise history. Remove ordinary upload/correction controls. Returning users can re-enter through Q01 when needed, without repeated full enrollment. Distinguish expired session, ended source access, unavailable file and temporary outage; none should falsely say the original submission vanished.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Yes as a scoped product choice. Foundant documents viewing a form after it is marked complete, while its dashboard separates completed requests from active work. That supports useful return access, not every original file or perpetual permission. Box and Dropbox distinguish collection closure from other access/custody behavior. [Foundant follow-up guidance](https://support.foundant.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411213334935-Follow-Up-Submission-Logic), [Foundant applicant guidance](https://support.foundant.com/hc/en-us/articles/4479853059991-GLM-Applicant-Tutorial), [Box FAQ](https://support.box.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044783734-Box-File-Request-FAQ), [Dropbox](https://help.dropbox.com/share/create-file-request).

GOV.UK advises clear outcomes, next steps and useful behavior for people returning through bookmarks. It does not authorize permanent public confirmation pages, a new PDF export or automatic email for Core. Accessibility and security guidance require deliberate timing/session behavior rather than a cosmetic expiry notice. [Confirmation pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/confirmation-pages/), [WCAG timing adjustable](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html), [OWASP sessions](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Session_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html).

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes, with explicit source adoption. Q02/Q04/Q05 reserved post-completion scope; Q06 supplies that choice for ordinary successful fulfillment. Q01 current authority, Q03 correction fencing, Q04 exact private reads and Q05 current-content-only history remain binding. ADR-0183 and Workflow intent distinguish historical completion from later remediation. Generic current task/status code is not authority to weaken those contracts.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Keep A. Require exact authoritative completion, a known finite source access boundary, business-command enforcement, qualified read-support operations, current source/recipient checks, truthful subsequent change and usable re-entry/expiry/help states. Keep the duration and anchor visibly unresolved rather than inventing a number or silently treating unknown as forever. These are activation prerequisites, not a reason to replace A with a decorative unavailable screen.

## Corrected decision to record

> After **normal source-confirmed fulfillment** of an ordinary private-document request, end ordinary recipient submission and correction while preserving a bounded, currently source-authorized read-only view of the permitted completion result, Q05 Submission history and independently qualified current-file preview/download.
>
> Fulfillment is a durable source outcome with exact evidence and relevant revision/scope. It is not inferred from upload progress, scan readiness, a generic task flag or a client callback. Preserve its history; later invalidation/remediation follows an explicit source-owned successor or correction path without silently reopening predecessor work or expanding the old audience/file scope.
>
> Read-only prohibits ungranted business changes. Narrowly authorized preview preparation, delivery recovery, audit/metering and source help may still operate under their own current authority. They must not alter fulfillment, evidence, ordinary correction eligibility or access clocks.
>
> Every protected recipient operation remains bounded by its finite, server-enforced source access contract and current identity/purpose/permission. Independently admitted source work may complete or reconcile under its own current authority after recipient access ends, without renewing that grant or exposing protected results through expired access. Normal completion, returning, login, reading and retries do not incidentally renew expired authority. This decision selects no number of days, clock anchor or renewal policy; those require explicit resolution before activation.
>
> Use the same accessible task context with truthful completion, applicable access-end wording, current file states and safe re-entry/help. Session expiry, source access end, cancellation/revocation, technical failure and retention remain distinct. No superseded-byte archive, permanent metadata fallback, new membership, retention extension or automatic reopening is granted.

### Keep the UI calm and precise

The completed page should resemble the request the recipient already knows. Show a clear heading, source-approved outcome, current files and a short readable history. The strongest narrower alternative, metadata-only access, is still legitimate; A intentionally keeps qualified current-file reading because that is useful follow-up within this task. No measured claim about reduced staff work is made.

Use a single clear help action backed by the actual responsible source route. Do not add a new request-access queue, forced “download everything now” step, export ZIP, countdown animation, acknowledgement checkbox or automatic expiry email. Any communication remains under P6/P17 and its separately authorized purpose.

Use an unambiguous local date/time and timezone for the applicable access end; a server instant, policy provenance and current source evaluation govern enforcement. Date-only or “in two days” copy must not conceal an ambiguous cutoff. If different actions genuinely have different source limits, present the relevant limit without claiming a single longer date covers them all. Do not infer recipient location from display timezone.

Missing or unresolved access policy is not an invitation to display **Available indefinitely** or a fabricated date. The affected completed-access profile cannot activate without a resolved finite contract. A temporary policy-resolution failure is a safe unavailable state, not a new expiry or permission.

### Accessibility and timing need their own proof

Use Core's Base UI/base-maia, semantic headings/lists, meaningful action names, stable focus and current-state announcements. Keep expiry, outcome and next steps available without hover or opening an older-history panel. Long/RTL names, zoom/reflow, keyboard, speech, screen readers, touch and low-bandwidth return journeys need actual verification.

WCAG 2.2.1 is Level A and requires assessing content-set time limits, including security-related ones. Its applicable mechanisms/exceptions must be established for the actual session and purpose-bound interactions; merely labelling a timer “security” or requiring sign-in is not proof. The longer-than-20-hours exception is a standards condition, not an Asym duration choice. Do not treat a visible deadline alone as conformance or claim that reauthentication automatically satisfies the criterion. [WCAG 2.2.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html).

The separate WCAG 2.2.6 inactivity/data-loss guidance is Level AAA; do not misstate it as a universal AA requirement. Preserve already committed results and qualified recovery, and assess any actual user-input loss separately. Session warning/adjustment or re-entry must respect source authority; an accessibility mechanism cannot silently renew a revoked purpose grant. The source's approved accommodation/recovery path and applicable timing conformance are activation gates. [WCAG 2.2.6](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timeouts.html).

Do not surprise the reader with repetitive countdown announcements or scroll/focus jumps. If a required restriction removes an active control, move focus to a safe meaningful status rather than a dead element. No UI can promise erasure of information already seen or downloaded.

## Source ownership, invariants and lifecycle

| Authoritative fact                        | Owner and invariant                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Normal fulfillment and its evidence       | The requesting source owns the exact outcome, relevant request/item/member revisions, trusted actor and completion evidence. A work-list projection or technical callback cannot supply it.                                     |
| Historical completion versus later change | Preserve the completed source act. Later invalidation/correction/remediation uses the owning contract and linked work/evidence; no silent reopening, retrospective relabelling or regrant.                                      |
| Recipient/action authority                | Compatible Q01/P4/P12 admission and the sole PDP govern the exact current principal, recipient revision, tenant/environment, request, purpose and action. Do not union staff/audit roles into the recipient view.               |
| Current eligible evidence                 | Source currentness and exact completed-request relationships govern which own members can be shown. No mutable latest-file lookup or new file/recipient may enter the old authority without an explicit qualified source basis. |
| Finite access boundary                    | The source owns the declared policy and effective end for each admitted capability. Server evaluation governs; browser timers and cleanup jobs are not authorization.                                                           |
| Session/proof and technical lifetimes     | Their existing owners govern freshness, re-entry, rendition validity and worker/delivery authority. None automatically resets the source period.                                                                                |
| Retention, holds and disposal             | Source records policy and shared custody remain authoritative. Ended access is not deletion permission; retained evidence/holds are not a reader grant.                                                                         |
| Assistance and communication              | The source help/remediation route and P6/P17 retain their authority, dispatch and history. Visiting/helping does not create an unselected extension, message, task or acceptance effect.                                        |

**Completion race.** Source completion and recipient admission compare the same relevant source revision and evidence set. If a correction is admitted first, completion must evaluate that resulting source state; it cannot certify the stale predecessor. If completion wins first, stale submission/correction finalization cannot add an ordinary received successor merely because upload began earlier. Preserve any genuinely admitted act and govern abandoned staging under its own custody rules. Q03's admission-time fence does not wait for scanning/preview readiness.

**Durable outcome and replay.** Commit the authoritative source outcome, relevant mutation fence and required history/effect intent together where they share the owner transaction. Use durable source effect identity across retries; no second completion, new deadline, duplicated notice or renewed grant follows a lost response. Projection, notification or provider failure after commit must not undo the completed fact. Reconcile unknown outcomes through the source rather than guessing from HTTP status. Do not hold source locks while streaming files or calling providers.

**Source work after recipient expiry.** Authorized source review, processing, completion and reconciliation of already admitted work may continue under their own current actor/service authority after the participant's access ends. This preserves the source outcome without renewing the recipient grant. Returning that result, including status/history metadata, still requires the caller's current disclosure authority. A valid worker cannot admit a stale participant correction, bypass source withdrawal or restore expired participant access.

**Read-only is not GET-only.** Explicitly admit only supporting operations required by an already authorized read or source help. Preview preparation/regeneration, bounded download preparation, access audit/metering and source-authorized worker processing may write technical state. They cannot mutate source evidence, acceptance, recipient authority or ordinary submission eligibility. A blanket completed-request POST ban would break valid readback; a blanket permission for “supporting operations” would be equally unsafe.

**Later invalidation.** A completed historical request does not become an open intake because a check changes, a user replies or a generic task is toggled. Present current source-approved safety/outcome information while preserving the original act. Any linked remediation/new evidence needs its own owner authority and current recipient scope. Do not silently deliver a newly substituted file through an old completed-view grant. Current privacy suppression can remove historical details without falsifying the protected source record.

**Expiry and streams.** At or after the applicable effective end, deny new affected protected metadata, history, full/HEAD/range/conditional/recovery requests. Evaluate this on the server even if an expiry job is late or a browser clock is wrong. Long streams retain inherited per-chunk expiry checks; source restriction stops new grants and terminates in-flight delivery where feasible. No managed-realtime allowance is imported as a download grace. Emitted/buffered bytes remain a measured limitation, not recallable data.

**Duration remains a separate decision.** No universal 14-day/30-day period, clock-start rule, pause behavior, warning lead time or renewal ceremony is adopted here. Existing P18 guest/session numbers and Workflow suggestions have different scopes. A future explicit owner policy may define its chosen anchor; Q06 must not accidentally choose one through implementation. Once resolved for a grant, ordinary completion retries, login, reads, help and technical regeneration cannot slide its end or revive expired/revoked authority. Missing/null/unresolvable policy cannot default to unbounded completed access.

**Database and privileged paths.** Require correct tenant/source/request/item/member/recipient relationships, immutable outcome/effect identities, trusted actor/time fields and valid completion/currentness combinations. Unknown source state must remain unknown. Preserve multiple current supporting members and independent uses; do not impose a universal one-file/latest-row model. Application-readable status fields or generic task updates cannot reassign ownership, broaden purpose, write expiry/clearance or reopen the source.

The eventual schema must justify PK/FK/unique/check constraints, required versus deliberately nullable fields, delete/hold behavior, bounded lookup indexes and policy version/history. Use source commands for sensitive transitions, not a client-written status or expiry. This is no new money calculation; financial precision and posted facts remain untouched. Verify actual grants, role composition, `USING`/new-row checks, views/functions/RPC/storage and service-role bypass paths. Omitted `WITH CHECK` may inherit `USING`; definer behavior depends on actual ownership/roles/FORCE/BYPASSRLS. SQL keywords alone prove neither safety nor a vulnerability. [PostgreSQL row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-rowsecurity.html).

## Category-by-category adversarial review

Each category states materiality, impact/severity, qualitative likelihood, evidence, effect on A and exact correction. **High** means substantial source-integrity, authorization or confidentiality impact; **Medium** means meaningful usability, reliability or maintenance impact. These are prospective design concerns, not 21 claimed production incidents.

### 1. Problem validity, necessity and alternatives

**Material concern: yes — Medium / plausible; demand unmeasured.** A could become a permanent archive or add continuing access without a real follow-up use. The strongest alternative B preserves outcome/history while ending byte reads. Foundant demonstrates useful completed-form readback but not universal original access. **Why it matters:** the capability has privacy/operating cost. **Effect:** retain A as the selected bounded product choice. **Permanent fix/exact change:** C01/C05/C07/C14/C20; current task-specific access, finite policy and meaningful return-task proof; no claim of quantified support savings.

### 2. Brittleness

**Material concern: yes — High / plausible.** A single closed/completed flag, browser clock, stored URL or technical expiry can stand in for several independent facts. A mapper defaulting unknown to not_started is an unsuitable precedent. **Why it matters:** false open or permanent access can result. **Effect:** require explicit source outcome and access state. **Permanent fix/exact change:** C01/C05/C09/C12; unknown remains unknown, server-evaluated bounds and separate clocks; no date/status inference.

### 3. Technical debt

**Material concern: yes — High / likely if generic patterns are copied wholesale.** Mutable task status, Support auto-reopening and technical agent completion do not supply native fulfillment semantics. **Why it matters:** reuse could silently reopen intake or misattribute business completion. **Effect:** reuse mechanisms only after source qualification. **Permanent fix/exact change:** C02–C04/C11/C19; one source-owned transition/read seam with existing owners, no new completion engine or helper that treats every callback as fulfillment.

### 4. Edge cases

**Material concern: yes — High / plausible.** Completion races correction; one supporting file becomes restricted; the session ends before the grant; a renderer expires while original access remains; the recipient changes. **Why it matters:** each can produce wrong content, lost work or misleading expiry. These follow accepted source/representation distinctions. **Effect:** amend the state contract. **Permanent fix/exact change:** C02/C06–C10/C12/C13; exact-member outcomes, separate states and safe source recovery with no automatic regrant/revival.

### 5. Footguns

**Material concern: yes — High / plausible.** Hiding Upload leaves a working mutation API; a broad POST ban breaks previews; clicking Get help or logging in resets access. **Why it matters:** apparent read-only UI may be insecure or unusable. **Effect:** define actions by domain effect. **Permanent fix/exact change:** C04/C05/C10/C13/C14; all-path business mutation denial plus a narrow read-support catalog, no incidental renewal or generic reopen control.

### 6. Tenant safety

**Material concern: yes — High / plausible.** Completed pages may be cached as immutable/public or resolved by uploader, old recipient, shared mailbox or a staff-role union. **Why it matters:** even outcome/title/history disclosure can expose private cross-tenant or ministry associations. **Effect:** preserve exact current context. **Permanent fix/exact change:** C06/C07/C11/C16; no scope pooling, current metadata authorization and context-bound cache/late-response fencing.

### 7. Database, RLS and authorization safety

**Material concern: yes — High / plausible; effective hosted policy unverified.** Client-editable completed_at, access end or ownership can convert a permitted row into an unauthorized completed grant; a service handler can bypass ordinary policies. **Why it matters:** presentation is not an authority boundary. **Effect:** C02/C05/C06/C11 become release gates. **Permanent fix/exact change:** required scoped relations, valid states, immutable semantic outcomes, trusted fields and actual role/USING/new-row/composed-policy/view/RPC/storage tests. No new money precision change; existing finance remains source-owned.

### 8. Overengineering

**Material concern: yes — Medium / plausible.** A new portal, per-request expiry workflow, configurable policy engine, download-all ceremony or blanket technical-write prohibition exceeds the task. **Why it matters:** it adds state, friction and failure paths. **Effect:** keep A small. **Permanent fix/exact change:** C04/C05/C14/C17; existing request surface and per-operation policy evaluation, qualified supporting operations and finite source profiles; no new provider or full future Workflow dependency.

### 9. UX/UI and user friction

**Material concern: yes — Medium–High / plausible.** Completion can disappear behind login, look like rejection, promise files until a date despite restriction, or leave only dead controls. **Why it matters:** recipients cannot understand whether their work succeeded or what to do.
