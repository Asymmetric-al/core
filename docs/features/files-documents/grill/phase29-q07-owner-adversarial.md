## Proposed corrected decision

For a prospectively qualified ordinary private-document request whose source-recipient authority remains eligible at the exact normal fulfillment boundary, resolve the ordinary completed-view end once as the earlier of **the trustworthy source-recorded fulfillment-occurrence instant plus 720 elapsed hours** and all then-binding earlier access ends applicable to that authority and scope. Preserve the exact source effect, policy version, recipient/request binding and resolution evidence.

That admitted end is a maximum, not a guaranteed period or an access grant by itself. It cannot be lengthened by removing a cap, changing a default, replaying completion, returning, reauthenticating, resending, helping or repairing a projection. A genuine later earlier source-access end can narrow its affected authority; explicit renewal/extension remains unselected. Current source/PDP, proof, privacy, safety and representation checks still apply.

Temporary technical failure or an owner-defined re-evaluable representation restriction is not automatically permanent grant revocation. Its qualified resolution may restore the permitted representation while the same source authority remains valid and the admitted end is unchanged. Ended, revoked or actually shortened authority does not revive merely because a representation recovers or a restriction is removed.

Independently authorized source work may fulfill after recipient access ended, or when completed-read admission cannot be established. Preserve that business outcome without granting or exposing new recipient access. Late processing preserves the original qualified anchor and end, and grants nothing once that end has passed. Apply Q06's read-only business mode, Q01 confirmation/current authority, Q02 preview, Q03 lineage, Q04 original delivery and Q05 history exactly as ratified.

## Proposed owner clauses

**Q07-O01 — Exact prospective scope.** Bind A to the new ordinary request source/purpose/cohort before activation. It is neither the universal Phase29 file lifetime nor the global P34, official-document, Support, application, resource-library or staff-access period. Preserve existing qualified specialist rights.

**Q07-O02 — Trustworthy normal fulfillment.** Use Q06's durable source outcome, evidence, relevant revision and trusted attribution. Upload, safe scan, preview, generic task completion, due-date passage or a browser message cannot start the clock.

**Q07-O03 — Source-recorded occurrence instant.** The exact qualified source contract establishes the fulfillment-occurrence instant and precision. Distinguish it from asserted business-effective dates, observation/receipt/processing times and mutable update times. Invalid, impossible or unproved timestamps cannot receive `now` or a guessed default. Do not require a fictional physical commit timestamp.

**Q07-O04 — Exact elapsed interval.** Thirty days means 720 elapsed hours, equivalently 2,592,000 seconds in the adopted timestamp model, not a calendar month, 30 local calendar dates, working days or end-of-day. Qualify timestamp precision and authoritative clock behavior. Localization changes presentation, not the instant.

**Q07-O05 — Resolve and retain the admitted end.** At eligible source fulfillment, resolve the earlier of O04's maximum and the applicable binding earlier source-access ends. Retain that resolved result and policy/effect provenance, rather than deriving a new deadline from mutable inputs on every read. The code-owned owner contract decides scope; a member-only limitation must not silently erase unrelated permitted supporting files.

**Q07-O06 — No automatic lengthening.** Cap removal/relaxation, default changes and source projection rebuilding cannot lengthen a previously admitted end. Later genuine shortening of the affected source authority remains binding; reverting that shortening is not automatic. Correct historical source facts through their owner without rewriting access evidence or using a corrected date to smuggle in an extension.

**Q07-O07 — Live predicate versus authority end.** Do not ratchet every missing preview, provider outage, expired representation or temporary owner-defined restriction into permanent grant termination. Source-approved clearance/recovery may restore only the representation permitted by unchanged still-valid authority and its existing end. Explicitly ended/revoked/shortened authority needs its own authorized successor rule; Q07 supplies no extension/revival rule.

**Q07-O08 — Historical eligibility and current access.** Establish the source-recipient eligibility, exact policy and binding ends relevant at fulfillment from trustworthy source evidence. Also require current authority at service time. A logged-out browser is not historical source ineligibility; a currently signed-in user is not proof of past eligibility. Reassignment and current role expansion cannot rewrite old recipient binding.

**Q07-O09 — One semantic resolution.** Duplicate, delayed, reordered and replayed completion deliveries converge on the same source effect, anchor and end. Conflicting identities/facts enter owner reconciliation, rather than first-writer, latest-timestamp or earliest-handler guesses. Correlate tenant/environment/source/request/recipient revision and effect, not email alone.

**Q07-O10 — Late delivery consumes elapsed time.** A five-day delay leaves at most the original remaining interval, not a fresh 30 days. A handler first seeing the effect after its end exposes no protected completed view. Source reconciliation uses original evidence plus current authority and never processing-time renewal.

**Q07-O11 — Read-admission failure is not business failure.** An unresolved policy, eligibility boundary or cap blocks affected participant admission. Staff/source processing may still complete under its separate current authority. A later repair cannot fabricate an old grant; it may restore a demonstrably existing, still-valid admission only within its original end and the exact owner recovery contract.

**Q07-O12 — Enforce all protected paths.** At or after the effective end, deny new protected recipient metadata/status/history/list/count/preview/original/full/HEAD/range/conditional/resumed/recovery reads unless separately qualified authority genuinely applies. **Q07-O13 — Inherited streams and cache.** Preserve Q04 pre-emission identity, whole-original permission, private response/cache behavior, per-chunk expiry and feasible source-restriction termination. No new universal per-chunk synchronous audit is invented; retain P12's tiered audit/budget contract. Already emitted/saved/partial bytes are not recallable. A request begun before end is not indefinite authority.

**Q07-O14 — Independent lifetimes.** Invitation/challenge expiry, proof freshness, browser session, source access, preview/preparation, worker authority and retention are distinct. Session refresh or newly prepared representations cannot move the source end; browser expiry alone cannot stop independently admitted source processing. An independently authorized use of shared bytes retains its own policy.

**Q07-O15 — Later change stays source-owned.** Completion/correction/review preserve Q03/Q06's exact race boundary. Later invalidation is truthful current restriction/remediation, not erased completion. No complete→open→complete reset, old predecessor revival, ordinary historical-file access or silent new-recipient/member retargeting.

**Q07-O16 — Truthful date and states.** Before fulfillment explain the bounded rule and already known earlier limit without inventing a completion date. Once resolved show actual access-end date, time and timezone in the existing request context, outside collapsed history. Do not guarantee uninterrupted access or availability of every file. Clearly distinguish source expiry, expired session, temporary service failure and unavailable representation within permitted disclosure.

**Q07-O17 — Safe accessible recovery.** Keep the existing qualified re-entry and accountable source-help routes. Re-entry requires current Q01 proof/source authority and keeps the end. Help does not automatically extend, recreate, email files or promise release. Do not use an expiry banner as an unqualified security exception to all accessibility timing requirements.

**Q07-O18 — No new side-effect program.** A chooses no completion email, expiry reminder, countdown, renewal UI, active-request duration or special post-cancellation/rejection period. Existing separately qualified source communications preserve P17 preparation/P6 dispatch. Readability does not create contactability.

**Q07-O19 — Retention and restoration.** Access end does not delete custody, extend retention, release holds or override independent source uses. Restore current adverse identity, access, disposition and source-state evidence before recipient serving. Unknown legacy owner/expiry remains restricted for reconciliation, not deleted, invented forever or granted 30 days.

**Q07-O20 — Adoption and proof.** Reconcile the native owner policy and exact compatible P4/P12/egress seams through canonical sources and declared projections at the authorized stage. No global default injection or new permission engine/policy-builder is required. Qualify source transactions, current roles/RLS, actual custody/streams, supported browsers, assistive technology and recovery before claiming the lane works.

## Qualification cases required before activation

1. Exact normal source fulfillment with valid scope resolves once; upload/scan/task/forged or mutable timestamp cannot substitute.
2. No earlier cap resolves to the 720-hour ceiling; a one-day cap leaves only one day; an already-ended authority creates no completed recipient access.
3. Removing/loosening the initial earlier cap leaves the issued end unchanged. A later true earlier source end narrows affected authority and subsequent relaxation does not silently lengthen it.
4. A temporary provider outage, expired preview or qualified temporary restriction can recover the same permitted representation under unchanged valid authority/end. An actually revoked/expired grant cannot.
5. Completion after session expiry but before source-authority end resolves without an open browser; later exact qualified re-entry neither starts nor shifts the clock.
6. Completion after participant source access ended remains a valid staff/source result without granting recipient status, history or files.
7. Delayed/replayed/out-of-order completion uses source-bound evidence and original time, including first delivery after the entire interval. Missing historical eligibility does not use current roles as a substitute.
8. Cap/recipient/policy change concurrent with fulfillment has one provable source ordering; conflicting evidence is not resolved by worker order or timestamps alone.
9. Missing/null/invalid policy or timestamp blocks affected admission, while independent source work remains possible. Recovery proves an original valid admission rather than generating a new period.
10. One-unit-before, exact-end and one-unit-after checks; supported precision/clock-skew bounds; spring/fall DST, month and leap-day crossings preserve exactly 720 elapsed hours. Localized text changes no authority.
11. New metadata, count, history, preview, original, HEAD, full/range/conditional/resume/recovery and cached paths deny at expiry as required. No periodic cleanup timing is part of correctness.
12. In-flight original delivery preserves exact bytes, per-chunk expiry and feasible restriction termination; no claimed byte recall or universal realtime grace. Partial browser files are honestly represented.
13. Completion/correction/review races preserve ratified Q03 and Q06. Later source remediation does not reopen or reset the original access clock.
14. Source-authorized restrictions, recipient reassignment, proof/address revision and current floors cannot transfer the old end to a new audience, member or purpose.
15. Current original and preview are independently eligible; a member-specific restriction does not accidentally remove unaffected current siblings or authorize forbidden historical bytes.
16. Access end does not mutate retention, holds, custody, shared independent uses, source acceptance or communication outcomes.
17. Mixed-version rollout, projection rebuild, cache restore, database restore and rollback preserve actual ends and adverse authority evidence; unknown legacy meaning fails affected admission safely.
18. Keyboard/screen-reader/touch/mobile/zoom/RTL/slow-network journeys distinguish completed truth, actual access end, expired session, temporary unavailable file and ended authority. Relevant shorter timing limits are separately qualified; 30 days is not blanket accessibility conformance.
19. The accountable help route is usable without revealing expired private context or silently sending/releasing/renewing documents. No support-queue service promise is invented.
20. Review actual authenticated source/RLS/provider/browser behavior; mocked time arithmetic or generated spec validation alone does not qualify the new request lane.
