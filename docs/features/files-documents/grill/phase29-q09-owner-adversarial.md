## Disposition

**Accept A with required amendments.** A permits useful late intake without claiming that the material is safe, accepted, timely or sufficient. Its permanent design must distinguish five facts: the requested target, a true hard intake boundary, finite recipient authority, the admitted receipt, and the source's remaining required work.

B, using the due date as the ordinary intake cutoff, remains the strongest simpler alternative where incoming work must stop predictably. A is reasonable for ordinary collection, provided the UI and source commands do not silently restore B through a timeout job or silently create unlimited access. No measured late-submission frequency or productivity benefit is claimed.

The hardest cases are not whether a red badge appears. They are which target applied when a receipt was admitted, what a correction requested after that date means, whether a later date edit rewrites history, and whether an unknown target is incorrectly treated as missing authority.

## Proposed corrected decision

For the ordinary native private-document request, treat its requested due date as a coordination target. Passing that target alone does not stop a current authorized recipient from making an otherwise-permitted initial submission or Q03 pending correction. Continue to enforce exact source scope, any actual hard intake cutoff, finite recipient authority, current proof, item acceptance/freeze and closure. Receipt after the target is not evidence acceptance, a new grant, an extension or a source-completion decision.

Record each admitted submission/correction through its existing exact source boundary with a trusted receipt and enough source target/revision/time-interpretation evidence to state its timing truthfully. Preserve original and successor receipts independently. Do not label timely received work late merely because scanning, preview or staff review continues. Current overdue recipient work is a separate source projection of what the recipient still needs to do; an unfulfilled request or missing business acceptance is not sufficient proof.

Existing authorized date changes affect the appropriate current source obligation without silently rewriting past receipt history. New or revised correction/additional work must have explicit source target applicability; Q09 creates no automatic interval, backdated obligation or new date-edit capability. Unknown target data does not invent a timing verdict or substitute for missing authority. Preserve independently permitted work when actual scope, hard boundaries and finite authority are known, and deny affected actions when their authorization cannot be proved.

Use readable requested-by wording and truthful received/under-review/correction states in the existing request experience. No target-driven automatic closure, rejection, acceptance, waiver, notification or late-review queue follows from A. Reuse exact existing owner rules and shared services. Finite active-access policy, source date interpretation and any separately authorized communication remain qualification gates; no numeric duration or date-edit role is selected here.
