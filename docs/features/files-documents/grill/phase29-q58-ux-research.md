**Recommend A**, with explicit audience qualification and honest shared lifecycle. This is a flexible product judgment, not a universal best-practice claim. It permits one publication when the actual content, meaning, language and lifecycle are suitable for both; it does not force sharing. B is a credible stricter policy for organizations whose audience-specific editions need independent control. There is no evidence here that every Asym organization has either pattern. The user decision should expose this tradeoff rather than silently treating the two audiences as one broad staff-or-missionary permission union.

No third option is needed. Both options retain the staff-reader outcome, current source/P12 authorization, existing publication/withdrawal and accessible reading. Neither adds public access, automatic notifications, subscriptions, acknowledgment, a new application shell, dynamic audience-rule builder or a second permission system.

## 4. A small complete reader and curator experience

**Staff reading.** Provide a clearly named Resources entry in the existing staff experience for the newly qualified P28 staff-reader purpose. The ordinary reader sees approved current document/resource guidance, not the authoring console, unpublished drafts or a global file browser. Search/category/language filters, summary, known format/size, Open/Download, review information and qualified successor guidance follow the same useful resource outcomes as the missionary experience. Actual staff identity/context and current grant qualify the action; staff rank, folder membership or being a curator elsewhere is insufficient. Do not require the staff member to impersonate a missionary or obtain curate or Support-read permission just to read this P28 resource. Support's already-owned Staff guides retain their proper entry/source and current consultation contract. Any navigation between owners reauthorizes there; Q58 selects neither cross-source body indexing nor guide copying/synchronization.

**One publication under A.** In ordinary resource preparation, show the exact current intended reader audiences and a concise consequence: this publication is offered to these qualified audiences. Audience names describe scope; they are not an authority engine. Elena must have actual source authority for the chosen publication and each intended audience. Show only audience/context information she may discover. Do not silently preselect a new staff audience, broaden old publications through migration or treat a changed profile role as a new publication approval. Preserve ordinary draft behavior: typing/choosing does not publish. Use the existing deliberate publication act; do not add a second routine moderator/confirmation merely because an audience is named.

**Common content really is common.** The shared publication has one exact admitted content/version/reference and its actual shared publication lifecycle. It cannot secretly serve staff-only paragraphs or a different file to missionaries under the same claimed publication. If wording, language edition, content meaning, required review, timing or withdrawal needs differ, use separate qualified publications. Existing field-projection privacy can narrow disclosure; it is not a new audience-dependent content-variant feature.

**Change impact.** Before a shared replacement, actual Q55 review/date act or withdrawal, the curator sees the relevant permitted audience impact in the ordinary source flow. A shared replacement changes the shared publication for each still-authorized audience; a publication withdrawal stops that publication's new delivery for all its audiences. Current individual/action restrictions may independently narrow delivery without inventing per-audience publication states. These facts must not be hidden behind a casual Rename/Move or a staff-only preview. Published bytes are immutable and old receipt/history facts are not rewritten. Existing safety/rights restrictions still win.

**Separate publications under B.** Make the audience-specific edition recognizable in authorized curator context; reader labels should remain simple and relevant without revealing another hidden edition. Two publications can lawfully reference the same immutable bytes, but that fact neither proves equal permissions nor transfers review/acceptance/publication history. Each publication has its own currentness, Q55 review plan, successor and withdrawal. No automatic cross-edition propagation is selected. A source-qualified preparation/reuse path avoids unnecessary re-upload while preserving actual deliberate independent publication.

**Reports and help.** Q57's problem-report lifecycle remains source-owned, private and tied to its exact observed publication. Sharing a publication never makes missionary reports visible to staff readers or combines them into public discussion. Staff-reader permission alone does not establish Q57 report-create/handling rights. If a complete selected staff-reader journey includes Report a problem, the compatible staff cohort must be expressly adopted under Q57/P28/P12; otherwise actual qualified Help remains available. No report or notification authority is inferred from an audience checkbox. Q55's compatible staff-resource maintenance also needs explicit source adoption; nothing copies prior review facts just because bytes match.

**Navigation and recovery.** A person with more than one context should not have to guess which app identity grants access. Reuse actual current context selection and return to permitted list/filter position. The same shared publication need not appear twice merely because two audience memberships match, but this is not authority to reveal membership details. A protected copied URL always rechecks current scope. No resources, no matches, load failure, denied context and an unavailable prior link remain distinct. No audience change grants historical content or a public URL. Shared readers do not acquire admin access; common native credentials are not cross-tenant grants.

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A staff-only update can leak if a shared audience label is mistaken for authorization or the curator forgets it serves missionaries too. B can drift into inconsistent duplicate guidance. Both need exact publication meaning, current per-reader scope and a visible change impact, not a broad role shortcut.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

We assume some guidance is genuinely suitable for both audiences and that a capable curator can approve it for each. We have not measured that overlap, update frequency or actual user preference. P28 staff document-resource readership remains an unresolved required outcome beyond Support's already-owned narrow Staff guides; a working native P28 staff-reader surface and its permissions are not established.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It extends the resource source with actual staff readership and decides whether one publication can serve both cohorts. It does not merge identities, byte uses, permissions, report audiences, public Media or document evidence. P28 owns publication, P12 current access and P29 custody; compatible Q55/Q57 adoption must be explicit.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Staff get a simple Resources reading path without curator privileges. A supports one common edition where appropriate; B makes independent editions and maintenance explicit. Readers should not need to know how many catalogs/tables exist. Curators must understand whether a change affects both audiences or only one publication.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Current knowledge/CRM/resource products document deliberate reader audiences and separate management powers. They also expose administrator bypass, inheritance and targeting-only semantics that Core must reject. This recommendation applies the useful product pattern under Core's stricter source rules; it is not a universal vendor consensus or a measured nonprofit outcome.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes if the new P28 staff reader purpose and publication audience meaning are explicitly adopted under the existing resource owner. Q02 rejects an ownerless general drive; P28 already supplies resource curation/publication/currentness; Q55/Q57 supply compatible maintenance with their exact boundaries. Support D27-C already supplies a separate narrow authored Staff guides source and independent consultation, which is preserved rather than duplicated or declared absent. Existing reader/curator roles, demo Knowledge UI or generic file metadata do not prove native qualification.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Recommend A: allow one exact suitable publication for deliberately qualified staff and missionary audiences, while retaining separate publications where content or lifecycle differs. Keep B fair for independent editorial control. Preserve the required staff reading outcome under both and do not turn this into a catalogue architecture, public sharing or notification question. Q58 remains unselected.
