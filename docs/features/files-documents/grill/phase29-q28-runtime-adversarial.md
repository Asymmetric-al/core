## Result and minimum permanent boundary

A is viable as a **source-owned staff evidence-admission command**, not as a generic file-link insert or a copy of participant upload. It binds the exact qualified existing byte version to an eligible target item with actual staff attribution, required source history and relevant evidence-set change. Existing source rights, technical qualifications, immutable original provenance and independent review remain. No automatic participant submission/readback, approval transfer, fresh access or source completion shortcut follows.

The essential new issues are: an addition must not silently replace another current candidate; unknown outcome must not delete shared bytes; new references must serialize with disposition; replay must recover original effects without requiring the old source rights to remain a new-admission permission; current disclosure remains separate. A wrong staff addition also needs a real source-authorized recovery path before launch. The root's proposed minimum is withdrawal of a still-unaccepted staff-added member, preserving history and other uses; atomic replacement is only an explicitly qualified source option. Neither is generic delete or authority to alter accepted/frozen evidence.

Reuse the existing source/P12/custody/command/history/worker boundaries and contextual shared UI. Do not add a universal document-permission framework, clone every object, force rescanning without a purpose need, require full File Manager/Workflow completion, or accept an unqualified public URL as private custody.

## Material findings and required language

Severity is the consequence if the proposed native path omits the rule; likelihood is qualitative exposure under ordinary use/races, not an incident rate. These are copying hazards and qualification requirements, not allegations of deployed native vulnerabilities.

### R01 — Readable or discoverable is mistaken for reusable

**High privacy severity; plausible.** Existing assets public-URL metadata and SELECT policy cannot establish permission to introduce evidence into another source. A tenant or staff label, existing download button, same filename/hash or shared email is not a reuse grant. Permission to see a private source does not prove that a target may acquire its bytes or associations.

**Required language:** authenticate the actual staff actor and independently prove current source discovery/read/selection/reuse permission and target staff-originating admission authority, including environment/tenant, subject or other source-defined relationship, purpose, consent/freshness and continuing restrictions. Caller IDs are validated selectors. Resolve the actual semantic subject through qualified source facts; do not infer it from uploader/filename/Party name or demand one universal subject shape for every document. Apply the existing P12/source capabilities rather than inventing a generic file authority. Protected source types may be ineligible for this ordinary action even when bytes are readable; reuploading the same bytes cannot bypass that restriction.

### R02 — Discovery leaks a protected match or source association

**High privacy severity; plausible.** An existing-file selector, duplicate warning, count or Used in display can reveal a restricted file before an add command ever executes. The public assets helper/CMS serializer are unsuitable precedents for confidential candidate enumeration.

**Required language:** authorize and scope candidate metadata before search, sort/facet/count/pagination/thumbnail/preview/serialization. Show only source-qualified eligible choices and safe non-enumerating explanations. Seeing one permitted use does not reveal all uses, original source details, holds or unrelated records. Revocation/context switch must invalidate protected results and reject late responses; a client filter/cache is not the access gate. No automatic matching, broad search corpus or global duplicate service is implied by A.

### R03 — Selected bytes/version changes silently, or technical proof is borrowed

**High integrity severity; plausible with revisions and legacy objects.** The old assets row's URL/provider metadata is not immutable source-version identity. Eve replay intake has digest/upsert:false but transforms/redacts its specialized content. CMS versions:false and image derivatives also do not establish original-evidence fidelity.

**Required language:** deliberate selection binds the exact immutable source/version/byte identity, integrity/provenance and target use. Provider move/copy or filename rename does not create a new business document, and provider-current URL/digest equality cannot substitute for exact meaning. Reuse compatible current technical qualification where owner rules allow; do not universally rescan/reupload, and do not inherit another purpose's stale/insufficient scan or preview profile. A qualified bounded target intake may admit the new use in Checking while further target checks run, but source/target authority, known exact custody/integrity and required admission proof must hold. Checking/unsupported preview is not accepted/usable evidence; quarantine or unsafe content gains no bypass. New required rendition/processing stays linked to the exact original; no transformation silently alters evidence or invokes an external viewer.

### R04 — Add existing silently replaces evidence, transfers approval or corrupts review basis

**High integrity severity; likely ordinary race exposure.** Q21's meaningful review unit may contain several supporting files; Q03 exact predecessor semantics are for deliberate corrections. A generic link insert or upsert could overwrite a single current candidate, add irrelevant duplicates, or leave a prior acceptance attached to a changed evidence set.

**Required language:** this action adds only the permitted staff-originating member to the current target evidence set. It must not silently replace an existing candidate or mutate accepted/frozen/closed work. If the source's cardinality or state requires an explicit replacement/correction transition, use a separately qualified deliberate source route rather than changing Add semantics. Record the relevant new evidence/review basis and fence stale accept/return commands. Preserve unrelated supporting files, accepted independent items and all other uses. Prior acceptance from another request/item/purpose is not transferable; new target acceptance remains Q21. Equal bytes do not automatically make one source occurrence redundant or approved.

### R05 — No practical correction for a mistaken staff addition

**High UX/integrity consequence; plausible.** A staff member can pick a wrong but technically eligible certificate/version. Requiring participant upload to undo the staff action, deleting original bytes or telling support to edit SQL defeats the assisted workflow. A broad editable evidence list would instead undermine Q03/Q21 finality.

**Required amendment:** before activating staff addition, provide a qualified deliberate source withdrawal of the still-unaccepted staff-added member, preserving receipt/admission history and other uses. This is not erasure of evidence history, original-byte deletion or automatic revival of a superseded candidate. The actual source decides who may withdraw and which current states allow it; actor ownership alone is not permission. Atomic replacement, if offered, must qualify its exact source effects/races separately. Accepted/frozen/closed outcomes retain their existing recovery constraints. Withdrawal alone does not create another staff email or fresh participant authority. No universal undo button, new workflow or unbounded edit power is selected.

### R06 — Admission races source/target rights, review, new evidence or disposal

**High integrity/privacy severity; normal concurrent exposure.** Existing financial key helpers and Eve single-artifact locks are adjacent examples, not a transaction spanning native source eligibility, exact target evidence set and shared-byte usage. A preflight can be valid when the picker opens and stale at commit.

**Required language:** the authoritative staff command orders the relevant source/target/byte-use changes: source rights/consent/freshness, target requirement/state/evidence head, acceptance/return/correction, new source use, disposition/deletion claim, integrity and applicable hold/retention policy. Recheck current required facts after waits; preserve an actual committed qualified occurrence, not browser time or an old transaction-start approximation. Same-tenant reference keys alone do not cover these predicates. Admission-first preserves its lawful required byte/use obligations; destructive-claim/restriction-first prevents an incompatible new admission. Use the owners' narrow coordination strategy, not a universal global lock or complete-system transaction. Keep expensive processing/provider I/O outside source locks and use required durable handoff for later effects.

### R07 — Replay after current rights change creates duplicate or erases genuine success

**High reliability/privacy severity; likely retry exposure.** Financial duplicate-key helper `correction-requests.ts:123–144` and generic workflow reuse can return by key alone. They do not prove exact staff intent, target version, add semantics or participant/staff provenance. A retry may find a now-closed target because the successful original later contributed to fulfillment.

**Required language:** bind a stable semantic operation to the deliberate source version, target item/requirement, actual actor/provenance and relevant explicit intent; server-resolved admission outputs are not invented client intent. Recover an authorized matching committed effect before treating changed target state as a failed new admission. Changed material conflicts; new transport keys cannot duplicate the same current member. The same already-current exact member may return a truthful no-op/existing result under source rules without fabricating a new actor/time/history. Do not impose a global forever-unique byte→item relation that blocks a later genuinely distinct authorized act after lawful withdrawal or changed source basis. Current disclosure can deny or redact recovery while the original admitted effect remains historical. Later loss of the actor's original read permission is not permission to erase the target use or demand reupload; a new action still requires current rights.

### R08 — Failed/unknown add triggers destructive cleanup of shared bytes

**High data-loss severity; plausible if upload failure patterns are copied.** Eve intake cleanup at `retention/control.ts:135–152` operates on its newly created artifact and handles completion errors. Q28 is adding a use of already-held bytes, potentially used by many sources. An ambiguous new-link commit cannot justify object deletion, unlinking existing uses or reverting a newer review.

**Required language:** coherently persist new use, evidence-set effect, mandatory original/staff provenance, required history and durable secondary intent. Provider/storage work is not a substitute for business commit. Unknown response recovers the exact operation; absence/error/denial are distinct. Failed or rolled-back target admission leaves the existing byte/source use intact. Only genuinely new abandoned staging copies may enter their separately authorized cleanup; original shared bytes and possibly committed uses must never be “compensated” away. Known add success survives a later preview/task/history-UI refresh failure. Generic best-effort logs are not mandatory durable business history.

### R09 — Original-source restrictions and independent target rights are collapsed

**High privacy/data-loss severity; plausible across source lifecycles.** Q02 permits shared bytes across independent typed uses without merging rights/currentness/retention. Eve's single-owner expiry/hold model is therefore not the complete multi-source contract. A hold may keep bytes but grants no new reuse; one visible link is not the complete use inventory.

**Required language:** new admission must be compatible with original-source reuse restrictions and target obligations. Preserve original receipt/provenance/retention anchors; do not reset an original retention clock or create an indefinite copy to evade disposal. A lawful new target may have its own owner-defined retention/access facts; expired original reader access does not automatically cap that independent use. Apply actual continuing origin restrictions/consent/safety/withdrawal where the owner declares them; neither universal cascading revocation nor universal independence is inferred. Removing one link or losing one actor's access does not delete shared bytes or clear another hold. Complete registered-use/hold/disposition authority must govern physical deletion; unknown coverage blocks destructive conclusions and incompatible new admission as required. A real policy conflict needs its designated owner, not a generic most-restrictive/longest-retention guess.

### R10 — Staff addition fabricates participant submission, readback, access or automation

**High privacy/attribution severity; plausible reuse of participant intake code.** Q02 own-submission access binds the admitted actor/recipient/request/occurrence/version, and Q21 retains actual human acceptance. A staff-supplied file may originate in a source the participant cannot see. Q26/Q27 currently own different exact notification meanings.

**Required language:** record the real staff actor and new-target admission time separately from original byte receipt, original uploader and described subject. Do not invent participant upload/confirmation/attestation, backdate source receipt, transfer prior acceptance or expose staff additions in Q04/Q05 own-submission readback/history by association. Participant disclosure/confirmation uses its exact source-authorized projection if separately supported. Staff addition is not original/fresh recipient access, completed-read admission or an expired-request renewal; Q22 may complete a source only through its full predicate without manufacturing those rights. Explicitly reconcile whether newly actionable **staff-origin** evidence enters Q27's exact source mapping, preserving original-policy/cohort/Off/no-backfill rules; do not infer it from a generic submission event. Q26 Request changes remains an actual review outcome, and withdrawal alone selects no mail. No new notification default or participant-library entitlement follows.

### R11 — API gates are mistaken for native SQL, storage and privileged enforcement

**High privacy/integrity severity; plausible copying risk.** The later assets migration enables RLS with public read; Eve tables revoke browsers and grant service writes; `withOperation` authenticates but supplies a privileged client. Each is source-specific. None proves immutable target/source/subject/version relationships or forbids allowed-to-forbidden row transformations in the native lane.

**Required language:** qualify actual effective table/column/function grants, all applicable policies and role memberships, old/new UPDATE/upsert/RETURNING behavior, triggers, views/RPC/definers, worker/service/import and storage paths. Encode stable same-scope typed relationships and native immutable attribution/effect/version identity through appropriate constraints/commands, not free JSON labels. Changing a parent or child must not retarget an admitted source/use to a different tenant, subject, purpose or byte version. Nullability and CHECK semantics must not admit missing authority arcs; foreign keys/unique constraints cannot prove domain authorization alone. UPDATE/ALL may inherit USING for omitted WITH CHECK; permissive OR/restrictive AND and BEFORE-trigger effects must be assessed precisely. Definer owner/role/FORCE/BYPASSRLS matters; keyword presence is not proof of bypass. No hosted vulnerability is asserted from the schema snapshot or these local migration reads.

### R12 — Legacy adoption or scale bypasses the new source boundary

**High migration/privacy consequence; medium operational likelihood.** Existing assets URLs, public editor objects, CMS images, local Support blobRefs and specialized Eve bodies have different meaning and proof. A UI selector can make them look uniformly reusable without actual custody or permission. Broad candidate/usage scans can also leak counts and miss complete use evidence.

**Required language:** activate only qualified native adapters/source families and explicit legacy evidence mapping. No mass linking/backfill, automatic public→private certification, byte equality→provenance inference, generic participant attribution or old accepted-badge transfer. Preserve retained user data; no cleanup/provisioning/provider change follows this review. Mixed writers/imports/restore must preserve exact use/provenance/restrictions and reconcile disposal before serving. Use bounded authorized candidate queries, target/source/version indexes, finite selection and measured source-use/retention reconciliation budgets, without prescribing arbitrary new volumes or a full-library scan per add. One contextual add action is sufficient; whole future products are not a hidden prerequisite.
