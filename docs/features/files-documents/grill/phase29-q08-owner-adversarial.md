## Disposition and strongest alternative

**Accept A with required amendments.** The ordinary initial access period can remain finite while a source deliberately authorizes another bounded read admission for a legitimate current request purpose. The old grant remains expired. The source must have a new, qualified basis for the new admission; neither expired credentials nor generic staff access supply it.

B, keeping this completed-request lane closed, is the strongest simpler alternative. It avoids a new issuance capability, but a real later need then depends on a separately qualified source route or cannot be met. A is a reasonable product choice if its source command, finite policy and assistance can actually be qualified. Staff availability and demand have not been measured. A does not imply that recipients are entitled to recurring access or that an approval always produces usable files.

The most consequential new distinction is **current eligibility to receive fresh source access versus current permission to read through the old grant**. The former must be proven; the latter is deliberately absent. Requiring the old grant still to be valid makes A impossible, while reactivating it contradicts Q07.

## Proposed corrected decision

Permit a currently authorized source staff member, through the existing request/help context, to deliberately approve fresh finite read-only access after the **proven ordinary timebox of an actually admitted completed-request view** has ended. This new source action must use a prospectively adopted exact policy and the existing P4/P12/producer-owned access boundary. It does not reactivate or rewrite the old grant, its end, the original fulfillment or any genuine later binding shortening.

Each fresh admission must remain within the same recipient and the **original ordinary completed-view scope**: currently eligible own-submission members, their independently permitted View/Download actions and Q05-permitted context. Intersect that outer bound with current source-purpose eligibility, privacy/security/use limits and the exact reviewed fresh scope. No other recipient, new purpose, superseded content, new member or broader personal library is authorized by this command.

Current eligibility for a new grant is distinct from the expired grant's ability to serve data. Staff may approve under the fresh-issuance policy without requiring the recipient to maintain an open browser. Protected recipient use nevertheless requires Q01's current qualified proof and exact assurance, together with all current source/PDP checks. Proof or link possession alone never grants fresh authority.

Issuance authority, body-read authority and ongoing issuer dependency must be explicit. Existing P12/source rules control whether later issuer departure/role loss affects an admitted recipient grant; this review neither preserves every grant forever nor revokes them all by assumption.

The complete finite duration/activation/end, repeat/overlap and relevant source-lifecycle policies must be decided and qualified before the new capability activates. This decision supplies no day count, new clock anchor, automatic renewal, staff role, notification or one-active-grant cardinality. Stable semantic effects and current policy/history checks prevent retries or concurrency from manufacturing additional windows. The completed request remains read-only, and retained evidence, independent source rights and communication outcomes keep their own authority.

**Q08-O18 — Truthful assistance and communication.** Show staff the currently authorized scope and actual finite result before the deliberate action; show recipients only their permitted outcome and current end after qualification. ## Required proof before activation

1. Only a qualified original ordinary admission and ordinary expiry can enter the fresh command. Unknown history, source revocation, genuine earlier authority shortening and actual content-use expiry cannot.
2. A permitted fresh command succeeds while the old grant remains expired; requiring old current read authority is not the implementation. The expired credential never serves data by itself.
3. Read-only staff, assigned support, general admin, operator/impersonation and service credentials without the exact current issuing scope fail. A properly authorized issuer can operate through a permitted narrow projection without an accidental body grant.
4. Issuer authority/delegation revocation races final commit correctly. After commit, the exact selected continuing-dependency rules hold, including grantor-departure cases required by P12.
5. Every fresh scope is bounded by the original ordinary admission and current source eligibility, including member identities, actions, metadata/history classes and recipient. Repeated grants cannot widen by union or chain.
6. Never-opened but originally admitted members are not incorrectly excluded; historically accessed but currently forbidden members remain denied. Independent supporting files are preserved.
7. Same email/shared mailbox/cross-tenant or changed principal cannot inherit access. Legitimate current contact correction/recovery follows its qualified exact same-recipient path without false blanket failure or silent retargeting.
8. Approved staff action before recipient sign-in exposes no private facts. Q01 fresh proof or explicitly qualified exact current reuse gates entry; ordinary JWT email flags fail.
9. Missing duration, anchor, end, repeat/overlap policy or unresolved ongoing dependency blocks affected activation/issuance. Neither P18 defaults nor Q07's original 30 days fill the gap.
10. The later selected finite temporal policy is tested at exact before/equal/after boundaries, under late delivery, proof completion, resend, retries, clock precision/skew and representation recovery; this proof cannot be written as a guessed anchor now.
11. Concurrent same-intent approvals, lost responses and duplicate transport keys produce the same durable effect. Changed payload conflicts; separately intentional issuance must satisfy current history/policy, not merely use another random key.
12. Repeat and overlap races obey the policy actually selected later. Test boundary and one-beyond cases without assuming either one active grant or unlimited admissions.
13. Approval versus source restriction/disposal/member change/policy change/reassignment has one exact source ordering. Stale preview cannot silently grant a different scope or longer end.
14. Failure after source approval but before delivery preserves approved truth and recovers through existing owners. No duplicate grant or false delivered/read claim follows a timeout.
15. Fresh access never reopens intake/correction, modifies completion, restores a superseded predecessor or imports other recipients' files. Current content/version changes remain exact owner operations.
16. Metadata/status/history/count/search, preview, whole original, HEAD/range/conditional/resume/recovery, deep links, caches and replayed receipts enforce current authority and exact scope.
17. Original-byte integrity, independent preview/original eligibility, tiered audit, per-chunk expiry, feasible termination and no-recall/partial-file honesty survive fresh admission.
18. Provider links and protected-action host transitions cannot revive old credentials or bypass exact recipient proof; current source grant and transport replacement remain distinct.
19. Holds/retention/independent byte uses neither make fresh disclosure permissible nor get destroyed/extended by it. Expired Support source content remains expired; any independent route reauthorizes with its actual owner.
20. Legacy cohort migration, unsupported version, restore and rollback cannot manufacture prior scope, lose adverse-end evidence or reactivate grants. A scoped stop preserves completed records and independent permitted work.
21. Staff and recipient keyboard/AT/touch/zoom/RTL/mobile/poor-network journeys preserve context, distinguish approval/proof/delivery/read and expose the real help path without promising approval or requiring a new upload.
22. Audit and operational evidence identify approval effect, original scope, actor/ongoing dependency, fresh policy/end and current denial without private bodies/secrets or unsupported human-reading claims. Repeated-use review uses governed metadata, not broad content analytics.

These must be reconciled into the native request owner, Q01-compatible P4/P12 seam and relevant producer/delivery consumers at the later authorized specification stage. These are explicit capability activation gates, not default permission to ship arbitrary values. Their unresolved state does not undo the selected product capability or previously working source services.
