## Recommendation and fair alternatives

Ask whether ordinary Private Document Requests should offer **tenant-controlled automatic reminders for actual outstanding recipient work, alongside deliberate staff reminders**.

**A — Offer both automatic and deliberate initiation, recommended.** The tenant can enable a qualified bounded automatic policy; authorized staff retain contextual Send reminder and the source-permitted controls. This supports repeatable collection without staff having to notice and initiate every follow-up. It adds genuine timing, stopping, overlap, recovery and noise-control obligations. Availability is not automatic enrollment or an initially Enabled program.

**B — Staff-triggered reminders only.** Staff deliberately choose when to follow up from current request context. This gives sensitive or unusual cases immediate human context and avoids automatic schedule state. Its cost is reliance on staff noticing and acting. It still requires a complete source-authorized reminder, current recipient/contact checks, safe content, durable intent/outcome and retry protection; it is not an ad hoc email workaround.

Illustration only: Elena has supplied two of three requested items. Jordan can deliberately remind her about the remaining work under either option. A additionally permits the tenant's qualified policy to initiate that reminder. If all material is received and Jordan has not reviewed it, neither option may call that staff backlog missing recipient work. If Request changes later creates a real next action, use that current source meaning. A withdrawal or re-offer can change actual work without being an unconditional immediate reminder or a reset of every clock.

Recommend A because a bounded optional automatic mode supports both personal and repeated collection while retaining B's contextual action. This is a product judgment, not measured Asym demand, proof of higher completion or an unchanged-cost claim. The founder already requires reminders; the genuine question is their supported initiation model.

## What must not be smuggled into the question

1. **No selected schedule/default.** A is permission to offer automatic initiation, not proof of an Enabled default, auto-enrollment, due-relative timing, repeat count, pause/restart interpretation or historic catch-up. Resolve an exact bounded source policy before activation. B is manual initiation, not notification prohibition.
2. **No clock conflation.** Requested by, access ends, actual source intake cutoffs, recipient proof/session, retention and reminder usefulness remain distinct. A date change, fresh-access act, withdrawal or re-offer must not reset all clocks. A reminder supplies no fresh grant; its current permitted action must actually be useful.
3. **No unfinished-status heuristic.** Source facts distinguish incomplete recipient work from received material waiting on checks/review, accepted evidence, nonblocking optional work and actual Request changes. Staff assistance may satisfy a need without a participant upload event.
4. **No imported purpose.** Q20 fresh access, Q26 Request changes, Q27 review actionability, Q31/32 clarification, Q35 cancellation and Q40 update notices retain their own triggers/defaults/pins/usefulness. A reminder is not a resend or relabeling of those events.
5. **No silent double-send or replay.** Manual/automatic overlap, policy disable/re-enable, source work changes and ambiguous provider outcomes require exact source rules. A stopped occurrence cannot simply revive when a worker retries. Those concrete details must be settled in the selected-answer review, not inferred from another notice's exception.
6. **No new audience or channel.** One actual source recipient/contact authority; no watchers, arbitrary address list, email-equality identity, forced Party/account or generalized bell/SMS policy. P17/P6 actual supported qualified delivery remains the only lane.
7. **No implementation platform by implication.** Native source records and existing durable execution/communications are the starting point. Neither choice requires a general schedule builder, Workflow installation, per-file polling, mandatory Realtime, new provider or zero-cost claim. Finite measured candidate, retry, fairness and retention profiles remain release evidence.
