## Narrow feasible source-owned design

This capability belongs in the existing requesting source's command layer and P12 grant mutation path. Keep the qualified native participant integration, source-host doorway, exact original-serving and shared UI/workflow infrastructure. Do not create another grant engine, generic grant settings product, independent access-request inbox, new auth issuer, file browser or scheduler.

The minimal durable facts are: which proved original ordinary admission ended; which same continuing source/request/recipient/purpose/requirement scope is being considered; who currently may administer it; one deliberate effect and its bound material review; resolved finite terms under the eventually chosen policy; genuine current restrictions and required evidence; and source outcomes needed to explain subsequent completion. These are invariants, not prescribed tables, column names or a requirement to retain all personal information forever.

The administrative command cannot require the recipient's old expired read permission to be valid. Conversely, the administrator's grant authority must not automatically disclose private originals. Separate source-authorized administrative context from contents, and recheck the issuer's current capacity at admission. Preserve source-specific continuing delegation/grantor-departure dependencies rather than inventing survival or automatic revocation on every staff change.

Before admission, prove actual ordinary expiry and still-unfinished source state. If source status or prior admission is unknown, remain closed for affected actions with an honest unknown/recovery state; do not convert an empty result or unavailable policy service into expired, missing, completed or active. Reconcile an uncertain already-issued fresh effect first. If the old source actually completed while its authority was valid and only a projection is stale, recover that existing completed result instead of issuing fresh unfinished rights.

Accepted/frozen/closed items stay protected even if another requirement is pending. A fresh admission permits actions under the exact current eligible scope, not every action ever allowed by the request. A hard intake cutoff may prevent new submission while independently eligible read/review remains; do not flatten all dates into a whole-request end. A genuine cap cannot be bypassed by calling a new row a continuation.

## Causal race and replay obligations

Use conceptual `J` for the new admitted source occurrence, `V` for its qualified finite unfinished end and `F` for the actual normal source fulfillment. These are not chosen columns or a fresh timing formula. A prospective continuation-to-completed admission requires the fresh authority to exist in the source causal order at F, remain valid at F (strictly before its applicable end), and satisfy historical source/recipient/scope predicates. Equal-precision timestamps do not replace causal proof. The source effect must durably commit; an internal SQL timestamp is not a physical commit timestamp.

Required races include:

1. Completion first: once the source actually completed before fresh admission, refuse this unfinished lane. Do not clear completion to grant it, and do not create retroactive completed access for a gap occurrence.
2. Fresh admission first: if source authority and eligibility hold at later F, the selected prospective Q10 amendment may admit Q07 reading for at most 720 elapsed hours from that F, under true caps. The fresh unfinished-only V is not a completed cap. No fresh numerical duration or Q08 eligibility is inferred.
3. F before V, delayed consumer after V: recover the same proved eligible completed outcome under current disclosure. Do not apply the stale unfinished expiry job or start a processing-time clock.
4. Upload starts under old authority and ends in the gap: start is not receipt or reservation. A fresh grant never backdates that admission. Recover an earlier proved admitted effect where one exists; safe reuse of unadmitted quarantined bytes would need its own qualified current admission and exact-byte custody checks, not a claim they were already received or a forced blind reupload.
5. Correction versus acceptance/closure: use the exact Q03 review-set and immutable-successor rules. A fresh grant cannot force an accepted item back to pending or restore a superseded predecessor when processing fails.
6. Two staff attempts or new transport keys: serialize the adopted source semantic conflict/overlap policy, including the no-row-yet case. One recovered intent stays one effect; neither one-lifetime-grant nor unlimited parallel grants is selected here. No lock around provider calls.
7. Revocation/recipient/source change while waiting: revalidate affected authority and material reviewed scope under the actual source/P12 mutation contract. Locking a grant row alone does not necessarily serialize changes to all other authority rows.
8. Commit response lost: preserve and recover the same source admission/outcome under current disclosure; no new key/end and no deletion of potentially admitted files. Required source evidence cannot be replaced by a best-effort log.

Once F is admitted, retry, duplicate callbacks, proof re-entry and projections cannot create another normal fulfillment or completed clock. Q08's previously ratified eligibility is not automatically broadened by this lane; further completed regrant requires its explicit compatible owner mapping. The original Q12 strictly-before-90 consequence applies to the original ordinary lifecycle, not a silently imposed cap or guarantee for Q13.

## SQL and service-path qualification

Qualify explicit schema/table/column/function grants and effective policies, including every privileged RPC, service/job path, views and storage operations. The intended P12 model keeps fine-grained capability logic at its sole resolver and coarse tenant RLS as defense in depth. That does not remove the need for structural same-scope integrity and the protected source mutation boundary.

Required relationships include tenant/source/request, old effect/new effect, current intended recipient binding, original requirement membership/version, any exact submission/correction edges, issuer/decision/evidence and resulting completed admission. Ordinary ID foreign keys alone cannot prove all of those relationships. Use constrained composite relationships or a qualified equivalent that survives both child and parent mutation; cover nullable references, state-dependent required values, finite timestamps, immutable original facts and deliberate delete/disposition behavior. Add the indexes required for actual bounded reads, FK operations and contention. A time-dependent CHECK is not ongoing expiry enforcement.

Do not freeze an unselected grant cardinality into a unique index. Semantic identity and current transition constraints are required, while the separate repeated-issuance/overlap rule remains an activation gate. Protect server-owned identity, attribution, source lineage, profile/time results and approval facts against direct and trigger-transformed changes. Client input may propose intended actions/selection where allowed; it cannot certify its own tenant, actor, current authority or historical eligibility.

PostgreSQL UPDATE/ALL may inherit an omitted WITH CHECK from USING; policies compose and BEFORE triggers affect the proposed new row. Inspect the effective old-row/new-row rules, not the presence of a keyword. A definer runs with its owner's privileges: bypass depends on owner/role/FORCE posture, not the keyword alone. Service-role requests and signed-in-user-token requests can have different RLS behavior. CHECK permits null unless constrained separately; null-bearing unique/FK values require deliberate semantics. These are technical requirements, not findings against nonexistent native SQL. [Policy semantics](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html), [RLS roles](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html), [constraints](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-constraints.html), [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security).

NOW/CURRENT_TIMESTAMP are transaction-start values; statement time is also not a post-wait current clock. Qualify the source occurrence/linearization and subsequent waits, commit, rollback and uncertain outcomes. Do not prescribe one universal SQL function or infer a physical-commit guarantee from clock_timestamp. [PostgreSQL clocks](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/functions-datetime.html).

Explicit intended grants/exposure must therefore be verified during deployment rather than inferred from public schema placement. No platform configuration or migration was changed. ## Private use, projection and recovery

A fresh grant is not a provider URL, a cache entry or a browser session. Short proof/session/provider/worker lifetimes remain independently qualified; no expired old capability is extended. Old links and tabs may reveal only the appropriately authorized doorway or safe ended/unknown state. Reusing acceptable proof is governed by Q01's exact context/freshness contract; it is neither a blanket reuse entitlement nor a mandatory new account/enrollment.

Every recipient action and sensitive administrative read re-proves its exact current context. Apply permission before metadata enumeration, totals, cursors, history/search and returned replay data. A refusal/error is not an empty authorized list. New grants must not cause the current/history projection to show unauthorized old versions or omit unrelated supporting files that are still current. Client caches should avoid identity/scope mixing and render meaningful stale/pending states; server enforcement remains authoritative even if invalidation fails.

Existing Q04 exact immutable-byte, digest-before-emission, safe filename/content headers, no-store, range/conditional/resume and in-flight expiry/revocation rules remain. A provider-signed URL with nominal TTL does not satisfy them on its own. Already disclosed bytes cannot be recalled. Neither normal expiry nor a lost fresh-grant reply authorizes deletion of submissions, audit history or another purpose's file use.

Shared Workflow can carry safe identifiers and bounded recoverable work. A worker must re-resolve the current effect/stage/recipient/purpose and its own authority before a protected effect; possession of an old event or active claim grants nothing. A grant committed with delivery unavailable is still a source outcome; notification is not proof of receipt or reading. No new notification capability, reminder schedule or background policy engine follows from Q13.
