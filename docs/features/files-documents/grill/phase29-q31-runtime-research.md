## Recommended single product fork

Ask only about **an optional participant email notice when authorized staff deliberately share a new native clarification reply**. Recommend **A — Offer the eligible notice initially On, with staff control in the same reply action**. **B — Offer the same eligible notice initially Off, with staff opting in for that reply** is a strong quieter alternative. **C — Keep the reply native, with the existing qualified help/manual contact route** avoids this new automated email purpose. All three preserve Q30's native reply, current authorized shared context, durable source work, safe recovery and the original access limits.

The reason to favor A is a product judgment: an external participant may leave the request after asking a question and may not know a staff response is available. A visible optional notice reduces reliance on repeated portal checking without forcing a separate manual email. B reduces unintended outward mail and repeated-reply noise; it asks staff to remember the notice when useful. C suits an effective established contact route, but adds manual coordination and risks a reply waiting unnoticed. These are reasoned tradeoffs, not measured Asym behavior or proof that a selected default will be understood.

Do not combine this choice with incoming staff email for participant questions. Q27 is the distinct source-actionable evidence-review occurrence, with its own Off default; it does not select a clarification alert or automatically settle that later policy. Do not combine Q31 with native work creation, a new bell, unread counter, reply receipt, reminder, digest, assignment notification, mailbox, email-reply ingestion or general notification settings platform.

The staff-facing control should affect this notice's optional participant-email step, not the native reply or every effect in a Delivery Plan. Its purpose and permitted destination should be visible where the reply is sent; preserve deliberate choice through validation/recovery. No arbitrary To/CC, destination override, free-form email body, separate routine confirmation or mandatory toggle ritual follows. Any tenant configuration must use the actual bounded P17 contract and publish a complete plan, with honest supported choices rather than a speculative audience/channel menu. Participant preferences, tenant plan, staff choice and current source/communication authority remain distinct; one cannot override the others. In the final A/B comparison, both propose the same initially offered eligible optional plan step with tenant disablement; only the initial per-reply staff choice differs. These alternatives do not create three tenant modes.

## Exact implementation boundaries if A or B is selected

1. **Source occurrence:** the notice is about one genuinely new deliberately shared staff reply that the source admitted for the exact participant/item context. A draft, text edit, private note, mere refresh, repeated transport request, participant question, new file, formal Request changes or fresh access is not that occurrence. The notice cannot exist on the claim that an uncommitted reply is available.
2. **Separate identity and acknowledgment:** give the native send and notice effect their own coherent durable identities/terms, linked to the same admitted source fact. Native receipt and owned staff handling remain Q30 truth. A known staff choice of Off yields no new mail; replay cannot turn it On after a default/plan change. Distinct legitimate follow-up replies are not deduplicated solely because text matches. Changed committed reply/recipient/notice intent conflicts rather than reusing a consumed slot.
3. **Closed destination:** derive the participant, source purpose and current eligible contact from trusted source/identity authority. Email match, provider `to`, a Support conversation address, task assignee or profile search is not authority. Do not notify a substituted recipient, widen old recipients after reconfiguration or create a new account. Contact repair/change must obey the original source/communication occurrence and prepared-identity rules; no worker-time silent retargeting or raw address override.
4. **Eligibility at all meaningful boundaries:** native staff authority, current participant sharing/clarification scope, reply visibility, source applicability, suppression, qualified content, effective plan and delivery path determine whether the email is available. Reprove the governing gates at preparation, submission admission and protected use, including after waits. A staff reply can remain lawful handling after the participant expires while its “reply available” email is no longer useful or permitted.
5. **Ended access:** no “open your reply” promise when the participant cannot currently retrieve that shared text. Expiry, fulfillment, revocation, recipient/purpose change or shared-text restriction can make a pending notice non-applicable. Use only a separately exact qualified useful notice/help variant when actually authorized; otherwise do not dispatch. Never reinterpret a sealed reply notice, automatically issue fresh access, reset a clock, revive an old notice after fresh access, or expose text in email to work around the missing permission. A legitimate Q20 fresh-access notice remains a different occurrence and choice.
6. **Minimal non-content notice:** permit only approved neutral awareness copy, truthful tenant/sender identity where appropriate and the existing qualified protected doorway. Exclude clarification excerpts, private notes, file contents, filenames, sensitive source/request/item labels, unnecessary participant/staff details, attachment previews, direct storage/provider URLs and arbitrary deep links. Subject, preheader, plain text, HTML, tags, history, errors and metrics all need the same minimization. The notice is not a transport for the reply body. A known safe entry point still rechecks current scope; receiving mail grants nothing.
7. **Explicit reply expectation:** the mail's Reply-To and copy must not imply that answering the email posts to the request. No email-reply ingestion is selected. Any monitored help mailbox is separately source/P17-qualified; it cannot be inferred from the staff author, broad Support address or a fallback account.
8. **Configuration semantics:** A and B differ only in the new visible choice's initial state when eligible; missing/legacy/ambiguous input is never guessed On. Tenant policy and participant restrictions may narrow permitted sending. Ordinary P17 plan changes remain future-only with immutable pins. If Q31 adopts a Tenant Off stop for affected unsubmitted work, explicitly specify the source/P17/P6 amendment, governing submission boundary, no missed-Off revival after later On and in-flight truth. Do not inherit Q26/Q27 exceptions by analogy or silently change unrelated notice purposes.
9. **Complete plan/recovery:** one complete bounded P6 release, including a proved zero-member result, has a permanent source-authorized occurrence identity. Unknown compilation/handoff is not zero or Off. Provider side effects remain outside source locks; native success survives optional notice suppression, delay, failure and uncertainty. Recovery resumes original qualified terms or exposes the correct unresolved state; it cannot make staff send the reply twice merely to retry email.
10. **Batching and noise:** transport aggregation is P17/P6 infrastructure and preserves every independent prepared member, recipient, utility deadline and outcome. It does not create a product digest, merge several staff replies into one semantic event, change a reply's timestamp, widen recipients or rekey/split uncertain work. Repeated legitimate replies may produce noise under A; the visible per-reply choice is the selected practical control unless another product decision explicitly authorizes coalescing. No “one email per keystroke,” per refresh, per provider retry or per duplicate transport key.
11. **Truthful outcomes:** distinguish native reply saved/shared under current scope from notice not requested, non-applicable/suppressed, queued, submitted/accepted, delivered, failed and outcome unknown. Provider acceptance is not delivery, delivery is not reading, and either is not source acknowledgment, review acceptance or fulfillment. Late or contradictory provider evidence follows the owner's reducer/quarantine and cannot rewind source history.
12. **Bounded proof, not a new platform:** qualify exact optional external-recipient material/utility/Recent-copy class, native role/RLS/privileged paths, atomic source/handoff/replay, before/after expiry and publication races, contact changes, wrong recipient/tenant, protected-doorway behavior, provider unknown outcomes, finite queues/rates/history and accessible composed user journeys. Use existing owner repair/operations surfaces. No whole Support/Workflow Studio dependency, new provider, new reminder service or arbitrary numeric retention/SLA is selected.
