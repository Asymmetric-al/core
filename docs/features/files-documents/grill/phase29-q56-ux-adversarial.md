## Product recommendation and strongest alternative

Build one small item-local preparation path beside ordinary upload. A person who already has a suitable file uses it directly. A person who wants one PDF selects or captures page photos, arranges them, checks the resulting intended set and submits through the existing source intake. The assembler is optional; it is not a new scanner application, general editor or policy platform.

The strongest narrower alternative remains direct images/prepared-PDF upload only. It avoids another authoring/derivation/recovery lifecycle and has documented collection-product precedent. It can require app switching and make separately received pages harder to read in order. Conrad has chosen the additional continuity benefit; honor that choice while keeping its cost proportional. There is no measured Asym frequency, mobile abandonment, time saving, page count or cost neutrality supporting either option. The illustrated person with paper pages is a scenario, not an observed ministry workflow.

The combined choice should not force an extra picker decision for every ordinary file. Keep the familiar upload entry and a clearly named optional **Combine photos into a PDF** action in that same item surface. A split action, two adjacent choices or another existing shared composition may implement this without a new shell; the product requirement is discoverability and a short direct path, not a prescribed new widget. Returning from camera/gallery or the assembler preserves safe item/filter/scroll context. Do not reset a source filter or change the target item because a photo was selected.

Recommend **one valid page is sufficient**. A minimum of two is arbitrary friction when a person removes a bad second page or simply wants one paper page as a PDF. Zero pages, an unsupported unresolved selected page, failed required decoding or an incomplete generation cannot submit the intended composition. No silent partial PDF. Maximum counts/bytes/pixels are finite measured qualification bounds, not invented numbers or copied vendor limits.

Use explicit quarter-turn rotation, addition, replacement/retake, removal and ordering only. These are controls for preparing this output; no automatic crop, perspective correction, filters, cleanup, OCR, redaction, signature creation, existing-PDF merge or general editing follows. The user's rotation changes the derived-output instruction, not the retained source image. A valid older contribution is never edited through this local page list.

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

An apparently helpful combine action could silently lose a page, substitute compressed/cropped content, claim a generated PDF is the original, expose private input history or strand the user after a lost response. The amendments require one exact source unit, immutable originals/output, whole-intent recovery and capable correction rather than a local canvas success message.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That the actual item permits photo evidence/PDF, the actor can supply it, a supported device can handle the bounded set and a source can define its fidelity/retention. We have not measured device mix, number of pages, failure rates or time saved. A current staff role, browser decoding success and camera permission do not prove these assumptions. Source/profile qualification remains mandatory.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adds one ordinary intake preparation/contribution mapping. Specialist/public/official/resource/financial uses keep their source contracts. Current source permissions govern both originals and output; real new candidacy integrates with existing review/work/effects. There is no full scanner, office editor, new archive, OCR corpus or second authorization system.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Normal upload remains fast. The person who wants one PDF gets an explicit ordered list, accessible small controls, one final intent, one-page support and repair without recapturing unchanged pages. Camera denial, inaccessible content, source closure and weak connectivity have truthful next steps. State copy distinguishes local preparation, receipt and review; no compulsory preview/read checkbox/second modal is needed.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Primary manuals support direct camera uploads and intentional page preparation. They do not prove that every application needs a scanner or that a PDF wrapper makes content accessible. W3C principles support non-drag equivalence, predictable input, focus and meaningful status; those are applied to actual complete tasks. The selected scope is an evidence-informed product judgment, not a universal vendor consensus.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes with the explicit ordinary-source/custody amendments and current Core UI/P12 boundaries. The existing avatar cropper/public URL and template-rendering seams are useful cautions, not ready private evidence implementations. The original/candidate/source distinctions align with Q02/Q03/Q47/Q53; new term and actual schema remain owner-qualified rather than inferred from UI labels.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Accept the chosen A+B with the bounded controls, one-page allowance, true input/output unit, practical retained-page correction, current whole-unit re-offer and complete recovery. Do not reconsider the founder's added convenience simply because engineering is required. Also do not expand it into OCR, crop enhancement, offline sync or a settings platform. Actual implementation/release proof remains outstanding.
