## Proposed corrected decision

For newly qualified ordinary private-document requests, prospectively declare one finite source policy in which the initial access deadline governs recipient access while the request remains unfinished. If the exact source normally fulfills the request before that deadline and while the relevant source-recipient eligibility and independent limits permit transition, admit Q06's read-only completed stage under Q07. The declared unfinished-stage deadline then does not also cap that properly admitted completed stage.

Resolve the completed end once from the trustworthy source fulfillment occurrence plus 720 elapsed hours and every genuinely applicable independent earlier limit. Preserve it under Q07's no-lengthening rules. This transition does not remove an overall purpose/privacy/security cap, undo genuine adverse shortening, change recipients/files, or relabel an existing expiry.

Prove the policy, unfinished deadline, exact source outcome/evidence and recipient eligibility at fulfillment; re-prove current authority before later disclosure. A consumer may materialize/recover a timely eligible transition after its source event was committed, even after the historical unfinished deadline, but it cannot create eligibility from today's state or a backdated completion. A stale expiry projection cannot erase a transition that the authoritative source already established. A genuine revocation remains controlling.

If the source actually fulfills at or after the exclusive unfinished deadline, or historical eligibility cannot be established, preserve independently authorized business completion without granting completed recipient access. On-time submission, pending staff review and technical work do not pause that deadline. Q08 is not automatic recovery when no ordinary completed admission existed.

Keep one source policy and sole PDP, exact Q03/Q06 mutation fences, current Q01 proof and Q02–Q05 private reads. Later invalidation, reopening or new work follows explicit owner commands and cannot restart the old clock. Preserve received evidence and independently authorized processing/retention. Initial duration/anchor, deadline-edit/extension/recovery powers, new cycles and notifications remain unselected.

## Precise temporal interpretation

For explanation only, let:

- `U` be the prospectively declared finite unfinished-stage deadline;
- `F` be the trustworthy source-recorded normal-fulfillment occurrence;
- `H` be the independently applicable hard source limits and adverse restrictions.

An ordinary completed transition requires trustworthy evidence that `F < U`, the source outcome is valid, and the required recipient/purpose eligibility and `H` permit it at that boundary. This is not a claim that a physical database commit timestamp is required. The exact source transaction/ordering/time contract must prove the boundary after relevant waits.

The admitted completed end follows Q07: no later than `F + 720 elapsed hours` or any applicable earlier hard end. `U` is excluded from that completed calculation only because this prospective policy already declared its unfinished-stage scope. Retain `U` as historical transition evidence; do not delete it or reinterpret it after the event.

Current reads additionally intersect live authority and current representation eligibility. A historical valid transition is not a frozen permission snapshot.

The completion fact can validly exist when no completed recipient admission exists. Do not impose a database invariant that every fulfilled request must have a recipient grant, a later end or an automatic fresh-access route.
