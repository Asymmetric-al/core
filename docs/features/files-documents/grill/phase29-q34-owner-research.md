## Recommended next question

**Should authorized source staff be able to cancel an eligible unfinished ordinary document request when it was issued in error or its legitimate purpose has ended?**

Recommend **A — Allow deliberate cancellation through the requesting source.** Give the whole request a truthful Canceled outcome, stop its ended ordinary collection/continuation under current source authority, and preserve received evidence, earlier decisions, required history and independent obligations. Cancellation is not successful fulfillment, evidence rejection, deleting files, ordinary expiry or canceling an enclosing application/process. Some items may already be accepted while the overall request is unfinished; their historical facts remain intact.

**B — Use an already-qualified capable source resolution without adding this ordinary cancellation action.** This remains credible where the source already gives staff a usable way to end the request through its business process. It avoids another action/authority to qualify. Its cost is extra navigation and potentially unclear participant status if the existing route is weak. It is viable only with a real capable resolution; waiting for access expiry, marking every item No longer required or telling someone to contact an administrator without an actionable route is not an equivalent solution.

No artificial third option is needed. Do not bundle participant self-cancellation/decline, pause/resume, automatic expiry cancellation, generalized reopening, cancellation email defaults, arbitrary sent-request editing, bulk cancellation or reissue into this one founder choice. Safe correction/recovery must be made concrete during the selected-answer review, without pretending that a canceled request automatically becomes eligible for Q13 or Q08 access.

The useful example is an ordinary request accidentally issued twice to the same person: staff need to stop the duplicate while leaving the intended request and both histories truthful. Another is a genuinely ended ordinary collection purpose. Avoid an example that presumes a document-request action can decline an application, waive safeguarding/consent evidence, cancel a financial duty or make a legal determination.

## Boundaries to make clear in the question and selected-answer review

1. **One new ordinary source power.** A authorizes a bounded contextual cancellation capability, subject to exact source/P12 qualification. Where a qualified command already supplies it, expose/reuse that command; do not make File Manager a second writer. B declines this new ordinary action but still needs a real effective source route.
2. **Eligible unfinished aggregate, not only blank requests.** Cancellation may be needed after partial receipt, review or acceptance. Preserve each original fact and current independent duty. Fulfillment-first leaves the completed request outside this ordinary action; cancellation-first prevents stale deferred Q22 fulfillment. Do not copy Q33's outstanding-unaccepted-item scope to every member of the request.
3. **A distinct business outcome.** Say Canceled, not Fulfilled/Accepted/Expired/Deleted. Do not implement it as all items No longer required, zero remaining count or hidden checklist removal. The source's complete current authority and lifecycle decide admission, and the original cause/time/basis remains attributable.
4. **Scope the stop correctly.** Stop the canceled request's new ordinary evidence intake/correction and continuation whose authority depends on that request. Apply source currentness at actual admission, not browser click time. Do not close unrelated requests, revoke unrelated independent permissions or erase needed record/safety work. Staff can retain independently lawful processing/history/remediation duties.
5. **No implied canceled-request archive.** Q06/Q10 completed access requires normal fulfillment; Q08/Q16 require an actual eligible historical completed root. Q13 requires genuine continuing unfinished purpose. Cancellation is none of those. A safe Canceled/Unavailable response may use an already-qualified status/recovery projection only under its real disclosure rules; no private metadata fallback, automatic thirty-day reading window or full submission archive is inferred. Exact postcancel read/support semantics need explicit owner mapping and, if genuinely a new product choice, separate ratification.
6. **Truthful intake and decision races.** A committed receipt before cancellation remains received; local selection/transfer is not receipt. Cancellation-first prevents new dependent admission; acceptance/fulfillment/source change/relevant text must order coherently. Unknown finalization is reconciled rather than claiming loss or deleting potentially referenced bytes. No stale cancellation can overwrite a later source outcome or auto-rebase its reason/basis.
7. **Independent concern resolution.** End obligations proved to belong only to the canceled collection. Retain independent safety/incidents, records and other source work. Otherwise cancellation either leaves a hidden permanent task or illegally dismisses an obligation outside its scope. Historical feedback stays attributable even when it is no longer an active demand.
8. **Privacy-safe reason and consequence.** Staff need the exact permitted request identity and meaningful stop consequence before a deliberate action. Capture the source's concise cause/basis without a generic essay/approval engine. Participant-safe explanation is not automatic publication of internal reasons, mistaken-recipient details, private staff history or sensitive relationship labels.
9. **Mistaken cancellation must have a concrete remedy.** Before admission, staff can correct/cancel their intended operation. After a terminal cancellation, do not promise universal Undo, resume, fresh unfinished access or generic Reissue. Bind an effective existing source remediation path; where a continuing legitimate ordinary need permits a genuinely new request, use independently qualified normal issuance, current recipient/purpose/readiness and exact trace linkage. Preserve Q01 proof reuse and Q28 exact evidence reuse only where independently permitted, without copied grants/acceptance or unnecessary reupload. This must be explicit in the selected-answer amendments, not silently inherited from the word Cancel.
10. **Durable result and replay.** Commit source cancellation, mandatory history and durable dependent follow-through coherently; external work stays outside locks. A lost response recovers the same original outcome under current disclosure. Known success survives ancillary refresh/notification failure, while unknown commitment requires reconciliation. Old replay cannot cancel a new request or rewrite later source truth.
11. **No automatic message or clock policy.** Q34 selects neither cancellation email nor a reminder/default, grace period, retention duration or expiry rule. Existing communications remain exact purposes and current eligibility. Already admitted external effects may still occur; cancellation cannot claim recall. A qualified safety/withdrawal fence cannot depend on optional notification success.
12. **Qualification without a platform.** Use existing source/history/work/help and P12/custody/communication owners. Prove real roles/RLS, exact relationships, direct/service/worker paths, concurrent outcomes, restore/mixed versions, finite workloads and composed accessible journeys before activation. No new cancellation engine, workflow designer, task-per-effect, permission system or whole future Studio prerequisite is needed.

## Practical tradeoffs and proof questions

A removes an unnecessary demand promptly and gives participants/staff an accurate end state. Its main risk is stopping the wrong request or misunderstanding the effect on already supplied work. The permanent answer is exact authority, clear reviewed context and qualified recovery, not silently deleting records or adding a universal second approver. B avoids a new direct action where an existing source process is already effective, but cannot be described as simpler if people must wait for expiry or chase someone without a real resolution path.

The selected-answer review should prove at least: duplicate ordinary request cancellation while the intended request continues; partial accepted evidence preserved; cancellation versus last-item fulfillment in both orders; upload/clarification already admitted versus cancellation-first; late worker/retry after cancellation; actual participant status/privacy with no inherited completed grant; staff authority loss; independent safety/retention work; mistaken cancellation recovery using a real source route; and current source-purpose handling of pending/in-flight communications. These are future proof scenarios, not tests run here or observed ministry incidents.

No measured Asym cancellation rate, response benefit or workload is available. External comparative and implementation research are parallel work. Their findings should support usability and identify reuse hazards without importing vendor delete/reopen/access rules or claiming a native implementation from generic task/status helpers.
