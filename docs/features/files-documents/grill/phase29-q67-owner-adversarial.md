# Q67 A — Owner adversarial review of supported external-event recording

29 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. **Selected: A, with required amendments. Full ratification pending.** This review proposes a bounded ordinary-source human producer for an already-defined supported external occurrence. It does not record an actual event, approve a GF policy or legal conclusion, change a canonical source, or qualify an implementation.

## Main conclusion

Accept A with a real source-owned Record / Correct / Retract / fresh re-acceptance lifecycle. Staff must be able to record a supported actual external fact without an integration for every case, but a typed date is neither automatic truth nor authority. The accepted result must disclose the actual human/evidence provenance and the source's permitted meaning; it must not claim independent legal verification or change the external business relationship itself.

Five material boundaries require explicit wording:

1. Policy configuration, an occurrence, a staff input/evidence basis, current accepted interpretation and physical disposition remain separate facts.
2. Initial Record needs its own current source power; policy editing, original authorship or record reading does not grant it.
3. Correction/retraction targets the actual admitted source basis. Retracting a staff input cannot erase independently valid native/integration support, prove the event never occurred or automatically reset the rule to not-yet.
4. Same real occurrence across producers is one source fact; recurring legitimate events cannot be blocked by a permanent subject/type or date-only key. A wrong subject is not repaired by reparenting historical records.
5. Late genuine facts can update current records obligations, but do not replay historical Workflow enrollment, messages or unrelated outward actions. Current records impact is not historical automation backfill.

The strongest B alternative—only qualified existing native/integration producers—reduces human input/reconciliation work. It is appropriate where those producers actually exist. It otherwise makes an externally dependent policy unavailable pending producer qualification, and cannot fill the gap with today, fake native completion or retain-forever. A is the more useful selected route provided its exact profile, authority, evidence and repair qualify; no generic event engine is necessary.

## Eighteen exact proposed owner clauses

**O01 — Bounded initial producer and owner.** Add optional authorized human recording of an already-defined supported external occurrence through the exact owning ordinary source. That source owns the accepted recording/current interpretation, history and records-purpose impact; existing business sources retain their own facts, P29 custody and P12 authority. Consume an existing authoritative event rather than create a competing one. Do not mint receipt, Q21 acceptance, Q22 F, Q34 cancellation, Q65 End, grant settlement, signature, contract status or relationship truth owned elsewhere. This route records the permitted evidence of an external fact; it does not cause that external business act or certify its legal effect.

**O02 — Qualified profile and tenant flexibility.** The actual source profile defines event meaning/version, subject/episode identity, permitted human and other producers, single/recurring occurrence semantics, sufficient evidence, actual date precision/calendar, compatible policy purposes and correction/impact rules before activation. Preserve Q66 tenant-owned supported values and role/delegation choices. Policy authoring is not initial occurrence authority and cannot create executable event semantics by arbitrary strings, scripts or a global type registry. A supported human route must be genuinely usable without a new integration or routine Asym code change for each lawful occurrence; an unqualified new semantic producer stays explicitly unavailable.

**O03 — Real current actor and scope.** Derive actor, Tenant/environment/source and authoritative recorded time from validated native context and current source/P12 powers. Separate Record, Correct, Retract, fresh re-acceptance, policy editing, evidence/body reading, protected-history and disposal powers. Use actual source organization/entity boundaries where applicable, current limited/no-Party paths where supported and qualified NHI/owner ceilings for other producers. Neither author/admin label, Drive access, service key, client IDs nor stale claims grants authority. Current qualified maintainers may repair; no original-author-only bottleneck. Recheck relevant authority after waits and before effect/result disclosure.

**O04 — Actual occurrence, time and evidence.** Record only the occurrence and precision the source actually admits, with actual event date/time separate from trusted recorded-at, actor and provenance. Late reporting may be valid; do not reset to now, midnight, upload time or modified time. Planned future milestones are not completed events. Unknown date, partial precision or conflicting evidence cannot be silently normalized; use only an expressly supported source representation/repair. Evidence can be a sufficient qualified existing reference, actual source fact or concise factual input; personal witnessing, a new upload, full body preview, essay or second approval is not universally required. Preserve actual staff-reported/source-admitted provenance; do not label it independently verified unless that occurred.

**O05 — One deliberate coherent Record.** In exact source context, offer Record [defined event], show known subject/event, relevant date/evidence and a concise safe consequence, then use one deliberate command. Inert preparation/GET/render/prefetch never records a fact. Admit source recording, exact version/basis, necessary history, current impact/control fence and recoverable original result coherently through the existing authoritative transaction or proved durable fence. Durable follow-through may be bounded/partial but cannot leave a stale permissive consequence usable while merely updating a badge. Source acceptance, pending records impact and physical disposition are distinct outcomes; no long transaction across providers or SDK-callback atomicity claim.

**O06 — Actual occurrence identity across channels.** Human/native/integration inputs refer to the actual same source occurrence where equivalence is proved. Define source semantic identity independently of transport keys, policy versions, filenames and editable occurrence dates. Do not impose universal once-per-subject/type or forever uniqueness that prevents legitimate recurring episodes, date correction or re-acceptance. Preserve each relevant input's provenance while avoiding duplicate accepted effects. A materially contradictory qualified input requires current source-defined resolution; no human-wins, newest-arrival-wins or integration-wins default. Untrusted or unauthorized alleged conflicts must not globally freeze unrelated records. Reuse qualified existing fact/evidence instead of inventing a general evidence graph.

**O07 — Correct the same actual occurrence.** Provide a real current source-authorized Correct recording route for a mistaken admitted date, precision, evidence or interpretation of the same actual occurrence. Target exact current revision/basis, show safe old/new consequence and retain actual correction cause through sufficient source context or necessary concise input. Append correction actor/time/current result; preserve original input and interpretation under lawful history policy. An unchanged visible date can still have a material evidence/authority correction, while a proved complete no-op creates no new occurrence/clock/history effect. Correction cannot alter an external contract or edit native business facts through this shortcut.

**O08 — Retraction and mistaken-retraction recovery.** Provide source-authorized Retract recording for an identified mistaken or no-longer-supportable recording, independent of unrelated invalid draft preparation. Retain original record, reason/basis, actor/time and current consequence. Retraction removes only reliance that this recording legitimately supplied; it is not evidence that the event never occurred and does not automatically mean not-yet or restart a clock. Independently valid native/integration evidence, other current duties and holds survive; the owner recomputes the actual accepted basis. If retraction was mistaken, offer fresh current source re-acceptance of the same proved occurrence identity linked to that history, with actual current evidence/authority, not replay of the old grant or a duplicate new event. Unresolved causes cannot be bypassed by a new key.

**O09 — Wrong identity and contradictory facts.** A wrong subject/event/episode cannot be repaired by reparenting immutable history. Retract/correct the mistaken reliance under the affected source, then separately admit the right subject's actual fact under its current authority, linking permitted remediation history. The two targets require their own powers; inability to admit the new target must not prevent independently authorized containment of the wrong one. Use source-qualified current resolution for contradictory producers; preserve independently correct facts and do not discard them merely because one staff input was retracted. No generic merge, forced replay or authority union is introduced.

**O10 — Current records impact and later material.** Evaluate actual current policy/version, records/classes/uses and relevant obligations from the accepted occurrence/basis. Preserve original assignments and historical event facts. A late true event may make a rule currently due, but no implicit activation-time purge follows. Required adverse/current-duty impact establishes the governing result or restrictive pending fence before unsafe consequences, with bounded owned continuation and truthful exact scope. A later qualifying record/use can reference the existing occurrence under its own admission/policy rules; do not fabricate another occurrence merely to start its clock, nor silently include it in an earlier fixed impact operation. Independently governed originals, assemblies, history, previews, communication/control and copies remain distinct.

**O11 — Custody, holds and irreversible boundaries.** Recording/correcting/retracting an event grants no read/share or hold-release authority and does not itself delete or restore bytes. Order relevant impact and source adverse facts against new uses/holds/writers/promotions and Q63 final dispatch admission. A protective change before that boundary wins; after a possible external effect, stop only still-preventable work and preserve truthful uncertainty/observations. Finite permit/worker/lease expiry cannot clear possible-effect fences; each new destructive submission needs fresh current admission. A correction after actual loss uses only lawful qualified recovery, otherwise records loss and capable incident/source remediation. No mandatory reupload, fictional Undo or DB-only restore claim.

**O12 — Historical-dark and no new outward cohort.** Accepting an old genuine occurrence may update its explicitly qualified current records obligations; this is the selected records-purpose effect, not replay of past workflows. Preserve Workflow WS-RQ-020/historical-dark: imported/historical occurrences do not silently enroll historical runs, replay communications, reopen access, renew references or trigger unrelated outward effects. Any separate historical backfill/consumer requires its own reviewed authorized bounded source path. This decision selects no new email, bell, reminder or participant task. Existing actual later business events retain their exact owners; neither blanket suppression nor fabricated events are permitted.

**O13 — Concurrency, original results and stale intent.** Bind each Record/Correct/Retract/re-acceptance to exact Tenant/environment/source subject/type/occurrence revision, full semantic intent/evidence basis and durable business-effect identity. Coherently persist source facts/history/control/result; repeated calls or another producer cannot duplicate the same effect. Recover a matching committed original before new eligibility under separately current disclosure; return original attribution plus any independently qualified current state. Changed intent conflicts; unknown original outcome remains unknown until reconciled. Relevant expected-basis/ABA checks precede no-op; unrelated changes and genuine no-ops need not force ritual review. Expired unverifiable control evidence rejects stale intent instead of reopening it as new. No replay can undo a later correction/retraction or revive an ended consequence.

**O14 — Structural and alternate-path enforcement.** Use typed same-scope subject/event-definition/occurrence/input/revision/policy/impact/command/evidence relations, deliberate nullability/delete behavior, valid precision/state combinations, semantic uniqueness and scoped indexes. Protect original attribution and immutable identities; do not cascade actor/source deletion into required evidence or reparent allowed rows into forbidden scope. One source/P12 decision layer and coarse Tenant RLS remain, with unified app/database context and pooled success/error/cancel/retry cleanup. Prove effective named/PUBLIC/default/inherited schema/table/column/sequence/EXECUTE, actual owner/FORCE/BYPASS/service/overload/search_path, old USING/resulting WITH CHECK and trigger-adjusted rows, SELECT/RETURNING/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/UPSERT/MERGE, views/REST/GraphQL/RPC/Storage, workers/imports/legacy/operator/restore. Zero rows, a keyword or an SDK wrapper is not success or authority. No arbitrary JSON command store or mandatory new table name is selected.

**O15 — Protected evidence and current projection.** Register recording/basis/impact/history fields through shared static classification and subtract-only projection. Names, date existence, event types, references, counts, conflict details, original results, search/export/logs and late cache responses require actual current purpose/field rights. Recording authority does not automatically grant agreement body or another producer's protected evidence; use sufficient safe context or a capable restricted-resolution path. Do not broaden access to explain a denial. Invalidate late/cached results after actor/Tenant/environment/source/basis changes; no persistent restricted offline queue, public URL fallback or sensitive bodies/tokens/paths in telemetry. Business history, security audit and diagnostics retain their separate lawful minima.

**O16 — Complete simple interaction.** Use existing source Records/context and shared Base UI/base-maia for Record, current result, Correct, Retract and fresh source re-acceptance. Preserve known context and lawful inputs; show actual date precision and separate recorded time where useful. A safe inline consequence review and one final act suffice; no universal wizard, extra modal, essay, upload or second approver. Make potentially already-due current records impact understandable without promising deletion. Retraction remains usable through sufficient source metadata when body access or unrelated correction preparation is unavailable. Support keyboard/AT/touch, visible unobscured focus, meaningful status without focus theft, 320-CSS-pixel reflow, long/Unicode/RTL/localized names and source calendars, weak-network original recovery and inert selection/blur/IME/prefetch. Deliberate commands keep origin/CSRF protection.

**O17 — Capable bounded operation and migration.** Required unknown/conflict/partial impact, missing authority, wrong target, departed owner and physical-loss cases need a real currently qualified source/records operator and supported correction/reconciliation route, not raw SQL or a powerless queue. Qualify finite event/profile/evidence size, lookup/history/impact groups, set-wise current checks, stable unique paging, locks/retries/control-retention/copy fanout and fair continuation under real tenant skew. Preserve exact P12 R6 logical budget, physical-shard carve-out and restricted pre-egress/uniformity requirements. Inventory actual legacy inputs/producer identities; never infer event occurrence from file age, imported timestamp or a policy default. Mixed-version readers/workers must preserve old meaning and adverse facts; deny unsupported new effects, retain original reconciliation, restore controls before serving and roll forward if old code cannot preserve the contract.

**O18 — Exact adoption and vocabulary.** Recommend ADR0067 for the durable initial human producer, multi-channel identity and correction/retraction/current-impact contract. No new glossary term is necessary: existing Document Records Policy and source fact/business-occurrence vocabulary suffice; define the actual record precisely in the source contract without another generic event entity. Preserve all 35 existing Files terms, root glossary and Q01–Q66. No conflicting prior prohibition needs rewriting: Q67 explicitly adds the qualified initial producer and its recovery alongside Q62 correction/Q66 configuration. At formal publication reconcile source authoring and declared OpenSpec/design/tasks, including the historical-dark distinction, rather than independently editing generated text. Actual policy/event profile/authority, native tests and intended-user proof remain gates. This review is not full ratification, legal certification, actual event, implementation, hosted mutation, formal publication or Q68 selection.
