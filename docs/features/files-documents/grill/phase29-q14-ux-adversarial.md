# Phase 29 Q14 — a simple thirty-day continuation experience

Research date: **21 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. The parent reports Conrad selected **Q14 A: up to 30 elapsed days from fresh source issuance**, and explicitly asked to avoid overengineering while delivering excellent UX/UI. Q01–Q13 are ratified. **The full Q14 amendment package remains pending ratification.**

This is documentation research and design review only. Only this note was written. No canonical document, product, provider, permission, dependency or UI was changed. No native grant, recipient, file, browser, assistive-technology or intended-user test was exercised.

## Verdict

Retain A and make the everyday flow small:

1. Staff review the existing request context and use **Grant access to continue**.
2. The source resolves the actual finite unfinished end from fresh issuance.
3. The recipient sees the real end, received work and the next permitted action.
4. A successful eligible completion changes to the already-selected bounded read-only view.

Do not carry C's pre-entry window, first-entry activation event, unused-approval substage or timer-start explanation into A. Do not build an editor, profile picker, reminder queue, routine second confirmation, new account or separate access dashboard for this choice.

The source safety, exact scope and durable recovery from Q13 remain necessary. Simpler UI does not mean optimistic permission or less truthful outcomes; those controls belong in the existing source path rather than new user steps.

## Selected timing and evidence limits

Use G for this fresh authoritative issuance occurrence; original I/U remains preserved. The nominal new unfinished end is **G + 720 elapsed hours**, under genuinely applicable earlier limits and current restrictions. It is not thirty calendar days, one month, first entry, delivery time or a requested-by buffer.

A successful valid source admission resolves its finite end. A cannot normally remain “approved, waiting for first entry to establish the end.” If an observer cannot retrieve an already committed result, that is uncertainty to reconcile, not an unselected activation policy. Proof, route availability, notification and current permitted use may still fail independently.

Thirty days remains a founder/product judgment. No representative Asym evidence proves that recipients are ready when staff act, that all work is a small correction, that review will finish in time, or that thirty days is an empirical optimum. The fourteen-day alternative has a credible shorter-exposure benefit; bounded first-entry timing protects a different delayed-discovery case. They belong in the decision record, not in extra UI or dormant implementation branches.

The matching thirty-day numbers have different owners:

| Period                      | Anchor and effect                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Q14 fresh unfinished access | Up to 720 hours from G; visits and corrections do not replenish it. Never entering does not keep it alive.                                                               |
| Q13/Q07 completed reading   | At most 720 hours from actual eligible normal fulfillment F, within the declared fresh read scope and genuine caps. It is not guaranteed, and it never preserves writes. |

One source receipt is not completion. Staff approval of fresh access is not approval of submitted evidence. Current proof is not a new grant. Further fresh-read eligibility under Q08 and repetition/overlap policy remain separate; no automatic second period follows from another expiry.

## Minimal copy and actions

Use current source truth and disclosure authority. The examples below are proposed wording, not tested-user results.

| Situation                                                     | Concise copy direction                                                                                | Action                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Staff is reviewing a valid proposed continuation              | Show intended recipient, eligible remaining work/read scope and the fixed timing rule with known caps | **Grant access to continue**                                            |
| Fresh source issuance is proved committed                     | **Access granted. Unfinished access ends [actual date, time and zone].**                              | Return to the existing request context; do not imply a message was sent |
| Issuance outcome is unknown                                   | **We couldn’t confirm whether access was granted.**                                                   | Supported status/reconciliation action, not another grant               |
| Issuance committed; a separately authorized invitation failed | **Access was granted, but the invitation could not be sent.**                                         | Repair only that supported failed communication; keep G/end             |
| Recipient is qualified and currently able to continue         | **Access while this request is unfinished ends [actual date, time and zone].**                        | Show only currently eligible task/file actions                          |
| Exact file is durably received; staff review remains          | **Received — awaiting review.**                                                                       | No redundant upload or recipient-overdue blame                          |
| Proof expired but source access may remain valid              | Explain that verification needs repeating                                                             | The existing qualified proof route                                      |
| Current authority/stage cannot be checked                     | Explain the temporary inability to confirm access                                                     | Safe retry/help; affected protected actions stay closed                 |
| Source proves this view has ended                             | **Access has ended.** only where existence may be disclosed                                           | Existing governed help; no promised renewal                             |
| Eligible completion with a current read-only admission        | **Request complete. Read-only access ends [actual end].**                                             | Current permitted reading/download/history, never ordinary writes       |

Before completion, a short conditional explanation may be useful:

> If the organization completes this request while your access is valid, we’ll show any available read-only access and its end here.

This avoids promising another thirty days to everyone. The exact completed end is shown when actual eligible fulfillment admits that view. If no admission or current disclosure exists, do not show a private completed page merely to explain its absence.

Avoid **“You now have thirty days”** on a delayed return, **“Invitation sent”** after only a source grant, **“Request rejected”** for access expiry and **“Files will be deleted”** without a separate actual custody rule.

## The ordinary journey

### Staff: review in context and grant once

Keep the existing request page. The staff actor needs the exact Q13 issuer capability; reading the request, reviewing its files or having sent the original message is not enough.

Show a compact meaningful summary of the source-bound recipient, pending requirements/actions, declared read scope and timing. Do not require the issuer to inspect file bodies if the grant capability does not include or require body-read access. Already accepted items remain read-only where permitted.

Before G is established, any computed preview date is an estimate under the fixed rule. After a valid source effect commits, use the stored actual end. Ordinary time advancing under unchanged reviewed terms does not require another confirmation; a changed recipient, scope or true limit does require the owning conflict/review handling.

The normal deliberate action is sufficient. No secondary “start timer” approval, period dropdown, editable end date, consent checkbox, mandatory reason essay or new reviewer role is introduced.

### Recipient: return to the task, see what remains

Use the existing Q01 doorway and compatible proof rules. A new device or reauthentication does not reset the fresh end. An old URL cannot disclose private request details without current authority.

Once qualified, show the current task and exact end in main content. Requested by remains separate. A late entrant receives the actual remaining opportunity, not a fresh full period. There is no first-entry event or unused window to explain under A.

Preserve authorized current files, receipts and concise history. Show the next actor: recipient action, file checks or staff review. Do not ask for an already received usable file again merely because access is fresh. A genuinely required correction still needs its normal exact-target action and current eligibility.

### Upload and correction: preserve real outcomes

Transfer progress is not receipt, and a transfer begun before the end does not reserve time. An unadmitted old operation cannot silently borrow the new grant. Where the source supports qualified custody reuse, a new deliberate current submission may avoid retransmitting bytes; the source receipt remains the actual new admission.

Q03 fences and current review lineage remain unchanged. A corrected successor admitted under current authority fences its predecessor while checks may still be pending; failure does not automatically revive the old file. Other supporting members remain intact.

If a response is lost, reconcile the same source effect. Closing a dialog or losing connectivity after dispatch is not proof of cancellation. Do not hide uncertainty behind a success toast or fix it by issuing another fresh period.

### Review and completion: name the actual actor

Source review shares this finite unfinished opportunity. A timely submitter is not overdue merely because review remains, and receipt does not start completed reading. Review may proceed independently without forcing the recipient online or requiring another upload.

When normal fulfillment qualifies during valid fresh authority, Q13 stops ordinary writes and resolves only the declared eligible completed-read scope. Its maximum is measured from F, not G, the last browser visit, email delivery or a delayed consumer.

For example, with no earlier cap and all predicates satisfied, fulfillment at fresh day 2 can yield a completed end no later than day 32; fulfillment at day 29 can yield one no later than day 59. These are conditional upper bounds, not promised access or a stored overall sixty-day cap. A genuine earlier completed restriction can shorten or prevent the view.

Do not copy the earlier general warning as “early completion always shortens access.” With this selected equal nominal thirty/thirty profile, that is not the ordinary uncapped arithmetic. Explain the actual stage and actual end instead; no change is automatically an extension or guaranteed extra thirty days.

Actual fulfillment after fresh authority ended does not invent recipient reading. A genuinely timely durable effect learned later is recovered with its original end and current restrictions. No further Q08 capability is inferred from this continuation's completion.

### Technical trouble and final expiry: recover the right thing

Only a source-proved ended state is business expiry. Expired proof, mail failure, a temporarily unreadable projection and a single unavailable preview require their own truthful handling. Preserve independently authorized work when only a nonauthorizing subview fails; fail closed for affected protected use when current authority cannot be proved.

The original fresh interval does not pause while support works, reset on a resend or extend after an outage. A repair that leaves too little practical opportunity is a real source/cohort issue; support cannot promise another grant because the interval feels short.

At actual expiry, give the permitted neutral/ended response and existing help. Repeat/overlap policy is still separate. Do not replace it with a renewal button, serial cloning, date edits or private email attachments.

## Lean accessibility and presentation

Use shared Base UI/base-maia, semantic tokens and familiar source components. The important UX work is accurate content and state, not a new layout system.

- Put the current end, Requested by if present, status and next action together in normal page content.
- Use an unambiguous localized date/time/zone with enough precision to communicate the real exclusive boundary. Formatting and browser time never change authority.
- Keep meaningful controls usable with keyboard, touch, zoom/reflow and long translated labels. Essential reasons cannot depend on hovering a disabled control.
- Preserve predictable focus on recovery and meaningful status announcements without stealing focus or reading out every poll.
- Support the existing permitted paste/autofill proof flow. A thirty-day source period does not justify a thirty-day session or an inaccessible short verification timer.
- Do not add a red countdown, artificial urgency, forced download or repeated warning acknowledgment.

WCAG's same-process redundant-entry criterion does not mandate cross-session storage or prohibit every repeated file upload. Existing source-confirmed work is preserved under its actual records and disclosure rules; no new private browser-draft persistence is implied. No static review establishes conformance or intended-user success.

## Bounded qualification and follow-through

The full parent review should retain source/RLS/current-use/race/restore safeguards already required by Q13. This UX note does not propose a new security framework, scheduling product, test harness or telemetry program.

The minimal new UX evidence is that intended staff can identify the right recipient/scope, grant once and recover the result; intended recipients can identify actual remaining time and their next action; reviewers do not confuse receipt with completion; and support can distinguish entry trouble, unknown state and ended authority. Test those journeys under actual caps, late arrival, weak connectivity and composed accessibility.

Any reproducible false end, duplicate effect, unauthorized disclosure or incorrect completion transition is a source/product defect requiring containment and exact regression proof. Any critical mobile/AT or cohort-fit failure blocks the affected activation claim. These are correctness triggers, not invented incident percentages or a new notification cadence.

The selected design remains one fixed unfinished clock and the existing conditional completed stage. Keep its visible flow small. Fresh repetition/overlap and any Q08 expansion remain open; the complete Q14 amendments await ratification.
