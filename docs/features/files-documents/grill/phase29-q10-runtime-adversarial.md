## Essential boundary semantics

Use conceptual names here only; no database column, storage design, SQL function or policy engine is selected.

- `U` is the exact applicable unfinished-stage end under a prospectively declared source policy.
- `F` is the one authoritative normal-fulfillment occurrence, proved with the required source/recipient/currentness predicates.
- `C` is Q07's admitted completed-read end, derived from `F + 720 elapsed hours` and genuinely binding independent earlier limits.
- The initial policy's anchor, numerical period, optional editing/recovery authority and any permitted first-entry mechanics remain unselected.

| Ordering                                                                                        | Correct result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eligible `F < U`, then consumer runs before `U`                                                 | Admit/represent the one original completed policy. Ordinary submit/correct closes; reads require current action and representation eligibility.                                                                                                    |
| Eligible `F < U`, consumer runs after `U` but before `C`                                        | The ended unfinished predicate does not veto the already valid completed stage. Recover only from durable original effect/eligibility evidence, preserve original `C`, and check present source caps and recipient authority.                      |
| Eligible `F < U`, consumer runs at/after `C`                                                    | Preserve source outcome/history for authorized custody. Do not disclose or restart ordinary access; Q08 requires its separately authorized fresh-access process.                                                                                   |
| `F = U` or `F > U`                                                                              | The unfinished end is exclusive. Source business completion may still be legitimate under independent authority, but cannot create ordinary completed admission from expired recipient authority. Receipt/upload-start before `U` changes nothing. |
| Caller starts before `U`, then waits until after `U` before the qualified transition            | Recheck actual time/state after the wait. Do not save an early application or transaction-start timestamp to turn the outcome into `F < U`.                                                                                                        |
| Source fulfillment succeeded but recipient eligibility/admission is definitely absent or denied | Keep valid source completion and its distinct no-admission outcome; do not undo source business facts merely to make readback possible. | Source effect/admission response is unknown                                                     | Reconcile stable intent with authoritative durable records. | Durable eligible effect/admission evidence exists but projection/outbox delivery failed         | Repair the same original view/end. The exact source occurrence model must be qualified. A timestamp sampled after one lock but before another material wait is not sufficient. A transaction's internal source effect, database commit and callback receipt are distinct instants; this review does not claim a SQL time function supplies a physical commit timestamp. Prove the actual linearization convention, boundary precision and rollback behavior, including a transaction that crosses an end while in progress.

No stage transition resets on a second completion command, reordered callback, generic task reopen, status repair, migration or restore. Explicit source invalidation/remediation may restrict access, but cannot silently recreate unfinished rights, resurrect superseded evidence, widen the original admitted scope or restart Q07.

## Completion, admission and durable follow-up

The smallest permanent path stays inside source/P4/P12 ownership:

1. Resolve a server-trusted source command identity, expected revision/member set and current policy/recipient scope.
2. Serialize against relevant acceptance, correction, source completion and authority restriction. Obtain trustworthy time after relevant waits; distinguish current eligibility from a historical record.
3. Preserve the source business effect and the exact recipient-transition decision/evidence in one coherent source-controlled durable outcome. Where an independently legitimate business completion can exist without recipient admission, record that distinction; do not make source truth depend on a working browser/session or successful notification.
4. Persist the original completion occurrence, applicable stage-policy meaning and genuine caps, eligible recipient scope, intended read-only outcome/end, and required audit/recovery identity before claiming admission. This is a required evidential relationship, not a prescription for a universal table schema.
5. Persist recoverable follow-up intent with the authoritative outcome before relying on asynchronous delivery. Existing shared ledger/claims may transport and retry that intent; they do not manufacture it or replace the source transaction.
6. Consumers re-read the actual source record and validate exact identity/revision; duplicate and reordered delivery converge on the original outcome. Separate permitted technical repair from ordinary business writes and current private disclosure.

The August donation saga is a useful atomic producer precedent. The generic Workflow ledger alone is not: a producer change followed by a separate ledger insert leaves a crash gap unless the source has a durable recoverable intent/scan mechanism. An Inngest send success is not proof the source effect committed or was accepted, and a failed send is not a failed business effect.

Likewise, the work-claim unique index prevents duplicate active rows, but cannot stop an old timed-out process from continuing external work. Native durable effects still need stable identity and source-state/version fencing. Scheduler concurrency settings, transport event dedupe, leases and source idempotency protect different boundaries.

For unknown outcomes, preserve the original intent and immutable files. The Eve artifact error cleanup is specifically unsuitable as a template: a transport error can coexist with a committed metadata transition. Deletion/expiry requires authoritative custody/disposal rules and cannot be compensation for an unclassified admission result.

## Authorization, SQL and history

The inspected Workflow tables enable RLS and revoke anon/authenticated direct access; the specific RPCs restrict execution. Eve artifact tables also revoke `PUBLIC`, anon and authenticated. These source statements are useful, but do not prove deployed grants, role membership, effective policy combinations or the absence of other server routes.

The application constructs a service-key client. Native source authorization cannot rely on ordinary browser RLS to police that server path. Inspect and qualify service invocations, direct mutations, security-definer ownership/search path, same-tenant relationship constraints and resulting-row changes. Never let a caller select a role/recipient/source revision merely because an existing internal helper accepts such parameters.

PostgreSQL policy semantics matter:

- For UPDATE/ALL policies, omitted `WITH CHECK` may inherit `USING`; absence of the keyword alone is not a vulnerability.
- Applicable permissive policies combine with OR; restrictive policies combine with AND and still need a permissive grant.
- Resulting-row checks run after BEFORE triggers. An allowed old row does not prove a transformed tenant, recipient, scope or deadline is allowed.
- A security-definer function runs with its owner's privileges. RLS bypass depends on role attributes/table ownership/FORCE RLS, not the keyword alone.

The required native proof includes actual anon/authenticated/intended-recipient/unrelated-recipient/staff/service roles, old and transformed rows, all deployed applicable policies and permitted direct paths. No such database-role test ran here.

Historical authority must be reconstructible without trusting a mutable present-day grant row. Preserve enough source-owned evidence of prospective policy applicability, exact recipient binding, source revision, true cap scope, genuine later shortening and the completion decision. This is not a demand for a general event-sourcing platform. An append-only recipient-safe receipt projection, source audit and private authorization evidence have different disclosure purposes; raw staff audit is not the Q05 view.

## Projection, cache, stream and restoration controls

The current `useTasks` sequence fence is useful UI precedent, but source authorization must precede metadata/counts/pagination/actions. A stale success response, cached idempotent result or previous identity's TanStack/client state cannot reveal the view after access ends. Errors must not become “no files,” “complete,” “expired,” or a silently extended deadline.

Under A, scheduled unfinished expiry and delayed status projections must be conditional on the correct stage/version/end. Once a valid completed stage exists, an old unfinished-expiry event cannot clear it, delete files or issue an authoritative expired state. Conversely, stale completion projection cannot reopen intake or defeat a real cap/revocation.

Q04/P12 byte-serving requirements remain: exact immutable original/rendition binding, current preview/download permission, private cache/header behavior, range/conditional/resume checks and expiry-aware metering/stream termination. The Eve 60-second bearer URL does not supply these guarantees. A stream started in the unfinished stage may not blindly keep going on old authority; if continuation crosses a transition/end, qualify current completed-read permission for the same exact bytes or terminate/re-enter safely. Do not invent a grace interval.

A restore or mixed-version rollout must not reinterpret an overall legacy end as a stage end, remove a genuine shortened cap, replay an expired proof as current, or run an old all-access expiry job against the new completed stage. Preserve custody/holds independently; access expiry does not imply disposal.

## Current official documentation used

- [PostgreSQL 17 date/time functions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/functions-datetime.html): transaction/statement/wall-clock functions differ; calendar-day arithmetic can differ from elapsed hours across DST. Use this to qualify the source clock, not prescribe a function as a physical commit timestamp.
- [PostgreSQL 17 transaction isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html): Read Committed can re-evaluate an updated target row after waiting while other rows still require careful snapshot/concurrency treatment. Row locking alone is not proof of a cross-record authorization invariant.
- [PostgreSQL 17 CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html): existing/resulting-row checks, inherited checks, policy composition and BEFORE-trigger ordering.
- [PostgreSQL 17 row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html): owner/BYPASSRLS behavior and cross-table policy race cautions.

No official documentation chooses the product's unfinished-stage duration or a universal transition architecture. Q10 A remains a source-owned prospective product choice requiring the above qualification.
