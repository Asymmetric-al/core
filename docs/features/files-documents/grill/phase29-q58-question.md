## The next decision

**When the same organization guidance is suitable for both staff and missionary readers, may one publication deliberately serve both qualified audiences, or must each audience have a separate publication?**

Recommend **A — Allow one suitable publication for both qualified audiences.** Keep separate publications available whenever content, language, rights, review plans or release/withdrawal needs differ. This is a choice about what a curator maintains and what a publication change means. It is not a request to choose one database, two catalogs or a storage provider.

Both options deliver the required governed staff-resource reading purpose and usable staff navigation, separately from curation. Neither removes the internal-library outcome or asks staff to impersonate missionaries. Q58 explicitly proposes the narrow staff-reader extension to the existing organization-resource publication owner; staff rank, an app route or a matching audience label is not permission. Compatible Q55 review information and Q57 report/own-return behavior for this new cohort must be expressly adopted under their actual source/P12 rights, not assumed to have expanded already. Staff reading grants no curator/report-handling power.

Existing Support **Staff guides** remain their own already-ratified D27-C source: authored instructional text for actual Support-capable staff, usable without creating a ticket. Q58 concerns organization document/resource publications. It does not migrate those guides, publish them to missionaries, copy their private text or create a replacement knowledge platform.

## A concrete journey

Illustration, not an observed ministry workflow: Elena curates an equipment-handling guide. Nora is an authorized staff reader and Maya is an authorized missionary reader. Both need the same approved instructions.

Under A, Elena prepares the exact guide and deliberately qualifies its intended staff and missionary audiences. She sees that one publication and its subsequent replacement, review plan and whole withdrawal apply to both admitted audiences. Nora finds it through the staff Resources experience; Maya finds it through the missionary Resources experience. Neither sees drafts, management controls, other people's reports or another audience's membership details merely by reading. The same person holding both contexts does not acquire an implicit union of permissions.

If Elena later adds staff-only handling instructions, that is a reason to prepare a separate qualified staff publication—not to hide paragraphs in the browser or silently change what the common publication means. A different language edition or independent review/release schedule likewise needs an appropriate separate source publication. Existing qualified reading renditions and accessibility alternatives remain under their current owner contracts; a presentation accommodation is not automatically a new editorial edition.

Under B, Elena prepares two deliberate publications from the outset. Both can initially use the same permitted immutable file version. She can update or withdraw the staff publication independently; the missionary publication continues only while its own current rights and safety qualify. The interface must not imply both were updated or reviewed when only one was. Copying a publication or reusing bytes cannot copy approval or historical review facts.

For either option, Nora gets useful text-first browsing, permitted search/filter results, honest format/language/known size, Open/Download, review information and qualified successor guidance. She does not need curate permission just to consult guidance. If she reports a problem, the explicitly adopted staff report purpose uses Q57's exact source receipt, private own return and qualified handling; sharing a publication never shares its readers' private reports.

## Why this is genuinely unresolved

The founder's §1/§12 requires coherent staff document work and a useful governed internal library, including a named owner for general-purpose resource records not already assigned. That requirement is not an optional new feature in this question.

Current P28 C38's actual reader is a missionary. C39 establishes explicit staff curatorial capability and exact organization/audience/version publication, while C40 governs current withdrawal and delivery. These contracts do not establish a general staff-reader grant or decide whether staff and missionary audiences can share one publication. Q55 and Q57 explicitly preserved this broader staff-resource coverage item. Q02 permits independently qualified typed uses of exact bytes; it does not make byte equality a shared publication, audience or permission.

There is already an owner for some staff guidance. Support D27-C admits its narrow authored Staff guides for current active-tenant Support-read authority, independent of a ticket. The unresolved resource-reader branch must coexist with that source. It cannot use the demo Knowledge screen as evidence of native completion, claim all staff guidance is ownerless, or require Support permissions to read otherwise qualified organization resources.

Therefore the next meaningful decision is shared versus mandatory separate **publication meaning and maintenance**, with explicit staff-reader adoption under either option. Source ownership, exact current permission, useful navigation, ordinary recovery and safe custody are requirements under both; an arbitrary architecture fork would not resolve the product question.

## Adversarial check of the recommendation

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A curator could mistake Both for a harmless presentation filter, expose staff-only content, or believe a shared withdrawal affects only the current surface. Shared identity could also be used to expose private reports or copy approval. Deliberate audience-qualified publication, one truthful shared lifecycle, current field/source checks and separate publications for real differences are necessary boundaries. They are not proven by a checkbox or shared file.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

We assume some guidance may have identical meaning and lifecycle for both cohorts; this is a product possibility, not measured Asym demand. We do not assume every staff member may read every resource or that curators can publish to every audience. We have no measured duplication cost, native cross-audience source path or user study. Support already has its own bounded Staff guides and must remain authoritative for them.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It completes an explicit staff-reader purpose for organization-resource publications and chooses whether common publication maintenance may be shared. P28/resource meaning, P12 identity/permissions/projection and P29 custody remain separate. Support guides, public CMS, official artifacts and private evidence keep their owners. Q55 and Q57 compatible staff adoption is named rather than silently inferred. No new catalog, public library, message or LMS is selected.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Staff get a useful reading path without management rights or missionary impersonation. Missionaries retain their current experience. A reduces obligatory duplicate editorial work when one edition fits; B makes independent maintenance unavoidable and explicit. Curators need clear change impact; ordinary readers need clear current guidance and safe recovery, not permission machinery or hidden duplicate editions.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Current official knowledge/CRM/resource products support deliberate audiences and separate reader/manager powers; some also show the usefulness of copied independent content. Their admin bypasses, inherited grants and nonsecurity targeting are cautionary examples. A is a reasonable flexible application of the useful patterns under Core's stricter source model, not a universal vendor rule or a claim of validated ministry outcomes.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes as an explicit narrow staff-purpose extension of the organization-resource owner, preserving Q02 custody separation, P28 C38–C40, current P12/shared projection and Support D27-C. It does not assert that Mission Control's shell or demo Knowledge already implements staff resource access. Formal owner/generated projection reconciliation and native activation proof remain separate.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Keep **A — Allow one suitable publication for both qualified audiences**, with separate publications whenever meaning, language, rights or lifecycle differ. B remains the stronger choice if mandatory audience-independent editorial control is preferred even for identical material. The founder chooses this single product fork; full selected-answer adversarial review follows that choice.
