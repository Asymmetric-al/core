## Conclusion and material scope conflict

The tenant requirement is substantive. Q62 C01/C02/C16, Q63 C02 and its no-editor consequence, and Q65 C03 currently exclude a general policy editor or describe bounded approved choices. Preserve their source safety and simple frontline review, but explicitly supersede the exclusion **for a narrow tenant-owned ordinary records-policy lifecycle**. Tenants must be able to create a blank policy, edit their supported business rules, establish approval under their actual delegation, activate an immutable version, change future defaults, deliberately apply qualified changes to existing records, correct mistakes, retire and reactivate. Selecting from Asym-coded values alone would not meet the new requirement.

This is not authority to redefine P12 capabilities or field classifications, create arbitrary executable rules, remove an independent hold, edit prior receipt/approval facts, or break another source's contract. The tenant's actually delegated records-policy owner may author and approve supported ordinary policy within this flow where their governance permits. An external signed policy document, Asym support intervention or second person is not universally required. Conversely, a draft editor, generic admin title or authenticated account is not automatically that owner.

The strongest simpler alternative is tenant selection among a provider-maintained approved catalog. It lowers authoring complexity but would make legitimate organization changes dependent on Asym and is now inadequate as the sole route. A blank, source-scoped policy form with versioned activation and current impact is the smallest complete solution. GF may be an optional draft starting example; it is not a hardcoded default, global template adoption or exclusive class system.

### Proposed glossary definition, not adopted

**Document Records Policy**:
A tenant-owned policy for retention, review and disposal of specified ordinary document records and uses, subject to their applicable obligations. It is distinct from an individual record’s policy assignment, a hold and permission to access the records.
_Avoid_: access policy, legal certification, deletion timer

## Seven founder checks

### What could go wrong with this answer?

Tenant flexibility could be implemented as arbitrary override or denied by retaining code-only business rules. Explicit ordinary ownership, real tenant authoring, immutable effective versions and independently enforced obligations avoid both failures.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That GF is useful as a candidate mapping context, now explicitly selected. Its approval, legal applicability, actual Tenant, work and system configuration remain unproved. No actual supported native policy command is inferred from this review.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adds tenant management of a narrow ordinary policy contract and explicitly amends earlier exclusions. It preserves all specialist authorities, shared authorization/custody and the complete Q65 A+B lifecycle; it does not create a platform-wide records DSL.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Tenant records owners can make legitimate supported changes through a capable flow. Issuers retain Q62's small default summary and normal action; they do not become policy experts or perform routine per-file approvals.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

The recommendation separates policy meaning, accountable activation, current application, history and physical outcome. Contemporary documentation supports tenant management and purpose-specific schedules, but supplies neither Core authority nor GF-specific legal conclusions.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes with the explicit source amendments above. It uses P12 capabilities/static projection rather than editing their meaning, keeps P18/P21/P23 rules scoped and treats OpenSpec as the declared source projection. Actual implementation remains subject to qualification.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Accept Q66 A with these required amendments and the new tenant-management behavior. Use the actual completed GF mapping as an inactive candidate, not a global default. Record a pending ADR0066 for the durable authoring/version/impact tradeoff and the single proposed Document Records Policy definition above. Full ratification and formal publication remain separate; no glossary is changed by this note.
