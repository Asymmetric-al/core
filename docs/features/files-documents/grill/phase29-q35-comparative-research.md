## One decision and recommendation

**When authorized staff cancel an ordinary document request, should an eligible
participant email be offered initially On, initially Off, or not added?**

Recommend **A — Optional eligible cancellation email, initially On with visible
staff control**. A participant may still be finding documents away from the
product. A short truthful notice can tell them that the organization no longer
needs more files for that particular request, without requiring them to discover
the change on a return visit. This is a product judgment about a plausible
awareness gap, not a measured Asym benefit or a universal best-practice rule.

| Alternative                                        | Everyday behavior                                                                                                                                             | Strong benefit                                                                                                                                                   | Cost and risk                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Optional notice initially On (recommended)** | Where this exact notice is qualified and available, the cancellation interaction visibly starts with email selected. Staff can turn it off before committing. | Makes cessation of work easier to communicate without relying on memory or a participant returning to the portal.                                                | A preselected outward action may be overlooked; requires clear consequence/recipient, safe identifying content, current disclosure authority and durable delivery handling. It must not mail an erroneous recipient by default. |
| **B — Same optional notice initially Off**         | The same qualified capability is available, but staff deliberately select email for each cancellation.                                                        | Gives deliberate per-act control and reduces accidental/duplicate disclosure; a strong fit when staff usually coordinate cancellations elsewhere.                | Participants may continue needless preparation when staff forget to opt in. It has substantially the same capability/privacy/recovery implementation cost as A; it is not a no-build alternative.                               |
| **C — No new cancellation-email capability**       | Keep Q34's bounded native outcome/help experience and use only an independently approved existing source outreach route when appropriate.                     | Smallest added product scope and no additional automated external disclosure. Strong where the existing source communication process is reliable and sufficient. | People outside the product may not learn promptly that the request ended. Manual outreach can be uneven; do not assume daily portal monitoring or an already-proven manual response SLA.                                        |

These are three mutually exclusive product alternatives, not three tenant modes
to implement. A and B share the same proposed qualified capability and bounded
tenant control over whether the optional step is offered; their per-cancellation
initial selection differs. An initially qualified tenant offering the step is
not the same fact as the per-act checkbox default. Existing source/P17 controls
may disable the notice without requiring a new settings platform. C adds no new
notice purpose at all; it must not quietly mean that the same new email exists
but starts unchecked.

Illustrative example, not an observed ministry workflow: Elena cancels an
ordinary document request that Nora has been preparing offline. Under A, Elena
reviews the exact safe recipient and selected email consequence while canceling;
Nora can receive a short notice that this specific request has ended. Under B,
Elena selects that notice deliberately. Under C, the native outcome remains
correct, and any outside communication uses an already-approved source route.
None says the larger application was rejected or that another request was canceled.

## Why A is recommended despite the strongest counterarguments

A cancellation ends work that the organization previously asked someone to do.
The participant may reasonably keep working until told otherwise. A short
purpose-qualified email can be useful without asking them to log in, open a
file or take another action. That is the reason for the recommendation; it is
not automatic consistency with Q31 participant reply notices or Q32 staff
awareness. Those are different occurrences, audiences and purposes.

The strongest B argument is the real danger of an unnoticed outward action.
GOV.UK's preselection warning directly challenges A. A must be presented as
an obvious effective choice in the cancellation review, with the actual safe
recipient and outbound consequence visible before commitment. Staff silence
must not be described as consent, and opening a menu or loading a cancellation
screen must never send anything. If representative users miss the selected
email effect, the design/default is not qualified; a theoretical distinction
between an operational setting and a questionnaire is insufficient proof.

The strongest C argument is that a new mail purpose adds external disclosure,
provider integration and recovery work for an event whose observed frequency
is unknown. A capable existing personal/source communication process may be
enough. C is reasonable when that process actually works, especially in highly
sensitive settings or when cancellation follows a conversation already held.
Do not present C as abandoning participants; do not assume its manual route is
reliable without evidence either.

No extra cadence, reminder, SMS, bell, global inbox, personal subscription centre,
message composer, bulk audience selection or communication workflow is needed
for this one optional occurrence. Native cancellation and Q34's existing safe
terminal experience remain the baseline under every alternative.

## Email disclosure is separate from portal access

A/B would add a **new exact cancellation-awareness purpose**. Current source
authorization must establish that this fact may be sent to this intended
participant at this validated destination, with an appropriate safe identifying
cue. Old invitation/address membership, reviewer authority or a matching email
does not establish that permission. Cancellation authority and outward disclosure
authority are different; an erroneous-recipient cancellation may require no email.

Portal expiry alone must not be treated as a universal ban on useful email.
A source-qualified factual notice may legitimately tell a still-authorized
contact that a request ended even when they can no longer enter it. Conversely,
the ability to address an email does not authorize disclosure of cancellation.
This distinction requires explicit source/P17/P6 qualification; it is not
inherited automatically from Q34's status view.

A linkless informational notice is a plausible baseline when it safely tells
the participant to stop work for the identified request. If a protected portal
CTA is included, it must satisfy Q34's exact retained original/already-qualified
fresh admission root, recipient, unchanged end and current restrictions. The
email must not create/renew a grant, expose a file/transcript/reason, automatically
challenge a recipient or redirect them to a replacement request. Expired web
access should not produce a misleading “View your documents” or guaranteed
“See why” action. Any help link/contact must be independently qualified for its
actual purpose. Declare valid message/action variants before preparation rather
than improvising an unauthorized fallback after content is sealed.

The useful message is about **this canceled request**, not all requests or the
parent application. It needs enough safe stable context to distinguish it from
another still-valid request or a separately issued remedy. A private filename,
internal cause or sensitive request title is not justified merely to make the
email recognizable. If no minimal authorized identifying cue can make the
message intelligible, do not send a vague misleading cancellation broadcast;
use the actual separately approved source route where available.

## Simple proposed staff interaction and message meaning

Offer the email choice only where the capability and this occurrence/destination
are qualified. Candidate label: **Email [participant] that this request was
canceled**. Show the actual authorized destination and the effective state
without exposing contacts the staff member may not inspect. No arbitrary new
recipient picker, all-team CC, copied household or collaborator audience follows.

Under A, the effective selection begins On; under B it begins Off. Staff can
change it before the meaningful cancellation action. Make the current consequence
obvious alongside **Cancel request**, or use equally clear action wording that
reflects cancellation plus the selected email. Do not bury the control below
the submit action or use a vague **Notify** label. A settings value must not
silently override the reviewed per-act intent. Detailed UI wording and source
admission behavior require the selected-answer review and intended-user proof.

Possible minimal body, only if safe identity/context is available: **The document
request [safe reference] was canceled. You do not need to send more documents
for that request.** It must not imply successful fulfillment, evidence deletion,
parent-process rejection or cancellation of a different/new request. Do not copy
the internal cancellation reason, filenames, file contents, clarification text
or review history into the message. No attachment or email ingestion is selected.
An actual permitted Reply-To/help route must be truthful; replying must not be
represented as reopening Q30 or automatically adding a message to the old request.

Cancellation truth must not depend on email delivery. Lack of a qualified notice,
provider failure, suppression or an unreachable address must not leave obsolete
intake open or undo committed cancellation. Precommit choice/eligibility changes
must be explained honestly; postcommit records must distinguish staff intent,
pending notice, submission for delivery, delivery failure/unknown outcome and
the already-committed native result. Do not say the participant read or received
the notice merely because a provider accepted it. Native retry must not duplicate
cancellation or mail, and later settings must not retroactively email an earlier
Off choice. Exact admission/recovery design is later qualification work, not a
new platform chosen by this question.
