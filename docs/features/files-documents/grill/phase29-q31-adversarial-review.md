# Phase 29 Q31 — Default-on participant notices for staff clarification replies

**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

**Status:** Conrad selected Q31 A and fully ratified its complete required
amendment package on 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q31 and all twenty-two
glossary definitions are ratified grooming intent. The adopted decision and
proof obligations remain intact; Q32's answer is not inferred. No implementation,
activation or formal owner/OpenSpec publication follows.

The useful product is a native staff reply with one visible optional email
choice. The permanent solution uses the existing source and communication
owners. It does not turn a document request into a mailbox or notification
platform. A remains preferable for asynchronous answer discovery, provided
preselection is obvious, tenant control is real, and delivery never becomes
authority to read private content or rewrite source work.

## Evidence and strongest alternatives

**Repository facts.** Q30 authorizes source-owned item clarification, including
eligible accepted-item discussion while the request remains unfinished, with
durable accountable handling and current shared-text permission. It expressly
does not select this email. Q20 fresh-access, Q26 Request changes and Q27
first-actionable evidence notices have different occurrences and audiences.
P12 owns trusted current authorization; the native source owns meaning and
recipient scope; P17 owns qualified content/plan/binding; P6 owns complete
release, delivery, recovery and body-free history. Q31 must adopt its own exact
binding, including Q01's closed request-recipient branch, rather than invent
a Party/account or copy another purpose's eligibility.

The [owner review](phase29-q31-owner-adversarial.md),
[runtime review](phase29-q31-runtime-adversarial.md) and
[UX review](phase29-q31-ux-adversarial.md) record independently inspected paths,
full incorporated contracts, external sources and limits. The
[original question](phase29-q31-question.md) and its three research notes remain
dated provenance. Local HEAD `3d85897ebf1515008ef5c676b1c58a15422fb9ab` and fresh
live develop `c4d36cb029121641b8882237632d0d6da434049e` are distinct; live is
unchanged from Q30's reviewed snapshot. No pull occurred. Current source is
evidence of implementation, never authority to weaken the governing design.

**Current implementation.** The inspected email sender requires an idempotency
key; Support stores outbound full-body messages; financial helpers persist
notifications and then send; legacy provider callbacks update delivery evidence.
None demonstrates native document-participant authority, permanent semantic
recovery, a complete P6 release, or correct Q31 expiry/Off ordering. Support's
tenant/staff CRUD policies and privileged adapter are not the required document
audience boundary. The inspected planned communications directory is absent;
bounded native-name searches found no Q31 producer. These are local static
observations, not a whole-repository or deployed absence proof.

**Current external evidence.**

- [FileInvite client-message controls](https://help.fileinvite.com/edit-your-email-sms-messages-to-clients)
  list Message Reply Received and allow message enablement controls. Its
  [portal messaging guide](https://help.fileinvite.com/messaging-in-the-client-portal)
  describes email awareness of messages. These support a configurable notice,
  not a proved per-reply default or Core's permission model; editions differ.
- [Submittable submitter notifications](https://submittable.help/en/articles/4926006-what-notifications-should-i-expect-to-receive-from-submittable-as-a-submitter)
  include organization replies and an email-off path with native activity
  checking. Its account preference is not Core's staff checkbox. Its broader
  body delivery/email reply ingestion requires separate contracts and is not
  needed for Q31.
- [Content Snare publishing](https://contentsnare.com/help/knowledge-base/step-5-publish-send/)
  and [FAQ](https://contentsnare.com/help/knowledge-base/reminders-faq/) support
  deliberate no-email/manual follow-through. Its guides differ on which events
  survive no-reminders mode; no exact event list is imported here.
- [GOV.UK checkbox guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/checkboxes/)
  warns against preselection because people can overlook a choice or submit
  an unintended answer. This is strong counterevidence, not an endorsement of
  A. Core's scoped operational default needs a visible consequence and actual
  comprehension proof; it is not a substitute for recipient consent.
- W3C's [status-message guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html)
  and [target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)
  support accessible feedback and usable controls. Conformance requires the
  composed interface, not just an accessible checkbox primitive.

**Best alternative: B, per-reply opt-in.** It offers the same capability with
less inattentive outward mail, but staff can forget someone awaiting an answer.
C removes this new email purpose, at the cost of return visits or manual
contact. A is a reasonable product judgment under Core's preference for reducing
repeated coordination. No measured Asym mail-reading, response, completion,
volume or default-preference evidence was supplied. Named example journeys are
test scenarios, not claimed ministry observations. No vendor pattern supplies
Core authorization, retention, recipient identity or a new channel.

## Ratified corrected decision

> For a genuinely new, deliberately participant-shared staff clarification
> reply on an eligible ordinary Document Request Item, initialize its visible
> optional recipient-email choice On when the exact source, actor, recipient,
> qualified message contract, effective tenant Delivery Plan and delivery path
> permit it. Staff may turn it Off for that reply. The native reply remains
> the authoritative answer; the email is a minimal approved cue to return.
>
> Enable the optional email step in the qualified system default. Authorized
> tenant administrators may disable it and configure only the exact contract's
> supported presentation, locale, sender and reply controls through existing
> P17 administration. Initially Who is the source-owned request recipient and
> How is email. Changing recipient/contact is authorized source/identity work,
> not a To/CC override. No fictional extra audience, channel or tenant mode is
> exposed. Staff choice, tenant publication, recipient preferences and current
> source/communication restrictions are separate and none overrides another.
>
> Show the authorized destination and email consequence beside the existing
> reply action. Preserve the explicit choice and permitted draft/focus during
> validation and recovery. Missing, legacy or ambiguous input never means On.
> A known material destination or eligibility change before native admission
> requires inline revalidation and a deliberate lawful reply-only choice if
> email is unavailable; do not silently downgrade the reviewed action. Normal
> transport delay can proceed through a valid durable handoff. After native
> commit, preserve original intent and truthful independent notice outcomes;
> failed, blocked or uncertain email never erases the reply or requires posting
> it again.
>
> Bind the source reply, actual staff author, participant/item context, notice
> choice and recoverable handoff durably at the original boundary. Release one
> complete bounded P6 plan under permanent occurrence identity, including a
> proved zero result only when exact top-level Live authority is proved. Order
> source admission with policy publication and retain its resolver-issued
> original policy, binding and recipient basis; a delayed worker cannot choose
> today's plan. Reserved or unknown binding cannot create a P6 parent or gain
> historical mail after activation. Replay cannot add mail or recipients after configuration
> changes; genuinely new replies remain distinct. Unknown proof is not zero
> or Off. Preserve immutable plan, recipient and prepared-provider identities.
>
> Re-prove current source, recipient, suppression, safe content/action and
> delivery authority at their governing boundaries. As an explicit Q31-only
> amendment to general future-only plan edits, published Tenant Off fences
> affected notice work at each actual P6 provider-submission admission,
> including unresolved original handoffs and retry admissions. Off-first
> stops submission; admission-first preserves the already-admitted operation
> and potentially in-flight truth. Off does not recall email or rewrite pins.
> Once Off has stopped affected work, later On does not revive it, add members
> or authorize a further same-key provider send. Evidence-only reconciliation
> remains possible. A genuinely new eligible reply after On is a new occurrence.
>
> Use only source-approved minimal notice content and the exact qualified
> protected doorway. Exclude clarification text/excerpts, filenames, private
> notes, sensitive labels and attachments. Even sender/relationship metadata
> requires source permission. A notice must not promise access to an unavailable
> answer. Expiry, closure, restriction or revocation cannot be bypassed by
> email, new access, a newer recipient or a completed-message archive. A help
> variant is usable only under an independently qualified exact contract.
>
> Qualify the exact optional external-recipient material, utility and Recent-copy
> posture before activation; do not borrow financial/staff classes or access
> periods. Q01's no-Party recipient authority does not grant readable Recent-copy
> access: use no readable copy unless its exact compatible current reveal
> contract is qualified, without creating a Party or blocking lawful reply/email
> solely for that optional copy. Keep native reply history with the source and communication history
> body-free. Use existing source/P12/P17/P6 administration, repair and operations.
> Add no new inbox, bell, digest, reminder, email ingestion, attachment channel,
> routine approval or whole-Workflow-Studio prerequisite. Incoming participant
> questions and supplementary staff notifications remain outside Q31.
