## Practical context

Illustrative scenario, not a claimed ministry practice: a recipient has supplied two of three requested items. Staff should be able to see the remaining work and send a deliberate reminder. The choice is whether the tenant may also enable a bounded automatic reminder program so staff does not have to notice and initiate every follow-up.

Now change the facts: all requested material has arrived and the recipient is waiting for staff review. A request-level “unfinished” or “overdue” badge is not enough reason to tell the recipient to submit again. If a valid Request changes decision later creates recipient work, the source must identify that actual next action. A Q47 withdrawal may leave work outstanding, but it must not blindly reset a reminder interval or generate an immediate extra message.

The intended benefit of automation is less manual chasing. Its cost is persistent timing, stop, retry and noise-control behavior that must remain correct as work changes. No Asym user study has established the frequency of this problem or a universally preferred reminder cadence.

## The question

**Should ordinary Private Document Requests support tenant-controlled automatic reminders for outstanding recipient work, alongside reminders deliberately initiated by staff?**

| Option                                                                                                  | Experience                                                                                                                                                         | Benefits                                                                                                                           | Costs and downstream effects                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Offer tenant-controlled automatic reminders, alongside deliberate staff reminders. Recommended.** | A tenant can enable a qualified bounded reminder policy. Eligible staff retain deliberate Send reminder and applicable source-level controls within tenant policy. | Supports both low-volume personal follow-up and repeatable collection without requiring staff to initiate every reminder.          | Adds a real source-owned timing/occurrence/stop contract, clear controls, duplicate prevention and delivery qualification. Automatic availability does not mean automatic enrollment or sending by default.   |
| **B — Staff-triggered reminders only.**                                                                 | Eligible staff choose when a reminder is appropriate and deliberately initiate it from current request context.                                                    | Simpler initiation state; staff can account for conversations, sensitive circumstances and exceptional timing before each message. | Staff must notice and act on outstanding work; follow-up can be delayed or inconsistent. Manual sending still needs current eligibility, safe material, authorization, durable outcomes and retry protection. |

**Recommend A.** It provides a useful option without forcing every tenant or request into it. Keep B's deliberate action available and make automatic behavior understandable and controllable. This recommendation is a product judgment supported by comparable features and Core's existing source/communication architecture; it is not evidence that automatic reminders always improve completion or that their operation is free.

## Current primary research and fair counterevidence

1. **Content Snare supports controlled automation.** Its request settings permit pausing automatic reminders and choosing a schedule again. It warns that resuming after time or due-date changes can affect expected triggers. This supports understandable pause/resume and timing semantics, not copying a vendor schedule or catching up every missed send. [Official pause guidance](https://contentsnare.com/help/knowledge-base/how-to-pause-automatic-reminders/), updated April 30, 2026.
2. **FileInvite shows why defaults and stopping need scrutiny.** Its app-oriented guide documents Almost Due and Ongoing options with Never defaults; Ongoing reminders have no automatic cutoff and require a manual stop even after completion. Its other-edition documentation describes different overdue stopping behavior. These are different documented product behaviors, not a universal best practice. Core should follow its actual outstanding-recipient-work purpose. [App invite guide](https://help.fileinvite.com/articles/invite-overview), checked October 25, 2026.
3. **Submittable supplies a strong B precedent.** Authorized staff can select outstanding Additional Form requests and deliberately send reminders. This makes manual-only a credible product choice, particularly where contextual follow-up matters. It is not proof that Submittable offers no automatic behavior elsewhere. [Official reminder instructions](https://submittable.help/en/articles/3088088-send-reminders-for-additional-forms), May 28, 2026.
4. **Levitate separates reminder delivery from access lifetime.** Its File Request guide says reminders do not extend a link's expiration; its automatic text follow-up uses configured business hours. These are useful separation/noise examples. Its channel, identity, contact, expiry and automation model must not be imported into Core. [Official File Request guide](https://help.levitate.ai/article/712-file-request), checked October 25, 2026.

These sources establish available product behavior and counterexamples. They do not establish nonprofit-specific demand, safety, conversion improvement, accessibility compliance or Core implementation. The founder's exact request and Core's governing owners determine the fit.
