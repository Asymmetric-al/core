Keep A. Complete the ordinary native request through its owning source when
all actual requirements are satisfied. Staff still make Q21's substantive
acceptance decisions. Do not require a second routine Complete request click
after the real work is done. Preserve legitimate source-owned requiredness,
authorized alternatives and genuine final obligations, rather than making every
tenant follow a hard-coded file count or adding a general workflow builder.

The sharpest amendments concern **what counts as all requirements, races that
can miss or incorrectly create completion, and the actual fulfillment time**.
Optional work must not silently become compulsory, disappear or be called
accepted. A completed request must not acquire a new access clock on recovery.

## Whole-product adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

The browser could mistake visible accepted rows for the full source requirement
set. Two final updates could each miss the other and leave finished work stuck,
or an inserted requirement could be missed and create premature fulfillment.
Recovery could move the completion timestamp, reviving access. A source fence,
durable reevaluation/effects and immutable actual occurrence solve these distinct
problems without a new workflow engine.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That the ordinary source can express its actual conditions and identify
authorized meaningful review. That a separate final checkoff has no additional
ordinary purpose. These are selected product intent and source qualification
obligations, not measured facts about ministries. Optional labels do not prove
nonblocking status; unknown requirement coverage is never true.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It chooses progression for ordinary native requests. Source satisfaction, item
acceptance, custody, tasks, access admission and communications retain separate
owners. Existing specialist final judgments remain. No global auto/manual mode,
new waive/hold/reopen capability, completion email or generic finalizer is added.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Staff do the substantive work once; users see what is received, awaiting staff,
still required or completed. The final action explains its closure consequence.
Recipients are not blamed for staff delay. A known accepted item with unresolved
completion stays visibly accepted; a failed refresh never demands reupload.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

It follows source-owned state, current authorization, durable effect recovery
and truthful accessible feedback. Research also supports intentional manual
wrap-up in other products. The choice is appropriate only because genuine final
work remains in the source rule, not because automatic progression is universal.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes, with the ordinary policy explicitly qualified at the source boundary and
reconciled into its real owners at publication. Generic task timestamps and
disabled catalog recipes are not implementations to rubber-stamp. Full referenced
OpenSpec sections retain their source/status; generated summaries are not owners.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Keep A with the clauses below. Preserve useful tenant/staff flexibility through
authorized source requirements and alternatives. Avoid a compulsory duplicate
finish action, but also avoid an ad hoc “keep open” control that contradicts the
selected rule after every actual condition is satisfied.

## Ratified corrected decision

For the ordinary native Private Document Request, the requesting source SHALL
record fulfillment when its complete current qualified requirements are
satisfied, without a separate routine staff Complete request action. Q21's
deliberate exact staff item acceptance remains necessary for ordinary file
evidence; it is not replaced by upload, safety checks, task status or a count.

The rule SHALL preserve genuine source-owned final obligations and existing
authorized requiredness, non-file, waived or not-required outcomes. Only current
authorized source changes can alter these conditions. Q22 adds no new waiver,
override, hold, generic workflow mode or arbitrary tenant rule editor. A real
final judgment is actual source work; a second acknowledgment with no independent
purpose is not a new default prerequisite.

The source SHALL establish one coherent exact fulfillment outcome against the
current requirement/evidence basis and applicable authority, with durable
history, semantic effect identity and required follow-up. Every relevant
qualified source change SHALL trigger or durably arrange evaluation, including
concurrent final updates; no lost wake may leave satisfied work indefinitely
unfinished. Unproved or incomplete facts SHALL not be treated as satisfied.

Genuinely nonblocking optional work SHALL not delay fulfillment. Its existing
durable receipts/history SHALL neither disappear nor become accepted merely
because the request completes. Relevant evidence dependencies and admitted
required-item corrections remain part of the source rule. Intake/closure races
SHALL resolve at the authoritative source boundary; a local selection or transfer
is not durable admission and an uncertain result requires reconciliation.

Fulfillment SHALL retain its original actual source occurrence, trusted
attribution and exact basis. Retry, task refresh, delayed observation, policy
change or later remediation SHALL not backdate, restart or rewrite it. Ordinary
intake/correction ends under the existing closure rules. Q06–Q19 continue to
determine historical completed-access eligibility and current reading; late
source completion cannot revive expired recipient authority.

The same request context SHALL explain remaining work and the final meaningful
action's possible consequence, then show known item and request outcomes
separately. Preserve accessible inspection, focus, progress and safe recovery.
Use existing source/P12/custody/task/communication infrastructure. No new routine
confirmation, second reviewer, new notification, access grant or full Workflow
Studio prerequisite follows. Q22-C01–C08 and Q22-P01–P18 below are required parts
of this decision.

## Ownership, invariants and lifecycle

The following are semantic distinctions, not a mandatory new database enum.
The source owns the request's requirement set and outcome. Custody owns byte
safety/readiness; P12 owns current capability; task/message owners consume only
the qualified source result. Human acceptance and automatic fulfillment retain
their separate attribution. The existing
eighteen terms distinguish request, item acceptance and completed access; the
single definition below names the distinct durable fulfillment fact they refer
to. It adds no new status engine, table or permission.

| Situation                                             | Required transition or invariant                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft, absent definition, canceled/withdrawn source   | No normal fulfillment through an empty query. Preserve actual lifecycle; only eligible published/active source progression can qualify.                                          |
| Required evidence received/checking                   | Receipt is not staff acceptance or satisfaction. A required unknown/safety/acceptance condition prevents fulfillment.                                                            |
| Some satisfied conditions                             | Preserve exact satisfied basis; show actual remaining work. An independent sibling's change does not erase valid unrelated acceptance.                                           |
| Final real condition established                      | Source progression records one fulfillment when all current conditions hold; no extra ordinary finish action. Cover all relevant mutation paths and simultaneous final changes.  |
| Relevant correction/change wins first                 | Fence old basis, evaluate current requirements; stale finalization cannot rebase. Relevant restriction clearance may trigger reevaluation, never itself inventing acceptance.    |
| Fulfillment wins first                                | Ordinary intake/correction closes. Later request attempts use actual terminal source rules; no silent reopening or automatic new grant.                                          |
| Nonblocking optional work pending                     | It does not create a hidden wait. Durable receipt/history remains truthful; unrelated processing is not an approval or completion prerequisite.                                  |
| Source result known, downstream result delayed        | Preserve fulfillment and actor/F; recover projections/follow-up. If only item acceptance is known, say that while request fulfillment remains unresolved.                        |
| Actual F after participant expiry                     | Source may complete under independent authority; no ordinary completed-access revival. Proven earlier timely F recovery retains its original end/current restrictions.           |
| Later safety/requirement/adverse discovery or restore | Preserve historical occurrence while applying current restrictions and source remediation. No changed F, duplicate effect, retroactive acceptance or restored revoked admission. |

## Domain language

Add one definition; preserve all eighteen existing definitions unchanged:

**Document Request Fulfillment**:

> The requesting source's durable record that an eligible Private Document
> Request satisfied its complete requirements on a specified source and evidence
> basis at an actual source occurrence. It is distinct from submission receipt,
> individual item acceptance, generic task completion, approval of a larger
> process and permission to read the completed request.
>
> Avoid: all files uploaded, all visible items accepted, completed task, access granted
