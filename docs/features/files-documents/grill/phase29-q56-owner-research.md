## Recommended question and practical alternatives

**Should ordinary request intake keep qualified camera/photo files and already-prepared PDFs as separate supplied files, or also offer an optional in-Asym path to assemble selected page photos into one multipage document?**

Recommend **A — Keep the complete existing intake baseline, without a built-in multipage assembler.** This retains usable phone-camera/file selection, source-qualified multiple photos in an item, and upload of an already-prepared permitted PDF. It does not remove camera support, require a scanner, prohibit multipage PDFs or make mobile completion an external-integration project. Manual Add existing and other ratified source commands remain available under their own rights. This is a complete initial product boundary, not a hidden commitment to build assembly later.

**B — Add optional multipage capture/photo assembly within the same eligible ordinary-item task** is meaningful. It could help a person review page order and completeness without switching apps, locate a bad page, retake it and deliberately submit a coherent assembled result. Its extra value is convenience and composition, not permission, evidence authenticity, content safety or acceptance. The source must own the exact selected page inputs, ordered assembly, separately identified output, admission/readback and repair. No current Core source proves that complete path.

Illustration, not observed ministry research: Maya has four paper pages for one requested item. A lets her send the permitted page photos as separate current supporting files, or choose a PDF she already prepared using an appropriate device tool. B additionally keeps page collection/order/checking and the assembled-document decision within her current Asym task. A costs more switching and manual organization when one PDF matters; B costs new input/output provenance, composition and recovery behavior. Neither can decide that the pages satisfy the source's requirement.
