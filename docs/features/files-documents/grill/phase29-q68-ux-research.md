A could turn uncertainty into overly definite formal prose. B could turn into indefinite prototype work that does not answer a concrete question. Preserve explicit evidence status, affected-slice blockers and finite outputs under both.

The current bounded census has not found a further product fork after Q67; it does not prove universal completeness. Actual GF adoption, supported live cases, policy applicability, native behavior and participant availability remain unestablished.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It advances the complete accepted Phase 29 contract without replacing specialist/public owners or treating one partner task as the whole product. All independent retention, access, communication, custody and recovery meanings remain.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

The purpose is coherent everyday work and recovery, not more settings. A captures the whole accepted task now; B obtains additional interaction evidence sooner. Neither can skip eventual real intended-user/accessibility checks.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Current primary guidance supports whole-task coherence, bounded risk-focused evaluation and truthful evidence. It does not establish a universal rule that formal specifications must precede or follow every prototype. The A recommendation is a reasoned local judgment.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes, if formalization uses the actual canonical owners, incorporates their complete clauses, preserves ADR/glossary decisions and separates publication from qualification. It must not publish an alternate source of truth or promote generic skill labels into runtime evidence.
