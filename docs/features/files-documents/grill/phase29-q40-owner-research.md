## Genuine next frontier and recommendation

**When staff publish an added item or a material instruction revision to an already-issued ordinary request, should an eligible optional email normally tell the participant that the request was updated?**

Q36 and Q37 require truthful current instructions/progress when the participant returns and explicitly reserve new notification meaning/defaults. A page update does not establish outside awareness or imply that participants check Core daily. Q39 template preparation is inert and adds no trigger. This is a new source-update awareness purpose, not a repeated decision about fresh access, Request changes, clarification or cancellation.

Recommend **A — qualified update email initially On, with visible staff choice and bounded tenant control.** The benefit is awareness of changed expectations when participants are away; the real cost is extra mail from genuine successive updates and the risk of overlooked preselection. The same optional capability initially Off is a strong alternative for teams coordinating directly or making frequent incremental changes. Native-only/manual qualified contact remains the lowest additional-capability alternative. Do not describe On as universal best practice or measured Asym preference.
