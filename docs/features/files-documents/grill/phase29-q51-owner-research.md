## Recommended question and practical alternatives

**Should authorized staff be able to deliberately draw a particular qualified colleague's attention to an exact document-review comment, or should coordination stay in the existing source review/task route?**

Illustrative situation: Elena has left a Q45 comment on the exact equipment-inventory PDF supplied by Nora. She needs Amir, who is already authorized for that source comment/context, to notice the question. A should let Elena deliberately address that comment to Amir through the governed staff-attention experience; B keeps the comment unaddressed and uses the existing permitted review/task coordination to involve him. Nora does not receive either internal comment or attention cue.

It should remain optional and operable after a comment is posted; inserting a chip or typing an `@name` must never itself deliver a cue. The question should not freeze whether the interface is an explicit action, composer selection or another accessible small composition.

## Boundaries for the researched question

1. Address an exact Q45 comment/source use/version/purpose. Do not retarget old comments to the newest upload or turn a cue into a file-wide/cross-use conversation.
2. The requester needs the actual source power to request attention, and a selected colleague needs independently current qualification for the exact comment/context. Read alone, staff rank, reviewer assignment or team membership supplies neither an automatic audience nor an invitation. Preview readiness or permission to every historical byte is not a universal prerequisite for lawful comment attention.
3. Discover/select only permissible staff identities in the current Tenant/context. No global directory search, hidden-access explanation, fake Party, arbitrary email or new outside recipient. Named targeting does not expand the colleague's rights.
4. A deliberately selected recipient is actual intent. Typing text, inserting a chip, opening a picker, saving an unrelated edit or retrying a committed action is not another cue. The precise supported author/requester and finite target-cardinality rules belong in the selected-answer review, not an invented UI/parser contract now.
5. Posting and maintaining comments remain useful without a recipient selection. Failure or absence of optional attention must not erase a valid source comment or silently relabel its communication outcome as successful. The final review must give concrete durable intent/result recovery without reposting the comment to retry notification.
6. Edited, withdrawn, restored, restricted, superseded-source or unavailable comments require current source/communication usefulness and safe navigation. Do not forward stale protected bodies, revive an ended recipient projection or silently point to a different comment/version. No read receipt, reply requirement, source completion, new deadline or automatic escalation follows.
7. Use the owned staff presentation model and exact typed destination; do not build another inbox, source task, ledger or shared subscriber/follower layer. A cue is not a review assignment, role change, accepted decision or required workflow.
8. P17/P6 qualification is explicit: exact new source meaning, recipient identity, purpose, content, presentation and end rules before activation. Do not mint keys in this question or inherit Q27/Q32/Support defaults. No email-default, SMS, broadcast, new audience-switch or generic preference-center decision is bundled.
9. Any preview/name/author text remains current source-field data. A notification is not permission to copy the whole comment, file name or Q50 internal name into external channels or technical telemetry. Existing body/history/read rights and source retention still apply.
10. Preserve a real B journey through already-qualified review/task coordination, with contextual references and current access; no screenshot/email workarounds are assumed or required. Neither option makes a full future workflow, new account, administrator ceremony or forced file download necessary.
