# Phase 29 Q30 — Item questions and replies

**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

**Status:** Conrad selected Q30 A and fully ratified its complete required
amendment package on 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q30 and all twenty-two
glossary definitions are ratified grooming intent. The adopted decision and
proof obligations remain intact; Q31's answer is not inferred. No implementation,
activation or formal owner/OpenSpec publication follows.

The useful product is small: **Ask a question beside the item → send once →
staff answer in that context → continue the actual document work.** Keep internal
notes private, show truthful send status and provide a capable source route when
the in-request exchange is unavailable. No new chat platform, global inbox,
per-message task, approval ceremony or notification engine is required.

**Strongest alternatives:** B reduces unsolicited native questions but retains

## Ratified corrected decision

> Allow the currently authorized intended participant to initiate text-only
> questions about a Document Request Item and reply to deliberately shared staff
> clarification within an eligible unfinished ordinary Private Document Request.
> This includes questions before a first upload and source-qualified fresh
> unfinished access. An accepted item may remain discussable when its source
> permits it; that grants no correction, unacceptance or fulfillment power.
> Preserve actual source restrictions without adding a new tenant mode menu,
> staff-invitation requirement, permission engine or general chat product.
>
> The requesting source owns eligibility, intended participant, item/purpose,
> relevant instruction/version context, participant-visible text and accountable
> staff handling. Use trusted actor/tenant/time and current source authority for
> admission, retrieval and recovery. Reading a file, an assignment, an email
> match or a mention is not clarification authority. Staff replies are new
> deliberate outward acts; internal notes and their drafts cannot become shared
> by toggling visibility or reusing a task thread.
>
> Provide enough currently authorized shared-text context to understand the
> exchange, without exposing internal review history, another recipient, another
> source use or superseded file contents. Keep each sent message's actual item,
> author, occurrence and intended basis stable. Corrections use a new permitted
> follow-up; ordinary sent-message edit/delete/unsend is not selected. Governed
> restriction, redaction and disposition remain available under their owners,
> with minimal lawful accountability rather than indefinite raw-text retention.
>
> Clarification is information for the source, not a file submission, correction,
> formal Request changes decision, waiver, evidence acceptance or fulfillment.
> Newly admitted relevant text must not be silently omitted by an older prepared
> human source decision. Order admission and affected uncommitted decisions
> through the current source boundary; present new context for deliberate
> revalidation without silent rebasing. No reply, read receipt, resolved flag,
> repeated file review or new final approval is required merely because text
> arrived. Unrelated work remains independent.
>
> A committed human decision is not undone by a later message. If final human
> work is already committed and Q22 fulfillment is merely deferred, clarification
> remains visible for owned follow-through; it does not invent another completion
> predicate or manual gate. Actual qualified source changes still govern Q22.
> Closure-first denies new ordinary clarification admission; send-first preserves
> the admitted message and accountable handling even if recipient access ends.
>
> A received acknowledgment requires one durable source message with exact
> attribution/context and a durable route into authorized existing source work.
> Required co-located facts/history/handoff commit atomically; secondary views
> recover from durable intent. Known success survives a failed refresh. Recover
> the same deliberate send after lost responses without duplicating text or work;
> changed material conflicts, and replay disclosure requires current authority.
> Do not deduplicate genuinely new messages merely because their text matches.
>
> Keep the flow contextual and optional: Ask a question, a clearly labeled text
> field, deliberate Send, truthful persistent status and safe return. Enter
> preserves normal multiline and input-method behavior; confirming an IME
> composition never submits the message. Preserve allowed
> drafts, reading position and focus through recoverable failures; do not copy
> drafts between audiences, automatically resend after reconnect or add private
> offline storage. Qualify mobile, keyboard, assistive technology, international
> text and slow-network use without a mandatory third pane or new account form.
>
> Existing access ends and source restrictions apply to new sends and disclosure.
> Activity does not extend access, reopen a request or grant completed-request
> conversation history. Staff can handle admitted work under independent current
> authority, but cannot claim a late reply is available to an expired participant.
> Use the existing capable source help/recovery route when native participation
> is unavailable. Fresh access never silently replays an old unsent message.
>
> Reuse source/P12/P9 and P6/P17 boundaries. New clarification does not inherit
> Q26/Q27 email events/defaults or select email ingestion, bells, reminders,
> typing/read receipts, attachments or arbitrary recipients. Native work must
> remain visible with external email off. Require actual-role database and
> privileged-path proof, finite measured resource/recovery budgets, migration/
> restore safeguards and real recipient/staff journeys before activation.

## Ownership and lifecycle invariants

The requesting source owns clarification meaning, eligible audience, exact item
context, current review relevance and disposition. P12 owns current authority;
P9 source work projects accountable handling; P17/P6 own any separately authorized
outward communication. File custody does not own this conversation. The client,
task status, provider response and read model are not alternate writers.

Required invariants: one deliberate send yields at most one admitted occurrence;
its exact actor/context/meaning remains attributable; current authority governs
each disclosure; internal content cannot change audience by convenience; a
received message has recoverable accountable handling; sent text does not change
bytes or decisions by itself; lifecycle closure cannot erase admitted work or
grant a new access period. Retention is owner-governed, not forever.

| State or ordering                                                    | Required meaning and outcome                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eligible unfinished item before upload                               | A can offer a question without manufacturing a file/version or asking staff to invite it.                                                                  |

| Draft                                                                | Local permitted user input, not source receipt, evidence or a new review dependency.                                                                       |
| Sending / commitment unknown                                         | Reconcile the same intended send; do not label definitely failed or send a second copy blindly.                                                            |

| Durably received                                                     | Exact message and required source-work obligation exist; no human-read/answer promise.                                                                     |
| Message precedes affected pending human decision                     | Older context cannot silently commit; refresh relevant information and permit deliberate current decision without reply/resolution ceremony.               |
