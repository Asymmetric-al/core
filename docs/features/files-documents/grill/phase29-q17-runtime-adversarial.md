## Seven exact safeguards

**R01 — Fix the source result, not a guessed formula.** A successful new fresh completed-read source issuance resolves and preserves its finite end under the subsequently adopted complete source calculation policy and applicable limits. The same end governs unused access. Preserve the qualified occurrence, exact effect/recipient/baseline/scope, timing policy and sufficient calculation/limit provenance. This does not choose a duration, unit, elapsed/calendar formula, arbitrary staff date picker, future scheduling or new timing subsystem. Missing complete policy still prevents issuance.

**R02 — Keep reviewed intent and generated outputs distinct.** The meaningful staff action binds exact source/recipient/completed baseline, reviewed member/action scope and timing terms. The source's actual occurrence/end are original effect outputs. A same-intent retry must reconcile the existing committed outcome before any new calculation; never include a newly generated “now/end” as if it were unchanged reviewed input. Materially different payload conflicts or follows the existing refreshed review; the system must not invent another intent automatically. Recovering an old effect is not issuing a new one, even when its period has already ended.

**R03 — Qualify current source time and state after waits.** Source/P12 checks must cover the relevant current issuer, recipient, purpose, scope, restrictions/disposition and adopted grant-history policy at the owned issuance boundary. Distinguish preliminary approval, queue/credential/provider/client time from the qualified source occurrence whose effect durably commits. A database timestamp read is not the physical commit instant. Boundary-spanning transactions, rollback, serialization retry and unknown commit require the existing source occurrence/ordering qualification; no universal SQL function is prescribed. A late response may report a historical successful issuance whose remaining usability is now shorter or ended; it cannot restart the period.

**R04 — A fixed end is a ceiling, not universal usability.** The prior completed F/end and earlier grants remain unchanged; their ordinary elapsed end does not itself cap a new Q08 grant. Genuine currently applicable source limits keep their actual scope. A member/action-specific earlier end cannot silently become the whole request's end, and a grant-wide end cannot promise every member remains usable until then. Proof/session, rendition and delivery failures do not pause or lengthen the clock. Preserve genuine binding shortening/adverse history; qualified temporary repair does not create or widen authority. There is no minimum usable interval guaranteed by A.

**R05 — Enforce historical integrity and the live boundary.** Tenant/source/recipient/baseline/effect links and original occurrence/end/policy meaning must remain trusted across INSERT/UPDATE/upsert/RPC/job/restore, including parent changes and transformed rows. Use actual effective privileges, constraints and source-owned mutation/serving checks; ordinary RLS is neither privileged immutability nor current authorization on a service bypass. A timestamp column alone does not prove finite, valid expiry. At every protected metadata/result/byte operation, evaluate current required authority and expiry with supported precision; no cache, stale projection, long credential or punctual worker substitutes. Q04 ongoing streams and Q05/Q16 partial/status-only behavior remain.

**R06 — Coherent evidence, uncertainty and delivery.** Required source effect/audit/follow-up intent must remain durable under the existing owner contract. General best-effort logging and dispatch records do not prove issuance. Lost responses, failed rendering/notification and client closure cannot turn an issued period into “not yet started,” cancellation or a replacement period. Providers remain outside source locks. Unknown commitment requires source reconciliation and current disclosure before private replay; it does not justify duplicate issuance or deletion of potentially admitted bytes. A recovered success cannot overwrite a newer current restriction in the displayed result.

**R07 — Reuse and rollout only within the selected lane.** Reuse the intended Q08 command, P12 boundary and shared UI; no entry activation state is required. Keep expiry enforcement independent of browser/process timers or cleanup workers; compatible bounded durable follow-up may remain where the owner already requires it. Preserve original terms/adverse outcomes through prospective migration, mixed code and restore without backfilling unknown ends from current policy. Repetition/cumulative/overlap, exact numerical/calculation profile and composed native qualification remain gates. No expired grant, source completion, correction lineage, retention/hold or independently authorized source work is reset.

## Technical semantics checked against primary documentation

- NOW/CURRENT_TIMESTAMP/transaction_timestamp use transaction-start time; statement_timestamp is statement-start and clock_timestamp changes during execution. A NOW check after FOR UPDATE can still use a pre-wait instant. Currentness and source occurrence need explicit qualification, not a function-name claim. [PostgreSQL 17 date/time functions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/functions-datetime.html)
- PostgreSQL timestamp/date types support infinity values. Required finite terms must reject unsupported/nonfinite/invalid state through the chosen schema/command contract, including direct privileged paths; NOT NULL alone does not establish finite expiry. Supported precision and exact boundary behavior still need qualification after the full profile is selected. [PostgreSQL 17 date/time types](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/datatype-datetime.html)
- ALL/UPDATE may inherit USING as WITH CHECK when omitted; omission is not automatically a vulnerability. Applicable permissive policies OR and restrictive policies AND; BEFORE triggers can transform the resulting row before checking. Review actual allowed transformations and command/role grants. [PostgreSQL CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html)
- Definer code uses its owner's authority; actual bypass depends on owner/role privileges. Table owners normally bypass unless FORCE applies, while BYPASSRLS still bypasses. Service-key initialization without a user token is a privileged path; current auth must be enforced at its owned boundary. [PostgreSQL row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html), [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security)
- Row locks protect retrieved rows, not absent future effects. READ COMMITTED rechecks do not atomically certify arbitrary cross-row authorization. ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING can lose to a concurrent row outside the statement snapshot; missing returned data is not proof of no effect. Qualify source invariants with stable semantic retry, without provider calls or new identities inside transaction retry. [PostgreSQL locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/explicit-locking.html), [isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html)

These references were checked in the current review session; PostgreSQL 17 is the cited documentation version, not a claimed hosted version. The local skill's shorthand about missing WITH CHECK and universal definer bypass is not used. No native DB roles, applied SQL or provider behavior was tested.

Include absent-row races, source limits reached during wait, rollback and committed-but-unacknowledged outcomes. A per-key lock or mocked ordering is not proof.
4. **Finite clock and serialization boundary:** selected precision/range, invalid/nonfinite/missing values, before/at/after cutoff, clock uncertainty and boundary-spanning transactions have the source-qualified result. Distinguish source occurrence from transaction-start and physical commit time. Late observer/recovery/restore preserves the original result; it cannot repair by stamping a new now.
5. **Scope-specific earlier limits:** genuine grant-wide tightening versus member/action-only limits, expired predecessor, short proof/session, provider TTL and temporary rendition failure yield the correct scoped outcome. Independent permitted subsets remain available when authorized. Removing an adverse cause cannot silently restore a longer end; no `min(all dates)` or stale success fallback.
6. **Real roles and transformations:** direct/RPC/view/definer/service/job paths cannot forge actor/recipient/baseline, mutate original occurrence/end or bypass exact source checks. Test allowed updates/upserts/parent retarget/delete behavior with actual effective policies and grants. Source invariant and privilege tests are required, not SQL string matching alone.
7. **Serving/operation failure:** every private metadata/history/count/result replay/HEAD/range/resume/full stream path respects current scope/expiry; Q04 active delivery remains qualified. Cache invalidation, worker or provider outage cannot prolong source rights. Required evidence failures do not falsely succeed; unknown completion does not trigger deletion or a fresh grant.
8. **Prospective rollout and bounded recovery:** legacy/mixed versions/restore preserve original identities/ends/policies and later restrictions without first-use activation, inferred historical values or outward replay. Exact indexed lookup and bounded existing source/P12 budgets suffice; no full-history reconstruction or timer per grant is required. Missing numerical and repeat policy visibly blocks activation.
