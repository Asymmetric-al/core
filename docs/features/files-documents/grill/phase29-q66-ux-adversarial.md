## Recommendation and material changes to the earlier question

Accept A with required amendments. Use the identified GF drafts as the worked mapping example, and provide an actual tenant-authorized ordinary-policy lifecycle: **Create policy**, optional **Copy to draft**, **Save draft**, **Review changes**, **Activate version**, **Apply to existing records**, **Correct policy assignment**, **Pause automatic disposal**, **Retire**, and qualified recovery. These are contextual actions in one source-owned policy area, not separate products or a mandatory sequence for every record. Ordinary staff retain Q62's meaningful approved summary and normal Issue action; they do not complete a policy form or certify law.

The explicit user requirement now selects capability beyond the original evidence-selection question. Q62's statements that no general policy editor was selected must be narrowly amended to allow this source-owned tenant lifecycle. Do not silently preserve an Asym-only preset catalog, require support/developers for ordinary supported changes, or treat the new requirement as permission to rewrite receipt dates, release holds, reveal bodies or delete everything. The tenant's properly delegated policy owner can establish and approve supported rules where its governance permits. A document link, generic admin title or draft-edit permission alone cannot confer approval power. No routine additional approver, external legal sign-off or reason essay is imposed unless the actual source requires it.

The strongest simpler alternative is a catalog of centrally maintained approved profiles with tenant selection. It reduces policy-authoring complexity and can suit a tightly regulated fixed cohort, but it does not satisfy this user's explicit requirement if normal changes require Asym intervention. The opposite alternative—an unrestricted rule builder—is flexible in syntax but creates ambiguity, unsafe expressions and a new governance product. The recommended middle is tenant-owned values and versions over explicit source-supported classes, scopes, typed triggers and consequences, with no arbitrary SQL, scripts, classification AI or generic condition graph. A genuinely new business event or specialist authority still needs its actual owner; a supported value change must not be disguised as such an extension merely to keep tenants dependent on engineering.

## Small complete interaction

The following is an intended UX recommendation, not an implemented screen. Use existing Core navigation and components. A policy-owner enters the tenant's source Records/Policies context, sees the policy's safe name, purpose, active version/status and whether any existing-record work needs attention. Do not display all internal policy controls to normal issuers or participants.

1. **Create or edit.** Create policy starts a blank draft; Copy to draft is optional. An incomplete but lawfully retained draft may be saved. Edit active policy prepares a successor draft; the active facts remain unchanged. A compact form groups relevant meaning: applicable ordinary purpose/classes, typed start and precision, permitted durations/limits and end handling, approval/applicability basis, and permitted scope/default/choice behavior. Only applicable fields are shown. No unknown duration becomes zero/forever, and no blank hold list proves no holds.
2. **Review and activate.** Present readable before/after consequences, including changed source scope, dates/units, execution mode and whether existing records need action. Change links return to preserved input. One deliberate Activate version action suffices when the actor can approve/activate and the source permits; actual required separation of duties still applies. A generic admin switch is not approval. Activation controls eligible future application within its reviewed scope, not all tenants or all old evidence.
3. **Existing records.** Apply to existing records selects a finite source-qualified scope and shows truthful impact: unchanged, affected, blocked/unknown or pending reconciliation, including current hold/conflict exclusions without leaking private reasons. This is not a hidden include-all checkbox. Existing legally applicable changes cannot wait behind optional UI: Q62 current restrictive pending enforcement and capable reconciliation must already prevent stale permissive consequences. The operator cannot simply opt out of an applicable duty.
4. **Routine work.** The issuer sees Q62's safe policy summary and any actual required choice. A valid chosen alternative is retained when a default changes. Access ends, Requested by and records rules remain distinct. Earlier staging/preparation still needs its own admitted purpose; a later policy cannot legalize it retrospectively.
5. **Control and recovery.** Pause automatic disposal acts on the exact policy/execution scope without validating an unrelated draft; it stops new preventable destructive admissions, not committed history or possibly-started effects. Retire stops new assignment, not preservation or all existing disposal. Reactivate is a fresh authorized act for the same immutable still-valid version, or a corrected successor is activated when meaning changed. Wrong assigned records use Q62 Correct policy assignment. Wrong policy meaning uses a corrected version and required impact; no history overwrite or magical Undo.

## Exact required UX amendments

**U01 — Real tenant flexibility and narrow scope.** Provide the tenant's actually authorized ordinary-policy owners with usable native create-from-blank, optional permitted copy, draft editing, version review/activation, scoped existing-record application, correction, containment, retirement and recovery. Ordinary supported changes SHALL NOT require Asym-only presets, production SQL, support/developer intervention or an external vendor account. Amend only Q62/Q63's prior non-selection of policy authoring; preserve their safety/source invariants and specialist owners. No generic DSL, automatic classifier or new legal authority is created.

**U02 — Separate capabilities and applicable governance.** Derive current actor/Tenant/environment/source rights from actual trusted context and P12. Definition, draft save, approval, activation, default/choice administration, impact application, correction, pause, retirement and viewing records are distinct capabilities, though one authorized person may hold several. Current tenant delegation may supply approval where governance permits; do not invent a compulsory second approver or outside lawyer. Generic staff/admin labels, supplied IDs, old role claims, document authorship and connected Drive access are not authority. Preserve supported no-Party actors without inventing identity records.

**U03 — Optional lawful examples and blank authoring.** Offer blank authoring equally to optional copy of permitted structured content. Example copy creates a new draft and bounded provenance, not approvals, memberships, defaults, assignments, event facts, holds, dispositions or legal conclusions. GF private material is not discoverable by other tenants merely because it is the first example. Later external edits never silently sync an active rule. Unsupported/inapplicable content remains a precise draft gap with a capable owner, not a hard-coded forever rule.

**U04 — Typed editable meaning with minimal form.** Let owners edit supported actual purpose/classes/scope, typed start event and precision/calendar semantics, applicable preservation/privacy values and units, end handling, permitted choices/defaults and approval/effectivity basis. No universal mandatory interval is introduced. Distinguish not applicable, known absent, not yet triggered, unresolved and check failed. Values must fit actual current authoritative obligations; no longest/shortest/first/forever guess. Do not force machine IDs or arbitrary expressions into the form. If a valid real case cannot be expressed by the qualified profile, preserve the draft and identify the actual source capability gap rather than silently reinterpret it.

**U05 — Safe draft editing and concurrent work.** Save draft is distinct from activation. Lawfully saved incomplete work remains editable; transient local input is not represented as durably saved. Active rule/history are not overwritten by keystrokes, blur, copied content or draft discard. Current expected-basis checks prevent another editor's draft/version from being lost; present a concise difference and preserve permitted input. No mandatory collaborative editor or branching UI is required. Discard affects only the exact authorized draft under its lawful retention, not active/in-use versions or history.

**U06 — Meaningful review without routine ceremony.** Before consequential activation/application/correction show safe current/proposed meaning, actual scope, changed trigger/units/bounds/mode and existing-record consequences. Preserve input and focus through Change and return. One deliberate final action is enough where current source governance allows; no blanket confirmation modal, checkbox, essay or second approval for ordinary draft save. A missing minimum safe consequence summary blocks that consequential action with a capable remedy, not protected disclosure or blind consent.

**U07 — Activation, prospective binding and required impact.** Coherently record the exact activated immutable version, reviewed applicable scope/default/choice effect, current approval basis, trusted actor/time and original result. Default behavior is prospective only where that actual policy permits; activating a version is not proof every existing record changed. A successor that applies to existing obligations must establish the actual current governing result or restrictive pending impact state before stale permissive consequences can proceed. Do not make legally required impact optional or require body-read rights merely to authorize policy management. Multiple compatible policies/variants may coexist under their source; no one-policy-per-tenant rule or arbitrary collision winner.

**U08 — Explicit bounded existing-record application.** The source-authorized owner can review and apply the current version to an exact finite eligible retained scope, using sufficient permitted metadata and current classification/trigger facts. Scope is never all data by default, a guessed date range, live unbounded search or a silent subset. Changes between preview and commit require current validation and honest conflict/review; no stale selection silently broadens. Durable work reports admitted, applied, unchanged, blocked/unknown and physical follow-through separately. A wholly known ineligible group is not a verified complete empty result when required coverage is unknown. Required impact may be bounded durable continuation, not one giant transaction or a manual per-file ritual.

**U09 — Rules change; historical facts do not.** Preserve original policy/version/binding/trigger/receipt/actor evidence while recording current applicability and later effects. A new definition never backdates an event, resets a period to edit time, cancels access, transfers purpose, restores acceptance or releases a hold. Q62 Correct policy assignment repairs a wrong admitted binding with actual source authority and safe before/after consequences. Wrong policy meaning is corrected through a fresh qualified version plus actual impact. A wrong correction is corrected with a new attributed act, not deleting old evidence or reverting current independent blockers.

**U10 — Effective pause independent of drafts.** Provide **Pause automatic disposal** at the actual supported policy/execution scope under source-authorized operational containment. It must work independently of unrelated invalid/unsaved draft fields, preserve that lawful work, and fence new automatic destructive admissions across rescans/keys/workers. It does not release preservation, grant a manual bypass, stop every already admitted provider effect or authorize indefinite privacy-bound extension. Stop/resolution affects its exact owned cause; independent holds/stops remain. Resolution is a fresh current decision permitting reconsideration only where policy allows, not old-attempt revival or catch-up purge.

**U11 — Retirement and mistake recovery.** Retire stops new assignments within the reviewed scope and records actor/time; it does not delete an in-use version, terminate its existing obligations or imply that all existing automatic work stopped. Explain independent containment and required impact when current meaning is unsafe. Accidental retirement can be repaired by an explicit fresh **Reactivate** of the same immutable version if current approval/applicability and competing binding/default basis still qualify, or by activating a corrected successor. Do not force a duplicate version solely because of retirement, erase the retirement, automatically reassign old records or revive old disposal intents.

**U12 — Honest outcomes and original-operation recovery.** Every consequential command binds exact reviewed semantic intent and expected basis to its original result. Matching retry resolves that original under current disclosure before new eligibility; mismatch conflicts, unknown remains unknown, and zero affected rows is not automatic success. No-op requires current authority and complete effect equality, not equal names or counters; it cannot create fake approval/history, reset dates or consume a later real change. A committed activation may now be superseded/retired/paused; show both truths safely. Offline clients do not queue consequential writes, and restored clients never auto-activate saved intent.

**U13 — Actual custody, irreversible limits and competent repair.** Existing Q63 final-admission, writer/reference/hold fences, bounded permits, original possibly-started attempts and per-copy reconciliation remain. Policy edit/pause/retirement is not physical restoration or all-copy deletion. Keep independently needed shared bytes and class/copy obligations; no unsafe use from a historical policy badge. Before activation qualify the actual tenant source/records routes for wrong rules, wrong assignments, hold conflicts, failed impact and loss/incident repair. A powerless Contact support label or SQL instruction does not satisfy this. Missing source semantics may need source-owner qualification; ordinary authorized repairs do not.

**U14 — Frontline and participant restraint.** Keep Q62's normal approved summary, current genuine required choice and one Issue action. A change of default preserves a still-valid deliberate choice. Show material changed meaning even when names match, but do not force new review for irrelevant changes or harmless identical effective refresh. Earlier draft/allocation/staging/processing needs its own admitted policy; final activation cannot retrospectively legalize it. Participants see only independently authorized understandable purpose/access information, no internal policy controls, new consent duty, raw reason, legal questionnaire, new notice or reupload demand.

**U15 — Private current projections and context changes.** Use existing shared static field classification/subtract-only projections for names, policy text, source links, counts, impact, hold causes, histories and results. Rights to policy definition/impact are distinct from document bodies and other-source transcripts. Recheck current membership/context/field authority on all reads and result recovery; discard late responses and unsafe caches on actor/Tenant/environment/source switch. No sensitive URL/query/analytics/general logs or persistent device draft by convenience. Independently lawful draft/history storage has actual source policy, not browser-local forever retention.

**U16 — Accessible Base UI interaction.** Retain Core Base UI/base-maia and existing tokens; no replacement component stack. Use labeled fields, contextual errors/Change names, keyboard and touch parity, 320 CSS pixel ordinary reflow, zoom, RTL/long labels, actual localized date/number/unit semantics, IME and weak-network flows. Focus stays visible and unobscured; meaningful save/pending/conflict/impact/result messages are programmatically exposed without focus steal or per-row chatter. Passive change/blur/navigation/prefetch and IME confirmation cannot activate/apply/pause/retire/correct; deliberate focused-button Enter/Space/click/tap remains operable. GET/render are inert, and actual authenticated mutations retain request-integrity/CSRF protections.

**U17 — Finite tenant operation and measurable qualification.** Reuse source policy/version/admission, custody and actual P9 operational routes. Qualify bounded option/draft/history/impact pages, current per-page access, stable deterministic paging with a unique tie-breaker, set-wise validation, bounded impact/retry/cleanup and tenant fairness. No all-history scan on typing, N+1 per record, speculative global preview, infinite retry or unlimited control retention. No guessed legal period, latency SLA, record count or recovery grace is selected. Native race/fault/access/copy proof and intended-owner/frontline tests are required before supported cohort activation; dashboards cannot substitute.

**U18 — Stage, adoption and trace.** The expanded Q66 decision must explicitly adopt tenant policy authoring and amend the narrow prior non-selection statements while preserving Q01–Q65 substance. Do not declare GF adopted, copy private policy bodies/numeric rules into global presets, infer actual customer use or collapse existing specialist owners. No new domain term is necessary merely for familiar policy/draft/version/action language. Root may select a concise term only if an actual unresolved business distinction warrants it. Formal source/OpenSpec/ticket publication, implementation and real release proof remain separate. This note does not close the future whole-root-package review.

## Seven founder checks

### What could go wrong with this answer?

“Fully flexible” could become arbitrary overwrite, a private GF document could become an approved global template, or one Save could silently rewrite old evidence/disposal. The required tenant lifecycle supplies real choice while separating rule versions, approval, application, facts and physical effects. Serious risks are governed by current source commands and proof, not warnings alone.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That supported ordinary tenant policy owners can be identified and delegated, that their actual obligations can be expressed by the source profile, and that custody/current-impact enforcement can honor those rules. Those remain explicit qualification facts. No actual GF use of GEN05/BP11, approval, legal duration, staffing level or desired automatic mode is inferred.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It expressly adds tenant-managed ordinary policy authoring and revises the prior non-selection limit. Q62 application/correction and Q63 disposal remain the enforcement contracts. Collection, partner reference, messages, public Media, official finance, resources, Support, imports and other specialist purposes are not merged into one tenant timer or generic editor authority.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Policy owners get actual self-service and recoverable versions; ordinary staff retain one meaningful summary and their normal action. A change in policy is reviewed once at the right owner/scope, not re-certified by every uploader. Invalid drafts do not block emergency containment. Participants receive only their purpose-qualified explanation and no new legal task.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Current primary products demonstrate tenant controls, version comparison and distinct application/retirement behavior, but also materially different propagation and lock-in. The recommendation is a source-grounded design judgment: keep explicit consequential intent and useful recovery, use proven accessible interaction patterns, and test real roles. It is not a universal vendor consensus or empirical Core result.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

It fits the explicit user requirement when adopted as a narrow source-owned extension and implemented through current Core Base UI/P12/source/custody boundaries. No existing native policy editor or fully qualified adapter is asserted. Real implementation must close the actual policy/legacy/consumer/runtime gaps, not reuse a public upload, generic metadata field or provider rule as authority.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Accept A with the user-required tenant lifecycle and these exact amendments. Keep GF only as the optional private worked example; enable blank tenant authoring and actual normal changes without support/developer dependence. Use prospective activation where lawful, explicit existing impact, independent pause, recoverable retirement and fresh corrective versions. Avoid adding a general expression engine, extra approver, mandatory policy wizard for issuers or notifications.
