## Main conclusion

Keep A. A mistaken duplicate request or an ended ordinary collection purpose needs a truthful whole-request outcome. Cancellation must be a deliberate decision of the requesting source, distinct from fulfillment, item No longer required, access expiry, evidence rejection and deletion. It may apply after partial receipt or acceptance while the aggregate remains unfinished. A task status or stopped Workflow run cannot supply it.

Three amendments are decisive. First, order cancellation against actual durable Q22 fulfillment, not the last review click, a true-looking browser count or a pending evaluator. Second, make terminal disclosure concrete: cancellation narrows a then-current finite unfinished participant admission to minimal Canceled plus safe help, without a new grant or period; unavailable/expired/unknown authority receives a neutral response. Third, mistakes have a lasting source-owned remedy, but terminal cancellation is not reversed through Q33's unfinished-item correction. Any genuinely new request requires independently authorized normal original issuance and its own current scope/readiness.

These are proposed amendments to the selected answer, not claims of present runtime support. The exact source/P12 admission, narrowed outcome-only projection and terminal remedy must be adopted before implementation and qualified before activation. No separate cancellation platform, generic reopen, new email purpose or full Studio dependency is needed.

## Proposed exact corrected decision

> Permit currently authorized source staff to deliberately cancel an issued, eligible unfinished ordinary Private Document Request when it was issued in error or its legitimate collection purpose has ended. Record one source-owned Document Request Cancellation with exact request and relevant basis, trusted attribution and actual occurrence. Partial receipt or accepted items do not by themselves prevent aggregate cancellation. Preserve those facts, required history and independent duties; cancellation is not fulfillment, item acceptance, No longer required, deletion, participant refusal or a larger specialist outcome.
>
> Qualify the actual cancellation capability independently of review, assignment, generic staff/admin/edit and recipient-grant powers. Order it with current source/authority changes, evidence and relevant Q30 clarification, and actual durable Q22 fulfillment. Cancellation first ends this request's ordinary intake/correction and clarification and prevents a stale deferred fulfillment; actual fulfillment first makes this cancellation unavailable. A final review click or pending evaluator is not fulfillment. Newly relevant text requires deliberate revalidation only of affected uncommitted human decisions, with no read/reply/Resolve gate.
>
> Cancellation stops ordinary participant file/history/transcript serving through this request. As an explicit narrow Q34 amendment, an independently valid finite unfinished admission current at cancellation may retain only minimal Canceled status and safe source help for its same exact intended recipient, existing end and every earlier cap. This includes an already qualified fresh unfinished admission. No current admission means no new status allowance. Current Q01 proof may be reused or re-established only for that exact existing admission; cancellation, login, help and retry cannot create or renew one. No cancellation reason, file list, transcript or completed-reading window follows. Independent revocation, changed recipient/source restriction, expiry or unresolved authority denies protected status and gives the safe neutral unavailable/help response.
>
> A mistaken cancellation has a lasting contextual source-remediation route. Preserve cancellation and record the mistake with accountable follow-through. Do not reverse the terminal request, restore an old snapshot, or borrow Q33's unfinished-item inverse. If ordinary evidence remains legitimately needed, an authorized operator may separately review and issue a genuinely new request only through independently qualified normal original issuance, linked to the mistake and subject to Q25 and Q01–Q19. Preserve independently permitted Q01 proof reuse and Q28 exact evidence reuse without copying grants, deadlines, acceptance or message intent. Qualify this or another effective existing source remedy before activating the affected journey; a contact label with no capable action is insufficient.
>
> Commit cancellation, mandatory history and durable follow-through coherently. Recover the same original effect after response loss under current disclosure and show later current state separately. End only work whose obligation depends on the canceled collection; already admitted evidence/messages, independent safety/records duties and lawful staff handling remain under their owners. Use the existing accessible request context and a clear deliberate action with its consequence. No automatic cancellation notice, blanket stop of independently valid staff mail, new access period, generic reopen/reissue, bulk action or whole-Studio dependency is selected.
