## Findings and exact narrow amendments

Severity/likelihood below describe consequences if an obligation is omitted; they are qualitative judgments, not measured incidents. C01–C06 are proposed incremental clauses, not another full authorization contract.

### C01 — Qualify the relevant predecessor and actual ending cause

**Risk: critical / medium.** Checking only that the original grant expired permits another admission while a later fresh grant remains valid, unknown or adversely ended. Checking only `now >= previous end` can classify a coincident purpose/overall restriction as harmless ordinary expiry. An expired summary flag does not establish why authority ended.

**Exact amendment:** For a subsequent standard fresh admission, retain proof of the original lawful admission and original purpose/requirement lineage, and prove the relevant preceding fresh admission's eligible ordinary unfinished expiry together with current source/lineage state. Resolve existing same-effect and materially relevant unknown outcomes first. A valid newer admission, source completion, cancellation, revocation, genuine binding shortening, purpose end or independent prohibition MUST NOT be bypassed by choosing an older expired row, waiting until another date passes or removing an adverse cause. Classify the ending from qualified source facts, including applicable coincident restrictions; apply every restriction at its real scope. Missing proof is unresolved eligibility, not fabricated expiry or a permanent business rejection.

This explicitly specializes Q13-C02/P01's original-expiry wording for later admissions. It does not erase original proof or create a source-administration power.

**Proof:** Original ordinary expiry followed by live/unknown/revoked/shortened fresh authority cannot issue another. A genuine independent purpose end exactly equal to nominal day30 remains effective. A temporary file/proof outage is neither a new ordinary expiry nor permanent revocation. A qualified normally expired prior period with current genuine unfinished purpose can proceed.

### C02 — Enforce nonoverlap at the correct lineage and preserve deliberate intent

**Risk: critical / medium.** Two staff actors can approve against the same old state; a losing attempt can later wake and become an unreviewed successor. An overly broad nonoverlap rule can block other recipients or legitimate independent source work. A cooldown added merely to avoid equality races changes the selected policy.

**Exact amendment:** At the existing source/P12 mutation boundary, permit no overlapping unfinished fresh admission in the same tenant, request, intended-recipient and original-admission lineage, including differently narrowed subsets in that lineage. Revalidate current issuer, recipient revision, source state, relevant admission history and source time under the owners' required fences. Same deliberate intent recovers its immutable result; a distinct intent must satisfy current nonoverlap and review. A stale unadmitted intent bound to an earlier predecessor MUST NOT wait through an intervening admission and silently become its successor. Materially changed lineage requires current review; ordinary passage of time under unchanged reviewed terms does not itself require another confirmation. No preapproved future issuance, automatic renewal, pre-expiry extension or new cooldown follows.

An actual new source effect may qualify at the previous exclusive end when causal order and all predicates prove nonoverlap. No artificial timestamp gap is required. This is a semantic invariant, not a prescribed new lock, table, sequence or global uniqueness rule. Other participants and independently admitted purposes retain their existing rights.

**Proof:** Two distinct racing approvals yield at most one currently admitted unfinished effect for this lineage; the other does not become a future grant. Same-intent replay yields one original result. Before/at/after-end cases use qualified occurrence/order; equal displayed timestamps alone prove neither overlap nor permission. Independent recipient lineages still operate. A current but temporarily unusable grant cannot be replaced to bypass its end or restrictions.

### C03 — Preserve the original ceiling and current purpose without creating a lifetime quota

**Risk: high / medium.** Repeated approvals can accumulate historical scopes, revive accepted writes or turn an abandoned request into indefinite file storage. Conversely, implementing the latest narrow grant as the permanent ceiling can wrongly prevent independently eligible original work. “No cap” can be mistaken for immunity from source constraints or operational controls.

**Exact amendment:** Each admission requires a new currently authorized decision for the same genuinely continuing original purpose. Its reviewed requirement/actions are bounded by original admitted lineage and current eligibility; declare current read, pending submission/correction and completed-read scopes independently under Q13. Do not union expired grants or automatically carry prior rights forward. A deliberately narrower previous grant alone is not a new permanent global denial; actual binding narrower restrictions remain effective. No new requirements, recipients, purposes, accepted/frozen writes or historical-byte rights follow. The standard lane has no preset lifetime count or cumulative-day ceiling, while genuine source-specific limits and existing operational safety controls remain binding. Request flags, a free-text explanation, an unchanged folder or a fresh internal ID do not prove purpose or reset lineage.

Review-only unfinished work may qualify without a new upload or manufactured requirement. Every period need not produce measurable progress, but every approval needs genuine current source purpose and authority. Do not add an AI relevance score, inactivity auto-close, universal mandatory essay, quota editor or arbitrary renewal count to solve this concern. Cloning the same purpose to evade restrictions remains prohibited.

**Proof:** An original A/B requirement set narrowed to A in a prior grant can later consider still-eligible B through a new valid review, without restoring any real adverse B restriction or importing unrelated C. Accepted own-file reading remains distinct from accepted-item writes. A long legitimate sequence does not fail merely because of an invented numerical quota; a purpose-ended source fails regardless of sequence length. Existing scoped safety budgets do not become hidden lifetime quotas.

### C04 — Keep every period and completion on its original causal facts

**Risk: critical / medium.** A repeated admission might anchor from the prior end, now-at-retry, initial issuance or an old stale review; completion during an access gap might acquire a new completed reading period. A new grant could silently rebind an old upload, invalidate accepted evidence or restart retention.

**Exact amendment:** Every newly admitted period uses Q14 unchanged: nominal unfinished end at its own qualified fresh source occurrence G plus720 elapsed hours, with a durably resolved actual end and genuine scoped limits; unused access has that same end. Prior grants, gaps and receipts remain unchanged. Apply Q13's completion rule without a new repeat-completion path: actual eligible normal fulfillment during that admission can admit only its declared current completed-read scope under Q07's fulfillment-anchored bound. Completion first or during a gap creates no retrospective recipient admission; recover genuine prior timely durable outcomes using their original evidence and current checks. A lawful atomic fresh admission then fulfillment can still qualify, based on causal eligibility rather than timestamp inequality. Source work and retained custody continue only under their independent authority. New grants do not reset retention, holds, acceptance, due-target history or old-operation identity.

**Proof:** A second grant issued five days after the prior ordinary expiry starts from its new G; the five-day gap remains unauthorized. Completion in that gap cannot be repaired by issuing the second grant. A timely prior completion observed late recovers its earlier completed view instead. Atomic new admission→fulfillment can qualify; reversed order cannot. An old unadmitted upload callback cannot borrow the new authority; truthful qualified retained-byte reuse still follows Q13. Q08's original completed-view eligibility is not expanded by any number of repeats.

### C05 — Make long history safe through existing bounded source proof and recovery

**Risk: high / medium.** With no preset count cap, an implementation may scan an ever-growing grant history on each read/issuance, truncate it and miss adverse facts, overflow a small count, or delete idempotency evidence and recreate old effects. A failed or post-commit response could be counted as a missing grant; a restored old snapshot could permit another.

**Exact amendment:** Reuse Q13's bounded indexed current-state/lineage proof, immutable outcome evidence, pagination, protected audit and restore rules. “Current complete lineage” means materially complete authoritative proof, not a mandated full-history scan for every request. Any maintained summary/reference must remain qualified against its source facts and current revision; it must not omit relevant adverse outcomes, ambiguous commitment, original ceilings or an intervening admission. Preserve the evidence needed for stable same-effect recovery and current permission under source retention; do not invent a new indefinite raw-log retention period. Capacity/safety failures must fail safely and truthfully, not fabricate ordinary expiry or silently change A into a business quota. Qualify actual supported history sizes and latency with existing owner budgets.

No new counter service, audit blockchain, scheduler or permission platform is required. P12's existing mutation fencing and scoped governed-read budget remain applicable rather than being replaced with blanket advice against all locks or per-request source checks.

**Proof:** Production-shaped long histories remain bounded and preserve original/adverse lineage; missing/tampered/stale summary evidence cannot permit issuance. Lost response and post-commit error recover the same admission. Restoring an earlier view of the lineage cannot reopen a spent predecessor or erase newer restrictions. Historical pagination cannot leak hidden grant metadata or cause a full raw history disclosure to recipients. No such runtime proof is claimed here.

### C06 — Specialize the reserved owner rules explicitly and retain the simple experience

**Risk: high / medium.** “Inherit Q13/Q14 in full” would also inherit their now-conflicting unresolved repeat reservations. Alternatively, a new repeated-access feature could duplicate the review UI, show a quota counter as entitlement, or require recipients to enter new accounts and resend files every month.

**Exact amendment:** Record the exact prospective Q15 specialization of Q13-C02/P01 for later-predecessor eligibility and Q13-C09/C12/P11 plus Q14-C08 for the selected nonoverlapping, individually reviewed repeat policy with no preset lifetime quota. Preserve Q14's earlier C09/C19/P13/P19 timing specialization, all other applicable owner safeguards and dated records. Reconcile canonical authoring sources and declared projections at the separately authorized publication step; no automatic backfill issuance or activation follows. Q08 timing/eligibility/repetition, communications, pre-expiry extension, configurable policies and runtime qualification remain separate.

Use the existing source context and one meaningful **Grant access to continue** action. Show the current authorized source/previous-access outcome and real remaining work or permitted reading, then the actual new end after confirmed issuance. A concurrent success elsewhere should lead to current authorized status/recovery, not an invitation to issue again. Distinguish active, ordinarily expired, restricted, source-completed and unknown outcomes without leaking private existence or sensitive refusal reasons. Preserve prior receipts and qualified existing files; no unnecessary reupload, new enrollment, repeated mandatory proof when current exact proof reuse is qualified, routine second approver, activation step or quota dashboard. Current recipient proof remains required before protected use; staff issuance does not itself establish it.

**Proof:** The recorded owner package closes only this standard repeat policy and no longer calls its count/overlap rule unselected. One current review/action handles each new deliberate decision; unknown/replay and already-active outcomes do not mint another. The existing composed UI remains clear under late entry, unavailable proof, another staff actor's issuance and read-only source completion. No lifetime-capacity counter or automatic “renew monthly” affordance appears.

## Proposed corrected meaning

Allow a properly source-authorized staff actor to consider another standard Fresh Unfinished Request Access after each proved eligible ordinary expiry of the relevant preceding admission, for the same genuinely unfinished original recipient/request purpose. Retain original proof and complete current lineage; issue no overlapping unfinished fresh authority in that lineage. Each new deliberate effect uses Q14's fixed up-to-720-hour rule. There is no preset standard lifetime count or cumulative-day ceiling, and no entitlement to another grant, automatic issuance, scheduled successor or cooldown. Genuine source restrictions and adverse history remain effective.

Each decision independently reviews current eligible original scope; past grants do not union or become a silent global ceiling. Use Q13's exact permission, custody, receipt/correction, current-serving and qualified completion rules unchanged, with the explicit predecessor/repeat specialization above. A gap stays a gap; a completed source cannot re-enter this lane. Q08 does not acquire new eligibility. Keep one existing staff action and request view, bounded source evidence and truthful recovery. No new glossary term or architecture is necessary.
