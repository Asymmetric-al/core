## Smallest complete permanent experience

1. Review the actual current requirement, evidence set and concerns in the ordinary request surface. No forced preview clicks, dwell, downloads or Mark reviewed action certifies judgment. Several files supporting one item still receive one item-level decision.
2. Select deliberately from an initially empty selection. Selection is local preparation. Keep **N selected**, the one-request context and **View selected**, with the complete permitted item/evidence/review context. Individual Accept stays direct.
3. A useful header control is **Select eligible items shown**: capture only those displayed finite rows at that instant. It never means all matches, hidden pages or future arrivals. Sort/page/filter within the same request retains explicit selected identities and bases. View selected exposes off-page scope; Deselect shown affects that scope, while Clear selection clears the full local selection. This is not a saved-selection product.
4. Use one deliberate **Accept selected (N)**, with selection and consequence clear inline. No routine second modal, essay or approver. Actual Q23 resolution input remains item-specific through the existing mechanism; a blanket batch note does not replace it.
5. Recheck exact reviewed source/item/requirement/instruction/evidence/review and current authority at admission. All NEW acceptances and required local history/result/durable follow-through commit coherently, or none do. No skip, hidden chunk, successor substitution or automatic reduced set. Preserve safe context for a deliberate revision after known failure; unknown commitment reconciles first.
6. Report actual results: **2 items accepted; 1 was already accepted** preserves another reviewer's prior actor/time. All-existing exact acceptance is current authorized read-only reconciliation, including after actual fulfillment; not a new acceptance or failure. If any NEW decision remains after closure, it cannot bypass closure.
7. Q22 evaluates or durably arranges its complete predicate. Visible rows or all selected items accepted do not prove F. Preserve known acceptance while fulfillment follow-through is unresolved, with owned recovery and no second approval. Explain actual completion/access consequences only when qualified.
8. **Accepting…** is in flight. Confirmed decisions remain known after failed refresh. **We couldn’t confirm the result. Check status** reconciles the original operation before changed-selection/new-key retry. Local clear/cancel before submission differs from browser abort with unknown server outcome. No reupload/reinspection/approval merely for technical repair.

## Strongest alternative and tradeoffs

B, individual Q21 acceptance only, keeps each action small, needs less selection/concurrency machinery and lets a changing item remain independent of another. It need not be cumbersome: the same request/evidence context can support individual actions without extra modals or full-page reloads. Actual Asym request size, review frequency, time saving and error rate are unknown.

A reduces repeated final actions after review, not substantive review itself. Its costs are larger accidental scope and all-or-none contention if a chosen member changes. Retain individual Accept rather than adding best-effort mode, cross-request approval, bulk negative outcomes or a configurable policy engine. A visible reduced-set retry after a known no-effect result supplies flexibility without silently changing intent.

Retaining selection across pages/filters is a product judgment: avoid losing work while making hidden scope visible through the count and View selected. It remains within the request/current permitted context, not durable offline data or cross-tenant selection. Selection and preview telemetry do not prove inspection.

## Eighteen exact proposed amendments

Severity/likelihood concern consequences of omission; they are qualitative, not incident rates.

### U01 — Unit and individual path (Medium / likely for affected users)

Group finite ordinary items from one request/Tenant/environment/purpose. Keep each complete evidence set and Q21 decision; several files do not require several approvals. Individual Accept stays direct. No cross-request/File Manager/application/payment/specialist outcome or bulk negative action. **P01–P03/P31.**

### U02 — Actual current human powers (High / plausible)

Each NEW decision requires actual human Q21/P12 acceptance power and source constraints. Under the consolidated complete-set rule, a command with any NEW decisions requires that actor's current acceptance power over every selected member, including recognized existing members; a wholly exact-existing reconciliation instead uses current disclosure without a new-action gate. Staff/no-Party identity, responsible team, assignment, discovery and byte-read differ. No forced claim, rights union or fake Party. **P04–P06/P22/P29.**

### U03 — Explicit finite selection (High / plausible)

Start empty. Select shown captures only displayed eligible exact rows/bases, never matches/future arrivals. The UI maintains a unique identity set; a submitted command containing duplicate item IDs, even identical repeated entries, is malformed and rejects without effects. Cosmetic ordering alone does not change the intended set. Expose count and complete permitted summary. Sort/filter/page/clear/selection/expansion are inert. Checkbox state is intention, not inspection proof. **P07–P09/P27.**

### U04 — Navigation preserves meaning (Medium–High / likely for affected users)

Retain chosen identities/bases across same-request sort/page/filter without adding, dropping or rebasing. View selected exposes off-page scope; Deselect shown and Clear selection differ. Newly forbidden members cannot remain disclosed in labels/counts. Show safe selection-needs-review and deliberate recovery, not automatic subset acceptance. No cross-context/offline persistence. **P08–P10/P29–P30.**

### U05 — Actual review, not clicks (High / plausible)

Bind current item/requirement/instructions/evidence/review and relevant authority. Required checks and sufficient inspection hold per item. No preview/dwell/download/Mark reviewed gate or AI certification. Unsupported/inaccessible preview is neither rejection nor original-byte permission; preserve actually sufficient qualified alternatives. **P03/P11–P12/P27.**

### U06 — Per-item Q23 resolution (High / plausible)

Allow same-evidence reassessment only with actual current concern resolution through fresh Q21 review. No Awaiting staff-only predicate, blanket Resolve, assumed upload cure or dropped independent concern. Use existing concise per-item resolution where needed, not a batch essay, new reason engine or fabricated upload. **P12–P14/P31.**

### U07 — Intended bases survive races (High / plausible)

Order acceptance against correction, instructions/evidence/review, withdrawal, NLR, safety/authority and closure. No successor or smaller-set substitution. Compare relevant dependencies, not unrelated labels/display names/whole-Tenant revisions. Historical acceptance or a badge alone does not prove exact current applicability. **P13–P16/P23.**

### U08 — Coherent NEW decisions (High / plausible)

One bounded source command commits all NEW selected acceptances and required local history/result/durable follow-through, or none. No partial loops, hidden chunks, skip or reduced-set retry. Providers stay outside locks. Failure means no NEW acceptances from this action, not erasure of independent facts. SDK calls do not prove atomicity. **P15–P18/P32.**

### U09 — Existing success retains its actor (High / plausible)

Recognize exact applicable acceptance without duplicate effects or current-reviewer attribution. Per-item results distinguish new, exact-existing and unresolved/blocked. All-existing reconciliation may be read-only after F under current disclosure, without new acceptance/F/history rewrite. If any NEW decision remains after F/cancellation, deny admission. No hidden identity/count leak. **P16/P19–P23.**

### U10 — Recover original before changed work (High / plausible)

Recover matching committed intent under current disclosure before fresh eligibility/reduced-set submission. Changed immutable operation conflicts; new keys cannot duplicate effects. Timeout/abort/navigation/refresh failure is not rollback. Preserve lawful dedup or reject unverifiable stale work. Technical recovery never requires another acceptance/fake upload/comment. **P18–P21/P24/P34.**

### U11 — Actual Q22 outcome and clocks (High / plausible)

Evaluate or durably arrange the complete source predicate, retaining real hidden/non-file/remaining obligations without disclosure. Explain completion/access consequences when qualified. Known acceptance survives unresolved F with owned recovery, not Complete approval. F is not count/click/stale transaction-start; uncommitted item drafts create no hold. **P23–P25/P31.**

### U12 — Small explicit action and honest copy (Medium / likely for affected users)

Use selection/current summaries/N selected/View selected/Clear selection and one final action. Adequate inline impact avoids routine second modal/essay/approver. Distinguish in-flight/new committed/existing/no-effect/stale/blocked/unknown. Persistent contextual results preserve lawful notes/position/focus, without jumping to another item. **P07–P10/P19–P21/P26–P28.**

### U13 — Accessible composed task (Medium–High / likely for affected users)

Shared controls have item-specific labels and true selected/disabled/mixed states. Keyboard/speech/AT/touch can inspect scope and act. Space or IME/suggestion Enter cannot also submit. Sticky actions do not hide focus; rerenders preserve it; announcements are not a flood. Controls/summary reflow at 320 CSS pixels with readable Unicode/RTL/long labels and localized values. No hover/drag-only path. **P26–P28/P30.**

### U14 — Participant and other effects separate (High / plausible)

Participant output reveals current approved status/permitted history/remaining work; staff delay is not recipient error. No account/access period/Q51 cue/new mail/reminder reset/blanket notices-Off policy. Existing source/P6/P17/P9 effects retain purpose/dedup and cannot repeat through batch/retry. Delivery/projection failure does not erase acceptance or prove F. **P24–P25/P29/P31.**

### U15 — Privacy on every output (High / plausible)

Selection/count/summary/result/accepted-by/history/errors/API/log/cache/export/support output uses current source/field rights. One visible member does not authorize the rest. Preserve attribution without private reviewer disclosure; limited results do not falsify source facts. Fence late output across context changes; no bodies/filenames/associations in general telemetry. **P06/P10/P22/P29–P30/P33.**

### U16 — Capable mistake remedy (High / plausible)

Local input is correctable before commit. After commit use actual source correction/restriction/remediation preserving actor/time/history, especially after F. No fake batch Undo/unaccept/reopen/snapshot restore; powerless contact labels are insufficient. Privacy restriction differs from acceptance reversal. Preserve independent work and clocks. **P25/P31/P34.**

### U17 — Bounded native slice and rollout (High / plausible until qualified)

Use existing source/API/P12/acceptance/aggregate/result boundaries with measured finite selection/query/lock/transaction/retry/history/rate limits. Preserve one fine P12 authority, coarse Tenant protection, immutable scope and all-role/alternate-path proof. No batch engine/Tenant lock/provider/bill guarantee. Mixed writers/imports/restore cannot duplicate or erase facts; scoped stop preserves lawful individual review. **P32–P36.**

### U18 — Evidence and stage (Medium / certain if omitted)

Trace exact owner amendments/proofs. Manuals/UI/mocks/models do not prove native concurrency/roles/AT/preference/cost. Test complete intended-user journeys. Keep B and assumptions visible. Grouping existing decisions alone needs no new term. No Q53, implementation, publication or ratification follows. **P35–P36.**
