## The reference user journey

Start with **one requested agreement document, one confirmed intended contact and one capable reviewer** to make the reference understandable. These are example cardinalities, not product limits. All ratified multi-item, staff/team, replacement, collaboration and bulk behavior remains in scope.

1. **Prepare in the relevant source context.** Staff sees the partner/context they may access and the actual requested document. Use clear source-provided instructions explaining what to supply and why. Reuse a qualified request definition where useful. Resolve recipient and reviewer from current eligible choices; do not create a new contacts or policy system.
2. **Review and issue once.** Show the intended person, requested item, instructions, Requested by target and readable approved-policy summary. Genuine source-required choices remain visible. Normal Issue is sufficient; no routine policy confirmation, second approver or legal essay. Missing policy or source authority gives the capable staff owner an actionable unavailable state, without inviting the participant to fix it.
3. **Confirm the intended recipient.** Open the correct request under the qualified assurance flow. Wrong account, forwarded link and expired access recover neutrally. Do not create tenant membership, imply organizational representation or force a full account if the approved limited-participant path is sufficient.
4. **Supply evidence.** A prepared PDF or supported photographs can satisfy the format profile where the source permits. Offer the ratified optional photo-to-PDF assembly when useful. Show exact selected files, meaningful progress, retry/resume and clear format/size errors. Do not convert or alter a signed/evidentiary original silently. Staff assistance remains honestly staff-supplied evidence.
5. **Explain receipt and review.** Suggested plain copy is “Received — awaiting review,” only after actual receipt; processing states remain separately visible when relevant. A scan being clean is not acceptance. A lost response resolves the original submission before suggesting another upload.
6. **Review the exact evidence.** Staff sees the current requirement/instructions, exact submitted version and permitted provenance/history. Use the existing Accept / Request changes actions. A concise contextual explanation distinguishes “This requested document has been accepted” from approval of the partnership or legal validity of the agreement. Avoid a repetitive warning modal.
7. **Correct and complete through the source.** An unreadable or wrong document follows the ratified correction/review path. Fresh evidence does not inherit acceptance. The source records item acceptance and request fulfillment only when its complete current predicate holds. The partner process consumes that exact result if its own qualified contract permits; it decides its separate handoff and remaining work.
8. **Keep records understandable.** Participants get only their permitted current/history/access view. Authorized staff see the applicable records summary, current exceptions and later truthful disposition outcomes. Independent uses/holds remain effective. Q63 automation runs only under the actual approved and qualified policy; no retention clock is invented from this story.

No new wizard, onboarding dashboard, e-sign flow, workflow canvas, records console or mandatory participant questionnaire is required for this reference. Detailed interface composition uses the existing source surfaces and shared components. Copy above is an illustrative comprehension target, not a new database state taxonomy.
