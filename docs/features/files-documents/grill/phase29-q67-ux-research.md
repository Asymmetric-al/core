## Twelve boundaries

1. **Defined event first.** Actual source supplies event/subject/scope/meaning, allowed producer/actor, sufficient evidence, precision and correction/effects. Policy label alone is insufficient. Missing definition blocks affected capture, not unrelated work.
2. **Initial capture is distinct.** A adds narrow authorized recording of an actual external occurrence. It is not rule editing, correction of a prior error, a general backdate field or authority to perform the external event.
3. **Fact versus plan/unknown.** Occurred means happened. A future plan is not an occurrence. Unknown, disputed, not-yet, inaccessible evidence and failed verification differ; no today/first-day/zero fallback. Use actual source date/instant/timezone meaning.
4. **Exact scope.** Review permitted subject/event/date and affected classes/uses before commitment. Blank scope cannot mean every record or all matching labels. Revalidate changed scope without leaking hidden counts or silently broadening effects.
5. **Current authority.** Trusted tenant/environment/source/P12 decisions govern initial record, correction, viewing and downstream effects separately. Role label, caller ID, Drive link or policy-settings permission is insufficient. Preserve actually supported no-Party paths.
6. **Proportionate evidence.** Accept trusted facts or permitted references/concise input where sufficient. No mandatory reupload/preview/essay/second approver by default. Required inaccessible evidence needs a competent restricted route, not blind confirmation or disclosure.
7. **Separate rules and facts.** Record occurrence does not edit period, reset old receipts, release a hold, end a contract, approve finance or grant access. Occurrence, recorded time, original binding and current applicability remain independently explainable.
8. **Existing producers.** Do not re-enter known native facts or arbitrarily override signed/system evidence. Equivalent duplicates and conflicting producer reports require source identity/order/reconciliation, not last-write-wins or a fresh clock per message.
9. **Recovery.** Preserve original result/actor/date/basis. Resolve lost replies against the original. Wrong occurrence/date uses current attributed source correction, not history deletion or blanket Undo. Qualify correction/current impact before offering capture.
10. **No disposal shortcut.** Q62/Q63/Q66 duties, holds, copy coverage, pending mandatory impact, Stop and current destructive admission remain. A late event can reveal elapsed time: no invented grace, reset to now or immediate unqualified deletion. Unknown data cannot justify indefinite retention.
11. **Small private UX.** Existing issuer/recipient work stays unchanged. Contextual action/date/basis/result with complete keyboard/AT/mobile/RTL/IME/weak-network support; passive interactions inert. No notice, reminder, legal form or global event console implied.
12. **Stage.** Q67 proposes a producer choice, not GF approval, an actual event/period, native qualification or activation. Preserve Q01–Q66/specialist owners. The actual profile/adapter must close its own source gates; selecting A cannot close R-POL/R-LEGACY by itself.

## Seven founder checks

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A date could be entered to accelerate disposal, affect the wrong subject, duplicate native truth or treat a plan as an occurrence. A requires a supported source act, sufficient evidence, exact scope, honest time/attribution and recovery. It is not a policy-editor shortcut or legal certification.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That a supported policy may need a real externally occurring fact, whose actual owner can define sufficient evidence and authority. This is a proposed capability, not observed GF use or a selected grant/contract trigger. Unsupported sources stay unsupported.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adds a source-owned producer for a defined fact, retaining native/integration paths. No global event platform, new grant/finance/signing action, changed policy-authoring permission or weakened custody/disposal rule follows.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

A qualified source actor can record an external fact in context without requiring an integration for every occurrence. Known facts aren't re-entered. A short meaningful date/basis/scope review has clear recorded/unknown/correction outcomes. Ordinary issuers/participants get no new form or notice.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Purview documents both operator-created and automated events plus scope/reversal hazards. Date/error-prevention guidance supports known-date entry and review without rituals. This is evidence-informed judgment, not universal consensus; no vendor period, indefinite state, future-fact allowance, API or broad scope default is adopted.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes through the actual source/P12/custody owner. Q66 defines policy meaning but does not make an event true. Q62 correction and Q63 disposal remain independent. No implemented native producer is claimed.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Recommend A as an additional narrow supported route; retain B as a strong simpler option where authoritative producers cover the actual workflow. Make event/authority/evidence requirements explicit, don't invent GF facts, and avoid the abandoned extra-case-priority vote.
