## Why preview coverage does not equal comparison coverage

This matrix describes inherited limits and prospective comparison hazards, not proven behavior of an unchosen engine.

| Reader lane                           | What an automatic comparison would have to say honestly                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Images / first-frame animation stills | There is no text-difference contract. A visual comparison needs a declared registration/tolerance/rendering scope and cannot imply all animation frames, metadata or OCR text were compared.                                                                                         |
| Static PDF                            | Text differences and page-image differences answer different questions. Scanned images, text order, graphics, forms, annotations and signatures cannot be represented as “unchanged document” just because one projection matches. No OCR or legal/signature assurance is inherited. |
| DOCX                                  | D32 shows declared static document/header/footer scope and omits revision markup/comments. Same represented text does not prove unchanged tracked revisions/comments or legal wording. A preview is not acceptance of revisions.                                                     |
| PPTX                                  | Visible static slides have original order/numbering; hidden slides/media/notes have omissions. Comparing represented slides cannot establish that omitted content stayed unchanged.                                                                                                  |
| XLSX                                  | Visible saved values retain coordinates and raw value/date semantics. A formula or external source may change while its cached visible result does not; missing cache is unavailable, not zero. No formula recalculation or complete workbook-change claim follows.                  |
| TXT                                   | Literal strict decoding is the narrowest prospective text lane. A comparison must declare whitespace/normalization options rather than quietly ignoring differences, and bound worst-case processing.                                                                                |
| CSV                                   | Delimiter/projection options are part of identity. Compare literal fields with stable coordinate/structural semantics; do not coerce numbers, drop leading zeros/headers, execute formulas or match unrelated columns merely because they look similar.                              |

Even A can mislead if it presents two partial renditions as complete originals. Every pane keeps the relevant coverage and availability information. If B is selected, “No changes found” must be restricted to the exact fully processed comparison scope. Incomplete, unsupported, unavailable, expired and failed cannot become zero differences. Exact verified byte equality is a distinct technical fact; it still does not transfer permission, approval or authenticity.

## Minimum technical boundaries for either choice

1. **Pair identity is deliberate and immutable.** Select exact source occurrences/versions and show earlier/later meaning from the owner. A filename, digest match, timestamp or a moving “latest” pointer is not the relationship. Current evidence, superseded history and unavailable bytes remain distinct. Arbitrary cross-source comparison is additional scope, not implicit access through a general picker.
2. **Authorize each input and the combination.** A right to view one current use does not expose a historical predecessor, another request, hidden filename, full use history or a second source. Source capability governs discovery, metadata, admission and every delivered representation. If a derived comparison contains material from both inputs, serving it requires current permitted disclosure of both, including their continuing restrictions. A successful prior generation is no access grant.
3. **Keep current acceptance separate from what remains on screen.** The reviewer may deliberately inspect a historical pair while Q03 fences the old candidate. Never let a pane's focus, changed-file count or green result select/approve the current evidence set. A newly admitted correction must not silently swap pane contents or rebase an acceptance action. Show the source change and require the existing current review boundary.
4. **Preserve the shared safe reader.** No auto-download, external Office viewer, public URL, TipTap editing/import, arbitrary parser network access or original mutation. Preserve exact original custody and provenance. Comparison is a reading aid, not redaction, accepted evidence, completed review, publication or document issuance.
5. **Preserve coverage and access through async/UI state.** Old requests and worker completions cannot repopulate revoked, different-context or newer selections. If one input becomes unavailable, the pair/result is no longer a valid complete comparison. Keep any independently still-permitted single-file reading truthful, without retaining protected fragments in a combined result or silently choosing a replacement version.
6. **Keep the source journey usable.** Retain context, notes, reading position, focus and clearly named file/version controls. Narrow screens need an intentional single-pane toggle/stacked alternative with visible identity. Do not force a comparison ritual before Q21 acceptance or mistake opening both files for sufficient inspection. Original download stays separately authorized.

## Additional requirements if B is selected

These are bounded consequences of a product choice, not a new global comparison platform. The finalized proposed B is text-change highlighting only. Visual-comparison distinctions above explain its limits; they do not select a visual-difference lane.

- Bind a result to both exact immutable input identities, each eligible source/restriction generation, representation/profile versions, declared text extraction/comparison algorithm and options, alignment assumptions, completeness/coverage, output digest and expiry. A profile change creates explicit new lineage. Two independently acceptable previews with incompatible coverage/settings are not silently comparable.
- Treat inserted/deleted/moved text/pages/rows and reordered evidence explicitly. Page number or row position alone cannot prove semantic correspondence. A skipped/failed page or one unavailable cell is not a deletion in the source. A text-only method must not mark the whole original unchanged when only formatting/images/hidden content changed.
- Define per-pair aggregate work/output/concurrency/queue budgets in addition to inherited per-file bounds. Two maximum-sized readers plus an unbounded diff are not qualified merely because each input is below D32's ceiling. No expensive parser/diff runs in list rows, keystrokes or database locks. Keep finite tenant-fair processing and distinguish transient recovery from deterministic unsupported input.
- Extend the existing generation/custody/claim/recovery seams rather than adding a new workflow engine. Semantic retry/coalescing must bind the pair and options; stale completion, cancellation, restriction, expiry, cleanup and Ready promotion must have coherent winners. Reads must not renew either input or derivative lifetime.
- Render changes safely as inert content. Use accessible text/structure and labels in addition to colour; do not place untrusted text/HTML in executable markup. Selection does not authorize summarization, AI retrieval, global indexing, external fetch, notifications or outbound data.
- Require sufficient comparison coverage qualification before claiming this lane supported. Leaving automatic comparison unavailable must preserve independently qualified preview and source-authorized review alternatives. Do not make every unsupported comparison a business rejection.
