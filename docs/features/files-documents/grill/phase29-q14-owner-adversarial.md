## 3. Six incremental concerns and minimal amendments

Severity means consequence if implemented incorrectly; likelihood is qualitative plausibility, not observed frequency. These are design requirements, not findings of native production vulnerabilities.

### M1 — Fix the actual fresh source end, not another approval or first-entry clock

**High consequence; high plausibility if generic timing helpers are reused.** The original `I`, old expired end, approval click, credential issuance, provider delivery or first visit can accidentally supply the anchor. A stored approval may otherwise remain indefinitely “waiting to activate,” importing C after A was chosen.

**Required amendment:** For this standard fresh profile, the qualified Q13 source issuance occurrence `G`, whose effect durably commits, establishes the nominal unfinished end **`G + 720 elapsed hours`**: exactly 2,592,000 seconds in the qualified source instant model. Resolve and preserve the finite actual end at this source effect under genuinely applicable earlier limits. No separate first-entry/unused countdown or delayed business-activation stage is selected. Never entering ends on the same actual unfinished boundary.

A preliminary administrative review may exist, but it is not a second hidden start. After successful confirmed issuance the original timing result must exist; unknown commitment remains unknown until reconciled. Source authority still does not prove valid recipient proof, successful delivery or ready content. Preserve Q13's source-occurrence/physical-commit distinction and current checks after waits, rather than selecting a timestamp API here.

**Proof:** draft/approval alone; actual source issuance; never-entered request; first entry late; wrong original `I`; lost response; same-intent retry; source operation across a relevant boundary; output lookup failure. No case creates a new period from observation or technical recovery.

### M2 — A selected thirty-day profile is neither a menu nor an old-end override

**High consequence; plausible.** “Up to” may become sender discretion; a generic minimum-date helper may include the expired original stage end and deny every fresh grant, or ignore a genuine cap to manufacture the full 30 days.

**Required amendment:** Apply A only to the expressly qualified prospective Q13 profile. Preserve original ended admissions unchanged. “Up to” means the selected fixed profile under genuine restrictions, not arbitrary sender/tenant duration, an extension power, a guaranteed minimum or a new universal maximum. Preserve action/item/representation/stage/purpose/overall scope: a hard intake cutoff is not automatically whole-stage expiry, and the old ordinary unfinished-only end is not a cap on this newly approved authority. True binding overall/purpose/adverse limits remain effective.

The existing Q13 eligibility check must positively establish lawful fresh authority; this is not permission to bypass original adverse history. Scope unknowns are not filled by 30 days. Removing a genuine shortening cause cannot recalculate a longer end. Temporary proof/representation recovery remains possible only under the existing valid authority and its unchanged terms.

**Proof:** legitimate fresh issuance after old ordinary end; still-binding overall end; per-file restriction; earlier intake-only cutoff; a later target; arbitrary submitted duration; genuine shortening then cause removal. Exercise current use through Q13's already-required doors rather than inventing a second clock resolver.

### M3 — Same number, two distinct business anchors

**High consequence; plausible.** Both unfinished fresh access and completed reading use 720 hours, inviting a shared `expires_at` shortcut, a blanket 60-day grant, or an end recalculated from completion observation.

**Required amendment:** Keep the fresh unfinished end anchored to `G` and the Q13 completed end anchored to exact eligible normal fulfillment `F`. While valid fresh authority exists, fulfillment stops ordinary writes and can admit only the already-declared narrow completed-read scope at no later than `F + 720h`, under genuine caps/current checks. Do not carry writes forward, union expired scopes, use `max` with the old end, wait until unused expiry to start completion, or use consumer/repair time. Pre-visit and lawful atomic fulfillment remain governed by Q13's causal proof.

For one uncapped fresh admission, eligible `F < U <= G + 720h` implies `E < G + 1,440h` (strictly before 60 elapsed days from this fresh issuance). This is an explanatory bound, not a stored overall cap, a promised two-month period, a measure from original `I`, or a repetition/retention rule.

**UX precision:** Unlike Q12's 60-day unfinished profile, equal 30-day nominal periods mean an uncapped completion at/after `G` does not shorten the nominal unfinished date: atomic `F = G` can match it; later `F` moves the nominal read end later. Genuine narrower read/overall limits or later restrictions can still yield a shorter actual end. Therefore display the actual completed end and mode; neither promise an extension nor carry over the blanket implication that early completion alone normally shortens this A profile.

**Proof:** lawful atomic `G/F`; fulfillment before first visit; completion near the exact end; actual late completion; timely effect observed after `U`; true overall cap; narrow read scope. Date equality alone neither proves nor rejects causal authority.

**High consequence; plausible.** Retrying under a newer clock/default can regenerate outputs, while dormant-entry state or a seven-day field from C can silently constrain A. Conversely, a 30-day raw timer can be mistaken for durable enforcement.

**Proof:** same intent after changed policy/time; no entry through expiry; entry immediately before/at/after end; stale first-entry/old expiry worker; process/tab suspended; repeated callback; old unadmitted upload after fresh issue.
