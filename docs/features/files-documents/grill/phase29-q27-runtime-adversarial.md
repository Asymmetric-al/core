## Material findings and exact prevention

Severity describes the consequence if the future native path omits the constraint or copies the adjacent pattern. Likelihood is a reasoned exposure assessment, not an observed incident rate. These are design/qualification gaps, not claims of a deployed native vulnerability.

### R01 — Delayed work resolves a later On policy or new membership

**High severity; likely exposure under asynchronous processing/configuration changes.** Finance `ensureCorrectionApprovalWorkflow:443–473` resolves settings and profiles each call. Per-recipient keys at `:123,131` can avoid duplicates for one profile while still admitting a newly resolved profile. Applying that pattern to Q27 lets an Off-era source event send after On or adds newly joined staff to an older occurrence.

**Exact amendment:** at authoritative source-actionability admission, durably bind the original effective plan/condition and complete finite candidate cohort basis—or sufficient authoritative versioned evidence to recover that exact original boundary—through the existing source/P17 owners. The resulting identity is a server output, not a client-supplied policy claim. Independent current narrowing still applies at delivery. No later worker may choose whichever plan/roster is current when it runs. Delayed publication or a duplicate callback cannot re-date the source occurrence. If original proof is unavailable, retain valid source work and an unresolved notification obligation; recover only original authoritative terms or safely stop, never backfill from a future On or newly available person. This does not require blocking valid receipt/actionable review because the mail subsystem is down.

### R02 — Checking/preview oscillation creates duplicate or misleading mail

**High correctness/noise consequence; likely with retries and processing.** Q03 ADR `0003-recipient-corrections-and-exact-review-lineage.md:18–31` makes correction current at durable admission, before checks may finish. Q21 ADR `0021-staff-acceptance-of-ordinary-requested-items.md:28–53` separates exact item/evidence-set review, safety, supported inspection and acceptance. A file-level ready Boolean cannot represent all of this.

**Exact amendment:** the source owns a durable occurrence for newly actionable ordinary **item review work caused by first/current corrected evidence**, with exact relevant requirement/evidence basis and causal relationship. Transfer completion, callback, clean scan, preview-ready bit, renderer retry, page refresh, task insertion or a repeated ready/not-ready observation is not independently a new occurrence. Same-basis ready→temporarily unavailable→ready recovery preserves identity; it does not manufacture another mail. A genuinely new admitted current correction may create distinct actionable work once its source predicate holds. Do not collapse all corrections by file hash/item/status, and do not treat every supporting file as a separate item acceptance or email. An unsupported preview may coexist with a qualified alternative inspection path; no universal preview-ready/all-scans/all-request-files gate follows.

### R03 — Email depends on the task projection, or task failure erases source work

**High reliability consequence; plausible.** Finance creates/links a task before notification and uses financial wrappers (`approval-notifications.ts:475–529`; `mission-control-tasks/store.ts:47–73`). Q24 already requires source-backed work, but that does not make task materialization the authoritative business event.

**Exact amendment:** preserve source work, Q03 inline warning and required projection handoff independently of optional email. The source's exact recipient/work basis may qualify notice before the task row/UI projection has materialized. Conversely, a task row alone cannot make unsafe or already-reviewed evidence actionable. A valid source admission with failed task refresh is known source success, not an invitation to reupload, re-review or create personal task copies. Unknown source commitment must be reconciled before compensating/dismissing anything possibly authoritative. Staff recovery shows actual projection versus source versus notice status without creating a second work queue.

### R04 — Future eligibility is mistaken for historical cohort membership

**High privacy consequence; plausible with teams/hand-offs.** Financial current profile-role lookup at `:213–253` is not the native source's qualified current review-work recipient set. Q24 ADR `0024-person-or-team-review-responsibility.md:45–76` separates responsibility, permission, membership and actual human action; coordination-only changes need not revoke independently valid source access.

**Exact amendment:** original source/P17 resolution binds the exact role/resolver/people basis permitted for this occurrence. Each intended person independently satisfies notice purpose, source scope and contactability; source admin, plan manager, reviewer and email recipient are separate powers. A team name, task, common email, creator, all-admin query or union of different people's grants is not authority. Later members may legitimately see current work under current source permission but are not appended to a past email occurrence. A source handoff/assignment notice is a different unselected purpose. At dispatch, original cohort membership is insufficient by itself: current source/role/contact/privacy narrowing must still pass. No nominal claim or exclusive reviewer lock is added.

### R05 — Partial resolution becomes zero, partial send or a silent smaller audience

**High integrity/privacy consequence; plausible at dependency failures or finite bounds.** Finance upsert results are individual keys (`:279–315`), with no complete-parent release here. Its per-profile key structure alone does not prove that a concurrent changed recipient set is the same occurrence. Unexpected empty results and incomplete queries can appear harmless while dropping eligible people or changing the historical meaning.

**Exact amendment:** use the existing sole P6 whole-plan entry with one complete finite source-authorized candidate envelope, including Off or truthfully empty cases. Preserve unique occurrence identity independent of plan/membership, exact immutable compilation, complete canonical children and release-last admission. Identical concurrent calls converge; changed/disjoint/partial sets conflict without append/truncation. Before any child can dispatch, its same-scope parent is released. Proven zero has explicit original reason/terms; unknown/partial/overflow is not zero. Bound overflow performs no partial writes and cannot split one logical occurrence into separately released groups. No extra arbitrary volume, quota, digest or cooldown is selected.

**Exact amendment:** coordinate relevant current evidence/item/review/source/plan/role changes at the owner-required admission boundary, including facts after lock waits. Resend's 24-hour key window is not permanent duplicate prevention or a license to rekey uncertain old mail.

**Exact amendment:** prove same-tenant/environment/source/item/basis/recipient/binding relationships and immutable admitted effect identity against real table/column/function grants, applicable policy combinations, triggers, old/new UPDATE transforms and privileged worker/service/RPC/view/storage paths. Definer executes with owner authority; bypass depends on actual role/table ownership/FORCE/BYPASSRLS. Neither a service-only comment nor a SQL-text unit test demonstrates native enforcement.

No sensitive filename, raw reason/evidence, attachment, direct storage URL, broad contact/roster display or staff credential in history. Existing authorized staff doorway shows current permitted work; forwarding/scanners/open tracking cannot confer source access or mark human review. Apply real erasure/export/log/cache/backup controls while preserving lawful body-free evidence.

### R11 — Operations silently turn Off/unknown into success, backlog send or cleanup

**High reliability/privacy consequence; plausible deployment and support exposure.** Finance sender settings lookup may be skipped (`approval-notification-email.ts:169–187`); notification rows have cascade relationships (`20260611150000:27–38`). Generic workflow-key reuse lacks complete material comparison at its helper. **Exact amendment:** known Off, unresolved original policy/cohort, true zero, eligible pending, current suppressed/non-applicable, provider uncertain and known delivered are distinct. Mail failure does not hide My Work, and source projection failure is not proof of no source effect. Use existing owned repair with safe outcome information, no reupload/review/newkey as a retry mechanism. Roll out prospectively; old missing inputs, imported source rows or plan enablement do not trigger historical staff mail. Restore reconciles original source/plan/cohort/attempt evidence before outward effects; missing evidence fails safe rather than force release, wildcard member reconstruction or destructive cleanup. Preserve lawful minimization and source history rather than invent permanent content storage.

### R12 — Scale creates partial cohorts, unfair polling or an email per technical event

**Medium/high operational consequence; plausible at larger teams or processing churn.** Finance retrieves all tenant profile preferences and staff roles without a native source-bounded cohort (`:213–226`); its generic attention list is capped100 (`mission-control-tasks/store.ts:130–153`). Those implementation choices do not qualify source-complete native resolution, counts or latency.

**Exact amendment:** use the exact source-current work/recipient index and P17 declared finite binding/global compiler limits, complete pagination where needed and early over-bound rejection. Scope queries before counts and paginate authorized history. Preserve one logical work identity and one notification occurrence for a source basis, not one process/task/mail for each team member, file page or readiness observation. Provider fanout is the existing independently keyed finite member set, not a new user-facing task per person. Measure source-resolution/atomic release/recovery budgets and tenant fairness using production-shaped bounded fixtures; choose no arbitrary new capacity number or whole-history scan.
