## 2. Practical alternatives and recommendation

**Illustrative scenario, not observed ministry usage:** Elena requests a two-page equipment inventory from Nora. Nora has paper pages and a phone. Both alternatives must let her use the ordinary permitted camera/file route. The choice is whether Asym itself creates a combined PDF from those images.

| Alternative                                           | What Nora and the reviewer experience                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Main benefit                                                                                                                                | Real cost/limitation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Upload photos or a prepared PDF (recommended)** | Where the item permits multiple images, Nora submits the page photos as distinct received files. If she already has a permitted PDF, including one prepared using a suitable device tool, she uploads it normally. The reviewer sees the actual received files and their current review basis. | Keeps one small intake journey and original-file identity. Does not duplicate a scanner/page editor or require everyone to create a PDF.    | Separate files can be less convenient to read in order. Preparing a PDF elsewhere can mean app switching, finding the saved file and possible session interruption. Device tools differ, may be absent or may use cloud storage; this is not a universal effortless fallback. |
| **B — Also combine photos into one PDF inside Asym**  | Nora chooses an optional assembly action, captures or selects a bounded set of images, checks their order/orientation, retakes/removes a page and deliberately submits the assembled candidate. Normal file/image upload remains available.                                                    | Keeps one ordered paper-document preparation inside the request. Can reduce the need to leave the request and find a separately saved file. | Adds preparation, ordered-page provenance, a derived PDF, memory/format limits and additional failure/recovery states. It does not improve illegible photos, make image text accessible or establish authenticity merely by packaging pages.                                  |

Recommend **A as the complete present scope**, not a temporary promise to enable B automatically later. Existing source-governed intake already covers useful paper-photo submission where its accepted format/count rules permit it. Current official products demonstrate both direct mobile upload and richer scanning; they do not establish that Asym users frequently need a built-in assembler or that its incremental costs are negligible. A avoids another authoring surface while retaining useful camera and prepared-file routes.

B is a credible choice if keeping page preparation in one flow is worth its extra permanent responsibilities. Its strongest case is a person with several paper pages and no suitable accessible/approved device tool, or repeated page-order confusion during review. Those are plausible scenarios, not measured Asym demand. Do not weaken B into a cumbersome mandatory scan wizard: one optional item-local action, a clear ordered list and one deliberate submission can be sufficient. Likewise, do not sell A as requiring a laptop, third-party account or paid scanner.

This is one capability question. It does not select exact format/count/byte budgets, a vendor, OCR, edge detection, automatic cropping, image enhancement, a general PDF editor, signatures, cloud scanning, scanner hardware integration, an offline application or new notification defaults. If B is chosen, its exact source composition unit and proof obligations must be explicitly adopted during adversarial review rather than hidden in implementation.

## 4. Small complete user experience

For A, the item shows its actual accepted formats and limits, **Choose files** and an available camera route. A simple instruction can say **You can add separate photos of each page** only where that source item really permits it. Selection is visibly separate from receipt. Taking another photo appends another selected file rather than silently replacing the earlier choice. A user can inspect/remove a local selection before deliberate upload without a routine confirmation dialog. Staff use the existing Q53 action and truthful staff attribution; participants retain their own current source terms. There is no mandatory PDF conversion, scanner recommendation, page-renaming ritual or new requirement to enter a sequence number for each photo.

If the source specifically needs one PDF, say that truthfully and offer the real qualified preparation/assistance path; do not pretend separate images meet the requirement or force an unapproved cloud tool. A device-prepared PDF is the received file, not proof of untouched paper, verified OCR or absence of prior transformation. Asym preserves what it actually received and what it actually knows. A source may impose stricter original/fidelity requirements.

For the strongest B, offer **Create PDF from page photos** as an optional item-local action, not a new global scanner. Its small list identifies each selected page, order and orientation. Use **Add page**, **Retake**, **Remove page**, **Move up**, **Move down** and bounded **Rotate** where that transformation is explicitly qualified. Show **Not submitted yet** while preparing and a clear final action such as **Create PDF and submit**, accurately adapted to participant versus staff source wording. This may authorize preparation/finalization in one deliberate intent; it is not necessarily two confirmation clicks. Generation, transfer, durable source receipt and inspection/review remain distinct outcomes. A failed required page must not silently yield a successful shorter document.

B cannot evade original fidelity by generating the PDF in browser memory before upload and calling it an untouched Submitted Original. Its source must qualify an ordered original-page input set, separately identified generated output, transformation/profile lineage, exact current candidate and readback/correction/withdrawal semantics. The new unit cannot be guessed from filenames or treated as a general Logical Document. Necessary retained inputs are protected and kept according to their real source obligations; not every abandoned local photo is automatically retained forever. Never mutate an accepted original, strip a signature, alter an independently admitted earlier contribution or merge unrelated source uses.

Neither route claims photos are saved to the gallery, retained after the browser is killed, uploaded while offline or automatically sent on reconnect. Preserve safe state where actually supported and explain the actual remaining recovery path. No preview failure should force an external viewer or expose a public/private bearer URL. An unknown original result is checked before resubmitting or changing preparation route.

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A may leave people switching apps or reviewers piecing together pages. B may discard original evidence, silently omit a page, produce unreadable output or lose an unsent sequence on a weak phone. Both fail if capture, upload and receipt are conflated. The proposed scope preserves normal files and real source recovery rather than promising a scanner fixes every intake problem.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?
