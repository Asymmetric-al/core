# Phase 29 Q11 — when the finite unfinished period starts

Researched 20 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. The parent reports Q10 A and all preceding Q01–Q09 decisions ratified. This is a proposed next founder question, not a selected Q11 answer or a numerical lifetime recommendation. Only this research note was written; no canonical document, product, provider state, message or permission was changed.

The ratified Q10 scope is the starting point: unfinished access is finite; eligible timely normal fulfillment can admit the separately bounded Q07 completed phase under genuine overall caps. This note must not silently turn the new unfinished deadline into an overall completed-read ceiling again.

## Recommended next single question

> Should the finite unfinished-access period start when the source first issues access to the intended recipient, or should the recipient have a finite window to enter, followed by a finite working period starting at their first qualified entry?

Recommend **A — Establish the finite unfinished end at source issuance.**

“Issuance” means the authoritative source act that first makes this exact request/recipient's scoped access available under its admitted policy. It is not draft creation, pressing a button that later fails, an outbox worker running, an email provider accepting a message, a delivery/open tracking event or the recipient's first browser visit.

The source's eventual qualified policy must make that act and end concrete and trustworthy. This question chooses the anchor/fixedness relationship, not the number of days, a universal calendar calculation, a staff date-edit capability or a new workflow.

B is a serious alternative if protecting time after delayed initial access is more important than having one end known at issuance. Its strongest form does not arbitrarily cut the working interval short just because first entry occurred near the enter-by boundary. It still cannot guarantee continuous/full access through genuine overall caps, revocation or source restrictions, and it does not solve staff review extending beyond the working end.

A different B variant could clip the working period to one fixed latest unfinished end. That variant is bounded, but it weakens B's main delayed-entry benefit and is not the strongest alternative presented here. No direct competitor evidence requires that clipping.

No inspected primary documentation establishes first-qualified-entry activation as a universal or predominant document-request practice. It remains a product alternative justified by its possible usability benefit, not competitor parity.

Do not add a rolling “while active” option, indefinite “until completed” option or automatic renewal. They create another permission policy and conflict with the required finite unfinished stage unless independently bounded. A manually selected absolute date is a possible fixed-end source policy, not necessarily a useful third founder choice. A requested-date-relative end also needs explicit source rules and cannot silently move with Q09 target edits.

## Preserve Q10's stage scope under either clock

The end being chosen is the **unfinished-stage** end U. It is not the requested-by target, an upload-only deadline, an email-token expiry, a session timeout or a new overall cap.

Under B, the finite dormant entry boundary L governs the opportunity to activate. A qualified entry T while that authority is valid establishes the finite working end U through the prospectively admitted rule. For explanatory purposes, if the rule is a finite working period W, the latest possible unfinished horizon is bounded by L + W. No value of L or W is selected here.

**L is an entry-stage boundary, not automatically a working-stage or completed-read cap.** Timely entry can admit work beyond L because that consequence was authorized from the outset, not because an expired grant was revived. A genuine separately binding overall limit still retains its declared effect. The parent reports the owner review confirms this prospective finite dormant/working distinction is compatible with Q10; it must not be retrofitted onto existing unconditional ends.

Under either option:

- Eligible normal fulfillment while unfinished authority is valid can admit Q07's completed reading under the prospectively declared Q10 policy.
- Completion after actual unfinished expiry does not revive recipient access. Timely fulfillment learned about later must be reconciled from the original evidence, not dated at recovery.
- Receipt or a clean scan is not fulfillment. Staff review can consume time under either clock, and a timely submitter must not be blamed for that review.
- Where the owning source already permits fulfillment before any recipient visit, apply Q10 to the actual historical eligibility and then-applicable finite boundary, including B's dormant boundary. No later visit may start an unfinished working period on a request already fulfilled; no new waiver or proof bypass is implied.
- Q07's completed maximum and current genuine overall restrictions remain. No full completed period is guaranteed.
- Q08 requires its actual prior completed-view admission. It does not supply automatic active-request recovery, new upload rights or a substitute for an unqualified initial policy.

The interval is a maximum under current authority, not a promise that every action remains available until its end. Completion, accepted-item fences, source closure, privacy/purpose restrictions and technical file eligibility can constrain individual actions earlier.

## What “issuance” must and must not mean under A

The recommendation is about one source fact, not a new visible step:

1. The source has the exact intended recipient, purpose, request scope and approved finite terms.
2. Its authoritative operation makes that original access available and preserves the admitted result/end.
3. The interface reads back the actual result. Any separately authorized invitation uses those terms; delivery observations remain separate.

Drafting, editing instructions and staff preview do not start a clock by themselves. A failed operation does not become issuance because the user clicked Send. A response lost after durable issuance is reconciled as the same act; retry must not issue again with a later end.

An email arriving late does not reset U. A reminder, resend, corrected delivery attempt or successful new code does not automatically establish a new source grant. Delivery failure can require truthful operational handling and an owned help route, but extension powers remain unselected.

“Usable access is issued” should mean the source legitimately established the access path, not a promise that the recipient has seen the message or every network dependency will remain available. If the source cannot issue a functioning qualified entry route at all, do not declare complete delivery/readiness merely because a grant row exists. Exact partial-failure recovery belongs in the source contract; this question does not backdate, pause or automatically extend the end.

Staff see one actual unfinished boundary from issuance. The sender need not type a custom date: the later source policy can supply the finite period and applicable caps. Display sufficient precision and timezone context without converting a chosen timestamp into a different instant.

## What “first qualified entry” would require under B

B needs more than tracking the first link hit:

- One exact intended recipient and current source scope must qualify under Q01. A shared email, forwarded link, email-open pixel, scanner GET, preview/prefetch, staff inspection or anonymous hit cannot activate it.
- The source must define the authoritative entry event. Merely sending a code, beginning sign-in or receiving a browser request is not proof of successful qualified entry. Conversely, no system can infer that the person read or understood the instructions from a server response.
- The finite enter-by boundary and finite working terms are fixed beforehand. An unopened invitation cannot wait forever. Timely activation may admit the working period beyond the enter-by boundary; true independent caps still win.
- First entry happens once. Double clicks, another tab/device, reauthentication, service retries and corrected files do not restart it.
- A failed or interrupted attempt needs a truthful known/unknown result and recovery against the same activation. Do not silently give a new interval because the browser did not receive the response.
- Before entry, staff can see the enter-by boundary, working-period rule and bounded latest possible horizon; after activation, show actual U. Do not display a fabricated exact working end beforehand. Genuine overall restrictions can still prevent a full nominal interval.

An intended recipient who opens the request just to learn which document is required may start the clock. That is a real B cost. Do not solve it by guessing “meaningful use” or adding a ceremonial start button without another deliberate product decision.

The ordinary Q01 entry can communicate the timing consequence concisely without another account, identity form or repeated confirmation. Actual accessible authentication, mobile interruption and state recovery must be qualified before claiming B protects practical working time.

## Reminders, delivery, privacy and observability

This decision adds no reminder cadence, automatic email or “you have not opened” tracking program. If a separately authorized communication exists, it uses current source terms and exact recipient scope; its send success/failure/click cannot become the clock's authority.

Staff can distinguish **source access issued**, **delivery state known or unknown**, **qualified first entry if B is chosen**, and **source fulfillment** where their role permits. Do not infer that a person read or understood a request from provider events. Avoid a blame-oriented “ignored” status.

Do not expose whether a private request exists, its timing or recipient on an unqualified link. Scanning resilience is achieved through the exact entry/authorization contract, not by trusting a User-Agent or disabling customer email protection.

The later qualification needs immutable source timing/provenance, explicit unknown states, replay safety and current authority after waits. It should retain minimal useful diagnostics rather than copying tokens, private filenames, email contents or sensitive restriction reasons into general logs.

## Recommendation, strongest objection and sequencing

Choose A for one known source-established unfinished boundary and the simpler ordinary recipient/staff model. B is defensible if protecting time after delayed entry is sufficiently valuable to justify its activation semantics and conditional end. The research does not measure that tradeoff for Asym.

The strongest objection to A is concrete: it can consume time before the recipient has a practical chance to act. A must not hide that cost behind the word “issued.” The eventual initial period and cohort plan must cover the real task, qualified delivery/re-entry path and source review; support cannot be assumed to have an unselected extension power.

After the founder chooses the anchor, adversarially qualify its exact event, finite bounds, delivery/entry failures, scanner-safe behavior, concurrent entry/retry/expiry, truthful copy and Q10 transition. Then choose the initial numerical policy and any separately desired recovery capability. No initial duration, automatic extension, reminder, new portal, grant-storage architecture or Q08 fresh setting is selected by this note.

The recommendation is source-grounded product judgment. There are no live comparator, runtime, browser/AT or user-study results here, and no numerical default has been presented as a modern best-practice requirement.
