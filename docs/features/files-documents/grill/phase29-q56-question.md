## One decision

**For an eligible ordinary document-request item, should people upload photos or an already prepared PDF, or should Asym also let them combine newly selected/captured photos into one PDF inside the request?**

Camera/photo intake and reliable multi-file handling are already required. This is the additional choice of creating a multipage document inside Asym. It is not a vote on whether mobile users may submit documents, a general PDF editor or a new scanner application.

The proposed cohort is one eligible ordinary item, used by an already-authorized recipient or a Q53-authorized staff actor with their distinct actual provenance and source powers. Nothing makes staff act as the recipient. It does not join previously admitted documents, merge across requests/sources or add a general File Manager composer.

| Option                                                | Practical journey                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Main benefit                                                                                                                                      | Real tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Upload photos or a prepared PDF (recommended)** | Use the qualified camera/gallery/file routes. Submit eligible page photos as separate originals within the item, or upload a PDF already prepared with a tool the user chooses. Asym does not add photo-to-PDF assembly.       | Completes the required mobile intake with fewer transformations and a clear original-file history. Users who already have a PDF send it directly. | Separate page images can be less convenient to read as one document. Creating one PDF may require switching to a device tool and finding the saved file; available tools vary.                                         |
| **B — Also combine photos into one PDF inside Asym**  | Optionally capture/select pages in the current item, inspect the finite set, arrange/remove/retake pages, deliberately create a PDF and submit through the qualified source path. A's ordinary upload routes remain available. | Keeps page preparation and ordering in the same task and can make a paper multipage document easier to submit and read as one candidate.          | Adds real generated-output lineage, source-original handling, page-set review/correction, bounded image/PDF processing and recovery. Browser generation is not automatically trustworthy, accessible, smaller or free. |

**I recommend A as the complete scope for this capability.** It retains camera/photo intake and prepared-PDF upload rather than building another document creator before there is evidence that in-app assembly's extra continuity is necessary. Native device tools supply a credible alternative, and separate photos must remain a useful route where the source permits them. A is a deliberate no-assembler decision, not a hidden “enable later when cheap” condition or an excuse to leave baseline upload incomplete.

B is a legitimate convenience, particularly when a user has several paper pages and wants one ordered submission without leaving the task. Its benefit is continuity and document organization, not proven cost savings or universal accessibility. No observed Asym volume, failure rate or user study establishes how often that benefit outweighs the added lifecycle. The founder can choose that benefit explicitly; it should not be dismissed merely because it needs careful engineering.

## Concrete user journeys

Illustration, not an observed ministry workflow: Nora is responding to an ordinary request whose source accepts a three-page paper document as either eligible images or a PDF. Elena is the authorized staff reviewer.

Under **A**, Nora opens the item, uses its ordinary upload control and chooses the camera or saved photos as her device supports. She can add another eligible page, inspect the selected files, remove a mistaken local selection before submission and see honest per-file progress and receipt. Elena reads the exact admitted supporting files in the existing item review context. Selection order or a filename is not silently promoted into a single immutable paginated document. If Nora prefers one PDF, she can prepare it with a suitable existing device tool, return and upload that file. Asym must preserve safe task context without promising that a mobile browser always keeps unsent selections alive while another app opens.

Under **B**, Nora also sees an optional accurately named action such as **Combine photos into a PDF**. She chooses/captures the intended pages, sees their order and orientation, and uses accessible controls to rearrange, remove or retake a local page before the deliberate creation/submission step. The interface clearly distinguishes local preparation, PDF creation, transfer, receipt, safety processing and staff review. It does not claim that generating a local PDF means the organization received it or accepted it. Existing ordinary upload remains available when assembly is unsupported or Nora prefers it; no silent fallback uploads a different page set after an unknown outcome.

For B, the exact selected input set/order and separate generated output must have honest source meaning. Preserve originals where the source requires evidence fidelity, and separately identify transformations. Running generation in the browser is not a loophole for silently mutating originals or claiming server-verified input lineage. Any accepted user-assembled incoming PDF has only the provenance the source can actually establish; it is not automatically a verified faithful copy of every selected image. If this feature promises verified retained inputs and exact correspondence, its actual validation/qualification must prove that promise. Do not retain every abandoned retake or create an indefinite raw-photo archive by default.

Elena must be able to understand exactly what was received and what she is reviewing. A generated PDF cannot silently replace a Submitted Original, inherit acceptance or conceal omitted pages. If B is selected, its source amendment must make the normal assembled submission/readback/correction/withdrawal unit precise; it cannot leave recipients to repair a composition by deleting unrelated files or staff to infer which version was accepted. Q03/Q04/Q05/Q21/Q47 and Q53 already govern the surrounding acts and remain authoritative until explicitly narrowed.

A staff upload uses the same appropriate preparation affordance only where Q53 actually authorizes it, retaining staff-supplied attribution. A donor, public visitor, unrelated staff member or former recipient gains no new access from camera permission or possession of photos. If a particular source requires PDF or forbids a format, neither option bypasses that rule. Neither assumes all devices have the same scanner, requires an outside cloud account or sends private documents to a public conversion service.

## Twelve proposed boundaries for the choice

1. **Keep required baseline intake.** Both choices include the actually qualified camera/gallery/file routes, purpose-accepted formats and finite multi-file handling, progress, retry/cancel/recovery and honest receipt. A omits only Asym photo-to-PDF assembly; it does not reduce mobile support to a desktop-only file picker or force a particular external account. If an actual item requires one PDF and the user lacks a suitable accessible/permitted tool, use existing source clarification or qualified staff assistance only where it can actually deliver an accepted format or authorized accommodation. Do not force a second device/cloud account, silently treat photos as meeting a PDF-only rule, or invent a format waiver. An absent capable route is a failed journey qualification for the affected cohort, not a completed baseline or a powerless Contact support handoff.
2. **One narrow ordinary context.** B, if selected, prepares newly chosen/captured page images for one eligible ordinary item under existing recipient or Q53 staff powers. It does not merge admitted records, move evidence across sources, impersonate recipients, publish resources or add a general document editor. Native source admission remains independently required.
3. **Meaningful deliberate selection.** Before submitting, show what the user intends to send. Under B, make the ordered finite page set and proposed PDF clear; arrange/remove/retake controls work without drag alone. No auto-submit from camera capture, file selection, reordering, blur or an image-processing completion callback. Do not infer essential page order from filenames or unreliable capture ordering.
4. **Preserve truthful original/output identity.** Preserve selected originals where source fidelity requires and identify conversion/rotation/resizing/metadata changes as distinct outputs. No client-before-upload loophole or silent Submitted Original replacement. Browser claims about inputs/order/equality are untrusted; the actual admitted provenance and any validated correspondence must be explicit. No blanket retention of every discarded retake or indefinite duplicate archive; actual custody/retention/discard governs.
5. **No silent fidelity or acceptance promise.** B's image PDF is a composed visual output, not automatically authentic, complete, signed, accessible, OCR-searchable, smaller or accepted. Keep source review/approval, scanning, preview and publication separate. No automatic crop, enhancement, redaction, signature creation, existing-PDF merge or full scanner suite is included merely by selecting assembly.
6. **Current tenant/source authority everywhere.** Resolve actual actor, tenant/organization, item, classification and allowed effect on the server. Current source/P12/custody govern original inputs, output and lineage links before storage/finalization/disclosure. Privileged jobs, database grants/RLS old/new rows, functions/views, Storage, caches, derived metadata and cleanup need the same qualified boundary. No general public-media/avatar seam, mutable path or browser-selected bucket/tenant can substitute.
7. **Complete source lifecycle if B is chosen.** Define how the exact composition becomes a submission, how staff read/accept it, and how an authorized recipient or staff corrects/removes/withdraws the intended unit under existing powers. A local page edit is not a mutation of an admitted immutable file. Whole-unit correction cannot silently orphan inputs, revive a superseded candidate, erase unrelated support or inherit acceptance. The selected-answer review must resolve these normal paths rather than leave an unnamed future choice.
8. **Honest preparation and failure.** Distinguish local selection/capture, composition, transfer, finalized receipt, processing and business review. Unsupported/denied camera, bad image, output-generation failure, backgrounded tab, reload and expired source authority need meaningful supported recovery. Do not promise durable offline drafts or cross-device resumption without qualification. No fallback uploads a different set after an indeterminate operation; recover the original committed outcome first.
9. **Safe abandonment and cancellation.** Stop camera use when no longer needed in any actual live-capture implementation. Clear/fence private previews and stale responses on context change. Removing a local page, abandoning generation, cancelling a grant and physically deleting admitted/staged objects are separate acts. Any uploaded preparation retains existing write-in-flight/reference/retention/hold/cleanup fencing; do not delete on route switch, parser error or local Remove alone.
10. **Bounded measured work.** Qualify actual image formats, decoded pixels/frames/page count, output/input bytes, memory, CPU, time, concurrent work, retry and supported-device limits before activation. Bound previews and processing without silently truncating pages or altering originals. Browser processing does not make CPU/memory free; server processing does not grant provider egress. Do not promise a smaller PDF or unchanged Supabase/Vercel bills, choose a package/vendor here, or make a whole-library pipeline a prerequisite.
11. **Accessible and private complete journey.** Reuse Base UI/base-maia/shared packages/ui; provide labeled file/camera/page controls, keyboard/touch reordering alternatives, focus/error/status behavior and usable narrow-screen/AT flows. International names, RTL/IME, orientation and slow networks remain real cases. No private photos, EXIF/location, thumbnails, page text or source identifiers in public caches, logs, analytics/session replay or unqualified durable browser storage. Content accessibility remains separate from control accessibility; no invented OCR obligation or silent waiver of a real source requirement.
12. **Evidence and scope discipline.** Device-tool instructions are optional assistance, not a guaranteed or mandatory journey. A prepared external PDF is the actual file received by Asym, not proof of custody of its unseen camera inputs. B's convenience is considered fairly but native qualification, complete fidelity/lineage and measured cost remain unproved. No Q56 answer, new glossary term, schema, formal specification or runtime activation is inferred; /to-prd and /to-issues stage boundaries remain.

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A can leave users switching apps to create a single PDF, and separate images may be less convenient for reviewers. B can lose pages, mishandle originals, imply false provenance or fail on lower-memory phones. Both can fail if existing avatar upload is mistaken for private document intake or camera permission is treated as source authority. Those are substantive source/UX requirements, not reasons to remove required mobile intake.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

The example assumes the item permits the relevant images/PDF and the user has current authority. We have no measured frequency of paper multipage submissions, app-switch abandonment, device mix or bill effect. We do not assume everyone has a scanner app, camera permission, reliable connectivity or a private cloud destination. Supported formats/limits need actual qualification rather than vendor copy.

### How does this affect the whole product?

A completes existing intake without a new composition purpose. B would explicitly extend ordinary recipient/staff preparation and exact evidence lineage, interacting with review/readback/correction/withdrawal and retention. It does not make a general library editor, financial evidence converter, official renderer or public Media product. General staff-resource audience coverage remains visible and unselected.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

A has the shorter product workflow for already available files/photos but may require an external preparation step for one PDF. B keeps preparation in context and gives deliberate page order, at the cost of an extra optional preparation surface and meaningful failure/recovery states. Both must keep ordinary upload useful, preserve safe task context and be accessible on phone/keyboard/AT without forced conversion.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Yes: comparable request tools document direct camera uploads; device tools offer multipage preparation; document products show deliberate page ordering. Their different choices do not establish that every web request must embed a scanner. W3C/browser evidence also rejects treating a capture hint or image-PDF wrapper as full scanning/accessibility. The recommendation is a proportionate product judgment, not a universal best-practice claim.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes when required intake remains source-qualified and exact originals/output meaning is preserved. Existing image/PDF code does not qualify this purpose. B needs an explicit narrow source amendment; A does not remove already-required camera or multi-file support. P12/P29, current source history and shared UI remain authoritative. No code or provider change is made in this question turn.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Recommend **A — Upload photos or a prepared PDF** as the complete capability scope, while presenting B's real in-task continuity benefit fairly. Do not quietly add assembly under camera support or defer it behind an automatic cheap-enough test. If B is chosen, its selected-answer adversarial review must resolve the normal input/output, admission, review/correction/withdrawal and resource-recovery contract before recording a finished decision.
