## Corrected decision and strongest alternative

For first and repeated **ordinary native fresh unfinished** and **fresh completed-read** admissions, offer one visible notice choice in the existing grant review. Initially select it only from a current qualified eligible projection. Staff may clear it and deliberately grant only where the source permits. Retain that exact choice in the approved source intent; no manual checkbox toggle is required to approve the visible default. The choice requests the source-qualified optional notice subject to the actual message/Delivery Plan/current dispatch gates. It neither edits the plan nor authorizes a recipient, sender, channel or disclosure by itself.

Keep one combined human action but separate grant, notice admission, preparation, provider submission/delivery, recipient proof and current use. Known grant success survives later email failure. A requested notice may be blocked/suppressed or unresolved under its qualified contract; preserve that disposition rather than rewriting the staff choice to Off, inventing a successful send or retrying the grant. Missing required durable source/secondary evidence is an invariant/reconciliation failure, not an ordinary optional-email failure.

The strongest alternative remains integrated default-Off with explicit staff opt-in. It reduces reliance on noticing a preselected outward effect and needs no second workflow step. A better supports a timely handoff while a fixed period runs, but omitted/unwanted-email rates are unmeasured. Keep A with visible current consequence, scope and recovery; do not claim external products prove this default optimal.

## Critical interpretation notes

**Requested, admitted and delivered are different.** “Notify” expresses a reviewed request through a permitted optional source condition. It cannot guarantee dispatch despite later loss of authority or provider delivery. A source-known ineligible result is distinct from a staff-unselected notice, and unknown eligibility/commitment cannot be recorded as either. Follow the exact adopted source/plan recovery contract for a selected notice that cannot presently be admitted; never silently discard required durable intent or interpret best-effort client state as the record. These requirements do not impose blanket grant rollback for a later transport failure or a new modal for every transient error.

**Plan changes cannot refill old occurrences.** ADR0026's exact replay includes proved zero. If a grant-only or contract-suppressed result was authoritatively fixed, turning a Tenant plan On, repairing a contact, changing a default or restoring a process must not create mail for that old occurrence. If the prior outcome was genuinely unknown/uncommitted, reconcile it under the pinned source/plan rules rather than declaring zero or minting a fresh grant. A separately permitted later intentional communication is a different owner-governed act, not hidden catch-up.

**Fresh unfinished is not a permanent message state.** A qualified G followed by fulfillment F may result in a valid completed read-only view before the email worker runs. The current source meaning and exact notice/action contract must handle that order without an invalid upload instruction or a silently changed sealed payload. This is a required source mapping/proof obligation, not a new founder choice about reopening completed work or adding another notification.

**No new identity or source consent claim.** Q01 already selected the closed request-recipient authority and proof requirement. Message optionality is not automatically marketing, a confirmation code, required security mail or general correspondence. P17's code-owned purpose/classification and P6's actual message-type-aware gate determine the exact treatment; an employee cannot relabel it to avoid suppression. No new organization-enrollment or permanent email preference is justified.
