## Essential clauses for the synthesis

**T01 — One actual completed baseline for either route.** Resolve the actual first completed-read admission for the exact tenant/environment/source request/original admission lineage/intended recipient. Preserve its qualified fulfillment F, ordinary end and then-binding policy/limits, provenance to the supporting original or Q13/Q15 authority, and separately admitted actions, own-submission member/original-version identities and Q05-permitted context. This is a logical source fact; it does not mandate a new table, exported JSON bundle or duplicated full private content. Scope evidence must be sufficient and authoritative for the actions sought. Neither writes nor a field named `completed` supplies it.

**T02 — Historical ceiling and current intersection are different facts.** Newly reviewed scope stays within that completed baseline and current source/action/member eligibility. Current issuance eligibility means Q08's authority to issue new access; it does not require the expired prior grant to pass its old read check. Preserve actual binding shortening/revocation/content-use/disposal rules. A narrower prior individual fresh read grant alone is not a new global ceiling; it cannot widen the first ceiling either. Q16 adds no later file, recipient substitute, historical correction predecessor, reshare or retention right. A source-qualified repair of the same immutable original/rendition remains permitted within current eligibility; substituting new original bytes is not repair.

**T03 — Admission is independent of use and representation.** No visit/download/render is required to prove a lawful admission, including a lawful status/context-only admission. Missing required historical authority/scope proof blocks that eligibility without inventing a business rejection or erasing independent source completion. A currently unavailable member/preview must not erase a proved baseline or unnecessarily suppress unrelated permitted actions; use owner-defined truthful partial/unavailable handling. Conversely, retained/available bytes and successful telemetry cannot manufacture absent permission. Do not add a new minimum file-count or body-access condition.

**T04 — Trusted relational and mutation boundary.** Exact source/recipient/route/member/action evidence is produced by the owning source, not accepted as permission from a browser/worker payload. Bind server attribution separately from a reviewed target. Effective constraints/privileges/RLS and owned service procedures must prevent contradictory cross-tenant/source/recipient references and unauthorized changes to historical facts, including parent retargeting and transformed/upserted rows. Current mutable presentation/contact data can change without rewriting the historical permission. Identity replacement/merge is not implicitly authorized by Q16.

**T05 — Recover exact effects; evaluate new effects currently.** Preserve original committed admission/evidence and semantic intent across callback duplication, out-of-order delivery, lost responses and projection rebuild. A delayed qualified recovery can establish the original facts without observation-time reset; an actual gap/no-admission cannot. Materially changed recipient/baseline/scope conflicts or requires the already-owned refreshed review, never an automatic retarget or broader replay. Recovered committed outcomes are not new grants and private replay output still needs current disclosure. Uncommitted old commands cannot acquire authority merely because Q16 becomes available; prospective policy and deliberate reviewed issuance remain required.

**T06 — Current source, private serving and durable evidence remain authoritative.** Serialize the relevant baseline formation/issuance versus restriction/retarget/disposal state under the owning boundary and recheck current facts/time after waits. Business completion may independently succeed without recipient admission, and a later current restriction does not rewrite that historical event. Required source/audit/effect intent must commit coherently; general best-effort logging, cache invalidation and workflow dispatch are not the source admission. Provider/delivery/preview work stays outside locks. A transport error never proves no committed effect or permits deleting potentially admitted bytes. Current authorization applies to metadata, private replay, provider handoff and ongoing Q04 byte delivery, not just button visibility.

**T07 — Bounded evidence and prospective rollout.** Keep a direct source-owned reference to the exact baseline and bounded eligible member/history access; a materialized index/head is not independently authoritative if inconsistent with source truth. Filter current authorized metadata before counts/order/pagination. Unknown/error is not an empty successfully authorized view. Legacy/restore qualification must preserve historical recipient/member/action/provenance, original limits and later adverse outcomes without reissuing authority or providers. Use the existing owner migration/retention process; no indefinite duplicate private snapshot store is selected. If minimum necessary evidence has legitimately been disposed of and can no longer be proved, this lane cannot infer it from remaining files/logs.

These clauses primarily specialize existing Q07/Q08/Q13 controls for the additional cohort. They do not impose a new physical schema, global lock, full event replay, extra approval layer, native role, renewal quota, timer or repeated fresh-read policy.

## Precise SQL semantics to preserve

- `USING` checks existing rows and effective `WITH CHECK` checks new rows. For ALL/UPDATE, omitted `WITH CHECK` can inherit `USING`; omission alone is not a bypass. Applicable permissive policies combine with OR, restrictive policies with AND, and BEFORE-trigger transforms precede the new-row check. Qualify the actual command, role, grants and combined policies, including upsert/RETURNING where used. [PostgreSQL 17 CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html)
- RLS is not general immutability. Table owners normally bypass it, subject to FORCE; superusers/BYPASSRLS bypass it. A definer function executes with owner privileges, so inspect owner, grants, search path and predicates rather than claiming every definer always bypasses. FORCE is not a cure for a BYPASSRLS role. [PostgreSQL row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html)
- Supabase service credentials without a user access token run with service-role bypass; a request carrying a user token follows that user's policies. The inspected admin client initializes with the service key and no user session forwarding; caller auth remains a separate value. [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security)
- An ID-only FK proves referenced existence, not the full tenant/source/recipient relationship; compatible composite constraints or equivalent all-writer enforcement must prove those joins. Cross-row/time-varying CHECK expressions are not enduring multi-row/current-authority invariants. Child-only triggers require parent mutation/restore qualification. [PostgreSQL constraints](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-constraints.html)
- FOR UPDATE locks returned rows, not an absent future baseline; distinct-key contenders require the actual source's shared invariant. READ COMMITTED row rechecks do not make a separately assembled multi-row permission snapshot atomic. ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING can decline insertion due to a concurrent row not visible in its statement snapshot; empty RETURNING is not reliable absence. [PostgreSQL locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/explicit-locking.html), [transaction isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html)
- Q16 creates no new clock. NOW/CURRENT_TIMESTAMP are transaction-start values; a wall-clock reading is not a physical-commit proof. Preserve the already-required qualified source occurrence, coherent committed outcome and after-wait/current checks; no new universal SQL function is prescribed. [PostgreSQL date/time](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/functions-datetime.html)

These official references were checked during this review. PostgreSQL 17 is the cited documentation version, not an asserted hosted database version. No active grants, deployed migration order, function ownership or provider state was queried. The gift-receipt service privilege observation is source-confirmed, not deployed-state qualification.

4. **Restriction/disposal races:** relevant revocation/shortening/recipient change/member restriction/disposal wins before source admission or private delivery; stale prepared review/currentness bundle cannot commit or disclose beyond the winning state. Already lawful historical facts remain unchanged; denied member does not imply all other current actions are denied. Test range/conditional/resume/cache/replay doors under existing Q04 contracts.
5. **Semantic replay and unknown outcomes:** duplicate same intent, changed baseline/recipient/action/member payload, two different intents sharing source, absent-row race, successful commit/lost response and failing outcome lookup. Recover the same committed source outcome before applying new-admission checks; current unauthorized replay reveals no private receipt/body. No automatic new grant, source reset or ambiguous cleanup.
6. **Required durable evidence and delivery separation:** source admission/required audit/follow-up rollback together where owned atomically; best-effort audit or invalidation failure cannot supply missing historical proof. Provider outage/render failure is not business-admission failure, lease expiry is not proof an external effect did not happen, and a fresh read effect does not reopen writes or replace original F/end.
7. **Bounded correct projection:** sufficient indexed source/baseline/member lookups with production-shaped history; permission filtering before metadata/count/order/page, authorized stable cursor handling under current changes, stale cache/refresh/unknown-versus-empty. No scan of every prior grant or raw staff audit required on each use; preserve P12's owned logical read budgets and exceptions rather than inventing a total-query quota here.
8. **Migration/restore/policy gating:** old code cannot fabricate the added cohort or mutate baseline facts; ambiguous historic data cannot be backfilled from current files/profiles/task timestamps. Rebuild qualified indexes/projections without new authority, outward sends or reset ends; restore adverse restrictions and exact IDs. Q08's missing fresh timing/repetition and native role/SQL/serving qualification visibly gate activation. No user-facing “working” claim from source review alone.
