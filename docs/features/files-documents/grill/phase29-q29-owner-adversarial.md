## Material challenges and exact amendment text

The following clauses are required consequences of A, not additional product choices or a mandate for new infrastructure. They should be incorporated into the parent's corrected decision and proof groups.

### O01 — Jointly permitted pair, never combined authority

**Risk:** Critical disclosure impact; plausible if implementation accepts two independently readable IDs or combines capabilities from separate roles, tenants, purposes or source contexts. Two successful read checks alone do not prove that their combination is authorized.

**Required text:** “Offer paired reading only for two exact versions whose relationship is admitted by the owning source for the current review purpose, and only while both inputs and their composed presentation are permitted under the current validated actor/Tenant/environment/context and applicable source restrictions. Evaluate each input and the pair; do not union roles, assignments, source uses, purposes, classifications or fields. Reuse existing P12/source enforcement rather than create a global Compare permission or second policy engine.”

**Qualification:** Exercise cross-Tenant, alternate assignment/role, source-purpose mismatch, restricted predecessor, source-safe labels/counts/search and current reauthorization at every applicable input/derivative/read boundary. Equal digest, filename, folder, common subject or reviewer assignment must not confer authority.

### O02 — Preserve actual lineage and current status

**Risk:** High review-correctness impact; likely without exact labels because “older/newer” is routinely mistaken for “previous/current,” accepted or corrected.

**Required text:** “The source supplies eligible exact related versions and their actual relation/status. A deliberate choice or source-proved default may open the pair; never infer a predecessor from nearest timestamp, filename or byte similarity. Preserve the opened identities while lawful; refresh or qualify source-status labels independently and make a changed source basis clear. An unchanged exact input is not proof that its former status or permission is still current.”

**Qualification:** Test branching corrections, unrelated older supporting members, same bytes in two different admitted uses, current accepted versus current candidate, staff-added versus participant-origin evidence, and permission-safe ambiguous/empty selection. No arbitrary cross-source file picker is selected.

### O03 — Pane focus is not the review target

**Risk:** High integrity impact; plausible when a contextual acceptance control acts on the selected pane or silently follows the latest version.

**Required text:** “Paired reading is optional assistance. Viewing, focusing, scrolling, finding or comparing does not accept evidence or prove complete review. Any source action continues to bind its own exact current item/requirement/purpose/evidence basis and capability. Q03 admission-time predecessor fencing, no revival and Q21/Q28 changed-basis guards remain authoritative. Do not silently replace either input, rebase a stale acceptance or make the historical pane actionable as the current candidate.”

**Qualification:** Correction, staff addition/removal/replacement, acceptance, closure and permission races must preserve the opened lawful pair and notes while independently fencing stale source actions. Optional comparison must not add a mandatory dwell/scroll/comparison ceremony or per-file approvals.

### O04 — Historical and official-document exclusions

**Risk:** Critical disclosure/official-truth impact; plausible if a shared Compare control appears wherever a retained predecessor exists.

**Required text:** “This is staff inspection under existing exact source rights. It creates no participant superseded-content entitlement, completed-access extension, original-download grant, audit-purpose union or discovery of the origin of staff-supplied evidence. Q05's history/content separation and Q28's truthful target provenance remain. P18 retains canonical exact-artifact serving, currentness and separately permissioned evidence history; Q29 neither re-renders an official artifact into an alternate authoritative copy nor exposes prior official revisions as donor choices.”

**Qualification:** Reject participant historical bytes and indirect thumbnails/search/snippets/counts/export leakage; preserve independently authorized current status. Verify official PDF object/generation/digest consistency and old-revision denial on donor routes. Authorized specialist staff history remains possible only through its actual owner contract, not a blanket historical-access ban.

### O05 — Distinguish technical failure, input denial and pair denial

**Risk:** High availability or disclosure impact; likely if a two-pane component has only a global Failed state or keeps the second pane by assuming the composed context still authorizes it.

**Required text:** “Treat checking, unsupported/limited/inaccessible content, transient technical failure, expired technical rendition and lost/unknown source authority as different states. A technical pane failure need not block independently permitted sufficient inspection or unrelated source work. Denied input content and all representations depending on it stop. If the pair relationship or composed purpose is no longer permitted, stop the composed pair; standalone reading may continue only through that input's independently valid source context. Use honest safe next actions without revealing denied existence or the private denial reason.”

**Qualification:** One healthy/one failed pane, one denied pane, revoked pair relation, session/context change, expired Ready output and unknown authority. No automatic original download, external viewer, raw provider URL, stale cache or forced re-upload. Unsupported preview is not automatic evidence rejection or permission to accept uninspected required content.

### O06 — D32 remains inherited, not reset

**Risk:** High safety/retention/capacity impact; plausible if pair orchestration restarts attempts, resets expiry, duplicates generation or presents two individually bounded readers as a proven workload.

**Required text:** “Compose the existing qualified exact reading representations with their full inherited coverage, source/restriction lineage, current-read checks, privacy, reuse/coalescing, attempt and output-custody deadlines. Opening or switching a pair does not renew a prior generation, source access, retention or receipt. A new technical generation is allowed only under D32's existing deliberate eligible-generation rules. Two valid per-file profiles do not establish combined device/worker/concurrency performance; qualify the finite composed workload before activation.”

**Qualification:** Concurrent opens and swaps, warm/cold/expired combinations, failed second input, restriction while generating, cleanup/promotion races, bounded mobile memory/CPU and fair overload. Preserve all declared format lanes; do not quietly reduce A to PDF/images, require equal formats, or assume every possible format pairing is qualified. No durable Pair table, new comparison output artifact or new workflow is required solely for A.

### O07 — Honest reading coverage and accessible presentation

**Risk:** High review and exclusion impact; plausible when attractive pane controls imply full-content inspection or unavailable text is silently treated as empty.

**Required text:** “Show each exact version's actual reading coverage and limitations. The absence of highlighting means no automated differences are offered, not that no change exists. Keep native text/semantics and full represented-scope Find where qualified, with pane-specific labels and search coverage. Pair layout must preserve usable independent reading, a single-column/version-switching equivalent, accessible contents and lawful reading context. Optional synchronization must not imply page/content alignment.”

**Qualification:** Different lengths/orientations/formats, hidden or omitted content, scanned/inaccessible PDF, DOCX revisions/comments, PPTX hidden/notes/media, XLSX saved values/formula-cache gaps and literal CSV/TXT. Test keyboard/screen reader/zoom/reflow/RTL/mobile and returning without losing the source task. Do not manufacture Changed/Unchanged, complete-review, equivalence, authenticity or legal-effect claims.

### O08 — Preserve existing comparisons and contain scope

**Risk:** High owner-contract regression; plausible if “no automated differences” becomes a global prohibition.

**Required text:** “A declines the newly proposed generic automated difference capability for this Phase29 increment. Existing P18 official-document, P22/P23 editorial/semantic and P23 Media current/candidate comparison contracts remain unchanged. No OCR/AI, visual/pixel difference, legal redlining, merge/edit/restore, comparison export, new provider, arbitrary cross-source pairing, automatic notification or source mutation is selected. A does not permanently prohibit a later deliberately qualified comparison capability.”

**Qualification:** Reconcile shared-reader integration against specialist owners rather than deleting their controls; prove that opening, closing, retrying or switching versions creates no source acceptance/publication/currentness/history mutation except the existing permitted technical access evidence.
