## Recommended next genuine choice

**Should authorized source staff be able to deliberately revise the participant-facing instructions for the same outstanding, not-currently-accepted ordinary item in an eligible unfinished request, or should issued instructions remain unchanged and material corrections use existing replacement paths?**

Recommend **A — Allow a deliberate versioned instruction revision.** Staff can correct what this same ordinary document need asks the participant to include without manufacturing a second item solely to carry corrected instructions. Preserve the earlier instructions, every receipt and its real basis, and show the new instructions prospectively. This is a source-authorized change with reviewable consequences, not live autosave or rewriting the meaning of earlier evidence.

**B — Keep the issued item's instructions unchanged.** Staff may explain an unchanged meaning through Q30. If the demand materially changes, use actual qualified Q33 No longer required and Q36 new-item publication, or independently qualified new issuance when the existing request cannot lawfully carry the replacement. The old instruction record stays intact and the new demand is visibly separate. This is simpler to reason about historically but may leave two closely related items, more handoffs and a less natural correction experience. It does not force another account, needless proof or duplicate upload where Q01/Q28 independently permit reuse.

The example is illustrative, not an observed Asym workflow: Elena asks Nora for an ordinary equipment inventory. The instructions accidentally omit the serial numbers needed in that same inventory. The item remains outstanding and unaccepted. A lets Elena deliberately publish revised instructions on that item while preserving what Nora was previously asked and anything already received. B leaves those instructions fixed and uses an authorized replacement route. Neither path may claim that a file sent before the change violated instructions that did not yet exist.

Do not broaden the question to changing requested document type, requiredness, recipient or purpose; altering accepted or terminal work; editing templates; or introducing a new email/default. A genuinely different document need uses the independently qualified new-item/request path. An unchanged-meaning clarification already has Q30 and does not need a new product decision. No artificial third alternative is needed.
