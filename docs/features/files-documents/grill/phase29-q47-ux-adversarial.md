## Disposition and the smallest complete journey

**Accept with required amendments.** The action must remove an exact current contribution from ordinary review consideration while preserving the received act, other evidence and independent custody duties. Its most consequential UX risks are a false deletion/recall promise, withdrawing the wrong unit, stale acceptance, and a mistaken withdrawal with no usable recovery. A labeled button alone does not resolve those risks.

Keep the action beside the exact received contribution in the existing request context. Show permitted identifying metadata and a short consequence, then one deliberate **Withdraw submitted file** action with Cancel. A separate modal, reason essay, typed filename or second approver is unnecessary. With a multi-file source contribution, identify the actual indivisible unit rather than pretending each visual row has independent mutation authority. The item remains required according to its source, and staff see the real current evidence basis.

After success, retain a source-visible **Withdrawn from review** status and, where currently qualified, **Offer this file again**. The latter is a new deliberate source admission using the retained exact version. It preserves original receipt and withdrawal history, records a new actor/time/current basis, and still needs current review. It is not Undo, a historical rollback, restored approval or a new participant archive. No forced download, preview or re-upload is necessary merely to reuse known lawful bytes.

Use one bounded tenant control in the existing source/P12 configuration: **Recipient self-service withdrawal**, initially **Enabled** for the qualified ordinary cohort. Disabling gates new recipient withdrawal only. It does not disable Q03, independently authorized readback, original-result reconciliation or qualified re-offer/resupply. Current scoped staff may assist through an explicitly new staff-withdrawal capability; staff resupply uses the actual Q28 staff-supplied evidence route. Administration, reading, reviewer assignment and staff rank do not automatically grant those actions.

The strong alternative remains correction plus capable source help: Dropbox documents correction, and Submittable routes single-piece withdrawal requests to the organization. It has fewer participant mutations and preserves staff interpretation of ambiguous cases. Its cost is dependence on staff before the right replacement exists. A is recommended for this selected ordinary flow as a product judgment, not a claim of universal modern practice or measured Asym demand.

## Sixteen exact proposed amendments

Severity denotes credible consequence if omitted: **H** high; **M** moderate. Likelihood is a design exposure judgment, not an observed incident rate. None is labeled Critical without evidence of deployment or exploitability.

### U01 — Exact ordinary current contribution and actor (H / plausible)

Allow the currently authorized request participant to deliberately withdraw only their exact current received contribution/use from an eligible unfinished ordinary request item. Establish current source/P12 action authority, participant context, actual contribution members and relevant evidence basis. No authority follows merely from upload/read/correction rights, an email match, folder membership, task assignment or possession of bytes. Where a contribution is indivisible, show its actual bounded member scope; do not split it or expand to a whole multi-file item silently. Current accepted/frozen/terminal restrictions govern; historical acceptance alone is not an invented permanent ban, but Q47 cannot undo acceptance to create eligibility. Checking or staff-waiting state alone does not forbid withdrawal.

### U02 — One narrowly named tenant control (H / plausible)

Provide **Recipient self-service withdrawal**, initially Enabled for the actually qualified ordinary cohort, in existing source/P12 tenant configuration. Only an actually authorized source administrator can change it. Disabled or unknown availability prevents new recipient withdrawal admission and offers a capable source route; it does not roll back prior withdrawals, hide lawful history, suppress original-result reconciliation, disable Q03/readback or independently qualified re-offer/resupply. Order Disable and withdrawal at commitment. Later Enable does not execute old intent or revive a stopped command automatically. No per-file custom rule builder, parallel permission engine, new tenant mode or mandatory staff approval stage is required. A configured default is not proof of current eligibility.

### U03 — Explicit staff assistance without impersonation (H / plausible)

Admit a separately scoped **staff-assisted withdrawal** of the same pending participant contribution as an explicit new ordinary-source capability. Require the same target/current acceptance/freeze/closure/candidacy invariants plus the staff actor's current action power. Record the actual staff actor and the source-bound cause, not a fabricated participant action or mandatory free-text essay. A participant help message alone does not authorize the mutation. Participant self-service Disabled or participant access expiry does not itself remove independently qualified staff authority; actual source restrictions still apply. Q28's staff-added removal does not supply this new power. Staff recovery uses existing qualified Q28 to add **new Staff-supplied Document Evidence**, with actual origin/actor/current target and any genuinely required participant assertions; no new staff participant-re-offer or Restore power is inferred for symmetry.

### U04 — Receipt remains; current review consideration ends (H / plausible)

At one authoritative source boundary, commit the exact withdrawal, attributable history, current evidence-basis change and required work/effect intent. The withdrawn contribution cannot remain a current acceptance candidate behind a hidden row. Preserve original receipt/time/terms, other members and independent uses, existing decisions and required retention/holds. Withdrawal is neither No longer required, refusal, cancellation, acceptance, fulfillment nor deletion. Actual remaining requirement and Q21/Q22 decisions alone determine work/completion. Late checks or stale projection refresh cannot reintroduce the withdrawn candidate; independent safety/records processing retains its own scope.

### U05 — Review, correction and lifecycle ordering (H / plausible)

Order withdrawal against acceptance, correction/new admission, relevant requirement changes, source closure/freeze, P12/current assignment changes and tenant Disable through the owning boundary. Withdrawal-first fences stale acceptance; acceptance-first cannot lose a member through a later ordinary withdrawal. Corrected/superseded targets cannot be silently retargeted. Preserve an already-open exact staff reading context, indicate changed consideration, and require a current relevant basis before review submission; opening or scrolling is not a review lock. Relevant dependency changes require resolution; unrelated files or harmless projection refresh do not create a global work lock. Withdrawing a correction never automatically restores its predecessor.

### U06 — Honest pending, retry and original-result recovery (H / plausible)

Distinguish local selection/transfer, unknown original receipt, received contribution, pending withdrawal, committed withdrawal, known failed/blocked and unknown response. Reconcile original receipt before targeting a received act. Recover a matching already-committed withdrawal before applying fresh current-state eligibility, under current result-disclosure authority. Stable operation identity must not retarget the current file, create a duplicate history/effect or report false failure because the target is now withdrawn. Changed intent, relevant stale basis, or a different target is not a retry. Leaving/closing a page after final action does not prove cancellation. Late old-context responses cannot reveal protected data or overwrite a newer projection.

### U07 — Small, explicit participant interaction (M–H / likely for affected users)

Use a visible contextual label such as **Withdraw submitted file** with exact permitted file/item/receipt context. Before the final deliberate action, explain the actual unit, that it leaves current review, whether remaining evidence is still required, and that it does not delete the received record or recall copies. An inline consequence review with Cancel is sufficient; no compulsory second modal, typed filename, reason, preview/download or replacement. If a source already has a qualified optional explanation field, keep it optional and within its own audience/retention contract; do not add a new narrative field merely for Q47. Selecting a menu item, changing a field or opening a preview must not itself commit withdrawal. Cancel before submission preserves local context and makes no target/business effect, while required protected-read/security evidence remains governed.

### U08 — Persistent source-safe outcome and metadata (H / plausible)

Show a durable source-visible **Withdrawn from review** receipt status, actual remaining action and any currently qualified recovery control after refresh/return. Explicitly qualify the minimal withdrawal/status/attribution and exact-target metadata projection needed for this ordinary journey; this is not a previous-file archive or new original/preview/download grant. Staff and participant projections use their own current reveal rules, including no-Party recipients. Required internal history may exist when its contents are not revealable. If allowed metadata cannot safely identify the contribution, do not provide blind confirmation, leak a hidden filename or withdraw the entire item; provide the capable scoped remedy. A missing/expired preview alone is not a prohibition when action identity and authority are otherwise established.

### U09 — Permanent practical remedy: offer the retained exact file again (H / plausible)

For a mistakenly withdrawn participant contribution, provide **Offer this file again** while the source currently permits a fresh participant supply/re-offer. This is a new source act, explicitly linked to the exact retained original version/contribution and withdrawal, in the same allowed request/item/recipient scope. Preserve original receipt and withdrawal; record the new deliberate actor, current admission time, current requirement/instruction/target basis and processing state. Do not fabricate a new upload, copy an old approval, overwrite original lateness, change a deadline/access period or describe the operation as restoring history. Reuse proven lawful durable bytes without needless transfer or preview; reuse current proof/check evidence only when its owner actually qualifies it. Only explicitly permitted post-admission processing may remain Checking. Retained bytes alone are not reuse authority. Changed cardinality, current replacement, requirement, authority or safety must be resolved before admission; no silent additive overflow, replacement or predecessor revival. Selecting this control does not itself admit the new act.

### U10 — Recovery remains truthful across later stages (H / plausible)

Do not use a disappearing toast or invented Undo timer as the sole recovery route. Re-offer is conditional on current source rights and retained usable material, and can remain available after the toast/preview disappears while that qualification persists. If fresh participant supply is no longer allowed, show the actual allowed source/help route. Qualified staff may use Q28 current reuse/admission where it truly applies, with staff attribution; neither expiry nor self-service Disable grants such power by itself. Accepted/frozen/fulfilled/canceled, disposed, redacted, unsafe, restricted or genuinely insufficient-evidence cases require an actual capable source/privacy remedy before the supported cohort activates. No generic Contact admin placeholder, direct SQL, fake reopened work or unnecessary new request counts as recovery. A separately justified new request is only for a real remaining need under its normal authority, not a rollback or privacy repair.

### U11 — Access, inspection and privacy are independent (H / plausible)

Apply actual current request proof/action scope and original/fresh finite access bounds; a withdrawal/re-offer does not renew them or create a Q41 share. Reuse permitted current proof without unnecessary enrollment or fresh challenge, while preserving actual assurance needs. A participant can withdraw without seeing bytes when the exact target is safely identified; a required source-specific affirmation on resupply must have its own meaningful qualified presentation rather than blind consent. Withdrawal does not promise deletion, no previous viewing, recall, or blanket historical access removal. Actual privacy/incident restrictions, lawful historical staff inspection, disposition and independent uses retain their owners. Keep sensitive file names, bodies, private explanations, tokens and incident details out of unqualified errors, logs, URLs, notifications and unrelated audiences; preserve independently qualified source contacts/envelopes/carriers under their own contracts.

### U12 — Current work and existing effects, without new notification policy (H / plausible)

Update/fence current Q24 source work against the actual evidence set; do not create a task per withdrawal or declare a review completed merely because a file disappeared. Explicitly extend the existing Q27 opt-in source-actionable review cohort to a genuinely newly actionable participant re-offer on its new actual evidence basis, preserving Q27's qualified plan/cohort binding, Off rule, material, deduplication and current usefulness. This is a required integration amendment, not authority inferred from vendor mail. Do not relabel it as the original upload or duplicate its original notification occurrence. Staff supply follows Q28's already qualified Q27 extension. Withdrawal alone updates work and does not generate a withdrawal notice; it does not itself cancel Q40 awareness of a requirement that still applies. Any earlier Q26/Q27/Q40 or other qualified effect uses its real purpose, original binding and current usefulness. This review selects no new notification purpose/default, bell, cadence, in-product acknowledgement, reply/Resolve gate or global Off rule. Exact existing-purpose reconciliation is required before activation; uncertainty cannot be papered over with an arbitrary send or no-send policy.

### U13 — Accessible, localized, low-bandwidth completion (M–H / likely for affected users)

Use existing Base UI/base-maia controls with complete keyboard/voice/screen-reader operation, meaningful accessible names, visible focus, adequate touch target spacing and non-color-only states. The target/consequence/Cancel/final action/recovery remain readable at narrow reflow and zoom; long Unicode/RTL names must not hide the distinguishing context. Render untrusted names/text safely. Optional input must respect IME and not submit unexpectedly on composition/Enter. Do not require a large preview, desktop or app download. Announce pending/result/error changes without repetitive alerts or focus theft; return focus meaningfully when the selected current row changes. A failed refresh must not erase a known committed result. Respect reduced motion and do not make transient animation the only state cue.

### U14 — Lean bounded storage, work and dependencies (M–H / plausible)

Reuse source/custody/P12/P9/shared UI commands and their existing durable-effect paths. There is no Q47 need for a new provider, workflow engine, background purge, broad Trash center, global archive, autonomous retry timer or notification service. No database writes on menu opening, keystrokes or pointer movement; independently required reads/security audit remain. Bound exact contribution membership, result/history projection, policy evaluation, retries and source work by qualified measured profiles. Actual new deliberate withdrawal/re-offer cycles are separately attributable and subject to existing lawful intake/abuse budgets, not an invented universal cooldown. Avoid copying bytes or rescanning the whole tenant. Retention is governed rather than lengthened forever for a convenience recovery button.

### U15 — Operational proof, migration and source-owned support (H / plausible)

Before activating this cohort, prove authoritative every-writer source enforcement, actual coarse Tenant RLS and P12 fine rules, privileged/service/browser/API/storage paths, current actor/scope, operation/result replay and relevant races. Preserve exact withdrawal/correction/re-offer lineage through migrations, stale workers, restore and deployment rollback; restoring storage cannot revive current candidacy or expired rights. Use minimal protected audit facts to explain who acted, on what exact source use, and which original operation committed. Support must reconcile unknown results and execute only scoped legitimate remedies, never edit history, impersonate the recipient or grant itself authority. Quantify finite limits against declared cohorts; vendor examples and adjacent Delete controls are insufficient proof.

### U16 — Keep selection, amendment, ratification and delivery distinct (M / plausible)

Q47 A is selected; the exact proposed tenant control, staff-assisted action, source-safe withdrawal metadata and retained-file re-offer must be explicitly reconciled with their source owners as amendments. No new glossary term is necessary solely for these familiar actions unless the owner proves a distinct enduring concept. Q01–Q46 and existing terms remain intact until actual adoption of a reviewed package. This note closes neither native implementation nor user qualification, and makes no measured demand/benefit/cost claim. The strongest B remains documented, even though A was selected. Formal publication and tickets remain separately authorized stages.
