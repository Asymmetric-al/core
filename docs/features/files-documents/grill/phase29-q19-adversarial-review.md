## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

Two staff actions could create overlapping periods, an old approval could
execute after an intervening period, or repeated grants could widen scope and
extend retention. Fix the exact context, predecessor, effect and owner fences.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That current purpose can justify repeated consideration and the native source
can prove the original ceiling/current lineage. Neither measured demand nor
qualified runtime exists in the evidence reached. Do not disguise those gaps.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It supplies one missing source repeat policy without changing completion,
financial truth, identity, official documents, storage custody or other rights.
Potentially long aggregate reading is real; general storage entitlement is not.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

People can receive later help in the same completed context. No arbitrary
quota/cooldown or forced account is needed. Show actual outcomes and repair the
right problem; never require another grant just to fix still-valid entry.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Current sources support governed reconsideration, separate delivery recovery
and clear contextual status. They do not prove unlimited repetition is optimal
or authorize importing mutable provider-link semantics into Core.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes, as an explicit Q08/Q16–Q18 successor using source/P12 fences. Equal
unfinished/read periods do not merge domains. The UI remains shared Base UI/
base-maia and the feature remains relevant contextual file use.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Keep A. Make the absence of a preset aggregate cap explicit, preserve current
purpose and exact scope, prevent concurrent/latent repeats and keep one simple
action. A separate renewal engine, policy menu or extra approval tier is unjustified.

## Corrected decision to record

Allow the exact currently source-authorized staff actor to approve another
fresh completed-read period after eligible ordinary expiry of the relevant
preceding period, within the same lawful Q16 completed-admission context and
intended recipient. Retain original admission proof, actual ending causes,
relevant predecessor/current lineage and today's purpose/scope restrictions.
An ancient expired root or an absent active-row result is insufficient.

Each new deliberate admission uses Q18's up-to-720-hour source-issued profile,
including unused access. There is no preset lifetime grant-count or cumulative
duration ceiling, mandatory waiting gap, automatic renewal, pre-expiry
extension or reservation for later issuance. No overlapping fresh read periods
may exist in the same tenant/environment/source/request/recipient/original
completed-admission context, even for different subsets. Other recipients and
independent rights retain their own rules. Do not terminate a valid period
merely to make room for another. This permits a long cumulative
sequence but promises neither another approval nor uninterrupted availability.

Every fresh reviewed subset is bounded by Q16's fixed completed ceiling and
current eligibility. A narrower discretionary prior subset does not permanently
shrink that ceiling; a broader prior grant cannot widen current rights. Genuine
restrictions retain their scope. Keep the request completed and read-only;
preserve original fulfillment, submissions, receipts, corrections and retention.

Recover a matching committed deliberate intent on its original terms under
current disclosure before evaluating it as a new request. New source effects
require atomic current-predecessor/nonoverlap/authority checks at the existing
owning source/P12 boundary. A stale uncommitted intent cannot move through an
intervening grant and become approved merely because that grant later expires.
Do not park an ineligible command for automatic future issuance. Ordinary
source waits still require qualified current checks; add no browser-click clock.

Reuse **Grant read access** and the existing completed context. Staff review
the actual eligible purpose, subset and timing terms once; material changes
require refreshed review, ordinary time passing alone does not. Show the actual
source result/end and current actions with accessible localized status. Separate
new admission, current use, proof, representation, communication and uncertainty.
Use compatible qualified proof without unnecessary re-enrollment, but do not
resurrect old expired credentials by rebinding them to the latest grant.

Keep this flexible through current scope selection, no arbitrary quota or
cooldown, useful help and preserved received work. No mandatory essay, routine
second approver, new request queue, date picker, renewal dashboard or automatic
notification is added. Existing genuine source/P12 requirements remain; a
completed review cannot overrule a real prohibition or make retained files a
general archive.

## Ownership, data invariants and lifecycle

The source owns the completed admission, deliberate grant effects, relevant
lineage and actual ends. P12/Auth owns exact administrative/use capabilities,
proof and applicable continuing dependencies. Custody owns originals/renditions/
retention/disposal. Communication owns any separately authorized delivery.
Caches, browser views, provider credentials, worker claims and current-head
projections are not independent write authorities.

Structural and mutation obligations are inherited, with these repeat-specific
invariants made explicit:

- Exact same-tenant/environment/source/request/recipient/completed-root/member/
  original-version relationships; required finite end/policy/actor evidence.
  Validate primary/foreign keys, nullability/defaults and semantic uniqueness;
  caller-supplied actor/owner/tenant/approval attribution is not authority.
- At most one overlapping fresh read period per fixed completed context.
  Different keys, staff actors or disjoint subsets do not bypass the rule.
  Prove empty-next-row cases; an empty SELECT FOR UPDATE locks no missing row.
  Use the existing sole mutation fence and compatible constraints, not a
  time-varying partial index, a worker lease or a new lock framework.
- Original effect identity, reviewed material input, source occurrence, end,
  scope and historical meaning cannot be overwritten to create a repeat.
  A source-maintained head/version may support bounded access but must remain
  coherent with authoritative effects and later adverse facts.
- Current permission is more than an end comparison. Check exact recipient,
  purpose, member/action and continuing grant-type dependency at use. Issuer
  administration is checked at issuance; neither blanket survival nor blanket
  revocation on every issuer role change is inferred.
- Preserve history required for safe replay/eligibility under its owner policy.
  Deletion, cascades, compaction, import and restore must not turn missing
  evidence into a new lineage. This is not an unlimited plaintext log-retention
  mandate; no credentials, tokens or private contents belong in general logs.
- Test effective SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE, USING/WITH CHECK, permissive policy
  composition, column grants and view/RPC/definer/service/job/storage paths.
  Omitted WITH CHECK may inherit USING; owners/BYPASSRLS need explicit scrutiny.
  An allowed update/upsert/parent move must not retarget or widen a grant.
- Source completion, evidence acceptance and retention clocks do not advance
  with repeat access. No money precision change is introduced. Existing P12
  logical governance budgets and documented exceptions remain; they are not a
  new end-to-end latency promise or a hidden lifetime eligibility quota.

| State / transition                  | Required meaning                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reviewed, not dispatched            | Staff can leave without a source effect. A preview of timing is not issuance.                                                                                                                   |
| Confirmed no source admission       | A confirmed pre-admission failure/rollback creates no grant. Retry must still satisfy current material source rules; transport failure alone proves neither rollback nor absence.               |
| Outcome unknown                     | Reconcile the stable intent. Do not say approval failed, enable an unrelated duplicate or invent an end.                                                                                        |
| Committed deliberate effect         | Preserve its exact original result and required audit/follow-up. Delivery/proof/preview may still fail independently.                                                                           |
| Currently permitted use             | Source-bound metadata/bytes pass current proof, purpose, scope, dependency and exclusive end. No provider credential or browser state substitutes.                                              |
| Eligible ordinary expiry            | Another independently reviewed Q19 decision may be considered. No auto-renewal, backlog reservation or minimum gap; gaps do not become retrospectively authorized.                              |
| Adverse ending/current restriction  | Keep actual cause and scope; nominal time passing does not wash it away. Independent unaffected actions retain their own proof; remediation stays with the owner.                               |
| Historical replay or restored state | Recover the old effect under current disclosure without implying current access. Reconcile head/history/later restrictions before new issuance or serving; never fall back to the latest grant. |

The request remains completed in every row. A new source effect, not a new
task status, owns each period. Three uncapped periods could total 90 elapsed
days of fresh grant windows; there is no selected finite aggregate maximum.
That arithmetic is illustrative, not usage evidence or guaranteed useful time.
