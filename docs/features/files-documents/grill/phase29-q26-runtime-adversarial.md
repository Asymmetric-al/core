## Result and smallest sound interpretation

The selection can fit the existing architecture. Keep one ordinary Q23 review command, its explicit reviewed optional-email condition, the exact source occurrence and the existing P17 bounded Delivery Plan/P6 communication path. Tenant configuration selects among that meaning-specific contract's declared recipient-role/step/channel choices; it does not invent recipients, grant private document access or publish a new transport. Qualify the exact native binding before activation. No second notification system, generic audience builder, private-file email attachment, reminder scheduler or new inbox is needed.

The tenant steering is a material clarification to the original single-recipient question. The complete effective consequence must be understandable in the review. An external recipient email checkbox controls its declared condition; it is not a switch for every independent required or optional staff/in-product step. If the source contract offers other recipient roles, give each role only its independently permitted facts/action, not the external recipient's private guidance or credential. Q26 does not itself select a new role list, channel adapter, reminder cadence, retention number or required staff notice. P17 qualification and current source authority remain the bounds.

This is compatible with useful source-help notices after submission access has ended when their exact contract and current recipient authority permit them. Do not introduce a blanket active-upload-only email rule, and never send stale instructions or manufacture a fresh access grant merely to make a message useful.

## Material risks and required amendments

Severity is the consequence **if a future native implementation copies the identified pattern or omits the boundary**. Likelihood is qualitative exposure under normal retries, configuration changes or concurrent work, not a measured defect rate. None is presented as an exploited or deployed native vulnerability.

### R01 — Configuration mistaken for recipient or disclosure authority

**High severity; plausible/high exposure with tenant expansion.** Finance `approval-notifications.ts:213–242` enumerates admin/super_admin profiles for financial approvers; the outcome at `:624–655` targets the requester. Neither is a generic source-authorized document audience. A same-tenant actor or newly chosen team is not automatically entitled to request existence, sensitive guidance, filenames or a participant action. A tenant plan-manager and review actor are also distinct capabilities.

**Required clause:** who/how means only current source-qualified recipient roles, independently resolved people/contact and contract-declared channels/steps. Plan publishing requires its actual current authority; review requires its own source capability. Routing/notification configuration never grants source/file access. Cross-tenant/environment, wrong purpose/item/recipient and unknown membership fail safely. A finite team role must not become all administrators, a free-form To/CC list, shared-mailbox identity equivalence or broad tenant permission union.

### R02 — One checkbox hides a multi-recipient or multi-channel consequence

**High privacy consequence; plausible.** P17 allows a complete bounded plan, whereas the original Q26 question presented one optional recipient email. Calling the control “Email recipient” while silently emailing additional staff or overriding other steps gives false assurance. Conversely, turning it off must not delete a separately required source step.

**Required clause:** preserve the exact control's declared condition and explain the effective permitted consequence in the same review, including other relevant independently configured steps without exposing unauthorized addresses/rosters. Show safe role/count/state summaries when exact identity is not viewable. Each role receives its own approved content/action; no recipient-list disclosure or reuse of the external participant credential. Source in-request guidance remains independent and is not automatically a new P6 in-product notification. Tenant who/how does not instantiate undeclared channels, cadences or reminders.

### R03 — Decision commits but selected notice intent is lost or misreported

**High reliability consequence; high exposure to ordinary failure.** Financial decision→audit→reload→hook ordering (`correction-requests.ts:369–415`) plus separate task/notice writes (`approval-notifications.ts:608–694`) does not prove atomic native source/notice persistence. The generic best-effort audit logger is not required business evidence.

**Required clause:** source outcome, reviewed choice, immutable occurrence and required handoff evidence must have a proven coherent durable boundary. Provider work remains outside source database locks. If P6 release is completed by a later worker, the committed source must retain an exact recoverable handoff and pinned/provable material, not reconstruct a new choice from defaults. No dispatch before authoritative source commitment. Crash/unknown outcome recovers original effect rather than issuing another review or deleting possibly committed work. An email failure after known review commitment preserves that review and current guidance.

### R04 — Same-effect replay changes choice, while later genuine reviews are suppressed

**High correctness/privacy consequence; high retry exposure.** Financial same-outcome replay (`correction-requests.ts:315–330`) invokes a hook that re-reads current preferences. Dedupe uses request+approved/rejected (`approval-notifications.ts:653,661`), a legitimate financial terminal-decision shape but not Q23 review cycles. A previously email-off native occurrence must not acquire mail after settings change; a later new Request changes occurrence after correction must not collide with the first.

**Required clause:** permanent native identity binds the exact authoritative review occurrence, source/item/review/evidence context and reviewed material, with complete plan/member identities below it. Resolve an existing authorized committed effect before attempting a new review against current pending state; replay returns its original choice/effect under current disclosure checks and does not reinstall obsolete guidance. Same key/material change conflicts. A new source review occurrence is distinct; arbitrary new transport keys are not evidence of one. Distinguish explicit user-selected material from server resolution outputs: a pre-admission legitimate resolver change is handled under source review/current-plan rules, not automatically labeled user intent tampering; once material is durably bound it cannot be recomputed opportunistically.

### R05 — Partial fanout, silent truncation or zero/unknown conflation

**High privacy/reliability consequence; plausible with expanded roles.** Existing finance bulk upsert and per-row sending are not P6 complete-plan proof. `insertApprovalNotifications:310–311` maps an unexpected non-array result to empty; selected outcome preferences catch failures as off. Neither demonstrates an authoritative zero-recipient decision.

**Required clause:** one bounded P6 compiler admission for the complete source occurrence, no loop of independently committed logical children. Independently derive and validate all applicable recipient-step members and excluded conditions; lock/uniquely coordinate the permanent occurrence, atomically record exact ordered membership and release last. No child is eligible until release. Unknown resolution/lookup/partial pagination is not zero. A valid zero records requested choice and exact reasons, not staff-off. Overflow rejects visibly before writes; do not silently cap, truncate or chunk the occurrence. Concurrent identical calls converge, changed/disjoint sets conflict, and a later plan/role/contact widening cannot append children to an already released result.

### R06 — Stale source/plan/recipient checked before waits or only once

**High privacy/correctness consequence; routine race exposure.** Financial recordDecision uses tenant/id/pending CAS and client-process timestamps (`:193–223`), not the native exact evidence/source/plan fences. Current plan or role state can change while resolving recipients or waiting for locks. A task status or cached review form is not authority.

**Required clause:** new review/source admission must preserve Q21–Q25 exact evidence, actor/current permission, responsibility and source-state ordering. The notice compiler/adapters must fence the relevant source/plan/generation and exact recipient state at the owner-required boundary, including after waits. Immutable history and current dispatch permission differ. Record qualified source occurrence times; do not backdate authority from browser time, worker receipt or a transaction-start timestamp after a wait. An SQL timestamp is not physical commit time. A known original effect remains historical even if the reviewer later loses authority; that does not authorize new delivery or disclosure.

### R07 — Pending message becomes misleading after correction, acceptance or expiry

**High privacy/correctness consequence; plausible in active work.** Direct finance sender has no native Q23 source-currentness checks. A Request changes occurrence may cease to be the current next action before preparation or submission; access may expire while the source guidance remains legitimately useful for help.

**Required clause:** independently reprove current applicability, recipient/contact, sender/channel, restrictions and safe action at preparation/submission/use under the exact owner contract. A correction, acceptance, fulfillment, closure, return supersession, disposal or revocation must not yield stale correction instructions or an invalid credential. Use only a declared truthful source variant where permitted; otherwise record non-applicable/blocked state. Do not mutate sealed prepared content into a different purpose or silently change recipient. Do not impose a blanket upload-active prerequisite where a qualified help variant is lawful. No notice, click, delivery, retry or credential repair resets access, grants a new period or changes review/fulfillment truth.

### R08 — New-row-only delivery loses work; provider key is treated as permanent proof

**High reliability consequence; plausible/high failure exposure.** Finance `:279–315,665–694` sends only newly inserted rows. A crash after row commit but before send is not distinguished from a row already delivered. Its test at `contribution-approval-notifications.test.ts:1398–1445` explicitly assumes the existing email row was recorded **and sent**; it does not prove the opposite crash point. Sender comments at `approval-notification-email.ts:150–153` must not be read as unbounded exactly-once proof.

**Required clause:** preserve actual P6 preparation/submission/attempt evidence and recover only the original authorized work under its exact immutable payload/key/current conditions. Definitely unsubmitted, possibly submitted, provider accepted and delivered are different. Unknown submission cannot be “repaired” by a fresh key or blind resend after a provider window. Resend's documented key retention is 24 hours, not permanent source idempotency; inherit P6/ADR0032 bounded retry/reconciliation rules without inventing another mechanism or timing limit. Already accepted mail cannot be recalled or proved read.

### R09 — Callback correlation or late events rewrite another recipient's truth

**High consequence; plausible asynchronous exposure.** Existing webhook verifies signature (`resend.ts:602–619`) and persists events, but payload tenant metadata is preferred (`:261–291,543–575`). Its status mapper folds sent/delivered/opened/clicked/delayed into sent and bounced/complained/suppressed into bounced (`:370–383`), then updates by tenant+message (`:737–758`). That is a coarse existing projection, not exact native ordered attempt evidence.

**Required clause:** authentic provider events must also correlate to durable exact connection/account/environment/message/attempt/member evidence; metadata, signature or common email alone cannot authorize cross-source mutation. Duplicate/out-of-order callbacks preserve append-only evidence and legal monotonic projections, never revive suppressed/cancelled source work, add recipients, change the optional choice, release an unreleased plan or mark feedback read. Missing/ambiguous/unavailable correlation is explicit and recoverable. Sanitized status/diagnostics must not leak raw callback contents, credentials, contact data or private guidance. This is a native copying warning, not a demonstrated attack against the deployed finance handler.

### R10 — Service-role or UPDATE paths bypass immutable tenant/source/recipient binding

**High severity; plausible if API-only assumptions are copied.** Finance migration has useful same-tenant NEW-row reference checks and browser revocation; it does not by itself define immutable native actor/review/source/recipient/material relationships. Its trigger applies on selected INSERT/UPDATE columns, and `GRANT ALL` remains available to service_role. The actual admin client constructor uses service-role authority.

**Required clause:** prove effective SQL grants, all applicable RLS policies and old/new-row transformations under real roles, plus RPC/view/trigger/worker/service/storage paths that can reach these records. Enforce trusted same-scope relationships and native immutable identity/choice/material through appropriate database and command boundaries; child validation is not parent-retarget immutability, and a nullable profile being deleted is not a new recipient. Privileged code must not trust tenant/actor/source/role/member lists from the browser. Do not claim missing explicit WITH CHECK alone is a vulnerability: UPDATE/ALL may inherit USING, permissive policies combine by OR, restrictive by AND. Definer executes with owner privileges; actual bypass depends on role/ownership/FORCE/BYPASSRLS, not the keyword alone. Test production-like grants and direct service paths separately; source-text assertions are insufficient.

### R11 — In-request-safe text or shared email becomes an outward private disclosure

**High privacy consequence; plausible.** Finance copy includes decisionReason and donation ID (`approval-notification-email.ts:107–117`), and profiles are used to find destinations (`:195–214`). HTML escaping does not grant permission to expose that information in an email preview, provider log, forwarded message or staff-role notice.

**Required clause:** use the exact qualified per-role minimal render DTO and protected action. External request-only recipient identity remains Q01 source-bound; no fake Party/donor/profile membership, shared-email identity merge, arbitrary To/CC, raw notes/filename/file attachments or direct storage links. Contact changes use actual source identity/current-contact rules, not a silent new recipient. Credential material stays out of browser editor state, logs, URLs/query, analytics/history/recent copies according to protected-action rules; GET/HEAD/scanners do no business work. Required credential repair may create new communication/prepared identity under the same lawful source review, not mutate old content or add a fresh review/grant. Exact optional external-recipient prepared-material/Recent-copy class needs qualification; an access duration is not mail retention.

### R12 — Release, restore or growing history silently sends an old backlog

**High privacy/reliability consequence; plausible operational exposure.** Finance rows cascade with parent deletion and carry little delivery state (`20260611150000:27–42`); handoff ledger has useful tenant/key/recovery indexes but generic key reuse is not native semantic proof. A migration that defaults every old nullable choice to true, restores dedupe rows inconsistently or re-runs current tenant rules could email historical returns.

**Required clause:** activate only qualified native source/cohort/bindings; preserve existing review-only/unknown/omitted history without inferring consent or choice. Restore retained source/release/attempt identities coherently and fail safe when provenance is missing; no new source event, plan widening, forced release or replay solely from redeploy/import. Keep minimal lawful immutable proof/tombstone where owner retention requires permanent duplicate prevention, with actual erasure rules; do not retain bodies indefinitely. Use bounded indexed lookups/recovery with authorization before counts/pagination and finite plan expansion. No full event-history scans per review or one workflow/task per potential recipient. Qualification must prove at-bound/over-bound and growing-history behavior; no invented capacity number is chosen here.
