## Finding and recommended question

The final ordinary-native completion policy is genuinely open. ADR-0021 explicitly selects neither automatic whole-request completion nor an additional Complete control. Q06/Q10 define lawful fulfillment and its consequences; they do not select the user interaction that produces that outcome.

**Once all current conditions of the source's complete ordinary request rule are satisfied, should the source fulfill it without a separate routine staff action, or require a final authorized staff Complete request action?**

| Proposed choice                                                        | Coherent meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Benefit and consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A — Source fulfills when its whole rule is satisfied (recommended)** | The ordinary source has an explicitly qualified completion policy that records actual fulfillment when the complete current rule is met. Q21's meaningful staff evidence acceptance and every other source condition remain. No redundant routine final click is required. | Less duplicate administration after the real work is done. The last meaningful action's possible completion consequence must be clear. It closes ordinary intake/correction and can admit the existing completed view only when its access predicates hold.              |
| **B — Final deliberate staff Complete request action**                 | Satisfying the other conditions makes the request ready for a separate source command. An actor with the exact current completion authority deliberately invokes it; the source rechecks the full rule.                                                                    | A distinct whole-request reconciliation/handoff can have real value. It adds an unfinished waiting state even after items are accepted. A delayed actual completion may fall after participant authority ends; the click cannot bypass criteria or backdate fulfillment. |

Recommend A as a bounded product judgment: Q21 already requires substantive human evidence acceptance. Do not add a second routine checkoff when no separate work remains. B is the strongest alternative if final reconciliation/handoff is itself meaningful source work. This recommendation has no measured Asym workload or usability basis. It does not claim competitors favor A.

Do not introduce a per-tenant/per-request auto/manual mode menu to evade the choice. Existing specialized owners retain their approvals and fulfillment predicates; neither answer generalizes to application approval, finance, official documents, CMS publication or every Workflow task.

## Consequences to disclose in the question

1. **Necessary item acceptance is not sufficient whole-request truth.** Include the source's real requiredness, applicable exact evidence, authorized non-file outcomes and other declared conditions. Do not infer completion from upload counts, all visible green rows, historical accepted flags, an empty query, draft/canceled state or missing definitions.
2. **One source meaning and writer.** A selects a qualified automatic consequence within the ordinary source. It does not authorize a task projection, storage callback or arbitrary Workflow rule to manufacture fulfillment. B requires the exact authorized source command, not a generic staff label or a new universal second reviewer. No schema, lock layout or new service is selected.
3. **Real occurrence, not a retrospectively inferred timestamp.** The last item acceptance can be part of a lawful source progression that also records fulfillment, but Q22 does not mandate a physical transaction layout. A later consumer observing all conditions cannot simply set fulfillment to the last acceptance time. Delayed recovery requires evidence that fulfillment actually occurred lawfully at that earlier time.
4. **Currentness at the actual boundary.** Required evidence, pending correction, current requirement/recipient and applicable adverse changes can defeat a queued/stale completion attempt. Preserve exact independent item acceptance where still applicable; do not demand reupload/reacceptance to repair a failed panel refresh or message.
5. **Completion closes ordinary mutations.** Under A, the final meaningful action may close intake/correction immediately through the source. Under B, accepted items remain accepted while the source awaits its explicit final action; the still-unfinished request does not reopen accepted-item corrections. Neither answer chooses post-completion remediation/reopening policy.
6. **Business completion and recipient read admission are separate.** Both answers keep Q06–Q19. Source work can finish after participant access ends without a current participant browser/session, but that outcome must not revive an expired unfinished grant or promise a new completed read period. Existing qualified recovery of a genuine timely occurrence remains possible.
7. **No hidden neighboring choices.** Named reviewer/pool, bulk acceptance, negative outcome taxonomy, extra confirmation/person, reminder cadence, completion email, new waiver powers, renewed access, tenant configuration and retrospective rewrite are not selected. Q20's fresh-access email default does not become a completion notice.
