## Recommendation and one genuine choice

Recommend asking whether authorized staff should have a direct **No longer required** action for an outstanding, unaccepted item in an otherwise eligible unfinished ordinary Private Document Request. This addresses an item requested by mistake or made unnecessary by a legitimate change. It does not address inadequate evidence: Q23 already supplies Request changes for that different problem.

**A — Allow No longer required, recommended.** The exact requesting source may deliberately stop requiring this item under its actual authority. Keep the item, any received evidence and attribution/history; show a distinct truthful outcome rather than Accepted. Remaining genuine requirements continue normally. If this was the final outstanding requirement, Q22 evaluates the complete current source rule and may fulfill the request when that rule genuinely permits it.

**B — Keep resolution in the existing source path.** Do not add the new ordinary-item action. Staff use an already-qualified source requirement-correction or resolution path with a capable accountable owner. This is stronger where an organization deliberately keeps the issued checklist fixed and changes obligations through a controlled business process. It costs more navigation and may require staff assistance. It must not promise a generic Cancel, reopen, replacement-request or reissue capability that has not been selected.

A is the better product judgment because it gives staff a truthful way to stop an unnecessary demand without asking for dummy evidence, clicking Accept on absent evidence, deleting history or leaving the participant in an endless correction loop. Its cost is a real new source authority and the risk of incorrectly removing required work; that risk needs exact eligibility, current review, attribution and honest completion consequences. B remains credible rather than a deliberately broken alternative. There is no need for an artificial third option.

The question is about **whether to provide this bounded ordinary-source power**, not whether File Manager owns business truth. That ownership is already settled. Do not present generic waiver tooling, whole-request cancellation, participant refusal, terminal evidence rejection, arbitrary requirement editing or notification policy as part of the same choice.

## Required boundaries if A is selected

These are review obligations and proposed product boundaries, not a prematurely ratified specification.

1. **A real requirement decision.** Record that the exact ordinary item is no longer demanded for this request purpose, with trusted staff/source attribution and the relevant requirement/instruction/context basis. Do not hide it, mutate received evidence to Accepted, equate file removal with requirement removal, or silently change every request using a template.
2. **Narrow eligible scope.** Start with an outstanding unaccepted item in an otherwise eligible unfinished ordinary request. Earlier accepted evidence/decisions and completed requests retain their own history and existing remediation owners; Q33 supplies no hidden unaccept, reopen, retroactive cancellation or predecessor revival. The label does not override nonwaivable specialist/source obligations or prove a broader application/financial outcome.
3. **Current actual authority.** Qualify source requirement-change/removal authority independently of inspection, acceptance, assignment, generic edit and recipient-access administration. Do not invent a permission name or extra reviewer merely to make a diagram complete; preserve actual applicable source controls and separation of duties.
4. **Nonvacuous fulfillment.** Removing the final outstanding item may complete the request only when the complete current explicit Q22 source rule truly permits it. A filtered/empty list, failed lookup or undefined requirement set cannot do so. All-items-no-longer-required is not automatically either fulfillment or cancellation: lawful complete empty/non-file fulfillment requires the actual source predicate and continuing eligible lifecycle. A whole request whose purpose ended cannot be laundered into successful fulfillment through repeated item removals.
5. **Preserve evidence and other duties.** Keep received history, source-required audit and independent file uses/holds/retention. Safety/quarantine, unresolved incidents and any independently required review remain their owners' work; removing this demand cannot label unsafe bytes clean or erase them. Do not demand a dummy upload or convert a failed preview into this business decision.
6. **Current participant work.** No longer required stops new ordinary submission/correction for that no-longer-demanded item under current source admission, while other eligible items remain usable. It does not automatically erase previously received content or grant new history/read access. An upload already transferring is not received evidence; admit or deny its final source step according to the actual ordered boundary, preserve any earlier durable receipt and safely reconcile uncertain finalization without deleting shared bytes.
7. **Order genuine races.** Requirement removal, acceptance/Request changes, participant or staff evidence admission/withdrawal, relevant requirement edits, Q30 relevant clarification and Q22 closure share the owning currentness boundary. A stale human decision cannot silently reinterpret a newer basis. Removal-first and admission-first both preserve truthful original results; delayed workers/replay cannot restore an obsolete requirement or emit stale follow-up instructions.
8. **Do not invent an unanswered gate.** A relevant received clarification can invalidate an older affected human decision's reviewed basis under Q30-C04. 9. **Honest single action.** Show the item, the fact that evidence will not be treated as accepted, and the qualified possible completion consequence in the existing request context. Use source-approved participant-safe explanation where applicable. Keep internal reasons/history separate from independently authorized participant-facing status and contacts. No routine essay, modal, approval or new waiver queue should be inferred; the selected-answer review must resolve proportionate explanation and mistaken-action recovery without silently adding powers.

No separate task completion or direct row update can counterfeit the decision.
11. **Existing access and communication clocks.** Q06–Q19 original/fresh access and Q22 actual fulfillment timing remain. Removing a requirement grants no fresh participant access and does not restart a deadline. Q26's Request changes notice, Q31's staff-reply notice and Q32's participant-message notice are not this event; no new email/bell/reminder/default follows from A. Existing queued work must remain subject to its actual current source-purpose rules rather than being rewritten to a new meaning.
12. **Qualification rather than extra machinery.** Later source/P12/P9/custody and applicable Workflow owner integration must prove same-scope relationships, immutable history, exact current authority, real-role/RLS/alternate entry points, races, recovery, current safe views and finite workloads.
