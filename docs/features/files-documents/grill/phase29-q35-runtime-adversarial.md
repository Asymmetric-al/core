## Verdict

**Accept with required amendments.** The useful fact is that this exact ordinary request was canceled, not that all document work ended or that a reply/access grant awaits. The primary risks are sending to a known-wrong former address, confusing mail authority with Q34 web access, re-resolving recipients/plans after the source act, treating provider uncertainty as safe retry, and allowing Disabled→Enabled to revive stopped historical work.

Tenant control must separate **whether the optional capability is available** from **the initial staff choice while available**, without prescribing extra UI machinery. System default Enabled+On is consistent with A; authorized tenant publication can make the initial choice Off or disable availability. A later default change cannot silently change an already reviewed explicit choice. Disabled is a stronger stop with the explicit Q35 pending-work semantics below.

## Exact proposed amendments

### C01 — One new cancellation-awareness meaning

Explicitly add one optional participant cancellation-awareness purpose for a genuinely committed Q34 C occurrence. C is its positive source fact, not a reason to suppress this purpose through a generic parent-canceled guard. No failed/abandoned cancellation, fulfillment, item removal, review reply, expiry, task status or provider state creates it. Keep Q34 native authority/terminal fence and existing Q20/Q26/Q27/Q31/Q32 meanings unchanged. The notice never cancels a broader application/payment or creates participant awareness as a business fact.

### C02 — Current recipient and separate mail/web authority

Resolve exact source cancellation-disclosure authority, intended recipient and eligible contact through source/P12 and the matching closed P6 branch. Q01 intended-participant proof, old invitation, profile address, provider raw input, creator/reviewer role or address equality is insufficient. Explicitly qualify any no-Party branch in the exact manifest/adapter; no fabricated Party/contact, fallback recipient or caller To/CC. Preserve original recipient/contact authority identity and revision, with current continuity proof and narrowing before each permitted boundary. Removed/recreated identity or a changed address is not continuity by identifier equality. Known-wrong old recipient means no notice to that address; a corrected contact must not silently retarget old work.

Email authority and Q34 web-outcome authority are independent. Expired/no web admission neither grants nor universally prohibits a separately lawful factual notice. A valid web root alone never authorizes the disclosure by email. A protected CTA uses only the exact surviving Q34 outcome root/end/current restrictions; no new canceled admission or completed period. Otherwise use an explicitly qualified linkless/safe-help variant where still truthful and useful, not a worker-invented fallback or dead upload link.

### C03 — Tenant flexibility and explicit staff choice

In existing bounded P17 administration expose Q35 availability Enabled/Disabled and, while enabled, the initial staff choice On/Off as distinct semantics. System default is Enabled+On. A compact grouped control can implement this; no prescribed two-toggle layout, general mode system, per-recipient preference designer or new platform is required. Only authorized tenant publishers may change it, under current tenant/source scope and existing publication review.

An initial-choice edit seeds genuinely new cancellation reviews; it must not silently flip a reviewed explicit choice, resurrect an original staff-Off occurrence or reinitialize a recovered committed cancellation. Missing/legacy/ambiguous input never means On. Staff may turn the current optional choice Off, but On never overrides Disabled/current source privacy, contact, suppression, utility or delivery readiness. Keep effective destination/consequence visible and preserve lawful choice/context through recovery. Default provenance and the human-reviewed choice are distinct from the actual applicable plan pinned at source admission: do not grandfather stale authority merely because an older review seeded On. After admission, original plan/material pins stay immutable.

### C04 — Native C survives optional unready work

Lawful native cancellation must remain available independently of optional mail. Preserve the original staff choice and truthful notice outcome; requested On but blocked/unavailable is not staff Off. Do not silently substitute a new recipient or falsely claim a combined successful send. When a known material destination/eligibility change is detected before the deliberate action, expose it inline and offer a clear lawful cancellation-only path without routine extra ceremony. Transport delay with valid durable handoff can proceed. After native commit, blocked/failed/unknown mail never erases C or asks staff to cancel again.

Commit C, mandatory source history, original choice and required source-owned handoff facts coherently. Recoverable unknown mail needs trustworthy original-boundary evidence of applicable policy/binding/recipient basis, or an authoritative means of proving that exact original basis later. No today's-settings repair, guessed empty cohort or false Off. If original basis cannot be proved, native C remains valid and historical notice remains blocked/no-send. Known no-mail because staff Off/Disabled/non-Live/no-lawful-recipient need not manufacture P6 work. Failed actual cancellation produces no notice. A second caller finding the already-canceled request cannot append mail to an earlier no-mail choice.

### C05 — Complete release and permanent semantic identity

Order source admission with applicable policy/binding/recipient changes and retain the resolver-issued original basis. Use the existing one complete bounded P6 plan-occurrence entry point, including a proved zero only with exact top-level Live authority. All member slots validate before any is claimable; no partial child loop, late appended member, parent rekey or recreated notice after activation. Unknown/Reserved original authority cannot create a parent or turn into a retrospective Live event. Current delivery safety narrows; it never resolves a new audience/plan for an occupied occurrence.

Keep native cancellation identity, communication occurrence/member identity and provider envelope/key separate. They persist beyond transient transport keys/leases. Exact replay recovers original result under current disclosure; changed immutable material conflicts. Duplicate clicks, canceled-page refresh, tenant default/availability re-enable and new observers create no new business or mail event. A later legitimate producer-authorized successor/correction requires its own actual contract; no new Notify/Resend product is implied. Provider-safe retry of an eligible existing operation is not new source cancellation.

### C06 — Explicit Q35 Disabled stop, no resurrection

As a Q35-specific amendment to general P17 future-only edits, tenant publication of **Disabled** stops affected original optional notice work at every actual provider-submission/call admission, including unresolved original handoffs, prepared work and later identical same-key calls. This is distinct from setting the initial staff choice Off, which seeds future reviews and does not silently cancel an existing explicit choice.

Order Disabled against the exact sealed submission/attempt fence that records Submission may have begun before first byte. Disabled-first permits no call; admission-first preserves that already-admitted operation and possible in-flight truth, without recall fiction. After Disabled wins for affected work, no later same-key follow-up, new-attempt call or new-key replay is allowed, even when the first operation remains uncertain. Evidence-only reconciliation continues. Re-enable permits genuinely new eligible C occurrences; it cannot revive old stopped/Off/zero work. Preserve provable intervening Disabled history/fence so a worker cannot miss an Off→On cycle. Do not rewrite original plan, sealed material, recipient, actor or provider outcome. Existing unrelated message purposes retain their own rules.

### C07 — Provider evidence and exact prepared recovery

Only exact immutable server-loaded prepared material and envelope may reach the provider. Re-prove current source/mail recipient/contact/privacy/suppression, utility, tenant availability, content/action and matching delivery authority at the governing boundaries. Provider acceptance, delivery lifecycle and native C are independent. A timeout, malformed response, lease expiry or duplicate webhook is not definite rejection/no effect. The sealed attempt phase never moves backward.

Follow the current P17 closed classifier and identical-envelope bounds: only the three permitted indeterminate causes can make at most two follow-up HTTP calls total after the initial call, before the earliest member/material and pinned provider-window deadlines, with current reproof and evidence reconciliation first when available. The explicit Q35 Disabled stop can prohibit even those otherwise permitted calls. Never rekey/replace accepted or indeterminate work, split an ambiguous envelope or re-resolve a changed destination/locale/sender/content after preparation. Sufficient definite-rejection evidence permits only the exact contract-approved next attempt under all live fences. Exact signed/provider connection/member evidence reduces monotonically; unknown remains truthfully unknown, not Sent/read or native failure.

### C08 — Minimal useful fact and safe action

Use only source-approved minimal cancellation fact and safely disambiguating context for this exact canceled request. Exclude internal reason, evidence/filenames, transcript, attachments, care/location details, unapproved free-text title, credentials and unrelated source/recipient data. Sender/relationship metadata also requires authority. It must not imply all future document work or an independently issued replacement ended, nor claim nothing was received/deleted/accepted.

A later remedial request or participant view alone is not a universal notice stop; current exact purpose and finite utility determine whether the original fact remains safe/useful. Conversely permanent cancellation truth is not unlimited send permission. Qualify and pin a finite product-owned usefulness rule, anchored to the actual original source occurrence or its explicitly defined timing contract, never queue retry/first visit. A protected CTA must remain lawful at use and cannot renew Q34; an approved linkless/help variant is a deliberate contract choice, not implicit dynamic downgrade. If exact useful identification cannot be disclosed safely, do not send a confusing notice.

### C09 — Separate material, copy and history ceilings

Before Live activation select and prove the exact closed optional external-participant material posture through P17's catalog generation. Current donor-financial30d and optional-staff7d classes are not applicable by analogy; no artificial bell/staff audience is added. The reviewed finite ceiling must be no longer than current notice utility/action authority. Seal the earliest class, intent/utility, protected-action and shorter source deadline; never extend it on repair, default/availability change or retry. Apply existing terminal-use stops, immediate decryption denial and primary/cached-material purge within24 hours, retaining body-free evidence. Reconciliation after purge cannot recover body from provider, Recent copy, logs, support or backup.

Readable Recent copy is a separate optional convenience: shorter-of tenant policy and exact eligible class ceiling, with denied/unknown/incompatible posture set to no readable copy. Exact Party/contact/source/capability reveal requirements remain; Q01/no-Party authority alone cannot satisfy them. Do not fabricate a Party or block otherwise valid native cancellation/email solely for copy convenience. Permanent communication history stays body-free; source C history remains source-owned. Preserve governed retention/erasure and current restrictions on restore, including safe independently qualified contacts/envelopes/protected carriers rather than a blanket egress ban.

### C10 — Structural integrity and effective authorization

Derive tenant/environment, actor, source C, recipient and applicable policy from trusted context. Validate exact same-scope/source relationships among C, choice/handoff, plan/member, preparation/envelope, provider connection and history. Enforce immutable original fields, valid states, permanent slot/material uniqueness and required non-null authoritative facts. Caller strings, email hashes and individual UUID existence do not establish authority; no cross-owner/default profile fallback or reparenting.

Test complete effective grants, RLS SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/UPSERT old/new-row behavior and policy combinations, trigger transforms, views/RPC/definer functions and service/admin/job/import/repair paths. Service-only grants do not substitute for exact command checks. Omitted WITH CHECK alone is not a demonstrated bypass; its inherited/combined semantics must be tested. Protect publication/default/Disabled transitions and outcome/reveal/cache/search/count access separately. Privacy-safe diagnostics cannot contain prepared bodies, raw addresses or credentials except the exact restricted material contract; no money mutation or new financial precision policy follows.

### C11 — Usable and finite operations

Use the existing cancellation review with one visible optional choice/effective destination, clear native-only path and separate persistent native/notice states. No routine message wizard, required essay, second reviewer, arbitrary recipient picker, automatic reconnect send or private offline archive. Preserve permitted focus/input/choice on recoverable failure; clear forbidden content and delayed results across tenant/user/source changes. Prove mobile/keyboard/AT/IME/RTL/locale/slow-network behavior and comprehension of tenant default versus Disabled versus explicit staff choice.

Keep existing source/P6/P17 recovery/operations, bounded indexed proof lookups, finite supported fanout, fair tenant scheduling, source/provider work outside short locks and measured latency/recovery/purge budgets. No extra ledger/queue/inbox, per-poll business event, infinite repair loop or whole future Studio dependency. Operators distinguish committed C, original choice, original Live/unknown/no-mail basis, release, blocked/suppressed/expired/prepared/unknown/provider evidence and material expiry without requiring private bodies. Critical authority/duplicate/privacy/missed-Disabled failures are stop-and-requalify defects, not merely monitorable warnings.

### C12 — Prospective activation and safe restore

Reconcile exact Q35 source/P12 identity/disclosure, Q34 protected action, P17 optional controls/material/purpose and P6 release/provider rules through canonical owners and full incorporated OpenSpec. The tenant-changeability preference applies through supported bounded contracts; this note does not silently rewrite earlier ratified message defaults. Native producer, original authority proof, complete release, optional-unready path, finite utility/material posture and tests must qualify before an exact Live generation/cohort is enabled.
