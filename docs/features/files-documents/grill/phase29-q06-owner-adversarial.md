# Phase29 Q06 — bounded completed-request readback: owner adversarial review

Owner-focused research, 2026-10-20. Conrad selected **A — Keep a bounded read-only completed view** and requested the full adversarial standard. This note recommends refinements for the review package; they are **not yet ratified**. Q01–Q05 and their complete amendments remain ratified. No formal specification, product/provider change, test run or GitHub mutation occurred.

## Disposition

**Accept with required amendments.** Normal source-confirmed fulfillment ends ordinary recipient submission/correction while preserving only currently authorized current-file preview/original download and concise participant history, until the source access end or an earlier relevant restriction.

The design must not reduce completion to a mutable boolean, turn “read-only” into a ban on necessary preview/audit operations, or let repeated completion/login/recovery extend access. Historical completion evidence and later current validity also need separate treatment.

The strongest alternative remains confirmation/status/history only after completion. It reduces continuing byte exposure but removes the already-selected current-file capability during legitimate follow-up. Ending all protected access is also coherent but increases dependence on another authorized route. A is a defensible product choice, not measured proof of universal recipient preference.

## Important findings

1. **Normal fulfillment needs exact source evidence.** A generic Done/Closed flag, number of uploads, clean scans or task completion cannot prove it. The source must identify the applicable request/requirement revision and its exact qualified result/evidence. Q06 does not invent the acceptance/waiver criteria.
2. **Completion and correction need a common ordering boundary.** If Q03 correction admission changes a member first, stale acceptance/completion cannot use the predecessor. If source completion wins first, an old form/upload cannot append a new ordinary submission. Source-held bytes and original received acts are not erased by losing the later race.
3. **Read-only does not mean no technical writes.** Authorized preview preparation/regeneration, access audit/metering, bounded delivery recovery and qualified help may still be necessary. They must not mutate evidence, grant access, reset clocks or reopen work.
4. **The finite access policy is an activation gate, not a chosen duration.** No number, anchor or renewal rule is selected. Missing/null/unknown policy must not become indefinite access; completion and reauthentication cannot silently mint a fresh period.
5. **Historical completion is not perpetual current validity.** P34 explicitly separates completion evidence from later invalidation/remediation. A later safety restriction or source correction may make a file unavailable or require new work without rewriting what originally completed. Do not silently import new files/recipients into the old completed view.
6. **Valid authentication does not restore expired source authority.** Session renewal and proof recovery must separately recheck the exact current source grant. Staff may complete source work after recipient access has expired; that does not restore the recipient's access.

## Proposed exact required clauses

The O identifiers are research traceability, not formal requirement IDs.

**Q06-O01 — Exact successful fulfillment.** Enter the ordinary completed mode only from a typed, authoritative native source result proving its normal fulfillment predicate against the applicable request/requirement/policy revision and exact relevant evidence. The source decides permissible acceptance/waiver meanings. Upload count, scan success, a task checkbox, deadline passage, generic Closed status and client fields cannot establish fulfillment.

**Q06-O02 — Preserve completion evidence.** Record the exact outcome, source time, relevant scope/evidence references and attributable source authority as durable history. Present historical completion separately from current safety, read eligibility and any later source outcome. Do not reconstruct completion from current rows after the fact or rewrite its evidence when a file, request instruction or Party later changes.

**Q06-O03 — Read-only business mode.** Ordinary recipient submission/correction and other ungranted business mutations are unavailable after normal fulfillment at every command boundary, including open old pages, direct APIs, batch paths, retried finalization and workers acting on stale participant intent. This does not deny separately qualified preview preparation/regeneration, audit/metering, bounded delivery preparation/recovery or help. Those supporting operations cannot alter evidence/acceptance, renew authority or reopen work.

**Q06-O04 — Continue only current permitted reads.** The mode can show current source-approved status, Q05 concise history and independently qualified Q02/Q04 actions for exact eligible current own-submission members. Being completed is no permission grant. Every metadata/list/count/history/preview/original/range/conditional/recovery request intersects current native identity, exact recipient/request/purpose scope, source state, safety, classification and applicable policy.

**Q06-O05 — No additional audience or content.** Preserve the completed request's exact source/recipient/member relationships, including independent supporting files. Do not turn the mode into all files uploaded by a user, a new recipient's history, superseded-byte access, staff assessments or a personal archive. An independently admitted use of the same bytes retains its own rights and obligations; none are pooled.

**Q06-O06 — Finite explicit access contract before activation.** The source must supply an effective versioned policy that yields a finite access end for the affected protected actions, with a qualified authoritative anchor/precision/time interpretation and current restriction behavior. Q06 selects no numeric period or anchor. Missing, invalid, unresolved or unqualified policy cannot activate an unlimited completed view. Do not invent P18's guest/session values or P34's suggested expiry as defaults.

**Q06-O07 — No incidental clock reset.** Normal completion, repeated completion delivery, source projection rebuild, login/session refresh, opening history, preview regeneration, download, help, cache recovery or browser close cannot reset an established access deadline or manufacture a new grant. Preserve the original semantic policy/anchor evidence under retry. A genuine later source renewal/extension requires its own explicitly qualified authority and policy; Q06 does not select that policy or an extension UI.

**Q06-O08 — Authentication and source access remain separate.** A shorter session may require the existing qualified entry/proof path. After successful authentication, recheck the exact current source authority before revealing the completed page; login alone cannot resurrect expired/revoked access. Expired invitation/challenge, expired browser session, source access end, changed recipient proof and unavailable source checks have distinct safe recovery. Do not manufacture membership or broaden mailbox-based access.

**Q06-O09 — Completion does not revive earlier access loss.** Staff/source work may legitimately complete after participant access ended. Preserve that source result without exposing it through an expired grant or minting a new one. If a current source grants only a narrower projection, show only that projection. No universal post-expiry status/history, generic completed badge or detailed denial is available to unauthorized visitors.

**Q06-O10 — Completion/correction/acceptance have one source order.** Re-prove the relevant request revision, current item/member review set, required source outcomes and applicable authority at completion admission. If correction wins first, stale predecessor acceptance or completion fails/re-evaluates. If completion wins first, ordinary late submission/correction cannot enter. Preserve multi-file scope and real aggregate dependencies without a gratuitous global lock. Local transfer start is no priority and does not reserve access.

**Q06-O11 — Retry preserves accepted effects.** A lost completion/correction response or delayed task/history projection returns/reconciles the same durable source result under current disclosure rights. Do not duplicate completion events, create another request or ask for re-upload because secondary delivery failed. Temporary uploads that never gained source admission remain governed staging; already admitted source evidence is not erased by later completion.

**Q06-O12 — Later invalidation is explicit source work.** A later safety finding, changed evidence validity or legitimate owner correction can restrict current reads and require an attributable linked correction/remediation/successor transition under its own source contract. It does not silently delete historical completion, convert the old page back to an ordinary open request, or reset access. Do not require a full Workflow engagement for the simple native source; preserve each specialized owner's exact remediation semantics.

**Q06-O13 — Later content changes do not silently retarget.** A completed-view reference/download stays bound to the specific selected received version. New files, a corrected owner artifact, recipient reassignment or revised requirement cannot silently appear under the old selection or acquire its read authority. Any source-approved change to the current member set needs its explicit compatible source command and fresh recipient/purpose qualification while preserving the original completion evidence. Absent that qualified contract, restrict the affected route and use source help/remediation rather than guessing.

**Q06-O14 — Security and other terminal causes remain distinct.** Cancellation, withdrawal, final rejection, compromise, security revocation, privacy restriction and source access expiry do not inherit normal-success continuation. Apply each source's exact affected action/session/representation fences. A cannot create a grace period, weaker fallback or promise that a disclosed end date protects access against an earlier authorized restriction.

**Q06-O15 — Existing full-path delivery and expiry still hold.** Reauthorize protected metadata and every new full/HEAD/range/conditional/resumed/recovery request. Preserve Q04 pre-emission exact-copy identity, whole-original permission, private caches/headers, per-chunk long-stream expiry and feasible termination of affected in-flight delivery. A completed page, prepared download or signed URL is no lasting authority. Emitted/local bytes cannot be recalled and a partial browser file is not full success.

**Q06-O16 — Processing uses its appropriate authority.** A participant session ending must not cancel independently source-authorized scanning, retention or reconciliation of received work. Conversely, a valid worker cannot renew recipient access, promote content for an ineligible purpose, admit a stale participant correction or bypass current source restriction. Preview/output cleanup and other independently admitted uses retain their own qualified lifecycle.

**Q06-O17 — Truthful calm completed UX.** In the same request surface, show the source-approved completed outcome and next step, including “No more files are needed” only when currently true. Remove ordinary submission/correction affordances; keep eligible read actions and concise history. Show the actual permitted access-end date/time with clear local/time-zone context once its policy is adopted, without a false guarantee against earlier restrictions or an unselected countdown/email/renewal program. Status must distinguish current source invalidation or unavailable checks from an unchanged success claim.

**Q06-O18 — Independent failure and safe help.** History/preview/provider failures do not themselves undo completion, extend access or disable independently valid current work. Unknown current source/authorization fails closed for affected operations. Q05's accountable source help remains available only through its permitted purpose/projection; after access loss, it cannot leak old private context, promise renewal, automatically email files or reopen the request. A generic unavailable link is not proof of a working recovery path.

**Q06-O19 — Retention and communications are not completion side effects.** Completion/access-end/read/help neither deletes retained evidence nor extends retention/releases holds. Current restrictions and governed disposition remain separate. Reading or completing this mode creates no new email, reminder, task, CRM interaction, signature or evidence acceptance unless a separately qualified existing source contract requires that distinct effect. P17 prepares authorized presentation; P6 alone dispatches and records communication outcomes.

**Q06-O20 — Scoped adoption, migration and restore.** Adopt the explicit native fulfillment/read policy and finite access contract through existing source/P4/P12/egress/custody owners before enabling a cohort. Inventory all commands/readers/deep links/old grants and retained records. Do not backfill normal fulfillment from generic Closed or last-updated timestamps, retroactively renew expired cohorts, or apply the policy to specialist sources. Restore current restriction/expiry/identity/disposition evidence before serving; rollback cannot reopen writes, revive grants or erase completion history.

## New amendments versus preserved decisions

**Selected new direction:** bounded read-only current-file/status/history continuity after ordinary successful fulfillment.

**Required Q06 refinements for ratification:** exact source completion evidence and race boundary; the narrow meaning of read-only including permitted supporting operations; finite source-access contract and nonrenewal invariant without picking duration; historical completion versus later source invalidation/content evolution; safe re-entry/recovery and all-path enforcement.

**Inherited:** Q01 confirmation/current native authority; Q02 preview and independent processing; Q03 exact immutable correction; Q04 original delivery/streaming; Q05 minimized history/no old bytes/help; existing source retention, communication and specialized domain meanings.

**Still unselected:** the actual access period, clock anchor, renewal/extension policy or UI, other terminal outcomes' native policies, new notifications, universal timeouts and retention rules. The finite policy remains an activation dependency to resolve next, not permission to ship an unlimited placeholder. No separate generic lifecycle or workflow platform is required.
