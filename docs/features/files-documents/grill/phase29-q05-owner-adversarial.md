# Phase29 Q05 — current files and source-authorized submission history

Owner-focused adversarial research, 2026-10-20. Conrad selected **A — Current files plus concise submission history**. This note recommends the required amendment package for the full review; those refinements are **not yet ratified**. Q01–Q04 and their complete amendments remain ratified. ## Disposition and strongest alternative

**Accept A with required amendments.** The permanent design is one small participant history projection over authoritative request/submission/correction facts. Current eligible files remain the work surface. The history explains durable receipt and correction without exposing superseded file contents or turning audit storage into a participant API.

The strongest alternative is B: deliberate, separately qualified historical-file reading within the request. B would help someone inspect exactly what was sent before correction and may reduce staff exchanges. A consciously trades away that ordinary self-service capability; it must not pretend a timestamp answers a question about an old scan's contents. B is not inherently a Drive or unsafe. Conrad has chosen A, so help/recovery must not covertly implement B.

The important changes are source/API behavior, not merely a collapsed timeline: deny superseded-byte paths across representations, minimize the history fields, preserve exact currentness during asynchronous history failure, and provide a real source-owned help route without granting historical bytes.

## Material findings and required clauses

These O identifiers are research traceability only.

**Q05-O01 — Define the selected scope precisely.** In the ordinary native request, show current eligible submission members and a concise currently source-authorized receipt/correction history. Do not offer ordinary preview/download of superseded submissions. Superseded means the exact member in the explicit correction lineage whose current review candidacy ended; an older independent supporting file may still be current. No blanket newest-file-per-request rule.

**Q05-O02 — Enforce A at the source, not just the UI.** Deny superseded-byte access through current-file APIs, byte IDs, thumbnail/preview URLs, full/range/HEAD/conditional/recovery paths, hidden serialized fields, prefetch, search and cached results. Do not send the old content or reusable URL and merely omit a button. Preserve Q04's exact-version and in-flight rules; already emitted local copies are outside recall, and independent specialized source rights are unaffected.

**Q05-O03 — One closed participant projection.** Derive history from typed authoritative source receipt/correction facts, with a deliberately small source-owned field contract. The default explanation needs the permitted event kind, event-time display, exact item/member relationship and its current/earlier meaning. Historical filenames, actor identities, reasons, raw IDs, sequence counts, technical outcomes and source associations are not default-safe merely because they are metadata. Additional fields need an expressly qualified source projection, not arbitrary JSON or a raw audit relation.

**Q05-O04 — No context or purpose union.** Use Q01's compatible native human/request admission and the sole P12 resolver for this participant purpose. A person who is also staff, an administrator, reviewer or audit reader does not receive the union of those histories in the recipient surface. A staff/evidence view retains its separately authorized purpose. P12 D42 is a precedent for this separation, not a file-history permission grant.

**Q05-O05 — Current identity continuity is explicit.** Bind reads to tenant/environment, source request, item/member, principal and exact recipient/purpose revision, with relevant inner scope. Mailbox equality, provider email verification, a shared login, Party merge/unmerge, principal relink, recipient reassignment or copying a request cannot retarget earlier history automatically. Preserve immutable event-time attribution; apply only source-qualified current identity/projection continuity. No account, donor or Party is fabricated to make history resolve.

**Q05-O06 — Durable acts, not transport anecdotes.** `File received` and `Correction received` come only from authoritative admitted source outcomes. Local selection, upload progress, abandoned staging, provider callback, queued scan, duplicate retry and an email notification are not new received submissions. Preserve the original admitted event identity/time across retry and projection rebuild. A correction's source fact, current-review change and required history/effect intent keep Q03's atomic boundary.

**Q05-O07 — Causal source order.** Order correction lineage by authoritative relationships/source sequence, using source time for display rather than treating browser clocks, file modification time, provider upload time or timestamp ties as proof of precedence. A row's current/earlier meaning derives from the current source state, not its position in a list. Multiple independent members remain distinct. Do not expose a global revision number that reveals withheld events.

**Q05-O08 — Current work survives a history-only failure.** If current authorization and the current-file/source facts are sound, history unavailability must not hide that work, undo its receipt, re-enable an old file or ask for another submission. Show a scoped retryable history error while the current permitted actions remain usable. Conversely, an unknown current authority/currentness basis must not borrow stale history as permission. Distinguish “no authorized entries to show,” “history unavailable” and a qualified coverage limitation without revealing withheld history.

**Q05-O09 — No regression from stale results.** Bind returned projections and pagination to their exact source/context/permission generation. Late responses, delayed invalidation, reconnect, tenant/recipient switches or cached pages cannot restore a superseded current file, expose retired history or mix entries from another purpose. Reauthorize every page/refresh and refetch current facts as needed. A cursor requests a slice, never grants an immutable historical access snapshot.

**Q05-O10 — Minimal history is not an immutable UI snapshot.** Preserve source facts and necessary provenance under their governing owner; render their current permitted projection. Later privacy restriction may remove a filename or event detail from the participant view without falsifying or deleting the protected source event. History is not a shadow archive, guaranteed complete forensic record, file-content search corpus or forever-retained audit copy.

**Q05-O11 — Preserve Q03 and Q04 exactly.** An admitted correction immediately ends predecessor candidacy, even while new checks run. Failure of new checks does not revive the predecessor. The history must not label received as safe/accepted or reintroduce old View/Download as “recovery.” Current qualified View and Download remain independent of history loading; ordinary correction eligibility and reviewer exact-version fencing remain source-owned.

**Q05-O12 — A real source-owned help route.** Where the recipient needs clarification or earlier content, provide the existing source-approved coordinator/help route with an accountable responder and a safe request/item reference before activation. It can clarify received facts or the next required action through currently permitted projections. Asking for help grants neither old bytes nor staff evidence authority. Earlier content may be disclosed only through an independently qualified source/records process that already authorizes that disclosure; do not create an ordinary-history toggle or automatic delivery lane under A. If no such basis exists, explain the permitted outcome honestly instead of promising release.

**Q05-O13 — Help must preserve the private boundary.** Do not direct people to email attachments, paste private file contents, expose bearer URLs or create a second Support inbox/archive. The source controls whether an existing shared task/contact/message interaction is appropriate; P17 prepares any qualified outbound presentation and P6 alone dispatches. No history view creates an email, reminder, read receipt, task, CRM interaction, approval or source acceptance. If no qualified help route exists yet, it is a concrete implementation gate for that profile—not a fake clickable placeholder.

**Q05-O14 — Retention is independent of presentation.** A does not purge old originals or required audit, and showing a concise receipt does not require retaining every old preview, name, reason or original forever. Apply the source's approved obligations, privacy ceiling, hold and governed disposition to each family. Omit restricted/disposed details safely; do not claim the old file was deleted merely because the participant cannot open it. Neither a history view nor help request resets retention or access clocks.

**Q05-O15 — Source correction and backfill are not inference.** Migrate/backfill only from proved source identifiers and admitted receipt/correction records. Do not infer a predecessor from filename, digest, chronological closeness, current user ID or provider upload date. Unknown lineage/coverage stays explicitly unresolved within its safe source repair process; no invented event, guessed actor, fabricated date or mass deletion. A repaired projection reuses exact semantic identities rather than duplicating receipt/history.

**Q05-O16 — Safe deployment and restore.** Enable A only when all reader/byte/preview routes and old links enforce the same ordinary-current scope. Preserve existing specialized rights and evidence identifiers. Fence stale writers and restore current restriction/disposition/identity history before serving restored metadata or bytes. Feature rollback/disable cannot resurrect old participant byte access or delete admitted source facts. A hide-only client release is not a safe cutover.

**Q05-O17 — Calm, accessible history.** Keep current files, current errors and next actions prominent. Use a secondary semantic list/disclosure that explains a small number of source-approved event kinds in plain language; it is not a version graph, full audit viewer, fixed-count archive or new application shell. Preserve focus/scroll, avoid reannouncing the whole list on refresh, and support keyboard/touch/screen-reader/mobile/zoom/RTL/date-time needs. No clickable old-file name, misleading disabled Download button or decorative timeline is required.

**Q05-O18 — Bounded read and privacy-safe diagnostics.** Filter membership and fields before counts, ordering, pagination and serialization. Use bounded pages and indexed source-scoped queries; do not load the entire raw audit then redact in the browser. Apply P12's existing tiered audit/budget/egress semantics, not a duplicate permission or telemetry system. General logs, queue envelopes and analytics must exclude private names, text, addresses, tokens and restriction reasons. Exposed IDs/cursors remain non-authorizing.

**Q05-O19 — No broader domain effects.** File history cannot amend claim/application/official-document facts, signed agreement status, public/resource publication, business review, requirement satisfaction, closed-request continuity or source disposition. Its displayed summaries are projections of those owners only where explicitly permitted. Specialized staff/evidence history remains intact; A is an ordinary participant policy, not a global history ban.

**Q05-O20 — Declare actual completeness.** Deliver the selected current-work-plus-history experience with source-backed events, safe states and an owned recovery route. Do not call a mock timeline, generic history failure, version-history placeholder, raw audit table, old PR title or metadata-only browser snapshot proof of the feature. Canonical owner/OpenSpec adoption and real-role/source/browser proof remain required at their authorized stages.
