**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

Keep the normal interaction small: review Cancel request with a clearly visible
optional email choice, then show the actual cancellation and notice results.
Tenants can disable this notice or make the staff choice start Off. These are
bounded controls in existing System Messages administration, not a new
notification product. The exact communication authority and recovery rules
below prevent that simple interface from hiding unsafe behavior.

**Alternatives and validity.** B supplies the same optional capability initially
Off; it reduces overlooked outreach but can omit a useful stop-work cue. C adds
no notice and relies on Q34 plus a capable approved outreach route. Both remain
credible. A is retained because an organization ending an issued demand should
make useful awareness easy. The user's added tenant control makes Default Off
available without removing the capability and Disabled available without
affecting cancellation. This is a scoped product judgment, not empirical proof
that every ministry prefers mail. No staff/participant interviews, cancellation
frequency, omission rate, volume or time-saving measurements are claimed.

**Scope interpretation.** This answer makes Q35's availability and starting
choice tenant-changeable. Existing qualified document-message controls may be
shown together in existing administration, preserving their distinct meanings.
It does not silently rewrite Q20/Q26/Q27/Q31/Q32, make required security/legal
messages optional, or introduce arbitrary recipients/channels. Their earlier
ratified defaults and exceptions remain intact.

## Ratified corrected decision

> For a genuinely new, durably committed Q34 cancellation of an eligible ordinary
> Private Document Request, provide one optional minimal participant email about
> that exact cancellation. Start the qualified system configuration Enabled with
> the staff email choice initially On. The source owns the occurrence, exact
> intended recipient, disclosure eligibility and usefulness; cancellation authority
> alone does not authorize external communication.
>
> Authorized tenant administrators may disable or enable this optional notice
> and, while enabled, choose whether the staff option initially starts On or Off,
> through the existing bounded P17 plan/publication surface. Staff may request
> On only while Enabled and currently eligible, and may always decline this
> optional notice for an otherwise lawful cancellation. Initial
> choice changes seed new reviews only; they do not overwrite an already
> initialized visible choice or change pending mail. Missing or ambiguous input
> is never On. Preserve the actual applicable plan at cancellation admission,
> with choice/default provenance kept distinct from delivery authority.
>
> Show the effective option, safe destination and consequence in the existing
> cancellation review. A known material destination or eligibility change before
> admission requires inline revalidation and a deliberate lawful cancellation-only
> path; do not silently substitute a recipient or conceal an unavailable notice.
> Cancellation and its immediate intake fence remain independently usable.
> Preserve required source history, the actual notice choice and original-boundary
> handoff coherently; email preparation, delivery or response failure cannot undo
> cancellation or require canceling again. Unknown original proof remains unknown
> and can recover only from trustworthy original facts, not today's eligibility.
> If original notice authority cannot be proved, keep cancellation valid and
> the historical notice blocked without sending or fabricating a zero result.
>
> Release one complete bounded P6 occurrence under exact top-level Live authority,
> including a proved zero only when that authority and zero are established.
> Retain immutable original plan, recipient and material pins and permanent
> semantic identity. Reserved/unknown binding creates no P6 parent and later
> activation cannot catch up old cancellations. Retries cannot add recipients,
> new mail, a replacement request or a newly chosen policy.
>
> As an explicit Q35-only exception to general future-only plan edits, published
> Tenant Disabled stops affected historical notice work at each actual sealed
> provider-submission admission, including unresolved original handoffs and later
> retry calls. Disabled-first denies admission; admission-first preserves the
> admitted operation's possible in-flight effect. Preserve intervening Disabled
> history across re-enable, even if no worker ran while disabled. Re-enable permits
> genuinely new eligible occurrences; it never revives old Off, zero or stopped
> work. Preserve evidence-only reconciliation, immutable pins and no recall promise.
>
> Independently prove current cancellation-disclosure/contact authority. A former
> invitation, address match, staff role or surviving web grant is insufficient;
> do not notify a known-wrong former recipient. Portal expiry alone neither
> authorizes nor universally prohibits a useful factual notice. Prefer a qualified
> informational message requiring no action, with only enough safe context to
> identify this canceled request. Any protected return remains within Q34's exact
> existing finite outcome-only admission; no new grant, renewal, file/transcript
> access or automatic replacement redirect follows.
>
> Re-prove current utility, recipient/privacy/contact, content/action and material
> authority at their owning boundaries. Exclude internal reasons, filenames,
> private source labels, evidence, conversation text and attachments. Qualify the
> exact optional external-participant material class and finite utility before
> activation; no borrowed financial/staff/web lifetime. Keep communication history
> body-free and use no readable copy unless its exact current reveal contract
> qualifies, without fabricating a Party or blocking lawful notice for that copy.
> Actual cancellation is this purpose's positive source fact, not a generic reason
> to suppress it; a later separate request is not an automatic stop either.
>
> Use existing source/P12/P17/P6 and base-maia/Base UI surfaces for concise tenant
> settings, one deliberate staff action and persistent truthful separate outcomes.
> Add no new inbox, notification engine, digest, reminder, extra channel, arbitrary
> audience, routine approver or whole future Studio prerequisite. Preserve Q34
> and all earlier meaning-specific notice contracts. Q35 adds no glossary term.
