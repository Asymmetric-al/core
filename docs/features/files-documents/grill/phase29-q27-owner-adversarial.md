# Phase29 Q27 — Opt-in staff review email: independent owner review

2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok. **Q27 A is selected; this complete amendment package is proposed, not yet ratified. Q01–Q26 remain ratified.** Scope is optional ordinary incoming-review staff email. No canonical or product change is authorized by this note.

## Disposition and precise meaning

**Accept A with the eight amendments below.** Default this exact optional staff-email step Off in a qualified native system/default Delivery Plan. Authorized tenants may enable it for future eligible source occurrences. Existing source/request/My Work projections, Q03 inline correction warnings, responsibility, current human review and source fulfillment remain independent and usable. Do not create a new bell event, per-submission toggle, task duplicate, reminder/digest or personal preference system.

The native source declares a meaningful occurrence for first or legitimately changed evidence that requires current ordinary human review on an exact source/item/requirement/evidence basis. It is neither a raw upload event nor every flip of a technical readiness flag. Bind the original effective notification policy at that authoritative occurrence through P17's trusted server-owned resolver. A later worker must not use today's On to send work that arose under Off. An unknown required original proof remains unresolved, without blocking valid source work or being mislabeled as Off/zero.

For enabled work, admit the exact finite concrete current source review-work audience and appropriate original generation/provenance; current checks can narrow it. A new assignee, team member, restored privilege or changed contact is not an extra child on an old email. Known Off can prove a complete zero-member result from the exact top-level source/plan authority **without enumerating recipients or contacts for the disabled step**. Email-only dependencies must not become receipt/review gates.

Recommend a new **Q27-specific** published-Off stop at the existing P6 submission-admission boundary: Off-first stops affected not-yet-admitted work, admission-first preserves possibly in-flight truth. Keep all immutable pins; later On cannot revive stopped work. This is an explicit proposed owner amendment to the general future-only plan rule, not silent inheritance from Q26. It makes the tenant's control understandable without introducing a cancellation engine.

The strongest alternative remains default On with tenant control. It helps intermittent reviewers who do not watch Asym. A is defensible because owned work already has a durable surface and repeated genuine submissions/corrections can be noisy, but no measured Asym engagement or review-speed evidence establishes superiority. Preserve B as credible; qualify real staff and tenant comprehension instead of claiming Off means staff were informed.

## Eight required incremental clauses

**Q27-C01 — Exact source work and stable recurrence.** Declare this ordinary native incoming-review email purpose for a real first or legitimately changed evidence basis requiring current human review under the exact source/item/requirement/purpose/evidence contract. Source admission/technical qualification/inspection/acceptance remain separate. Do not derive the event from every file/member, raw scan result, preview-ready flag, task materializer or status row; do not require all requested items first. The first authoritative qualifying occurrence for the same basis has one durable semantic identity. Re-evaluation, technical outage/recovery/readiness oscillation, duplicate signals, projection rebuild or restored connectivity do not create fresh email. A genuine admitted correction or source-relevant evidence change can qualify a distinct occurrence without duplicating the logical task or silently reusing old authority. Q03 immediate correction fence/warning remains regardless of later email readiness. If initial receipt was not yet source-actionable, a genuinely later first qualification is not falsely backdated to receipt; preserve the authoritative source occurrence and policy ordering.

**Q27-C02 — Off default and original-boundary policy.** The qualified native system/default plan disables this optional staff-email step; authorized tenant publication may enable future eligible occurrences. No per-submission staff/participant checkbox or personal preference center follows. A valid inherited system Off is known policy; missing/corrupt/unproved lookup is not automatically known Off, On or a successful empty result. The authoritative source occurrence must establish, or retain trustworthy provenance to recover, its exact applicable notification-policy/admission boundary through the P17 server-owned resolver and immutable binding context. Do not let a producer/client supply routing/plan authority or later async work select today's On for an old occurrence. When proof is available, coherently record the resulting original admission/pins. When required email proof is unknown, preserve valid source work and unresolved original handoff/recovery; reconcile the historical applicable result or remain truthfully non-admitted, never fabricate original permission. Later On, wider configuration, task creation or a new worker cannot add email to a known Off/zero or otherwise ineligible past occurrence.

**Q27-C03 — Precise source audience with only necessary proof.** For an enabled step, admit the source's exact finite concrete current review-work recipient set/generation: a qualified named responsible staff person or independently qualified concrete recipients for its team route. Membership, a task row, shared address, generic staff/read capability, source creator and historical reviewer do not supply the notice purpose. Require the exact source/P12 eligibility, contactability and privacy projections; keep source/admin/exercise/message-plan permissions distinct. Reuse canonical source responsibility, not a requirement that task projection has already materialized. Preserve original enabled-cohort provenance; later assignment/membership/contact/config changes can narrow serving but cannot append new people to the old occurrence or manufacture assignment email. For known Off, exact top-level source/plan authority can prove a complete zero without resolving unused disabled-step recipients/contacts. For enabled work, unknown/partial/stale/over-limit required cohort proof releases nobody and cannot become zero or a subset. Do not borrow Q01's participant-only authority as a staff-recipient fallback; qualify the actual staff delivery branch without fake accounts or permission grants.

**Q27-C04 — Coherent durable effect, complete release and no work loss.** Source facts and mandatory history/handoff intent are durable and coherent through existing source/outbox ownership; providers stay outside locks. Preserve the source occurrence's original effect identity and trusted policy/recipient provenance across retry, lost reply, transport-key expiry and worker replacement. Use the sole P17/P6 whole-plan compiler with complete finite candidate set, exact immutable bindings/pins, distinct zero and independently keyed children. An exact replay returns the original outcome; changed source basis/plan/recipient/condition/member set under the occupied identity conflicts, not appends/trims/rerenders. If optional admission is unavailable or unknown, independently authorized receipt/correction/review and required source/My Work warnings remain; do not invent source failure, duplicate task, new correction, new bell or acceptance merely to repair mail.

**Q27-C05 — Explicit current Off and source stop boundaries.** As an explicit meaning-specific Q27 amendment to general future-only plan-edit rules, published Tenant Off narrows this optional step's affected current dispatch eligibility at the existing qualified P6 submission-admission fence. Off-first stops affected work that has not crossed it; admission-first preserves potentially in-flight/unknown truth. Do not rewrite original plan/content/recipient pins or unrelated notice policy. Preserve enough existing owner stop/fence history that a later On cannot revive stopped work even if a delayed worker missed the intermediate Off; no new cancellation engine or bulk re-send is introduced. Current source correction, review/fulfillment/cancellation, recipient assignment/authority, privacy/contactability, sender/connection/action and applicable utility checks remain authoritative. Obsolete unstarted notices stop at the exact source boundary; no recall guarantee for admitted external work. Same-source temporary readiness/representation recovery is not a fresh notice or revival of a terminal notice.

**Q27-C06 — Honest independent outcomes and bounded recovery.** Existing source request/My Work and Q03 warnings remain authoritative and do not depend on optional mail. Distinguish known tenant-disabled/not-admitted, unresolved required proof, admitted pending, suppressed/non-applicable, definitely failed, provider-accepted/delivered and unknown outcomes. No staff per-action choice exists to invent or backfill. Current evidence/review/task state is not a provider result; delivered/opened is not read/reviewed/accepted and one person's email does not certify team awareness. Preserve committed source facts through later email failures. Follow exact P6/P17 immutable preparation, provider-window/retry and signed-correlation rules; unknown acceptance is not a new-key send or fallback authorization. Repair returns to the same lawful notice where permitted, otherwise keeps honest non-admission/terminal truth. Staff may still act under independent current authority after participant access expires; email never renews or reopens that access.

Ordinary staff can understand permitted effective policy without plan/roster/private-contact administration; setup is not a prerequisite to source work. Preserve body-free history, lawful source guidance/custody retention and current metadata/egress controls. **Q27-C08 — Native data, bounded operations and prospective adoption.** Prove same-tenant/environment source/item/basis/recipient-generation/plan relationships, permanent unique effects, safe explicit state/nullability, immutable original attribution/pins and guarded current projections across effective grants/RLS USING/WITH CHECK, views/RPC/service jobs and alternate paths. No new policy engine, per-request resident process, hardcoded quota/cooldown/digest or role fanout. Later authorized publication narrowly resolves Q24's notification reservation and Q03-C17 for this native staff purpose, separately reconciles C02's original-occurrence binding and C05's explicit Off amendment into source/P17/P6/ADR0026/OpenSpec, and preserves Q26's different recipient purpose. Qualify exact native keys/roles/recipients/material/channel/operations/user proofs before prospective activation. Old payloads, imports, current pending-work rebuilds, rollout/rollback and restore cannot manufacture notification eligibility or catch up historical mail. Exact native seams are required, not completion of whole future studios. No new glossary term is necessary.
