## Ranked frontier and recommendation

The highest-leverage remaining practical fork is **whether the ordinary product permits automatic disposition under an actual approved source policy, or always requires a fresh authorized records-operator release of an eligible proposal.** This is not a vote on holds, current authorization, complete usage coverage or deletion proof. Those safeguards are already mandatory under both choices. It determines who or what supplies the final ordinary source authorization for routine policy-eligible work and the corresponding operational burden.

Recommend **A — Allow policy-authorized automatic disposition when its exact source policy permits it**, retaining genuine source-required human review. The strongest alternative is **B — Require deliberate records-operator release for each bounded ordinary disposition proposal**, even when the governing policy would permit automatic execution. A finite reviewed batch may be supported; B need not be a ritual click on every file. Neither allows retention expiry by itself to delete anything.

## Why the ordinary fork is still open

Founder §14 and Q01–Q62 require source-directed retention/disposition, exact current policy/use/hold/copy checks, current mutation authority, verified physical outcomes and truthful recovery. They also forbid inferring deletion from an unrelated act such as removing a view, canceling a request, withdrawing a contribution, changing a policy or seeing no visible usage. They do **not** specify that all routine ordinary disposition requires a fresh human command, or that all policy-eligible ordinary bytes must be automatically deleted.

Q62 applies approved policy and supplies narrow assignment correction. Its C12 says actual owner disposition authority and complete current coverage govern destructive actions; C09 requires current successor impact or restrictive pending state. That does not choose whether a qualified ordinary policy can supply standing authorization for an exact automated source act. Correct assignment remains a different command and cannot serve as a Delete action.

The important counterexample is P23 D27. Its full current clause25 expressly selects retain-until-explicit-review, no launch auto-purge, no automatic disposition from a shorter policy, and one explicit source disposition authorization after all current conditions agree. That is already decided **for Media** and cannot be overwritten by an ordinary request decision. Conversely it is not an unspoken platform-wide rule that settles ordinary evidence. P18/P21 purpose-owned records precedents also do not own the ordinary mode.

Therefore both A and B can preserve existing authority while choosing a new narrow ordinary operating rule. A supports a policy-authorized automatic path only when the actual approved policy admits it. A policy that requires case-specific human determination still requires it; an unresolved policy cannot be treated as automatic authorization. B adds the ordinary product requirement for fresh human release wherever that is lawfully compatible with the source's duties. Neither option can promise compliant operation for a cohort whose required disposition deadlines, human decision requirements or safety conditions cannot be met.

## Twelve proposed boundaries for the question

1. **Ordinary source scope only.** Decide the routine disposition mode for the explicit ordinary source cohort. Do not reopen P23 Media's manual/no-auto-purge contract, P18/P21 duties, resource/Support records or independently admitted temporary ZIP/preview/staging cleanup.
2. **Actual policy authorization precedes either mode.** Both require approved purpose/family/applicability/trigger and current disposition authority. A permits automatic source authorization only when the actual policy expressly allows it; source-required human determination is never skipped. B requires a fresh ordinary operator release, not a new power to amend policy.
3. **Eligibility is not completion.** Reaching a period, changing assignment, canceling/F/withdrawal, removing a folder/view, seeing empty usage or getting provider acknowledgment neither authorizes nor proves physical disposal by itself. Preserve each independent source fact.
4. **All current guards apply at the irreversible boundary.** Policy version/effectivity, actual scope/actor or service authority, complete registered uses/holds/processors/copies and live writer/reference fences are re-proved after waits. A cached candidate list or old human approval is not an enduring delete grant.
5. **No invisible new permission.** Policy administration, automatic-operation authority, operator review/release, use/body/history viewing and provider execution are separate actual source/P12 powers. Generic staff/admin status, saved preference, assignment to a review or possession of an object path grants none of them.
6. **Finite truthful operator work.** B reviews the actual bounded proposal and consequences; no all-matches blanket release or forced file-body opening. A exposes actual blocked/failed/unknown work to a capable source operator. Sensitive use reasons remain protected. Missing necessary safe review context blocks that action, not a demand for broader file access.
7. **No timeout-as-approval or indefinite waiting.** Neither mode invents a grace period, pause duration, legal extension, deadline or automatic approval from inactivity. Actual required-disposition duties and privacy ceilings remain binding. The cohort needs a capable timely source/records escalation and repair route; an unserviceable manual queue is not a complete B.
8. **Original intent and physical uncertainty.** Preserve exact intended scope/versions and semantic operation identity through retries, different transport keys, partial outcomes, stale policy or lost responses. No generic retry-all, success from deleted rows, duplicate irreversible effect or operator attribution fabricated for automatic work.
9. **Holds, new uses and interruption.** A current qualified hold/restriction can stop new affected disposition before its actual irreversible boundary. A late stop cannot falsely promise cancellation of already-completed deletion. Worker lease expiry does not make deletion-claimed bytes promotable while a physical call may still finish.
10. **Copies/history/restore remain owned.** Verify all required copy-class outcomes and governing backup expiry/suppression; keep only lawful minimized proof. Correction or policy change cannot resurrect disposed data or old access. Lawful recovery is separately qualified; irrecoverable loss needs actual source/incident remediation and truthful limits.
11. **Small understandable UX.** Use source-owned safe proposal/status/history and capable repair within existing shared surfaces. Distinguish eligible, awaiting required review, authorized/in progress, blocked, partial/unknown and verified completion without inventing one giant global file-state enum. No new email/reminder/default recipient duty is selected by this fork.
12. **Stage and remaining policy facts.** Neither option approves a legal schedule, fills R-POL's missing classes/periods or closes R-LEGACY/native/provider/user proof. Qualify one actual profile before activation. No provider, deletion run, formal publication, new glossary term or Q63 answer is inferred.
