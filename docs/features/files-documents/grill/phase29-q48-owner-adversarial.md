## Judgment and principal findings

**Accept with required amendments.** A fills a real founder-required collection journey, but “automatic reminders” alone is not a usable or safe policy. The initial default, actual work predicate, exact cadence/count, current action, pause/restart and overlap behavior must be specified. B—deliberate staff reminders only—remains simpler and more context-sensitive. No measured Asym demand, completion improvement or cost result establishes that A is universally better.

The maximum justified omission severity is **High**, not Critical; these are prospective failure modes, not observed production incidents.

- **High / likely without source qualification:** unfinished request, missing acceptance or late task date is not outstanding recipient work. All-received material waiting for staff must not be chased. Q37 reassessment and Q47 re-offer make this distinction particularly important.
- **High / plausible:** cloning permission Access-request or contribution-approval reminders transfers the wrong audience, temporal authority and stop policy. Q48 needs its own ordinary-source occurrence and exact P17/P6 binding.
- **High / likely:** a cap based on provider success alone permits parallel or uncertain sends to overrun it; a cap based on every failed preparation wastes it. Reserve before admission, permanently consume once the first external call may have occurred, and never refund that consumed fact.
- **High / plausible:** Off/On, pause/resume, new work or plan edits can revive queued old mail or reset a lifetime limit unless source epochs and immutable occurrences distinguish new permission from historical intent.
- **High / plausible:** manual and automatic sends can race or use one another's uncertain outcome as a reason to rekey. Use one active occurrence lane, shared24-hour spacing and source-owned semantic identity, with the exact inherited provider classifier.
- **Medium / likely:** vague “run at9” makes an ordinary9:00:01 worker miss indefinitely or turns a long outage into a catch-up burst. The proposed60-minute original automatic call window closes this normal journey.
- **High / plausible:** automatic-Off can be accidentally overloaded into manual contact prohibition, or recipient Stop automatic can become a new access/preference system. Narrow controls and actual P3 contact floors must remain separate.
- **High / plausible:** optional external participant material can silently borrow financial30-day or staff7-day retention, or require a fake Party to show a support copy. The exact new material/reveal branch must qualify without widening history.

## Concrete corrected policy proposed

These numbers and lifecycle choices are **new Q48 product judgments offered for ratification**, not prior source facts or empirically optimal values. They are bounded defaults, not a general schedule builder.

1. **Purpose/cohort.** One optional email reminder purpose for actual outstanding recipient work in an eligible issued unfinished ordinary Private Document Request. Manual and automatic initiation use the same source eligibility/contact/action contract with distinct truthful origins. No watcher broadcast, attachments, new bell/SMS, specialist deadline/financial program or whole-Workflow dependency.
2. **Tenant control.** Automatic reminders initially Off. A source-qualified tenant policy manager with applicable automation management authority deliberately activates a reviewed declarative policy with non-sending preview/test and activity logging. New-request defaults are7 calendar days,09:00 in a known source/tenant-qualified IANA zone, and3 lifetime automatic reminders per issued request; allowed fields are1–30 calendar days, valid local HH:mm and1–10 automatic reminders. The platform maximum10 remains hard. No unknown zone or malformed value becomes a default silently.
3. **Enrollment/settings.** Under an active qualified policy, newly issued requests enroll unless authorized staff explicitly choose automatic Off. Existing requests need a deliberate qualified activation, never a historical bulk backfill. Each request captures exact policy defaults/revision. Later non-Off default edits affect new plans, not existing ones. Use current tenant settings is an explicit request-plan successor: new timing anchor, count preserved, control state preserved, old dependent automatic work stopped. Raising/lowering a captured cap does not erase consumed count. No per-request number editor is needed initially.
4. **Distinct powers.** Tenant policy management differs from source Send reminder, request enrollment/Pause/Resume/Off and recipient Stop automatic. Grant only the actual source/P12 actions. Ordinary staff are not forced to gain automation administration for a narrow request action. Automatic execution uses currently qualified tenant/source service authority; it does not impersonate the original human configurator. Routine configurator departure alone does not destroy an otherwise lawfully owned program; actual authority dependencies still apply.
5. **Calendar clock.** From trusted initial enrollment, explicit resume/settings adoption or genuine no-work→work anchor, take its date in the captured IANA zone, add N calendar days and resolve the captured local time. Also require at least24 elapsed hours after that fresh anchor. For a fold use the later occurrence; for a gap use the next valid local instant, preserving resolution evidence. Shared24-hour spacing after any prior possible Q48 send can push scheduling to the next normal daily slot. Requested by and browser/organization-time changes do not drive or rewrite this clock. Preserve exact nominal instant and interpretation; no holiday/business-day engine.
6. **Actual work transitions.** Receipt of all currently requested recipient work stops affected automatic occurrence utility before staff acceptance/F where appropriate. A genuine later source action can create work and a fresh courtesy anchor without restoring an old occurrence or count. Partial progress, target edits, ordinary metadata changes and added work while already waiting do not repeatedly postpone existing remaining work. Q37 reassessment, nonblocking optional Q36 rows and Q47 re-offer are evaluated by actual source facts, not a hardcoded event-name list.
7. **Finite window and missed work.** An automatic occurrence may make a provider call only in `[nominal_slot, nominal_slot +60 minutes)`, subject to earlier current stops.9:00:01 is normal, not a lost schedule. All follow-up calls share that original end. After expiry, coalesce missed slots to one future normal slot; no historical queue of sends or shifting an old deadline. A genuinely new future recurrence is not repair/rekey of an expired/unknown occurrence.
8. **Count and spacing.** Reserve one available automatic slot when admitting the occurrence and serialize the request's active Q48 lane. Convert the reservation once to permanent consumed count at the first sealed provider-call boundary where external submission may occur. Never refund after that boundary, including definite later failure or unknown outcome. A proved never-crossed terminal no-send can release only its reservation. Retries share the same count. Manual reminders do not consume the automatic cap, remain available after it, and distinct occurrences share a non-overridable24 elapsed-hour minimum after the latest possible Q48 call. Each possible call, including a permitted same-envelope follow-up, advances the shared latest-call fact and next automatic waiting anchor under the captured calendar cadence; it does not clear stops or count twice. The24-hour distinct-occurrence floor does not itself prohibit or authorize an original occurrence's bounded follow-up. The UI must not equate consumed/unknown with delivered.
9. **Manual/automatic overlap.** The single active lane is for admitted occurrences, not the whole future enrollment: an automatic date next week cannot block today's lawful manual action. Concurrent Send reminder and due work have one owner order; recover the winner/pending result or offer the actual repair path, not another message. Manual pre-admission spacing failures explain the next lawful time and do not silently queue a later send. No per-click new occurrence, polling-based identity or payload-selected route.
10. **Stops and re-enable.** Automatic tenant-Off, request Pause/Off, recipient Stop automatic and a superseding plan stop affected old automatic authority at every new provider-call admission, including same-key follow-ups. Once an intervening stop exists, later On cannot revive old work even if no worker observed the interval. Re-enable requires actual request resume and a new anchor; spent count remains. Automatic controls do not disable an independently lawful manual reminder. Broader source/privacy/contact prohibition gates both. Unknown proof blocks affected calls without falsely declaring a terminal source fact.
11. **Recipient control.** Offer a source-qualified request-scoped Stop automatic action under current exact recipient proof; no full signup, magic bearer grant, broad unsubscribe center or hidden source waiver. Staff cannot clear that recipient stop. A currently authorized participant may deliberately clear only their own stop where the source permits; this does not clear staff Pause/Off, global-Off, contact suppression, create enrollment or restore access. If an already configured plan thereby becomes otherwise eligible, use a fresh full waiting anchor, never a pre-stop overdue slot. If the qualified action is unavailable, actual source help remains; do not weaken proof to expose it.
12. **Useful action/access.** The initial cohort requires a currently qualified submit/correct/resupply route for the requested work. Proof/session steps may still occur on return, but no invented or expired grant is promised. Ended access blocks this reminder; staff use the real access-repair route. There is no new fallback help-message variant, automatic access renewal, email-to-submit path or relabeling of Q20/Q40. Source closure and actual fulfillment stop work; request status alone does not decide unrelated source duties or prior notices.
13. **Current contact and safe content.** Bind the actual intended recipient/source/purpose and exact eligible Party/contact or explicitly adopted no-Party authority. Read permission, mailbox equality, staff choice or tenant Enable is not contact permission. Current P3 do_not_contact/do_not_email, suppression/privacy and exact source floors apply at resolution and every call. Safe request identity, a concise truthful remaining-work cue and the qualified action suffice; no stale missing-count, private filename/body or copied protected history by default.
14. **Communication admission/recovery.** Source occurrences and program changes have coherent current/revision/history/result and genuinely required handoff. P17 requires the exact Live generated binding/effective plan; P6 releases the complete bounded candidate set, including proved zero, all before any child is claimable. Unknown original authority cannot be reconstructed from today's plan or called zero. Reconcile a matching committed operation before new eligibility/no-op, under current disclosure. Old recovered results cannot replace current status. A genuinely new command still needs current capability, action eligibility and expected relevant basis; complete-meaning equality returns unchanged without a fabricated business revision, anchor reset, enrollment, count change or effect, while required security evidence remains. Already-active Start/Resume is not a free reschedule. Changes under the same semantic identity conflict; ABA and all relevant source/policy/recipient/plan races require exact ordering, not a whole-request global lock.
15. **Material/delivery.** Propose a new closed optional external reminder prepared-material class with an absolute24-hour ceiling, further shortened by the source occurrence's original utility/action/privacy/safety and provider limits; the automatic60-minute window normally dominates. Manual occurrence utility ends at its original24-hour source-admission deadline or an earlier stop. Sealed pins/bytes never re-resolve. Use P17's closed classifier: only concurrent-idempotent, exact provider5xx and proved network/timeout permit at most two same-envelope follow-ups total after the initial call, within all current fences and earliest deadlines. Accepted/indeterminate work cannot be rekeyed/replaced. A closed unknown old lane may later coexist in history with a genuinely new scheduled recurrence, preserving old count/outcome, never as a resend repair.
16. **History, small UX and qualification.** Durable history is body-free; prepared material is not support history. Adopt an explicit initial Recent-copy ceiling of zero for this optional reminder purpose: no_readable_copy, no fake Party and no blocked lawful reminder merely for that convenience. A future readable-copy expansion requires explicit owner/class/reveal qualification; the inherited shorter-of tenantOff/7/30 and contract0/7/30, unknown=0 and no-resurrection rules do not themselves activate it. Offer contextual safe settings, actual count/outcome/next-time/paused/blocked states, explicit Save/Cancel and Send, no toggle-send, no essay/routine second approval, accessible mobile/keyboard/AT/plain-text/images-off/RTL/weak-network recovery. Qualify finite source/current/history/worker/retry/material budgets, actual every-door PDP/RLS, native races, restore/migration and load before activation. No cost/native passing claim follows this document.
