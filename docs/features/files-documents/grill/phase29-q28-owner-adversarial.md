## Concrete adversarial checks

- **Two supporting files already exist.** Elena adds a certificate for Nora.
  The existing participant file remains. A one-candidate target reports the
  actual conflict instead of replacing it. An acceptance begun on the earlier
  basis cannot silently accept the changed set.
- **The wrong staff file is already added.** Authorized pending withdrawal
  removes that contribution from current use and preserves the prior act.
  Accepted or closed mistakes require qualified source remediation. It does
  not follow that every request needs a newly invented reopening command.
- **A replacement admits as Checking and then fails.** Failed admission leaves
  both old membership and history untouched. After committed replacement,
  later checking failure does not revive the predecessor; the current failed
  state and owned recovery stay truthful. The final C07/P12 now states both
  cases, consistent with Q03's admission-time fence.
- **The same bytes serve different requirements.** A digest match alone neither
  deduplicates separate valid source acts nor transfers acceptance. Final C03
  correctly keys equivalence to source contribution meaning and lineage.
- **The old uploader leaves.** The original actor's role loss alone does not
  erase a lawfully independent target use. A restriction whose actual scope
  covers the target still blocks affected use; copying bytes cannot evade it.
- **The participant cannot inspect the original source.** Staff assistance
  does not expose that source, its other documents or its audit. A separately
  permitted safe target status may be shown. Required personal affirmation
  cannot be satisfied by blind confirmation or fabricated consent. Review
  feedback must name an actually authorized next actor: a participant cannot
  be directed to Q03 own-submission correction for a staff contribution they
  cannot amend. Final C06/P15 preserves staff recovery and genuine new
  source-authorized participant work as distinct routes.
- **Participant access expires before review.** Independently authorized staff
  work may continue. Q25 does not become a continuous intake gate; source
  prohibitions still apply. Later fulfillment cannot invent timely recipient
  access or reset a thirty-day window.
- **Review email is Off, then enabled later.** A staff contribution obeys its
  original first-actionable policy/cohort. Later On or roster change cannot
  notify old work. A removal or retry is not a new incoming-work event.
- **A retained source is near disposal.** Source permission and durable custody
  must hold at admission and the target reference must be protected coherently.
  A hold preserves bytes without granting use or reading, and withdrawing one
  target use cannot dispose of another owner's required evidence.
