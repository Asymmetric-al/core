# Phase 29 Q49 A — Guided linked replacement: independent UX adversarial review

2026-10-25, Asia/Bangkok. **Disposition: Accept with required amendments.** Conrad selected guided linked replacement for a different intended person and requested a complete adversarial review. Q01–Q48 and the 29 existing glossary definitions remain ratified; **Q49 is selected, not yet ratified**. This note proposes exact requirements, not native implementation or a passed user study. No Q50, new provider, message, canonical edit, formal specification or ticket publication is authorized here.

## Recommended complete experience

Keep A. Provide one contextual **Prepare replacement** flow from the ordinary request: identify the legitimately different person; select and edit source-approved definitions; review actual remaining need, recipient, relevant reviewer coverage and new original-issuance terms; then deliberately complete the qualified handoff. The ordinary successful result is an actually canceled old request and an actually issued new request, linked where disclosure is permitted. A replacement draft alone is not completion.

Keep an independently qualified urgent source stop available while preparation continues. If that stop already occurred, it remains true even if the replacement is unfinished, abandoned or fails. Finishing the replacement then uses current independent original-issuance authority and the actual retained correction lineage; it does not reopen the old request or require a fake second cancellation. A supported repair must be available after reload or staff departure, not only in a transient toast.

Avoid unnecessary recollection. Earlier accepted or otherwise sufficient evidence remains attributed to its real source and can inform what work is genuinely still needed. Do not ask the new person to upload another copy merely to recreate old progress or claim another person's submission. If evidence needs a new use, Q28 supplies it only through its actual qualified, separately attributed staff action; no automatic transfer or inherited acceptance follows.

The smallest practical controls are the existing source/P12 capabilities for preparation, editing, old cancellation/restriction, new issuance and scoped operator recovery, plus existing communication/reminder controls. Do not add a generic transfer administrator, permission designer, clone settings page, mandatory reason essay, routine second approver or new UI stack. People who may prepare but not perform the final source acts can preserve a lawful draft and use an actually capable authorized continuation; a new approval queue is not required merely for that distinction.

**Coordinated bounded workflow requirements for the amendment package:** at most one recoverable active replacement preparation and at most one ever-issued direct replacement per predecessor, with one predecessor for that replacement. Repeated entry resumes the actual draft/outcome rather than creating duplicates. A draft is neither a lock on the original nor a hold on fulfillment. Discarding or deliberately superseding an unissued draft preserves source history and any earlier urgent stop. After issuance, another genuine mistaken-recipient correction proceeds from the current successor, not by issuing another sibling from the old predecessor. Cancellation, retention cleanup or restore cannot free that issued successor slot. These are proposed product/identity constraints, not a prescribed physical schema or an invented lifetime cap on legitimate correction.

## Eighteen exact required amendments

Severity refers to credible consequence if omitted: **High** for privacy, authority, false source effects or material lost work; **Medium** for recoverable friction/operational complexity. Likelihood is an exposed-path estimate, not measured production frequency. Each amendment is intended to be incorporated into the consolidated exact decision/proofs if accepted after review.

### U01 — Keep the selected source operation narrow (High / plausible)

Q49 replaces the intended person for an eligible ordinary request through a new linked request, not an in-place recipient edit or broad ownership transfer. Establish a genuine continuing source need and actual eligibility of the different person. Preserve Q01 same-person contact/proof recovery, representatives and specialist owners separately. No automatic global Auth/Party/contact mutation, completed-request reopening, financial reassignment or new collaborator follows. A name/address string, unfinished label or user-interface role is not authority. Do not implement B's pristine shortcut as an undocumented fallback.

### U02 — Use actual capabilities, with proportionate tenant flexibility (High / plausible)

Declare and enforce source/P12 rights for reading reuse candidates, preparing/editing/discarding replacement drafts, canceling/restricting the old request, issuing the new request, reading lineage and scoped recovery. Existing source grant administration supplies tenant/staff flexibility; no general policy language or new settings platform is needed. A preparer need not have all final powers, but cannot execute effects they lack. A capable authorized staff/operator continuation must exist without blanket access to private content or a routine extra approval queue. Unknown required authority blocks the new dependent effect, not unrelated lawful work.

### U03 — Reuse a closed, reviewable set of definitions (High / likely if omitted)

Copy only selected source-approved item/type/instruction/title material into inert new preparation, with its exact source revision and provenance. Proposed candidate text must be readable by the actor and independently suitable for the new recipient at final issue; free text can contain private facts. Do not copy answers/files, accepted status, participant assertions, Q30/Q45 text, old recipient identity, grants/proofs, messages, current review assignments as authority, clocks or reminder controls. Do not use AI redaction or an unchecked “safe to share” assertion as a permission substitute. Missing or forbidden required definition material needs safe new authoring/qualified template help, not silent deletion or unsafe fallback.

### U04 — One recoverable preparation, with no hidden source lock (Medium / plausible)

Keep at most one active source-owned replacement preparation per predecessor and at most one ever-issued direct successor; the replacement has only one predecessor. Re-entry returns its current permitted draft or outcome; creation races cannot spawn invisible siblings. Use deliberate discard/supersession for an unissued draft under current rights. Preserve actual edits, field versions and original operation identity; refresh only by explicit relevant reconciliation, without overwriting typed input. A draft creates no old-source hold and must not block receipt, review, fulfillment or urgent stopping. Source-authorized operator recovery handles departure/lost draft access without impersonating its author or revealing it to every staff member. Generic Issue and alternate writers must enforce the bound draft's real handoff; unlinking or relabeling cannot bypass it.

### U05 — Request only genuine remaining work and qualify readiness (High / plausible)

Use actual source need to choose the new request's items. Show permitted prior progress to staff so accepted/sufficient evidence is not needlessly recollected; do not make the new person attest to someone else's material. Preserve old acceptance without copying it as new acceptance. Any new evidence use is a separate qualified Q28 act with real origin and current review. Validate the new recipient/purpose, needed definitions, Q24 review responsibility, Q25 readiness, actual route, and applicable current defaults. Reuse valid existing coverage where it genuinely applies without another assignment ceremony. No empty replacement or fabricated demand should exist merely to complete the UI flow.

### U06 — Normal handoff must actually end and issue (High / plausible)

One deliberate final action must admit the qualified old cancellation and new original issuance as a coherent source operation, with exact old/new identities, permitted linkage, obsolete-proof/intake/communication fences, required history and original recoverable result. Full current readiness precedes the ordinary coupled commitment. Do not report completion after canceling the old request while leaving only an unissued new draft. Sequential client requests, an outbox or UI spinner alone do not prove the required result. External provider calls remain outside short source transactions; source success and invitation delivery are separate facts. If the implementation cannot prove this coherent supported path, that cohort is not ready to activate.

### U07 — Urgent old stop remains independent and recoverable (High / plausible)

An actual qualified old-source cancellation/restriction can happen immediately without a replacement draft, new address or functioning mail; a narrow access restriction is not automatically cancellation. Retain that outcome if preparation later fails or is discarded. A previously lawfully canceled predecessor may support Q34's independently authorized remedy when its actual cancellation, continuing legitimate replacement purpose and unconsumed successor relationship are proved, even when preparation did not precede the stop. Canceled status alone is insufficient. Start or resume that preparation and later issue only through current authority/readiness for the remaining acts; do not require cancellation power merely because someone already performed the stop. Never reopen the old request, run a second fake cancellation or re-notify it. Fulfillment-first or unrelated adverse closure invalidates a stale normal handoff; a genuinely remaining new need requires actual source qualification. Show Old request canceled / Replacement draft or Needs attention truthfully and provide capable next actions.

### U08 — Preserve source history and current disclosure (High / plausible)

Old receipts, bytes, attribution, instructions/timing, accepted decisions, source obligations, required retention/holds and conversation remain with the old source. Cancellation is not erasure or false fulfillment. Previously admitted processing/review may continue only under its independent current authority; late unadmitted work cannot borrow the new person's scope. Current old-recipient serving follows Q34's exact finite outcome-only rule and other restrictions; no old transcript/history view is added. Staff may inspect permitted prior evidence without granting it to the new person. Do not turn inability to see evidence into a mandate to recollect it or a conclusion that it does not exist.

### U09 — New original proof and access, not transplanted clocks (High / plausible)

Admit the new request through actual original-issuance contracts for the new person, using Q01's qualified native context, fixed approved destination and appropriate proof. Proof may be completed on entry; the person need not be online during staff issue. Reuse genuine current proof only where its exact context/freshness rule permits. Do not copy old proof, old completed ceilings, old Q13/Q15 periods or a mutable email field; do not backdate or silently renew access. Show new Requested by separately from actual Access ends/qualified terms, using their governing source rules. Preserve the old clocks as history, not as new-person settings.

### U10 — Integrate existing communication and reminder purposes explicitly (High / plausible)

Old cancellation uses Q34/Q35's actual source/recipient-disclosure qualification and current optional choice; wrong-recipient cancellation can require suppression. New invitation/follow-through uses the exact qualified original-issuance contract. Keep both material/recipient/effect identities independent and show their actual consequences when relevant; do not add a new “recipient changed” notice, mandatory two-message sequence or blanket notifications-Off policy. Q48 old plan/usage/stops/unknowns remain original history and affected old calls stop. A legitimate new request for a different person gets only its own current Q48 eligibility and normal issuance choices; no copied plan, individual preference, old next time or count. New identity is not a noise-limit workaround. Existing broader contact restrictions bind both, and no global cross-purpose/cross-request spacing promise is invented.

### U11 — Original semantic outcome before new action (High / plausible)

Commands derive source/Tenant/environment/actor/time from trusted current context and bind exact intended old/new source revisions, safe chosen definitions and requested effect to a durable semantic identity. Coherently persist the source outcome, required attribution/linkage and complete follow-through. On retry, recover the matching committed original result under current disclosure before applying current fresh-action gates or no-op logic. Changed intent conflicts. Canonical unchanged lawful draft/control saves should not fabricate new business revisions or repeat issue/cancel; stale expected basis still conflicts even after values change away and back. No provider-key or lease expiry can mint another replacement. Historical success must not overwrite current state.

### U12 — Revalidate material changes and order source races (High / plausible)

Race old receipt, clarification, staff evidence, acceptance, requirement edit/removal, fulfillment/cancellation, new-person scope, reviewer/readiness and permission changes against final handoff at the authoritative boundary. Preserve admitted prior outcomes; reject or explicitly reconcile a stale material basis, without blindly replacing the user's edited text or silently dropping items. Source changes unrelated to the reviewed handoff should not demand needless re-entry. Unknown pending original outcome is not absent evidence. Old proof/finalization/processing/result paths, including alternate reads and late responses, must honor the correct winning authority and current restrictions.

### U13 — Linkage is private metadata, not inherited access (High / plausible)

Authorize replacement-link existence, source titles, old/new recipient identities, counts, status, history, diagnostics, preview and navigation independently. Neither recipient gets the other's identity, reason, files or transcript from the relationship or old invitation. No redirect from an old protected link to the new person's private request. Staff displays must not leak inaccessible linked facts through counts, autocomplete, stale caches, errors or result recovery. Minimum permitted correlation can support operations without raw recipient or instruction bodies in telemetry. A denied/unknown relationship is not represented as a complete no-history answer.

### U14 — Correct mistakes through current source acts (High / plausible)

Before issue, edit or discard the inert preparation under current rights. After issue, preserve the canceled predecessor and actual new issuance. Wrong new recipient or unsafe definitions need immediate qualified restriction/cancellation and a deliberate further correction of the current successor or actual source/privacy remedy, not Restore of old access or a second sibling from the old request. Existing Q33/Q34/Q36/Q37/other source remedies apply only where their actual scopes fit. When no new request is warranted, a real source operator records/remediates the mistake and addresses improper exposure/disposition under current authority. Do not pretend another request repairs already disclosed data. No transient Undo is the only recovery path.

### U15 — Small contextual flow with visible consequences (Medium–High / likely for affected users)

Keep the flow in existing source request work with a clear old-request status, new person, editable safe definitions, actual reviewer/readiness and new timing. Present one reviewable final action whose label/consequence matches both old cancellation and new issue, such as Cancel old request and issue replacement when that is the actual operation. An urgent-stop-first case instead says Issue replacement. These are semantic copy examples, not frozen button geometry. No field selection, typing, blur, preview or draft autosave cancels, issues or sends. Preserve known drafts, selections, scroll and return context. One final review need not be a second modal or every-field acknowledgement. Success persists with links to currently permitted old/new outcomes; delivery can remain pending/failed/unknown independently.

### U16 — Complete accessible and international recovery (Medium–High / likely for affected users)

Use Core Base UI/base-maia and shared controls, not another shell. Keyboard, screen reader, speech and non-drag pointer must complete selection/edit/review/commit/recovery. Preserve meaningful focus, labels, adequate targets, inline/error-summary linkage and concise status announcements. Old/new person and destination stay legible at narrow mobile/zoomed layouts; support long Unicode names, RTL, IME and explicit date/zone meaning without invented first/last-name assumptions. Preserve lawful input on errors; do not silently trim a destination into a different person. No required original-file preview, download, full account or desktop. Poor networks reconcile original results, and context change fences private late responses; no silent offline queued issue/cancel/send.

### U17 — Native constraints, bounded work and additive rollout (High / plausible)

Use minimal source-owned draft/lineage/effect facts and existing request/custody/communication owners. Enforce exact same-scope old/new relationships, acyclic direct-successor lineage, at-most-one admitted replacement, immutable source attribution, safe draft revision and stable operation identity through native constraints and the authoritative mutation boundary. Apply P12's single PDP and actual effective old/new-row, grants, RLS, function/trigger and every-writer proof. Bound selected definitions, payload/history, lookup/query/transaction work and repair batches; no unbounded lineage scan or per-field policy loop. Migrate prospectively without guessed historic replacement links; preserve adverse stop/history on restore and fence incompatible writers. No new provider, generalized workflow/clone/event-sourcing framework or unchanged-bill claim.

### U18 — Exact evidence, operations and stage boundaries (Medium / plausible)

Keep original and replacement business facts, minimal lawful audit and technical diagnostics separate. Give actual scoped operators meaningful blocked/unknown/stale states and supported repair; no direct SQL/provider-console edits or unlimited blind retries. Define finite workload/repair/material/retention boundaries through current owners before activation and measure native behavior. Require real staff/new-recipient/old-recipient/admin/support/AT journeys and source/role/race/provider/restore proof. Vendor docs and a synthetic model are not those results. No new glossary term is proposed by this UX review merely for a link or draft; domain owners may identify a justified concept separately. All 29 existing terms remain unchanged until any later explicit ratification.
