## Verdict and material findings

**Accept A with the concrete amendments below.** A terminal source outcome is the right bounded feature; a task deletion, access revocation or loop of item removals is not an equivalent implementation. The highest-consequence traps are stale fulfillment after cancellation, cancellation inferred to grant completed access, loss of already received evidence, and a misleading Undo/reopen promise after the terminal act. A lasting source remedy can remain small and practical.

The inspected source does not establish a qualified native ordinary cancellation command. Findings describe prospective failures and concrete hazards in copying adjacent implementations, not exploitable defects in a nonexistent endpoint. Existing code is not assumed correct because it exists or has tests.

## Exact proposed amendments

### C01 — Bounded terminal outcome

The native ordinary source SHALL support deliberate **Cancel request** only for an exact issued, still-eligible unfinished ordinary Private Document Request issued in error or whose legitimate purpose ended. Record Canceled distinctly from Fulfilled, rejected application, expired access, item No longer required, temporary pause, draft discard and file deletion. Partially received or accepted evidence does not itself make an unfinished aggregate ineligible: preserve those facts. Specialist/nonwaivable obligations and broader business outcomes remain with their owners. Do not cancel an already fulfilled/closed request through this action or use cancellation to avoid a still-binding requirement. This is a new bounded source capability where absent, not an inferred existing generic task command.

### C02 — Exact current authority and deliberate basis

Resolve trusted tenant/environment/actor and validated source/request selectors. Require actual current source/P12 whole-request cancellation authority, independently of staff/admin labels, file reading, Q24 assignment, Q21 review, Q33 item change and grant administration. Retain a concise source-grounded cause/basis; request short staff input only when the necessary basis is otherwise absent. Protect internal reasons separately. Review the exact current request and meaningful consequence before commitment. A stale permission, source purpose or affected basis after a relevant wait must conflict/deny rather than silently rebase. Do not demand preview/download or inspection of every file where independent source facts already prove the request was mistaken or its purpose ended; an optional-view outage must not block an independently qualified source action. Preserve real separation of duties without inventing a universal new permission atom, second reviewer or policy editor.

### C03 — Cancellation versus real fulfillment

Cancellation and Q22 fulfillment MUST share the actual aggregate currentness boundary or an equivalent proved version/fence protocol. A final human acceptance, all-visible-items flag, worker claim, queued evaluation or deferred follow-through is not durable fulfillment. A lawful cancellation can win while the source remains eligible; it then prevents stale Q22 work from inventing Fulfilled. Actual fulfillment-first prevents stale cancellation from rewriting the outcome. Do not add an arbitrary waiting period, manual Complete gate or cancellation hold to manufacture an opportunity to cancel. Neither partial accepted work nor pending technical follow-through alone determines the terminal winner.

Evaluate current complete source facts/lifecycle after relevant waits. Record cancellation's actual qualified occurrence and source basis, preserving any earlier item facts and any independently proved fulfillment. Never backdate to browser/request/transaction-start time or derive cancellation from expiry/worker error. A canceled request cannot become Fulfilled by removing its child items or observing an empty list. Unknown/failed/filtered source proof is not a valid terminal outcome.

### C04 — Immediate authoritative fence, bounded projections

The committed source cancellation MUST immediately govern every ordinary intake/review/current-use admission path even if child task or UI projections update later. Do not require an unbounded all-child mutation or a provider call under a source lock. Durable intent and bounded reconciliation may update derived work, but an old projection cannot authorize a late submission, review mutation or fulfillment. Concurrent final-item updates, Q33 removal/compensation, acceptance/Request changes and relevant source edits must preserve exactly one valid current aggregate result without lost independent history.

New relevant Q30 text admitted first fences an older affected uncommitted cancellation decision and requires deliberate reconsideration; unrelated text causes no needless conflict. Cancellation-first denies new ordinary clarification and outward participant replies for this now-ended request context unless an independently qualified different source route supplies actual authority. Previously admitted messages remain truthful source work. No Read/Reply/Resolve gate is created, and retained staff handling is not permission to promise a reply is available through ended participant access.

### C05 — Evidence and independent obligations survive

Order cancellation with participant/staff evidence finalization, exact correction, staff association/replacement/withdrawal and source review. Transfer/selection is not durable receipt. Cancellation-first denies the old request's new ordinary admission; receipt-first preserves the exact received act, immutable version/lineage and any committed acceptance. An uncertain finalization must reconcile before claiming Received, Lost or safe deletion. Do not demand a duplicate transfer just to repair a notice/projection.

End only obligations whose duty depended on this now-ended ordinary request; preserve their history. Keep independently binding safety/incident work, lawful custody, retention/holds, other source uses, admitted staff follow-through and earlier acceptance. Cancellation cannot clean quarantined bytes, revoke other uses, destroy shared files, mutate old review attribution or waive a specialist obligation. Technical reconciliation may continue under its own authority without reopening business intake. Source completion/cancellation and the disposition of storage are separate acts.

### C06 — Concrete canceled participant boundary

Cancellation ends ordinary participant business continuation for this request: no new submission/correction, item acceptance on their behalf or ordinary Q30 send/reply path. It MUST NOT create Q06/Q07 completed reading, a new 720-hour period, Q13/Q16 fresh access, or a historical file/conversation archive. Serving an old filename, receipt history, text or bytes still needs an actual independent current source authorization; retained custody or a valid login is insufficient.

As an **explicit Q34 source/P12/Q01 amendment**, cancellation narrows only the independently valid finite unfinished participant admission then current—original or already-qualified fresh—to an outcome-only scope: **Canceled plus safe source help**. Preserve that exact existing recipient/admission root, unchanged end and all applicable earlier caps. If no independently valid admission existed at cancellation, no outcome allowance is created later. Optional status/proof readiness must not delay an independently qualified cancellation. Unresolved original eligibility denies protected status without recording uncertainty as proved absence; later recovery may establish only the exact admission actually valid at the original cancellation from trustworthy durable evidence, never choose a later root. This is not inherited Q06 completed access or proof that a once-valid URL remains authoritative. It carries no internal cause, file list/body, transcript, other-recipient facts, renewed grant or new access clock.

Q01 may reuse or re-establish proof only for that same retained recipient/admission root within its unchanged ceiling and current restrictions; repairing proof is not a new access admission. Q13/Q16 cannot renew a canceled request. Independent revocation, recipient change, privacy restriction, uncertain original/current basis, wrong recipient or expiry denies protected status and uses neutral unavailable/help without private request existence disclosure. Ordinary file/history/transcript serving and intake are stopped; any independently authorized staff history/handling or other source use remains under its own current authority. Independently qualified source contacts/protected carriers remain usable under their unchanged contracts. Prove the cancellation-time eligibility and subsequent narrowing before activation; do not create a retrospective allowance from a current session or guess an absent historical root.

### C07 — Durable original semantic effect

Coherently persist source cancellation, immutable original attribution/material/occurrence, mandatory history and same-database follow-through intent atomically. A qualified distributed boundary must provide equivalent durable truth rather than declare success from a best-effort task/email write. Keep external and expensive processing outside source locks. The cancellation's semantic identity survives transport-key expiry, fresh retry keys, multiple observers and worker leases. Duplicate current cancellation must not append fictitious repeated business decisions; changed material conflicts rather than reattributes the original act.

Recover a matching committed operation before treating its resulting terminal state as a failed new attempt, subject to current disclosure. Original result recovery and current outcome must remain distinguishable. Replay/restore cannot reapply cancellation to a new request, follow a remedy link to a different target, erase a later error record, revive an old demand or recreate stale messages. A precommit rollback has no source success; ambiguous commitment reconciles rather than guessing a new mutation is safe. Known cancellation survives an auxiliary refresh/projection failure. Do not copy personal-task optimistic rollback as proof that a timed-out source command failed.

### C08 — Lasting remedy without inherited reopening

Before commit, staff can inspect/correct/abandon the intended cancellation without a business effect. After commit, the canceled record remains terminal. Provide a lasting contextual route from request/history to record the error and obtain an actually authorized source remedy; a transient toast, support label or raw SQL procedure is insufficient. **Q34 does not inherit Q33's unfinished same-item compensating action:** that action cannot reopen this canceled aggregate or restore its old demands/grants/deadlines.

For a legitimate continuing ordinary need, explicitly qualify a separately reviewed **new** request through independently authorized normal original issuance, linked to the cancellation mistake, with current purpose, recipient, Q25 readiness and Q01–Q19 access controls. No automatic request, generic Reissue, cancellation reversal, copied credentials or copied acceptance. Preserve separately permitted Q01 proof reuse and Q28 exact evidence reuse through their owners; prove both allowed and denied cases and avoid unnecessary re-confirmation/reupload solely to repair state. Another already-effective source remedy may apply instead. Error recording, technical recovery and new issuance are distinct actions/authorities. A cohort cannot activate without a real capable remedy for supported mistakes, including when the original actor left or participant access expired.

### C09 — Actual keys, effective RLS and privileged-path proof

Use enforced same-tenant/environment/source/request/item/evidence/decision relationships, valid terminal combinations and current pointers, required trusted original attribution/occurrence and permanent effect uniqueness. A client cannot reparent the cancellation, forge a predecessor/new request relation, mutate the original actor/time or lose retained evidence through a cascade. Actual command authority governs mutable cross-row facts; a row-local CHECK alone does not establish them.

Test the complete effective grants and RLS SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/UPSERT old-row/new-row behavior, policy combinations, trigger transforms, views, RPC/functions and service/admin/job/import/restore entry points. Privileged paths that bypass RLS still enforce exact source authority and scope. Omitted WITH CHECK is not by itself proof of a bypass: UPDATE/ALL may inherit USING, and combined policies matter. Do not copy a broad care staff policy or treat service-only grants as native capability proof. Include independently authorized storage/read models/caches/counts/search and idempotent response disclosure.

### C10 — Privacy and existing communication semantics

Keep governed minimal business history and source-safe explanations distinct from internal rationale, security audit and technical traces; append-only business facts do not require perpetual personal prose. Apply source retention/redaction/disposition and restrictions to exports/backups/indexes/cache recovery. Q34 private rationale/history bodies/internal diagnostics or unauthorized filenames/recipient details/credentials do not enter routine logs/events/notices/URLs. Preserve independently qualified source contacts, recipient envelopes and protected-entry carriers through their unchanged owners.

Cancellation adds no notification purpose, tenant default, bell, reminder or recipient picker. Existing Q26/Q27/Q31/Q32/P6/P17 work retains its original occurrence, policy/recipient/material pins and exact current source-purpose checks. Cancel obsolete demands under those contracts, not all tenant mail. Q31 cannot promise an unavailable reply; Q32 staff awareness/handling of already admitted text may remain independently useful, but only while actual purpose/authority/utility qualifies. Preserve admitted provider/in-flight truth; cancellation is not recall or permission to rewrite a prepared operation. No new outward communication or fresh access is implicit in mistaken-cancellation repair.

### C11 — Useful UX and finite operations

Use the existing request context with a named deliberate Cancel request action, enough safe current context, and the actual terminal/access/repair consequence before commitment. One meaningful action suffices where review/correction is possible; no unconditional modal, essay, extra approver or timed Undo. Present persistent Canceled distinctly from Fulfilled, Accepted, No longer required, expiry and technical failure. Preserve lawful focus/input/reading context and discard stale responses after identity/tenant/request/purpose changes. Prove keyboard/AT/mobile/touch/reflow/RTL/IME/locale/slow-network behavior and truthful known/unknown results.

Use bounded indexed currentness/aggregate and semantic-recovery queries, short consistently ordered transactions or an equivalent proved protocol, finite measured retry/reconciliation budgets and fair tenant scheduling. Complete proof is not a paginated UI count or an unbounded scan. Parent-source fencing permits bounded child projections without reopening a gap. Safe operator evidence must distinguish denied/stale/unresolved/committed-with-pending-follow-through, with actual accountable routes and no task/event per poll. No universal item count/latency/SLA is established by this review; qualify supported workloads before activation.

### C12 — Prospective activation and restore

Reconcile the new ordinary source outcome with Q22, Q03/Q21/Q23/Q28/Q30/Q33, P12/P9, custody and applicable Workflow bindings through their canonical owners. Preserve specialist contracts and actual Q06–Q19 access meaning. Enable only cohorts with real native command/schema/read models, terminal disclosure, lasting remedy, finite budgets and the composed proof below. Do not require the whole future Studio or create a parallel ledger/permission engine.

Old/mixed writers/workers/imports cannot fabricate cancellation from missing files, expired access or dismissed tasks, overwrite terminal truth, or grant completed reading. Add compatible readers/constraints/commands/recovery before activation. Scoped stop/rollback preserves committed facts, safe independent work and lawful history; use roll-forward where downgrade would lose truth. Restore must reconcile later cancellation, remedy links, revoked authority and original effect identities before resumed work. Backups do not authorize stale fulfillment, reissued access or repeats of historical provider effects.
