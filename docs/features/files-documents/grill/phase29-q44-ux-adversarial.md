# Phase 29 Q44 — Bounded ZIP delivery: independent UX adversarial review

2026-10-25, Asia/Bangkok. Conrad selected **A: one bounded private ZIP for a deliberately selected set of eligible ordinary request-file originals**. The selection is not ratification of this amendment package. Q01–Q43 and the existing 26 glossary definitions remain governing. This note writes no canonical files, implementation, provider/account settings, private data or actual messages. Native qualification and the final consolidated review remain separate.

## Conclusion and strongest alternative

Keep A, with explicit scope, a simple readable manifest, truthful preparation/delivery states and a usable partial-result decision. One initial Download can prepare and deliver the same complete reviewed eligible set when the actual current source and browser path qualify. Slow or returning users see the known operation result and Ready/Download. A changed subset requires a new deliberate choice; the initial intent is not permission to substitute different contents.

The smallest human manifest is **one UTF-8 plain-text contents/outcome file** alongside the intact originals. Use short labeled sections and one readable block per included file, rather than a wide fixed-column table. It records the package's exact included members, safe delivery-name mapping, byte lengths and the qualified digest algorithm/value, plus source-permitted completeness and omitted-work outcomes at preparation. It contains no active page, macros, source bodies, transcripts, bearer links or generic private source paths. Keep the same meaningful summary in the accessible native UI. The package's own integrity evidence belongs outside its embedded manifest to avoid a self-referential hash requirement. This format is a product judgment, not a standard mandating a specific filename or a claim that plain text alone guarantees accessibility.

**B remains credible:** a prepared set of deliberate individual downloads avoids archive construction/extraction and isolates large-file transfer/retry. It can be better for small sets, partial local retrieval and constrained devices. Its manifest, exact selection, current rights and per-file recovery still require design. A adds a temporary artifact and extraction cost; it is not universally faster, smaller or cheaper. A retains existing qualified single-file Download, but does not select B's second full batch interface, a format menu, multipart archives or a new download-management platform.

No observed Asym workflow, usual batch size, bandwidth profile, staff preference, task-time saving, conversion metric, cost or tested browser behavior is established. These recommendations are reasoned design judgments constrained by current source owners and primary documentation.

## Exact proposed amendments and useful interaction wording

### U01 — One finite source-admitted collection

Bind the command to the trusted staff actor/current assignment, Tenant/environment, exact ordinary source/use/version identities, original-download/export purpose and a finite reviewed selection. Validate the entire command's required source/P12 authority before preparation; unknown whole-command authority blocks rather than filtering into an apparently lawful subset. Current metadata discovery, Preview, Q41 external access and folder/view membership are not bulk authority. A selected original stays exact; do not substitute a new correction or latest pointer. A repeated exact selection reference may normalize without duplicate effect, but equal bytes in different uses never silently merge their provenance or authority. Do not freeze arbitrary reviewer-only or unfinished-request-only restrictions: actual staff source eligibility controls.

### U02 — One deliberate intent where safe, clear asynchronous return where needed

For a qualified simple case, one visible Download ZIP intent may prepare and begin delivery of the same complete reviewed eligible set under current authority. Preparation and delivery are separate facts, not necessarily separate clicks. Checkbox/filter changes do nothing beyond selection. If preparation is slow, the active source/list context shows persistent Preparing status and a safe way back to its actual result. Returning to a ready result requires deliberate Download; do not unexpectedly start a new browser transfer after navigation/reconnect. No global download center, ready email, new notification purpose, forced reason or routine second approval is introduced. Actual existing P12/source step-up or governed-export requirements remain when they truly apply.

### U03 — Partial results require a meaningful explicit choice

If the original reviewed set cannot be delivered unchanged, never label it complete or silently auto-download the remaining subset. With whole-command authority known, an individual member's failed, denied, unavailable or unresolved eligibility/integrity can be recorded distinctly and excluded from bytes. A positive currently validated subset can be offered only if its partial operation is independently allowed. Show a truthful incomplete result and currently permitted member outcomes; use language such as Ready with missing files and Download available files (N), only where the count and context themselves are authorized. The action deliberately admits the exact shown subset and its frozen manifest. Retry unresolved work and change selection are separate deliberate options when qualified. Denied names/details/counts are not revealed to explain incompleteness. If no file is eligible, produce no empty-success ZIP. If even a safe incomplete meaning cannot be disclosed, do not offer a misleading partial artifact.

### U04 — A readable text manifest is the complete human minimum

Include one clearly identified UTF-8 plain-text contents/outcome file, with a reserved safe member name that cannot collide with an original; CONTENTS.txt is illustrative. Provide short labeled sections: preparation identity/time with meaningful zone/precision, what selection this artifact represents, whether that selection is complete or partial, and one block for each included original. Each block supplies its delivery entry name, minimal currently disclosable exact source/use/version mapping, byte length and qualified digest algorithm/value. Include only permitted omitted-work outcomes and clarify that they are facts at this artifact's preparation, not permanent assertions about the source. Opaque preparation/member references may provide exact mapping without exposing storage keys or private linked records. Human-readable authorized labels should keep the list useful; opaque identifiers alone are not sufficient identification when safe descriptive context is available.

Keep the same essential contents/outcome information in a semantic native UI before download; a person must not need to open an archive to learn it is incomplete. Hashes are available integrity evidence, not a mandatory manual-check ritual. Internal machine-readable custody/operation facts may remain structured under existing owners, but do not add an exported JSON/CSV/HTML/PDF format menu. A package hash is recorded in governed external artifact evidence; the embedded file lists member integrity and need not hash the ZIP that contains itself. The manifest is a protected artifact, not an official Records Export Package, accepted-evidence statement, certificate of authenticity, verified destination receipt or public share.

### U05 — Safe delivery names, exact original bytes

Create collision-free safe relative archive entries from qualified delivery names; never serialize source folder paths, absolute paths, drive/UNC names, parent traversal, special device targets or symlinks. Handle case/Unicode-normalization collisions, trailing dots/spaces, reserved names, very long names and the manifest's reserved namespace without overwriting or dropping files. Preserve useful international names when safely supported; qualify a readable alias when necessary and show the permitted mapping. Aliases do not rename source evidence or change the original's bytes/type. Escape or visibly encode filename control/newline/bidirectional-spoofing characters in human manifest/UI contexts so a name cannot forge a new status or conceal an extension. Apply correct UTF-8/archive metadata and test common extractors. Do not parse, unpack, execute, merge, watermark, recompress media or convert an admitted original to package it. A submitted ZIP remains one exact ordinary original if its source permits delivery.

### U06 — Freeze the package and govern every delivery admission

Once an archive and manifest are sealed, their included set, names, outcomes and bytes are fixed under an exact artifact identity. The entire composed artifact must remain currently authorized for the requester at delivery and subsequent applicable range/conditional/recovery admissions. Permission loss for an included original or a protected manifest field blocks that old artifact; do not remove a member in place, reseal under the same identity or change its meaning after a Download action. Recovery is a new deliberately reviewed currently authorized collection/artifact where allowed. Temporary authority uncertainty is not permission, but neither is it automatically a permanent business cancellation. Current P12/source read/egress/cut-off rules govern any active stream; do not invent a per-byte policy engine or promise zero already-admitted bytes after cancellation. A stage URL, prior readiness or digest is not a new access grant.

### U07 — Describe what the system actually knows

Distinguish selected, preparing, ready complete, ready partial, failed/unknown, canceled/expired and delivery facts without demanding one giant status enum. A ready package is not saved locally. Initiating a response or browser action supports Download started, not Downloaded successfully; transport completion may be recorded if actually observed, but does not establish local save, human reading or external custody. A browser may choose a location/name, decline, interrupt or open its own controls. Preserve unknown results honestly until the qualified reconciliation path resolves them; a transient UI refresh failure does not undo a committed preparation. Show actual permitted size/count information and measured preparation progress only; no fabricated percentage, ETA or guaranteed compression savings.

### U08 — Bound cancellation, expiry and recovery concretely

Leaving the page does not itself manufacture a cancellation. A deliberate Cancel preparation ends future preparation/distribution for that operation through the authoritative boundary and schedules actual qualified cleanup without deleting source files or other consumers' copies. If serving was already admitted, say so accurately; cancellation cannot recall received bytes, and a canceled operation must not become Ready from a late worker. Expiry ends artifact availability at its actual qualified deadline; read/retry/viewing never silently renews that deadline. A newly deliberate Prepare again may create a fresh bounded operation for the same exact still-eligible intended set; changed versions/subsets require current review. Recover an original committed result before treating a lost-response retry as new work. Do not rebuild an expired/revoked artifact automatically under an old request key or retain readable bodies forever merely to support deduplication.

### U09 — Keep failures recoverable without building B or a platform

Provide supported current-authorized retries for unresolved preparation/delivery, a safe smaller explicit selection and existing single-file Download. These do not add B's prepared batch-list product or fire many downloads automatically. Retry only the actual unresolved intended work, while recognizing that an explicitly new corrected/partial archive has a new identity and coherent manifest. Never pretend source work is rolled back because the browser failed or duplicate local copies can be erased. Archive compatibility failure gets a practical original/smaller-set path, not a required desktop sync client, cloud connector, paid extractor or provider-console intervention. Source-scoped support can inspect minimal restricted operation evidence and repair/reconcile staging without gaining all original-body rights. Retain useful bounded operation results in the originating context; no indefinite archive library or automatic cleanup/notification platform is required.

### U10 — Accessible selection, progress and manifest interaction

Use Core's existing Base UI/base-maia components and native source/list routes. Show selected-set scope, exact-current-version context when meaningful, real permitted count/size, Download ZIP, Cancel preparation, Ready and actionable partial/failure recovery with safe wording. Use persistent result/status, not a disappearing toast alone. Keep focus stable, announce material phase/error changes through appropriate semantics, and avoid announcing every byte or every row. Selection, removal, partial-set review, download and recovery work with keyboard, AT, voice and non-drag single-pointer controls. Reflow controls at 320 CSS pixels; test zoom, long international names, RTL, IME and low bandwidth. The essential human manifest is navigable as labeled content without fixed-width column alignment. Preserving drafts/selection never means retaining newly revoked names/bodies in caches or offline storage.

### U11 — Device copies and protected diagnostics remain honest

A normal ZIP is a container, not encryption, malware clearance, DRM or a revocable local object. The source's actual download/export qualification determines whether local copies are permitted; do not add an arbitrary consent box or generic legal warning to every authorized download. Native iPhone/iPad extraction exists; do not label the feature desktop-only, and do not claim every large archive/device is usable. Minimize private names, paths, predicates, source associations, manifest bodies, tokens and file contents in logs, analytics, traces, crash output, URLs and public caches. Preserve required operation/custody/egress evidence under its lawful retention and current disclosure, separately from optional readable presentation. No download automatically emails a participant, opens an external viewer, grants a colleague access, fulfills a request, accepts evidence, restarts retention or authorizes deleting source bytes.

### U12 — Qualify the complete native path and no larger product

Adopt the exact ordinary source/custody/P12 download, artifact, manifest, current-disclosure, cleanup and recovery contracts through their actual owners. Keep Phase21 Records Export Packages, Phase18 official artifacts, Q04 participant retrieval, Q28 origin restrictions, Q41 external shares and Q42/Q43 organization unchanged. Measure admitted member-count/byte/name/manifest/CPU/memory/duration/concurrency/egress/storage/retry/retention limits and extraction compatibility; do not copy vendor quotas or claim no bill increase. Unknown whole-command authority, integrity or required source readiness cannot be called operational delay to evade denial. Use native bounded workers/streaming/serving as qualified by engineering, without prescribing a new vendor, browser whole-library ZIP processor, queue platform, offline replica, password-distribution flow, archive explorer or general export engine. No new domain term is necessary merely to restate this temporary ordinary bundle. Real-role, alternate-path, source-change/cleanup races, failpoints, restore/old-client, workload and composed accessible task proof precede activation.
