## Strongest alternative and decision

**B — Existing capable Help/source coordination only** avoids a new private record and its correction, retention and operational costs. It is stronger when concerns are rare, usually require discussion, or an existing qualified Help team already resolves them without losing resource context. A generic Help page existing in code does not prove that service is capable for this audience.

Choose **A with required amendments** because the selected job is reporting a problem with a particular organization resource and following its maintenance outcome. It saves the reader from reconstructing the subject and gives curators a direct source-owned record. Keep B available for personal assistance and out-of-scope cases; do not automatically duplicate reports into it. No public votes, attachments, AI triage, custom workflow, assignment engine or new provider is justified. If actual authority/stewardship/privacy/return paths cannot pass their gates, do not activate a decorative form; ordinary resource access and independently qualified Help continue.

## Ratified decision and amendments

**Q57-C01 — Selected capability and owner.** Add optional Report a problem to the actual P28 organization-resource reader journey, with real handling by currently qualified resource curators in existing resource management. The first reader cohort remains the governed missionary audience; general staff-reference readership remains a separate explicit coverage item. P28 owns each report, its exact publication context, handling and source history; P4/P12 owns identity and current permission, P29 the underlying bytes/custody, and the existing resource owner retains publication/review/withdrawal. Reporting creates no resource-authoring, ordinary-file-comment, access-request, personal-support conversation or public-feedback power. Preserve qualified Help for personal assistance and cases outside this purpose. The stronger simpler alternative is existing capable Help alone; A deliberately adds contextual reporting because it preserves the subject and maintenance responsibility. No measured ministry demand, time saving or cost reduction is claimed.

**Q57-C02 — One short deliberate form.** Place Report a problem beside the permitted resource's existing information/actions without interrupting ordinary Open/Download. It is available when safe source metadata and current reporting authority identify the publication even if its body/download cannot open; a successful preview, download or proof of reading is not required. Use one labeled bounded plain-text field, What is wrong with this resource?, with useful example and purpose/privacy help. Identify the actual handling audience and explain that reporting is not anonymous; do not request known identity/contact details again or reveal a staff directory. One useful sentence can suffice. Reject blank/whitespace-only, invalid or over-limit input with an associated error; do not silently truncate. No category questionnaire, attachment, screenshot, rating, rich editor, URL fetch, mention delivery, AI quality judge or routine second confirmation is required. Send report is deliberate; typing, blur, IME composition, selection or opening a resource does not submit. Cancel before submission creates no report. No mandatory field implies that Core can verify the truth of a reader's allegation.

**Q57-C03 — Exact target and new-admission boundary.** A new report binds an exact currently reportable organization/resource/publication and its relevant version/language/purpose basis, with trusted actor and actual source occurrence time. The server resolves the proposed target; caller titles, links, resource IDs and timestamps are not authority. Require the relevant publication/report basis to remain eligible at admission after waits. If it changed, preserve lawful draft text, show only current permitted context and obtain deliberate refreshed intent; never silently attach the observation to a successor. A stale or denied link cannot enumerate hidden resources or create a historical-report right. This strict rule applies to NEW reports, not recovery of a committed original or separately authorized maintenance of an already received report. An external-resource report identifies the approved reference actually held and the reader's stated observation, not an immutable snapshot or verification of the remote website. No arbitrary fetch, screenshot or document-body snapshot is added.

**Q57-C04 — Coherent receipt, responsibility and three states.** Use only Open, Handled and Withdrawn as the business handling states; privacy restrictions, retention, loading and unknown outcome remain independent facts. The initial state is Open. Reader presentation may say Awaiting review after a genuine Report received confirmation; never confuse that with an in-flight send or Q55's resource-review status. Source admission atomically records the exact report/body revision, original actor/time/target, Open state and revision, a qualified durable handling route, mandatory business history and the original operation result. A source-owned route need not identify an online individual, but it must be capable of current authorized handling and stewardship takeover. An unowned/unqualified route cannot yield a false successful receipt. Show pending reports in existing resource management with permitted context and useful filters. This is a projection of the authoritative report, not a separately mutable task or copied handling state; no new outbox is required merely to read committed reports. No new general inbox, assignment engine, personal-owner lock, task per report or mandatory In progress state. Reading a report does not claim it, lock reader edits or manufacture a handling event. Curator absence or departure uses supported source stewardship with current authorization; it never grants every staff member the report body.

**Q57-C05 — A complete permitted return journey.** Provide a small Your reports view inside Resources, plus contextual access to the reader's own reports when appropriate. It contains bounded current-authorized report records, not a global inbox, unread obligation, conversation, subscription or archive of resource bytes. The owning source explicitly admits the reporter's minimal receipt/status projection and separately admits report text, title, outcome and history fields. A reader may retain a permitted minimal receipt/status after resource replacement or library withdrawal only while current independent report-purpose rights allow it; identity equality or retention alone grants nothing. Lack of current source context, membership/assignment where required, or a restrictive rule denies the affected projection. Where allowed, a neutral Resource unavailable label replaces a hidden title/link; report content/outcome may be withheld independently. No receipt link restores historical resource access or internal notes. Show actual original submission and relevant edited/handled/restored timestamps with localized display and clear meaning. Current report state is distinct from content safety, freshness, resource availability and proof a curator read it. Preserve known receipt when a secondary refresh fails.

**Q57-C06 — Correct an open report without rewriting history.** Permit the original reporter, under current independent report-edit authority, to edit their own Open report's text against the exact current report revision. Keep tenant, organization, publication target, original author and original receipt time immutable. The source retains attributable correction history and a new content/state basis; ordinary projections show only currently permitted text, not every prior private body. Editing remains possible after resource replacement/withdrawal only where current report-purpose rights permit it; it grants no source-byte access. A true canonical no-op, checked after original-result recovery and current authority/basis, creates no revision or business event. An intervening state/content change, including an Open→Handled→Open cycle, makes a stale edit conflict even when values look equal. A curator's stale handling form must refresh after a real edit. Staff cannot impersonate the reporter or silently rewrite their allegation. Wrong target uses deliberate withdrawal plus a new currently eligible report; changing a report's target is not an edit. New information after Handled is a new deliberate report on a currently eligible publication, with no automatic copying of protected old text or new chat thread.

**Q57-C07 — Withdrawal and recovery of an accidental withdrawal.** Permit the original reporter to deliberately withdraw their own Open report under current report-withdraw authority, with concise consequence copy. A source restriction or missing retained body can block restoration, with an actual supported source/privacy repair or fresh-report path where permitted. ## Seven founder checks

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A report could expose protected context, be received without capable handling or be mistaken for resource review/fix. Wrong actors, partial writes, stale forms and restored sensitive history are material risks. Exact current report-purpose rights, coherent source outcomes, supported repair and native proof are required; three state labels alone do not prevent them.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

We assume actual governed P28 resource context and capable source stewardship, not all-staff access or a curator continuously online. Report demand, handling capacity, task-time savings, native implementation and incremental costs are unmeasured. Retention, authorship and a valid session do not establish current report or resource access.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adds one P28 report purpose, explicit bounded reader/curator maintenance actions and a small lawful return projection. Files custody, resource publication/review, P12 permission, personal Help, privacy and existing source effects retain their own authorities. Broader staff-reference readership remains a separate scope decision; no global inbox, task engine, notification or financial model is added.

One contextual note and a durable receipt reduce copying. Your reports, own Open editing, Withdraw/Restore and curator outcome correction make ordinary mistakes recoverable. Current primary knowledge/CRM products support contextual feedback and accountable handling; W3C supports clear predictable forms, status and correction.
