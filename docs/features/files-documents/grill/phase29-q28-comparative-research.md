# Phase 29 Q28 — Staff-supplied reuse of existing evidence

**Research date:** 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q27 and their amendments are ratified. Q28 has no selected answer. This is next-question research, not a full selected-answer review, formal specification or native qualification.

## Recommended decision

**For a new ordinary request/item, may authorized staff deliberately supply an eligible document Asym already holds, or must the participant first affirm that exact document's use for this new request?**

Both options avoid download and re-upload. Both preserve the exact existing bytes and original provenance, create a separately admitted destination-purpose evidence relationship, and retain the new item's independent staff review/acceptance. Actual source-specific confirmation, consent, freshness or personal-attestation requirements prevail under either option.

| Option                                                      | Practical behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Benefits                                                                                                                    | Costs and boundaries                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Allow deliberate staff-supplied reuse (recommended)** | Authorized staff choose an eligible existing version for the exact new ordinary item and deliberately supply it. Record it as staff-supplied. No blanket new participant confirmation is required where neither source nor destination requires one. | Supports assistance and avoids asking a participant to repeat a mechanical handoff when qualifying evidence already exists. | Staff can choose the wrong person/version or assume old information is still appropriate. Staff supply is not proof that the participant uploaded, affirmed, consented or signed today.                         |
| **B — Require fresh participant affirmation**               | Staff prepare the same exact eligible candidate, but the intended participant must affirm its use for the new item before it enters normal submitted-evidence review. No byte re-upload is required.                                                 | Gives the participant a direct opportunity to confirm current applicability and correct staff assumptions.                  | Adds a response and authorized-presentation dependency, even for routine assistance. Confirmation is not review acceptance or proof of legal identity. Ended access cannot be revived merely to get the answer. |

Recommend A for the ordinary baseline, with honest staff attribution and genuine source-required participant acts retained. B is a strong participant-control alternative, especially where current endorsement is useful even when not otherwise required. Neither is universally optimal on the evidence gathered.

## Practical example

Illustrative situation, not a claimed ministry practice: Asym lawfully holds Nora's certificate. A new ordinary request needs that certificate, and the original and destination sources permit use of this exact version. Elena has actual authority to supply it; the example does not assume every prior document qualifies.

With A, Elena opens the target item, selects **Add existing file**, checks the permitted person/version/context and commits **Add to this item**. The result is staff-supplied evidence awaiting the new item's normal review. It does not invent a new upload, pretend Nora acted today or transfer the old item's acceptance.

With B, Elena prepares that same exact candidate. Nora confirms its use for the new item through an authorized current presentation. She does not upload the bytes again. If the necessary material cannot safely and accessibly be presented, blind confirmation is not a substitute. A substantive source-required affirmation remains necessary under A too.

In both cases, the original uploader/source, actual staff actor, exact version, new purpose and reviewer remain distinct. Receipt, technical usability, acceptance and source fulfillment remain separate. The participant does not need a general file-browser account.

## Boundaries for the founder question

1. **Authority at both ends.** Original-source permission for the exact reuse/disclosure and destination authority for the exact evidence admission are both necessary. Read/download rights, an old approval, a common email, equal bytes or a familiar filename are insufficient. Neither A nor B can make a prohibited use valid by confirming it.

2. **Truthful provenance.** A records staff supply as staff supply, with the actual actor, target and admission time while preserving original lineage. It must not say the participant uploaded, affirmed or signed now. B's affirmation is an additional precise act, not a rewrite of file provenance. Staff help does not authorize changing participant statements.

3. **Independent acceptance and requirements.** Q21's deliberate decision on the new item/evidence basis and Q22's full source requirement predicate remain. Existing acceptance does not transfer. No universal second reviewer is added: separate supply/review powers and actual separation-of-duties rules govern. Currentness, consent, certification and specialist obligations are not reduced to a clean scan or possession.

4. **Participant visibility is separately authorized.** Q02/Q04/Q05 own-submission readback does not automatically expose staff-supplied evidence, original-source metadata, other uses or private notes. The target's safe preview/download/history projection needs explicit qualification. B requires enough permitted information for meaningful affirmation; A cannot fabricate a participant act to reuse an existing reader path.

5. **Current target, no hidden repair powers.** The new ordinary item must admit the operation under its current rules. Participant expiry alone does not universally prevent independent authorized staff work, but real intake prohibitions, frozen evidence and closed/fulfilled work remain binding. This choice does not reopen a request, replace accepted evidence, alter requirements, renew access, reset clocks or extend retention.

6. **Exact use and safe selection.** Reuse preserves immutable bytes while creating a separately admitted use. Shared custody does not merge source meanings or obligations, and copying bytes is not a permission workaround. The existing target context can supply a filtered picker; authorize metadata/search/preview and recheck exact actor/source/version/target at commitment. No hidden duplicate match, provider URL or automatic download is required.

7. **Race and recovery honesty.** A participant correction, concurrent staff addition, changed target instruction or source restriction while the picker is open cannot silently overwrite current evidence or switch versions. Unknown outcome recovers the original intended add, not another copy/use. Empty eligible results differ from unavailable coverage; safe errors cannot reveal a protected file's existence.

8. **Other source and communication meanings remain separate.** Phase18 official artifacts, Phase21 evidence, Phase22/23 media, Phase26 conversation attachments and Phase28 resources retain their owner rules. Q26/Q27 notices apply only to their exact admitted events; this decision selects no automatic affirmation email or other new notice. The chosen answer's review must qualify native staff-origin evidence/readback/event bindings rather than inherit participant-upload behavior accidentally.

These are necessary context for an informed A/B choice. They do not claim every source family or the native staff-supply capability is already implemented. The selected answer receives the complete adversarial review before ratification.

## Strongest B and recommendation

B gives the participant a direct opportunity to reject outdated or inappropriate reuse. Current Submittable guidance favors participant involvement for sensitive or complicated changes; Content Snare illustrates a participant completing prepared information. If a current personal assertion is materially needed, A must honor that source requirement as well.

A avoids imposing a new response dependency on every ordinary reuse where both sources already allow staff supply and require no fresh assertion. FileInvite and Submittable demonstrate useful staff assistance with bounded roles/provenance. The recommendation fits flexible shared custody only when it is an accountable staff act, not an unrestricted “we already have it” shortcut.

The costs are real: A requires trustworthy selection, authority and provenance; B adds availability, presentation and comprehension dependencies. Neither is proven faster or safer across all Asym contexts. Both avoid re-upload, and neither should add a mandatory long reason form, generic signature/consent ceremony, second actor or notification engine without its actual source need.
