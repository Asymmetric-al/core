## Historical question and alternatives

**Should authorized staff be able to cancel an issued, unfinished ordinary
document request when its purpose has ended or it was issued in error?**

Q33 ends an unnecessary individual item demand. It expressly does not cancel
the whole request. Q22 distinguishes cancellation from successful fulfillment:
staff cannot turn an ended purpose into success by marking every item No longer
required. Q23 requires capable source resolution but has not granted a new
ordinary cancellation action. This is therefore a genuine next product choice.

| Option                                                                                | Everyday experience                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Benefit                                                                                                                                            | Cost                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Allow authorized staff to cancel an unfinished ordinary request (recommended)** | Staff with the actual source cancellation authority deliberately end this request's remaining ordinary work. Show Canceled distinctly from Fulfilled and preserve received evidence/history and independent obligations. | Ends a mistaken or obsolete whole demand directly, without deleting files, pretending requirements were satisfied or waiting for access to expire. | Requires a clear terminal source outcome, current access/work effects, visible consequences and a real remedy for mistakes.                                                                 |
| **B — Use existing source resolution routes without adding ordinary cancellation**    | Add no new ordinary Cancel request action. Staff use an already-qualified capable disposition/resolution route owned by the requesting source.                                                                           | Avoids a second interaction where the source already has effective withdrawal or termination procedures.                                           | Can require leaving the request or a staff handoff. The route must actually resolve the demand; revoking a link, waiting for expiry or removing every item is not an equivalent substitute. |

**Recommend A.** People need an honest way to stop work that the organization no
longer needs. A clear source-owned Canceled outcome is easier to understand
than a request that remains apparently outstanding or is falsely completed.
Reuse an existing qualified source command where one supplies the exact
behavior; otherwise qualify the bounded ordinary capability. Do not add a
competing File Manager writer, cancellation workflow builder or generic
permission for every reviewer/administrator.

A/B are product alternatives, not two tenant settings to implement. Draft
discard, participant withdrawal, bulk cancellation, cancellation notices and
general reopening are not bundled into this question.

## Concrete example and user journeys

Elena issued an ordinary request to Nora for supporting documents. Before the
request finishes, Elena establishes that the entire request was issued by
mistake. One item may already have been received or accepted. With A, an
authorized Elena cancels the remaining request; its history remains truthful.
With B, she uses the source's actual capable resolution process. This is an
illustrative test scenario, not an observed ministry workflow or frequency claim.

clear explanation that the request will end without being fulfilled. Explain
the actual access and recovery consequences before commitment. Capture only
the source-required decision basis; no routine extra approver, mandatory essay
or reason-code platform is proposed. Participant-facing guidance and internal
history need separate disclosure rules.

## Current primary research and strongest alternative

The [comparative research](phase29-q34-comparative-research.md) preserves the
primary-source register, differences and complete journey analysis.

- [Submittable Additional Forms](https://submittable.help/en/articles/2670501-how-can-i-track-additional-forms)
  distinguishes organization rescission from completion and prohibits rescinding
  completed forms. It is a close whole-request analogy. Its particular email
  and access effects are not inherited by Core.
- [Dropbox file requests](https://help.dropbox.com/share/create-file-request)
  explicitly supports closing a request so an old link cannot accept new files,
  while keeping closed requests available to the requester. Reactivation is a
  separate documented action; it does not authorize a Core reopen shortcut.
- [TaxDome client-request actions](https://help.taxdome.com/article/actions-with-client-requests)
  permits archiving only completed requests and distinguishes irreversible
  deletion. This is evidence that Archive, Delete and Cancel are not interchangeable
  across products. Do not copy a Complete-first workaround into Q22.
- [Foundant Close Requests](https://support.foundant.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404592155671-Close-Requests)
  describes approved-grant cleanup with domain-specific follow-up consequences.
  It is not Core's cancellation semantics; the comparative note records why its
  close/revert behavior cannot be imported by label alone.
- [Content Snare security settings](https://contentsnare.com/help/knowledge-base/security-settings/)
  describe closed/archived access consequences. [FileInvite's app-edition overview](https://help.fileinvite.com/articles/invite-overview)
  distinguishes archive from completion and documents ongoing reminder controls.
  These reinforce that current intake, access and downstream work need explicit
  source semantics; a cosmetic archive flag is insufficient.

The research supports giving people an explicit way to end collection. It does
not establish that every comparable product uses the same cancellation state,
preserves the same history, or applies the same permissions and recovery rules.
Core's source ownership and Q22 completion remain governing.

The strongest argument for B is that a requesting source may already manage
withdrawal/termination well. A should expose that authoritative operation where
appropriate rather than create a duplicate mechanism. B cannot mean a decorative
contact link without an operator able to resolve the request. Neither option
lets an expired link or a hidden list row stand in for a business disposition.

No Asym cancellation volume, staff/participant interview, measured drop-off or
time-saving result is available. A is a product judgment grounded in the existing
request lifecycle and current comparable capabilities. Representative-user proof
must later verify that Canceled is understood as ended work, not accepted evidence,
successful completion, deletion or automatic rejection of a larger process.

## Repository reconciliation and scope boundaries

The [owner research](phase29-q34-owner-research.md) and
[runtime research](phase29-q34-runtime-research.md) preserve exact source anchors,
fresh live inspection and evidence limits.

1. **Actual open capability:** Q22's canceled/withdrawn exclusions and Q33's
   item-only boundaries refer to distinct source outcomes; they do not already
   select this ordinary cancellation command. Q23's capable-resolution duty is
   preserved. Q34 would add a bounded whole-request action where absent.
2. **Source authority:** actual source/P12 cancellation permission and current
   lifecycle govern. Generic task status/delete, folder actions, staff identity,
   reviewer responsibility and grant revocation do not confer this power.
3. **Terminal truth:** show Canceled distinctly from Fulfilled. Preserve actual
   prior receipt/acceptance and normal fulfillment if it won the race. Partial
   accepted items do not make the aggregate ineligible merely by analogy to Q33.
   Never cancel all items to manufacture fulfillment or rewrite history.
4. **Access and duties:** end this request's ordinary intake/continuation through
   current source admission. Independently lawful staff follow-through, other
   purposes, history, custody and retention remain governed. Cancellation does
   not admit Q06/Q10 completed reading, a fresh Q13/Q16 period or any new history
   scope. Current protected terminal disclosure must be deliberate and minimal.
5. **Existing effects:** reconcile pending source work and actual current-purpose
   checks. Existing delivery identity and committed provider truth remain; no
   generic recall, blanket all-email stop, repurposed message or automatic new
   cancellation notice is selected. Do not copy vendor reminder behavior.
6. **Recovery and usability:** the source must resolve mistakes and uncertain
   results through a real qualified route, preserving closure and current rights.
   No generic reopen/restore authority or new-request shortcut follows from a
   canceled status. Keep the action and safe result in the existing surface.
7. **Proportionate design:** use one source outcome and existing work/history/
   custody/permission boundaries. No extra tenant mode, full Studio prerequisite,
   cancellation engine, destructive cleanup or new communication platform.

Q33 already establishes its own exact item-removal correction and terminal
remedy. This question does not reopen or expand that ratified decision. Broader
sent-request revisions, participant withdrawal and notice policy remain separate
choices. P28 resource-library curation/publication/withdrawal also remains settled.
