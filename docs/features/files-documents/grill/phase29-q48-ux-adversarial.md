2026-10-25, Asia/Bangkok. ## Disposition and concrete policy proposal

**Accept with required amendments.** A reminder must concern real outstanding recipient action through a currently valid source path. An unfinished request, passed target date or staff review queue is not that predicate. The practical experience is one small reminder card in the request, a bounded tenant profile, a deliberate Send reminder action and a recipient control over automatic follow-up. The scheduler/dispatch proof is deeper than the visible UI, but does not require a new general automation, preference, inbox or campaign platform.

The following policy is a reasoned product proposal coordinated with root/owner/runtime during this review. Its numbers are **not empirical best practices, legal requirements or vendor defaults adopted by analogy**. They provide one reviewable starting policy rather than leaving cadence, restart and noise as an unnamed release decision.

## Why this shape is preferable to the strongest alternatives

**Due-relative automation** can be useful near real deadlines, but ordinary Requested by is optional and editable, and Q09 permits otherwise-authorized late intake. A pure before/on/after target schedule creates extra semantics for absent/unresolved targets, date moves and catching up missed dates. The proposed calendar interval from actual work/enrollment uses current due information only when safe and relevant; it does not make a date mandatory or become a hard cutoff. This is a simplification judgment, not proof that due-relative schedules are wrong generally.

**Elapsed N×24 hours** is a viable technical alternative, but it can shift the displayed local send time through daylight-saving changes. The selected calendar-date rule with a separate elapsed 24-hour floor is explicit about that distinction. Its cost is occasionally delaying a daily candidate when the floor or DST rule prevents the nominal slot; show the real next time rather than claiming exact periodic delivery.

**Staff-triggered only** remains the strongest product alternative. Submittable documents such a flow; a staff member may know about a sensitive circumstance, promised delay or recent call that is not represented in source work. A therefore needs comprehensible Pause/Stop, recipient control and real limits. Staff can still manage every reminder manually. No Asym frequency, failure rate, preferred cadence, task-success gain, labor saving or billing effect has been measured.

## Eighteen exact required amendments

Severity **H** means a credible high consequence if omitted, **M** a moderate one. Likelihood is a design-exposure estimate, not an observed incident rate. All remain prospective safeguards/proof, not claims of implemented behavior.

### U01 — One new exact source reminder purpose (H / plausible)

Qualify ordinary recipient-work reminder email as its own exact source/P17/P6 purpose, with both automatic and deliberate staff initiation. Do not borrow contribution reminders, task flags, permission Access-request rules or a generic deadline key. Actual source work, recipient, target scope, permitted content and useful next action control eligibility; the scheduler only proposes occurrences. Received work awaiting staff checks/review is not recipient work. Q21/Q22 acceptance/fulfillment, Q23 corrections, Q33 requirement removal, Q34 cancellation and Q47 receipt/re-offer retain their actual meanings. No workflow installation is needed merely to make native reminders work.

### U02 — Deliberate tenant activation and future enrollment (H / plausible)

Automatic starts Off until the actual source/tenant management power completes applicable guarded activation. A meaningful preview and non-authoritative test show profile, recipients' qualified shape, stop/count semantics and consequences without a real send. After Enable, future eligible issued requests use the inherited plan unless staff choose Off; no old request is enrolled silently. Existing requests need deliberate Start. Current source authority always applies; unknown policy is not On. Native request issuance/receipt/review and independently qualified manual reminders must still work while automatic is Off or unavailable.

### U03 — One bounded tenant profile, captured per request (M–H / plausible)

Use the proposed interval, cap, local time and IANA-zone bounds/defaults in the policy table. Reject invalid/nonfinite/fractional counts, unsupported zones and unqualified defaults, rather than guessing or clamping silently. Capture actual effective profile/version in the request plan. No per-request numerical editor or policy scripting language is necessary. New tenant defaults affect future plans; explicit Use current tenant settings can update an existing plan with previewed next timing, preserving lifetime count and the recipient's stop. Settings adoption does not silently resume a paused/stopped plan. Known current source defaults may resolve without redundant tenant rows; unresolved profile meaning blocks dependent automatic work.

### U04 — Exact calendar schedule and elapsed spacing (H / plausible)

Compute due civil date by adding N calendar days to the actual anchor's local date, at captured HH:mm/zone. Resolve a fold to its later occurrence and a gap to the first valid time after it, retaining sufficient calculation evidence. Apply the 24-elapsed-hour courtesy floor after a fresh automatic anchor and the separate 24-elapsed-hour Q48 exposure floor; if either excludes the nominal slot, choose the next normal daily local-time slot. Do not use browser clocks, UTC midnight, a mutable organization-zone lookup or a claim that calendar N days always equals N×24 hours. A time-zone-rule upgrade must preserve already admitted occurrence identity and historical interpretation, not create another send. Display next actual time/zone when known; otherwise show why it is unavailable.

### U05 — Work transitions, pause and missed schedule (H / plausible)

First Start/enrollment, explicit Resume, or a proven false→true return of recipient work creates a new waiting anchor and full interval, without resetting count. Continuing partial work edits or target changes do not restart the timer. When no recipient work remains, automatic is quiet even if the request remains open for staff. Unknown work is not false and cannot mint a new transition or reset. Every Q48 possible provider call, including automatic/manual origin and permitted follow-up attempts, advances the next cadence anchor to its actual possible-call time without resetting the count or extending that occurrence's original call window. Automatic calls are allowed only in the original half-open 60-minute slot window and under earlier limits. A wake within that window is ordinary delayed execution; a wake at or after its end skips/coalesces to one next future normal daily slot, with no backlog burst. Staff Pause/Off is distinct from source work becoming quiet. Source changes still refresh current content/usefulness; an unchanged schedule does not authorize stale wording.

### U06 — Stop, disable and explicit restart (H / plausible)

Tenant automatic Disable and request Pause/Off stop affected new automatic occurrences and later unadmitted provider calls; re-enable does not resume stopped existing plans. Explicit per-request Resume requires current plan/source/P12 qualification, preserves used count and starts a new full interval. A committed/possible external attempt remains true and cannot be recalled. The stop mechanism must apply to every automatic class in this declared plan, not leave a second hidden ongoing loop active. Manual initiation remains independently governed unless broader source/contact restrictions prohibit it. Do not transpose another purpose's Off policy or treat unknown as explicit Off.

### U07 — Lifetime cap and truthful budget accounting (H / plausible)

The automatic cap is per issued request, not per episode, actor, transport key, plan revision, pause or recipient-work reset. Reserve a slot coherently before possible outward exposure. Release only a proved zero-exposure suppressed reservation through its original outcome; once a provider call may have happened, retain the used count even after rejection or unknown result. Retrying the same occurrence does not spend a new business slot or reset the counter. Changes to profile, date, instructions, fresh access, receipts or Q47 resupply cannot refill the cap. Show usage as automatic occurrences/attempts with truthful outcomes, never as a delivered count. After exhaustion, manual reminders can still qualify subject to current rules.

### U08 — One manual/automatic lane and noise rule (H / plausible)

Manual Send reminder and automatic candidates share exact request/intended-recipient/Q48-purpose occurrence coordination and the 24-hour floor between distinct occurrences, measured from the earlier occurrence's latest possible call. P6-permitted identical-envelope follow-ups belong to that same original occurrence, keep its original deadlines and update the latest-call fact; this floor does not itself authorize or forbid them. At most one active Q48 lane owns unresolved submission/call recovery within its actual finite original call/usefulness lifecycle. A future scheduled plan does not occupy that delivery lane or block manual work all week. A manual possible-call occurrence postpones the next auto by N calendar days and does not spend the automatic-only count. Do not use a new transport key, manual path or later worker to replace unknown exposure. After the original lifecycle closes, preserve its unknown/used history and permit only a genuinely new currently qualified occurrence; do not block lawful future reminders forever or silently rekey the old one. Distinct legitimate requests and other message purposes are not text-deduplicated or merged.

### U09 — Recipient stop is a small source capability (H / plausible)

Add explicit source/P12 authority for the current intended recipient to Stop automatic reminders for this request through their actually qualified native context. It changes only automatic reminder preference, not work, access, all communications or source fulfillment. Show its exact scope before the deliberate action. Staff/tenant/profile changes cannot clear it; the recipient may explicitly allow automatic reminders again under current authority, applicable tenant plan and retained count. This clears only their own stop, never enrolls a missing plan or clears staff/tenant stops; if otherwise eligible, it starts a new full waiting interval instead of exposing an old overdue slot. Do not invent a bearer unsubscribe grant or let link possession impersonate the recipient. Email can direct them to the protected management action; proof is completed on entry as needed. If that route is no longer available, automatic reminders already lack the required current work route, and safe source assistance handles legitimate preference repair. Existing broader contact/privacy suppressions still bind manual sends.

### U10 — Current useful route without access renewal (H / plausible)

The first email cohort requires actual outstanding recipient work and a current qualified route for it. It does not send an expired upload CTA or rewrite into a help-only variant after preparing material. No new link grant, copied original grant, reissue, completed access or source reopening follows from reminder scheduling. A recipient need not be online or have an active completed proof exchange when email is prepared; an actually valid entry/proof/action path suffices, with proof enforced when used. Wrong recipients, current restrictions, no safe route or unknown required authority block the dependent occurrence. Staff repair access through already qualified source procedures, without canceling otherwise lawful work or manufacturing a new request.

### U11 — Current material and small safe email (H / plausible)

Use minimal current source-approved context that distinguishes this request and describes the truthful next action. Do not disclose private filenames, document bodies, internal review comments, sensitive rationale or unrelated relationships in subject/preheader/body/logs. Requested by is an optional current coordination fact, not an access guarantee or invented overdue verdict. Keep original receipt timing/history intact. The first purpose has no attachments, rich campaign editor, SMS, push, tracking-as-reading, reply ingestion or automatic forwarding. Plain text/images-off/mobile mail must preserve safe identification and a descriptive qualified action. Preserve separately qualified source contacts, recipient envelopes and protected carriers under their own contracts, rather than a blanket ban on necessary transport material.

### U12 — P17/P6 release and every-call currentness (H / plausible)

Bind the original source occurrence, plan/recipient/policy/material and complete release through P17/P6's actual owner contracts. Recheck relevant source actionability, contact/policy stops and remaining utility at every owning admission/provider-call boundary, including later retries; a worker's initial candidate scan is not sufficient. The first manual optional external-message material class has an original-preparation ceiling of 24 hours, shortened by source/provider/other contract limits; the automatic 60-minute slot window is a stricter call bound and is never extended by queue delay. Propose initial Recent readable-copy ceiling **0**, with body-free business/effect history; any future readable-copy change needs its own exact owner qualification. Sealed delivery material and minimal outcome/replay evidence retain their separate actual contracts. Sealed material cannot be rewritten into a different request/action/recipient. Suppressed, failed, rejected, queued, potentially submitted, delivered and unknown remain distinct. Safe evidence-only reconciliation cannot cause another send. Existing source/P6 retention/retry/idempotency rules must be explicitly qualified for this purpose; no direct provider call or speculative generic deadline producer is an alternative owner.

The manual source occurrence additionally expires no later than 24 hours after its original source admission; later preparation cannot move that deadline. The material ceiling from first preparation and automatic slot window only further narrow it, never extend the source limit. This closes delayed-preparation cases explicitly.

### U13 — Preserve other decisions and communication purposes (H / plausible)

Q36/Q37/Q46/Q47 can alter genuine source work/current terms but create no extra Q48 occurrence by themselves. The explicit work transition/ongoing-anchor rules decide timing. Initial notices, Q20 fresh-access delivery, Q26 Request changes, Q31 reply awareness, Q35 cancellation and Q40 requirement-update awareness keep their own purpose and original act. No source message is relabeled as a reminder to bypass preference, privacy or count limits. No global one-email/day, digest, escalation, permission Access-request cadence or generic Workflow business-day default is imported. Existing broader contact limits still apply.

### U14 — Compact staff/admin UI with no accidental send (M–H / likely for affected users)

Use one reminder card showing automatic state, captured profile/time zone, used/maximum count, latest actual outcome and next eligible time when known. Offer only actions valid in the current state: Start, Pause/Resume, Off, Use current tenant settings and Send reminder as appropriate. Staff do not need to open files, create a full account for the recipient, write a reason essay or pass a second approval to make a normal lawful send. Present recipient/request/current effect before final Send. Editing interval/time, selecting an option or opening a preview does not itself send. A known too-soon, recipient-stop, exhausted-auto, pending-unknown or no-action state needs an explanatory next step, not an unexplained disabled button or false Sent. Manual after cap does not bypass the 24-hour lane guard.

### U15 — Complete accessible international and weak-network flow (M–H / likely for affected users)

Use shared Base UI/base-maia controls with keyboard, voice, screen reader and touch operation, adequate target spacing, meaningful names/focus return and non-color-only meaning. Reflow narrow/zoomed views without hiding the counter, zone, destination or Stop/Cancel. Localized dates may vary presentation but not stored calendar semantics; handle long Unicode/RTL names and IME safely. Announce important pending/result/error changes without a chatty timer or forced focus. Preserve lawful input when validation or response fails, reconcile the original command and prevent late other-context results from exposing private information or replacing current status. No silent offline send queue, preview/download dependency, forced desktop or motion-only state.

### U16 — Source-owned correction and operating recovery (H / plausible)

An accidental plan enable/pause/profile adoption or recipient stop is repaired by a new currently authorized deliberate setting action, not by rolling back history or pretending mail was unsent. Preserve genuine stopped-effect history and post-call count. Wrong destination or unsafe material uses the actual privacy/source repair route; do not retry through another address, shared mailbox or invented channel. Supported failures need capable staff/operator actions with actual scope and minimized evidence; no SQL/provider-console workaround, raw body access or powerless Contact admin link. Native request/receipt/review/help stays usable when reminder automation or email is unavailable.

### U17 — Small bounded implementation and rollout qualification (H / plausible)

Use existing source/P12/P9/P17/P6 and qualified scheduling primitives. Persist only source plan snapshots/control revisions, actual work-anchor/occurrence/used-budget/lane/outcome facts and required history; no generic event-sourcing, rule language, calendar or new preference platform is selected. Prove native same-scope/actor/recipient links, exact semantic uniqueness, concurrent reservation/count/lane rules and every permitted writer/reader. Bounded indexed candidate scans, fair tenant batches, short transactions, finite retry/material/usefulness limits and current stops are necessary. No per-keystroke/pointer writes or full-tenant scan per request. Prospective migration must not backfill On or replay old dates; restore applies adverse current stop/history before selection. Actual native workload/cost/AT/user proof remains required; no free-tier guarantee.

### U18 — Explicit policy adoption and evidence honesty (M / plausible)

Treat this profile, tenant enrollment/default, recipient stop, lifetime counting, calendar interpretation, no-backlog restart, manual overlap and exact first reminder purpose as explicit required amendments for ratification. The question chose automatic capability; it did not already decide these consequences. Owner bindings and guarded-activation requirements must be complete rather than deferred to an unnamed policy review. No new term is necessary unless domain review identifies a distinct enduring concept; a new implementation table alone is insufficient. Q48 is selected pending full review/ratification, not shipped. Retain B and alternative timing models as real tradeoffs and distinguish product judgment from vendor documentation and native proof.
