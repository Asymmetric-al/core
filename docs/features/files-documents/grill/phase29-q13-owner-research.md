## Recommended next single decision

The more consequential next branch is **whether the source can deliberately grant fresh finite access to the same still-unfinished request after its ordinary access period expired**. Settle that capability and its coherent completion consequence before numerical Q08 fresh-read settings. Otherwise the ordinary journey has a known recovery boundary but no chosen in-product response for legitimate unfinished work.

Suggested question:

> If ordinary access has expired but the request is still legitimately unfinished, should specifically authorized staff be able to approve fresh, finite access to finish the same permitted work, or should access to that request remain ended?

| Option                                                   | Exact proposed behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Practical tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Allow source-approved fresh access (recommended)** | A deliberate new finite admission for the same intended recipient, request and purpose, bounded by the originally admitted requirement scope and current permitted work. Old access/end remains ended. If the source normally fulfills while this fresh authority is valid and eligible, it can admit the Q07-style completed view from that actual fulfillment. | Preserves existing work, receipts and review context rather than requiring duplicate requests. Adds a consequential source/P12 grant command, exact timing/repetition policy and lifecycle proof.                 |
| **B — Keep this request's access ended**                 | No fresh unfinished-access admission through this lane. Keep accountable source help and any independently authorized, genuinely distinct source procedures.                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Simpler authority model with less repeat-access exposure. Some legitimate outstanding work needs another properly governed resolution; ordinary cloning, SQL edits or email attachments cannot be the workaround. |

A is a proposed **prospective owner amendment**, not behavior already supplied by Q08/Q10 or by an existing Renew button. B is a credible simpler alternative, not a claim that support may silently re-create the same admission under another ID.

The choice contains one coherent capability: lawful continuation of the same unfinished work, including what happens if that work then completes. It does not choose a new number, anchor, repeat count, overlap rule, maximum cumulative lifetime, notification cadence or staff role name. Those are explicit activation dependencies. In particular, A does **not** mean another 60 days, unlimited repetition or a one-use-only grant.

## Why the completion consequence must be visible in this question

Q10 currently admits completed reading only from exact eligible normal fulfillment while the applicable unfinished authority exists. Q11 requires original source admission/eligibility at the causal fulfillment point. Q12 supplies the original 1,440-hour profile; none supplies recovery after it ends.

If Q13 merely says “permit more uploads” and leaves its lifecycle implicit, implementations can either incorrectly deny every later completed view because the original deadline expired, or silently award a new completed period without owner approval. The founder should understand A as this explicit continuation model:

- The original expired admission remains immutable and ended.
- A separately authorized fresh admission must actually exist in the authoritative source order **before or as part of a lawful atomic operation establishing eligibility at normal fulfillment**. A staff click before completion or a later backdated grant is insufficient.
- Actual eligible normal fulfillment during that fresh unfinished authority closes ordinary submission/correction and admits a bounded completed read-only view under Q07's at-most-720-hour rule and genuine applicable independent caps.
- The completed anchor is the actual normal source fulfillment, not fresh approval, credential delivery or repair time. No completed interval begins merely because fresh unfinished access was granted.
- Completion that wins a race before a fresh unfinished admission is established cannot be repaired by pretending the grant existed earlier. Independently valid source completion still stands without an invented recipient admission.
- A request already legitimately complete is not reopened or cycled solely to obtain this capability. Later invalidation/remediation retains the owning source procedure.

This is a narrowly proposed extension of Q10's applicable-authority mapping. It preserves no revival of old rights; it deliberately creates new authority only after the founder chooses A and the exact owner contract is adopted. The question should name this consequence plainly rather than present it as already ratified.

## Scope of a new unfinished admission

The proposed upper bound is the **originally admitted request/requirement purpose scope intersected with current eligible work and the fresh decision's reviewed subset/actions**. This preserves the same intended recipient under the current source-authorized identity/contact revision. It is not a raw email or literal stale mailbox match.

This differs from copying Q08's completed-file membership bound. Completing outstanding requirements necessarily allows newly received files and eligible corrections for those same requirements. A fresh grant bounded only to the old file IDs would make the capability unusable; a grant that treats the whole request as open could wrongly reopen writes/corrections to accepted items or admit new requirements. Independently current, permitted own-file readback of accepted items is not prohibited. Keep exact requirement lineage, evidence-set revisions, permitted submission/correction actions, current own-file readback/history and current proof separate.

No historical-byte archive, other recipients' submissions, staff/internal evidence, general folder access, arbitrary uploads or change to business acceptance follows. Q02/Q04/Q05 keep their exact current-own-submission reading rules. Newly admitted corrections retain Q03's immediate predecessor fence and do not create a new lifetime by themselves.

If A later creates the first legitimate completed-view admission from this continued lifecycle, record that admission and scope accurately. Its eligibility for a later Q08 fresh read is not selected by Q13: any proposal to broaden Q08's originally ratified completed-view eligibility requires an explicit compatible owner decision before activation. Do not silently union scopes, add later files outside it or grant Q08 automatically. Even an admitted future Q08 path would retain its own current review and qualified policy. This is a boundary/dependency to preserve, not adoption of that further capability now.

## Who may approve and how to keep one authority

Require the exact requesting source's fresh unfinished-access issuance capability and applicable P12 administrative scope/assignable ceiling at the effect boundary. Merely having file-read, reviewer, support, task assignment, staff title, service-role database access or original sender status is not sufficient. Map the actual source grant type and continuing issuer dependency; do not assume every later staff departure revokes it or every grant survives forever.

Use the existing source request context for one meaningful deliberate decision. Review the source-approved intended recipient, current outstanding requirement scope, permitted actions and qualified timing consequences. Do not add an arbitrary recipient/date picker, required essay, second universal approver, extra account or new console by default.

Preserve the original ended admission and its end. The fresh effect has its own deliberate stable identity, immutable approved scope and timing outcome, attribution and required source/audit/follow-up evidence. Retry recovers that same new effect under current disclosure; it does not mint another fresh admission. Source completion, revocation, recipient changes and competing issuance require current exact ordering. Provider calls stay outside source locks and P17/P6 own communication; approval need not imply an automatic message.

Q01 current assurance remains required before recipient use. Compatible current proof may be reused only where expressly qualified for the exact current request/recipient/purpose/fresh-authority context. Neither “new grant always requires a new account” nor “old verified JWT always suffices” follows.

## Dependencies deliberately not chosen here

If A is selected, these must be resolved and qualified before activation:

- Fresh unfinished duration and anchor, exact finite end calculation and any unused-admission bounds; **not automatically another 1,440 hours**.
- Deliberately separate repetition/overlap/cumulative-policy rules. No unlimited repeat, one-active-grant cardinality or one-shot count is inferred.
- The exact capability/type and current/continuing-dependency mapping through the existing sole PDP and native participant successor.
- Prospective Q10/Q07 continuation-to-completion mapping and legitimate first completed-admission lineage. Any later Q08 applicability to that lineage requires its own explicit compatible owner decision; it is not selected here. No eligibility backdating or completed-source reopen.
- Existing action-specific cutoffs, ordinary versus adverse expiry classification, scope revisions, retained evidence, real source concurrency and failure recovery.
- Recipient/reviewer/support usability, protected entry, database/service/storage enforcement, private metadata and bytes, provider/browser/assistive-technology qualification.

These dependencies refine a consequential capability; they must not become a claim that selecting A activates partial recovery. If the product cannot offer coherent completion without settling one of them, keep that lane unqualified rather than promising a working endpoint.

Choosing B also needs a useful owned help route. It does not grant a new replacement-request flow by implication, allow deleting accepted work, or authorize staff to bypass the decision through cloning/attachments. A truly distinct source need can use its already-authorized procedure; that is not an automatic alternate ID for the same expired grant.
