# Phase 29 Q35 — Independent UX and comparative adversarial review

Reviewed 2026-10-23, Asia/Bangkok. Conrad selected A: optional participant
cancellation email initially On, with staff control, and explicitly requested
tenant changeability to prevent unwanted noise. This review proposes exact
amendments for that selected answer. It does not treat selection as ratification
of every amendment or as implementation/activation authority. Q01–Q34 and the
twenty-three existing definitions remain ratified.

Only this assigned working note is authored. The historical Q35 question and
comparative note describe the unanswered stage; the current explicit selection
is authoritative. Source/runtime findings are attributed to the parallel owner
and runtime reviews rather than represented as independently repeated here.

## Recommendation and the strongest alternatives

Keep A, with the bounded controls and proof below. A person may still be
collecting documents after staff end the particular request. A short, safe,
recognizable cancellation notice can end that unnecessary work without requiring
a portal visit. This is a plausible product benefit, not a measured Asym result.
Native cancellation remains effective when email is Off, unavailable or fails.

The user's added flexibility is best expressed as two existing-owner settings:
whether this optional notice is available, and whether its per-cancellation
choice starts selected in a genuinely new cancellation review. The initial
qualified configuration offers the notice and starts that choice On. An
authorized tenant administrator can disable availability or change the initial
choice to Off. Authorized staff can change an eligible choice in the cancellation
review. No new global notification engine is necessary.

**B, initial per-act Off, is a strong alternative.** Outbound defaults are easy
to overlook, may duplicate a conversation already held and can disclose a
relationship at an erroneous address. It retains essentially the same underlying
delivery/privacy/recovery cost. The tenant's configurable initial Off meets that
operating preference without pretending it makes the capability cheap to build.
GOV.UK's advice against preselected checkbox options is real counterevidence;
calling the control an operational default does not remove the comprehension
risk. A must pass actual staff comprehension and participant interpretation proof.

**C, no new email purpose, is the real no-build alternative.** A capable existing
source communication process may already suffice, particularly for sensitive
or rarely canceled requests. Its cost is reliance on effective human follow-up
or a participant returning to the product. Neither daily portal use nor reliable
manual follow-up has been measured here. These remain fair product alternatives,
not three new tenant modes or an invitation to add reminders, personal preference
centers, channels, bulk sends or a message composer.

## Exact proposed amendments

Severity and likelihood describe harm in an unamended prospective design, not
deployed vulnerabilities or incident statistics. H denotes material authority,
privacy or business-truth harm; M denotes meaningful confusion, wasted work or
operational burden. Likelihood is qualitative and conditional. Each clause is
intended to become permanent source-owned requirements plus release proof.

### U01 — One exact purpose and committed occurrence

**H / plausible if a generic event is reused.** The cancellation notice MUST
refer to one genuine committed Q34 cancellation of an ordinary request and its
exact qualified participant. Opening a review, changing settings, previewing
content, a task status, expiry, archive, Q33 item removal, email retry or a new
replacement request MUST NOT create this occurrence. No notice can announce a
cancellation whose source result is unknown. The source owns occurrence,
audience, disclosure and usefulness; P17 owns the exact catalog/presentation,
bounded plan and generated routing; P6 owns complete release, recipient-specific
intent, dispatch, provider evidence and recovery while consuming current source
and canonical contact/consent/suppression gates. Prior families do not authorize
this new purpose. **Proof: P01, P02, P15.**

### U02 — Two bounded tenant controls and a stable reviewed choice

**M / probable confusion without explicit semantics.** Existing authorized P17
tenant configuration MUST separately govern this notice's availability and
initial per-cancellation selection, with the initially qualified values Enabled
and On. An authorized administrator may change either. The default seeds only
genuinely new cancellation reviews. It MUST NOT overwrite an already initialized
or reviewed On/Off choice merely because staff did not toggle it. A visibly
seeded value becomes the explicit submitted value at deliberate cancellation;
missing input or an older client does not imply On. A default-only change need
not cause a pointless conflict for an existing review. Availability and current
authority are still revalidated. Staff may request On only while Enabled and
currently eligible; they may always decline the optional notice for an otherwise
lawful cancellation. Requesting On does not override tenant disable, source
restrictions or canonical contact gates.

A common document-notification settings entry MAY expose the already-owned
purpose-specific controls. It MUST NOT silently impose Q35 defaults, pending-Off
behavior or a new master switch on Q20/Q26/Q27/Q31/Q32. Preserve those exact
ratified semantics. Use existing settings authority and feedback, not a new
permission editor or whole future Studio prerequisite. **Proof: P03, P04, P16.**

### U03 — Explicit Q35 disable effect, including unresolved sends

**H / plausible at configuration/transport races.** This is a proposed explicit
Q35-only stop rule, not inherited from another family. Tenant Disable MUST bar
each new sealed provider-submission attempt/call at its authoritative admission
before first byte, including original unresolved handoffs and later calls using
the same key. An operation already admitted before Disable may still complete
or remain unknown. Keep that possible in-flight truth and continue evidence
reconciliation; do not promise recall or relabel it Not sent. Disable is not an
instruction to discard original identity/evidence or ignore callbacks. Preserve
durable intervening Disabled history even if no worker ran before re-enable;
checking only the current Enabled value is insufficient. Re-enable
MUST NOT revive occurrences previously Off, disabled or suppressed. A default
change alone neither cancels an authorized queued occurrence nor creates one.
User-facing feedback MUST distinguish a stop before any admission from an
already admitted/unknown outcome. **Proof: P05, P06, P12.**

### U04 — Reviewable outward consequence without a new ritual

**H / plausible missed preselection; M / probable if labels are vague.** In the
existing Q34 cancellation review, show the exact request, cancellation effect,
effective email choice and authorized destination sufficiently clearly to review
before submission. Candidate label: **Email the request recipient about this
cancellation**. A clear safe recipient/address summary accompanies it. Keep
**Cancel request** unmistakable and place the current email consequence adjacent;
provide a distinct keep/back action. Do not rely on a vague Notify label, an
off-screen checked box, a tooltip or a notification that appears only after send.
Staff can change an eligible choice without a separate composer, second approver,
typed-name ritual, mandatory essay or extra routine confirmation.

Current availability/recipient changes must produce truthful inline feedback,
not a stale promise of email. Optional-mail ineligibility MUST NOT hold legitimate
Q34 cancellation hostage. If staff requested On but it is blocked or unresolved,
retain that requested choice and actual outcome; do not rewrite it to staff Off.
Opening a review or changing the checkbox never sends. Preselection is neither
recipient consent nor authority. Qualification MUST demonstrate that staff can
predict the outward effect and identify the recipient. **Proof: P04, P07, P19.**

### U05 — Exact recipient and disclosure authority without artificial identity

**H / plausible, especially mistaken-recipient cancellation.** Qualification
MUST establish current permission to disclose this exact cancellation to the
exact intended participant through the eligible route. Old invitations,
historical association, matching email strings, household membership, reviewer
role, prior reply authorship or staff cancellation authority are insufficient.
Do not expand to CCs, collaborators or everyone ever associated with the request.
Known-wrong-recipient issuance requires suppression of further unauthorized
disclosure. Current identity/contact changes MUST NOT silently reroute the sealed
notice to a different person or substitute new recipient material.

No Party/account may be fabricated solely to send the notice. An optional
readable history copy requires its own compatible current reveal path; absent
that path, retain lawful body-free evidence without a readable copy rather than
blocking independently lawful email or creating a Party. A staff UI may show
only destination/context that the staff member is authorized to inspect.
**Proof: P02, P08, P09, P14.**

### U06 — Safe, useful identification and minimal factual content

**H / plausible when requests look alike or a replacement exists.** The notice
MUST identify only the canceled request with enough source-qualified safe context
for the participant to distinguish it. Candidate meaning: **The document request
[safe reference] was canceled. You do not need to send more documents for that
request.** It MUST NOT imply that another valid request, application, case or
independent obligation ended, that evidence was deleted, or that the canceled
request was successfully fulfilled.

Do not insert internal rationale, filenames, document bodies, clarification
text, care/location facts, review history or arbitrary private request titles
into subject, preview, body, attachment or URL to achieve identification. A
random reference the participant cannot recognize is not automatically useful.
If minimal authorized identity cannot make the message intelligible, do not send
a vague stop-all-work message; retain cancellation and any genuinely qualified
source outreach route. This is not a new free-text template/composer requirement.
**Proof: P08, P10, P19.**

### U07 — Useful email is separate from Q34 portal access

**H / plausible if old access checks are repurposed.** Web expiry alone neither
grants nor universally forbids a useful factual cancellation email. Its own exact
current disclosure/contact purpose MUST be qualified. A linkless informational
variant can be valid, including when no Q34 outcome-only allowance survived at
cancellation. That is an explicitly qualified variant, not a worker-invented
fallback after sealing.

Any protected return CTA MUST obey Q34's exact surviving recipient/root, unchanged
end/caps and current restrictions. It cannot create/renew access, reveal files,
transcript or reason, challenge into a new grant, or redirect to a replacement.
If entry becomes unavailable before the participant opens it, return the current
safe Q34 unavailable/help result. Do not promise that the email preserves access.
Use an independently qualified actual Reply-To/help route if provided; do not
promise automatic email ingestion, a reopened Q30 conversation or a response SLA.
**Proof: P09, P11, P14.**

### U08 — Native truth and durable requested effect survive failures

**H / plausible at ordinary lost-response/handoff failures.** Required source
facts, exact staff choice and recoverable original-boundary communication handoff
MUST be coherent. The engineering layout may vary, but optional-mail preparation,
queue, provider or optional-copy failure MUST NOT undo committed cancellation or
leave obsolete intake active. If mail proof is temporarily unavailable, preserve
lawful native operation only with trustworthy original-boundary unresolved
handoff/evidence; unknown must not become proved zero or later-settings inference.
Recover cancellation and notice by their original semantic identities. If the
original notice authority cannot be proved, retain valid cancellation with a
truthful blocked/no-send historical notice outcome, not a promise of eventual
mail, invented zero or newly granted present-day eligibility.

Show Canceled only on durable source evidence; otherwise show/check the uncertain
native result. Separately distinguish not requested, blocked, pending,
provider-submitted, failed and unknown as justified by evidence. Provider
acceptance is not delivery or reading. Double-click, refresh, another tab and
retry MUST NOT create another cancellation, notice, replacement or intake grant.
Unknown provider outcomes require evidence-led recovery, not a blind resend.
**Proof: P06, P12, P13.**

### U09 — Current usefulness, finite material and immutable preparation

**H / plausible for delayed/recovered work.** Qualify this exact notice family's
closed prepared-material class, finite usefulness/retention boundaries and
operator recovery path. Permanent truth that a request was canceled does not
justify unbounded late email. Do not borrow Q34's web lifetime or a financial/
staff-email timer. Before each allowed provider admission, revalidate current
purpose, exact recipient disclosure, contact suppression and usefulness while
preserving original approved version/material/recipient pins. No latest-template,
registry or address substitution may silently rewrite sealed content. A
separately issued request does not automatically invalidate the old cancellation
fact, but safe distinguishability and useful meaning still must hold.
**Proof: P05, P10, P12, P17.**

### U10 — Privacy-safe history, diagnostics and remaining owner contracts

**H / plausible when generic history/log tools are reused.** Cancellation history,
provider evidence and optional readable copies have distinct authority and
retention. Keep private rationale/history bodies, internal delivery diagnostics
and unauthorized sensitive facts out of participant egress, support exports,
analytics, logs and browser/offline storage. This restriction does not prohibit
independently qualified source contacts, actual recipient envelopes or protected
entry carriers under their own unchanged contracts. No raw message body or
secret token is needed for routine status telemetry.

The participant never needs staff mail-delivery diagnostics to understand the
Q34 terminal result. Existing authorized help/source disclosures remain intact.
Preserve Q34's source fence and each prior notice's own current-purpose rules;
Q35 neither resurrects old reminders/conversations nor universally cancels every
independently lawful staff notice. **Proof: P14, P15, P17.**

### U11 — Accessible settings, cancellation and plain email

**M / probable without composed proof.** Use the existing base-maia/Base UI
surface with visible labels, proper programmatic names/relationships and state,
keyboard/voice operation, stable focus and meaningful asynchronous feedback.
Changing a setting/choice must not submit or unexpectedly change context. Do not
rely on color, icons, images, toasts, hover or a tiny unlabeled switch. Reflow
must preserve the request, destination, choice, consequence and action at narrow
width/zoom; touch targets and focus must remain usable with the applicable
WCAG scopes/exceptions. Avoid repeatedly announcing passive polling.

Prove long translated text, RTL isolation for addresses/references, locale-safe
identity cues and IME composition. No accidental Enter submit during composition.
The email's text/plain and images-off presentation must retain useful sender,
request identity, cancellation meaning and actual help. Keep messages concise
and linkless when that is sufficient. Low-bandwidth/reload recovery must preserve
the lawful original choice/result without creating an offline private archive.
**Proof: P18, P19.**

### U12 — Small qualified rollout and measured noise

**M / plausible scope expansion; H / plausible unsafe rollout.** Prospective
activation requires compatible settings/readers/writers, native owner/P17/P6
integration, exact source/authorization tests, finite measured budgets and named
operators. An old client omitting Q35 intent must not receive an inferred On.
Do not backfill old cancellations or fabricate events when a tenant enables the
capability. Restores must reapply later restrictions and permanent identities;
rollback must preserve cancellation/history and admitted-provider uncertainty.
Use the scoped stop rather than losing evidence or reopening requests.

Measure actual misunderstanding, accidental selection, duplicate/unwanted mail,
blocked/unknown outcomes, delivery recovery and support burden with bounded,
privacy-safe evidence. Do not infer success from opens or add tracking pixels,
read states, reminders, digests, a global inbox or another platform to measure
this feature. Publish meaning through canonical owners and regenerate their
declared projections; this working review does not authorize implementation.
**Proof: P16, P17, P19–P21.**
