## Disposition and actual decision

**Accept with the amendments below.** Conrad's combined selection preserves manual Q28 Add existing and Q53 Upload and add, and adds optional pre-intent hints. It supersedes the researched A option's exclusion of a matcher; it does not combine two contradictory policies or automatically prefer reuse. Manual discovery remains the strongest simpler alternative: it already completes the job and avoids extra confidential lookup and client work. B adds plausible value when an eligible held version has unfamiliar metadata. That value and its cost are not measured Asym facts.

The smallest complete product is a quiet, bounded lookup in the ordinary staff evidence-intake context. A browser fingerprint is untrusted query input, not proof of the incoming bytes or their legitimacy. Only independently disclosable and eligible held-version candidates may appear. A deliberate existing-file choice invokes Q28; a deliberate upload invokes Q53. One intended addition cannot accidentally execute both branches after a double click, stale result or lost response. This needs the existing source-operation boundary, not a new global chooser, digest lock or duplicate-management platform.

The proposed helper control is one tenant-governed enable/disable value within existing source-configuration ownership. Enabled is the qualified cohort's initial default, not activation of unqualified sources. Off stops new helper work and presentation. It does not withdraw independent Add existing/Upload and add rights, invalidate an ordinary Q28 selection solely because it originated in a hint, or prevent original-result recovery. An unresolved effective policy produces no hint, not a fabricated known-Off or zero-result fact. A known qualified default can resolve absence of an override. A fresh authorized read after Off→On is permitted; stale responses do not replay. Per-selection dismissal is local assistance, not a new privacy preference or permanent source stop.
