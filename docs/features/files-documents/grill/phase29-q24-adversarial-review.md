# Phase 29 Q24 — Person or team review responsibility

**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

**Status:** Conrad selected Q24 A and fully ratified its complete required
amendment package on 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok: qualified person/team review
responsibility with flexibility, excellent UX/UI and no overengineering.
Q01–Q24 are ratified grooming intent. This preserves the complete adopted
decision and proof obligations; Q25's answer is not inferred. No implementation,
activation or formal publication follows.

Keep A. Let a qualified team remain responsible while an authorized human does
the review. Do not make that person claim the work or become its nominal owner
just to perform an otherwise lawful action. Show clear responsibility in the
existing request and My Work experience, record who actually decided, and make
missing coverage recoverable through the source that owns the work.

Responsibility is not an access grant. Equally, changing responsibility is not
automatically a revocation of every independently valid permission. The current
source/P12 rules decide who may inspect, review and administer responsibility.
This distinction prevents both confidential-data exposure and needless handoffs.

**Strongest alternative B:** require a named accountable assignee before ordinary
review actions become actionable; a team only routes to that person. B offers
clear personal accountability and may suit a source already organized that way.
It need not make that assignee the sole authorized reviewer or add a Claim
button. Its cost is an extra assignment dependency when qualified staff genuinely
share work. Both options already require the actual human decision-maker in
history; B is not just better audit attribution. Neither option decides a new
request-issuance gate.

**Simpler/no-build path:** reuse a valid source responsibility and existing work
surface. If a named individual already fits, A needs no team setup. Shared
responsibility is enabled only through a source-qualified route, not an arbitrary
group dropdown. Do not disguise an unsupported team lane as complete.

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A team label could hide abandoned work, grant access by accident, leak a roster,
or cause repeated handoffs with no historical actor. Default replay could restore
an old owner. The remedy is a qualified source route, separate authority, current
human decisions, stable work identity and real recovery—not mandatory claims.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

We do not know actual tenant staffing patterns, turnover rates or preferences.
We assume some sources can qualify shared responsibility because the governing
contracts support it; each actual source still needs proof. Being offline is
not evidence of ineligibility; a failed lookup is not an empty team. Existing
support must be capable of resolving the issue, not merely display a contact.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adopts one ordinary responsibility choice across the native request and
shared work projections. It creates no team credential, file permission, new
fulfillment predicate, donor obligation, recipient deadline or broad application
decision. Specialist approvals and NHI administration stay with their owners.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Staff see who is responsible and can perform permitted review without redundant
claiming. Handoffs preserve received evidence and earlier decisions. Participants
see truthful source status/help rather than staffing churn or new upload demands.
Failure copy distinguishes unavailable lookup, needed assignment and denied
authority, preserving known success and independently permitted work.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Document collection and CRM examples support clear responsibility distinct from
contact, observers and permissions. They also demonstrate a credible named-owner
choice. Current guidance favors explicit authority and accessible recoverable
interaction. No source proves that shared teams always outperform individuals;
that claim is unnecessary to justify flexible A.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes, with source-owned qualified destinations, P12 decisions, P9 projections and
actual human attribution. It would conflict if implemented as an all-admin
fallback, generic assignee mutation, team grant, hidden named-person prerequisite,
or mandatory whole-Studio dependency. The amendments exclude those outcomes.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Retain A with the clauses below. Narrow “qualified team” to real source-backed
responsibility and recovery. Make authority changes precise enough to enforce
revocation without gratuitously restarting still-valid review. Preserve source
flexibility instead of adding algorithms, calendars or a configuration engine.

## Ratified corrected decision

> For ordinary native document review, the requesting source may designate an
> eligible person or an explicitly qualified team route as accountable for the
> specified pending review work. A qualified team may remain responsible while
> a currently authorized human performs the applicable Q21 acceptance or Q23
> Request changes action, without a mandatory personal claim or nominal transfer.
> Every substantive decision retains its actual human actor and exact current
> source/evidence/review basis. Specialist individual-review and separation-of-
> duties requirements remain authoritative.
>
> Responsibility, membership, task assignment, participant contact and review
> permission are distinct. Responsibility grants no access by itself, and a
> responsibility change does not universally revoke independently valid access.
> The source/P12 boundary checks current administration authority, destination
> qualification and actual reviewer authority separately. Real membership,
> grant, clearance or source-policy changes affect subsequent actions at their
> applicable scope; they do not rewrite lawful historical decisions.
>
> Reuse valid source responsibility and qualified defaults. Preserve deliberate
> handoffs against stale defaults and projections; apply actual source-defined
> policy changes through the qualified source transition. No creator/first-row/
> all-admin fallback, new automatic routing policy or universal snapshot-versus-
> dynamic rule is selected. If responsibility or needed eligibility is missing,
> ambiguous, invalid or cannot be established, represent the actual condition,
> deny only dependent protected actions and provide a capable source-owned
> recovery route. A team name or task row alone is insufficient. Temporary
> absence and lookup failure do not prove an empty team.
>
> Preserve one coherent source responsibility and logical work identity,
> attributable changes, received evidence, prior human decisions, correction
> relationships and fulfillment history. Order material handoff/review races
> through the source; durable retry and delayed projection must not restore
> obsolete responsibility or authority. A coordination-only update must not
> force unnecessary reupload, reinspection or review confirmation.
>
> Use the existing request/My Work experience, with clear person/team identity,
> truthful current status, predictable accessible changes and safe recovery.
> Keep participant help distinct from private routing, rosters and review
> history. Preserve all existing source completion rules, specialist boundaries,
> recipient rights and clocks. Q24 adds no global issuance/intake/completion gate,
> permission grant, extra reviewer, mandatory claim, exclusive lock, notification
> default, per-member task copies or general routing/availability engine.

## Ownership, invariants and lifecycle

These are distinguishable business/knowledge conditions, not a mandated new
database enum or a workflow engine. A projection may display only what is known
and currently permitted.

| Condition or authoritative fact            | Owner and invariant                                                                          | Valid behavior / forbidden shortcut                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Review responsibility                      | Requesting source designates the supported person/team route for specified work.             | Resolve or change through qualified source rules; tasks and display names cannot author it.             |
| Eligible person responsibility             | Destination qualifies under current source conditions; actual reviewer separately qualifies. | Reuse it; no forced team migration or redundant confirmation.                                           |
| Qualified team responsibility              | Source route has usable work and capable recovery; current actors qualify per action.        | Team can remain responsible during human decisions; no compulsory Claim or NHI reviewer.                |
| Known unresolved/invalid destination       | Source knows resolution failed or target is no longer valid.                                 | Appropriate staff see assignment-needed recovery; no first match/all-admin fallback.                    |
| Known no eligible actor                    | Route may remain identifiable, but dependent action cannot currently proceed through it.     | Capable source response; do not fabricate actor, revive access or erase history.                        |
| Unknown qualification                      | Required current facts cannot be established.                                                | Deny dependent protected action, retry/reconcile honestly; not proof that team is empty.                |
| Temporary human absence                    | Staffing fact only to the extent source policy recognizes it.                                | Independently authorized colleague may act; no new presence service or automatic revocation.            |
| Handoff committed / result unknown         | Source mutation/history/effect owns the outcome.                                             | Recover source result and reconcile projections; no fresh effect merely to fix a lost response.         |
| Actual review and historical actor         | Source Q21/Q23 decision records actual human and exact basis.                                | Membership/handoff does not rewrite prior act; substantive correction uses its separate owner.          |
| Fulfillment and recipient access           | Q22/source owns F; existing admissions own access deadlines.                                 | Missing staffing is no new predicate once requirements are satisfied; handoff does not reopen or renew. |
| Task, contact and notification projections | P9 projects source work; approved contact and P6/P17 communication have distinct purposes.   | One logical work identity; assigned does not mean notified, accepted or publicly identifiable.          |

Two individually valid actions must not jointly violate the source invariant.
If a material revocation or handoff commits first, a dependent stale review must
fail/reconcile. If a lawful review commits first, later reassignment preserves
its history. If the handoff changes only coordination and the reviewer remains
independently authorized, do not manufacture evidence invalidity. Existing
correction, return, acceptance and closure races continue to use Q03/Q21–Q23.
