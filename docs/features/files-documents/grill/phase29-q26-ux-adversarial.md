# Phase 29 Q26 — Default recipient email and bounded tenant notification control

**Research/review date:** 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok. Conrad selected Q26 A and additionally asked for tenant control of who is notified and how, with flexible, excellent UX and no overengineering. Q01–Q25 and their amendments are ratified. This note proposes incremental amendments for the selected answer; it does not ratify them, change canonical files, or prove runtime behavior.

## Independent conclusion

Keep the selected visible default-on optional recipient-email choice in the existing Request changes interaction. Treat tenant configuration as a real product requirement, not a footnote saying that the backend already has a Delivery Plan. Expose the exact source's supported plan configuration and its effective consequences through the existing communication administration surface. Keep everyday review short: safe current guidance, an understandable optional email choice, and one deliberate review action.

This does **not** mean that this new source already supports staff/team audiences, arbitrary recipients, every installed channel, a message composer, or a new notification platform. The owner reviewer confirms that the concrete initial audience is the source-resolved Request recipient; optional email enable/disable and permitted content/locale/sender/reply settings are available only within the declared contract. A team responsible for review is not thereby a notification audience. Other exact roles, steps and channels may be exposed when independently declared and qualified, not as fake or aspirational dropdown choices. If the requested tenant outcome needs an additional audience or channel beyond that envelope, record the actual product/owner gap instead of claiming that configuration already satisfies it.

Default-off in the same interaction remains the strongest alternative. GOV.UK explicitly cautions against preselected checkboxes. Default-on is a product judgment favoring awareness of new recipient work; a label and a successful accessibility audit alone cannot demonstrate that staff understand or want the external message. The full proof needs intended staff to distinguish recipient email from source guidance and from any genuinely separate plan effects, including on mobile and under repeated review.

### Applicable accessibility evidence

- [WCAG 2.2 SC 3.2.2, On Input](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/on-input.html), Level A: do not create an unexpected context change merely by changing a control. Q26's proposed toggle selects the pending consequence; it does not itself commit or send. This is not a blanket prohibition of dynamic updates or autosave in unrelated settings.
- [SC 4.1.3, Status Messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html), Level AA: in-scope result/progress/error messages must be programmatically available without forcing focus. Not every content refresh is an announcement, and repeated rerenders should not repeatedly announce that mail is pending.
- [SC 1.4.10, Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html), Level AA, and [SC 2.5.8, Target Size Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html), Level AA: test the actual control, destination summary, action and errors under narrow/zoomed layouts; the target-size criterion has stated alternatives/exceptions and is not a universal 44-pixel requirement.
- [SC 3.3.8, Accessible Authentication Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/accessible-authentication-minimum.html), Level AA: the Q01 entry journey must retain supported paste/autofill and an applicable accessible authentication mechanism. Receiving a notice does not substitute for required proof, and a correction notice is not a new account requirement.
- [SC 2.2.1, Timing Adjustable](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html), Level A: assess applicable session and content-set limits against the criterion and its real exceptions. A future fresh-grant option or a long nominal access period does not automatically qualify every timeout; no new grant duration or renewal is selected here.

These are informative Understanding documents explaining their corresponding success criteria. This research has not tested the composed UI, email clients, keyboards, touch devices, screen readers, zoom, translations or real recipients. Shared primitives and visual polish are not that proof.

## Practical lean experience

### Tenant administrator

Use the existing communication configuration area, focused on **Document request: changes requested** or the owner's actual localized event name. Show a compact effective summary: source audience, optional recipient-email enabled state, any other genuinely qualified steps, and permitted content/sender/locale/reply choices. Show where an inherited setting comes from when that affects editing; do not imply a configuration is locally editable when it is locked by the contract or a superior policy.

The administrator can change only the choices this source actually allows. For the initial audience, show **Request recipient** and an explanation that the request owns who that person is. Do not offer invented reviewer-team or all-tenant options. A source-qualified participant change may be available from the request to someone with that authority; no plan setting can silently retarget historical recipients, prepared notices, proof or protected access.

Provide a safe preview of the declared message and consequence using permitted sample or currently authorized facts. State when placeholders or samples are shown. Distinguish a template preview, valid configuration, saved configuration and an active/eligible occurrence. Viewing a preview must not send a test email; no mandatory live test to the real recipient is part of routine setup. Existing authorized test facilities, if any, retain their own safe scope and honest proof limits.

Save uses the existing deliberate administration interaction. Preserve previous effective settings until a valid change is actually saved; a transient success toast is insufficient when the save is unknown. Plan changes affect occurrences according to the existing pinning/publication rules. A newly enabled default does not resurrect old staff-off decisions, resend history or turn prepared messages into a different audience/purpose. A current safety restriction may block dispatch without rewriting what was originally requested.

### Reviewer

Review the current item and supply Q23's minimum safe actionable guidance in the existing interaction. Where eligible, show **Email recipient** as the selected optional control. The permitted recipient label or masked destination must be enough to avoid confusing two visible requests, without forcing disclosure of information this reviewer may not see. Recipient identity and safe request context are the basis; an address is not a substitute identity.

The final action can say **Request changes and notify** when it truthfully includes the selected notice, with an adjacent short **Email recipient** consequence. When off, **Request changes** is appropriate. These are copy examples, not a mandate to squeeze a long translated sentence into a button. If the real plan has other qualified effects that still occur, a concise summary explains them; do not use “quietly,” “no notifications” or “no one will be told” unless those statements are actually true for the action.

There is no second routine confirmation. Staff can turn the optional email off when already assisting the person. The choice persists through a missing-guidance validation error or same-intent recovery. Opening another genuinely new review may use its eligible default again; that does not justify checking a resumed review back on.

### Recipient and return

The email is a minimal safe doorway to current request work. It must not be the only place to find necessary guidance. Q01 confirmation still applies, without a new full account or redundant sign-in when current proof already suffices. Once admitted, show the current item and actionable safe feedback. Do not show old filenames/private reasons merely because they were embedded in a previous notification.

If the recipient already corrected the item, the current received/review-pending state is authoritative. If staff accepted it or the request completed, show the authorized current state, not an old re-upload instruction. If authority ended, offer the existing safe help path with no protected metadata leak or automatic regrant. If only session/proof expired, use the ordinary accessible proof route; do not claim the source grant expired. If current evidence cannot be established, state that uncertainty and withhold protected information rather than inventing an empty or complete state.

Returning twice, refreshing, opening on another device, downloading an authorized current file, or reading feedback does not create another review notice. Sending an email is not a new retention period, correction admission, read grant, requested-by date or access extension.

## Twelve end-to-end journeys to qualify

These are realistic constructed cases for testing, not claims that Asym users were observed doing them. Each journey includes the practical stopping point; failure should not demand that users understand internal plan compilation or provider internals.

1. **Eligible normal review.** Elena has source review authority. Current recipient Nora and the optional email path are eligible. Elena records useful guidance, sees the selected email consequence and commits once. The item becomes Changes requested; the actual notice evidence is separately available. Nora follows the protected doorway, supplies current Q01 proof if needed, sees current guidance and uses the existing correction path. No second account, second routine confirmation or new permission is created.

2. **Direct assistance and intentional off.** Elena is already helping Nora through an authorized channel. She turns this optional email off. A guidance validation error does not turn it back on. On successful commit the UI says the review was recorded and this recipient email was not requested. Any actual independent plan effects retain their own qualified behavior, and the UI never falsely promises nobody will be notified. Nora can still read the permitted guidance in the request.

3. **Tenant setup without a policy builder.** An authorized tenant administrator opens the existing source message configuration, sees the Request recipient audience and only supported choices, previews safe sample content, disables or enables optional recipient email and saves. The result states whether the change was saved/effective. A later new review uses the actual eligible plan. Historical off decisions and prepared messages do not change. No fake team/SMS option, arbitrary To/CC, or mandatory real test send is added.

4. **Reviewer lacks notification administration or roster visibility.** Elena can review the item but cannot edit the plan or inspect private contact details. She sees a safe destination/consequence summary sufficient for the action. If mail is unavailable, the explanation names the user-relevant state without exposing protected settings; a capable authorized repair path exists. She can perform a lawful review-only outcome explicitly where supported, without obtaining administrator rights or copying an email address elsewhere.

5. **Policy changes and slow network before commitment.** Elena selected email; the recipient changes, the tenant disables the relevant step, or a material safety restriction appears before admission. The system does not silently send to the new person or clear the selected intent and report combined success. It gives the actual safe conflict/eligibility result and preserves reusable input. A mere nonsemantic display-label change does not force Elena to reconstruct the review. Unknown plan state is not declared disabled or valid.

6. **Double-click, browser close and unknown commitment.** Elena commits; the connection drops. A return/retry recovers the original occurrence and reviewed choice. If committed, show that outcome and independently known notice status. If definitively uncommitted, resume safely under current authority. If still unknown, say so and reconcile. A new default does not overwrite off, and neither repeated review nor a fresh-access action is the mail retry mechanism.
