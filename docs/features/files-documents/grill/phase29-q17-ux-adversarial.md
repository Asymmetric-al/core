# Phase 29 Q17 — a fixed fresh-read end with truthful recovery

Review date: **21 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. The parent reports Conrad selected **Q17 A: fix the fresh completed-read deadline at qualified source issuance**, requesting full adversarial review, excellent UX/UI and no overengineering. Q01–Q16 and their full amendments are ratified. **The Q17 amendment package remains unratified.** No numerical period, duration-calculation profile, repeat/count/overlap rule or notification policy is selected by this note.

Only this research note was written. The Q17 question, Q08/Q16 boundary and current official primary research were inspected. No canonical file, runtime, UI, private record, permission, provider or dependency was changed or exercised. No native grant, browser/AT or representative-user test is claimed.

## Disposition

**Keep A with focused refinements.** Preserve one meaningful **Grant read access** action, one actual admitted end, the existing completed read-only context and source-owned recovery. Remove first-entry activation, unused-entry deadlines and timer-start ceremonies from this selected model. Do not retain the alternative as dormant UI or an optional per-recipient mode.

The essential distinction is **resolved business timing versus current usability**. Once the fully specified future policy is in place and source issuance succeeds, its actual finite end is established even if the recipient has not entered, proof has expired, a preview is unavailable or a separately authorized message failed. Inability to retrieve that recorded end is an observation/contract problem; it is not a valid ordinary “waiting for first entry to establish the end” state under A.

At present, numerical/profile and repetition decisions still block activation. A cannot justify issuing permission with an invented period, a null end or a promise to fill in terms later. No formula or value is borrowed from Q07, Q14 or Q15.

## Strongest B and the actual cost of A

The strongest B has a finite unused entry window followed by the full finite reading interval after timely qualified entry, subject to genuine independent caps. Its entry deadline is not secretly a second reading cutoff. It protects nominal reading time from delayed discovery, but introduces another source transition and two terms. For an equal reading duration, its overall potential interval from issuance can be longer because it includes time before entry.

A removes that transition and provides one immediately knowable source end. It also spends the opportunity during discovery, proof and technical delay. A person may arrive with too little time or after it has ended, even though they did nothing wrong. This is a real product cost, not something fixed by better wording or an assumption that staff only grant when recipients are ready.

No measured Asym evidence shows which delay patterns dominate or that every later read need is simple. The eventual complete profile and supported cohort must be useful without assuming an unselected repeat grant. If real qualification shows that the opportunity routinely fails, revise the profile or model explicitly through its owner. Do not hide compensation in resends, pause logic, date edits or automatic renewal.

## The practical end-to-end journey

### 1. Staff reviews a real current read need

Use the existing source context. Establish the actual eligible completed admission, current intended recipient, legitimate purpose, permitted scope and genuine limits. A valid root alone does not require another grant: independent staff work or repair of already-valid access may be the correct action.

The actor needs Q08's exact issuance capability, not merely file read access, prior sender identity or a generic staff role. Review can use authorized metadata without adding private body-read powers. Keep **Grant read access** as the one meaningful action. A duration/date picker or a mandatory justification essay does not follow from choosing A.

Before the source occurrence, any concrete end preview is conditional on the adopted rule and current facts. After successful issuance, show the actual recorded end. Changed material recipient/scope/limits require the existing current review; normal time advancing under unchanged terms does not demand a second confirmation. No profile should be inferred while its governing duration terms remain unselected.

### 2. Issuance succeeds, fails, or has an unknown result

| Actual fact                                                | Honest outcome                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Source proves the act did not admit access                 | Explain that read access was not granted and the actual safe recovery. No source period or success toast is invented.                                                                  |
| Commitment is unknown                                      | **We couldn’t confirm whether read access was granted.** Reconcile the same deliberate effect rather than issue another one.                                                           |
| Valid source admission commits                             | The end is resolved. **Read access granted. Access ends [actual date, time and zone]** can describe the staff operation, without claiming notification or current recipient usability. |
| Admission committed but its result/end cannot be retrieved | Treat that as observation or contract failure. Recover the recorded result; do not show a selected business state called “waiting for first entry.”                                    |
| A known committed result is observed after it ended        | Label the result as historical and show actual current state separately. Do not promise a newly beginning period or display an old green result as present permission.                 |

A known unsupported required handoff cannot be called ready. Required source-admission conditions must be qualified rather than hidden by a success indicator. This does not add a mandatory remote health probe or new availability service to every grant. If valid issuance already occurred and a route later fails, the original end stays real and the appropriate owner repairs the route without reanchoring time.

### 3. Notification is delayed, absent or fails

No automatic email or other notification is selected. If an existing independently authorized communication was actually attempted and failed, state that failure separately from the grant. **Read access was granted, but the message could not be sent** is appropriate only when those facts are known.

A provider accepting a message does not prove the recipient received, opened or understood it. Resending, repairing an email address under its legitimate owner process, or refreshing a proof credential does not move the admitted end. Do not substitute email-open or link-click analytics for the source clock.

The absence of an automatic notification feature is not a reason to build a new queue or silently issue later. Staff need accurate operation results and the real supported handoff; selecting the channel/cadence remains separate.

### 4. The recipient returns promptly, late, or never

Use Q01's existing qualified entry and compatible proof rules. After current permission is established, show **Read-only access ends [actual date, time and zone]** alongside the completed status and allowed actions. The first visit does not begin anything new. There is no Enter by deadline, Start reading timer action or unused-to-active business status under A.

A late arrival gets the actual remainder. Do not say **You now have [full duration]**, and do not frame the reduced time as the recipient's fault. If the source proves access has ended and those facts may be disclosed, use **This request is complete. Access to this view has ended** with the existing help route. No visit at all produces the same actual end, without implying rejection or abandonment of the original completed work.

An old bookmark, current login or downloaded copy is not current grant authority. Before qualification, do not reveal private existence, deadlines, filenames or admission history merely to explain a closed view.

### 5. Proof takes time or fails

Successful grant issuance does not imply Q01 proof is ready. Expired proof, a wrong account or an unavailable verification mechanism must be diagnosed accurately. Reuse compatible valid proof where the source permits; do not require another full account or a timer-specific verification ceremony.

Under A, time spent recovering proof does not pause or restart the source period. That consequence must remain visible in supported recovery and qualification. A temporary proof failure is not proof of business expiry, and passing proof is not proof that every file action is currently permitted.

Preserve accessible paste/autofill and source-required assurance. Short proof time limits need their own assessment; a future long read period does not excuse an inaccessible short step or silently authorize extension of business permission.

### 6. Reading is status-only, partial or temporarily unavailable

Q16's actual completed ceiling stays intact. A deliberate status/context-only grant is useful on its own terms and uses the same source end; it requires no file to be opened to start timing. It must not produce preview/download controls or a false statement that no files were ever submitted.

If one authorized preview fails, keep independently verified current status or other actions available and explain the local failure. A permitted download is available only if its separate current checks pass; preview failure is not automatic permission to deliver the original. A missing nonauthorizing history subview is not a successful empty history.

Unknown root/current authority closes the affected protected use. Do not silently substitute a narrower grant for a file-access action that failed; any material change to reviewed scope needs truthful source review. Repairing a qualified rendition of the same authorized original can create a new derivative revision, but cannot add another authorized original version, member or action.

The main source end is not a promise that each file remains usable until then. A genuine earlier file/action limit preserves its own scope. Conversely, one file's limit or a short representation URL must not become an earlier end for unrelated authorized context by indiscriminately choosing the earliest date.

### 7. The end arrives while the view is open or a download is attempted

The source's exact current-use boundary remains authoritative; an open tab, client countdown, old URL or read started earlier cannot silently reserve access beyond it. Existing Q04/Q08 serving rules govern admission, active delivery, range/resume and failures. Q17 should not invent another stream policy or claim that permission is determined only when the page first loads.

Show a truthful ended/current-restriction state without a success claim that the browser saved or read the file. Starting an explicit download is not evidence of completion, and a failed or interrupted response needs accurate recovery. Do not automatically download a copy before expiry as a workaround.

The business request was already complete. There is no new “finished reading” business event, fresh ordinary completed period or resumed upload/correction phase after this grant. The selected clock does not alter retained custody or copies already lawfully downloaded.

### 8. Support helps after delay, technical trouble or actual expiry

Support separates grant authority, proof, handoff, delivery, representation, observation and custody. An old committed result stays historical even if it is recovered today. Current live access is a separate authorized fact, not the response that happened to arrive most recently.

Repair the actual defect without date edits, a replacement intent, a clone or an emailed private attachment. If access genuinely ended, the existing governed help route can explain actual available options. Q08 repetition remains unresolved, so support cannot promise another period because a previous one was unused or spent on technical trouble.

A persistent lack of useful opportunity is a source/product/cohort issue to fix explicitly, not a reason to hide an extension inside technical recovery. Preserve valid independent staff work and rights while closing only the affected protected path.

## Accessibility and timing truth

Keep the actual end, current state and next action in ordinary main content using the existing Base UI/base-maia system. Include enough localized date/time/zone precision to communicate the real exclusive boundary. Formatting must not move the instant, silently round it up/down into a different promise, or turn it into a date-only deadline where time matters. Timezone changes and device clock errors do not change source authority.

If relative time is displayed, it is supplementary. Essential meaning cannot rely on color, a tooltip, a fleeting toast or a per-second countdown. Preserve sensible keyboard/focus after recovery, touch targets, reflow/zoom and long translations. Announce meaningful results, not every timer tick or poll.

Assess actual content-set timers and their applicable WCAG provisions. No duration has been selected, so no longer-than-twenty-hours exception can yet be asserted for this future profile; even where one later applies, it does not qualify short proof/session/UI timers or prove usability. Reauthentication, adequate timing and accessible document content are separate concerns. A technically accessible viewer frame does not make an inaccessible source file accessible, and Q17 adds no OCR, image-only conversion or office-editing promise.
