## Disposition and meaning of flexibility

**Keep A with focused safeguards and the existing small experience.** Flexibility means another currently justified decision is not rejected solely because a prior fresh period existed, and the new reviewed subset may match today's legitimate need within Q16's unchanged ceiling. There is **no preset repeat-count or cumulative-time cap and no artificial waiting interval**. It does not mean a recipient is entitled to approval, that staff can select arbitrary days, or that a previous broad grant remains a current right.

The same-lineage periods remain sequential: no overlap, future reservation, automatic approval, sliding end or pre-expiry extension. Each deliberate new source act receives Q18's **up to thirty elapsed days—exactly 720 hours nominally—from its own qualified issuance**, subject to genuine scoped earlier limits and current authority. An approved period may never be used. Its end does not move to compensate for that fact or for failed delivery.

No required waiting gap means that a qualified new decision can be considered once the relevant ordinary expiry has actually occurred. It does not promise instantaneous approval at that boundary or permit a stale pre-expiry command to lie dormant and execute later. Appropriate currentness/concurrency checks remain necessary. Real throttling and declared capacity budgets are not a hidden repeat quota; neither may an implementation quietly create a lifetime count or cooldown to avoid building correct recovery.

Individually finite periods can accumulate over a long history. This is an acknowledged product cost of A, not something made finite by calling each period temporary. Continuing real source purpose, exact historical scope and current authority prevent this from becoming a general drive; merely retaining a record or file is not a sufficient reason to approve another period. A current read need does not reopen completed work as unfinished.

## Strongest B and the no-new-grant case

**B's strongest case is genuine finality:** one fresh source-issued opportunity, including an unused one or a post-admission delivery failure, then no more in that lane. It bounds the number of fresh opportunities and removes later grant decisions, even where an otherwise legitimate read need remains. It is defensible if the source wants that explicit finality or cannot govern repeated decisions meaningfully. It must not be weakened by an assumed clone, different actor or external-attachment workaround. A confirmed failed/rolled-back pre-admission attempt is not an issued opportunity; an unknown outcome requires reconciliation.

A deliberately chooses continued consideration over that count rule. Its additional opportunity and potential support workload are not quantified. If later operational evidence shows rubber-stamping or a source whose purpose no longer supports repeated reading, address that actual problem explicitly rather than inventing an unannounced numerical cap or relying on automatic renewal.

For either option, a new permission is often the wrong action in a particular case—not a demonstrated frequency claim. Independently permitted staff review can answer a source-status question; a still-valid proof, handoff or qualified rendition can be repaired without another grant. The actor may decide there is no new eligible recipient need. Help does not authorize copying protected bytes elsewhere, and an expired recipient grant does not itself prevent qualified staff from reviewing a new request under separate administrative authority.

## Eight practical journeys

Copy below is proposed intent, not a tested screen or universal public error response. Protected information is shown only under current authority. A clear main-content state with one relevant next action is preferable to a collection of persistent warning banners.

### 1. Staff revisits a real completed-read need

The existing completed request shows the authorized relevant prior outcome and current source context. Staff review the intended recipient, useful eligible subset, ordinary-expiry history and applicable limits, then choose **Grant read access**. Brief context can say **“Grant a new read-only period of up to 30 days.”** The old end is not editable. Before the actual source occurrence, a projected end is labeled an estimate.

The review must be substantive without a mandatory justification essay or second screen. Repeated history can inform the decision but must not trigger an invented count quota. It is legitimate to decide no new grant is needed because staff can resolve the source question independently or because no permitted recipient read purpose remains. Do not require illicit body access to establish issuing authority. Keep ordinary completed status; no fabricated outstanding upload task is needed to justify a reading purpose.

### 2. Two authorized staff act from the same ended state

Each may review the same available context, but only one new live same-lineage period can be admitted while it remains valid. The other action must reconcile the actual source outcome. Where disclosure permits: **“Read access is already active. Ends [actual date, time and zone].”** This is not the second staff actor's newly granted period and does not necessarily promise that its intended subset was included.

If the admitted subset differs from the losing actor's proposal, show the authorized current result and explain that the proposal was not applied; do not silently union scopes, overwrite the winner, call the second operation successful, or promise it will be applied after expiry. The source's supported resolution must preserve the no-overlap rule. A later eligible decision needs a new meaningful current review and intent. No invisible background queue or waiting timer turns the old reviewed command into a future approval.

### 3. Dispatch succeeds, the reply is lost, or an old command returns

Distinguish a confirmed no-effect failure from an unknown result. For uncertainty: **“We could not confirm whether access was granted. Check the result before trying again.”** The recovery action reconciles the same semantic effect rather than creating a new permission under another transport identifier.

If recovered now, **“Read access granted”** describes the actual source act only; separately show its current end/state. If that act has since expired or a later period exists, identify it as the earlier result where authorized, not today's successful new admission. Never show a stale toast that appears to reset the latest date. Delivery failure stays separate from grant failure; the UI cannot promise the recipient was told, entered, viewed or saved anything. Closing the UI after dispatch is not cancellation of a committed act.

### 4. An expired recipient returns through a bookmark

Before current disclosure permits a specific result, use the existing neutral entry/help behavior. Do not expose a prior period count, staff actor, file list or exact expiry merely because a link is syntactically valid. After qualified disclosure and proven ordinary expiry, **“Read access has ended”** with the existing source-owned help route is clear.

Help may lead to consideration; it is not an automatic “Renew” button, promised thirty days, new request queue or guaranteed response time. It must actually reach the responsible existing route, without requiring an extra account or collecting an unnecessary essay. If new currently usable access is later admitted, the same completed context shows allowed reading actions and the actual end. Returning late does not start a new thirty days. No automatic download is triggered by successful proof or return.

### 5. The business period is valid but proof, session or preview is broken

Explain the actual recovery: existing qualified verification for proof/session failure, or a truthful item-level preview problem. Do not call these states “expired request” or direct the user to obtain another period when the current one can be repaired. Session expiry does not mean the source clock ended, and extending a session does not extend source access.

Allow supported paste/autofill, accessible error identification and stable focus. A user on a shared device, mobile network, assistive technology or a changed timezone should not need repeated manual transcription or a new full account solely for this feature. Do not restart a download after sign-in without the deliberate supported action. Preserve independently valid status or other files if one nonauthorizing subview fails; required authority uncertainty blocks the affected use. A qualified rendition can be repaired for the same authorized original without adding a new original version or grant.

### 6. The earlier period was unused

A never-entered or unsuccessfully notified period still has its original source end. While it remains valid, repair the actual handoff/proof problem; do not create overlap or shift the deadline. After eligible ordinary expiry, the user is not categorically excluded because they did not use it. Staff may consider a fresh current need under A, using the same review—not a special quota exemption or an automatic replacement.

On a new confirmed act, show **“Read access granted. Ends [actual date, time and zone].”** Do not say that a failed earlier invitation has been undone or that the person now has thirty guaranteed usable days. No unused-period counter, blame message or proof-of-prior-download requirement is needed. The absence of a visit is also not evidence of delivery failure or lack of need.

### 7. The next useful subset differs

Use the actual completed root, current eligibility and new deliberate selection. A previous discretionary grant of file A alone does not itself prohibit considering file B later if B was inside the actual root and is currently permitted. Conversely, an old broad grant does not revive a now-restricted file, permit a later version or authorize a union of all old grants. No arbitrary file or recipient picker expands the source context.

If the user only needs permitted status/context, that is a legitimate narrow result with no implied body access. If expected file access cannot be admitted, explain the true outcome; do not silently substitute status-only and call the promised action successful. When a specific member's limit blocks it, preserve unrelated independently authorized work. Any material scope change during review needs the existing refreshed review, but merely advancing time under unchanged terms does not require another confirmation. There is no pre-expiry subset overlap exception.

### 8. Adverse ending or the issuing staff member's authority changes

Security revocation, genuine binding shortening, ended purpose, disposed material and relevant source dependency changes must remain effective in their actual scope. Passing the old nominal expiry does not wash them into eligible ordinary expiry. Support uses the source's qualified remediation path; it cannot clone the request, change email or attach bytes elsewhere to evade a prohibition. Retained source evidence can remain available to independently authorized staff without recipient access.

If an actor loses issuing authority before a new act, the stale screen cannot authorize that act. If the historical issuer changes role after admission, enforce the mapped continuing grantor/delegation rule: do not automatically revoke everything, and do not assume all prior grants survive. Another currently qualified staff actor may perform whatever current source policy actually permits, without automatically inheriting the old actor's body-read rights. User copy should give the correct safe current next step, not expose sensitive internal role/delegation details or falsely call the thirty-day timer the cause.

## Keep the implementation and evidence proportionate

This decision needs the existing contextual action, meaningful current review, inherited source mutation/current-serving safeguards and honest outcomes. It does not call for a general renewal platform, policy-builder UI, recurring task, new notification service, recipient queue or permanent file archive. Source-native correctness is consequential even when the visible UI is small; a disabled button alone cannot enforce sequential grants.

Use existing accountable source, security, UX/accessibility and support owners. A reproduced unauthorized overlap, bad scope, disclosure or reset is a correctness failure to contain and repair, not a reason to impose an invented quota. Actual sustained operational burden or poor user outcomes may justify an explicit later policy reassessment; this note selects no new measurement program, reminder cadence, SLA, threshold or repeat-count restriction.

The source documentation establishes bounded patterns and distinctions. It supplies no empirical Asym demand, universal optimum, current native capability, fully accessible composition or successful intended-user outcome. Eight journey cases and the table above are required proof targets, not tests reported as passed. Q19's full package still requires founder ratification, and native implementation/activation requires its separate qualified evidence.
