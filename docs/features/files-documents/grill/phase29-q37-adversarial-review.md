# Phase 29 Q37 — Deliberate versioned item-instruction revisions

**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.** Conrad selected Q37 A and
fully ratified every required amendment and the exact new glossary term on
2026-10-23, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q37 and24 glossary definitions are ratified
grooming intent; the prior23 definitions remain unchanged. The exact decision
and proof obligations remain intact. This is a finished decision review, not
native implementation, formal owner/OpenSpec publication or activation evidence.
Q38's answer is not inferred.

The product should remain small: authorized staff review and publish **Update
instructions** for the same eligible outstanding item. Preserve what was asked,
what was received and what staff actually decided. A new instruction revision
changes the current requirement basis; it does not create new evidence, reject
earlier evidence, grant acceptance or guarantee that a recipient has read it.

**Strongest alternative B:** preserve issued instructions; Q30 explains unchanged
meaning, while actual Q33/Q36 or independently qualified new issuance handles
materially changed work. B avoids this new same-item revision capability and
keeps expectations visibly stable. Its real cost is extra contexts/handling;
removing the last blocker can fulfill the request before Q36 can add replacement
work. No atomic Replace, draft hold or access-copy shortcut is already authorized.
Adding first is also a distinct authorized act, not a universally harmless recipe.

A pre-response-only rule has credible comparator support, but still needs safe
in-flight behavior and a later correction route. We do not add it as another
tenant mode. Meaning-preserving explanation is a useful no-build alternative
only when the actual requirement remains unchanged.

**Product judgment:** A is the better permanent choice for correcting instructions
for the same ordinary document need. It preserves continuity when staff correct
an omission or an excessive detail, without pretending earlier work had a
different meaning. This is not proven industry consensus, measured ministry
demand or a quantified Asym efficiency gain. The Elena/Nora inventory example
is illustrative. User frequency, comprehension and actual benefit remain
unmeasured; representative proof is required before benefit claims or activation.

## Ratified corrected decision

> For an eligible issued unfinished ordinary Private Document Request, the requesting source SHALL permit currently authorized staff to deliberately publish a prospective revision of participant-facing instructions for the same outstanding, not-currently-accepted Document Request Item and continuing document need. Keep its intended recipient, legitimate purpose, requested document type, requiredness and independent technical/specialist constraints unchanged. The source qualifies what is the same need; arbitrary prose, a client label or an automatic semantic classifier cannot authorize a different demand, waiver, schema/form edit or stronger assertion. Q30 explanation and Q23 feedback cannot substitute for the actual revision command. Generic reviewer, task, file-read, assignment, administration and Q36 addition powers alone are insufficient.
>
> Preserve three distinct facts: the immutable published instruction basis associated with an earlier receipt or review, the currently authoritative instruction revision, and any later deliberate review against its exact current basis. Publish attributable successor content rather than overwriting the earlier instructions or relabeling submissions. Drafting is inert; it does not alter participant demand, current review selection or fulfillment. Publishing exactly unchanged current material creates no new business revision. Deliberately correcting back to older wording, when currently permitted, is a new prospective act on the current basis, not restoration of old scope, decisions or clocks.
>
> Existing durably received evidence remains received under its actual submitted-under basis. Revision creates no new bytes, receipt, Submission Correction, participant affirmation, Request changes decision, rejection or acceptance. Preserve Q03 current-candidate and corrected-predecessor fences and independently applicable review concerns. Retained current evidence may be deliberately reassessed under the explicit new requirement basis through Q21/Q23 without fabricated reupload; ordinary file evidence still requires qualified staff acceptance. Instruction publication supersedes the instruction criterion only under its source-authoring authority; it does not change historical Q21/Q23 decisions or grant review-resolution power. Determine current guidance applicability through qualified source rules, preserving independent concerns without retaining a concern whose only binding criterion has lawfully ended as a hidden blocker. Any required human resolution or reassessment remains a distinct current act by an authorized reviewer; no automatic concern classifier or new per-concern platform is required. Show the real next work and actor, including staff reassessment when needed, rather than resetting every item status or blaming the recipient by default.
>
> Coherently establish the revision, its actual qualified handling route, relevant review coverage, prospective scope/currentness consequence, required history and durable reevaluation/recovery intent. For required participant work, apply an explicit source/P12/Q01 instruction-scope successor within the valid original or fresh period, retaining its exact end, reviewed subsets, earlier caps and current restrictions. Reconcile Q13/Q15/Q36 lineage rules explicitly for this same-item instruction successor; Q36 does not itself grant revision authority. After ordinary expiry, required participant work needs separately authorized actual fresh admission for the revised basis under existing eligibility and nonoverlap rules. No uncommitted promise of a later grant or hidden inaccessible demand is publication of usable work.
>
> A qualified participant route does not require the recipient to be online, acknowledge the revision, finish proof exchange, receive email or successfully render a page when staff publish. A qualified route means actual admitted authority and a proved capable source handling path, not continuous presentation/provider availability or prior evidence acceptance. A recoverable technical delay may retain known publication and owned reassessment/recovery with truthful availability; missing or unknown required authority or a fundamentally absent route cannot be relabeled a delay. Required Q01 proof/current source authority still gates each protected use, with qualified proof reuse where permitted. Independently lawful current staff evidence/review may proceed without recipient renewal where no participant act or assertion is required. Show actual responsibility and participant availability, preserving independently valid old rights; if participant action later becomes necessary, establish its actual authorized route or capable source remedy before demanding it. Reuse valid Q24 coverage and qualify only the revised declared work under Q25; do not rerun all original issuance or require a roster, presence check or extra approver.
>
> Order publication against relevant current instructions, evidence/correction, prepared review, Q30 context, Q33 removal, source restrictions and actual Q22/Q34 terminal acts through one qualified source boundary or equivalent version/fence protocol. Acceptance, removal, freeze or closure first denies an ineligible ordinary revision; publication first fences materially affected stale decisions and operations for deliberate current review. Not-currently-accepted does not mean never historically accepted, but this action cannot clear acceptance to create eligibility. A transfer or open form is not durable receipt; preserve receipt-first facts, revalidate affected unadmitted work and never retarget an old command to the new revision. Preserve unrelated lawful work. Drafts create no completion hold, optional nonblocking work remains nonblocking, and relevant publication/reassessment durably arranges complete-predicate reevaluation without another routine Complete action.
>
> A revision does not reset any requested-by target or access clock, erase legitimate earlier lateness, fabricate pre-revision fault or create an overlapping/fresh period automatically. Record actual publication after relevant waits. Normal completed access requires actual timely qualified fulfillment and its declared member/version/action/context scope; later Q08/Q16/Q19 recovery remains within that actual fulfillment-created ceiling and current restrictions, never today's instructions or a union of historic grants. An individual narrow admission stays narrow without becoming an invented permanent global ceiling for later separately authorized admissions within their real source universe. Independent source processing after recipient expiry may finish work without reviving participant access.
>
> Use one clear Update instructions flow in the existing source/item UI. Let staff inspect permitted current wording, review the proposed text and relevant consequence, then deliberately publish. On authorized participant return, make current instructions and the true update time clear alongside preserved progress and the submitted-under basis needed to understand the work. Show truthful published, unchanged, uncommitted, conflict or outcome-unknown states and capable recovery; preserve permitted input/reading context/focus and discard stale cross-user/tenant/request results. No mandatory essay, read receipt, reply, second approval, per-file ritual, account, download, new diff engine or private offline archive follows. Updating a page does not prove awareness or understanding, and this decision selects no new email/default/reminder/channel or fake existing source event.
>
> Retain original instruction content and references under their actual source retention, hold and privacy rules; retention is not perpetual participant disclosure. Every old/current instruction body, history cue, comparison, export and action result requires current exact permission. Where the source already permits operative linked instruction resources, use their qualified exact reference/version or capture the operative requirement in authoritative text; a mutable external link alone must not be represented as a preserved requirement snapshot. Do not add a crawler, archive, attachment feature, public fallback or restore deleted resources through a prose correction. Authorized redaction/disposition preserves permitted provenance and honest unavailable/unknown history, not fabricated blank text or reconstructed meaning.
>
> Correct mistaken instructions through a new currently qualified source revision while that action remains eligible. Q33 applies only under its actual outstanding/unaccepted conditions; accepted, removed, frozen or terminal cases use an actually capable recorded source/privacy remedy with historical facts and other lawful uses preserved. A new request may address a legitimate separate need, but does not repair unnecessary collection or permit bypassing adverse restrictions. Do not offer universal Undo, silent unaccept, destructive version rollback, predecessor revival or a powerless contact.
>
> Enforce trusted tenant/environment/actor/source context and exact item, predecessor, evidence and scope relationships on every write, protected read and replay. Preserve one authoritative current instruction basis per eligible item, immutable publication/receipt/review provenance, valid states and permanent semantic effect identity. Under current result-disclosure authority, resolve a matching already-committed identity before treating the call as a new revision or current-material no-op; later acceptance/removal/closure cannot erase that original effect. Same intent across transport retries recovers the original result; changed material conflicts and stale replay cannot republish an obsolete revision. Effective grants, RLS USING/WITH CHECK, UPSERT, views/functions/triggers, storage and privileged workers/imports must preserve those boundaries. Co-located source facts and required outbox intent commit atomically; equivalent cross-owner designs must prove coherent admission and recovery. Optional projection/provider failures cannot undo known publication or supply new authority; external work stays outside short source locks. Existing pending communication/work retains its exact source purpose and pinned facts while applying its own current usefulness/action/disclosure rules to affected changes. Neither blanket suppression, a fake new event, sealed-material rewriting, unknown-attempt rekey nor recall is authorized by revision alone.
>
> Adopt prospectively through exact source/P12/Q01/Q03/Q13/Q15/Q21–Q25/Q28/Q30/Q33/Q36 and completed-access owner reconciliation, canonical authoring and declared OpenSpec projections. Qualify real roles, all private doors, controlled races/replays/faults, current evidence reassessment, bounded production-shaped workloads, migration/restore and composed accessible user journeys before activation. Do not invent historical revisions or granted scope from mutable legacy text. A prospective baseline is usable only with proved current source eligibility and truthful capture provenance; unknown earlier basis stays unknown. Gate incompatible writers/readers, stop new revisions without undoing admitted work and use roll-forward when downgrade would lose facts. Reuse existing source, identity, custody and work infrastructure; no general revision engine, configurable approval platform or whole future Studio dependency is required.
