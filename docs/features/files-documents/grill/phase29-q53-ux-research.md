# Phase 29 Q53 — New local files supplied by staff for ordinary request evidence

2026-10-26, Asia/Bangkok. **Question preparation only: Q53 is unselected.** Q01–Q52 are fully ratified; the 31 glossary definitions remain unchanged. This independent UX/research note proposes one product choice and its necessary boundaries. It changes no canonical document, runtime, source permission, provider, account or message and claims no native implementation or intended-user validation.

## Why this is the next question

The original founder brief requires purpose-owned intake, usable staff/recipient request journeys, truthful provenance, separate technical safety and human acceptance, and recovery from incomplete uploads. Those requirements do not by themselves settle which ordinary source acts staff may perform. A complete read of Q28's ratified ADR establishes that its staff-assisted admission is limited to **eligible existing exact file versions** the tenant already holds. Q36 and Q47 use that qualified existing-file path; they do not quietly authorize uploading a new local file. Q41 concerns sharing already-admitted material, and Q39 copies safe request definitions rather than evidence.

## One practical choice, two fair options

**Should authorized staff be able to upload a new local file directly to an eligible ordinary requested item as staff-supplied evidence, or should new files enter through the recipient's upload route while staff retain qualified reuse of existing files?**

| Option                                                                                 | Actual experience                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Practical advantage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Real cost and limit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Allow qualified staff upload of new local files (recommended)**                  | From the actual ordinary request item, authorized staff select a local file and deliberately add it as a new staff-supplied contribution. The source records the staff actor and actual admission time, then follows ordinary safety and review. Add existing file and recipient upload remain available. | Handles a document staff already lawfully received outside Asym without requiring the recipient to perform a second transfer solely to get it into this item. Keeps assistance in the actual request context with truthful provenance.               | Adds a new intake actor and its upload, privacy, stale-state and recovery paths. Staff may select the wrong file or assume it replaces a personal statement. Source authority, actual attribution, current review and usable recovery must address those risks.             |
| **B — Recipient upload for new files; staff reuse of eligible existing files remains** | Staff ask the recipient to upload a new file through the qualified recipient route. Staff can still review, coordinate and deliberately add already-held eligible exact versions under Q28.                                                                                                               | Preserves participant-origin submission and control for new incoming files, with fewer staff-upload admission paths. It is particularly defensible where participation, present intent or a source-required statement is central to the requirement. | A lawful copy already held outside Asym cannot enter this ordinary item directly through staff. The recipient may need another transfer or restoration of actual qualified access. Staff cannot upload to an unrelated library first merely to manufacture Q28 eligibility. |

**Recommendation: A within the ordinary source boundary.** Assistance is a real documented collection/grant-management pattern, and truthful staff attribution can preserve its practical value without pretending that the recipient uploaded or affirmed anything. This is a product judgment, not evidence that Asym ministries frequently receive files outside the product, that staff intervention is always preferable, or that this feature will save a measured amount of time or money.

B's strongest case is agency and evidentiary clarity, not a deliberately cumbersome interface. When the actual source requires a fresh recipient statement, signature, consent or personal action, A must preserve that requirement; merely possessing a local file is not a substitute. Conversely, do not impose a fresh recipient acknowledgement on every ordinary staff contribution just to reproduce B inside A. Actual source needs decide. No artificial third mode, admin-only universal role, generic upload drive, email connector, camera/OCR pipeline or new permission editor is necessary for this choice.

## Illustrative end-to-end experience

**Illustration only, not an observed ministry workflow.** Elena has requested an equipment inventory from Nora. Nora has supplied a copy to authorized staff outside Asym. A staff member can already use Q28 if that exact version is independently and lawfully held by an admitted source, but cannot assume a new local upload is covered by that decision.

With A, the staff member opens the eligible Equipment inventory item and chooses **Upload file**. The request, intended subject, item and current requirement remain visible. The local file selection is identified safely and the consequence is clear: it will be added as **Staff-supplied evidence**, not as a submission by Nora. Use the existing uploader and a deliberate **Upload and add** or equivalent clearly scoped action; preparation, transfer and source admission are distinct facts, not necessarily separate required clicks. Show only source-required extra information. There is no routine explanation essay, second approver or ceremonial preview click.

The interface distinguishes uploading, technical checking, source admission and human review. A successful transfer alone does not mean the item is accepted, nor that the entire request is fulfilled. The actual reviewer can inspect the admitted exact version and use Q21/Q23; Q52's selected-item acceptance remains a separate deliberate review action. If a personal statement is genuinely still needed, show that remaining recipient work through its qualified source route. If only staff review remains, do not continue presenting a false demand that the recipient upload the same file.

With B, the same item and ordinary coordination remain available, but the staff member directs Nora to the existing qualified upload route for new bytes. B does not create a new sign-up requirement, extra confirmation ritual or general prohibition on staff assistance. If Nora's access has expired, the actual source's access/recovery rules govern; a new upload cannot be smuggled through a public asset area or fabricated resource record.

If the staff member selects the wrong file before admission, remove it from the local selection or cancel the transfer using the qualified upload lifecycle. If an eligible unaccepted staff contribution has already been admitted, A needs the actual source's narrow **Remove from this item** recovery for this new contribution class, preserving history and other uses. The name of a control is not its authority: Q28's existing-version removal cannot silently be assumed to cover a newly introduced intake actor. Accepted/closed mistakes require a real existing source/privacy remedy; no blind Undo, provider deletion or forced re-opening follows.
