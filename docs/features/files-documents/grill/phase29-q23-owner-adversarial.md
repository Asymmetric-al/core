## Proposed corrected meaning

For the ordinary native request's exact unaccepted, source-eligible item, a currently authorized staff reviewer can record a deliberate recoverable **Request changes** outcome with sufficient source-approved participant guidance to understand what needs attention and the real next step. The item is not accepted or satisfied by that outcome. Its actual requirement/instructions and current evidence basis remain authoritative; feedback cannot silently add requirements, change purpose, waive work or revise recipient access.

Existing Q03 self-correction remains available without first receiving a staff request. A permitted new correction preserves receipt/history and becomes new evidence for review, not automatically accepted evidence. An earlier Request changes decision is not automatically a finding about successor bytes. If the concern was mistaken and the **same current evidence** meets the actual requirement, staff can make a fresh qualified exact-basis acceptance under Q21 without a fabricated upload, preserving the earlier decision as history. There is no generic erase, Undo, mark-resolved or override shortcut.

Non-remediable work uses an actual qualified source disposition or accountable help path. It must not become an unexplained endless correction loop, a false terminal item decision, or a promise of a nonexistent Cancel/Waive/Reopen command. Q22's whole-request predicate and optional-work semantics remain; feedback does not create a hidden hold or reset any access clock. Current recipient-safe guidance is a narrow source projection, not raw staff notes, a review archive, a general chat thread or a selected email policy.

## Eight essential incremental clauses

These reuse Q01–Q22 rather than duplicate their complete platforms or grants. All are proposed required amendments to A.

1. **Q23-C01 — Recoverable source outcome only.** Request changes is a deliberate currently authorized staff review outcome for the exact ordinary unaccepted, source-eligible item and relevant current evidence/requirement basis. It leaves that evidence unsatisfied, introduces no terminal ordinary Not accepted action and does not set technical safety, application decline, waiver, cancellation, publication, retention or access facts. Preserve existing specialist final dispositions and Q03 self-correction without a prior staff-request prerequisite.
2. **Q23-C02 — Useful bounded guidance.** Persist enough source-approved, recipient-safe guidance to identify the actual concern and permitted next step in the existing request context. This may use qualified structured guidance or a concise explanation; do not require an essay, arbitrary reason taxonomy, new chat or duplicate identity entry. Feedback does not amend purpose/instructions/requiredness: real changes use the source's exact authorized versioned command. Distinguish participant guidance from internal rationale; current feedback is not a grant to all older review notes/audit/history or superseded bytes.
3. **Q23-C03 — Current basis and ordered review.** Bind the exact tenant/request/item, requirement/purpose, relevant evidence set and current review context. Use the existing source/P12 boundary and relevant revision/fence with correction, acceptance, closure and adverse changes. Correction-first prevents a stale Request changes/acceptance from targeting successor bytes; acceptance/closure-first prevents ordinary feedback from reopening the item. Conflicting reviewers cannot silently replace each other's material guidance. Preserve unaffected supporting files and siblings; no blanket request/tenant lock.
4. **Q23-C04 — Honest re-review without forced upload.** A new admitted correction becomes new exact evidence awaiting required review; prior feedback remains attributed to the old basis, not automatically asserted against new bytes. When staff discover a concern was mistaken or actual unchanged evidence already qualifies, a fresh authorized exact-basis Q21 decision can accept the same current evidence without a fake upload. Retain earlier feedback and its true successor relationship/current applicability. No delete/edit-history, generic Resolve/Undo, waived required check, fictional recipient correction or timestamp rewrite follows.
5. **Q23-C05 — Real next step and non-remediable cases.** Offer correction only while existing source/recipient rules allow; feedback grants no rights, extends no deadline and reopens no accepted/closed work. Distinguish technical outage/unknown checks from content deficiency and recover admitted work without unnecessary upload where possible. Before activation, the source must supply a truthful accountable disposition/help route when correction is impossible or access unavailable. Do not fabricate a Cancel/Waive/Reopen/decline command, promise renewal or leave an unexplained infinite correction loop. Independent authorized staff work after recipient expiry cannot disclose through ended access.
6. **Q23-C06 — Q22 progression and durable recovery.** Required unsatisfied work remains in the full completion predicate; genuinely nonblocking optional feedback creates no hidden completion hold. Reevaluation follows relevant qualified source changes. Preserve admitted receipts, independent acceptance and actual fulfillment/ends. Coherently record the review decision, required minimal history and durable follow-up intent; same intent recovers its result with original actor/time under current disclosure, changed material conflicts. Known success survives refresh/provider failure; unknown commitment reconciles before new work. Source closure removes unavailable resubmission promises; no new ordinary post-completion review permission is added.
7. **Q23-C07 — Current projection and lean UX.** Keep one contextual staff action and a clear participant status/concern/next permitted step. Show new correction received/awaiting review truthfully rather than calling it automatically accepted or repeating an old verdict as current. Preserve valid reading context, notes and focus; discard private/stale UI results when actor/tenant/request/purpose/disclosure changes. Use accessible supported content/controls, status text, mobile/AT/localized/poor-network recovery. Q20 does not select feedback email; in-product outcome cannot depend on delivery. No offline review cache, new account, mandatory extra confirmation or entire Workflow console follows.
8. **Q23-C08 — Exact source adoption and proof.** Prospectively qualify the ordinary source Request changes command and narrow participant projection, preserving Q03/Q05/Q21/Q22/P12 and specialized owners. Resolve only the reserved ordinary negative-review scope; reviewer routing/bulk, feedback email, broader text-response forms, new waiver/reopen powers and terminal item action remain unselected. Use canonical authoring and full incorporated OpenSpec contracts at authorized publication. Prove real roles/database/races/replay, effective grants/RLS/service paths, mixed versions/restore and composed reviewer/recipient/help journeys at bounded measured workload.
