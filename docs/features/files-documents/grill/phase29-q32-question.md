## Historical question and alternatives

**When a participant asks a question or replies inside a document request,
should supplementary staff email start On, start Off, or be omitted?**

Q30 already requires a durable message and an accountable source-work route
before acknowledging receipt. That native handling stays in every option,
independent of email. Q31 settles participant awareness of staff replies; Q27
settles first-actionable evidence-review mail. Neither settles staff awareness
of an incoming clarification. This is a new exact notice capability in A/B,
not an existing event whose default can be copied.

| Option                                                             | Everyday experience                                                                                                                                                                       | Benefit                                                                                                                                                   | Cost                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Notify staff by default, with tenant control (recommended)** | Enable the optional staff clarification email step in the qualified initial plan. Authorized tenant staff can turn it Off. Only the source-qualified staff handling audience is eligible. | A direct question can reach responsible staff who are not currently looking at the request. No extra participant checkbox or separate staff contact step. | Repeated replies and short acknowledgments can create noise. Even a minimal email requires qualified recipient, privacy and delivery controls.           |
| **B — Keep staff clarification email opt-in**                      | Offer the same optional capability, initially disabled in the qualified tenant plan. Authorized tenant staff can enable it.                                                               | A quieter starting point for teams whose native work process already reliably surfaces questions.                                                         | An absent handler may miss timely awareness unless the tenant enables mail or follows native work reliably; native visibility does not prove monitoring. |
| **C — Keep awareness in native work without a new email notice**   | Preserve the question, current authorized work and capable existing help/recovery. Add no supplementary email for this occurrence.                                                        | Avoids this additional delivery purpose and its operating burden.                                                                                         | Depends on staff checking their work or an existing effective coordination process. No optional Q32 email exists to enable.                              |

**Recommend A.** A participant has deliberately addressed a human question to
the organization. A small cue can help staff discover it after leaving the
request. The research gives a stronger reason for message awareness than an
assumption that every ordinary file event needs mail. This recommendation does
not change Q27's evidence-review policy and does not assume every tenant needs
email: B is a strong quieter alternative, and A includes tenant disablement.

A/B differ in the initial tenant-plan state of the same optional email step.
They are not per-message participant choices, three tenant modes or a proposal
for another notification-settings platform. Apply any existing applicable staff
preferences/restrictions through their actual contracts; no new personal
preference center is selected. Default On is a product judgment, not evidence
that messages will be read or answered within a promised time.

## Concrete example and user journeys

Nora asks which dates her certificate must cover before uploading. Elena is
currently authorized to handle that clarification through the source-owned
route. The question is durably received and visible in native work under all
options. With A, an eligible minimal email can cue Elena to return. With B, the
tenant must first enable that optional step. With C, Elena relies on native
work and the existing coordination process. These are illustrative test
scenarios, not observed ministry workflows.

| Journey                                              | Required experience and boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Participant asks before any upload                   | No fake upload, evidence-ready event or extra Submit for review action. One deliberate message, one truthful native receipt and owned handling.                                                                                                                        |
| Participant sends a follow-up or brief thanks        | Each genuine message has its source identity; transport retry is not another message. A may create more cues, so the tenant can disable them. No secret digest, unread heuristic or presence-based suppression is introduced.                                          |
| Staff are away from the request                      | Eligible email is a cue to open the current authorized staff work. It does not promise an immediate response or prove the native route is regularly monitored.                                                                                                         |
| Team handles the work                                | Use the source's qualified handling audience and permissions. Do not notify the whole team, last reply author, request creator, all admins or Q24 reviewer merely because those identities are convenient. Exact finite membership and current narrowing must qualify. |
| Tenant disables the email                            | Native receipt and accountable work remain. The setting explains its scope; Off cannot mute source obligations or other notice purposes.                                                                                                                               |
| Staff membership, assignment or permission changes   | Keep native work owned and visible under the source's valid reassignment/recovery rules. Notification preparation/dispatch cannot silently expand or retarget an older occurrence to a new roster.                                                                     |
| Participant access ends after an admitted question   | Lawful staff handling may remain necessary. Evaluate the actual staff action/usefulness independently; do not automatically discard its notice solely because participant access ended. No notice may promise that the participant can still receive a native answer.  |
| Email fails, is suppressed or has an unknown outcome | The question remains received, with its source-owned handling and any current work intact. Supported repair reconciles the original notice; no participant resend, reopened work or extra task per attempt.                                                            |
| Sensitive context or unsupported channel             | Minimal approved email and current protected staff access only. No participant text, filenames, care/location detail, arbitrary address or SMS fallback. Even relationship/sender metadata needs permission.                                                           |
| Mobile, AT, multilingual or intermittent use         | Keep the participant's existing simple message action and staff's contextual work route. Preserve truthful status, accessible labels/focus, supported localization and safe recovery without a new account, inbox or private offline cache.                            |

Do not place a participant-facing Notify staff checkbox beside Send. Choosing
whether an accountable staff route receives supplementary email is tenant/source
policy, not permission for the participant to bypass staff restrictions or mute
required handling. Participant copy should confirm receipt without asserting
that staff were emailed, read it or will answer immediately. Staff email links
back to the source; replies by email do not enter Q30.

## Current primary research and strongest alternatives

- [FileInvite notification preferences](https://help.fileinvite.com/articles/managing-notification-preferences)
  explicitly lists incoming borrower messages as Enabled while individual
  request-submission notices start Disabled. This is unusually relevant evidence
  for distinguishing human-message awareness from file activity. The page
  points users of a different login edition to another knowledge base; these
  are documented edition-specific defaults, not tested universal behavior.
  Its personal settings and two-hour submission batching are not Core scope.
- [Content Snare's incoming-email settings](https://contentsnare.com/help/knowledge-base/manage-emails/)
  list comment notification emails as enabled by default with controls to
  disable them. The same page includes a daily-limit setting; it does not
  establish that every comment always causes one immediate email. No cadence
  or personal-preference platform is imported.
- [Submittable reviewer notifications](https://submittable.help/en/articles/4927182-what-notifications-should-i-expect-from-submittable-as-a-reviewer-for-an-organization)
  support message awareness and opting out, with native activity checking.
  Their messaging routes differ by who initiated contact, with other messages
  going to the organization's main email. That is evidence that recipient
  routing matters, not permission for Core to use a broad shared mailbox or
  the last person who replied.

The strongest alternative is B. Teams with a demonstrably reliable native-work
process may prefer fewer emails, particularly during rapid exchanges. A's
advantage is useful out-of-app awareness; its costs include noise and outward
metadata exposure. The presence of native work alone does not establish that
someone checks it, and a vendor default does not establish Asym's ideal default.
No actual Asym message volume, response-time, mail-reading or preference study
was supplied. Test substantive questions, repeated exchanges, absent staff,
team handoff and sensitive cases rather than only a favorable happy path.

## Repository reconciliation and scope boundaries

The [owner research](phase29-q32-owner-research.md),
[runtime research](phase29-q32-runtime-research.md) and
[comparative research](phase29-q32-comparative-research.md) retain source anchors,
current evidence, limitations and independent full-question checks.

1. **Keep the native baseline.** Q30-C05 requires received text to have durable
   accountable source handling; email neither creates nor completes that work.
   Q30-C07 preserves admitted staff follow-through after participant access
   ends. Q22 fulfillment and Q30's handling/currentness rules do not gain an
   email, read, reply or second-approval gate.
2. **Define a separate meaning and audience.** A/B introduce supplementary
   awareness of a genuinely received participant clarification through the
   existing source-owned route. Q24 Document Review Responsibility is not
   automatically the recipient cohort. Q27's evidence-actionability event and
   Q31's participant notice remain distinct. P12/source authority must qualify
   the actual finite staff audience before any delivery; no invented team
   broadcast, fallback mailbox or notification subscription authority.
3. **Retain original occurrence authority.** The exact source receipt,
   then-effective policy and needed handling-audience basis must be recoverable
   through trusted source/P17 resolution. Current narrowing cannot become
   worker-time roster expansion. P6 owns complete bounded release, permanent
   semantic identity, proved zero versus unknown, exact provider recovery and
   body-free history. A native message received under Off is not later catch-up
   work when mail becomes On.
4. **Separate tenant edits from current stops.** General P17 plan edits are
   future-only. Q27 and Q31 each adopted their own narrow pending-work Off rule.
   Neither is automatically inherited by Q32. A selected-answer review must
   explicitly reconcile this meaning's precise Off/submission/retry behavior
   and current source usefulness without mutating historical pins or promising
   recall. No generic cancellation engine is implied.
5. **Use a safe staff action.** P17 owns exact content/plan/presentation; P6 owns
   delivery/history. Keep mail minimal and source-approved, without message
   bodies, filenames, private labels or attachments. Use the existing qualified
   staff doorway with current read/handle authority. Participant expiry alone
   is not the staff notice's expiry; independently invalid staff work, source
   privacy or loss of authority can still make the notice inapplicable.
6. **Qualify the exact consumed seams.** This new optional staff-email purpose
   needs a real Live binding, default plan, audience condition, useful action,
   material/utility/Recent-copy classification and repair/operations proof.
   The existing optional-staff material class requiring an in-product sibling
   cannot be borrowed by inventing a bell. Native source work is not that bell.
   Do not import Q31's external-participant material posture either.
7. **Keep the solution small.** Reuse existing bounded tenant administration
   and source-work UI. No per-message participant or staff opt-out wizard, new
   inbox/bell, subscriber directory, reminder, digest, read receipt, email
   ingestion, additional channel, automatic assignment notice or full future
   Workflow Studio dependency. A new staff-alert purpose does not automatically
   authorize any of those capabilities.
