## Recommendation and strongest alternative

**Accept A with required amendments.** A deliberate exact-copy action fits an existing document task and need not create a Drive, archive or new sharing system. It remains a separately authorized original-byte disclosure, not an automatic consequence of upload or preview permission.

The strongest alternative remains ordinary **preview-only**, with a separately qualified source help/delivery route. It reduces routine disclosure of comments, hidden sheets, source metadata and active document features. It does not prevent photographing or capturing visible information, and preview omissions/accessibility failures still need a real recovery path. This is a reasonable source-specific restriction, but imposing it on every ordinary request would unnecessarily limit the selected exact-copy outcome. Neither option provides measured ministry benefit or a guarantee that downloaded files are harmless.

The core permanent design is small: one exact source-authorized read action over shared private custody, with truthful browser handoff and error behavior. Do not build another permission engine, export package, file library, workflow or per-file staff approval ceremony.

## Critical source findings

1. **Original and preview are different disclosure projections.** D32 deliberately omits DOCX comments/revision markup, hidden spreadsheet content and presentation notes. Its safety and coverage record cannot authorize a full original. Conversely, preview conversion failure does not itself make an independently qualified original unavailable.
2. **There is no global uploader entitlement.** Q01 confirms the source-authorized recipient, not exclusive human ownership, and Q02 grants only its exact readback. Shared mailbox use, another request, prior assignment, byte equality or a copied link cannot substitute for the exact current source/recipient/principal lineage.
3. **Revocation is not URL expiration or recall.** P12's managed-realtime allowance is not a download grace period. P12 requires per-chunk expiry checks on long streams; D16 requires stopping new grants, terminating in-flight reads where feasible and admitting that already emitted bytes cannot be recalled.
4. **Currentness must be ordered without mutating a running file.** A correction that wins before a new read admission must prevent ordinary download of its superseded target. A response already underway remains bound to its original immutable version and cannot splice in successor bytes. New/resumed/range requests require current permission and cannot acquire historical access merely because a prior stream started.
5. **Preserving an original is source-specific.** Native ordinary-request evidence fidelity cannot override P22's source-discard rule, P18's official-history restriction or P21's restricted evidence projection. A sanitized or accessible alternative must be named as a different representation.
6. **Downloading is a read, not a new submission or export package.** Repeat attempts do not create new file versions, acceptance, signature, consent, CRM interaction or P6 communication. They do create the access evidence/budget effects required by the existing egress owner. Do not incorrectly deduplicate away actual repeated reads.

## Proposed required exact clauses

These O identifiers are research traceability, not published requirement IDs.

**Q04-O01 — Narrow profile and action.** The ordinary native document-request profile permits a separate deliberate `Download submitted file` action for the exact currently admitted own-submission occurrence/member. The current source, PDP, classification, purpose, recipient/principal binding, environment and relevant inner scope must all allow original-byte delivery. Upload, preview, correction, held custody or mailbox equality alone grants nothing. No staff-by-staff file unlock becomes the normal path.

**Q04-O02 — Exact original identity.** Original means the immutable bytes actually received for the specifically selected submission, including a corrected submission when eligible. It does not mean the first attempt, current-by-filename, a summary, converted preview, redaction or official generated document. Resolve source occurrence and immutable byte/copy identity together. Provider keys, filename and ETag are not substitutes for the admitted digest/length/integrity record.

**Q04-O03 — Whole-original disclosure qualification.** The source's original-download projection must permit the entire unchanged file, including embedded metadata, comments, hidden content and other features omitted by the preview. If that projection is not permitted, deny this action and offer only a separately qualified, clearly named alternative/help route. Do not silently strip, redact, recompress, repair, re-render or substitute bytes while labelling them the submitted file. Do not discover/execute hidden features merely to present a download button.

**Q04-O04 — Independent original-delivery profile.** Qualification covers the actual file family and feature set, integrity, current hygiene/disposition, content type, allowed delivery mode and source restrictions. Known malicious, quarantined, indeterminate or unqualified original delivery is unavailable even to its uploader. A clean scan alone proves neither authorization, absence of active behavior, authenticity nor acceptance. Supported preview is neither required nor sufficient. No new universal format/size ceiling is invented here; exact source profiles must be closed and qualified before activation.

**Q04-O05 — Current authorization at every door.** Derive trusted scope from the Q01-compatible native context; use the sole PDP and exact source action. Authorization precedes protected filename/type/size/status, original bytes, redirects, full/range/conditional/HEAD responses, errors and result recovery. Source selectors request scope and do not grant it. No public context, generic `authenticated` role, caller-supplied purpose, raw provider URL, stale JWT or privileged-worker token may stand in for current recipient authority.

**Q04-O06 — Exact currentness and correction race.** The server binds each read admission to the chosen source occurrence, immutable version and applicable source restriction/currentness generation. If a winning correction or other source transition makes that target ineligible before admission, the read returns a safe changed/unavailable result; it does not download a newer version silently. The user returns to the current authorized item and deliberately selects the intended file. Neither a query named `latest` nor a digest match can erase this distinction.

**Q04-O07 — Honest bounded in-flight delivery.** A read admitted before a later source change is an already-started delivery, not a continuing historical entitlement. Keep its exact version fixed; honor current source revocation/restriction and terminate affected in-flight delivery where feasible. Long streams recheck expiry per chunk as required by P12, with source/epoch invalidation enforced through the qualified delivery boundary. Every new/resumed/range request reauthorizes; expired or superseded permission cannot reopen the stream. Do not promise recall of emitted/buffered/local bytes, a universal managed-realtime grace period, or an indefinitely valid initial grant. Actual provider/CDN buffering and termination behavior must be measured before claiming the boundary works.

**Q04-O08 — Immutable repeatable recovery.** Retries of a permitted download return that exact version; reconnect/range validators must never join old and new bytes. Missing/corrupt/incompletely verified copies fail honestly and use source/custody repair, not a preview fallback, plausible regeneration or different version. A separately verified identical provider copy may serve the same byte identity under the same authority; provider movement does not create a document. Repeated actual reads remain independently subject to current access audit and budgets.

**Q04-O09 — Private delivery, inert browser handling.** Use the qualified Asym-controlled private delivery boundary. No reusable raw storage/provider URL, public/CDN fallback or service-worker/offline cache bypass. No active document executes in the privileged application origin. The action hands off the unchanged qualified file for download; it does not open a third-party office service, run macros/formulas, install software or submit private bytes to public scanning. Safe response/header/name handling must preserve bytes and prevent header injection, path leakage and content sniffing. Native browser behavior still needs qualification; a download attribute alone is not the boundary.

**Q04-O10 — Source-approved metadata.** Show useful permitted file context, type and size without treating a name as identity or type validation. Normalize/escape header and display names with Unicode/RTL accessibility; do not leak full local paths, provider keys, restricted original names or unrelated source metadata. A safe display/download filename may differ from the untrusted local filename without changing the promised original bytes. Preserve stricter owner discard rules; do not populate generic filename fields merely because the model allows them.

**Q04-O11 — Deliberate low-friction interaction.** Place the action beside the exact submitted-file item, accessible by keyboard and touch without first opening a viewer or entering File Manager. One deliberate click is sufficient for an ordinary eligible download; add no universal confirmation/reason/identity form. Source-required stronger assurance still applies. Page entry, hover, prefetch, a scanner, preview failure and automatic retry must not silently trigger recipient download or create a claimed human action.

**Q04-O12 — Truthful download feedback.** Describe only observable facts: preparing/starting delivery, browser handoff, interrupted/unavailable and permitted retry. Do not claim `Saved`, `Downloaded successfully`, `Read` or `Accepted` from a grant, HTTP status or browser handoff alone. Show progress only when actually measurable and useful; do not buffer an entire private file in browser memory solely to manufacture a progress bar. A lost connection may leave a partial browser-managed file; explain recovery without changing the source receipt.

**Q04-O13 — Independent states and accessible alternatives.** Pending business review is not by itself a reason to withhold an otherwise eligible submitted original. Safety checking may block original delivery; preview conversion checking alone does not. Distinguish confirmed unsafe, unavailable dependency, lost access, changed version, unsupported original profile and corrupt/missing source with safe disclosure. Keep View and eligible correction independent. An accessible alternative/help route is real and source-qualified, not an automatic original/external viewer fallback or a promise that the original is accessible.

**Q04-O14 — Existing tiered audit and budget.** Integrate the file read with P12's egress/audit/rate owner. Preserve audit-before-serve for restricted/break-glass and the established durable outbox behavior for the applicable other tiers; do not add a new global synchronous audit round trip per chunk. Record authorized source/version/purpose and actual technical outcome in protected access evidence. Ordinary telemetry/queues/errors contain no raw content, names, addresses, secrets, provider URLs or restricted source associations. A successful download does not create a CRM Activity, message, signature, consent or proof of verified external custody.

**Q04-O15 — Read/cleanup and retention remain separate.** New delivery cannot start after a source restriction or deletion claim makes it ineligible. Any bounded in-flight/read-custody coordination must serialize correctly with cleanup and cannot extend source retention indefinitely, override a hold or prevent required restriction. Downloading does not reset request/session/retention clocks, release held evidence, certify external custody or authorize source disposal. Preserve other independently admitted uses of shared bytes.

**Q04-O16 — No bundled audience or history expansion.** Q04 does not grant other recipients' submissions, staff files, previous-version browsing, post-closure continuity, bulk ZIP export, public sharing, email, external Drive transfer, offline sync or general personal storage. Existing qualified source downloads remain valid under their own owners. A browser copy is outside recall, not permission to create a new Asym source use.

**Q04-O17 — Compatible adoption and rollout.** Add the narrow action/projection to the existing native request owner and Q01-required identity/PDP successor through the later authorized owner/OpenSpec package. Qualify all active reader routes, headers, range/conditional paths, old URLs, migrations, copies and restore behavior before activating a cohort. A new button or gateway cannot retroactively revoke legacy public/presigned paths. Unknown legacy identity or containment is restricted/reconciled, never automatically certified or deleted.

**Q04-O18 — No broader semantic authority.** Original-byte delivery changes no source requirement satisfaction, acceptance, claimant confirmation, application decision, signature validity, official currentness, resource publication or decision bundle. Review remains bound to exact source evidence. Access audit is evidence of delivery behavior, not proof the recipient or reviewer understood the content. A new correction may change the source review set under Q03; a download never does.

For the source-change/in-flight row, the qualified implementation must prove its actual point of read admission, expiry checks, invalidation and termination behavior against the existing P12/D16 guarantees. “Where feasible” is the inherited truthful limitation for bytes already underway, not permission for a deliberately nonrevocable new delivery design. ## Required proof before claiming operation

1. Real participant/native-context/PDP/RLS tests for exact source, request, recipient, principal revision, environment, legal entity where relevant, all metadata and denied/not-found behavior. Privileged service mocks are insufficient.
2. Original-content fixtures prove exact received bytes across full/range/retry and independently verified provider-copy recovery. Include corrections, duplicate filenames/digests and missing/corrupt copies.
3. Original-disclosure and file-profile tests include hidden comments/sheets/notes/metadata, active or unqualified features, scanner outage, quarantine and stricter owner restrictions. No silent sanitation or false authenticity claim.
4. Ordered authorization/correction/expiry/restriction versus read admission, headers, first byte, later chunks and range/resume; test live deployed buffering and termination. No stale historical access or mixed-version stream.
5. Real deployed response/CDN/browser tests cover HEAD, full/range/conditional/error paths, safe headers/names, no public/raw-URL leakage, no caching/transform/service-worker bypass and no app-origin execution.
6. Repeated deliberate downloads, lost responses, double clicks, browser blocking and interrupted/partial saves have truthful feedback and approved audit/budget behavior without duplicate business effects.
7. Disposal/read-custody, hold, provider movement, independently admitted uses and restore reapply current restrictions before serving. Neither download nor preview extends retention.
8. P18/P21/P22/P23/P26/P28 specialized adapters preserve their meanings, projection rules and existing legitimate download restrictions/rights; Q04 never becomes a generic original getter by blob ID.
9. Keyboard, screen reader, speech/touch/non-drag, mobile, zoom/reflow, Unicode/RTL, long filenames, source-safe errors and poor connectivity work end to end. The download action is findable without a viewer, and copy does not claim unobservable device save.
10. A representative participant test can distinguish the submitted original from a preview/first attempt, identify the version, recover a failed download and understand that acceptance is unchanged. Report observations and limits, not invented statistical confidence.

## What is new versus inherited

**New selected direction:** ordinary eligible recipients have a deliberate original-download action without routine per-file staff unlocking.

**Required Q04 amendments for explicit ratification:** the exact original-content disclosure/profile boundary, precise correction/read and in-flight/recovery semantics, truthful browser handoff and naming, and the narrow action/projection's complete qualified delivery contract. These make the access choice concrete rather than quietly assuming upload or preview rights suffice.

**Inherited settled constraints:** Q01 exact confirmation/native identity/PDP amendments; Q02 no-Drive and exact readback; Q03 immutable corrections/current-review fencing; source-specific evidence/currentness/retention; existing P12 tiered audit/egress/expiry; D16/D32 private delivery and no recall claims. This review does not reopen those choices.

**Not decided here:** participant prior-version archive, post-closure duration, source review dispositions, deletion/privacy policy, new supported format families, universal byte/stream limits, provider allocation or legal compliance. Exact finite original-delivery profiles and operational bounds are required qualification inputs; no profile may activate with an unknown permissive fallback.
