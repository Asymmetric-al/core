## Historical question and alternatives

**Should staff-approved fresh read access also be available for a completed
view that was lawfully admitted after a fresh unfinished-access period?**

Q08 already allows consideration after the original ordinary completed view
expires normally. Q13 creates another legitimate way to reach a completed
view: fulfillment while fresh unfinished authority is valid, including a
later period allowed by Q15. Q13 explicitly reserved whether that view could
later use Q08's fresh read recovery. Q14/Q15 did not silently answer it.

| Option                                                         | Exact proposed meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Benefit and cost                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A — Include both valid completed-view routes (recommended)** | Retain Q08's original route and also allow consideration after eligible ordinary expiry of a proved completed view lawfully admitted under Q13, including a valid Q15 period. That exact completed admission supplies the added route's historical read-scope ceiling; current authority and a new deliberate Q08 approval still apply. | Consistent help for a legitimate later reading need regardless of which qualified work period preceded completion. Reuses the intended Grant read access action, but requires proof of the exact completed admission and compatible source qualification.                            |
| **B — Keep fresh read recovery limited to the original route** | Q08 remains limited to original ordinary completed-view admissions. A view reached through Q13/Q15 retains its already-ratified ordinary completed period, but cannot receive further fresh reading through this lane after it ends.                                                                                                    | Smaller eligibility surface and stronger finality for the fresh-unfinished route. People with otherwise lawful completed views receive different later assistance based on how they reached completion. Independent source rights/help remain, without a clone or attachment bypass. |

Neither option grants access merely because the source is completed or files
are retained. Neither selects Q08's still-open fresh duration, anchor, unused
bounds, repetition/cumulative or overlap policy. Q14's thirty days and Q15's
unfinished repetition rule do not transfer automatically.

## Recommendation and practical example

Recommend **A**. Both routes already require a real source-admitted completed
view with a finite end and defined read scope. Applying the same current
staff review to each avoids making the earlier work-period history a reason
for different help. This is a product judgment about coherent service, not
an established industry rule or measured ministry demand.

Hypothetical decision test: Sam completes during original access and receives
a lawful completed view. Maya completes during a valid staff-approved fresh
period and also receives a lawful completed view. Both completed views later
expire normally, and each recipient has a legitimate current reading need.
Under A, both can be considered under the same Q08 process. Under B, Sam can
be considered but Maya cannot use this particular recovery route. Neither
option guarantees a grant or changes the underlying completed work.

The strongest reason for B is an intentional finality policy for the group
that already received fresh unfinished access. That is a genuine restriction
the founder may choose, not a requirement established by current code.
Conversely, A is not permission to retrieve any file forever or create a first
completed view for someone who never had one admitted.

## The exact ceiling matters more than the route label

For the added route, use the completed admission actually established at
qualified fulfillment, including its recipient, original lineage, read actions,
eligible member set and Q05-permitted context. Intersect that ceiling with
current eligible scope and the newly reviewed subset. Do not substitute the
broader original unfinished requirements, a previous write grant, retained
folder contents, access logs or a union of prior periods.

For example, if original requirements included A and B but the lawful completed
view admitted only A, later fresh reading can consider currently eligible A.
It cannot recover B merely because B was once requested or remains in storage.
That completed-view ceiling is a real boundary. A later deliberately narrower
Q08 read grant alone does not rewrite it into another permanent global limit;
actual binding restrictions still apply. Each later read grant does not become
a new baseline that can widen through repeated grants.

“Admitted” means the source durably established the completed permission.
The person need not have visited, downloaded or successfully rendered a preview
during that period. Nonuse is not absence of authority. Equally, a screenshot,
download log, generic completed status or retained bytes cannot manufacture
missing admission proof. Source-qualified recovery of a real timely outcome
preserves its actual fulfillment/end/scope rather than starting it at repair time.
A valid completed admission limited to permitted status/context reading is not
excluded merely because it admitted no file-body access; that limited scope
remains the ceiling.

## Cases that stay distinct

| Case                                                                                                              | Proposed A outcome                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Original ordinary completed view expires normally                                                                 | Existing Q08 eligibility remains under its current safeguards.                                                                                          |
| A valid first or subsequent fresh unfinished period leads to a lawful completed view, which then expires normally | Added eligibility for deliberate Q08 consideration, bounded by that exact completed admission and current restrictions.                                 |
| Completed view existed but recipient never opened it                                                              | Nonuse alone does not disqualify; prove actual admission and eligible expiry. Current recipient proof still gates new use.                              |
| Source completed in an access gap or without admitting a recipient completed view                                 | No first-ever completed permission is invented; no gap backfill or reopening. Independent source work remains true.                                     |
| Real timely completed admission is observed late                                                                  | Recover its original facts first. Fresh reading can only be considered under the resulting proved history; observation does not reset the ordinary end. |
| Genuine revocation, binding shortening, purpose/content-use expiry, disposal or other source prohibition          | Preserve actual cause and current scope. Normal-looking dates or removal of an adverse cause do not bypass the restriction.                             |
| Current completed access remains valid but proof or preview fails                                                 | Use the existing qualified recovery path, not another source grant.                                                                                     |
| A rendition of the same admitted original is repaired                                                             | Existing representation/current-use rules apply; no replacement original, later file, superseded bytes or broader action is added.                      |
| Q08 fresh timing/repeat policy or native qualification is missing                                                 | Issuance remains gated. This eligibility choice does not provide those missing rules or proof.                                                          |

Existing Q13 qualified atomic admission then fulfillment can establish the
completed view; timestamp equality alone proves neither permission nor
exclusion. Write-only authority does not imply completed reading. Actual
source fulfillment, acceptance, corrections, ordinary completed ends,
retention/holds and historical outcomes remain unchanged in both options.

## Keep the UX small

Use Q08's intended **Grant read access** action in the existing completed-request
context. Staff see the authorized recipient, eligible read scope, real prior
outcome and qualified terms, then make one meaningful decision. No separate
screen or role is needed merely because completion followed a fresh period.
Administrative review need not expose file contents the actor cannot read.

Recipients return through current qualified entry to the same completed,
read-only context, with actual current terms when resolved and safe help.
They do not need to understand internal admission-route names. No reupload,
new account, request clone, forced download, edit/correction control or automatic
message follows. Preserve Base UI/base-maia, accessible status/focus, localized
dates and low-bandwidth recovery already required by Q08.

Historical permission, current usability, proof, representation and delivery
remain distinct. Missing history is an inability to establish eligibility,
not a fabricated business rejection or permission to infer a broader ceiling.
Existing independent rights and staff work remain available when authorized.
