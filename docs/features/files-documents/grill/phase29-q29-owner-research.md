## Recommended next question

**For Phase 29 staff version review, should Asym provide a clear paired reading
view of two permitted exact versions, or also identify and highlight text
changes for explicitly qualified text-comparison profiles?**

- **A — Paired reading, without automated text-change highlighting
  (recommended).** Show the two source-authorized exact versions with clear
  labels, declared reading coverage and preserved review context. Staff inspect
  the actual permitted representations and decide what matters. Use side-by-side
  presentation where it is readable, with an accessible stacked or switchable
  equivalent on narrow screens. This selects a useful review capability without
  promising that software found every change.
- **B — Paired reading plus qualified text-change highlighting.** Also supply
  detected text additions/deletions/changes for explicitly qualified text-bearing
  profiles. This could help staff notice a changed date or paragraph in a long
  resource document. It adds a real representation and accuracy contract: exact
  inputs, supported extraction coverage, comparison rules, bounded processing,
  meaningful omissions, accessible change navigation and recovery. It does not
  promise image/scan comparison, OCR, layout comparison, formula comparison,
  hidden/revision-content coverage, legal agreement redlining or every D32
  preview format.

**Recommendation: A for Phase 29.** The already-chosen exact-evidence review
journey benefits directly from keeping both versions in context. The available
source evidence does not establish a representative volume of long textual
revisions that makes automated highlighting necessary for this phase. A is less
costly to qualify and maintain, but its real cost is manual attention: staff may
miss a small meaningful change. B is a credible stronger review aid when that
need is central. B is not inherently unsafe or an office editor; it simply
deserves its own qualified scope. Selecting A would not permanently prohibit a
later qualified text-comparison capability.

The question decides the additional Phase29 comparison scope, not whether
exact versions, useful previews, review context or source-owned existing
comparison contracts should exist.

## Scope guards for a fair question and later review

1. **Two exact authorized versions.** The user must have the current source
   permission for both represented inputs and the intended purpose. Generic
   staff rank, filename, common digest, shared folder or review assignment is
   not permission. Do not leak a restricted prior version through its title,
   counts, snippets or difference output.
2. **Source lineage and version meaning remain explicit.** “Earlier” is not
   synonymous with “accepted”; “newer” is not synonymous with “current.” Label
   candidate, accepted, superseded, evidence-only or unavailable using the
   source's actual meanings. Equal bytes do not prove equal provenance or rights.
3. **Read-only assistance.** Neither A nor B merges files, accepts revisions,
   restores a version, replaces a source membership, signs an agreement,
   accepts evidence, publishes media or imports spreadsheet rows. Existing
   deliberate source actions retain their own permissions and exact basis.
4. **No new participant historical access.** The scope is authorized staff
   version review in existing File Manager/source contexts. Q05's ordinary
   participant current-only content decision stays unchanged. Q28 target
   disclosure remains independent of original-source discovery.
5. **No silent content swap.** A concurrent upload or newly accepted version
   cannot replace either pane while someone reads. Show a meaningful current
   source change and allow a deliberate switch. Acceptance still binds the
   current eligible exact review basis, not whichever pair was previously open.
6. **Reading profile limits remain real.** Displaying two qualified renditions
   does not make omitted content available. Unsupported/unsafe/incomplete or
   inaccessible input needs its truthful state and existing authorized recovery;
   no automatic original download, public URL or external-office fallback.
7. **B needs a precise detection claim.** A text-change result covers only the
   supported represented/extracted text under its qualified profile and rules.
   “No detected text changes in this compared scope” cannot mean “the files are
   identical,” “nothing important changed” or “the agreement is legally the
   same.” Where extraction fails or coverage is unknown, there is no valid
   complete comparison result. Do not silently fill gaps with OCR/AI.
8. **Text alone can miss material differences.** An unchanged saved value can
   mask a changed formula; an unchanged paragraph can accompany a changed image,
   signature, layout, revision markup or attachment. Those are reasons for
   scope clarity and actual qualification, not a claim that B must solve every
   format before any text lane can be useful.
9. **One proportional context.** Do not require a new compare workspace,
   mandatory double-pane use, every-page dwell checks, a separate reviewer,
   explanatory essay or confirmation dialog. Preserve notes, focus, chosen
   versions and location where lawful. Accessible mobile use may present one
   labelled version at a time without claiming automatic page alignment.
10. **No new retention or public derivative.** Combined/difference output is
    itself sensitive derived material governed by both inputs and its purpose.
    It does not acquire longer retention, broaden access or become an official
    artifact/search corpus. Current authorization and source restrictions apply
    through generation, promotion, reads and recovery.
11. **Keep B bounded but complete if selected.** Selecting B commits to usable
    text-change assistance for its chosen profiles, with sufficient extraction
    fidelity, multilingual/ordering treatment, accessible change navigation,
    failures and workload qualification. Merely renaming a side-by-side reader
    “Compare,” or shipping a permanently unavailable control, would not fulfill B.
12. **Preserve existing comparison owners.** A leaves P23 current/candidate
    media comparison and P18/P22/P23 template/editorial/semantic comparison
    obligations intact. It excludes only newly proposed automatic text-change
    highlighting from this generic Phase29 increment.

## Practical example and implementation consequences

Elena is reviewing a corrected field handbook uploaded for admitted Asym work.
Both the earlier and proposed versions are currently permitted to her for that
source purpose. With A she can keep both exact versions open in the same review
context and inspect the changed paragraph herself. With B the product can also
draw her attention to detected text changes where the format/profile qualifies.
If a scanned signature or formula changes outside that profile's coverage,
neither the text result nor a “no detected changes” message may conceal that
limit. She still makes the actual source review decision.

A requires exact pair selection, existing source authorization, shared reading
controls, truthful version/coverage state and accessible context retention. B
adds a qualified extraction/comparison result with its own input/profile
lineage, detection semantics, coverage/failure state, bounded processing and
proof. Both reuse the established custody and owner boundaries. No parser,
provider, library, universal format list, new schema design or permanent
global compare permission is selected in this question.
