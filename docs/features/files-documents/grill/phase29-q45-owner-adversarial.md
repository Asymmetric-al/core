The posted target is immutable. A currently authorized author may correct their own text or optional location by an attributable successor revision. Ordinary withdrawal hides active content while preserving only owner-required history. A deliberate currently authorized Restore may restore the latest lawfully retained withdrawn content as a new revision; it cannot bypass governed restriction, redaction, disposition or source ending. Scoped source administration can restrict/withdraw/restore under actual powers and post separately attributed correction, never impersonate or silently rewrite another person's words.

Structurally relevant comment changes participate in the source's current review basis. They fence affected uncommitted prepared decisions but neither undo committed decisions nor create a comment-resolution completion gate. ## Exact proposed amendments

### C01 — Actual ordinary source and powers

Adopt source-owned internal staff **Document Review Comments** for exact ordinary Private Document Request evidence uses/file versions, including Q28 contributions only within their actual originating and target restrictions. ### C02 — Immutable target and deliberate complete admission

Each posted comment binds exact Tenant/environment, source/request/item/evidence use, immutable original file version and business purpose with trusted actual author/context and occurrence. Source identity, target and original attribution cannot be retargeted after posting. A draft and pointer are local uncommitted intent; deliberate Post coherently admits bounded nonblank feedback text, optional qualified location, actual source/context basis, mandatory history and operation result. A concise observation suffices; no required title/reason, minimum essay or semantic quality classifier. Empty markers do not replace explanatory text. A wrong-target correction uses an independently authorized new comment on the right target and appropriate maintenance of the old one; never silently move a posted comment or reuse a draft across audiences/versions. Preserve lawful whole-version comments without mandatory location or successful preview.

### C03 — Own corrections as attributable revisions

A currently authorized author may correct their own current text and optional location through a deliberate source command bound to the exact current comment revision. Commit a new attributed revision when that content/location actually changes; an exact-current Save changes is an honest unchanged result without a fabricated Edited revision or source-basis increment. Retain earlier content only under actual source/history/privacy rules, and never overwrite the originally recorded review basis. Current edit authority is distinct from historical authorship; do not impersonate an author, infer a grant from their departed account or change the immutable source target. A text-only correction can retain an unchanged stored location whose metadata remains permitted even when its technical rendition has expired; label navigation unavailable rather than silently dropping it. Adding or moving a visual anchor requires a currently qualified exact mapping. Deliberate Remove location may make the same-target comment whole-version. No silent stale-write rebase or generic body/history restoration.

### C04 — Withdraw and qualified Restore

Ordinary withdrawal is a deliberate source-owned presentation change, not physical source deletion or erasure of prior review facts. Bind its expected current revision and actor, hide current body/location from ordinary active presentation and expose only a currently permitted minimal withdrawn indication where useful. A retained unchanged anchor may restore as location-unavailable when mapping is not current, never guessed or silently dropped. Lost responses or old Post/Edit/Restore retries cannot reappear after a later withdrawal. No arbitrary Trash window, perpetual raw-body retention or automatic backup revival follows.

Ordinary moderation does not silently rewrite another author's body or location. Give staff a concrete source-context repair/escalation route for unavailable authors, mistaken target, privacy issues and corrupt mapping without direct SQL/provider-console edits, generic data takeover or reopening business decisions. Governing content removal remains distinct from reversible ordinary withdrawal.
