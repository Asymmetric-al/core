# Q65 — What staff need the accepted partner document for after handoff

2026-10-28, Asia/Bangkok. Independent frontier and primary UX research after the parent's Q64 ratification. **Q65 is unselected.** This note does not approve a retention period, new read permission, source adapter, glossary term, implementation or disposal. Only this assigned work note is written.

## Frontier finding

The potentially useful next choice is the **continuing business purpose of the accepted agreement document**, not another upload feature, record tab or handoff notification. Q64 selected collecting and staff-reviewing a sending/supporting partner's requested agreement document for onboarding handoff. GEN-05 documents requested agreements, reviewed documents and handoff; it does not establish a new ongoing partner-reference use, an authoritative agreement register or a lawful retention schedule.

A scoped search of Workflow Studio, the current source-owner constraints and related PRD material found those GEN-05 references and generated acceptance scenarios, rather than a separately qualified post-handoff partner command. This is a bounded source finding, not proof of repository-wide or hosted absence. The owner/runtime independently agree that P9 already reserves the Files context surface: whether staff have a record tab is not a new product fork.

**Proposed question:** Should the first profile cover collection, review and onboarding-handoff evidence only, or also an ongoing partner-reference purpose?

Do not phrase the first option as “the purpose ends at handoff.” Actual historical assurance, correction, incident and record obligations can continue. The choice is whether to adopt an additional operational use, not whether every byte or historical fact disappears at a particular event.

## Practical alternatives and provisional recommendation

| Option                                                                       | Concrete scope                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Benefit                                                                                                                                                               | Cost and limit                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Collection/review/handoff evidence only (provisional recommendation)** | Keep the first reference's adopted business purpose narrow. Current authorized staff may use its history/evidence for that actual purpose under its approved policy; separately qualified source uses retain their own authority. Do not add a new general ongoing partner-reference use merely because a file exists. | Smallest purpose and permission footprint; avoids silently creating an agreement archive or a continuing-use obligation without an established need.                  | A person doing later partner work cannot rely on this collection purpose alone. The actual receiving/continuing source must qualify the new need; a thin “handoff done” state cannot substitute for a real usable result.          |
| **B — Also support ongoing partner reference**                               | Explicitly adopt an additional qualified business use so authorized staff can return to the exact accepted document when doing subsequent partner work. Its source/purpose, reader authority and policy must be defined, and it can reuse qualified bytes without pretending a new upload or approval occurred.        | Keeps useful evidence in context for later staff work and turnover; avoids unnecessary external copies or recollection when the actual continuing need is legitimate. | Requires actual continuing-purpose authority, current readers, lifecycle and policy. It cannot mean perpetual access, automatic latest-version following, legal/current-agreement certification or a broad staff document archive. |

My provisional preference is A until an ongoing use is deliberately established. B is a strong practical alternative when later partner work actually needs the document. This is an evidence-informed scope judgment, not a claim that shorter retention or fewer screens are universally safer. Neither option determines a legal period, immediately disposes of evidence at handoff, changes participant access, or bypasses Q62/Q63. Root/owner coordination determines the final question wording; no answer has been selected here.

**Illustration, not observed ministry practice:** Elena collects and reviews the agreement document for an onboarding handoff. Later Sam takes over partner work and wants to consult that document. Under A, Sam must have a real current purpose and source route; merely knowing the partner or having an organization record is insufficient. Under B, the adopted continuing-reference use can support that task once its actual authority is qualified. Both show which exact document was accepted and preserve the original reviewer/time; neither says it is still legally effective or the partner's latest agreement.

## Boundaries for a fair question

1. Ask about the additional ongoing business use, not whether existing Files navigation, history or safe return exists.
2. A retains lawful collection/review/handoff assurance, current authorized history and independently qualified uses; it is not a broken-handoff or immediate-delete option.
3. B must explicitly qualify its actual partner/source purpose and permitted readers. Record access, a job title or a visible card is not file-read authority.
4. Both preserve exact accepted-version identity and actual source provenance. Neither silently substitutes a newer upload or labels an old version current/legal/valid.
5. Participant and third-party sharing rights remain separate from staff reference. No recipient period is extended and no notification default is changed.
6. Adding or removing a source use is not physical copying, deleting, merging or revoking unrelated uses. Current holds/copy coverage and Q63 guards remain.
7. Actual retention class, trigger, bounds, hold powers and permitted mode remain approved-source inputs. Handoff completion, file age or relationship status is not automatically the clock.
8. No signing, partnership approval, organization representation, membership or giving decision follows from this choice.
9. Later corrections, privacy restrictions and known adverse source state must affect current reference use through actual source rules without rewriting accepted history.
10. Safe unavailable/unknown states and a capable source repair route remain mandatory. Neither a generic Contact support label nor an unqualified external copy completes the journey.
11. Reuse existing source-context UI and metadata discovery; no new archive, policy engine, subscription, reminder, clone/sync or latest-version platform is implied.
12. Preserve all Q01–Q64 and unexecuted native/policy/legacy/user gates. This is not evidence that actual customer use or source configuration has been observed.
