2026-10-24, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q41 and all required amendments are ratified; the glossary has 26 terms. **Q42 is a researched, unselected question.** Only this note is authored. No runtime, provider, permission, canonical owner, OpenSpec, ADR, issue or PR change is made here.

## Recommendation and actual product choice

Ask whether an ordinary request-document entry has **one home folder or Unfiled, with labels and saved views**, or also supports **explicit non-owning references in additional folders**. Recommend the first model. It provides a predictable location and Move action while using the already-required labels, source views and authorized search for other discovery routes. This recommendation is a judgment about a smaller coherent product, not measured Asym behavior or a claim that references are unsafe or expensive in themselves.

The strongest alternative is useful when staff naturally navigate several folder-based working arrangements: they can find the same entry in each without selecting an artificial primary working context or learning an equivalent filter. Such a reference need not duplicate bytes, storage costs, business meaning or permission. Its additional product obligations are intelligible home-versus-reference presentation, reference-only removal, unavailable-target behavior and duplicate selection handling. Both choices remain credible.

Illustrative example: Elena has an equipment-inventory PDF received for an ordinary request. She wants to find the same source entry under **2026 Visit** and **Equipment**. A gives it a home in 2026 Visit and a permitted Equipment label/view. B also permits an explicitly identified reference in Equipment. Neither permits Elena to disclose the evidence merely because she can organize it, nor gives an outside Q41 reader the staff tree.

This is not an existence question about folders, labels, views or bulk handling: those are already required. It is not a storage-provider, folder-permission, general-drive or universal-document-identity decision.

## Boundaries for either answer

1. **No mandatory filing.** A source receipt or Q28 use remains findable through its permitted source/list without creating a folder or label. Unfiled is legitimate; appearing in search, source views or saved views is not an extra home.
2. **No folder-derived rights.** Current source/P12 rules govern organization commands and every disclosed target/container fact. An accessible container does not authorize a private target title, reference, count or other-use association. Do not copy weaker shortcut-title exposure from another product.
3. **No meaning or version mutation.** Filing does not accept evidence, revive a requirement, change currentness, move a business source, inherit a correction, combine equal-byte uses or introduce external history access. Source-approved entry/version mapping remains explicit.
4. **No hidden deletion.** Moving a home, removing an extra reference, removing a folder and disposing evidence are different actions. Neither option authorizes recursive evidence deletion, hold bypass or an automatic trash policy. Exact removal/conflict behavior belongs in the selected-answer review under preservation constraints.
5. **No specialized-owner replacement.** Page/Article and Media trees keep their separate contracts. Do not copy their five-level limit, Tenant/Site scope, rehoming policy, transaction semantics or adapter into ordinary files without qualification. Q41 sharing and source-specific public/resource publication remain intact.
6. **No extra platform bundled.** This question does not choose personal versus shared folder audiences, a new schema builder, smart folders, global hierarchy, automatic filing, public links, cross-tenant roots, external sync or provider. Shared UI and source-view components may be reused only where their actual semantics fit.
7. **Honest aggregate and recovery behavior.** Finite bulk selection preserves exact intended source uses, authorization and outcomes. Several references do not silently multiply downloads or business effects. Stale destinations/targets require safe supported resolution rather than redirecting to another scope. Organization restore cannot restore ended read authority.
8. **Practical accessible controls.** Move and any Add reference action work without dragging; source/target context, focus, status, keyboard/touch, long/RTL names and low-bandwidth handling stay understandable. No exhaustive tree or extra approval ceremony is selected merely by A or B.

The runtime peer's bounded current-source census supports the implementation limits: specialized Media/CMS list and selection helpers and personal CRM views are adjacent mechanisms, not proof of a completed ordinary filing/reference system. This note does not claim a deployed test, absence throughout every repository path, or an existing generic API. The actual ordinary reference, command and intended-user journey still require qualification.
