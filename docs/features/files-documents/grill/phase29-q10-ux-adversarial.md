# Phase 29 Q10 — truthful unfinished and completed access journeys

Researched 20 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. The parent reports Conrad selected **A: a finite unfinished-request boundary, followed by Q07 completed reading after eligible timely fulfillment, under genuine overall caps**. Q01–Q09 are ratified. The proposed refinements below require the parent's synthesis and founder ratification; this note does not select the initial anchor/duration or qualify an implementation.

Evidence comprises the corrected scope-first Q10 question, the ongoing ratified Q03/Q06/Q07/Q08/Q09 boundaries, prior local research, and current primary documentation. Public sources were read again for this review. No private comparator account, real message, upload/download, source mutation, browser, assistive technology, participant study or runtime test was exercised. Only this research note was written.

## Recommendation

Keep A with explicit stage meaning and truthful recovery. Use the same request page before and after fulfillment. The recipient should understand **what was received, who acts next, what they can do now, and when that access ends** without learning about grant records, event consumers or policy minima.

The most important limitation must remain visible: **submission does not equal normal fulfillment**. A person can submit on time, await staff review, and still lose unfinished-stage access if fulfillment has not qualified before that boundary. Do not disguise this cost, blame the recipient, induce duplicate uploads or promise an unselected extension. Staff need relevant context and a real help/recovery owner; no new reminder cadence, approval queue or early-certification shortcut follows.

A also does not promise a full completed interval. Q07's 720-hour maximum, genuine overall caps, later binding restrictions, current recipient/action scope and file availability continue to control. A source-approved completed transition is prospective, not a reinterpretation of old unconditional ends.

## One coherent page, several truthful states

Keep the actual state dimensions separate even when the interface is visually small:

- **Recipient work:** missing, received, further source-required action, or no further submission currently requested.
- **Source processing:** checking, reviewing, qualified normal fulfillment, or another current source outcome.
- **Recipient authority:** unfinished-stage access, admitted completed reading, ended, temporarily unprovable, or an independently governed restriction.
- **File actions:** each exact current file may have independently qualified preview/download availability.

Do not expose these as four new taxonomies users must manage. Project them into a clear heading, one short explanation, the current action and the relevant date. Keep safe concise history secondary under Q05. No large state matrix or second portal belongs in the product.

“Request complete” means the source's actual normal fulfillment. “File received” is a durable submission fact. “Awaiting review” means source work remains. “Access ended” says something about the caller's current rights, not whether the stored evidence exists or the organization has finished its work.

### What not to promise to a person waiting for review

Do not say “You have done everything, so access is protected,” “We will extend this if review takes longer,” or “You will receive 30 days after we finish.” None follows from A. **No further submission is currently requested** is narrower and truthful when supported.

Likewise, do not use “You must finish before [U]” when the remaining action belongs to staff. The finite limit still applies, but the source owns review and fulfillment. The copy must not offload an organizational policy problem onto the recipient.

If the product's intended cohorts routinely need staff review beyond U, the policy must be resolved before activation. This review has no measurements establishing such frequency, and it does not authorize extensions, extra notifications or artificial fulfillment to fix the problem.

## Staff, reviewer and help journeys

### Staff issuing or overseeing the request

Use the source's existing request context. Show the recipient, purpose, unfinished-stage limit and any known genuine overall cap to the extent the staff role may see them. Explain that eligible fulfillment admits Q07 reading, while fulfillment after recipient expiry does not automatically do so.

Do not add a new date picker, approval tier or mandatory essay. The ordinary source profile can supply the rule. A role allowed to administer access is not thereby allowed to read file bodies or every sensitive metadata field.

For a request awaiting staff work, make the relevant access consequence available in that existing context. For example, when authorized and true: **Recipient access while this request is unfinished ends [U]. Completing the request after that time will not restore access.** This is contextual operational information, not a new notification schedule or pressure to approve deficient evidence.

The source owner must have an accountable plan for requests that reach U before fulfillment. That plan may include truthful help and independently authorized continued review; it need not include a newly created extension power. Before activation, validate that the intended cohorts can actually receive the chosen completed journey.

### Reviewer considering completion

The reviewer reads an exact version. Q03 admission of a correction immediately fences its predecessor, even if checks/preview remain pending. The view must not silently replace the bytes under the reviewer or allow a stale acceptance to certify the old candidate.

The approach of U does not relax safety, substantive acceptance or exact evidence-set rules. A click on Complete before U is not proof of timely durable fulfillment if the source boundary resolves later. No backdating or optimistic completed banner is justified.

If source fulfillment succeeds but the response is lost, reconcile that same source act. If fulfillment has not committed, retry under current rules; do not backdate it to the click or first attempted send. An independently authorized staff completion after recipient expiry remains legitimate source work, but its result must distinguish **request completed** from **recipient completed view admitted**.

A reviewer should not be forced to keep a browser open, ask the recipient to log in, or obtain a new recipient confirmation merely to complete otherwise-authorized source work. Historical eligibility at the authoritative fulfillment occurrence and current disclosure are different checks.

### Help and ended access

The request page must lead to a real source-approved support route. An ended or forwarded entry must not reveal whether a request exists, its filenames, recipient, late status or source outcome to an unqualified caller. Neutral help can be available without those disclosures.

Support verifies its own permission and the relevant source facts before discussing a record. It can explain a proved ended state or route a technical recovery; it cannot create rights by promising a resend, asking for attachments through email or directly editing stored dates.

An actual missing completed-view admission is not a routine Q08 renewal case. Conversely, a delayed/failed projection of a valid admission should be repaired from its evidence rather than forcing unnecessary fresh issuance. Neither case warrants making the recipient repeat already received work.

## Delayed processing, uncertainty and failed reads

There are three materially different cases:

1. **Timely fulfillment/admission is proved; processing or the UI learned it later.** Preserve the original Q07 occurrence/end. U must not wrongly close a completed phase already admitted under prospective A. Current overall caps and current action authority still apply.
2. **Timely fulfillment/admission is not proved and authority is uncertain.** Deny affected private actions while resolving the original facts, with a truthful temporary state. Do not substitute the latest task flag, today’s policy or a provider timestamp.
3. **The source proves fulfillment occurred only after unfinished authority ended.** Record legitimate source completion without inventing prior recipient admission. Help is not an automatic extension.

An outage in one projection should not block independently proven current permitted work. For example, history may fail while current files remain authorized, or an uncertain completion result may occur while unfinished authority is independently still valid. Once U passes, however, an unproved transition cannot be treated as permission merely because the old page was open.

Use one stable attempt/result recovery path for lost submissions, corrections or fulfillment. **Received**, **Not received**, and **Cannot yet confirm** are not interchangeable. Do not delete potentially committed bytes, create duplicate receipts, shift admission times or show an invented successful cancellation because the user navigated away.

If E is valid but a safe file representation is unavailable, retain the true completed/access facts and explain the action-specific failure. A download response starting is not proof that a device saved the file. Neither a failed preview/download nor time spent resolving it resets E.

## After completion: changes without a hidden restart

A admits one eligible completed phase under its original source policy; it does not introduce a completion/reopen loop.

- Subsequent security, privacy, purpose or safety restrictions can stop affected current reads. Keep the historical completion and received facts unless the owning source explicitly governs a factual correction.
- A newly discovered unsafe file does not authorize serving an unsafe original, reviving a superseded file, or reopening self-service correction automatically.
- New clarification, additional work or correction after fulfillment needs its own explicit source-authorized process. The completed page may show a safe current next step, but cannot reuse remaining U time to restore writing.
- Genuine source correction of an erroneous completion must preserve attributed evidence and apply its actual authority consequences. It is not a way to reset the completed anchor or manufacture a new full interval.
- Restoring a file representation within unchanged valid authority is different from reviving an expired/revoked grant. Show the actual current end.

Do not promise that a completed request means no further source-related work can ever exist. Do not turn every future obligation into another generic file-library task either.

## Accessibility, mobile and constrained connections

Use the existing shared Base UI/base-maia experience. A state transition should not introduce a new UI library, complex timeline or dashboard. The user should be able to identify the current state and action with keyboard, speech, touch and assistive technology.

- Put the state, consequential date and next action in semantic main content. Give repeated file actions meaningful item context; do not rely on color, a hover tooltip or icon alone.
- For asynchronous transitions, provide suitable status semantics without polling announcements or focus theft. A full page/context transition needs coherent title/heading/focus rather than an extra alert repeating the same result.
- Preserve safe context across proof refresh. Allow supported code paste/autofill. Do not require retyping known recipient information, a new full account, another upload or an unselected timer-start ceremony.
- Evaluate actual content-set session and task timing under WCAG 2.2.1 A. Its >20-hour exception does not choose an initial duration or excuse short nested timers. 2.2.5 reauthentication is AAA; it does not alone establish AA conformance or permission to revive source authority.
- Reflow long dates, filenames, translated copy and RTL without hiding the deadline or primary action. Use sufficient temporal precision without rounding an earlier limit into a later promise.
- Test mobile file providers, background/tab suspension, network loss and re-selection. Do not promise that every browser preserves a local file input after restart.
- Restore already-admitted server facts rather than transferring files again. A start-before-expiry transfer does not reserve permission; unknown results still require reconciliation.
- Do not claim resumable/background/offline capability that is not qualified, and do not persist a private local archive to make recovery appear effortless.
- Preserve Q02's accessible-content distinction: keyboard-operable viewer controls do not make an image-only or semantically inaccessible document accessible. This decision adds no OCR or conversion promise.

These are proposed acceptance outcomes. No runtime screen, screen reader, mobile device or intended-user session was tested here.

## Strongest overall-ceiling alternative

B retains a real benefit: one overall initial maximum can be simpler to communicate and can limit total potential original access more tightly. Some source purposes may reasonably prefer that boundary.

A instead lets eligible timely fulfillment admit a separately bounded read-only phase beyond the unfinished limit, where no genuine overall cap stops it. It better matches the selected follow-up purpose, but requires a trustworthy transition and more careful conditional copy before completion. The temporal limit is not eliminated, and staff delay past U remains a real unresolved operational possibility.

No competitor documentation proves A is universally safer, easier or preferred. No ministry study or measured completion distribution was found. The recommendation rests on consistency with the selected Asym scope, with B's cost and benefit kept explicit.

## Falsifiable proof outcomes, not passed tests

1. A qualifying timely fulfillment transitions beyond U into the exact admitted Q07 read-only phase when no genuine overall cap prevents it; an old unconditional cap remains binding.
2. U expires without fulfillment: no automatic completed view, no upload/correction continuation, and no invented rejection/deletion/recipient-lateness status.
3. On-time submitted work awaiting staff review preserves receipt and shows the correct next actor before and after the relevant boundary, subject to current disclosure.
4. Timely fulfillment with delayed consumer/replay retains original F/E. A fabricated or late fulfillment cannot obtain the same result.
5. Uncertain fulfillment around U gives a truthful temporary state; independently valid actions remain available, while unproved authority never serves protected bytes/metadata.
6. Current caps/revocation/expiry racing fulfillment or read change the actual allowed action; old timestamps and old UI cannot revive access.
7. Correction admission racing reviewer completion fences the exact predecessor and preserves siblings; source completion cannot certify the stale candidate.
8. Lost submission/correction/completion responses recover one effect and true original timestamp. Known nonreceipt, unknown outcome and post-commit denied disclosure remain distinguishable.
9. After fulfillment, later restriction/remediation does not restore writes, reset E, revive an old original or erase the prior event. A genuinely new source obligation has an explicit owned path.
10. A valid completed admission with a failed projection is repaired as that admission; absence of a prior admission is not disguised as Q08 renewal.
11. Session/proof expiry permits qualified re-entry only within current source authority, preserving safe context without extra enrollment, unauthorized replay or source-time reset.
12. A partial preview/download/history failure keeps independently proved current work and true access state. No false empty history, deletion or “saved on device” claim appears.
13. Expired, forwarded, reassigned and cross-tenant links disclose only permitted neutral or qualified detail; staff grant management does not imply file-body read access.
14. Real keyboard, speech, screen reader, touch, zoom/reflow/RTL, long dates and low-bandwidth/mobile interruption paths remain understandable and operable.
15. Representative recipients understand received versus completed, who acts next, unfinished expiry versus Q07 end, and the lack of guaranteed extension. Staff understand that access timing is not permission to certify deficient evidence.
16. Intended source cohorts have a workable review/help plan at U and genuine caps, without relying on a hidden renewal or falsely claiming that all recipients receive completed readback.

No new numeric warning lead, service SLA, initial grant duration, activation anchor, reminder schedule or renewal count is proposed. Operational reconciliation and performance budgets must be concrete in the qualified source design; their values were not researched or selected here.
