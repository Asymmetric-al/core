# Phase 29 Q36 — Independent UX and comparative adversarial review

Reviewed 2026-10-23, Asia/Bangkok. Conrad selected A: allow source-authorized
staff to add an ordinary item to an eligible issued unfinished request for the
same continuing legitimate purpose and intended recipient. This note proposes
exact amendments and proof for that answer. Selection does not itself ratify
every amendment or qualify native implementation. Q01–Q35 and the twenty-three
existing glossary definitions remain ratified.

Only this assigned note is authored. The historical question and comparative
research retain their unanswered-stage status; the current explicit selection
controls this review. Owner/runtime findings remain attributed to their parallel
research. No product, canonical source, schema, provider or private data is changed.

## Judgment and strongest alternative

Accept A with the amendments below. Keeping a modest additional need alongside
related ongoing collection can reduce fragmentation and preserve the context of
earlier work. That is a product judgment requiring user proof, not a measured
Asym benefit. A materially changes the source requirement set and participant
scope; it cannot be implemented as a broadly authorized row insertion.

B remains strong: prepare and issue a separately authorized request for the
additional work. The original can then fulfill independently under its actual
requirements. B is particularly useful when the purpose, recipient, sensitivity
or timing is different, when the original is terminal, or when the source cannot
qualify a safe amendment. It does not require canceling the original, revoking
Q33 correction/removal or repeating valid proof/uploads where Q01/Q28 permit
reuse. It is not a fake Reissue shortcut or an unqualified manual workaround.

The simple user interaction should remain **Add requested item**, with enough
information to review the new demand and its consequence, followed by a truthful
result in the existing request. No new notification default, read acknowledgment,
revision platform, broad editor, extra approver or arbitrary grace period is
needed to make that experience complete.

## Exact proposed amendments

Severity and likelihood refer to the prospective unamended design, not deployed
incidents. H means authority/privacy, historical truth or material business harm;
M means meaningful confusion, wasted work or operational cost. Likelihood is
qualitative, not a measured incident rate. P references below are future proof.

### U01 — Exact bounded addition authority

**H / plausible if generic editing is reused.** Permit only a deliberate new
ordinary requested item on the exact eligible issued unfinished source, for the
same continuing legitimate purpose and intended recipient. Derive trusted tenant,
actor and source context and establish actual current source/P12 authority.
Reviewer assignment, file access, team membership, an admin label, Q22 reevaluation
or an old link is not requirement-authoring authority. Do not turn A into editing
an existing demand, evidence acceptance, recipient change, template mutation,
specialist waiver or reopening. The new item's real source-defined blocking or
nonblocking meaning must be explicit; preserve Q22-certified optional work as
nonblocking rather than making every added row a completion hold. **P01, P02.**

### U02 — Concrete prospective scope successor

**H / plausible if the request URL is treated as authority.** Adopt an explicit
source-owned versioned requirement/recipient-scope successor for the approved
new lineage under Q01, with the necessary narrow prospective amendment to
Q13/Q15. Record which new item, continuing purpose, intended recipient, actor,
relevant source revision and current access basis are authorized. Retain original
admissions and history; fence obsolete scope where required rather than mutating
the meaning of a previously issued grant. A valid original or already-qualified
fresh unfinished period may carry the new lineage only through that exact
adoption, with its existing root/end/caps/adverse restrictions unchanged and no
overlapping new grant. Keep unrelated rights and legitimately narrowed old subsets
intact. Qualify the new lineage's actual action/read and applicable subsequent
completed-scope consequences explicitly under the existing owners; this does not
create a new completed-history entitlement or a cancellation-view expansion.

An individually narrow active/fresh/recovery admission remains narrow while in
effect; it does not by itself permanently shrink the genuine source-approved
universe for a later separately authorized admission. Actual eligible timely
fulfillment creates the normal completed-reading ceiling from its then-qualified
amended scope, under the existing current-own-submission/member/version/action
limits. Later Q08/Q16/Q19 recovery stays within that actual historical ceiling;
neither today's larger definition nor a union of historical grants expands it.
An individually narrower recovery does not replace the original ceiling. Staff
processing after recipient expiry can fulfill the source without reviving access.

Current proof may be reused only when Q01 actually qualifies it for the amended
context. Do not require a new challenge merely because a row was added, or infer
authority from a matching mailbox, account or old token. Revalidation applies at
each owner-controlled read/use/admission boundary. Previously admitted old work
retains its lawful original facts; unadmitted operations cannot borrow authority
from the new successor. This is a concrete new source/access capability requiring
publication and native proof, not a claim that current owners already allow it.
**P03–P05, P09.**

### U03 — Concrete publication and a usable route for the required work

**H / plausible inaccessible or overbroad demand.** Preparation is inert. Final
publication coherently admits the new item, its exact prospective scope and
relevant review coverage. Source addition, participant availability, proof and
communication remain distinct facts. When recipient action is required, the
flow must establish its actual capable participant path, not publish a stranded
demand and promise unspecified repair later. If current authority or a required
admission fact is absent, retain an uncommitted proposal and the actual B/source
resolution, not an Added/usable claim or hidden completion blocker.

An expired period cannot be revived by addition. Publication for required
participant work must be bound to a separately reviewed/authorized fresh
unfinished admission explicitly including the approved lineage under the Q36
reconciliation and existing repetition, fixed-end, nonoverlap and currentness
rules. One meaningful combined act with a coherent authorized handoff is possible;
this need not be another mandatory modal. Otherwise use a qualified B/source
route. Revocation, ended purpose, wrong recipient and unknown authority are not
expiry shortcuts. Preserve the exact qualified direct-recipient/no-Party branch
without creating a Party, account or household association.

Where authorized staff can actually supply/review lawful existing evidence under
Q28 and recipient action is not required, do not force fresh recipient access
merely to enable that legitimate staff operation. Qualify the real staff path,
source/target evidence rights, attribution and coverage; acceptance remains
separate. Show actual participant availability honestly. A missing participant
path cannot be relabeled staff handling to hide a stranded demand; later required
participant action still needs qualified scope/access or a capable source remedy.
Failure to qualify a proposed addition must not block lawful unrelated old work.
**P04–P06, P11, P13.**

### U04 — One real order against completion and cancellation

**H / plausible at final-review races.** Authoritative addition and its scope/
history facts must be ordered coherently with relevant requirement changes,
Q22 fulfillment and Q34 cancellation. Addition first makes its actual required
work part of the later full completion predicate; actual durable fulfillment or
cancellation first denies this unfinished-source action. Source-certified
optional work remains nonblocking. A draft, open form, planned addition or staff
intention cannot hold completion open. Final upload/review click or a queued
completion evaluator is not itself durable fulfillment. Use actual ordered
occurrence time, not a backdated browser or transaction-start timestamp.

Do not reopen a terminal request, discard earlier acceptance or let a stale
completion worker ignore an admitted requirement. Preserve lawful already
admitted operations and revalidate affected unadmitted intent. Unrelated activity
need not cause a blanket conflict or force the participant to start over.
**P07–P09.**

### U05 — Competent coverage with minimal repeated work

**H / plausible orphaned work; M / probable unnecessary setup.** Establish actual
capable Q24/Q25 person/team coverage for the newly declared work. Reuse valid
existing coverage where it genuinely includes the new need; do not force manual
reviewer reselection, disclose private rosters, confer authority by assignment,
or rerun every first-original issuance check indiscriminately. A relevant scope/
coverage change must be revalidated; missing new-work coverage leaves the proposed
addition unadmitted with a real qualified resolution path, not an orphaned task.

New empty items must not fabricate an actionable receipt or staff-review event.
When an actual subsequent receipt/question/reply satisfies a ratified owner
contract, its normal qualified work/notice behavior still applies. Existing
items remain handled under their actual source rules. **P02, P06, P15.**

### U06 — Preserve progress and show what was added

**H / plausible history corruption; M / probable confusion.** Preserve every
existing receipt, exact version, acceptance/Changes requested decision, question,
attribution and history on its original basis. New item identity must be new;
do not relabel an old item or copy acceptance to make the added requirement look
satisfied. Q28 can supply exact existing evidence only through its actual source/
target authority and attribution, with acceptance remaining separate.

The current permitted view must distinguish the added item and actual outstanding
work without making earlier effort look lost. A count may change, but it must not
reset submitted/accepted work or imply failure to provide something that did not
exist. Use a concise addition cue and lasting source history, for example an
authorized **Added [date]** indication. No Read, Resolve, acknowledgment, artificial
newness timer, generalized diff engine or mandatory request-wide resubmission is
needed. Staff must not claim the participant has seen it from a page load.
**P10, P11, P16, P19.**

### U07 — Honest timing without a new deadline policy

**H / plausible false adverse meaning; M / probable late-addition friction.**
Retain Q09 requested-by target and Q10–Q19 access timing as different facts. Show
the actual added-at occurrence and the currently applicable target/access status
where authorized. Do not backdate the demand, mark the new item late for time
before it existed, or rewrite earlier timely submissions as late. Existing
request-level lateness may still be true; the addition must not erase it either.

Addition neither extends access nor resets the original target, timestamps,
repetition budget or prior adverse facts. If a target has passed or usable access
is short/unavailable, staff and participant need truthful context and an actual
qualified next step. Do not invent an automatic grace period, new item due-date
policy, pre-expiry renewal or guarantee of enough time. A separate normal request
may be appropriate when its own authority qualifies. **P04, P07, P12, P19.**

### U08 — Reviewable commitment and a capable mistake remedy

**H / plausible erroneous new demand.** Before commitment, let authorized staff
review the exact request/recipient context, new item description and actual
completion/access consequence and correct the proposed item. Use the existing
request surface with a clear **Add requested item** action; typing, field blur,
menu opening or draft autosave must not publish new demand. Require only the
real source facts, not a reason essay, second approver, typed-name ritual or
routine second modal.

After a mistaken addition, retain its history. Q33 No longer required applies
only when its exact outstanding/unaccepted and unfinished-source conditions hold;
its correction cannot be used to replace this with a different obligation. For
already accepted or terminal work, use the actual capable source/history/privacy
remedy with current authority, preserving existing decisions and terminal truth.
Do not promise generic Undo, unacceptance, deletion or reopening. A new ordinary
request is appropriate only for a genuine separately authorized remaining need,
not a pretend reversal of unnecessary evidence already collected. Qualify the
real remedy/operator for supported stages; a contact label alone is insufficient.
**P13, P14, P19.**

### U09 — Durable identity, truthful outcomes and recoverable failures

**H / plausible under lost-response or projection failures.** The owning source
must coherently commit the new item, required history, relevant scope successor
and required durable handoff/evaluation facts. Keep semantic addition identity
across transport retries, tabs and recovery. An exact retry returns the original
effect under current disclosure; conflicting changed material must not quietly
become another item or overwrite the first. Another genuinely authorized addition
is separate intent, not a transport retry.

Distinguish draft, committed addition, actual participant availability and unknown
result. A failed required source commit is not success; a lost response is not
proof of failure. If secondary projections fail after commitment, retain the real
source fact and repair it without adding again. Do not create a hidden required
item from an uncommitted draft or pretend a local-only save was published. Recovery
must preserve current scope/closure restrictions and lawful input without
storing private context in an offline archive. **P07, P09, P17, P18.**

### U10 — Privacy and prior notification contracts stay exact

**H / plausible disclosure; M / probable noise if events are reused.** New
participant-visible requirement text and history must obey exact source disclosure
and minimum-needed content rules. Internal rationale, sensitive source labels,
private team details, unrelated filenames and other people's facts cannot leak
through a new-item cue, count, error, search result, export, log or cached view.
Current authorization governs every surface; clearing forbidden context on a
tenant/user/request switch includes delayed responses. This does not suppress
independently qualified source contacts, legitimate recipient envelopes or
protected carriers under their existing contracts.

The addition itself creates no new email purpose, reminder, SMS, bell or global
notification setting. Do not manufacture a Q30 reply, rejection, receipt, access
event or cancellation to obtain another family's outward effect. Genuine later
qualifying events retain their own Q20/Q26/Q27/Q31/Q32/Q35 semantics and settings.
Native visibility does not guarantee someone away from the portal knows about
the addition; any existing outreach must be actually qualified and is not
secretly mandatory provider work inside the addition transaction. **P15, P16.**

### U11 — Accessible small interaction and preserved ongoing work

**M / probable without composed proof.** Use existing base-maia/Base UI with clear
labels, programmatic names/state/relationships, keyboard and voice operation,
stable focus, useful validation and persistent accessible status. Do not rely
on color, hover, icon-only meaning or disappearing toast. Reflow/zoom and touch
must preserve the exact item, consequence and commit/correct controls. Announce
meaningful asynchronous result changes without repeated polling interruptions.

Prove long translations, RTL, mixed-direction identifiers, IME composition,
international names and locale/time-zone presentation. Enter during composition
must not publish a demand. Current participant work must survive lawful scope
refresh where possible; invalid/stale work receives a clear current result with
recoverable input, not silent loss or automatic resubmission on reconnect.
Respect reduced motion and contrast. No new native app, provider, portal inbox
or multi-stage editor is necessary for these requirements. **P18, P19.**

### U12 — Prospective qualification, bounded operations and fair fallback

**H / plausible unsafe rollout; M / plausible unnecessary platform work.**
Publish the exact new source authority and Q01/Q13/Q15 successor, Q22 ordering,
Q24/Q25 coverage and relevant Q28/Q30/Q33 integration through canonical owners
and full declared OpenSpec companions. Do not edit generated meaning independently
or activate from this grooming note. Compatible schema/readers/writers/recovery
must precede enablement; old clients, imports, generic task edits and restore
cannot manufacture addition authority or broaden past grants. Restore/rollback
must preserve later closure/restrictions, lineage, history and permanent identity.

Use bounded indexed source/projection/recovery work with measured finite budgets,
fairness and actual named operators. No new whole-history scan, per-row service,
second queue, arbitrary fixed item cap or entire future Studio prerequisite is
selected. B remains a real qualified separate-issuance option for new scope that
cannot safely join this source; do not automatically cancel/clone the original.
Measure surprise work, lost-progress perception, added-item confusion, late-target
misinterpretation and recovery burden without private-content analytics or new
read tracking. **P20–P22.**
