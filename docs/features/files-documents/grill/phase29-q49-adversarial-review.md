## Research and strongest alternatives

The strongest alternative is a tightly proved pristine same-request successor, with guided replacement once any contribution or clarification exists. It preserves a familiar reference for early mistakes, but needs an additional whole-history eligibility race, recipient lineage and clock/control branch. It is feasible when explicitly qualified; it is not inherently unsafe. A chooses one consistent correction shape and recovers its convenience through safe reviewed definition reuse. The no-build baseline is current authorized Q34 cancellation plus independently authored new issuance; it preserves truth but does not supply the complete contextual prefill and recoverable relationship required by this selected answer.

Current primary comparisons were checked on 2026-10-25. They describe products, not Core permission or proof:

- [Submittable reference correction](https://submittable.help/en/articles/4109464-how-do-i-resend-or-change-a-reference-request) uses cancel/new reference request within an editable application context. This supports linked continuity without old recipient authority transfer; its applicant account/approval steps are not copied.
- [Adobe recipient replacement](https://helpx.adobe.com/sign/web/users/manage-agreements/replace-recipient.html) and [Dropbox Sign's narrow replacement](https://help.dropbox.com/view-edit/replace-signers-pending-request) support bounded same-request continuity with old access ending and an audit trail. Their signing thresholds, roles, notifications and deletion advice do not govern ordinary source evidence.
- [Submittable ownership transfer](https://submittable.help/en/articles/9456944-transfer-submission-ownership-for-organizations) intentionally transfers much broader application material and financial associations. It is serious evidence that ownership continuity can matter, but confirms why Q49 must not silently become that broader product.
- [Content Snare duplication](https://contentsnare.com/help/knowledge-base/duplicating-requests/) explicitly separates questions from answers and warns that copying answers can leak sensitive information. [HubSpot record cloning](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/clone-a-record) provides editable prefill, excludes activities/attachments and clears designated unique/sensitive values. These support bounded reviewable reuse; Core does not copy answers/associations or assume unmarked prose is safe.
- [GOV.UK Check answers](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/check-answers/) and [W3C error prevention](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/error-prevention-legal-financial-data.html) support reviewable/correctable consequential actions. An unfinished predecessor may be canceled through Q34 at the handoff. A predecessor already lawfully canceled may support Q34's independently authorized remedy when its actual cancellation and current replacement purpose are proved; a canceled status alone is insufficient. Actual fulfillment, a specialist source or an unrelated purpose does not enter this ordinary replacement path. Same-person mailbox correction/lost-inbox recovery, representation, collaborator addition, ownership transfer and source-specific remediation retain their own qualified paths; neither email/name similarity nor a staff checkbox proves those cases.

> Separate the current source/P12 powers to prepare/read/edit a replacement draft, reuse origin definitions, administer responsibility if changed, cancel or restrict the predecessor, issue the new request and repair a blocked result. Generic staff/admin status, reviewer assignment, file Read, possession of a link and the fact that someone created the draft confer none of those powers. Tenants allocate these actual capabilities through existing qualified source/P12 controls and preserve their legitimate narrower policies; staff can prepare now, save and resume, discard preparation, stop old access independently when authorized, or complete the qualified handoff. Add no new universal tenant settings panel, routine second approver, mandatory reason essay or policy bypass. Require a concise source basis from trusted facts or only the necessary staff input where the source needs it.

> Keep at most one active source-owned replacement preparation per predecessor, using the existing ordinary draft facilities and a narrow protected origin relationship. Repeated Start returns the existing permitted preparation or a safe actionable result; it does not create another draft or reveal hidden draft contents. Other currently authorized staff may continue it without impersonating its creator, gaining their rights or requiring their live session. Preparation, autosave, opening the form and merely selecting another person have no cancellation, recipient authority, notification or reminder effect. A draft does not reserve future issuance, hold fulfillment open or block the predecessor's safety or normal source operations. Deliberately discarding an unissued preparation leaves an active predecessor unchanged and never reverses an earlier committed stop; a later genuine preparation gets its own truthful identity.

> Offer source-qualified reuse of selected current item definitions, not cloning an issued request. This is an explicit narrow amendment beyond Q39's template-to-new-draft capability. The reusable field set consists only of currently compatible ordinary definition content such as the item label, requested type, instructions and source-qualified requiredness, with fresh item identities and recorded origin/version provenance. Any new item or supporting material must enter through its independently qualified authoring/source path.

> Use current source meaning to propose genuinely remaining work. Previously accepted or otherwise source-sufficient material is not automatically recollected from the new person, and optional nonblocking work does not become a new completion hold by copying it. Person-specific material may nevertheless be genuinely required for the new person; old acceptance is neither automatic new satisfaction nor a universal prohibition on a new legitimate requirement. Staff review which legitimate requirements apply now and can adjust permitted ordinary definitions before issuance without rewriting the predecessor's requirements. Old acceptance never becomes new-item acceptance, and the new recipient is not required to attest that they supplied someone else's material. Independently qualified Q28 reuse remains a separate attributable evidence act with its own origin/target authority; it is not an automatic copy or prerequisite for replacement. If no legitimate new recipient work remains, do not manufacture a replacement merely to repair a label or obtain another notification allowance; use the actual source outcome/remedy.

A permitted draft edit may change the proposed new recipient or definitions before issuance, but it is not an update to the predecessor's recipient or global Auth/CRM/contact identity. Use expected relevant basis and conflict-safe draft saves; preserve lawful user edits rather than silently refreshing them from a changed source or template. Re-evaluate relevant source work, disclosure and readiness at final review and commitment. Changes that affect the reviewed recipient, definitions, timing, responsibility or notification choice require review of the actual new proposal; unrelated source changes must not cause pointless universal reconfirmation. Caller-controlled IDs, flags or normalized address guesses cannot provide authority.

**Document Request Replacement:** A source-authorized new request issued for a different intended recipient in place of an eligible prior request, with an explicit relationship preserving each request's identity and history. It is distinct from draft preparation, an address edit, reopening, or transfer of earlier evidence or access.

The term identifies the issued source outcome, not draft preparation, a mutable email field, copied evidence or a new transfer platform. The exact definition is adopted as the 30th canonical glossary term; all 29 earlier definitions are preserved.

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

Safe-looking prefill can leak private prose; a generic Issue can bypass the guided flow; partial writes can leave two recipients active or an old cancellation plus a draft labeled success. The exact copy boundary, source-enforced bound-draft gate and atomic normal handoff close those paths. Urgent containment is deliberately separate and is never rolled back to hide a failed replacement.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That the source can establish a legitimate different person, current remaining work and a qualified native issuance/cancellation boundary. None follows from mailbox control or an existing helper. Actual Asym demand, user comprehension and operating budgets remain unmeasured and require activation evidence. The recommendation is a product judgment, not universal vendor consensus.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adds a narrow source-owned preparation/replacement relationship and explicit Q34/Q25 composition. It preserves current identity/contact owners, acceptance/fulfillment, custody, P17/P6 and scoped reminder controls. It does not migrate CRM identity, public media, financial records, application ownership or specialist duties.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Staff can reuse appropriate definitions, save/resume and stop unsafe old access without rebuilding everything or waiting for email. Recipients see their own actual request and proof path. Old progress remains correctly attributed, new work is reviewed rather than blindly repeated, and status/recovery is persistent and accessible.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Primary products support both narrow replacement and cancel/new flows, with different history/disclosure consequences. Reviewed prefill and clear consequential-action review are documented design patterns, not permission to clone private data. Current PostgreSQL and Core owner contracts ground transaction/authorization behavior. No exactly-once, unchanged-cost, automatic sanitizer or compliance claim is made.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes, with the explicit source/Q34/Q25/Q39 and other named compatible amendments. Q01 already requires correction; Q49 chooses the guided linked shape. Current Auth, grants and reminder helpers are evidence to qualify, not a ready-made implementation. Native source work does not require the whole future Workflow Studio.
