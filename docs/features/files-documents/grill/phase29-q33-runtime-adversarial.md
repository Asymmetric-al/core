## Exact proposed amendments

### C01 — Meaning and eligible scope

The native ordinary source SHALL support a deliberate No longer required decision only for an exact outstanding unaccepted item in an otherwise eligible unfinished request, when that item's demand was mistaken or no longer applies to the legitimate current source purpose. The outcome changes requiredness; it is not evidence acceptance, participant submission, rejection, generic legal/financial/application waiver, whole-request cancellation or file deletion. Preserve specialist/nonwaivable obligations and remaining genuine source duties. Inconvenience, repeated failed attempts or inability to supply evidence does not itself authorize removal. Already accepted items and completed requests retain their current source remediation boundaries.

### C02 — Durable source facts and database integrity

Use the requesting source's authoritative command, not a task checkbox, File Manager mutation or unrestricted collection write. Bind tenant/environment, request, exact item, relevant requirement/source basis and original human decision identity in enforced source relationships; a caller cannot relink the outcome to another tenant, source, item or requirement. Preserve original actor, qualified occurrence and material decision facts, with governed disposition rather than casual overwrite/delete. Valid-state and semantic uniqueness controls must reject impossible combinations and direct fabrication. Qualify actual grants, all applicable RLS policies, old/new-row restrictions, views/RPC/security-definer boundaries, trigger transforms and service/import/job paths. Source authority checks remain necessary on privileged paths that bypass ordinary RLS.

### C03 — Actual current authority, bounded context and replay disclosure

Resolve the real actor and scope from trusted server context; evaluate the source/P12 requirement-change authority separately from reading, accepting evidence, assignment and grant administration. Preserve applicable separation of duties without inventing a universal new permission atom or second reviewer. Revalidate authority and lifecycle at the qualified command boundary after relevant waits. Unknown required authority denies the new effect; a failed auxiliary view does not disable independently qualified actions. Recovering an original result requires current permission to disclose it, not retention of the original actor's current write power and not impersonation by the recovering caller. Identity/tenant/request/purpose changes discard stale client results and context that is no longer lawful.

### C04 — Exact evidence, intake and independent work

Order removal with participant correction/admission, staff evidence admission/replacement/withdrawal and review using the owning currentness boundary. Selection and transfer are not receipt. Removal-first prevents the now-ended ordinary demand from accepting a stale required-evidence finalization/correction; safely reconcile unknown admission. Receipt-first preserves the exact durable receipt, immutable lineage and qualified history even when the demand later ends. No scan failure revives an old candidate and no No longer required decision makes bytes safe or deletes other uses/holds. The action changes this item's demand, not all grants, conversations or request work: preserve other eligible items, actual Q30 clarification eligibility and owned follow-through, and independently authorized receipt/history views. Genuine source-certified nonblocking work and independent safety dependencies retain their meanings.

### C05 — Current human decision and ordered aggregate transitions

The reviewed basis must contain the relevant current source/requirement/instruction/evidence/review dependencies, including newly admitted relevant Q30 text. A changed affected basis conflicts and requires deliberate revalidation; no worker silently rebases the decision. Relevant-text-first fences the older affected decision, but adds no Read/Reply/Resolve gate; unrelated work proceeds. A committed removal remains historical if later text arrives, including while Q22 follow-through is deferred. Acceptance-first makes the item ineligible for this action; removal-first fences stale acceptance/Request changes or obsolete demands. Requirement revision and correction cannot silently retarget the decision. Use a request aggregate boundary or equivalent complete version/fence protocol for final updates, membership and closure, without a tenant-wide lock or unnecessary cross-item serialization.

### C06 — Coherent commit, permanent identity and truthful recovery

Commit the source outcome, mandatory history/current-state relationship and required co-located durable follow-through atomically. Secondary projections recover from durable intent; provider/expensive work stays outside source locks. One original deliberate operation retains its semantic identity beyond transport-key expiry, leases, double clicks or a new observer. Changed material conflicts. Concurrent attempts cannot create duplicate current source effects, and genuinely later corrective decisions remain separately attributable. Recover an original committed result before treating its resulting state as a failed new attempt, while showing any currently permitted later state distinctly. Replaying an old removal after a later correction returns historical truth and never reapplies removal or stale outward/work effects. A rollback before commit leaves no successful source fact. Ambiguous commitment stays unresolved until authoritative reconciliation; it is neither success nor proof that a new mutation is safe. Known source success survives refresh/history-view failures.

### C07 — Complete nonvacuous fulfillment and actual time

Each relevant removal or compensating requirement decision evaluates or durably arranges Q22 reevaluation of complete authoritative current facts and eligible lifecycle. No count of visible files, task completion, missing definition, failed query or filtered/paginated empty list can fulfill. A legitimate explicitly permitted complete empty/non-file source predicate may qualify; do not invent a universal one-file minimum. All-items-No-longer-required is neither automatically Fulfilled nor automatically Canceled. A whole request whose purpose ended cannot be laundered into successful fulfillment by removing items. Preserve real safety/non-file/final-judgment obligations and source-certified nonblocking work. Both orders of concurrent final changes must produce no premature closure and no missed final transition.

Record the actual source removal/correction occurrence and the actual qualified Q22 fulfillment occurrence separately where they differ. Neither browser/click/upload time, task time, pre-wait transaction start nor later observer time substitutes. Recover a prior occurrence only from durable proof. If removal succeeded but fulfillment is unresolved, show that distinction and recover technical follow-through without a second business approval. Q06–Q19 clocks and timely-F conditions remain unchanged; late completion or a retry cannot revive expired access or mint another completed-read period.

### C08 — Permanent, bounded correction of a mistake

The supported ordinary journey SHALL provide a lasting contextual correction path from the item/history, accessible after reload or from another authorized session—not only a short-lived toast. For the same still-eligible unfinished request, a currently authorized source operator can deliberately correct a mistaken removal through a **new compensating source decision**, linked to the original and checked against the current requirement, authority, evidence and lifecycle. This is the bounded recovery amendment proposed for ratification; it is not presumed to exist today. Keep both decisions and real actors/times. Do not restore a pre-removal snapshot, silently revive an old Request changes finding or predecessor, copy acceptance by equal bytes, invent new requirements unrelated to the corrected item, or extend participant access. Existing still-lawful evidence is reassessed under current Q21/Q23 rules; do not demand dummy or duplicate bytes solely to repair state.

Compensation and Q22 fulfillment race through the same current source boundary: compensation-first changes the predicate and fences stale completion; fulfillment-first cannot be reversed by this path. For a fulfilled/ended/ineligible request, preserve the historical result and make the actual source remedy concrete. One minimal permitted remedy is an authorized source operator recording the mistake and separately reviewing a genuinely new request **only through independently qualified ordinary original-issuance authority**, including its actual Q25 readiness and Q01–Q19 recipient/access rules, linked to the mistake. This does not create a generic Reissue/reopen/reset command, automatically select a recipient, copy credentials/evidence acceptance or send a new message. Another already-effective domain remedy may apply instead. An activated cohort must prove such a capable route for terminal mistakes; a support label, direct SQL repair or an imagined future issuer is insufficient. If no actual route can be qualified, that affected journey cannot activate.

### C09 — Truthful, narrow disclosure and unchanged access

Show the persistent outcome distinctly from Accepted/Changes requested in the same current request context; do not silently disappear the item. Explain the currently qualified possible completion consequence before the meaningful action. A concise safe source-approved explanation or sufficient status/instructions can be enough; keep internal reasons and sensitive care/location/third-party context separately authorized. No participant history/download permission follows from an audit record, and no source status grants bytes. Staff may act independently after participant expiry while disclosure remains governed. Preserve accessible focus, lawful drafts/reading position, mobile/keyboard/AT/localized and slow-network recovery; no private offline cache, routine essay, modal or new account is required.

### C10 — Reuse the real owners; do not create a new communication event

Source/P12 owns authority and requiredness; P9/shared work projects the source outcome; P29 custody owns bytes/retention under their consuming purposes; Q22 owns fulfillment. Reconcile applicable registered Workflow source binding without making the future Studio a prerequisite. Removing or correcting the demand updates/fences obsolete current work but is not a new Q26/Q27/Q31/Q32 event, bell, email, reminder, participant promise or provider operation. Any existing queued effect retains its actual current source-purpose contract. No automatic fresh access or outward message is smuggled into recovery.

### C11 — Finite load and operational recovery

Qualify bounded indexed lookups for current source proof, full aggregate evaluation, semantic recovery and history. Full means authoritative completeness, not unbounded materialization or a UI page limit. Use short consistently ordered source transactions or an equivalent proven protocol, measured finite worker/retry/reconciliation budgets and fair tenant scheduling. There is no need for a tenant lock, event on every poll, per-file approval ritual, whole-roster snapshot, generic approval queue or whole-document parser. Define safe scoped stop, accountable technical recovery and minimal operational evidence distinguishing denied/stale/unresolved/committed-with-pending-follow-through, without private bodies or raw credentials in logs.

### C12 — Native proof and activation, including rollback/restore

Enable only qualified native cohorts with the actual command, user journey, permanent correction route, complete Q22 rule and relevant owners integrated. Test real roles and privileged alternate paths against the fully migrated schema; string and mock assertions do not replace it. Old writers, mixed clients/workers, imports and restored data cannot fabricate No longer required from missing uploads, dismissed tasks, empty sets or orphaned flags. Preserve existing facts and restrict activation prospectively unless independently authorized historical migration proves exact semantics. Scoped rollback/stop must not undo committed removal/fulfillment or resurrect revoked access. Roll-forward/restore must reconcile later source decisions and effect identities before work resumes; replay cannot erase a compensating decision or re-create old demands. The source remains the authority during recovery, not a UI status or backup age.
