## What is already settled, and why Q24 remains real

Workflow's general design already permits **named responsibilities** which resolve to one eligible principal, a permitted team queue or needs_assignment. It also permits justified named people. That is a common mechanism and source qualification framework, not a universal guarantee that every native source supports a shared actionable queue.

The owner-binding contract explicitly says broader queue/delegation/return/reassignment must have each source's own vocabulary, purpose, recipients, visibility, consequence and recovery. Q21–Q23 reserved ordinary routing while selecting its review outcomes. Therefore Q24 may narrowly choose A's ordinary team-route support or B's named-person prerequisite without contradicting the common owner. It must not re-decide the already-set rule that assignment grants no access.

P9's **N role-qualified assignees plus one primary** is a CRM relationship/portfolio rule. It does not imply exactly one document reviewer, exclusive review authority, a queue membership model or a new generic responsibility slot. P9's My/All task lists are shared projections, not the document source's assignment authority.

## Scope needed in the question

1. **Source-derived resolution, not a creator default.** Use the ordinary source's qualified responsibility policy/context to propose or resolve the destination. This question does not hard-code the request creator, first database match, first available staff member or all administrators. It does not choose a new tenant policy editor, automatic routing algorithm or source-of-default precedence.
2. **Responsibility and authority stay separate.** A selected person or team destination must be currently eligible under the source's actual rules. Each human decision still needs exact current source/P12 review authority, purpose, evidence and attribution. Adding somebody to a team or assigning a task cannot itself expose evidence, grant capabilities, select a protected specialist reviewer or remove separation of duties.
3. **An accountable team route is real work ownership.** A is not “leave it in a list and hope.” The owner must qualify the destination, eligible actor resolution and the route for absent/ineligible membership or stuck work. Missing/ambiguous eligibility has an explicit source-safe needs-assignment/recovery state; no guessed privileged fallback. This does not mandate a particular new Team/Group schema or provider queue.
4. **No claim or exclusive lock is selected.** A can allow an authorized member to perform source review without a compulsory separate Claim action. It does not choose claim expiry, exclusive reservation, workload balancing, presence, vacation substitution or automatic reassignment. Q21/Q23's exact current review-state and evidence race protections remain regardless of routing.
5. **Distinguish review actionability from collection issuance.** B proposes a named-person prerequisite before the review can be performed. Neither option silently decides whether an otherwise authorized request can be issued or receive files while responsibility needs assignment, nor an invitation/email policy. Any such source readiness/admission decision must remain explicit rather than smuggled into this fork.
6. **Do not expand the unit of ownership by implication.** This is ordinary review responsibility, not a universal single exclusive reviewer for every item, separate approval per physical file or control of downstream specialist decisions. Preserve exact source-defined item/requirement dependencies and legitimate existing responsibility boundaries. Do not add a per-item assignment designer through the wording.
7. **Source-owned handoff and currentness remain required.** Task UI cannot patch an assignee and claim the source changed. Current actor/destination/scope and expected source heads determine any qualified transition, with attributable history and safe continuation/needs-assignment semantics. A routing change does not rewrite earlier review actors, acceptance, guidance, fulfillment, recipient clocks or access.
8. **No inherited private roster disclosure.** Staff need only their current allowed work projection. Participants see source-approved next action/help, not internal team membership, rejected routes or sensitive staffing. A route name is not consent to expose a person's name/address, internal notes or earlier history.

## Dependencies and qualification limits

If A is selected, the ordinary source must qualify its permitted team route, current eligible human action, accountable recovery and exact source mutation/projection boundary. That is a narrow compatible owner adoption, not a requirement to build a generic queue engine or full Workflow Studio first. Existing source-controlled shared-task infrastructure is the reuse direction; no schema or provider is frozen by Q24.

If B is selected, the source must qualify the named-responsibility condition, lawful assignment/handoff and missing-person recovery before review becomes actionable. It must not invent new document disclosure merely because the name was selected, force a second substantive reviewer or treat one person's departure as erasing lawful prior decisions.

Under either choice, new outcome-specific permissions are still exact source mappings; there is no assumption that one acceptance capability grants administration of review destinations or that a destination administrator may perform substantive review. The creator-default rule, initiation with unresolved responsibility, named backup/leave policies, mandatory claim model, notices, bulk transfer, review SLA and numeric workload are separate questions or later owner qualification as appropriate, not selected here.
