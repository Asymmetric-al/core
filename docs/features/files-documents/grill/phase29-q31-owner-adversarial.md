## Recommendation and the main correction

Keep the selected product small: the authorized staff member shares one native reply and sees one optional, visibly selected email notice choice when eligible. The initial qualified tenant plan enables that optional step, and authorized tenant staff can disable it through the existing bounded plan surface. The notice is a safe cue to return, not an email copy of the clarification. Native text and accountable handling remain independently useful.

The main owner amendment is the exact effect of **Tenant Off**. General P17 plan changes are future-only. Q31 should explicitly add its own narrow current-stop rule: published Off stops this notice's affected work before its actual provider-submission admission, including delayed or unresolved original handoffs. An intervening Off must remain effective even if a worker next observes On. Preserve the recorded plan/content/choice and any admitted in-flight evidence; Off cannot recall email. Once Off wins, no later same-key follow-up or replacement provider call is authorized for that older admission. This is a proposed Q31 amendment requiring ratification, not automatic inheritance of Q26, Q27 or P24 reminder policy.

The second correction is that **the native act, its original email choice and current send permission are three different facts**. An old choice cannot be rewritten from today's plan. A selected notice that becomes blocked remains requested-but-blocked. A native reply already committed is never posted again to repair email.
