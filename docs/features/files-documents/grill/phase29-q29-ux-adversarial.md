## Strongest competing case

The strongest B is **A plus detected text additions/deletions and change navigation for an explicitly qualified finite text profile**. A changed date, amount, negation or sentence in a long, substantially stable document is exactly where manual paired reading can miss something that a good text comparison would surface. Adobe's navigation is a concrete precedent. B need not compare pixels, understand contracts, handle every previewable format or replace the source reader to be worthwhile.

A remains a defensible scope judgment because this increment can make the selected heterogeneous readers useful together without adding an extraction/alignment result that appears more complete than it is. Neither choice proves complete review, authenticity or no change. No observed Asym workflow volumes, timing or error-rate study establishes which saves more staff effort. Do not describe A as inherently more accurate, cheaper in measured operations or a permanent ban on later comparison. Existing source-specific comparison, including P23 Media current-versus-candidate inspection, remains required within its existing owner contract.

## Exact proposed amendments

These are incremental product contracts, not prescribed component names, a new workflow engine or a new schema. Final identifiers belong to the parent review.

### UX-A01 — Meaningful source-owned pair and orientation

> Enter paired reading from the existing source item/history/review context. Present the permitted exact versions and their source-established relationship; offer a source-defined useful default only when that relationship and both permissions are established. Do not pair by matching filename, upload order, equal bytes, contact membership or a generic cross-source file picker. Keep permitted exact-version identity and actual source status distinguishable in each view. Human-readable labels and relevant permitted time/attribution are sufficient in the main UI; technical identifiers may remain in details. Chronological earlier/later is not accepted/current, and left/right is not an authority label. Reordering or changing presentation cannot change those meanings. One readable version or a permission to see history metadata does not create a two-version reading right, nor justify exposing denied alternatives or counts.

Why required: two files named `proof.pdf`, an earlier unaccepted attempt or a historical accepted version create a plausible wrong-baseline comparison. A useful entry needs actual lineage, not another all-files selection ceremony. A historical accepted label, when lawful to display, must remain historical and purpose-specific rather than implying present applicability.

### UX-A02 — The reviewed pair and current source decision remain distinct

> Keep the opened exact pair stable while its content remains currently permitted. Separately retain the source's actual current review target and source-relevant requirement/evidence-set context; pane focus, a page switch or chronology never retargets acceptance. When a correction or relevant source change occurs, retain permitted reading position and independently authorized notes, show a persistent accessible notice, and offer a deliberate return/open-current action. Currentness or permission that cannot be established is not presented as confirmed current. Existing Q03/Q21 guards prevent stale acceptance; do not silently replace bytes, accept the historical pane, revive a predecessor or treat the selected pair as the whole evidence set. Comparison remains optional assistance, with no compulsory open/scroll/dwell/compare checkbox or extra approval stage.

Why required: a staff member can be reading two older versions while the source has a third candidate, or comparing one file while an item requires several. Showing two exact documents correctly still fails if the acceptance target is ambiguous.

### UX-A03 — Independent usable reading and reversible presentation

> Compose the existing qualified readers in one contextual surface. Use adjacent panes where useful; provide an equally usable single-column or explicit version-switching presentation at narrow widths and for users who prefer it. Keep version identity, active controls and coverage discoverable, preserve each exact version's own page/sheet/zoom/pan/find position while currently permitted, and restore the source return point and focus on close. Page/sheet numbering retains source meaning. Unequal pagination, changed sheet names or differing qualified formats do not establish an automatic correspondence. Independent navigation is always available. Any synchronized scrolling is optional, can be turned off and does not claim semantic alignment. Do not add synchronization merely to complete A.

Why required: matched scroll percentages or page numbers can place unrelated paragraphs alongside each other. Mobile toggling that resets to page one on every switch makes long reading impractical. Preserve state within authorized operation, not by inventing a private offline cache.

### UX-A04 — Coverage and find remain exact and version-specific

> Carry each input's actual reading profile and material coverage limits into the pair. A fully rendered pair does not certify that omitted revisions, comments, hidden content, media, formulas, signatures or image details were inspected or unchanged. Keep each Find operation explicitly bound to its exact version and complete declared represented scope, including not-yet-displayed content. Do not silently use browser find over two mounted documents as whole-document search. No matches requires completion of that version's declared search scope; incomplete retrieval is Search incomplete and image-only content remains nonsearchable. Rebinding a pane invalidates results from its former input. Page/sheet/cell/line references preserve source coordinates. A shared all-versions search, global content index or automatic cross-version result alignment is not required.

Why required: identical visible final-text renditions can hide different DOCX revision states, and XLSX visible saved values can omit a consequential hidden change. Find is useful manual assistance only if staff know which version and which represented content it searched.

### UX-A05 — Failure, unknown access and revocation have a safe readable outcome

> Treat preparation, limited coverage, unsupported content, expired/missing rendition, transient failure, current-access-unconfirmed and denied/revoked access as distinct internal causes with concise user-safe messages and only valid next actions. A transient data/network/renderer failure alone is not proof of revocation; already-delivered context may remain only while the qualified current disclosure conditions still hold. When current access cannot be established at a required recheck, withhold the affected protected output until it can; do not present a cached copy as an authorized offline fallback. On restriction/revocation, stop affected delivery and remove restricted displayed content and app-held derivatives, including hidden mounted panes, text/accessibility layers, thumbnails, find snippets/results and disallowed identity metadata. Restore focus to a safe status or return control without exposing the removed identity. Preserve another independently authorized readable version and source work where allowed; mark the pair incomplete rather than asserting a completed comparison. If the source no longer permits the paired use itself, stop the composed view; any standalone reading must have its own actual source scope, not an inferred fallback entitlement. Preserve notes only within their own current authority. No automatic original download, private/signed-provider URL, external comparator, retry loop or renewed retention is a repair. A subsequent allowed retry rechecks the exact intended version under existing limits.

Why required: merely hiding a pane leaves its text in the accessibility tree or search results. Conversely, one failed preview should not erase independently permitted work. This requires clearing state the product controls, not claiming remote erasure of information a person already saw, copied or deliberately downloaded. If permission is unknown, the UI must not invent a sensitive reason such as the existence of a hidden replacement or a restriction category.

### UX-A06 — Accessibility qualifies actual inspection

> Qualify the composed experience for keyboard, supported assistive technology, narrow/zoomed screens, touch and supported languages. Give each document region and its controls explicit version context, a logical reading/focus order and a discoverable route between the two; color or physical position is not sufficient. Keep applicable chrome usable at 320 CSS pixels and 200% text zoom, retaining legitimate per-document two-dimensional pan for fixed pages or wide values. Preserve available qualified text, reading order, table coordinates and language/direction semantics in the represented content. Disclose when a scan, missing text layer or inadequate reading semantics prevents sufficient inspection for a reviewer; accessible buttons alone cannot qualify the review path. Use only an existing currently authorized sufficient alternative or the existing source support/review route. No new OCR, translation, fabricated description or mandatory download follows.

Why required: two perfectly labeled canvas images still cannot support a blind reader's required text review. The alternative must cover the actual source-required content; swapping to an incomplete text rendition is not enough. Controls should use the established Base UI/base-maia system and known reader patterns; no bespoke component system or accessibility preference platform is required.

### UX-A07 — Calm bounded preparation, recovery and continuity

> Make preparing either input visible without blocking permitted source work or a ready independent pane. Use truthful per-input waiting/failure status, one coherent return path and accessible material state announcements; do not emit fake percentages, constant repeated announcements or completion mail. Reopening, switching layout or retrying follows the existing authorized generation/reuse and expiry rules, not new work on every UI change. Qualify the pair's total load and scheduling within the existing D32 constraints; do not assume two maximum-size files or two simultaneous conversions fit because one file does. Defer unnecessary work and provide a usable one-view-at-a-time path where appropriate. No new numeric performance promise is selected here.

Why required: waiting for the slower of two inputs before displaying anything makes slow-network use needlessly poor. A content-preserving toggle should not keep reprocessing the originals or extending custody. Bounded scheduling remains an implementation choice, not a mandated dual-rendering engine.

### UX-A08 — Scope and truthful product language

> Present this as reading versions together with no automatic identification of changes in this increment. Do not synthesize Changed/Unchanged, all-changes-reviewed or equivalence states from equal previews, matching page counts, elapsed time or viewing telemetry. The feature adds no edit/merge/accept-changes/Make Current action, comparison export, annotation suite, new notification, participant history entitlement or general drive. Existing source acceptance and specialist comparison remain intact. Preserve the candid manual missed-change cost and the legitimate later option of separately qualified text differences.

Why required: a button named Compare versions can be reasonable, but its result must not imply hidden machine analysis. A small contextual explanation is enough; no repeated warning modal or mandatory acknowledgment is necessary.
