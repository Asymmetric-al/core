## What the two priorities can and cannot settle

A closes the source/consumer/requirement/recovery register through exact evidence and makes remaining product conflicts explicit. It is complete when each required outcome has a lawful authoritative path and measurable acceptance, or a precise blocker for its affected capability; a blocker is not successful readiness. It does not require waiting for an entire future product, nor does it itself execute all release tests.

B tests comprehension and interaction in lightweight synthetic nonproduction material, including error/expiry/recovery and real supported accessibility needs. Actual human research is separately arranged and truthfully reported. It can expose a product decision for return to Conrad; it cannot close native authorization, custody or operational proof. No browser/client-only demo becomes a second source of truth.

Both priorities preserve the founder's mandatory private request→submission→exact review→authorized share journey and public asset→qualified placement/release→withdrawal journey. A pilot must evaluate actual appropriate intended users and task success/understanding/recovery; mock or agent-only tasks are labeled separately. A verified checklist entry requires its evidence, not a count of past questions.
