# Phase 29 Q21 candidate — source acceptance, not upload success

Research date: **21 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. The parent reports Q20 and its full amendments ratified; **Q01–Q20 are the accepted baseline**. After owner review, the parent chose the bounded Q21 framing below: ordinary native item acceptance versus an expressly added upfront collection-only mode. **No founder answer is selected here.** This note records the comparative evidence and the earlier contracts that constrain either option.

Only this research note was written. The founder brief, current feature vocabulary and exact Q03/Q09/Q10 decision boundaries were inspected, alongside current public primary product documentation. No canonical document, source rule, product, account, provider, permission or UI was changed. No private files, native acceptance command, competitor account, browser/AT or intended-user test was exercised.

## Main finding

**Do not ask a blank-slate “staff acceptance versus safe upload automatically completes it” question.** The founder's brief and ratified decisions already distinguish durable receipt, safety/preview readiness, review, source requirement satisfaction and overall normal fulfillment. A successful storage transfer or clean scan cannot manufacture accepted evidence or fulfillment.

The bounded product choice is **how the ordinary native requesting source decides that a requested item is satisfied**, while keeping those facts distinct: require explicit staff acceptance of the exact current requested item, or also add an expressly declared collection-only mode whose source purpose can be satisfied by qualified receipt evidence. The latter is a deliberate product/source-policy addition with owner amendments, not a shortcut from a scan result or a retroactive switch after material arrives.

My recommendation is **A: explicit staff acceptance of the exact current requested item/evidence set before ordinary item satisfaction**, with a compact action in the existing context. Preserve already-authorized non-file/manual/waived outcomes and specialized source satisfaction rules. Do not impose a universal new human gate on expense, official-document, application, public-media or other existing domains.

This single question does **not** choose an extra whole-request Complete button, automatic whole-request completion, reviewer assignment/pool, bulk acceptance or a rejection/change-outcome taxonomy. Item satisfaction and source fulfillment stay distinct. Prior investigation considered the overall-completion affordance as an alternative next topic; the parent has now deliberately kept it outside Q21.

B's earlier source satisfaction is a meaningful user tradeoff: Q03 authorizes ordinary self-correction only while the exact item remains pending and otherwise eligible. ## Keep the review experience simple

For the ordinary reviewed lane, the recipient sees truthful receipt and next actor: **Received**, **File checks in progress** when applicable, then **Awaiting review** where that is the real state. A timely submission waiting on staff is not overdue merely because the source is not yet fulfilled. Do not show a generic green Done badge that a reasonable recipient would read as accepted when it only means transferred.

Staff use the same item/request context with the exact current version, relevant instructions and qualified view/download action. A simple **Accept item** action can record the substantive source decision; any **Request changes** or terminal outcome must be the actual owner-approved command, with clear useful recipient guidance. This note does not invent a universal rejection/waiver/reopen power or require a new review queue.

An item can include several supporting files. The decision binds the actual relevant set and exact versions, not just the row currently visible. Preserve other members and accepted independent uses. A corrected candidate immediately fences its predecessor per Q03; neither a stale screen nor a delayed acceptance can silently target the new file. Opening a preview does not lock the item or constitute review completion. If the source permits inspection through an authorized original when preview is unsupported, do not invent a universal all-previews-ready gate.

After a real decision, show the actual item status and any remaining source work, preserving the user's reading position. A lost response reconciles that command rather than performing it twice. A failure to refresh another panel is not evidence that acceptance failed. If required authority/currentness is unknown, close the affected decision safely while preserving independently valid inspection and received-work evidence.

Avoid combining unrelated consequences into the accept button: no automatic public sharing, legal identity verification, payment, official-artifact issuance, broad file reuse or retention change follows. Q20's selected fresh-access email default does not select review-result or completion messages. Those use their exact existing source/P6/P17 contract when authorized.

The access policies matter only as inherited consequences of real source facts. Do not invent another clock choice here. Staff review can outlast recipient access without blaming the recipient or reviving access; actual qualified fulfillment determines the already-ratified completed-stage behavior. Review permissions, grant-administration permissions and body-reading permissions remain their own qualified actions.

## Proposed single founder-facing framing

**For ordinary native document requests, should each requested item require staff acceptance, or should Asym also offer an upfront collection-only mode?**

- **A — Require staff acceptance for ordinary items (recommended).** Received files wait for the qualified reviewer to accept the exact current item. Existing specialized and already-authorized non-file/waived source outcomes remain unchanged.
- **B — Also offer an upfront collection-only mode.** When the purpose is only to collect qualified material, the source can satisfy that collection item without human/content approval under its declared policy. The meaning must be clear before submission, and pending-only self-correction may end sooner.

Both retain exact source commands/evidence, Q03 correction lineage, current permission, immutable versions and truthful receipt/safety/review labels. Neither permits a clean scan to approve business evidence or chooses a whole-request Complete button. B is a substantive mode and owner amendment, not a hidden implementation optimization.

The final record must preserve specialized predicates and already-authorized non-file/manual/waived outcomes, without adding a waiver power. It must not silently choose bulk acceptance, reviewer assignment, outcome taxonomy, review-message defaults, staff SLAs, automatic reminders, a configurable workflow, file formats or a global policy editor. Exact currentness, races and command identity remain engineering obligations under their owners.

No founder answer or canonical policy is selected. This is bounded research and a recommendation, not a full adversarial review or native readiness claim.
