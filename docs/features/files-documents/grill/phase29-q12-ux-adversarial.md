# Phase 29 Q12 — clear and usable sixty-day unfinished access

Research date: **21 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. The parent reports Conrad selected Q12 A: up to sixty elapsed days from original authoritative issuance. Q01–Q11 are ratified. This is an adversarial UX/comparative research contribution for the selected Q12 answer and proposed amendments, **not a claim that the full amendment package is ratified or that native behavior is implemented or qualified**.

Only this research file was written. Public primary documentation and existing local Q11/Q12 research were inspected. No product account, provider state, recipient session, upload, download, email, implementation or canonical document was changed. No runtime, browser, assistive-technology or user test was performed.

## Verdict and strongest objection

**Retain A**, with the exact qualifications below. A finite 1,440-hour nominal unfinished period is coherent with the fixed issuance anchor and conditional completed stage. Its usability depends on the real remaining opportunity, trustworthy state, current authorization and an honest source-owned help path.

The strongest objection remains B, thirty elapsed days: it permits unused or unfinished authority to remain potentially available for less time. Sixty offers more time for delivery, recipient work, permitted correction and staff review, but this research contains no Asym completion-time distribution, privacy incident rate or evidence that ordinary cohorts need the extra thirty days. It would be wrong to call B unusable or hostile, just as it would be wrong to call A a proven modern standard.

There is no quantified relation here between twice the nominal interval and twice the risk. Current scope and permission checks still matter at both durations. A must be qualified for its actual ordinary cohorts rather than justified by an assumed future extension, copied vendor number or pressure to mark work complete early.

The central UX rule is:

> Explain the actual source result and actual remaining opportunity in the existing request context. Do not make the recipient manage the underlying clock and permission state machine.

## What the chosen period means

The nominal original unfinished end is the qualified original issuance instant plus **1,440 elapsed hours**. The admitted end respects genuinely applicable earlier limits and its recorded provenance; current restrictions may constrain access or individual actions sooner. It is not two calendar months, sixty business days, a new period on first entry or a requested-by target plus a hidden buffer.

The period includes time before first qualified entry, permitted recipient work and the source's review. It does not pause for an offline browser, slow upload, proof failure, mail delay, staff vacation or a correction request. This describes the selected policy, not a recommendation to ignore those operational problems.

Q10 remains separate: eligible normal fulfillment while the applicable unfinished authority is valid may admit Q07's bounded completed view. Receipt, a safe file, staff opening the page and requested-by timing are not fulfillment. Q08 is not an automatic fallback after unfinished expiry.

The selected number does not create a sender date picker, organization policy editor, per-request override, grace period, reminder cadence, automatic notification, expiry extension, recipient substitution or fresh grant.

## Journey 1 — staff prepares, reviews and issues once

Staff should work in the existing source request context. A draft does not start the period. When issuance is appropriate, the staff member sees the approved ordinary timing rule, exact intended recipient and purpose, and known earlier limits relevant to the action.

Before the source issuance instant exists, show the rule and a clearly qualified estimate if useful. Do not present a precise preview timestamp as already committed. Ordinary time advancing under unchanged reviewed terms should not repeatedly demand confirmation.

If a requested-by target falls after the actual controlling end, show the concrete mismatch to authorized staff. For example:

> The requested-by date is later than the access end. This request does not allow submission after [actual end].

That is explanatory text, not an automatic universal refusal based solely on a nonauthorizing target. Source policy must resolve the actual compatibility and readiness of the intended task. Q12 cannot silently move either date, create an override or permit a staff member to choose another lifetime. Do not issue a known unusable task and claim successful setup simply because the formula calculated.

The normal deliberate issuance action remains one action. A number selected in planning should not become a second consent checkbox, “start sixty days” button or extra explanatory wizard.

After the source commits, the same context shows the actual result and actual end. Invitation delivery, if separately authorized, remains an independent outcome. **Access issued** is not **Email delivered**, **Recipient read**, **Recipient entered** or **Request completed**.

| Proved staff outcome                                                | Example copy                                                                      | Appropriate next step                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Original issuance committed; communication has not been established | **Access issued. Unfinished access ends [actual end].**                           | Show only the real communication state if available.                                                               |
| Issuance committed; sending the invitation failed                   | **Access was issued, but the invitation could not be sent.**                      | Supported delivery repair/retry under current scope; the existing end remains.                                     |
| Issuance result is genuinely unknown                                | **We couldn’t confirm whether access was issued.**                                | Reconcile the same intent. Do not offer “create another” as a timeout fix.                                         |
| Source proves issuance did not occur                                | **Access was not issued.**                                                        | Explain the actual actionable cause without exposing private policy details; a new authorized attempt follows Q11. |
| A material term or authority changes before commit                  | Explain that the action needs the current source review or is no longer permitted | Do not hide a substantive change behind an old sixty-day preview.                                                  |

Confirmation must remain findable in the source context after navigation, not only a disappearing toast. If an observer is temporarily unavailable, say what cannot be confirmed; do not erase a previously known source fact or invent a newer end.

## Journey 2 — recipient arrives promptly, late or on another device

Before Q01 qualification, use the privacy-safe entry flow. Do not reveal a private request's existence, recipient, file names, due date, expiry reason or completed status merely because a link or ID was supplied.

After qualification, show the actual permitted current view. The key timing text remains:

> Access while this request is unfinished ends [localized date, time and zone].

Where useful, a secondary relative phrase can supplement that exact end. It must not replace it, become stale authority or imply an extra minute/day through rounding. **“You have sixty days” is wrong on a late first visit.** Even at issuance it is misleading without regard to genuine earlier constraints and conditional action eligibility.

The selected nominal interval should not dominate the recipient page. The user needs to know what material is requested, what has been received, what they can do now and the actual relevant end. The sixty-day rule is useful for source policy and explanation; the resolved date is the useful everyday fact.

Show **Requested by [target]** separately when such a target exists. Do not fabricate a target when absent. A passed target does not itself disable an otherwise-authorized action. A target later than access end does not promise access until that target.

At the same place, preserve Q10's qualified explanation:

> If the organization completes it before then, we’ll show any available read-only access and its end here.

This does not guarantee thirty completed days or any completed access if true independent conditions prevent it. Do not use a long caveat panel before every action; contextual current status and one concise explanation should carry the ordinary journey.

Returning from another device or with refreshed valid proof should show the same original source end, subject to current restrictions and any valid completed transition. A new session is not a fresh sixty-day opportunity.

## Journey 3 — submit, recover uncertainty and await review

The recipient chooses the exact required file/member and sees the normal Q02–Q04 context. Q12 adds no file-type permission, new preview power, document editor, original-history access or general-drive behavior.

Distinguish these states:

| State                                          | Truthful meaning and copy direction                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| File selected locally                          | **Ready to send**; the source has not received it. Do not start or extend access.                                                                             |
| Transfer in progress                           | **Uploading** with truthful progress. A full progress bar is not a receipt or reservation before expiry.                                                      |
| Source admission outcome unknown               | **We couldn’t confirm whether the file was received. Check its status.** Use “Checking…” only while actually performing reconciliation.                       |
| Exact immutable submission durably admitted    | **File received [source receipt time].** Checking may still be pending; no acceptance or completed-view promise.                                              |
| File admitted but later found unusable         | **File received, but it cannot be used [safe reason/action].** Preserve receipt truth; current corrective ability depends on actual authority and item state. |
| Recipient work received; source review remains | **Received — awaiting review** or the actual source equivalent. Do not label the recipient overdue merely because the organization has not completed review.  |

If only a preview or individual file check fails, preserve independently authorized valid request status, other current members and supported actions. Conversely, if current authority itself cannot be established, protected use fails closed; a last-known cached screen is not permission to continue. Keep partial outages and authority failure distinct.

Submission near expiry is not automatically invalid merely because there may be little review time. Q09's target does not become a hidden minimum lead time. At admission the source must satisfy actual action authority; the product must not invent an unpublished “too close to expiry” cutoff. It should also not promise completion or a full review interval that has not been committed.

If review finishes after source access ends, authorized staff work and source evidence may persist. That does not establish ordinary recipient completed admission from expired authority. The recipient should not be told their on-time file vanished or was late merely because review consumed the remaining period.

## Journey 4 — correction near the end

A longer initial interval does not give every correction another sixty days. Show the exact item being corrected, current end and currently available action. Preserve Q03: source admission of the immutable successor under current authority immediately fences the predecessor, even while checks or preview are pending; unrelated supporting files stay unchanged.

The date governing the new correction obligation follows its actual source-declared relationship under Q09. Do not inherit an unrelated old overdue label or rewrite the original receipt. Conversely, a new requested-by target does not extend U.

Do not add a generic “Are you sure? Time is running out” dialog, required explanation, second approver or automatic reason form. The existing exact target/file review and deliberate correction action are the right place to make the effect clear.

| Boundary case                                                  | Required outcome                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| User selects another file, then cancels before any admission   | The old submission remains as it was. Local selection is not a correction.                                                              |
| Transfer fails before admission                                | Preserve the old source state; do not show a received successor or fence solely from browser optimism.                                  |
| Admission succeeds before the applicable end; response is lost | Recover the same successor and receipt. Do not upload again, shift receipt time or restore the predecessor.                             |
| Checks later fail                                              | Truthful received-but-unusable state; no automatic predecessor revival. Another correction requires its own current eligibility.        |
| Admission occurs at or after the exclusive end                 | No new admission merely because upload began earlier. Preserve earlier durable evidence and use the appropriate source-owned help path. |
| User cancels while the outcome is unknown                      | An aborted request or closed tab is not proof the source cancelled. Reconcile; do not promise removal or old-file restoration.          |
| Reviewer acts on the old candidate                             | Current source revision/fence wins. The UI must explain the changed item instead of accepting stale review.                             |

Near-expiry staff requests deserve operational scrutiny: the source must not knowingly promise a correction opportunity that current authority cannot provide. That is not an invented minimum number of days or permission to autoextend. Where no approved recovery exists, describe the limitation honestly and address it through source governance before claiming the cohort works.

## Journey 5 — time passes during source review

The sixty-day limit is not a reviewer service-level agreement. Do not promise that staff will finish before it. Equally, do not make recipient-facing expiry the only way the organization discovers its review responsibility.

Within existing authorized request/review context, make the true next actor and actual end available. Useful staff copy:

> Submission received. Review is still required. Recipient unfinished access ends [actual end].

This is contextual operational information, not a new dashboard, notification series, escalation ladder or threshold policy. It should not suggest that staff can preserve access by marking a task complete without the source's actual fulfillment conditions.

Where the source already allows completion without an online recipient, the person need not log in or download merely to admit a valid completed stage. Normal completion still needs its exact historical eligibility and current source contract. Displayed activity, opening a file or staff access to the record is not enough.

The operational-fit question is not simply whether sixty days feels generous. Test the intended source journey with its real entry route, true earlier caps and actual review responsibilities. If those make the available period ineffective, the release claim fails for that cohort; do not cover the failure with support promises.

## Journey 6 — source completion and return

After eligible timely fulfillment, show the actual completed view and its actual Q07 end. The old unfinished-only end must not block a valid transition. This is a state change in the existing purpose-bound task, not a new portal enrollment.

If completion is recorded late by a consumer but its qualified source occurrence and eligibility were earlier, recover the original outcome. Observation time never creates a new completed period. If that original completed end is already past, do not reopen access merely because the projection was repaired.

If a return occurs near or after the displayed old U and current transition status is unknown, do not assert “access expired” solely from that old unfinished projection. Resolve the current source stage; protect data while required authority is unproved. If independently qualified read authority exists, do not suppress it because a nonauthorizing history or preview call failed.

After a source outcome changes or further work is requested, do not automatically restart sixty days or reopen writes. Apply the actual existing owner contract; new intake/recovery capability remains separate. A completed page can report current permitted outcome truth without pretending the task can never change.

| Actual unfinished authority ended and no eligible completed transition applies | Proved ended-access state only where disclosure is permitted; otherwise neutral entry/help                      | Automatic reauthorization, arbitrary new original grant, or an offer to email sensitive files |
| Recipient proof remains valid but source access was revoked/shortened          | The source restriction wins; remove protected actions and details as required                                   | “You still have sixty days” because the cookie or old page is valid                           |

Help should identify the real supported contact route or source process without requiring private tokens, raw files, passwords, a new universal account or an essay. Do not promise “ask us to extend” unless that exact capability is separately approved. Q08 cannot be offered as a remedy where its prior completed-admission predicate never held.

On shared devices, a long business interval must not imply a long session or reveal private request content to the next user. Use current identity/scope checks and existing session controls. Source expiry does not recall already downloaded copies, erase browser history in the real world or delete retained source records; avoid claiming such outcomes.

## Clock presentation and anti-urgency rules

The ordinary period is exact elapsed time. Source computation, timestamp precision and exclusive comparison belong to the qualified source contract. Display/localization must preserve that actual instant, including across daylight-saving transitions, travel, hydration and client clock skew.

Recommended presentation:

- Actual localized end, explicit date, time and timezone in normal page content.
- Separate requested-by label when present.
- Current item/status and next actor.
- Concise current action or safe help where needed.

Use the end label as an exclusive cutoff: access ends at that instant. Avoid “through [date]” if it would imply the remainder of the day, or “until 5:00” if the displayed precision hides a materially different instant. Enough precision must be available to state the real end without forcing every view to become a technical timestamp dump. Any prospective source precision normalization must occur before admission under the source contract; formatting may not round permission later.

A relative “time remaining” display is optional, not chosen by Q12. It must not become the enforcement mechanism, inaccurately promise a whole extra day or announce every tick. Do not add artificial scarcity, red warning walls, guilt about delayed arrival or claims that the person is “losing their documents.” An actual end deserves clarity, not pressure to submit unsafe/incomplete material or falsely complete review.

No fixed “seven days left,” “twenty-four hours left” or other notification/warning trigger is selected by this research. If a future proposed warning is useful, it still needs its own owner, accessibility behavior and truthful action.
