## Recommendation on question scope

Prefer **one complete finite fresh-unfinished timing-profile decision** now, rather than separate questions for model, duration, units and unused-admission bound. Q13's reservation of separate founder decisions means these matters were not selected by Q13; it does not require four separate turns for parts of one useful temporal profile.

The profile must visibly select its anchor, elapsed quantity and treatment of never-entered access. A model-only question would be justified if the first-entry behavior itself requires deeper product discovery and no responsible numerical proposals can be offered. The owner documents do not establish that need: numbers can be proposed honestly as product judgments, just as Q12's 60-day ordinary profile was. Repetition/overlap remains a separate consequential decision and must not be buried in the timing profile.

Recommended question:

> For a staff-approved fresh opportunity to finish the same request, which timing profile should apply: a fixed period from fresh source issuance, or a finite entry window followed by a work period from qualified entry?

Offer complete concrete profiles so the answer settles actual behavior, not just a slogan. The wording below is a recommendation to the parent, not a selected founder answer.

All numbers in this table are **proposals**, not borrowed owner requirements. The root's chosen next-question structure uses these three complete alternatives; that is a question-design decision, not the founder selecting any option. A free-form founder answer can choose a different profile.

## Why Q11 does not already choose A

Q11 defines original Document Request Access Issuance and the original finite end. Q13 creates a separate fresh admission while preserving the ended original. Q13 expressly reserved its new anchor/activation/duration policy. Reusing the simpler source-occurrence model is a reasoned recommendation, not permission to apply Q11 or Q12 automatically.

For A or B, the anchor must be the qualified fresh **source authority** effect that makes the new access available under approved terms, not opening a staff dialog, clicking before commitment, preparing a message, provider acceptance, delivery, proof renewal or a recipient visit. If an existing source operation combines review/approval and actual issuance, its qualified causal effect can supply the occurrence; an uncommitted click cannot.

The currently unselected “approval now, source activation at a later event” interpretation cannot be smuggled into A. The fixed-at-issuance profile should say when that source authority becomes effective and what finite actual end is established, subject to recipient proof and current use. Committed authority is still distinct from usable proof, working entry route, delivery and content readiness.

## C needs an explicit pre-entry authority rule

To be a complete profile, C must state what exists before first qualified entry. The recommended coherent C is a **source-issued conditional authority with a finite pre-entry end**, not a merely recorded approval with no source eligibility at all until the recipient arrives.

For explanation only:

- Let `G` be the qualified fresh source issuance occurrence and `L = G + 168h` its exclusive ordinary latest-entry boundary.
- Before entry, `L` supplies the finite applicable fresh unfinished boundary, with actual source predicates and independent caps still required.
- First qualified entry `T < L` resolves one nominal unfinished work end `T + 720h`, with applicable genuine narrower restrictions. The original policy states this conditional consequence prospectively; it is not a staff extension after expiry.
- No entry at or after `L` can activate an unentered expired opportunity. Retries recover a proved timely entry rather than starting a new interval.
- `L` governs the not-yet-entered unfinished state. It is not an undisclosed overall cap on a valid work period or completed view.

**Completion before entry:** Q13 permits eligible source fulfillment without an online browser. Under this coherent C, actual valid fresh authority before `L` can support the selected Q13 completion consequence when all source predicates hold. Once the source is complete, a later qualified visit enters the completed read-only view and cannot start a 30-day unfinished clock. The pre-entry unfinished end does not then cap a valid completed view through its obsolete stage role; true independent caps still apply.

If instead a proposed C would create only approval, with no source authority until first entry, then source completion in that interval cannot acquire Q13 eligibility. That is a substantively different profile and must be stated openly, not left as an implementation interpretation of “activation.” This is the largest hidden decision to avoid in a complete-profile question.

The first-entry event must complete Q01's exact current proof and source admission requirements. It is not a link scanner, GET/HEAD, automatic prefetch, background polling, account callback, failed proof, old verified-email flag, staff preview or first upload. Compatible proof can be reused only where qualified. An existing deliberate source/entry effect may provide the boundary; no extra timer-start button or account enrollment is selected. Concurrency, response loss and completion racing entry require one qualified source order.

## Boundaries common to every proposed profile

1. **Original admission stays ended.** No editing its expiry, restarting its 60 days, backfilling a gap or binding old unadmitted operations to a new grant. Each fresh source effect has its own approved intent and preserved timing outputs.
2. **The fresh bounds are unfinished-stage bounds.** Q13's actual eligible completion closes writes and resolves the declared completed view at no more than fulfillment plus 720 hours. It can yield an earlier or later final read date. Do not take a maximum with the unfinished end or impose an obsolete unfinished-only limit as an overall cap.
3. **Keep actual limits scoped.** Genuine whole-stage/overall/purpose restrictions can shorten or deny access. Upload-only, per-file, proof/session or requested-by limits do not automatically become whole-stage dates. Current denial does not reset history; removed genuine adverse causes do not restore a longer ended authority.
4. **No guaranteed usable interval.** A late delivery consumes A/B time. C can be shortened by genuine independent restrictions; first-entry time is not a promise to bypass them. Current file readiness and recipient proof can independently make an action unavailable.
5. **Current source facts control completion.** An already-completed source, actual gap completion or completion after valid authority ended gets no retrospective grant. Lawful atomic effects and delayed reconstruction remain qualified by their original source order, not observer time.
6. **Q08 stays separate.** Its own timing/repetition and any explicit eligibility expansion for a continuation-created completed view remain unselected. Neither a matching number nor later completion imports Q08.
7. **No repeat policy hidden in one interval.** Choosing 14/30 days or an entry window says nothing about allowed repeat count, overlap, cumulative exposure, automatic approval or lifetime maximum. It also does not choose one-active-grant cardinality. Those must be separately adopted before activation.
8. **No new staff picker or deadline-edit power.** The founder selects the ordinary fresh profile; staff later reviews current source-approved terms. Genuine different-source policy remains subject to its owner, not per-request convenience.
9. **Proof and delivery are not clocks by accident.** Credential replacement, current proof reuse, failed email, technical retries, source review and a paused workflow cannot start/reset a business period except the expressly selected first-entry effect if C wins.
10. **Retention remains independent.** None of these intervals is a file deletion, hold, cache, signed-URL, cookie or worker-lease lifetime, and no old copy can be recalled by expiry.
