| Option                                                              | Practical experience                                                                                                                                                                                     | Benefit and cost                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Staff email initially On, with tenant control (recommended)** | Each genuinely admitted new participant clarification may produce its qualified minimal staff email through the existing tenant-controlled optional step. Authorized tenant staff can turn the step Off. | Helps staff who are away from Core discover a person waiting for help. Short follow-ups and acknowledgments may create unnecessary email, and even a neutral notice requires source disclosure authority. |
| **B — Staff email initially Off, with tenant opt-in**               | The same notice capability and native work exist, but the tenant deliberately enables supplementary email.                                                                                               | Avoids assuming that every team wants per-message mail and reduces startup noise. Staff may miss an incoming question if they do not monitor current source work or discover the setting.                 |
| **C — Native clarification work only**                              | Add no staff clarification-email capability in this phase. The existing qualified source/request/My Work route carries handling, with its actual capable recovery/help.                                  | Avoids this extra delivery purpose. All three preserve Q30's real native message receipt, current authorized shared context and accountable handling. A/B differ in the tenant step's initial enablement, not in whether email is required, who owns the work, or whether a participant chooses staff addresses. Do not add a per-message participant checkbox, staff subscription directory, arbitrary audience control, routine personal claim or new bell event. These are product alternatives, not three tenant modes to implement.

Illustration: Nora asks which year a certificate must cover before she can upload it. Elena is the person currently qualified through the source route to handle that clarification but is away from Core. A provides an additional permitted cue; B lets the tenant choose it deliberately; C relies on current-work handling. Neither a Received acknowledgment nor email submission proves Elena read the question or promises an answer time.

I initially tested the superficially consistent Q27-style Off choice, but **Q27 does not create a general staff-Off policy**. A new human clarification is a different event from evidence first becoming reviewable, and Q30 does not establish that staff check Core daily. On is the better bounded starting recommendation for this specific waiting-person scenario, with strong B for active/high-volume teams. This is a product judgment, not measured urgency, response-rate evidence, a mandate to reply to every courtesy message or a universal best practice. The independent comparative pass reports direct message-notice On examples; those examples may support the judgment but cannot supply Core authority or prove the default's benefit.

## Closure and expiry differ from Q31

Q30-C07 explicitly preserves already-admitted text and lawful staff handling after participant access ends or the request closes. Therefore **participant expiry or request fulfillment is not by itself a universal Q32 staff-email stop**. The participant's ability to send/read and the staff member's current authorized follow-through differ. A queued staff notice may remain useful when its exact staff source-purpose/action remains permitted. It must not promise that the participant can still read or reply, reopen access, demand a reply, or create a completion hold.

Likewise, Q32 should not invent read/unread, replied/unreplied or Resolve as a new source truth solely to manage email. Current source applicability, restriction, routing eligibility and the qualified purpose decide what remains useful. A minimal factual received-message cue need not falsely state that an unanswered task is still actionable. No automatic online/read suppression or digest should be smuggled into the initial-default choice.
