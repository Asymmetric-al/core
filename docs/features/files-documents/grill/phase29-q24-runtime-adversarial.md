## Required technical amendments

R01–R10 below are research traceability for the consolidated Q24 clauses, not new published requirement IDs or an imposed SQL layout.

**R01 — Responsibility and actor remain distinct.** Ordinary source responsibility may resolve to one eligible individual or an explicitly qualified team route. Team-owned work does not require personal claiming as a universal prerequisite. Every actual Q21/Q23 decision is made and attributed to its real currently authorized human. Do not impersonate the assignee, use a team service account as reviewer or infer a second-person requirement. Preserve specialist restrictions.

**R02 — Check administration, destination and execution separately.** Any supported establishment/change/repair of responsibility requires the exact source-authorized administrator or system binding, exact current scope and the proposed destination's eligibility. Taking a review decision independently requires current operation authority and all applicable source facts. Reading, task editing, grant administration, assignment and review are not interchangeable permissions. A target being eligible does not authorize the caller to assign it; a caller being authorized does not make every selected target eligible. Assignment grants no new access. Where the source permits a coordinator without evidence-reading rights, show only its approved administrative projection rather than manufacturing evidence access. No new global permission atom or administrative UI is presumed necessary.

**R03 — Real eligible route, honest unresolved state.** Resolve current exact tenant/source/purpose and applicable person/team/assignment identity, current status and source constraints. No display-name, bare email, ambiguous first row, copied finance role list or fallback tenant. A team label/queue record does not prove a live path to an authorized human. Zero eligible or indeterminate resolution cannot be called ready or completed; preserve an explicit source assignment/eligibility problem, visible only as permitted, with an accountable route capable of resolving it. Do not require two available people or choose a timer-based staffing policy. Q24 does not introduce a new collection/intake gate or alter access clocks.

**R04 — Source-current changes, not blanket exclusivity.** Reassignment, team/membership change, suspension, expiry, clearance change and source closure must affect future actions according to the exact source/P12 rules. Recheck relevant facts after waits and order their source effects coherently with Q03/Q21/Q23/Q22. Loss of a required source authority path denies uncommitted action even if a task/claim remains. A responsibility change alone neither grants nor revokes independent access; if the source permits an independently authorized actor to help, do not infer exclusion from the named owner or team label. Prior lawful decisions keep original attribution/time unless an actual owner rule explicitly invalidates current applicability. No automatic rereview, acceptance reset, reopening, grace period or new fulfillment occurrence.

**R05 — Qualified inheritance and accountable repair.** Reuse a source default/responsibility binding with its actual currentness and inheritance semantics. Record the resolved source identity/basis needed to explain and safely change it. Do not silently reinterpret existing pending work from changed display names, new defaults or a different team; conversely do not impose a new universal snapshot policy where the source already defines continuing resolution. Supported reassignment/repair records old/new responsible destination, actor/reason and affected pending work as the owner requires. A recreated person/team/assignment does not inherit a removed identity. No generic availability, bulk transfer or routing editor follows from A.

**R06 — Stable shared work and durable effects.** Reuse the exact source work identity and authoritative receipt; do not create per-team-member tasks or use transport invocation time as ownership. Establishing/changing responsibility and required audit/history/handoff must be coherent, atomic where co-located; no provider work under source locks. Recover the same committed intent before reevaluating it as a stale new mutation, with current result disclosure. Changed intent conflicts, and genuine later responsibility work gets a fresh authorized occurrence rather than suppressing it forever. Concurrent initializers and distinct keys cannot produce competing current owners or duplicate shared tasks. Unknown commit is reconciled; do not dismiss possibly authoritative work or infer absence from an error/empty parse.

**R07 — Non-regressing projections and actual pending work.** P9/shared tasks and any separately authorized communications project current source responsibility and outcome. Late, reordered, repeated or restored events cannot reinstate old ownership, close a newer work generation, reopen completed review or change original decision timestamps. Source-independent UI failure does not erase known source success. Unknown or denied eligible work is not an empty inbox; task preferences, read state or notification settings cannot suppress the business obligation. Q22 keeps its full source predicate: a missing responsible label cannot become an additional completion gate after all actual required conditions are satisfied, and unresolved actual review cannot be hidden as complete.

**R08 — Purpose-safe visibility and every access path.** Current source permission governs workload lists, counts, ordering, pagination, search, assignment pickers, staff detail, history, task snippets and error/replay outputs before disclosure. Item eligibility does not imply access to every file/member/internal reason. Participant contact/help is a separate approved projection, not automatic reviewer-name or team-roster disclosure. Preserve exact Q01–Q05 metadata/byte serving/currentness controls; team assignment does not create public/share/provider links. Discard stale client responses across tenant/actor/source/purpose changes; retain drafts and reading context only while current disclosure permits. No automatic review/assignment email follows from Q20 or A.

**R09 — Enforced relationships and privileged paths.** Require trusted immutable source/tenant/identity links and protected current-state transitions, not only nullable FK existence or same-tenant child checks. Validate permitted updates on both old and resulting rows, including trigger transforms, changed queue/assignee/purpose, nulling, parent changes and deletion. Qualify exact browser/Data API/service/RPC/definer/worker/import paths and real roles; caller-supplied actor, owner, eligibility, approval or source timestamps cannot become authority. Follow P12's existing exact Tenant and epoch model rather than building a task-local grant engine. Current application helper/role maps and legacy tenant helpers do not by themselves qualify that model.

**R10 — Bounded operation, rollout and restore.** Use scoped indexed current-source/destination/operation lookups and bounded authorized history/recovery, not one all-tenant/full-roster scan per action or permission-change fan-out on the safety-critical path. Declare and measure supported workload/recovery budgets before activation; no invented staff SLA or numeric cap is needed to select A. All participating writer/consumer versions must preserve identities and newer restrictions; stale imports/generic queue/status fields cannot backfill qualified native responsibility. Restore source outcomes/authority/currentness and reconcile outward work before resuming; roll forward or stop unsafe actions without erasing valid review. Empty/archived/unqualified destinations remain unresolved, never automatically assigned to a recreated label. Owner recovery must be real, not a task nobody can read or act on.

## SQL and timing precision

Evaluate effective grants and RLS, not policy keyword presence. For UPDATE/ALL an omitted explicit WITH CHECK can inherit USING; permissive policies combine with OR and restrictive policies with AND, and applicable SELECT/RETURNING requirements also matter. BEFORE triggers can transform the proposed new row before its policy check. None of those observations says the inspected finance/task tables currently expose a native browser mutation. [PostgreSQL CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html).

SECURITY DEFINER uses the function owner's privileges; bypass depends on that role, table ownership/FORCE and BYPASSRLS/superuser status. FORCE can subject an ordinary table owner, but does not override a superuser/BYPASSRLS role. The membership RPC's explicit auth.uid()/tenant predicates and revoked PUBLIC execute are real restrictions; its definer marker alone is not a vulnerability. The service client uses independent credentials at construction, so source checks are essential and deployed role behavior still needs actual proof. [PostgreSQL RLS](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html), [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security).

Row locks protect retrieved rows, not a nonexistent initial responsibility/task row or every membership concurrently inserted/removed elsewhere. Read Committed target-row rechecks do not automatically create coherent multi-row target eligibility and source authority. Use the source's proven head/revision/transaction/constraint boundary with bounded retry; distinct transport keys cannot protect a shared source invariant by themselves. [PostgreSQL locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/explicit-locking.html), [transaction isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html).

The already ratified source-time rules apply unchanged: qualify live authority after relevant waits, preserve a committed source occurrence rather than client/action-start/task-projection time, and never reset access or backdate review through a responsibility repair. No universal SQL function or physical-commit timestamp implementation is prescribed here. The official pages above were fetched 2026-10-22; they are versioned semantics references, not a claim that the hosted database runs Postgres 17 or was queried. No new SQL/Auth implementation was performed.
