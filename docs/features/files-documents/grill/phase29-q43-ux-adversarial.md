# Phase 29 Q43 — Shared ordinary file views: independent UX adversarial review

2026-10-25, Asia/Bangkok. Conrad selected **A: qualified ordinary staff authors may publish their own eligible shared views without a separate view-manager designation**. Selection is not final ratification of these amendments. Q01–Q42 and the 26 existing terms remain governing. This note proposes the smallest complete lifecycle for the first ordinary Private Document Request evidence-entry cohort. It changes no canonical documents, code, accounts, providers, messages or live permissions.

## Recommendation and strongest alternative

Retain A, with an explicit, visible change from a private view to a shared view on the **same stable saved-definition identity**. One deliberate Share action commits the exact reviewed definition and finite permitted audience. Exploring filters remains local; saving a shared change is deliberate. A personal copy is an explicit new private definition, not a compulsory artifact of every publication.

Mandatory copying on Share is not needed for safety. It creates two similarly named definitions and uncertainty about which one colleagues use. Same-identity sharing is simpler if the interface clearly shows Private/Shared, the actual current audience and maintainer, and the consequence of Save. This is product judgment supported by comparable direct-sharing patterns, not measured Asym research. A person may explicitly make a personal copy when independent evolution is useful.

**B remains a sound operating alternative.** A designated publishing cohort can curate naming, remove overlapping definitions and make shared conventions more consistent. It is particularly credible when many people depend on a small standard library. Its cost is a real handoff for an ordinary author's useful view and dependence on that cohort's capacity. A does not eliminate curation or privacy checks; B does not make sensitive predicates safe or make each reader's results equal. No source establishes Asym's workload, staff availability, savings, adoption or incident rate.

The bounded audience proposed here is a finite selection of currently eligible staff **Active Tenant Assignments** in the same Tenant, environment and ordinary-source scope, within the author's actual sharing ceiling. It is not an Everyone setting, guessed team, arbitrary email, Party relationship or a new group directory. Shared readers initially receive use of the definition, not collaborative maintenance authority. Existing source/P12 administration supplies continuity. This is an explicit adoption proposal, not an already implemented general sharing facility.

## Exact proposed amendments

### U01 — Keep the selected power and source scope precise

Add eligible own-view publication to normally granted ordinary-source staff view-authoring scope, without a separate view-manager designation. Mere file Read, staff rank, review responsibility or a Party association is insufficient. The source owns the view definition; immutable creator attribution is distinct from current maintainer. First scope is Tenant × environment × ordinary request evidence-entry discovery, not a global cross-product file catalog, participant portal or Q41 share. No source action, evidence acceptance, filing, document version, retention, grant or communication changes because a view is published or used.

### U02 — One private definition becomes deliberately shared

A new personal view starts private. Share reviews the exact current saved definition and chosen audience, then changes sharing on that stable identity. If there are unsaved local changes, make clear which definition is being shared; a deliberate combined Save and share of those reviewed changes is acceptable, without a mandatory second save or approval ritual. Never silently share an older saved basis or unintended local draft. Preparation/checkbox changes do not publish. Share does not automatically create a duplicate private original, pin a colleague's tab, become their default, send email or create a task.

### U03 — Select a real, finite permitted audience

Select current eligible staff Active Tenant Assignments in the exact scope and within the publisher's current permitted disclosure ceiling. Show enough authorized identity to distinguish people; do not enumerate protected staff or copy arbitrary email/profile IDs. This selection gives definition-read/use only, not shared edit or file rights. No automatic future-member inclusion, Everyone, inferred team or cross-Tenant matching. A replacement assignment or email match is not the same selected assignment. Validate the whole submitted audience and exact definition coherently; a stale/ineligible member must not be silently omitted or substituted while reporting the reviewed audience as shared. Preserve safe preparation for a deliberate correction. Actual permission-policy expansion, protected grants, self-benefit or separation-of-duties rules retain their existing P12/P3 gates; ordinary publication inside an already qualified envelope gets no new universal approver.

### U04 — Treat the definition itself as protected information

Titles, descriptions, selected fields, literal filter values, folder/label references and audience identities can disclose private facts even with zero result rows. Qualify their disclosure to the intended audience before publication and at current read/use. Sharing must not expose hidden references, counts or restricted predicates through the selector, chips, URL, error, copied definition or audit screen. Do not silently strip an unauthorized predicate to broaden the query. A genuinely absent authorized value is different from an unavailable definition, denied field or unknown authority. Any supported viewer-relative operand must have its actual schema-defined meaning for the current viewer; do not freeze a creator's person identity or invent a new relative-filter language.

### U05 — Evaluate current live results for the current viewer

A shared view is a reusable query, never a snapshot, membership list, reviewer assignment or copy of the publisher's results. Execute source-authorized fields, search, filtering, sorting, counts, facets and pagination under each viewer's current scope. Equal view definitions can yield different results and counts. Scope labels remain visible: ordinary request files, not all products or every file in the organization. An unavailable or partly unqualified result is not a successful complete zero. Opening files, changing their source state and exporting remain separately authorized; no new export is selected. A saved-definition link needs current definition permission; a qualified ad-hoc query-context link does not require access to its author's private saved-view record. Neither link publishes a definition or grants result access.

### U06 — Local exploration, deliberate shared maintenance, explicit personal copies

Changing filters, sort or columns while exploring must not silently change the canonical shared definition. Show a clear local-change state and offer Reset to shared view, Save a personal copy, or, only for the current authorized maintainer, Save shared view. State that saving the shared definition changes what its audience starts from. Do not promise persistent per-user overrides unless the current named-view owner actually supports them. A personal copy has a new stable identity, is private by default and contains only currently permitted definition material; it inherits no shared audience, maintainer power, creator access or stale hidden fields. A duplicate name never means overwrite. On canonical revision conflict, preserve lawful local work, explain the actual conflict and allow refresh/review or a current qualified personal copy; no automatic merge or last-writer overwrite.

### U07 — Maintain continuity without a general private-view takeover

Initially the publishing author is the maintainer while currently authorized. Shared readers do not become editors merely by receiving the view. A separately qualified ordinary-source administrator can perform supported shared-definition maintenance, transfer to an eligible current maintainer, stop sharing or retire/remove a shared view. Show the actual current maintainer where permitted; immutable creator history remains attributable. Ordinary creator departure or loss of publish power ends that actor's writes, but does not alone remove independently valid viewers. Current definition/source restrictions and any actual P12 restricted-grantor dependency still control continued use. A departed owner must not strand a shared view behind an engineering-only database repair. Administration of shared definitions does not confer blanket inspection or takeover of never-shared private views. Resolve ownership lawfully before making an orphaned shared view private; do not manufacture a former author's access.

### U08 — Separate Hide, Stop sharing and Remove

Hide/unpin is personal organization and affects no colleague. Stop sharing removes the selected reader audience and retains the view privately for its current lawful maintainer; it does not delete evidence or grant a former maintainer access. Re-sharing requires a current reviewed definition and newly deliberate permitted audience; old readers are not silently restored. Remove retires the reusable definition for everyone who used it, with a clear consequence and current authority, without deleting source files, copied independent views or other people's private arrangements. Old links/defaults must show safe unavailability and a deliberate path to ordinary request files or a qualified alternative, never silently substitute an unfiltered query or an identically named view. No universal Undo, resurrection of old audiences or new recycle-bin platform is required. A mistaken stop-share can be corrected by a fresh lawful Share; a removed definition can be recreated only from currently authorized material as a new private definition, then deliberately shared if needed.

### U09 — Current authority, ordering and recoverable outcomes

Recheck source, definition, maintainer and exact selected audience at each authoritative mutation and protected read, including direct APIs and privileged paths. Tenant/assignment switches invalidate stale selectors, rows and selected commands. Order edit versus edit, save versus unshare/remove, share versus permission loss, and transfer versus departed-author save against the original reviewed definition/sharing basis. If a response is lost or a button is pressed twice, reconcile the original semantic outcome before new-action eligibility, disclosing only what the current actor may learn; never repeat a stale share or personal copy as a new operation. A later Stop sharing/removal wins against old queued writes. If replay evidence is no longer available, reject unverifiable stale mutation rather than reconstructing a new intent from a name. Network interruption is not proof that sharing failed or succeeded.

### U10 — Make the small flow complete and accessible

Use existing native table, picker and dialog patterns. Keep Share view, Shared with, Maintained by, Save shared view, Save a personal copy, Stop sharing and Hide for me distinct; wording is illustrative and should be tested with real staff. A concise consequence alongside the final action suffices where safe; no mandatory reason essay, duplicate confirmation or separate approval stage. Announce saved, pending, conflict, unavailable and selection errors without focus loss; errors identify a safe actionable correction. Keep keyboard, voice, screen-reader and single-pointer non-drag paths complete. Support 320 CSS-pixel reflow for controls, zoom, long translated names, RTL and IME without clipping the actual audience or save consequence. Preserve safe edits during slow-network conflicts and offer intentional retry; never retain revoked private material just to preserve a draft. Keep existing source discovery usable when shared-view services are unavailable.

### U11 — Quiet discovery, privacy and operational repair

New shared definitions become available to the intended staff library without automatic email, notification badges, forced tab changes or a required acknowledgment. The reader chooses to open/pin a permitted view. Authorized maintainer/source-admin tools must expose the minimum actual scope and operation facts needed to repair audience, orphaned ownership, invalid criteria and misleading names. Logs, analytics, errors, browser persistence and crash reporting must not become a corpus of private view prose, predicates, audience identities or result rows. Keep narrowly necessary operation/revision/disclosure evidence under governing retention, separately from optional readable history. Do not promise recalling a definition or result already lawfully disclosed; current revocation prevents later protected disclosures. Personal copies remain independent but never escape current source/field/definition restrictions.

### U12 — Qualify a bounded shared-view contract, not a platform

Adopt the exact definition, authority, audience, maintenance, stopped/removed-state and disclosure contracts in the current ordinary-source/P9/P12 owners. Keep source-defined typed metadata queries and current Q38/Q42 boundaries; no arbitrary code/query upload, body search, dynamic group engine, collaborative editor, approval queue, static list, subscription, workflow, new export, paid provider or broad administrator role. Measure realistic authorized directory/definition/result query cost and bounded pagination; do not fetch every staff member or execute every shared view simply to draw a selector. Census old writers/readers, restore/import/background paths and caches before activation. Native multi-role, mobile and accessibility qualification remains required; external documentation and this review do not prove implementation or cost.
