## Conclusion

The selected behavior is useful if it remains a small, factual awareness choice
at actual Q36/Q37 publication. The principal hazards are unintended mail from a
missed default, false demands for participant work, duplicate-looking access and
update notices, unsafe request identification, and recovery that changes an old
publication or selected choice. A successful source update, email submission,
delivery and human understanding must remain different facts.

The strongest alternative is the same capability initially Off. It better
protects intentional communication during frequent amendments or direct staff
coordination. Native-only plus actually qualified manual outreach is a smaller
alternative; it costs a separate remembered action. Neither alternative is
inferior by definition. No Asym amendment frequency, user preference, privacy
frequency, fatigue, forgetting or completion benefit was measured.

The additional amendment that closes the Q20 overlap is narrow: in a genuinely
new review of one combined fresh unfinished admission and Q36/Q37 publication
to the same exact qualified recipient and destination, preserve Q20's existing eligible On choice and
initialize the **additional** Q40 choice Off before Q40 has an initialized
choice. Explain why and show the two distinct purposes/consequences. Staff may
choose either, neither or explicitly both where lawful. This is a proposed
Q40-only initialization exception, not a composite message, delivery deduper,
later automatic switch or change to Q20. U04 specifies the full rule.

### U01 — A closed factual publication purpose

**H / plausible:** a generic update trigger turns ordinary edits into outward
disclosures or misrepresents source decisions.

Q40 SHALL originate only from actual source-admitted Q36 ordinary item addition
or Q37 material instruction publication. Preserve exact source, request, item/
instruction basis, intended recipient, actor, actual publication time and
permanent occurrence identity. A corrective eligible publication can be new;
retry, exact no-op, draft/template Save/Apply, autosave, scan/provider callback,
task mutation, clarification, review verdict, removal or cancellation is not
this occurrence. Use the actual closed bindings; do not create generic
request.changed semantics or infer materiality with an AI classifier.

The purpose is recipient awareness that this request changed. It SHALL NOT mean
participant action is owed, prior evidence was rejected, current review finished,
another upload is required, or access/deadline restarted. Q28 staff handling and
Q37 current reassessment remain lawful without inventing participant work.
Proof: P01/P02/P10.

### U02 — Real bounded control and conspicuous initial choice

**H / plausible:** staff publish an unwanted external message or administrator
settings silently change an already reviewed action.

Adopt initial qualified tenant availability Enabled and a per-publication
initial On choice, subject to U04's exact overlap exception. Authorized tenant
administrators may disable/enable this purpose and choose its initial On/Off

when Enabled and eligible, and may always decline this optional notice for an
otherwise lawful publication. This does not revise other purposes or create a

Seed a genuinely new review once from the current qualified configuration.
Preserve an initialized visible choice through ordinary validation/recovery,
even if staff never toggled it. A default-only edit affects later new reviews;
it does not rewrite initialized or pending intent. Missing, ambiguous, legacy

separate admission check. Show **Email the recipient about this update**, safe
actual destination and the final effect adjacent to Add item/Update instructions.
No hidden/collapsed default, forced toggle, mandatory essay or second approval.
Proof: P03/P04/P18.
