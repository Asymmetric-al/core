## Recommendation and the actual choice

Recommend an **eligible optional cancellation email initially On, with a visible staff control at the deliberate cancellation action**. A participant preparing documents may not return to discover the canceled status; a narrow factual notice can prevent wasted work and uncertainty. Keep native cancellation authoritative and independently usable. The email is an optional communication of the exact committed outcome, not fulfillment, proof that the participant knows, a new access grant or a route back into canceled conversation.

An initially Off choice is credible where staff deliberately select the communication for each case. If A/B compare initial On versus Off, keep the proposed optional capability and qualified default plan offering it the same; only the staff choice's initial state differs. Authorized tenant plan controls and actual recipient/privacy/readiness rules independently narrow either option. Do not accidentally bundle two separate defaults into one answer.

The strongest no-build alternative is **no dedicated cancellation-notice feature**, retaining Q34's native outcome plus an already-qualified source-owned manual contact route where lawful. That route has genuine navigation/consistency costs and is not an automatic capability to email arbitrary addresses. It differs from offering the same optional email with its checkbox initially Off. Root should present only the alternatives that help the one product decision; no forced third option or new tenant-mode menu is necessary.

This is a product judgment, not a measured Asym frequency or time-saving result. The reason to favor On is that the human deliberately ended an issued demand, so a correctly addressed factual notice often completes the interaction. The strongest risk is disclosure to an unintended recipient—especially when the original request itself was sent in error. That requires actual current notice authority, not confidence that an invitation was once sent.

## Email and web authority must be separate

A cancellation email is itself a disclosure of the cancellation fact, and even sender/relationship/request labels can be sensitive. It needs its **own exact qualified source communication purpose and current recipient/contact authority**. A surviving Q34 outcome root cannot automatically authorize mail. Equally, expiry or absence of a Q34 web outcome root must not universally prohibit a separately authorized factual email saying that this exact request no longer needs ordinary submissions.

This is the important distinction from Q31's reply-available cue. A lawful cancellation notice can sometimes be complete without requiring the participant to open anything. If a protected action is included, it must use only the exact current Q34 outcome allowance/root and unchanged end; no new canceled grant, proof shortcut, file/history/transcript access or dead upload link. When no lawful protected destination exists, the approved notice must remain useful and truthful without that destination or use an independently qualified safe source-help contact. Do not make optional CTA readiness a prerequisite for native cancellation.

Wrong-recipient cancellation is the critical negative journey. If the request was sent to the wrong address/person, possession of that old invitation, an old `to` field, an address match or a historical provider response is not current permission to send another message. Do not notify the former wrong address automatically or silently retarget the same notice to a newly discovered contact. Establish the intended participant and actual source/identity authority through the real owner. No lawful recipient means the cancellation succeeds with no lawful external notice; unknown authority cannot become a broadcast, default address or guessed zero.

Content should be a minimal source-approved fact about the **exact canceled request** and its ended ordinary demand. Exclude internal reasons, filenames, submitted text, attachments, sensitive purpose labels, secret URLs and claims of deletion/acceptance. Do not say the enclosing application was rejected, or that no documents will ever be needed, or that a separately issued replacement is canceled. Copy must stay accurate if delayed, if the participant already saw the status, or if a later source remedy exists.
