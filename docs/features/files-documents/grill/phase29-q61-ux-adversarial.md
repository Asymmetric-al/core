## Outcome and material findings

Keep A. The permanent simple experience is a consistent set of contextual file controls whose actions come from the actual business source. Users should understand the file/version, what has really happened, who acts next and how to recover. They should not need to understand custody architecture, open a general drive to complete a claim, or repeat a valid upload merely to fit a new screen. One universal status, permission or Restore action would make the experience less truthful.

This pass found three material consolidation precisions rather than another optional feature:

1. **Retain separately owned Trash behavior.** Page/Article recovery and P23 D27 Media Trash are both real inherited contracts. Media has its own folder rehoming, impact, Restore and independent disposition rules. Neither supplies a generic ordinary-evidence delete/restore command. A blanket “Trash is Page/Article-only” statement would be wrong.
2. **Ordinary retention and legacy profiles are not complete.** The current program decisions-and-gates row for Phases29/30 still reserves purpose retention/holds and supported legacy source profiles/owner import-reversal semantics. R-POL-ordinary and R-LEGACY are actual affected-capability source blockers; they cannot be closed by copying an official-receipt retention period, retaining everything forever, or saying implementation will decide.
3. **ReUI is actual reusable UI material, not file-product behavior.** Fresh free source inspection exposes static sharing badges/name keys, simulated random upload completion and fixture rows. Those parts must be replaced by stable source identities and acknowledged current results while retaining the accepted visual system. The existing profile-avatar uploader is also a different producer, not ordinary private custody qualification.

These findings preserve the selected scope. Any unresolved actual policy choice must return through the owning decision process; an engineering fact is researched directly. The full register does not declare native or specification readiness while a material source consequence remains unresolved.

## Twenty exact consolidation requirements

These are required preservation/acceptance language for Q61's reconciliation, not new business powers. A genuine incompatibility with an existing owner must be resolved explicitly rather than hidden inside these words.

**U01 — Complete scope and bounded output.** The reconciliation shall retain every ratified Q01–Q60 outcome and the founder's public, official, financial, Support, resource, operations and future-consumer outcomes. Each journey shall have its actual actor/source, allowed action, success, refusal, stale/concurrent/unknown result, expiry, durable recovery and required proof, or a precise affected-capability blocker. No count of decisions or empty checklist establishes readiness. Do not copy all ADRs into a competing authority.

**U02 — Contextual actions and exact identity.** The File Manager and embedded controls shall act on the exact source-owned use/version/occurrence/publication/artifact, never infer business identity from filename, byte equality, folder, provider key or row position. Keep current source and next actor intelligible. Existing permissions govern every row/action; no global Share, Restore, Accept or Delete command is inferred from a common toolbar.

**U03 — Truthful states and known results.** Selected, transferring, technically prepared, source received, checking, review needed, accepted, fulfilled, released, delivery attempted and physically disposed shall retain their separate owner facts. Show the smallest meaningful current state; do not display backend-state jargon unnecessarily. A later refresh failure shall not erase a known successful result. Unknown remains unknown and leads to original-result recovery.

**U04 — Minimal deliberate action.** Preserve one meaningful contextual final action where the existing owner permits it, with the actual recipient/version/set/consequence visible. Typing, blur, checkbox changes, file selection, IME composition and opening a calendar or picker do not perform consequential actions. Avoid mandatory extra preview, read acknowledgment, reason essay, approver, confirmation modal or disabled-setting matrix unless the exact source requires it. Existing irreversible-impact review remains.

**U05 — Recovery before fresh retry.** Recover the original operation under current disclosure before creating a new intent. Preserve permitted draft and selection after recoverable failure; do not promise unsent local memory survives device/tab loss. Known committed business facts persist with original attribution. Cancel/Stop only prevents effects still preventable at the actual owner boundary; it never claims remote recall or silently queues an offline write.

**U06 — Current authority and safe presentation.** Every discovery result, facet/count/order/page, metadata/body/rendition, full/range/conditional/HEAD delivery, recovered result and export shall use current source/P12 authority and its field projection. Independent purpose rights may permit safe reporting/removal without resource-body access; they do not widen unrelated content. **U07 — Intake and provenance.** Preserve participant, staff-supplied and source-generated origins; real actors never impersonate one another. **U19 — Migration and evidence layers.** Inventory actual retained bytes and reached readers/writers/jobs/scripts/URLs; map source meaning and perform a single qualified authority cutover. **U20 — Completion and next stage.** Deliver the reconciled register, exact policy/conflict/blocker ledger and source-grounded acceptance/validation plan. A blocker is not successful readiness. Do not add a feature, formal PRD, ticket, glossary term, implementation, provider action or ratification by inference. Focused real user validation remains required later; no endless prototype alternatives or documentation expansion.
