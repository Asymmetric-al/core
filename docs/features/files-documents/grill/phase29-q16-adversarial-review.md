## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

“Both completed routes” could become “every completed request,” using current
files or unfinished write scope to manufacture a broader read grant. Bind the
new decision to the actual fulfillment-created completed permission instead.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That this exact recipient's historical completed admission, scope and ending
cause can be proved and that a current permitted need remains. A screen, email,
download log, mutable task or current role cannot substitute for that proof.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It extends Q08's eligible historical origin while preserving current permission,
source completion, exact member/action limits and independent custody. It does
not add an archive, sharing role, request reopening or another completion act.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Staff use the same Grant read access action. Recipients return to the same
read-only context, without learning internal route categories, proving a past
download or uploading their evidence again. Status-only access stays honest.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Consistent source-governed recovery, clear scope and separate current checks
are sound patterns. Comparators support recognizable return/read experiences,
not this exact entitlement. B is a valid narrower alternative; existing valid
access may simply need proof/preview repair rather than another grant.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes as one explicit owner amendment using Q08's intended source/P12/UI path.
Q13/Q15 already permit the additional completed view. The current helpers are
adjacent evidence, not proof that the native capability exists or is safe.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Retain A. Fix the historical permission ceiling, preserve partial/status-only
cases and current restrictions, and keep unresolved Q08 clocks/counts gated.
No origin picker, second grant type or general policy engine is warranted.

## Corrected decision to record

Allow Q08 fresh completed-read consideration after eligible ordinary expiry of
either a proved original ordinary completed-view admission or a proved completed
view lawfully established at normal fulfillment under valid Q13 fresh unfinished
authority, including an eligible Q15 period. The same intended recipient and
current permitted source purpose must still qualify. Actual source completion
without a recipient completed admission does not qualify.

For either route, use the actual fulfillment-created completed permission for
that recipient/request lineage as the fixed historical read ceiling. Its exact
members, original versions, actions and Q05-permitted context bound the new
reviewed scope, intersected with current eligibility and restrictions. Do not
substitute original unfinished requirements, writing permission, retained
folders, later files, access logs or unions of grants. A later narrow Q08 grant
alone does not replace that ceiling; a genuinely narrow completed admission
itself cannot be ignored as though it were only a later discretionary subset.

Durable source admission, not a prior visit or successful renderer/download,
establishes the historical fact. Proved status/context-only completed scope may
qualify only for its allowed metadata actions; it creates no file rights. Do
not silently downgrade a failed file request into a misleading metadata success.
Repair of a qualified representation of the same admitted original remains
separate from adding a new member or action.

Preserve original fulfillment, ends, receipts, corrections, acceptance and
adverse history. Gap completion, write-only authority without completed-reading
admission and guessed legacy data cannot create a first view retrospectively.
Recover a genuine timely outcome from trustworthy source evidence with its
original facts; repair/observation time does not reset the old period.
Actual revocation, binding shortening, purpose/content-use limits and disposal
retain their real scope, including when they coincide with a nominal end.

Use the exact current Q08/P12 issuing authority and existing source mutation,
semantic effect/recovery and private-serving boundaries. The old grant need
not remain usable to authorize staff consideration, and staff administration
does not itself establish recipient proof or content-read rights. Revalidate
current material facts after waits and before protected use. Unknown required
proof closes the affected action safely while preserving independently
established eligible subsets and source work.

Keep one meaningful Grant read access action in the existing completed-request
context. Show authorized scope, qualified timing terms and actual current
status; distinguish issued, usable, proof-ready, notified and unknown outcomes.
No new account, lineage selector, repeated confirmation, reupload, intake,
correction, automatic message or drive follows. Complete Q08 fresh timing,
unused and repetition policy and native qualification remain prerequisites;
this choice does not invent defaults or authorize real issuance yet.

## Ownership, invariants and database boundary

The source owns actual fulfillment and the completed permission established
there, including its policy/evidence, exact recipient/request relationship,
member/action ceiling and admitted end. Auth/P12 owns current issuer/recipient
context and permissions; custody owns immutable originals and qualified
representations/retention. UI, cache, access telemetry, task status and provider
URLs are mechanisms or projections, not proof of that permission.

Conceptually, new scope is **historical completed ceiling ∩ current permitted
scope ∩ deliberately reviewed subset**. This is a permission invariant, not
a prescribed schema or another policy engine. The ceiling is not a frozen
copy of every displayed status/history string. Current facts may be projected
through the same authorized fields/context without adding new members/actions.
Nor must a preview have rendered during the original interval to be qualified
later for the same authorized original.

“First completed admission” means the lawful completion-established permission
for this exact lineage. It does not mean MIN(created_at), first observed row,
first page visit or a later Q08 grant. Original and fresh unfinished periods
are causal provenance, not separate independently widening read authorities.
The permission may be proved through the source's qualified durable evidence;
this review does not mandate an additional physical grant table or reconstruct
rights from what a modern policy says should have happened historically.

Preserve trusted same-tenant/source/recipient/fulfillment/admission/member
relationships, required provenance, valid finite-time state and immutable
attribution/history. Clients may select an allowed handle; they cannot certify
the route, historical ceiling, actor, purpose, current eligibility or timestamps.
An allowed update must not retarget a root, insert a later member, swap an
original version or erase a genuine limiting fact. Foreign keys/uniqueness,
delete behavior and the sole mutation boundary must protect these invariants
without relying only on application convention. Root/member joins must preserve
the exact relation even within one tenant. No money-column change is introduced.

Effective grants and RLS qualification must cover SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE,
old-row USING and resulting-row WITH CHECK, policy composition, column/function
rights, views, RPC/definer ownership/search paths, service/job and storage paths.
Omitted ALL/UPDATE WITH CHECK can inherit USING; missing syntax alone is not a
finding. Parent permission is not automatically a child member policy, and
coarse tenant RLS does not replace source/P12 checks. Constraint failures,
counts, pagination and replay must not disclose another recipient's history.
The adjacent receipt migration illustrates why an “immutable” comment plus
no UPDATE policy is insufficient evidence for privileged paths. Qualify actual
effective grants and mutations; this is a bounded reuse warning, not a claim
of a deployed receipt or native Q16 vulnerability.

Use indexed exact baseline/member lookup and authorized pagination under the
existing budgets. Complete proof of the selected scope does not require fetching
every stored file or replaying every unfinished period for every read. Missing
root/ordinary-expiry proof blocks issuance. An unrelated unavailable member or
nonauthorizing subview does not invalidate independently proved work. A smaller
permitted subset may be deliberately reviewed, without silent scope substitution.
Required source proof and minimal replay/audit evidence follow owner retention;
missing evidence cannot default to a broad legacy origin or new admission.
