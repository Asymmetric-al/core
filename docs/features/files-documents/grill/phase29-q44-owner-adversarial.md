## Decision and smallest complete product

Keep A. A finite collection can arrive as one understandable download with its permitted contents/outcome record, while ordinary individual Download remains available. B remains a strong alternative for a few independent files, constrained devices and file-specific retrieval. A is neither universally faster nor cost-neutral. Its benefit requires actual bounded, current-authorized delivery, not merely a ZIP library or Download button.

The minimum complete ordinary product is one source-authorized finite preparation intent, an exact independently qualified member set, one immutable sealed ZIP/result manifest, and distinct delivery attempts. Preserve the original intended selection even when individual members cannot be included. A complete unchanged result can honor the original deliberate Download action; a changed nonempty subset requires an explicit **Download available files** choice after a truthful permitted result. No empty ZIP is presented as success. A command-wide source/P12 denial or unknown authority is not converted into a smaller permitted command.

The result is private to the initiating principal's exact qualified staff assignment/context, not a shared archive audience. Every delivery rechecks the whole included byte and manifest scope. A revoked member cannot be removed from an old sealed ZIP in place. A changed collection requires a deliberate reviewed new preparation; an unchanged still-valid result can be retried without rebuilding or extending its end. Package cancellation/expiry, source retention, holds and actual cleanup remain separate. No new glossary term, archive center, email, connector, residual-export engine or universal file permission model is needed.

## Exact proposed amendments

### C01 — Explicit ordinary source authority and first cohort

Adopt one ordinary staff browser-download ZIP for an explicit finite selection of exact original evidence contribution/use versions held by the Private Document Request source, including Q28 evidence only where its actual origin/target restrictions permit this export. The current principal needs the source's exact original-download and bulk-export purpose under P12, independent of Read, preview, staff rank, reviewer assignment, custody, Q04 participant access, Q41 shares, folder membership or view publication. Keep specialist artifacts and D26 records exports on their own owner routes. No money, acceptance, fulfillment, cancellation, source retention or onward-sharing fact changes. No universal unfinished, accepted or preview-ready prerequisite is invented; exact source authority controls current and historical staff export.

### C02 — Original intended selection and exact candidate

Bind the actual tenant/environment, requesting principal and validated staff assignment, source/purpose, and finite intended source-use/version identities to a durable command. Normalize exact duplicate selections to one reviewed binding/count, but do not merge distinct uses by name or digest. Reject malformed, cross-scope or incompatible candidates as invalid commands rather than silently repairing their meaning. Never rerun a saved view, expand a folder/item/history, choose later matching rows or follow a new version at preparation time. Preserve the original intended set independently from admitted members and visible outcome projection. Caller IDs are requests, not authority; incomplete/unknown authoritative selection is not a proven zero.

### C03 — Whole-command gates before member outcomes

Evaluate actual source/P12 export-purpose, classification, residency, budget and whole-segment restrictions for the command before expensive member processing or disclosure. A command-wide denial or indeterminate required decision blocks; filtering a few members cannot evade it. Once the command and the partial-result operation are independently permitted, evaluate every intended member against current exact source/custody authority. An individual denial, unavailability or unresolved eligibility/integrity is a truthful non-included outcome, never proof of absence or successful work. Such a member may coexist with a separately proved permitted subset only if the subset/manifest operation itself remains lawful and its disclosure does not leak the excluded target. The original selection remains incomplete. Zero proved includable members yields no empty-success ZIP.

### C04 — One deliberate complete download, explicit partial action

Preparation and transfer are separate facts, not mandatory separate confirmations. For a complete unchanged reviewed selection, one deliberate Download may prepare and deliver when qualified; slow/returning work exposes persistent progress and a Ready download. If included scope differs, present permitted actual completeness/outcomes and require deliberate Download available files for the exact nonempty subset before bytes begin. Do not automatically deliver the smaller ZIP, turn the original selection into approval of different contents or force a routine modal/essay. A person may choose a smaller/new selection or ordinary single-file Download. Partial intent acknowledgement does not override a denied member or whole-command restriction.

### C05 — Immutable ZIP, safe contents record and integrity

Construct a bounded private artifact containing the exact admitted unchanged originals plus one readable UTF-8 contents manifest (`CONTENTS.txt` or an equivalently qualified accessible plain-text name) generated from the authoritative outcome basis. Its member names, permitted source/version mapping, sizes, integrity values and completeness/outcome explanations are independently protected; include no secrets, raw provider paths, active authorization links or denied-item details. Safely reserve the manifest name and normalize delivery paths/collisions without overwriting a distinct member or altering original bytes. Export members are ordinary files, not generated symlinks or executable navigation. An eligible original archive is opaque content; never recursively extract it. Prove all included bytes and the final ZIP/manifest mapping before marking that result ready. Bind an external protected whole-ZIP digest to the exact result; do not attempt a self-referential hash inside its own bytes. Provider ETags are not automatically the selected content hash. Sealed member set, names, manifest and artifact bytes never mutate in place.

### C06 — Current authority for the entire sealed result

The initiating principal must use the exact still-valid staff context; a guessed result ID, another hat, shared view audience or copied URL creates no archive access. Workers use a qualified least-privilege context and the original requester/source binding, re-resolved at execution. Reauthorize every included original and embedded manifest field at actual delivery admission and each applicable metadata, HEAD, full/range/conditional/recovery operation, with inherited stream expiry/restriction checks. Do not serve a previously prepared ZIP if any included material is no longer disclosable; block the old artifact rather than redact/replace it in place. A different subset needs a deliberate new reviewed preparation. A temporary technical/proof/authorization block can recover to the unchanged still-admitted result within its original terms if the source permits; unknown is not automatically a terminal revocation. An actual terminal source withdrawal remains terminal. No raw reusable signed URL/public bucket/CDN fallback or recall promise.

### C07 — Coherent admission, original result and replay

Coherently commit the finite command basis, actor/context, required history, work/reference protection and durable follow-up before dependent external preparation. Bind each sealed artifact and manifest to its exact admitted command/member basis. Use durable semantic operation identity and intent comparison: a matching committed replay reconciles the real result before new-command eligibility, under current result disclosure; a changed payload conflicts. Crash or lost response cannot create a second intended selection, rebuild a sealed result unnecessarily or relabel committed work failed. Retain owner-governed minimal uniqueness/revocation/result evidence or reject stale operations after readable-history expiry so old retries/restores cannot restart terminal work. Do not hold database locks across provider transfer or create a parallel state platform.

### C08 — Retry, cancellation, expiry and changed-result recovery

Retry an unresolved preparation on its original exact basis and bounds; retry a still-valid sealed result as another delivery attempt, not another build. Do not assume a lost transfer response means no bytes arrived. A source-context Cancel stops that preparation/result from accepting new work or delivery and requests exact private cleanup; it is not Q34 request cancellation or source deletion. Order Cancel/expiry/source restriction against preparation completion and delivery admission, honoring feasible inherited in-flight termination without claiming recall. Cancel/expiry are terminal for that old result and cannot be undone by a worker, default change or restored backup. A user can deliberately Prepare again as a new currently authorized command with reviewed scope; this never automatically substitutes latest bytes, repeats completed remote effects or resets the old deadline. Temporary blocked results that were not ended remain distinct from terminal results. No universal Undo, automatic residual archive or fresh-key retry masquerading as recovery.

### C09 — Source lifecycle and origin independence

Source evidence, request fulfillment/cancellation, participant access, review, source-use withdrawal, retention and package lifecycle remain independent. Request completion/cancellation by itself is neither a new staff export grant nor a universal ban on independently lawful historical export. Apply the actual current source's export rule to the exact material. Q28 origin restrictions, declared continuing permissions and target-use limits remain; copying bytes does not bypass them. A later correction/rename/move cannot silently change a sealed result, resurrect disposed content or create historical authority. A current owner rule may make the old version ineligible, and current source repair may restore an otherwise still-admitted exact result only where actually lawful. Do not borrow Q40 notice utility, Q41 external-share scope or participant access clocks.

### C10 — Structural and effective protection at every native path

Use tenant/environment-aware typed relationships, immutable source/use/version/result bindings, operation uniqueness, explicit finite ends and current state/authority checks. Derive actor and assignment from trusted context. Prevent general update/UPSERT/trigger/service paths from retargeting a package, changing its requester/member set or reopening it. Native grants and RLS SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE, old-row USING/new-row WITH CHECK, views/functions/RPCs, storage policies, worker/service and operator paths must enforce the same source/P12 boundary. A privileged worker or table owner is not a product principal. Keep read/prepare/cancel/cleanup powers separate and result lookup privacy-safe. Browser caches, late responses, filenames and errors are scoped to current user/tenant/purpose; no protected-body telemetry or durable offline replica.

### C11 — Finite work, staging, holds and actual cleanup

Before activation, qualify and record finite count/byte/preparation-time/memory/concurrency/retry/delivery-window/staging/storage/egress limits, with tenant fairness and actual infrastructure evidence. Enforce authoritative sizes and bounds before uncontrolled work and during processing; never rely solely on client estimates. Absolute operation and delivery deadlines derive from the actual source/P29 profile and earlier actual source-imposed material-availability caps; preparation, retry, redownload and recovery cannot slide them. Current session/JIT/step-up validity remains a separate serving gate: its expiry may temporarily block an otherwise still-admitted result without rewriting that result's fixed end. No guessed vendor, Q06, P17 or P18 duration is adopted. Expiry stops delivery independently of copy cleanup. Apply actual source-owned copy-class retention and hold scope; a source-record hold does not automatically preserve every optional ZIP forever. Where an actual hold covers a staged/package copy, preserve that copy under restricted owner control, account its capacity and do not extend ordinary download access. Unknown cleanup/hold applicability forbids destructive guesses, not all other lawful source work. Record remaining/unknown copies and reconcile through supported scoped operations; report verified disposition only after required outcomes. Stop or bound new admissions under capacity pressure rather than silently excluding held/orphan bytes from budgets. No source deletion, hold release or verified-custody claim follows from package expiry/download.

### C12 — Protected history, metering and truthful evidence

Preserve required source business history, restricted access/security evidence and minimal operation/cleanup facts under their actual owner schedules. Record intended scope versus admitted members, source/assignment/purpose and relevant revisions, preparation/seal/outcome, permitted partial acknowledgement, exact artifact identity/integrity, current allow/deny, transfer observations, cancellation/expiry and cleanup/hold results. Do not conflate offered/download-start/server-stream-complete with browser-saved/human-read/accepted/verified custody. Apply actual P12 setwise projection, tiered audit/outbox and hard export budgets at every egress; telemetry is not the authoritative ledger. General logs/analytics/queues omit private names, bodies, tokens and protected associations. Restricted operator diagnostics have their own purpose/read audit and cannot become a general body-access workaround.

### C13 — Complete accessible staff and operator journeys

Use existing source-context selection and shared UI, clear permitted count/size, persistent Preparing/Ready/partial/unavailable/canceled/expired status and the actual Download/Download available files/Cancel/Prepare again actions appropriate to state. Preserve safe selections and reconcile lost replies; do not start download from focus, filter changes, a passive preview or reopening history. Support keyboard/non-drag pointer selection, mobile reflow, long/international names, zoom, focus restoration and restrained AT progress announcements. Normal individual Download remains available under its own rights. Scoped operators can reconcile known operation/provider outcomes, retry permitted exact cleanup and escalate an actual source/hold/integrity conflict without SQL/provider-console repair or broadly granting export. If the downloaded selection was mistaken, stop future availability and use source/privacy remediation; do not claim local-copy recall or that a new archive fixes already disclosed data.

### C14 — Exact publication, rollout and restraint

At authorized specification publication adopt the concrete ordinary-source export purpose, P12/P3 capability/projection/egress, P29 custody/cleanup and shared UI/operation contracts through canonical owners and complete consumed OpenSpec; regenerate declared projections together. Qualify actual native roles, current reads, concurrency/replay/restore, provider integrity/cleanup and intended-user/AT flows before enabling a bounded cohort. Expand/fence readers and writers before activation; unproved legacy/public URLs or restored rows confer no archive authority. Rollback may stop new preparation and affected delivery while preserving denial/history/cleanup facts. No new glossary term is required: ordinary ZIP result must remain distinct from the existing Records Export Package. Preserve all26 definitions. No archive center, general workflow/ACL engine, new mail, cloud destination, source-byte conversion, format menu, mandatory second approver or whole future File Manager prerequisite follows.
