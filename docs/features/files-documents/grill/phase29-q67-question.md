## The practical question

A tenant has an approved Document Records Policy whose supported start depends on a defined business event. Choosing that rule does not establish that the event happened. Some occurrences may already be recorded by their owning business source; others happen outside Asym.

**For a supported external occurrence, should the owning source let an authorized staff member record what happened and its actual date, or require the fact to arrive through an already-qualified business source or integration?**

Illustration only: an approved source rule might depend on a particular agreement actually ending outside Asym. The question is how the owning source receives that fact. It does not choose agreement-end as a policy trigger, say that a real GF agreement ended, define legal termination, change a retention period or authorize deletion. The actual source/class, event meaning, eligible actor, evidence and consequences must qualify before this route is offered.

| Option                                                                                            | User experience                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Strongest benefit                                                                                                                                        | Tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Allow authorized staff to record a supported external occurrence in context (recommended)** | In the exact source record, an eligible person records the already-defined event, actual occurrence date and only the evidence/reference the source requires. The source validates/adopts it and shows the permitted result and consequences. Existing qualified native/integration facts remain usable. | Supports real work outside software without requiring an integration for every eligible event. Preserves tenant flexibility and a small contextual flow. | Human mistakes and conflicts with existing facts need exact scope, current authority, evidence rules, history and correction. Typing a date is not automatic authority.                              |
| **B — Use only events already recorded by a qualified business source or integration**            | Asym consumes the existing authoritative occurrence. It offers no separate manual external-occurrence capture for records handling. A person may still perform the owning source's normal qualified business action.                                                                                     | Fewer new input/reconciliation paths; appropriate when existing systems reliably own all relevant facts.                                                 | A policy depending on an unsupported external occurrence cannot use a guessed date. Its affected path waits for a qualified producer or supported source resolution; independent duties still apply. |

**Recommendation: A, as an optional source-owned route.** It meets the tenant-flexibility goal while retaining B's existing producers. A does not create a free-form event registry or make policy administrators authoritative for every business fact. Tenants can limit the route to actual eligible roles and sources through existing capabilities; no blanket grant to all staff or admins follows.

The ordinary interaction should be brief: known source record and event; **When did [event] happen?**; any genuinely required reference; a concise impact summary; one deliberate **Record [event]** action. Do not make users re-enter known context, upload a document merely for ceremony, obtain an extra approval when the source does not require one, or edit the policy itself to represent an occurrence. The source chooses actual field precision and evidence requirements.

## Twelve boundaries under either answer

1. **Defined source meaning.** The real ordinary source must already support the exact event/class and own the asserted fact for that purpose. A adds an authorized initial human capture route, not arbitrary event names, global events or a policy-form date override. A records-purpose assertion cannot mint financial settlement, contract termination, receipt, item acceptance or Q22 fulfillment owned elsewhere.
2. **Current actor and tenant authority.** Use actual native identity, current tenant/environment/source/P12 capabilities and source evidence rules. Policy-edit, record-view, authorship, admin title, Drive access, service key and stale claims are not initial occurrence authority. Tenant roles can govern eligible actors through existing qualified permissions.
3. **Exact subject and permitted impact.** Bind the source subject/obligation, event meaning/revision and current relevant scope. Blank or unknown subject is not all records with a label. Preview only safe permitted impact; no hidden counts/hold disclosures or arbitrary cross-tenant selection. Revalidate material changes before the final act.
4. **Occurrence versus recording.** Preserve the actual occurrence under the source's date precision/calendar/time zone separately from server-derived recording time and actor. Late reporting may be valid; it does not rewrite when staff recorded it. A planned future milestone is not a completed event. Unknown or partial precision follows an explicitly supported source route; never default to today, midnight, last-modified or a guessed date.
5. **Evidence proportionate to the source.** Use existing sufficient trusted references or concise genuinely required evidence. No blanket document upload, second approver, legal essay or content inspection is introduced. Inadequate or conflicting evidence cannot be converted into authority by a checkbox or a successful save.
6. **One source fact across input channels.** Reuse an existing authoritative occurrence instead of recording it again. Reconcile manual/native/integration duplicates and contradictions under source identity/revision and relevant expected basis; do not let arrival order, matching display labels or a new transport key create a second clock. Workflow consumes the same source result.
7. **Coherent consequence and recovery.** Preserve authoritative occurrence/history/original result and necessary current impact/control effects through the actual source boundary with durable bounded follow-through. Lost responses recover the original result under current disclosure. A recorded occurrence, pending policy impact and verified physical disposition are distinct outcomes; current safety cannot wait on an optimistic badge.
8. **Correction and late records.** Use the owning source's authorized correction/retraction/applicability path, preserving original facts and current effects. No generic Delete event/Undo, in-place reparenting, rekeyed replay or rollback of escaped physical effects. A later matching record/use follows its qualified current policy/coverage path; do not manufacture another occurrence merely to start it.
9. **Disposition, holds and privacy.** A reported historical event may make a rule due, but it grants no disposal or hold-release authority. ## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A generic date-entry feature could impersonate a business owner, broadly trigger unrelated records or bypass current disposal safeguards. A is expressly a qualified source route for an already-defined occurrence and subject, with current authority, provenance, history and correction.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That some intended tenant workflows need external facts is a product rationale, not measured usage. No specific GF agreement, event, legal rule, actor or actual occurrence is assumed. B remains reasonable where existing sources reliably supply every required fact.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adds or declines one bounded human producer route alongside existing source/integration facts. It preserves domain ownership, tenant policy control, Workflow's coordination role and custody's independent execution authority.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

A can let an eligible source actor record a known occurrence in a short contextual flow. B removes that extra route but may require an actual existing source/integration. Neither asks staff to edit a policy to pretend its event happened or restate context Asym already knows.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Current primary product documentation supports separating rules, subjects and actual events, with both human and automated input. Core adopts that distinction and clear date/review UX while rejecting broad-scope and unsafe temporal defaults.

### Does this fit Asym’s existing repo and product direction?

Yes as a proposed ordinary source capability, subject to the exact source contract. Q62 already distinguishes not-yet facts and correction; Q66 configures the rule. Initial human event capture is a separate choice and cannot borrow a specialist's authority or a generic task's completion.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Recommend **A — optional authorized source recording of supported external occurrences**, while retaining qualified native/integration facts and all existing safeguards. Keep B as the simpler producer-only alternative. Q67 remains unselected; detailed amendments follow review of the user's answer.
