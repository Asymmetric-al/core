Assignment administration, destination eligibility, substantive review, participant contact and technical execution are distinct responsibilities and rights.

## Eight proposed incremental clauses

1. **Q24-C01 — Real person or team responsibility.** The ordinary source may hold review responsibility in an eligible person or an explicitly qualified accountable team route. Define the route's exact tenant/source/purpose and supported work, current eligible-actor resolution and capable source recovery. Team responsibility can remain current without a mandatory named individual/Claim prerequisite for each review. A bare group label, queue row, watcher list or contact address is insufficient. Missing/ambiguous/unknown eligibility must be represented truthfully; no first-row or all-admin fallback, fake person or new queue principal is permitted.
2. **Q24-C02 — Separate authority and actual human decision.** Each Q21 acceptance and Q23 Request changes action requires its actual currently authorized human, trusted tenant/environment/context, exact source capability/purpose and current evidence/review basis. Route membership/assignment does not itself grant reading, review, administration, download or historical access. Routing administrators need their own exact source authority and do not thereby gain substantive review rights. Preserve source-specific individual qualification and SoD. An NHI may only perform separately qualified coordination under existing current human-owner/ceiling rules; it cannot stand in for the human reviewer or acquire authority merely because a team is responsible.
3. **Q24-C03 — Reuse qualified defaults without silent overwrite.** Reuse a valid existing source responsibility and resolve any new default through its qualified source policy/context, displaying only permitted meaning. Do not hard-code creator, first match, all admins or arbitrary Team. Re-render, retry, policy-default edit, creator departure or membership-query error must not silently replace a deliberate current selection. A real default-driven/reassignment transition requires the source's explicit qualified policy and current authority/heads with attributable history; Q24 selects no new automatic routing algorithm, precedence menu, round-robin, workload scoring or calendar policy.
4. **Q24-C04 — Current eligibility and scoped churn.** Recheck actual actor eligibility and materially applicable source responsibility conditions at the source decision boundary, including after waits. Preserve the distinction between route existence, current responsible destination, actor access, missing actor, unknown lookup and actual absence policy. If no authorized actor can act, mark the dependent review unavailable/needs assignment and use real source recovery; retain the identity/history of the known route where appropriate. Do not invent a grace period, automatically transfer to an arbitrary backup or treat transient lookup failure as proven empty membership. No person/route rename grants or revokes access by itself.
5. **Q24-C05 — Source-owned changes and exact recovery.** Responsibility changes use the exact qualified source command: current actor/destination/scope and relevant expected heads, attributable successor/effect, old/new responsibility and permitted pending-work consequences. Tasks Hub projects source truth; it does not patch a generic assignee as authority. Reassignment neither universally revokes review permission nor preserves a now-invalid authorization: reprove actual current source/P12 rules and relevant route dependency, fencing material drift while retaining independent valid work. Preserve previous human decisions and one current coherent responsibility; duplicate/lost replies recover the same effect under current disclosure and stale consumers cannot overwrite a later source transition. Do not add a universal exclusive lease, review/view lock or per-member source task.
6. **Q24-C06 — No hidden issuance, workflow or clock change.** Q24 determines ordinary review responsibility/actionability only. Missing review eligibility does not create a new global request-creation, source-issuance, recipient-intake, read or source-completion gate; actual existing source rules still apply independently. Preserve Q03/Q21/Q23 exact item races and Q22's full completion rule, historical decisions, original/fresh recipient scope and clocks. Do not reopen accepted/completed work, alter requiredness, create notices or grant access to repair staffing. If an actual dependent source action is blocked, show that exact problem and safe next owner rather than implying the recipient is at fault or work is ready.
7. **Q24-C07 — Clear current work projection.** In the existing request/My Work context, distinguish current responsibility from actual recorded reviewer and the participant contact/help route. Show only current purpose-permitted route/status/action; participant disclosure does not include internal roster, routing history, staff availability or identity by default. Queue listing, counts, search and replay apply current source/P12 permission before results. Preserve one logical source task with useful staff views; no new queue console, repeated member copies, mandatory claim, extra reviewer, notification default or team-wide evidence sharing. Retain accessible mobile/AT/keyboard/localized/poor-network behavior and discard stale private results on context/authority change.
8. **Q24-C08 — Compatible owner adoption and qualification.** Prospectively qualify person/team responsibility, exact source actor/admin and eligibility rules, currentness, genuine missing-route recovery and P9 projection under the ordinary native source.
