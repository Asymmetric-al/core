# Phase 29 Q28 — Authorized staff reuse of existing evidence

**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

**Status:** Conrad selected Q28 A and fully ratified its complete required
amendment package on 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q28 and all twenty-one
glossary definitions are ratified grooming intent. The adopted decision and
proof obligations are preserved; Q29's answer is not inferred. No implementation,
activation or formal source/OpenSpec publication follows.

The useful product is small: **Add existing file**, a contextual eligible-version
choice, **Add to this item**, then normal review. An accidental unaccepted staff
addition has **Remove from this item**. The necessary precision belongs behind
those actions: real authority, truthful attribution, exact evidence, independent
review and safe recovery. There is no new general drive or approval engine.

**Strongest B:** staff prepare the exact eligible document, but the intended
participant must affirm it before review. This can catch stale or inappropriate
evidence. It also requires authorized presentation and participant availability;
it is not inherently safe merely because a person clicked Confirm. A retains
all actual participant-assertion requirements without imposing that dependency
on ordinary assistance. Keeping existing upload-only behavior or making staff
download/re-upload is not a better no-build alternative: it weakens the ratified
reuse outcome and can obscure provenance without improving authority.

## Ratified corrected decision

> Allow currently authorized staff to deliberately add eligible existing exact
> file versions as **Staff-supplied Document Evidence** for an eligible ordinary
> Document Request Item, without a new participant confirmation solely because
> bytes are reused. This is an explicit ordinary-source staff-admission amendment,
> not authority inferred from custody, reading, reviewer assignment or staff rank.
> Preserve all actual source-required participant statements, consent, signatures,
> freshness, specialist rules and separation of duties. No generic override or
> new permission engine follows.
>
> Add only a new independently authorized target use. Bind the source and target,
> intended subject, requirement/purpose, exact immutable version and current
> relevant evidence set. Preserve original provenance and record the actual
> staff actor and target admission time. Do not fabricate a participant upload,
> affirmation, new original receipt or inherited approval. Addition is additive
> within source cardinality; it never silently replaces existing participant or
> staff evidence, accepted/frozen material or evidence in another use.
>
> Prove current reuse/admission authority and qualified durable custody/integrity
> at commitment. Only target processing that an explicit bounded intake profile
> permits after admission may remain Checking. Quarantine, missing authority or
> unproved custody cannot be bypassed. Current inspection and Q21 deliberate
> acceptance of the new item/evidence basis remain necessary; Q22's complete
> source predicate alone determines fulfillment. The same person may supply and
> review only when actual source powers and separation rules allow.
>
> Keep participant status, file reading and history separately source-authorized.
> Staff-supplied evidence does not become the participant's own Q02/Q04/Q05
> submission by association. Where disclosure is permitted, show truthful staff
> attribution, review status and remaining action without exposing protected
> origin context. No new preview/download/history entitlement is created. Actual
> required affirmation needs a meaningful qualified presentation, never blind
> confirmation or a forged participant act.
>
> Provide authorized **Remove from this item** for the exact current unaccepted
> staff-added contribution while the target still permits it. Preserve its
> admission and withdrawal history, other evidence and shared bytes. If a
> replacement is offered, qualify an atomic withdrawal/addition with explicit
> lineage. A later check failure after committed replacement preserves that
> lineage without automatic predecessor revival. Ordinary removal never deletes a file, changes participant-supplied
> evidence, reverses acceptance or reopens fulfilled work. Accepted/closed mistakes
> use existing source remediation. An activated cohort must have usable recovery,
> not a support label that requires direct database repair.
>
> Order admission/removal, target review/correction/closure, relevant authority
> and custody disposition through the owning boundaries. Advance the relevant
> evidence basis and prevent stale acceptance or lost concurrent membership.
> Coherently commit the authorized use, retention/reference protection, required
> history and durable follow-up; keep provider work outside locks. Recover a
> matching committed operation before treating its resulting state as a new
> attempt, under current disclosure. Retries or duplicate current membership
> create no duplicate effect; changed material conflicts. Unknown outcomes never
> justify deleting shared bytes, repeating a transfer or asserting success.
>
> Preserve independent uses and actual continuing source restrictions. Later loss
> of the original staff member's read role alone does not erase a valid target
> use. Restrictions whose declared scope covers the target still apply; copying
> bytes is no bypass. Unlinking one use cannot purge another use or hold, and
> retention is not access. Participant expiry alone need not stop authorized
> staff work, but source intake prohibitions remain. Reuse never renews participant
> access or invents historical/completed-read eligibility; Q06–Q19 remain intact.
>
> Explicitly include a newly admitted staff contribution/replacement that creates
> genuinely source-actionable ordinary review in Q24's existing work and Q27's
> qualified opt-in notice meaning. Preserve Q27's original policy/cohort binding,
> Off rule and same-basis deduplication. Removal alone updates/fences work without
> inventing mail. Q26 applies only to a genuine Request changes decision. No new
> bell, participant notice default, reminder or approval ceremony is added.
>
> Use the existing accessible request context: **Add existing file**, an eligible
> exact-version choice, **Add to this item**, truthful Checking/Awaiting review
> and source-owned recovery. Keep supported tenant role/source flexibility and
> ordinary review. No forced re-upload, long explanation, new account, routine
> second approver, automatic matching, bulk workflow or global policy editor.
> Activate only after real authorization, database, race, disposal, replay,
> migration and intended-user/accessible-inspection proof for the declared cohort.

## Ownership, invariants and lifecycle

**Authority:** P29 custody owns immutable bytes/integrity and physical copies;
the original source owns provenance and applicable release/use restrictions;
the target source owns the new contribution, evidence set, review, withdrawal
and fulfillment. P12 decides current powers. P9/My Work projects source work;
P17/P6 own qualified notices. Search/cache/preview/provider URLs are projections
or evidence, never a second write authority.

**Invariants:** one deliberate staff act is never a participant act; exact byte
identity is not business equivalence; duplicate current equivalent membership
does not create another event; historical distinct acts remain distinct. Only
current exact authorized evidence is accepted. Unaccepted withdrawal preserves
history and other uses. Every live admitted use is protected against physical
disposition according to its owner. Continuing restrictions apply at their
declared scope. Fulfillment, participant access and delivery are independent.
