## Outcome and principal findings

A resource review target is a planning signal. Passing it must neither withdraw an independently lawful publication nor falsely certify its current content. Keep actual resource review, next target, publication/version, safety/rights, reader access and retention separate. Add a small P28-owned review/date capability to the existing curator journey; neither generic task dates nor an existing Reviewed label establishes that command.

The most important amendments are: exact publication-bound review evidence; honest optional scheduling; current-target advisory semantics; source-zone civil dates; genuine review versus rescheduling/factual correction; source-authorized repair of an erroneous review record; capable stewardship after departure; and native current-authorization/history/replay guarantees. The control must work without another review engine, approval stage, mandatory essay, fake upload, recurring schedule or notification program.

**Final product judgments agreed with root:**

- Ordinary next-review target is optional. Known deliberate absence is **No review scheduled**, not Never expires or unknown. Actual stronger source policy can require a date and prohibit Clear.
- Record review is one deliberate source act, binding exact current publication/review basis and trusted acceptance time. It atomically records the explicit next-target choice: today/future civil date or No review scheduled. An unchanged future target may remain when visibly confirmed. No hidden rollover, silent clearing or backdated new review.
- Change review date is a separate planning act. A legitimate prospective reschedule may change the current due projection while retaining the old/new target, actor and change time in source history. Last reviewed remains unchanged. No Fresh/Verified/Reviewed replacement badge.
- Due is based on the complete chosen source-zone calendar day: with known target D, it is due when the trusted current civil date in the captured qualified IANA zone is greater than D. Display-zone changes do not reinterpret a stored target.
- Publication may compose an actual explicitly declared review in one coherent source act. Plain Publish, Save, upload, scan or read never synthesizes a resource-review fact.
- A mistaken review has a linked attributed correction/remediation route. Retain original actor/time; derive current Last reviewed from applicable, noninvalidated review facts; atomically confirm the current target rather than roll back a newer target/publication/restriction.

These are proposed permanent source amendments, not claims that P28 already contains their commands or schema.

## Alternatives and restraint

The strongest rejected product alternative is B, making review date an additional publication cutoff. It is coherent for an intentional periodic assurance rule, but Conrad selected A. No hidden review-date denial, overdue default filter that removes all due items, or “safe until reviewed” block may implement B under A.

A stronger **sticky missed-review** variant was also considered: once a target passes, retain Review due until actual review despite rescheduling. I initially raised that as a possible safeguard, then rejected it after comparison. It introduces a second planned date/continuing obligation and extra cycle semantics that A did not select. The simpler current-target model is truthful if actual Last reviewed stays separate, changes are attributable, no automatic date reset exists and no green freshness guarantee is inferred. A past target remains a historical missed plan; changing the current plan does not erase that fact or assert a review occurred.

Requiring a next date on every publication/review is another stronger policy. It is not derived from the founder's requirement to support review-date metadata. An explicitly optional ordinary target, with visible known absence and stronger actual source requirements preserved, gives staff flexibility without manufacturing a universal cadence. Likewise, a generic review platform, reviewer-only role, task per target, reminder program or automated verification agent is unnecessary.
