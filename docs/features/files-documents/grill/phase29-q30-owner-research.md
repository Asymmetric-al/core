## Recommended next question

**While an ordinary native document request remains unfinished and the participant has current eligible access, may that person ask a text-only question beside the request/item, or only reply when invited, or use the existing help route?**

- **A — Allow questions and replies (recommended).** The eligible participant may initiate a clarification about the existing request/item and reply to deliberately shared staff guidance in that context. Staff use an explicit participant-safe reply action. Internal exact-version review comments remain separate.
- **B — Replies only when staff invites clarification.** Staff deliberately opens a narrow opportunity to answer; the participant cannot start an unsolicited native question. This limits incoming work while retaining contextual explanation when staff needs it. It costs staff an extra initiation step and leaves pre-submission questions on the help route.
- **C — Keep native collaboration internal.** Staff retain the required exact-version review comments; participants receive Q23's actionable guidance and use the qualified, accountable help/contact route for questions. This is a complete alternative only if that route can actually resolve the request, preserves permitted context and does not demand an unnecessary new upload.

Example: a participant is asked to replace a statement because a reviewer thinks a page is missing. The participant can explain that the requested page is already present, or ask which period is required. Q23 already allows staff to reassess the same exact evidence and forbids demanding fabricated new bytes. A makes that exchange easier to keep with the affected item. No owner contract found requires all such clarification to become Support email.

The recommendation is product judgment, not a claim of measured Asym usage. A is stronger than page/region annotation as the next question because it decides a missing participant action in the core collection-to-review journey, with a clear alternative and direct effects on staff workload, disclosure and recoverability. Pointer mechanics can follow. It is also stronger than reopening missionary-resource curation: the live Phase 28 formal package already assigns that library to authorized organization staff and expressly rejects an ordinary extra moderator and missionary submission queue.

## Why this is genuinely unresolved

Q29 adds paired reading only and does not amend them. A/B therefore require an explicit new founder choice. C preserves the presently selected native participant scope without removing mandatory internal comments or accountable help.

## Independent challenge of A

- **Workload is real.** A adds incoming questions that need ownership. If ministries cannot reliably monitor source work, an attractive composer could produce worse outcomes than C's staffed help route. Q24/25 responsibility helps identify an owner but is not measured response capacity or a public SLA.
- **Participants may send substantive new facts.** “Text is not automatically evidence” must not become “ignore text while accepting the old file.” A source may need to deliberately revise guidance/review state or accept new qualified evidence through its own command. A cannot bypass Q21/Q23 current-basis checks; it also cannot silently introduce a blanket comment-causes-review-lock rule.
- **Question initiation and replies differ.** B is a meaningful middle choice for organizations wanting controlled response opportunities. A's benefit before submission must be stated openly; selecting it is broader than merely letting someone answer a returned-item note.
- **History and expiry can surprise.** A narrow live exchange is not a permanent mailbox. Participants need truthful remaining access and the owned route once access ends. Do not promise a late staff answer will be visible after the grant ends, or extend the grant merely to deliver it.
- **Private discussion can leak through convenience.** Reused bytes, a team assignment, shared folder or a single “Comments” pane cannot combine source-purpose audiences. Separate composition and retrieval are required even if the visual treatment is unified.
- **C is not no help.** It retains deliberate actionable guidance and a capable help path. Its cost is context switching and possible staff reconciliation; do not claim A has measured faster completion or that C forces re-upload.

These challenges do not defeat A. They justify a bounded native clarification product with real source ownership and preserve B/C as credible alternatives. No settled owner clause requires choosing C merely because the participant capability is unbuilt.
