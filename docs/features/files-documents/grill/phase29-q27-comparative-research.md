## Early independent recommendation

The useful next fork is **whether to actively notify the responsible staff when new or corrected evidence becomes source-qualified actionable review work**, alongside the already required current request/My Work view. It is not whether review work should exist, whether staff have permission to inspect it, or whether every uploaded file should send an email.

The parent proposes the following final lean pair, subject to owner confirmation of the exact source occurrence/audience. Recommend **A — staff email initially off, with tenant opt-in**, as a cautious ordinary default alongside existing current work, while retaining the strong case for B:

## The source distinctions the question must make clear

1. **Receipt is not review readiness.** A source can truthfully receive immutable submitted or corrected evidence while required safety or usability checks remain pending. If the proposed alert promises that a human can review, it needs the actual qualified review-ready predicate. Do not announce Ready merely because storage upload finished, a scan callback fired, or a file row exists. Conversely, an unsupported preview is not necessarily unreviewable when the source permits an exact original and necessary inspection is possible under Q21.

2. **Item work is not whole-request completion.** A multi-file item can require review of an exact evidence set; unrelated outstanding request items need not universally block review of that item. FileInvite's all-documents threshold must not silently add an all-request gate to Core. The founder should understand whether the new notice is about a meaningful current item or a whole request, not discover that later from message volume.

3. **New work is not a new task per file or per team member.** Preserve Q24's one logical work identity and current responsibility. A new qualified correction may be a distinct source occurrence even if a familiar item is already on the work list. Technical retries, rereads, rerenders and repeated callbacks are not new work. This does not invent a cooldown or digest to dedupe genuinely different decisions.

4. **Responsibility is not notification authority.** A qualified review team does not automatically authorize email to everyone, a shared mailbox, the creator, all admins or former members. The new staff-notice meaning needs a declared finite audience resolver and safe facts. It must not reintroduce a mandatory personal claim just to acquire an email recipient. If no eligible audience can be established, preserve source work and use existing capable recovery instead of inventing a fallback recipient.

5. **Source work stays actionable independently of delivery.** Both A and B retain current request/My Work visibility and source authority. Notification failure/off/suppression does not remove evidence, recreate work, prevent valid review, change recipient clocks or manufacture reviewer acknowledgment. Delivery or opening is not proof that the human accepted responsibility or inspected evidence.

6. **Recipient submission is not consent to a broadcast.** The participant should not have to choose staff recipients, write a separate email, know internal team membership or manually trigger review after completing the actual source submission. A staff-awareness notice must expose only its qualified minimal facts and a current protected route. Q26's optional recipient-facing email control is a different direction and source event.

7. **Currentness survives handoff.** Responsibility, evidence and authority may change before dispatch or opening. Preserve actual source/plan identity and current access. Do not let old email assign, accept, claim, reopen or display a superseded evidence set. The exact behavior of a newly qualified staff recipient after reassignment belongs to its source/notification contract, not a generic email address substitution.

These are boundaries for a coherent next question, not a full 22-category amendment package before the user chooses. Source conditions and audience must be concrete enough for the choice to be meaningful; implementation-level thresholds and proof belong to the selected answer's review.

## Practical example and lean behavior

Illustrative example, not an observed ministry workflow: Nora supplies a corrected document after Elena requested changes. The current source establishes that the exact evidence now supports the next ordinary review step. The request's qualified review responsibility is still Elena or an eligible team route.

- In both options, the existing request/My Work entry shows the current work and current responsibility; there is no second task for each upload member or each team member.
- With A, the source's qualified optional staff-email step initially stays off. An authorized tenant can enable it when the staff handoff benefits from email. A staff member using the existing work list can complete the same review normally.
- With B, the same work is visible and the qualified staff-email step initially starts on, subject to tenant opt-out and actual eligibility. The declared staff audience may receive a minimal notice leading to the existing work context. Nobody has to open a mail composer or require Nora to send a separate handoff.
- If Elena is already reviewing when Nora corrects the relevant evidence, the existing Q03 currentness warning still protects Elena's view and acceptance under either option. Email settings cannot hide that source change or let stale evidence be accepted.
- The recipient's successful submission message says what the source actually received. It does not say that Elena read it, promise a review time or make email delivery a condition of receipt.
- If Elena already reviewed the updated evidence before a delayed notice is opened, the doorway shows current authorized truth. It does not restore the old review or demand another acceptance click.
- If a team cannot currently resolve an eligible notice audience, the system does not silently broadcast or undo the submission. Existing source-owned responsibility recovery remains visible to authorized staff.

For tenant setup, reuse an event-specific P17 entry with the actual supported audience/channel and clear effective state. Do not add a per-submission staff alert checkbox to the recipient form, a mandatory staff inbox setup step to every review, a new read/unread queue, an auto-claim action or a notification preview requiring access to private documents.

## Strongest alternative, costs and sequencing

**Why A is defensible:** current work already exists in the source-backed request/My Work context, and the ordinary default need not automatically produce another external communication. Extra email can duplicate visible work, interrupt concentration and expose additional metadata outside the protected application. FileInvite's submission default and Content Snare's explicit volume controls support taking this cost seriously. The tenant can opt in within the actual qualified envelope. This is not an assumption that all staff work continuously in Core or a claim that email is unnecessary.

**Why B is strong:** once review is required and the evidence supports it, active awareness can help someone away from the application return to the right work. Corrections can arrive after a previous review, and intermittent staff may reasonably rely on email to know when action is needed. Existing My Work visibility does not prove that the human notices it. These are real tradeoffs, not measured ministry prevalence or proof that current My Work is inadequate. B retains tenant control and must not be portrayed as indiscriminate email.

**No-build fallback:** if the native staff-notice purpose cannot yet be qualified, existing current source work and its capable responsibility/recovery remain the minimum truthful product. A separate known lawful staff communication path can be used only if already authorized; it does not justify copying file links or claiming native alerts work. This is an implementation/activation limit, not proof that either option's complete tenant-configurable capability is ready.

**Recommended next question:** one staff-awareness default, with the source meaning and exact initial channel/audience declared. Do not simultaneously ask for first-submission versus correction notification, per-file frequency, digest window, queue sorting, round-robin assignment, acknowledgement deadlines, escalation, count quota or a new notification service. If owner review shows that a fundamental source occurrence or audience choice is not yet supportable as a coherent profile, resolve that material product fork first rather than burying it in a default setting.

This remains more useful immediately after Q26 than another access-clock question. It should also remain bounded: once ordinary collection, review and the two-way handoff are coherent, move on to the still-unfinished broader File Manager/media/library work rather than expanding into every possible notification event.
