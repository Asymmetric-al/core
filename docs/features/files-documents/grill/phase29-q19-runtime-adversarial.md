## Seven narrow safeguards

**S01 — Sequential effects, exact context, no hidden quota.** The selected policy permits only separately reviewed current source-authorized new periods after the relevant prior period's eligible ordinary expiry. Nonoverlap spans the same tenant/environment/source/request/intended-recipient/original-completed-admission lineage, including different staff keys and disjoint member/action subsets. It does not prohibit unrelated source rights or compatible credential overlap inside one admission. There is no preset repeat-count/cumulative-day ceiling, mandatory waiting gap or advance reservation; there is also no promise of later approval or uninterrupted availability. Q18's full scoped profile applies independently to each actual new effect.

**S02 — Recover original commitment before evaluating new issuance.** Match stable deliberate identity and bound material payload; recover any committed outcome on its original occurrence/end/history under current disclosure before current-predecessor/new-admission checks. A later current grant does not invalidate that old receipt or become its substitute. Failed responses, unused periods and failed delivery do not undo a committed admission; definitive pre-admission rollback creates none; unknown commitment/lookup stays unknown. Different transport keys alone cannot create new deliberate intent, and a changed payload cannot replace the original silently.

**S03 — No ghost approval or automatic lineage advance.** A genuinely new/uncommitted intent must still match the materially reviewed current predecessor/source context and current authority at admission. An intervening admission changes that basis even if it has since expired; do not silently move an old approval to the next generation. Use the existing meaningful refreshed review when required. Mere unchanged passage of time is not by itself a reason for a second confirmation. Do not turn an early attempt into a stored reservation/scheduled future grant. Ordinary request processing crossing the exclusive boundary must be judged by the source-qualified occurrence/current checks, not an invented client-click cutoff or positive cooldown.

**S04 — One source-owned concurrent invariant.** Atomically serialize the relevant new-admission/current-state transition against competing intents and relevant issuer/recipient/purpose/scope/restriction/disposition changes, including the absent-next-row case. Preserve exact same-context predecessor/baseline/effect links and original outputs. Existing source/P12 fences, constrained relations and a bounded authoritative head/version or equivalent can suffice; no schema/global mutex is prescribed. A status flag, per-key advisory lock, worker lease, disabled button or provider dedupe is not the whole invariant. Conflicts/retries retain stable intent and revalidate current facts without provider work under the lock.

**S05 — Preserve ceiling, ending cause and current scoped usability.** Prove the relevant predecessor's actual eligible ordinary expiry with its own immutable terms and source cause; do not select any old expired row while a newer relevant outcome controls. Fresh scope remains the fixed Q16 completed ceiling intersected with current eligibility/new review, not expired-grant union or a last-subset ratchet. Whole-purpose revocation/shortening/disposal remains binding; member-only restrictions or technical failure cannot be converted into a permanent whole-context ban. No gap backfill, new F, intake reopening or extra completed stage. Current proof/metadata/byte/stream protection remains Q01/Q04/Q05/P12/Q16–18, not proof that the old recipient grant is still live.

**S06 — Trust and data enforcement across every writer.** Derive actor/tenant/administrative authority server-side; client-selected handles do not certify predecessor, historical scope, current recipient or grant type. Exact baseline/predecessor/member/action/recipient relations and legal finite timing must survive INSERT/UPDATE/upsert, final BEFORE-trigger transforms, parent mutation, direct/RPC/definer/service/job paths and restore. Existing RLS/grants must be assessed effectively rather than by keywords. Required effect/audit/follow-up remains coherent; general logs and cache invalidation cannot provide missing source truth. Private replay/constraint errors/counts/old history must not leak a different context.

**S07 — Bounded growth, lawful retention and recovery.** Resolve exact current source/head and original baseline without sorting arbitrary timestamps or reconstructing every prior period on each use. Keep any materialized head/index consistent and source-owned; missing/conflicting proof closes affected issuance/use instead of guessing from “latest.” Bounded staff/recipient projections follow their separate disclosure rights; filter before counts/order/pagination. Preserve minimum required semantic outcome/adverse evidence under the existing records policy, without extending file retention or building a raw audit archive. After lawful evidence disposal, inability to prove a needed effect is not permission to manufacture it. Mixed versions/restore must not revive a prior head, discard intervening restrictions, duplicate an old deliberate effect or replay providers. Native qualification still gates activation.

These requirements specialize repeated admission. They do not impose another routine approval, a grant settings page, quota engine, new identity role, SQL extension, storage copy, renewal worker or historical-file browser. They also do not make each source restriction globally irreversible beyond its actual owner contract.

## Exact PostgreSQL and privilege semantics

- FOR UPDATE locks retrieved rows, not a nonexistent next grant. At READ COMMITTED, target-row rechecking does not certify an independently assembled multi-row source predicate. ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING can lose to a concurrent row invisible to its statement snapshot; an empty return is not proof of no committed effect. Use the source's actual shared invariant and whole-operation stable retry. [Locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/explicit-locking.html), [isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html)
- Index expressions/predicates require immutable functions. A partial unique index based on `expires_at > now()` is not a valid moving-time uniqueness mechanism, and falsely labeling a time-dependent helper immutable would not fix it. A status-based unique index needs source-qualified transition/current-time handling; its entries do not change merely because wall time passes. [CREATE INDEX](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createindex.html)
- UPDATE/ALL omitted WITH CHECK can inherit USING; omission alone is not a vulnerability. Inspect applicable permissive OR/restrictive AND composition, resulting rows after BEFORE transforms and command/column/SELECT/RETURNING requirements. An allowed row-level update still needs the native historical-meaning invariants. [CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html)
- Definer functions run as their owner; bypass depends on actual role/table ownership/FORCE/BYPASSRLS and privileges. Browser revocations do not prove service-owned invariants. In the inspected Eve caller, actor parameters are built from server auth; this review does not mislabel them arbitrary browser-controlled values. [Row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html), [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security)
- An ID FK proves referenced existence, not the full tenant/source/recipient/predecessor relationship. Child-only trigger checks require parent mutation and final transformed-row qualification; cross-row CHECK cannot be the invariant, and CHECK/null behavior is not NOT NULL validation. NOW is transaction-start time after a wait as before it; preserve Q17/Q18's qualified source clock and finite precision. [Constraints](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-constraints.html), [time functions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/functions-datetime.html)

Primary PostgreSQL locking/isolation/index docs were rechecked for this review; the other precise policy/time semantics were checked in this ongoing Q16–Q18 session and applied to the re-read paths. PostgreSQL 17 is the local-config/documentation major, not a claimed hosted version. No live grants/roles/catalog/constraint execution was tested.

## Meaningful future qualification cases

These belong at the real native source/transaction/serving boundary once implemented; **none was run here**. Do not substitute a mirror model, source-string assertion or adjacent helper mock for their outcomes.

1. **Both origins/normal repeats:** first fresh and subsequent fresh admissions for both Q16 origins preserve the same completed baseline, independently resolved Q18 outputs and correct eligible predecessor. No fixed lifetime quota is accidentally imposed; many periods do not become a guarantee or extended retention.
2. **Distinct simultaneous intents:** two staff actors with different keys, two tabs, overlapping subsets and disjoint subsets compete against the same ended context. At most the source-permitted sequential result exists; loser cannot silently create the next period. Include first-fresh/absent-head/absent-next-row races and relevant source-row deletion/retarget.
3. **Old committed replay:** retry an original intent after it ended and one or more newer effects exist. Recover the exact old outcome before new-current-head checks, then return only currently permitted disclosure. Do not return the newest grant or claim old success is live access.
4. **Uncommitted stale approval:** an earlier reviewed predecessor becomes stale through an intervening effect that later expires. The old distinct intent cannot auto-advance or create a new key. Material refreshed review differs from ordinary unchanged time passing; no per-request confirmation loop or hidden age limit is introduced.
5. **Boundary and reservation:** just before/at/after exclusive expiry, late lock release, serializable retry and delayed processing retain qualified source G/end and after-wait currentness. No positive cooldown or client-click time rule; no stored pre-expiry reservation, scheduler-driven renewal or unauthorized gap backfill.
6. **Outcome uncertainty/durability:** actual rollback, successful commit/lost response, failed outcome query, required audit/follow-up failure and failed secondary delivery have distinct truthful outcomes. No ambiguity creates capacity, deletes possible admitted evidence or silently reissues. Provider retry occurs outside locks with preserved source identity.
7. **Real authorization/data paths:** actual recipient/staff/delegated/service/job roles, direct table/RPC/view/definer paths and INSERT/UPDATE/upsert/parent transforms reject cross-tenant/root/recipient/member/predecessor substitution and unauthorized historical/end changes. Current admin/assignable ceiling and actual continuing dependencies—not generic staff/read access—govern new issuance.
8. **Scope/adverse history:** baseline A+B, prior subset A, later eligible subset B; later retained C; exact superseded predecessor; status-only; member-only restriction; whole-purpose revocation; ordinary expiry coincident with an adverse cap. No union/ratchet, global false ban, fallback file promise or bypass through waiting/new ID.
9. **Credential and every-door current use:** shorter session/link replacement within the same period does not count as or issue a source period. Expired/revoked credentials cannot resolve to the latest grant as a bypass. Supported current proof reuse remains. Metadata/count/order/replay/cache/HEAD/range/resume/active bytes obey their current source authority and actual applicable end.
10. **History growth/retention/restore:** production-shaped long histories use bounded exact current proof and authorized pagination within declared owner budgets. Corrupt/dangling/stale heads, lawfully missing proof, old code, restore and policy transitions fail affected use safely without old-head revival or duplicate outward effects. Prove coherent reconstructable indexes without inventing new authority or requiring all historical file bodies.
