## Main conclusion and strongest alternative

A is proportionate when the source actually knows the governing rule. Automatic application must mean authoritative resolution of a qualified source policy, not a preselected radio answer, template field or guessed legal period. When the source deliberately requires a choice among fully approved compatible alternatives, a qualified actor still chooses. Missing policy content or ambiguous applicability is a different state and cannot be fixed by choosing the first or longest policy.

The important amendment is a complete binding lifecycle: truthful historical admission, current authoritative application, explicit later successor impact and a narrow actual correction of mistaken assignment. Otherwise a small default interaction can either freeze obsolete rules forever or make a live default silently rewrite retained records. Neither outcome is acceptable. The ordinary correction proposed here is new narrow source behavior; it is not inherited P18/P21 administrator authority or a universal per-file policy editor.

The strongest B remains explicit confirmation/selection on every new request. It makes policy context conspicuous but repeats a decision when the source has exactly one lawful result and does not prove that the clicker understood or could determine the rule. A retains deliberate choice where it is real and visible explanation where a fact is already resolved. No evidence establishes measured Asym error reduction, routine request volume or staff preference; this is a reasoned product judgment.

## Sixteen exact proposed source clauses

**O01 — Scope, eligibility and actual policy content.** Apply selected A to newly admitted eligible ordinary request-evidence bindings whose actual source has a complete qualified applicable records policy. The source owns purpose, family/applicability, policy approval/version/effectivity, allowed actor choices and current resulting obligation; P29 performs qualified custody execution. This decision does not populate missing schedules or make all ordinary purposes ready. Preserve specialist owners, all Q01–Q61 meanings and both glossaries. Where a material policy field is missing, identify the exact affected cohort, responsible source/records authority and evidence needed; do not invent a fallback period or a new generic rule system.

**O02 — Default, required choice and unavailable are distinct.** When trusted current source context resolves a valid approved default, apply it without an extra required confirmation, showing its permitted governing meaning in normal review. Offer only fully approved compatible alternatives that this actor may actually choose. If the source deliberately supplies no default but expressly requires and authorizes an individual choice among those alternatives, request that choice before the consequential act. Known selection-required is not unresolved applicability. Unknown, incompatible, conflicting or unapproved policy cannot be cured by a dropdown, arbitrary first/shortest/longest selection, empty value, generic administrator or checkbox. Preserve real source-required individual determinations under A.

**O03 — Purpose admission before every durable consequence.** Earlier durable request preparation, saved private prose, staging allocation, upload or expensive processing must have its own actual source-purpose admission and applicable obligations. A later issuance summary cannot retroactively authorize those records. Keep independently lawful inert preparation available; do not require the final evidence binding to authorize a separately admitted staging purpose, or treat a blocked new issue as permission to retain preparation forever. At actual new source issuance resolve the reviewed intended purpose/choice under current source authority and establish the required authoritative binding/history/result coherently. Existing receipt/intake/worker acts retain their own current checks and source rights; Q62 adds no broad retrospective access cutoff.

**O04 — Review meaningful consequences, not stale browser state.** The normal summary states the safe governing policy, covered source purpose/classes, actual start meaning and relevant limitations; no false single deletion date where events or obligations differ. Validate selected compatible alternatives and expected relevant source/assignment basis after waits. A changed governing purpose, eligibility, selected variant or consequential policy meaning cannot silently be substituted at issuance. Refresh and explain that change while preserving safe input. Identical effective explanation need not trigger another approval or confirmation ritual; the source still establishes the actual current version and expected basis. An unchanged name is not proof of unchanged policy, and an unrelated metadata revision alone is not a policy change. Use actual source-defined fields, not a new generic relevance classifier.

**O05 — Historical admission and current application are separate facts.** Retain the exact policy/contract/binding version, resolved source context, actual selected/default origin, typed trigger evidence and trusted actor/time required to explain each admitted consequence. An issued request's historical binding is not an instruction to keep every later record on that version forever. Each evidence/history/technical/copy class retains its own actual qualified resolution and current governing applicability. A default configuration edit affects newly qualifying work as defined by its source; it neither silently rewrites prior admissions nor automatically applies a different policy to all existing bytes. Do not infer that one request summary replaces per-class obligations.

**O06 — Qualified successors require actual impact and current enforcement.** A law, policy, source fact or approved binding change affecting retained records uses the responsible source's explicit versioned successor/impact path. Identify its bounded affected scope and exact prior/current basis; preserve prior policy/trigger facts and append the resulting current applicability. Order successor/correction, new admission and final disposition at the actual source/custody boundary. A stale permissive disposer cannot continue because only a request label was updated. If impact cannot be safely resolved immediately, block the affected irreversible operation and report the specific reconciliation state under its actual owner; do not claim complete application or strand an ownerless queue. No blanket grandfathering, immediate mass deletion, new generic scheduler or universal grace period is selected.

**O07 — Actual mistaken-assignment correction.** Add a narrow ordinary-source **Correct policy assignment** remedy for a specified erroneous admitted binding, under separately qualified current source/records correction authority. It reviews the exact target/expected current binding, truthful error basis and proposed actually applicable approved result; the cause can come from trusted source context or necessary concise input, without an automatic essay/second approval. Preserve the original admission and append correction actor/time, cause, prior/resulting binding and actual affected scope/results. Correct a wrong policy assignment, not the policy's legal content, request recipient or underlying source purpose by fiat. Unsupported or ambiguous applicability goes to its actual approving owner; the issuer need not receive administration powers or hidden records. This is an explicit proposed ordinary amendment, not borrowed P18/P21 extension authority.

**O08 — Correction repair and consequences remain narrow.** A mistaken assignment correction is repaired through another current authorized attributed correction over the current basis, not rollback, deleting history or replaying an earlier binding as current. Corrected timing must derive from proved original trigger facts and the applicable rule, never the edit time merely for convenience. Preserve independent holds, other uses, access restrictions, source decisions, acceptance/F/cancel and any separate required correction. Shortening or lengthening is allowed only through the actual qualified resulting rule, with all floor/ceiling/hold/disposition requirements. Report Assignment corrected separately from any genuinely pending custody/disposition work. No claim to restore lawfully destroyed bytes or control external copies follows; irrecoverable loss uses actual incident/source remediation and truthful limitation.

**O09 — Original intent, ordering, no-op and finite replay.** Bind every actual issue/choice-application/correction/successor operation to its source, target, material reviewed intent and expected relevant basis. Reconcile a matching committed operation before applying new eligibility, while separately authorizing current result disclosure. Changed material intent under the same identity conflicts; unknown outcome reconciles instead of choosing a new key. A new same-current-effective assignment may be a true no-op after current authority/basis validation: no new visible policy history, date, retention start, source occurrence or downstream work. Stale expected basis, including change-away-and-back, is not rescued by identical labels. A no-op that changes no governing revision does not prevent a later legitimate current-basis change. Retain only lawful finite replay/control evidence and reject unverifiable old intent safely rather than recreating it after purge or restore.

**O10 — Clocks, copies and independent purpose remain explicit.** Requested by, original/fresh access ends, review due, receipt, checking, acceptance, F/cancellation, withdrawal, technical-copy expiry, policy resolution and physical disposition are different facts. A known not-yet-occurred trigger is different from missing/unknown evidence; no substitute time is guessed. Shared exact bytes, Q28 reuse, Q53 staff origin and Q56 selected originals/output retain all independently admitted use/hold obligations. History, communication, security audit, preview, export, staging and backup copy classes keep their actual purposes; one UI policy choice does not lengthen or shorten them indiscriminately. A hold retains evidence without granting ordinary read/use, and release of one hold is not disposal approval.

**O11 — Current rights and protected disclosure.** Separate authority to prepare/issue, view summary/details/history, select an approved variant, administer policy, correct assignment, apply/release holds and execute disposition. Use P12 current selected-context authority and one shared static field classification/subtract-only projection; no fake Party, role-label grant, union of hats or new fine-grained SQL engine. Trusted Tenant/environment/source/actor owns mutations; caller IDs are validated selectors. Apply current purpose/field restrictions before labels, options, counts, error detail, comparison/history, result recovery, export and byte/derived serving. Protect internal legal/hold/safety details; a participant explanation is separately authorized. A policy summary neither grants file access nor exposes another source use. The new missionary-read floor remains binding where actually consumed.

**O12 — Structural integrity and all-door qualification.** The source must enforce typed same-scope references, required policy/version/class/target relations, actual effect uniqueness/current winner and immutable historical attribution. Prevent an authorized update from changing Tenant/source/recipient, backdating the actor, inventing an approved policy or stripping independent constraints. Coherently persist source effects, required history, original result and mandatory durable follow-through; providers do not run inside human/long source transactions. Prove actual table/column/sequence/schema/function/named/PUBLIC/default/inherited grants, policy composition, old and resulting USING/WITH CHECK, SELECT/RETURNING, UPSERT/MERGE/bulk, trigger transformations, views/RPCs/GraphQL, definer owners/search paths/FORCE/BYPASS and service/NHI/import/worker/restore paths. Application/database Tenant context agrees and resets on pooled success/error/cancel. Zero rows or a cached policy flag is not success.

**O13 — Small complete UI.** Use the existing ordinary preparation/review surface and source-local current binding/history controls. A is a visible governing fact, not a required preselected classification answer; only a real choice uses selection controls. One normal deliberate Issue suffices, and one deliberate qualified Correct assignment suffices for repair. Detail expansion, typing, radio changes, blur, focus, IME composition, browser Back and Cancel remain inert until their actual deliberate source action. Preserve safe draft, choice, focus and return context across refresh/failure; invalidate private stale state after context/permission loss. Show safe policy unavailable, selection required, changed basis, original result unknown, assignment corrected and actual pending consequence distinctly. Provide a current capable maintainer route where the issuer lacks authority; no routine SQL/provider-console workaround or powerless Contact admin promise.

**O14 — Accessible, bounded implementation over existing sources.** Qualify keyboard/AT/touch/non-hover operation, visible labels and focus, restrained status messages, Core touch targets, narrow-screen/zoom, RTL/CJK/long names, localized actual source time and weak-network recovery. Use Core's actual shared components and stable source identifiers, not fixture policy names, client-only booleans or unlicensed demo behavior. Query only bounded authorized compatible options/history with stable unique continuation and current per-page/set-wise permission; no per-file policy N+1, whole-history scan, global default lock or generic rule-language editor. Populate finite actual source workload, mutation/replay/impact/copy and operational budgets before activation; imported preview/image/official-policy numbers are not ordinary quotas.

**O15 — Narrow adoption and controlled rollout.** Ordinary source owners adopt these default/choice/binding/correction commands explicitly with their P12/P9/custody/records dependencies; P18/P21 and other specialists remain unchanged. Q39 templates admit only permitted definition/reference fields and resolve actual instance policy; no old request, folder, saved view or copied binding carries live authority. Earlier admitted records retain truthful original facts; backfill cannot invent a historical policy or trigger. Unknown legacy mapping remains an affected-cohort blocker, not deletion or blanket indefinite approval. Deploy compatible current readers/correction/disposition fences before dependent writers; scoped rollback stops new affected work without undoing committed bindings, holds or decisions. Restore reconciles later policy/disposition/adverse history before serving or workers resume. No new notice purpose, automatic email or policy-change task is selected; independently required qualified existing effects retain their contracts.

**O16 — Qualification, vocabulary and stage.** Declare one actual ordinary cohort and its complete approved policy/variant/correction/successor profile before activation, including real accountable operators. Preserve unresolved class/applicability/trigger/floor/ceiling/hold/copy/legacy dimensions as exact source-policy blockers rather than guessed engineering values. Prove the positive, negative, boundary, currentness/race, original-result, all-role/all-door, physical-copy, mixed-version/restore, finite-load and composed-user outcomes below. This review does not run those native tests. A pending-review ADR0062 is appropriate because the chosen interaction and durable assignment/correction semantics are meaningful source behavior; it must not become a competing platform records specification. No new glossary term is needed. Full Q62 ratification, formal owner/OpenSpec publication and later tickets/implementation remain separate user-controlled stages.
