**C03 — Coherent combined intent without losing native work.** Persist the admitted native reply, exact staff choice, relevant original plan/recipient/notice terms, minimum required history and durable handoff coherently at the source boundary. **C04 — Permanent identity and complete release.** Bind one native send and one linked communication occurrence/effect with distinct purpose-appropriate durable identity. Delivery/open/click is neither human reading nor source acknowledgment/acceptance/fulfillment. Late/out-of-order/conflicting evidence follows P6's independent axes and quarantine, not a scalar overwrite.
