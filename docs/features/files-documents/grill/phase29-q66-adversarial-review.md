# Q66 — Global Fellowship worked example and tenant-controlled records policies

2026-10-29, Asia/Bangkok. **Fully ratified by Conrad: Q66 A, explicit full tenant flexibility/changeability and all required amendments.** Reviewed disposition: Accept with required amendments; the complete corrected decision is adopted.

Use the identified Global Fellowship drafts to work through real record-purpose distinctions. Give authorized tenants actual self-service ownership of ordinary document records policies: create, copy, edit, activate, change eligible existing applicability, correct mistakes, pause/resume automatic disposal, retire and reactivate. GF is evidence for the worked example, not a hardcoded policy, mandatory template, live Tenant identity or approved schedule.

Tenant control must mean more than choosing Asym presets. It also cannot mean altering past facts, granting access by editing retention settings, erasing independent obligations, ignoring a hold or claiming deleted bytes were restored. The permanent solution is a narrow source-owned policy lifecycle with current authorization, typed business fields and truthful change effects. It does not need a general rules language or another records platform.

The [ratified ADR0066](../docs/adr/0066-tenant-controlled-ordinary-document-records-policies.md) carries the identical decision and adopted Document Records Policy definition. The [applied consequential amendments](phase29-q66-consequential-amendments.md) preserve exact old/new wording for affected Q62/Q63/Q65 clauses. All other prior decisions, 34 earlier Files terms and the entire root glossary remain preserved; the supersession is explicit and historically traceable.

## The tenant experience and editable contract

Policy managers use an existing tenant/source settings context, not the ordinary request form. Start blank or copy an authorized definition; edit relevant sections; review the change and its scope; activate deliberately. Ordinary issuers continue seeing Q62's concise approved summary and using normal Issue. Reference users keep Q65's contextual Use/Open/Replace/End.

| Tenant can control                                           | Required meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Names, descriptions and readable summaries                   | Tenant language and terminology, including supported translations, without changing stable source capability or field meanings.                                                                                                                          |
| Supported ordinary record classes/purposes and applicability | Tenant-defined business groupings and values through typed, validated source scopes; labels alone are not legal classification or grants.                                                                                                                |
| Retention/review rules and units                             | Actual supported event/date, duration/bounds, legitimate review/continuing-purpose handling and source calendar/time-zone semantics. Shortening and lengthening are both possible when lawful; no imported vendor cap or permanent indefinite-mode lock. |
| Disposition mode                                             | Review/manual or expressly authorized automatic mode under the actual source contract and Q63 proof; missing approval is not a default mode.                                                                                                             |
| Effectivity, defaults and allowed variants                   | Clear current scope for new eligible uses; explicit qualified treatment of existing records and mandatory current duties. No arbitrary first-match policy precedence.                                                                                    |
| Responsible routes and approval evidence                     | Eligible tenant roles/teams through existing P12 authority and actual records responsibility. No universal extra approver or Asym approval ticket; a real source-required approval remains required.                                                     |
| Lifecycle and recovery                                       | Edit drafts, discard unused drafts lawfully, activate, revise, apply eligible existing changes, correct assignments, pause/resume disposal, retire and reactivate qualified unchanged versions.                                                          |

The actual field schema must support the selected business controls; “supported” cannot be used to offer only fixed presets or force Asym code edits for ordinary values. New platform capabilities, unsupported trustworthy event producers, legal authority outside the tenant, raw SQL, permission bypass and immutable past acts are different concerns and cannot be created by arbitrary form input.

Suggested controls: **New policy**, **Edit policy**, **Review changes**, **Activate version**, **Apply to existing records**, **Correct assignment**, **Pause automatic disposal**, **Resume**, **Retire**, and **Reactivate**. Show only actual permitted actions. This describes complete outcomes, not a demand to display ten buttons together. Place secondary actions in contextual menus and progressively disclose advanced fields.

An Edit action prepares the next draft for governing changes while the current effective version remains visible. A proved non-operative label/detail correction can use a simple attributed metadata save without version churn; field names alone do not prove a change is cosmetic. A consequence summary states “New eligible uses” and, when applicable, “Existing records: update required / in progress / blocked / completed.” A policy becoming active is not proof every existing record or physical copy has changed. Safe counts and examples require current disclosure; do not expose hidden holds or sensitive source records just to explain a restriction. No optimistic “All updated” or automatic deletion from a settings Save.

## Ratified decision and amendments

**Q66-C01 — Selected example and actual tenant control.** Use the identified GF drafts as the worked mapping example, retaining their actual unestablished approval/applicability status. Ordinary document records policy definitions and supported business values SHALL be tenant-owned and self-service for currently authorized tenant actors, including custom creation and later change; not hardcoded GF/Asym defaults, preset-only selection or routine Asym developer/support intervention. No GF body, private legal value, organization identity or approval is automatically shipped, copied or activated for other tenants.

**Q66-C02 — Scope, ownership and prior-contract reconciliation.** The ordinary source owns its tenant policy definitions, effective versions, applicability/defaults, bindings, change results and business history; P29 owns custody/disposition execution and P12 current authorization. Preserve specialist source owners and all Q01–Q65 invariants. This explicit authoring requirement extends the earlier no-general-editor selection limits in Q62/Q63/Q65 through the exact consequential amendments; it does not create a universal policy/permission/workflow engine. Platform-owned stable capability/field/source semantics may remain code-owned; ordinary tenant business rules and values are not thereby code-owned. No P18, P21 or projection-policy approval model is imported as universal ordinary authority.

**Q66-C03 — Complete structured configuration.** Support tenant-defined ordinary policy names/summaries, relevant classes/purposes/applicability, compatible trusted start events/dates and precision, actual retention/review bounds and units, disposition mode, effective scope/defaults/variants and eligible responsibility/approval references. Allow valid shortening, lengthening and changing between supported finite or continuing/review-based treatment when actual authority permits; no arbitrary vendor cap or permanent mode lock. Validate typed combinations, ambiguity, calendar semantics, supported representation and finite technical bounds. Do not accept arbitrary SQL/scripts, caller-created event meaning, free-form timers, legal conclusions or cross-source applicability by label. Multiple compatible policies/classes can coexist with unambiguous source resolution and independent obligations.

**Q66-C04 — Editable policy versus independent facts and duties.** Tenant editability does not rewrite original receipt, acceptance, fulfillment, historical binding, actor/time, prior effect or a third party's obligation. Source-qualified corrections and lawful successor changes remain possible with actual evidence and history. A policy edit cannot itself remove an independent hold, supersede a signed obligation, override independently required protection, grant read/share rights, reset a trigger or certify missing bytes restored. Actual duty/applicability mistakes must have their capable authorized correction/release route; do not make an erroneous constraint permanently uncorrectable or offer an unqualified bypass switch.

**Q66-C05 — Drafts, copying and preparation.** Permit blank creation and copying of authorized reusable definitions into inert tenant drafts without inherited approval, effectivity, actors, record assignments, holds or operational results. The GF worked example does not authorize public template distribution. Editing/saving/discarding a draft changes no effective record policy or disposal eligibility. Draft data has its own lawful purpose, access and cleanup; do not persist private notes or bytes before admission. No mandatory preview, reupload, policy questionnaire on each request or forced provider copy is introduced.

**Q66-C06 — Tenant authority and delegation.** Authoring, approval/activation, default administration, existing-record application, correction, hold/Stop handling, history/impact viewing and byte reading remain distinct actual source/P12 powers. Authorized tenant records actors can perform supported policy work without an Asym approval ticket; a single actor may complete a change when the actual source permits, with no universal second approver. Tenant responsibility/delegation uses existing eligible roles/teams and real authority, not original-creator-only controls or broad admin/service-role assumptions. Approval evidence may be referenced but cannot manufacture the current actor's authority. Unsupported self-escalation or changes to another tenant remain forbidden.

**Q66-C07 — Trusted current identity and context.** Derive tenant/environment/source and actual actor/author/approver attribution from validated native identity and current source/P12 context. Caller identifiers are validated selectors. Preserve actual supported limited/no-Party and system/NHI paths without inventing profiles, memberships or demo tenants. User metadata, stale JWT role claims, Drive IDs, organization names, document access or a service key are not authority. Recheck relevant facts after waits and before effect/result disclosure; application/database context must agree and reset through pooled success/error/cancel/retry paths.

**Q66-C08 — Policy lifecycle with truthful recovery.** Distinguish mutable draft, immutable activated meaning, current default/applicability, retired-for-new-use and disposal pause states. Changes to activated governing meaning require a successor draft; preserve the operative version until a qualified activation/change. A separately authorized, attributed non-operative metadata edit may change a label or description without a new semantic version only when the source proves no change to meaning, applicability, approval basis, instructions or the minimum safe consequence summary. Material or uncertain changes use a successor; a caller metadata-only flag is not proof. Preserve historical displays/provenance and current expected-basis/result checks. Retire stops new assignments within its admitted scope without deleting history, existing obligations, active-use protections or needed recovery. Permit deliberate Reactivate of an unchanged retired version under fresh current qualification, retaining retirement/reactivation history; changed meaning requires a successor. Do not force a duplicate version just to repair retirement, resurrect old command intent or silently reactivate by reading/restoring. Lawful unused-draft deletion is separate from retention of used policy evidence.

**Q66-C09 — Activation and temporal applicability.** Activate exact approved meaning/current authority and new-use scope through one deliberate reviewed source command. Prospectivity is the default for a discretionary default change, not immunity from a mandatory current duty. Preserve original assignments while applying actual lawful successor effectivity. Before affected irreversible consequences can proceed, establish the current governing result or a restrictive pending-impact fence for records whose obligations change; do not wait for optional UI cleanup while an old permissive disposer runs. Record actual governing versus recorded/activated dates truthfully, including qualified later discovery of an earlier effective duty. No arbitrary backdating or old-fact rewrite.

**Q66-C10 — Deliberate existing-record application.** Provide a real tenant-authorized Apply to existing records path with exact source/class/use scope, relevant expected basis and current compatible impact. Explain safe eligible/blocked/unknown scope and consequences before commitment; previews are not authority and may not leak hidden records. Never treat missing/denied coverage as zero, silently expand a cohort, skip incompatible records as success or let a label-only comparison hide changed meaning. Bounded durable processing may report partial completion with exact per-target outcomes and capable reconciliation; no global all-record transaction is required. Explicitly reviewed eligible subsets are allowed when the source permits and unresolved mandatory obligations remain safely fenced.

**Q66-C11 — Corrective changes without erasure.** Preserve Q62's source-authorized Correct policy assignment and actual trigger/applicability correction. Wrong versions, defaults or cohort changes are repaired through current attributed successor/applicability/correction operations, not history deletion or blanket Undo. Equal displayed policy/date can still be a real approval, scope, trigger or duty correction. Show current permitted facts and actual expected outcome; a new correction does not reset old clocks, release holds, alter Q65 evidence or pretend physically lost material recovered. Prior usable definitions may seed a new qualified change; prior operational authority never revives merely by copying them.

**Q66-C12 — Pause, resume and urgent containment.** Authorized tenants can pause new automatic-disposal admissions in the affected scope independently of invalid or unsaved policy drafts, impact jobs or replacement readiness. Commit the actual Stop boundary and distinguish acknowledged pause from earlier possibly dispatched work still requiring reconciliation. Pause grants no indefinite retention exemption and cannot satisfy a due privacy obligation; route that conflict to the capable owner. Resume requires current policy, authority, coverage, unresolved-effect checks and a deliberate current act; stale workers/commands cannot resume or dispatch through an old generation. No instant provider recall or physical restoration is promised.

**Q66-C13 — Distinct classes, triggers and representations.** Bind each actual ordinary record/use/copy class independently, including originals/assemblies, collection history, ongoing references, later corrections, messages, controls and required copies. Compatible shared definitions need not be duplicated, but reused bytes do not merge obligations or permission. Use trustworthy typed source events/dates, exact declared precision and approved calendar/time-zone semantics; distinguish not-yet-triggered, unknown, no applicable rule and legitimate continuing/review treatment. Views, copies, edits, policy publication, reference End and completion are not clocks unless the actual rule says so. Later records cannot inherit an elapsed old interval accidentally. No money/currency or posted financial facts change.

**Q66-C14 — Coherent effects, concurrency and idempotency.** Bind each consequential change to tenant/environment/source, policy/version/target generation, complete semantic intent and durable business-effect identity. Coherently commit authoritative facts, necessary control fences/history, original outcome and durable follow-through at a real source transaction or proved fence. Reconcile conflicting activation/default/impact/correction/Stop/hold/disposal operations and stale ABA intent at that boundary, rechecking after waits. Recover a matching committed original before new eligibility under current disclosure; changed intent conflicts, complete no-op preserves original attribution, and unverifiable expired intent fails safely. Repeat keys/events/chunks cannot duplicate effects, skip targets or reopen retired/paused state. No SDK callback, optimistic UI or long transaction spanning provider IO substitutes for proof.

Preserve complete current use/hold/processor/writer/copy coverage, Q63 finite dispatch permits, current generation, possible-effect fences, Stop, reconciliation and restore semantics. A tenant-owned policy for retention, review and disposal of specified ordinary document records and uses, subject to their applicable obligations. It is distinct from an individual record’s policy assignment, a hold and permission to access the records.
_Avoid_: access policy, legal certification, deletion timer

Adopt this ordinary Files term beside File Purpose. It distinguishes the business policy from P12 access policy and P18's specialized Records Schedule Contract. Draft, version and impact need no separate glossary entities. All 34 prior Files terms and the entire root glossary remain unchanged; Files now has 35 terms.
