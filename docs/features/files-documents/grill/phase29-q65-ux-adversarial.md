## Recommendation and early corrections

Use a deliberate contextual **Use for partner reference** action after actual source acceptance, available immediately or later from the qualified source context. Carry the already-known partner, exact accepted basis and approved purpose/policy meaning into a short normal review. Do not require reupload, opening the document, a legal essay, a new policy form or a second approver merely to add the use. Acceptance and handoff alone do not automatically add it.

The ongoing reader uses the admitted partner reference and current rights; they do not need permission to browse the original request's entire history. Conversely, seeing the partner record, having been the original reviewer or knowing an upload URL grants no reference authority. The original participant's finite access window remains independent.

**Cardinality refinement after root challenge:** do not impose one agreement per partner or one active reference per requirement lineage. Prevent an equivalent duplicate for the same actual partner target, purpose and exact accepted basis. Distinct legitimate needs, evidence units, versions or periods may coexist when the source permits. End or Replace targets a selected use/generation; neither action silently edits every reference to the bytes.

The simplest default is no ongoing use until deliberate admission. This is not an extra tenant settings matrix: the actual source capability and policy determine availability. A combined Accept/Use action is not required; a routine two-system transaction should not be invented to save one click. The source may later expose a genuinely coherent combined act only if its entire reviewed meaning and separate result contracts qualify. The selected minimum remains a complete contextual post-acceptance action.

No new archive, agreement validity register, automatic version-following service or folder/rename inheritance is needed. The existing source Files context supplies navigation. The actual source operation and rights must still be implemented and qualified; its name in this note is proposed user-facing language, not an assertion that an API already exists.

## Sixteen exact recommended amendments

**U01 — Preserve collection and add optional purpose.** Keep collection/review/handoff history and independent lawful uses unchanged. Ongoing partner reference is an additional explicit source-purpose use, not automatic inheritance from acceptance, handoff, partner membership or a file association.

**U02 — One deliberate contextual admission.** Offer the normal action after actual source acceptance, immediately or later. Carry known partner/context and valid policy facts forward; disclose relevant current consequences before commitment. No mandatory new upload, preview, legal essay, extra approver, global settings matrix or workflow installation.

**U03 — Exact evidence and legitimate cardinality.** Bind the actual partner target, purpose, exact accepted evidence set/version and current source basis. Support Q21 multi-file evidence and Q56 indivisible assemblies as qualified units. Suppress only equivalent duplicates; allow distinct legitimate needs/units/periods under source policy. No global one-agreement-per-partner rule.

**U04 — Current authority with separate projections.** Admission, read, end, replacement, history and privacy correction require their own actual source/P12 powers. Ongoing readers need the admitted reference and current permission, not unrestricted original-request browsing. Seeing the partner, being the author or possessing a URL supplies none of those rights. Keep no-Party/identity boundaries and tenant/environment context qualified.

**U05 — No false legal/current status.** Preserve exact acceptance provenance without certifying authenticity, legal validity, signature, representation, current operative agreement, partnership approval, membership or giving authority. No automatically followed latest file or in-place mutation of admitted evidence.

**U06 — Policy and copy protection before admission.** Qualify the continuing purpose and actual relevant policy/custody conditions before adding the use. Admission must coherently protect its exact inputs against disposal/reference races; already claimed or irreversibly removed copies cannot become usable by creating a row. Missing policy remains an affected-action gate, not an invented default or indefinite-retention instruction.

**U07 — Truthful result and replay.** Commit the source use, required attributable history and original result coherently. Unknown response recovers the original under current disclosure; retries and equivalent duplicates do not produce another use or shift dates. Changed semantic intent and stale expected basis conflict; do not infer success from zero affected rows or a transport response.

**U08 — Purpose-scoped return and unavailable states.** Use existing source-context navigation. Apply current field/byte projection to titles, counts, history, thumbnails, links, search, exports and recovered results. Known restriction and temporary evaluation failure remain distinct; no public fallback, automatic external viewer or cross-source context cache.

**U09 — End without deleting history or unrelated uses.** End the selected exact use/generation by a deliberate current-authorized act, independent of unrelated invalid draft fields. This ends that operational route, not original acceptance/history, another use, retention policy or all bytes. Preserve current end/result attribution and safe history under its own purpose.

**U10 — Wrong target and accidental end recovery.** Do not retarget an admitted use or restore an ended generation. End the wrong use and perform a fresh qualified admission for the right target. A mistaken end can use the same retained exact evidence only through a new current admission. Actual privacy/operator restrictions still apply; no generic Undo bypass.

**U11 — Explicit replacement and adverse evidence.** Replace a selected reference only through a qualified source act with reviewed old/new scope, current acceptance/rights and honest failure outcomes. No automatic latest following. Corrected/invalidated acceptance or current source restriction must affect reliance as its owner requires; retained history is not current eligibility. Known unsafe old use can be stopped independently of preparing a replacement.

**U12 — Separate all clocks and outside effects.** Reads, reference admission/end/replacement and partner status do not reset original receipt, acceptance, fulfillment, participant access or existing retention facts. Continuing-purpose policy governs its own actual obligations. No new email, subscription, recipient invitation, renewal, signature or financial effect is inferred.

**U13 — Accessible deliberate controls.** Keep visible unobscured focus, meaningful AT status without focus theft, ordinary keyboard and touch operation, narrow reflow, Unicode/RTL and long safe labels. Selection/blur/navigation/prefetch/IME confirmation is inert; focused-button Enter/Space and deliberate click/tap remain usable. Preserve permitted input and current exact context on recoverable error.

**U14 — Actual capable support and source independence.** Missing authority/policy, source restrictions, unknown outcomes and misdirected use reach an actually authorized source/records/privacy operator. A generic Contact support label or empty queue is not a completed remedy. Ordinary collection work remains independent of optional reference and configurable workflow.

**U15 — Bounded rollout and effective doors.** Reuse qualified source/context/custody primitives, not record-level permission inheritance or a new archive. Actual legacy uses need census/meaning, not name/label backfill. Test effective grants/RLS/function/service/storage/worker/restore and pooled context; current all-door projection and copied-byte obligations remain. Bound and measure set-wise discovery/history/copy work and stable paging.

**U16 — Evidence and stage honesty.** Record the selected optional capability and exact amendments without claiming current APIs, legal policy, native/provider or intended-user qualification. Preserve all Q01–Q64 and other consumers. A user-facing label alone does not justify a generic new glossary concept; the source owner should define only a necessary distinct business use, without changing File Version, acceptance or collection meanings.

### Proposed vocabulary after source-owner alignment

The root now explicitly proposes an ordinary partner-document-reference source owning the admitted use lifecycle, with P9 providing context and P29 custody retaining its own role. In that narrow scope, **Partner Document Reference** is useful business vocabulary: it distinguishes an admitted ongoing use from a URL, original collection history or legal agreement status, without inventing an entity engine.

Recommended concise definition: **“A separately admitted, source-owned use of specified document evidence for ongoing work with a particular partner. It is distinct from the original collection record, a sharing permission, and a determination that an agreement is valid or current.”** This is proposed only. It refines U16's vocabulary judgment, does not change File Version/acceptance/collection meanings, and is not a claim that the glossary has been edited or ratified.
