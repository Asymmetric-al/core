Do not add a third option granting access to every completed request. Completion without an actual eligible completed admission is a different, substantially broader permission decision. Nor should A be described as “everyone can always get their files back.”

## The narrow scope that makes A safe and meaningful

The added eligibility source is **the completed view actually admitted at the qualifying fulfillment occurrence**, not the entire original request, the latest grant row, an unfinished read scope or a collection of files currently retained by staff. Its exact recipient, source, fulfillment, members/actions, policy, end and original lineage must be provable. That view may have arisen during the first or a later eligible Q15 fresh period; the number of prior periods itself does not enlarge the completed view.

For a proposed new Q08 grant, intersect:

- The exact admitted completed-view scope being used as the historical ceiling.
- Currently eligible same-recipient own-submission members, permitted actions/context and current source restrictions.
- The newly reviewed, deliberately approved subset.

These are conceptual bounds, not a prescribed table or another authorization engine. Never union prior unfinished/fresh grants, substitute the broad original requirement list, infer reading from a previous write permission, add later files or restore superseded bytes. A previous narrower **Q08 fresh-read** choice alone does not rewrite its historical ceiling, but actual binding source restrictions continue to apply. The Q13 completed-view ceiling itself remains narrow; it is not merely an incidental narrower fresh-read choice that Q08 can ignore.

An example makes the distinction practical: an original request includes requirements A and B, but the valid fresh period declares completed reading only for A and the source admits only A's eligible current submission at fulfillment. Under proposed Q16 A, later Q08 consideration can cover currently eligible A within that admitted scope. It cannot include B merely because B was once an original requirement, was readable under an earlier expired grant, or remains in staff storage.
