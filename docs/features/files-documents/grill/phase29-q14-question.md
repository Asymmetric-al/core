## Historical question and alternatives

**Which timing profile should apply when staff issue fresh access to continue
an otherwise-eligible unfinished request?**

| Option                                                                 | Complete proposed timing profile                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Practical tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Up to 30 elapsed days from fresh source issuance (recommended)** | Establish the unfinished end once at the new qualified source issuance plus 720 hours, under genuine earlier limits/current restrictions. It ends even if the recipient never enters.                                                                                                                     | One predictable end for staff and recipient; more room than B for delivery, remaining work and source review, without repeating the initial sixty-day profile. Delayed discovery consumes that opportunity. |
| **B — Up to 14 elapsed days from fresh source issuance**               | Same fixed model, with 336 hours. Unused access ends on that same boundary.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | A shorter possible exposure period and simpler finality. Leaves less room for recipient work, proof/delivery problems and staff review; no assumed further renewal repairs the shortfall.                   |
| **C — Enter within 7 elapsed days, then up to 30 elapsed days**        | Fresh source authority initially has a finite 168-hour unfinished entry window. A first qualified source-confirmed recipient entry before its applicable end resolves one 720-hour working period from that entry. If no entry or eligible source completion occurs in time, the unused opportunity ends. | Preserves a fuller working opportunity after delayed discovery. Adds a second boundary, entry/completion races and more explanation; unfinished authority can extend to almost 37 days from fresh issuance. |

All three preserve genuine action/purpose/overall restrictions, current proof,
original history and Q13's scope. They are complete mutually exclusive product
profiles, not a sender menu or permission to edit dates. “Up to” retains actual
limits; it does not create discretion to choose any duration below the number.

## Recommendation and strongest alternatives

Recommend **A**. It gives the continued task a known finite end at issuance,
including delivery, recipient action and source review, while giving less
nominal unfinished time than the original sixty-day opportunity. It does not
assume the recipient is already ready, has read a message or needs only a tiny
correction; first submissions and review-only periods can still be legitimate.

Thirty days is an explicit product judgment, not an inherited Q07 completed
period, a vendor standard or a measured ministry optimum. There is no current
representative completion-time or support dataset proving fourteen, thirty or
the seven-day entry window sufficient. Useful cohort qualification remains a
release gate. B is the strongest simpler/tighter alternative; C is the strongest
alternative when protecting working time after late entry is more important
than a single known end.

C has both an anchor benefit and a longer possible total unfinished opportunity
than A. Do not attribute every benefit to first entry alone. It does not solve
delivery or travel delays longer than its own seven-day entry window.

The independent runtime/UX researchers initially preferred choosing the anchor
before the numbers. This synthesis instead offers complete profiles so the
founder can assess real consequences in one decision rather than separately
choosing clock, units and unused bound. That is a sequencing judgment, not a
technical finding against either approach. No number is silently selected.

## What fresh source issuance means

For these alternatives, `G` is the qualified source occurrence of this new
deliberate fresh admission whose effect durably commits. It is not the
original issuance `I`, the original end, a draft review, staff click, message
acceptance, first email open, technical token creation or consumer observation.
Do not equate a stored transaction timestamp with physical commit proof.

For A or B, that source act establishes the finite unfinished opportunity,
subject to Q01 proof and current permission before use. A separate preliminary
review or provider step is not a competing hidden start. If issuance cannot be
established, report its actual unknown/failed state and reconcile; no fallback
clock starts from whatever timestamp is available.

Resolve the admitted end and calculation/limit provenance once. Retry looks up
the same deliberate effect before any calculation and recovers its original
outputs. Clock movement, new HTTP keys, delivery/proof recovery, revisiting,
corrections, changing defaults or resending do not restart it. Genuine source
shortening/current restrictions retain their scope and history.

For A, the ordinary nominal end is `G + 720 hours`. For B it is `G + 336 hours`.
Actual live admission must have valid finite remaining terms under the source's
current restrictions. Missing/invalid policy or an already-ended opportunity
does not become a guessed default, minimum duration or grace period.

Q11 chose fixed original issuance timing for original access. It did not choose
this fresh profile. Selecting A or B deliberately adopts a compatible timing
shape for this separate source act, with its own number. Q12's original
1,440-hour result and old expired grants remain untouched.

## C's complete pre-entry and working semantics

C is not an unbounded invitation or an approval with no source authority until
someone visits. At fresh issuance, the source admits finite conditional
unfinished authority with nominal entry end `L = G + 168 hours`, subject to its
genuine applicable limits. Current Q01 proof still gates protected use.

If first qualified entry `T` occurs while that unfinished authority remains
valid and before the exclusive applicable entry end, resolve the working end
once at no later than `T + 720 hours`, preserving true independent caps and
binding shortening. The entry-only boundary does not also clip that newly
admitted working stage. Later entry/proof renewal does not reset `T` or its end.

“Qualified entry” must be the source-confirmed first intentional entry of this
same recipient into this same fresh admission while the source is unfinished,
with Q01-required proof/current eligibility. Link scanners, prefetch, analytics,
inert landing/file GETs, other recipients or a per-file view cannot start or
restart it. Device change alone establishes nothing; a genuine first qualified
entry may occur on any supported device, and later entries cannot restart it.
Use a qualified source transition, not client time or telemetry.
No extra ceremonial “start my timer” button or new account is proposed.

Because valid fresh source authority exists during the finite entry window,
eligible normal source fulfillment before its end may use Q13's completed
transition even if the recipient has not visited. The source need not wait for
an open browser to finish independently authorized review. After fulfillment,
a later visit enters the eligible completed view; it cannot start an unfinished
working period. If the unused window actually ends first, later fulfillment
does not create retrospective recipient eligibility.

The first-entry, expiry and fulfillment transitions must have one qualified
source order, preserving current scope and authoritative occurrence/commit
semantics. Entry exactly at the applicable exclusive end is too late. A click
before the end without a qualified source effect does not reserve time.
Recover a proved timely durable effect from its original evidence when observed
later; no observation-time reset. A lawful atomic entry/fulfillment transition
is judged by source eligibility and causal order, not artificial timestamp gaps.

An “inactive approval until entry” variant would have different pre-entry
completion consequences. It is not the C offered here and cannot be substituted
by an implementer after selection.

## Completion stays separately bounded

Every alternative preserves ratified Q13: one actual eligible normal
fulfillment during valid fresh authority stops ordinary writes and may admit
only the declared completed-read scope. Q07 bounds that reading by up to
720 elapsed hours from qualified fulfillment under genuine applicable caps.
No union of expired scope, read permission inferred from writes or Q08 expansion.

The numerical coincidence between A's proposed unfinished thirty days and
Q07's completed thirty days does not make them the same period. A submission
or staff approval does not start completed reading. An unfinished-only end is
not an overall cap, and completed access never preserves unfinished writes.

Hypothetical elapsed-day examples assume no earlier cap and all required
source/recipient predicates; they are not promises of access:

| Situation                                                    | Consequence                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A or B: recipient first enters on day 6 from fresh issuance  | A has at most 24 unfinished days left; B has at most 8. Entry does not replenish time.                                                                                           |
| C: first qualified entry occurs on day 6                     | The working end is at most day 36 from fresh issuance. It is not clipped to the entry-only day-7 boundary.                                                                       |
| C: no qualified entry and source remains unfinished at day 7 | Unused unfinished authority ends; there is no perpetual approval waiting for a visit.                                                                                            |
| C: source fulfills on day 5 before any recipient entry       | If historically eligible, completed reading may run to at most day 35. A visit on day 8 can enter that valid completed view, not start a new working clock.                      |
| Recipient never enters; source actually fulfills on day 8    | A/B may still qualify the completed transition under their valid authority. C's unused day-7 authority has ended, so that later fulfillment cannot create recipient eligibility. |
| A: source fulfills on day 29                                 | If eligible, completed reading may run to at most day 59 from fresh issuance. Day 30 was unfinished-only, not an overall cap.                                                    |
| Any option: authority truly ends before actual fulfillment   | Staff/source work may still finish independently, but no completed recipient eligibility is invented.                                                                            |
| Any option: a timely durable transition is observed late     | Recover the original qualified outcome/end, then enforce current restrictions.                                                                                                   |

Derived latest completed-read ends for this **one fresh admission**
are explanatory only: A's eligible completed end is strictly before `G + 60`
elapsed days; B's before `G + 44`; C's before `G + 67`. These follow from
exclusive unfinished boundaries and Q07's completed maximum, not newly selected
overall caps. They do not measure from original `I`, choose a cumulative repeat
limit, change retention or authorize another grant. Under C, pre-entry
fulfillment alone yields a completed end strictly before `G + 37` days.

## UX and operational implications

All options use the existing request context, Base UI/base-maia and one
meaningful staff review/action. Review the selected policy and known controlling
limits before issuance; show authoritative actual results afterward. Advancing
time under unchanged reviewed terms does not require a second confirmation.
Material recipient/scope/timing changes still require refreshed review.

A/B offer a single unfinished date immediately after established issuance:

> Access while this request is unfinished ends [date, time and zone].

C must explain its two stages plainly. Before first entry, authorized staff see
the actual enter-by boundary and the proposed after-entry working duration.
After qualified entry, show the actual working end. If completion wins first,
show the completed state/end instead. Protected recipient metadata still needs
current proof; no anonymous page may reveal the request merely to show a timer.

Keep Requested by separate. Show Received/awaiting staff review and the actual
next actor where applicable. Proof/session expiry, delivery failures, unavailable
representations and unknown effects remain distinct from source expiry and
business rejection. Do not imply guaranteed notification, thirty days remaining
after a late visit, automatic extensions or loss of already received files.

All profiles include source review time. They create no staff-response SLA.
No evidence establishes that staff approving fresh access means the recipient
is online, reachable or ready. Real cohort qualification must cover low
bandwidth, delayed delivery, travel/timezones, proof difficulty, partial work,
staff handoff and review completion without further recipient action.

Accessible proof, focus/status, keyboard/touch, localized absolute dates and
help remain required. The business-period length does not qualify shorter
session/verification/upload timers. Do not introduce countdown-only meaning,
new technical token lifetimes or unsafely persisted private browser drafts.

## Scope and next step

number, anchor and finite unused treatment. It does not select repetition,
overlap, cumulative lifetime, pre-expiry extension, arbitrary customization,

and completion consequence remain ratified regardless of the profile chosen.
