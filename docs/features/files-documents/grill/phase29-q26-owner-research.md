## Recommendation and genuinely open question

Ask one bounded product question: **In the ordinary native Request changes review, should the visible optional recipient-email choice start on when eligible, with staff able to turn it off, or start off and require staff to turn it on?** Keep both choices in the same existing meaningful review action. Do not make the alternative an extra send wizard.

Recommend **A, initially on only when eligible, with explicit staff control**, as product judgment: Request changes communicates that recipient work needs attention, so offering its qualified notice in that action reduces an easy omission. This is not evidence that every recipient prefers email, that a sent notice is received, or that every return should send. **B, initially off with deliberate opt-in in the same review**, is a strong lean alternative: it reduces unexpected outward communication and fits staff who already contacted the person, at the cost of an easily omitted notice. Neither changes the independently required current guidance in the request.

This is a new ordinary-native default, not repetition of Q20. Q20 ADR30–44 selects fresh-access notices only and expressly excludes review notices. Q23 ADR148–150 and Q23-C06/C08 expressly leave review-result notices unselected. Q23 already determines the meaningful negative action and safe guidance; Q24–Q25 determine responsibility and initial readiness. No further access-clock or reviewer-routing decision is required before this bounded notice-default question. Initial invitations/proof remain separate, but their default is not a prerequisite for researching this outcome.

## Scope of both alternatives

- Only a genuinely new authorized **ordinary native Request changes** source decision on eligible submitted evidence; no generic negative status or infrastructure failure.
- The same visible optional control in the existing review, showing its permitted destination and consequence. A initializes selected only when the current source, actor, recipient, Live message contract, effective Delivery Plan and channel permit it. B uses the same eligibility but requires selection. Staff choice must remain stable through validation/recovery; absent or old payloads do not become true.
- Q23 current safe guidance remains source-owned participant presentation. It is not automatically a new P6 in-product notification event, nor is it contingent on sending email.
- Source business-review authority, message/content/disclosure authority and source-action capability remain separately qualified. Assignment, ability to read an item, or grant administration cannot imply all three. No forced new permission atom follows.
- Both exclude acceptance/fulfillment notices, original invitation/proof defaults, reminders, fresh-access notices, staff alerts, other channels, bulk/per-file emission, digests, global preferences and specialist mandatory notices. They do not add send-again, recipient editing, composer, attachment or arbitrary-contact powers.
- This is the ordinary optional source condition. It cannot disable an already-required specialist message, modify tenant Delivery Plan settings, turn a prohibited/disabled step on, or create a contract key at runtime.

## Boundaries that the question must not obscure

1. **Persisted review is not delivery.** The source decision/guidance/selected choice and required durable handoff need a coherent qualified boundary, while external send outcomes stay separate. A known committed Request changes result survives later email failure. Unknown commitment must be reconciled, not resubmitted as a new return.
2. **Use the actual review occurrence.** Item ID plus Changes requested status, bytes hash or an old status-only dedupe key does not identify a genuinely new reviewed source decision. Exact replay preserves false/true and original outcome; a later real decision is not silently suppressed by an earlier notice. This does not select a digest, per-file send or cooldown policy.
3. **Currentness reaches delivery and action use.** Correction, fresh reassessment/acceptance, requirement change, reassignment, closure or authority change may make old wording/action inapplicable. Respect exact source fences and immutable prepared identity. Do not instruct the person to re-upload after a resolved/closed item merely because a queued template exists. An in-flight email is not guaranteed recallable.
4. **Do not make active upload access a universal email prerequisite.** Staff may lawfully record an outcome after participant access ends. A separately qualified safe source notice/help variant can still be possible, but cannot promise unavailable correction, create a grant, renew access or substitute a latest grant into an old action. If no qualified useful variant exists, expose that notice outcome safely. Do not invent a new renewal/help policy here.
5. **In-request guidance is not automatically email-safe.** Source-approved minimum subject, preview, body and action have their own fact wall. Do not blindly include feedback, filename, private reason or request existence. Full raw staff notes, attachments and direct storage links are excluded absent their exact owner authority. The existing protected context is where applicable safe current guidance belongs.
6. **A checked choice is not a send entitlement.** Exact no-Party/Party source recipient revision, scope, consent/suppression, verified sender/channel, effective plan, Live contract and staff capability remain necessary. Preserve selected intent for blocked/non-applicable/zero-child results; do not misreport these as staff-off. Do not silently turn a confirmed combined intent into review-only before admission if its material terms changed; safe explicit resolution remains required.
7. **Do not expand Q23.** No required staff solicitation before existing Q03 self-correction; no new terminal item outcome, text-only participant channel, requirement editing, undo/reopen, global comments or old-note history. Required item remains unsatisfied, optional nonblocking work does not prevent Q22 fulfillment, and a notice changes neither rule.

These are inherited compatibility requirements and subjects for the selected-answer review, not a new 22-category qualification or Q26 ratification.
