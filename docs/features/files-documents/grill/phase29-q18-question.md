## Historical question and alternatives

**How long should the standard staff-approved fresh completed-read period last:
up to thirty elapsed days or up to fourteen elapsed days from its new source
issuance, with genuine earlier limits preserved?**

Q17 already fixes the deadline at the new qualified Q08 source issuance and
uses that same deadline for unused access. Q16 supplies both eligible historical
completed-view origins. This question supplies the standard finite interval
and exact elapsed interpretation together; it does not reopen the clock-model
choice or offer a per-recipient timing menu.

“Up to” means lawful earlier limits may shorten or block access. It does not
give staff an arbitrary duration/date picker or create a minimum usable period.
We choose one standard policy here; both Q16 origins use it. Source-specific
genuine restrictions remain binding rather than becoming another staff option.

## Recommendation and practical example

**Recommend A.** With Q17's issuance-based end, the period runs before the
recipient necessarily arrives. Thirty days provides more tolerance for delay
without adding first-entry activation, grace, automatic renewal or a new
support workflow. This is a product judgment, not an empirically established
ministry requirement or a universal security standard.

Illustrative case, not a claimed real workflow: a recipient first returns
five elapsed days after issuance, with no earlier controlling limit.

- A leaves twenty-five elapsed days.
- B leaves nine elapsed days.
- Neither starts a fresh interval at that visit. A genuine earlier limit or
  current restriction may leave less or prevent the affected action entirely.

Thirty matches the nominal count already chosen for the ordinary completed
view and fresh unfinished profile, which may help comprehension. That is a
secondary benefit only: their source events, scope, lifecycle and policy
identities remain separate. Their old ended periods do not cap this new grant,
and Q14's unfinished-to-completed transition does not occur again here.

Fourteen is the strongest tighter alternative if shorter availability matters
more than tolerance for delayed return. It reduces the length of opportunity,
not the consequence of a disclosure that already occurred. Neither option
changes what the recipient may read or promises recall of downloaded copies.

## Exact meaning of either option

1. **Elapsed, not calendar.** One day in this profile means exactly 24 elapsed
   hours. Compute from the qualified new source occurrence using trusted
   instants, not a local calendar month, end-of-day, date-only string or
   business-day calendar. Localize the resulting actual end for display; DST
   or travel must not change the duration. Current source checks and supported
   precision still govern the exclusive end: before is eligible in time, at
   or after is not.
2. **One source result.** Q17's original committed occurrence/end and provenance
   survive retries and late observations. No new interval begins at first
   entry, proof renewal, delivery, rendering or recovery. A missing end needs
   reconciliation, not now-plus-thirty/fourteen. Numerical policy must be
   reviewed/adopted before real issuance, not invented after it.
3. **Limits retain scope.** Genuine grant-wide/purpose limits may shorten the
   whole period. A file/action-only restriction still affects only that scope;
   it must not truncate unrelated authorized reading. Technical session/provider
   expiry is not a whole-grant cap. The predecessor's ordinary end is historical
   eligibility evidence, while genuine continuing prohibitions remain binding.
4. **The request stays completed.** No new fulfillment, another completed-read
   stage, broader history, later-file membership, upload/correction, retention
   reset or drive entitlement follows. Q16's exact original-version/action/
   context ceiling and current checks remain. A thirty-day policy for another
   source is not silently merged with this one.
5. **Keep the UX small and honest.** Use the existing Grant read access action
   and actual end in the same completed context. Label estimates before source
   issuance; after a qualified result show the admitted date/time/zone and
   meaningful scoped limits. Do not promise notification, readiness, a full
   interval to a late visitor, or another approval after expiry. Preserve
   accessible proof/status/focus, mobile/low-bandwidth recovery and separate
   qualification of shorter authentication/session timers.
6. **No resident timer is authority.** Existing source/private-serving checks
   enforce the end, independent of browser timers and punctual workers. Exact
   unit arithmetic is necessary but does not qualify native authorization,
   concurrency, storage serving, restore or user journeys.

If selected and ratified after its full review, the profile would specialize
