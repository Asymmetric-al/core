## Finding, permanent shape and strongest alternative

Add a small private report about one currently reportable P28 resource publication, with an actual capable source handling route and a truthful return path. Use only three business states: **Open, Handled and Withdrawn**. Viewing or investigating is not a lock or a fourth In progress state. Keep privacy restriction/disposition and current disclosure independent from those states.

The reader can edit or withdraw their own Open report. A mistaken withdrawal has a narrow current-authorized Restore. A currently qualified curator can Mark handled with a concise audience-safe outcome, correct that outcome without falsely repeating handling, or Reopen mistaken handling. A reader with new information after genuine handling submits a new deliberate report about a currently reportable publication; they do not gain a chat/reopen command. Source-local Your reports supplies the lawful receipt/current outcome without creating a global inbox or continuing resource access.

Strict current-publication admission is the smallest complete new-report rule. If publication changes before a new report commits, preserve lawful text, disclose the currently permitted context and require explicit refreshed intent. Do not quietly attach an old observation to a different version. Once a report was actually admitted, maintenance, handling and minimal own-result disclosure use their current **report-purpose** rights; they do not require the resource to remain published and do not revive its bytes. This distinction closes the normal old-resource/report journey without granting a historical resource browser.

The strongest simpler alternative was existing qualified Help/source coordination. It can remain the right route for personal/private assistance and can avoid a new source report lifecycle where already effective. A was chosen for less context loss and direct source maintenance. No measured Asym volume, efficiency, report quality or reduced Support load was established. Document360's larger workflow, irreversible Closed and source-dependent withdrawal side effects are counterexamples to copy cautiously, not an automatic blueprint. The proposed permanent path has no public discussion, rating, automatic email, assignee bottleneck, read receipt, SLA or generic workflow engine.

### O-C02 — Source identity and strict current new-report admission

The resource owner SHALL admit the report against one exact current reportable organization/publication/version or approved external-reference basis, current qualified report capability and safe identifying metadata. Bind immutable Tenant/environment/source/target and trusted native reporter/context plus actual source occurrence sampled after relevant waits. Caller title, URL, claimed identity or prior reading cannot supply authority. A source-related publication revision change before NEW admission requires a refreshed permitted context and deliberate new reviewed intent; preserve lawful text, do not silently retarget or pretend the successor was observed. Unknown/withdrawn/denied target cannot be reported through a hidden-resource selector. This strict-current rule is an explicit bounded product judgment, not a claim that reports on all historical publications are inherently invalid. External targets record the approved reference and reader's claim, without fetching or asserting an immutable remote snapshot.

### O-C03 — Explicit current report-purpose authority, separate from bytes

Adopt report create, own-current read/edit/withdraw/restore, qualified handling/outcome correction/reopen and scoped administration as exact source/P12 actions. ### O-C04 — Three business states and coherent durable acceptance

Use Open, Handled and Withdrawn, with current privacy restriction/disposition as separate facts. Open means a received report awaits a handling disposition; it does not promise someone is online or has read it. Admit one exact report with its current text revision, original target/actor/time, required history, source handling responsibility and durable operation result coherently in the actual authoritative transaction. No Received result before the owner can recover the promised report. Viewing, draft typing or route entry neither admits a report nor creates In progress, an exclusive assignment or a processing lock. ### O-C05 — Capable curator route without an assignee bottleneck

Existing P12/source administration supplies qualified stewardship repair; an operator can diagnose/repair only within their actual purpose and cannot gain report bodies by operating a queue. Unavailable management UI is not automatically lost source custody, but an actual missing capable route needs owned repair.

### O-C06 — Own Open edit and withdrawal with exact consequences

While the report is Open, its reporter may deliberately edit its current text or withdraw it under current report-purpose authority and the exact expected report revision. The observed target and original reporter/receipt remain immutable; an edit does not retarget to a newer publication or impersonate someone else. Edit/Withdraw are not ordinary reporter actions on a Handled report; sensitive mistakes retain the capable privacy route rather than a forced historical rewrite.

## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

A report could leak a protected resource or private note, be accepted without a capable handler, or be falsely treated as a review/publication decision. Three states and a textbox do not prevent those errors; exact report-purpose rights, coherent outcomes and real source repair do.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

We assume a qualified P28 publication and actual report/handler route, not all-staff access or continuously online curators. We have no measured report volume, task-time gain or native source implementation. Current disclosure may be narrower than retained history or the reporter's memory.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It adds a P28 resource-source report lifecycle and a narrow return view. It does not turn Files into a second resource owner, broaden staff readership, create Support conversations or import Q45/Q51/P6 effects. Q55 review facts remain independently meaningful.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

One small form and durable result reduce copying. Own Open editing, Withdraw/Restore and safe current return close ordinary mistakes. Curators can handle, correct an outcome or reopen without artificial multi-step cycles, forced essays, per-report assignment or chat.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Official products support context and accountable handling but differ on irreversible closure and withdrawal. This proposal deliberately keeps recoverability and history under current source rights, with accessible private UI. Vendor patterns do not prove a universal default or Core native safety.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes as explicit P28/P12 adoption with current source identity, one authority and shared UI. Generic Support code/static roles do not implement it. Root vocabulary already holds the P28 library definition; a new Files glossary entity would misplace ownership.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Accept A with the exact required amendments above. The proposed bounded Restore and outcome correction complete real recovery without a generic Trash/ticket engine. No extra product fork is needed within this scope; full consolidated-package review and explicit ratification still follow.
