## Source effect versus current head: the lean model

Preserve one immutable outcome per deliberate admitted intent: exact tenant/environment/request/original admission/intended recipient, reviewed predecessor and material scope/policy, trusted issuer and first-admission G/end, plus required evidence. Prior admissions remain ended; they are not rewritten as the current one.

A small source-owned current head/version or equivalent constrained relation can identify the relevant most recent authoritative admission/outcome without replaying all history on every use. It is a mutable index of current source truth, not a second authority engine or permission cache. Updating it must be coherent with the source admission, required audit and follow-up intent, through Q13/P12's existing boundary. Schema shape is not selected here.

Do not reconstruct the head from largest UUID/sequence/created_at or the most recent email. Timestamp/sequence allocation does not necessarily match committed causal order. Do not fall back to an ancient ordinarily expired predecessor if the head is missing, inconsistent or points to unknown/adverse/completed state. Use owned reconciliation; fail closed for affected issuance without fabricating a denial or data loss.

An authorized historic outcome lookup must not require its old issuer/recipient rights still to be exercisable. The service may need to recover that internal fact to avoid duplication, while current disclosure can deny the requesting caller names, scope, links or raw response JSON. “Already committed” is neither a current read entitlement nor permission to return another grant's result.

## Admission/recovery order that avoids the new pitfalls

This is a behavioral sequence, not prescribed SQL or an extra orchestration layer:

1. Resolve the trusted command context and stable deliberate intent within the exact lineage. A client can supply an allowed selector/intent handle, not certify actor, scope, predecessor eligibility or time.
2. Look for that intent's existing source outcome. If found and its material input matches, recover exactly it under current safe projection. Its original G/end do not change even if it expired and later grants exist. Conflicting material input rejects. Lookup failure is unknown, not absent.
3. For an intent proved not yet admitted, establish the reviewed predecessor/material state through the source's protected concurrency boundary. Prove original lawful admission, this relevant predecessor's eligible ordinary expiry, no overlapping unfinished admission in the same selected lineage, still-unfinished business source, current recipient/action eligibility and current issuer scope/assignable ceiling. Recheck time/authority after relevant waits.
4. Coherently record one new Q14 admission, its original finite terms, the new authoritative lineage/head and required evidence/follow-up. A conflicting insert or CAS loss is not a fresh permission to choose a later predecessor/key. Reconcile the actual outcome or return an honest current conflict for renewed review.
5. Commit before providers and expose only currently authorized result facts. Delivery, proof, cache and observer failure cannot move G/end or trigger another grant. Closing a dialog does not undo the source effect.

If another staff actor already admitted a grant, the losing distinct intent may be shown an authorized “access already granted” outcome with its real provenance. It must not pretend the losing intent/actor created that effect or bind the failed attempt silently to the other actor's grant. A positive result from one source command and a current-action projection are different facts.

Same-intent retry and distinct-intent retry require different handling. An already committed old intent must be recovered despite head movement. An unadmitted intent does not get to advance to whichever predecessor is current now. Normal time moving during the same reviewed context is not new material intent; a new intervening grant, source completion or recipient/scope change is.

## PostgreSQL qualifications: no-row and phantom races

FOR UPDATE locks rows returned by the query. A query finding no live admission does not by itself lock the absence against a different issuer's insert. Likewise, a per-intent advisory lock cannot serialize two distinct intent keys for the same lineage. Use the source's existing stable parent/head/mutation fence and appropriate database constraints or a qualified equivalent. This is not a mandate for a global lock, a custom lock service or SERIALIZABLE everywhere. [PostgreSQL locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/explicit-locking.html).

Read Committed commands use snapshots; updates can re-evaluate a changed target row without making every other source/authority row current as one business predicate. ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING may decline insertion because of a concurrent row not visible to that statement's snapshot. An empty RETURNING result is therefore not proof of no committed effect; recover under the actual transaction/isolation contract. If serializable/repeatable-read retries are used, retry the whole eligible operation with stable intent and revalidation, not provider effects or a new key. [PostgreSQL isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html).

Nonoverlap must be enforced at its exact scope: same tenant/environment/request/intended recipient/original lineage, including narrow subsets within it. Do not block unrelated recipients or other source rights. At the prior exclusive end, old authority is ended; Q15 adds no positive cooldown or a promised uninterrupted handoff. Current source time governs even if a background status sweep has not run. An early status change cannot create eligible expiry.

Do not encode cross-row nonoverlap as an ordinary CHECK querying other rows, or depend on a time-varying CHECK to keep evaluating itself. Required state/identity/time values need deliberate null/finite semantics. Exact unique/FK/exclusion constraints may be useful where appropriate, but the source mutation must still prove the business predicates; no new exclusion-index design is frozen here. [PostgreSQL constraints](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-constraints.html).

Protect tenant/environment/lineage/predecessor/actor/G/end against permitted-old-to-forbidden-new transformations, including trigger changes and direct service/RPC writes. An arbitrary current-head update must not retarget or skip the source history.

Qualify actual table/column/function grants, views, service/job/storage invocation and definer ownership/search path. The coarse tenant RLS layer is defense in depth; detailed capability decisions remain in the sole P12/source path. Omitted WITH CHECK in ALL/UPDATE can inherit USING; policies combine and BEFORE triggers affect the proposed row. Missing syntax alone is not a finding. A correct tenant policy does not itself make a field immutable or serialize a repeat predicate. No deployed grant/policy evidence was gathered. [PostgreSQL policies](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html).

Native admission must use the exact current source capability/live administrative scope and ceiling, preserving actual continuing dependencies. Client role/tenant strings, service privilege, a historical successful grant and a job claim cannot substitute. Existing Q13 serving/read-replay rules protect metadata, counts, history, private JSON and bytes after admission.

## Bounded operation, restore and rollout

Use indexed exact intent and current-lineage/source lookups plus the existing P12 read/mutation budget. An unbounded list of every previous grant is neither necessary for a normal current decision nor compatible with no preset lifetime quota. Required original/predecessor integrity should be established by durable constrained facts; authorized detailed history can remain separately paginated. Do not expose total counts or history existence before current projection permission.

Retain only the original/adverse/effect provenance required by the actual source and records rules. No forever personal-data retention follows from repeatability. If lawful disposal leaves too little proof to safely admit a repeat, do not guess or silently reconstruct rights; use the owning source resolution. This does not prescribe a new archive system or arbitrary tombstone schema.

Prospective rollout must prevent old writers from overwriting one grant row, bypassing the current predecessor, applying a one-use or automatic renewal default, or computing thirty days from retry time. Restore must preserve both current head consistency and later adverse facts. An internally consistent old database snapshot can still lack later revocation/history; it is not automatically safe to resume outward access. Use the existing owner restore/containment and reconciliation gates. No backfill from task/email dates or max ID, and no direct SQL manual repair as ordinary operation.

Completion, cancellation and review-set changes retain Q13/Q14's race rules. A source completed in an access gap cannot be reopened for a repeat; timely prior completed admission observed late must be recovered. An old upload/callback cannot borrow the newest grant. Q08 remains separate. These are inherited controls, not new subsystems for Q15.

## Focused amendments and falsifiable proof

Seven minimal additions specialize Q13/Q14; their other controls remain incorporated by reference:

1. **Exact sequential scope:** a new deliberate repeat requires the relevant preceding admission's proved eligible ordinary expiry and no overlapping unfinished admission in the same chosen lineage, including narrow subsets. No global cross-recipient prohibition or positive cooldown is added.
2. **Predecessor-bound review:** bind the materially reviewed current lineage/source context. An unadmitted stale intent cannot auto-advance across intervening grants; a different relevant predecessor requires appropriate new review.
3. **Recover before new admission:** existing matching intent returns its original outcome, not the latest grant, even after later effects. Keep immutable original outputs; conflicting input and lookup uncertainty cannot mint permission. Current disclosure is independent.
4. **Atomic source head/effect:** current-lineage transition, new finite Q14 effect and required audit/follow-up must have a coherent commit/recovery outcome through the owner mutation path. Protect absence/concurrent insert and authority-after-wait cases, not just identical keys.
5. **Scope remains original plus current:** original purpose/requirements bound each separately reviewed subset; no union of expired grants, implicit predecessor shrink-only ratchet or gratis widening. Accepted/frozen items and current restrictions remain protected.
6. **Bounded trusted representation:** structural same-scope constraints, protected head/history, indexed current lookups and separately governed pagination/retention. Do not infer causal latest from timestamps or introduce unbounded scans/counter infrastructure.
7. **Compatible history:** preserve source identity and later adverse facts through mixed versions, restore and repair. Missing provenance fails safely; no native qualification is inferred from adjacent helpers or mocks.

Future proof must cover the following meaningful cases using the actual implementation and real roles/transactions, not artificial mocks of these prose rules:

| Case                                                                                                 | Required outcome                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Two issuers, distinct keys, same ended predecessor; no current-admission row yet                     | At most one overlapping result can exist; loser gets truthful authorized recovery/conflict, never a new delayed successor.                                             |
| Same-intent retry before/after its grant expires, and after another grant is admitted                | Same original effect/end/attribution; present disclosure check; no false predecessor conflict or newest-grant substitution.                                            |
| Unadmitted old intent waits through an intervening grant and its expiry                              | Cannot silently issue against the new predecessor; relevant current review is required.                                                                                |
| Concurrent completion/correction/acceptance/recipient/revocation versus repeat                       | Source-required order and current authority choose a valid outcome; no gap eligibility, accepted-item reopening or stale-scope grant.                                  |
| Lock wait crosses expiry or issuing authority changes; rollback/serialization failure/unknown commit | Exact qualified timing and one stable intent; no stale time, duplicate source effect or external work before committed authority.                                      |
| Status sweep absent/early; source head missing/inconsistent; outcome lookup fails                    | Source time and coherent history govern; no fabricated expiry, available slot or implicit default grant.                                                               |
| Same tenant IDs with foreign original/predecessor/recipient; direct/trigger/service updates          | Structural relationships, effective old/new-row rules and current P12 source command reject forbidden changes.                                                         |
