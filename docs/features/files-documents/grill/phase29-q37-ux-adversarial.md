# Phase 29 Q37 — Independent UX and comparative adversarial review

Reviewed 2026-10-23, Asia/Bangkok. Conrad selected A: deliberate versioned material
instruction revision for the same outstanding, currently unaccepted ordinary
item, same document need/purpose/intended recipient, in an eligible unfinished
request. Selection is not ratification of this amendment package or native
qualification. Q01–Q36 and twenty-three glossary terms remain ratified.

Only this assigned note is edited. Current owner/runtime claims are attributed
to the parallel reviews. No canonical, implementation, schema, provider, private
data or real-message change is made.

## Judgment and strongest alternative

Accept A with the exact constraints and proof below. A missing instruction can
be corrected while preserving one understandable item and its evidence. The
critical distinction is between the historical instruction basis of a receipt,
the current requirement, and a deliberate current review decision. Publishing
new prose establishes none of the participant's missing facts and does not
itself accept or reject the file.

B remains a credible alternative: keep issued instructions fixed, use Q30 for
unchanged-meaning explanation, and use actual qualified Q33/Q36 or new-request
routes for changed work. It gives a stable original expectation and avoids this
new revision capability. It may fragment a continuing need, and it cannot promise
atomic Replace: removing the last blocker can fulfill the source before Add.
Neither option permits unaccepting evidence, disguising a different need as
clarification, or reopening terminal work.

A should remain a small **Update instructions** interaction. A general amendment
engine, automated semantic diff, issue classifier, forced acknowledgment, new
notification policy, account or extra approver is unnecessary. The benefit is a
product judgment requiring intended-user evidence, not a measured Asym outcome.

## Exact proposed amendments

H means authority/privacy, historical truth or material business harm; M means
meaningful confusion, wasted work or operational cost. Likelihood is qualitative
for an unamended prospective design, not a deployed incident rate. Proof groups
below are future native/user obligations, not executed tests.

### U01 — Bound the new authority and current eligibility

**H / plausible.** Require an actual current source/P12 instruction-authoring
capability for the same outstanding, currently unaccepted ordinary item, same
document need, intended recipient and continuing purpose in an eligible unfinished
request. Review, file-read, assignment, task-edit, admin, Q23 feedback and Q36
addition powers alone do not grant it. Preserve specialist restrictions. Do not
change requested type, requiredness, recipient, purpose or other requests through
a template. A label such as clarification cannot bypass materiality/source checks.

Currently unaccepted does not mean never accepted in its history. Any independently
lawful source change that makes the current item eligible retains historical
acceptance and all real restrictions; Q37 cannot remove acceptance to manufacture
eligibility. A Q30 answer may explain unchanged meaning but cannot silently publish
a new requirement. **P01, P02.**

### U02 — Deliberate prospective publication, exact material and old basis

**H / plausible.** Preparation is inert: menu opening, typing, blur and autosave
do not publish instructions, change recipient scope or hold Q22 completion. Review
the minimum permitted current material, proposed instructions and effect, then
admit one attributable successor with true publication time and exact reviewed
basis. Preserve every prior instruction revision, receipt association, correction,
feedback and decision under its lawful retention contract; never overwrite what
the participant was originally asked or pretend the new version applied earlier.
Submitting material identical to the exact current validated instructions is a
no-op, not another business revision. Validate current authority/basis; do not add
an AI semantic classifier or separate cosmetic-edit mode. Returning to an earlier
different version remains a new current corrective act, not this no-op case.

Where existing owners permit linked instruction resources, preserve/qualify the
exact material references and applicable versions; a snapshot of prose cannot
silently track changed linked content or restore withdrawn bytes/rights. Q37
does not add an attachment, rich-media or general content-management capability.
The operative requirement must be evidenced by retained authoritative text or an
already-qualified exact reference; a mutable external URL alone cannot prove the
earlier instruction. No new attachment store, external fetcher or URL snapshot
service is implied.
Missing optional archive preview is not a universal veto when sufficient qualified
source facts establish the proposed act; missing required material authority is.
**P03, P04, P16.**

### U03 — Reassessment is a current source act, not fabricated resubmission

**H / plausible; M / probable wasted effort if skipped.** The old receipt remains
received under its actual instructions. Publishing a revision alone must not
create rejection, acceptance, new receipt, participant affirmation, new bytes or
an automatic reupload requirement. An ordinary file-evidence item still needs
a deliberate current qualified Q21 acceptance decision. That decision may use
lawful existing evidence against an explicit current review basis; it must not
copy an earlier acceptance or silently relabel the old receipt.

Absent a separately qualified deliberate current review/correction decision,
show received evidence as awaiting actual staff reassessment, not automatically
Changes requested or newly actionable participant rework. An instruction edit
alone cannot generate a Q26 feedback notice. This does not require an extra ritual
when the source already supports the distinct current review act properly.

If more is needed, use an actually qualified current participant correction/
supplementation or lawful staff evidence path with accurate attribution. Preserve
the distinction between custody, receipt, participant assertions and acceptance.
Do not tell a participant that a staff omission made their earlier compliant
submission defective. Genuine old defects remain genuine. **P05, P06, P11.**

### U04 — Retain independent concerns without stale-feedback loops

**H / plausible.** Preserve prior feedback with its real basis. Only concerns
actually superseded by the qualified revision cease to govern current work;
independent substantive issues remain. Do not mark every old Changes requested
resolved, reinstate obsolete instructions, or leave the participant trapped in
contradictory current guidance. Relevant human reassessment must identify the
actual next responsibility and current conditions before further acceptance or
correction. Q30 text and Q23 source feedback retain their own meanings.

Instruction-authoring authority does not grant Q21/Q23 acceptance or review-
resolution authority and cannot rewrite historical decisions. A concern whose
sole binding criterion has lawfully ended must not remain a hidden blocker;
determine current applicability through qualified source rules. Any required
human resolution or reassessment is a distinct current act by an authorized
reviewer, without making every publication depend on an extra approval.

This requires source-qualified current interpretation and attributable history,
not automated text classification, a new issue taxonomy, a per-concern workflow
platform or a forced Read/Resolve acknowledgment. Ordinary UI can present the
current instructions and meaningful current feedback with their applicable basis.
**P06, P07, P19.**

### U05 — Concrete current/fresh/expired scope and lawful staff route

**H / plausible.** Qualify the material instruction successor under actual Q01
and applicable unfinished/recovery owners; Q36 addition authority is not revision
authority. Reconcile exact action/read/review/context consequences and preserve
individual scope, finite end/caps, prior adverse facts and current restrictions.
Old tokens, matching email and historical association cannot cover the revision
by implication. Reuse current Q01 proof where genuinely qualified; no universal
new challenge or Party/account creation follows.

For required participant action, publication must have an actual usable qualified
route, including a real separately authorized fresh admission where ordinary
expiry requires it. Unknown/revoked/wrong-recipient/purpose-ended authority is not
ordinary expiry. Otherwise keep the proposal uncommitted and use a capable source/
B route. Independently lawful staff Q28 evidence/Q21 review can proceed without
renewing recipient rights only where no participant act/affirmation is required.
Show actual participant availability, preserving independently valid old rights.
Do not hide stranded participant work behind a staff label. Relevant Q24/Q25
coverage can be reused; missing new scope coverage must be established without
blanket original-issuance replay or compulsory reassignment. **P08–P11.**

Qualified publication means the actual authorized finite route/scope and owned
follow-through exist. It does not require the recipient to be online, acknowledge
the change, finish Q01 proof, render the page or receive mail before staff can
publish. Q01 proof is applied/reused at its actual action boundary; known scope
must not be confused with completed participation.
The route requires actual admitted authority and a proved capable source handling
path, not prior evidence acceptance or continuous presentation/provider
availability. A recoverable technical delay retains known publication and owned
reassessment/recovery with truthful availability; absent or unknown required
authority or a fundamentally absent path cannot be labeled a technical delay.

### U06 — One source order, including acceptance and removal

**H / plausible.** Order revision, acceptance/correction, materially relevant Q30
text, Q33 removal, fulfillment and cancellation at the actual authoritative source
boundary or a proved equivalent. Acceptance/NLR/terminal state first denies an
ineligible ordinary revision. Revision first means a prepared old-basis decision
cannot ignore the changed material expectation. Q37 never revives a removed demand.
Every relevant admitted change evaluates or durably arranges the complete Q22
predicate; drafts cannot hold it open and no new routine Complete action follows.

Preserve already admitted receipts and lawful unaffected work. Revalidate materially
affected uncommitted intent without universal aggregate-version invalidation.
Transfer is not receipt; unknown upload/admission outcome cannot be guessed into
either received or lost. Use actual ordered instants after waits, not browser or
transaction-start time. **P12, P13, P17.**

### U07 — Completed scope remains a historical ceiling

**H / plausible.** Normal completed reading requires actual timely qualified
fulfillment and its explicitly admitted member/version/action/context scope.
Later recovery remains inside that actual fulfillment-created ceiling and current
restrictions, not latest instruction text, request definition or historic grant
union. A narrow individual admission remains narrow while in effect but does not
by itself create a permanent global cap on later separately authorized scope
within the genuine source/fulfillment universe. Staff processing after recipient
expiry may finish the source without reviving participant access. **P09, P14.**

### U08 — Honest changed expectations and timing in a small UI

**M / probable confusion; H / plausible adverse misrepresentation.** Use an
explicit **Update instructions** action in the existing item context. Show the
permitted current/proposed text and actual consequence before one meaningful
publication; no compulsory essay, second approver, typed-name ritual or routine
second modal. On authorized return distinguish **Current instructions** from the
**Instructions when this file was received**, where their bodies may lawfully be
shown. A concise **Instructions updated [date]** cue and source history can explain
change without a general diff engine or reading/acknowledgment gate.

Preserve progress and actual next actor. Show true revision time, applicable
requested-by target and remaining access where authorized. Do not blame someone
for missing information before it was required, erase genuine prior lateness,
or reset target/access dates. Explain known timing incompatibility and the actual
qualified remedy; invent no duration estimator, grace period or sufficiency
guarantee. Opening current instructions does not prove understanding. **P15, P19.**

### U09 — A mistake is corrected prospectively, not rolled back

**H / plausible.** Before publication, staff may correct/discard the inert proposal.
After publication, while the same current outstanding/unaccepted/unfinished
eligibility still holds, allow a deliberate current qualified corrective revision
linked to the mistake. It is another attributable successor under current rights,
not restoration of an old grant, snapshot, draft, feedback state or publication
time. Existing receipts and independent concerns remain on their real basis.

If acceptance, NLR, fulfillment or cancellation makes ordinary revision ineligible,
use attributable source remediation and the actual qualified privacy/use/disposition
route where necessary. No generic Undo, silent unacceptance, deletion, NLR reversal
or reopening. A new request addresses a genuine remaining need; it cannot undo
unnecessary evidence already collected. B's Q33/Q36 sequence remains non-atomic:
actual fulfillment may intervene, and no draft hold or ordering trick guarantees
replacement. Qualify capable stage-specific operators before activation.
**P13, P18, P22.**

### U10 — Durable identity and truthful failure recovery

**H / plausible.** Coherently admit revision, current authoritative scope/route,
required history and owned reassessment/reevaluation follow-through. Preserve
one semantic revision identity across lost responses, tabs, retries and restore.
Same intent returns the original effect under current disclosure; changed material
requires current review rather than silent overwrite or duplicate publication.
Concurrent distinct authorized revisions respect source order and dependencies.

Known publication survives secondary projection failure and is repaired without
publishing again. Unknown commitment is neither success nor safe-to-repeat proof.
Replay after a later correction, NLR or terminal result reports the original effect
with permitted current status and never revives old text as current authority.
Keep external/provider work out of source locks; no new provider is needed for
the instruction action. **P03, P17, P20.**

### U11 — Privacy, accessibility and communication remain exact

**H / plausible privacy harm; M / probable accessibility friction.** Retaining
historical basis does not grant every viewer a permanent archive of old instruction
bodies. Each current participant/staff/admin reader gets only authorized necessary
context, with actual safe recovery if required material is unavailable. Current
source/custody privacy, retention and disposition still apply. Minimize sensitive
titles, people/location facts, evidence, old/new prose, references and secrets in
URLs, errors, counts, logs, analytics, exports and cache. Preserve independently
qualified contacts/envelopes/protected carriers under their own contracts.
An unavailable/restricted historical body must not appear as empty, unchanged or
nonexistent. Use an authorized submitted-under cue and truthful availability;
preserved internal basis is distinct from permission to show the whole old text.

Use existing base-maia/Base UI with visible and programmatic names, relationships,
state, keyboard/voice/AT operation, predictable input, stable focus, accessible
validation and persistent status. Test reflow/zoom/touch/contrast/reduced motion,
long text/locales/RTL/IME and low bandwidth. No unintended Enter during composition,
reconnect publication or cross-user/tenant delayed-response disclosure. Preserve
lawful recoverable input, not an offline private transcript.

Revision itself creates no new email/default, reminder, inbox, channel or read
state. Do not fabricate a Q23 rejection, Q30 reply or access event to trigger a
different notice family. Genuine later events keep their actual contracts. A
person away from the product may not see the change; no guaranteed awareness,
daily visit or response SLA is inferred. **P16, P19–P21.**

### U12 — Small prospective qualification and bounded operation

**H / plausible unsafe rollout; M / plausible platform expansion.** Publish the
exact new command/current review basis through canonical source/P12/Q01/evidence/
access/completion/coverage/correction owners and full declared OpenSpec companions.
Preserve prior ratifications. Compatible native readers/writers/recovery precede
activation; old clients, whole-row edits, imports, jobs and restored data cannot
overwrite instruction history or manufacture current scope. A stop for new
revisions must preserve lawful admitted work/recovery and later restrictions.

Use bounded indexed source/history/projection work, current per-operation private
checks, finite measured capacity and actual operators. No new whole-history scan,
global lock, alternate completion engine, CMS platform, permanent edit quota or
whole future Studio prerequisite is selected. Measure misunderstanding, needless
reupload, stale feedback, duplicates and recovery burden with minimized evidence,
not private-content analytics or reading trackers. **P20–P22.**
