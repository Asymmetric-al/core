## Next decision and independent recommendation

**Further fresh completed-read periods are the right next dependency.** Q08 created the capability, Q16 fixed the eligible histories/ceiling, Q17 fixed issuance as the clock and Q18 supplied 720 elapsed hours. Staff still cannot know whether another distinct fresh source admission is allowed after a fresh period ends.

Recommend A: allow another **deliberate, currently source-authorized** fresh completed-read admission after the relevant preceding period has actually ended by eligible ordinary expiry, preserving the same completed source/intended-recipient lineage and Q16 ceiling/current scope. Each admitted effect uses the Q17/Q18 fixed source-issued profile and retains its own history. No automatic renewal, date extension, reopened work or new document copy is needed.

For one complete practical question, A can explicitly propose **sequential periods with no preset lifetime grant-count or cumulative-day ceiling**. Those are new Q19 product terms, not consequences already supplied by Q15 or by the word “further.” A's absence of a preset quota would not guarantee any later approval; each still needs current purpose, administration, eligibility and exact scope. Many deliberate finite periods could nevertheless create a long total access opportunity. State that tradeoff directly rather than claiming a finite individual interval bounds the whole lifetime.

B is coherent: **at most one successfully committed fresh Q08 admission** for this original completed request/intended-recipient/baseline context. It is a stronger finality rule and limits repeated opportunities; it may deny a later legitimate reading need even when the same source would still permit it. Nonuse or a failed notification does not erase a real committed grant. Existing independently authorized help/source work remains, but no clone, new ID, email attachment or changed completion date can evade the chosen limit.

The recommendation is product judgment about permitting current governed help rather than an arbitrary one-time cliff. There is no observed recipient repeat-demand distribution or proven optimal quota. A should not become a configurable renewal platform; B should not be presented as eliminating native concurrency/semantic-history requirements. Both require those controls.

## Scope and partial availability must not drift

“Same scope” means **Q16's fixed completed ceiling ∩ current eligibility ∩ this new reviewed subset**. It does not mean copying the previous period wholesale, unioning old periods, returning all original unfinished requirements, or forcing every future subset to be same-or-narrower than the immediately previous subset. A previously narrow individual grant alone did not permanently shrink the original ceiling. A genuinely binding restriction does retain its effect.

For example, if the actual completed baseline admitted files/actions A and B, a prior discretionary fresh period covering only A does not erase B from the historical ceiling. A later deliberate current review could consider eligible B under the adopted Q19 rule. It cannot admit C merely because C is now retained or was once requested. If B's current source/content-use right has ended, it remains unavailable. This is a hypothetical scope test, not observed demand or permission to issue anything now.

Keep ending cause and scope separate. A genuine source-wide revocation/purpose prohibition cannot be relabeled ordinary expiry by waiting for a nominal date or minting another ID. Conversely, a failed preview, expired session or member-only restriction must not be promoted into a permanent whole-request prohibition. The relevant predecessor's eligible ordinary expiry and each proposed action's current eligibility need source proof with actual cause/scope, not an `expired` status bit alone. Do not use Q19 to adjudicate new adverse-restoration rights.

Q16's partial/status-only view rules remain: one unavailable member need not block independent permitted work; a status-only grant cannot silently promise files; no eligible action cannot be disguised as a successful file grant. None of these changes the required completed baseline or lets unknown root/ending proof default to access.

## Repeat, replay and repair are three different operations

| Situation                                                                                                                           | Required meaning under either option                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Same source intent already committed, reply lost or delivered late                                                                  | Recover that **exact** effect, its original source occurrence/end and current safe projection. Do this before applying new-admission predecessor/quota checks; later grants do not make the old outcome vanish.        |
| Same key but materially different baseline/recipient/scope/policy intent                                                            | Conflict or existing source-owned refreshed review; never replace the original effect silently or fabricate a new retry key.                                                                                           |
| Original effect genuinely did not commit                                                                                            | No admitted period is invented or counted. Retry the legitimate original intent under current source rules. A timeout/failed lookup does not establish this state.                                                     |
| Grant committed but unused, proof remained unavailable, notification failed or period ended before the response arrived             | It was still a source admission with its actual Q18 end. Whether another distinct admission is allowed is exactly Q19, not technical recovery.                                                                         |
| Current source grant remains valid; link/session/proof/representation failed                                                        | Repair the actual mechanism under the same authority/end. No new source period is required solely to fix that problem.                                                                                                 |
| A distinct deliberate new intent reviewed against the current relevant ended predecessor                                            | Apply the chosen repeat/overlap/current-purpose rules and current source/P12 authorization, then admit at most the effect that policy permits. No old end is edited.                                                   |
| An uncommitted intent was reviewed against an older predecessor, while an intervening fresh period was admitted and has since ended | Do not silently advance the stale intent to the next generation. Resolve material current lineage and obtain the existing meaningful review appropriate to the new basis; a long queue wait is not new human approval. |
| Outcome/history required to establish previous admissions is unknown                                                                | Reconcile safely. It is neither an unused one-time slot under B nor proof of repeat eligibility under A.                                                                                                               |

Counting must follow actual source effects, not draft approvals, clicks, messages, provider credentials, page visits or timer records. Under B, deleting/expiring a row or changing identity labels cannot restore a consumed one-time allowance; minimum required source evidence follows the owner retention policy. Under A, a new deliberate approval is still a distinct source effect, not an endless retry of one original intent. Error correction/revocation remains source-owned with preserved history; this question does not invent an exception quota or a fresh-grant loophole for mistaken issuance.

## Minimal source-owned implementation consequences if A is selected

Use the intended Q08 command and existing source/P12 mutation path. Bind the deliberate intent to the exact completed baseline, intended recipient/current scope, materially reviewed terms and relevant predecessor/current-source state. Preserve every admitted effect's original Q18 source occurrence/end; a new allowed effect receives its own source-issued end, not a changed old end or a new F.

The authoritative previous/current effect must not be inferred from MAX(timestamp), greatest ID, most recent email, browser state or a client assertion of “last grant.” A direct source-owned current-head/version or equivalent constrained relation can support bounded exact lookup; no specific extra table, event reconstruction engine, generic sequence service or all-history scan is prescribed.

Two staff actors can review the same ended period. Before a genuinely new effect, the source must atomically enforce the adopted no-overlap/repeat rule and relevant current issuer/recipient/purpose/member/adverse predicates, including cases with no preexisting next row. Per-idempotency-key dedupe, a disabled button, queue concurrency and an expiring worker lease are insufficient by themselves. A competing newer admission cannot be silently overwritten or adopted as the other actor's original outcome. Providers remain outside source locks.

Committed same-intent recovery is different: it should return the old exact result under current disclosure even if its predecessor is no longer current or a later period exists. This ordering avoids turning normal replay into an unjustified new effect or false failure. An uncommitted stale separate intent does not get that exemption; current material review/lineage governs it.

Expiry does not require a sweep to flip a status. The actual source time/terms and ending cause apply, with Q17/Q18's after-wait/exclusive-boundary and coherent-commit qualification. A new period cannot backfill unauthorized reads in a gap. Old terms/adverse facts survive policy changes/restore, and newly repeated scope must still be proved against the one Q16 completed ceiling, not derived by chaining expired permissions.

## Credential and source lineage stay separate

`docs/adr/0037-scanner-safe-exact-artifact-access.md:44–67` distinguishes producer-owned exchange/current authorization from credentials and bounds routine link-replacement overlap for that purpose. A possible Q19 rule forbidding simultaneous **source periods** is not permission to change compatible transport overlap inside one source admission. Conversely, creating a new credential or retaining an existing Auth session does not prove a new Q08 source grant.

Reentry may reuse current compatible proof only under Q01/P12/source rules. Do not require another account or needless sign-in solely because this is a later period. Do not make an expired/revoked source credential sufficient again by resolving it to whichever grant is newest. Delivery/notification remains separately authorized; no automatic reminder/resend/renewal is selected.
