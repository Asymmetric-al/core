We judge that established scope is mature enough to formalize, but no actual intended-user comprehension, operative GF schedule or native source qualification is assumed. B depends on real research access; A depends on preserving blockers rather than filling them with plausible rules.

It moves the agreed multi-owner Phase 29 scope into its formal representation or deliberately gathers more user evidence first. Both preserve specialist owners, current authorization and independent activation gates; neither changes product powers.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes. Current document authority distinguishes formal planning publication from implementation and qualification, and preserves source ownership and complete OpenSpec companion clauses. The session explicitly reserves /to-prd and /to-issues as separate stages.
