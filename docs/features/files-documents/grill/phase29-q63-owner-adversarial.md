## Main conclusion

The important distinction is between finding a candidate, authorizing an exact source operation, and admitting a particular physical destructive attempt. A policy can authorize the ordinary automated path, but neither an old candidate list, queue claim, operator release nor a worker lease is a perpetual delete grant. Current policy, source authority, all uses/holds/copies and live writers must be resolved at a qualified final source/custody dispatch-admission boundary. Providers remain outside the database transaction; no fiction of multi-system atomicity is needed.

After that boundary, a remote effect may already be possible even if its completion is unknown. A later hold/stop cannot promise preservation of that affected copy. It protects remaining preventable work and triggers truthful reconciliation and, when needed, source/incident remediation. A deletion claim must survive lease expiry while the physical effect remains possible. Stopping one operation must also prevent immediate automatic rediscovery of its same stopped target/basis until the actual cause is resolved; otherwise the advertised stop is false.

A is preferable for a routine deterministic cohort whose actual policy expressly authorizes it, because mandatory routine release can become a backlog or habitual clicking. B remains defensible as a deliberate operating choice, but a human click cannot establish missing use coverage or create a legal extension. Source-required human determinations remain mandatory under A. P23/D27 manual/no-auto-purge and specialist or temporary-copy rules remain unchanged.

## Eighteen exact proposed clauses

**O01 — Ordinary automatic path and strict scope.** Permit a qualified ordinary source policy to authorize automatic disposition of its exact admitted record/use/copy cohort without a fresh routine staff release. The policy must expressly allow that mode; missing/unknown policy is not On. Retain every source-required human determination and actual disposition duty. Preserve P23/D27 explicit-review/no-auto-purge, P18/P21/Support/resource/public/import/development source policies and already-qualified transient preview/ZIP/staging cleanup. No generic Delete everything, universal schedule, retention period, provider or new approval platform follows.

**O02 — Complete qualified policy before activation.** The actual source/records/privacy authority supplies the exact supported family/applicability, policy version/effectivity, mode, actor/service powers, typed trigger/date precision, preservation/privacy bounds, human-required exceptions, holds/conflict route, covered record/copy obligations and actual disposition/recovery requirements. Cohort activation requires those fields and a real capable operator path; source approval is not inferred from a label or machine-readable row. No active configuration, existing eligible-record sweep, new default period or legal guarantee is implied by selecting A. Q62 governs actual approved binding/current impact; incompatible or unresolved ordinary cohorts remain blocked at their affected boundary.

**O03 — Candidate discovery is nonauthorizing.** A timer, index, eligibility projection or background scan may locate bounded candidates but creates no disposition permission, hold release, source deletion, new grant or proof of unused data. The authoritative ordinary source constructs only a finite exact work scope over stable record/use/immutable object-generation identities and its actual policy basis. A mutable query or newly eligible member cannot silently enlarge an already-authorized operation. Use the existing qualified executor/source work mechanism; do not add a generic scheduler, client polling loop or independent disposal authority.

**O04 — Source authorization and execution actors are truthful.** Automatic source authorization records the actual approved policy/mode/effectivity, exact scope and governing facts through current qualified source/P12 authority, with the real system/NHI actor and accountable owner. It is not recorded as a fresh human approval or inherited from the request issuer. Human-required work records the real authorized human act and its exact reviewed finite scope. Definition/admin rights, source authorization, manual decision, stop/hold/correction, content/history viewing and provider execution remain separate powers. Current native credential, role label, caller actor/tenant IDs, an old human release or possession of a provider key is insufficient.

**O05 — Shared records and physical targets stay distinct.** Source-approved ending/disposal of one use or record does not authorize removal of a physical copy still required by another independent use, source, hold, recovery obligation or assembly lineage. Preserve exact source identity, immutable version, copy class and independently required references. The actual source must distinguish its record/use result from the physical target result; it cannot call all bytes destroyed while they are lawfully retained for another purpose. Physical deduplication or a matching digest never merges authority. Any source-record minimization, reference retirement and retained disposition proof must follow its own approved class/current rules rather than cascading through unrelated records or keeping a full sensitive archive forever.

**O06 — Complete current coverage before destructive admission.** Before a destructive target can proceed, prove the actual current policy/mode/effectivity and source authority, applicable floors/ceilings/hold/conflict result, complete registered owner/version/use/copy/processor coverage, exact immutable physical target and closure/fencing of all relevant writers. Include drafts, source evidence, shares, prepared communications, previews/renditions, public/recovery/delivery references, exports, uploads, builders, import/restore paths and other actually registered consumers. A visible empty list, inaccessible hidden reference, stale adapter, failed query or incomplete census is not unused. Unknown completeness blocks affected destruction; current lawful unrelated work may continue.

**O07 — Final dispatch admission is the linearization boundary.** A queue claim/lease or earlier source authorization is not the final irreversible permission. Qualify a final source/custody dispatch-admission boundary, after relevant waits, that atomically orders the actual current proof against new holds, references, restrictions, policy changes and target writes, establishes the durable exact attempt and immutable target write fence, and then permits only that admitted external operation. Providers execute outside short database transactions. Do not promise that a hold committed before the literal HTTP call always wins across a separate check/network gap. A hold/adverse fact ordered before final admission wins; after admission a remote effect may already be possible. If a source requires a stronger guarantee, that adapter/cohort remains unavailable until actual proof supplies it.

**O08 — Possibly started means preserve uncertainty.** After dispatch admission, crash, timeout, transport failure or lost response cannot be treated as definitely not started without qualified evidence. Reconcile the original exact target/attempt/provider identity and record known success, known failure or unresolved result; no new key or new target generation as a retry. Preserve completed physical facts even if policy later changes; do not erase what happened or claim recovery by rolling back SQL. A later hold/stop protects remaining preventable work and identifies any target already possibly affected; never falsely report that all copies were preserved. Lease expiry cannot release the deletion/write fence while an admitted effect may still complete. A definitely-not-started result may release/repair only through the source's current qualified reconciliation rule, not elapsed time alone.

**O09 — Finite batches have truthful independent results.** Bound selection/authorization and provider work; keep exact membership/identity and source-declared indivisible groups. Independent targets that remain qualified may proceed while another target becomes blocked, but no stale target or hidden extra member may execute and no partial operation may be labeled wholly disposed. Shared-copy/indivisible-source constraints must finish their own required admission coherently; no fake distributed all-or-none guarantee is made for providers. Retain actual per-target/group results and a currently authorized aggregate, including withheld or unknown outcome detail where disclosure is restricted. Any manual proposal explicitly reviews its finite permitted scope; required body inspection remains source-qualified rather than a ritual for every file.

**O10 — Stop remaining work is real operational containment.** Provide an actual current source/records/custody-authorized **Stop remaining disposal** act for an exact operation/target scope when its cause and authority qualify. It fences still-preventable dispatch and must prevent automatic rediscovery/re-admission of the same stopped target/basis while the cause remains unresolved. It is not merely closing a UI job that the next sweep immediately recreates. Record actual actor/cause/time and known completed/possibly-started/remaining outcomes. Stop does not cancel a provider effect that may already have escaped, restore bytes, release a hold or create a new legal extension. Preserve independent urgent restriction/hold authority; one stop cannot weaken another source duty.

**O11 — Recover stopped versus uncertain work correctly.** Reconcile possibly-started work only under its original identity and current provider/source rules. Separately, an actual authorized cause-resolution can permit the policy to reconsider safely unstarted stopped targets; any subsequent work is a fresh current source authorization over the current exact basis, automatic only if the policy still permits it. It never resumes the stopped intent or silently clears the stop when a timer/mode toggles. A mistaken stop therefore has a supported current repair, not an irrevocable dead end or a new permanent hold. No arbitrary timeout, automatic approval from silence or indefinite waiting is introduced. Actual preservation/privacy/required-disposition duties and their conflict authority remain; operator absence or backlog requires the real timely takeover/escalation path before the cohort is considered qualified.

**O12 — Policy and assignment changes do not create unsafe sweeps.** Apply Q62's historical binding/current successor/attributed correction disciplines to the actual ordinary policy and mode. Preserve original authorization and policy/trigger facts while evaluating current effects. A new automatic mode, shorter period, cleared error or repaired setting does not silently release stale proposals, backfill prior approval or dispose existing records without its qualified prospective/impact treatment and fresh current admission. A more restrictive rule, relevant hold or source correction fences unstarted consequences immediately through the actual current boundary; after dispatch admission record honest possible/completed results and remediate. No old policy immunity forever, summary-only relabel with stale permissive worker, or caller-selected global rule is allowed.

**O13 — Physical completion, backups and external copies are honest.** Disposal completion is scoped to the actual authorized record/copy obligations and verified outcomes. Metadata deletion, object-not-found at an unproved key, a delete marker, provider acknowledgment or local disappearance does not prove all required versions/copies are gone. Register active, derivative, staging, backup/recovery and applicable external/provider classes; verify each required result under its owner. If an approved backup rule requires bounded retention/suppression until later expiry, expose that actual state and never claim physical erasure everywhere. A download is not verified destination custody and supplies no external erasure guarantee. Restore reapplies current policy/correction/hold/revocation/disposition suppression before serving or workers; database backup alone cannot restore object bytes.

**O14 — Mistaken disposal has actual incident recovery, not Undo.** Before final dispatch admission, a current qualified stop/hold/restriction or Q62 correction can prevent remaining effects. After a copy may have been affected, retain exact outcome uncertainty, preserve unaffected required copies and use actual authorized incident/source/records recovery. Only independently lawful qualified recovery can restore data; it must not revive expired access, invalid source eligibility or lawfully suppressed disposed material. If required bytes cannot be recovered, record actual loss and provide the accountable source remediation with truthful limits. Correcting a policy, assignment, authorization or result record is not recreated bytes. No automatic new request, acceptance, source waiver, notification or financial/public state change follows.

**O15 — Current permissions, structure and every door.** Preserve one fine-grained source/P12 decision, static field classification/subtract-only projection and prescribed coarse Tenant RLS. Use trusted Tenant/environment/source/actor/service context, exact scoped immutable references and defined state/copy/trigger types, suitable uniqueness/current-winner constraints, non-null required scope and immutable attributed facts. Qualified global policy definitions need not be duplicated by Tenant, but their bindings/alternatives/operations remain compatible and scoped. Recheck disclosure for candidates/proposals/counts/blocked reasons/history/result recovery and byte access; no hidden source/case leaks or forced preview grant. Qualify effective schema/table/column/sequence/PUBLIC/named/default/inherited EXECUTE/overload grants; old/resulting policies, trigger transformations, SELECT/RETURNING/UPSERT/MERGE/bulk, views/materializations/REST/GraphQL/RPC, definer owner/search path/FORCE/BYPASS, service/NHI/provider/import/restore/operator paths and safe pooled Tenant context. A valid credential, zero-row change or boolean flag is not successful source authorization.

**O16 — Small useful UI and lawful evidence.** Use the existing source Records/status/history view and bounded actual source/P9 operational work for exceptions or mandated human decisions, not a new disposal console, inbox, saved-view system or task for every routine record. Explain only permitted policy/class/scope/mode, actual result and an effective next action. Distinguish eligibility, required authorization/review, blocked/unknown, admitted/processing, partial result, stopped remaining work and verified stated-scope disposition as views of independent facts. No blanket all-copy claim or giant global enum. Detail/menu/render/GET/prefetch, typing/select/blur/IME and Cancel remain inert; actual stop/review/retry/cause resolution is deliberate and request-integrity protected. Preserve lawful input/focus and original outcome across navigation/network faults, current context changes, keyboard/AT/touch/mobile/reflow/RTL/locale. Keep business history, required security audit, minimized replay controls and technical diagnostics separately scoped/scheduled; no bodies, secret keys, raw private paths or full sensitive snapshots in routine logs.

**O17 — Finite work and controlled activation.** Qualify actual bounded candidate/authorization/target/copy/processor/batch/concurrency/query/lock/retry/reconciliation/history/cache/retention/restore/cost profiles with units, boundary behavior and real workload; no copied Eve100/500 limit or vendor timing. Use set-wise indexed current queries with stable unique continuation and current per-page rights, preserve actual P12 R6 exceptions, avoid global locks/N+1 and whole-history admission scans. No provider call inside a long source transaction. Map retained data and every old writer/read/job/provider copy before migration; unknown legacy evidence stays explicitly unqualified. Deploy current guards/readers/recovery before favorable automatic writers; scoped disable/rollback preserves original facts, stops and write fences. Restored or older code cannot revive stopped work or destructive eligibility. A supported source policy and capable operators/native proofs are activation gates, not monitoring afterthoughts.

**O18 — Exact adoption and stage.** Record selected A with these ordinary source authorization, dispatch-admission, stop/reconsideration and verified-outcome amendments. Formal canonical source/OpenSpec/design/tasks must incorporate actual owning clauses when authorized; a generic provider example or short generated scenario cannot supply authority. Keep R-POL-ordinary's actual policies and R-LEGACY/current-native/user evidence open where unqualified; this review chooses no period or legal rule. Pending-review ADR0063 is justified by the genuine automatic-versus-fresh-release tradeoff and durable irreversible-source consequences. No new glossary term is needed: policy, source authorization, disposition and copy outcome can be defined in the contract without a new File entity. Preserve Q01–Q62/33 Files definitions and root vocabulary. No ratification, actual delete, implementation, hosted mutation, formal publication, commit/push or Q64 is inferred.
