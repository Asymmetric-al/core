## Disposition and corrected meaning

**Keep A, with the eight amendments below.** For a genuinely new ordinary Request changes decision, offer one visible recipient-email choice in the existing review, initially selected only when the exact source, actor, recipient, qualified Live message, effective Delivery Plan and channel permit it. Staff may turn it off. The source review and current safe in-request guidance remain independent from notification and actual delivery.

Make tenant configuration concrete: provide this event's entry in the existing System Messages/Delivery Plan experience, initially showing the source-owned **Request recipient** audience and its optional email step. The native system/default plan enables that optional step; authorized tenant staff can publish Off and use the contract-permitted content, language, sender and reply controls. This exact default-plan adoption is part of the proposed amendment, not a claim that missing/legacy/unqualified settings already mean On. Show where the source's recipient comes from and expose its existing authorized change-recipient route where actually available. Changing the person is a source-recipient revision operation, not editing an email list.

This is a useful bounded who/how configuration, not a fake multi-audience picker. A further named recipient role or step may appear only if independently declared and qualified for this exact event. **Q26 does not invent a staff/team subscription, automatic copy-to-reviewer, SMS/push channel or general audience builder.** Q24 responsibility does not grant notification disclosure. The per-review checkbox controls this identified optional recipient-email step; it is not a whole-plan kill switch or permission to suppress an independently required step.

The strongest alternative remains default-off in the same review. A carries a real risk of overlooked outward communication; the consequence and effective recipient must be understandable, with actual comprehension proof. A can be defended for action-needed awareness without claims that all users prefer email. Tenant controls and the per-action choice are complementary; no extra routine confirmation, separate send wizard or new notification platform is necessary.
