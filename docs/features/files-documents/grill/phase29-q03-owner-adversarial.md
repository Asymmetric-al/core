## Disposition

**Accept with required amendments.** The policy is viable and fits the ordinary new native private-document-request profile. The essential amendment is an explicit, source-admitted correction boundary: after durable private byte finalization and current source admission, the exact correction becomes the pending review candidate for the targeted occurrence, and the corrected predecessor can no longer acquire a new acceptance through that occurrence. This must happen without waiting for technical preview readiness. A later unsafe or unusable correction must not silently revive the file the recipient explicitly corrected.

This is a new ordinary-profile policy choice, not an existing universal owner rule. Existing contracts already require immutable successors, source qualification, exact-version decisions, current authorization, safe retries and concurrency fencing. They do not yet select this exact correction-admission/current-candidate policy for the new profile.

## Required exact semantics

1. **Closed ordinary-profile capability.** `Send corrected file` is available only to a currently admitted actor for their exact source-authorized submitted occurrence while that occurrence remains unaccepted and open to correction. Overall request openness, original uploader metadata, matching mailbox, an old link, an open tab or preview permission is insufficient. A freeze must be an explicit source state; simply viewing a file does not freeze it. Q01's qualified current session/proof rules remain; this does not impose a new email ceremony on every click.

2. **Exact intended target and preserved cardinality.** A correction names the exact prior submission occurrence/member it corrects. Filename, content digest and proximity never choose a target. It replaces only that member's current review candidacy within the admitted source use; it does not discard other supporting files, change another recipient's submission, or supersede other independently admitted uses of the same bytes. Adding supporting evidence is a separate source intention. Do not assume one file per requirement.

3. **Four different boundaries.** (a) selecting a local file or beginning transfer; (b) exact private bytes durably finalized and source correction admitted; (c) required hygiene/processing established; (d) source business acceptance. Technical preview readiness is a separate property, not an additional business decision. UI, events, database constraints and workers must not collapse these boundaries.

4. **Permanent ordinary-profile admission rule.** Before (b), the original pending candidate remains and no review lock is taken. At (b), current authority, source correction eligibility, request/recipient binding, target occurrence, relevant review-set revision, intake constraints and finalized immutable byte identity are checked. One short source transaction appends the attributable correction/submission result, establishes the explicit successor relation, changes the targeted current review candidacy, fences stale acceptance, and records safe audit/outbox facts. No transaction spans upload, scan, browser thought time, notification or provider work. Do not claim a distributed transaction with storage. Lost finalization/admission responses are reconciled through the same operation identity.

5. **Explicitly corrected is not still acceptable by default.** Once (b) commits, further acceptance of the predecessor for that corrected occurrence fails even while the successor is checking. It is unsafe to wait for preview success: staff could otherwise approve the document the sender has authoritatively identified as wrong. Until source-required hygiene is satisfied, the correction remains received/checking and cannot satisfy the requirement. A failed or unsafe correction does not silently revive or approve its predecessor. Source-authorized recovery may allow another correction or an explicit existing source decision; no new automatic restore/choose-old-version right is invented.

6. **The source chooses the right conflict boundary.** Correcting and reviewing compare the targeted occurrence plus every relevant source review-set dependency. Avoid a universal whole-request revision that makes unrelated items conflict. Equally, do not check only a file pointer when an owner decision concerns a bundle: P41's exact decision evidence set and P21's material confirmation dependencies must still be protected. This defines required behavior, not a frozen table/pointer schema.

7. **One authoritative race outcome.** If correction admission wins, an acceptance based on the predecessor conflicts and the reviewer must inspect the current source-approved candidate/bundle before another decision. If acceptance, closure, an explicit freeze or another incompatible source transition wins, the stale correction cannot replace that decision or reopen the item. Its private staging is handled by governed cleanup/reconciliation and its UI reports the true outcome. Neither browser transfer start nor notification arrival establishes priority.

8. **Concurrent corrections do not silently replace each other.** Two corrections against the same prior target/review-set revision cannot both become the active successor for that target. The loser receives a source-safe conflict, reloads permitted current state, and requires a deliberate currently permitted correction rather than automatic retarget/rebase. Different source-independent members can proceed without artificial request-wide conflict; source-dependent bundles remain fenced. Same-operation same-payload retry returns one outcome; same key with different intent/content conflicts. Content deduplication is not submission identity.

9. **Worker results are version-bound observations.** Each scan, finalization, preview and notification callback binds its exact source occurrence, immutable byte identity, technical generation and applicable source restriction/disposition fences. A late success for an older version may complete permitted evidence about that version, but cannot make it the current candidate, accept it, revive revoked access, renew retention or satisfy the successor. Cleanup cannot delete promoted/held/shared-use objects through a stale staging claim.

10. **Browser loss is not source revocation.** An independently admitted worker uses its current qualified source/service authority after the participant closes the browser or its session expires. The participant must regain current access for a new command or protected read. Actual source withdrawal, recipient-binding change, tenant deactivation, hold/disposition or safety restriction invokes the applicable worker and read fences; a valid worker does not create continued participant access.

11. **Source limits stay real.** Type/size/cardinality, current requirement version, deadline, legal entity and recipient/source restrictions remain authoritative at correction admission. Starting transfer before a due time is not a universal extension. A request edit cannot silently retarget already-staged bytes to a new purpose/recipient. Retries do not mint new quota or reset time, and a bounded recoverable failure must not require starting a new request or duplicating the entire journey.

12. **No automatic inherited review meaning.** A clean scan or preview proves neither content correctness nor business acceptance. Previous approval, signature, claimant attestation or acceptance cannot flow to the correction. The normal applicable review path remains; Q03 does not add a universal new reviewer/two-person ceremony, but it does preserve every existing exact-version requirement.

13. **Truthful, calm same-context UX.** Show the source-authorized exact file/item and the consequence of sending its correction in the existing request; keep the primary action literal. Distinguish `Sending`, `Received — checking`, `Ready for review`, an approved safe failure message, and source acceptance only when those facts are true. Once the correction is admitted, show that it is the file awaiting review. A progress bar finishing is not acceptance. A preview outage alone must not block an otherwise valid correction. Stale reviewer content must be visibly identified before any decision; never move their decision to newer unseen bytes.

14. **Helpful recovery without broadening rights.** Interrupted transfer before admission leaves the prior candidate intact and supports bounded safe resumption. Ambiguous submission shows `Checking whether your correction was received` and reconciles before offering a new action. An unusable admitted correction offers another currently permitted correction or the source-approved help route. A finalized/frozen item offers its source correction/reopening route. Do not tell recipients to email private files around controls. Retain input in memory where possible, preserve focus, announce meaningful status accessibly and do not require preview visibility to operate the correction control.

15. **Preservation does not grant an archive or deletion.** Earlier evidence and decisions remain under source retention/hold/disposition. A correction is neither erasure of a sensitive mistake nor permission to browse/download all old versions. Current permitted receipts may accurately identify supersession without introducing an earlier-version archive. Another source use or hold remains independently controlling. Any wrong-sensitive-upload response uses the qualified owner privacy/disposition path.

16. **No duplicated domain machinery.** Use the same source request, shared task mechanism, private byte lifecycle, authorization owner and existing notification owners. Do not make a second task/inbox, generic document approval truth, ownerless correction service, file editor, workflow engine or full Phase34 prerequisite. Source adapters must declare eligibility, meaning, version/currentness and lifecycle before activation. This is permanent bounded behavior, not a compatibility shortcut around predecessor owners.

## Specialized owner reconciliation

- **P21:** native request submission correction is not claimant confirmation, a submitted Claim Version edit, expense approval or financial correction. Its own helper evidence must satisfy the staged/scanned/current-link requirements before being linked into the claim; a material evidence change requires the owner-defined fresh confirmation/submission. Preserve exact item/split and many-to-many evidence coverage. Q03's early native review fence cannot silently rewrite this order.
- **P18:** official artifact replacement uses source-owned issuance/correction authority and exact purpose currentness. The valid-predecessor fallback exists because the source has not necessarily withdrawn that document. The new native profile explicitly treating an admitted correction as removal of the old pending candidate from further review is a different meaning. Neither rule overrides the other.
- **P26:** reuse qualified private preview mechanics only through the exact request-source/recipient adapter already required by Q02. No Support staff permission transfer. Preview failures do not roll back business submission/currentness. Preview-ready success does not create source acceptance or original download rights.
- **P28/P23:** resource/media publication and editorial currentness retain their own owner commands. A recipient correction cannot silently publish a replacement catalog resource or invalidate independently valid publication. Catalog preparation and source reuse remain permitted under Q02's no-general-drive boundary.
- **P34/P41:** immutable resubmission and source evidence qualification remain. Native simple document correction can use the shared bounded task/evidence seams without building full Workflow Studio. Application acceptance, reference category/quantity, exact decision bundles, agreements and signature successors are not generic file acceptance.

## Acceptance evidence required before implementation can be called qualified

1. Real source transaction tests prove both correction/review race orders and no phantom receipt after rollback; add simultaneous same-target corrections and independent different-target corrections.
2. Include a multi-file requirement and an aggregate evidence decision. Correct one member; preserve the others; stale aggregate decisions fail while unrelated independent work succeeds.
3. Fault injection at transfer, durable-byte finalization, source commit, outbox publication, scan completion, preview promotion and response delivery. Each retry has one attributable outcome and no ambiguous UI success.
4. A scan-pending correction immediately prevents acceptance of its predecessor. Scan-failed/unsafe then preview-failed fixtures must never auto-revive it. Existing exact-version history remains lawful and unchanged.
5. Worker callback replay/reordering, stale cleanup, recipient/session expiry, source withdrawal, recipient revision change, tenant disablement and legal hold all preserve their distinct fences.
6. A P21 collaborator cannot modify a submitted claim through the ordinary control; P18 artifact replacement retains its own currentness policy; P41 acceptance/signature rules and bundle versioning are unaffected.
7. No cross-tenant, cross-recipient, cross-source-use, raw-object or guessed-version access. Replayed results require current viewing permission. No raw private filenames, content, credentials or diagnostics in ordinary telemetry/events.
8. Participant and reviewer tests cover keyboard, screen reader announcements, mobile/zoom, safe status wording, multiple tabs, interrupted/slow upload, response loss, out-of-order updates, expired access and source-approved help. Staff cannot approve a newer version they did not review; participants can correct when preview is unavailable.
9. Confirm the canonical owner/OpenSpec successor and source adapter contract are adopted before activation. Passing isolated tests, a published ADR, or an apparent enabled button does not prove hosted RLS, storage behavior or the complete human journey.

## Exact amendment status and residual scope

Required new Q03 policy amendments are clauses 1–8 and their source-qualified operational consequences: especially early source admission, targeted member/review-set fencing, no silent predecessor revival, and deliberate race/conflict recovery. The underlying immutable bytes, current authorization, owner-specific decisions, retention/holds, Q01 recipient proof and Q02 exact readback are inherited settled constraints, not new questions.

This note does not decide original download, post-closure duration, a participant previous-version archive, sender deletion, post-acceptance reopen policy, source-wide mandatory two-person review, universal numeric quotas, provider selection or database schema. None is necessary to make the chosen pre-acceptance correction path concrete and safe.
