# Phase 29 Q08 — new read access after ordinary expiry

**Status:** Historical researched question prepared 2026-10-20. Conrad
selected and ratified A with the complete required amendment package on
2026-10-20: allow fresh staff-approved access. Q01–Q08 are ratified grooming
intent. No fresh duration/anchor, repeat/overlap rule, notification or
implementation is selected by this record.

## The one decision

When an ordinary completed request's access period has ended, but the
organization has a legitimate current reason to let its intended recipient
read the submitted material again, **should appropriately authorized staff
be able to approve fresh, limited read-only access through the same request?**

Recommend **A — Allow authorized staff to approve new, limited read access**.
The expired authority and its history remain ended. This is an explicit new
source decision, not a reset of Q07's clock or a hidden extension.

This question concerns **ordinary timed access expiry**. It does not authorize
overriding security revocation, a binding purpose/content-use end, disposal,
recipient disqualification or another source prohibition. Retained bytes and
a person's possession of the old link are insufficient reasons for new access.

## What is already settled

Q07 provides an exclusive ordinary maximum of 720 elapsed hours from normal
source fulfillment, preserves earlier and subsequently shortened binding ends,
and denies implicit renewal through login, help, replay or resends. Q06 keeps
the request completed/read-only; Q03 does not reopen corrections; Q05 does not
provide ordinary superseded-file access.

Those decisions deliberately leave a **separately authorized fresh-access
policy** unresolved. A can be compatible only if the source expressly admits
fresh access for the current permitted request purpose, recipient, scope and
finite period while preserving the old
grant and completion evidence. Creating a new ID alone cannot evade a binding
limit. B can also be coherent: the completed-request lane remains closed and
existing separately authorized assistance retains its own rules.

## Two practical options

| Option                                                             | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Benefit and cost                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Staff can approve fresh, limited read access (recommended)** | Through the existing governed help/source workflow, staff with the specific current authority can approve a new finite read-only scope for the intended recipient. The same completed request supplies context; ordinary submission/correction stays closed. | Provides a controlled in-product way to meet a legitimate later need without another upload or business request solely to retrieve files. Adds an access-issuance command, explicit source policy, history and qualification work; staff must be authorized to grant, not merely view. |
| **B — No fresh access through this completed-request lane**        | Keep the ended view closed. The recipient uses existing governed assistance; any independent document-delivery or source-rights path must already be authorized on its own terms.                                                                            | Smallest completed-request capability and a firmer lifecycle boundary. A legitimate later retrieval may require another qualified source process, or the organization may have no permitted way to supply it. B is not permission to email private attachments or bypass the system.   |

Both retain useful, privacy-safe help. Neither grants automatic self-renewal,
permanent access, general file-library membership or a private metadata
fallback after expiry. A third option that simply auto-renews every old link
would contradict the settled direction, so it is not presented as an equally
sound default.

## Recommendation and strongest alternative

Choose A. Q07 deliberately makes ordinary access finite, while organizations
may still have legitimate source-authorized follow-up work. A keeps that
decision in the owning source and creates a reviewable alternative to ad hoc
workarounds. The user can return to familiar context; the organization can
approve a limited purpose without reopening intake or granting broad account
access.

This is product judgment. We have not measured how often Asym recipients
return late, whether staff are available promptly, or whether existing
independent delivery paths already cover the need. No particular ministry
travel, staffing, shared-device or compliance workflow is asserted as fact.

B is the strongest simpler alternative. If the organization wants ordinary
completed requests to be a strict one-period service and already has suitable
governed delivery/support, another access capability may be unnecessary.
The tradeoff is deliberate: help may explain the limit without being able to
restore this view. It must not advertise a copy or deadline extension it
cannot lawfully and technically provide.

A does not make every staff member a gatekeeper for every read. It concerns
the exceptional new access decision after expiry; subsequent permitted reads
use the existing qualified recipient journey.

## What current official documentation supports

- [Tresorit shared-link management](https://support.tresorit.com/hc/en-us/articles/360005537333-Manage-shared-links),
  updated 6 October 2026, says inactive/revoked links cannot be reactivated and
  a new link is required. This supports distinguishing an ended share from
  a new one; it does not establish Core's recipient, purpose or duration rules.
- [SafeSend Secure File Transfer: Resend Access Link](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/safesend/secure-file-transfer/usage-guides/online-portal/resend-access-link)
  restricts that action to active, non-expired documents and permitted firm
  settings. This is useful evidence that resending delivery information and
  authorizing post-expiry access are distinct operations.
- [Submittable Next applicant guidance](https://next.support.submittable.com/hc/en-us/articles/41242987982231-Applicant-FAQs)
  leaves organization-profile access with its organization administrator,
  rather than allowing platform support to grant it. This supports ownership
  of permission decisions; it does not prove a completed-file renewal feature
  or justify adding organization membership to Asym recipients.
- [Microsoft guest expiration](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/admin/manage-guest-expiration-for-a-site)
  describes administrator extensions that apply to all of a guest's access on
  a site. That is a counterexample for scope: Asym's request-only rule must
  not accidentally restore a whole site's, person's or tenant's access.
- [GOV.UK confirmation-page guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/confirmation-pages/)
  calls for helpful behavior when people return through a bookmark, including
  useful contact guidance if the original view is unavailable. It does not
  require granting more access or preserving private confirmation indefinitely.

The evidence supports owner-controlled assistance and clear distinctions among
authentication, resend and permission. It does not establish a universal
requirement to renew, a safe renewal count or another automatic 30 days.
The [comparative research](phase29-q08-comparative-research.md) documents wider
examples and conflicting vendor wording without treating them as Core policy.

## The smallest clear journey if A is selected

1. **Recipient:** sees the already-selected permitted ended/help state and
   uses the real source help route. No promise of approval, private details
   before proof, forced account creation or new attachment upload.
2. **Authorized staff:** opens the existing source request, checks the current
   purpose and recipient, and reviews the proposed exact read scope and actual
   finite end before a deliberate **Grant new read access** action. This is
   illustrative wording, not a selected new page or implementation.
3. **Source:** makes a fresh current permission decision and records the
   decision, scope, end and relationship to the ended authority. The original
   completion and deadlines remain unchanged; duplicate action/retry cannot
   create additional grants or silently move an end.
4. **Recipient:** uses Q01's qualified current entry to the allowed read-only
   context, with the new actual end displayed. Q04 independently qualifies
   each original/preview; Q05's superseded-content restriction remains.

Use the existing shared Base UI/base-maia request controls and the same
meaningful file actions. Reuse verified context to avoid unnecessary repeated
data entry, while preserving current proof and explicit recipient confirmation.
Do not turn a contact/help action into an automatic grant or background email.
The staff issuance decision and any separately authorized delivery notification
retain distinct outcomes.

This journey does not prescribe a free-text justification essay, per-file
manual clicking, a new support queue or a multi-person approval workflow.
The source must still know and audit the permitted purpose and scope.
Current authority decides whether a staff actor can issue access; possession
of a broad staff role, service key or ability to read a file is insufficient.

## Binding boundaries for either option

- **Same completed work:** new access is not a new submission, correction,
  acceptance, completed-date rewrite or request reopening.
- **Proven prior admission:** this path concerns an ordinary read admission
  that actually existed and expired. It cannot repair unknown historical
  eligibility by labelling an initial grant a renewal.
- **Exact scope:** the source may expose only the same recipient's currently
  eligible own-submission members and Q05-permitted context within the same
  or a narrower part of the previously admitted completed-view scope.
  No arbitrary historical version, folder, person-wide library or
  other request comes along automatically. A new purpose or another
  participant's submitted material is outside this decision.
- **Current authority:** the proposed grant needs compatible P4/P12 admission,
  explicit authority to issue, current recipient/purpose permission, privacy/
  safety floors and independent representation eligibility.
- **Hard prohibitions:** elapsed ordinary access is different from expired
  content-use permission, disposal or security revocation. This route cannot
  route around a still-binding restriction or reverse Q07's preserved genuine
  earlier authority shortening by minting another grant.
- **Old authority stays ended:** an old bearer link cannot become renewed
  permission by itself. URL/token mechanics are an implementation decision;
  a safe same-request route may resolve current new authority only after the
  required confirmation. No new URL shape is selected here.
- **No perpetual-drive escape hatch:** fresh issuance is a deliberate
  source-qualified act for a current admitted purpose, not an entitlement to
  keep renewing indefinitely. Repetition limits and timing require an explicit
  policy before this capability can activate.
- **Independent custody:** retention of evidence neither authorizes new
  disclosure nor makes unavailable/disposed material reconstructible.
- **Honest assistance:** B preserves existing independent rights and help;
  it does not imply that staff must supply a copy, use external storage or
  invent a new document request as a workaround.

## What this question does and does not settle

Q08 chooses whether the ordinary completed-request experience should include
**an explicit source-authorized staff capability to issue fresh finite read
access after ordinary timed expiry**. That is the product boundary under review.

It does not choose the new period's duration/anchor, repeat limits, exact
permission-catalog binding, request-help UI, notifications or a source
exception policy. The original Q07 completion-based 30-day cap remains intact;
it is not automatically reused as a 30-day period from a staff click.
If A is chosen, those dependencies must be explicitly resolved and qualified
before this capability activates. Choosing B creates no new issuance dependency.

Active-request duration also remains unresolved, but it governs the earlier
intake stage and need not precede this post-completion capability choice.
No source profile may activate with an incomplete finite authority contract.

At question preparation, no ADR-0008 or glossary term had been created for the then-unanswered question. No tests,
