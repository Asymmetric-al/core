## Exact minimal technical amendments

The following R identifiers are this note's traceability for the consolidated review. They do not prescribe schema names, add product features or constitute ratification.

**R01 — Gate the actual first original source act.** Before first ordinary recipient collection access is admitted, the source must establish the qualified Q24 person/team review route for declared work, in addition to all existing issuer/recipient/purpose/entry/custody requirements. A valid supported default can satisfy the gate without a picker, claim, extra approval or online check. Missing, invalid, ambiguous or indeterminate required readiness denies that new issuance, preserves permitted preparation and exposes a capable safe staff resolution. Do not expose usable original collection authority first and mark it waiting merely to satisfy the rule later. Apply the gate at every supported original-issuance entry, not just email/UI.

**R02 — Prove declared review capability without circular future evidence.** The source resolves readiness for its actual declared requirements, purpose and applicable sensitivity/scope, using an eligible individual or usable qualified team route. A label, queue row, generic staff count, notification preference or creator role is insufficient. Do not require permissions on nonexistent uploaded-file rows, freeze an entire future roster, demand online presence or promise future capacity/turnaround. Distinct legitimate reviewers may cover source-defined work where supported; no universal single-reviewer/cardinality rule follows. Real later evidence/classification/safety/current review rights are checked when they exist, with owned recovery for unexpected restrictions and no declassification shortcut.

**R03 — Keep issuer, routing administrator, eligible route and reviewer separate.** The source establishes exact current issuer context and operation authority. If responsibility needs changing, only the existing qualified administrator/system binding may change it; eligibility of the target does not authorize the caller to assign it. The person who later reviews must independently satisfy Q21/Q23. Assignment creates no capability, and the issuer need not be silently granted the target's private evidence rights. Missing P12/current context is not cured by readiness, a demo fallback, service role or source proof from another tenant/purpose. Supported automated issuance retains its actual NHI/owner constraints; Q25 introduces no new automation issuer lane.

**R04 — Recover original success before the new gate.** Under appropriate current source lookup/recovery authority, identify and compare the original semantic operation before evaluating new-issuance readiness or calculating new I/end. An already committed same intent returns/reconciles the original admitted result and allowed history, even if today's review route is no longer ready. Current result disclosure and any requested new action remain separately authorized; original receipt does not renew private access or require revealing forbidden details. A failed/filtered lookup is not proof of nonexistence. Readiness failure must not relabel a committed original as a draft, fabricate a replacement, restart terms or rerun task/communication effects.

**R05 — Separate material intent from resolved outputs.** Preserve exact caller-intended source/recipient/purpose/material policy terms and any deliberately chosen material target. Same identity with incompatible material intent conflicts. Server-resolved current responsibility/eligibility witness, first I/end and other admitted results are output/basis, not values a retry regenerates into a different request. Before any original effect commits, a legitimate resolver result may change under the same unchanged source intent; revalidate it at admission without blindly pinning stale readiness or falsely forcing a new request. Once committed, preserve the admitted outputs and proof; later currentness is a distinct source fact. Use minimal protected provenance, not a permanent copied team roster or secret-bearing payload.

**R06 — Coherent current admission and exact time.** First issuance, required readiness/authority evidence, original identity/terms, mandatory history and selected durable handoff must be coherent and atomic where co-located. Order actual source/recipient/role/membership/grant/requirement changes with the admission boundary; recheck material facts and live time after relevant waits. No read-then-insert gap, broad random-key lock or prior preflight snapshot can substitute for the real invariant. Preserve the already ratified qualified source occurrence whose effect commits; a JS timestamp, NOW(), callback or worker-start time is not physical-commit proof. Original limits are resolved once at actual I. External provider calls stay outside source locks; no perpetual staff reservation is introduced.

**R07 — Distinguish preparation, scheduling and delivery.** Authorized draft/task/preparation does not itself start recipient authority or the original clock. If an already-supported scheduled command only prepares future issuance, the actual later source command checks current authority/readiness and establishes I then. If a source lawfully issues now and merely schedules delivery later, I/end and the readiness decision are already fixed now; delivery retries do not reissue. Preserve exact command semantics and Q11 identities instead of imposing a new schedule product or moving clocks to invitation delivery, recipient proof, first upload or reviewer acknowledgment. GET/HEAD/scanner visits cannot perform a new business issuance.

**R08 — Coherent faults and source work.** Preserve known source success through secondary task/history-view/delivery failures and recover the exact required handoff without duplicating it. Missing mandatory decision evidence is not a successful new issuance; unknown commitment requires reconciliation before new work or destructive cleanup. Do not treat a silent empty plan, a generic task row, no notifications or a handoff ledger entry as readiness or recipient authority. Permitted pre-issuance repair work remains separate from the original recipient issuance and cannot trigger its outward invitation/access inadvertently. Real source success is not undone to recover a stale task panel.

**R09 — Keep the new gate prospective and scoped.** Q25 applies to new first original ordinary issuances governed by this policy. It does not retroactively require a Q25 readiness witness from a legitimate earlier issuance or change already-issued intake, fresh source admissions, existing credential/resend recovery, Q22 completion or access limits. Later readiness/authority loss still triggers the source/P12's actual current restrictions and Q24 recovery; nothing here promises continued unauthorized intake or adds an automatic blanket stop. Do not fabricate readiness history, infer a new I from a restore/import, resurrect expired access or copy the original gate onto Q13/Q15/Q08 policies without their own explicit source decision.

**R10 — Enforce and qualify the source boundary across data and operations.** Protect tenant/environment/source/recipient/declared-work/responsibility/operation relationships and original actor/time/terms from caller-controlled mutation, same-tenant reparenting, null trusted fields, stale parent changes and misleading readiness flags. Verify effective grants, RLS old/new-row checks, SELECT/RETURNING, views/RPC/functions/trigger transformations and privileged source/worker/import paths with actual roles. Current purpose limits readiness diagnostics, candidates, counts, history, cached outputs and replay; show a safe usable staff next step without disclosing protected rosters or evidence. Use indexed bounded current-resolution/original-operation lookup and durable existing recovery; measure finite source budgets before activation, without a new staffing SLA or all-member task fan-out. Mixed versions and restore must preserve later source decisions/restrictions before outward work resumes; gate unsupported writers rather than silently bypass the new invariant.

## SQL/currentness precision

For effective RLS, inspect real privileges and all applicable policies. UPDATE/ALL may inherit WITH CHECK from USING when it is not explicitly supplied; permissive policies combine with OR and restrictive policies with AND. BEFORE triggers can transform the resulting row before policy checks. Thus neither missing keyword text nor one strict-looking policy establishes protection or bypass. Service-role command tables require their actual trusted source checks, and table/column/SELECT/RETURNING privileges matter. [PostgreSQL CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html).

Definer functions run with their owner's privileges; table-owner/FORCE and superuser/BYPASSRLS behavior must be distinguished. Native checks cannot be inferred from SDK construction, while the actual caller-bound membership RPC described in Q24 has real auth.uid()/tenant restrictions. Current policy/role/trigger deployment was not queried. No new native browser policy or hosted-engine claim is made. [PostgreSQL row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html).

Read Committed can observe different snapshots across statements; waited-on target rows are rechecked but arbitrary multi-row readiness and issuer/source facts are not automatically coherent. Row locks cover retrieved rows, not a nonexistent original effect or all future membership mutations. Unique tenant/transport keys alone cannot coordinate different keys for the same original semantic source event. Use the qualified source/P12 revision and transaction/constraint mechanism, with bounded conflict retry and safe no-row handling; no universal new table/locking algorithm is mandated. [Transaction isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html), [explicit locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/explicit-locking.html).

NOW()/CURRENT_TIMESTAMP are transaction start; statement_timestamp is statement start and clock_timestamp is the instant called. None certifies physical commit or resolves the source race by itself. After a lock wait, an old timestamp cannot backdate readiness/authority or the first issuance. The source-qualified occurrence and durable effect must be ordered correctly with actual invalidating changes; later restriction applies on its own actual scope. [PostgreSQL time functions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/functions-datetime.html).

Primary Postgres 17 semantics were rechecked on 2026-10-22. This is a versioned reference, not confirmation of hosted Postgres version or native SQL execution. Current Supabase/source-role limits remain those documented in the Q24 review; no SQL or auth implementation was changed.
