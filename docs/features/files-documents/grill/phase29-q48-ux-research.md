## One practical question, with two fair alternatives

**For eligible ordinary document requests with actual outstanding recipient work, should Asym be able to send reminders automatically under a tenant-controlled source plan, while staff can still send a reminder deliberately—or should every reminder require a deliberate staff action?**

| Option                                                                                  | Practical behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Strongest advantage                                                                                                                                                                    | Real cost                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A — Allow optional automatic reminders plus staff-triggered reminders. Recommended.** | A qualified enabled plan can initiate the reminder without a staff member pressing Send each time. Staff can still use the normal deliberate reminder action and can control the automatic plan through bounded supported settings. | Routine follow-up need not depend on someone remembering to revisit every still-actionable request. Teams that want manual handling can retain it.                                     | Adds real scheduling, stop/current-usefulness, duplicate, policy-change and delivery-recovery obligations. Incorrect automation can nag someone who already supplied everything or who has no valid next action.               |
| **B — Staff-triggered reminders only.**                                                 | Staff review the current request and deliberately send each eligible reminder through the supported source communication flow. No scheduled program independently initiates a reminder.                                             | Gives staff a current opportunity to account for context the source may not represent—an agreed pause, sensitive circumstances or recent conversation—and has fewer autonomous states. | Follow-up depends on staff attention and availability; repetitive work may be missed or delayed. Manual initiation does not itself prevent wrong recipients, stale source facts, duplicate clicks or misleading expired links. |

**Recommend A as an optional capability**, with a small visible plan and exact source eligibility, not universal automatic sending. It provides the automation choice while preserving a credible manual path. This is a product judgment informed by documented comparators and the founder's required outcome; no Asym follow-up volume, error frequency, response-time gain or recipient preference has been measured.

This question does **not** select an Enabled-by-default policy, numerical cadence, maximum count, business-day calendar, quiet hours, time zone inference, per-item schedule, repeat-after-correction rule, paused-plan catch-up rule, new SMS channel, digest, escalation or configurable workflow engine. Those operational consequences must be resolved concretely in the selected-answer review before ratification/activation, not silently borrowed from another notification purpose or a generic GEN-06 proposal. Keeping them unselected here does not make them optional release work.

Both options include useful native reminder functionality. B is not a powerless “email them yourself” instruction or removal of the founder's reminder requirement. Both require current source/P12 permission, a qualified purpose/destination, safe content and a real usable next step. A does not require a complex rule builder simply because a comparator has one.

## Illustrative participant, staff and tenant journey

Illustration only: Jordan requested an equipment inventory and a receipt from Elena. The inventory is received and awaiting staff review; Elena still needs to supply the receipt. Nothing here claims that this is a measured ministry workflow.

With A, Jordan can see whether an automatic reminder plan applies and its actual current state. An eligible occurrence can remind Elena about remaining recipient work without asking her to resend the inventory. If Jordan has agreed to wait, supported pause/stop controls prevent the plan from continuing unnoticed. Any deliberate staff reminder uses the same true source context and coordinates with the plan's pending occurrence; it is not a second unrelated mail pipeline.

With B, Jordan reviews the missing receipt and chooses the deliberate reminder action. The UI makes its intended recipient, current work and delivery consequence clear. Jordan must return for later follow-up; the product does not pretend that a schedule will do so. Neither option treats a browser click, pending email, provider acceptance or opened email as proof Elena received, read or completed the request.

Once Elena has supplied the remaining work and it is only awaiting checks/review, neither option should tell her that she has failed to provide it. Actual source-required correction or newly added work can later create recipient work again through their own rules. Q22 fulfillment remains its actual complete source predicate; the reminder's recipient-work predicate need not wait for staff to mark the whole request complete.

Requested by and Access ends remain separate. A reminder cannot extend a grant, open completed/canceled access, or imply that an expired upload link is currently usable. If the supported purpose admits a truthful qualified help action, that must be explicitly declared; otherwise the occurrence cannot claim a valid submission route. A stale link is not repaired by silently sending a fresh grant or inventing a new request.

## Twelve boundaries that keep the choice practical

1. **Existing outcome, new initiation choice.** Founder §10 already requires reminders and shared/native owners. Q48 asks whether a qualified schedule can initiate them, not whether reminders exist or whether a whole Workflow Studio must be installed. As the owner check confirms, P17 D47–D55 describe permission Access requests; their reminder rules are not automatically ordinary document-collection policy.
2. **Actual outstanding recipient work.** Source current work, not a generic incomplete/overdue badge, scan status or staff backlog, determines whether a reminder is useful. Preserve Q09/Q46 receipt history and Q21/Q22 review/fulfillment. A received contribution awaiting staff does not by itself justify chasing the participant.
3. **Current source/P12 action and destination.** A plan or Send button is not permission. Qualify the actual source, intended recipient, contact purpose, permitted disclosure and current capable next action. Neither same email nor prior invitation automatically proves current authority; no Party/full account is required merely for a message if the source qualifies a direct recipient.
4. **Access and timing remain distinct.** Reminders do not extend/reissue/reopen grants or convert Requested by into a hard cutoff. Target-only changes preserve historical acts. Expired access cannot be concealed by a working-looking portal CTA; a separately qualified safe help variant needs its own declared usefulness rather than ad hoc content replacement.
5. **Small transparent controls.** Under A, show whether the actual plan is active, paused/stopped, ineligible or unresolved, with a truthful next eligible occurrence if it is knowable. Provide authorized bounded plan management in existing context; avoid a new general scheduler, scripting UI or silent dependency on hidden defaults. Under B, clearly show that staff initiation is required.
6. **Real stop and resume behavior.** Actual submission/no remaining recipient action, source cancellation/fulfillment/No longer required, revocation/privacy limits and a plan stop must be reconciled before an affected occurrence can send. Exact per-purpose stop, in-flight, resume and new-work recurrence semantics need explicit selected-answer review. An already possibly sent act cannot be recalled by a UI status change; neither queued nor stopped automatically means delivered.
7. **No duplicate-looking overlapping paths.** Staff-triggered and scheduled attempts must use the source's qualified occurrence/coordination rules and original-result recovery; a manual action is not permission to bypass the automatic plan's applicable contact/noise limits. A date edit, missed tick, worker retry, pause/resume, readiness recovery or page refresh cannot silently become many fresh reminder sends. Different legitimate requests/purposes are not automatically merged or deduplicated by similar text.
8. **Safe useful content.** Use minimum permitted context that distinguishes the actual request and current action. Do not expose filenames, sensitive rationale, staff review notes, another person's information or protected relationship merely to make a reminder more detailed. No automatic attachments or bodies. If safe identification/action is impossible, provide the qualified native/source help instead of a confusing broadcast.
9. **Current input, immutable sent history.** Q36/Q37 new requirements, Q46 target edits and Q47 withdrawal/re-offer may change genuine current work, but do not rewrite receipt or already delivered-message history. Reevaluate actually affected pending purpose/content; no universal re-send, new interval, backdating or reupload is inferred.
10. **Truthful failure and native continuity.** Failed/suppressed/unknown mail remains distinct from Off, no current work and delivered. Reconcile the original effect before retrying. The request, receipt, help and staff work stay usable without email; technical delivery failure does not reopen or cancel the request or force another participant submission.
11. **Accessible routine flow.** Show target, recipient and consequence before a deliberate staff send; changing a schedule field does not itself send a manual reminder. Use shared controls, keyboard/screen reader/touch/reflow, long-name/RTL/localized date guidance and status announcements. Minimal plain-text/images-off email must remain useful; no preview, attachment download, full signup or desktop prerequisite just to understand the next step.
12. **Bounded follow-up, not a platform.** Exact profile limits, effective defaults, enabled state, cadence/zone/count/horizon, current-eligibility evaluation, cancel/resume/overlap semantics and actual dispatch proof are required before activation. They are not selected by this question and must not remain an unnamed later TODO if A is adopted. Reuse source/P6/P17/P9 and actual scheduling infrastructure through their contracts; no new provider, global digest, calendar, reminder rules engine or blanket cost claim follows.
