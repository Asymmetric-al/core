# Phase 29 Q32 — Default-on staff awareness of participant clarification

**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

**Status:** Conrad selected Q32 A and fully ratified its complete required
amendment package on 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q32 and all twenty-two
glossary definitions are ratified grooming intent. The adopted decision and
proof obligations remain intact; Q33's answer is not inferred. No implementation,
activation or formal owner/OpenSpec publication follows.

Keep the product small: a received participant question or reply remains in
its source-owned handling route, with an optional minimal staff email enabled
by default and controllable by the tenant. Do not turn a notice into permission,
assignment, a demand for a response, or a second inbox. A remains reasonable
for direct human communication; the strongest alternative is initially Off
for teams reliably working from native views or avoiding external metadata.

source handling, with a qualified person/team or protected recovery route.
