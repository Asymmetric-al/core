## Recommended question and practical alternatives

**Should a currently authorized recipient be able to withdraw the exact current, unaccepted contribution they submitted to an ordinary request item, without having a replacement file ready?**

**A — Allow deliberate withdrawal from review, recommended.** In the existing request, the recipient can identify the exact pending contribution and deliberately stop its use as current evidence. Preserve its true receipt, history and source-required bytes; leave the item/request subject to its actual remaining requirements and current authority. This avoids forcing someone to leave known-wrong material as a current candidate while finding a replacement.

**B — Keep correction and qualified source help.** Do not add a recipient withdrawal command. A recipient can still use Q03 to send a qualified correction when ready, or use the actual source/privacy help route when replacement is not the right remedy. This retains a more stable received candidate set under staff/source control, reducing another recipient mutation and its recovery/race cases. Its cost is waiting for a replacement or capable staff action before changing that current candidacy. Do not imply that Q28 lets staff remove participant-supplied evidence; it expressly does not.

Illustration, not measured ministry practice: Elena accidentally submits an outdated equipment-inventory PDF to Jordan's request. The corrected inventory is not ready. With A she can withdraw that exact pending contribution now; Jordan must not subsequently accept it as current evidence. With B she sends a Q03 correction later or reaches the source owner for a currently permitted remedy. Neither option deletes the organization's received record, cancels the whole request, waives the inventory requirement or recalls a copy Jordan already read.

A is a useful ordinary self-correction capability, not a universal right to erase evidence. Its cost is another meaningful source transition that can interrupt prepared review and needs clear recovery. B is a serious alternative where recipient-controlled currentness is undesirable. No observed Asym frequency, measured productivity improvement, legal conclusion or universal best-practice claim is made.

## Why this remains open

The complete Q03 corrected decision (`grill/phase29-q03-adversarial-review.md:54–64`) permits a **new immutable correction successor** for an exact pending submission. It does not supply a replacement-free withdrawal act. Q03-C08 at:125 explicitly leaves deliberate source correction, disposal and cancellation as separate owner commands; Q03-C16 at:133 protects retention and specialized meaning. “No deletion granted” does not prohibit a future source-owned candidacy withdrawal—it prevents misrepresenting the existing correction permission as erasure.

Q28 ADR `0028-staff-supplied-existing-document-evidence.md:62–70` does select **Remove from this item**, but only for an exact current unaccepted **staff-added contribution**. It explicitly says ordinary removal never changes participant-supplied evidence. Q45's author-comment withdrawal/Restore concerns source-owned observations, not received evidence. Q33 removes an item demand and Q34 cancels a whole request; neither is withdrawal of one participant contribution. Q46 changes a coordination target and creates no further evidence mutation or notification.

This is consequently a new bounded recipient/source action, not reopening a settled policy or inferring authority from an adjacent action label.

## Exact target and cardinality

The target is **one exact currently admitted participant-origin contribution/occurrence/use in this request item**, not a physical blob, filename, all files uploaded by the person, every use of equal bytes, the item requirement or the whole request.

Q03 full review:84 retains source-defined cardinality: a source may define a multi-file submission, and the UI must identify exactly what is being corrected. Its row:101 and C02:119 preserve other supporting members and independent uses. Q47 must likewise respect the actual admitted source unit. A single-file contribution is the simple example, not a new one-file-per-item invariant. If a source's contribution is an indivisible multi-file unit, the action must identify that unit rather than silently split it. It cannot withdraw unaffected supporting contributions.

Eligibility is more precise than “request open”: current intended-recipient/source authority, an exact current participant contribution, an unaccepted and otherwise eligible item/review basis, and no applicable freeze, removal, cancellation, fulfillment or other source prohibition. A current source-qualified Request changes state may still be eligible. A contribution whose replacement is already admitted is historical, not a new current withdrawal target. The ordinary action cannot clear acceptance to create eligibility.

## What A would add, and what the question does not preselect

If A is chosen, later review must explicitly adopt a participant withdrawal operation through the ordinary source, Q01/P12 action scope and the evidence/review currentness boundary. Durable admission would remove only that exact contribution's current candidacy, preserving the original received act and required history, advancing the relevant evidence basis and preventing stale acceptance of the withdrawn target. Selecting/opening the action is not that effect.

The requirement remains governed by its real source predicate. Withdrawing evidence does not make the requested item No longer required, accepted or fulfilled. Other qualifying members and independent duties remain; no universal “zero files means complete” or “one withdrawal makes every item missing” inference is permitted. Q03's corrected predecessor remains fenced; withdrawing its successor must not silently revive the earlier one.

This would not promise deletion, erasure, recall, automatic scanner cancellation, old-file disclosure or a privacy/compliance outcome. A source may retain the original and continue necessary safety/records processing under its own authority. Current participant metadata and bytes remain under Q02/Q04/Q05 and actual source/P12 rules; withdrawal creates no new historical-file grant. The exact withdrawn-state readback/projection and any recovery mechanism require an explicit bounded amendment in the selected-answer review, not an implicit broadening in the question.

Actual acceptance, correction, requirement removal, cancellation, fulfillment and current authority may win a race first. Then the ordinary recipient command must not bypass that state. Same-operation recovery preserves the actual admitted result without reapplying it to newer evidence. Exact uses, independent members and old notes/history cannot be retargeted to another version.

The question selects neither a generic Undo/Restore policy nor a required replacement/duplicate upload, notification default, whole-request withdrawal, arbitrary staff removal power, retention clock, bulk action, new account or direct object-store deletion. It should give the founder a clear candidacy choice and reserve its full exact amendment/recovery review for the answer.
