# Q62 — Automatic approved policy application with a clear summary

2026-10-28, Asia/Bangkok. **Fully ratified by Conrad: Q62 A and all required amendments.** Reviewed disposition: Accept with required amendments; the complete corrected decision is adopted.

The simple path is right: resolve the approved rule for the actual request purpose, explain it in normal review, and use the ordinary deliberate Issue action. Staff should make a real decision only when the source requires one or offers an authorized alternative. The policy summary must describe authoritative facts rather than create them. Retention is the source's rule for preserving and disposing records; it is separate from who may read them and when request access ends.

This review adopts no universal duration, new legal determination, general records console, custom policy language, mandatory second confirmation or blanket per-file timer. It specifies what makes automatic application correct, understandable and recoverable. Its exact decision is also recorded in [ratified ADR0062](../docs/adr/0062-approved-policy-application-for-ordinary-requests.md). Independent [owner](phase29-q62-owner-adversarial.md), [runtime](phase29-q62-runtime-adversarial.md) and [UX](phase29-q62-ux-adversarial.md) reviews retain their source evidence and crosswalks.

The strongest alternative is **B: explicit application on every request**. It makes the issue more salient and may help in heterogeneous contexts where staff must supply relevant facts. It also adds a step when there is only one valid answer and can become automatic clicking. A is better for an already-resolved source rule, with required choices preserved. No-build use of the existing source admission mechanism is preferred if it already supplies these outcomes; duplicate infrastructure is unnecessary. Deferring records meaning, a free-form duration field, or one global default would contradict the governing owner contract.

This is a reasoned product judgment, not proof that every tenant prefers it. Intended-user testing must establish whether staff can explain the scope/start, correct a genuine choice and recover from failure without mistaking access expiry for deletion. No new donor checkout, public-site policy step, recipient consent checkbox or online-giving workflow is warranted by this staff request decision.

## Ratified decision and amendments

**Q62-C01 — Scope and chosen behavior.** For an eligible new ordinary Private Document Request, apply its actual approved source-purpose records policy automatically where that source supplies a qualified default, and show a clear authorized summary in the existing request review. The normal deliberate Issue action is sufficient; add no routine retention confirmation, second approver, reason essay or extra page. This governs ordinary request-evidence application only. Official/financial/Support/public/resource/import and other source policies retain their owners and independent qualification. No universal duration is selected. Q66 adds narrow tenant-controlled ordinary Document Records Policy authoring; routine issuance retains this simple flow and does not require editing a policy.

**Q62-C02 — Real source policy before application.** The responsible source/records/privacy owner SHALL supply the actual supported purpose/record classes, material applicability facts, approved effective policy/version and allowed tenant binding/variants, typed start event and date precision, preservation/privacy limits, hold authority, covered copies, successor/correction and disposition rules. Require only facts relevant to that source. Under Q66, currently authorized tenant source/records actors can create and revise supported ordinary business-policy definitions and values, not merely choose Asym presets. The source contract bounds capability meaning, actual authority and independent duties; it does not make all tenant business values code-owned. Filename, folder, Site, label, uploader role, browser locale, old template, digest, free text and a caller-provided policy reference do not establish it. A qualified policy may have a source-defined event that has not yet occurred; that is not missing policy. Missing or incompatible material policy blocks the affected new consequential act, not independently lawful unrelated work.

**Q62-C03 — Default, choice and uncertainty.** Distinguish a proved default, a source-defined deliberate-choice case, a missing/incompatible policy, and a temporarily unknown check result. Apply only a proved default. Offer alternatives only when the source has approved their compatibility and the current actor is authorized to choose. If that source requires individual determination or selection without a default, obtain it deliberately; A does not waive it. Never select first/longest/shortest/forever as a fallback, treat confirmation as policy approval, or turn unresolved applicability into a valid dropdown. A user may issue under a fixed default without acquiring policy-administration or alternative-selection power.

**Q62-C04 — Current trusted context and one authority.** Derive authenticated actor, Tenant, environment, source identity and authoritative timestamps/attribution from trusted context and current source/P12 decisions. Verify actual current membership, scope, capability, field and restriction policy rather than trusting mutable user metadata, stale role claims, supplied actor/tenant fields, a stored label or access to the screen. Preserve existing supported actor types without fabricating a Party or granting a new role. Policy definition, default administration, selection, issuance, history viewing and correction remain distinct permissions. UI, API, worker and retry use one authoritative business boundary; no competing client or provider policy authority.

**Q62-C05 — Clear ordinary UX.** Display approved policy name, safe covered purpose/evidence and actual start meaning in normal review as readable text. Show relevant limits or a concise summary of several classes without a false single deletion date; details are available progressively. Keep Access ends, Requested by and Review due separate. If a minimum truthful safe summary cannot be provided, the cohort is not qualified for this flow; do not reveal protected details or require file-body access to fill the gap. Only show Change when a real authorized alternative exists; selection-required cases use an explicit labeled choice. Preserve other inputs and focus through details, changes, validation and return. The existing Base UI/base-maia system SHALL support keyboard/AT, touch, reflow/zoom, long/translated names, RTL, actual source date/time semantics, IME and weak networks. No essential hover/color-only meaning, auto-submit on change/blur, auto-issue after repair or optimistic final success. GET/render/prefetch/details/menu inspection are inert with respect to issuance, admitted correction, send and disposal; supported deliberate draft saves retain their own admission. Existing command-origin and CSRF/request-integrity protections apply to the actual cookie-authenticated commands.

**Q62-C06 — Each consequential stage is admitted.** Earlier durable draft data, allocation, upload/staging or expensive processing needs its own independently admitted Q02 purpose and applicable policy before that act. A later issuance summary cannot retro-authorize bytes or private prose. Resolve current final request policy before new issuance; each later evidence use/copy is governed by its actual source class and qualified binding path. Preserve and recover only independently lawful preparation when a later act cannot proceed. Reuse valid source facts and sufficient permitted evidence without a repeated governance form, forced reupload or making every staff member a records expert.

**Q62-C07 — Coherent authoritative commitment.** Commit the admitted request policy basis, required attributed source history and original business-operation result coherently with the actual issuance/evidence act; do not issue first and hope to attach a policy later. The retained basis identifies the exact applicable source policy/version/binding, material scope and whether the actual source default or a deliberate authorized choice supplied the binding, and trigger facts or truthful not-yet-triggered state, with trusted actor/time and necessary provenance. Reuse the source aggregate/transaction or a proved equivalent fence; no distributed framework is prescribed. Required secondary follow-through is durable and retryable, without holding short database locks across providers. A failed summary refresh does not erase a committed result or turn it into failed issuance.

**Q62-C08 — Races, relevant change and retries.** Order issuance/admission against relevant purpose, policy/default, choice authority and restriction changes on the actual authoritative basis, rechecking after waits. A material change to what staff reviewed requires safe explanation and a fresh deliberate final act; an unchanged effective result or unrelated global edit does not force ritual review. Preserve a still-valid deliberate choice when a default changes; identical policy names do not prove unchanged meaning or valid authority. A proposed assignment may be a verified no-op only after current authority, expected-basis and complete intended semantic-effect checks, with no new business history, trigger or downstream occurrence while retaining required security audit. A real factual assignment/trigger correction is not a no-op merely because the resulting effective policy or date is unchanged. Stale change-away-and-back intent is not rescued by equal labels, and a no-op does not prevent a later legitimate current-basis change. Bind idempotency to the original business effect and validated intent, not one transport call or a file ID alone. Recover a matching committed original before assessing a retry as new work, under current disclosure. A mismatched retry conflicts; another actor's result is not falsely attributed to this actor. Unknown commit remains unknown until reconciled. Retry/replay must not create duplicate issuance, reassign a newer policy, reset dates, revive access or superseded applicability. Preserve the source's stale-intent/expiry/cleanup/restore fences. After source-qualified recovery/control evidence expires, reject unverifiable stale intent rather than treating it as new; deleting readable result history cannot reopen an old business effect.

**Q62-C09 — Historical assignment and current applicability.** A changed default is prospective and does not silently rewrite admitted facts. A later policy successor may affect existing records only through the owning source's approved effectivity/impact rules and recorded attributable result. Preserve original policy/version/binding/trigger evidence while retaining actual current applicability and restrictions; a historical assignment is neither today's authority by itself nor immunity from later applicable rules. Late uploads, replacements, new source uses and restored records follow that source's explicit qualified current path; do not globally choose either newest-policy-for-everything or frozen-policy-forever. A required impact/reconciliation operation must identify affected records/use classes and commit the current governing result or an explicit restrictive pending state. Fence affected irreversible consequences while impact cannot safely be resolved; a stale permissive disposer cannot continue because only the summary changed. Track that work durably with a capable owner, finite bounds and recovery, and distinguish assignment success from pending physical consequences.

**Q62-C10 — Narrow mistaken-assignment correction.** Provide a real source/records-authorized **Correct policy assignment** route to correct an identified mistaken admitted assignment; ordinary issuance permission alone does not grant it. Target the exact current binding and relevant evidence scope with expected basis, truthful correction reason/basis and trusted actor/time. Show the permitted current assignment, proposed approved correction and its consequences before one deliberate Correct assignment action. Use trusted factual context or concise input genuinely required by the source; no blanket extra reason essay, second approver or blind correction. Preserve the original record and append the resulting current applicability and impact under the approved source rules. A wrong correction is itself corrected through the same current attributed path, never by deleting history. A source-proved trigger correction records its actual evidence and corrected current interpretation while preserving prior facts; it is not an arbitrary reset. Do not reset to edit time, clear independent holds, delete bytes, change recipient/purpose ownership, reopen/fulfill/cancel a request, reinstate acceptance or automatically issue replacement work. Where the required target policy or correction scope is unqualified, retain safe existing truth, prevent unsupported new/destructive effects and route to the capable owner; do not invent dates. Independently authorized urgent access/privacy containment need not wait for this correction. If required bytes are already gone, correction must not claim to restore them: use only independently lawful qualified recovery, and otherwise record irrecoverable loss and route actual incident/source remediation with truthful limits. The correction route and its rollback/restore semantics must be qualified before the affected cohort activates.

**Q62-C11 — Distinct classes, uses and clocks.** Preserve each admitted evidence original/version, selected assembly input/recipe/output, preview, request/history/audit, communication, export, staging/replay/provider/backup purpose and its actual obligations. Reused bytes do not merge permissions, provenance or retention; a new request does not erase the old use or reset its trigger. Receipt, Checking, acceptance, F, cancellation, withdrawal, removal from a view, access expiry, retention eligibility and verified disposition remain distinct. No download, policy inspection, default save, template copy or correction starts/restarts a period unless the actual source rule explicitly identifies that typed event. Show not-yet-started separately from unknown, and use the source's date precision/calendar/time-zone semantics rather than browser arithmetic.

**Q62-C12 — Holds, privacy limits and physical disposition.** Applying or changing a policy grants no read/share/publication right and releases no hold. Current complete registered use/hold/processor/copy coverage and actual owner disposition authority govern destructive actions; an empty visible list or expired period alone is insufficient. Resolve genuine preservation/privacy conflicts with the designated owner, not a universal longest/shortest rule. Fence disposition against new references, holds, finalizations and promotions, with current proof at destructive admission. Partial/unknown physical outcomes remain visible to qualified operators and retry the same intended operation; no metadata-only Deleted everywhere claim. Restoration applies current correction/revocation/disposal restrictions before serving and never revives expired access. Database backup alone is not byte backup.

**Q62-C13 — Database and every-path enforcement.** Use typed same-Tenant/environment/source relationships with actual foreign-key or equivalent validated reference integrity, null-complete scoped composite relationships, suitable uniqueness for semantic effects/current applicability and valid state/trigger combinations, deliberate nullability and delete rules, immutable admitted attribution/history and source-qualified lawful privacy correction. One current assignment per defined source-use scope is not one policy for every record class. Qualified platform-global capability or specialist-source definitions need not be duplicated into Tenant ownership. Q66 ordinary tenant business-policy definitions and versions are tenant-owned; their assignments, permitted variants and effects require actual source/Tenant compatibility. Actor/tenant/approval/policy provenance fields are never caller-write authority. Keep P12 fine-grained current source decisions and coarse Tenant RLS; browser table grants cannot bypass the command or expose forbidden fields. Verify effective table/column grants, policy composition, old `USING` and resulting `WITH CHECK` behavior, SELECT/RETURNING/upsert paths, views, function owner/search path/named and inherited EXECUTE, service/BYPASS roles, storage, direct API/RPC, imports, jobs and restore. An allowed update must not reparent or transform a permitted row into forbidden scope. Application and database Tenant/environment context must agree through actual supported transactions, with safe pooled cleanup on success/error/cancellation/retry; a callback of separate calls is not assumed atomic. Verify schema/sequence/default privileges, trigger transformations and any exposed GraphQL/MERGE/bulk paths as well. Zero affected rows is not automatically business success. Neither FORCE RLS nor a DEFINER keyword proves safety. No binaries/policy copies in Auth metadata, broad user-editable JSON or new schema/API names are mandated by this review.

**Q62-C14 — Safe projections and disclosure.** Authoritative operational facts belong to their source in Asym Postgres; summaries/options/current badges are permission-filtered projections. They must not become editable policy authorities. Register relevant binding/trigger/history fields through the existing shared static field classification and subtract-only projection contract; do not introduce a competing field-policy mechanism. Revalidate disclosure for every read/result recovery and reject late results after user/Tenant/environment/source changes; clear unsafe client caches. Key any permitted cache by the complete relevant scope and authority basis, and do not use stale cache as admission proof. Policy names, alternative existence, hold/case details, errors, history, counts, logs and exports require the same field/purpose protection. Participants receive only independently authorized understandable explanations, never internal records controls or a new consent/attestation duty. Avoid persistent sensitive device caching, tokens or private text in telemetry. Actual lawful audit/tombstone retention and privacy repair are source-defined, not retain-everything forever.

**Q62-C15 — Failure and capable operation.** Distinguish no applicable policy, required choice, temporary verification failure, relevant conflict, committed success and unknown outcome. Keep permitted input and show the next effective action. A policy-owner handoff must reach someone with the actual authority and usable correction/qualification path; routine repair cannot require raw SQL or provider-console edits. Reuse existing source/P9 work where operational follow-through is actually needed, without creating a new issue queue or a task for every valid request. No new email, bell, reminder, subscription or provider effect is implied; existing P6/P17/Q25–Q48 effects retain their own occurrence/current-utility rules. Durable business history, security audit and technical diagnostics remain separate; diagnostics use safe correlation identifiers and stage/outcome codes.

**Q62-C16 — Finite performance and minimal implementation.** Reuse existing source admission/policy/version, trusted context, request review, records correction and custody seams wherever they meet the contract. Q66 adds a narrow ordinary-source tenant policy editor and complete change lifecycle. Do not turn it into a universal rule/SQL language, AI classifier, content scanner, separate authorization engine, snapshot platform or per-file polling service. Before activation qualify explicit finite budgets for policy options/resolution, mutation/current checks, history/impact work and repair/cleanup under representative roles/Tenant sizes. Use set-wise bounded reads, appropriate scope/relation/operation indexes, stable paging and tenant fairness; avoid N+1 per file, full-history admission scans and unbounded fan-out. Retain actual P12 R6 logical-governance bounds and its physical-shard/restricted-tier distinctions; do not invent a global query number or import another feature's latency SLA. Numeric operating budgets are measured owner-approved qualification inputs, never guessed legal periods.

**Q62-C17 — Migration, rollout and rollback.** Introduce only additive compatible representations/commands where required. Inventory retained records, all writers/readers/imports/jobs/direct paths and actual policy/trigger evidence before mapping; never fabricate historic assignment, actor, date or approval from an upload timestamp. Unknown legacy records retain qualified containment/repair and cannot gain new destructive eligibility. Activate per supported source cohort only after policy, current authorization, correction, copy coverage and native tests pass. A kill switch stops new unsafe acts while preserving lawful existing custody/read/recovery and recorded obligations. Rollback must not restore bypassing old writers or reinterpret new data as ungoverned; keep compatible protections or roll forward. Reconciliation/restoration applies adverse facts before access. Old-code/new-schema and new-code/old-schema behavior and backfill resumes require explicit proof.

**Q62-C18 — Traceability, proof and stage.** Q62 A and all required amendments are fully ratified: this complete18-clause decision and22-category review are adopted. Preserve all Q01–Q61 decisions and both glossaries; no new glossary term is needed. ADR0062 records this genuine ordinary application/correction tradeoff and does not become a platform retention authority. At explicit formal publication, reconcile canonical owner requirements and generated OpenSpec projections, design, tasks, exact source/consumer contracts and later tickets/tests/release evidence; do not hand-edit generated views. Resolve actual ordinary policies and legacy mapping where still missing, then qualify the proof groups and intended-user journeys below. Full ratification authorizes recording this package and researching one Q63 question without selecting its answer.
