# Phase 29 Q27 — Opt-in staff email for actionable document review

**Review/research date:** 2026-10-22, Asia/Bangkok. Conrad selected **A — Keep staff email opt-in**. Q01–Q26 and their amendments are ratified. This independent UX/comparative review proposes precise amendments and qualification cases; Q27's full amendment package has not been ratified by this note.

## Independent conclusion

Keep the selected default Off for the exact optional staff-email step. Make the setting real and discoverable in the existing event-specific P17 administration surface, while keeping ordinary source/My Work review and Q03 inline correction awareness fully useful without any notification setup. An administrator should understand who may receive email, why it can be sent, and when a change takes effect. Review staff should not have to manage mail policy before they can review.

The most important repair is temporal clarity. **Future-only On is about a genuinely new authoritative actionability occurrence, not upload age or the time a background worker runs.** Work already actionable while Off does not acquire email because an administrator enables the setting or a retry/projection runs later. Evidence received while required checks were pending may first become actionable after On; that can be a genuinely new occurrence. A later real correction can change the review basis while the generic task still says Pending. Recovery of the same basis, a repeated check or a responsibility-only handoff does not invent a new submission/correction event.

The owner reviewer confirms that the source's finite admitted review-work recipient set, independently qualified for this notice, is the audience seam. A team label, task row, general read permission or shared address does not authorize a broadcast. A materialized task is not a prerequisite for the native source notice; conversely, email is never a substitute for current source work or a reason to create another bell occurrence.

Default On remains a strong alternative for intermittent, part-time or cross-system reviewers. None of the evidence proves that Asym staff open Core daily or that Off improves turnaround. The selected default reduces automatic external traffic; that benefit comes with a discoverability and missed-handoff cost that must be tested honestly. Tenant flexibility cannot mean exposing a hidden switch nobody can find, nor a mandatory setup wizard that turns optional mail into a review gate.

### Accessibility evidence and limits

The following WCAG 2.2 Understanding pages were refreshed; they explain the corresponding success criteria and do not certify a product:

- [SC 3.2.2 On Input](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/on-input.html), Level A: changes to controls should not unexpectedly change context. In this design, editing a draft notification setting must not unexpectedly publish it or send historical notices; existing P17 publication remains the meaningful action. This is a product safeguard, not a universal WCAG ban on autosave.
- [SC 4.1.3 Status Messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html), Level AA: in-scope status changes must be programmatically available without requiring focus. Distinct source, configuration and delivery outcomes need useful announcements, not constant queue-update chatter.
- [SC 1.4.10 Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html), Level AA: preserve information/functionality at applicable narrow/zoomed presentation, with its specified exceptions. Long actor names, effective policy text and errors must remain usable; a desktop settings screenshot does not prove this.
- [SC 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html), Level AA, and [SC 2.5.8 Target Size Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html), Level AA: test actual focus visibility and control target/spacing. The former is the minimum partly-visible criterion, not a claim of complete visibility; the latter contains exceptions and is not a blanket 44-pixel mandate.
- [SC 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/accessible-authentication-minimum.html), Level AA: staff return must retain the applicable supported authentication mechanisms, including password-manager/paste/autofill support where relevant. Email links do not silently grant a role or replace current authentication/authorization.

Use Core's existing Base UI/base-maia patterns; actual composed keyboard, AT, zoom, mobile, localization and email-client proof is still required. Accessible controls do not prove file-content accessibility, private guidance safety or user comprehension. This review proposes no OCR, AI, document editing or additional login flow.

## Lean intended experience

### Tenant administration and onboarding

In the existing notification administration context, show an entry such as **New document review work — Staff email: Off**. Use the owner's final event label; avoid a generic “Document notifications” setting that could be mistaken for Q26 participant correction notices, initial invitation, assignment or completion email.

Explain in one short sentence that source work still appears in Core and that enabling this step requests email for future qualified review-work occurrences. Show the supported audience role and an honest effective state. A draft On value is not an effective published plan, and effective On is not proof that every staff member or every occurrence is eligible.

A relevant contextual link or setting summary can make this discoverable from the work experience for users allowed to inspect it. Do not interrupt every reviewer with a modal, require a choice before first request issuance, or show a constant warning that email is Off. For a non-admin, expose only permitted information and a real route to an authorized owner; do not turn the link into an automatic access request, new queue or guaranteed SLA.

When an administrator enables the setting while work is already pending, explain that existing actionable work remains in the work view and will not be retroactively emailed. Use a safe synthetic example to distinguish a new actionable occurrence from a late worker or old queue row; do not enumerate protected records just to illustrate the effect. Existing review-and-publish controls provide the deliberate action. No second routine approval or real-recipient test send is required by this amendment.

When turning Off, the proposed qualified copy explains that affected email not yet admitted for sending will stop, while some already-sent or in-flight messages may still arrive. This behavior is a new narrow Q27 amendment requiring owner publication, not a claim that all P17 plan changes automatically behave that way. No unchanged plan/content is rewritten to manufacture cancellation, and On cannot revive the old stopped work.

### Staff reviewer

With the inherited default Off, the reviewer opens the ordinary source/My Work context and sees current authorized work and responsibility. There is no notification setup step, compulsory personal claim or per-submission email toggle. A current submitted item is not labeled accepted because a safety check passed; a received item still checking is not advertised as ready for inspection.

If a correction arrives during review, Q03's inline currentness warning remains required, even with staff email Off. Preserve the reviewer's reading context and exact inspected basis rather than silently swapping the displayed file. The predecessor's acceptance is fenced under Q03; an email setting cannot suppress the warning or permit stale acceptance. Opening the newly current evidence follows its actual qualified inspection path, not a universal preview-only requirement.

If the tenant opts in and this occurrence is eligible, email offers a minimal doorway to current authorized work. If the work is already reviewed when the person opens it, show that truthful result and any independently permitted remaining work. Do not require another review just to dismiss the email, mark a new notification as read, or synchronize an unrelated task state.

### Recipient and support

The participant's successful submission/correction feedback reports the source receipt and next stage accurately. It does not claim a reviewer was notified, read the evidence or will respond by a new deadline. The participant does not need to know the review team's members, select addresses or send a second email to make review work exist.

Authorized support can distinguish configuration Off from a suppressed/failed/unknown notice and from source work not yet actionable. They repair the original supported handoff when permitted; they do not ask the participant to re-upload, turn unrelated notices On, disclose a private roster, send from a personal inbox or create a new review action to get another email. If the source currently cannot establish an audience or notice qualification, current independently lawful review remains available through its own owner boundary.

## Fourteen complete journeys

These are constructed qualification scenarios, not observed Asym users or invented ministry practices.

1. **Normal inherited Off, no setup detour.** Priya leaves the qualified default untouched. Nora submits evidence; when the source establishes actionable ordinary review, Elena's current request/My Work context reflects it. Elena reviews using current authority. No email configuration, new bell, personal claim or recipient message is required. The participant sees truthful receipt/status, not a promise of staff awareness.

2. **Administrator chooses On for intermittent reviewers.** Priya finds the event-specific existing setting, sees its declared audience and future effect, previews safe synthetic content, and publishes the complete permitted change. She is told which state is draft versus effective; if publication is unknown, the UI does not say On. Future qualifying occurrences can request mail, subject to actual staff eligibility. Existing work is not backfilled or hidden.

3. **Already actionable before On, worker delayed.** Nora's current evidence became actionable while Off. Priya enables On before the handoff worker runs. The worker recovers the authoritative original occurrence/policy and its valid no-mail result; it does not send a retrospective notice. Elena can still find and review the pending work. A manually rerun job or repaired address cannot turn that occurrence into a new one.

4. **Received before On, first actionability afterwards.** Nora's upload was received while required source checks prevented actual review. Priya enables On. The source later establishes the first actionable occurrence on that basis. That new occurrence may qualify for mail under its actual governing plan. The earlier received/checking status and Q03 predecessor fence remain truthful; no universal all-request wait or preview-only path is introduced. If the same basis merely recovers a previously established readiness state, it does not get a new email identity.

5. **Correction during an open review.** Elena is reading a prior exact version when Nora's correction is durably admitted. Under either mail setting, Q03 fences predecessor acceptance and gives the existing inline currentness warning without silently replacing Elena's file or losing reading context. The corrected basis may remain checking before becoming reviewable. Neither an email outage nor Off hides the warning; safe inspection follows the source's qualified path.

6. **Reviewer cannot manage the plan.** Elena can inspect and review but lacks plan-publication/contact/roster rights. The interface shows sufficient permitted information to explain this optional email state and a capable authorized help route if relevant. It does not expose a full member list, make her an administrator, or require her to opt in before acting on current source work.

7. **Qualified team route and later staffing change.** The source has a qualified responsible team route and a finite independently qualified staff notice set. The notice does not automatically go to every member or a shared mailbox. A material membership/authority change affects current applicability at its proper boundary. A later coordination-only handoff preserves the work and prior source history; it is not a fresh Q27 submission/correction event or an excuse to silently readdress a sealed notice.

8. **Tenant turns Off with pending mail.** Priya publishes Off under the proposed Q27 amendment. If this occurs before P6 submission admission, the affected unsubmitted optional step stops. If submission was already admitted, mail may still arrive; the UI does not claim recall. Source work, Q03 warnings and unrelated notices remain. Re-enabling does not revive the stopped or historical zero/off occurrence.

9. **Policy/audience unknown or temporarily unavailable.** The source work is valid, but plan or complete audience proof cannot be established. Staff see the actual safe unresolved state; the system neither declares Off/zero nor sends to a guessed fallback. Independently authorized review remains usable. A later repair reconciles the original handoff under its exact governing meaning, rather than applying today's On as a backlog sweep.

10. **Known source work, failed or unknown delivery.** The source/actionability occurrence and intent are durable. Provider response is lost or a verified failure occurs. Current work stays pending/available or reviewed according to source truth. Authorized support sees safe original identifiers and qualified recovery. Nobody repeats a review, recreates a task, requests a new recipient upload or changes the message identity just to retry mail. Provider accepted/delivered never means staff read it.

11. **Reviewer opens a delayed or forwarded email.** Someone already accepted the current evidence, the request fulfilled, or Elena lost access. The existing staff doorway resolves current authenticated authority. It shows permitted current outcome/work or safe denial/recovery, not the old evidence set or false outstanding task. Forwarding cannot confer review rights; a session-only expiration uses the normal supported authentication route without fabricating source revocation.

12. **Sealed or confidential context.** A care-related or otherwise restricted source item may require its own exact approved notice facts.
