## Proposed corrected meaning

Before admitting first original ordinary recipient authority, the requesting source must establish that its current declared review work has a valid qualified Document Review Responsibility and a current source/P12-qualified human review path. A qualified team can satisfy this without choosing one personal claimant or copying its full roster. Readiness covers the source's declared purpose/profile and genuine declared review restrictions; it does not preauthorize unknown future bytes, require nonexistent file-row grants or guarantee future availability, throughput, acceptance or turnaround.

Preserve original semantic identity and recover any already-committed issuance under current disclosure **before treating this as a new admission**. For genuinely unissued work, check the new prerequisite with all existing issuer/recipient/purpose/currentness requirements at the authoritative source boundary, after relevant waits. Missing/invalid/indeterminate review readiness blocks this initial admission, while preserving authorized preparation and a real source repair route. The source must not start access, report issued or create outward invitation effects as though the blocked admission succeeded.

Once issuance lawfully exists, later staffing loss does not erase it, restart its clock or turn a resend/recovery into a new issuance. Existing current-use and source restrictions still govern. This decision is prospective for first original ordinary issuance only: it does not silently add a gate to already-issued intake, Q08 completed-read recovery or Q13/Q15 fresh unfinished admissions. It does not retroactively require fabricated Q25 readiness evidence for a legitimate earlier issuance.

## Eight proposed incremental clauses

These extend Q11's original-admission contract and specialize Q24's deliberately reserved issuance question. They inherit all Q01–Q24 safety/access/outcome rules.

1. **Q25-C01 — Exact prospective admission scope.** For first original ordinary Document Request Access Issuance, require a current qualified Document Review Responsibility and eligible review path for the declared source work before recipient authority is first admitted. Enforce at the actual source mutation on every supported entry path, not merely email UI. Draft/task creation, credential work, delivery, first visit and first upload remain distinct. The gate does not by implication apply to existing issued intake, resends, Q08 or Q13/Q15 admissions, nor authorize a new issuance mechanism.
2. **Q25-C02 — Meaningful minimal readiness.** Reuse the source's valid eligible person or explicitly qualified team responsibility and prove current source/P12 human review eligibility for the declared purpose/profile and genuine declared review restrictions. Cover actual required review work rather than finding any unrelated staff member. A team need not resolve to a named claimant, fixed witness, online person, redundancy count or copied whole roster. Do not require future file-row grants, previews or acceptance of unreceived content, impersonate a reviewer, create a team principal or promise future staffing/SLA. Later actual content and specialist requirements remain independently qualified.
3. **Q25-C03 — Original result before a new gate.** Resolve the existing original semantic effect before attempting new admission/readiness/timing. A matching committed issuance preserves its real identity, readiness/policy basis where actually required and recorded, time/end and durable effects; current permission still governs disclosure/use. A later gap cannot make that effect Not issued, create another original grant or restart time. Unknown commitment must reconcile; changed material intent conflicts under the source contract. Legitimate earlier-policy issuance is not retrospectively denied for lacking new Q25 proof.
4. **Q25-C04 — Current authority and causal boundary.** Independently prove source issuer/tenant/environment/recipient/purpose/entry/custody authority and the added review-readiness prerequisite at actual new admission after relevant waits, against the required source/policy/responsibility/eligibility heads. Source-supported ordering must prevent a material earlier loss from being ignored and preserve a genuine issuance-first outcome followed by later loss. Reproof may use another current eligible path within the same legitimately selected team when permitted; no whole-roster snapshot or global tenant serialization is imposed. Keep providers outside locks and source facts, mandatory history/proof and required durable intent coherent.
5. **Q25-C05 — Authorized intent versus proof outputs.** Preserve user-authorized material request/purpose/recipient/responsibility selectors and any reviewed policy/terms; do not silently substitute an unapproved named person/team or broaden scope. Distinguish these from source-resolved current eligibility evidence, default evaluation, trusted occurrence time and admitted end. A changing server witness is not automatically changed user intent, and pre-admission lawful repair must not require a clone request or fake new identity. Material policy/scope drift receives the existing safe conflict/review path; valid deliberate responsibility is not overwritten by stale defaults.
6. **Q25-C06 — Useful blocked-state recovery.** Missing/invalid/ambiguous/unproved readiness blocks this initial admission with truthful source-safe status, retained authorized preparation and a capable authorized repair route. Lookup failure is not empty staffing; source setup qualification is not a successful instance check. Do not start the original period, claim Issued/Sent, create success-contingent invitation effects or auto-grant reviewer rights to bypass the failure. Repair/recheck alone does not issue; no standing automatic issuance/send subscription follows a saved draft. An already-supported retry/deferred source intent may run only with its actual current authority and original-effect recovery. Avoid repeated pickers, Claim, extra approval and unnecessary confirmation.
7. **Q25-C07 — Preserve independent rights, lifecycle and privacy.** Once issuance lawfully exists, staffing changes retain Q24's scoped recovery and actual current restrictions without erasing issuance/evidence, resetting or pausing clocks, inventing an intake/read/completion hold, or auto-extending access. Q10/Q22's true fulfillment and completed-admission conditions remain. Present only permitted readiness/responsibility/repair information; the issuer need not gain roster/private personnel data or the reviewer's document-read rights. Keep current counts/errors/caches/replay and late browser results source/P12-scoped. Distinguish issued, delivered, confirmed, received and reviewed outcomes in the existing accessible journey.
8. **Q25-C08 — Explicit adoption and qualification.** Bind the gate prospectively to the exact ordinary original-source profile/policy and reconcile the Q11/Q24 reservation through canonical source/P12/P9 and applicable Workflow owners at authorized publication. Source activation/contracts/recovery must already be qualified; that does not replace this per-instance current proof. Old/new writers, imports and restore must not bypass the new cohort or invent proof/invalidate lawful older admission. Prove real authorization/database/service paths, material readiness/issuance races, same-effect replay, clock/effect preservation, bounded measured workload and composed staff/recipient/help browser/AT outcomes. No new policy engine, role, staffing service, queue or clock mechanism is required.
