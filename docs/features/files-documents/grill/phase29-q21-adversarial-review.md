**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

**Status:** Conrad selected Q21 A and ratified its complete required amendment
package on 2026-10-21, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q21 are ratified grooming intent.
This review preserves the exact corrected decision and proof obligations.
Q22's answer is not inferred. This is grooming documentation, not implementation,
activation or formal owner/OpenSpec publication.

Keep A. An ordinary requested item that calls for file evidence needs a deliberate
acceptance by authorized staff before that evidence can satisfy the item. Keep
the action in the request's existing review context. Bind it to what was actually
reviewed, preserve the history, and give staff and recipients an accurate result.
Do not add another approval engine, routine second approver, compulsory essay,
preview-click ritual or approval per supporting file.

The important refinement is that **staff acceptance is necessary, not sufficient
to override other source requirements**. It does not certify legal authenticity,
approve an application or payment, publish a file, grant access or automatically
fulfill the whole request. Exact source rules still determine those outcomes.

## Whole-product adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

“Accept” could apply to a newer unseen file, an incomplete multi-file item or
another tenant; generic task completion could be mistaken for evidence approval.
A successful decision with a lost response could be repeated or attributed to
the retrying actor. An accepted file later found unsafe could either remain
wrongly usable or cause history to be silently rewritten. The clauses below
prevent those outcomes through existing source authority and durable evidence.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

That ordinary native file-evidence requests justify staff review and have an
eligible responsible review path; demand and capacity are not measured here.
That the reviewer has a qualified way to inspect the evidence needed for the
decision; a partial preview alone may not suffice. That existing source acceptance
can sometimes be reused; equivalence must be proved, not guessed from bytes.
All are bounded requirements or explicit release evidence, not ministry facts.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It establishes the ordinary native file-evidence review gate. Custody still owns
bytes, the requesting source owns acceptance/satisfaction, P12 authorizes the
decision, P9 projects work and P6/P17 handle separately authorized messages.
Specialist financial/application/public-content decisions remain their owners'.
No general “approved file” property or automatic collection-only mode follows.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

Staff inspect the item and its current evidence in context and deliberately
accept it. Participants can distinguish work they have submitted from work
awaiting staff. Corrections and stale screens produce a clear update without
silently replacing viewed content or losing reading position. Failed refresh,
temporary preview outage and expired recipient access do not misreport the
business outcome or force needless reupload/reapproval.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

It follows demonstrated review/status patterns, clear consequential actions,
exact-version concurrency and source-owned durable effects. Optional-approval
and collection-first comparators are legitimate counterevidence to a universal
manual gate. A is deliberately scoped to this ordinary reviewed cohort.
Accessibility requires actual composed verification, not just shared primitives.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes, with Q03's atomic correction/acceptance boundary and the owner-specific
meaning required by the founder brief and Forms and Evidence contract. Generic
task completion, financial approval shortcuts, arbitrary workflow automation
and provider scanning cannot replace it. Source currentness, exact evidence
and existing alternative source outcomes are retained.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Keep A. Specify exact scope, meaningful review coverage, current authority,
atomic admission/replay, historical integrity and simple truthful UX. Do not
add new modes, a second approval tier, whole-request completion ceremony or
unselected communication just to make the review seem more rigorous.

## Ratified corrected decision

> For ordinary native Document Request Items whose stated requirement is file
> evidence, require a deliberate acceptance decision by currently authorized
> staff before that evidence can satisfy the item. The requesting source records
> the exact current item, requirement/purpose and reviewed evidence set, with
> trusted decision attribution. Staff acceptance is a required condition, not
> permission to bypass technical qualification or the source's other predicates.
>
> Reuse the existing request review context and one clear acceptance action.
> An item may have several supporting files; no approval per file, compulsory
> preview clicks, extra reviewer, routine essay or second confirmation is added.
> A valid existing acceptance for the same exact source/item/requirement/evidence/
> purpose basis may be recognized without duplicate review or effects. Approval
> for other bytes or another use cannot be inherited.
>
> Preserve the Q03 acceptance/correction race boundary, stable operation recovery
> and current authorization. Retain who accepted what and when; later source
> invalidation or current safety/access changes do not silently rewrite that
> history, revive a predecessor or reopen completed work. Keep receipt, checks,
> inspection, staff decision, current item satisfaction and whole-request
> fulfillment distinct and accurately presented.
>
> Existing authorized non-file/waived outcomes and specialist source rules remain.
> This selects no collection-only mode, generic approval flag, new waiver power,
> whole-request Complete control, bulk review, assignment policy or review email.
> All Q21-C01–C08 and Q21-P01–P18 below apply.

## Domain language

Add one resolved business term, **Document Request Item Acceptance**, to the
feature glossary. It is useful because acceptance is a durable item-level fact
with a different meaning from receipt, current satisfaction and fulfillment.
Do not add a parallel “approved file” concept or implementation details.

> The requesting source's record of a deliberate, authorized staff decision to
> accept specified submitted evidence for a Document Request Item's stated
> requirement and purpose. It is distinct from receipt, technical safety,
> current satisfaction of all source conditions, whole-request fulfillment and
> approval for another use.
>
> Avoid: approved file, upload success, preview ready, request complete,
> verified authenticity.

The previous seventeen definitions remain unchanged. This eighteenth definition
records the ratified concept; the full amended Q21 package is adopted.

## Ownership, invariants and lifecycle

The requesting source owns item definition, applicability, evidence membership,
acceptance, satisfaction, remediation and fulfillment. P29 custody owns byte
identity/technical state and qualified representations; a derivative cannot
rewrite the source. P12 owns review permission. P9 task state is a projection
of source work; P6/P17 own authorized communications. UI counters, processing
callbacks and audit logs cannot become a second acceptance writer. One accepted
item may have several files; the same bytes can have independent uses with
different decisions. There is no global one-file/one-approval invariant.

| State or boundary                                            | Required behavior and forbidden inference                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1. Received, checks unresolved                               | Preserve durable receipt. Unknown/failed required safety blocks unqualified acceptance, not receipt history; no “wrong document” business rejection from a technical error.                                              |
| 2. Current reviewable evidence                               | Staff can inspect through a qualified permitted representation and review exact instructions/set. Viewing does not lock or accept the item.                                                                              |
| 3. Review basis or authority changed                         | Explain stale/ineligible state safely; preserve reading context. No silent switch to latest bytes, another recipient, revised purpose or another staff member's powers.                                                  |
| 4. Correction races with acceptance                          | One valid ordering at the shared source boundary. Correction-first fences old candidate immediately; acceptance-first fences ordinary correction. Independent work proceeds where no actual aggregate dependency exists. |
| 5. Acceptance admitted                                       | Preserve the exact attributable immutable decision and coherent required state/history/follow-up. One effect, not generic task or provider success.                                                                      |
| 6. Acceptance response lost or two staff decide              | Recover exact original outcome/current permitted result. Existing winner retains original actor/time. Uncommitted changed intent requires review; committed replay does not duplicate acceptance or effects.             |
| 7. Item accepted, request otherwise unfinished               | Show accepted item and remaining actual work. Other required predicates/items/source alternatives remain; no automatic whole-request completion policy is inferred.                                                      |
| 8. Source fulfills, possibly after recipient expiry          | Apply actual trusted source occurrence and existing finite-access rules. Late staff completion remains business truth without reviving or backdating participant access.                                                 |
| 9. Later invalidation, role change, missing bytes or restore | Preserve historical acceptance; resolve current source applicability and allowed serving/remediation separately. No erased actor/history, copied approval, automatic predecessor revival or reopened fulfillment.        |

Required races include absent-row/first-decision races, two reviewers, a
correction to one supporting member, a requirement change and permission/safety
withdrawal during a wait. Locking or CAS is an implementation choice only if
the complete invariant is proved; checking one pending flag is insufficient.
Data types must preserve exact identity, source timestamps and revision semantics.
No money column or financial arithmetic is added; existing source money facts
remain outside the acceptance command. Delete behavior must preserve necessary
history without keeping sensitive payloads beyond lawful owner retention.

## Category-by-category adversarial review
