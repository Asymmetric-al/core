1. Staff starts in the permitted partner/source context, chooses the actual intended contact and document requirement, and reviews current instructions and the approved policy summary. 2. The recipient uses the qualified Q01 path. A forwarded URL, shared address or partner name cannot establish intended-person proof or organizational representation. A qualified no-Party lane remains usable without manufacturing a CRM Party or membership.
3. A signed/evidentiary original is never silently converted. Actual source-required format remains decisive.
4. Before confirmation, **“We couldn't confirm whether this was submitted. Check submission status.”** recovers the original operation instead of encouraging a fresh duplicate.
5. Staff reviews the current instructions, exact evidence and permitted provenance. **“Requested document accepted”** is an appropriately scoped success; **“Agreement approved”** is not. Recipient work, staff review and partner handoff remain distinct. Existing clarification/change-request mechanisms explain actionable corrections without a mandatory essay or compulsory preview click.
6. Wrong files, withdrawal, changes during review, wrong-person requests and mistaken acceptance use their ratified current-state remedies. Staff assistance stays staff-attributed. Where retained evidence can be lawfully reused, a correction does not force unnecessary new bytes or falsely restore old approval.
7. Q22 controls whole-request fulfillment. A partner source may consume the exact result only through its qualified meaning and command. Failed handoff/notice is a separate recoverable outcome; it does not undo a committed acceptance or justify repeating it.
8. Later access, source-use retention, independent copies/holds and disposition remain separate. The existing records/status view reports actual qualified state. No invented retention clock, evergreen access, new notification, or fictional disposal Undo follows from this reference.

These are comprehension/acceptance targets using existing source actions, not a new state enum, mandatory warning modal, upload wizard, policy console, e-signing service or partner approval platform.
