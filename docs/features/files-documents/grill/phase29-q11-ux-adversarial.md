# Phase 29 Q11 — trustworthy issuance and usable fixed-end journeys

Researched 20 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. The parent reports Conrad selected **A: establish and retain the finite unfinished-access deadline at source issuance**. Q01–Q10 remain ratified. This note proposes refinements for adversarial review; it does not ratify them, choose the initial duration/calendar arithmetic, add recovery powers or qualify implementation.

Evidence: the current Q11 question/comparative research, the ongoing Q01–Q10 review, and current official documentation. No private recipient account, real email, upload/download, provider mutation, browser/assistive-technology session or intended-user study was exercised. Only this research note was written.

## Recommendation

Keep A. Establish the end from one trustworthy source issuance and show the actual result in the existing request context. Ordinary visits, retries, resends, proof refresh, target edits and corrections do not move it.

The necessary qualification is **issued is not the same as delivered, usable right now, entered, submitted, reviewed or completed**. The sender needs an honest outcome when access was issued but the invitation failed. The recipient needs an honest recovery when proof or the entry service is unavailable, rather than an incorrect “expired” screen.

The strongest alternative remains B: a finite enter-by window followed by a finite working period after timely qualified entry. B can protect its nominal working interval from delivery/first-visit delay, without being indefinite and without clipping that interval to the enter-by date. A avoids the additional activation state; it does not eliminate delayed-entry hardship. No study establishes which cost dominates for Asym.

## Issuance readiness versus later entry or delivery failure

The selected source act must establish the supported scoped entry path required by its contract. A known missing route, unsupported identity method, absent necessary source binding or invalid recipient scope cannot be hidden behind “request ready.”

This does **not** mean a provider call, recipient login or an end-to-end email round trip must occur under the issuance transaction. The source/owner pass must define what durable issuance establishes and how supporting effects recover. The UI must not wait indefinitely for mailbox delivery and then silently redefine that time as issuance.

Distinguish three cases:

1. **Definite pre-issuance failure:** no original source effect occurred. Explain the actual correctable issue or service problem and preserve safe draft context. Do not claim an end has started.
2. **Durable issuance, later delivery/entry defect:** preserve original issuance/end and surface the actual problem. Recovery repairs the supported route or communication under current authority; it does not silently re-anchor to the first successful delivery, proof or page load.
3. **Unknown issuance outcome:** reconcile the original intent before declaring success/failure or creating another request. A spinner timeout is not proof that no effect exists.

If a serious configuration defect is discovered after an apparently successful source act, the owner must distinguish the historical act, its actual validity and the failed usability claim through an attributable procedure. Do not erase or backdate the evidence merely to grant a fresh interval. Q11 adds no void-and-reissue, extension or compensating-grant capability.

A post-issuance service outage can consume practical time under A. That is a real product cost. The accountable source plan and later duration must be qualified for intended cohorts; silently pausing or extending the clock is not the repair authorized by this choice.

## Staff journey: review, issue, see the actual outcome

### Review once

In the existing source context, show the intended recipient and requested purpose/items to the extent the actor may inspect them. Show the applicable timing terms and known controlling limits. Do not add a new recipient picker, date editor, explanation essay, second approver or permanent policy builder.

If the later policy resolves U relative to actual issuance, the pre-action page can explain the rule and show a clearly qualified preview where useful. It must not claim a tentative timestamp is already committed. Normal time passing under the same reviewed rule need not trigger endless reconfirmation; material scope, recipient or authority changes keep their proper guards.

### Issue deliberately

Use the existing intentional source action. While the result is uncertain, show meaningful progress and reconcile that same attempt. Double click, another tab and a network retry are not permission to issue a new request with a later end.

Do not enable another independent issue action merely because the browser lost its response. Nor should one staff tab's lock become a permanent inability to recover; the source owns the outcome.

### Read back the source result and communication result

After a proved issuance, display the actual U and current source state. Keep the invitation result separate if sending is part of an already-authorized workflow.

## Recipient journey: enter, act and return

### Initial arrival

Q01 remains the narrow qualified entry. A page hit, email-open event or success redirect neither creates nor renews source authority. Keep automated link scanning from consuming proof or exposing the request through unqualified paths.

After current qualification, show the requested work and current applicable end:

> Access while this request is unfinished ends [localized date, time and zone].

Where relevant, **Requested by [target]** stays a separate coordination fact under Q09. A permitted late file is not an input error. Do not present an expired proof token as this source deadline.

Keep Q10's conditional follow-up explanation where useful:

> If the organization completes it before then, we’ll show any available read-only access and its end here.

Display any actual earlier controlling consequence. The sentence does not promise a full completed interval, perpetual reading or that submitted files will always be available.

### Submit and await source work

Retain Q03's exact correction target and Q09's receipt/recovery semantics. Upload start, transfer 100%, a clean scan and a browser click do not establish authoritative source receipt or fulfillment. None changes U.

If all current recipient work has been received, show **Received — awaiting review** or the actual checking state. Do not tell the person to “complete review by [U],” upload again to keep access alive or chase staff as a compulsory next step. The source owner needs an operational plan; no new reminder schedule is selected.

Re-entering on another device must recover the same current source facts and original U. No new account, timer-start confirmation or repetition of known identity fields follows merely from the clock choice.

Do not promise a local file selection survives reload or mobile suspension. If confirmed nonreceipt requires reselection, explain that accurately. If receipt is unknown, reconcile first; do not use another upload as a substitute for finding the first outcome.

## Support, delivery and recipient changes

The help route needs a real accountable owner and permitted record lookup. A person with an old/forwarded link must not learn private request existence, recipient, filenames, actual deadline or source outcome through error differences. Staff/support roles do not automatically inherit body-read or grant-edit permission.

A mail bounce can be evidence of delivery trouble without proving that the intended recipient identity should be replaced. Editing an address, substituting an alias/shared mailbox, reassigning the request or granting another person's access requires the actual source identity procedure; Q11 creates none.

Where a resend or proof reissue is already permitted, it must use current approved recipient/scope and respect current source authority and communication rules. A broken link does not authorize asking the recipient to forward a private token, sending originals as attachments, disabling scanning or copying credentials into support logs.

Support can distinguish a valid source grant with broken delivery, failed proof, an ended grant and an unknown source result. Its response should solve the supported failure without implying new time. If an outage/delay makes the remaining period unusable, escalate through the accountable source process; no recovery power or numeric compensation is silently selected.

Do not infer “ignored” or “has not reviewed” from missing clicks. Even trustworthy request entry establishes access, not comprehension or substantive review.

## Timezone, precision and exclusive boundaries

I and U below explain the model; they are not user-facing vocabulary. I is the trusted original issuance occurrence; U is its resolved finite unfinished end.

- Preserve I/U and their admitted source terms. The browser clock, provider send/delivery time, last visit and a requested-date edit do not replace them.
- Q11 does not choose elapsed days versus an absolute source date or calendar arithmetic. Those terms must be resolved before activation; a bare date cannot silently become UTC midnight or local end-of-day.
- Render the same instant intelligibly across locale, timezone and DST. “Today,” “tomorrow” or “in two days” may supplement a real timestamp but must not be the sole consequential boundary.
- At the actual exclusive U, unadmitted actions are no longer authorized through that unfinished stage. A label must not imply “on or before” when the exact boundary is exclusive, or round the timestamp later.
- Do not derive expiry solely from a client countdown or a delayed cleanup job. A skewed device must not grant time or falsely decide the source ended; source checks resolve current authority.
- An upload begun before U has no implicit reservation. A source effect durably admitted earlier remains historically true even if its response arrives later.
- Normal source fulfillment before the applicable boundary preserves Q10's transition. U is not transformed into an overall completed cap, and genuine independent caps still bind.

Exact precision and localization must be qualified at real supported boundaries. Do not add more visible decimal precision than the source can prove, but do not hide meaningful precision by formatting it into a later promise.

## Accessibility and simple interaction

Use shared Base UI/base-maia controls and one source request context. No extra design system, activation screen, time-policy builder or engagement dashboard is required.

Review and issue should be keyboard/speech/touch operable with a clear action name. Async issuance and invitation results need visible persistent feedback and suitable status semantics; do not leave an unexplained spinner, disabled button or disappearing toast as the only outcome.

Preserve focus during background state changes. A major page transition needs a coherent heading/title/focus destination; an inline status change needs appropriate announcement without repeating every poll or reading raw provider errors.

Proof entry must allow supported paste/autofill and clearly identify errors. Recovery should preserve safe context without unrequested submission/download replay. Existing valid compatible proof need not be discarded merely because source or delivery state changed.

Assess content-set session/proof/opportunity limits under actual WCAG requirements. SC 2.2.1 is A; security is not a universal exemption, and >20 hours is an exception, not a recommended duration. SC 3.3.8 and 4.1.3 are AA. The separate SC 2.2.5 reauthentication criterion is AAA; preserving context is useful but does not itself certify AA compliance or restore expired source permission.

Test long translated dates, RTL, zoom/reflow, small screens, mobile file providers, tab suspension and poor-network recovery. Keep essential status/time in main content, not color, hover or a ticking countdown. Do not require previewing/downloading every file merely to learn the current state.

A long source interval is not evidence that short nested authentication timers are usable. Conversely, a short remaining interval on late arrival is not by itself proof of a WCAG failure; assess the actual mechanism and applicable exception. No user or AT testing was performed here.

## Fair comparison with the strongest B

B would admit a finite dormant entry window, then a finite working interval following timely first qualified entry. Its enter-by boundary would not automatically clip that prospectively admitted working interval. Genuine overall caps/current restrictions still apply, and repeated entry would not restart it.

That design can preserve nominal working time when invitation discovery is delayed. It remains finite, compatible with Q10 when prospectively qualified, and does not require using marketing telemetry. Its cost is a distinct qualified-entry source event, another lifecycle boundary and two timing explanations—not an inherent inability to be safe.

A instead gives a source-established end from issuance and avoids that activation event. Its cost is earlier consumption of practical time, including delivery trouble. Keeping A requires explicit policy/usefulness qualification and honest operational handling, not weakened portrayal of B or invented evidence that ministries usually respond immediately.

## Proof outcomes required before claiming usable access

These are future falsifiable outcomes, not tests performed:

1. Draft/prepared/scheduled/click/provider events cannot create I/U; actual qualified source issuance does, once, under current exact scope.
2. Known missing required entry configuration does not produce a false ready claim; later route failures remain distinct from source expiry and preserve original evidence/time.
3. Issuance commits but email dispatch fails: sender sees the true source end and separate failure; supported communication recovery does not duplicate or re-anchor access.
4. Issuance succeeds with a lost response across concurrent tabs/retries: one original source effect/result is recovered, subject to current result-disclosure permission.
5. Suppression, delayed mail, recipient-server acceptance and automated clicks never become source time, proof of a human review or a new first-entry period.
6. A permitted resend/proof reissue respects current source/recipient/communication scope and does not revive expired rights or silently retarget the request.
7. Code expired, code invalid, proof delivery unavailable, source temporarily unknown and source truly ended have distinct safe and accessible recovery.
8. Relative review terms versus actual issued U remain understandable, and material recipient/scope changes cannot be silently accepted under the original intent.
9. Source precision, exact boundary equality, timezone/DST, locale, clock skew and date-only ambiguity do not alter the admitted end or falsely extend display.
10. Upload/correction/start-before-expiry and unknown outcomes follow Q03/Q09; earlier durable receipts survive delayed responses and staff-review expiry without false recipient blame.
11. A timely Q10 transition is recovered even after U; late actual fulfillment or unknown historical eligibility cannot manufacture completed admission or Q08 eligibility.
12. Source/overall restrictions and current proof guard all metadata, files, history, cached/replayed results and neutral support paths across changed recipient/tenant/session.
13. Real sender/recipient/support journeys distinguish request issued, invitation status, current access, received evidence and source completion without technical vocabulary.
14. Keyboard, speech, screen reader, paste/autofill, touch/mobile file providers, zoom/RTL and low-bandwidth/tab-suspension paths work with truthful persistent feedback.
15. Intended cohorts have a workable initial period and accountable delivery/entry/review failure handling without hidden extensions, extra approval queues or private attachment bypasses.

No initial numeric period, warning lead, timer pause, restoration SLA, reminder cadence, compensation interval, staff-edit power or alternate delivery channel has been selected.
