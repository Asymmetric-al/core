## Recommendation and genuine choice

The strongest next product fork is whether an eligible recipient can send a short text-only clarification in the exact ordinary request/item context, alongside the required staff review comments and source-approved recipient guidance. Recommend **A — Bounded item questions and replies during valid source-approved unfinished access**: it gives a recipient a direct way to explain “the document already contains this on page 2” or “I cannot supply that document” without fabricating a replacement upload. **B — Staff-invited replies only** adds that interaction only when authorized staff explicitly invite a clarification, giving staff narrower control while adding a dependency when the participant needs help before anyone asks. The invitation must stay within the same valid source-approved unfinished access; it is not a new grant, deadline or mandatory record type. **C — Native staff-only review/guidance plus the approved help route** keeps questions outside the native request. C is simpler and can serve tenants whose existing contact channels work well; it requires context transfer and staff to bring any resulting clarification back into the exact review. All three retain required staff version comments and participant-safe current guidance.

Do not ask whether version-bound staff comments exist at all. Founder brief lines 213 and 217 already require useful review comments anchored to intended version, page/location where supported, and purpose, with confidentiality and no generic Support/private-care notebook. Likewise, sharing itself is not an optional new baseline: lines 235–245 already require source/P12 capabilities, exact purpose/audience/action/expiry/revocation, authorization-preserving reference links, and current-authorized private retrieval. The next fork is the participant's extra native text interaction, not a new messaging platform or source-permission model.

This recommendation is product judgment about a concrete exception journey, not an Asym user-study result or claim of measured faster completion.

## Consequences to preserve if A or B is chosen

1. Bind the reply to the actual tenant, confirmed recipient admission, request, item, source purpose and relevant instruction/review/evidence basis. Attribute the author through trusted context. A reply may explain an existing document or inability to comply; it is not Submitted File evidence, file correction, a waiver, acceptance, source truth or automatic resolution. A later authorized human may use it during the existing deliberate reassessment.
2. Keep participant-safe exchange distinct from private staff exact-version review comments and restricted notes. Neither sharing an item nor replying reveals internal discussion, previous recipients, other items, staff identities/rosters or historical file contents. Do not offer a private-to-public toggle that retroactively publishes a private thread.
3. Admit only current, eligible source-authorized participation. Fulfillment, revoked authority, recipient replacement and access expiry govern the operation at submission and recovery; a draft or old page is not a grant. Do not add an access timer, reset deadlines on activity, enable completed writes, or create a general personal mailbox. Existing safe help routes remain usable when in-request participation is unavailable.
4. Do not let a correction silently reattach old text to a new version. Preserve the original basis and show current context distinctly; require any later action to bind its actual current basis. A clarification must not fence the predecessor as if new bytes were admitted, count as a new evidence set, or require all document review to restart merely because someone typed.
5. Keep the recipient's action optional and simple. No second Submit for review ceremony, response obligation, compulsory essay, extra review stage or conversational “resolved” action is implied. Staff can read it in the same existing source work and maintain source-approved current guidance. A source-owned attention indication may help; it must not duplicate the review task or become acceptance/read-receipt evidence.
6. Save/acknowledge the exact source interaction coherently and reconcile a lost response under the same operation identity. Preserve lawful input/focus on recoverable failure; do not display a committed message as failed because refresh failed, duplicate it on retry, or silently replay it after access was freshly admitted. Render text safely and bound length, pagination and operations without creating a universal chat framework.
7. Reuse current P6/P17 infrastructure only for an explicitly qualified communication occurrence. Q26 and Q27 are not blanket defaults for new replies. No email reply ingestion, arbitrary To/CC, new Support conversation, cross-record inbox, mentions, attachments, OCR/AI, broad search corpus or notification engine follows from short in-request clarification.
8. Qualification later needs actual limited-recipient and staff source-role journeys, current-permission and exact-basis races, correction/acceptance/fulfillment ordering, recovery under expiry/revocation, protected content/read models, retention, bounded workload and accessible mobile/keyboard/AT interaction. Source read-only and audience correctness must be proved beyond a working composer.
