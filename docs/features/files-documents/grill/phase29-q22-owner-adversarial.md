## Eight proposed incremental clauses

These inherit Q01–Q21; they do not replace their private serving, review, safety, retention, database or source-owner controls.

1. **Q22-C01 — Exact ordinary rule and bounded flexibility.** For an eligible ordinary native request, record normal fulfillment automatically once the source's complete current qualified completion rule is satisfied. Preserve exact applicable Q21 acceptance, authorized non-file/waived/not-required outcomes, source-required final work and actual dependencies. Source-certified nonblocking optional work is not a new gate. Unknown, denied, stale or incomplete evidence, missing definitions and draft/canceled/withdrawn state cannot produce success. Existing authorized source changes retain their exact capabilities, revision/history and disclosed consequences; no new waive/hold/override/editor or configurable mode follows.
2. **Q22-C02 — Lawful automatic source authority.** Declare this consequence in the ordinary source's action/eligibility contract and use the existing source writer/P12 execution context with current scoped authority. A source-controlled consequence of a qualified last meaningful action may fulfill without another staff click. Do not infer an arbitrary completion capability from staff/read/reviewer/grant-administration status, impersonate a reviewer, bypass NHI/current owner restrictions, or create a task/storage/Workflow completion authority.
3. **Q22-C03 — Relevant currentness and closure.** Resolve completion against the exact request/requirement/evidence basis and relevant source/cancellation/authority changes at one coherent authoritative boundary. Preserve Q03 correction ordering and independent items. Closing first denies later ordinary admission, even if transfer started earlier; correction first cannot leave predecessor evidence satisfying the rule. Already admitted optional material keeps truthful receipt/history and its independent custody rules; its mere existence is neither acceptance nor a new post-completion write/review right. Genuine aggregate/safety dependencies remain binding without locking unrelated work.
4. **Q22-C04 — One real occurrence and time.** Persist one actual lawful fulfillment occurrence, exact source basis and trusted attribution with permanent effect identity. Recover a matching committed outcome before attempting a new transition; different material conflicts and two final-condition actors cannot create duplicate fulfillment/effects. Preserve original occurrence/time on retry. Do not backdate a current snapshot to last acceptance, transaction start or a client click, and do not require physical commit timestamp or one SQL layout beyond the existing source causal/currentness contract.
5. **Q22-C05 — Automatic progress and recoverable effects.** Every qualified relevant source change can trigger bounded reevaluation through existing durable source/outbox/reconciliation ownership; lost or early events and concurrent final conditions cannot leave a permanent missed completion. Coherently preserve required source outcome/audit/effect intent, run providers outside locks, and distinguish waiting/blocked/unknown/confirmed completion. Known item acceptance and known fulfillment survive secondary refresh/delivery failure. Recovery is technical/source reconciliation, not a new routine Complete approval or repeated recipient/reviewer work.
6. **Q22-C06 — Existing completion consequences only.** At actual eligible fulfillment apply Q06–Q19 unchanged: stop ordinary intake/correction and admit only the exact qualifying original/fresh completed scope and end. Independent lawful source work can complete after recipient expiry without read admission, online recipient or revived authority. Never infer historical eligibility, widen a narrow fresh view, reset an end, make retained bytes readable or require notification/preview success as an unrelated business gate. Later remediation does not silently erase/reopen/reclock the completed occurrence.
7. **Q22-C07 — Clear contextual experience.** Explain the possible fulfillment consequence before the last meaningful source action where known, show the real accepted/remaining/fulfilled outcome afterward and preserve reading context/focus. A stale screen is not a completion promise. Use safe current reasons and actual next actor; no private hidden-item disclosure, blame for staff delay, fabricated progress, redundant acceptance, extra approver/modal/essay, automatic completion notice or offline completion promise. Keep meaningful source-specific final work visible without inserting B as a universal last checkoff.
8. **Q22-C08 — Exact successor and qualification.** Prospectively specialize Q21's open final-completion reservation for this ordinary cohort and explicitly map Q03/Q06/Q10/Q13, P12, source/P9 and applicable Workflow/GEN06 bindings before activation. Preserve all other owners and original/fresh access policies. Regenerate declared full OpenSpec projections from canonical owners at authorized publication. Prove source authority, currentness, durable races/recovery, honest migration/restore and actual staff/recipient accessibility/usability at bounded workload. No new platform, generic policy engine, unbounded scan or per-request resident process is required.

## Glossary recommendation and remaining limits

One additional term is justified, not mandatory new machinery: **Document Request Fulfillment**. The existing18 definitions distinguish acceptance and completed access from fulfillment but never define the central durable business fact itself. Suggested two-sentence definition: **“The requesting source's durable record that a Private Document Request met its complete applicable conditions for normal completion. It is distinct from item acceptance, a task checkmark, recipient read access and later source remediation.”** Avoid `all files uploaded`, `all rows green`, `final click`, `access renewed`. It does not prescribe a new table, enum or service.

Exact native capabilities, complete source rule and source-supported edit/waiver/final-work vocabulary still need formal owner integration and implementation qualification. This review does not invent these powers or choose routing, negative outcomes, bulk review, reminders, new completion mail, reopening or tenant policy configuration. Their absence cannot be papered over by a generic Complete override. Preserve existing authorized flexibility and surface the exact remaining qualification seam.
