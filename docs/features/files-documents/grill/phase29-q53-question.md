## One decision

**When staff already possess a file that is not yet held in qualified Asym custody, should authorized staff be able to upload it directly to an eligible ordinary request item as staff-supplied evidence, or should the recipient upload new files themselves?**

“New” here means new to qualified Asym custody. It does not mean recently authored, authentic, legally fresh or compliant with the requirement. Both options retain Q28's deliberate reuse of eligible exact files already held in Asym.

| Option                                                                           | Practical experience                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Benefit                                                                                                                                                                       | Real cost                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Allow staff to upload new files as staff-supplied evidence (recommended)** | In the eligible request item, an authorized staff member chooses Upload file, selects the local file, and deliberately adds it under their own attribution. Normal checking and review follow. Add existing file remains available. | Avoids asking the recipient to repeat an upload when staff legitimately possess the needed file and the source permits staff evidence. Keeps the task in its correct context. | Adds a real private staff-intake and finalization path, with wrong-item, provenance, safety, concurrency and recovery obligations. It cannot replace required personal participation.                    |
| **B — Recipients upload new files; staff reuse existing eligible files only**    | Staff direct the recipient to the existing request upload. Staff may still use Q28 for independently admitted eligible files already held in Asym.                                                                                  | Fewer staff-intake paths; new submissions retain the actual recipient-origin journey and any required participant actions.                                                    | Repeats a transfer or delays work where staff already legitimately possess the file. It must not encourage a general-folder upload workaround or falsely imply legal authenticity from recipient upload. |

**I recommend A.** It is a narrow extension of staff assistance, with truthful origin and unchanged review. Staff supply is optional; a source that genuinely needs the recipient's affirmation, signature, current declaration or other personal act still requires that act. No new broad upload permission, portal impersonation, automatic approval or tenant policy engine follows.

## Concrete illustration

Illustration only, not an observed ministry workflow: Elena has an ordinary request item for an equipment inventory. Nora, an authorized staff member, receives the inventory file outside Asym and already has it on her device. The source permits staff-supplied evidence for this item and does not require a personal affirmation that Nora cannot provide.

With A, Nora opens the correct request item, sees its recipient/purpose and instructions, selects Upload file, checks the chosen file and uses the clear add action. After verified private receipt and successful source admission, the item shows the truthful staff-added contribution and its actual Checking or Awaiting review state. It does not say Elena uploaded or affirmed it, and it is not accepted merely because Nora supplied it. If Nora separately has review authority, the ordinary Q21/Q52 review path remains available under its actual duties.

With B, Nora asks Elena to upload the file through her own request access. If the file is already held through another independently legitimate source use, Q28 still permits qualified reuse. Neither option permits inventing a resource record or upload elsewhere solely to evade this choice.

This question does not select email ingestion, mailbox access, remote-URL fetching, scanning paper, camera-to-PDF assembly, OCR, e-signing or automatic duplicate matching. The example describes a file already legitimately available on the staff member's device.

## Twelve proposed boundaries for A

These make the option concrete; the selected answer would still receive its complete adversarial review and exact amendments before ratification.

1. **One ordinary target and real power.** Use an existing eligible ordinary Document Request Item whose source currently permits this staff-origin act. Resolve actual trusted Tenant/environment, staff principal/context, subject/purpose and source admission power; neither read permission, task assignment, issuer status nor file possession suffices. Preserve qualified no-Party staff and current role/source restrictions. No permission union, participant impersonation, invented record or general upload folder.

2. **Truthful origin.** Bind an immutable exact version to a newly attributed staff contribution. Show the actual staff actor and trusted admission time where disclosure permits. New to custody does not prove document freshness or origin. Do not invent a participant-attributed upload or participant-attributed Submission Confirmation, assent, signature, received date, authenticity or inherited approval. Preserve the real staff-attributed durable-receipt record. Keep any actual required participant action explicit; a generic source note is not verification.

3. **A small contextual interaction.** Offer Upload file alongside Add existing file within the eligible item. Show safe recipient/item/purpose/instructions and chosen-file details before the deliberate add action. File selection may prepare an authorized private transfer but must not imply business acceptance. No routine second approver, essay or new settings wizard. Reuse shared controls and existing source metadata; do not add fields merely because provenance matters.

4. **Qualified private intake.** Reuse a real source-bound session with actor, Tenant, exact target, immutable key, permitted profile, size/count/type limits, expiry and retry identity. The client cannot choose authority, classification, readiness or public visibility. Finalization independently verifies the exact object's identity/integrity and current target eligibility; extension, browser MIME, progress100%, provider callback or URL possession is insufficient. No public bucket/URL fallback or ordinary-file use of a specialist image/artifact route.

5. **Honest receipt and checking.** Distinguish selecting/transferring, durable verified receipt, source admission, allowed Checking, usable inspection, human acceptance and fulfillment. Post-admission processing remains Checking only where the qualified profile permits it. Unsafe/unknown content never becomes readable or accepted by staff rank. Preserve actual original bytes and declared rendition coverage; no new OCR/conversion/format guarantee. Existing preview limits are not automatically upload limits.

6. **Additive evidence and safe removal.** Staff intake adds a source-qualified contribution within real item cardinality. It does not silently replace participant evidence or accepted/frozen material. Preserve Q28's authorized Remove from this item meaning for eligible unaccepted staff contributions, actual history, other uses and retention. Any later replacement must use an independently qualified source command; no generic Delete/Undo or revival of a predecessor. Committed mistakes need a capable owner remedy.

7. **Admission order and custody protection.** Coherently establish durable source membership/reference/retention protection, exact attribution, result and required follow-through after verified receipt. Order finalization against current authority, source/requirement/evidence changes, acceptance, No longer required, cancellation, withdrawal, actual fulfillment and disposition. A transfer in progress does not hold a request open; if the target stops admitting it, do not attach elsewhere or reopen it. Preserve the known source result and safely reconcile staged material under its actual cleanup owner.

8. **Recover original work.** Recover or reconcile the original upload/finalization before repeating work. Resume/retry a proved incomplete transfer within its qualified grant and avoid unnecessary retransfers; prevent duplicate admitted source contributions and downstream effects without promising physical exactly-once transfer or storage. Recover a matching committed operation under current disclosure before fresh lifecycle checks. Unknown finalization is not failure and must never trigger blind deletion of bytes that may already be referenced. Local cancel, transfer stop and source withdrawal are distinct. If qualified multi-file intake exists, report each file honestly; no claim that remote transfers are atomically all-or-none or a new multi-item bulk-intake product.

9. **Keep review and fulfillment separate.** Q21/Q23/Q52 review still requires current exact evidence, sufficient inspection and actual review power; staff supplying and reviewing can be the same person only where the source permits. Q22 alone determines complete-source fulfillment. A staff upload does not satisfy participant statements, waive work, create a Complete action or infer approval from a clean scan. Q25 original issuance readiness is not a new ongoing gate.

10. **Respect audiences and effects.** Participant status, permitted reading/history and staff material remain independently source-authorized; staff-added does not become the participant's own-submission entitlement. Staff action after recipient access expiry requires independent current authority and does not renew access. Reconcile this genuine new staff contribution with the existing Q28/Q24/Q27 source-actionability meaning; no unselected participant email, bell, Q51 cue, reminder reset or blanket suppression follows. Provider delivery failure cannot erase known admission.

11. **Accessible recovery and restrained resources.** Provide keyboard/non-drag/touch file selection, persistent truthful transfer/checking/failure states, safe retry, focus/reading preservation, long/international/RTL names and weak-network behavior. Clear protected local state and fence late responses on context/access changes; no unapproved offline queue or device cache. Enforce measured numeric purpose-profile, transfer/processing/query/retention/cleanup budgets and tenant fairness before activation. No new provider or zero-bill claim, automatic remote fetch, connector, duplicate engine or broad library is necessary.

12. **Owner adoption and terminology.** If A is selected, explicitly extend the ordinary source/Q28 staff-admission contract for newly received bytes, bind custody/P12/P9/P6/P17 mechanics and proofs, and consider the precise existing Staff-supplied Document Evidence definition refinement. Do not silently widen it now or add a second synonym. Under B, independently legitimate existing reuse remains, but an artificial upload elsewhere cannot manufacture admission. ## Adversarial check

### What could go wrong with this answer?

Staff could upload to the wrong item, impersonate a recipient, bypass a personal requirement or treat storage success as admitted evidence. Unknown finalization could lead to destructive cleanup. Boundaries1–10 require exact source authority, truthful attribution, verified custody, ordered admission and original-operation recovery.

### What hidden assumptions are we making?

The illustration assumes lawful file possession and a source that permits staff assistance; neither can be inferred from a device file or staff title. Asym frequency, usability benefit, native pipeline and cost remain unmeasured. A new-file staff lane is a product choice, not something already selected by Q28.

### How does this affect the whole product?

It supplies one missing ordinary staff intake route while preserving recipient acts, custody, review, fulfillment and other owners. It does not change public Media, official artifacts, specialist evidence, broad resource ownership, imports or external connectors. Existing effects need exact compatible source binding, not another notification engine.

### How does this affect the end-user experience?

A removes avoidable repeated participant transfers where assistance is legitimate; B keeps the smaller participant-origin path. Both retain Add existing. Contextual Upload file, actual staff attribution and clear received/checking/review states are preferable to a drive detour or entering as the participant. Actual participant-required actions remain visible rather than silently satisfied.

### Does this follow modern best practices?

Primary collection and grant platforms demonstrate staff assistance, but their portal access, attribution and attachment meanings differ. Core adopts the useful contextual action while rejecting misleading participant attribution and broad bearer access. Personal signatures remain personal; accessibility and custody are separately qualified.

### Does this fit Asym's existing repo and product direction?

Yes if explicitly admitted by the ordinary source and implemented through shared private custody and packages/api. The current public/metadata-only/specialist upload seams do not prove it. Q02 prevents ownerless storage, Q28 supplies truthful staff-use semantics and Q21/Q22/Q52 preserve review/fulfillment. Exact base-maia and source-owned permission boundaries remain.

### Should we adjust the recommendation?

Recommend A with those boundaries. The meaningful decision is whether staff may supply new local bytes, not whether to add a generic upload system. Defer duplicate suggestions until its underlying intake is settled; do not bundle cameras, email integration, OCR or a second glossary entity. B remains a credible narrower alternative where participant-origin intake is preferred.
