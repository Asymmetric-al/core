# Phase 29 Q33 A — UX and comparative adversarial review

Review date: 2026-10-23, Asia/Bangkok. Conrad selected A: permit authorized
staff to mark an outstanding ordinary requested item **No longer required**
when it was mistaken or no longer applicable, within an eligible unfinished
request. This is a proposed amendment review, not full ratification, native
qualification, activation or formal owner/OpenSpec publication. Q01–Q32 and
the twenty-two existing definitions remain settled. Only this file is authored
by this reviewer.

**Recommendation: accept A with the amendments below.** It supplies a useful
ordinary correction without asking a participant for a dummy file or disguising
the absence of evidence as acceptance. The most consequential unresolved design
details are the visible effect on fulfillment, real recovery from a mistaken
decision, and retaining truthful evidence and participant guidance. These are
needed to make the chosen action usable, not grounds for a waiver platform.

The strongest alternative remains B: use an effective existing source correction
route and avoid a competing mutation. A should expose that same authoritative
command where it exists, or qualify the narrow ordinary-source addition where
it does not. Ease of use is not authority to dismiss a difficult requirement.
No measured Asym correction frequency, user preference or time saving is known.
Examples and likelihood judgments below are analytical scenarios, not reports
of observed ministry use or confirmed production defects.

## Risks and exact proposed amendments

Severity describes the possible consequence if the design omits the safeguard.
Likelihood is qualitative exposure in a plausible eligible journey, not an
incident rate. High means false source outcome, unauthorized obligation change,
material disclosure, or an unrecoverable misleading result; Medium means
substantial confusion, unnecessary work or preventable exclusion. No finding
below asserts that an implementation currently has the defect.

### U01 — Requirement change mistaken for review or file removal

**High; plausible whenever similar item controls share a screen.** A familiar
reviewer may use an easy dismissal to clear unavailable/poor evidence. A status
dropdown can make requirement change look interchangeable with acceptance.

**Proposed clause:** “No longer required is one deliberate source-owned decision
that this exact outstanding unaccepted ordinary item is no longer demanded for
this request's legitimate purpose. It is not evidence acceptance, rejection,
deletion, Q28 contribution withdrawal, participant self-waiver, whole-request
cancellation, or a specialist override. Current requirement-change authority
must be qualified independently of inspection, acceptance, assignment, generic
edit and participant-access powers. Existing safe source commands are reused;
the selected addition may qualify a bounded ordinary-source command, but UI
visibility cannot confer authority.”

Proof: real reviewer without this capability cannot invoke it through any entry
point; authorized source staff can; identical templates/other items are unchanged;
missing or inconvenient evidence alone supplies no lawful inapplicability.

### U02 — An incidental click causes an unreviewed obligation change

**High; possible with dense menus, touch input or ambiguous controls.** A generic
Done/Remove action, autosave toggle or unlabeled icon conceals the real effect.

**Proposed clause:** “Use a clearly named deliberate action, such as Mark no
longer required, in the exact item/request context. Before commit, show what
stops being requested, that submitted evidence is not accepted by this act,
and any material completion or recovery consequence. Let staff correct their
selection or abandon the action before commit. Proportionate review may use
the existing item view or a focused action surface; it does not require a
universal confirmation modal, extra approver, red button or separate wizard.”

Proof: staff can explain the effect before activating it, distinguish the action
from evidence controls, cancel without mutation, and avoid accidental activation
with keyboard/touch. A last-item consequence must not first appear after success.

### U03 — Too much reason entry, or too little accountable decision basis

**Medium; plausible for every invocation if the form is over- or under-specified.**
A compulsory essay creates needless work and a disclosure hazard; a bare flag
may fail to retain the source basis required to understand the decision.

**Proposed clause:** “Record trusted actor/time, exact item/request and the
current source decision basis required for this change. Reuse meaningful source
facts or existing reviewed context rather than demand repeated prose. Require
concise additional justification only when the actual source needs information
not otherwise captured. No universal free-text essay, new reason taxonomy,
reason-code editor or second approval is selected. Internal rationale is not
automatically participant-visible. A participant-safe explanation is supplied
only through the source's authorized disclosure; the factual status itself
must remain understandable without private detail.”

Proof: simple mistaken-item correction works without an invented essay; an
actually required missing source justification is explained and validated;
internal care/location/financial detail does not appear in participant views.
Do not ask for a new free-text reason solely to manufacture Q31 email eligibility.

### U04 — Last/all items falsely imply fulfillment or surprise closure

**High; plausible in the expressly supported final-item journey.** “Three of
three documents accepted” is false if one was no longer required. A filtered
empty view cannot establish that the source has no remaining obligations.

**Proposed clause:** “Before the action, explain its possible request-completion
consequence using the complete current source basis. At the authoritative
boundary, Q22 evaluates every applicable requirement and independent obligation.
Only an eligible explicit source predicate may permit a complete empty/non-file
set; missing, filtered, inaccessible or undefined facts cannot prove fulfillment.
Do not turn an ended whole-request purpose into successful completion through
item removals. Present the actual durable outcome and distinguish accepted
evidence, no-longer-required items and any remaining work. Do not add another
routine Complete request approval or reopen a fulfilled request because the
visible item list later changes.”

Proof: last-item and all-item cases with permitted empty predicate, required
non-file/safety work, inaccessible rows, concurrent accepted item, source-purpose
termination and failed full-predicate lookup. Test staff/participant comprehension
of actual completion, not just status-badge rendering.

### U05 — Mistaken action has only a fictional Undo or decorative help link

**High; possible human error, with greater consequence if the action fulfills.**
“Use source correction” is insufficient unless the product can name a capable
route. A transient Undo can silently resurrect obligations or reopen history.

**Proposed clause:** “Qualify a real correction route for an accidental Q33
decision. While the request remains eligible and unfinished, correction is a
new deliberate exact source decision under current authority, current item
meaning and lifecycle. It preserves and supersedes the earlier decision, makes
any renewed requirement explicit, and does not restore stale instructions,
feedback, messages, acceptance or ended access. It is not an inverse replay of
the original operation or a general requirement editor.”

**Terminal amendment:** “If fulfillment/closure occurred first, Q33 supplies
no inverse, reopening or retroactive change to that occurrence. A responsible
source operator must be able to record the mistake and use a genuinely capable
separately authorized remedy. One bounded proposed remedy is a separately
reviewed new ordinary request through independently qualified original-issuance
authority and Q25 readiness, linked to the mistake under current disclosure.
It must determine its own legitimate requirements, audience, clocks and access;
no automatic clone, Reissue shortcut or copied old grant is supplied. This is an
explicit recovery amendment for ratification, not a claim that original issuance
or another remedy is already implemented. If no actual capable remedy can bind,
the affected terminal journey cannot activate behind a contact-only placeholder.”

Staff must see the real correction limitation/route before a potentially final
change. Error prevention can also let them review and correct before committing;
it need not promise universal reversal. New issuance retains its own existing
authorized notice behavior; Q33 creates no new notification purpose.

Proof: correction before completion; completion wins the race; authority revoked;
source purpose changed; correction cannot revive old effects; real terminal
operator/route and outcome; new issuance never inherits expired credentials,
unreviewed recipients or the old fulfillment occurrence.

### U06 — Participant sees disappearance, rejection or an impossible demand

**Medium; plausible whenever a participant returns to the changed item.** A row
vanishing or remaining red with “Upload again” leaves the participant guessing.

**Proposed clause:** “Within the currently authorized request view, retain an
understandable No longer required status and identify that no further submission
is requested for that item. Preserve permitted prior receipt/review history and
keep remaining eligible work clear. Do not make the status look like Accepted,
Changes requested, a new rejection, or proof that every received file was reviewed.
New ordinary upload/correction for this demand stops; independent safety work
and file uses remain governed. No new history access follows from preserving
history internally.”

Proof: never-started, partially uploaded, received-awaiting-review and earlier
Changes requested cases; existing received file remains true to its review state;
the participant can identify whether any work remains elsewhere.

### U07 — Concurrent evidence, review or clarification produces contradictory truth

**High; possible with two actors or background finalization.** A late upload or
old action must not erase a removal, acceptance or relevant later context.

**Proposed clause:** “Order Q33 with the exact source's evidence admission,
review, requiredness changes, relevant clarification and closure. Recheck the
current material decision basis at admission. An earlier committed receipt or
decision remains historical truth; transfer progress alone is not receipt.
If removal wins before final evidence admission, show that the item is no longer
requested and resolve the transfer safely under existing custody rules. Relevant
clarification can invalidate an older affected uncommitted basis, but creates
no mandatory Read/Reply/Resolve gate. Replaying the old removal after a later
correction recovers its historical result; it cannot remove the requirement again.”

Proof: both orders of upload finalization, staff contribution/withdrawal, accept,
Request changes, relevant clarification and fulfillment; late workers; removal,
correction, then retry of the original removal. No shared-byte deletion or bogus
participant retry instruction.

### U08 — Slow or uncertain results invite repeated business actions

**Medium; plausible on low-bandwidth/mobile connections.** The UI may report
failure after a committed result, or optimistically report completion before it.

**Proposed clause:** “Represent pending, confirmed, rejected/conflicted and
unknown outcomes truthfully in the existing interaction. Reconcile an uncertain
result against the original operation before offering a fresh action; retries
recover the same business effect. A failed refresh cannot erase known success.
Present original success and any later permitted current state distinctly. Ignore
stale responses after user/tenant/request changes; do not replay offline changes
automatically or create another item/request as a network-recovery shortcut.”

Proof: response lost before/after commit, failed refresh, double activation,
tab switch, tenant switch, reconnect, and a subsequent authorized correction.
Pending text and retry controls remain accessible; ordinary lawful work elsewhere
continues without pretending the uncertain action failed or completed.

### U09 — Explanation or history escapes current disclosure and access rules

**High; possible with sensitive source context or expired access.** The fact that
an item changed does not reopen a participant link or authorize internal reasons.

**Proposed clause:** “Apply current disclosure to item status, receipt/history,
reason, actor identity, contacts and recovery links. Internal reasons and staff
diagnostics do not automatically become participant text. Preserve independently
authorized source contacts and history where lawful; do not blanket-hide them.
Ended, expired, revoked or invalid participant access stays ended unless a separate
qualified source action grants access. Staff may act only under their own current
authority. The expired/closed entry shows only its permitted neutral status and
real safe help route, without revealing item names, private rationale or credentials.”

Proof: recipient grant expiry, revocation, stale deep links, staff permission
changes, shared-device context, restricted internal reason and actual permitted
participant contact. No payload, log, toast or error over-discloses private content.

### U10 — Accessible interaction breaks when status changes or controls move

**Medium; plausible for keyboard, AT, zoom and touch users.** A colored badge,
disappearing focused row or toast alone cannot carry this change reliably.

**Proposed clause:** “Provide a meaningful accessible action name, text status,
labeled reason input when applicable and a persistent result. Dynamic status
messages must be programmatically available without unnecessary focus theft.
Maintain predictable visible focus when an action panel closes or a control is
removed; do not move the entire list solely to dramatize success. Support keyboard
operation, zoom/reflow, adequate target sizing, touch, long translations and
source text without clipping the consequence. Preserve input through correctable
errors, including composition input; do not accidentally submit on an IME key.”

Proof: actual Core components at narrow viewport and 400% zoom as applicable,
keyboard-only action/cancel/error/recovery, representative screen readers,
large/translated labels, composition entry, sticky overlays and text-only results.
No new layout system or mandatory three-pane interface follows.

### U11 — Status change silently becomes a message or restarts old work

**Medium; possible if existing notice triggers are matched loosely.** A reason
field can accidentally be treated as a shared reply, or old correction reminders
can continue demanding a now-unnecessary item.

**Proposed clause:** “Q33 itself creates no email, SMS, bell, reminder or new
participant credential. A stored source rationale is not a Q30 shared reply,
Q26 Request changes occurrence, Q27 actionable-evidence occurrence, Q31 staff
reply or Q32 participant message. Existing admitted/queued effects retain their
own exact source-purpose, current-usefulness and dispatch rules; do not rewrite
them to announce Q33 or assume every old clarification notice is irrelevant.
Any genuinely separate shared clarification or new issuance must pass its own
existing deliberate action and qualification.”

Proof: no notice from status/audit text alone; obsolete handling instructions
cannot issue as current; truthful independently qualified receipt-awareness may
continue when its own purpose remains valid; no clock, access or task revival.

### U12 — Product reports or launch evidence confuse fewer demands with success

**Medium; plausible when existing generic completion metrics are reused.** A new
status can make dashboards suggest all files were accepted or a policy met.

**Proposed clause:** “Use the source's actual outcome consistently across the
request, current work, permitted history and consumed reporting. No longer
required must not increment evidence-accepted counts, erase previous Changes
requested, prove a broader domain outcome or complete unrelated tasks. Fulfillment
uses the durable Q22 occurrence. Qualify actual user understanding and native
authorization/recovery before activation; documentation and comparator patterns
are not implementation or usability proof.”

Proof: existing projections/reporting consume correct meaning; no extra metrics
platform is required. Intended-user checks must test what staff and participants
believe happened, not whether they can locate a button.
