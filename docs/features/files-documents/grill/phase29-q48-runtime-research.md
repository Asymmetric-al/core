## Recommended question and scope

The useful unresolved choice is **A: offer a tenant-controlled automatic reminder program alongside deliberate staff Send reminder**, versus **B: support deliberate staff reminders only**. The founder already requires reminders, so neither option removes that baseline. A is my recommendation as a product judgment: it permits routine follow-up without requiring staff to initiate every occurrence, while retaining contextual manual control. No measured Asym workload or completion-rate evidence establishes that A is universally preferable.

Both options require a real ordinary-request source purpose, current recipient eligibility, a useful lawful next action, safe content, durable identity and honest delivery recovery. Manual sending is not a shortcut around those requirements. A adds automatic due-occurrence selection and persistent timing/stop/noise behavior. This does not require a new scheduling platform or the installation of the whole configurable Workflow Studio.

The question does not select automatic Enabled-by-default, enrollment, numerical cadence, reminder count, a business-day calendar, escalation, digesting, SMS or arbitrary user-authored schedules. A selected-answer review must close a concrete bounded initial policy and its activation requirements; an indefinite future-policy placeholder would not constitute an activatable capability. Existing dates or reminder-shaped fields cannot silently supply those product decisions.

## Practical boundaries for either answer

1. **Current next actor.** Define ordinary recipient work from the source. Unfinished request, passed target, unaccepted evidence or pending completion alone cannot stand in for missing recipient action. A first receipt can remove a reminder's usefulness while staff review and Q22 completion remain pending. A genuinely requested correction can create current recipient work under its own terms.
2. **Actual recipient and action.** Resolve the exact source recipient and current purpose-eligible contact/action. A profile email, same mailbox, watcher, task assignee or provider address is not proof. A reminder does not create a credential, renew an expired admission or convert Requested by into an access cutoff. A qualified current help/recovery action must be described honestly; do not promise unavailable uploading.
3. **Source-owned time.** Distinguish target date, activation time, actual due occurrence, material lifetime and access end. Use the source's qualified civil-date/instant meaning. Target edits, additions, instruction changes, withdrawal/re-offer and pause/resume do not automatically reset counts, rewrite old occurrences or cause a backlog burst.
4. **Specific controls.** Automatic availability, request-level participation/pause where adopted, deliberate staff sending, channel availability and a broader communication prohibition are distinct. A chosen design must explain their actual effects and precedence. Do not infer default Off from Q27 or silently borrow another purpose's permanent stop/re-enable behavior.
5. **Real occurrence identity.** Manual action, automatic due occurrence, transport retry and a genuinely new reminder have different meanings. Close manual/automatic overlap and later Workflow coordination under permanent source identity and a bounded noise policy. Neither a changed retry key nor an uncertain provider result authorizes another message.
6. **Correct stopping and recovery.** Current source, recipient, policy, material and action facts must be checked at the applicable admission and actual submission/retry boundaries. A fact can stop an unsubmitted purpose without erasing earlier provider facts. An operation already possibly submitted remains potentially in flight; no recall, guessed success, silent reroute or rekey follows.
7. **Minimal safe material.** Keep the notice about a lawful action, without private file bodies, filenames, comments or unnecessary intake detail. Any protected CTA uses the actual qualified route and current authority. Do not imply mailbox delivery, a link click or a provider acceptance proves participant receipt or source completion.
8. **Native work survives optional delivery failure.** Missing configuration, unresolved eligibility, material unavailability and uncertain delivery are distinct from deliberate Off or proved zero. Preserve source/native state and a lawful repair route. A mail problem does not require recreating a request, asking for duplicate evidence or undoing a valid source act.
9. **Small bounded operations.** Reuse the capable native orchestration and communication seams after exact source qualification. Bound candidate selection, occurrence creation, fairness, recurrence, fanout, attempts, material/history retention and outage recovery. Measure the actual indexed queries and delivery load; no mandatory per-file polling, Realtime, new provider or unchanged-bill claim follows.
10. **Prospective rollout.** Preserve immutable occurrence and adverse-policy history across writer changes, backup restore and future coordination. Existing requests cannot become a guessed enrollment or historical mail blast. Prove real effective roles/grants, coarse tenant defense and the one fine-grained P12 decision at every applicable path; do not treat a worker's service credential as source authority.

Its 24-hour window is not a reminder cadence, permitted retry lifetime or source policy.

P17 also distinguishes its bounded same-envelope provider follow-up calls from a genuinely new message occurrence. Any applicable call budget remains the original communication contract's budget; it is not a count of allowed reminder occurrences. A source scheduling profile must supply its own deliberate recurrence/noise meaning while preserving the shared transport contract.
