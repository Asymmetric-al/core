Research and review date: **21 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. The parent reports that Conrad selected **Q21 A: qualified staff acceptance of the exact current ordinary requested item/evidence set**. Q01–Q20 and their amendments are ratified. This note proposes incremental Q21 amendments for the parent's full adversarial review; **the complete Q21 package is not ratified by this note**. Earlier question/research headers describe their preparation state, not a later founder reversal.

Only this research note was written. This pass read the current Q21 question, the earlier comparative note and the owner research, and refreshed the primary sources below. It builds on the exact Q03/Q09/Q10 contracts already inspected in the comparative pass. ## Main conclusion

The selected policy can remain small: **show the requested item and its current evidence, let qualified staff inspect what the source actually requires, then provide one deliberate item-acceptance action**. A request item may contain several supporting files. Do not turn this into one approval ceremony per file, a new review wizard, compulsory explanation, second reviewer, global queue or configurable workflow. Merely opening, scrolling, downloading or scanning evidence never accepts it.

The important complexity belongs behind the action: exact source authority, exact reviewed evidence and relevant revisions, durable outcome recovery and the Q03 correction fence. The interface should explain a material change without silently switching the document under the reviewer. It should distinguish staff waiting from recipient work and show only outcomes that the source actually recorded. A polished viewer and an enabled button are not proof of any of these properties.

**No universal preview-readiness gate:** unsupported preview alone need not prohibit review through an existing qualified exact-original or other source-approved representation route. Conversely, a thumbnail, first page, truncated conversion or successful download does not establish that the reviewer could inspect the content needed for the purpose. If the source-required inspection cannot be supported safely and accessibly for that reviewer, do not accept blindly or invent a bypass. Preserve the received fact and use the existing source support/review path. This adds neither a new viewer, supported format nor permission.

## Strongest collection-only alternative, fairly retained

**B would be genuinely useful if the native source's entire requirement were qualified collection for later independent work.** Requiring a staff click merely to reproduce an already-sufficient receipt can waste attention, invite rubber-stamping and keep honest recipient work appearing unresolved. Content Snare's optional use of approval and Submittable Next's submission-dependent prerequisite are concrete reasons not to call human acceptance an industry necessity. They do not establish how frequently Asym needs collection-only, or its exact implementation.

Conrad chose A for this ordinary file-evidence lane. Keep its useful meaning: staff judge the exact evidence for the requested purpose. Do not quietly implement B through automatic approval on scan, download, age, staff inactivity, receipt count or a workflow callback. B would need its explicit upfront source profile, qualified satisfaction result and honest not-reviewed meaning. It could end the pending correction opportunity earlier and, only when the real overall source predicate is met, start completed-view timing earlier. That tradeoff is real, not a reason to make either mode misleading.

Choosing A does **not** turn existing ordinary attachments, imports, reusable media, specialized applications, finance, official documents or authorized non-file/waived/not-required outcomes into universal human-review requests. Reuse an already-legitimate exact acceptance for the same evidence and purpose when the source permits; do not repeat review just because another projection displays it. Acceptance for different bytes, another purpose or an obsolete requirement is not transferable.

## Minimum qualification evidence, without another product framework

1. **Exact source effect:** native command/readback establishes current actor authority, relevant requirement and evidence identities, technical prerequisites and the durable accepted outcome. Storage success or a generic task transition alone is insufficient. Show that existing exact valid acceptance and authorized non-file/waived paths are preserved rather than duplicated.
2. **Races and retries:** correction versus acceptance in both orders; two reviewers; relevant source revision change; authorization loss; network result loss; old intent replay. Prove currentness and one effect at the source boundary, not solely disabled-button behavior. Meaningful unchanged scope must not be invalidated by unrelated UI metadata changes.
3. **Inspection coverage:** representative supported originals and representations, multi-file sets and material preview omissions. Demonstrate when the source permits an exact-original route and when inspection is genuinely unavailable. Filename, file count or “viewer loaded” cannot prove content sufficiency.
4. **Source consequences:** each item versus overall request, no acceptance on receipt/scan/view, real qualifying fulfillment versus no fulfillment, and source work after participant access expiry. Existing Q06–Q19 semantics must be demonstrated without choosing a new Complete control or extending access.
5. **Safe disclosure and content:** current metadata/body/decision access, restricted siblings, untrusted labels and document instructions, unsafe/unknown checks, honest neutral error behavior. No acceptance route may accidentally publish, email, authorize wider use or send private content to a public helper.
6. **UX and accessibility:** actual mobile/zoom/keyboard/AT paths for ordinary review, currentness change, unknown result and unavailable inspection. Confirm that scope and the next actor are understandable; do not equate component accessibility with accessible uploaded documents. Content-dependent limitations must be represented truthfully.
7. **Intended-user check:** verify with representative staff and recipients that “submitted,” “waiting for review,” “item accepted” and the actual source's overall status are understood. Check multi-file coverage and the consequence that normal pending correction ends on acceptance under Q03. No conversion, speed, support-volume or satisfaction claim is made before real evidence.

These are proof obligations for later qualification, not assertions that a new mandatory test suite, telemetry platform, review SLA or universal monitor is required. Existing source/runtime checks should carry the obligations wherever they already provide the needed evidence. The parent's full review can assign accountable owners and exact release responses without inventing numerical operating thresholds from comparator documentation.

## Deliberately unresolved and evidence limits

Q21 does not choose a final **Complete request** control or automatic last-item completion, reviewer assignment/pool, bulk review, negative outcome taxonomy, reopening/undo authority, new waiver controls, review-result notification, reminder cadence, recipient messaging, additional approvers, supported format expansion, OCR/AI translation or a policy editor. Q20's fresh-access email default is not a review-notification default. Source-owned existing consequences remain; absence of a new choice does not remove existing required behavior.

“Accept item” and the example messages are recommended plain language, conditional on exact current source truth and disclosure. The final source may require a more specific purpose label. Avoid broad “Approved,” “Verified,” “All done” or “Nothing else is needed” claims that extend beyond this decision. Do not tell the participant that their whole process is complete merely because staff accepted one item.

The strongest residual product risk is staff attention: A can leave submitted work awaiting review, including past participant access expiry. That is the honest cost of this selected source meaning, not evidence that recipients failed or permission to auto-approve. Actual workload and collection-only demand remain unmeasured. The accessible, concise review path reduces avoidable ceremony; it does not prove adequate staffing, content accuracy, security or native release readiness.

**Recommendation for synthesis:** keep A with UX21-01 through UX21-08 and the exact inherited owner boundaries. No new broad product capability is needed to make this decision coherent. Complete the source/runtime and actual-user qualification before claiming that it works. Only the assigned research note changed.
