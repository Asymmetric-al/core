# Phase 29 Q21 runtime research — who accepts a submitted requirement?

2026-10-21, Asia/Bangkok. Q01–Q20 are ratified. Q21 is unselected. This bounded research identifies the next product decision; it is not a full adversarial review or implementation plan.

## Recommendation and the exact open fork

Recommend a deliberate source-authorized staff acceptance decision for an ordinary native document requirement. A received file first becomes eligible for inspection under the existing checks; it remains awaiting review rather than satisfying the requirement merely because storage/processing succeeded. Staff accept the exact current submitted item/evidence set for that requirement, or use the source-owned correction/needs-information path. No second generic approval engine, universal two-person approval, mandatory acceptance essay or automatic reminder is implied.

The strongest simple alternative is to explicitly add an upfront **collection-only source mode** alongside staff review: the source declares before issuance that this requirement asks for receipt of the prescribed eligible material, and automatically records satisfaction once all declared intake conditions pass. That is an accepted source command with a deliberately narrower business meaning, not “malware scan clean = document approved.” It must not assert content correctness, legal identity, authenticity, adequate evidence or downstream application/financial approval. If offered for Q21, this additional mode and its narrow owner amendment must be explicit; do not quietly treat it as existing runtime or reinterpret an already issued review requirement.

A single useful question can therefore choose the ordinary native review policy: **require staff acceptance of the current requested item before satisfaction**, versus **also permit an upfront declared collection-only mode with qualified receipt and source satisfaction**. The alternatives should be compared on real meaning, added choice and consequences. An automatic system that tries to judge all document semantics/quality is not the strongest simple alternative and is unsupported by the inspected code. Existing source-authorized non-file or waived outcomes remain with their owner and are not converted into mandatory file acceptance by A.

Do not re-ask whether upload, safety checks, acceptance and fulfillment are different. That separation is settled. Nor should this question select whether “all requirements accepted” immediately fulfills the whole request, whether staff must press an extra Complete button, reviewer assignment/routing, waiver power, rejection reasons, response deadlines, or review notifications. Those are separate source semantics; review should avoid adding a redundant final ceremony by assumption.

## Why this remains open, and what is already binding

The original request and ratified decisions strongly favor accountable exact review but do not expressly establish that every ordinary requirement must have a human decision. They define who owns acceptance, immutable evidence and the consequences once acceptance/fulfillment occur. That leaves the performer/default of the new ordinary source satisfaction decision as a genuine product fork, provided the automatic alternative does not discard already-ratified distinctions.

For example, receiving a legible-looking document called “reference.pdf” does not establish that it is the requested reference, covers the right person/time, or satisfies the request. A file-type/scan gate cannot make those judgments. Conversely, a source that explicitly asks only for a copy to be collected may not need a second human acknowledgment. These are illustrative scenarios, not observed ministry workflows or evidence of demand.

The new question should make the automatic option honest. “Automatically satisfy when safe” is incomplete unless the source declares what receipt is enough to satisfy, what exact conditions prove it and what it does not claim. A user-selected filename, content type, file count, viewer open, scanner callback or generic task status must never supply that source authority.

## Boundaries to preserve whichever answer is selected

1. **Requirement versus whole request.** Accepting a specific requirement is not automatically accepting unrelated supporting files or the entire request. Preserve declared requirements and exact current evidence sets, accepted siblings, existing source-authorized non-file/waived outcomes, Q03 supersession and the source completion predicate. No new all-or-nothing/bulk acceptance, waiver power or final Complete-button policy is selected here.
2. **Current exact version.** Staff or an automatic source command must bind the exact admissible current candidate and relevant request/evidence revision. Corrections admitted while someone reviews stale bytes fence acceptance of that prior candidate. Previous safety/approval does not transfer to a new file.
3. **Safety versus semantic decision.** Received, pending safety, safe-to-inspect, reviewable, accepted/satisfied and fulfilled remain distinguishable. Quarantine or unknown safety cannot be treated as accepted evidence. A qualified representation outage is not itself rejection of source evidence; the exact permitted review method needs source qualification.
4. **Authority versus assignment.** Task possession, email, preview/download permission or a generic admin role is not the exact review/satisfaction capability. Staff attribution comes from trusted context. Automatic satisfaction has a qualified source-policy actor/effect; it cannot impersonate a human reviewer.
5. **No new clocks or admission revival.** Review and background work may continue under independent source authority after recipient expiry. Actual fulfillment then follows Q07/Q10/Q13 eligibility; no backdating to upload time or scan time, no automatic access extension, no resetting completed-read time. True source satisfaction and recipient read admission remain separate outcomes.
6. **One native source seam.** Reuse custody, exact source commands, P12, shared tasks and P6/P17. Do not require configurable Workflow Studio, add a second document ledger or repurpose generated-document acceptance. Q20 covers fresh-access notices only; it does not authorize acceptance/rejection/fulfillment emails.
7. **Recover durable outcomes.** A lost receipt or acceptance response recovers the same qualified effect with current disclosure; an email/preview/task projection failure does not undo source acceptance or ask the participant to submit a new action. No arbitrary rejection reason, waiver authority, reopening or file deletion follows from a failed technical step.
