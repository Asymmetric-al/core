## Six focused findings and exact proposed amendments

### C01 — The eligibility basis is a lawful completion-established admission, not a route label

**Critical severity / medium likelihood.** A caller, migration or helper could choose a `fresh` label, generic completed status or earliest created row and manufacture previous permission. With multiple previous unfinished grants, taking the latest or broadest one also invents a completed view. A denied/rolled-back/zero-authority attempt must not be treated as expired permission merely because a row or timestamp exists.

**Exact amendment:** In addition to Q08's original ordinary path, permit consideration after eligible ordinary expiry of the completed read admission actually and lawfully established under Q13 while the relevant Q13/Q15 unfinished authority was valid. Resolve its exact tenant/source/request/intended-recipient lineage, qualified fulfillment, declared and admitted read scope, historical eligibility, policy and end from authoritative source evidence. A route selector, earliest/most-recent row, access log, retained file or generic Completed/Expired status is not proof. The “first completed admission” means this lawful completion-established permission for the exact recipient/request lineage, not first observation, first visit or a later fresh Q08 grant.

Preserve Q13's causal order, including lawful atomic fresh admission then fulfillment. Completion first, completion during a gap or after actual unfinished expiry supplies no backfilled recipient permission. Recover a real timely durable effect using original evidence and terms; do not create one at repair time. Attributable correction of a proven derived-record error remains possible under Q10; arbitrary owner discretion, guessed intended rights or source completion alone cannot replace missing actual admission.

**Focused proof:** Original and Q13/Q15-derived lawful completed views qualify only after their eligible expiry; route spoofing, another recipient's first row, write-only/no completed admission, rolled-back attempts, conflicting historical outcomes and gap completion do not. A valid atomic transition qualifies without requiring artificial unequal timestamps. Late observation recovers the original effect rather than granting from now.

### C02 — Keep the exact completed member/action ceiling fixed

**Critical severity / medium likelihood.** The original unfinished requirement set can be broader than the completed view. Reusing Q15's original requirement ceiling here would grant files/actions never admitted for completed reading. Conversely, copying the most recent narrower Q08 grant as the ceiling can wrongly remove the ability to consider another originally eligible member. Combining grants accumulates rights.

**Exact amendment:** For the added path, each proposed Q08 scope must be a deliberately reviewed subset of the exact completion-established admission's member/action/context ceiling, intersected with current same-recipient eligibility and all current source restrictions. Do not substitute original unfinished requirements, prior write rights, any union of expired admissions, current folder contents or later-added files. Preview, unchanged-original download and Q05-permitted metadata/history remain distinct actions. The completed admission's narrow ceiling is binding; a later narrower Q08 fresh-read choice alone does not redefine that ceiling or a global restriction. Genuine binding narrowing remains effective. Later fresh-read grants cannot become new baselines through which the ceiling grows.

This is a permission ceiling, not a frozen copy of status/history text. Current source projections remain truthful and minimized within it. Source remediation/new work does not silently enlarge it or reopen intake/correction. Independent specialist/source rights remain separate.

**Focused proof:** Original requirements A/B followed by an admitted completed view of A permit consideration only for currently eligible A. A preview-only admission cannot gain original download. Later files, source-remediation replacements, superseded originals, another participant and expired-scope unions remain excluded. A later fresh Q08 subset of A does not erase independently eligible members of the actual completion-established ceiling, where future repeat policy permits consideration.

### C03 — Preserve unused and metadata-only admissions without empty file promises

**High severity / medium likelihood.** Requiring a prior click/download wrongly excludes lawfully admitted but unused access. Treating every completed view as file access can broaden a status-only admission. Requiring every old rendition to have existed or rendered at F can unnecessarily block qualified present representations; treating an empty current file set as success misleads users.

**Exact amendment:** Proved historical admission does not require that the recipient visited, authenticated then, rendered a preview, downloaded or saved a file. Current Q01 proof still gates new protected use. Distinguish admitted rights from past/current representation readiness. A newly qualified rendition of the same admitted original may be used only under existing Q02/Q04/Q08 representation and current-read rules; it supplies no new member, replacement original or action permission.

A deliberately admitted and currently eligible Q05-permitted status/metadata-only scope may remain eligible for a deliberately reviewed metadata-only Q08 grant. It must not acquire preview/original bytes or be labelled file access. With no eligible reviewed actions, use truthful source-qualified help rather than a misleading empty grant. This preserves Q08's already-ratified metadata-only case at review line222; it is not a new fallback.

Unknown base admission or expiry blocks affected issuance. Do not, however, demand every excluded historical member or every history-display fragment merely to consider an independently proved eligible subset. Partial representation/history failure remains scoped under existing owners; no unknown fact is inferred to broaden rights.

**Focused proof:** Never-used but proved admitted A can qualify; download telemetry without source admission cannot. Status-only stays status-only. No file-action scope yields no file promise. A repaired qualified preview of the same authorized original works when eligible; repaired bytes cannot add missing rights. An unavailable excluded member does not force a full-history scan or suppress an independently authorized reviewed subset, while unknown essential base proof remains closed.

### C04 — Revalidate actual expiry cause and present authority for both paths

**Critical severity / medium likelihood.** The new historical path can become a shortcut around revocation, a genuine shortened end, disposed content or a replaced recipient. A stale scope review or valid old identity can be mistaken for present authority. Conversely, requiring the expired grant to authorize the administrative operation makes the selected capability impossible.

**Exact amendment:** Both paths must satisfy Q08's eligible ordinary-expiry cause and current permitted purpose, not merely an end-date comparison. Preserve security revocation, genuine binding shortening, purpose/content-use expiry, disposal and other source prohibitions, including coincident nominal ends and removed adverse causes, at their actual scope. Qualified temporary representation or explicitly reversible restrictions remain distinct from ended source authority.

At the existing source/P12 boundary require the exact current issuance capability, live administrative scope/assignable ceiling and qualified continuing grantor dependencies. Staff read, support assignment, a role label or service credentials are insufficient. The expired recipient grant need not pass its old read check, and the recipient need not be online. Every subsequent protected use still requires current Q01/native/source/P12 proof. Governed same-recipient contact recovery remains available; raw email matching or an arbitrary new recipient does not inherit the ceiling.

Bind the actual completed admission and reviewed material scope to the deliberate command, recheck after relevant waits, and recover the exact same committed effect on retry under current disclosure. A route change, later scope, new transport key or vanished result must not create a fresh identity or rewrite its baseline. No inherited current-serving, audit, source fence or data/role proof is replaced.

**Focused proof:** Genuine adverse/unknown endings cannot be relabelled ordinary expiry through the added route. Current issuer loss, recipient revision, disposition and scope races have valid source-serialized outcomes. Administrative approval works where authorized despite old read expiry; current recipient use remains closed until properly proved. Same email/different recipient cannot inherit history. Lost-response recovery retains the original reviewed basis/result.

Blanket “same as other fresh access” would instead import Q14's30days and Q15's no-quota/nonoverlap policy into Q08, even though those were expressly left open. No thirty-day fresh-read default, fixed-at-issuance anchor, unlimited repeats, one-use rule, or Q15 no-overlap cardinality follows here. Ordinary Q07 completed ends and old grants do not reset.

Adopt prospectively through qualified source cohorts and compatible readers/writers/workers. Existing history can support future consideration only when actual lawful admission/expiry/current restrictions are proved; there is no automatic grant backfill. Missing or inconsistent provenance fails affected consideration safely. Preserve originals and later adverse facts through restore; retention/disposal remains separately governed.

**Focused proof:** Trace resolves only the eligible historical route/ceiling. Both routes remain gated when Q08 timing/repetition/type mapping is missing. No older task, URL, file or one-use assumption becomes a grant. Mixed versions/restore cannot replace a narrow completed baseline with original requirements or erase adverse history. Selecting A is never reported as native readiness.

### C06 — Use one source action and bounded proof, without displaying internal route machinery

**Medium-to-high severity / medium likelihood.** A new route-specific wizard or role would duplicate Q08, expose unnecessary provenance and make legitimate recipients learn internal grant history. Requiring all prior grants to be scanned or copied into review would create expensive history traversal. Hiding missing eligibility as rejection or promising files on a metadata-only grant would damage trust.

**Exact amendment:** Reuse the existing intended **Grant read access** review/action and completed-request context. Show only source-authorized recipient, actual eligible actions/members, current restrictions, qualified timing terms and truthful result. Do not require original-body read permission merely to administer access. Recipients see current permitted reading/status and their actual terms; no new account, reupload, internal route-name choice, forced download, generic sharing control, mandatory essay, duplicate confirmation or notification program is introduced.

Reuse bounded indexed source proof, authorized pagination, existing sole mutation/current-use boundaries and immutable outcome linkage. A maintained source reference or summary must remain coherently qualified; it cannot be separately writable shadow authority or infer missing history. No full-chain/full-folder scan or new evidence store is mandated. Material partial failures remain scoped. Missing eligibility proof is unknown/unavailable consideration with neutral owned help, not fabricated business rejection, guaranteed approval or an attachment workaround.

Preserve current every-door metadata/byte checks, participant-history minimization, current representation qualification, tiered audit, privacy, native tenant/data/RLS/service fences and existing capacity budgets. Source work, technical preview preparation and retained custody remain independent of an open recipient browser; new recipient read permission never resets retention or authorizes general storage.

**Focused proof:** Both routes reach the same accessible Core context; one meaningful current review suffices. Metadata-only and empty/unavailable outcomes are accurately described. Recipient proof/recovery works without unauthorized existence leakage. Historical evidence/current authority cannot be chosen by a caller-controlled route selector. Bounded lookup/history paging and actual real-role/current-serving/browser/AT journeys remain required, not asserted passed.
