## Conclusion and useful meaning of flexibility

**Keep A, with one clear source-owned completion rule and the existing meaningful staff actions.** Q21 staff review remains substantive. The source fulfills only after every actual current condition holds; an accepted-file count, clean scan, empty query or generic task badge cannot complete a request. If the work includes a genuine final aggregate judgment, that judgment is an actual source condition and must still be satisfied. A removes the routine extra Complete click after the real work, not real work itself.

For staff and tenants, flexibility means their legitimate requirements and specialist processes retain their source-owned meaning, while already-accepted work need not be repeated to refresh a screen. It does not silently add a per-tenant auto/manual mode, indefinite hold, last-minute approval override, waiver/reopen power or editable access clock. Existing authorized changes to requirements remain possible under their actual contracts; Q22 neither grants new edit powers nor forbids lawful existing changes. Such changes must remain attributable and cannot erase earlier acceptance/fulfillment history or manufacture eligibility after expiry.

The smallest interface is the existing request context: actual requirements and evidence, a clear meaningful action, a short explanation of the completion consequence, and a persistent actual result. No new final-review screen, compulsory essay, routine second reviewer, review wizard, automatic email, reminder or separate notification queue is needed by this decision.

## Strongest B and the risk A must own

A final deliberate completion action can be useful when a person must reconcile individually acceptable parts, choose a controlled handoff or establish a genuinely distinct final decision. That is B's strongest case, and direct comparators demonstrate the interaction. B need not mean another person, multiple modals or a bureaucratic essay. It can be one compact existing-context action.

The cost is an additional responsibility and wait after item review. It can leave work unfinished until someone acts, including beyond participant access expiry. It also does not reopen Q03's accepted items for correction, authorize missing requirements to be bypassed or make a guessed earlier “ready” time the real fulfillment time. These are reasoned tradeoffs, not measured incident frequencies.

With A selected, **the source predicate must include every genuine remaining condition**, including an already-required aggregate judgment where applicable. It must not contain a newly invented discretionary “wait for someone to decide whether to complete” gate that secretly reinstates B. If the ordinary profile cannot express the real required work coherently, resolve that owner issue before activation; do not complete from a partial list or launch a generic workflow editor as an incidental fix.

Automatic fulfillment can be a surprise when the recipient is preparing optional material or staff do not realize a final meaningful action will close intake. That is A's concrete usability risk. Address it with understandable policy/consequence information, exact admission ordering, preserved receipt truth and qualified help. Do not invent a grace timer, pause switch or mandatory final confirmation as a hidden policy reversal.

## Minimum proof before calling the experience qualified

1. **Source predicate and consequence:** prove complete current scope, required item acceptance, real remaining conditions/alternative outcomes, no empty-query shortcut, qualified automatic consequence and correct distinction between item result and fulfillment. This is actual source/database evidence, not a green UI test.
2. **Concurrent intake and changes:** demonstrate corrections, source-approved optional/pending intake, material requirement revisions, adverse state and two actors in both valid orders. No received work disappears, no previously uncommitted attempt borrows a later authority and no unrelated item is reset.
3. **Permanent result recovery:** source admission/mandatory evidence, lost responses, repeated consumers and failed projections preserve one effect and actual F. Distinguish unobserved current state from known historical truth; prove practical safe help without a new final-click fallback.
4. **Access and time:** timely versus late fulfillment, late observer recovery, earlier genuine limits, source/session/proof distinction and later restrictions honor Q06–Q19. Display actual precision/timezone/end without changing the instant. Regrant availability or a long nominal period does not establish accessibility conformance for every shorter timer or user journey.
5. **Privacy and serving:** source/current-user disclosure protects completed results, optional receipts, staff information, counts, files and history. A partial ancillary outage does not erase valid source success, while unknown required authority cannot become permission. Q02/Q04/Q05/Q21 exact-version and content-accessibility limits persist.
6. **Composed UX/accessibility and intended users:** staff understand which action can finish the request, which other conditions may remain and what completion means; recipients distinguish submitted, staff-pending, complete, unavailable and ended access. Test actual keyboard/AT/touch/reflow/localized/poor-network paths and representative document content. A polished demo, source read or unit test cannot establish this evidence.

These are qualification obligations and expected outcomes, not claims that the tests passed or a new test/telemetry product is needed. The parent synthesis may assign existing accountable owners and observable failure responses. No numerical review SLA, automatic notification cadence, staffing guarantee or workload budget is invented from public documentation.

## What remains unselected

A is not a guarantee that staff can always complete every request, that completion always creates recipient access, or that all source work finishes immediately. Unknown technical results need reconciliation; real unsatisfied work needs its actual actor. In ordinary success, users should need only the meaningful action they were already taking and a clear result. The selected policy's lack of a generic final checkpoint is an acknowledged tradeoff, not evidence that such checkpoints are obsolete.
