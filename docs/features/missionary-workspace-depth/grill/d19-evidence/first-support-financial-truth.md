# D19 evidence — first support for the ministry/project

Research date: **2026-10-16 UTC**. Research only. The founder selected **A: first
support for the ministry/project**, including when the supporter already gives
elsewhere in the organization. This note proposes the precise financial/history
meaning needed to carry out that choice; it does not ratify further policy,
amend formal owners, qualify a production adapter or create tasks/messages.

## Recommended permanent meaning

Create the eligible internal personal-follow-up work for the **first qualifying
recorded monetary support of one exact ministry subject by one source-qualified
supporter**, using P13's current effective contribution-line facts and P14's
qualified support attribution where applicable. The subject is a stable typed
P9 **Ministry Assignment or Ministry Project**, not the worker, login, current
roster, public page, campaign title or a loose set of designation codes. Both
P9 subject types already carry one exact Tenant and Legal Entity. No new
cross-entity aggregation policy is needed for the ordinary profile.

“Qualifying” needs an explicit short explanation in the eventual UI: an actual
source-admitted monetary gift allocated to this ministry, with the history and
supporter attribution needed to establish that it is first. Do not claim this
person never supported the ministry in any other way, is new to the organization,
or has never given outside the history the source can prove. Noncash gifts and
unvalued in-kind support are real support but are not converted into money for
this proposed initial financial trigger. If the product wants an all-tender
“first support of any kind” claim, that needs a separately qualified noncash
predicate; a positive internal valuation is not a substitute.

Keep three truths separate:

1. **Financial/recognition eligibility now:** the owner-qualified positive
   effective support and history result through exact source revisions.
2. **Original reason and task identity:** the immutable facts used when this
   first-support purpose was admitted, plus later source changes. A corrected
   earliest gift does not create a second “first” identity.
3. **Actual human work:** pending, suspended, deliberately not needed or completed
   interaction/work history. A refund cannot unsend a thank-you, erase a real
   call, or reopen terminal human work automatically.

The stable purpose slot is conceptually **Tenant + canonical supporter Party +
typed ministry subject + personal-first-support purpose**. The subject pins its
Legal Entity; the producer also retains exact source-entity evidence. Currency,
worker, assignment participant, gift ID, designation revision, delivery attempt,
recurring plan and current earliest-date winner are **not** reasons to mint a new
first-support purpose. This is a logical semantic key, not a final SQL schema.

An unresolved held occurrence may complete its original admission when its
source becomes qualified. When a task's financial basis disappears, only its
solely source-generated first-support requirement leaves actionability through
the task owner's qualified withdrawal/disposition. A separately deliberately
accepted call, promise or other qualified undertaking remains actionable through
its existing owner; the task is not a recurring source-controlled review task.
Correcting an **erroneous withdrawal** may restore that same identity only with
proof of the original live admission, no intervening human terminal disposition,
current eligibility and an explicit owner correction receipt. A valid refund
followed by another gift does not restart the first-support purpose. If another
eligible gift already preserves the pending support basis, update its source
explanation within the same purpose instead of manufacturing a second task.

**Cross-review correction — reconciled to ratified D19 C11:** an invalid,
unhandled admission does not consume genuine first-support coverage. If owner
correction proves the supposed earlier support never belonged to this
subject/person, a later genuinely live, source-certified first support may
re-admit the **same purpose/task identity** through a qualified owner command,
preserving the invalidation and new admission evidence. No human handling,
independent undertaking or genuine prior qualifying support may contradict that
proof. This is distinct from correcting an erroneous withdrawal: the original
withdrawal may have been correct because the admission itself was invalid.
Neither gift replay nor a later gift alone supplies this proof, and historical
repair alone does not produce catch-up work. Genuine prior support followed by
a refund never restarts a welcome purpose. This correction supersedes this
note's earlier restriction to erroneous-withdrawal restoration; it adds no
successor-task engine or claim of executed verification.

## Proposed exact source contract

### Eligible occurrence and scope

The P13/P14 producer must return a source-qualified **support occurrence**, not a
raw ledger row copied into P28. Its evidence includes exact tenant/entity, typed
ministry subject and compatible historical designation/purpose mapping, canonical
supporter identity with lineage, underlying contribution/line coverage,
money/recognition vocabulary and path, source gift date/basis, effective revisions,
finality/support-visibility ruling, provenance, history coverage and current safe
result authority. A minimum Boolean/reason projection is sufficient; P28 needs
neither an all-donor ledger export nor hidden historical rows.

- Require actual source-admitted, **positive effective monetary support allocated
  to this subject**. Exclude fee-cover/system-fund-only amounts, unrelated split
  lines, zero/voided/reversed amounts, unposted intake, failed/processing attempts,
  commitments, match expectancies, noncash internal values, liquidation proceeds,
  processor payouts and P21 account/assessment math. No minimum donor-worth
  threshold or currency conversion is needed.
- Apply P13's exact tender/finality and missionary-support visibility contract.
  D19 does not unilaterally make provisional ACH visible. For the initial
  follow-up profile, waiting for source-qualified settled/cleared eligibility is
  the conservative recommendation for provisional tenders; the owner must
  explicitly qualify any admitted provisional treatment and truthful wording.
  This is not a claim that every existing P13 private financial view waits.
- A supporter may be a canonical person, household or organization. The legal
  donor, recognized supporter and eventual contact recipient remain distinct.
  Use **P14-certified support paths**, not “any positive credit.” Solicitor,
  org-contact, tribute annotation or household membership alone cannot imply
  personal giving or produce recipient work. An amountless informational role
  never satisfies the positive support predicate.
- P14 must certify subject allocation for recognition. A partial header-scoped
  credit on a split gift cannot be assigned to a ministry merely by joining any
  matching child, copying the header amount or inventing pro-rata allocation.
  Direct qualified lines remain usable when a distinct recognition path is
  unresolved. This is a bounded named-projection extension, not a new credit fold.
- A first-support predicate is existential across qualified currency buckets:
  one eligible positive EUR gift and a later USD gift are support by the same
  Party to the same ministry, not two firsts. Keep exact ISO currency if showing
  an amount; do not add amounts or rank firstness by converted value.

### First, history and time

Compute firstness relative to the **qualified complete support history** of that
Party/subject and the declared support-path profile, through a coherent source
watermark. Do not use the loaded page, first row delivered, first imported row,
first current assignment, trailing roster window, active commitment or a legacy
donor-level `first_gift_date`.

The source result has at least **qualified first**, **known prior qualifying
support**, and **unknown/unavailable** meanings. Finding one older qualified
support fact can establish prior support even if wider history is incomplete;
the absence of a row cannot establish first without coverage. A lawful source
history/coverage marker may support qualification without exposing the history
to the missionary. Missing retained history is not silently treated as zero.

For the explanatory earliest date, use P7's current source gift-date fact and
its actual precision. Do not convert a date-only gift into browser midnight or
sort it by import/webhook receipt time. Same-day equally precise events form a
tie for this purpose: one stable task/coverage result is enough. A deterministic
technical tie-break does not prove which donation physically happened first.
Later backdating or corrected attribution may change the earliest evidence;
retain the original as-known admission evidence and its qualified successor.
No new accounting/history engine or frozen-by-value financial total is needed.

### Prospective effects versus historical repair

History import/backfill/migration establishes a baseline and can disprove a
first-support claim; it emits **no** catch-up first-thanks tasks merely because
old support became known today. Conversely, a delayed genuine live gift is not
an import just because delivery/reconciliation happens later. Preserve its
original source origin and the qualified prospective activation boundary.

Corrections are not new acquisitions. A historical redesignation/late import
must not fabricate new live first-support work. But a correction can qualify an
originally live, still-unconsumed occurrence whose allocation/attribution was
blocked: reevaluate that **same** original occurrence/purpose under current
authority. A blanket “corrections never activate work” would lose legitimate
repair. No age cutoff or catch-up lookback is selected here.

When a refund/return/NSF/chargeback or source correction invalidates the pending
basis, update or remove only the solely source-generated requirement from
actionability through its qualified task-owner disposition, retaining its history.
A separately deliberately accepted undertaking remains with its existing owner;
opening, claiming, commenting or changing a reminder alone does not establish
one. A partial refund leaving eligible positive allocation is not automatically
a new first or an invalidation.
An unresolved, not-yet-admitted held occurrence is distinct from a withdrawn
independent task. The former can qualify after repair. The latter may be restored
by the explicit erroneous-withdrawal correction described above, or the same
purpose/task may be re-admitted after an invalid, unhandled admission when a
later genuinely live first support satisfies the separate owner-qualified proof
in the cross-review correction. A gift replay or a new gift alone is insufficient.
Genuine prior support followed by a refund never resets first-support coverage.
A human-completed interaction or deliberate terminal not-needed outcome remains
historical. No automatic correction message, retrospective retraction or
successor-task engine is implied.

### Recognition does not create another contact program

Known P14 support history may prevent falsely calling a later direct gift
“first,” even where the older support path could not trigger personal contact.
In particular, prior via-church support is not erased when a member later gives
directly or signs up. That later action may establish contactability but does
not retrospectively authorize a church-member acknowledgment. The separate
P14 exclusion still applies even when the person is independently contactable.

A DAF's legal sponsor is not silently substituted for the identified advisor,
and anonymous/undisclosed advisors remain anonymous/undisclosed. Household
recognition does not multiply the original gift into personal first-gift tasks
for every spouse. Existing source recipient selection and allowed purpose must
identify the intended person/household or organization. A generic org-contact
role is not that authority. Genuine received employer matches and workplace
support use their exact P14 paths; an expectancy or a new match campaign is not
new received support. These paths can inform history without authorizing a new
message or task subtype.

D19's required personal work and P14 organizational acknowledgment can be
distinct intentional purposes. Reuse the same obligation where it is actually
the same purpose; do not automatically mark personal interaction done just
because a receipt contains thanks, and do not reproduce a P14 acknowledgment
stream under a new task label.

## Material concerns — eight dimensions each

Severity is potential harm; likelihood is conditional on a design mistake, not
a measured production incidence. Each finding records harm, severity,
likelihood, trigger/root cause, evidence, implication, exact clause and proof.
All proofs below remain qualification obligations, not tests run in this note.

### FS19-01 — Posted, promised or provisionally received is treated as qualified support

1. **Harm:** thanks is prompted for money that failed, is still processing or was never contributed.
2. **Severity:** high for financial truth and donor trust.
3. **Likelihood:** high if a `posted` flag, P16 initial attempt or active roster row is the trigger.
4. **Cause:** conflating payment, contribution, commitment, recognition and support-visibility axes.
5. **Evidence:** S04–S05, S11, S14; P13:993–996; P16:533–560,1101–1105.
6. **Implication:** require the exact qualified source occurrence and tender/visibility policy; no P28 financial status.
7. **Clause:** “First-support work SHALL require source-admitted positive effective monetary allocation to the exact subject and current source-qualified finality/visibility. Promise, attempt, processing, expectancy, payout, account balance and raw posted status SHALL NOT substitute.”
8. **Proof:** card success, action-required, ACH processing/success-return-exposed/qualified settled state, check recorded/cleared/NSF, cash, zero gift and active unfulfilled commitment produce the correct distinct eligibility results.

### FS19-02 — Worker, designation or page changes restart history

1. **Harm:** repeated first-thanks, wrong-ministry disclosure or support attributed across legal entities.
2. **Severity:** high; critical where restricted scope crosses entity/tenant boundaries.
3. **Likelihood:** high if identity comes from current assignment, a display label or designation list.
4. **Cause:** treating financial target, CRM subject, page and current responsible actor as one ID.
5. **Evidence:** S02–S04, S12, S19; P9:1274–1277,1301–1308; P13:811–815,1648–1658.
6. **Implication:** stable typed ministry subject with exact entity and qualified historical mappings; no automatic cross-entity union or historical fund-merge repoint.
7. **Clause:** “First-support identity SHALL bind one exact source-owned ministry subject and its Tenant/Legal Entity. Assignment, participant, page, designation-code, currency or account changes SHALL NOT reset it. Scope mapping and successor continuity require exact owner authority; name similarity cannot establish continuity.”
8. **Proof:** worker succession, spouse departure, two workers on one project, same label in two entities, fund merge and new page preserve or deny scope correctly; shared project produces one obligation under current responsibility, not one first per worker.

### FS19-03 — Incomplete or hidden history becomes ‘first’

1. **Harm:** long-term supporters are incorrectly welcomed as new, or hidden giving is revealed through firstness.
2. **Severity:** high for trust and privacy.
3. **Likelihood:** high with capped API lists, roster windows, partial migrations or filtered history.
4. **Cause:** absence in an authorized visible list is mistaken for complete source absence.
5. **Evidence:** S11:1071–1078; S16:requests99; S18:182–187; S17:4837–4843.
6. **Implication:** owner-qualified history and minimum result authority, not client-side MIN. Unknown causes no automatic first-support claim; ordinary permitted gift/manual work continues.
7. **Clause:** “A qualified first result SHALL require coherent history coverage and authority for that result. Capped, missing, redacted, expired or unavailable evidence SHALL NOT become known absence. The missionary SHALL receive only permitted result/reason fields, not hidden history or an existence-leaking diagnostic.”
8. **Proof:** older gift beyond 250/730-day limits, partially imported history, erased/unavailable history, concurrent source cursor changes and hidden older gift cannot yield false firstness or reveal the hidden record; a proven older fact can establish prior support without pretending all history is complete.

### FS19-04 — Every positive credit becomes a new donor or contact recipient

1. **Harm:** solicitor, spouse, employee, honoree or church member receives inappropriate outreach; money is double-counted.
2. **Severity:** high, potentially critical for anonymity/restricted individuals.
3. **Likelihood:** high if all credits share one “supporter” or recipient rule.
4. **Cause:** mixing legal donor, support-recognition role and communication eligibility; treating header credit as line attribution.
5. **Evidence:** S07–S10, S13; P14:399–420,442–454,1023,1167–1177.
6. **Implication:** exact P14-certified support path and separate purpose/recipient ruling; no new recognition allocator or contact rights.
7. **Clause:** “Recognition MAY qualify only through the named P14 support-attribution projection for this exact subject. Arbitrary credit roles, household membership and a matching child line SHALL NOT establish personal giving or a recipient. Church-member acknowledgment remains excluded; neither later contactability nor D19 overrides it.”
8. **Proof:** disclosed/anonymous DAF, church plus member, two-spouse household, solicitor/org-contact/tribute, received employer match versus expectancy, and partial header credit across two ministries retain the right identities, scope and contact exclusion without fan-out.

### FS19-05 — Currency, fee cover or split lines create extra firsts

1. **Harm:** first tasks duplicate by currency/line, or support is claimed for the wrong allocation.
2. **Severity:** high for data integrity; medium for repeated unnecessary work.
3. **Likelihood:** high when header totals or monetary thresholds are reused.
4. **Cause:** treating each line/currency as a distinct relationship or summing mixed values.
5. **Evidence:** S04:784,818; S08:422–454; S16:99; ratified D9 financial basis is separate from P21 credited/net money.
6. **Implication:** Boolean existence over exact positive qualified buckets; no total or FX calculation is needed.
7. **Clause:** “Only the subject's qualified non-fee-cover effective allocation SHALL establish monetary support. The first-support purpose SHALL span that subject's qualified currency buckets without adding currencies or creating a first per currency/line. Noncash valuation and liquidation proceeds SHALL NOT stand in for this monetary predicate.”
8. **Proof:** one split payment to two subjects, fee-cover-only target, two same-subject lines, equal amounts in distinct gifts, USD then JPY, partial/full line refund and unvalued in-kind gift preserve exact firstness and monetary meaning.

### FS19-06 — Correction/refund destroys real history or creates a new first

1. **Harm:** duplicate outreach, deleted evidence of an actual interaction, or a stale task about reversed support.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** high if the task key includes the current earliest gift ID or source state is destructively replaced.
4. **Cause:** conflating current effective basis, original first-assessment evidence and terminal human work.
5. **Evidence:** S01:171–173; S04:789–817; S07:342–363; S18:153–183.
6. **Implication:** stable purpose, cause-aware withdrawal/correction and immutable interaction evidence under the independent-task contract; an invalid unhandled admission does not consume genuine first support, while genuine prior support followed by refund does; no automatic retraction message.
7. **Clause:** “Source corrections SHALL re-evaluate pending work through the same permanent first-support purpose. Only the invalidated solely source-generated requirement SHALL leave actionability without fabricated human completion; separately qualified undertakings SHALL remain with their existing owner. An unresolved held occurrence MAY qualify after repair. Restoring a task after erroneous withdrawal SHALL require an explicit owner-qualified correction proving the original live admission, erroneous withdrawal, no intervening human terminal disposition and current eligibility, with a durable correction receipt. Separately, an invalid, unhandled admission SHALL NOT consume genuine first-support coverage: where owner correction proves the supposed earlier support never belonged to the subject/person, a later genuinely live, source-certified first support MAY re-admit the same purpose/task through a qualified owner command, preserving invalidation history, provided no human handling, independent undertaking or genuine prior qualifying support contradicts that proof. Gift replay or a later positive gift alone SHALL NOT reopen work. Genuine prior support followed by refund SHALL NOT restart the first-support purpose. Completed/not-needed human dispositions and admitted communication evidence SHALL remain.”
8. **Required proof (not an executed test claim):** partial refund remains positive; full reversal removes sole basis; remaining second gift updates cause; genuine support plus refund plus new gift cannot restart first; proved erroneous withdrawal repairs the same identity once; invalid unhandled admission for the wrong subject/person followed by a separately source-certified live first re-admits the same identity with both histories retained; human handling, independent undertaking or genuine prior support prevents that re-admission; held original live occurrence can qualify; completed thank-you survives refund; correction versus completion preserves truthful history without duplicate effects.

### FS19-07 — Imports/backdating become acquisition events, or delayed live gifts are lost

1. **Harm:** historic task floods or missed legitimate follow-up after delivery delay/repair.
2. **Severity:** high for operational correctness.
3. **Likelihood:** high if the consumer uses arrival time or blanket “ignore all corrections.”
4. **Cause:** confusing origin, source gift date, observation time and prospective effect eligibility.
5. **Evidence:** S06; S12:1128; S17:4859–4861; S01:171.
6. **Implication:** baseline-only import provenance persists through repair; real live occurrence may qualify late under its original identity.
7. **Clause:** “Historical import/backfill SHALL update history without acquisition-task effects. Delayed live events SHALL retain their original live provenance. A correction MAY qualify the same originally live unconsumed occurrence but SHALL NOT turn a historical occurrence into a new live gift or reset purpose identity. Source date corrections SHALL preserve original and successor dating evidence.”
8. **Proof:** old gift imported today disqualifies mistaken first without catch-up; delayed current gift still qualifies; current gift with repaired ambiguous attribution qualifies once; historical redesignation remains baseline; same-day/coarse dates produce one task without fabricated timestamp order.

### FS19-08 — Party merge or household repair rewrites donor truth and replays work

1. **Harm:** duplicate first-thanks, incorrect legal donor or broadened access after merge/unmerge.
2. **Severity:** high, critical for wrong-person disclosure.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible whenever duplicate identities or household corrections exist.
4. **Cause:** overwriting frozen financial foreign keys or treating a new canonical ID as a new person.
5. **Evidence:** S13–S15; P4:A9; P14:355,1149–1159.
6. **Implication:** current canonical identity/merge lineage is separate from immutable financial identity and past purpose receipts. A merge can consolidate duplicate active work, not erase two actual past interactions.
7. **Clause:** “Governed Party identity repair SHALL reconcile first-support history/purpose aliases without rewriting frozen donor facts, broadening contact authority or replaying consumed effects. ### FS19-09 — Campaign or correction-policy gaps are silently solved in P28

1. **Harm:** invented attribution/history or unauthorized posted-gift correction.
2. **Severity:** high for finance and authorization.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible if campaign membership is used as a substitute ministry map or task repair invokes finance writes.
4. **Cause:** treating source-code mapping, account role or task administration as financial-owner policy.
5. **Evidence:** S16 E7/C-01; S18:188; S04:793–815.
6. **Implication:** gate only the affected adapter. Direct qualified ministry line mapping remains usable when it does not depend on E7. D19 does not require resolving all finance gaps before any native work.
7. **Clause:** “A first-support producer depending on unresolved E7 temporal campaign mapping SHALL remain unavailable for that association, without guessed live/frozen attribution. D19 SHALL neither settle C-01 nor perform posted corrections. Independent qualified source mappings and manual work SHALL continue.”
8. **Proof:** current campaign reparenting cannot rewrite first history; previously unmapped source code cannot silently acquire past support; direct qualified line path still works; a task flag cannot write a correction or choose approver policy.

### FS19-10 — A task source becomes a second financial or communication authority

1. **Harm:** drifting financial counters, duplicate acknowledgments or automatic donor-facing messages.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** high if task-generation shortcuts copy amounts/statuses or emit generic send events.
4. **Cause:** defining first-support as a mutable P28 financial flag, raw provider event, duplicated roster or workflow-specific identity.
5. **Evidence:** S01, S07:362, S11:1056, S17:4859–4863, S18:162–183.
6. **Implication:** bounded source projection + stable source-purpose work command; no second money/recognition fold or communication producer.

It SHALL NOT mutate money, infer receipt/delivery completion, create consent, emit donor-facing thanks, or fork the same purpose for future workflows. 8. **Proof:** repeated/out-of-order provider/domain events, source projection retries, P35 enabled/disabled, organizational receipt/DAF acknowledgment, completed personal interaction and provider outage cannot produce duplicate money, tasks or sends; manual authorized interaction remains usable.
