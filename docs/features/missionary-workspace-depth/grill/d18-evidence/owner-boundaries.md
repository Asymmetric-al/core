# D18 — newsletter writing assistance: owner and permission boundaries

Research only, October 16, 2026 UTC, against repository baseline
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813`.

The current review assignment reports the founder selected A: optional newsletter
AI writing help, organization-selected API credentials and limits, optional
missionary use, and understandable actions that preserve the author's voice.
That answer supersedes the earlier Q18 awaiting-answer snapshot. Detailed clauses
and defaults below are recommendations for the review, not separately ratified
amendments, formal owner changes, implementation or activation evidence.

## Main finding

Extend one existing shared AI control plane with **one newsletter-writing
purpose and one P28-owned suggestion/application contract**. P21 D10 already
owns tenant provider connections, revisioned secrets, certified bindings, egress,
usage, budgets and revocation. P22 D16 is a useful source-bounded writing precedent,
but its `public-profile drafting` binding, Page targets, exact action catalog and
24-hour suggestion timer do not automatically authorize newsletter use. P40 later
consumes the same foundation; completing its entire workbench is not a prerequisite.

The smallest useful product is: eligible current newsletter prose → deliberate
writing-help action → protected suggestion → compare/edit → deliberate Use into
the current draft. D7's ordinary preview, content review and separate Send remain
authoritative. No model selects recipients, sends, publishes, mutates a Page or
retrieves a supporter roster to learn the missionary's voice.

## Corrected decision candidate

> Offer optional writing help for eligible newsletter prose through a new exact
> newsletter-writing purpose in the existing tenant AI control plane. Authorized
> tenant administrators choose a capability-certified connection/model and its
> supported limits. Saving or testing a key does not enable this or another
> purpose. Missionaries choose whether to invoke the help; ordinary writing,
> editing, preview and sending remain complete without it.
>
> Show the exact eligible text the action will use. A small action catalog can
> improve grammar, clarity or length while treating the author's meaning and
> voice as the baseline. It does not invent facts, testimony, quotations, doctrine,
> outcomes or stronger fundraising claims. A suggestion remains separate until
> the currently authorized author compares it and deliberately applies it to the
> exact current draft. AI use cannot send, schedule, publish, approve content,
> choose recipients or change upstream stories.
>
> Tenant keys, ordinary edit access and author selection do not override current
> egress restrictions. Apply exact source classification and minimum-data limits;
> send no private CRM/care/restricted context, recipient data, imported files,
> personal-message history or hidden background context. An unpublished newsletter
> draft may be an eligible source under this separately qualified purpose; it need
> not first be published to obtain writing help.
>
> Keep source-linked, content-minimized evidence, bounded private suggestion
> retention, truthful cost and unknown-outcome handling, and simple manual recovery.
> An unavailable or exhausted AI feature leaves the author's ordinary draft intact.

This candidate does not choose a provider, default model, numerical budget,
language/translation expansion, exact retention period or every action label.
Those require explicit review judgments and qualification, not inherited Page
settings or a second product question about whether A was selected.

## Smallest useful settings, authority and lifecycle

**Tenant experience.** Reuse Settings → AI features with a **Newsletter writing
help** purpose entry. Select an existing connection or connect a supported one,
inspect provider/model/region, data sent, retention/training posture, billing
owner, current limits and health, then deliberately activate its binding. Recommend
new deployments remain Off until the exact binding is qualified and activated;
this is a D18 review recommendation, not proof that the user separately selected
an initial default. Never activate all AI features by saving a key. Reusing a key
does not merge feature budgets or give a newsletter editor expense/Page access.

**Missionary optionality.** The smallest sufficient implementation is explicit
per-invocation use with no background/per-keystroke calls, nagging or degraded
manual flow. The selected optionality does not require a second permission
system or persisted personal toggle. If the product offers a personal **Hide
writing help** preference, label it as such and use it only to subtract that
person's newsletter assistance. It cannot enable an organization-disabled
purpose, revoke colleagues' independently authorized rights, erase ordinary
drafts, invalidate previously accepted text or affect Send. A personal usage
choice does not establish a new coauthor veto or source-wide AI policy by inference.

**Actual rights.** Distinguish connection/credential administration, binding and
limit administration, protected cost/diagnostic read, source text read, writing
invocation, suggestion read/apply and ordinary newsletter edit/send. These are
semantic powers for the existing capability registry, not a demand for separate
UI switches or one grant screen per action. Tenant administration does not imply
private body access. A missionary's invocation capability does not authorize
provider setup, another author's private draft or another Tenant's source.

**Eligible text.** Register the newsletter-prose source family and its allowed AI
egress in the existing whole-field policy; apply exact row/source/person floors
and current purpose checks. A draft being access-controlled/unpublished does not
automatically make every paragraph prohibited internal/care context. Conversely,
copying known restricted notes, import cells or private correspondence into an
editable field cannot make their lineage safe to send. Do not claim perfect
semantic detection of every sensitive fact in arbitrary newly typed prose. Prove
structural source restrictions and honest user guidance; no speculative PII or
AI-moderation engine is introduced. An unresolved eligible-source policy blocks
that AI path, not ordinary newsletter work. Eligible authored prose may contain
ordinary personal details intentionally included for its permitted audience;
there is no truthful blanket promise that “no personal/private data is sent.”
Explain the exact selected disclosure. Known prohibited sources stay excluded
unless their actual owner supplies a lawful current qualification; neither a
paste nor an invocation is that qualification.

**Initial source recommendation.** One selected text range or one eligible
narrative block of an exact saved draft revision, plus the code-owned action
choice and minimal qualified language context. No selection must never silently
mean the whole newsletter, its recipients or its entire content history. Preserve
links/placeholders/protected values through an exact source-owned typed rule rather
than sending recipient-expanded personalization or asking the model to repair
markup. Initial same-language editing need not include translation, voice-model
training, memory, automatic story retrieval or a generic free-form chatbot.

**Suggestion lifecycle.** Freeze the current source/semantic target and a
minimum-data manifest. Invoke once through the exact binding and a bounded
request. Validate output as inert proposed text and store a private immutable
suggestion with source and invocation references. Compare-before-Use freshly
checks actor, draft revision/selection digest, source safety and applicable
binding/revocation state, then invokes the ordinary D7 draft command at most
once. Concurrent editing requires a new comparison instead of last-write-wins.
Accepted text is ordinary current draft content under D7's version/retention
rules; turning AI off later does not erase it or withdraw a valid prior Send.

**Disablement and late outcomes.** Turning the purpose off or revoking the source
fences new egress/invocation and new application of stale unaccepted suggestions
under the qualified rule. Replacement keys never silently migrate in-flight work
to another binding/account/model. Already transmitted input or provider processing
cannot be promised recalled, uncharged or erased merely by a local Cancel. Late
output and cost evidence are reconciled without restoring unauthorized content
access or mutating the draft. Complete manual work remains available.

## Budget, billing and retention distinctions

The founder's limits requirement is meaningful but no inspected owner supplies a
universal monthly amount, period timezone, token count or request-rate default.
The D10 binding owns a finite purpose-qualified envelope. A compact control can
show a spending limit and usage where an actual certified cost bound exists,
alongside a code-qualified per-request input/output limit and rate/concurrency
protection. Do not build a general tenant billing or financial forecasting product.

- Distinguish estimated/reserved cost, observed provider usage/cost and the
  provider's final account invoice. Operational usage evidence is not a gift,
  Field Account expense, reimbursement, payment authorization or accounting entry.
  This feature does not create Asym payments, top-ups, markup or a new billing rail.
- Enforce the Asym purpose envelope before dispatch using current binding, rate
  and concurrency checks plus atomic reservation of the maximum qualified request
  exposure. Include every billable class for the exact provider/model/tier where
  a monetary hard-cap claim is made; an output-token limit alone is not that proof.
- Unknown provider outcomes retain their safe reservation/uncertainty until
  reconciled. A timeout is not proof of zero cost and cannot authorize a duplicate
  invocation or release funds needed to cover the first attempt.
- Explicitly define the selected budget window, currency, reset boundary and
  binding-change behavior. Lowering a limit blocks new admissions when committed
  exposure already consumes it; it cannot recall or erase costs already incurred.
  Key rotation, feature toggles or profile replacements do not reset spend history.
- An Asym feature limit does not cap unrelated uses of the same provider key/account.
  Provider-side account limits and alerts have their own exact semantics. If a
  conservative per-request monetary bound is unavailable, qualify a truthful
  supported usage/rate envelope rather than advertise an unenforceable hard dollar cap.

For retention, separate ordinary newsletter drafts/versions, transient source
packages, suggestion bodies, credentials, body-free invocation/usage evidence,
provider copies and backup copies. P22's 24-hour suggestion-body lifetime is a
useful reviewed precedent but **not an inherited D18 timer**. The main review
must explicitly choose and justify a bounded D18 body lifetime and early
Use/Discard/revocation/supersession behavior. A new exact schedule may reuse
qualified execution mechanics without inheriting another domain's retention
authority. Do not keep full prompts/results indefinitely for style learning or
evaluation. Provider retention/training posture must be qualified for the actual
tenant account/tier/endpoint; a key, advertised option or local delete does not
prove zero provider retention. Access denial, primary purge, cryptographic erasure,
backup expiry and provider deletion remain separately evidenced facts.

**Concrete lifetime recommendation requested during review:** adopt a fixed
24-hour candidate-suggestion lifetime from server-recorded suggestion creation,
with no renewal on read. Earlier Use, Discard, source invalidation or actual
revocation ends availability and application of that candidate. This is an
explicit D18 product judgment aligning the Page precedent, not inherited law or
measured optimality: the candidate is a transient duplicate, while the original
draft remains recoverable through D7. Bound provider-input/source-package bodies
separately by the exact invocation/recovery deadline. State and prove the primary
body-erasure timing and backup/provider treatment rather than equate a UI expiry
with all-copy erasure. Use leaves the accepted ordinary draft and necessary
content-minimized provenance under their own source schedules.

## Consequential concerns and exact proposed clauses

Likelihood is conditional engineering judgment, not measured incident frequency.
Each concern includes all eight requested dimensions.

### O01 — The Page helper or generalized workbench substitutes for the missing source

- **What:** Newsletter text uses the Page binding, a system-message key, an Eve
  operator tool or an invented second AI service.
- **Why:** Those interfaces do not own this source purpose or draft acceptance;
  alternatively, waiting for all P40 makes a small feature needlessly dependent.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely if the existing writing UI is copied without owner reconciliation.
- **Evidence:** S02–S07, S10.
- **Answer impact:** Accept A with an explicit newsletter-purpose/source amendment.
- **Permanent fix:** Extend D10's closed registry and P28's newsletter producer;
  retain P17/P6/P22 and source-domain boundaries.
- **Exact proposed clause OC01:** “D18 registers one qualified newsletter-writing
  purpose on the shared D10 control plane. P28 owns its source package, suggestion
  and ordinary-draft application; the foundation owns connection, credential,
  binding, egress, invocation, usage and revocation. Neither the Page purpose nor
  a system-message key substitutes. Whole-P40 completion is not required.”

### O02 — Keys and settings become broad content rights, or optional use becomes compulsory

- **What:** A key activates every feature, an administrator reads private drafts,
  or generation runs automatically when a missionary writes.
- **Why:** Tenant availability, source authorization and a person's choice to use
  help are different; dormant settings and role labels establish none of them.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely with a single tenant AI boolean/key or automatic editor hook.
- **Evidence:** S02, S03, S05, S09, S11.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve tenant control and voluntary use with a compact existing UI.
- **Permanent fix:** Purpose activation, current invoke/apply admission, separate
  administration and explicit per-use request; no new compulsory workflow.
- **Exact proposed clause OC02:** “Only authorized tenant administrators configure
  the exact certified newsletter binding and limits. Saving/testing a credential
  activates nothing. Every writing request is deliberate and independently
  authorized. A missionary may use the complete editor without AI. Personal
  presentation preferences cannot widen tenant permissions, alter other users'
  rights or change accepted newsletter content or sending authority.”

### O03 — Private storage is confused with prohibited context, or selection launders it

- **What:** All unpublished prose is denied, making the feature unusable; or
  selected/private CRM text is sent merely because the author can read/copy it.
- **Why:** Draft audience, field classification and permitted model egress differ.
  P10 supplies source restrictions, not a perfect semantic PII detector.
- **Severity:** Critical for prohibited disclosure; High for denying the selected job.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible to likely with a broad “private” check or selection-only gate.
- **Evidence:** S01, S08, S09, S12.
- **Answer impact:** Qualify the actual newsletter-prose field family and purpose;
  no public-publication prerequisite or private-context expansion.
- **Permanent fix:** Shared static field policy, current source/person floors,
  explicit minimum source package and protected-provenance handling.
- **Exact proposed clause OC03:** “Unpublished newsletter prose may enter this
  newly qualified purpose when its exact source/field/row safety permits. Show
  the minimum selected input; selection, BYOK and editing rights never declassify
  protected context. Exclude recipient-expanded data, private CRM/care/restricted
  sources and cross-purpose bodies/files. Do not claim semantic screening proves
  arbitrary pasted prose safe or introduce a second classification engine.”

### O04 — Voice preservation becomes invented testimony or broader model authority

- **What:** Lengthening adds facts, tone changes religious meaning, or pasted
  instructions cause retrieval, links, scripts or stronger donor-pressure claims.
- **Why:** Fluent model output and a human's source text are not truth or tool authority.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible without an evaluated narrow action/output contract.
- **Evidence:** S01, S03, S04, S10; P22 decision log 2588–2648.
- **Answer impact:** Keep useful action choices without guaranteeing perfect voice preservation.
- **Permanent fix:** Small code-owned same-source action catalog, inert typed
  output, human comparison and representative meaning/voice evaluation.
- **Exact proposed clause OC04:** “Treat the selected text as untrusted data and
  the author’s meaning/voice as the baseline. The certified action may transform
  eligible wording but cannot invent facts, testimony, quotations, doctrine or
  financial claims. No tools, browsing, retrieval, memory or write authority are
  supplied. Output remains inert proposed text; protected values/structure are
  preserved by source rules, and the author reviews before Use.”

### O05 — Applying a suggestion silently overwrites newer work or authorizes a send

- **What:** Output autosaves into a changed draft, edits an authorized issue, or
  bypasses ordinary content/send review because AI was already “approved.”
- **Why:** Suggestion acceptance, current newsletter drafting and issue sending
  bind different source facts and effects.
- **Severity:** High; Critical where the overwrite/disclosure reaches an authorized send.
- **Likelihood:** Likely with a replace-text callback against mutable editor positions.
- **Evidence:** S04–S06, S11.
- **Answer impact:** Adds a precise Use operation, not another staff approval workflow.
- **Permanent fix:** Semantic target/source digests, current reauthorization,
  expected revision, idempotent ordinary draft successor and no send port.
- **Exact proposed clause OC05:** “A suggestion remains separate until deliberate
  compare-before-Use. Use re-proves current actor/source/binding safety and exact
  target revision/selection, then creates at most one ordinary newsletter draft
  successor. Stale or partial output cannot overwrite current work. Applying AI
  text never approves, sends, schedules, publishes or changes recipient authority.”

### O06 — Turning AI off promises recall or silently changes the execution route

- **What:** Revocation returns late protected results, old jobs use replacement
  keys, or Cancel is reported as proof the provider stopped and charged nothing.
- **Why:** Local authority is enforceable before new admission; provider processing
  already begun is an external fact, not a transaction rolled back with the draft.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible with naive cancellation, retry or dynamic credential lookup.
- **Evidence:** S02–S05, S09.
- **Answer impact:** Keep simple Off/Unavailable behavior with truthful boundaries.
- **Permanent fix:** Pin binding/credential and operation identity, fence new work
  and late result access, reconcile uncertain external outcomes without fallback.
- **Exact proposed clause OC06:** “Disablement/revocation fences new governed
  invocation and application under current policy. In-flight work retains its
  original binding/credential identity and cannot silently switch provider,
  account, model, region or purpose. Late results cannot regain revoked access.
  Cancel is not a claim of provider recall, erasure or zero cost; already accepted
  draft text and independently authorized newsletter work retain their own owners.”

### O07 — A displayed budget cannot actually bound spend

- **What:** Concurrent requests exceed the cap, timeout releases a still-billable
  reservation, or “monthly limit” falsely includes unrelated provider-account use.
- **Why:** Request admission, maximum exposure, observed usage and final billing
  differ. Output-token limits and delayed provider reports do not alone prove cost bounds.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely under concurrent/unknown outcomes if accounting is check-then-call.
- **Evidence:** S02–S03 require budget/rate envelopes and safe unknown-outcome handling;
  precise provider cost mechanics require the separate provider qualification.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve tenant limits but label their real scope and evidence.
- **Permanent fix:** Atomic bounded reservation/admission, certified maximum
  billable exposure, explicit period/reset semantics and truthful reconciliation.
- **Exact proposed clause OC07:** “The feature enforces its current tenant-purpose
  envelope before dispatch, including concurrent reserved and observed exposure.
  A monetary hard cap requires a conservative certified bound covering every
  billable request component. Unknown outcomes retain uncertainty/reservation
  until reconciled; they do not permit blind retry. Rotation/re-enable cannot
  reset spend. Asym limits govern this feature's requests, not the whole external
  provider account, and usage observations create no financial posting or payment.”

### O08 — Suggestion history becomes an indefinite content or style-training store

- **What:** Raw prompts/results leak through logs or persist indefinitely; a Page
  timer is copied as supposed newsletter policy; local deletion is called provider erasure.
- **Why:** D7 drafts, transient suggestions, provider copies and durable evidence
  have distinct source and custody purposes.
- **Severity:** High; Critical for reached restricted content.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible with generic AI tracing or unqualified provider defaults.
- **Evidence:** S02–S04, S08, S10.
- **Answer impact:** Requires a bounded D18 retention decision and exact provider posture.
- **Permanent fix:** Minimized content-free evidence, private expiring bodies,
  independent ordinary-draft retention and truthful primary/provider/backup disposal.
- **Exact proposed clause OC08:** “Choose and qualify a D18-specific bounded
  source/suggestion-body lifecycle; Page D16's 24-hour rule is precedent, not
  inherited authority. Keep only necessary protected source/invocation/digest,
  action, usage and outcome evidence durably. Do not retain bodies for voice
  learning, generic search, support or telemetry. Applying text moves it only
  through ordinary D7 draft ownership. Local access denial, physical purge,
  backup expiry and provider retention/deletion are separately evidenced.”

### O09 — Broad model choices or proof shortcuts create a permanently unsafe dependency

- **What:** A tenant pastes any URL/model/key and obtains a working-looking
  feature; a generic chat completion or Page demo is called newsletter qualification.
- **Why:** A credential can be valid while the account's model, region, retention,
  language, budget behavior or newsletter source contract is unsupported.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely without purpose-specific certification and release gates.
- **Evidence:** S02–S05, S09–S11.
- **Answer impact:** Bound choices to qualified adapters without denying tenant choice
  or forcing every tenant to use one universal key/model.
- **Permanent fix:** Exact purpose capability certification, source and current
  access proof, manual continuity, provider drift/disable procedures and honest status.
- **Exact proposed clause OC09:** “Offer only currently newsletter-capable certified
  provider/model/region/data-handling profiles through the shared settings. Test,
  configured, certified, activated and successful invocation are distinct. Before
  activation prove useful authorized source-to-suggestion-to-draft behavior,
  forbidden egress, concurrent limits, stale/revoked outcomes and manual fallback.
  No provider, dependency, deployed capability or whole-P40 completion is inferred.”
