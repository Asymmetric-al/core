# D18 evidence — newsletter voice, meaning and safe writing actions

Research date: October 16, 2026 UTC. This note supplies content/voice findings for
the D18 synthesis. It is research, not a ratified amendment, model evaluation,
provider choice, production validator or activated writing feature. No model call,
real newsletter, donor record or personal writing sample was used.

## Decision and scope

**Accept with required amendments.** Optional writing assistance should help Maya
express what she already means. A small contextual menu is appropriate: **Fix
spelling & grammar, Rephrase, Make more compelling, Simplify, Lengthen, Shorten**.
Use the selected eligible newsletter wording as the voice reference. Show the
original and a suggested replacement; only **Use suggestion** creates an ordinary
draft edit. Manual editing remains complete and sending stays independent.

“Preserve my voice” is an important design goal and evaluation obligation, not a
provable promise that an arbitrary model always preserves tone or meaning. Do not
create a voice-training store, personality questionnaire, brand-profile subsystem,
donor persuasion model or automatic history retrieval to deliver this small job.
The strongest simpler alternative is manual editing with existing templates and
ordinary spelling tools; retain that complete route when AI is off, unavailable,
unsupported or unhelpful. No evidence gathered here quantifies time saved or
proves missionaries prefer model assistance for every newsletter.

## Governing repository boundaries

- [Q18's source choice](../phase28-q18-newsletter-writing-assistance-research.md)
  concerns selected newsletter text, not D15 personal email, CRM history, D17
  import files, care records, recipient targeting or translation.
- [ADR-0133](../../../../adr/0133-source-bounded-public-page-writing-assistance.md:24)
  explicitly recognizes fluent but false, religiously/culturally altered or
  stale writing suggestions. It supplies a sound comparative safety pattern:
  minimum sources, review-before-apply and a semantic target. Its accepted Page
  purpose, action catalog, translation rider and retention timer do **not**
  automatically authorize a newsletter invocation.
- [P40](../../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:5243) owns suggestion
  evidence and coordination; source owners retain acceptance. Untrusted text is
  not an instruction source, confidence is not approval, and current authority
  and source revision must be reproved.
- [P40's retained obligations](../../../../prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md:1338)
  require source-linked suggestions and human acceptance through the existing
  command, not direct canonical AI writes. The existing shared tenant AI control
  plane is the extension point, not a separate missionary key/settings service.
- [Ratified D7](../phase28-d7-adversarial-review.md:412) keeps newsletter drafting,
  content admission, recipient eligibility and dispatch distinct. Source-owned
  safe personalization, approved links and required unsubscribe/identity content
  must remain governed even when surrounding prose is edited.

These are intended owner contracts. This research did not discover or test a
qualified newsletter model adapter, live editor port or deployed provider binding.

## Six actions with precise semantics

These are proposed action contracts, not assertions of current model behavior.
All actions operate in the source language, preserve the author's perspective and
applicable meaning, use only the admitted source package and return a suggestion.

| Action and concise helper text                                            | Permitted transformation                                                                                                      | Must not change or invent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fix spelling & grammar** — “Correct errors while keeping your wording.” | Minimal spelling, punctuation and grammar corrections in a qualified source-language/spelling profile.                        | Stylistic rewrite, new claims, material tense/negation/certainty, names, doctrinal terminology or wording inside a direct quotation/Scripture passage. Ambiguous changes should be left for the author rather than guessed.                                                                                   |
| **Rephrase** — “Say the same thing another way.”                          | Alternate sentence construction and flow for the same propositions and author intent.                                         | Who did what, whose testimony it is, chronology, relationships, scope, amount/currency/period, conditionality or the strength of a claim.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Make more compelling** — “Make your message clearer and more engaging.” | Improve the opening, order, transitions and emphasis of supplied material. Clarify the existing invitation or purpose.        | Invented impact, deadlines, matching gifts, guarantees, restricted gift use, personal testimonials, beneficiary details, guilt/shame, fear, spiritual pressure or promises of blessing in exchange for giving. Do not turn an update/prayer request into a monetary ask without supplied intent.              |
| **Simplify** — “Use clearer words and shorter sentences.”                 | Reduce unnecessary jargon and syntactic complexity while retaining material distinctions.                                     | Remove caveats, flatten uncertainty, change specialized ministry meaning, infantilize readers or treat English readability formulas as a universal language-quality score.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Lengthen** — “Expand the ideas already here.”                           | Expand transitions and explanations already supported by the supplied text; preserve the existing level of factual certainty. | Add new events, conversations, statistics, future commitments, locations, people, diagnoses, quotations or Scripture. If there is not enough information, explain that adding details to the draft first will help; do not fill space with invented material or insert factual placeholders as finished copy. |
| **Shorten** — “Keep the key message with fewer words.”                    | Remove repetition and secondary wording while retaining essential message and a clear existing invitation.                    | Lose qualifiers, change who benefits, omit a material financial restriction, alter quotations or remove required newsletter structure/disclosures. If the text is already concise, return that honest result instead of pretending a meaningful reduction occurred.                                           |

“More compelling” is not itself harmful or prohibited: a clear opening and an
authentic, specific, user-supplied request are useful. The distinction is truthful
communication versus invented persuasion. The product need not moralize at the
writer or display an ethics checklist for every paragraph. These boundaries belong
in the action policy, model evaluation, source controls and concise result review.

There is no fixed promise that Lengthen doubles text or Shorten removes 50 percent.
Whitespace word counts do not generalize across scripts. Show before/after length
only with a qualified locale-aware measure, if useful; the primary outcome is
better usable wording that preserves meaning. The separate invocation token/byte
budget is a resource boundary, not a writing-quality target.

## Voice and content requirements to carry into the main decision

**VC-01 — Author control and a truthful voice promise.** “The writing helper SHALL
preserve the supplied author's perspective, intent, characteristic vocabulary and
language/spelling where compatible with the selected action. It SHALL not claim
guaranteed voice preservation. No background voice profile, prior-newsletter scan,
donor profile or third-party author imitation is required. Review and deliberate
Use remain necessary; rejection leaves the original unchanged.” Correcting grammar
can legitimately change wording. Preserve voice does not mean preserve every typo,
nor force every author into standardized promotional language.

**VC-02 — Source-limited context.** “Invocation SHALL show and bind the exact
eligible selected text and any explicitly admitted context. Selection and an AI
opt-in SHALL NOT declassify care/restricted data or consent to recipient profiling.
Do not silently append the full newsletter, other drafts, attachments, recipient
list, CRM/private prospect data or history to improve style.” If more facts are
needed, Maya can add eligible details to her draft and request again. A new
context-library or profile questionnaire is unnecessary for this initial job.

**VC-03 — Closed same-language actions.** “The six named actions SHALL use
code-owned purpose-specific semantics as defined above. Translation, citation
research, source retrieval, new financial analysis, recipient selection and
autonomous outreach are not implicit effects of a writing action. An unsupported
language or materially ambiguous mixed-language selection SHALL offer a clear
manual path or a narrower eligible selection, never silently default to English.”
Language detection can assist, but it must not silently replace authoritative
source language. Do not impose a new per-invocation language form when the source
already has a qualified unambiguous language.

**VC-04 — No fabricated facts or theological substitution.** “A suggestion SHALL
not invent or materially alter facts, attributed quotations, Scripture text or
references, financial claims, names, relationships, places, outcomes, urgency,
conditions or degrees of certainty. It SHALL preserve the author's existing
religious/cultural meaning and not supply a different doctrinal stance or spiritual
promise. Source claims remain authored claims, not AI-verified facts.” Maintaining
this rule requires representative human evaluation and visible review; a prompt
or a numerical/name scanner alone does not establish compliance.

**VC-05 — Protected structure outside generative control.** “Source-owned required
footer/identity/unsubscribe blocks, recipient-personalization tokens, links and
other protected structured nodes SHALL remain outside the model's authority to
change. The semantic editor port SHALL preserve their exact identities, types,
destinations and multiplicity, and reject unauthorized structural changes before
Use. Generated text SHALL not become executable HTML, a new link or a newly
recognized personalization directive merely because it resembles markup.”

Where existing source structure identifies a quotation/Scripture block or fixed
fact component, leave it protected or outside the selected editable prose. Do not
build a universal natural-language fact/quote parser to claim all prose is protected.
Unstructured quotations and factual qualifiers still need the model-policy and
human-review proof. If a selected sentence mixes protected and editable content,
the editor must show a bounded editable range or explain why that selection cannot
be rewritten; never flatten the whole newsletter into uncontrolled text.

**VC-06 — Honest comparison.** “Show the original and candidate together, with an
accessible change view as an aid, an unchanged-source indicator where true and a
deliberate Use suggestion action. Do not label the candidate verified, fact-checked,
approved or ready to send. The review SHALL remind the author to check meaning,
facts and voice without repetitive confirmation dialogs.” Recommended concise copy:
**“Review this suggestion. Check that the facts, meaning and voice are still yours.”**
Show the complete candidate as well as changed fragments; a character diff can
hide changed references, dropped caveats or context. Do not show a false numeric
“voice match” score or claim a high similarity score proves correctness.

**VC-07 — Stable selection and current revision.** “A suggestion SHALL bind an
editor-neutral semantic target identity, source revision and exact target digest;
raw DOM offsets or a text search SHALL NOT alone identify its destination. Apply
rechecks current authority and source consistency through the owning draft command.
A stale result SHALL not overwrite newer wording or be attached to a repeated
paragraph elsewhere merely because its text matches.” The existing owner's
concurrency contract determines whether a new comparison can safely rebase;
this note does not invent silent rebasing or a separate revision ledger.

**VC-08 — No silent quality fallback.** “Truncated, structurally invalid, wrong-
language, stale, rejected or unusable output SHALL not be applied or counted as a
successful edit. A failure preserves the original and explains the available
manual/retry path. A model refusing to expand unsupported material is an honest
no-change outcome, not a reason to invent facts or silently switch providers.”

**VC-09 — Acceptance is ordinary editing, not sending.** “Use suggestion SHALL
create only the exact admitted draft edit through the existing owner command.
It SHALL not choose recipients, update source facts, change the newsletter's
privacy/reach, publish or send. AI use SHALL not skip ordinary source safety,
preview, approval or dispatch requirements.”

**VC-10 — Evaluation by action and language.** “Before activation, qualify every
enabled action × supported language/spelling profile against representative
newsletter passages, deliberate semantic traps and real author comprehension.
Critical fabricated claims, altered protected structure, prohibited egress or
unauthorized effects in the release suite SHALL block activation of that profile.
Report case coverage, reviewer method, observed failures and limitations; no model
self-score, generic benchmark, acceptance-click rate or token-overlap score alone
qualifies meaning or voice preservation.” Zero observed critical failures in a
bounded suite is a release gate, not proof that the live probability is zero.

## Meaningful proof required before release

Use independently authored fixtures for all six actions: neutral personal update,
prayer request, gratitude, qualified financial ask, tentative plans, first-person
and shared/team authorship, names/quotes/Scripture, dates/currency/periods, anonymized
locations, sensitive context that must be refused before egress, mixed-language and
right-to-left passages, repeated paragraphs and protected links/tokens. Include
minimal source detail for Lengthen and already-concise text for Shorten.

For factual fidelity, reviewers compare each output claim with the actual admitted
input and identify unsupported additions, material omissions and changes of
certainty/actor/meaning. For voice, representative authors judge whether the
candidate retains their perspective, vocabulary, register and desired tone; record
their explanations and edits. For task usability, users must understand what text
is used, distinguish suggestion from saved draft, review/reject/apply safely and
continue manually. Assess each supported language with competent readers rather
than extrapolating English results.

The comparison may use word-level highlighting where meaningful, but full readable
original/candidate views must work for keyboard/screen-reader users, mixed direction
and small screens without color-only meaning. User tests should not require authors
to share private real newsletters with researchers when consented/redacted or
synthetic representative material can establish the specific interaction.

Monitor only residual quality after release proof: **signal** = a confirmed invented
material claim, changed protected meaning or wrong-target application; **threshold**
= any confirmed occurrence; **owner** = P28 content/product owner with the shared AI
purpose owner; **response** = disable the affected action/language/model profile,
preserve minimized evidence, add the case, correct and requalify before restoring it.
Do not disable unrelated manual newsletter authoring or infer all model outputs are
bad from one localized issue. Lower-stakes voice dissatisfaction is useful author
feedback, not a security incident or a mandate to collect all drafts automatically.

## Actual local counterexamples and limits

[probe.py](prose-probe/probe.py) ran with Python 3.12.14 using the standard library,
invented text and **zero model calls**. [results.json](prose-probe/results.json)
records **16/16 passed counterexamples**. The probe shows that matching digits,
names, word inventories, high text similarity or placeholder sets can miss material
changes; visible text misses changed URLs; raw offsets and repeated-text digests
cannot alone identify the correct semantic target; and word counts differ across
scripts. It also distinguishes a stale target from an unchanged block identity.

This proves the narrow counterexamples exist. It does **not** test a model's actual
writing quality, effectiveness, refusal rate, theology, factual accuracy, style
preservation, production editor, concurrency enforcement, accessibility, provider
privacy or throughput. Those remain explicit activation obligations. The probe is
research evidence, not a validator implementation to adopt into product code.
