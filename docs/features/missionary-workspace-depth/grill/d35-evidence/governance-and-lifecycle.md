# D35 — governed missionary continuation of an expired referral

Research date: **October 18, 2026 Asia/Bangkok**. Repository
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` and current working tree.
The founder selected **A: missionaries may deliberately continue eligible expired
referrals when the organization permits; authorized staff may still act**, and
requested simple tenant customization/disable control and adversarial review.
D34's limited history and amendments are ratified. D35's exact control defaults,
temporal semantics and detailed clauses below are **proposals for the answer
review**, not already ratified amendments or deployed capabilities.

This note changes only this research file. It does not alter formal owners,
applications, permissions, source records, D34 evidence or external systems.
No old model, probe or verifier was run. No deployment/database/provider/user
study was inspected or qualified in this lane.

## Recommendation and honest source gap

**Accept with required amendments.** The smallest useful result is one contextual
**Continue referral** action backed by P9, governed by one organization-wide
missionary-continuation setting and existing exact P12 action permissions.
It deliberately starts a new current period of work on the same logical referral,
preserving the original introduction and earlier expiry/continuation history.
It is not a browser status edit, generic Undo, new referral copy, grant-restoration
operation, new cultivation enrollment or automatic communication.

The resulting active referral may legitimately satisfy the ordinary relationship
condition used by the current authorized active view. A rule that “continuation
can never change anything readable” would make the selected return-to-active-work
outcome unnecessarily inert. Equally, setting Active cannot revive an expired or
revoked capability, widen the current field ceiling, expose protected referral
context or override channel/purpose contactability. **Current qualified active
eligibility is new source truth; old permission resurrection is not.**

The bounded source investigation has not established a complete P9 continuation
producer, an ordinary referral-window policy or its precise recipient/expiry
schema. P9 graph ownership alone cannot prove those things. The missing source
contract must explicitly support this action before activation; a generic donor
status route is not an interim implementation. No generic referral engine, new
provider, new per-entry approval queue or entire unrelated phase is necessary.

The strongest simpler alternative remains authorized staff continuation through
the same source owner. It reduces missionary action scope but adds a handoff and
does not deliver the selected self-service action. It remains usable while a
tenant disables this doorway. Neither staff nor missionary role names create
authority; all source operations need their actual current capabilities/scope.

## Exact proposed clauses

**G-C01 — Qualified actor and simple organization control.** “Offer one
organization-wide control for new missionary continuation of eligible expired
referrals. Turning it On permits use only by missionaries whose current P12
action authority and exact source/work scope allow it; it grants no capability
by itself. Staff keep independently authorized source actions. The same person
using an authorized staff context is evaluated as that context, not denied or
privileged merely because they also have a missionary identity. No override
hierarchy, separate missionary preference or per-referral approval queue is
introduced.”

“Recommend Off when no qualified deliberate setting exists; preserve an explicit
qualified value across upgrades. Record this default as a proposed D35 product
judgment, not founder-confirmed fact. Use existing P12 authority to administer
the control; continuation permission does not confer settings or grant management.”

**G-C02 — History is not action authority.** “Missionary continuation requires
source proof that the referral was legitimately within the actor's authorized
referral work, exact currently eligible responsibility/work scope, current
continuation permission and current source/privacy conditions. D34 historical
read authority alone is insufficient. Creator, prior membership, current owner
label, same email, guessed ID or possession of an old link does not authorize
the command. Preserve legitimate multiple prior recipients; do not invent
first-recipient exclusivity. Staff use their separately qualified source scope.”

This path does not transfer a referral to a different missionary/ministry. A
genuine new assignment or scope transfer needs its own qualified owner action;
it cannot be smuggled into the payload. A valid no-ministry source scope remains
usable without creating a dummy Ministry Assignment.
The action's qualified scope must admit an eligible expired source: requiring
an already active referral as a precondition would make Continue impossible.
Current action/work authority is not a requirement to fabricate a new staff
assignment or an active relationship before accepting the continuation.

**G-C03 — Source eligibility, not a generic Active edit.** “P9 must establish
the exact logical referral, prior follow-up period/revision, current ordinary
expiry and current eligible recipient/work context. Withdrawal, invalidation,
privacy revocation, relationship correction, absent provenance or erased evidence
cannot be reclassified as ordinary expiry by Continue. Previously visible fields
are not source proof. Do not require a new referrer confirmation, mandatory note
or claimed donor assent unless the actual governing source policy requires it.”

**G-C04 — Small, permanent continuation effect.** “One accepted Continue starts
one prospective current follow-up period on the same logical referral and within
the reviewed scope. Preserve the original introduction and all prior start,
expiry, correction and continuation facts. Record the actual actor, source/policy
basis and accepted outcome through P9's native transaction/audit. Do not erase an
earlier expiry, create a parallel referral graph, reuse a copied contact or turn
a historical period into a newly verified introduction.”

The semantic period/cycle is needed to reject old intents after a later expiry;
it is not a required new ledger/table or a generic state-machine framework. The
exact existing-owner storage representation remains an engineering choice.

**G-C05 — Honest window policy.** “The continued period uses one qualified
ordinary referral-window policy, prospectively anchored at the source's accepted
continuation time. Capture the policy and exact resulting boundary needed for
truthful display and future expiry. Do not reuse an already elapsed deadline,
quietly make the referral indefinite, or add a D35-specific duration hierarchy.
Missing or ambiguous governing window policy makes this command unavailable
until that source policy is qualified; it does not justify guessing a number.
D35 itself selects no numerical duration or required new-introduction evidence.”

The acceptance-time anchor and reuse of the ordinary policy are explicit
recommendations. The inspected sources do not establish an existing window value
or demonstrate that policy implemented. This is a concrete dependency, not a
claim that recording Active alone completes the lifecycle.

**G-C06 — New active eligibility without revived permissions.** “After a
successful source continuation, evaluate ordinary active-referral reads and
actions against current authority. The new source relationship condition may
make an already qualified active projection eligible. Do not restore revoked,
expired or departed grants, union historical scopes, reveal old private context
or change consent. Contact details appear only where the current active-purpose
field/source policy allows them; actual outreach still checks current channel
and purpose contactability. The D34 expired-history projection itself stays
minimal and read-only.”

“This action does not assign permissions or attest fresh contactability. Any
actual disclosure-policy widening must follow ADR-0211; one routine eligible
continuation under an already qualified policy does not require a new second
human approval merely because the actor now meets its active-row predicate.”

**G-C07 — Off is prospective command containment.** “At the authoritative
acceptance boundary, Off blocks new missionary continuation. It neither unwinds
an already accepted continuation nor re-expires active work, revokes independent
grants, removes qualified D34 history, cancels human undertakings or disables
authorized staff actions. Current source/privacy revocation still acts
independently. The UI states: ‘Stops new missionary continuations. Referrals
already continued remain active under their normal rules.’”

**G-C08 — Atomic current proof and stale intent.** “Serialize the exact current
source period/revision, source eligibility, selected work scope, current tenant
policy and actor authority with acceptance. Recheck authoritative time after
waits. Concurrent Continue, Off, withdrawal, correction, transfer, merge or safety
restriction must yield an attributable valid order; no check-then-write gap may
accept under superseded authority. A policy Off→On cycle does not validate an
old reviewed policy revision. A later explicit attempt requires fresh current
review/admission; there is no queue of denied or stale continuations.”

**G-C09 — Lost response and repeated expiry.** “One deliberate continuation
intent has at most one source effect for its exact predecessor period. Two
concurrent submissions cannot extend the window twice or create two active
copies. After success, an accepted-outcome recovery returns that same effect
under current result-disclosure rules, even if the setting is now Off. It
does not continue again. If the new period later expires, an old retry remains
the old intent; a genuinely new continuation requires new current authority
and explicit intent against that new expired period.”

“Reuse P9 expected source revisions and narrow source change/intent correlation
where qualified. Do not build a generic `crm_command_logs`/provider-idempotency
or renewal ledger contrary to A17. A change of request ID alone never converts
an obsolete source/policy review into a current accepted action.”

**G-C10 — Other work and ending states remain independent.** “Continue does not
enroll or reopen P27 cultivation, update its recorded stage, create a Request,
log a call/interaction, make a promise, create/send a newsletter or resurrect
completed/cancelled tasks. Independently valid planned/promised follow-up remains
usable without duplication. Normal referral expiry can end only requirements
owned by that source; it cannot cancel an independent human undertaking.
Any later source-owned next-action integration must preserve shared task meaning
and exact effect identity.”

**G-C11 — Correction, identity and cardinality.** “Correcting an erroneous
expiry and deliberately continuing a valid expiry remain different commands.
The former corrects source facts under correction authority; the latter records
a genuine later period of work. Party merges, aliases, new row IDs and changed
assignees preserve logical source lineage and cannot reset command deduplication.
Reconcile already-active source state and source uniqueness through P9; do not
mint another active copy or merge unrelated introductions by name/date. A stale
command cannot rewrite newer responsibility or corrected history.”

**G-C12 — Retention, departure and migration.** “Continuation cannot resurrect
erased, held-only or currently undisclosable material or reset retention of old
referral bodies. Active current work and historical evidence each follow their
actual source retention purpose; D27/D28 preparation rules are not borrowed.
Departure/suspension/revocation ends the affected authority. Return, reassignment
or account/Party merge alone restores no old grant or active period. Before
rollout, qualify retained source identities, historic work scopes and expiry
precision; do not turn every imported Expired label or ended edge into an
actionable referral. Preserve explicit settings and accepted source facts
through upgrade/containment; no old endpoint may bypass the new contract.”

**G-C13 — Simple and truthful interaction.** “Place Continue referral on an
eligible authorized source entry, with a short description of returning it to
active work and the source-confirmed resulting window where available. Use the
normal shared action feedback/recovery pattern, without a separate renewal
dashboard, mandatory essay or second staff approval. Success confirms the
accepted state; a timeout is not success. An already accepted result remains
recoverable without a second action. If source or permission no longer allows
continuation, provide the permitted truthful outcome without hidden-detail
leakage, direct-database repair or a fabricated successful contact.”

**G-C14 — Owner reconciliation before activation.** “Formally reconcile the
P9 continuation source/window/cycle contract, P12 exact action and setting scope,
P3/P10 active-view disclosure policy, P28 consumer purpose and the relevant
OpenSpec/current-control views before activation. Prove actual transaction,
authorization, retry, migration, disclosure and whole-user outcomes. A selected
actor, plausible mock or finite counterexample model is not qualification.
Failure of this bounded capability does not block unrelated native daily work.”

## Minimal lifecycle and control outcomes

| Event                                                                       | Required outcome                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ordinary expiry, no action permission or tenant Off                         | D34 history may remain readable when independently permitted; no continuation occurs.                                         |
| Eligible missionary deliberately continues while On                         | One accepted source continuation, new current period and truthful current active projection.                                  |
| Authorized staff continue while missionary control is Off                   | Staff's independently qualified source action remains available; no role-label shortcut or actor disguise.                    |
| Source/policy/authority changes before acceptance                           | Re-evaluate at acceptance; reject obsolete intent without a hidden queued continuation.                                       |
| Source continuation commits, response is lost, tenant subsequently goes Off | Recover the accepted outcome under current disclosure; do not undo it or create another cycle.                                |
| Two missionaries/staff attempt the same expired period                      | P9 accepts one coherent source effect; the other gets the current permitted result/conflict, never a second window extension. |
| Off→On with an old dialog or command                                        | Require fresh current review/intent; the old policy revision cannot authorize the new request.                                |
| A continued period expires again                                            | Retain both periods; only a new deliberate current command can continue it again. Old success retries remain old.             |
| Current source permits active view but consent forbids email                | Permitted current record/work may be visible; prohibited email remains unavailable. No fabricated consent or send.            |
| Withdrawal, invalidation, privacy loss, departure or erasure                | Follow the actual current source/access/disposition owner; Continue is not their reversal.                                    |
| Mistaken expiry corrected by its owner                                      | Record the correction as correction, not a fabricated new pursuit cycle or interaction.                                       |

## Material findings for the main 22-category review

Likelihood is a qualitative design-risk assessment, not measured production
incidence. Each finding supplies all requested dimensions and exact clauses;
proof items are requirements, not claims of tests already executed.

### G01 — Read history becomes a self-issued action or grant

- **What:** D34 visibility, prior recipient status or a missionary role is treated
  as permission to continue and restore former contact fields.
- **Why:** viewing an expired record is intentionally narrower than changing
  its lifecycle; source facts cannot mint revoked permission.
- **Severity / likelihood:** Critical / plausible with a generic status writer.
- **Evidence:** S02, S03, S06, S09.
- **Answer impact:** A needs a separately qualified action and current projection.
- **Permanent fix:** exact source/action scope and current active-view evaluation.
- **Exact proposed clauses:** G-C02, G-C03, G-C06.
- **Proof:** history-only caller fails Continue; authorized actor succeeds without
  revived grants/private context; legitimate current active fields remain useful.

### G02 — Tenant control is either ineffective or unnecessarily elaborate

- **What:** browser-only Off can be bypassed; “customizable” becomes multiple
  switch layers; D33's default is falsely called founder-selected for D35.
- **Why:** settings must govern admission without confusing users or overriding
  independently qualified staff work.
- **Severity / likelihood:** High for bypass, Medium for complexity / plausible.
- **Evidence:** founder direction, S06, S12; no selected exception hierarchy.
- **Answer impact:** select one minimal proposed organization control and label
  its recommended default accurately.
- **Permanent fix:** existing P12 administration, one source admission condition,
  explicit defaults/upgrade meaning and no per-entry approval.
- **Exact proposed clauses:** G-C01, G-C07, G-C13.
- **Proof:** Off blocks API/privileged missionary admission; staff authority and
  already accepted work survive; initial/upgrade controls reflect exact policy.

### G03 — “Active” hides an undefined future expiry

- **What:** a status flip reuses an elapsed deadline, silently creates indefinite
  pursuit, or invents an arbitrary renewal number/fresh-introduction rule.
- **Why:** the selected action must produce a coherent real current window; the
  inspected source does not establish that producer or policy.
- **Severity / likelihood:** High / high if the gap is left as an opaque boolean.
- **Evidence:** S01–S05, S15; D34 explicitly reserved the expiry policy.
- **Answer impact:** accept actor direction with a concrete source-policy gate;
  do not falsely claim the complete continuation lifecycle exists.
- **Permanent fix:** reuse a qualified ordinary window policy prospectively,
  preserving actual accepted boundary and prior periods.
- **Exact proposed clauses:** G-C03, G-C04, G-C05, G-C14.
- **Proof:** actual resulting boundary is valid and displayed truthfully; no
  elapsed or missing policy accidentally yields active indefinite access.

### G04 — Retrying a prior continuation silently renews a later period

- **What:** lost response, two concurrent clicks, changed request ID or replay
  after the next expiry produces another continuation.
- **Why:** financial-style provider keys or client double-submit prevention do
  not establish source-period identity; duplicate renewals can extend access.
- **Severity / likelihood:** High / plausible in ordinary weak-network use.
- **Evidence:** P9 A17 S05, P12 current source proof S06, D34 stale result rules.
- **Answer impact:** requires source/cycle-aware command recovery, not a new
  generic journal.
- **Permanent fix:** expected source/policy revisions, one narrow accepted outcome
  correlation and preserved logical referral lineage.
- **Exact proposed clauses:** G-C04, G-C08, G-C09, G-C11.
- **Proof:** concurrent attempts yield one new period; accepted lost-response
  recovery stays one effect after Off or later re-expiry; a new legitimate cycle
  remains possible through a new current explicit intent.

### G05 — Off or re-enable mutates accepted work or revives stale intent

- **What:** Off re-expires current referrals/cancels work, or On later executes
  formerly rejected commands automatically.
- **Why:** configuration of new admission is different from historical source
  truth and ongoing lawful responsibilities.
- **Severity / likelihood:** High / plausible with mutable boolean-only gating.
- **Evidence:** S02, S06, S11; D33's scoped prospective-control precedent S12.
- **Answer impact:** proposed temporal semantics must be explicit, not inherited
  silently from unrelated controls.
- **Permanent fix:** prospective acceptance fence and policy-revision freshness;
  preserve accepted outcomes and independent obligations.
- **Exact proposed clauses:** G-C07–G-C10.
- **Proof:** both race orders of Continue/Off, stale On→Off→On, and accepted
  result recovery behave as specified without background resubmission.

### G06 — Continuing a referral creates another relationship workflow

- **What:** continuation auto-enrolls/reopens P27 cultivation, changes a stage,
  creates duplicate tasks, logs a call or implies donor agreement.
- **Why:** referral source state, cultivation intent, actual interaction and
  shared human undertaking are different owner contracts.
- **Severity / likelihood:** High / plausible if generic status hooks are reused.
- **Evidence:** S01, S07, S08, S11.
- **Answer impact:** A authorizes the referral action only.
- **Permanent fix:** source-specific effect list; preserve independently valid
  work and require explicit owner actions for other domains.
- **Exact proposed clauses:** G-C04, G-C10, G-C13.
- **Proof:** source state changes once with audit but no interaction, send,
  enrollment, automatic next-step duplicate or resurrected consumed child.

### G07 — Reassignment, multi-hat context or merge widens scope

- **What:** current ownership transfers old recipient authority, a staff/missionary
  label bypasses Off, or a merge/new ID resets source history and deduplication.
- **Why:** current context, action authority and canonical business identity must
  remain separate from presentation labels and technical row IDs.
- **Severity / likelihood:** Critical / plausible in legacy actor/assignment paths.
- **Evidence:** S04, S06, S08, S14.
- **Answer impact:** narrow the action to current qualified scope without
  first-recipient exclusivity or an all-missionaries grant.
- **Permanent fix:** trusted active context, exact source lineage and current
  handoff/identity owner rules; preserve independent staff authority.
- **Exact proposed clauses:** G-C01–G-C03, G-C08, G-C11–G-C12.
- **Proof:** legitimate prior handoff and no-ministry context work; guessed scope,
  another tenant, invalid actor hat, merge/alias or unrelated reassignment fails.

### G08 — Policy widening approval is skipped or imposed on every action

- **What:** the new action expands read policy without governance, or a routine
  continuation under a qualified policy requires another staff approval.
- **Why:** both misread ADR-0211; one undermines exposure control and the other
  recreates the staff handoff the selected action is meant to remove.
- **Severity / likelihood:** High for unauthorized widening, Medium for friction /
  plausible at the new source/access seam.
- **Evidence:** S08–S10, especially ADR-0211:37.
- **Answer impact:** keep source-policy qualification and ordinary command
  authorization distinct.
- **Permanent fix:** actual widening follows existing maker-checker; eligible
  commands resolve current policy without becoming policy amendments.
