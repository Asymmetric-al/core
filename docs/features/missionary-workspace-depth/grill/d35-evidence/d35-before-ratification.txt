**Disposition: Accept with required amendments.**

**A and tenant customization/disable are selected. The corrected decision and
required amendments below await explicit ratification.** Reviewed October 18,
2026 Asia/Bangkok against `9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` and its

## Exact corrected decision to record

> **D35 — Optional missionary continuation of an eligible expired referral.**
> Authorized staff can enable or disable **Allow missionaries to continue
> expired referrals** through one organization-wide control. It starts Off for
> new or unconfigured tenants; upgrades preserve an existing explicit qualified
> choice. Existing permissions determine which missionaries may act and within
> which scope. No second permission hierarchy or per-project setting matrix is
> introduced. Authorized staff retain their independently governed action rights.
>
> When enabled and currently authorized, Maya can deliberately choose
> **Continue referral** for an eligible, ordinarily expired referral. The
> source returns that same referral lineage to active work through one new
> follow-up cycle, preserving the original introduction and prior expiry/history.
> The effect and any source-defined new window are explained before action.
> Unknown or unqualified window terms cannot be replaced with an invented
> deadline or an Active label. No universal duration is selected here.
>
> Use one contextual action with clear feedback. No routine approval per item,
> mandatory explanation, new referrer-reconfirmation form or duplicate task is
> required by this decision. A source-required prerequisite remains binding and
> must be explained honestly. Success is shown only after authoritative
> acceptance; the current permitted view shows where the work is now. If the response is lost,
> safe recovery finds the original outcome without continuing it a second time.
>
> Turning the control Off stops new missionary continuations at acceptance.
> It does not undo accepted continuation, expire active work, remove otherwise
> permitted history, cancel independent follow-up or revoke staff's separate
> authority. Re-enabling never submits or replays an old rejected or stale action.
> Tenants also retain their ordinary controls over staff and missionary action
> grants; the missionary setting is not a global CRM shutdown switch.
>
> Continuation is not contact consent, a donor response, a new introduction,
> cultivation reopening or a completed interaction. It does not send anything,
> revive old tasks or restore revoked grants or protected historical content.
> Current policy is evaluated anew for active-work fields: a legitimate active
> relationship can satisfy an existing current read condition, but cannot bypass
> privacy or contact restrictions. Withdrawal, invalidation and privacy
> revocation cannot be bypassed by calling them ordinary expiry.
>
> Preserve source identity, current responsibility, prior-cycle evidence and
> applicable retention. Do not restart old retention deadlines or restore erased
> bodies. A genuine new source cycle follows its own qualified lifecycle rules.
> Retrying, competing actors and an old response after another expiry produce no
> duplicate cycle or revived side effects. Correction remains a separate
> authorized source action, not a generic Undo.

The founder explicitly selected missionary continuation and requested tenant
control. The one-setting shape and initial Off default are **proposed product
judgments**, not claimed founder-selected defaults or measured industry norms.
They make optional authorization expansion deliberate while preserving current
staff work. The feature being disabled is missionary self-service. Tenants can
also restrict staff action grants through normal permission administration; no
additional all-CRM switch is necessary to provide that control.

**Strongest alternative: authorized staff only.** It keeps missionary authority
narrower and can suit an organization that wants centralized judgment. It adds a
handoff whenever Maya wants to resume otherwise eligible work. The proposed Off
default preserves this operating choice; enabling qualified missionary action
removes the routine handoff without creating an approval queue.

**Existing generic contact-status editing** is an unsuitable shortcut: it mixes
person-wide status with a particular referral cycle and lacks the necessary
history and retry meaning. **A required approval or explanation for every
continuation** adds friction unsupported by this selection or the researched
examples. **A general renewal engine, automatic renewal or a switch matrix**
solves speculative workflows. The permanent solution is one native source action
consumed by the existing list and controlled through existing tenant governance.

An accepted continuation preserves one logical referral lineage and records a
new active follow-up cycle without rewriting the original introduction or
prior expiry. This is a **semantic requirement**, not a prescribed new table,
event-sourced architecture or reuse of a particular graph row. The P9 owner must
reconcile its exact representation with canonical time-bound edge/history rules.

| Effective case                                                                                              | Correct outcome                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ordinary expired cycle, enabled missionary lane, current scoped action authority and qualified source terms | One accepted continuation of that cycle.                                                                                                                   |
| Eligible independent staff action while missionary setting Off                                              | Staff's own current source/action authority determines acceptance; no fabricated missionary bypass or role-string shortcut.                                |
| Off, missing grant, ineligible source or unknown window terms before acceptance                             | No new missionary continuation. Offer only permitted status/error information.                                                                             |
| Two actors or different requests target the same expired cycle                                              | At most one transition; the other sees a current permitted outcome/conflict, never a second cycle.                                                         |
| Accepted transition response is lost, then setting turns Off                                                | Recovery may disclose the original outcome under current read authority, without executing the action again.                                               |
| Definitive denial, then time-only expiry, Off→On or grant restored                                          | Old command stays stale/no-effect; current deliberate review is needed for a new attempt.                                                                  |
| Original accepted command arrives after its new active cycle later expires                                  | Recover its original result, not another continuation. A new cycle requires a new deliberate command tied to current source facts.                         |
| Withdrawal, invalidation, privacy revocation, reassignment or identity correction wins first                | Re-evaluate exact eligibility; never coerce an adverse state into ordinary expiry.                                                                         |
| Accepted continuation followed by a valid later restriction/correction                                      | Preserve acceptance history while current state/access follows the later authoritative change. Do not display stale success as a current active guarantee. |
| Read, filter, login, import, message or timer                                                               | No continuation.                                                                                                                                           |

Core invariants: exact trusted tenant and source scope; one accepted continuation
per expired cycle; no replay into a later cycle; original performer/history
preserved; no generic grant restoration; no contact/send effect; one source of
current state; no old retention reset or erased-body resurrection; independent
staff authority and valid unrelated work remain independent.

## Exact required amendments

These are proposed decision/design amendments for later authorized owner
reconciliation, not formal source contracts or implementation in this turn.

**C01 — Scope and organization control.** Offer the optional missionary
continuation lane through one organization-wide **Allow missionaries to continue
expired referrals** control, initially Off for new/unconfigured tenants. Preserve
existing explicit qualified choices during upgrades. Existing P12 grants control
eligible actors/scopes; no per-ministry setting matrix, custom role engine or
missionary personal toggle is introduced.

**C02 — Independent staff authority.** Off blocks new missionary-lane acceptance,
not independently authorized staff action, current reads or accepted active work.
Staff remain subject to their own tenant/source/action grants, which tenants may
restrict through ordinary permission administration. A caller-controlled role,
surface or mode SHALL NOT convert a missionary request to staff authority.

**C03 — Exact eligible source and responsibility.** Admit only a currently
source-qualified ordinary expired referral and exact current work scope and
responsibility. D34 history visibility, authorship, former assignment, same
email, a guessed identifier or a generic archived/ended status is insufficient.
A historical recipient may read history yet lack current action responsibility.
Use qualified work scope without manufacturing a ministry or cultivation record.
The action purpose must admit eligible expired inputs: requiring an already-
active referral before Continue is circular. Do not create a fake relationship
or staff assignment to satisfy that prerequisite.

**C04 — One continuing lineage and truthful history.** Continue the same logical
referral through one new active cycle, preserving original introduction, prior
expiry/cycles, corrections and attribution. Do not create another Party, merge
same-name referrals, clear historical dates or overwrite provenance. The source
owner SHALL qualify the representation against canonical relationship/history
invariants; this decision does not prescribe a new archive or table.

**C05 — Qualified new-window terms.** Before acceptance, the source must determine
the valid new active-window terms under an explicit current policy and disclose
their permitted user-relevant effect before action. No old expired deadline,
invented duration, ambiguous Active flag or silent perpetual extension is valid.
D35 selects no universal duration, recurrence limit or mandatory fresh-referrer
evidence. The missing exact window/eligibility policy remains an explicit next
source/product decision and a gate for this action only, not all workspace work.

**C06 — Current authority at acceptance.** Derive tenant, actor, attribution and
eligible responsibility from trusted server context. Recheck source/cycle,
relevant policy/grant revisions, current time, privacy and window terms at the
qualified atomic acceptance boundary after waits. A relevant change ordered
before acceptance that removes required continuation eligibility or authority
prevents the effect; otherwise re-evaluate under current qualified terms. An
unrelated read-field/grant change is not itself grounds to cancel still-
authorized continuation. An accepted action is not silently rolled back when
the setting later changes.

**C07 — One atomic native effect.** Commit the source state/revision, required
audit/activity and minimal accepted intent/outcome correlation together under
P9 A17. At most one continuation may consume
the same expired cycle, including distinct requests and staff/missionary races.
Do not rely on disabling a button, dedupe scans or a transport retry key alone.
Any required secondary projection receives its minimal durable intent with the
source transaction; replay projects that accepted result, never re-executes
Continue. External I/O does not belong inside the source transaction.
Use the existing native source boundary; no generic provider gate, CRM command
journal, donation idempotency header or new workflow service is introduced.

**C08 — Recovery across policy and later cycles.** Bind intent/outcome recovery
to trusted tenant, actor, action and the exact source/cycle/context and reviewed
relevant revisions. Reusing correlation with different reviewed intent conflicts.
Persist
minimal correlation with qualified source transition/change evidence. A retry
of accepted intent recovers that outcome without mutation; a stale or definitively
rejected intent cannot later gain effect through policy/grant restoration or
time alone making its old source eligible. Preserve that terminal no-effect
guarantee through minimal qualified source-owned outcome correlation or an
equivalent proven source safeguard; a new deliberate action needs a fresh intent.
Do not create business records for unauthorized or nonexistent-source probes. An old
accepted retry cannot open a later expired cycle. Return only currently permitted
result information, preserving original performer attribution.
Recovery evidence follows its qualified retention rules. Preserve terminal
outcome evidence through the intent's admissible lifetime, or prove a source-owned
expiry/revision fence permanently rejects that old intent after evidence retires.
If evidence is lawfully unavailable, return a safe unavailable/unconfirmed outcome
without re-executing the command or inventing a new intent. No arbitrary TTL or
permanent recovery ledger is implied.

**C09 — Clear retry and state feedback.** Distinguish success, current already-
continued state, definitive rejection and unknown transport outcome. Never
report Active optimistically or treat lost response as certain failure. Safe
Check status/retry must recover the original intent before any new attempt.
After later source changes, show current state plus permitted historical outcome
without claiming an earlier success still means Active now.

**C10 — Read/action/contact separation.** History admission does not grant
continuation; continuation does not grant consent, waive Never Ask or restore
revoked permissions. A legitimate active source relationship may satisfy an
existing current read predicate; evaluate the post-action projection anew under
all current field/source/privacy rules. Do not freeze the active view to the
expired projection or blindly restore old contact fields/private context.

**C11 — No implied cross-domain effects.** No send, donor response, interaction,
goal credit, receipt, money, new introduction, cultivation reopening, automatic
task or revival of completed/cancelled work follows solely from Continue.
Required source continuation audit/activity is not an outreach interaction.
Preserve independently valid promised follow-up; other source effects require
their own explicit qualified contract.

**C12 — Adverse states and corrections.** Withdrawal, invalidation, privacy
revocation, retention loss, changed scope/responsibility and identity merges
require current source evaluation. They cannot be bypassed through Continue.
Another active edge or source-distinct introduction cannot be silently merged
or treated as this intent's acceptance to satisfy active logical uniqueness.
Use the qualified source collision/reconciliation outcome.
Mistaken expiry/continuation correction remains a separate authorized source
operation with preserved history; no generic Undo or role-based override is
introduced. Multiple referrals remain distinct even for the same Party.

**C13 — Database and privileged-path equivalence.** Qualify exact grants, RLS,
views, functions/RPCs, service-role paths and direct-table routes. Old-row and
new-state constraints must preserve tenant-aware relationships, valid cycle
state and trusted actor attribution; an allowed update cannot move the source
to another tenant/owner or smuggle a forbidden state. Check actual USING and
effective WITH CHECK, invoker/definer ownership and grants; omitted WITH CHECK
or SECURITY DEFINER alone is not proof of a bypass. No money column is added.

**C14 — Minimized disclosure and all egress.** Register/classify action-review
and result projections through ADR-0209 and use the shared current resolver.
Do not expose a broad source DTO, hidden refusal reason, referrer identity,
private note, contact details or new owner through denial/success/recovery.
Counts, search, cursors, caches, Realtime, logs, exports and generated artifacts
retain their existing qualified purposes. D35 grants no new export/AI/offline
roster privilege. Preserve P12 denied/missing uniformity, including its required
statistical timing proof.

**C15 — Response and context fences.** Re-evaluate current disclosure at the
server release boundary; revocation before release blocks unpermitted result
fields even if the action previously committed. Do not undo that accepted action
to simplify the response. Controlled clients discard known-obsolete tenant/
context/revision results and evict forbidden content. Later revocation cannot
recall bytes already lawfully released or external copies.

**C16 — Retention and deletion.** Preserve lawfully required source history and
applicable erasure/hold rules. Continuation SHALL NOT reset old personal-data
retention deadlines or restore erased/held-only bodies. A genuine new source
cycle may have its own qualified retention trigger; do not mistake this for
permission to retain old content. Restore and projection rebuild reapply current
privacy, erasure and cycle state before serving. D27/D28 preparation rules are
not referral rules.

**C17 — Minimal configurable UX.** Use the existing settings and referral list.
Show a clear contextual Continue referral action only where currently available,
with concise effects/window terms and source-required prerequisites. Require no
routine modal, explanation, approval-per-item or new referrer form. Preserve
existing settings-save semantics and truthful effective/pending policy state.
No new archive, queue, bulk continuation or switch hierarchy is selected.

**C18 — Accessible result and recovery journey.** Preserve exact shared Base UI/
base-maia components, semantic tokens, keyboard/touch support and WCAG 2.2 AA
outcomes. Prove focus after a disappearing row, non-colour state, useful live
feedback, persistent permitted outcome or recovery information, 320 CSS-pixel
reflow, zoom, international
names and source-accurate localized dates. Essential errors/help must not depend
on hover or a disappearing toast. No offline automatic privileged submission.

**C19 — Source policy and configuration governance.** Use existing authorized
tenant configuration and scoped action grants, with a relevant effective
revision, including a stable governed absent/unconfigured state. Do not generate
a new default revision on every read or treat absence as permission. Enabling
alone does not grant every missionary authority. Policy
narrowing, stale settings saves and concurrent enabling obey existing owner
ordering. Policy saves atomically commit the selected value/revision, required
audit and source-owned outcome. Policy save and continuation acceptance share a
stable authoritative ordering fence, including absent-policy/first-enable races;
stale saves conflict. Actual projection-policy widening requires ADR-0211; ordinary source
continuation under already-qualified policy does not acquire routine distinct-
human approval merely because a current read predicate now becomes true.

**C20 — Operations and audit.** Preserve source lifecycle history and identifiers-
only actor/policy/transition evidence separate from technical diagnostics and
outreach metrics. Record original performer and any separate recovery observer
truthfully. Operators must diagnose unavailable policy/source, unknown outcomes
and projection disagreement through existing tools, without copying private
bodies, direct DB repair or creating another continuation.

**C21 — Performance and quantitative proof.** Use bounded indexed exact-source
operations and shared permission/list contracts. Contend at the affected source
and relevant authorization boundary rather than serializing every tenant or
loading all referral history. Measure exact query/page/latency/recovery budgets
with production-shaped tenant and concurrency fixtures; inherit applicable P12
budgets and test boundary values. Do not invent throughput or use cache freshness
as authority. Avoid per-row network authorization fan-out.

**C22 — Retained data, rollout and containment.** Census actual old statuses,
referral identity/cycle/provenance, permissions and policy. Default unconfigured
missionary continuation Off; preserve explicit qualified choices. Do not turn
old ended/imported rows into eligible expiry or infer grants by role. Qualify
mixed-version routes, raw grants, restores and old clients before activation.
Contain new missionary acceptance without deleting history or reversing already
accepted work; rollback must not reintroduce broad legacy status mutation.

**C23 — Dependencies and unresolved scope.** Reuse native P9/P12/P10/P4 and shared
UI/API sources. Qualify the exact continuation, window/eligibility, correction
and recovery contracts before activating affected behavior. Missing support gates
that lane, not independent staff/missionary work. No external CRM, AI, Studio,
email provider or new package owns this transition. Do not silently settle the
separate window-policy choice, infer consent or import cultivation reopening.

**C24 — Traceability and honest proof.** Carry selected D35, glossary and exact
clauses through the later authorized owner reconciliation, OpenSpec/design/tasks/
tickets, implementation tests and release evidence. Preserve original research.
Distinguish selected, ratified, integrated, implemented and qualified states.
Finite examples and vendor documentation are not real RLS, concurrency,
accessibility, usability or production proof.
