# D36 — organization-set renewed referral periods

Research date: **October 18, 2026 Asia/Bangkok**. Every primary source below was freshly opened on October 18, 2026. The founder selected **A: authorized staff configure a finite renewed-referral period; Maya sees the resulting end date without choosing an individual date**. This note supplies evidence and recommendations for the answer review. It does not ratify additional amendments, select a numerical duration, prove runtime behavior or change earlier evidence.

## Finding

The inspected missionary documentation recognizes expired referrals but supplies **no established numerical renewal period, default or range**. General CRM documentation demonstrates configurable clocks, but those clocks often serve different purposes and reset for actions that must not silently extend Core's referral work. A display filter, inactivity warning, assignment deadline, deleted-record recovery period and personal-data retention policy are not interchangeable with a renewed follow-up window.

The practical recommendation is one clear organization setting with one supported unit, a visible resulting end date at continuation, and no missionary date form. Use an existing qualified organization value where one exists; otherwise require a deliberate staff value rather than presenting an invented number as a ministry standard. Exact day semantics and supported bounds still require owner qualification. This is design judgment informed by the evidence, not measured ministry practice.

## Keep the clocks separate

| Clock or state                            | Its purpose                                                                           | D36 boundary                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Renewed follow-up window                  | Defines the finite active period resulting from the accepted deliberate continuation. | The selected organization setting governs this purpose only.                                                           |
| Original referral date and earlier cycles | Preserve when the introduction and previous source events actually occurred.          | A new active period does not rewrite the original date or earlier expiry.                                              |
| Next personal task or appointment         | States when a specific intended action is due or scheduled.                           | Editing it does not extend the referral window; expiry does not automatically erase independently valid promised work. |
| Inactivity/recency indicator              | Summarizes time since a defined activity or update.                                   | No silent renewal through editing, viewing, a note, gift or message.                                                   |
| Grant/contact permission duration         | Governs whether an actor may read, act or contact in the current context.             | A future follow-up end date is not a guarantee that access/contact permission lasts until then.                        |
| Retention/recovery period                 | Governs lawful storage, erasure or recovery of particular evidence/data.              | Neither expires nor extends merely because this operational period is configured or continued.                         |

These distinctions do not require six user-facing timers or a new policy screen. The ordinary user needs the relevant end date and truthful current capability. The owning contracts retain the other meanings.

## Single-unit, default and range recommendation

**Unit:** prefer one positive whole-number **days** setting, rather than a days/weeks/months selector or a business-hours calendar builder. This is a simplicity recommendation, not a benchmark requirement. A field label such as **Renewed follow-up period (days)** makes both purpose and unit explicit. Whole-number entry avoids fractional-day formatting and conversion choices; the domain still needs a precise definition of a day.

**Time meaning:** do not silently treat calendar days, elapsed 24-hour periods and business days as equivalent. The source owner must qualify one interpretation, the start event, boundary precision and any required calendar/timezone. Reuse an appropriate existing qualified calendar contract where possible. Do not infer a timezone from private location data, add an individual date picker or make staff configure holidays solely for this feature. Show the actual source-computed end date, and the time/zone where necessary to avoid a false end-of-day promise. Numerical entry alone is not sufficient temporal qualification.

**Default:** if a qualified organization renewal value already exists, show its saved value. If none exists, recommend **Not configured** with a clear setup action; do not silently save a number taken from another product's examples or filters. A missing required period blocks the affected continuation path, not independent permitted workspace work. The existing D35 missionary Off/On control is separate; no value such as zero or blank means both Off and Never expires. Reusing an initial-referral setting is not already selected and must not be assumed.

**Range:** use a finite supported positive-integer range and reject unsupported values without rounding, truncation, clamping or silently changing units. Under the proposed whole-days unit, the positive lower bound is one day; that is mathematical input validity, not evidence that one day is a suitable ministry policy. No inspected source justifies a particular upper bound. The exact product/engineering maximum must be declared and qualified before activation, with the same value in form help, source validation and date computation. Do not derive it from storage precision alone or present it as a ministry standard. This note deliberately does not invent that maximum.

**Saving/editing:** retain normal explicit settings Save/Cancel behavior and distinguish saved value from an unsaved draft. Show scope and the effect of an accepted change. A prospective-only change is the simplest recommendation for already accepted cycles, but the founder answer itself did not select retrospective behavior; the main review must make any such amendment explicit. An invalid draft must not accidentally overwrite the saved policy or prevent an independently authorized D35 Off action. Changing the period must not itself continue anything.

All recommendations should fit existing organization permissions and [shared Base UI/base-maia controls](../../../../../packages/ui/AGENTS.md:19). No separate renewal-rule engine, per-missionary override, automatic extension or additional notification system is justified by these sources.

## Minimal proposed journey and copy

Authorized staff see one saved organization period in the existing referral settings context, enter a supported value and save. Suggested helper: **“Used when an eligible expired referral is continued.”** Add the approved calendar or policy-change meaning where material, using a short nearby explanation rather than a dense hint. Do not display a guessed impact count when the exact qualified query is unavailable.

Maya sees the existing **Continue referral** action, with a source-computed statement such as **“New follow-up period ends [date and required time/zone].”** The bracket is a placeholder for the actual permitted result, not a proposed numerical duration. No editable date control is offered under selected A. Material terms that change before acceptance must not be silently substituted for what she reviewed. The owner must distinguish an illustrative preview from binding accepted terms and ensure the wording matches.

After confirmed acceptance, say **“Referral continued”** and show the accepted period/current permitted state. Unknown outcomes retain D35's honest recovery; a changed setting, later expiry or retry cannot be treated as a fresh continuation. Reading or accepting a displayed period does not send anything, log an interaction, restore consumed tasks or grant contact consent.

Validation should state the actual issue: **“Enter a whole number of days”** or **“Enter a period within the supported range shown.”** Once a numerical maximum is qualified, name it directly rather than hiding it behind a generic error. Keep the unit in the accessible label, preserve safe input, and check phone keypad entry, keyboard focus and screen-reader error association in the actual Core components. These are proposed checks, not a conformance result.

## Strongest alternative and evidence limits

The strongest flexibility alternative was **optional individual date adjustment within organization limits**, with the organization's proposed date prefilled. It would not require Maya to choose a fresh date on every use. It could accommodate case-specific timing, but no inspected source or actual user research establishes that need for Core. It introduces date-setting authority, an editable control and additional boundary explanations.

Selected A is practical because it gives the organization control while keeping the missionary's action short and consistent. Its tradeoff is real: a particular piece of work cannot receive a different end date through this ordinary continuation action. Do not disguise that tradeoff by adding an unselected staff-per-record override, a separate task-date loophole or a hidden advanced date field. Later evidence may justify a distinct amendment; this research does not make it.

Focused qualification should cover invalid/missing configuration, end-date computation around calendar boundaries, a policy change between preview and acceptance, unchanged accepted cycles under the selected edit policy, unrelated edits not extending the window, permission ending before the window, and retention ending independently. The result must remain understandable on a phone without requiring knowledge of internal policy versions. These are future proof needs, not an executable model or completed test.
