# D36 — renewal-period ownership and temporal evidence

Research date: **October 18, 2026 Asia/Bangkok**. Repository
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` plus the current working tree.
This is a research-only owner note. D35 and its amendments are ratified. The
founder has selected D36 A: authorized staff configure a finite renewed period;
Maya sees its end date and does not choose a date for each continuation. The
precise temporal/configuration clauses below are **proposed amendments**, not
already ratified details, formal owner changes, runtime qualification or activation.
The [preserved original Q36](q36-before-answer.txt) is historical evidence of
the unanswered question; the current Q36 note records the selected answer.

No earlier model, probe or verifier was run. No application, formal owner,
OpenSpec, schema, ticket, external service or earlier evidence was changed.

## Conclusion and simplest permanent path

Accept A with the narrow owner amendments below. Use one organization-wide
positive whole-number **Renewed referral period (days)** in the existing referral
settings. Recommend one day mean **exactly 86,400 elapsed seconds**, anchored at
the authoritative acceptance of Continue after required waits and serialization.
This is a D36 product/engineering recommendation, not an inherited referral rule.
It avoids a second calendar, business-day selector, timezone policy and per-item
date override. Keep the D35 missionary On/Off control separate: Off is not zero
days or an absent period, and an invalid unsaved number must not prevent Off.

Capture the accepted start, exact expiry, number of days and relevant policy
revision once through the native P9 boundary. Subsequent changes affect future
accepted continuations only. An ordinary staff use of the same Continue action
should use the same period; separately authorized corrections remain distinct.
The shared staff terms are a proposed consistency amendment, not a new staff
authority inferred from the founder's answer about Maya.

No organization duration, maximum of 365 days, universal default, initial-referral
period, indefinite option or recurrence limit is selected. The implementation
must publish and enforce a supported finite integer range and checked timestamp
representation before activation; a mathematical finite value alone does not
prove that it is supported. Missing/unqualified configuration gates this source
command, not independently qualified source actions, current reads or accepted work.

The strongest simpler alternative is a fixed Asym-wide duration. It removes a
setting but contradicts the chosen organization control and lacks evidence for
any particular number. The former B date adjustment is now unselected; do not
restore it through an advanced panel or an assumed staff override. No-build is
honest temporary containment until the source contract is qualified, not a
completed delivery of the selected continuation journey.

## Exact proposed owner clauses

**G-C01 — One renewed-period setting.** “For ordinary Continue referral, use one
organization-wide positive whole-number renewed period in elapsed days. One day
means exactly 86,400 seconds. No per-person, per-project, per-cycle override,
date picker, indefinite option, business-day calendar or additional switch is
introduced. This setting governs renewed cycles only; it does not change the
initial-referral contract. Reuse an existing setting only after its exact scope,
unit, authority and migration meaning are qualified; do not equate merely
similarly named initial and renewal settings.”

**G-C02 — Administration, validation and missing configuration.** “Only current
P12-qualified administration may save the renewed-period setting. Validate a
supported finite positive integer and checked result representability at save
and use. Publish the supported range consistently in the source contract, API
and UI before activation. Do not invent a default, round a decimal, coerce zero,
accept infinity or silently clamp. Missing or invalid qualified configuration
prevents this Continue command with a permitted actionable explanation; it does
not end existing cycles or block unrelated work. No user-facing Clear period
operation is added. Keep the period visible/configurable while missionary
continuation is Off; Off does not require a valid unsaved numeric draft.”

**G-C03 — Source acceptance and exact interval.** “After required waits and
serialization establish current source/cycle, relevant policy and authority,
capture one trusted source acceptance instant A. It becomes authoritative only
with the successful native transaction. Derive E = A + N × 86,400 seconds with
checked finite arithmetic; commit A, E, N, relevant policy revision and the
existing D35 cycle/outcome evidence together. The accepted renewed window is
[A,E); at E it is no longer active by this window. Client, request-start,
transaction-start-before-wait, queue, worker, rendering and response times are
not substitute anchors. No physical database commit timestamp or new clock
service is required.”

**G-C04 — Fixed accepted terms and prospective policy edits.** “A successful
period change applies to later accepted continuations only. It does not lengthen,
shorten, recalculate, restart or terminate an accepted active or historical
cycle. Preserve the actual accepted source values rather than dynamically
joining today's policy to reconstruct yesterday's expiry. Changes to display
timezone, locale, daylight-saving rules, missionary travel, assignment, edits,
interactions, reads or delayed workers cannot move A or E. Source-authorized
factual correction remains distinct and attributable; this clause does not
immunize an erroneous source fact from correction.”

**G-C05 — Policy save and continuation races.** “Use the native source's exact
relevant policy revision and D35 source/cycle/context fences. Policy save
versus Continue has one qualified serialized outcome: accepted under the
reviewed current policy or rejected for refresh; never partly old and partly
new. Concurrent administrator saves cannot silently overwrite a newer policy.
Missing-to-configured initialization and a value changed away and back obey the
same relevant revision proof. No policy backfill, scheduled job or upgrade may
accept a previously rejected continuation. Reuse D35 terminal-intent and
lost-response safety rather than a new configuration/CRM command journal.”

**G-C06 — Honest end-date preview without a second date decision.** “Show the
fixed period and an estimated resulting end date; make the exact time and
display timezone clear where needed to avoid a through-day promise. The source
computes the final E from actual acceptance. Ordinary elapsed seconds between
review and acceptance do not require a redundant confirmation or exact preview
timestamp equality. A relevant policy/material-term change or a different
displayed calendar end date requires refreshed terms and a new deliberate
intent before effect. The preview must carry its qualified display-zone/date
basis for that comparison; changing display zone never changes the source
clock. After success show the actual accepted boundary, subject to current
disclosure. Maya never chooses a per-referral date.”

**G-C07 — Staff consistency without extra powers.** “The same ordinary Continue
referral action uses the same organization period for an independently
authorized staff or missionary actor. D35 Off still blocks only new missionary
acceptance; it does not revoke independently authorized staff action. A staff
role label supplies neither a per-referral date override nor policy-management
permission. Separately qualified staff correction or other source operations
remain independent, with their own authority and semantics.”

**G-C08 — Source window and access remain separate.** “An active renewed window
is one source eligibility fact, not a grant lifetime or consent. Current P12
grant expiry, scope, recertification, purpose, privacy, cannot-pursue and
contactability remain independently enforceable. A grant expiring before E
ends its own access path without rewriting the referral window; an independent
valid path remains separately evaluated. Do not silently extend a grant to E
or truncate E to a grant's expiry. A hard source-lifecycle cap or prerequisite
that conflicts with the proposed renewed period must be resolved through its
qualified contract before acceptance; no hidden clamp, fabricated override or
new cap-setting hierarchy is introduced.”

**G-C09 — Effective expiry without timer authority.** “All affected current
source eligibility, active-list/detail/action and cache/release paths enforce
the same accepted E and current source conditions. A late worker, suspended
browser, reconnect, stale Active flag or old result must not retain this
window's active eligibility after E. A reconciliation worker may materialize
the source-owned outcome but cannot extend/restart it or own the clock. Exact
ordinary expiry may permit a fresh D35 action only under its current rules;
no background action or old successful/rejected intent renews a later cycle.”

**G-C10 — No unrelated lifecycle or disclosure changes.** “Renewal and numeric
policy changes neither reset old retention/erasure triggers nor restore erased
bodies, ended grants, private context, tasks, cultivation, outreach or consent.
Preserve independently valid promised follow-up and current work. Admit only
the existing qualified review/result/history fields under current disclosure;
new end-date metadata is classified at its source/projection. A displayed
window is not an export, roster, AI or notification permission.”

**G-C11 — Existing policy-change governance, not routine approval.** “Register
the renewed-period parameter and administration under the qualified source/P12
contract. A routine parameter save or Continue within that contract does not
create a new per-referral approval queue. Do not use the parameter to change
field ceilings, audiences, historical scope, grant rules or resolver purposes.
Any actual projection-policy widening still follows ADR-0211's server
classifier and distinct-human approval; an unqualified broader change cannot
evade it by being named a source setting.”

**G-C12 — Upgrade and restore honesty.** “Do not backfill a missing or legacy
value from a vendor display filter, old generic status, unrelated coaching
calendar, initial-referral setting or an invented duration. Preserve explicitly
qualified prior renewal choices and accepted exact cycle boundaries. Unknown
old semantics remain unknown; qualify their exact migration or hold the
affected continuation lane. Restore/rebuild must preserve current policy and
accepted outcomes while reapplying current authority/erasure, not create a new
cycle or re-run a retired intent.”

**G-C13 — Bounded implementation and proof.** “Use P9's native source transaction,
P12 authorization, shared projection and ordinary UI/configuration components.
No second timer engine, graph, scheduler, generic policy language, provider
dependency, separate task ledger or new notification is justified. Qualify the
exact owner source/policy representation, temporal arithmetic, all reached
authorization paths, current reads, recovery and deployment before activation.
Documentation and an abstract model alone are not runtime or accessibility
proof.”

## Material concerns — all eight requested dimensions

Likelihoods are qualitative design judgments, not measured production rates.
Each concern states what, why, severity, likelihood, evidence, answer impact,
permanent fix and its exact proposed clause. Proof requirements are separate.

### R01 — A day has two incompatible meanings

- **What:** A developer uses local calendar-day addition while another path uses
  elapsed hours; the date-only UI implies validity through midnight.
- **Why:** DST, a timezone change or midnight crossing makes identical configured
  values expire at different instants or misleads Maya about the usable period.
- **Severity:** High; it affects source eligibility and potentially record access.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible if generic date helpers or D11's convention are reused.
- **Evidence:** S02/S13/S14; no inherited referral unit, and scoped owners explicitly
  distinguish elapsed and calendar time.
- **Answer impact:** Keep A; add explicit elapsed-day unit, acceptance anchor,
  exclusive expiry and honest date/time presentation as proposed amendments.
- **Permanent fix:** One source-derived immutable instant, with display separate.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C01, G-C03, G-C04 and G-C06.

### R02 — “Finite” conceals unsupported or missing policy

- **What:** Zero, negative/decimal/overflow input, absent setting or a guessed
  default produces indefinite or unusable renewed work.
- **Why:** Numeric finiteness does not establish supported timestamp arithmetic,
  domain bounds or an organization choice; swallowing invalid values hides loss.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible at configuration, migration and boundary values.
- **Evidence:** S02 leaves bounds/defaults unselected; S03 C05 rejects invented
  deadlines; S15 establishes the still-unqualified source.
- **Answer impact:** Keep A; require published finite supported bounds and exact
  validation before activation without selecting a company duration here.
- **Permanent fix:** One source input contract enforced at save/use; no default,
  rounding, coercion or clamp; preserve independent operations.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C02, G-C08 and G-C12.

### R03 — A later policy edit rewrites accepted work

- **What:** Current policy dynamically recomputes existing expiries, or a setting
  edit/Off/migration retroactively expires or extends every old cycle.
- **Why:** It changes Maya's accepted terms without another source transition,
  may extend relationship-based visibility and obscures historical truth.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible with a single current configuration joined at read time.
- **Evidence:** S03 preserves accepted effects under later Off; S06 preserves
  source history; Q36 explicitly left mid-cycle behavior for this review.
- **Answer impact:** Add prospective-only policy semantics as an explicit proposed
  amendment, not a claim that the founder already chose them in A.
- **Permanent fix:** Persist exact accepted A/E/N/policy revision; correction is
  separately authorized and attributable.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C03–G-C05 and G-C12.

### R04 — Policy races or waiting clocks admit unreviewed terms

- **What:** Continue uses a stale numeric value, a pre-wait timestamp or an old
  intent; competing saves overwrite policy or a midnight change alters the shown date.
- **Why:** A click can produce different terms from those reviewed, or a retry
  can open a new cycle merely because time/policy changed.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible with concurrent administration, slow networks and retries.
- **Evidence:** S03 C06–C08 already require fresh atomic acceptance and terminal
  intent safety; S13 supplies a scoped after-serialization clock precedent.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve A's one deliberate action; require refresh only for
  relevant changed terms/date, not every elapsed second or a routine modal.
- **Permanent fix:** Relevant policy/source fences and one source anchor/outcome.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C03, G-C05, G-C06 and G-C09.

### R05 — Window duration is mistaken for permission duration

- **What:** Renewal extends access grants, stops independent staff paths, restores
  private context or silently truncates the referral to an unrelated grant deadline.
- **Why:** Source state, current rights, retention and contactability are separate
  owners; collapsing them both overgrants and breaks legitimate work.
- **Severity:** Critical for protected disclosure; otherwise high.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible because active referral is already a read predicate.
- **Evidence:** S05/S07–S09/S11; D35 C10/C16 explicitly preserve the separation.
- **Answer impact:** Keep A; no new rights, grant lifetime, retention or export choice.
- **Permanent fix:** Current owner enforcement at each relevant use/release, with
  independent source facts and valid grant paths retained.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C07–G-C11.

### R06 — Staff consistency becomes a new role-based override

- **What:** Staff silently get arbitrary renewal dates, or D35 missionary Off and
  missing configuration are used to revoke unrelated staff/source authority.
- **Why:** The founder chose Maya's simple organization period, not a new staff
  privilege or a global CRM shutdown switch.
- **Severity:** High for authority bypass; medium for avoidable obstruction.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible when routes or role labels select different code paths.
- **Evidence:** S03 C01/C02/C17; S07 administration and execution are distinct;
  S10/S11 require server/source authority.
- **Answer impact:** Propose the same ordinary Continue terms for both actors;
  label this consistency choice honestly and preserve separate qualified actions.
- **Permanent fix:** One source command/parameter and independently resolved action
  and administration capabilities; no role-name override.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C02, G-C07 and G-C11.

### R07 — Expiry waits for an unreliable projection or scheduler

- **What:** Active access remains until a worker updates a status, or an expired
  page/cache/old response restores the active row after its deadline.
- **Why:** Job delay and cached state cannot extend the owner-defined window;
  otherwise the visible date and enforced source state disagree.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible on suspension, network loss, outages and delayed workers.
- **Evidence:** S03 current acceptance/recovery; S07 current resolver; S13 source
  clock remains authoritative and fixed expiries continue.
- **Answer impact:** Keep A; add all-reached-path enforcement, not a new real-time
  countdown service, stronger freshness claim or universal scheduler.
- **Permanent fix:** Evaluate accepted E at qualified current-read/action/release
  boundaries and preserve source outcome semantics during reconciliation.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C03, G-C09 and G-C13.

### R08 — A small setting becomes a second policy/time product

- **What:** Duplicate initial/renewal hierarchies, per-ministry overrides,
  business-day calendars, mandatory review queues or new tasks are added.
- **Why:** They are neither selected nor needed for A, create hidden coupling and
  increase the number of conflicting source and UX states to maintain.
- **Severity:** Medium, becoming high if duplicate authority drifts.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible if generic automation/permission frameworks are reused
  without a bounded source contract.
- **Evidence:** S01–S03 narrow scope; S06 native write; S08 actual policy widening
  is not generalized approval; S12 distinct cultivation owner.
- **Answer impact:** Keep A and explicitly exclude additional product controls;
  do not weaken genuine existing owner floors to achieve visual simplicity.
- **Permanent fix:** One scoped parameter, existing D35 action, minimal ordinary
  validation/recovery, and actual source-policy qualification.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C01, G-C02, G-C10, G-C11 and G-C13.

### R09 — Documentary confidence substitutes for source qualification

- **What:** A selected number/unit or passing abstract example is presented as
  a functional referral producer, safe migration or deployed access enforcement.
- **Why:** P9's general graph/write contract does not define this complete cycle;
  legacy statuses and timers cannot supply missing authority by convention.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible if ratification is conflated with implementation.
- **Evidence:** S10/S11/S15 and D35 C23/C24; active changes are intended behavior.
- **Answer impact:** Accept with required amendments and exact release gates;
  do not defer the selected useful feature indefinitely or claim it already works.
- **Permanent fix:** Reconcile the actual owner source/spec and prove the composed
  flow before activation; preserve initial evidence and qualified old choices.
- **Exact proposed clause:** G-C12 and G-C13.

revocation or source correction must not be counted as unexplained divergence.

is selected here.
