## Why this is the next genuine choice

The [founder brief](phase28-founder-brief.md), §7, explicitly leaves correction
ownership to resolve. D1–D4 now distinguish restricted preparation, deliberate
staff availability, no new continuity reader and ordinary archival. Sharing
does not itself grant the missionary continuing permission to change the
organization's authoritative contact details.

Do not ask whether an already authorized edit needs routine approval: P9 already
provides direct source-owned edits, and no universal approval queue is inherited.
The actual remaining choice is whether the **ordinary missionary workspace
purpose should include a bounded contact-correction capability**, or whether
staff remain responsible for those corrections. Existing independently held
permissions are neither removed nor expanded by the question.

This advances the support-raising journey and clean-data goal. An arbitrary
retention period is not a suitable next question without the relevant records
policy. A mandatory next action on merely saving preparation would reopen
settled voluntary-versus-managed-work boundaries. Neither is needed to resolve
this contact-maintenance choice.

## Proposed founder question

**Scenario:** Maya has made Beth's contact available to staff. Beth later gives
Maya a new phone number. The missionary is working with Beth in her permitted
support-raising workspace; this is an ordinary contact correction, not a change
to login, giving or communication consent.

**Choice:** should the normal missionary workspace include the bounded ability
to correct eligible shared contact details, or should staff remain responsible
for applying those corrections?

- **A — Missionaries can make ordinary corrections directly (recommended).**
  Include a bounded missionary correction capability for eligible contacts and
  fields in their permitted workspace, subject to the organization's current
  permissions and source restrictions. Use one short edit/save interaction and
  explain that it updates the organization's shared contact details. This can
  reduce handoffs and stale information; errors also affect other authorized
  users, so source validation, conflict handling and history matter.
- **B — Staff apply shared-contact corrections.** The ordinary missionary
  purpose does not add this editing capability. Staff retain responsibility
  through their qualified CRM path. This centralizes stewardship but adds
  coordination and delay. It does not automatically commission a suggestion
  inbox, approval engine, notification or new task type; any needed handoff
  must be separately qualified rather than described as already implemented.

Recommendation A is a **product judgment**, not a proven ministry workflow or
an already selected entitlement. The founder decides the capability; engineers
must qualify exact P9/P12 scope and field behavior. A phone correction is the
clean scenario. Other fields need their own existing contracts; do not silently
extend ordinary phone correction into account identity or email consent.

## Inherited guardrails and consequences to carry forward

The authoritative contact remains source-owned by P9 with P4 identity and P12
authorization. Do not create a private override, rely on who first entered or
shared the contact, broaden CRM search, expose another ministry's history, or
infer write permission from reading. Preserve existing independently authorized
staff editing. Source-required policy qualification is not a per-edit ceremony.

Present the shared effect where the missionary edits: for example, “Updates the
contact details available to your organization's staff.” Show only fields they
can currently correct, accurately explain unavailable actions, retain entered
work on a conflict, and save once through the authoritative source. A lost
response needs same-operation recovery; a newer staff/donor correction cannot
be overwritten silently. Contact correction is not a newsletter opt-in, a
verification, a send, an identity merge or a change to private preparation's
audience.

If A is selected, require exact capability/field/source qualification and outcome
tests; do not ask the founder to design grants, SQL or a conflict algorithm. If B
is selected, resolve the real staff handoff without assuming a new queue. Do not
record either as accepted until the founder answers. Both options preserve D1–D4.
