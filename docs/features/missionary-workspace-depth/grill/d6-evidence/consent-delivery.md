# D6 evidence — native newsletter consent, confirmation and delivery

Research-only review, October 16, 2026 UTC. The parent reports the founder selected **direct native newsletter signup, with the missionary choosing whether the public Page shows it**. The recommended confirmation/lifecycle details below are design judgments, not additional choices attributed to the founder. No application, root decision, session, formal specification, tracker, live provider or recipient was changed.

## Recommended permanent policy

Use one clearly identified native newsletter purpose belonging to the qualified ministry/source in its organization. A public submission records a **pending signup**, not a subscriber, verified person, donor, supporter, protected-content reader or account. For every **new admission and re-subscription**, send one purpose-specific mailbox confirmation and require a deliberate confirmation POST. An already active subscription at the same exact source/recipient/address revision is a no-op: no repeat challenge, welcome mail or extra subscriber. Use the source owner's canonical normalization; do not merge people, infer aliases, overwrite CRM names or infer consent from sharing a mailbox.

Recommend **no first-release Auth-verified shortcut**. A current login, old verified flag or matching donor email does not establish fresh consent to this particular newsletter. A future qualified exact-address proof reuse could be considered without changing the fresh consent requirement, but implementing that cross-purpose assurance branch is unnecessary for the chosen simple signup. Google currently recommends mailbox confirmation after subscription address entry; this supports the policy as deliverability/abuse judgment, not a claim that every jurisdiction legally mandates double opt-in. Resend separately requires specific active consent; a clear standalone **Subscribe to [newsletter]** action can express that intent without adding a redundant mandatory checkbox to a form whose sole purpose is this subscription. Mixed-purpose forms cannot bundle it.

Confirmation proves control of the delivered mailbox capability for this narrow instruction, not a unique natural person, an account claim or continuing ownership. Use the existing P17 protected-action doorway with the exact producer-owned authority: inert GET/HEAD, no auto-submit, deliberate same-origin POST, exact purpose/source/address/issuance/revision and expiry checks. Do not require a login, donation or new account just to subscribe. The new confirmation meaning must qualify its exact source producer, preparation class, sender, safe content and action before activation; it cannot borrow an Auth signup or financial receipt key.

The actual newsletter remains **human-authored P28 communication outside the P17 system-message catalog**. It uses the shared qualified content/preparation facilities and P6 sole intent/submission/evidence path. The automatic proof email is a separate exact system meaning; it is not permission to put the newsletter body in a catch-all system template or to call Resend directly. P28 must remain useful with P32 external synchronization disabled.

### Page entry Off is not consent withdrawal

The root's proposed source amendment is coherent and should be explicit: **Page-entry Off closes new signup occurrences**, including submissions from a previously cached form. Already accepted exact-purpose pending signups may finish within their immutable expiry and current source/recipient/safety authority. Existing subscribed readers remain subscribed. Show that consequence beside the control.

In contrast, **consent withdrawal**, a closed newsletter purpose, revoked source authority, known prohibited contact use or applicable safety/suppression stops the affected pending confirmation and/or unstarted newsletter effect according to its exact source policy. Page Off does not itself close the newsletter, withdraw readers or turn on the older external **Request newsletter** action. Reopening the Page cannot revive expired/revoked proof, old consent or withdrawn sends. Unsubscribe stays reachable even when the public entry/Page is absent.

## Duplicates, re-subscription and old links

- One accepted operation with the same immutable input returns its original permitted result; changed meaning conflicts. Distinct public attempts for the same pending source/address must not generate unlimited challenges or replace a still-valid proof merely to defeat another visitor. The qualified source combines stable occurrence identity with bounded pending-challenge coalescing/cooldown; it must not impose permanent unverified email/list uniqueness that blocks legitimate future enrollment.
- A later accepted consent withdrawal fences every earlier uncompleted admission instruction for that exact scope. A stale confirmation, delayed worker, restored backup or older uncertain consent-On operation cannot reactivate it. Re-subscription is fresh explicit intent plus fresh purpose confirmation and source revision; it preserves independently applicable higher restrictions.
- Re-entering a different address starts deliberate new meaning. It neither moves the old subscription nor edits the canonical contact/claim automatically. Reassignment, merge and source succession require existing owner qualification; equal text or a new Page owner cannot inherit old subscribers or proof.
- **Old human footer link:** a valid link can open an inert, login-free landing for its exact current permitted list/destination scope; a new deliberate action creates a fresh current-epoch withdrawal. It cannot open full Preferences or reveal unrelated subscriptions. It must not be disabled merely because its originating message is old, nor redirected to a different person's/address's/list's authority.
- **RFC8058 POST:** its token identifies one exact bounded withdrawal source operation. First use can apply only to the then-current qualified recipient/list scope; accepted retry returns its original safe result and cannot undo a later freshly confirmed admission. This is a reduction-only, cookieless, unauthenticated, non-redirecting path with the exact RFC field. Do not claim an identical replayed provider POST can be distinguished from a fresh human intention. Preserve a working manual current-withdrawal path rather than silently expanding old command authority.
- Confirmation and unsubscribe are different protocols. Both have inert GET, but RFC8058 unsubscribe deliberately requires no additional confirmation/login/cookie; it must not inherit the protected confirmation doorway's extra POST ceremony. An unsubscribe does not erase every contact/history record, affect another ministry/list or cancel giving.

These old-link semantics are a recommended explicit D6/P3/P6 source amendment. P17 already requires exact recipient/contact-revision/list-purpose/consent scope and idempotence; it does not fully specify the native re-subscription-generation protocol. Qualify first-use-after-re-subscription, accepted-replay-after-re-subscription and manual fresh withdrawal separately rather than claiming the current text proves the whole lifecycle.

## Required finite source profiles

Do not inherit P25 external-request seven-day dispatch/thirty-day body clocks or treat Resend's twenty-four-hour request-key window as a confirmation-token lifetime. The native source must publish one small code-owned lifecycle profile before launch, with finite constants for pending/issuance usefulness, resend/cooldown/attempt limits, pending raw-data retirement, proof-evidence custody, and semantic replay/withdrawal evidence. No per-missionary TTL or retention settings matrix is needed.

**Concrete review candidate, not an adopted fact:** a confirmation issuance and pending admission expire **72 elapsed hours after initial durable acceptance**, without extension by read/retry/resend; after terminal admission, rejection, expiry, withdrawal or supersession, unused proof secrets and the redundant pending raw payload leave the active source and purge within **24 elapsed hours**, subject to exact lawful custody exceptions that never preserve send/admission authority. The 72-hour choice is product judgment to allow ordinary weekend/time-zone delay; it is not an email standard or provider limit. Test and finalize that profile before activation. A deliberate fresh signup after expiry is new source meaning and remains abuse-bounded. Confirmed recipient/contact data stays only in its proper active newsletter-purpose source under its own qualified retention, not in an immortal form submission.

Consent evidence needs sufficient minimized source/purpose/disclosure-version/method/time/address-revision/actor-or-guest/operation lineage to prove the instruction and prevent resurrection. Its exact post-withdrawal retention/hold schedule must be reconciled with the existing P3 privacy owner before activation; there is no source basis here for a universal seven-year or permanent raw-email archive. Withdrawal/replay safety evidence may outlive the readable pending form but may not re-enable it. P6 prepared bytes have independently nonextendable utility/action/material deadlines and existing terminal purge rules; the new proof email and human newsletter need their own admitted material classification. Recent sent copy for proof secrets must be Off; a newsletter's support-copy policy cannot be inferred from financial classes. Provider/mailbox custody is independent and cannot be promised to disappear with Core's source purge.

## Exact owner amendments required

1. **P23 D26 / ADR0170 / P22 Page owner:** certify one bounded P28 native subscription adapter and Page placement; reconcile the older P32-only subscription-owner sentence with later program ownership. Preserve one Primary Outcome, released source identity, private interaction and current acceptance checks. Page control must distinguish requested visibility, effective released entry, accepted pending work and real purpose closure. See [page-control owners](page-control-owners.md) for delegated exact Page-authority evidence.
2. **P25 EX11–EX13/EX16:** preserve the external request-only contract, including its no-visitor-email and frozen missionary handoff semantics. Add a narrow explicit native entry/success distinction rather than silently superseding request history. Native signup must not inherit its two-child missionary notice or request7/30-day clocks. Where both products are supported, each public action names its destination and consequence; a safe single native entry need not add a confusing chooser or auto-fallback.
3. **P28/P3/P4/P9/P12 source/recipient authority:** own one exact native list-purpose fact and its current destination/proof/consent/revision/evidence lifecycle. Canonical recipient source may retain its purpose-specific address under qualified custody; P6's body-free intent/history rule is not a ban on that source. The existing P6 no-Party branch avoids fake donor/Party creation but is not an already-qualified newsletter source. No same-email person selection or shadow contact truth. The identity workstream owns this exact representation proof.
4. **P17/P6:** qualify the exact automatic confirmation meaning/protected action/prepared material; qualify the human newsletter's lower-level preparation path without forcing it into the system catalog. Preserve the preparation/sealed-submission producer gap, complete-set compiler, permanent semantic dedupe, current dispatch safety and existing sole queue/evidence spine. Reconcile current Resend suppression scope and exact one-click headers/action with tests; do not activate legacy `notification_queue`, API Contacts or an alternate sender as a shortcut.
5. **P32:** native subscribers gain no automatic Mailchimp export/enrollment. External export needs independently admitted source purpose/current consent/export eligibility and existing sync owner. Reconnect, import, migration and source rename cannot create opt-in. P28 is not blocked on P32's product implementation.
