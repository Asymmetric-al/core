# D6 — product and identity synthesis

Research only, October 16, 2026. No application, formal owner specification,
permission, live configuration or communication was changed. This is a scoped
source review, not exhaustive review of every predecessor or runtime proof.

## Selected outcome and recommended permanent shape

The founder selected **direct native signup** and specifically wants the
missionary to understand and control what appears on their public giving /
profile Page. The Q6 note remains pre-answer research; its pending label is not
the current decision. The founder did not select a proof protocol, universal
default, new CRM access, per-Page permission system or new identity authority.

Recommend one short native **email-newsletter** entry on the exact qualified
Page: a clear named newsletter, optional single Name, required Email, concise
purpose disclosure and one deliberate action. Keep giving, account creation,
protected Ministry Updates and unrelated newsletters out of this transaction.
Show the truthful next step and accepted state; proof pending is not subscribed,
and subscribed is not delivered. This recommendation does not remove the
separately requested native physical-newsletter workflow.

Use one Asym-owned subscription source: P28 owns the exact newsletter
relationship/lifecycle; P4/P9 qualify its minimum recipient/contact authority
and any independently proved Party binding. P28's instruction/membership remains
the single native fact under P3's consent contract and wider restrictions; P6
consumes its current eligibility and owns send-time verdict/snapshot, suppression
evidence and communication outcomes, not a second subscriber master. P17 owns
prepared content/sender identity; P23 owns the
public intake occurrence; P22 owns the Page/release/control integration; P12
owns current capability and purpose admission. **The minimum recipient source
is an owner extension to qualify, not a declaration that a usable source
already exists.** It must allow lawful newsletter participation without making
up a donor, claimed person or supporter relationship. Do not reuse D1 private
prospect preparation as a subscription store.

P6 already admits a manifest-qualified same-Tenant no-Party authority revision
as an alternative to its Party/contact branch. That is useful structural
precedent, not permission to pass an arbitrary email. A narrow source-owned
recipient/contact record is not a second CRM: it must have no independent
canonical-person, household, contact-edit, cultivation or finance authority.
The P6 restriction on durable destination addresses applies to communication
events/intents; it does not prohibit the properly owned recipient source from
retaining its purpose-specific destination under its separately qualified
lifecycle. No provider contact/list becomes the primary subscription source.

For missionary controls, recommend one understandable control for showing the
qualified newsletter entry in the exact Page context, using the Page owner's
existing change/release semantics and current permission. Explain the effective
public result and whether publication/review remains necessary. Do not expose
the technical Native/External provider choice as a visitor decision. A hidden
entry does not unsubscribe existing readers or disable independently authorized
sends. Exactly how the Page control fences new stale-form intake and pending
confirmation must be reconciled with the page/intake/consent owners; no silent
history rewrite or claimed global newsletter shutdown follows from hiding it.

## Strongest alternative

The request-only doorway is the strongest alternative: it preserves P25's
minimal, unverified handoff and does not require a public subscriber lifecycle.
Its cost is routine human coordination and an ambiguous end-to-end outcome for
the person who wanted to join. Direct signup fits the founder's selected native
product and removes that handoff when qualified. Preserve request-only behavior
for an actually externally managed newsletter; do not silently substitute it
when native signup is broken or present two competing buttons merely because
both technical lanes exist. There is no measured conversion/support-cost claim.

## Material concerns and exact proposed clauses

Severity is the harm if the stated failure occurs. Likelihood is conditional
design risk, not observed production incidence. Clauses below are proposed
review amendments; source facts identify what is already inherited.

### PS01 — Native ownership is not already reconciled

- **Risk:** High severity / likely if the new button is wired to the old request
  or P32 adapter: the visitor is falsely told they joined, or native work gains
  an external-sync dependency.
- **Evidence:** Brief lines197–211 requires native independence and an explicit
  signup amendment. P25 EX11–EX12, experience.md:161–185, owns request-only
  meaning. P23 decision-log:6551–6556 still assigns newsletter
  consent/confirmation to P32. Active P25 crm-core OpenSpec:22–35 forbids
  subscription from the old request.
- **Impact/fix:** Explicitly reconcile the older routing rather than treating it
  as compatible by wording. **Clause:** “The qualified native newsletter
  subscription is a new P28-owned relationship/purpose integrated through
  P23/P6/P17/P12 and the P4/P9 recipient owner. P32 remains external sync. A P25
  request and its existing history retain their original meaning and cannot be
  migrated, replayed or relabeled as consent or subscription.”

### PS02 — Email possession is not person identity or a CRM write

- **Risk:** Critical / plausible when reusing a guest-gift matcher: an attacker
  changes an existing person's name/address, discovers a hidden record, or
  obtains another person's history through a shared mailbox.
- **Evidence:** P4:152–165 separates unverified capture, source-specific guest
  attribution, possession, claim and access; :185/222 forbids automatic merge;
  :227–235 forbids email/row fallbacks; :491–495 preserves distinct people with
  shared email. Durable crm-core OpenSpec:46–64 preserves separate identities
  and forbids low-confidence automatic links/overwrites. P9 B17:661–679 requires
  an exact proved person and field/purpose command for canonical correction.
- **Impact/fix:** Subscribe the exact qualified destination/purpose without
  claiming which real-world person controls it. **Clause:** “Public supplied
  Name and Email are purpose-bound assertions. They never automatically create,
  claim, merge, correct or grant access to an existing canonical Party. A
  qualified internal identity link is independent, same-Tenant and nonrevealing;
  signup remains usable without inventing such a link.”

### PS03 — One native source, with no forced fake donor or shadow CRM

- **Risk:** High / likely if the existing donor table or provider Contacts API
  is chosen as a convenience: meaningless donors, inconsistent addresses,
  duplicate consent and provider-dependent membership result.
- **Evidence:** P6:189/199 and :226–227 permits only exact manifest-qualified
  Party/contact or no-Party authority branches; source-less email fallback is
  forbidden. P9:610–613 reserves Party label writes to the owning subtype;
  :388–398 rejects role-as-authority and describes historically reserved contact
  normalization, not proof of a complete current recipient service. Durable
  crm-core OpenSpec:14–26 fixes Asym/package-api ownership.
- **Impact/fix:** Qualify the smallest native recipient seam needed for this
  purpose and retain a single writer. **Clause:** “Each subscription references
  one exact source-owned recipient/contact authority and current purpose state.
  Source identity, destination revision, optional proved Party link and provider
  transport identifiers remain distinct. No fake donor, canonical Party-on-email
  shortcut, copied personal-contact record, provider-owned membership or general
  subscriber CRM is introduced.”

### PS04 — Duplicate submissions and shared mailboxes need different meanings

- **Risk:** High / plausible: retries multiply mail, unverified first claims
  block legitimate joining, or one mailbox silently combines two people.
- **Evidence:** P25 EX11:171 forbids permanent email/missionary uniqueness for
  unverified requests; EX14:205 requires semantic operation uniqueness. P4:185
  makes merge source-approved; :491–495 rejects shared-email identity union.
  P6:199 binds immutable plan/member meaning and rejects changed-input replay.
- **Impact/fix:** Distinguish operation replay, exact newsletter destination
  membership and person identity. **Clause:** “Identical issued-operation
  replay resolves once; changed meaning does not overwrite it. Pending interest
  cannot permanently reserve an address. Qualified duplicate membership in the
  same newsletter/destination converges without merging people or importing
  unrelated preferences; different newsletters/tenants remain independent.
  Public results do not reveal prior identity, membership or suppression.”
  Exact source keys, abuse limits and proof recovery are engineering
  qualification; do not copy the request lane's cardinality into subscriptions.

### PS05 — Newsletter authority must remain narrower than CRM authority

- **Risk:** Critical / plausible if a new subscriber becomes a supporter edge,
  D2 shared contact or D5 editable contact automatically.
- **Evidence:** Active P25 identity OpenSpec:22–37 admits guest-origin request
  detail without fabricated relationships and excludes role/assignee/spouse
  inheritance. P9:278–288 limits missionary CRM browsing. P12:1542–1556 fixes
  one current PDP/context. D1–D5 separately govern prospect preparation,
  deliberate staff contact admission and exact permitted corrections.
- **Impact/fix:** Give only the selected newsletter job its exact source
  projection/actions. **Clause:** “Subscription and newsletter management grant
  no general Party search, supporter/financial view, staff notes, contact edit,
  D2 sharing, cultivation, coaching or historical request access. A missionary
  may perform a newsletter action only through its exact current purpose
  capability. Separate existing rights remain separate.”

### PS06 — Missionary control is not a new all-purpose Page permission

- **Risk:** High / plausible if generic missionary role or Page ownership is
  treated as publication, subscriber-list and sending authority simultaneously.
- **Evidence:** P22:330–345 distinguishes source/publication/giving truth;
  :349–367 requires exact active contributor authority and release semantics.
  P25 EX12:185 rejects generic Page-editor/missionary inheritance for protected
  recipient details. The new founder instruction specifically requests control
  over what is shown, not broad Page administration.
- **Impact/fix:** Offer the selected narrow control through the exact owner
  path; current authority and actual effective result drive the UI.
  **Clause:** “The eligible missionary controls newsletter-entry display for
  their exact permitted Page through the existing Page/source permission model.
  Display intent, released visibility, intake readiness, subscription state,
  list visibility and send authority are distinct. The interface explains
  pending/review/unavailable states without presenting an ineffective On state
  as public availability.” No novel per-contact or per-Page ACL system follows.

### PS07 — Simple form must work for international and guest visitors

- **Risk:** Medium / likely with a generic donor-registration form: unnecessary
  abandonment, incorrect identity data and exclusion of single-name visitors.
- **Evidence:** P25 EX11:161–165 expressly provides optional single Name,
  editable Email, no donation/account/required surname, safe canonical address
  handling and private prefills. P9 B17:663–679 preserves unsplit Unicode names.
  P23:731–734 preserves semantic accessible no-JS public submission.
- **Impact/fix:** Reuse the concise field discipline, not the request-only proof
  semantics. **Clause:** “Native signup requires only its purpose-necessary
  email and consent/proof; Name is optional, unsplit and international. No gift,
  account, phone, postal address or profile completion is required for email
  subscription. Signed-in prefill is editable convenience and cannot silently
  substitute a represented person's destination or update a canonical contact.”

### PS08 — Honest choices and results prevent false entitlement

- **Risk:** High / likely if success copy, giving context or login carries
  implied benefits: visitors expect protected stories, a donor account or
  guaranteed mail; missionaries believe a form submission is verified support.
- **Evidence:** P25 EX11:163/169, EX15:219 separates accepted request from
  enrollment and forbids unsupported success claims. P22:343–345 separates
  public/checkout/gift states. Brief:197–219 separates topic consent, destinations,
  delivery and protected readers. P6:150–153 separates transport and consent.
- **Impact/fix:** Name the newsletter and next step; keep giving usable.
  **Clause:** “The visitor is told what newsletter they chose, what remains to
  happen and what was accepted. Signup neither requires nor proves giving,
  support, login, protected-reader membership or receipt entitlement. It
  authorizes no unrelated topic, welcome campaign, personal outreach, print
  mailing or automatic post notice. No arrival-time or inbox-delivery promise
  is inferred from source acceptance.”

### PS09 — Page hiding, withdrawal and existing membership are not one switch

- **Risk:** High / plausible if a display toggle mutates the audience or a
  stale form bypasses a current withdrawal: readers disappear or unwanted new
  membership continues without the missionary understanding it.
- **Evidence:** P22:355–372 separates current exposure/containment from saved
  reach. P25 EX12:187 and EX16:225 preserve frozen accepted work and re-prove
  applicable current restrictions; EX14:205 forbids consent revival on rollback.
- **Impact/fix:** Give the control one plain effect and reconcile exact pending
  work at the owner. **Clause:** “Hiding newsletter entry changes its exact
  public acquisition surface, not existing subscriber consent or every sending
  purpose. Stale Page/form intake re-proves the effective source setting. Any
  already accepted operation retains its truthful history; confirmation and
  further effects follow the explicitly qualified source continuation policy.
  Re-showing cannot re-subscribe anyone or replay old requests.” The continuation
  policy must be stated before activation; this note does not silently choose it.

### PS10 — Existing financial and communication truth must survive

- **Risk:** Critical / plausible if a giving-page subscription is joined to
  donor records or consent is reset during a merge, correction or rollback.
- **Evidence:** P4:185/221 protects immutable money/history and most-restrictive
  consent. P6:152–153 retains current send-time gates and one suppression source.
  Brief:109/197–211 preserves finance owners and no gift-derived enrollment.
- **Impact/fix:** Keep source consequences narrow. **Clause:** “Subscription,
  withdrawal, contact correction, canonical identity repair and Page changes
  never alter gifts, legal donors, receipts, commitments or finance access.
  Newsletter activation cannot clear broader suppression or inherit verification
  from Auth, an old address, a gift or a shared mailbox. Current source gates
  apply before every consequential communication.”

## Decisions versus qualification

Direct native signup and missionary display control are selected. The exact
native recipient source, idempotency/revision representation, safe public
resolution, bounded abuse handling, live content profiles, purpose grants,
source migration and no-JS/mobile behavior are owner/engineering work to make
that selected outcome honest; they are not new founder microquestions.

The confirmation policy is not selected by “direct.” Recommend explicit mailbox
confirmation for a new enrollment unless the exact current request/purpose/
destination proof is independently qualified; no universal Auth-verified bypass.
That is a proposed review amendment, not inherited P25 request behavior or a
claim of real-world person verification. Likewise, an initial display default
and the pending-confirmation consequence of hiding must be explicitly stated in
the final review rather than silently inferred. They can be presented as clear
minimal design amendments; escalation is needed only if an unresolved source
conflict would change the founder's intended behavior.

This decision does not select an Epistle-style aggregate preference center,
recipient-selected frequency, automatic digest, new staff CRM admission policy,
automatic welcome series, physical-address public signup or externally managed
subscription migration. Those are real independent scope choices if proposed,
not prerequisites that should inflate this first native signup.
