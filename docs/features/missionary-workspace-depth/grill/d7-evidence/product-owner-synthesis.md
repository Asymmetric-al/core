# D7 — product, owner and parity synthesis

Research-only, October 16, 2026 UTC. Baseline:
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813`.

The founder selected Q7 A: a deliberate missionary decision for each newsletter,
with complete control and understandable, excellent UX. The new rider requests
a fully functional, integrated newsletter experience at or beyond Epistle's
feature parity. This is new direction, unlike the historical DOCX that the
founder explicitly supplied as context only. This note proposes the corrected
interpretation and amendments for review; it does not create a formal PRD,
ratify prior amendments, modify owner contracts, implement or activate anything.

## Product conclusion and strongest alternatives

**Accept with required amendments.** Deliver the complete native newsletter
job, including preparation, eligible content reuse, deliberate issue approval,
appropriate scheduling, recipient controls, replies, media, print and truthful
results. A text box and Send button, a provider link, or a polished mock is not
an adequate response to the rider.

The specific A choice limits the broad parity phrase: it does not authorize
standing permission to compose and send future digest issues without reviewing
each issue. The product should make preparation easy enough that the missionary
does not repeatedly rebuild layouts or copy the same story. Automating draft
assembly, validation, current exclusions and execution of an already approved
issue can support A. Automatically choosing future issue content and audience
under a recurring standing instruction would instead implement unselected B.

**Strongest alternative:** B's optional automatic digests reduce recurring effort
and can honor reader-selected frequency without review of every issue. It is a
valid product model, not inherently unsafe. The founder selected the tradeoff
of direct issue control. Describe the automatic-digest difference honestly in
the parity comparison; do not implement B under the name “Epistle parity,” and
do not claim literal feature-for-feature equivalence while omitting it.

**Smallest alternative / no-build:** keep external newsletter tools and offer
links or exports, or ship only manually composed plain email. Either lowers
native scope but leaves the expressly requested complete, integrated job
unfinished. Existing owner primitives should reduce implementation work; an
external tool dependency or repeated manual re-entry is not the chosen result.

## Corrected decision candidate

> **D7 — Complete native newsletters with deliberate issue authorization.**
> Provide a fully usable missionary newsletter workflow integrated with Asym's
> qualified content, public-page, recipient, consent, delivery and print owners.
> The missionary prepares an issue, understands its content and exact permitted
> audience, and deliberately authorizes that issue to send now or at its chosen
> one-off time. Publishing a Ministry Update, enabling public signup, choosing
> an editor template or saving a draft does not authorize a newsletter.
>
> Reduce recurring preparation through recoverable drafts, reusable approved
> presentation, eligible Ministry Update and media reuse, audience review,
> truthful previews and governed tests. A prepared or suggested digest remains
> a draft until this issue receives its deliberate authorization. No standing
> automatic-digest or unreviewed future-issue authority follows from parity.
>
> Give readers the qualified subscription/preference/withdrawal and response
> paths appropriate to this newsletter. Do not convert reading, gifts, follows,
> ordinary contact corrections or public Page access into newsletter consent.
> Preserve existing post-email preferences and protected-reader rights rather
> than merging them with native subscription. Replies, delivery diagnostics,
> media and print must work through their exact owners with honest outcomes.
>
> Benchmark the complete newsletter jobs against verified Epistle behavior and
> current comparable tools. Record each job as included and proved, requiring
> a bounded owner amendment, or intentionally different because of the chosen
> per-issue authorization. Do not claim complete parity from an attractive
> editor or generic provider capability. Keep unrelated MPD, finance, coaching,
> CRM, campaign automation and broad analytics under their already recorded
> scope and owners rather than importing the competitor's whole product.
>
> “Missionary control” means effective control of the actions and material the
> missionary is currently authorized to use. It does not bypass organization
> permissions, content release, safety, consent or custody. Use one understandable
> issue journey without a new approval hierarchy, second communication system,
> general form builder, duplicate inbox or forced staff administration UI.

This is candidate wording, not a claim that every implementation contract is
already qualified. A's explicit send decision does not impose manual approval
on independently authorized P22 automatic editorial publication or unrelated
system messages. Nor does this new choice ratify D6's separate proposed
confirmation or 72h/24h profile merely because the newsletter uses its audience.

## Material cross-cutting concern register

Likelihood below is engineering judgment without the fix, not a measured rate.
Each exact clause is proposed wording for the root's review, not a new formal
specification. Source references point to the inspected register above.

### PG-R1 — “parity” quietly overrides the selected send model

- **What/why:** Standing digest delivery could appear as a convenience while
  actually sending future unreviewed issues, contrary to A.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** High when copying Epistle cadence controls.
- **Evidence:** Q7's exact A/B distinction; brief87–89/217; latest A plus rider.
- **Answer impact:** Keep A and the complete newsletter goal; record the exact
  automatic-digest difference rather than reopening the same question.
- **Permanent fix:** Bind each send authority to an understood issue/content and
  audience; make parity status explicit per job.
- **Exact clause:** “Every newsletter issue requires deliberate issue-specific
  authorization. Automatic preparation creates a draft only; the parity target
  introduces no standing automatic-digest or future-content send authority.”

### PG-R2 — complete functionality becomes a superficial editor or vague promise

- **What/why:** Shipping composition without reliable audience, replies, print,
  delivery or recovery fails the complete job while a vague parity claim hides it.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** High without a bounded acceptance ledger.
- **Evidence:** Current brief193–203; platform continuous flows; new parity rider.
- **Answer impact:** Requires complete newsletter jobs, not the competitor's
  whole MPD platform and not a single first demo treated as completion.
- **Permanent fix:** Trace every included job to its source, owner amendment and
  positive actual user journey, with an explicit intentional-difference register.
- **Exact clause:** “Newsletter completion requires the documented end-to-end
  job ledger, including recovery and reader outcomes. A prototype, provider
  feature, optional future phase or external manual handoff cannot satisfy an
  included native job; intentional differences remain visible.”

### PG-R3 — full missionary control becomes staff, publisher or consent authority

- **What/why:** Author controls can accidentally grant sender administration,
  unredacted recipients, unrestricted publication or subscriber editing.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** Medium.
- **Evidence:** P12 independent atoms; P22 contributor identity; D1–D6 boundaries.
- **Answer impact:** Deliver a useful positive missionary path with exact grants;
  do not expose staff configuration or turn every issue into a new staff queue.
- **Permanent fix:** Existing source/purpose capabilities; source-owned approved
  options and shared UI, no shadow role or per-contact permission system.
- **Exact clause:** “Missionary control applies only to the exact currently
  authorized newsletter actions, content and audience. It grants no broader
  Page, CRM, finance, consent, sender-configuration or protected-history power.”

### PG-R4 — published Update approval is mistaken for newsletter disclosure approval

- **What/why:** Reuse can email protected text/media to a different audience,
  mutate the canonical story, duplicate history or launder private care notes.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High without explicit reuse semantics.
- **Evidence:** P22 D11; P27 D26; current brief187–191/215–221.
- **Answer impact:** Include low-effort reuse with exact eligibility, not blanket
  copy-paste or a second public story source.
- **Permanent fix:** Source-referenced immutable eligible revisions and separately
  admitted newsletter placements, content and audience; preserve provenance.
- **Exact clause:** “Reuse selects exact currently eligible source revisions and
  approved media. Newsletter preparation does not change the canonical Update
  or extend its audience; private CRM, Support or care material cannot be copied,
  quoted, summarized or relabelled into newsletter content.”

### PG-R5 — media parity is asserted despite an explicit still-image-only owner

- **What/why:** Generic media support does not establish safe video/audio custody,
  serving, email representation or restricted-worker behavior.
- **Severity:** High–Critical. **Likelihood:** High if the existing owner is assumed.
- **Evidence:** P22 D9 `468–474` and out-of-scope `886–887`; brief85–91.
- **Answer impact:** Carry verified newsletter media jobs into bounded source
  amendments and proof. Do not silently erase them or import a generic platform.
- **Permanent fix:** Exact accepted formats, source custody/serving and email-safe
  representation; source withdrawal covers reached derivatives. Qualify only
  necessary capability, without waiting for all media/future-phase work.
- **Exact clause:** “Verified newsletter video/audio jobs require an explicit
  compatible media-owner extension and proved email/read behavior. Existing
  still-image approval is not that qualification, and a link or player is not
  safe merely because it renders.”

### PG-R6 — useful replies are replaced by a fake inbox or wrong Reply-To

- **What/why:** Using the missionary's mutable profile email can bypass P17
  destination authority; a new inbox can copy private mail or claim replies that
  arrived only outside Asym.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** High if ordinary Reply-To is treated as arbitrary.
- **Evidence:** P17 `2202–2218`, `3753`; brief111/221; current closed purposes.
- **Answer impact:** Include a real understandable reply job, not an inert button;
  exact native purpose/delegation remains an owner extension.
- **Permanent fix:** Confirmed bounded newsletter reply destination and responsible
  recipient; preserve external-mail versus source-recorded-response truth. Reuse
  P22 response source only for its admitted audience/context.
- **Exact clause:** “Newsletter replies use one qualified native reply purpose
  and confirmed destination with current responsibility. External receipt is
  not Asym interaction history. No mutable profile address, copied mailbox,
  generic inbox or wider contact permission is inferred.”

### PG-R7 — one-off scheduling becomes recurring authority or changed delayed content

- **What/why:** A later worker can send a newer draft, add new subscribers or use
  old permission; editing the schedule can duplicate accepted work.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** High under real retries/concurrent edits.
- **Evidence:** ADR0032; P6 source fence; P25 EX04; Q7 explicit issue decision.
- **Answer impact:** Support useful one-off scheduling under A with exact source
  authority; no need for another routine founder configuration question.
- **Permanent fix:** Exact approved issue, bounded schedule/source generation and
  idempotent correction/cancellation; current narrowing, immutable external work.
- **Exact clause:** “A one-off schedule executes only its authorized issue and
  approved recipient ceiling. Material successor content or widened audience
  requires deliberate new authorization; current exclusions can narrow without
  a new author approval, and ambiguous external work is never rerendered or rekeyed.”

### PG-R8 — parity adds new cadence controls or silently disables existing preferences

- **What/why:** A frequency UI may promise nonexistent behavior, combine approved
  issues without approval, or suppress existing post notices behind the scenes.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Medium–High.
- **Evidence:** P25 EX04; Q7 overlap warning; current brief217; selected A.
- **Answer impact:** Keep reader control through supported purpose preferences;
  neither B nor a new blanket dedupe rule is selected.
- **Permanent fix:** One explicit meaning for every preference and source-purpose
  effect; readable overlap disclosure and real support for any advertised choice.
- **Exact clause:** “Newsletter preferences do not alter Show, post-email or
  protected reading rights. Do not present automatic cadence that A cannot honor,
  silently recombine issues, or suppress a separately authorized purpose merely
  because some content overlaps; semantic retry dedupe remains separate.”

### PG-R9 — print, logs and diagnostics reveal more than the ordinary newsletter

- **What/why:** Label exports, household grouping, attachments, content histories
  and delivery diagnostics can reveal restricted identity or private addresses.
- **Severity:** High–Critical. **Likelihood:** Medium.
- **Evidence:** Brief197–201/219; P22 media semantics; P6 body-free history;
  D6 bounded recipient authority.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve required print and useful results under actual
  source permission; do not remove them as a convenience or assume email grants print.
- **Permanent fix:** Qualified current export/content/destination custody,
  international address and household rules, privacy-minimized diagnosis;
  distinguish each physical and transport outcome.
- **Exact clause:** “Print, labels, export and diagnostics use exact current
  source/custody authority. Email membership does not grant a postal address or
  private history; exported, printed, mailed, accepted, delivered and read remain
  distinct, and downloaded material cannot be promised recall.”

### PG-R10 — newsletter sends become fabricated interactions or coercive analytics

- **What/why:** A batch send or open can become a personal conversation, donor
  interest score, coaching activity target or broad CRM event.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Medium.
- **Evidence:** P27 D26; brief187–191/221/233; platform source boundaries.
- **Answer impact:** Include useful delivery evidence, retain optional voluntary
  actual relationship history and separately qualified coaching.
- **Permanent fix:** Typed source evidence and narrow result projections; no
  behavioral conclusion or wider audience inferred from transport telemetry.
- **Exact clause:** “Delivery and diagnostic events remain communication evidence.
  They do not create a personal exchange, prove reading, measure missionary
  performance, imply donor willingness or grant coaching/CRM visibility.”

### PG-R11 — incomplete owners become an excuse for scope removal or a parallel implementation

- **What/why:** Missing preparation/media/reply/recipient producers may be hidden
  behind a mock, direct provider call or an indefinite future-phase dependency.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** High given recorded producer gaps.
- **Evidence:** Prior inspected #897 gap retained in session; program native
  independence; active planning versus runtime boundary.
- **Answer impact:** Keep included native capabilities in the delivery contract
  while gatekeeping only their activation; independent workspace work continues.
- **Permanent fix:** Exact source adapter and existing allocation trace, capability-
  scoped migration, positive real proof and truthful unavailable states.
- **Exact clause:** “Missing owner qualification blocks that capability's
  activation; it does not delete the accepted newsletter job, authorize a local
  substitute, or make unrelated entire future phases prerequisites.”

## Traceability, rollout and acceptance

Before claiming the review closes product scope, the aggregate should record
the complete newsletter-job ledger and its intentional automatic-digest
difference. An unsupported competitor assertion remains an evidence gap, not
proof that a mandatory feature can be silently dropped or invented.

The later explicitly authorized formal work should map:

1. **Purpose and action:** intended user/job, exact source, permitted missionary/
   reader actions and known owner amendment. Bind normative scopes once, through
   canonical sources and their generated OpenSpec views, not competing prose.
2. **Content and audience:** newsletter draft/issue, selected Update revisions,
   media placements, subscriber/recipient authority, protected/public variants,
   sender/reply destination and print/export policy. Prove a useful positive
   path as well as prohibitions.
3. **Lifecycle:** draft, reviewed/authorized, scheduled, executing, partial,
   complete/unknown/cancelled meanings; current adverse source fences, immutable
   prepared work and retained evidence. Existing P6 mechanics are consumed, not
   reimplemented as a new campaign engine.
4. **Safe adoption:** census existing content, drafts, templates, recipient facts,
   direct senders, settings and histories; preserve accepted source meanings and
   privacy; fence incompatible writers; test mixed versions and safe recovery.
   D6 signup histories and old external requests cannot become new send consent.
5. **Actual proof:** missionary prepares/reuses/resumes an issue on a phone,
   understands current recipients and exclusions, previews/tests, schedules or
   sends once, recovers interruption and partial failure; reader understands
   the newsletter, can reply and withdraw; authorized print work produces usable
   artifacts. Include tenant/cross-purpose denial, media withdrawal, changed
   address/consent, coauthor conflict, unknown provider outcome and retention.
6. **Representative quality evidence:** real task comprehension/accessibility,
   supported clients/media/print output and measured source budgets. Repository
   text, an SDK, a synthetic SQL model, screenshots or competitor marketing alone
   do not prove a fully functional newsletter or exceptional UX.

The exact P6 preparation/sealed-submission producer remains a named affected
gate until current qualification establishes otherwise; this pass did not
re-read the live ticket or change tracker state. New media/reply purpose and
newsletter composition/scheduling contracts are additional capability-specific
qualification, not a demand to finish every P22/P29/P31/P32 feature first.
No invented retention, throughput, subscriber count, cadence, send time or
conversion budget is introduced here.

After those gates pass, monitor bounded residual wrong-audience/duplicate-send
incidents, sender/reply failures, source-reuse drift, prolonged unknown results,
unsubscribe failures, misleading status and repeated user misunderstanding
through existing owners. Monitoring is not a substitute for tenant isolation,
consent, complete source acceptance, useful replies/print or actual delivery proof.

## Explicit status boundaries

- **Founder-selected now:** per-newsletter deliberate authorization; complete,
  understandable missionary control within valid authority; a fully functional,
  integrated newsletter target benchmarked against Epistle at or beyond its
  relevant capabilities.
- **Inherited:** source ownership, current safety/permission, system-message
  exclusion for human newsletters, distinct public/content/subscription/delivery
  truth, native independence, no new general inbox or compulsory shared history.
- **Review interpretation/amendment:** complete preparation/reuse/scheduling/
  reply/media/print journey, bounded owner extensions, job-based parity ledger
  with explicit manual-send difference. Exact external comparisons must be
  evidenced; architectural details are not newly founder-ratified choices.
- **Not authorized by this turn:** automatic future-issue sends, competitor-wide
  MPD/CRM/finance expansion, every future integration, new stack adoption, D6
  ratification, a formal PRD/ADR, tickets, implementation or live sending.

The specificity conflict is resolved by the selected A choice and explicit
parity disclosure. A new founder permission question is not needed merely to
ask whether “fully functional” really means useful drafts, replies, recovery
and printing. If exact owner evidence reveals a materially different product
right that cannot satisfy the chosen job without changing it, surface that
concrete tradeoff rather than a generic request to approve engineering work.
