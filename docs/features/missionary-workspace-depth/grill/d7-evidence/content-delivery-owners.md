# D7 — Native newsletter content and delivery owner review

Research only, 2026-10-16. This note records source facts, proposed narrow owner amendments and proof obligations. It does not amend an adopted PRD/OpenSpec, activate a sender, prove runtime readiness or authorize external mail. D7 selects deliberate missionary authorization of each newsletter, with a complete, understandable native experience and the Epistle parity ambition. D6 native signup was selected and reviewed; this note does not invent separate ratification or reopen its admission decision.

## Finding

The coherent implementation is a P28-owned newsletter issue and deliberate send decision using qualified shared authoring/preparation primitives and the one P6 recipient-specific delivery spine. P22 continues owning any reused Ministry Update and media release. P3/P4/P9/P12 and the qualified D6 native list remain the recipient, contact, consent and authorization owners. The current P17 system-message catalog, layout roles, reply-purpose catalog and prepared-material classes do **not** already qualify human newsletters. Those omissions need explicit bounded owner extensions, not a fabricated system-message key, borrowed financial template, provider Broadcasts store or another scheduler/history pipeline.

The specific D7 choice excludes a standing automatic digest or recurring send authority. It still supports rich drafting, templates, deliberate approved Update reuse, private preview, qualified test copies, a one-off Schedule send decision, edits/cancellation, honest per-recipient results and safe recovery. Content approval or publishing an Update does not authorize a newsletter send. Existing independently qualified post-email notices are not silently disabled by this decision.

## Material concerns, amendments and proof

Severity describes the impact if omitted; likelihood is a design-review estimate rather than measured incident frequency.

### O1 — A native product is falsely declared complete by reusing system-message settings

**High / likely.** S3 explicitly excludes human newsletters from system-message contracts and present Layout Roles, while S2's current compiler resolves exact catalog/binding authority. S1 requires native P28 independently of P32. Neither a template row nor an existing test-send route bridges these contracts.

**Proposed clause:** “P28 owns the native newsletter issue, source occurrence, exact list-purpose audience, draft/review/send authorization and source cancellation/utility fences. Qualify the minimum shared human-authored document, layout, preparation and recipient binding seam through P17/P6. A human newsletter is not registered as an operational system-message meaning. All external copies still traverse P6's sole compiler/intent/preparation/submission/evidence path; no direct provider call, new outbox, provider template/list mirror, or alternate history.”

**Proof:** Native compose→review→schedule/send→results works with P32 and SMS unavailable; arbitrary catalog keys/bindings/raw provider options reject. Trace the real accepted issue through durable source occurrence, complete compiler result, preparation producer and sealed envelope. Identify code ownership for the current preparation gap before activation; documentation completion is insufficient.

### O2 — Public content approval or an editor role becomes newsletter send authority

**Critical for restricted-content disclosure / plausible.** P22 public/supporter release and newsletter delivery are distinct (S5/S6), and P17 publication sends nothing (S3). Contributor status, displayed missionary, spouse, financial access and notification access cannot substitute for a P28 send grant.

**Proposed clause:** “A currently authorized missionary deliberately selects Send now or Schedule send for one exact immutable issue, exact disclosed audience selection and timing. Record that attributable decision independently of content review/release. Satisfy applicable current organization review and P10 safety requirements; review may not waive a failed structural proof. Content approval alone never sends. A new material content, audience, sender/reply, asset, locale or timing decision needs fresh per-issue authorization and any applicable exact review.”

Simplest recommended sequence: prepare, satisfy required review, then confirm Send now/Schedule send. If a later design offers **Submit and send after approval**, its combined control must explicitly authorize the exact issue/audience/timing and explain that consequence; an ordinary Submit for review button must not acquire that meaning. D7 does not itself choose a new mandatory two-human review policy. Reuse the applicable source review owner, and qualify its newsletter use rather than assuming P22's public policy or P17's protected-system policy automatically governs every newsletter.

**Proof:** Reviewer approval without send decision emits no mail; contributor revocation between preview/confirmation/due time stops unstarted dependent work; concurrent edits stale exact authorization; a newer unrelated private draft does not silently replace the approved issue. No spouse/new assignee/history viewer inherits send permission.

### O3 — Approved Update reuse launders protected material or follows mutable latest content

**Critical / likely under naïve copy-and-send.** S5 gives independently admitted public/supporter projections, not universal reuse. S7 separates reading and notifications. S11 forbids laundering private CRM/care/Support material through quotes or summaries.

**Proposed clause:** “Insertion from Ministry Updates selects an exact eligible canonical revision and admitted audience projection, records provenance, and creates deliberate newsletter draft content. It does not create another Update or grant the newsletter audience source-reader rights. Newsletter review covers body, subject, preheader, titles, captions, alt text, assets/thumbnails, link destinations, filenames and quoted material. Current restrictive source/asset dispositions fence dependent unsubmitted delivery. Ordinary later source edits never silently replace pinned issue content.”

For a general native newsletter list, the default reusable projection should be the source-approved public-safe content. A protected projection requires separately proved newsletter disclosure eligibility for every recipient and the channel; “subscriber,” “donor,” an editor's own access or a source hyperlink does not prove it. Do not place protected body/media in email merely because the linked reader would require authentication. A removed source item cannot be silently omitted from an already approved issue to make the remainder send; changed issue meaning needs a new deliberate authorization.

**Proof:** Use markers in private notes, protected title, alt text, EXIF/filename, copied reply and linked thumbnail; none leaks to ordinary newsletter, test, preview, provider tags/history or print. A safety withdrawal before final admission stops dependent copies; already received email is not claimed recalled. Update edit after scheduling never falls forward to latest.

### O4 — Rich authoring is reduced to a textbox, or parity is implemented using unsafe HTML/media

**High / likely scope failure.** S1 requires a complete experience, while S3/S6 have safe structured/pinned primitives. Current P22 D9 rejects video, and current P17 system layouts exclude marketing. This is an actual extension gap rather than permission to drop the selected experience or import an arbitrary editor/provider.

**Proposed clause:** “Provide a native issue draft with recoverable autosave, version conflicts, subject/preheader, readable structured sections, approved reusable layouts, safe image placement with contextual alternative text, links and the required identity/unsubscribe footer. Templates and saved sections copy into drafts or pin immutable dependencies; changes never fan out into scheduled issues. The same qualified server compiler produces HTML and plain text for preview, review, test and recipient preparation. An existing approved source revision may be reused only through its exact projection.”

Qualify a bounded video job explicitly: the email can render an accessible approved poster/text link to an independently authorized source-owned playback destination, with an equivalent plain-text destination. This is not an arbitrary iframe/script/embed in email or a private signed URL published as “media.” P22/P23/P29-compatible media/hosting must supply the exact supported source, safe thumbnail, retention, playback authority and withdrawal contract. Native video upload/transcoding or audio cannot be claimed ready from the current still-image contract; choose the smallest qualified representation that genuinely fulfils the user job and record any unmet parity. No broad P29 completion or new vendor is automatically a prerequisite.

**Proof:** Autosave loss/restore, two editors, offline/reconnect, invalid paste, safe link schemes, screen reader outline, mobile, images blocked, long content, plain text and representative email clients. A template update or media replacement cannot rewrite a scheduled issue. Video preview/link must not expose private media or imply unsupported inline playback.

### O5 — A general newsletter subscription is treated as donor/supporter identity or a live audience filter

**Critical for disclosure / likely.** S2 allows only qualified exact Party/contact or no-Party recipient authority; S7 forbids membership additions and retargeting in a released plan. D6 signup is exact list-purpose permission, not supporter admission.

**Proposed clause:** “At deliberate authorization, freeze a complete source-qualified intended recipient set and its exact recipient/contact revisions, purpose, preference basis, exclusions and content variant. Derive recipients through current source authority, never browser To/CC/BCC, provider contact search, a gift, stage, tag, spouse or email-string merge. Rechecks may narrow this set but cannot add new subscribers, replace excluded recipients, retarget changed email addresses or widen content access. Each external copy exposes one recipient only.”

Show the reviewed denominator and excluded/blocked/physical-only counts only through the missionary's exact current recipient projection. Distinguish recipient/destination counts from unique human counts; a shared mailbox is not proof of one person or combined rights. The final command must validate the reviewed selection/version, not silently accept a stale audience as current. A legitimately new recipient or corrected address requires a new deliberate source-authorized issue occurrence, with existing success/unknown history visible to prevent accidental resend.

**Proof:** New signup after scheduling is excluded; unsubscribe/re-sub does not revive the canceled old member; changed address is not substituted; shared mailbox and Party merge do not union permissions; hidden donors do not leak through counts/search/reasons. N and N+1 source-member limits must be explicit. Resend's 100-message request limit is not a newsletter audience limit. Large-list source qualification must preserve complete frozen membership and bounded P6 compilation; no producer loop releasing a partly accepted audience as though the whole command succeeded.

### O6 — Schedule send creates a second scheduler or indefinite late sending

**High / likely.** S2/S9 own current fences and immutable timing; S10 makes Inngest only an executor. P17:1301 explicitly keeps product scheduling in Asym, even though its vendor-support rationale is now stale (W5 below).

**Proposed clause:** “Schedule send is one deliberate future execution of one issue. The source records the selected local date/time, named time zone, resolved UTC not-before instant, exact authorizing actor and nonextendable utility deadline. Display the resolved time and zone before acceptance and in scheduled state. Reject or explicitly resolve nonexistent/ambiguous daylight-saving times; later browser/account zone changes do not silently move the appointment. Use the existing durable source/outbox/due-intent machinery, not provider scheduled_at, a per-Tenant scheduler or a recurring digest.”

Finite allowed scheduling horizon, maximum late-start allowance and maximum useful retry window need a code-owned qualified native-newsletter profile before launch; no silent unlimited delay. A send delayed beyond its last useful instant becomes expired/needs a fresh deliberate decision, not catch-up after arbitrary downtime. Those constants are product/engineering review judgments, not inherited P25 request seven-day, P17 financial thirty-day, Resend thirty-day scheduling or twenty-four-hour idempotency clocks. Scheduled source content custody is not the same as already sealed recipient provider material; don't create long-lived personalized execution artifacts merely to keep a future issue scheduled.

**Proof:** DST gap/fold, explicit zone change, expired scheduled work, restart after long downtime, queue handoff failure, clock bounds and duplicate wakeups. Schedule acceptance is durable source intent; provider outages after acceptance do not roll it back or claim sent. Readiness known unusable at acceptance needs honest blocked/correction behavior, not accepted work with no recoverable path.

### O7 — Cancel/edit promises recall, or changes the old send in place

**High / likely.** S2/S9 separate definitely unsubmitted from submission may have begun; source invalidation must serialize with actual effect admission. UI navigation/closing a dialog is not server cancellation.

**Proposed clause:** “Cancel scheduled send advances the exact source fence in the same transaction as its accepted cancellation. If it wins before submission admission, affected unstarted copies cannot submit. If some copies were already admitted, accepted or indeterminate, stop only remaining unstarted copies and retain each earlier result without a recall claim. Lost response reconciles the same cancel operation. Canceling or editing does not delete history, unsend mail, change consent or automatically issue a replacement.”

Recommended Edit scheduled issue behavior: explicitly stop the old unstarted authorization and return a new editable draft for review and a new Send/Schedule decision. Do not leave the previous schedule silently running behind the editor. If any copy crossed the boundary, show the partial consequence and prepare a clearly distinct correction only through a new deliberate decision; never relabel it a retry. A user may prepare a separate new draft while an old schedule remains only when that continued old schedule is explicit.

**Proof:** Both orders of cancel versus seal/submission admission, timeout after cancel commit, duplicate cancel, edit while sending, partial batch groups and permission loss. No “Canceled” whole-issue label when admitted/unknown copies remain. Reopening or restoring a draft cannot revive an old send fence.

### O8 — Repair sends a different issue or repeats accepted/unknown recipients

**High / likely.** S9 and P17:2421–2489 contain exact recovery rules. The system-message allowance for a compatible prior publication is unsafe if interpreted as permission to send last month's human newsletter.

**Proposed clause:** “A native human issue has no automatic fallback to another issue, previous content, language, recipient or sender. After author authorization, preparation must preserve the exact approved issue and dependencies. Source, content or authority repair that changes that meaning requires a new explicit decision. A retry is only the exact permitted residual original work; known acceptance and unresolved submission are excluded from a fresh send.”

Provider uncertainty does not forbid every HTTP retry: ADR0032:88–98 permits at most two follow-up calls total for its narrow recognized causes, with identical sealed envelope/key, current safety and utility, and within the provider window. Malformed mapping, contradictory evidence and key/payload conflict are reconcile-only. Definitely rejected work may receive a new attempt only when the exact contract permits it. Batching is subordinate transport; a bad/missing response member cannot be split into new submissions merely to “finish.”

**Proof:** Crash before/after source acceptance, preparation, sealing, provider acceptance and evidence commit; lost response; exact replay after 24 hours; contradictory webhook; strict rejection; malformed/short/duplicate/reordered batch response; connection rotation. Preserve independent recipient counts and safe one-action repair without exposing raw bodies or provider errors. No global “resend failed” includes unknown or accepted members.

### O9 — Withdrawal, email change or a suppressed provider address is bypassed by a scheduled snapshot

**Proposed clause:** “At each final unstarted effect admission re-prove current source authority, exact list-purpose consent, contact revision, content eligibility, safety, sender/reply readiness, provider-suppression evidence and utility. Accepted consent withdrawal fences matching unstarted work immediately. Already admitted/uncertain work reconciles truthfully; it may complete later without proving withdrawal failed. A later fresh opt-in, changed address or provider suppression removal never revives old occurrences.”

Use the D6 qualified visible HTML/plain-text withdrawal route and RFC8058 headers. Preserve the distinction between a fresh human footer-link withdrawal intent and retry of an already accepted provider one-click command; do not silently add login, auto-activate on GET, broaden list scope or let replay undo later fresh confirmed consent. Page-entry Off only closes new public signup intake; it is not consent withdrawal or closing the newsletter purpose.

**Proof:** Withdrawal-versus-final-admission in both orders; unsubscribe/re-sub plus old command replay; old human link deliberate withdrawal; Page-entry Off with existing readers; email correction; complaint/suppression before due time. No force-send, alternate profile/provider/domain/region, auto-physical fallback or consent inferred from address proof.
