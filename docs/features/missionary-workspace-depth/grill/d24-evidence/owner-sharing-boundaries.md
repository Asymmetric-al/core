# D24 evidence — coaching activity-summary ownership and sharing boundaries

Research date: **2026-10-17**. The founder selected **A — optional coaching
activity summaries**. D23's corrected decision and amendments are now ratified.
This note is research for the D24 review; it is not an owner amendment, formal
specification, permission grant, implementation or runtime qualification.

The founder subsequently answered **“Continue into future periods of the selected
target (recommended).”** Continuation is therefore selected. Retaining already
admitted periods from the exact first reviewed period is an explicit proposed D24
amendment, not a separately ratified choice or an inference from D10 financial
history. The remaining exact clauses are review recommendations and later proof
obligations, not implemented behavior.

Only this evidence note was written. No application/schema, formal owner/ADR/
OpenSpec, live setting, provider, dependency, ticket or external communication
was changed. No new database, provider, browser or user experiment was performed.

## Findings that matter before the corrected decision is recorded

**D11 is duration, not future-period admission.** Its selected direction permits
an ongoing share without a user-selected end date, subject to current governance.
The reviewed D11 clauses expressly leave modules and selected historical periods
unchanged when duration changes. Extending a share cannot admit another source,
target, reporting period or history by implication.

**D10 does not answer the activity question.** Its ratified fixed earlier-period
scope concerns support-summary history. It expressly excludes activity history.
It also identifies a fixed start with an advancing end as a separate standing
disclosure choice. An explicit D24 standing activity grant may choose that model;
it cannot be inferred from financial history or from “ongoing.”

**For the now-selected continuation, propose retaining periods deliberately admitted
under that scope.** A current-only view that makes the previous week disappear on
Monday would weaken ordinary coaching. The recommended D24 amendment is an exact
reviewed first period followed by later eligible periods of the same selected
target series, while this module share remains valid. Those admitted periods stay
within this activity share subject to current rights, safety and source retention.
This is an explicit accumulating activity-history grant, not an implication from
D10 or D11. No period before the reviewed start is included. The first reviewed
period can itself begin before the Share click; show its actual dates rather than
invent a cutoff at invitation/save time. No trend chart or arbitrary date query is
needed to make previously admitted periods useful.

**The shared resource is more precise than “Calls made.”** D23 permits several
personal plans and periods over supported measures. Bind each selected source
target/series to its human owner, canonical measure and exact ministry/workspace
purpose. A future replacement, another person's plan or a new scope must not
enter merely because its label or measure matches. A role, project membership or
Goal Version container is not that binding.

**The simplest output is a small typed profile.** Canonical measure, exact admitted
period, qualified target quantity/version, qualified recorded count, truthful
coverage/currentness and safely qualified lifecycle state are sufficient. Personal
target names can contain donor or private context and are excluded from the
recommended initial profile. No supporter identities, source rows, notes, bodies,
event timestamps, drilldown, export, performance rank or write action follows.

**Sharing an aggregate is a separate source purpose.** The coach need not receive
raw event/body SELECT merely to obtain an authorized summary. Conversely, a
missionary's personal count and willingness to share do not bypass source
classification, protected counterparties or current summary-purpose admission.
The aggregate input and released output both require qualification.

## Material concerns — eight dimensions each

Likelihood is qualitative engineering judgment, not a measured incident rate.
Exact clauses are candidate D24 amendments. C01 records the founder's explicit
future-period continuation selection and distinguishes the proposed retention of
already admitted periods. The detailed design has not been separately ratified.

### R01 — Access duration is mistaken for continuing data admission

1. **What:** “No end date” silently exposes new periods, new targets or accumulating
   history even though the missionary approved only particular information.
2. **Why it matters:** This changes substantive disclosure rather than display
   convenience; a clock/calendar rollover can become an unauthorized grant.
3. **Severity:** Critical for unauthorized disclosure.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible if a live dashboard query is reused as sharing scope.
5. **Evidence:** O01 boundary 5; O02:68–75; O05:26–31,53–56,87–93; O06:31–34,167–175.
6. **Answer impact:** A and future-period continuation are now explicitly selected;
   D11 did not supply that answer. Retention of admitted periods is a proposed D24
   amendment that must remain distinct from D10 financial-history scope.
7. **Permanent fix:** Explicit source-owned period-admission semantics, independently
   from episode duration, module eligibility and source retention. Proof P01/P02/P03.
8. **Exact clause C01:** “Activity-summary sharing SHALL continue into later
   eligible periods of the exact selected source target series while the sharing
   basis remains valid. The review SHALL show the exact first admitted period and
   continuing scope. As the proposed D24 history amendment, already admitted periods
   remain part of this activity share under current rights, source retention and
   the qualified profile. No period before that reviewed boundary, replacement
   target or other module follows. This standing activity scope SHALL be explicit;
   coaching duration and D10 financial history are not its authority.”

### R02 — A loose measure label or project grant broadens target identity

1. **What:** A new plan labelled Calls made, another project participant's targets,
   or a reassigned/merged source is admitted under an old target selection.
2. **Why it matters:** Stable display names and subject membership are not the
   actual owner/purpose; automatic joins can expose unrelated people or ministries.
3. **Severity:** Critical.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with measure-only or project-wide identifiers.
5. **Evidence:** O02 D8-C03; O07 C02/C05; O09 canonical project/assignment clauses.
6. **Answer impact:** Requires an exact resource binding without a new target ACL
   framework or team-performance product.
7. **Permanent fix:** Existing typed source references and exact P12 resource/purpose
   scope. A replacement is reviewed rather than inferred from aliases. Proof P04/P05.
8. **Exact clause C02:** “The activity share SHALL identify its Tenant, coaching
   episode/recipient, personal target owner, exact canonical ministry/workspace
   subject and purpose, selected target/series and canonical measure. New targets,
   replacement series, other participants, changed owners or changed ministry/
   measure meaning SHALL not enter because an ID, label, project or membership
   appears related. Required Legal Entity scope follows entity-bearing sources,
   never a mutable organization default or a finance/page alias.”

### R03 — Target ownership or a second hat becomes sharing authority

1. **What:** A target owner assumes a right to disclose all inputs, a coach borrows
   staff rights in the same session, or an administrator configures a ceiling and
   thereby receives personal data or edits the missionary's plan.
2. **Why it matters:** Person, grantor, recipient, target owner and acting authority
   can differ. Their union is not an authorized coaching projection.
3. **Severity:** Critical.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible in multi-hat/multi-tenant dashboards.
5. **Evidence:** O02 D8-C02/C04; O03 C09; O11 A5; O13 current active context; O15.
6. **Answer impact:** Preserves A with exact powers; no new self-coaching ban or
   distinct-person rule is invented for ordinary sharing.
7. **Permanent fix:** Existing P12 current capability resolver and source grantor
   checks; keep configuration, inclusion, source reading and target editing separate.
   Proof P05/P06/P07.
8. **Exact clause C03:** “One validated active Tenant/acting context SHALL govern
   each operation. Organization configuration sets only its admitted ceiling;
   currently authorized sharing commands establish the selected module scope.
   Target ownership, a staff/Coach label, finance rights or another hat SHALL not
   imply source-sharing authority, reads or mutation. Coaching remains read-only.
   Existing independently valid powers remain usable only in their proper context;
   a same-human role switch cannot satisfy ADR0211's distinct-human approval.”

### R04 — Sharing a count is implemented by granting raw source access

1. **What:** Engineers enable event/participant SELECT so an invoker aggregate works,
   or compute all personal facts under service authority and redact only afterward.
2. **Why it matters:** Both approaches bypass the summary-purpose source boundary;
   body removal alone leaves protected counterparties and event existence exposed.
3. **Severity:** Critical.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible under generic reporting/view reuse.
5. **Evidence:** O03 C12; O04 summary-purpose distinction; O07 C13/C14; O10 C08/C12;
   O11/O13/O14.
6. **Answer impact:** Summary eligibility is compatible with aggregate-only access;
   it neither demands raw rights nor authorizes all personally readable inputs.
7. **Permanent fix:** Source-qualified post-floor input projection for this purpose,
   then current field/output admission at release. Proof P07/P08/P09.
8. **Exact clause C04:** “Each activity summary SHALL admit source facts for its
   exact coaching purpose before aggregation and reprove current release authority.
   A separately qualified aggregate-only permission requires no raw event, participant,
   contact or body grant. Implementation SHALL not widen those table/view permissions
   to enable the summary. Personal visibility, a source reference or the missionary's
   willingness to share SHALL not bypass classification, history or source floors.”

### R05 — Private names and the shared Goal Version leak through serialization

1. **What:** Custom target names, financial fields, source IDs or drilldown actions
   are copied from the personal card/Goal Version into the coach's DTO.
2. **Why it matters:** A personal name such as “Call [protected person]” is content,
   while sharing an activity denominator does not publish a whole source container.
3. **Severity:** Critical for protected disclosure; High for unselected scope.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with shared components and object-spread serializers.
5. **Evidence:** O01 boundary 2/3; O07 C02/C13/C14; O08 shared owner; O14 static fields.
6. **Answer impact:** Narrows the initial profile, not the useful summary job.
7. **Permanent fix:** Classified typed summary output and read-only card with no
   unauthorized source action. Proof P08/P10.
8. **Exact clause C05:** “The initial activity-summary profile SHALL contain only
   qualified canonical measure, admitted exact period/scope, target quantity and
   applied revision, recorded count, safe coverage/currentness and lifecycle status.
   Personal target names, donor identities, source activity rows, event timestamps,
   notes/bodies, financial/public fields, drilldown, export and writes are excluded
   unless separately selected and qualified. The shared Goal Version owner SHALL
   not cause whole-object serialization or visibility-as-export permission.”

### R06 — Repeated periods and overlapping measures reveal protected facts

1. **What:** A safe-looking count is combined with another period, current update,
   target percentage, residual, overlapping Calls/Conversations or another subject
   to reveal a protected occurrence or counterparty.
2. **Why it matters:** N-name removal and one small-cell test do not prove the finite
   offered outputs safe together; a percentage can reconstruct a hidden count.
3. **Severity:** Critical for sensitive context.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible for sparse or overlapping source scopes.
5. **Evidence:** O03 C12; O04 finite financial profile precedent; O10 current count
   authority; O13:1664 existing aggregate floor; O14 derived-field classification.
6. **Answer impact:** Requires the real activity-purpose profile to be qualified;
   no new global threshold or promise of safety against all background knowledge.
7. **Permanent fix:** One finite registered profile evaluated across offered
   measures/periods/repeated releases; suppress unsafe outputs and revealing
   complements with a neutral unavailable state. Proof P09/P11.
8. **Exact clause C06:** “Qualify the offered activity profile, its period progression,
   corrected/repeated releases and overlapping measures together under existing
   P3/P10/P12 inference floors. Apply the existing small-cell rule where applicable
   using the owner's defined protected grouping, not a guessed number of calls or
   a copied financial threshold. Withhold unsafe results and revealing complements,
   including ratios, remaining counts, deltas and tooltips. Do not expose the hidden
   exclusion reason or claim that aggregates are anonymous.”

### R07 — Dates, current corrections and snapshot history are conflated

1. **What:** Save time truncates a selected period, imports appear in the wrong
   cohort, old counts freeze permanently, or a new applied quantity is mixed with
   an incompatible target/source version.
2. **Why it matters:** A numerical comparison can be coherent-looking but false;
   fixing it by copying historical snapshots creates a second activity ledger.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible without explicit target/source version and period binding.
5. **Evidence:** O05 fixed dates/current values distinction; O07 C09–C11; O10 dates,
   occurrence identity and current source correction.
6. **Answer impact:** No additional product question is needed for truthful current
   source meaning inside admitted periods.
7. **Permanent fix:** Reuse D23 period geometry, applied denominator versions and
   source corrections under the admitted sharing range. Proof P02/P12/P13.
8. **Exact clause C07:** “The admitted period SHALL retain D23's exact source calendar
   boundaries and actual occurrence meaning. A deliberately reviewed whole period
   may include eligible activity before the Share click; no other earlier period
   follows. Display the applicable target revision and current source-qualified
   count/coverage coherently. Authorized source corrections and in-scope quantity
   changes may update that result; neither creates a period grant or an as-of snapshot.
   Import/create/save time SHALL not substitute for occurrence time.”

### R08 — Target revision silently becomes a new disclosure scope

1. **What:** A source quantity edit unnecessarily demands a new permission ceremony,
   or a calendar/measure/scope change automatically widens the shared data because
   it uses the same series ID.
2. **Why it matters:** Treating every edit as a grant creates friction; treating
   every revision as already authorized exposes materially different information.
3. **Severity:** High; Critical when the revision changes protected scope.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible when content version and authorization version are conflated.
5. **Evidence:** O05 standing ranges; O07 C05/C11; O14 routine edits versus policy widening.
6. **Answer impact:** Requires a bounded transition contract, not new ACL machinery.
7. **Permanent fix:** Separate source target/quantity versions, admitted period profile
   and share scope. Preserve D23 prospective geometry without granting through it.
   Proof P03/P04/P12/P14.
8. **Exact clause C08:** “An in-scope target-quantity edit is source content, not a
   new share grant, and SHALL follow the selected period's target-version meaning.
   Changing owner, ministry/workspace purpose or canonical measure requires a new
   explicit sharing choice. Calendar/schedule changes SHALL preserve D23's prospective
   boundary and be requalified against the captured sharing/profile scope; a change
   beyond that scope requires deliberate sharing review before release. Source or
   transport revision alone SHALL never be authorization identity.”

### R09 — Removing a module revokes too much, or a toggle revives ended rights

1. **What:** Removing activity summaries ends financial/coaching access unnecessarily;
   re-enable restores old target/history grants; or temporary source withholding is
   treated as terminal revocation and requires a new invitation.
2. **Why it matters:** Users lose control or useful independent access, and delayed
   recovery can republish data they deliberately stopped sharing.
3. **Severity:** Critical for revived disclosure; High for destructive coupling.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with one generic active flag or toggle.
5. **Evidence:** O02 C04; O03 C07/C08; O06 C05–C07; O07 target lifecycle is separate.
6. **Answer impact:** Clarifies source lifecycle under either fixed or continuing scope.
7. **Permanent fix:** Existing module/episode boundaries, current release fences and
   truthful separate saved-choice/effective-access/source-availability states.
   Proof P15/P16/P17.
8. **Exact clause C09:** “Removing this module SHALL end only its exact dependent
   activity-summary access; independently valid coaching/financial rights and
   source records remain. Ending the episode or its applicable source grant SHALL
   fence all dependent release and cannot be reversed by retry, restore, old links
   or policy relaxation. Temporary source/privacy/eligibility withholding is distinct
   from an ended grant and may recover only while the original sharing basis remains
   valid. A stopped module needs a fresh deliberate currently authorized inclusion.”

### R10 — Preview, Save and caches do not represent the same current grant

1. **What:** Preview shows the missionary's broader personal view, a stale save
   widens a newer scope, or a coach sees cached results after removal/expiry.
2. **Why it matters:** A visually simple control can misdescribe who receives what;
   asynchronous completion does not make an old permission current.
3. **Severity:** Critical for disclosure; High for lost/ambiguous user intent.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with multi-tab/mobile recovery and reusable caches.
5. **Evidence:** O02:41–48; O03 C13; O06 cutoff/recovery; O10 C05; O13 view-as; O15 recovery.
6. **Answer impact:** Requires existing owner command and current-authority mechanics,
   not repeated confirmations or a second approval for routine eligible inclusion.
7. **Permanent fix:** Exact reviewed scope and version, source-owned atomic mutation,
   idempotent result recovery, read-only preview under recipient and previewer bounds.
   Proof P06/P16/P18/P19.
8. **Exact clause C10:** “The existing sharing command SHALL bind trusted actor,
   recipient, exact target/period/profile scope, expected revision and semantic
   operation identity, atomically retaining required audit/outcome. Preview uses
   the proposed coach-purpose projection bounded also by the previewer's current
   authority; it is not impersonation or the personal dashboard. Recheck at apply
   and protected release, fence stale results and partition cache by exact current
   context/scope. Recover a lost response once without restoring a narrowed grant.”

### R11 — Numerical coaching becomes surveillance or a portfolio performance score

1. **What:** Missing rows become inactivity, a coach can change targets, new measures
   appear automatically in a portfolio, or personal/context totals are summed/ranked.
2. **Why it matters:** Voluntary planning and source-qualified read-only coaching
   become an unselected methodology and disclosure product.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible when importing sales/coaching dashboard conventions.
5. **Evidence:** O02:41–66; O03 C09/C11; O07 non-additive targets; O10 C01/P33.
6. **Answer impact:** Does not invalidate A; preserves its voluntary read-only purpose.
7. **Permanent fix:** Deliberate inclusion, neutral recorded-evidence labels and
   exact independent subject cards; no ranking, write or secondary effect.
   Proof P05/P07/P20.
8. **Exact clause C11:** “Activity summaries SHALL describe eligible recorded
   evidence, not all real activity, diligence or relationship quality. Missing,
   partial, withheld and unavailable remain distinct from complete permitted zero.
   There SHALL be no ranking, combined effort score, compulsory report, coach edit,
   automatic message/task, public publication or financial effect. Multi-subject
   coaching enumerates independently permitted contexts; it does not add overlapping
   personal/weekly/monthly figures into team or portfolio performance totals.”

### R12 — Owner intent or old fixtures are promoted to deployed sharing proof

1. **What:** D9/D10 synthetic grant mechanics or D23 counterexamples are cited as
   proof of a new live activity-summary producer, source floors or safe history.
2. **Why it matters:** New purpose/profile/target joins remain untested while the
   module appears fully authorized and accurate; source gaps can be hidden by stubs.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible without explicit evidence classification and release gates.
5. **Evidence:** O01 proof limits; O07 D23 remaining actual integration gates;
   O15 intended active owner changes; no D24 runtime experiment in this note.
6. **Answer impact:** A is selected direction; actual activation remains gated.
7. **Permanent fix:** Trace exact accepted D24 scope into the P28/P9/P27/P6/P12
   owner changes and exercise the actual composed paths. Reuse meaningful earlier
   evidence with original limits; do not add a seeded lab merely for another count.
   Proof P01–P21.
8. **Exact clause C12:** “Activation SHALL require the actual target/source/period/
   sharing adapters, source-purpose and current release authority, scope-changing
   races, privacy profile, restore and user-understanding proof. Formalize the
   selected target lineage and period policy before exposing it. Missing capability
   remains unavailable without fake numbers or wider source grants. Research and
   prior fixture results SHALL retain their exact limits and are not runtime proof.”
