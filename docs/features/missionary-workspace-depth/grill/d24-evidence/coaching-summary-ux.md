# D24 — Optional coaching activity summaries: UX evidence

Research date: **October 17, 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. Research and proposed acceptance
design only. The founder selected optional activity summaries when the
organization permits: Maya deliberately shares eligible counts, targets and
periods; Jordan reads the summary without supporter names, bodies or individual
activity records. Target creation does not share it. D23 is ratified.

**Temporal answer selected:** continue into future periods of the same selected
target. The main review's proposed precise profile starts with one explicitly
reviewed first whole period, then admits qualified future periods of that target
while sharing remains active. Already admitted periods remain under current
rights, duration and qualified retention bounds. No earlier period, new target or
materially changed scope enters automatically. Correcting counts inside an
admitted period remains different from admitting a new period. These detailed
review amendments are proposed, not separately ratified.

No app, schema, formal owner/specification, live setting, provider, contact,
dependency or external message changed. No authenticated vendor test, prototype,
user interview, capacity test or accessibility-conformance result is claimed.

## Recommended small complete shape

Put **Activity summary** in the existing **Coaching → Jordan → Sharing** view.
Keep the existing organization, missionary/ministry and intended coach visible.
Maya selects eligible targets/measures and exact period scope, reviews a compact
permitted summary, and uses **Save sharing** once. The same page shows the saved
scope and **Stop sharing activity summary**. It does not require another coach
invitation, a report designer, a dashboard builder or a separate weekly report.

Jordan opens one authorized coaching context from the existing portfolio. The
summary uses canonical measure labels, exact dates, recorded counts, applicable
target quantities and honest coverage/currentness. The plain text is useful on a
phone without a chart. The summary does not expose source-history links, private
target names, donor detail, cross-person totals or ranking. Five calls including
two conversations remain two overlapping measures, never seven activities.

These are product/UX recommendations constrained by the selected outcome and
owners below. Source qualification, current permission and inference resistance
remain implementation gates; a convincing preview is not proof of any of them.

## Selected temporal direction: keep the UI small

There are three independent questions: which target/source scope is selected,
which periods are admitted, and how long the coach relationship/share is valid.
A source correction inside an admitted period changes its truthful value; it does
not add a period. D11's **No scheduled end** does not answer period admission.

| Compared temporal approach                                       | Useful behavior                                                                                                                                         | Cost and exact limits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Explicit selected periods — considered alternative, not selected | Preview enumerates concrete target/period pairs. New periods require another deliberate change. Counts can still reflect authorized source corrections. | Narrow and easy to inspect, but repeated weekly sharing adds clerical work. Do not implement this extra mode merely because it was compared.                                                                                                               |
| Future periods of the same selected target — selected direction  | Maya deliberately authorizes that target beginning with an exact reviewed first period; qualified future periods follow while sharing is active.        | Proposed precise profile retains admitted periods under current rights, duration and qualified retention bounds. It includes no earlier period, new target, replacement scope or new measure automatically. A one-off target has no new recurring periods. |

Use a plain summary such as **“Starts with [exact first-period dates]. Future
periods of this target are included while sharing is active. Shared periods stay
available within the applicable access and history limits.”** The actual
source-qualified limit must be understandable when it affects access; do not
invent a retention duration or imply permanent history. Earlier periods require
their own authorized deliberate inclusion, not an automatic lookback.

Do not use only **Keep updated**, which could mean corrections, fresh counts,
future periods or all future targets. The founder selected continuation, so a
second mode selector or general recurrence/access-rule builder is unnecessary.
The first reviewed whole period may contain dates before Save; show them. Later
qualified corrections and applicable target revisions update admitted periods
without a repeated sharing ritual, while changed source/target scope remains
outside the original admission.

Invitation acceptance after a period rolls over does not reset the reviewed first
period to the then-current one. Organization Off fences the prior activity scope;
On makes fresh deliberate inclusion possible but does not revive that old scope.
Where the coaching relationship itself remains qualified, this needs no new
coach invitation. Access duration and required current reviews still apply.

## Maya's complete journey

### Choose and understand

Start in the existing recipient-specific sharing page, with **Sharing with Jordan
Lee**, **[Organization]**, and **For [authorized workspace/ministry]** visible.
This is not an all-coach setting. Existing independently shared support information
and access duration remain visible without repeating their whole setup.

Use a small **Activity summary** section. **Choose activity summaries** opens or
reveals eligible target/period choices. Initial inclusion is empty until Maya
chooses; an existing share loads its actual saved selection. Do not silently
preselect all four measures because she created four personal targets.

Each choice identifies canonical measure, exact source scope, period and target
quantity. There can be several Calls made targets with different periods or
quantities, or even identical visible fields and different private names. Maya's
own currently authorized selector may show her private alias marked **Not shared
in this summary** to identify the target. That owner-authorized selection payload
is distinct from the coach projection and preview. One checkbox named **Calls**
must not ambiguously grant all targets.
Use the existing target identity behind clearly labelled rows. Group exact
periods where helpful without inventing a new coaching calendar. If all choices
fit, show them directly; introduce search/paging only when needed, never a hidden
all-pages selection or an unexplained arbitrary product cap.

Personal target names can contain donor names, locations or care context. The
minimal coach projection uses **Calls made**, **Conversations**, **Personal
messages**, **Meetings held** and safe admitted context. It does not copy Maya's
private alias into coaching-preview payloads, Jordan's page, coach receipts, URLs,
notifications, analytics or exports merely because the count is permitted.
An alias visible in Maya's selector requires her own current field authority;
it does not become a shared label or an AI-generated substitute.

### Review and save

One compact inline preview, with an expandable full reading view only if useful:

> Dates follow this target's calendar.  
> Starts with this period; future periods of this target are included while sharing is active.  
> **Save sharing**

Dates/quantities are fictional examples, not defaults. Expose a geographic
timezone/city or calendar label only when source-qualified for this audience;
otherwise use the safe calendar explanation above. Exact period dates remain
mandatory. Include concise **What
counts** help, particularly that Calls made includes unanswered calls and Personal
messages records sent/provider-accepted outreach without confirmed delivery.

The preview contains a complete compatible permitted count/target comparison or
**Recorded count unavailable**. An independently permitted target may remain,
but no partial numerator may appear against the full personal target. The preview
represents **this proposed share**, not every piece of information
Jordan might independently be authorized to see. Generate it through the exact
proposed coaching projection and Maya's own current preview rights. Do not fetch
Jordan's entire view with impersonation or fetch all private history and hide
columns in the browser. If the preview is incomplete, unavailable or no longer
permitted, explain that truth and do not mislabel a placeholder as verified.

Select/check changes update draft state and its proposed review; they do not
grant access. One **Save sharing** submits the deliberate scope. For an existing
share, indicate newly added/removed eligible rows before Save without another
confirmation page. The source owns current validation, expected revision and
durable acceptance. A correction after preview may change the displayed recorded
count within unchanged scope; silently adding another target/period is different
and forbidden. Do not freeze source facts to make a preview appear exact forever.

After acceptance, show a persistent **Activity summary shared with Jordan** and
the accepted scope. If access is still invitation-pending, say **Included in the
pending invitation** rather than claiming Jordan can already view it. Sharing
is not notification delivery: no automatic email, notification or new task is
needed to complete D24. Any later notice requires its separate owner/choice.

## Mobile and accessibility acceptance design

Use the existing shared layout and a single primary action. Keep recipient,
organization and scope visible near the consequential action. Long international
names and translated canonical labels wrap; do not rely on a truncated name,
avatar or color alone to distinguish recipients or contexts. Exact dates retain
the source calendar. A geographic IANA zone/city or other revealing calendar label
is not automatically disclosed; show only a source-qualified safe label or
**Dates follow this target's calendar.** Localizing display cannot move D23 periods.

Group selection controls with labels/legend and associated help. Preserve logical
keyboard order, visible focus, explicit names for Change/Stop actions and a clear
return target after closing preview. Inline preview updates should not steal
focus. If a dialog is used, its existing primitive owns containment and dismissal;
the composition still needs manual verification. No nested modal or hover-only
definition is necessary.

Check non-excepted content at equivalent 320 CSS-pixel width, text resizing and
desktop zoom; a simple stacked list avoids unnecessary two-dimensional scrolling.
Use adequately sized labelled hit areas; test adjacent period controls against
SC2.5.8 rather than quoting an incorrect universal 44-pixel AA rule. Qualifying
save/error/update status needs programmatic announcement and persistent visible
truth, without announcing every count refresh. A table uses real header/data
relationships; cards use meaningful headings/labels. Charts never replace text.

Weak connections must allow recovery through the original accepted operation,
preserve still-authorized draft input, and keep independent navigation useful.
Do not add a protected offline activity cache or claim offline stop succeeded.
Automatic accessibility checks supplement manual phone, keyboard and assistive-
technology work; this note reports no executed UI test or conformance result.

## Material concerns: eight dimensions, exact clauses and proofs

Likelihood is qualitative review judgment, not a measured incident frequency.
The clauses below are proposed requirements for the main synthesis, not formal
owner changes or evidence that implementation exists.

### U01 — A saved filter is mistaken for a permission boundary

1. **Failure:** Preview hides donor rows but the coach can clear a date filter, follow a source link or fetch private target names.
2. **Importance:** Names omitted from the visible card do not make the underlying disclosure safe.
3. **Severity:** Critical.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible when adapting a general dashboard or personal view.
5. **Evidence:** D8-C12; D23-C14; S05/S06 explicitly separate views/filters from underlying access.
6. **Decision impact:** Retain A with an actual summary-purpose projection, not browser redaction.
7. **Permanent fix:** Source-admitted aggregate output, canonical safe labels, no raw-history endpoint/grant, preview under exact current rights.
8. **Exact clause UX24-C01:** “The coaching summary and its preview SHALL release only the exact currently authorized summary projection. Filters, hidden columns, personal target visibility and client rendering SHALL NOT establish authority. No supporter identity, private alias/body or individual activity record/link SHALL be admitted by this module.”

**Proof:** Coach attempts direct/modified links, arbitrary date parameters, private
aliases and stale payloads; previewer has fewer rights than recipient. Maya can
distinguish identical-looking targets through her own authorized private aliases,
but no alias crosses into the coaching preview/projection. No raw or hidden data
is released, and safe summary-only access works without raw SELECT.

### U02 — Ongoing access silently grows into new targets or periods

1. **Failure:** D11 ongoing sharing, a new month, a target duplicate or a changed scope broadens the activity grant without review.
2. **Importance:** The same settings label may conceal materially more history or future disclosure.
3. **Severity:** Critical for unauthorized disclosure; high for comprehension.
4. **Likelihood:** Likely if “Keep updated” is the only temporal contract.
5. **Evidence:** Q24 reserves future/history meaning; D10 distinguishes periods/values; D11 distinguishes duration; S01's automatic report windows are vendor behavior only.
6. **Decision impact:** Preserve the selected future-period direction with the precise reviewed first-period and retained-history profile proposed by the main review.
7. **Permanent fix:** Record exact target identity and first whole period; separately represent source corrections, qualified future-period admission, history limits and share duration.
8. **Exact clause UX24-C02:** “The accepted sharing scope SHALL identify the selected target/measure, source purpose and exact first reviewed whole period. Qualified future periods of that same target SHALL continue while sharing remains active; already admitted periods SHALL remain subject to current rights, duration and qualified retention bounds. No earlier period, new target or materially changed scope SHALL enter automatically. Corrections and applicable target revisions within admitted scope SHALL retain current source truth without creating a new sharing ceremony.”

**Proof:** Period rollover, new target with the same label, target scope replacement,
late entry/correction, end-date extension and stop/restart. Verify same-target
continuation and retained admitted periods within qualified bounds; no test may
infer arbitrary earlier history or permanent retention. Acceptance after rollover
retains the reviewed first period; organization Off/On requires fresh inclusion
without automatic old-scope restoration or needless re-invitation.

### U03 — Preview and save give a misleading audience or acceptance claim

1. **Failure:** Maya previews her richer personal view, selects a checkbox that shares immediately, or sees “shared” before invitation acceptance/current authorization.
2. **Importance:** Disclosure cannot be meaningfully reviewed or reversed from a misleading state.
3. **Severity:** High, critical if forbidden data is shown.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with generic dashboard cloning or optimistic toggles.
5. **Evidence:** D8 preview and invitation boundaries; S03 acceptance; S07 review; S11 predictable input.
6. **Decision impact:** Use one short deliberate Save and a bounded preview, not a permission wizard.
7. **Permanent fix:** Separate draft/proposed/saved/pending/active states and render exact recipient/subject beside the action.
8. **Exact clause UX24-C03:** “Selection and preview SHALL NOT activate sharing. The review SHALL identify the recipient, organization, authorized source context and intended disclosure, bounded also by the previewer's rights. One deliberate qualified Save SHALL establish the accepted scope; pending or ambiguous results SHALL NOT be labelled active sharing.”

**Proof:** Change selections without Save, lose Save response, revoke while previewing,
open the wrong organization, and save for an invitation-pending coach. UI and source
state agree; no richer personal preview or invitation-delivery-as-access claim.

### U04 — Multiple targets and overlapping measures imply false performance

1. **Failure:** Calls and Conversations become additive, identical labels collapse different periods, or low recorded counts imply effort/quality.
2. **Importance:** A useful coaching aid becomes a misleading performance report.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Likely with generic KPI totals and percentage charts.
5. **Evidence:** Ratified D23 units/versions; S01/S02 provide comparable jobs, not Core formulas; D8 voluntary history.
6. **Decision impact:** Keep several clear rows without a summed score or dashboard builder.
7. **Permanent fix:** Canonical measure, exact target/period identity, recorded-only wording, no rank/deficit/inferred quality.
8. **Exact clause UX24-C04:** “Each shared row SHALL retain canonical measure, exact period/source scope and applicable target version/state. A count comparison SHALL be complete, source-compatible and currently qualified; otherwise show unavailable actual, with independently permitted target information only. No partial numerator SHALL be compared with the full personal target. Overlapping targets/measures SHALL NOT be added, averaged or scored as unique activity or effort.”

**Proof:** Five calls/two conversations; weekly/monthly Calls made targets; temporary
quantity edit; accepted email without delivery; counts over target; pause/end.
Participants can explain the numbers without inventing seven activities or a score.

### U05 — Missing, withheld, updating and zero collapse into one state

1. **Failure:** A timeout/default progress value shows zero; a missing module reveals private target existence; stale counts survive revocation.
2. **Importance:** False precision causes mistaken coaching judgments and privacy leaks.
3. **Severity:** High; critical for revoked/hidden data release.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible given the existing Progress undefined-to-zero default.
5. **Evidence:** Local source recheck; D23 coverage rules; D8 exact disclosure; S10 status guidance.
6. **Decision impact:** Honest states are necessary, not optional polish.
7. **Permanent fix:** Qualified completeness/currentness, current release checks and distinct neutral states without hidden-count hints.
8. **Exact clause UX24-C05:** “Complete authorized zero, unavailable, updating, partial source, paused, absent and no-longer-authorized states SHALL remain distinct without exposing forbidden reasons. Displayed counts SHALL describe recorded evidence only. Unavailable actual SHALL NOT be reconstructed through percentage, remaining, met flags, bar values, ARIA or other derivatives. Omission/status copy SHALL NOT reveal hidden membership; calendar labels require source qualification; stale responses SHALL NOT restore revoked content.”

**Proof:** True zero, undated evidence, failed refresh, partial source, no module
right, revoked old tab and multi-context switch. No false zero, hidden exclusion
count, false ended-share claim or stale protected value.

### U06 — Stop is blocked or affects the wrong thing

1. **Failure:** Invalid unsaved additions prevent stopping; Stop ends all coaching or personal targets; a retry revives access.
2. **Importance:** A missionary cannot promptly control the exact optional disclosure.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with a single generic settings Save or Delete action.
5. **Evidence:** D8/D11 separate module/lifecycle authority; D23 target pause differs from sharing; S03 documents removal but not Core's module semantics.
6. **Decision impact:** Provide a precise independent stop using the existing owner.
7. **Permanent fix:** Stop only the current module scope, independent of draft validity, durable receipt recovery and honest effect language.
8. **Exact clause UX24-C06:** “Stop sharing activity summary SHALL be independently usable without accepting unsaved additions. Its accepted effect SHALL end this share's activity-module access, not personal targets, source recording or independent modules/rights. A lost response SHALL recover the original operation; no success, revival or recall of already seen information SHALL be invented.”

**Proof:** Invalid draft plus Stop; stop/Save race; timeout after accepted stop;
other module remains; coach has another independently qualified right; attempted
old-link/retry revival fails. Input is not silently saved while stopping.

### U07 — A multi-person coaching portfolio becomes surveillance or data export

1. **Failure:** Portfolio totals, default progress badges, private titles, export or staff-hat actions expose more than one selected summary.
2. **Importance:** Aggregation and convenience can defeat per-recipient/subject consent and create pressure.
3. **Severity:** Critical for unauthorized disclosure; high for voluntary-use harm.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with CRM leaderboards and multi-tenant dashboard reuse.
5. **Evidence:** D8 portfolio/current-role boundary; D24 read-only selection; S05 generic admin powers explicitly not adopted; S04 varied coaching practices.
6. **Decision impact:** Keep a complete multi-context reading job without comparative reporting or new writes.
7. **Permanent fix:** Permission-filtered portfolio, one scoped detail, no raw drilldown/delegation/export or default attention effect.
8. **Exact clause UX24-C07:** “Portfolio and detail SHALL enumerate only currently admitted coaching contexts and summary outputs. D24 SHALL NOT add cross-person totals/rankings, performance alerts, raw-record drilldown, exports, delegation, coach editing or automatic tasks/messages. Existing independent capabilities SHALL require their explicit authorized context.”

**Proof:** Multi-coach, multi-project, same human in two organizations, staff+coach
hats and a single permitted summary amid hidden targets. No foreign context,
portfolio inference, pressure badge or unexpected action is exposed.

### U08 — Review and recovery are inaccessible or needlessly cumbersome

1. **Failure:** Dense grids, tiny checkboxes, hover-only meaning, focus jumps or toast-only results prevent safe phone/keyboard use.
2. **Importance:** Optional sharing becomes difficult precisely when users need confidence in scope.
3. **Severity:** High for inaccessible consequential actions; medium for friction.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with generic analytics and nested modal composition.
5. **Evidence:** S07–S12; exact Maia rule; local primitive observations, not browser proof.
6. **Decision impact:** One shared form and readable summary are sufficient; no new component dependency.
7. **Permanent fix:** Semantic labelled controls, inline review, logical focus, sufficient hit areas, reflow and persistent outcome/recovery.
8. **Exact clause UX24-C08:** “Manage/preview/save/update/stop and coach reading SHALL remain operable with keyboard, touch and assistive technology at qualified reflow/zoom. Essential audience, unit, date, state and outcome SHALL NOT rely on hover, color, charts or transient toast. Preserve still-authorized input on recoverable failures without new protected offline storage.”

**Proof:** Narrow phone, 320 CSS-pixel reflow, text resizing, long localized names,
screen-reader table/list navigation, keyboard selection, focus return, adjacent
touch controls, slow Save and permission loss. Check semantics and behavior with
manual tests in addition to automated checks; do not report either as performed here.
