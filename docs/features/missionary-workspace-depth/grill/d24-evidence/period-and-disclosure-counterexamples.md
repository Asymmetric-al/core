# D24 — Coaching-summary periods and aggregate disclosure

Research date: **October 17, 2026 UTC / Asia/Bangkok**. The founder selected
optional coaching activity summaries. This is one evidence workstream, not a
formal owner amendment, deployed feature or approval to disclose live records.
The founder subsequently selected **Continue into future periods of selected
target**. Continued periods of that selected target are therefore the selected
direction, not an inference from D11's ongoing duration. Retaining periods already
admitted from the exact reviewed first period is an explicit proposed amendment,
subject to current rights, source retention and the qualified output profile;
it is not permission for earlier history.

**Accept with required amendments.** Share an understandable summary of the
selected target and qualified recorded activity through the existing coaching
projection. Keep the target denominator, source facts and permission scope under
their existing owners. The named missionary is intentionally identified; this
is not an anonymized dataset. Protected supporter facts may still be inferred
from a count, date, percentage, difference or combination of allowed summaries.

## Period scope is separate from duration, source correction and target editing

Four distinct questions must remain separate in source state and clear in review:

1. **How long is the coaching episode active?** D11 governs the end condition.
2. **Which target/measure/subject is included?** Exact selected target identity
   plus its admitted definition/scope; a personal name is not a permission key.
3. **Which periods are included, including future and historical behavior?**
   The founder selected continuation into future periods of the selected target.
   The proposed amendment deliberately retains already admitted periods from the
   exact reviewed first period while current rights, retention and the qualified
   profile allow. A filter, `ends_at = null` or financial-history permission cannot
   add earlier periods, another target or materially changed scope.
4. **What values describe an already admitted target-period now?** Recommended:
   its source-owned currently applied target quantity/version and currently
   corrected eligible recorded facts, with current output qualification. Fixed
   dates are not an as-known-when-shared snapshot.

For the fourth question, follow the applied denominator for that particular
period. If Maya changes the current quantity from eight to four under D23, the
admitted summary should update truthfully rather than preserve a shadow coach
goal of eight. D23 preserves earlier closed-period denominators; this does not
backcast the new value into earlier weeks. Show the applicable quantity/version
meaning where needed. Recommended help: **Shared summaries update when you change
this period's target or correct recorded activity.**

Following an ordinary quantity change does not authorize a new target, canonical
measure, ministry subject, calendar interpretation or wider period set. A same-ID
replacement may materially change those meanings and needs the existing sharing
review/current authority. Target pause/end is target lifecycle; share removal is
disclosure lifecycle. Neither should silently erase the other's truthful history
or treat a paused target as a zero denominator.

Continuation must state what happens when an admitted period becomes past.
The proposed amendment retains those already admitted periods while the current
share, source retention and output profile permit it. This is explicit accumulating
activity-history scope from the reviewed first period, not a moving current-only
window or a promise of permanent retention. D10's financial precedent separates
these meanings without granting activity history. Show the first period's exact
dates even when its whole-period start precedes Save; never add earlier periods.
One-off custom targets have no recurring future periods to invent.

## Minimal exact proposed clauses

**PD01 — Specific optional module.** Activity summaries SHALL be deliberately
included in one existing qualified coaching share within current organization
limits. Bind exact tenant, coach context, typed subject, target, canonical measure
and output profile. Target creation, invitation acceptance, an ongoing episode
or financial-history permission SHALL NOT include this module or new targets.

**PD02 — Selected continuation and explicit retained periods.** Maya SHALL
deliberately share the selected target from the exact reviewed first period
onward. Qualified future periods of that same target SHALL continue while sharing
remains active. As an explicit proposed amendment, already admitted periods SHALL
remain available only while current rights, duration, source retention and the
qualified output profile allow. Review SHALL explain this retention and show the
first whole period's exact dates, including reviewed dates earlier than Save.
No period before that first period, new target or materially changed
measure/subject/calendar scope SHALL enter automatically. Each offered/released
query requires finite source-qualified canonical period bounds; this does not
force an end date onto the selected ongoing rule. View filters and duration edits
SHALL NOT widen this scope. One-off targets SHALL not invent recurring periods.
Separate deliberate inclusion uses the existing command; no historical query or
cohort/report builder follows.

**PD03 — Live source meaning for admitted periods.** Within currently admitted
dates, the summary SHALL consume that period's currently applied P28 target
quantity/version and current qualified recorded activity. Explain that ordinary
target changes and source correction can update the displayed summary. Preserve
source target/correction history; do not create a coaching snapshot or second
target store. Closed-period denominators retain D23 meaning. Different target
and activity sources may carry separate currentness evidence; do not demand an
unnecessary global transaction or falsely imply that stale data is current.

**PD04 — Input and output qualification.** The trusted producer SHALL admit
constituent facts for the exact activity-summary purpose under current source,
subject, history, field and privacy floors before arithmetic. Independently
authorize release of the exact resulting coach projection. This SHALL require
no raw constituent SELECT, supporter directory, message-body access or activity
drilldown for the coach. Post-count redaction and a privileged service credential
do not replace source admission. The metric must identify its actual admitted
recorded cohort rather than imply all real personal activity.

**PD05 — Offered combinations and privacy.** Register and qualify the finite
offered profile and its combinations under P3/P10/P12. Apply the existing
small-cell floor with an explicitly qualified protected population/contribution
unit; activity-event count is not automatically distinct-person population.
Evaluate repeated releases, overlapping periods/subjects, corrected values,
known complements, percentages, remaining-to-target and all secondary output.
Withhold unsafe values and offered reconstructing complements with uniform safe
states, or use only source-qualified honestly labelled coarsening. No arbitrary
noise, new universal N, anonymity guarantee or generic privacy engine is selected.

**PD06 — Compatible comparison.** A target-versus-count comparison SHALL use the
same admitted target definition, actor/subject, period/calendar and compatible
coverage. A permitted subset or unavailable component SHALL not be presented as
complete personal attainment. Missing/private/paused/absent target and unknown or
incomplete actuals SHALL not create a percentage, false zero or failure score.
An independently admitted target quantity can remain useful when a count cannot
be released. A withheld numerator SHALL not reappear through a percentage,
remaining figure, bar, accessible label, total or hidden JSON.

**PD07 — Current release and derived state.** Cache/result identity and release
validation SHALL bind exact tenant, coach/subject, sharing episode/revision,
target definition/applied-period revision, source coverage/revision and current
projection authority. Current restrictions invalidate or suppress affected
values; late responses after context or grant change are discarded. A previously
valid computation is not continuing permission. Do not combine independently
stale numerator/denominator values as a current comparison or expose hidden
excluded-row counts to explain a changed result.

**PD08 — Corrections do not change grants.** Current source correction, late entry,
deduplication or lawful deletion SHALL rederive admitted period membership and
actuals without altering the selected sharing dates or fabricating an occurrence.
Correction/import/update time is not membership. A fact moved outside admitted
dates leaves that result; its new destination is not thereby shared. Unknown or
temporarily unavailable qualification is not zero or source invalidation. Values
may recover inside an unchanged valid grant when source qualification returns;
removed/narrowed/ended rights SHALL not recover through that mechanism.

**PD09 — Clear summary-only journey.** Review canonical measure, exact target,
permitted recorded count or safe availability state, dates, subject and update
meaning in the existing Sharing flow. Personal labels, notes, locations and
custom strings are not automatically public-safe sharing fields. Preview SHALL
use the coach's qualified output within the previewer's own authority, not copy
Maya's broader personal dashboard. The coach gets no View activity route, raw
records, hidden source IDs or extra permissions through a card. No ranking,
mandatory log, penalty, notification, export or new task follows from this module.

**PD10 — Atomic source command and bounded recovery.** Scope/duration/selection
changes SHALL use current trusted identity/subject, expected sharing revision,
durable exact result and required atomic audit. Validate the allowed old and
proposed new state; repeated/late requests cannot restore removed periods or
silently switch targets. Temporary source withholding, saved selection and
terminal share state remain distinct. Recover through existing source commands
and bounded rebuild, not raw SQL edits or a shadow permission/target ledger.

## New executed evidence and its limits

Ran [counterexample-probe/probe.py](counterexample-probe/probe.py): **18/18 finite
counterexamples reproduced**, Python **3.12.14**. Exact inputs and observations
are retained in [results.json](counterexample-probe/results.json). This is new
D24 work; no D23 calendar or earlier probe was rerun.

An independent temporary-directory rerun produced identical parsed results.
All ten local evidence/artifact links resolved, and repository Prettier formatting
passed. These checks verify the note and finite demonstration's reproducibility,
not a production privacy or authorization implementation.

The probe demonstrates exact arithmetic/set reconstructions and distinguishable
histories that an unsafe shortcut collapses. Examples include target eight and
62.5 percent reconstructing count five, seven raw rows retaining two forbidden
contributions after redaction, ten calls involving one synthetic supporter,
same-key target/scope replacement, and current-value recovery differing from
revoked-grant revival. These numbers are examples, **not product thresholds**.

It does not implement a safe aggregate profile, prove identity in real source
records, determine the protected population, prove the current P12 decision,
test persistence/RLS/transactions, establish a freshness SLA or certify privacy.
Reconstruction shows that a particular release combination can reveal a value;
it does not claim a real coach has the needed background knowledge or that every
small count is unsafe. The period-scope alternatives remain counterexamples to
inference from duration. The founder subsequently selected continuation; that
product evidence resolves the choice independently of this unchanged probe.

No PostgreSQL, hosted Supabase, provider, browser, user study, accessibility or
load test ran here. No dependency, application, formal specification, live
sharing, ticket or external contact changed. Required real proof is finite-profile
source qualification, current authorization and safe release first, then actual
sharing/target/source races and correction, then usable phone/keyboard/assistive-
technology journeys. Monitoring cannot substitute for a known unsafe reachable
release, and no new privacy or reporting platform is justified.
