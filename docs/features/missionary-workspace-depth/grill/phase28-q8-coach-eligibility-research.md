# Q8 frontier: eligibility for invited coaching

Research date: October 16, 2026 UTC. Original pre-answer research retained.
The founder subsequently selected A and requested a full coaching-role/dashboard,
multi-subject, UX and Supabase review. The completed [D8 review](phase28-d8-adversarial-review.md)
records the corrected decision and required amendments, subsequently explicitly
ratified by the founder on October 16, 2026 UTC. That corrected decision governs;
the original options below remain historical research. No implemented coaching
capability is claimed.

## The single product choice

**Should organizations be able to let missionaries invite coaches from outside
their staff?**

- **A — Allow outside coaches when the organization permits it (recommended).**
  Eligible internal staff can also be invited. Each coach uses their own identity
  and receives only separately authorized, read-only coaching access.
- **B — Limit invited coaching to organization staff for the first release.**
  This narrows initial onboarding and identity-assurance scope. Staff status alone
  still grants no coaching access.

Illustrative scenario, not claimed ministry research: Maya wants help from a
trusted coach at another ministry. A accommodates that relationship without
misclassifying the coach as staff. B defers it and keeps the initial eligible
population smaller. No measured demand for external coaching is established by
the inspected sources; recommending A is a product judgment.

  separates invitation acceptance and identity binding from authorization. An
  invitation alone grants nothing; qualified source commands and current P12
  checks determine access for the intended principal and exact tenant context.

These are intended owner boundaries. This research does not claim that Core
already implements or has qualified an external-coach onboarding journey.

## Keep the decision and interface simple

If A is selected, use the existing organization permission owners; do not invent
a second access registry or assume routine staff approval for every invitation.
The missionary should understand who is being invited, that acceptance is pending
until completed, and that coaching is a limited view. Show the exact permitted
information before access is granted, with a clear way to end access. Organization
policy must shape the available action and explanation truthfully.

An outside coach is not automatically staff, a Support Assignment participant,
the missionary's impersonator or a CRM/finance user. No shared login, email-match
shortcut or new right to donor identities, raw giving, private notes, care
records or Field Accounts follows. Internal coaches face the same exact coaching
authorization boundary. Optional coaching must not become compulsory enrollment,
activity logging, rankings or an inference that missing logs mean inactivity.

Only eligibility is presented now. The exact modules, historical scope and
duration depend on that answer. Identity assurance, current tenant authorization,
safe invitations, revocation, redaction and recovery must be qualified through
their owners; these are not optional safeguards or reasons to ask the founder
to design implementation internals.
