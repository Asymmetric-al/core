# D1 restricted prospect start — product and delivery challenge

Research-only independent review, 2026-10-15 America/Los_Angeles. The founder selected option A: a restricted start inside the organization's native CRM before deliberate sharing. This review does not ratify additional access, sharing, retention, offboarding or implementation decisions. It is evidence for the requested adversarial review, not a specification or ticket proposal.

**Recommendation: Accept with required amendments.** The selected ordinary visibility direction is defensible and explicitly permitted as an open product choice by the current roadmap. Neither user research nor runtime evidence establishes that it is already a safe, useful complete product. Preserve A while precisely bounding its meaning and keeping unresolved dependent rights visible. Do not reinterpret it as permission for a shadow CRM, automatic sharing, new exceptional staff access, or indefinite private storage.

## Findings

### PS01 — A plausible problem is not validated demand

**Categories:** 1, 21, 22. **Severity:** Medium. **Likelihood:** Unknown; no representative interviews or observed Asym prospect journeys were supplied.

**What and why:** Early relationship planning before sharing is a recognizable job, and the founder has selected its ordinary audience. There is no evidence that missionaries avoid the current product because staff see tentative prospects, that A reduces tool sprawl, or that the extra access/continuity complexity pays for itself. Competitor feature descriptions do not answer those questions. It would be misleading to claim research proves adoption, trust or fundraising improvement.

**Evidence:** Product Intent Mission And Scope/Long-Horizon Success; current Q1; benchmark note's explicit missing private-prospect policy evidence.

**Answer impact:** Accept A as product direction; mark expected benefits as hypotheses. This is not grounds to silently substitute B or demand invented validation before every next grooming question.

**Permanent fix:** Keep the need falsifiable and retain an evidence gap through representative testing. Observe the actual task and misunderstanding, not only what people say they prefer.

**Exact candidate clause:** “The expected benefit of restricted early prospecting is a product hypothesis, not an established adoption or fundraising result. Before release, representative missionaries and responsible organizational staff must complete the entry-to-permitted-handoff journey and correctly explain its audience and ownership. Record observed failures and revise the design if it causes duplicate work, unsafe privacy expectations or avoidable off-platform tracking.”

### PS02 — The strongest cheaper alternative must remain honestly described

**Categories:** 1, 8, 17. **Severity:** Medium. **Likelihood:** Likely that A costs more to operate than a single common authorized audience; the size of the difference is unmeasured.

**What and why:** The strongest alternative is not a spreadsheet or all-staff access. It is immediate visibility of source-qualified prospect work to specifically authorized relationship staff, with genuinely private notes still separately protected. That could reduce collision and departure handling. It sacrifices the chosen delayed ordinary disclosure and is not a no-cost privacy equivalent. A no-new-private-prospect build can retain existing authorized Party/referral/task work, but cannot claim to deliver A if those owners do not qualify the restricted entry.

**Evidence:** Q1 alternative; P27 P01/P03; Product Intent Administrative Foundation; roadmap P28 open choice.

**Answer impact:** A survives. If evidence later shows its coordination cost is unacceptable, return the actual tradeoff to the founder rather than replacing it with B in implementation.

**Permanent fix:** Avoid false binary framing and silent fallback semantics.

**Exact candidate clause:** “Restricted early visibility is the selected ordinary behavior. Existing authorized Party/referral/task paths may remain usable, but neither an immediately staff-visible entry nor an external personal database may be presented as completion of this choice. If the restricted path cannot be qualified at proportionate cost, bring that concrete tradeoff back for an explicit scope decision.”

### PS03 — ‘Private prospect’ could silently narrow to private notes only

**Categories:** 4, 5, 9, 10, 15. **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Likely if storage and copy conflate identity, contact facts and planning context.

**What and why:** Maya's offered choice included a newly entered prospect and early planning. A design that immediately exposes a newly supplied name/email through canonical Party search while only hiding a note could technically say it has private context yet contradict the user's natural reading of A. Conversely, hiding every existing canonical record would wrongly withdraw other users' established source rights. “One CRM” alone resolves neither case.

**Evidence:** Q1 ordinary visibility versus existing independent records; relationship/finance note's three layers; P27 P03 minimum-result authority; source owners retain Party/contact rights.

**Answer impact:** A needs an explicit object boundary, not a unilateral determination that all Party/contact data is private or shared. This is a material follow-on choice if existing source rules require wider disclosure.

**Permanent fix:** Resolve newly supplied identity/contact facts, source linkage and restricted prospect context separately before enabling the write. Do not manufacture a duplicate Party to evade a hidden match.

**Exact candidate clause:** “The restricted-start decision covers the newly entered prospecting work, not merely a private notes box. It does not withdraw anyone's independently authorized existing source facts. Before this capability is enabled, the owner contract must state the audience of newly supplied identity/contact facts, the prospect context and their linkage. If those facts cannot be accepted without disclosure inconsistent with the reviewed audience, do not silently disclose them or create a bypass duplicate; expose the qualified unavailable or resolution path.”

### PS04 — Protecting A does not require a second product or universal sharing engine

**Categories:** 2, 3, 8, 10, 11. **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Possible; the existing workspace has legacy supporter/task models and incomplete source bindings.

**What and why:** A private prospect database, bespoke task queue, personal contact schema, separate permission engine or new generic sharing workflow would make two versions of the same person/work permanent. “Private now, synchronize later” is precisely the fragmented system the founder rejects. The opposite mistake is forcing every prospect into a P27 continuing enrollment just because that model exists.

**Evidence:** P27 P01 permits earlier Party/referral/task work; P02 forbids confidentiality as duplicate enrollment identity; root CRM ownership; current workspace census; Product Intent Non-Goals.

**Answer impact:** Preserve A with existing source ownership and the smallest bounded extension. No physical schema/table choice is settled by this review.

**Permanent fix:** Separate the needed audience policy from implementation structure; require reuse or a narrowly justified compatible owner extension.

**Exact candidate clause:** “Restricted prospecting remains organization-owned native CRM work through P9/P3/P10/P12 and, when deliberately admitted, P27. It adds no personal system of record, competing task/interaction history, synchronization layer, generic sharing engine or duplicate continuing enrollment. Existing owner records are reused where qualified; any indispensable new source record or permission is a bounded explicit owner extension, not inferred from this visibility choice.”

### PS05 — A tentative name must not become managed partnership work by default

**Categories:** 4, 5, 9, 10, 11. **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Possible, especially if a board/pipeline is reused as the entry screen.

**What and why:** Privacy is an audience property. It does not establish a ministry purpose, active cultivation, a stage, compulsory next step or shared logging obligation. Making entry create all these obligations would turn an apparently low-friction address-and-reminder action into a management program. It would also misrepresent the friend's assent and place pre-ministry work behind a fabricated ministry reference.

**Evidence:** P27 P01, P03 and D26; founder brief §§6–9; Q1 excludes cultivation admission.

**Answer impact:** Constraint preserves A. No separate access grant or new type of task is implied.

**Permanent fix:** Keep entry, source-qualified next work and later deliberate managed admission distinct while allowing one explicit action to contain only separately authorized intended effects.

**Exact candidate clause:** “Adding a restricted prospect records only the expressly reviewed authorized entry and work. It does not admit cultivation, select a stage, imply supporter assent, impose logging/targets or create a financial, communication or coaching effect. Authorized Party/referral/task work may precede an identified ministry; no dummy ministry or silent enrollment is created.”

### PS06 — Sharing and departure can become undeclared exceptions to A

**Categories:** 4, 5, 10, 13, 15, 17. **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Likely to arise over the product lifetime; the chosen policy is unresolved.

**What and why:** “Only me until I share” can be mistaken for personal ownership, guaranteed secrecy from all organizational functions, indefinite exclusive access after departure or authority to erase org records. Operations may then solve the mismatch by silently exposing data to a manager or transferring it to a successor. Either path selects a new access policy without the founder. Earlier private notes also cannot be copied, summarized or relabeled as D26 shared history at promotion.

**Evidence:** Q1 explicit exception/promotion/departure deferral; P27 P05 responsibility/access separation; D26 exact audience and historical-access rules; Product Intent administrative completeness.

**Answer impact:** Preserve A's ordinary audience; keep exception, retention, departure and sharing policy open. The existence of future organizational obligations does not itself grant anyone access.

**Permanent fix:** Bound the promise and make unresolved lifecycle policy an affected-capability gate, not a vague “admins can resolve it” escape hatch.

**Exact candidate clause:** “This choice selects ordinary prospect visibility only. It neither grants exceptional staff access, successor access, historical access, personal export/deletion rights nor indefinite retention. Deliberate sharing, correction and departure handling need their own qualified owner rules before affected use. Private early notes remain private-note records and cannot be republished as shared partnership history. No manager, spouse, team member, coach or replacement worker receives access by implication.”

### PS07 — ‘Safe resolution’ can hide permanent manual glue

**Categories:** 2, 9, 12, 17, 18. **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Possible; hidden-match frequency and available source resolvers have not been measured.

**What and why:** Uniform hidden/absent denial is a necessary privacy floor, but an everyday create flow that often ends in unowned “contact support” would be unusable and might pressure staff to inspect protected data or create duplicates. A research note saying “needs resolution” is not proof that an authorized resolver, queue, minimal action projection or recovery process exists. Inventing a new all-seeing resolver is an access decision.

**Evidence:** P27 P03 paired hidden/absent behavior; P27 Q13 no routine direct SQL repairs; Product Principles System Behavior Over Repeated Manual Glue Work; Q1 leaves exact resolution UI open.

**Answer impact:** A stands; a complete native path still needs an actual qualified resolution or honest bounded refusal. Do not decide new staff visibility as engineering hardening.

**Permanent fix:** Qualify source-owned cause/receipt/action boundaries and measure real interruption frequency. Ordinary unproblematic entry should not become a manager-approval ceremony.

**Exact candidate clause:** “A blocked match or admission must have a truthful permitted outcome and, where resolution is supported, an identified existing qualified owner/action. Do not invent resolver access, an unowned queue or a duplicate workaround. Before release, demonstrate the ordinary case and the supported blocked case, measure their user/operational burden and keep protected reasons out of unauthorized results.”

### PS08 — This first decision can accidentally absorb the whole Phase 28 program

**Categories:** 3, 8, 11, 19, 22. **Severity:** Medium. **Likelihood:** Likely in an exhaustive review if every future interaction becomes a prerequisite to recording A.

**What and why:** Private entry touches many owners, but it does not require the entire calendar/mail connector, offline platform, coaching product, native newsletter, workflow engine and financial attribution stack before its direction can be accepted. Conversely, completing restricted entry would not complete those required Phase 28 outcomes. Broad dependency language can either stall useful work or erase hard features.

**Evidence:** Program delivery contract, P28/P31/P32/P34/P35/P39 chapters; P27 Q15 scoped financial gates; founder brief §§3/11/14.

**Answer impact:** Accept the visibility direction now with exact downstream questions tracked. Gates attach to concrete affected capabilities.

**Permanent fix:** State the selected semantic delta and excluded inferences; avoid adding future-provider or whole-phase prerequisites.

**Exact candidate clause:** “D1 settles only the ordinary starting audience for prospecting work. Exact admission and lifecycle dependencies remain recorded by owner and affected capability. Optional workflows, external newsletter sync, SMS, AI and full offline synchronization are not blanket prerequisites; their absence cannot waive required native Phase 28 outcomes or justify substitute authorities.”

### PS09 — Performance claims can conceal either leakage or unacceptable friction

**Categories:** 7, 15, 16, 21. **Severity:** High for disclosure; Medium for operational delay. **Likelihood:** Unknown until actual source queries, counts and tenant distributions are measured.

**What and why:** A private audience makes complete search/count/deduplication harder than hiding rows in the UI. Loading all candidates and redacting afterwards is unsafe and will not scale. A fabricated universal millisecond target or fixed contact cap could force truncation or weaker authorization. On the other hand, “secure” is not a license for an unusably slow entry path. P27 hidden/absent results require a bounded observable contract; they do not authorize claiming mathematically constant network timing without evidence.

**Evidence:** P27 P03 paired-observable cases; Q12 measured capacity; founder brief §11 authorization before search/count/page/export; static census's capped reads and post-load work.

**Answer impact:** No change to A. Numeric targets and actual indexing remain measured engineering work, not new founder policy invented here.

**Permanent fix:** Qualify bounded authorized server work and declare workload/capacity before release. Test accessible ordinary and denied paths under representative scope/skew without exposing global private counts.

**Exact candidate clause:** “Prospect discovery, counts and match resolution must be bounded and authorized before disclosure or client loading. Declare and measure the supported dataset/concurrency profile, end-to-end task and source-query budgets before activation; do not invent unlimited scale, hide truncation, or weaken access to meet a target. Paired hidden/absent cases must satisfy the source's qualified observable-result contract.”

### PS10 — Minimal observability must not become monitoring of private relationships

**Categories:** 15, 17, 18, 21. **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Possible if product analytics and support diagnostics inherit raw names, note bodies or private labels.

**What and why:** Staff need to diagnose failed saves, duplicate commands and stuck owner work without learning whom a missionary considered contacting. Prospect volume, missing logs or private-stage activity could also become accidental coaching surveillance. Without any operational signals, failures may remain invisible; with broad payload logs, the restriction is cosmetic.

**Evidence:** P27 Q13 separation of business history/audit/diagnostics; D26 no compulsory logs/quotas/rankings; founder brief §§7/10/11; program contract event minimization.

**Answer impact:** Preserve A. Audit/diagnostic authority is not established by this decision and must not be broadened by calling it support access.

**Permanent fix:** Use minimal operational evidence and qualified source access; test telemetry outputs along with UI. Distinguish operational failure measures from missionary performance measures.

**Exact candidate clause:** “Operational evidence may record permitted opaque operation identifiers, states and reason codes needed for recovery; it must not expose private prospect identities or narrative to unauthorized staff, telemetry or alert recipients. Private entry counts and missing voluntary records must not become coaching enrollment, performance rankings or reporting obligations. Each diagnostic or audit view retains its own current source authority.”

### PS11 — Retained legacy data cannot be assigned the new meaning by default

**Categories:** 3, 10, 14, 20. **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Likely that reached retained data contains ambiguous provenance; actual volume/production contents remain unknown.

**What and why:** Existing single missionary donor associations and separate tasks do not prove whether an old contact was private, shared or already relied on by staff. Making all existing contacts restricted could withdraw operational visibility and conceal obligations. Making them all shared could expose personal context. Importing old notes as newly eligible shared context would directly violate D26. No production census was performed.

**Evidence:** Static workspace census; P27 Q11 retained-data migration; D26 no imported/private-note republication; user explicitly says do not assume an empty production database.

**Answer impact:** A applies to newly admitted qualified behavior. Existing-data classification requires evidence and source-owned mapping; no automatic reclassification approved.

**Permanent fix:** Census before activation, preserve established rights and unresolved provenance, fence old incompatible writers, and verify restored/replayed data cannot widen access.

**Exact candidate clause:** “The new ordinary visibility choice does not retrospectively label retained contacts or notes private, shared or newly authored. Inventory reached data, source lineage, existing permissions and unresolved work before cutover; preserve accepted source facts and restrictions. Ambiguous provenance remains an explicit source-owned exception, and incompatible old writers or restore paths must not bypass the qualified audience rules.”

### PS12 — An unfalsifiable ‘secure and simple’ clause is not delivery proof

**Categories:** 9, 12, 13, 21, 22. **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Likely if the decision is recorded as complete design after this conversational review.

**What and why:** The choice cannot be validated by a happy-path screenshot, in-memory access test, static schema or unanimous model reviewers. A denial-only implementation might pass leakage tests but fail the requested job; a frictionless happy path might expose data on search/export/recovery. No new implementation, role-based database exercise or intended-user pilot is authorized or demonstrated in this review.

**Evidence:** P27 P03 AC04/05; Q11–Q13/Q16; founder brief §14; current session evidence limits.

**Answer impact:** Accept with explicit testable limits; the direction is neither implemented nor release-qualified. Exact audience-changing successor clauses require their own decisions.

**Permanent fix:** Use a compact trace from each approved sentence to observable positive/negative evidence; prove both usable allowed work and denied paths at their actual source seams.

**Exact candidate clause:** “Record D1 as a selected visibility direction with explicit unresolved dependent rights, not implementation or release qualification. Before activation, demonstrate a permitted entry/save/resume journey and current-authority reads, plus denied staff/foreign-context paths, hidden-match handling, concurrent/repeated submission, lost-response recovery, stale-client/context changes and retained-data cutover through the actual source/database/UI seams. A denied-only prototype or passing documentation test does not complete the user outcome.”

## Exact candidate decision wording

The following is proposed research wording to put before the founder or incorporate only where it preserves the selected intent. It is not a formal requirement set and does not authorize implementation:

> **D1 — Selected direction, subject to the recorded owner and lifecycle decisions.** A missionary may begin prospecting work within the organization's native CRM with a restricted ordinary audience, rather than exposing the new prospect entry and early planning to ordinary relationship staff from entry. Existing independent source records and other users' established source authority remain unchanged.
>
> The source contract must distinguish newly supplied identity/contact facts, the restricted prospect context and deliberately shared partnership work. The choice must not be reduced silently to hiding only a note while disclosing the new prospect elsewhere. The qualified entry may use existing source records or a bounded compatible owner extension; it creates no personal CRM, duplicate continuing enrollment, automatic cultivation, consent, money, publication, coaching or compulsory reporting.
>
> Deliberate sharing, historical scope, correction, exceptional access, retention and departure continuity remain separate unresolved choices. No new staff, spouse/team, coach or successor rights are granted here. Private early notes cannot be copied, summarized or relabeled as shared partnership history. Where an exact source or policy is missing, the affected capability remains explicitly unqualified; no workaround duplicate, silent audience widening or invented resolver is allowed.
>
> This records the product direction only. Required Phase 28 native outcomes remain in scope, and source, runtime, migration, usability and operating evidence must qualify their actual affected paths before activation.

## Consolidation guidance

This independent review contributes mainly to categories 1/2/3/8/9/10/11/16/17/18/19/20/21/22 and intersects the security/concurrency categories. It does not replace the independent identity, permissions, database, lifecycle or privacy reviews. Consolidate overlapping concerns by exact failure, not by repeating the same clause twenty-two times. The user requested each category individually; categories with no additional distinct finding should say so and refer to the applicable shared finding without fabricating hazards.

The review recommends **Accept with required amendments**, not Reject and not unconditional Accept. It deliberately does not settle the next material access question. The narrowest durable result is one selected initial-audience rule, preserved owner boundaries and an honest register of the remaining rights and proof—not a general personal-contact platform.
