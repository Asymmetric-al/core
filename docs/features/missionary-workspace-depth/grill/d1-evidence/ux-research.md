# D1 UX and benchmark research — restricted prospecting start

Research/retrieval: **2026-10-15 America/Los_Angeles** (2026-10-16 UTC).
The founder selected a restricted start for a missionary's new prospecting work.
This review pressure-tests that direction without selecting its unresolved
admission fields, exceptional-access policy, sharing audience, historical scope,
or departure rules. It is research and proposed clarification, not a formal
specification, implementation, permission grant, or runtime qualification.

## Recommendation and strongest alternative

**Keep restricted start, with visible and truthful access boundaries and a
short ordinary path.** A missionary should be able to prepare a possible
relationship without an accidental staff-visible development effort. The
organization still owns its native CRM. The interface must distinguish the
missionary's newly entered work from independently authorized organizational
facts about the same person.

The strongest alternative is **immediate visibility to specifically authorized
relationship staff**. That can improve coordination, reduce concealed parallel
work and simplify handoff when the missionary is absent. It may also avoid some
resolution delays when matching existing records. Those benefits are plausible
product reasoning; no inspected study quantifies them in missionary work. The
cost is earlier disclosure of tentative personal relationships and less control
over when preparation becomes shared work. Staff visibility does not itself
solve wrong matches, private notes, current consent, duplicate effects or
appropriate financial access.

Restricted start has real costs: more access states to explain, more demanding
identity resolution, possible coordination delays and an explicit continuity
policy. A complex security wizard would make those costs worse. A persistent,
plain audience explanation, one short capture form and a separate sharing review
are the preferred response. Giving staff even an identifying prospect header
before deliberate sharing would materially change the selected policy; it is
not minor UX hardening and is not recommended silently.

## Exact recommendation language for consolidation

> Keep the missionary's new prospecting work restricted from ordinary staff
> visibility until deliberately shared, within the organization's existing CRM.
> Show who can currently see this work in plain language. Existing authorized
> facts about the same person retain their own access rules. Saving, matching,
> sharing, recording an interaction and starting managed partnership work remain
> distinct actions. Sharing does not publish private notes or earlier private
> history, create contact permission, or enroll anyone in newsletters or coaching.

> Make entry short and recoverable. Ask only for information needed by the
> qualified admission path; do not require a financial ask, promised gift,
> narrative, next task or sharing decision merely to save permitted early work.
> Show only authorized identity suggestions. When a source owner cannot resolve
> canonical reuse or shared admission without revealing protected existence, use
> the same bounded outcome for absent and hidden-existing work, with an authorized
> repair route that does not expose the reason. A qualified source may still save
> restricted preparation while identity remains unresolved; report that success
> separately from pending identity or admission. Never describe an uncertain
> preparation-save outcome as completed or definitely failed until that outcome
> is known.

These clauses preserve the selected intent and inherited boundaries. They do not
authorize a universal name-only record, pending-record store, new access-review
service, particular sharing recipient set, or any exceptional staff access.
Those exact source capabilities and remaining product choices still need resolution.

Owner evidence clarifies two independent boundaries. [P27 REQ27-P01 and
AC27-P01-04](../../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-27-donor-development/partnerships.md#req27-p01--continuing-work-belongs-to-an-identified-relationship-purpose)
preserve authorized Party/referral/task work before an identified ministry; no
dummy Ministry Assignment is required. The [D1 owner review](owner-boundaries.md)
distinguishes a proposed qualified P9 preparation source from canonical identity
and shared admission. It can retain an unresolved attributed assertion without
claiming those later outcomes. The exact source extension is not yet qualified;
this distinction neither invents an implemented producer nor adds product policy.

## Proposed phone journey for evaluation

This is a concrete research candidate, not approved screen copy, field schema or
release scope. It uses the existing Core components and source-owner command
boundaries; the exact restricted-preparation source still needs qualification.
It does not require a new wizard, drawer primitive or separate prospect database.

1. **Start in the current organization and actor context.** From the missionary's authorized
   relationship list, choose an ordinary “Add person”/“Add prospect” action; final
   terminology needs testing. Show the relevant current context and a compact
   explanation that this new prospecting work starts restricted. Show a Ministry
   Assignment only when applicable and authorized; early P9 preparation and
   authorized Party/referral/task work must not require an identified or dummy
   ministry. Do not ask the user to understand Parties, tenant IDs or enrollment
   uniqueness.
2. **Capture only what is currently known.** Propose one short screen with a
   source-supported name/identity input and an optional contact method. Label
   optional fields explicitly. A known contact must not require a postal address,
   spouse, financial goal or essay. If owner admission genuinely requires more,
   explain that exact need rather than marking a large form mandatory. Selecting
   an existing permitted person and recording new work should not require
   re-entering their canonical information.
3. **Handle authorized identity suggestions in place.** Display only candidates
   the missionary may see, with minimal approved distinguishing details and a
   clear choice. Do not expose the organization directory. If the actor cannot
   resolve canonical identity or admission, give the qualified neutral result and
   real repair action without blocking a separately permitted preparation save.
   Never offer a hidden-match card or require changing the entered identity to
   bypass a block. Pending identity resolution does not negate a durably accepted
   preparation save; it must not be represented as successful canonical identity
   resolution or shared admission.
4. **Save with one deliberate action.** Keep audience text near the form and use
   an unambiguous Save label. Prevent accidental repeated activation while
   communicating progress, but do not rely on a disabled button as duplicate
   protection. Inline validation names the issue, retains other safe values and
   returns focus meaningfully. Typing, matching and tapping Save do not contact
   the prospect.
5. **Explain the actual result.** Acknowledged preparation save opens or reveals
   that accepted preparation with its persistent audience label. Any unresolved
   canonical identity or shared-admission outcome remains separately clear.
   Suggested state language: “Saving…”; “Preparation saved”; “Preparation not
   saved. Correct the highlighted information”; or “We couldn't confirm whether
   this preparation was saved” with a real status-check/recovery action.
   These are examples only. Say that input remains available only when it does.
   An unknown outcome is never phrased as a definite failure or fresh-create prompt.
6. **Let ordinary work continue.** The user can return to the same list position
   or later add source-qualified detail. An optional next action must not become
   a blocking task, managed enrollment, logged conversation, coach invitation or
   newsletter subscription. The chosen restricted policy does not hide staff's
   separately authorized existing organizational facts.
7. **Review sharing later.** When the qualified sharing path is intentionally
   selected, show the precise proposed information, approved audience and access
   effect. Offer a clear confirm action and an easy cancel that preserves the
   restricted state. No private-note/history publication option is inferred.
   After acceptance, show what changed; preserve uncertainty if acknowledgement
   is lost. Sharing never depends on a hover-only tooltip or a color change alone.

Avoid a lengthy policy acknowledgement at every save. The short explanation
should communicate the actual protection, with further detail available. Also
avoid a generic “Anyone with permission” badge: it describes a technical truth
without helping the user predict who sees the work. Exact audience names/categories
must themselves be safe to show and based on qualified current authority.
