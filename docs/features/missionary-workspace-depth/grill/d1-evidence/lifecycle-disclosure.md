# D1 adversarial evidence: lifecycle and secondary disclosure

Research-only review of the founder's accepted **restricted start** direction, as relayed by the parent grill. This file proposes clarifications; it does not ratify new access, retention, departure or sharing rules and is not a specification or ticket plan. No GitHub, provider, live-data or supporter action was taken.

## Verdict

Keep the restricted-start direction, but define it as protection of the missionary's **newly entered prospecting context**, not a promise that the person is absent from the organization's CRM, that every related record is author-only, or that organizational retention ceases. The existing source owners can support this direction without a personal CRM, private task table, second notes service, or new event bus. Their current contracts do not yet prove this exact visibility policy is implemented.

The largest concrete gap is propagation: a restricted primary record can still expose its existence or words through the existing timeline, audit, task, communication, import and workflow pathways. The source policy must govern those derivatives before they are selected, counted, emitted or returned. UI hiding and redacting the final body are insufficient.

**Do not equate “restricted start” with Phase 10's `security_level=restricted`.** The former is a proposed ordinary relationship-work audience; the latter is a separately governed person-level safety classification with special identity, residency, audit, encryption and publication rules. Preserve the strictest applicable source rules, but do not classify every new prospect as a high-risk worker or use the protected-identity room as a generic private-notes product.

## A minimal coherent lifecycle

This is an explanatory outline for the decision review, not a proposed new state machine or schema.

1. **Record tentative work.** One exact organization/actor/source context accepts only the needed contact assertion and planning facts. Canonical identity resolution remains with P4/P9 and must not reveal protected matches. Preserve who supplied the data, what was actually asserted, and when; no verified identity, donor consent, supporter assent, cultivation enrollment, gift or coach membership follows merely from saving. Existing independently authorized organizational facts remain governed by their original owners.
2. **Use the restricted context.** Its allowed owner reads, edits and personal follow-up use the shared source services. Notes, task titles, activity, audit access, search, queries, exports and recovered results obey the exact audience and classification. An assignment alone cannot authorize a detail read. No routine staff broadcast, workflow enrollment or generated outreach follows from record arrival. Any actual email/print work requires its own current source purpose and contact permission.
3. **Deliberately share eligible work.** The user sees which current facts and work will become available to which qualified audience. Accepted sharing must not retroactively publish private notes, imported messages, Support/care history or private-derived summaries. P27 newly authored shared updates remain voluntary and retain exact historical-access rules. Sharing is not subscription, public publication, financial access or automatic cultivation admission; an expressly included owner action would need its own visible consequence and current authority.
4. **Correct or narrow.** Corrections retain truthful provenance. Narrowing stops future unauthorized reads and effects at the source boundary, invalidates derived access and rechecks current results. Already read/exported/delivered copies cannot be recalled. If actual outward submission has begun or is unknown, preserve its honest outcome rather than marking it prevented or sending a replacement. Removing access cannot rewrite an already accepted source event.
5. **Stop work or remove an entry.** Clearly distinguish no longer pursuing this relationship, removal from a personal working view, deletion of an eligible body, ending a permission, and lawful records retention. The exact source decides each permitted action. Completed tasks, financial facts, suppression, proven contact choices and permanent effect tombstones do not disappear because the prospect entry is removed. A private note is not required to survive forever, and an audit requirement is not unlimited ordinary access.
6. **Handle departure and recovery.** Current access and affected human-owner-dependent automation stop immediately. Preserve required records and unresolved obligations, but do not automatically hand all private content to a successor. A qualified transfer/exception is a separate decision and command. Restore/reimport reapplies restriction, expiry, consent and no-replay controls before any ordinary read or effect; a missing old operation result is not permission to perform the action again.

This outline needs no additional private Tasks table, broad private journal, copied CRM, per-entry team engine or general-purpose policy platform. It does need exact source-owned audience semantics not supplied by P9's current `standard|restricted` note field alone.

## Suggested clarification language

The parent can adopt the safeguards that preserve founder intent after review; the separate choices below must remain unratified until answered.

> A newly entered prospecting context may start with ordinary visibility limited to the missionary who recorded it, inside the organization's existing CRM and subject to current source permissions and safety rules. This does not conceal independently authorized organizational records about the same person, create a second person or partnership, or itself grant contact, newsletter, coaching or financial permission.

> The restriction covers the context's contents and existence in notes, tasks, activity and authorized history, previews, search, counts, exports, notifications, recovered responses and derived features. Technical logs and workflow payloads contain only their qualified minimum evidence. Required audit remains separately protected and does not provide ordinary staff a back door to the context.

> Later sharing is deliberate and identifies its current audience and eligible facts. It does not copy, summarize, relabel or retrospectively share private early notes, imported correspondence, care/Support content or other protected sources. One shared source record and its lawful history remain authoritative.

> Revocation and withdrawal stop applicable future access and unadmitted effects. They do not recall already delivered or exported material, erase independently required records, or turn an uncertain external outcome into a safe retry. Recovery and restoration cannot revive revoked access, consent, expired content or previously ended effects.

Use **limited ordinary visibility** or equivalent plain-language explanation rather than promising “Only you can ever see this.” Exact exceptional access, retention and departure rules are not yet chosen.

## Product choices that cannot be silently added

- **Exceptional organization access:** which named purpose, authorized people, reason, approval and user disclosure—if any—can access restricted early context? Organization ownership alone does not answer it. P10 protected-worker identity grants and P12 tenant recovery cannot be repurposed as a blanket private-prospect access rule. In particular, tenant recovery explicitly excludes restricted-tier plaintext.
- **Departure and inability to continue:** whether restricted early context may transfer, remain retained but inaccessible to ordinary staff, be removed under records policy, or receive a narrowly authorized exception. Distinguish unresolved accepted organizational duties from tentative personal planning. Avoid treating a successor's need for continuity as retrospective consent to read everything.
- **Removal and retention:** what the user can remove, which source facts/bodies remain under which approved purposes and holds, what their UI honestly says, and who may retrieve retained evidence. No universal “delete everything immediately” or “organization keeps every note forever” rule is supplied by Q1 or P29.
- **Sharing scope and historical access:** exact eligible current facts/work and collaborator audience; whether any existing source record can change audience under an explicitly approved owner rule. P27's no-private-note-laundering boundary remains binding. A broad “share entire history” action is not a minor safeguard.

## Evidence tests needed for this direction

These are proposed falsifiable proof obligations, not tests performed or implementation tickets.

1. **All projections from one fixture:** create one limited-visibility prospect with unique markers in name/assertion, note, title, description, activity and history. The owner can perform admitted tasks. Same-ministry worker, spouse, relationship staff with unrelated Party rights, coach, newsletter-only recipient, ordinary admin, public visitor and second tenant see no unauthorized marker or existence signal through lists/detail, search/filter/sort/count, timelines, next-task summaries, export, direct data/RPC/views, logs, notification previews, Realtime and job diagnostics. Do not assume a finance grant reveals this context.
2. **Audit is not a bypass:** edit and remove a private note; ordinary readers cannot recover text through before/after snapshots, audit API, activity summaries or backup-derived views. A separately permitted audit reader sees only its source-approved scope and produces required minimal audit-of-access. Required audit failure rolls back acceptance or blocks disclosure according to its owner; no silent missing evidence.
3. **Sharing is bounded:** deliberately share eligible contact/work context. Assert precisely what becomes available and show audience comprehension. Old private note markers, imported correspondence, staff-only history, Support/care contents and AI summaries of them remain unavailable. Changing assignment, adding a coach or editing a stage cannot perform the share. Newly authored D26 context remains voluntary and creates no hidden task/send/coaching outcome.
4. **Race both orders:** exercise save/read/recover/export/notification admission versus access loss and content restriction at real source/database boundaries. Accepted-before-revoke work stays accepted while later unauthorized body recovery is denied. Revoke-before-admission stops the effect. Client context changes discard stale responses/cursors even when abort fails; an old response cannot restore a removed record or clear another context's draft.
5. **Newsletter/finance purpose isolation:** prove P25 frozen intended-recipient guest-request access without creating a supporter, and deny roster/finance/note enumeration from that capability. A new assignee cannot read the old request. Test the exact seven-day first-work and thirty-day body clocks; Off/current suppression stops applicable unstarted sends, with source outcome retained for already crossed/unknown submission.
6. **No derivative by arrival:** create/import/reimport a tentative contact and note under duplicate retries. Assert no automatic enrollment, public release, historical first-gift task, newsletter admission, staff alert or external communication. Independently required receipts/financial service work for an existing Party still follows its own source rather than being globally blocked by this context's privacy.
7. **Task convergence:** migrate reached legacy `missionary_tasks` through a retained-data census; preserve identity, provenance, legitimate completion and unresolved work. Old/new writers cannot create duplicate shared tasks or revive canceled effects. Task completion alone creates no conversation, donor response or financial effect. Missing source authority produces a qualified unavailable path rather than exposing task details through a fallback.
8. **Byte/AI/import custody:** test filenames, thumbnails, original/preview retrieval, signed-link expiry, attachment removal, rendered titles and download audit, as applicable. A clean scan or shared folder grants no access. Import plan and target command bind source bytes, mapping/version, current scope and target revision; stale/unknown plans cannot publish private context. Restricted-tier data never enters search/AI; any other assisted use requires its exact source/processor admission rather than owner visibility alone.
9. **Departure/restore:** once founder policy is chosen, test exact member/grant end, owner-dependent automation and possible transfer against active tasks, queued effects and retained history. No generic successor inheritance or reactivation of canceled sends. Restore a backup taken before withdrawal/deletion; apply current restrictions/expiry/tombstones before readability or dispatch.

Proof should use real source services, actual restricted database roles and controlled concurrency plus ordinary owner/staff browser journeys. A mock, documentation test, role-name check or green demo route cannot qualify this boundary. Numeric timing/currentness guarantees need the exact owner test environment; no universal zero-latency or all-copy recall promise is made here.
