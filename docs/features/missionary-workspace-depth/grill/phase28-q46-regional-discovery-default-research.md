## One question

The founder already chose region browsing, a world map and tenant On/Off control.
This lets missionaries explore approved public ministries by broad region.

**For a new organization, when should Explore ministries become available?**

- **A — Off until staff enable it (recommended).** Authorized staff deliberately
  offer it when useful for their organization.
- **B — On when ready.** It becomes available automatically once the feature and
  its approved information are ready; authorized staff can turn it off.

Both preserve current source, permission and safety requirements. A does not
add an approval queue or setup wizard; B cannot bypass qualification. This
question concerns new organizations without a prior explicit choice. Existing
choices remain unchanged; unavailable or unreadable policy never implies On.
The library, independently published Pages and public reach remain separate.

## Evidence and recommendation

The [D13 research and primary-source review](phase28-d13-regional-map-decision.md#evidence-and-limits)
checked repository source and official UI/accessibility/map guidance on October 16. Its one reversible control, honest readback, independent list recovery and
accessible selection remain useful design precedents. They do not prove an
activation default optimal. No new authenticated competitor walkthrough, user
study, map runtime or performance measurement is claimed here.

Recommend A as a product judgment: the organization deliberately decides whether
this additional workspace entry is useful. The cost is one staff action and
lower initial discoverability. B removes that setup action but makes a new
workspace entry appear automatically when ready. Neither option changes which
information may be disclosed. This is a usefulness/activation decision, not a
claim that already approved public information is intrinsically unsafe.

Engineering retains the D13 obligations for source-owned policy, current safe
projection, same-scope list/map results, stale-response rejection, accessible
phone use and clear unavailable/empty/error states. Unknown migrated policy
needs deliberate qualified treatment, not an accidental On backfill. No provider,
map library, geolocation, personal override or per-region setting is selected.
