## The one choice

Maya leaves the organization. Her unshared preparation is no longer ordinary
current work. D27 allows her to resume eligible preparation if she later returns,
but it does not say how long that preparation remains available.

**Should each organization choose how long unshared preparation is kept for a
possible return after a missionary leaves?**

- **A — Organization-set period (recommended).** Authorized staff choose a
  finite retention period within Asym's supported privacy and source rules. The
  applicable period and its consequences are explained clearly.
- **B — Asym-standard period.** Asym provides one finite standard period for
  this purpose; organizations do not customize it.

Both options remain subject to applicable erasure, records and hold requirements.
Neither grants staff or coaches access, restores old permissions, restarts
sharing, changes independent CRM/financial records or makes purged content
recoverable. A hold can require evidence retention without permitting ordinary
body access. No specific duration is selected by this question.

**Recommendation: A.** A small organization setting can accommodate different
continuity needs while making the consequence visible. This is a product
judgment, not evidence that every ministry needs a different period. B is the
strongest simpler alternative: fewer settings and more uniform support, at the
cost of no tenant control over this ordinary retention period. Any applicable
mandatory exception still governs; B is not a claim that one duration overrides
all jurisdictions or source obligations.

## Why this is a genuine unresolved choice

- [D3 reserved choices](phase28-d3-adversarial-review.md:43) expressly leave
  deletion-on-departure and retention duration unselected.
- [D3-C06](phase28-d3-adversarial-review.md:249) requires qualified body, evidence,
  hold, derivative, trigger, duration and restoration rules. Missing material
  policy choices must return to this grill. Retaining a record grants no read
  authority. That is an engineering boundary, not a selection of A or B.
- [D4](phase28-d4-adversarial-review.md:58) selects ordinary archive/return,
  leaving retention duration and automatic purge unselected.
- [D27-C07](phase28-d27-resume-preparation-adversarial-review.md#d27-c07--retention-holds-and-erasure)
  permits resumption only while source-eligible content exists. As clarified by
  the subsequent D28 answer, selected eligible Resume before expiry ends the
  departure schedule under qualified normal current-work rules; original history
  remains intact. No purged body is recovered or universal timer chosen.
- [P9/P12 ownership review](d27-evidence/governance-and-ownership.md) identifies
  preparation source truth and current administration/authorization boundaries.
  A retention setting must use those owners; it cannot create a second private
  CRM or introduce an administrator reader.

The distinction is who controls the ordinary period, not whether Core must obey
its source and privacy obligations. An immediate-removal alternative was
considered but would sharply reduce the just-selected return capability. An
indefinite-retention alternative was rejected as an unsupported promise. Neither
is quietly selected here.

## Fresh primary research and limits

The root and an independent UX researcher directly read
[HubSpot's inactive-contact retention settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/privacy-and-consent/manage-data-retention-policy-settings)
on October 17, 2026; the page states **last updated September 28, 2026**. It
documents an optional automatic-deletion setting, authorized configuration of an
inactivity period and a separate recycle-bin recovery interval. It asks
administrators to consider reporting effects before activation. This is current
CRM precedent for clear, scoped policy configuration and consequences.

Do **not** import its numerical periods, inactivity/open-tracking triggers,
contact-deletion scope or recovery mechanism. Maya's unshared preparation is a
different source, and confirmed departure is different from inactivity. A
competitor's retention setting establishes no ministry requirement, legal
obligation or Core implementation. No vendor account, participant interview or
production behavior was tested.

The D27 primary research and current-source findings remain preserved in
[its evidence](d27-evidence/ux-and-primary-research.md) and
[authorization analysis](d27-evidence/database-and-authorization.md). They are
prior evidence, not a newly executed source census. Neither they nor this public
documentation establish an appropriate number of days for Asym.

## Consequences to resolve after the answer

If A is selected, keep configuration in the existing authorized organization
settings: one plainly named period with a concise description of its scope and
effect. No per-contact timer, policy script, staff-content inspection, new
recovery product or mandatory missionary wizard follows. Recommend one
organization-wide policy for this source; its exact controls remain subject to
the answer review. Show Maya the applicable policy where it affects her work
without suggesting that retained preparation remains accessible after departure.

If B is selected, explain the standard in those same relevant places without
showing an editable tenant control. Neither choice adds an automatic export or
notice to supporters, a new outbound channel, or a restoration guarantee.

The answer review must resolve the exact source-owned trigger, ordinary-body
versus evidence scope, default and supported durations, policy change effects
for existing records, expiry, return/expiry races and permitted correction.
Confirmed departure must come from its authority, never missing logins or a
client flag. Shortening a policy cannot quietly erase work without qualified
impact review; lengthening cannot resurrect purged content. Current access,
retention and independently authorized records remain separate.

Do not add a second retention clock or let Resume reset one. The review must
reconcile how a genuine return affects disposition with D27-C07, rather than
preserving a deadline or cancelling one by assumption. Numerical defaults and
bounds need source/privacy and representative ministry evidence, not borrowed
vendor numbers. Any still-material product tradeoff returns as one clear choice;
engineering obligations are resolved from evidence.
