## Candidate normative clauses

These clauses are recommended exact language for the main review. They are not
already ratified merely because Q30 A was selected.

**G01 — Independent one-time purpose.** When the D30 criterion is enabled and
all current source, authority and live-provenance conditions hold, P28 SHALL
admit the special-gift reason for effective received monetary support whose
exact eligible scope is confirmed as supporting an identified concrete one-time
need. Additionality, amount threshold, firstness or commitment fulfillment SHALL
NOT also be required. A non-recurring payment, ordinary recurring-support
campaign, end date, goal name, generic appeal association or absent commitment
SHALL NOT establish this meaning.

**G02 — Minimum authoritative evidence.** The qualified producer SHALL preserve
the concrete one-time purpose and applicable terms/revision, exact gift/portion,
source origin, occurrence precision, relevant communicating Party/standing,
trusted recorder and recording time, and current confirmation/correction state.
It MAY consume an existing qualified permitted purpose fact or a bounded
authorized attestation of directly received/reviewed evidence. The gift-to-purpose
association must be proved, not obtained by matching labels, dates or amounts.
An attestation SHALL remain an attributable human assertion, not a claim of
independent verification. Unknown or conflicting evidence SHALL not be confirmed
by default. No routine per-gift approval queue or mandatory negative classification
of every ordinary gift is introduced.

**G03 — Reuse purpose owners without fictitious records.** P27 SHALL retain
authority over actual Requests, Appeal purpose/terms and their lifecycle. P28
SHALL reference those exact qualified versions when used. Where the gift has an
eligible documented one-time purpose without an existing Request/Appeal, the
bounded P28 purpose assertion SHALL retain the minimum specific purpose evidence;
it SHALL NOT require creation of an invitation, campaign, need registry or goal.
This assertion SHALL neither edit an existing need's terms nor become authority
for coordination, financial designation or reporting. A shared need represented
by an existing owner source SHALL not be copied into a second mutable master.

**G04 — Explicit actor extension.** As an explicit D30 extension of the selected
D29 actor class, authorized staff and missionaries MAY confirm/correct this
purpose when the organization grants the exact qualified P12 action and
source/subject scope. Gift read, task edit, D29-only confirmation, Request edit,
relationship or role name SHALL NOT imply it. Policy administration and source
purpose editing remain separately authorized. One deliberate authorized save
may record and confirm eligible evidence; neither a second actor nor staff
status is automatically required.

**G05 — Financial and disclosure boundaries.** Confirmation SHALL NOT mutate
posted money, designation, restriction, source-code/campaign attribution,
commitment application, receipt, communication or consent. P13/P14/P16 retain
their facts. Every read/derived reason/export/notification/result recovery SHALL
use current source/P12 permission. Raw evidence access and confirmation are
separate. Only a currently permitted purpose description may appear; task
visibility grants no need-body, donor-body, sensitive location, coach or AI access.

**G06 — Narrow tenant participation.** D30 SHALL be tenant optional, initially
Off, and require deliberate qualified activation. This is an explicit narrow
amendment to required native behavior for this one-time-need reason only. It
SHALL NOT disable D19, D29, D22's independently configured amount reason,
fulfillment, permitted recurring attention or authorized manual work. Once
enabled and qualified, native admission SHALL require no additional manual task
creation or optional Workflow Studio installation. The proposed policy SHALL be
one organization-wide On/Off control with no per-need overrides. This scope is an
explicit product recommendation awaiting ratification, not an answered founder
clarification or a rule inferred from D16/P27 review controls.

**G07 — Original receipt and prospective eligibility.** Activation SHALL define
an ordered prospective interval for the exact D30 criterion. Policy membership
SHALL be determined by the first owner-certified eligible received-support
occurrence, not the gift date, webhook arrival, later label, import or worker
lookup. A genuine later source-finality event can first qualify after activation;
historical acquisition cannot. Purpose evidence may precede receipt but creates
no task before received support. First valid later confirmation may admit the
same originally live, still-unhandled gift only while its applicable enabled
interval continues. This expressly carries D29's narrow late-first-confirmation
exception into D30, without permitting old-history reclassification.

**G08 — Off, re-enable and accepted repair.** Off SHALL stop new D30 admissions
and end that interval for unadmitted candidates. Accepted reasons and durable
intents SHALL remain recoverable. Re-enable SHALL start a new prospective
interval, not revive never-admitted earlier candidates or Off receipts. Admission
and Off SHALL have one authoritative order. Correction SHALL withdraw only an
actually invalid affected reason; unavailable evidence is not invalidity.
Qualified restoration of the same already admitted, originally live and still
unconsumed purpose MAY repair existing work while Off. Handled coverage cannot
replay through correction or a new rule version. A technical pause/outage and a
business-policy Off state SHALL not be conflated.

**G09 — Closure preserves the proper facts.** Goal attainment, operational
closure or archival of a need's Request/Appeal SHALL NOT itself erase the
confirmed gift purpose, complete follow-up or reopen solicitation. A current
source correction that proves a different/invalid gift purpose SHALL requalify
the affected reason. A received gift recorded after closure MAY qualify only
through its actual eligible source and D30 policy/provenance; closed coordination
does not itself establish rejection or permission. Purpose-dependent solicitation
remains fenced by P27. No gift-care task may silently be relabeled to evade a
source communication or consent restriction.

**G10 — One shared effect.** D30 SHALL use D20's canonical contribution,
qualified Party, typed ministry and special-purpose member. Multiple needs,
appeals, eligible portions, D29/D30 reasons, retries or evidence revisions SHALL
NOT multiply that member. Preserve exact independent gift records/reasons and
reviewed-set history. Purpose admission, minimal history/result and durable
identifier-only projection intent SHALL commit atomically; task materialization
may recover asynchronously. Done/Not needed remains the code-owned independent
follow-up contract and conveys no financial or communication outcome.

**G11 — Historical meaning and lawful disposition.** Retain only source-qualified
minimal purpose/effect evidence, with its original terms and attributable
correction. A current title/goal edit SHALL NOT rewrite the historical purpose.
No universal retention timer, duplicate evidence body, undeletable sensitive
snapshot or borrowed D28 preparation-retention policy follows. Source erasure,
restriction and restore SHALL preserve lawful minimal effect history while
denying unauthorized disclosure and preventing replay. Recorder departure does
not itself falsify an earlier valid affirmation.

**G12 — Guarded simple settings.** The fixed rule SHALL use current qualified
automation-management authority mapped through P12, a declarative definition,
exact saved-policy preview, an inert test of the actual evaluator and durable
activation/activity evidence. Neither preview nor test may create business
intent, task, donor communication or source history. Loading/unavailable is
distinct from persisted Off. Turn off remains usable without saving an invalid
draft or requiring activation tests. An actual disclosure-policy widening
separately follows ADR0211; ordinary eligible confirmation does not require it.

**G13 — Exact release gates.** Every supported route SHALL qualify its own
source-purpose projection, financial scope/lineage, privacy, current command,
P12 action, correction/retention and D20 effect semantics. E7, C-01 or an
unqualified recognition route SHALL gate only operations depending on that
unresolved authority. Do not assume the current generic task API implements
this feature, or present a manual task fallback as completed D30. Deployment
SHALL preserve live/history provenance and consumed effects; rollback or a
producer pause cannot delete accepted work or re-admit handled gifts.

## Decisions versus implementation work

The unanswered scope question is genuinely product-level: whether tenant control
needs per-need exceptions. The strongest case for the exception model is an
organization that wants routine need-gift follow-up but excludes selected large
campaigns. That workflow has not been verified here, and source identity plus
prospective override precedence adds real cost. One organization-wide policy is
therefore the economical recommendation. If the founder selects overrides,
exact need identity, override authority, effective precedence and interval
semantics must be added; no new Need registry is automatically justified.

The initial Off recommendation must be presented explicitly in the amendment:
it prevents unreviewed task activation and uses the established guarded
configuration pattern. It is not justified by an asserted legal requirement or
measured user preference. The reviewed amendment can be ratified without an
additional question if accepted; it cannot be mislabeled as already selected.

Exact schema names, capability identifiers, lock order, indexes, event transport,
reason enum names and adapter layout are engineering decisions. Qualification of
authoritative need evidence and provenance is substantive implementation proof,
not another founder poll or a license to defer required behavior indefinitely.
The source-family contract must distinguish accepted one-time gift purpose from
financial restriction/application; label coincidence cannot bridge the owners.

## Focused proof requirements

1. With policy On, an exact qualified prospective received gift for the recorded
   one-time vehicle repair purpose creates its D20 member without additionality,
   threshold or promise fulfillment. General support, annual recurring appeal,
   one-off payment and generic goal/link alone do not qualify.
2. Existing purpose records are referenced with exact terms; an eligible
   unsolicited gift requires no fictional Request. An unauthorized actor cannot
   record/confirm a purpose by calling the ordinary task endpoint or by holding
   a D29-only action. Confirmation changes no financial fields.
3. Both admission/Off orders, delayed original-live confirmation, genuine later
   finality, Off-period receipt, re-enable and already-admitted restoration have
   the G07/G08 results. Unknown policy/source is neither Off nor false success.
4. D29+D30, two needs, overlapping appeals and duplicate delivery produce one
   member. Changed purpose/partial reversal requalifies only the affected reason,
   not other reasons or already handled history.
5. Closing/funding/archiving a need preserves valid received-purpose meaning and
   independent work; no solicitation or ended effect revives. A true purpose
   correction is distinguishable from operational closure.
6. Policy management, need-source editing, confirmation, raw evidence reading,
   financial attribution and task handling are independently denied when absent;
   cross-tenant, wrong Legal Entity, stale assignment and protected need-title
   cases reveal no forbidden details through lists, errors or preview.
7. Inert activation test produces no tasks/intents/history. Worker outage after
   accepted intent recovers once. Unavailable E7-dependent measure does not block
   a separately qualified direct purpose route or money posting.

Before future formalization, reconcile P28's native purpose and customization,
P12's exact actions, P27's permitted purpose/closure projection and shared task
owner semantics. Carry G01–G13 and the actual selected policy scope into formal
requirements/design/tests at the authorized stage. No new monitor is offered as
a substitute for these invariants or release gates.
