# D30 evidence — one-time-need purpose and financial lifecycle

Research-only owner review, October 18, 2026 UTC. The founder selected A:
qualified received support for a specific one-time need may independently
qualify for personal follow-up without being described as additional support.
The organization-control clarification—one organization-wide On/Off policy or
an organization default with per-need choices—is pending at this writing. The
precise amendments below are proposals, not ratified requirements or proof of
implemented source commands. D29 and its amendments are now ratified.

Only this evidence note is authored by this workstream. No formal owner
contract, application, schema, provider, ticket or live data was changed.

## Recommended interpretation

Accept A with bounded amendments. Extend P28's qualified special-gift purpose
producer with a specific-one-time-need reason. Require both an exact versioned,
bounded need-purpose context and attributable evidence that the exact received
gift or portion was accepted for that purpose. A payment made once, an appeal
end date, a campaign tag or a current label does not establish those facts.

This is stewardship purpose, not a new legal restriction, financial designation,
commitment application, Need ledger or fundraising management system. A need
may be documented in an existing qualified source without requiring a new
appeal, project, campaign, fund or public page. Existing P27 Request/appeal terms,
P13 accepted-purpose evidence or a governed publication are candidate sources,
not automatically qualified universal Need records. The adapter must establish
their exact scope, version, meaning and current eligibility.

If a legitimate documented need has no existing independently managed source,
the smallest proposed extension is a bounded purpose context within the same
P28 operational-purpose producer. It carries the identified purpose and source
provenance necessary for qualification, not a separate goal/balance/expense,
financial-allocation, obligation or publication engine. Do not silently assert
that such a source already exists or force the founder to create an appeal to
make the selected ordinary journey work.

## Proposed practical lifecycle

The specific purpose's original meaning, the donor's acceptance, received money,
the organization's automation policy, current need coordination and human task
handling are separate facts. No single editable “Need status” owns all of them.

| Case                                                                                                              | Bounded outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Donor accepts an exact documented vehicle-repair purpose; eligible money is received                              | The qualified D30 reason may admit within the selected policy and prospective cohort, regardless of additionality.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| The gift is for ordinary recurring ministry support through a campaign ending this month                          | No one-time-need reason merely from the campaign/end date/payment frequency. Other qualified reasons remain independent.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| A giving page preselects the vehicle purpose and the donor accepts the clearly disclosed exact gift intent        | A source-qualified captured acceptance may establish purpose without forcing a redundant checkbox or repeat staff confirmation. A preselection, page visit or internal tag alone cannot.                                                                                                           |
| Public copy says vehicle repairs but the accepted gift is general support without qualified need-purpose evidence | Do not infer the mapping. Preserve received money and ordinary work; qualify the exact acceptance or use the authorized human-evidence path.                                                                                                                                                       |
| One gift includes ordinary support and a specified vehicle portion                                                | Preserve the original exact source-bound portion and independently prove its current eligible survivor under D29's rules. Do not classify the whole gift.                                                                                                                                          |
| One gift supports several identified needs                                                                        | Preserve each accepted purpose scope and its evidence, but resolve one D20 gift-purpose member per compatible Party/subject lane. Do not add overlapping portions or need totals as if disjoint, or create one task per need.                                                                      |
| A need becomes funded, expires, closes or is archived after the gift was accepted                                 | Preserve original gift-purpose evidence and already admitted work. Coordination status alone does not falsify accepted purpose, declare the follow-up done or reopen solicitation.                                                                                                                 |
| Money arrives after the need closes                                                                               | P13 and the actual purpose owner determine whether receipt/acceptance is eligible. If they qualify it and the D30 source/policy cohort admits it, stewardship may follow without reopening the appeal. D30 cannot force acceptance, silently redirect money or override an ineligible designation. |
| A donor statement is recorded later for an eligible live-cohort receipt                                           | Allow source-qualified first confirmation of that same unconsumed purpose, subject to the selected current admission policy and its generation semantics. No historical reclassification sweep.                                                                                                    |
| Need label changes or its future purpose becomes ongoing                                                          | Preserve the original accepted version. A new label/future purpose does not retroactively reclassify prior receipts or grant a new task reason.                                                                                                                                                    |
| The original need/gift attribution was erroneous or donor intent is contradicted                                  | Append qualified correction and requalify only affected unhandled reasons. Any required legal-purpose, designation or application correction stays with its owner.                                                                                                                                 |
| A wholly need-directed scope is partially refunded                                                                | Its reason may remain when the source proves positive eligible surviving support for that same accepted need purpose.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Mixed support is partially refunded without identifying which purpose portion was affected                        | Withhold affected purpose-specific reasons until source qualification resolves them. Do not guess ordinary-first, proportional, minimum or surviving need allocation. Other independently valid reasons and human work survive.                                                                    |
| Gift already covered under another compatible special-gift reason                                                 | Add permitted factual context if qualified, but no new gift member, task or replay of handled work. Distinct first-support/receipt/fulfillment obligations remain separate unless their owners prove shared action coverage.                                                                       |

The proposed receipt cohort follows D29's explicit discipline but requires its
own D30 qualification: prospective durable originally-live received-support
membership, no preactivation receipts, no history-only imports, and no new
membership from edited need definitions, policy changes or worker timestamps.
Later genuine purpose confirmation is allowed for an eligible unconsumed member
of that cohort; this is not a catch-up feature. Exact organization-policy
continuation/Off semantics must reconcile with the pending customization answer.
Neither need closure nor the policy switch may alter financial acceptance facts.

## Material findings and exact proposed clauses

### F01 — “Specific one-time need” could become an unqualified label

- **What:** A campaign end date, temporary label or user-supplied need ID may be
  treated as a proven bounded purpose.
- **Why:** Existing campaigns, appeals, Requests, designations and pages have
  different identities; none is universally a one-time Need source.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High without a closed source-purpose adapter.
- **Evidence:** S01, S08–S12; P13:1620 and P27 F75–F77 explicitly separate meanings.
- **Answer impact:** A needs a precise source extension, not a hidden assumption
  that the repository already has a canonical Need ledger.
- **Permanent fix:** Qualify versioned bounded-purpose context from actual
  owners; use the smallest P28 operational context only where necessary.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “D30 SHALL qualify a specific, bounded one-time
  purpose and its exact accepted version independently of payment cadence,
  campaign dates, labels and membership. It SHALL consume an explicit qualified
  owner context; absent such a context, the bounded P28 purpose producer MAY
  supply only the required identified purpose and provenance. No generic Need,
  financial allocation, balance, fundraising or workflow engine follows.”
- **Proof:** Ordinary recurring appeal ending this month versus actual vehicle
  repair need; changed label; unrelated public project; legitimate documented
  need with no appeal; unknown source context must not become false success.

### F02 — A default could be treated as acceptance, or valid acceptance could gain needless ceremony

- **What:** A preselected destination could automatically prove donor intent, or
  every donor could be forced to click a new confirmation for a purpose already
  clearly disclosed and accepted.
- **Why:** Internal defaults differ from actual source-qualified acceptance;
  acceptance need not require a custom extra checkbox.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High in page/cart/import integration.
- **Evidence:** S05 accepted-purpose snapshot; S07:1542–1557 exact accepted intent;
  S01 explicitly rejects default-fund-alone inference.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve a simple ordinary giving journey while requiring
  the exact source to establish the assertion.
- **Permanent fix:** Captured exact accepted publication/terms or qualified human
  attestation; no clickstream, tag or bare default as substitute.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “A disclosed default MAY contribute to D30 proof
  only when its exact identified need purpose and gift scope were included in
  the source-qualified intent the donor actually accepted. Preselection, page
  presence, campaign attribution, completion of an unrelated payment or current
  copy alone SHALL NOT establish that acceptance. Already qualified evidence
  SHALL be reused without a compulsory duplicate donor or staff affirmation.”
- **Proof:** Default accepted unchanged; donor deliberately selects another
  purpose; page copy changes after acceptance; generic general-support donation
  from a vehicle page; unqualified imported default; stale giving binding.

### F03 — Stewardship purpose could rewrite legal restrictions or financial applications

- **What:** Recording a need could move money, impose a legal restriction,
  release excess funds or classify a commitment payment as additional.
- **Why:** P13 legal accepted-purpose authority, P16 application and P28
  stewardship are independent meanings with different evidence and powers.
- **Severity:** Critical.
- **Likelihood:** High if a common mutable purpose field is reused.
- **Evidence:** S04–S06, S14–S15; P13:793–815; P16:1005–1031.
- **Answer impact:** A does not need an additionality assertion and does not
  authorize financial mutation or resolve C-01/E7.
- **Permanent fix:** Separate operational reason, exact financial references and
  source-owned conflict resolution; preserve original accepted legal purpose.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Need-purpose confirmation SHALL change no posted
  financial column, legal restriction/preference, accepted excess-use authority,
  designation, commitment application or receipt. A gift may fulfill a commitment
  and still qualify for the identified need; application alone is neither proof
  nor veto. Contradictory authoritative instructions for the same scope SHALL
  require owner resolution. Need fulfillment, closure or surplus SHALL confer
  no authority to redirect or spend money.”
- **Proof:** P28 writes leave financial and application facts unchanged; ordinary
  promise for a need; ambiguous same-portion instructions; surplus after goal;
  change of designation or legal purpose through its independent owner only.

### F04 — Multiple needs or portions could create duplicate money or task coverage

- **What:** One gift could be counted repeatedly, entirely classified from one
  small portion, or create one task per need/evidence version.
- **Why:** Need labels are reasons/context; D20's business member is the actual
  contribution/Party/typed-ministry/special-follow-up purpose.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High when arrays of need references are treated as allocations.
- **Evidence:** S02 C05; S03 C03–C04; S09 F79 line/amount coverage and no overlap sums.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve mixed and multiple-need gifts without a second
  allocation ledger or per-need task identity.
- **Permanent fix:** Exact source-bound whole/portion evidence, no inferred sums;
  D20 compatible member and independently qualified current need reasons.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Each need assertion SHALL identify its exact whole
  or expressly stated portion at the referenced source revision, retaining the
  original meaning separately from current surviving support. Explicit fractions
  MAY resolve exactly under source precision without rounding. Multiple needs,
  allocations, assertions and revisions SHALL NOT multiply one D20 business
  member. Overlapping or duplicate purpose scopes SHALL NOT be summed as disjoint
  money; unresolved allocation meaning SHALL remain unqualified.”
- **Proof:** One gift 30 vehicle/20 medical/50 ordinary; repeated corroborating
  statement; overlapping purpose labels; two equal gifts; cross-ministry split;
  exact half versus indivisible half; one reason withdrawn with another valid.

### F05 — Need closure/funding could cancel stewardship or reopen solicitation

- **What:** Funded/closed need could erase valid follow-up, or a later gift could
  reopen appeal membership and generate new solicitation.
- **Why:** Need coordination, current giving eligibility, past accepted purpose
  and independent human work have different source predicates.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High with a generic shared `active` flag.
- **Evidence:** S11 F84; S12:33–35; S06:2347–2364; S07:1566.
- **Answer impact:** A should handle late legitimate support without overriding
  financial acceptance or creating a new solicitation permission.
- **Permanent fix:** Preserve qualified original purpose; independently reprove
  source receipt acceptance and D30 admission; leave coordination closed.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “A need becoming funded, expired, closed or archived
  SHALL NOT by itself invalidate accepted gift-purpose evidence, complete or
  cancel independent follow-up, or reopen solicitation. A later receipt MAY
  qualify only when the actual source accepts eligible support for that purpose
  and D30's cohort/policy admits it. If source giving eligibility has ended,
  D30 SHALL NOT override it or silently substitute another purpose. Goal
  attainment SHALL NOT be inferred to close every appeal or designation.”
- **Proof:** Gift accepted before closure but posted later; new accepted late
  offline gift; prohibited new checkout after source closure; funded appeal still
  coordinating; completed task; independent promised call; actual source refund.

### F06 — Definition edits and late evidence could manufacture historical admissions

- **What:** Renaming a need, converting an ongoing purpose to one-time, enabling
  a rule or recording a late statement might generate work across old receipts.
- **Why:** Current purpose metadata and recent processing timestamps do not
  prove what a donor accepted or whether the gift is in the prospective cohort.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High in migration, replay and configuration paths.
- **Evidence:** S02 C07; S03 C05/C11; S11 F86; S13 imports fire no tasks.
- **Answer impact:** Permit useful delayed qualification without selecting a
  historical catch-up campaign or a new freshness duration.
- **Permanent fix:** Exact original purpose/version and durable source-live
  cohort; defined current policy qualification; record late facts truthfully.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “D30 SHALL qualify a prospective source-live receipt
  cohort and preserve original accepted purpose versions. No preactivation
  receipt, history-only import, definition edit, policy enable/re-enable or
  processing timestamp SHALL create live membership. First genuine later
  confirmation MAY qualify an unconsumed receipt already in the admitted cohort
  under the applicable current policy. Definition correction SHALL preserve prior
  evidence and may withdraw an invalid reason; future-purpose changes SHALL NOT
  silently reclassify earlier gifts.”
- **Proof:** Pre/postactivation receipt race; closed need with delayed eligible
  receipt; later confirmation; historical import; edited need name/meaning;
  new organization policy; replay of an already handled gift.

### F07 — Refunds could invent which need still has support

- **What:** A partial refund could be assigned to ordinary support or the least
  convenient need, or original evidence could be rewritten to a new balance.
- **Why:** Money conservation does not establish purpose-specific surviving
  coverage when the source cannot identify the affected portion.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Medium; source contracts explicitly admit partial corrections,
  but no production frequency is claimed.
- **Evidence:** S02 C05; S05 append-only fold; S14:1027–1031; S03 C11.
- **Answer impact:** A needs exact correction semantics, not local refund math.
- **Permanent fix:** Source-qualified survivors and reason-specific uncertainty;
  original assertion immutable; restore only the same unconsumed live purpose.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “P13/P14 SHALL remain authoritative for effective
  surviving support. Preserve the original need assertion and original amount.
  A wholly need-directed scope MAY retain its reason with source-proven positive
  eligible surviving support. For mixed or multiple-purpose scope, an
  unattributed partial reversal SHALL withhold affected need-specific reasons
  until qualified resolution; no ordinary-first, proportional or minimum guess
  follows. Source correction MAY restore the same still-unconsumed originally
  live purpose under current eligibility, preserving history and independent work.”
- **Proof:** Original 25 of 125 for vehicle with identified 10-of-25 refund;
  whole-vehicle gift partial refund; unknown mixed reversal; full reversal;
  another valid amount/additional reason; restoration after valid withdrawal.

### F08 — Recognition and need titles could widen identity or contact access

- **What:** A specific medical/travel/security need could leak through a reason,
  or recognition could fan out to advisors, spouses or church members.
- **Why:** Task visibility is not unrestricted purpose-body or recipient authority.
- **Severity:** Critical for sensitive scope.
- **Likelihood:** Medium–high if the task stores a raw need label or Party joins.
- **Evidence:** S10 F80/F82; S13; S02 C04; existing P3/P10/P12 owners remain binding.
- **Answer impact:** A authorizes a factual purpose reason only through a qualified
  projection, not public need discovery or a new audience.
- **Permanent fix:** Purpose-minimized projection and exact current recipient
  route; no private-note republication, hidden need counts or church-member stream.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “D30 SHALL disclose only the current permitted
  need-purpose reason and source scope. Task access SHALL NOT expose private need
  descriptions, correspondence, care/location facts or otherwise hidden needs.
  Exact P14 attribution, anonymity and recipient restrictions remain governing;
  a purpose statement creates no contact, household fan-out, church-member
  thank-you or financial authority. Actual communication remains independently
  authorized.”
- **Proof:** Restricted need with generic safe reason; no safe projection;
  anonymous advisor; shared email/spouse; church roster; multiple needs with one
  hidden; permitted detail access revoked after a reviewed task opens.

### F09 — E7 could be bypassed or become an unnecessary global prerequisite

- **What:** Source-code remapping could silently prove need purpose, or all
  D30 work could be blocked until campaign-reporting ambiguity is resolved.
- **Why:** E7 concerns unresolved temporal campaign attribution, not every
  independently evidenced gift-purpose relation.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High when campaign links are the convenient existing join.
- **Evidence:** S08:456/1608/1631–1633; S12:41–45; S09 F79.
- **Answer impact:** A remains valid with an independently qualified exact source;
  affected mapping-derived lanes stay unavailable without deleting native scope.
- **Permanent fix:** Declare the actual adapter and evidence used; no new live
  mapping policy, no duplicate gift-to-appeal financial attribution store.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “D30 SHALL NOT resolve E7 by selecting live campaign
  remapping, frozen attribution or a parallel financial association. A reason
  depending on that unresolved meaning SHALL remain unavailable until its owner
  qualifies the adapter. Independently qualified exact donor-accepted need
  purpose MAY proceed without the affected received-toward-appeal measure.
  Missing association proof SHALL NOT become known absence or a broad stop on
  received money and unrelated legitimate work.”
- **Proof:** Same source code remapped after receipt; previously unmapped code;
  direct exact-purpose evidence unaffected by remap; unavailable appeal total
  alongside valid independently qualified need reason; no hidden money total.

### F10 — Pending customization could become unselected policy, access or cleanup behavior

- **What:** The review might silently choose per-need switches, mutable historical
  rule meaning, automatic cleanup of accepted work or new confirmation grants.
- **Why:** The founder's independent policy-scope clarification is pending;
  permission, trigger availability, source facts and task coverage are separate.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High without explicit status and source-bound policy meaning.
- **Evidence:** S01; S02 selected exact-action pattern; D20 coverage; the current
  clarification is an unresolved founder answer, not a source implementation fact.
- **Answer impact:** Accept the purpose criterion while keeping only the genuine
  policy-scope choice pending. No technical schema decision needs a new question.
- **Permanent fix:** Resolve the pending scope; use existing P12 settings and one

- **Exact proposed clause:** “Organization customization SHALL configure only

## Minimal amendments and proof boundaries

Later formalization should amend only the exact seams:

1. **P28 purpose admission:** the specific-one-time-need reason, exact bounded
   source context and attributable acceptance, compatible D20 member, selected
   organization control and explicit source cohort.
2. **P13/P14 consumer qualification:** current eligible received support,
   surviving exact portions, proper recognition/recipient scope and correction
   lineage. No mutable financial fields or new money allocation.
3. **P27/other actual purpose-context owner:** explicit reference/version and
   acceptance semantics, safe closure/late-fact consumption, no appeal membership
   inference or alternative attribution ledger. Register only real source kinds.
4. **P12/P3/P10:** exact purpose confirmation and policy-management actions,
   source/subject ceilings, current minimally disclosed reason and derivative
   access. D29's actor class is a useful selected pattern; a new D30 purpose
   mapping must still be explicitly qualified, not inferred from general access.
5. **Existing shared-task source contract:** same semantic member, multiple
   independent reasons, exact reviewed revision set and handled coverage,
   correction/Undo/handoff under D20. No extra task engine.

Required proof covers accepted defaults versus metadata; exact purpose-version
identity; no-appeal legitimate need; mixed and overlapping scopes; source-qualified
survivors; ordinary fulfilled promise versus additionality; funded/closed versus
new financial acceptance; late facts/cohort ordering; multiple reasons and
consumed coverage; source-authorized need disclosure; E7-dependent versus
independent lanes; and the selected policy's actual admission races.

This workstream performed document inspection and structural verification only.
It did not run application/database probes, evaluate actual permissions, test
hosted data, contact ministries or measure runtime performance. No fixed legal
retention duration, numerical throughput, refund attribution algorithm or
unselected tenant-control shape is claimed. Existing D29 retained-evidence
qualification and D20 source/task proof limits still apply.
