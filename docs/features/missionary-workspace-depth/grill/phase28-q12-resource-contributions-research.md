## The original product choice (now answered)

**For the initial release, should missionaries be able to submit resources for
other missionaries to use?**

- **A — Organization-managed library (recommended).** Authorized organization
  curators supply and publish resources; missionaries find and use what they
  are permitted to access. No missionary submission workflow is added initially.
- **B — Also allow missionary submissions.** Missionaries can propose resources
  for authorized review and publication into the shared library. Submission does
  not make material visible to other missionaries automatically.

Illustrative scenario, not user research: Maya has a useful support-raising
guide she wants others to find. Under A, the organization manages what enters
its resource library through its authorized publishing work. Under B, Maya can
submit the guide from her workspace for review. The decision changes contribution
rights and the publication job, not merely where a Resources tab appears.

Recommend A for a focused initial resource experience: dependable organization-
provided guidance without an additional submission/review workflow. B may bring
valuable field experience into the collection, but needs an accountable publication
owner, truthful submission states and contributor feedback. Neither benefit is
claimed as measured Asym demand. The founder can choose the broader scope now.

Both alternatives retain organization-controlled audiences and current document
access. A person separately authorized as an organization curator keeps those
powers through that context; “missionary” is not a ban on separately held rights.
The choice concerns the ordinary missionary contribution journey, not existing
CRM attachments, donor documents, personal drafts or public Ministry Updates.

No new community marketplace, course system, geography sharing, social feed,
public publication, auto-notification or external storage dependency follows.
Selecting B would require a bounded native submission/publication contract; it
would not preselect a workflow engine or permit unreviewed shared uploads.
