## Disposition and decisive boundary

**Accept with required amendments.** Maya may resume an exact eligible, retained preparation source entry she originally authored in the same organization and ministry, after current original-author identity, assignment, source-history, classification and retention rules authorize discovery and use. Resumption is a source-owned working-context effect on the same record. It is not a grant replay, an author rewrite, archive return, canonical-contact creation, copy/import, backup restore or retained-data ownership transfer.

**A current working-context association is compatible with the owner model only if it is not authorization.** It may make a deliberately selected eligible entry convenient to continue under the current assignment; it cannot confer history visibility or cause all previously active entries to appear automatically. The source can reuse an existing association mechanism. D27 need not freeze a new relation, duplicate resumed-state ledger, ACL engine or per-entry staff approval queue. Original author and origin assignment remain evidence of where preparation came from; current assignment and history permission are independently resolved present authority.

## Lean invariant and transition requirements

The following is exact proposed source/spec language, not a migration design.

1. **Author and permission are different facts.** The preparation source SHALL preserve immutable original-author identity and original tenant/ministry/assignment provenance. Present use SHALL independently require a verified current principal linked to that original author through the qualified identity owner, the exact current Active Tenant Assignment, the same organization and ministry, and explicit current preparation-history permission. A matching name/email, recreated login, role, assignment or historical author ID alone SHALL grant neither discovery nor use. D27 SHALL NOT invent identity merging or account recovery; where identity lineage is unresolved, deny without exposing candidate content.
2. **Authorize before discovery.** The exact history decision SHALL apply before search, totals, suggestions, badges, snippets, selection, previews, linked-record lookup, deep-link resolution, exports, read receipts, audit-body recovery and cached results. No count or “earlier preparation exists” prompt SHALL reveal ineligible records. Administrative ability to configure resumption SHALL NOT grant private-body access. An unavailable/denied history check SHALL not claim content was deleted or that the user never created any.
3. **Same record, deliberate continuation.** Resume SHALL select an exact currently eligible source entry and establish or reuse its source-owned current working-context association. It SHALL not copy the body, rewrite original author/origin assignment, make a new canonical person, promote private assertions to shared CRM, or treat continuation state as access authority. New/current membership never inherits the previous membership's grant. If the existing source supports this effect without a new association relation, reuse it.
4. **Preserve separate state.** A retained record's active/archived working state, source revision, body disposition, identity provenance and current authorization SHALL remain separate facts. Previously archived preparation remains archived; ordinary D4 Return to active prospects remains explicit. Departure/return alone SHALL not bulk-resume earlier active items. Repeating Resume for the same admitted source/current-context intent SHALL be a no-op or recover its same result, not another record or repeated activity.
5. **No retention reset or historical promotion.** Resume/read/retry SHALL not reset source retention triggers, original creation/authored timestamps, contact assertion verification dates, lawful holds or disposition. Retained audit/tombstone metadata without a readable body SHALL not be presented as recoverable preparation. No promise of indefinite retention or restoration of purged data is introduced. A genuine subsequent content edit follows its source's already qualified correction/retention rules; D27 cannot silently treat a continuation-association timestamp as a new content creation time.
6. **Current facts and restrictive changes prevail.** Resumption SHALL show the authorized source's current eligible revision with clear provenance for earlier personal assertions. It SHALL not restore an old snapshot over later corrections, survivor/merge restrictions, contact suppression or a stricter classification. Retained links do not grant linked party/contact visibility. Conflicts requiring correction go through the source's existing conflict path; do not expose a hidden duplicate/merged record to explain denial.
7. **Tenant-aware keys and immutability.** Eligible preparation and continuation records SHALL use nonnull tenant/source-context/author bindings and tenant-consistent FK references where expressible; membership-based authority references the exact assignment, not bare person. Use a composite unique key or equivalent authoritative constraint for at most one current continuation effect per source/current assignment/context. Caller input cannot change origin author, origin assignment, tenant, ministry, retained-history cutoff, approval, policy revision or audit actor. Derive or verify those from server/source authority. Unknown legacy provenance SHALL not be filled with the returning user by guess.
8. **Atomic resume and semantic replay.** Resume SHALL validate current permission, disposition, exact source revision and current assignment generation at its authoritative commit point. Commit the working-context effect and required minimal business audit atomically. A business operation ID binds that exact intent; a repeated ID with changed meaning rejects. Recovery of a committed result SHALL reauthorize before returning content. A stale grant, old assignment, restore or replay SHALL not revive ended permission. Real current time applies after waits at expiry boundaries, not only transaction-start time.
9. **No outward effects.** Resume SHALL not restart old grants/shares, coaching episodes, subscriptions, provider connections, reminders, scheduled sends, workflows, incomplete/completed/cancelled tasks or communication preferences. It SHALL not replay old outbox records. D27's association receipt is provenance, never an executor instruction. Normal later user actions require their separate current command/consent rules.
10. **Disposition and deletion integrity.** Original author attribution necessary for lawful history SHALL not be lost by an accidental Auth/profile cascade or rewritten to staff. Choose source-owned restrict, durable nonlogin attribution/tombstone or qualified pseudonymization behavior based on actual retention/erasure duties; do not retain all body plaintext merely to keep an FK alive. Source purge and current denial must take effect before restored data is disclosed. An independent canonical party, financial record or another worker's preparation must never be deleted or retargeted by this workflow.

No new monetary fields, financial arithmetic, copied gift facts, task counters or arbitrary configurable workflow engine are required. Dates used for provenance/retention need their source-defined timezone/instant semantics; UI locale formatting cannot move a cutoff. Bounded text, nonblank/source validity checks, valid state combinations and indexes should follow the qualified preparation source rather than a new D27 content schema.

## RLS, commands, storage and cache requirements

Keep the preparation source protected behind its exact server boundary unless a separately qualified browser contract exists. In exposed schemas use least-privilege grants plus RLS; do not inherit the staff-note grants/policies. Anonymous users get no D27 read/write. Broad authenticated SELECT or author-only SELECT cannot express current assignment/history/retention qualification. A user being the original author is a necessary provenance predicate, not an authorization shortcut.

For an allowed mutation, examine both old-row and proposed-row policy behavior, SELECT prerequisites, column immutability, every permissive policy combined by OR, unique/FK errors and direct Data API access. Explicit `WITH CHECK` does not itself stop a permitted row being relabeled into another source/context if the check is too broad. Use one purpose-specific source command with trusted actor and immutable scope fields for the multi-record continuation/audit effect. A policy enum, UI-hidden column, app-level Zod schema or service-role call does not protect alternate directly granted paths by itself.

Inventory views/materialized views/functions/RPCs/GraphQL, role ownership, BYPASSRLS and actual default grants. Invoker views require safe underlying privileges; never grant raw historical bodies solely to make an invoker view readable. Narrow definers need qualified names, secure search path, minimal owner/EXECUTE scope and current source checks. Privileged service paths and repair/backfill jobs must enforce the same P12/source decision rather than bypassing it because RLS is enabled. No new Storage object/download, AI index, body-rich telemetry, public link or offline body store is authorized.

Protected client keys/results must bind actor, current assignment generation, tenant, ministry, source/history policy epoch and admitted source revision. On same-user assignment/context loss, clear reached private state, cancel work where possible and reject late results. Existing user-ID cache clearing is useful but insufficient for a same-Auth-ID return. Authorization precedes fetching/displaying a result as current; do not show an old private page as a placeholder while checking access. Browser Back, another tab, a restored mutation receipt or an old file handle must not reintroduce ended history access. Previously authorized viewed bytes cannot be recalled.

Lists should query the eligible source slice before paging and avoid loading every tenant note into memory. Index exact tenant/author/source-scope/eligible-disposition/ordering predicates and current assignment lookups as justified by measured plans. Pagination must not leak hidden totals, and counts must use the same authorization as rows. No unmeasured “fast at scale” claim is made.

## Proof still required before activation

Actual source inventory and original-author lineage qualification; source-owned retention/history/disposition rules; P12 exact current grant mapping; nonempty migration/backfill; direct database/API role tests; same-user assignment and context-switch browser tests; real transaction races; no hidden discovery via totals/errors; no author/scope mutable-field bypass; no implicit privilege from restored data; source-correction and suppression preservation; idempotent continuation; lost-response recovery; archive-state preservation; no restarted outbox/provider effects; actual indexed bounded query plans; and rendered accessible UX testing.

Test allow and deny with actual unrelated/old/current author identities, old/recreated assignments, wrong tenant/ministry, staff settings administrator, coach, successor and service/repair actor. Positive tests must prove the exact allowed row is returned, not merely that a query succeeded with an empty result. Erased/unavailable/metadata-only records must never become readable body success.
