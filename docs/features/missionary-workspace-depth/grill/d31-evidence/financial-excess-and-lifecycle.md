# D31 financial excess and lifecycle evidence

Research captured 2026-10-18. The founder selected Q31 A: every eligible positive,
confirmed amount beyond the applicable commitment qualifies, with no additional
minimum. The detailed owner amendments below are recommendations for this review,
not separately ratified requirements or proof of implemented financial behavior.
Only this research note is changed; D30 evidence and formal owner contracts remain
untouched.

## Outcome and the actual owner gap

Accept the selected product direction with an explicit, bounded P16 comparison
extension. P13 owns effective received money; P14 owns attribution/recognition;
P16 owns the commitment, lawful financial association, terms and comparison;
P28 consumes the authorized comparison through the existing D20 gift-purpose
member. A missionary does not certify a comparison or change money by completing
a follow-up.

The gap is concrete. P16 F.3 and O.11 cap authoritative applications at target
capacity. Therefore the existing application amount cannot represent the excess
portion. P16 M.2's `X = max(F - P, 0)` can instead arise from a later reduction of
the promise and does not identify a new gift. Neither unmatched money nor current
`X` is a sufficient substitute. Existing provider, donor-instruction and structured
mapping evidence may prove a comparison only to the extent that it actually binds
the entire relevant received portion to the exact comparison scope. An authority
that names only capped application allocations does not authorize the leftover by
implication.

The smallest permanent extension is source-owned comparison evidence attached to
the existing commitment/received-money association commands and projections. It
records exact input references, applicable terms, causal position, calculation
and correction lineage. It is not another money ledger, editable balance,
fulfillment application, donor-intent classifier, generic rules engine or task
engine. It never reserves or consumes money capacity.

## Concrete proposed comparison contract

### Exact scope, terms and financial association

There are two bounded comparison profiles:

1. One exact recurring occurrence line under the donor terms applicable to that
   occurrence. It is not a normalized monthly amount, all future recurrence,
   an assumed unpaid balance or another recurring period.
2. One exact fixed-pledge designation line under the donor terms applicable to
   the new gift, across its whole agreed target set, including scheduled future
   targets and genuine undated remainder. An installment is not the full fixed
   commitment. Other designations, commitments and currencies are not pooled.

The association proves the intended comparison scope for the full relevant
received-support portion, including any amount that cannot be applied because the
target is already fulfilled. Closed proof families are complete provider lineage,
authenticated exact donor scope instruction, approved authenticated structured
source mapping, or a qualified finance-staff determination with its exact source
evidence and current action authority. These are an explicit extension of P16's
financial evidence contract, not permission to reuse any existing opaque evidence
string. The last path remains staff-confirmed. No new missionary confirmation
button or D29/D30 purpose permission grants it.

Ordinary commitment context can establish this financial association without a
new donor statement that the gift is intentionally additional. Conversely, a
shared Party, date, amount, fund, memo, recognized supporter, household or campaign
does not establish it. An ended/nonexistent/released target is not silently
restored; a new late gift requires its own valid comparison scope. Later ordinary
closure of a once-valid scope does not falsify an already accepted historical
comparison.

### Causal allocation: two concurrent gifts cannot each claim the same excess

For one immutable comparison scope and applicable-terms version, let:

- `T` be its exact total promised support, not its remaining target capacity.
- `G_i` be this gift's exact eligible received-support portion attributable to
  that scope, in the same currency and source-supported minor units.
- `B_i` be the complete, source-certified prefix of previously accepted comparison
  associations attributable to that same scope and terms interpretation, excluding
  `G_i`. It is not the chronology of donor payments or gift dates.

P16 certifies:

```text
E_i = max(B_i + G_i - T, 0) - max(B_i - T, 0)
```

The prefix and gift use the same canonical contribution portions. Eligible gross
support is not net processor proceeds; fee cover is separately classified and
cannot inflate support by merely being included in the payment total. Existing
financial corrections determine current eligible support. No P28 FX, rounding,
fee subtraction, recognition allocation or guessed split is permitted.

The owner must append a stable causal association ordinal/revision while accepting
the source association under the existing typed commitment scope serialization
boundary, before asynchronous D31 evaluation. Where application and association
are accepted together, both reference that same authoritative command boundary;
the comparison does not create an additional application. The command extends
P16's permanent lock order to include its exact comparison scope and validates
source, terms, prior-prefix completeness and authority coherently. This is a
required owner amendment: P13's per-header `seq`, timestamp sorting, provider
delivery order and D31 worker order do not already supply it.

This ordinal orders legally accepted source associations, not a claim about the
chronological order in which a donor made payments. A genuinely new first lawful
association, including a delayed resolution of the original scope of a live
receipt, receives its position at that P16 owner command. It appends to the
accepted association order; it never invents an earlier association or inserts
itself before already accepted comparisons. The source must prove the gift's
original intention, applicable terms and receipt provenance, and certify the
complete accepted prefix under those same scope/terms semantics. Later new donor
purpose is not delayed proof of original intent.

In contrast, delayed delivery, retry or reconstruction of an association that was
already authoritatively accepted must recover its existing position. It cannot
receive a second ordinal because a task evaluator ran later. Missing original
position or prefix completeness in that recovery makes the comparison unavailable.
Financial commands can legitimately serialize in either order; the guarantee is
independence from D31 consumer delivery/query order, not independence from all
scheduling of first source commands. Raw webhook arrival or provider timestamps
are not themselves authoritative acceptance. The gift can still post, be receipted
when eligible and undergo ordinary authorized P16 resolution.

For `T = 100`, source order `A = 60`, then `B = 60` gives `E_A = 0`, `E_B = 20`.
The reversed legitimate source order assigns the 20 to A instead. Neither order
can produce two amounts of 20. Leave-one-out comparison against the final total
would wrongly give each gift 20 and is prohibited. A delayed D31 consumer cannot
choose between these orders or create a second accepted association. If A was
received first but was genuinely not associated until after B's lawful source
association, B then A is the actual accepted order, not a backdated A then B.

No canonical monetary portion is simultaneously allocated to overlapping
comparison scopes for this purpose. Explicit source splits may use disjoint
portions whose sum stays within effective eligible money. Recurring-to-fixed
coverage retains F.4's single-use linked meaning: a derived fixed cap is not a
second independent attribution of the same excess. Comparison evidence does not
reserve money or change any application. Reusing prior gifts as baseline context
for later comparisons is not reapplying them or admitting their old tasks again.

### Terms changes, correction and current surviving proof

`T` and the meaning of `B_i` and `G_i` must be constant for an individual
comparison. A genuinely prospective term revision gives gifts to which those new
terms actually apply a new version and a freshly source-certified baseline under that version's
exact same scope. It does not reuse a cached prefix with different scope
semantics or rewrite earlier certificates. This needs no separate configurable
epoch engine: the existing immutable term versions and source comparison records
carry the boundary. A baseline may include earlier authorized portions still
applicable under the new terms, but their participation creates no new gift or
historical reason. Excess amounts across different terms versions are not one
additive financial balance. A delayed first association of an earlier gift still
uses its proven originally applicable terms; its new association time does not
make later donor terms applicable to that older gift.

Preserve the original certified `E_i`, terms, order and dependencies. Separately
re-prove current surviving excess with the same frozen order, scope and originally
applicable terms, using current corrected effective source portions. Its amount
cannot exceed the original certified `E_i` or currently surviving eligible `G_i`.
This cap is source-owned proof about the original attributable surplus, not a
consumer money formula or an instruction to move surplus to another gift.

In the `60 + 60` example, a reversal of A invalidates the prefix that supported
B's 20. B's current marginal excess becomes zero, so its unhandled D31 reason
withdraws; the original 20 remains historical evidence. B's existing capped D11
application is not increased or reapplied. An exact reversal correction restoring
A can restore the same unconsumed B reason. A correction reducing B to 50 makes
its surviving excess 10; an uncertain partial reversal that cannot establish the
surviving source vector makes the comparison unavailable until the owner resolves
it. No oldest/newest/proportional guess is allowed.

Ordinary later donor increases/decreases, plan edits, internal release or current
`X` do not retroactively redefine that original comparison. A proven factual error
in the original terms, attribution, ordering or money instead requires an explicit
linked source correction that supersedes the certificate and requalifies the same
original occurrence. New donor intent is not such a correction. Source dependency
revisions must invalidate stale admission/handling proofs before asynchronous
recomputation; a current gift revision alone is insufficient when a predecessor
gift changed. An accepted historical authority's later expiry, consumption or
revocation for future use does not by itself falsify that historical association;
ordinary later scope closure likewise does not erase it. A correction or dispute
that actually challenges the original validity can invalidate its current proof.
New association/application commands and source corrections require their own
current qualified authority; historical evidence is not fresh application
permission. Current consumer action and disclosure authority remain independently
required.

### Prospective admission and existing task lifecycle

The D31 route needs its own qualified activation boundary tied to original live
received-support occurrences. A postactivation gift can acquire its first genuine
comparison proof later only when its original scope, terms and receipt provenance
are proven and its authoritative association position is assigned or recovered
under the preceding first-acceptance/recovery distinction. This does not admit preactivation gifts, history
imports, later changed donor purpose, new criterion mappings or inferred history
backlogs. Activation is a release boundary, not a new tenant On/Off setting.

Every qualified positive current amount, down to one supported minor unit,
qualifies. Unknown, zero and negative are different results. D22 amount thresholds
and D29/D30 optional reason policies do not gate D31. D31 evidence joins one D20
member, not one task per certificate, reason, target, amount change or retry.

If source correction leaves no D31 amount, withdraw only that reason. Other
qualified reasons and deliberate human undertakings remain under the shared task
owner. Coverage already accepted for the exact gift-purpose is not undone by a
later certificate revision, and a handled gift is not reopened for gratitude
again. Existing owner-permitted correction of mistaken human completion remains
distinct from financial source correction. No comparison or task action sends a
message, changes consent, issues a receipt or edits a commitment.

## Material concern register

### F01 — Capped fulfillment cannot certify excess

- **What:** Implementing D31 from D11 applied amount, unmatched remainder or M.2 X.
- **Why:** Capacity removes the excess from applications; X can result solely from a later promise reduction.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely without an explicit producer extension.
- **Evidence:** P16 F.3 :983–995, M.2–M.3 :1378–1393, O.11 :1887–1899; D21 C04 :198–204.
- **Answer impact:** A remains useful, but cannot ship truthfully through those existing scalar shortcuts.
- **Permanent fix:** A distinct, source-bound P16 comparison certificate with no financial mutation.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “P16 SHALL produce the above-commitment comparison from exact received-support attribution and applicable terms. Capped applications, unmatched remainder and current applied-above-commitment X SHALL NOT independently certify a new gift's excess.”
- **Proof:** Fulfilled commitment plus a new eligible $5 must produce a positive result without an over-capacity application; term reduction without new money must not.

### F02 — Insufficient authority for the portion beyond the cap

- **What:** An instruction covering the applied 40 is treated as scope authority for all 60.
- **Why:** Existing O.11 authority allocations sum exactly to the instructed/applied amount, not an unspecified residual.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely if existing proof families are reused by name alone.
- **Evidence:** P16 F.5 :1005–1017; O.11 :1857–1889.
- **Answer impact:** No new donor additional-intent requirement, but the whole compared portion needs actual financial scope evidence.
- **Permanent fix:** Extend the closed P16 association evidence contract for the full compared amount; preserve current staff action authority and automatic/manual certainty.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Each comparison SHALL prove the full exact received portion's intended commitment scope through a qualified closed source authority. Authority for capped allocation entries SHALL NOT extend to leftover money by inference; D29/D30 purpose rights SHALL NOT grant financial comparison rights.”
- **Proof:** Capped-only instruction fails; separately qualified full-portion association passes; same Party/memo or missionary attestation does not.

### F03 — Comparing the wrong commitment or installment

- **What:** A large installment is flagged despite future fixed-pledge remainder or another explicitly intended target.
- **Why:** Recurring periods and whole fixed promises have different meanings.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely with legacy amount/frequency/header comparisons.
- **Evidence:** P16 F.2 :972–979, F.6 :1019–1023, O.9 :1757–1803; D21 C04.
- **Answer impact:** A means any positive excess after the right comparison, not every gift larger than an installment.
- **Permanent fix:** The two closed source profiles and exact authorized portion membership above.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Recurring comparison SHALL bind one exact occurrence line; fixed comparison SHALL bind the full exact designation-line commitment including future and undated remainder. Neither normalized cadence, installment size, unknown commitment nor another promise SHALL substitute.”
- **Proof:** Fixed $1,000 total with $100 installment and $150 received is not $50 above the total; exact recurring occurrence can have its own different qualified result.

### F04 — Concurrent gifts double-claim one surplus

- **What:** Two 60 gifts against 100 each receive a 20 certificate.
- **Why:** Final-total leave-one-out or asynchronous worker order is not causal allocation.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely under independent evaluators without a source serialization contract.
- **Evidence:** P13 :792 only per-header sequence; P16 F.3 permanent locks; ADR0206 atomic owner state. The numerical counterexample is arithmetic reasoning, not existing runtime evidence.
- **Answer impact:** Requires a source owner amendment, not a new founder materiality question.
- **Permanent fix:** Stable source association ordering, complete prefix and single-version marginal proof, accepted before D31 work.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “P16 SHALL serialize first lawful comparison acceptance at the canonical source scope and preserve that assigned position. A genuinely new first association SHALL append at its owner command; retry or recovery of an accepted association SHALL recover its existing position. It SHALL certify `max(B + G - T, 0) - max(B - T, 0)` over the complete prior accepted prefix under one scope/terms meaning. D31 consumer delivery/query order, raw provider timestamps and leave-one-out final totals SHALL NOT allocate excess.”
- **Proof:** Concurrent source admissions produce one authoritative 0/20 allocation, stable through replay and reversed materializer order. Genuinely delayed first association appends once; recovery of an already accepted association cannot invent or replace its order.

### F05 — Mixing baseline and target versions

- **What:** New T is compared with an old cached baseline of differently scoped contributions.
- **Why:** Correct arithmetic over inconsistent meanings is still a false financial statement.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible during donor amendment, re-designation or delayed evaluation.
- **Evidence:** P16 F.4 :1001–1003, M.3 :1391–1393, O.9 :1771–1785.
- **Answer impact:** A requires exact terms actually applicable to the gift, not immutable creation terms forever or blindly current latest terms.
- **Permanent fix:** Bind each certificate to a single immutable terms/scope interpretation; recertify baseline for later applicable versions.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “B, G and T SHALL use one source-proved scope and applicable-terms version. Prospective amendments SHALL affect gifts to which the new terms actually apply, using a freshly certified coherent baseline; they SHALL NOT rewrite old certificates, change an earlier gift's terms because its first association is delayed, or turn historical money into a new occurrence.”
- **Proof:** Later ordinary increase/decrease does not change an old certificate; a subsequent live gift compares under the then-applicable terms and correctly attributed prefix.

### F06 — Source reversal leaves a false current excess

- **What:** B retains its 20 after A, whose coverage made B excessive, is reversed.
- **Why:** Only B's own source revision is watched, or original evidence is confused with current proof.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible without dependency-aware comparison revisions.
- **Evidence:** P16 F.7 :1025–1031 and O.11 :1891–1905; D20 C11 :273–281.
- **Answer impact:** Historical original truth is preserved while current task eligibility can withdraw or be unavailable.
- **Permanent fix:** Current proof uses the same frozen order/scope/terms with corrected effective portions, capped original attribution; dependency revision fence blocks stale use.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Current surviving excess SHALL be re-proved against current canonical corrections to the exact original dependencies, without exceeding the original certified surplus. A prior-prefix reversal SHALL invalidate dependent stale proofs immediately; correction SHALL NOT reapply money, transfer surplus to another gift or reopen handled work.”
- **Proof:** A60/B60 then reverse A yields B historical20/current0; restore A may recover the same unhandled B20; ambiguous partial reversal yields unavailable, not guessed support.

### F07 — Fees, FX or duplicated recognition manufacture excess

- **What:** Payment total, net proceeds, multiple credits or linked recurring/fixed routes inflate the compared support.
- **Why:** Several legitimate projections refer to the same money but are not additive source portions.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible across mixed gifts and attribution routes.
- **Evidence:** P13 :238, :816–823; P14 :325–347; P16 F.4 :997–1003.
- **Answer impact:** “Any positive” applies to exact eligible support, including one minor unit; no hidden tolerance or rounding.
- **Permanent fix:** Exact same-currency canonical portions, separately classified fees and nonoverlap; linked coverage preserves one source use.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “The comparison SHALL use exact eligible gross support portions in the source currency's supported minor units, separately from fee cover and processor fees. It SHALL NOT perform P28 FX, rounding, net-fee subtraction or recognition fan-out. Disjoint source splits and existing linked-coverage rules SHALL prevent duplicate monetary attribution.”
- **Proof:** $100 support plus $3 fee cover against $100 is not $3 excess; two credits do not make two support amounts; one minor unit of actual eligible support does qualify.

### F08 — Financial evidence becomes a new missionary write privilege

- **What:** A missionary marks a gift “above commitment” using D29/D30 confirmation, task edit or general CRM rights.
- **Why:** Product proximity hides a change of authoritative domain and actor powers.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible when reusing nearby purpose confirmation UI.
- **Evidence:** P16 F.5, H.4–H.6; D29 C06 :259–270; ADR0206.
- **Answer impact:** The selected A adds automatic qualified attention, not financial editor authority.
- **Permanent fix:** Source-owned commands and P12 exact action checks; missionary consumes permitted reason or existing help path.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Only the qualified financial owner command and current authorized actor SHALL establish or correct financial comparison evidence. A task, D29/D30 confirmation or shared contact SHALL confer neither that action nor protected input access.”
- **Proof:** Same-account different hat, tenant and ministry scope tests; permitted task reader cannot read hidden commitment terms or execute confirmation through an alternate route.

### F09 — Delayed proof becomes retroactive history mining

- **What:** Historical imports, new mappings or a worker restart turn old gifts into a backlog.
- **Why:** Receipt provenance and original scope/terms are replaced by evaluation time, or delayed first acceptance is confused with replay of an existing association.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely without a written activation/cohort contract.
- **Evidence:** D20 C05 :216–223, D21 C12, D29 C07 :272–294.
- **Answer impact:** Late genuine proof of a postactivation live gift must remain useful; unrestricted historical catch-up was not selected.
- **Permanent fix:** Explicit original-live D31 cohort and late proof exception only for proven original facts.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Late first comparison qualification MAY admit a still-unhandled originally live postactivation gift only when its original scope, applicable terms and receipt provenance are proved, with an authoritative association position assigned at first owner acceptance or recovered for an already accepted association. Preactivation receipts, history imports, new criteria, later changed purpose and invented/replaced ordering SHALL NOT create D31 occurrences.”
- **Proof:** Delayed authentic resolution of an eligible live gift succeeds once; identical history import or later donor purpose change fails; durable accepted intent survives worker delay.

### F10 — One certificate becomes another task or another thank-you

- **What:** Different terms, targets, comparison revisions or coexisting D29/D30 reasons multiply work.
- **Why:** Evidence identity is mistaken for business gift-purpose identity.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Likely if each criterion materializes its own task.
- **Evidence:** D20 C03–C08 and C11; ADR0183 :622–646.
- **Answer impact:** A's lack of threshold must not turn routine source repair into repeated gratitude.
- **Permanent fix:** Existing permanent D20 member/coverage and independent-task lifecycle.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “D31 SHALL add or withdraw only its reason on the existing exact gift-purpose member. Certificate revisions, multiple targets and additional valid reasons SHALL NOT create duplicate members or reset covered work; deliberate independent undertakings SHALL survive source-reason withdrawal.”
- **Proof:** D29/D30/D31 on one gift create one compatible member; source correction preserves other reasons; reviewed completion does not fabricate a message or receipt.

### F11 — A vague source gate quietly omits the selected behavior

- **What:** The team calls every difficult case unqualified indefinitely or ships only manual Add.
- **Why:** The existing owner gap is mistaken for permission to discard the native requirement.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Plausible without producer acceptance examples and explicit amendment destinations.
- **Evidence:** D21 C03 :187–196; P16 F/O.11 gap; source/implementation distinction in `docs/ai/document-authority.md` :7–46.
- **Answer impact:** A is accepted as real product behavior, not a permanently deferred aspiration.
- **Permanent fix:** Qualify the two defined profiles, closed authority paths and exact integration before activating each route; preserve ordinary finance workflows while unavailable.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Release of a D31 comparison route SHALL require a demonstrated positive excess, exact negative cases, concurrency/correction and current-authority proof through its owning commands and task integration. A route SHALL NOT be presented as implemented from a semantic fixture or replaced permanently by manual follow-up.”
- **Proof:** Both recurring and fixed supported profiles, full-fulfillment-plus-new-gift, delayed lawful proof, dependency correction and minimal-positive precision pass the actual command integration. Evidence explicitly identifies which routes were tested.

## Formalization destinations and acceptance limits

At a later authorized formalization step, amend P16 F.1–F.7 and O.11 with the
separate comparison association/certificate, exact profiles, scope ordering,
terms-consistent marginal rule, nonoverlap, authority and correction semantics;
reconcile M.2–M.6 so current X is never conflated with new-gift attribution. Amend
the P13 owner integration only where receipt/source association ordering and
current dependency facts are emitted, preserving per-header postings and immutable
money. P14 needs only the explicit consumer/role contract if an admitted recognition
route is used; it does not become a comparison producer. Reconcile the corresponding
active and durable OpenSpec owners rather than treating this note as their amendment.

P28 owns reason admission and the D31 original-live cohort; P9/ADR0183 and D20 own
shared task projection, one gift-purpose coverage, reviewed handling and independent
undertakings. P12 owns exact command and derived-result access. P6/P17 delivery,
receipts and financial mutations remain separate. No D31 rule builder, amount
configuration, compulsory donor confirmation, new money ledger or provider is needed.

Required proof is more than the arithmetic example: real source commands must
establish the association and order, retain complete source dependencies, serialize
concurrent gifts and corrections, expose coherent current results, and atomically
accept the existing durable task intent under current authority. A narrow producer
gap blocks only its dependent route; it does not justify blocking valid money
posting or treating unknown as zero. These are implementation/qualification gates,
not requests to ratify a new amount threshold or widen missionary finance rights.
