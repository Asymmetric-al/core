# D31 governance, financial source contract and admission evidence

Reviewed **October 18, 2026 UTC**, against commit
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` and the working tree. The founder
selected Q31 A: any eligible positive confirmed excess qualifies, with no extra
materiality threshold. This research note proposes required precision and
safeguards; it does not ratify them, qualify a deployed financial producer or
change formal specifications, application/schema, providers, tickets or live data.
Only this evidence file was written. Initial D30 evidence remains unchanged.

## Conclusion

**Accept with required amendments.** The selection is a positive-result rule,
not permission to compute `gift minus pledge` from a missionary screen. P16
must own the exact financial comparison and its source attribution; P28 admits
the resulting reason into D20's existing shared work. Every positive supported
minor-unit result qualifies after the other source/current-authority conditions
pass. There is no new amount setting, tenant Off switch, percentage, grace amount,
personal override, donor-intent checkbox or manual Add prerequisite.

The substantial gap is precise and fixable: P16's existing fulfillment operations
are capacity constrained, and its **Applied above current commitment** measure
can rise when a commitment is reduced without new money. Neither fact produces
the needed new-gift excess result by itself. The permanent solution is an
explicit P16 comparison-attribution extension, separate from money/application
authority, with the bounded contract below. It is not a new P28 financial ledger.

No further founder question is required for a database identifier, event order,
source adapter or authorization mapping. The accepted materiality choice does
not let engineers silently substitute a different comparison scope or preserve
an undefined `source_qualified = true` flag.

## The explicit source contract needed

This is a recommended **P16 owner extension**, not a claim that the cited tables
already implement it. P16 exposes a versioned, read-only comparison result from
its authoritative commands/folds. It does not expose an unrestricted endpoint
that accepts a caller's `excess_amount` or `is_extra` assertion.

The contract must bind all of the following, with exact same-Tenant/Legal-Entity
relationships where applicable:

1. The canonical newly received business contribution and exact relevant monetary
   source portion/lineage; current effective money/corrections; currency and
   supported precision; typed ministry scope and distinct qualified follow-up
   Party versus Commitment Party. Multiple role/recognition paths are not money.
2. The actual comparison scope and donor terms applicable to that gift. The
   proposed closed profiles are an exact recurring occurrence line and its
   accepted terms, or a fixed-pledge designation line across its full agreed
   target set, including future and undated remainder. A monthly equivalent,
   current arbitrary installment or unrelated commitments cannot substitute.
   Linked recurring/fixed paths keep their original attribution semantics.
3. The lawful source attribution connecting this exact money portion to that
   comparison scope, including the admitted evidence kind, immutable originating
   authority reference and any later correction. Existing provider lineage,
   authenticated donor scope instruction, approved structured mapping or
   separately qualified financial staff evidence can support a route only when
   it actually proves this relationship. A capped application's existence is
   not proof about its unallocated residue. Any additional comparison-scope
   fact is source owned and grants no extra financial write authority.
4. A complete, coherent financial baseline and durable owner acceptance order for
   the relevant source scope. Preserve which previously accepted source associations
   consumed the applicable commitment and which exact portion of this new gift
   is beyond it. Two concurrent gifts cannot each consume the same remaining
   capacity, attribute the same excess twice, or have their reason determined by
   downstream task-worker arrival. A lawful first comparison association receives
   its order when the owning command accepts it; either valid serial order of
   concurrent first commands is permissible. Delayed delivery of an association
   already accepted reuses its existing order. Exact ordering/correction semantics belong to P16 and
   must be published with the producer, not inferred from a timestamp, UUID or
   mutable gift date. Prior historical fulfillment can be valid baseline input;
   it does not thereby acquire a new task occurrence.
5. An exact positive excess amount/portion tied to this new gift, or an explicit
   no-excess/unknown/needs-source-resolution result. The value cannot exceed the
   source-proved relevant surviving support. Application to another period,
   another commitment, catch-up, fee cover, unrelated allocations, noncash and
   unreceived payment are not silently included. No subtraction over a partially
   authorized/loaded list or binary floating-point arithmetic is permitted.
6. Original live-receipt provenance, applicable terms and comparison version,
   complete relevant source revisions/cursors, current correction status and
   authorized projection. An explanatory result is not permission to reveal
   every operand. Unknown authority/currentness is neither positive proof nor
   financial zero.

This gives implementation an auditable producer obligation. It is more precise
than a generic certification flag, yet avoids choosing table names, a second
permission engine, universal commitment model or independent money ledger.

The coordinated financial review recommends a **strict marginal comparison**.
In exact compatible minor units, let `T` be the donor terms applicable to this
gift's exact scope, `Bprefix` the complete effective comparable money in previously
accepted comparison associations in P16's durable scope-specific acceptance order,
and `G` this gift's
lawfully attributed amount in that scope. The original result is:

```text
E = max(Bprefix + G - T, 0) - max(Bprefix - T, 0)
```

P16 must prove the order, complete prefix and attribution through its source
commands/facts; neither the expression alone nor a generic database query is
sufficient. One frozen applicable scope/terms version governs both `Bprefix`
and `G`. A prospective terms revision applies only to new gifts under that
version and requires a source-certified baseline over the same eligible
canonical portions; it does not rewrite old certificates. A genuinely new first
association, including lawful delayed resolution of the gift's original scope,
is appended at its actual source acceptance with that complete accepted prefix.
It does not invent or insert an earlier position based on the receipt date.
Delayed delivery or reconstruction of an already accepted association instead
must recover its original position and prefix; a task worker cannot allocate
another one. Prior certificates retain their existing prefixes. This is a legal
source acceptance order, not a claim about the chronology of actual giving.
Concurrent first owner commands may lawfully serialize either way; redelivery
and downstream processing cannot change the resulting accepted order. This comparison does
not apply over-capacity money or consume source
capacity again. Preserve the original `E`, applicable terms and prefix witness.
Current validity uses the same causal order/scope and applicable terms with
current source money/corrections, capped at original `E`. For example, against
100, an earlier 60 and later 60 yield 20 on the later gift. Reversing the earlier
60 makes that later gift's current excess zero; it does not require fictional
fulfillment reapplication. Lawful restoration can restore the same unconsumed
reason. Ordinary later promise changes do not rewrite this causal meaning.

A proven error in the originally captured terms, money or attribution requires
an explicit source correction that supersedes the original certificate with
its evidence and prior history intact. It is not hidden recalculation against
today's terms and cannot manufacture a new received-gift occurrence. An
originally live, unhandled gift can receive its first valid qualification through
that correction under G07; handled/history-only restrictions remain. This is a
proposed explicit owner extension, not an assertion that current P16 already
publishes these certificates.

## Candidate exact decision and clauses

> **D31 — Any positive, source-confirmed excess.** When the financial owner
> establishes that an exact eligible new gift contains a positive amount beyond
> its applicable recorded commitment under the qualified comparison contract,
> P28 admits personal follow-up through the existing shared gift-purpose path.
> There is no additional minimum amount or new tenant setting. “Above
> commitment” is a financial comparison, not a statement about donor intention,
> generosity or another payment authorization. Maya sees the permitted factual
> reason and handles the exact reviewed gifts once; sending remains separate.
>
> Unmatched money, a larger installment, changed terms, fee cover, catch-up,
> advance coverage and incomplete data cannot establish that comparison by
> themselves. Financial facts and applications remain with P13/P16; D29/D30
> purpose confirmation never grants a financial comparison or correction right.

**G01 — Mandatory positive-result admission.** Once the qualified financial
result, original-live provenance, current source/consumer authority and uncovered
gift-purpose conditions hold, P28 SHALL admit the D31 reason through D20's
durable path. Every positive amount representable at the source's supported
currency precision qualifies; zero or negative does not. No amount threshold,
percentage, hidden tolerance, tenant On/Off, personal override or manual Add
step SHALL be introduced by D31.

**G02 — Explicit P16 extension.** P16 SHALL own a versioned comparison-attribution
contract that proves the exact new source gift portion, applicable typed
commitment scope/terms, lawful attribution, coherent baseline, source ordering,
exact excess, current corrections and original live provenance. This contract
SHALL be distinct from fulfillment application and its capped capacity. P28
SHALL consume its permitted result rather than derive one from donor headers,
pledge scalar fields, unmatched money, current X or general history.

**G03 — Defined comparison scope.** The proposed supported comparison profiles
SHALL identify an exact recurring occurrence line under its accepted terms, or
an exact fixed-pledge designation line across the full agreed target set including
future/undated remainder. A single fixed installment, monthly equivalent,
entire donor portfolio or an imagined infinite recurring total is not that
scope. Linked recurring/fixed coverage SHALL follow P16's source attribution
once; a result above one derived cap is not automatically above another
commitment. Exact scope must be readable in the permitted explanation.

**G04 — Gift-bound causal proof.** The financial owner SHALL certify which
positive portion of this new gift is beyond its applicable commitment under
terms actually applicable to the gift. It SHALL preserve deterministic source
acceptance ordering, complete relevant effective accepted-association prefix and gift lineage, exact
minor units and coherent revisions. The proposed strict marginal result SHALL
be `max(Bprefix + G - T, 0) - max(Bprefix - T, 0)` using the source-proved
scope, complete ordered prefix, attributable gift amount and applicable donor
terms defined above. Current surviving excess SHALL use that same scope/order
and applicable terms with current source corrections, capped at the original
result unless a proven error explicitly supersedes the original certificate.
Each lawful first association SHALL receive its position when the owning command
accepts it, including a genuine delayed first resolution. It SHALL NOT be
backdated into an existing prefix. Concurrent first commands MAY serialize in
either lawful order; after acceptance, provider redelivery and downstream
processing SHALL NOT change that order. An already accepted association delivered
late SHALL reuse its original position and prefix, not receive a new one.
Ordinary later donor changes SHALL NOT
retroactively create or erase the historical comparison; a genuine correction
of original evidence SHALL requalify it with lineage. Source-scope authority
must cover the amount in question; an application/mapping limited to capped
targets SHALL NOT certify the leftover by implication. No synthetic over-capacity
application is permitted.

**G05 — Independent reasons.** D22's eligible-gift-amount setting, D29's
additional-purpose evidence, D30's one-time-need policy and other native reasons
SHALL retain their separate predicates. They neither gate nor supply D31 proof.
A gift can carry several valid reasons on one D20 member. No new communication,
consent, receipt, financial posting or donor classification follows.

**G06 — Financial actors remain financial actors.** P16's qualified command and
P12 action SHALL govern financial scope evidence, application, comparison
correction and any required staff resolution. D29/D30 confirmation, task edit,
gift viewing, missionary role, coach status and ordinary automation management
SHALL NOT grant these actions. A separately authorized financial staff result
retains its evidence/actor classification. P28 may expose only the currently
permitted repair route; Maya SHALL not be forced to edit financial facts to
finish ordinary personal work.

**G07 — Prospective cohort and delayed first resolution.** Define the D31
activation cohort at the original owner-certified eligible live received-support
boundary, independently of later application, comparison or task-worker delay.
An originally live, still-unhandled cohort gift MAY first qualify when genuine
source application/comparison resolution or correction of its original erroneous
facts becomes available. A new first comparison association uses its actual
source acceptance position under G04 while proving the gift's original scope,
applicable terms and live provenance; an existing accepted association keeps
its original position. Neither path silently changes prior prefixes. This explicitly clarifies D20's historical-reclassification
exclusion for delayed D31 qualification. Historical-only acquisition, preactivation
receipts, newly invented comparison criteria, a later instruction repurposing old
money or an ordinary later term reduction SHALL NOT manufacture that occurrence.
Prior historical fulfillment may inform a qualified new gift's baseline without
admitting tasks for the old gifts. Gift dates and transport timestamps cannot
create cohort membership. Unknown lineage requires owner resolution.

**G08 — Current correction and authority lifetime.** A lawful accepted
application/attribution SHALL remain a historical fact when its originating
instruction is later consumed or expires, its recorder departs, or the scope
undergoes ordinary closure. These events alone do not falsify its accepted proof.
Current command and source/consumer projection authority remain independently
required; preserving history grants no current read or action permission. New
comparison association or financial application/reapplication still requires
current source authority for that action; correction
uses the exact governing correction fact, not renewed use of old authority.
Source correction/reversal SHALL withdraw only the invalid affected D31 reason,
preserve other reasons and human history, and restore the same previously
admitted still-unconsumed purpose only when current evidence permits. Ambiguous
partial reversal SHALL remain uncertain; no proportional guessing. No new gift
event or completed-work replay follows from correction.

**G09 — Same member, exact reviewed coverage.** Contribution, qualified Party,
typed ministry and special-purpose identity SHALL remain D20's business key.
Comparison targets, excess amount/version, criterion version, financial line
replacement, staff recorder and transport request SHALL NOT create another
handling opportunity. Exact reviewed member versions govern completion. A new
unreviewed gift remains uncovered; an unrelated new member does not invalidate
a still-current selected review. Generic task patch/delete or title matching
SHALL NOT bypass the qualified handling/withdrawal path.

**G10 — Derived privacy and truthful currentness.** The above-commitment fact,
amount and comparison target each require their qualified current source/P12
projection. Gift visibility alone cannot expose a protected commitment through
a reason, filter, count, notification, export, coaching summary or recovered
result. A permitted aggregate may be used only under its own registered
projection, never by fetching hidden operands in the client. Unknown/withheld/
updating is not no commitment, zero excess or no follow-up needed.

**G11 — Durable lifecycle, not another Off policy.** Accepted source result,
required minimal history/result and identifier-only effect intent SHALL be
durable under the owning transaction/qualified protocol; asynchronous shared
task projection recovers idempotently. A technical admission pause, outage or
deployment kill switch SHALL preserve live provenance and accepted work; it
SHALL NOT silently inherit D30's business Off interval or discard recoverable
originally live candidates. Recovery re-proves current source/authority and
covered state, with no historical sweep or duplicate effect. Required retention,
restriction, erasure and restore preserve lawful minimal identity/history without
indefinite copied financial/person narratives.

**G12 — Specific qualification and proof.** Before enabling each supported
source profile, publish and demonstrate its exact comparison inputs/output,
attribution authority, original-live boundary, ordering/concurrency, corrections,
P12 projection, D20 admission/coverage and compatible rollout. A missing dependent
route blocks that route, not unrelated finance or native work. No unspecified
generic source flag or manual task placeholder satisfies the selected feature.
Use current source/test/runtime evidence separately from intended owner contracts.

## Synthesis and formalization destinations

Record the no-threshold selection with the explicit source/admission amendment,
then carry the comparison-attribution requirement to P16, exact new action/projection
mapping to P12, received-money/correction source proof to P13/P14, and native reason
admission to P28. Keep D20/P9 coverage and ADR0183 independent completion unchanged.
The formal promotion must reconcile the exact comparison profiles and causal
ordering, not merely replace the phrase “source qualified” with an adapter name.

Real proof must include positive smallest-unit results, scoped/no-commitment and
installment counterexamples, two gifts racing one remaining capacity, delayed
qualification, historical mapping, term changes, exact/ambiguous reversal,
authority expiry versus correction, repeated transport and handled coverage,
cross-tenant/currency/Party poison and forbidden derived disclosure. These are
release requirements, not tests claimed executed here. No monitoring-only escape
is proposed for a financial meaning, authority or duplicate-handling invariant.
