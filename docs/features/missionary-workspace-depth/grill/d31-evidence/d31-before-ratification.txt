## Exact corrected decision to record

> **D31 — Follow up on any confirmed excess.** Any strictly positive, exact
> source-confirmed excess attributable to an eligible new received gift or
> identified portion qualifies for native personal follow-up. One valid minor
> currency unit is sufficient. There is no additional minimum, percentage,
> rounding tolerance or tenant amount setting for this reason.
>
> The financial owner must establish the applicable commitment scope, original
> terms, exact currency, lawful gift association, complete comparison and that
> gift's attributable excess. A larger installment, unmatched remainder, absent
> commitment, fee cover, advance/catch-up payment, another commitment's money,
> exchange difference or later reduction of a commitment is not this proof.
> Donor-stated additional purpose and one-time-need purpose remain independent;
> neither is required or inferred.
>
> Maya sees **“Support above the recorded commitment”** in her existing compatible
> follow-up, with the permitted exact amount and a concise explanation when useful.
> Multiple reasons do not duplicate the same gift's work. Every gift retains its
> own evidence and D20 reviewed-group coverage. Sending or recording an actual
> interaction remains a separate deliberate decision.
>
> Corrections revalidate the affected source evidence without changing received
> money, applying a gift, rewriting completed human history or replaying handled
> work. Incomplete or stale evidence withholds this reason; other valid reasons
> and correctly received money remain intact. Finance staff resolve genuine
> source problems through the owning workflow; Maya is not asked to edit money
> or commitment terms to obtain or silence a task.

**Required owner amendment:** P16's capped fulfillment applications and its
current aggregate excess are insufficient to implement this decision. Add a
bounded P16-owned comparison-association and per-gift excess proof as specified
below. P28 consumes that proof and owns task-purpose admission; it does not own
a new money ledger or calculate excess from portal totals. The exact producer
contract must be reconciled into P16 and its declared OpenSpec projections when
formal specification is authorized. Publication of this review does not amend
those contracts by implication.

**The root problem is meaningful stewardship without manufactured “extra” gifts.**
The earlier commitment was already fulfilled in Q31's scenario, so its later
$5 gift does not also create fulfillment work and hide this choice. Manual
follow-up remains useful, but substituting it for every small confirmed excess
would fail the selected native behavior.

**Strongest alternative: a tenant minimum excess per currency.** It might reduce
task volume for an organization that wants a materiality policy, but adds a second
amount rule, excludes some genuine small gifts and needs its own temporal rules.
No evidence establishes the correct minimum. The founder selected A. A hidden
epsilon, provider minimum, whole-dollar rounding or D22 threshold would silently
implement B. Normal task handling remains available; a classifier error must be
fixed at source, not concealed with a larger threshold.

**Comparable practices do not override Core.** MPDX's May 3, 2021 fee article
documents a fee-covered difference producing an alert and a workaround that edits
the commitment. Adopt the counterexample, not the workaround or its old fee rate.
Blackbaud's installment-overage application and Bloomerang's scheduled/extra
parts show why a payment total is insufficient. TntConnect's pledge/currentness
heuristics can miss small extra gifts; its task-updating description is only a
limited UX precedent. None proves Core's financial semantics or permissions.

The proposed P16 extension is necessary and deliberately bounded. Its two
comparison profiles are:

- **Recurring:** the exact recurring occurrence line and its applicable accepted
  amount-per-occurrence terms. No monthly equivalent, average, latest amount or
  comparison against a different occurrence.
- **Fixed pledge:** the exact pledge designation line's complete agreed scope,
  including scheduled future and valid undated capacity. One installment is not
  the full promise. Different designation lines or commitments remain distinct.

An exact gift portion must have a lawful, complete comparison association to
that scope through the owner's closed authority. P16's provider lineage,
authenticated gift-bound donor instruction, approved authenticated structured
remittance mapping or separately authorized staff resolution retains its own
conditions. A memo, donor name, equal amount, import column or D29/D30 purpose
confirmation is not financial authority. Authority covering only capped
application entries does not certify their leftover by implication: it must
cover the entire compared received-support portion. Attributed amounts cannot be reused
across comparison scopes; recurring-to-fixed derived coverage is not new money.

The association is **not an application above available capacity**. It records
why the received support belongs to this comparison, with exact source references,
terms and correction lineage. Actual fulfillment applications retain all P16
capacity, allocation and inverse rules. This extension cannot route, reserve,
refund, settle or reapply money.

**Per-gift attribution must conserve the excess.** P16 serializes accepted
comparison associations under its existing typed source/target lock discipline
and assigns a durable order within the exact comparison scope. A P13 header
sequence cannot order different gifts to one scope. A lawful first association,
including genuine later resolution of an earlier live gift's original intent,
appends its position at that owner command. It never backdates itself ahead of
accepted associations or silently changes their prefixes. Either valid concurrent
source acceptance order is permissible. The rule does not claim independence
from the timing of those legitimate first commands.

Delayed processing of an already accepted association must recover its original
position. Provider redelivery, downstream worker scheduling, user-entered dates
and query order cannot change it or create another ordinal. If that original
position/completeness cannot be proved, this comparison remains unavailable.
The distinction permits late first matching without inventing historical order.

For a qualified gift portion `g`, let `T` be its applicable agreed target in
canonical minor units, `B` the complete owner-certified prefix of previously
accepted associations to the same scope, and `G` this portion. `B` does not mean
all gifts with earlier receipt dates. Both use eligible gross received support
before processor fees, excluding separately classified fee cover, and the same
exact currency. The owner's per-gift original excess proof is:

```text
E_g = max(B + G - T, 0) - max(B - T, 0)
0 <= E_g <= G
```

`B` is not a portal aggregate, a paginated/RLS-filtered list, a mutable pledge
counter or an extra financial balance to edit. It is an exact source projection
over conserved, nonduplicated association evidence. `B`, `G` and `T` must share
one immutable scope and applicable-terms interpretation. Each gift freezes its own
genuinely applicable terms; ordinary later term versions apply prospectively and
require a freshly source-certified baseline under that version. Existing term
versions carry this boundary; no separate configurable epoch engine is needed.
Excess figures across different terms versions are not an additive financial balance.
Recomputing an old gift against today's smaller target is forbidden. This
formula is insufficient without the preceding authority, completeness and
scope proof. It does not authorize inference from any unapplied remainder.

For two $60 gifts in one applicable $100 scope, canonical A then B yields
**$0 for A and $20 for B**. A leave-one-out calculation can incorrectly award
$20 to both. Replaying either gift yields the same evidence/effect, not another
$20. When prior gifts already fulfilled the scope, a new qualified $0.01 portion
can have $0.01 excess. No new checkout or source minimum follows.

**Original evidence and current validity are distinct.** Keep original terms,
prefix and result. Revalidate that same causal comparison against qualified
source corrections; ordinary surviving excess cannot exceed the original proved
amount. If A above is reversed, B's current attributable excess becomes zero;
withdraw its unhandled reason while preserving the original $20 explanation as
history. Do not silently apply B's money to replace A. A qualified restoration
may restore the same reason only if its D20 effect is still unhandled. An actual
correction proving the original captured facts wrong explicitly supersedes that
certificate under source authority; it is not an ordinary later amendment or a
new gift. A corrected result never bypasses permanent handling coverage.

A dependency revision is essential: reversing A can invalidate B while B's own
gift revision is unchanged. Admission and handling must fence the comparison's
relevant dependency revision as well as gift, terms, scope and current authority.
Ambiguous partial reversals follow P16's unresolved/inverse behavior; P28 cannot
choose a proportional surviving excess. Missing evidence is not confirmed zero.

A lawful accepted association survives ordinary consumption, expiry or future-use
revocation of its originating instruction, recorder departure and later scope
closure. Those events do not make its historical acceptance false. New source
commands and applications require their own current authority; corrections require
qualified correction authority, and current consumer action/disclosure remains
independently checked. A delayed first match retains the terms actually applicable
to its original gift, even when newer terms exist at association acceptance.

The lifecycle below describes required domain outcomes, not another configurable
state machine or a new set of task statuses:

| Situation                                                    | Valid next outcome                                                                                                                 | Forbidden shortcut                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Money received, comparison unresolved                        | Owner resolution can establish a positive, zero or still-unavailable result; independent work continues.                           | Unknown as zero/excess, missing money, or a new missionary finance task.           |
| Qualified positive result, live and unhandled                | Atomically admit reason and durable task intent after current revision/authority checks.                                           | Eligibility-only manual fallback or silent small-amount filter.                    |
| Accepted intent, projection delayed                          | Retry/upsert the same business effect, reconciling intervening source corrections.                                                 | Duplicate gift/task or falsely showing an invalidated reason as current.           |
| Current task, reviewed gifts                                 | Accept only the still-valid reviewed set; new unrelated members remain pending.                                                    | Completing unseen members or requiring a second interaction after a lost response. |
| Evidence becomes uncertain or is conclusively corrected      | Uncertain dependent use pauses; confirmed invalid reason withdraws. Preserve independent reasons/promises and historical evidence. | Treating an outage as falsehood, moving money or erasing human history.            |
| Reason withdrawn and original evidence legitimately repaired | Restore the same unhandled effect if valid; permanent handling coverage still wins.                                                | New identity, automatic reopening or historical task sweep.                        |
| Gift-purpose work handled                                    | Preserve coverage and history; correct under D20's scoped rules.                                                                   | Another follow-up because a certificate, amount, reason or transport ID changed.   |

## Complete journey and UI

**Staff:** no amount configuration or new financial review queue. Contextual help
can say: **“Any confirmed support above the applicable commitment can create
personal follow-up. There is no minimum excess amount.”** Do not display a fake
zero-minimum field or borrow D30's switch. Genuine source issues use the existing
finance resolution workflow with a safe reason and exact record references.
Already qualified evidence requires no second human confirmation. A deployment
qualification gate is an engineering control, not a tenant policy setting.

**Grace:** the ordinary giving experience is unchanged. She answers no extra
question, approves no stewardship rule and receives no automatic message from
D31. The system does not assert that she intended an additional gift, change her
future instruction or treat an excess as debt, credit or overpayment. A received
gift remains received even if **Commitment match: Needs review**.

**Maya:** use the existing task row and detail. A representative permitted view:

```text
Follow up with Grace
Maya's ministry · 1 gift

Support above the recorded commitment
Received gift                         USD 5.00
Above the applicable commitment       USD 5.00

Why this follow-up?                 [disclosure]
```

Money is supporting context; the personal next action remains primary. For a
partly qualifying gift, label received gift/eligible portion and excess separately.
Never disclose other allocations or organization-wide giving to justify a local
reason. The factual category itself requires permission; generic wording is not
a way around source privacy. No sensitive hidden fields are sent to the browser.

**Why this follow-up?** gives a short permitted source explanation. Where original
and current terms differ or the comparison period affects meaning, identify the
permitted applicable scope/period and say **“Based on the commitment terms
applicable to this gift.”** Do not imply comparison with today's terms or disclose
restricted current terms. It need not expose the calculation or make Maya
review a ledger. Correctly explain received-but-unresolved and accepted-but-still-
updating states. Unknown is neither zero nor “not eligible.” Do not turn every
uncertain gift into a missionary research task.

**Tiny values stay legible:** one valid minor unit displays exactly, for example
USD 0.01, JPY 1 or BHD 0.001 in a source-qualified lane. Canonical metadata also
contains four-decimal units; this does not launch those currencies. Use canonical
currency formatting and explicit locale, not a fixed USD helper, compact numbers,
rounded zero or converted estimate. Do not imply a higher future recurring amount
or donor value. No flashing badge or urgent priority follows from excess alone.

**Two gifts:** the existing D20 task shows both distinct permitted gift entries
and their reasons. Maya reviews and handles the selected current group once.
Adding a second reason to one gift does not add another gift, total, activity or
completion. An unrelated new gift stays pending without invalidating a still-
current reviewed subset. A material correction to a reviewed gift does require
refresh/review; retain Maya's draft and explain what changed without exposing
restricted facts.

**Correction or source delay:** preserve genuine human work. If the sole unhandled
reason is conclusively withdrawn, apply D20's source-withdrawal behavior; other
independent reasons remain. If the source is merely unavailable, show an honest
updating/unavailable state and withhold dependent action rather than invent a
negative fact. Completed history stays historical; a late source repair cannot
create another handled event. A network-lost completion response must recover
the accepted result, not ask Maya to repeat the interaction.

**Mobile/accessibility:** reuse exact `base-maia`, Base UI and semantic Zinc tokens
through shared `@asym/ui`. Use stacked gift entries, clear text labels, ordinary
typography/spacing and a secondary disclosure. Support keyboard/focus, screen
readers, 320 CSS-pixel reflow, zoom, non-color status and established touch targets.
Announce successful actions or material refresh needs without focus theft.
Display the source's relevant period/timezone; do not infer financial periods
from the browser clock. Low bandwidth can delay a projection without losing a
draft or making a retry duplicate work. No animation, gamification, new dashboard,
ledger editor, donor score or alternative component library is needed.

## Required decision and specification language

These clauses are the exact amendments proposed for ratification and later
canonical specification. They are requirements, not claims about current code.

**C01 — Materiality.** The native above-commitment reason SHALL qualify every
eligible source-confirmed `excess_minor > 0`. It SHALL have no additional amount,
percentage, epsilon, provider-unit minimum or tenant On/Off setting. D22/D30 and
other reasons remain independent; operational pause is not a tenant policy.
When qualification, current authority and uncovered-effect conditions hold,
P28 SHALL admit the reason through D20's durable native path without manual Add
or Workflow Studio enrollment.

**C02 — Authority and truth.** P13 owns money, P14 recognition, P16 comparison/
application, P28 reason/admission, P9/D20 work and P12 authorization. Posted
financial facts SHALL remain immutable. Operational evidence SHALL reference
their exact owner facts without changing designation, recognition, fulfillment,
receipts, recurring instructions, contact permission, sending or interaction.

**C03 — Comparison scope.** A recurring comparison SHALL bind the exact occurrence
line and applicable accepted terms. A fixed-pledge comparison SHALL bind the
complete exact pledge designation-line scope, including scheduled future and
valid undated capacity. Missing, hidden or unmatched targets SHALL NOT be zero;
one installment, monthly equivalent or unrelated scope SHALL NOT substitute.

**C04 — Source association.** P16 SHALL supply a closed, authorized, complete
association of exact received gift portions to comparison scopes, independently
of capped fulfillment application. Provider lineage, authenticated donor
instruction, approved structured remittance and authorized staff resolution
retain their governing conditions. No caller assertion, memo, purpose confirmation,
matching heuristic or service credential SHALL create financial authority.
Authority SHALL cover the full compared portion; authority limited to capped
application entries does not certify the leftover by implication.

**C05 — Conserved per-gift proof.** P16 SHALL certify per-gift attributable excess
using its authoritative scope order and complete exact prefix, applicable terms,
currency and nonoverlapping portions, with `E=max(B+G−T,0)−max(B−T,0)` and
`0≤E≤G`. The prefix, gift and target SHALL share one frozen applicable-terms/scope
interpretation; a prospective new version requires a newly certified baseline,
not reuse of a differently defined cached prefix. `B` is the complete preceding
accepted-association prefix, not all earlier-dated receipts; `B` and `G` use
eligible gross support before processor fees and exclude separately classified
fee cover. A lawful first owner association SHALL append once at its actual
serialized command, including a later original-intent match, without backdating.
Either valid concurrent source acceptance order is permissible. Delayed processing
or replay of an already accepted association SHALL preserve its ordinal; provider
redelivery/downstream worker/query order SHALL NOT change attribution. No valid
certificate exists without qualified order/completeness. Derived recurring-to-fixed
coverage SHALL NOT duplicate source support or create another gift effect.

**C06 — Evidence identity.** Proof SHALL bind Tenant, financial Legal Entity,
canonical gift/portion, typed ministry/project, eligible Party, comparison kind/
scope, original applicable terms, currency metadata meaning, causal association,
source and dependency revisions, original live provenance and current correction
status. Provider account/environment/binding SHALL be bound where applicable.
Opaque references MAY expose these facts through one adapter; duplicate ledgers
or a new universal proof platform are not required.

**C07 — Exact units.** All monetary inputs, sums, intermediates, comparisons and
transport SHALL be exact checked canonical integer units with validated currency.
Reject unsupported precision, nonfinite values and overflow before coercion.
Do not silently round or cast unbounded BIGINT/NUMERIC to JavaScript numbers.
Use existing safe-number bounds where sufficient and a qualified lossless owner
representation otherwise. Provider charge/payout constraints SHALL NOT quantize
stewardship excess or widen transactable currency lanes.

**C08 — Exclusions and uncertainty.** Fee cover, net-settlement difference, FX,
catch-up, authorized advance, other targets, absent commitment and current aggregate
excess alone SHALL NOT establish a qualifying occurrence. Financial owners SHALL
resolve the exact meaning. Unknown/stale/incomplete/denied is distinct from zero
or false; withhold only dependent qualification while preserving other truth.

**C09 — Live admission.** D31 SHALL have a prospective qualified live received-support
cohort, not a tenant materiality interval. Genuine later first source resolution
or correction may admit the same original still-unhandled live gift. Historical
acquisition, ordinary term edits, new later repurposing instructions, remaps or
criterion revisions SHALL NOT manufacture a new live receipt. Historical valid
support MAY contribute to the comparison baseline; its own tasks are not replayed.

**C10 — Corrections and terms.** Preserve original proof/terms. Revalidate current
surviving excess against qualified corrections of the same scope/order, bounded
by original proved excess unless an authorized correction explicitly supersedes
erroneous original facts. Ordinary later term changes are prospective, not
recalculation of old-gift eligibility. Withdrawing earlier coverage SHALL invalidate
dependent proofs without silently reapplying another gift. Ambiguous reversals
SHALL follow source-owned unresolved treatment, not guessed proportions.
Ordinary instruction consumption/expiry/future-use revocation, recorder departure
or later scope closure SHALL NOT invalidate a lawful historical association.
New association/application and correction commands require their respective
current authority; current consumer action/disclosure remains independent.

**C11 — Durable work identity.** Reuse D20's canonical contribution/Party/typed-
subject/purpose member and compatible task. Reason codes, certificate revisions,
amount changes, provider events and retries SHALL NOT create new gift effects.
Done/Not needed coverage remains permanent under D20 correction rules. Independent
valid reasons and unrelated new members remain distinct. Withdrawal of the sole
D31 reason SHALL NOT erase an independently promised/planned human deliverable;
preserve that work under D20's source-withdrawal rules.

**C12 — Atomic acceptance and recovery.** P28 acceptance SHALL atomically persist
the source-purpose state/revision, semantic command receipt, safe audit and durable
identifier-only task intent. Cross-owner processing SHALL use the qualified durable
version protocol, not an assumed distributed transaction. Current source and
dependency revisions and P12 authority SHALL be revalidated at the final boundary,
including after waits. Time-based authority SHALL use the actual acceptance time,
not a transaction-start timestamp that predates expiry. Pending projection SHALL
be retriable from durable intent.

**C13 — Reviewed handling.** D20 completion SHALL bind the exact reviewed eligible
gift set and relevant evidence/authority revisions. A correction to a reviewed
member invalidates stale handling; an unrelated new member does not invalidate
the unchanged reviewed subset. Lost responses recover the existing accepted result
only under current disclosure authority. Otherwise return the existing safe denied/
unavailable outcome without protected existence/details; never re-execute the effect.
Completion SHALL NOT assert actual sending or create an extra interaction.

**C14 — Persistence invariants.** Tenant, scope, actor, author and audit attribution
SHALL derive from trusted command context. Use tenant-aware keys/FKs, typed
relationships, required non-null scope/currency, valid state/amount checks,
unique source/correction/reason identity and tenant-scoped command idempotency.
Protected associations SHALL NOT be generically retargeted. Deletion SHALL NOT
cascade away required source history or permanent handling coverage; source-owned
redaction/retention applies. No mutable purpose field belongs on posted money.

**C15 — Database authorization.** Grants, RLS `USING` and `WITH CHECK`, views,
RPCs, security-definer functions, storage, realtime and service-role paths SHALL
preserve the same tenant/action/scope and protected-transition boundaries.
Permitted old rows SHALL NOT be transformed into forbidden new states. Review
effective policies and bypass roles; table-owner/service access is not domain
authority. Fence source-relevant legacy generic task mutations before reuse.

**C16 — Disclosure.** Every surface, cache, export, notice and generated artifact
SHALL resolve current permission for the reason category, exact amount and each
detail. A task relation, generic label or shared identity SHALL NOT grant access.
Compute from complete authorized source truth, then disclose a minimal permitted
projection; do not compute from an audience-filtered subset or return hidden
financial fields to the browser. Authorize the combined arithmetic disclosure:
gift amount plus excess can reveal restricted commitment capacity even when
individual fields appear innocuous. Recheck at final disclosure release; reject
late responses after Tenant/actor/scope changes. Revoke reached cached access
through P12 rules; already released information cannot be recalled.

**C17 — Ordinary UX.** Reuse the existing task list/detail with the factual reason
“Support above the recorded commitment,” exact permitted gift/portion/excess,
optional concise explanation and existing group handling. Explain the applicable
historical terms/scope/period when needed to distinguish today's commitment,
without exposing restricted facts. Add no minimum field,
new queue, donor question, extra routine confirmation or missionary finance editor.
Preserve drafts, clarify saved/pending/unavailable outcomes and never use guilt,
donor-value labels, inferred intent or excess-driven urgency.

**C18 — Presentation and accessibility.** Use canonical currency/locale formatting
that preserves one supported minor unit, source-defined dates/periods and shared
Core UI. Prove keyboard/focus, non-color status, screen reader messages, 320 CSS-pixel
reflow, zoom, touch and low-bandwidth recovery. Render the exact permitted value;
compact rounding, fallback USD and currency conversion SHALL NOT explain admission.

**C19 — Bounded performance.** Process affected exact scopes/revisions with indexed
tenant/source/cursor queries, keyset pages, bounded jobs and fair per-tenant
capacity. Do not rescan every donor's history per task read or hold financial
locks while awaiting a provider/UI. Rebuildable source checkpoints MAY accelerate
complete prefixes, but SHALL be versioned projections with correction invalidation,
not write authority. Qualify concrete load and latency limits before activation.

**C20 — Repair and diagnosis.** Distinguish received money, comparison unresolved,
qualified reason, accepted intent, projected task, handled effect and withdrawn
reason. Provide source-owned diagnosis/retry without direct database repair or
manual fake tasks. Durable business evidence SHALL retain source/terms/correction
and actor lineage separately from minimized technical telemetry and security audit.
Logs SHALL NOT include supporter names, message bodies, credentials or unnecessary
financial payloads; retention/export follows the owning privacy policy.

**C21 — Provider boundaries.** Consume qualified canonical financial evidence;
never call a provider from the task renderer or treat a webhook as a new gift.
Account/mode, duplicate/out-of-order delivery, corrections, provider amount units
and source schema versions require adapter proof. Unqualified lanes SHALL remain
unavailable for this reason; they SHALL NOT silently fall back to amount heuristics.
No new provider, AI classifier, CRM vendor or Workflow Studio dependency is needed.

**C22 — Migration and rollout.** Use additive versioned records, default-denied
new writes, explicit source/schema capability checks and a prospective cohort.
Old code SHALL NOT fabricate proof, bypass protected mutations or discard handling
coverage. Backfill qualified comparison history separately from task admission;
do not replay historical tasks. Pilot, pause, reconcile and roll forward through
the owning runbook. Rollback after writes SHALL preserve evidence/coverage and
resume accepted intents safely, not restore old heuristic producers.

**C23 — Proof and traceability.** Carry D31/C01–C24/P01–P32 through later authorized
P16/P28 glossary/owner clauses, ADR decision if needed, OpenSpec, design/tasks,
tickets, implementation tests and release evidence with exact links. No public
documentation, synthetic model or passed link check SHALL be labeled runtime,
financial-source, database, performance or usability qualification.

**C24 — Monitoring and accountability.** Adopt the signal/threshold/owner/response
table below before activation. Correctness, isolation and exact-money violations
are release blockers and incidents, not tolerable monitored risks. Observed task
volume or low-value feedback MAY inform a future explicit product decision; no
hidden materiality threshold may be introduced operationally.

## Consolidated adversarial findings

Severity describes consequence **if the unsafe design ships**. Likelihood is a
qualitative engineering assessment, not an observed incident rate: High means a
