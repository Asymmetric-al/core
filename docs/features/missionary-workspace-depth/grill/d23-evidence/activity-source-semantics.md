# D23 evidence — activity units, source meaning and truthful counts

Research date: **2026-10-17, Asia/Bangkok**. Research only. The review assignment
records selection of A, allowing optional outreach-attempt targets, followed by
the founder's explicit choice of supported activity types with personal
customization. Exact measure definitions below are **review recommendations**, not
a new ratification or a claim of implementation. Target configuration, management
and coaching disclosure choices remain with the main review. Only this evidence note is authored; no application,
formal owner/spec, schema, live data or provider operation changes.

## Recommended bounded contract

Use a small catalog of understandable, source-qualified measures, with separate
optional targets. P28 owns target experience and its permitted derived view; P9
owns the underlying manually recorded encounter, P27 owns exchange/outreach
meaning, and P6/P17 own communication occurrence and outcome. D15's qualified
personal-send path supplies outreach evidence through that same spine. No P28
activity ledger, message archive, universal productivity unit or metric-formula
builder is needed.

The following four measures are a **minimum recommended catalog**. They are not
four mutually exclusive buckets and must never be added into a total of activities.
Flexibility means choosing useful supported measures and personally customizing
targets within the qualified product controls; new source meanings require named
owner profiles. The founder's clarification does not select arbitrary custom
activity types or a metric-formula builder.

The main review's proposed label is **Personal messages**, with **N recorded** and
persistent, concise help: **“Recorded as sent or accepted for sending. Delivery is
not confirmed.”** This avoids a headline claiming proved delivery or completed
exchange. A source-qualified manual observation is not a provider receipt; retain
its provenance. An acceptance followed by a bounce remains a true
submission/outreach occurrence, with the separate failure visible where permitted.
A correction proving that submission never occurred changes the count. Unknown
outcomes contribute no confirmed submission; this is not a claim that no outreach
occurred. A request to improve a target never permits D15's unsafe resend.

**Exchange identity is a real bounded owner extension.** P27-P33 qualifies exchange
recency; it does not define a message-thread sessionization algorithm. Count the
qualified encounter once, not each incoming bubble, each calendar day or each
provider thread. Source facts must identify the actual encounter and preserve known
same-encounter links across narratives and adapters. An isolated relevant human
response can support a qualified exchange without message pairing, but a series of
fragments from that same exchange must not manufacture several conversations.
Do not add time-gap, text-similarity, word-count or sentiment rules. If correlation
is uncertain, preserve that uncertainty and resolve it through the existing source
correction/admission path; do not silently merge distinct encounters or assert
duplicate-free precision. This does not promise detection of every duplicate human
assertion or require a transcript, second approval or compulsory logging.

## Attribution, grouping and period meaning

- An answered outgoing call can contribute **one call and one exchange**. A held
  meeting with exchange can contribute **one meeting and one exchange**. Five calls,
  two answered, means five calls and two exchanges, not seven underlying activities.
- A joint call or meeting has one underlying occurrence. Separate missionary
  participants can each have one personally attributable participation where the
  source qualifies it; a project/team distinct-occurrence measure must not sum those
  personal counts. Do not award a call-made count to every participant in a call
  someone else initiated. Joint authorship or a CC address alone proves no sender
  activity; D15's single sender remains exact.
- Two spouses, household members or several supporters on one encounter do not
  multiply the event. P6 has recipient-copy rows: their cardinality is not the number
  of human outreach actions. A qualifying group personal message remains one authored
  occurrence where that source profile is permitted; D15's initial one-recipient
  profile is not widened. A bulk newsletter remains outside personal outreach.
- The activity actor is the actual caller/sender/participant, not necessarily the
  person recording it or today's assigned missionary. Current assignments, a Party
  merge, a profile rename or a former staff member's departure cannot reattribute
  historical work. One event linked to several exact permitted subjects may appear
  in their independently qualified context views, but no global sum of those views
  is an unduplicated total. No automatic household/team/project fan-out follows.
- A period uses the source's actual occurrence and precision. Preserve source date
  for a date-only event; for an instant use the target's explicitly qualified calendar
  and timezone. Unknown dates do not become creation time, save time or epoch. A
  period boundary cannot silently assign an undated event to this week. The target
  period/timezone choice is owned by the main review, not selected here.
- Current historical counts are a current authorized fold of qualified historical
  occurrences. Later correction, deduplication, restriction or allowed deletion may
  change an earlier count or its certainty. Recording a past event today does not
  count as today's activity. Do not freeze erroneous totals or invent an as-known-at
  reporting engine. A retained body-free fact survives body expiry only when its own
  owner retention/disclosure contract permits it.
- **Recorded zero** is valid only for the exact qualified, current, sufficiently
  complete permitted count scope. It is never **zero real activity**. Loading,
  unavailable, stale or undated relevant evidence must not silently become zero.
  A reader must not learn the existence or number of forbidden records through an
  “excluded private activity” badge. The overall data contract remains partial
  evidence because logging is voluntary.

## Material concerns — eight dimensions each

### AS23-01 — Raw log types turn intentions into completed activity

1. **Harm:** calls never made and meetings never held inflate apparent progress.
2. **Severity:** high for product truth and potential monitoring harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high if existing generic kinds/statuses directly feed targets.
4. **Cause:** counting `call`, `meeting`, task Done or UI launch rather than occurrence and outcome.
5. **Evidence:** S02:2371/2389; S03 engagement; S09; S11.
6. **Implication:** the real manual source must capture enough outcome/date/actor meaning without mandatory prose or provider integration.
7. **Clause:** “Only an accepted source fact matching the selected measure's exact unit SHALL contribute. A plan, generic completion, note, audit emission, launch or default timestamp SHALL NOT establish activity. Ordinary attributable manual occurrence evidence SHALL remain sufficient where the source permits it.”
8. **Proof:** answered/unanswered call, actual versus cancelled/no-show meeting, dialer open, task Done and generic note fixtures; a true manually recorded encounter counts once without transcript.

### AS23-02 — Accepted, sent, delivered and exchanged are collapsed

1. **Harm:** unknown/rejected mail appears sent or a delivery event fabricates a conversation.
2. **Severity:** high for factual trust and unsafe repeat outreach.
3. **Likelihood:** high with simple success/failure booleans or queue-to-toast reuse.
4. **Cause:** one generic “sent” state or count driven by button/local job acceptance.
5. **Evidence:** S05/S06/S07; fresh S12/S13.
6. **Implication:** count qualified submission evidence, state what that proves, retain failure separately; never resend to improve the count.
7. **Clause:** “Local acceptance and unknown/non-submitted outcomes SHALL NOT contribute to a confirmed personal-message submission count. Qualified provider acceptance or attributable manual sent evidence MAY contribute once, with truthful provenance and delivery limits. Later delivery failure SHALL NOT erase a real submission; a source correction proving no submission SHALL correct it. No such evidence alone establishes exchange.”
8. **Proof:** draft, queued, accepted, lost-response unknown, definitive rejection, acceptance then bounce, late genuine acceptance, duplicate receipt and actual human reply; current authority applies to every resulting view.

### AS23-03 — Recipient copies, narratives or reply fragments multiply work

1. **Harm:** one group interaction becomes several calls/meetings; chatty exchanges dominate a misleading count.
2. **Severity:** high correctness/privacy risk.
3. **Likelihood:** high with P6 row counts, timeline unions or per-note aggregation.
4. **Cause:** confusing source evidence and recipient-copy identity with human occurrence identity.
5. **Evidence:** S02:2371/2393; S04 R07/R10; S05:31/51.
6. **Implication:** count distinct source-qualified occurrence; the exact P9/P6 correlation is a required bounded adapter, not a heuristic.
7. **Clause:** “Each measure SHALL count its qualified underlying occurrence once within its actor/subject/period scope. Repeated transport, import, recipient copy, contribution and reply evidence for that occurrence SHALL NOT multiply it. Ambiguous correlation SHALL not be guessed from text, dates, names or thread ID. A real separately qualified later occurrence remains countable.”
8. **Proof:** one group message with several person-copies; two narratives of one meeting; several fragments of one exchange; two genuinely separate meetings; retry and late provider/manual corroboration; ambiguity cannot silently merge or overstate precision.

### AS23-04 — Personal attribution silently follows recorder or assignment

1. **Harm:** Maya is credited with a colleague's call, a replacement inherits past work, or team totals double count.
2. **Severity:** high when counts reach coaching or management.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible with current-assignee/profile joins or author-equals-actor assumptions.
4. **Cause:** conflating source recorder, actual participant, target subject and current owner.
5. **Evidence:** S04 R07/R08/R10 and AC12; S02:2405; S08.
6. **Implication:** source-qualified actual caller/sender/participant attribution; group count uses distinct occurrences.
7. **Clause:** “Personal activity SHALL be attributed to the actual qualified actor/participant, independently of recorder and current assignment. Joint participation MAY appear in each independently permitted personal measure; project/team occurrence counts SHALL deduplicate the shared occurrence. A household, CC, team or assignment link SHALL not create activity participation or historical access.”
8. **Proof:** staff records Maya's meeting, two missionaries attend one meeting, one initiates a joint call, replacement assignment, departed author, Party merge and cross-tenant participant spoof; personal counts and any qualified project count retain distinct meaning.

### AS23-05 — Overlapping measures become an effort score

1. **Harm:** five calls/two conversations becomes seven activities or an arbitrary “success rate.”
2. **Severity:** high for coercive/false interpretation; medium for isolated arithmetic display.
3. **Likelihood:** likely if cards are summed or a universal target percentage is invented.
4. **Cause:** treating overlapping predicates as disjoint categories or equal effort units.
5. **Evidence:** S01 question example; S02:2377; S08; D8 voluntary information.
6. **Implication:** separate labeled measures/targets; no ranking, time/effort weighting or inferred relationship value.
7. **Clause:** “Calls, personal submissions, exchanges and held meetings SHALL remain separate potentially overlapping measures. No sum, weighted productivity score, inferred effectiveness rate or missionary ranking follows from their presence. Target progress describes the chosen recorded measure only.”
8. **Proof:** five calls/two answers remains five calls and two exchanges; meeting also exchange; no grand total or zero-log failure; brief decline is an exchange without success/failure scoring.

### AS23-06 — Save time or viewer travel moves activity between periods

1. **Harm:** late entry gets this week's credit; date-only/unknown events gain invented precision.
2. **Severity:** high for historical correctness, medium for a single misleading card.
3. **Likelihood:** high given inspected current writer/mapper defaults.
4. **Cause:** `created_at`, now, midnight conversion or viewer timezone substitutes for occurrence evidence.
5. **Evidence:** S02:2373/2397; S04 AC12/13; S11.
6. **Implication:** precise timestamp/date-only/unknown source contract with the target's declared period/calendar; no newly chosen period default here.
7. **Clause:** “Dated progress SHALL use actual source occurrence precision and the qualified target period. Date-only facts SHALL retain their source date. Unknown, future planned or invalid future-completed evidence SHALL not be assigned a completed activity period using recording/update time or epoch. Backdated correction SHALL affect the actual qualified period.”
8. **Proof:** last-week call entered today; date-only travel; DST boundary instants; unknown date; future appointment; correction moving one event between weeks without changing total occurrence identity.

### AS23-07 — A private fact leaks through an aggregate or changed audience

1. **Harm:** coaches/staff infer private contact, sender, date or sensitive work from counts and deltas.
2. **Severity:** high; critical where protected identities/care are exposed.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible when aggregate access is assumed safer than source access.
4. **Cause:** target opt-in treated as sharing, metric reads using author/service privileges, or hidden-count diagnostics.
5. **Evidence:** S03:917–930; S04 R03–R06/R14; S02:2375/2405–2409; S08.
6. **Implication:** metric/date/count/source-link/history projection needs its exact current source-qualified audience, distinct from target selection.
7. **Clause:** “An optional target SHALL grant no disclosure right. Counts, dates, deltas, completeness labels and links SHALL respect current exact Tenant/subject/field/history authority and qualified aggregate inference limits. Private notes/message bodies or their existence SHALL not be published through a target. Independently retained body-free evidence requires its own permission and retention profile.”
8. **Proof:** own permitted private outreach versus coach projection; one-hidden-event differencing; revoke/read/cache race; unqualified older history; source-body expiry; hidden records do not appear as an excluded-count warning.

### AS23-08 — Missing, stale or voluntary history is called zero activity

1. **Harm:** a missionary is treated as inactive for not logging; users act on incomplete counts.
2. **Severity:** high for coercion and trust.
3. **Likelihood:** high with standard empty-array/failure defaults.
4. **Cause:** recorded subset mistaken for all real work; permission and source lag ignored.
5. **Evidence:** S02:2373/2377/2417; S08; source-complete versus first-page distinction at S02:2401.
6. **Implication:** say recorded, preserve known coverage/currentness, use neutral unavailable states without disclosing forbidden evidence.
7. **Clause:** “Zero SHALL describe only an actually qualified zero recorded count in the declared permitted scope. Source failure, stale/incomplete relevant coverage and unresolved occurrence dates SHALL not become zero or complete actual-activity claims. Voluntary missing logs SHALL never establish inactivity, poor effort, debt, penalty or loss of normal use.”
8. **Proof:** actual zero, no logging, incomplete page, lagging source, denied read, unknown date and network failure show distinguishable truthful states; larger authorized history is counted without loading only the first display page.

### AS23-09 — Correction, deletion or restore leaves an immortal total

1. **Harm:** corrected duplicates still inflate history, removed private facts remain exposed, restored data reappears.
2. **Severity:** high integrity/privacy risk.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible with additive counters or cached maximums.
4. **Cause:** P28 maintains independent accumulated truth instead of a revision-aware source projection.
5. **Evidence:** S02:2373/2401/2409; S04 R08–R10/R14–R16; P27 restore AC27-P32-05 at partnerships:2341–2343.
6. **Implication:** source-owned correction/removal plus recoverable revision-aware projection; past totals can truthfully change under current rights.
7. **Clause:** “Corrections, qualified deduplication, reattribution, restriction and permitted deletion SHALL reconcile affected activity counts and periods without inventing new activity. Accepted provenance and lawful retained facts remain source-owned; body expiry alone neither proves activity absent nor authorizes indefinite derivative retention. Restore SHALL reconcile current restrictions and corrections before serving affected counts.”
8. **Proof:** void wrong entry, combine duplicate narratives, redate/reattribute, restricted latest encounter, lawful disposal, body expiry with/without qualified fact retention, and restore predating these changes; old projections cannot override current source.

### AS23-10 — Flexible labels or retained rows silently redefine source truth

1. **Harm:** an arbitrary custom type, imported “Done,” generic email or renamed target is treated as a proven exchange.
2. **Severity:** high semantic correctness risk.
3. **Likelihood:** high if flexible configuration maps arbitrary labels directly to counters.
4. **Cause:** catalog presentation/configuration mistaken for qualification; legacy generic rows blindly backfilled.
5. **Evidence:** S01 explicit metric frontier; S02/P9 owner meaning; S10 intended source contract; S11 incomplete retained kind/date shape.
6. **Implication:** finite source-qualified metric definitions, stable meaning/version and honest migration; personal target customization does not redefine activity facts.
7. **Clause:** “Every countable measure SHALL name its exact owner-qualified unit, outcome predicate, actor/subject attribution, date precision, source identity, correction and disclosure profile. Labels, tags, custom kinds, imports and task status SHALL not change those meanings. Retained rows lacking required evidence SHALL remain historical context/explicitly unqualified, without invented dates, participants or automated backfill credit.”
8. **Proof:** rename label; incompatible kind/outcome; generic historical call row; duplicate import; custom label named Meeting or Conversation; a newly qualified supported source profile becomes usable without changing the meaning of already recorded evidence.

## Proof status and smallest permanent delivery

The permanent path is **one native record-once source flow plus qualified derived
counts**, usable without phone/mail integrations, P35 workflows or compulsory
notes. Optional D15 sending can supply an already qualified occurrence without a
second log. A bounded catalog is simpler than quotas, arbitrary formulas, external
activity surveillance, mailbox ingestion or a second encounter store.

The ten concerns above require actual source positive/negative/boundary tests and
cross-source duplicate/correction/current-audience proof before activation. A table
of synthetic qualified facts can test aggregation but cannot qualify P9 encounter
identity, P6 acceptance, current P12 grants, conversation correlation, source dates
or human comprehension. This note executes **no runtime/database/model tests**.
It performs targeted repository reads and two unauthenticated public documentation
fetches only. No user observation, performance target or provider operation is
claimed. Optional participation, supported measure meaning and new coach disclosure must
not be ratified by an implementation shortcut.
