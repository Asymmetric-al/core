# D23 — Flexible activity targets and record-once UX

Research date: **October 17, 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. Research-only evidence and
recommendations for the founder's selected **A: separately labelled outreach-
attempt targets**, alongside actual exchanges/held meetings. The founder requests
substantial customization and a simple, excellent experience. The founder has
explicitly confirmed **supported activity types with personal customization**.
Arbitrary new activity types, formulas and a dashboard builder are not selected.

No app, dependency, live tenant, donor record, provider or formal specification was
changed. Public documentation is not authenticated product testing. No interviews,
usability result, performance capacity or runtime source qualification is claimed.

## Recommended product shape

Give Maya a small **My activity targets** area in her existing workspace. Start
with no imposed target. **Add target** opens one short form: a supported measure,
a positive whole-number quantity and its period. Optional personalization covers
her own name for the target and whether it appears on her overview. Keep the
canonical measure and actual date range visible under a personal name. A label
must never redefine what counts.

Weekly (the recommended initial choice) and monthly presets plus **Custom dates**
offer a useful bounded range without a planning spreadsheet. Show the actual
dates, week boundary and applicable timezone. Use a qualified tenant work-calendar
default when available; otherwise show a zone/week-start suggestion for explicit
personal confirmation. An explicit personal adjustment at setup remains available.
Retain the chosen calendar for each period, with later calendar changes effective
next period. Presets are conveniences, not agency quotas or a fixed Sunday start.
A custom range can express a short campaign, part-time season or longer horizon.
Repetition, effective edits and pause/resume use the existing target owner;
recommendations below do not establish a new scheduling engine.

Five recorded outgoing calls that produce two conversations remain **five calls,
of which two became conversations**. A call can satisfy two distinct measures, but
the cards are not additive. No combined “seven touches,” weighted productivity
score, ranking, streak or red missed-effort state should appear. A useful one-way
message remains outreach, without a requirement that the recipient reply.

Use qualified source facts once. Let a missionary inspect what contributes to a
count, correct the source where permitted, and see the recalculated result. Do not
make them log once for a contact, again for a target and again for coaching. A
target is a view of admitted source evidence against a chosen quantity; it does
not establish an encounter or grant access to one.

  not outreach, sending or conversation evidence.

  qualification. Creating a target or a count grants no new staff/coach access.

**Confirmed customization boundary:** supported activity types with substantial
personal control. The recommended controls cover measures, quantities, periods,
labels, visibility, ordering and stopping/resuming. Small optional organization
presets use the same supported fields and exact P28 configuration authority. Their
deliberate adoption creates an editable personal version; later preset changes
never overwrite it or enroll anyone. They do not authorize arbitrary
formulas, weights, raw counters, message-body inspection or new event meanings.
A free-text name cannot declare an activity to be a real conversation. Personal
labels provide flexibility while the canonical measure remains visible. A future
custom-type expansion would be a separate product/source decision, not a hidden
extension of this answer.

### Daily use, logging and drilldown

Illustrative card, with a fictional quantity rather than a recommended target:

> **Stay in touch**  
> Calls made · [exact period]  
> **5 recorded / target 8**  
> Includes answered and unanswered calls.  
> View recorded calls · Edit target

A separate Conversations card may show **2 recorded** from those same calls. Do not add
the two cards together. Display **Recorded** persistently; 5 does not claim to be
every call Maya made. If she records more than the target, retain the true text
count; a visual bar may stop at its end without truncating the number or creating
an extra-performance score. No confetti, streak or notification is necessary.

For a **Personal messages** card, use **N recorded**, with the concise definition
**Recorded as sent or accepted for sending; delivery is not confirmed.** This
avoids making a provider's accepted-for-sending outcome look like completed
delivery in the headline. Manual assertions and provider evidence keep their
source distinctions in permitted detail; neither proves a conversation.

Where source-list access is authorized, **View recorded calls** opens the ordinary
permitted history filtered to the same measure, subject and period. It explains
what counts, shows occurrence precision and the actual result, and provides a
source correction action only when allowed. An independently authorized
aggregate-only view offers no drilldown or hidden-membership explanation.
The count must not pretend the first page is the whole set. If projection is
updating, say **Updating recorded activity…**; if unavailable, say **Activity counts
are unavailable. Try again.** A qualified complete empty result can say **No
recorded calls in this period**, never **You made no calls**. Do not expose a hidden
record count to explain why something is absent.

From the contact, existing task or activity view, **Record activity** uses the same
qualified source. Keep known authorized person/ministry context filled in. Let
Maya choose the actual method/outcome, occurrence date at its true precision and
optional purpose/note. For a call, **No answer** and **Spoke with them** are possible
plain labels, subject to the canonical outcome contract; do not preselect success.
An explicitly unknown date is valid source context but cannot be assigned to a
precise week through recording time. Explain that exclusion only through permitted
source context, without forcing a guessed date.

Show the real audience before save. Do not imply all notes are shared or private,
offer a new per-record ACL or silently add coach visibility. A note is optional;
recording an outcome needs no transcript. **Save activity** acknowledges the source
save. If the target projection is still updating, say so rather than failing the
saved record or inviting duplicate entry. A permitted **Save and finish follow-up**
may compose the existing operations, but must expose distinct outcomes if needed;
ordinary Done/Not needed remains usable without logging an interaction.

## Material concerns — eight dimensions and falsifiable clauses

Likelihood is qualitative design/engineering judgment, not a measured incidence
rate. Proposed clauses require owner integration and actual implementation proof.

### A01 — Flexible labels become arbitrary or misleading measures

1. **Failure:** A personal name or unqualified custom type changes counting meaning; a rename rewrites history.
2. **Importance:** Users cannot tell what their target measures or compare periods honestly.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible if “fully customizable” is implemented without the founder's clarification.
5. **Evidence:** E06/E10/E11/E12 show distinct labels, definitions and reporting dependencies; founder confirmed supported types with personal customization.
6. **Decision impact:** Preserve substantial personal control within that selected scope.
7. **Permanent fix:** Stable owner-qualified measures, visible canonical meaning and explicit historical treatment.
8. **Exact clause AUX01:** “Personal labels and presentation preferences SHALL NOT change or conceal a supported measure's canonical counting meaning. D23 SHALL NOT introduce arbitrary new activity types or formulas; a free-text label SHALL NOT establish an exchange, attempt or calculation. Definition changes SHALL preserve prior target and source meaning.”

**Proof:** Rename a Calls made target “Conversations”; its subtitle and count
still disclose the real measure. Retire/rename a supported definition without
changing old outcomes. No custom-type creator, generic formula or hidden default
classifier appears.

### A02 — Attempts, exchanges and completed tasks inflate activity

1. **Failure:** Five calls plus two conversations display seven touches; checkbox/dialer/email-open events manufacture actual contact.
2. **Importance:** The feature becomes an inaccurate effort score and undermines source truth.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Likely if task Added/Done counters are copied from comparable CRMs.
5. **Evidence:** E01/E03/E06/E09; P27 REQ27-P33 and D19/D20.
6. **Decision impact:** A permits separately meaningful measures, not a new universal activity total.
7. **Permanent fix:** Exact source fact/outcome profiles, overlapping-set explanation and one underlying encounter identity.
8. **Exact clause AUX02:** “Attempt, personal exchange, held meeting and task completion SHALL retain distinct source meanings. Five calls including two conversations SHALL NOT become seven underlying activities. Multiple contributions, delivery retries, imports or overlapping target views SHALL NOT create another encounter. Task completion, app launch and passive tracking SHALL NOT manufacture outreach or exchange.”

**Proof:** Five calls/two exchanges; a no-answer call followed by a later callback;
two notes for one meeting; a scheduled no-show; provider-accepted email without
reply; duplicate import. Each measure reports only its qualified meaning.

### A03 — Period edits and unknown dates silently rewrite progress

1. **Failure:** Creation time fills missing occurrence; travel changes buckets; a midweek edit rebases historical targets invisibly.
2. **Importance:** Numbers look precise while referring to different facts and expectations.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible; the inspected mapper already substitutes creation time.
5. **Evidence:** Local `mapActivity`; P27 actual-occurrence rules; E07/E08/E13.
6. **Decision impact:** Flexibility needs visible, stable periods rather than more calendar controls.
7. **Permanent fix:** Exact period/zone, true occurrence precision, explicit edit effective boundary and preserved versions.
8. **Exact clause AUX03:** “Target periods SHALL have explicit source-defined calendar boundaries and timezone. Activity SHALL use qualified occurrence precision, never recording/update time as an unknown-date fallback. Quantity/period changes SHALL reveal their effective scope and preserve prior expectations. Source corrections SHALL update eligible recorded counts without inventing historical activity or silently freezing known errors.”

**Proof:** Date-only/unknown events, late entry, month boundary, DST/travel, custom
period crossing a year, current/next-period edit and later source correction.
Unknown cannot enter a precise period merely because it was logged today.

### A04 — Pause, hide and end have ambiguous effects

1. **Failure:** Hiding a card stops measurement; Pause erases current history or Resume produces a backlog.
2. **Importance:** Missionaries cannot step away or reduce targets without accidental data changes.
3. **Severity:** Medium.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with a single generic Active switch.
5. **Evidence:** E06 separates dashboard placement and period settings; selected voluntary use; lifecycle behavior here is an explicit recommendation.
6. **Decision impact:** Keep flexibility understandable without a schedule-management application.
7. **Permanent fix:** Separate presentation from target lifecycle, with exact simple effects and retained source history.
8. **Exact clause AUX04:** “Hide, pause, resume and end SHALL have separately stated effects. Hiding SHALL change presentation only. Pause SHALL immediately pause the current active comparison and stop future target periods while preserving source activity, independent recording and the original target/history. Resume SHALL show the current range and quantity, with a next-period alternative for repeating targets; current-period resume SHALL include all eligible recorded activity in that full period. Resume SHALL NOT create missed-period backlog or automatic proration. One-off ending SHALL preserve original target/history and record an ended state, not achieved activity.”

**Proof:** Hide/show; pause midperiod and immediately remove the active comparison;
record/correct activity while paused; resume the current full period or deliberately
choose the next; resume after several periods; end a one-off target. No activity is
deleted, paused-time activity lost from the eligible resumed period, false
achievement written, missed-period target created or share silently changed.

### A05 — Helpful drilldown or coaching becomes unauthorized disclosure

1. **Failure:** A count, target name, period comparison or source link reveals protected work; creating a target shares it automatically.
2. **Importance:** Aggregates and custom labels can disclose people/context even without message bodies.
3. **Severity:** Critical.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with cross-surface reuse and small groups.
5. **Evidence:** D8-C11/current projection rules; P27 exact field/history permission; vendor role models are not Core authority.
6. **Decision impact:** New measures need exact disclosure qualification, not inherited blanket coach access.
7. **Permanent fix:** Permission-shaped facts and derivatives, deliberate qualified sharing, no hidden membership hints.

## Alternatives, qualitative validation and remaining limits

**Strongest simpler alternative:** retain actual-exchange/held-meeting targets
only, with attempts visible in ordinary history. The founder selected A, so the
review preserves attempts as a first-class optional target meaning. Within A,
fixed agency quotas are too rigid; a universal formula/custom-report builder is
unnecessary. The founder explicitly selected supported types with personal
customization. The recommended controls implement that distinction without
turning ordinary planning into a new analytics administration surface.

Test the full journey with actual new and established missionaries and authorized
staff/coach users at their permitted surfaces, including mobile and assistive
technology. Ask participants to choose a target, explain five calls/two exchanges,
change its quantity, pause/resume, record an unanswered call without notes, correct
an occurrence date and explain an unavailable count. Check that they understand
what is shared and can use ordinary tasks without logging. Treat critical
misunderstanding as a release issue; a small qualitative study is not statistical
proof of universal usability or productivity benefit. No such study occurred here.

Implementation still needs exact source/outcome catalog, personal/subject scope,
period/lifecycle rules, correction/retention, separately authorized source drilldown,
durable save/projection recovery and performance qualification. Those obligations
must not be hidden behind competitor screenshots or an unqualified existing form.
No vendor's arbitrary limit, extra dependency, mandatory recording workflow or
automated supporter contact is required to complete this experience.
