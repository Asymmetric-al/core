## Minimal source and lifecycle contract to qualify

The existing Goal Version owner can hold an optional, typed target definition: exact tenant/personal owner, qualified measure identity, positive whole target quantity, admitted calendar/period, version and current lifecycle. These are logical facts, **not a proposed new table list**. The source owner must resolve exact identity/cardinality and whether a target series needs stored period snapshots or can derive them from retained versions. A scope-qualified reference must bind the actual tenant identity/assignment or typed source subject required by that owner; a global `profile_id`, bare numeric ID, display name or label is not sufficient authority.

The **actor** is the currently authorized person saving the target. The **target owner** is the missionary whose deliberate target is being configured. The **credited participant** is the actual qualified caller/sender/participant of each source occurrence. A staff member recording Maya's conversation does not earn Maya's target credit; a current task assignee is not retroactively the participant. Conversely, a person appearing in a recipient list does not become a sender or participant. Any delegated editing must use the exact authorized action, never transfer target ownership as a side effect.

Each measure needs a finite owner-defined unit, qualifying outcomes, canonical source identity, participation rule, occurrence precision and correction behavior. For example, unanswered and answered outbound calls can both count toward recorded outbound calls, while only qualified actual exchange counts toward the separate exchange measure. The five calls/two exchanges example produces **5 calls and 2 exchanges**, not seven interchangeable activities. A held meeting can also be an exchange; do not add overlapping measures into a score. A qualified personal message submission remains distinct from delivery/read/reply and from bulk newsletter delivery. Recipient copies, retries, narratives, task links and multiple partnership projections do not multiply one occurrence. Repeated real calls remain distinct even when the number, person and minute match.

P27's recency rule can recognize a genuine brief human reply. It does **not** by itself define whether several message bubbles constitute one or several target-count encounters. That cardinality must come from the qualified P9/P6 source contract. No arbitrary time-gap, sentiment, thread or text-deduplication heuristic is justified. A personal label cannot supply missing qualifying facts; the selected supported catalog must preserve these invariants without new custom types, user-written SQL or an arbitrary rules engine.

The target number must be an exact positive whole supported integer with a finite transport/storage range. Reject fractional, NaN/infinite, negative and overflow values before coercion. Zero is not an overloaded “Off” representation; any future zero-target semantics would need an explicit owner choice. Unset has no denominator and no ratio. More recorded occurrences than the target remain an honest count; do not drop extra events or silently clamp the numerical fact. No target percentage implies fundraising success or completeness of real-world activity.

## Authorized counting and cache behavior

The server first qualifies the exact viewer context, target owner, measure, selected period and source-fact projection. It selects only admitted source evidence, resolves current canonical occurrence/participant identity, and counts distinct qualified occurrences **at that grain**. A join to multiple recipient/participant/contribution rows must not multiply that grain. Personal counts for two actual participants may each include a shared meeting if that measure qualifies them; a project total is a separate owner-defined distinct-occurrence measure, not the sum of personal counts. D23 does not itself authorize a project/team rollup.

“Authorize before aggregate” means that the permitted aggregate input/profile is resolved before generating or releasing the count. It does **not** require granting an invited coach raw event-table SELECT. A separately qualified aggregate-only disclosure may be supported by the source projection; it must not reveal hidden event existence through totals, differences, calendar gaps, cache status, links or errors. No new arbitrary privacy threshold is selected here.

`COUNT(*)` counts returned rows, including zero rows after a successful query; SQL cannot tell whether missing input reflects no records, a failed fetch, incomplete source coverage, unknown dates or lack of authority. **“0 recorded calls in this permitted period”** can be honest when that precisely scoped query is complete. It never proves Maya made no real calls. Partial recorded evidence can remain useful if labeled accurately; hidden/restricted existence must not be exposed as a count of excluded events. An unknown date remains outside a definitive weekly cohort, with only independently permitted uncertainty disclosed. Do not replace it with creation time or date-zero, and do not demand transcripts or exhaustive logging to make a count possible.

Use the owner's explicit calendar/timezone and half-open periods. The parent's proposed setup seeds the admitted Tenant work timezone for deliberate confirmation; device travel or session defaults cannot silently select another calendar. Weekly/monthly/custom-date definitions and repeat/end behavior need bounded explicit semantics; custom dates do not imply a recurrence-language editor. Instants map through the qualified named calendar; date-only facts retain their original date semantics rather than being fabricated into midnight instants. An unknown date cannot be placed reliably in a week. Captured timezone/calendar meaning must survive travel and preference changes; a UTC timestamp does not retain its originally supplied IANA zone. Week-start, DST, year boundary and applicable precision are part of the source/target contract, not whatever the database session or browser happens to use. Avoid both an arbitrary user-facing timezone builder and a silent hardcoded choice.

Any reusable derived result binds the target definition/period meaning, current relevant source revisions/coverage and authorized projection. The actual owner must qualify invalidation/rebuild and release fences; a timestamped cache entry is not authority. Source correction/permission narrowing can invalidate a previously valid count. A materialized view is stored data, not automatic reapplication of current base-table policies. A public or broadly browser-readable aggregate table cannot safely store sensitive counters merely because bodies are absent. Retention, backup restoration and browser state must respect the same source restrictions before serving; append-only audit does not mean permanent private-content retention.

## Commands, constraints and concurrency

Create/change/stop/continue belong to the existing qualified target command. Trusted context supplies tenant, actor and effective permission; the owner validates the intended personal target scope, fields, measure and period. Required expected revision and stable semantic operation identity prevent lost updates and duplicate accepted intent. Bind retries to exact meaning; a changed payload under the same identity conflicts. A genuinely new deliberate edit is not deduplicated merely because the number matches a past one. Unchanged effective intent returns an appropriate unchanged outcome without manufacturing another business version; retain mandatory existing audit.

Commit the qualified version/head and required acceptance/audit together. Required local failure must leave no accepted new head; downstream display failure is a recoverable projection state. Lost responses recover the original authorized result, which may already be superseded, rather than replaying a new Save. Loss of edit permission need not erase a separately permitted read-only receipt; loss of disclosure permission cannot be bypassed by retry history. No generic CRM/provider journal is introduced for this feature.

Expected revision alone does not fence permission changes. The real current PDP/source acceptance mechanism must serialize or revalidate the relevant exact authority with the target transition. Prove both Save-first/revoke-first and Stop-first/Continue-first orders, plus stale current-week edits versus the next-week boundary. Source-count generation/release similarly needs coherent relevant source/target/current authorization evidence; reading three separately fresh values in unrelated snapshots does not guarantee a coherent result. This is an implementation qualification requirement, not a prescription for one global tenant lock or another epoch engine. Unrelated permission changes need not invalidate unchanged effective rights when the owner can safely requalify exact intent.

Database constraints backstop that contract: same-tenant typed references, source-owned immutable owner/origin/author fields, valid state combinations, stable target/version identities and referential retention. A user who can edit both target A and target B must still be unable to retarget A into B with an ordinary update. Scope columns are not ordinary editable fields. Required target/history references must not cascade-delete on routine assignment removal or account departure; the existing retention/source owner determines lawful restriction/anonymization, not a new blanket eternal-retention rule.

No raw grants should be widened to make a target widget work. Review effective SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE grants, table- versus column-level grants, all applicable policies, view/function ownership and executable paths. P12 remains the sole fine-grained decision source; RLS uses the unified trusted Tenant source. Applicable omitted `WITH CHECK` inherits `USING`, so omission alone does not establish a mutation bypass. Conversely, a tenant predicate cannot protect mutable owner/actor fields within that tenant. SECURITY DEFINER and views depend on actual ownership/privileges; FORCE RLS does not constrain a superuser/BYPASSRLS role. A justified bounded definer needs explicit current checks, least grants, a trusted search path and restricted PUBLIC EXECUTE. Service credentials confer no product authority.

## Material findings — eight dimensions each

### DB23-F1 — Counting display rows creates false outreach

1. **Harm:** duplicate narratives, recipients or links inflate activity; actual repeated calls collapse; logger receives someone else's credit.
2. **Severity:** high for false decisions and protected attribution.
3. **Likelihood:** likely if reusing the generic timeline/actor fields without qualification.
4. **Cause:** technical row grain, attribution and business occurrence are conflated.
5. **Evidence:** P9 B9 multi-endpoint emissions; P6 per-person-copy grain; P27 C04/C12/P33 one encounter and actual participants; inspected mapper lacks these distinctions.
6. **Effect on A:** retain separate optional measures, but require source-certified occurrence/participant semantics and no combined score.
7. **Exact clause:** “Activity progress SHALL count the current qualified canonical occurrence once per admitted measure and actual participant/scope. Recorder, current assignee, recipient copy, narrative, delivery, task link, share and replay SHALL NOT independently create another occurrence or participant. Overlapping measures SHALL remain separate; one source-defined group occurrence SHALL NOT be multiplied by recipient copies.”
8. **Production proof:** one call with two narratives and two endpoints, group send with several recipients, actual repeated calls, staff recording for Maya, two real participants, recipient-only person, reply-thread cardinality and merges. Use actual P9/P6 owner adapters, not seeded IDs.

### DB23-F2 — Created time, missing data or cached success changes reality

1. **Harm:** old/undated activity counts this week; corrections cannot lower progress; partial history becomes zero or an effort claim.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** likely with the inspected date fallback/capped display fetch or an increment-only counter.
4. **Cause:** entry time substitutes for occurrence; irreversible aggregates omit coverage/current source evidence.
5. **Evidence:** current mapper at 292–305, capped fetch at 532–549; P27 C07/C11/P33; primary aggregate/date/materialized-view rules.
6. **Effect on A:** qualify recorded facts and date precision, retain useful partial information without false completeness, and make corrections reversible at the projection level.
7. **Exact clause:** “Progress SHALL use the source occurrence's admitted date precision and target calendar, not created/updated time. Corrections, restriction and deduplication SHALL rederive affected current authorized counts, including decreases or loss of certainty. Missing, loading, unavailable, incomplete or undated evidence SHALL NOT become actual zero activity, reliable performance or a fabricated cohort.”
8. **Production proof:** late entry, date-only travel, unknown date, DST/week/year boundary, event beyond display cap, source-date move between weeks, source restriction and duplicate merge; distinguish zero recorded results from no actual work.

### DB23-F3 — Target ownership and customization create a second authority

1. **Harm:** a staff/delegate edit becomes another person's target, a private activity metric reaches a public financial goal pin, or custom labels redefine source facts.
2. **Severity:** critical for unauthorized disclosure/change; high for false semantics.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible when reusing one financial-goal form, generic JSON or global profile ownership.
4. **Cause:** source container is mistaken for permission and meaning; actor, target owner and credited participant collapse.
5. **Evidence:** P28 owner constraints 128–139; P12 exact contexts; P27 C04/C12; founder-selected supported types with personal customization.
6. **Effect on A:** extend the existing Goal Version owner with bounded typed activity semantics; no second registry or implicit authority expansion.
7. **Exact clause:** “The P28 Support-Raising Goal Version owner SHALL own optional activity-target definitions through exact current personal scope and action rights. Actor, target owner and source participant SHALL remain distinct. Financial edit/share authority, project assignment or public goal binding SHALL NOT grant activity-target mutation/disclosure. Each selectable measure SHALL have a qualified source unit and correction contract; labels/formulas cannot manufacture eligibility.”
8. **Production proof:** same IDs across tenant/type, delegated logger versus target owner, field-scoped financial-only edit, existing public v1 pin after activity v2, unsupported custom measure, retired measure and scope switch while drafting.

### DB23-F4 — Edits, retries and weekly continuation rewrite target history

1. **Harm:** concurrent Save loses intent; changed denominators rewrite prior weeks; cancelled targets silently return; a timeout creates duplicate versions.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible with mutable totals or optimistic browser-only state.
4. **Cause:** no distinct target definition/version/period meaning, accepted-result identity or current-authority fence.
5. **Evidence:** P27 command and atomic recovery contract; inherited D16 mechanism evidence with explicit limits; weekly ownership and current-period edit proposal above.
6. **Effect on A:** keep one clear Save/stop flow while preserving exact accepted history and truthful recovery.
7. **Exact clause:** “Target commands SHALL bind exact current owner/scope, intended values, admitted period meaning, expected head and stable operation identity. Required version/head/evidence/outcome SHALL commit atomically. Current-period quantity changes SHALL be visible and SHALL NOT rewrite earlier-period definitions. Period/measure/calendar changes and continuation SHALL follow the qualified prospective owner contract; stopping a target SHALL NOT erase events or create missed work.”
8. **Production proof:** same-intent retries with changed transport keys, changed meaning under reused key, no-op Save, required-audit fault, Save/revoke both orders, Stop/Continue both orders, stale browser after week rollover, read-authorized result recovery after loss of edit permission. D23 has not executed these actual target paths.

### DB23-F5 — An innocent count discloses protected activity

1. **Harm:** coach/staff/private-context access widens through counts, dates, trends, notifications or caches despite hidden event bodies.
2. **Severity:** critical where restricted people or ministry context is inferred.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible with aggregate-first redaction, broad views or stale cached permissions.
4. **Cause:** body-free is mistaken for public; target opt-in is treated as coach/history permission; invoker view forces raw table grants.
5. **Evidence:** P27 C03/C08/C12; P12 current projection/floor; primary view and materialized-view behavior; D8/D9/D10 bounded prior experiments.
6. **Effect on A:** activity tracking and sharing remain independently qualified; no raw coach SELECT just to render a summary.
7. **Exact clause:** “Target definitions, counts, period gaps, comparisons, source links and recovered results SHALL use the exact current permitted projection and history scope. No target, financial-summary grant, assignment or coach role SHALL infer another disclosure. Aggregate-only access SHALL NOT require raw source SELECT; materialized/cache results SHALL be fenced against current source restrictions and authorization before release.”
8. **Production proof:** two coaches with different resources/history, source body expiry versus separately retained fact, revoke during computation/release, old cache/Realtime/exports, narrow current period versus prior history, hidden activity inference and public goal output. No synthetic fixture establishes inference resistance or recall of already read bytes.

### DB23-F6 — Coarse RLS is asked to enforce the business command

1. **Harm:** raw/service writes reassign target owner, tenant or author; stale context accepts an edit; partial evidence survives failure.
2. **Severity:** critical for tenant/authority escape.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible if adapting a generic table editor or privileged preference endpoint.
4. **Cause:** row access is confused with column/transition permission; composite integrity and immutable fields are omitted; parallel SQL permission logic diverges from P12.
5. **Evidence:** P12 1540–1587, P27 C04/C05, current primary policy/function/view rules and the server data boundary.
6. **Effect on A:** use the actual source command with current PDP plus structural database backstops; no new fine-grained RLS engine.
7. **Exact clause:** “Trusted context SHALL determine tenant/actor and the owner SHALL validate target subject and current field/action scope. Same-tenant typed references, valid states, immutable origin/owner/author and least effective grants SHALL backstop the command. Direct, view, RPC, worker and service paths SHALL preserve the same constraints and current P12 boundary; ordinary assignment removal SHALL NOT cascade-delete retained lawful history.”
8. **Production proof:** actual nonsuperuser roles, allowed-row-to-forbidden-owner mutation, cross-tenant FK, permissive-policy OR, privileged function/service path, stale assignment, expected-head conflict and audit rollback. Qualify the actual schema/grants rather than treating prior toy outcomes as Core proof.

### DB23-F7 — A convenience counter becomes a diary, quota or operations engine

1. **Harm:** duplicated records drift, broad history scans become expensive, missing logging causes pressure or automatic outreach, and stop/restore replays work.
2. **Severity:** high for privacy/behavior; medium for avoidable debt and workload.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible if progress is built as writable increments with per-week jobs and copied source bodies.
4. **Cause:** derived count, planning denominator and operational obligation are conflated; no bounded rebuild/retention boundary.
5. **Evidence:** P9 composed timeline, P27 C07–C09 voluntary/source-owned history, P28 weekly-target ownership and PostgreSQL stored-view staleness.
6. **Effect on A:** one optional comparison over qualified existing sources; no private diary, copied bodies, automatic follow-up or silent score.
7. **Exact clause:** “Activity targets SHALL remain optional planning comparisons over qualified existing sources. No independent writable success ledger, copied contact body store, compulsory logging, automatic contact/task/stage/consent effect or performance ranking SHALL result. Derived queries/rebuilds SHALL be tenant/scope bounded and current-authority safe; rollout/restore SHALL preserve source origin, target history and restrictions without inventing historical target obligations.”
8. **Production proof:** targets Off with ordinary work intact; source edits without count increments; restore of restricted facts; absent optional workflows; complete bounded period query beyond display pagination; declared production-shaped workload and latency/rebuild/revocation budgets. No measured scale or UX claim is made here.
