## Exact corrected decision to record

> **A — Optional, deliberately shared activity summaries.** When the organization
> permits it, Maya may include selected eligible activity targets and their
> recorded counts in an existing coaching share. Creating a target, accepting a
> coach invitation or enabling the organization setting does not share it.
>
> Maya chooses the exact target and period scope, sees a preview of what the
> named coach may actually receive, and uses **Save sharing** once. The review
> identifies the organization, ministry/workspace, canonical measure, dates and
> sharing duration. Target names that Maya wrote privately are not automatically
> disclosed. New targets and materially changed measures or ministry scopes need
> a separate deliberate inclusion.
>
> Jordan receives a read-only summary: qualified recorded counts, applicable
> target quantities and clearly explained dates and state. He receives no
> supporter names, message bodies, private notes, individual activity records or
> links to them. Five calls including two conversations remain five calls and two
> conversations, never seven activities. These numbers describe recorded evidence,
> not Maya's effort, relationship quality or all work performed.
>
> A comparison must preserve the selected target's actual meaning. Asym must not
> silently remove restricted activity from the numerator and present the reduced
> result as complete progress against Maya's broader target. If a safe, compatible
> comparison is unavailable, it says so; independently permitted target information
> may remain. A missing count is not zero, and a hidden count must not be recovered
> from its percentage, remaining amount, chart or accessibility text.
>
> Within admitted periods, summaries follow qualified source corrections and the
> target version actually applicable to that period. They are not frozen copies
> of Maya's numbers at the time of sharing. Current permissions, source privacy,
> expiry and revocation govern every release. **Stop sharing activity summary**
> ends this module's access independently of other coaching modules, personal
> targets and source records. Previously seen information cannot be recalled.

> Maya deliberately shares the selected recurring target from the exact first
> reviewed period onward. Qualified future periods of that same target continue
> while sharing remains active. Already admitted periods remain reviewable while
> current rights, duration and qualified source/retention bounds allow. Periods
> before that reviewed start and new targets or materially changed scopes are not
> automatically included.

The founder explicitly chose **continuation into future periods of the selected
target**. Keeping already admitted periods reviewable is the now-ratified explicit
amendment, so a period does not disappear merely because it just ended. This is a
D24 activity-purpose scope, not permission inherited from D10 financial history or
D11 duration. It does not authorize unlimited retention. The first reviewed whole
period can include dates before Save, as D23 already permits; show those dates.

**Strongest alternative:** keep numbers personal and use voluntary qualitative
check-ins. It minimizes numeric disclosure, but cannot provide the chosen shared
activity-versus-target conversation and would explicitly narrow the intended
coaching capability. Screenshots or manually retyped reports introduce stale
copies and lose useful access controls. A is preferable with a qualified native
summary. It does not require a new reporting product, calendar, questionnaire,
coaching CRM or analytics vendor.

MPDX's agency-specific compulsory logging guidance conflicts with Core's voluntary
contract and is rejected. General CRM administrator visibility, dashboard builders,
leaderboards and freely adjustable filters do not override Core's exact source
purpose. P12's aggregate floor is retained, not replaced by a newly invented
universal “at least five calls” rule. Ten calls can involve one supporter. The
missionary is deliberately identified; this is not an anonymized dataset.

## The complete user journey

### Maya: select, understand, save and remain in control

Use **Coaching → Jordan → Sharing**, with the organization and authorized ministry
context plainly visible. Add one **Activity summary** section alongside the
already independently shared modules. No new invitation or general dashboard
designer is needed. Organization policy controls eligibility; it does not select
targets on Maya's behalf.

For first inclusion, show eligible target rows unselected; when editing, load the
actual saved selection. Each row identifies its canonical
measure, scope, applicable target and concrete period. Two Calls made targets
must be distinguishable by their dates/schedule and admitted context. Maya may
also see her own currently authorized private target name in the selector, marked
**Not shared**, to distinguish otherwise identical plans. Bind the exact target
lineage; that private selector label never enters Jordan's preview or payload. Selection changes a draft and inline preview, not
access. A simple selection list suffices; paging/search is added only when needed,
with no hidden all-pages selection.

The adjacent preview says **Jordan will see**, then displays the actual qualified
summary projection. Example, only if both comparisons are authorized and complete:

| Activity      | Recorded | Target | Period             |
| ------------- | -------: | -----: | ------------------ |
| Calls made    |        5 |      8 | 12–18 October 2026 |
| Conversations |        2 |      3 | 12–18 October 2026 |

Caption: **“Recorded activity only. Conversations may be included in calls.”**
The numbers/dates are examples, not a workload recommendation or verified release.
Use the canonical source calendar; never rebucket for Jordan's browser timezone.
Expose a geographic timezone name only when separately safe. Otherwise explain
**“Dates follow this target's calendar.”** The exact dates remain understandable.

Review copy includes **“Summaries update as eligible activity is recorded or
corrected and applicable targets change.”** Period copy states **“From [first reviewed period] onward, including future
periods of this target while sharing is active.”** Explain that admitted periods
remain reviewable within the current rights and qualified retention bounds. **Save sharing** is the sole primary action. Display the
durable saved scope and result. Invitation-pending is different from active
access; saving intended scope does not claim Jordan can already read it.

Maya can return to change the selected scope. A change to an ordinary applicable
quantity updates the admitted summary's content; it does not create another
approval ritual. A new target, measure, participant or ministry scope does need
deliberate inclusion. A calendar/schedule change outside the admitted period
profile must not expand disclosure silently.

**Stop sharing activity summary** is independently available even if unsaved
additions are invalid. It stops only this module, does not save those additions,
and does not pause/delete targets or end independent modules. A failed/unknown
response says **“We couldn't confirm the change. Check sharing status.”** Recover
the original accepted operation; do not falsely announce success, re-create a
share, or require re-entry of private information. No mandatory reason or staff
handoff is justified for stopping permitted voluntary sharing.

### Jordan: useful read-only review across several contexts

Reuse D8's permission-filtered coaching portfolio. Show admitted person/ministry
and organization labels; open one context. A coach working with several people
can navigate those contexts without a combined donor roster, cross-person totals,
ranking, hidden-person count or default red performance badge.

In detail, show **Activity summary**, canonical units, exact admitted periods,
recorded values, applicable target quantities and qualified state/currentness.
A period control navigates only admitted periods. It is not an arbitrary date,
cohort or donor query. No **View calls**, donor link, source export, target editor,

Independent staff powers require an explicitly separate authorized context.

Text is the primary representation. A restrained progress bar may supplement a
fully qualified comparison; it is absent when it would reconstruct a hidden
count or imply complete attainment from incomplete evidence. Over-target counts
remain exact text. No sum/average of overlapping measures, automatic task, donor
notification, weekly nag or performance inference is added.

Use shared **base-maia**, Base UI and semantic Zinc tokens. Group labelled inputs,

## Exact required amendments

These clauses and the future proof obligations below were proposed at initial
review and are now explicitly ratified. They record decision requirements for
later authorized specification; they do not amend formal governing documents or
claim implementation.

### C01 — Optional, specific inclusion

The activity-summary module SHALL begin unshared. Current organization permission,
Maya's deliberate inclusion and the exact existing coach/subject episode are all
required. Target creation, organization enablement, invitation acceptance and
another module's permission SHALL NOT include activity summaries automatically.

### C02 — Reuse authoritative owners

P28 Goal Versions SHALL own target definition and applicable quantity/version.
Qualified P9/P27/P6/P17 sources SHALL own actual occurrences, participation and
outcomes. P3/P10/P12 SHALL own current disclosure. The coach view SHALL remain a
derived projection, with no target clone, writable counter, activity ledger or
parallel fine-permission engine.

### C03 — Exact semantic binding

Every selected summary SHALL bind exact tenant, coach context, typed
missionary/ministry scope, actual participant, target lineage, canonical measure,
period/calendar and qualified output profile. Null/empty scope SHALL NOT mean all.
Same labels/IDs after a material meaning change SHALL NOT transfer authority.

### C04 — Source-purpose aggregate admission

The qualified coaching purpose SHALL admit source facts before arithmetic and
output release. Aggregate-only access MAY exist without raw source-row/body
access. The implementation SHALL NOT grant source SELECT, impersonate Maya or
elevate a coach to staff/missionary to make a summary work. An unrestricted count
followed by client/server hiding of source details SHALL NOT qualify.

### C05 — A compatible comparison or honest unavailability

Count and target SHALL have compatible measure, actual participant, typed scope,
period/calendar, cohort meaning and complete qualified coverage. A permission-
reduced subset SHALL NOT be presented as full personal target attainment. The
initial profile SHALL show a qualified complete comparison or unavailable actual;
independently permitted target information MAY remain. No guessed denominator,
partial-progress score or copied personal count SHALL bridge missing authority.

### C06 — Minimal fields and joint inference safety

The registered projection SHALL include only qualified canonical measure,
permitted context/dates, recorded count, applicable target and necessary
state/currentness. Private target names, supporter identifiers, bodies, notes,
individual records/links and unauthorized geographic calendar labels SHALL be
excluded from coaching output and its preview. Maya's separately authorized own
selector MAY display her private label marked Not shared; that is not part of the
recipient projection. P12's applicable aggregate floor SHALL be qualified for
the protected contributor/cohort unit. Zero, overlapping periods/measures,
successive releases, complements and derived/chart/accessibility values SHALL be
assessed jointly. A withheld count SHALL NOT be reconstructed by other outputs.
No universal count threshold or anonymity guarantee is introduced.

### C07 — Explicit same-target continuation

Maya SHALL deliberately share the selected recurring target from the exact first
reviewed period onward while sharing remains active. Qualified future periods of
that same target SHALL enter the share. Already admitted periods SHALL remain
reviewable while current rights, duration and qualified source/retention bounds
allow. No period before the reviewed first period, new target or materially
changed scope SHALL be automatically included. A one-off target admits its exact
reviewed period and supplies no future recurring periods.

Access duration, admitted periods, current corrections and target revisions SHALL
remain separate. View filters SHALL only narrow the admitted set. No free-form
historical query, unlimited retention or financial-history inheritance follows.
The applicable bounded activity-history/volume profile SHALL be qualified, not
falsely described as an already settled organization limit. Whole reviewed
periods retain D23's meaning, including any deliberately reviewed dates earlier
than Save. Expired/unavailable retained data SHALL not be silently reconstructed
from another unauthorized source. No extra period-mode selector is needed for
this chosen release behavior. Invitation acceptance gates access; it SHALL NOT
rewrite the saved first-period boundary after a rollover. If the saved scope is
no longer admissible, require qualified scope review rather than substituting dates.

### C08 — Current values, stable meaning

Admitted periods SHALL reflect qualified corrected activity and the source-owned
target version actually applied to that period. Routine quantity changes within
the admitted definition SHALL NOT require redundant sharing approval. They SHALL
NOT backcast quantities into earlier periods or expose all private edit history.
New target/measure/participant/scope and calendar changes beyond the admitted
profile SHALL require deliberate inclusion before disclosure widens.

### C09 — Preview and one deliberate Save

Draft selection and preview SHALL NOT activate sharing. Preview SHALL use the
actual proposed recipient projection, additionally bounded by the previewer's
current authority, and identify recipient, organization, scope, exact dates and
duration. One authorized Save SHALL establish the intended scope; invitation-
pending, accepted and ambiguous outcomes SHALL remain distinct and truthful.

### C10 — Independent Stop and terminality

Stop activity sharing SHALL be independently usable despite invalid unsaved edits,
affect only this module's current grant and preserve source records, personal
targets and independent rights. Current expiry/restriction SHALL be enforced at
release without waiting for cleanup jobs. Old tabs, retries, policy re-enable,
end-date edits and backup restoration SHALL NOT revive ended sharing. Organization
Off SHALL fence affected current and pending activity scopes; On alone SHALL NOT
clear those fences. Fresh deliberate, source-authorized inclusion is required,
without re-inviting a still-valid coach or changing other modules. Genuine
temporary source unavailability remains distinct from an ended/fenced grant.

### C11 — Target lifecycle is not sharing lifecycle

Pause/resume/end SHALL retain D23 target semantics without silently stopping or
creating sharing, deleting facts, inventing a zero denominator or a missed-period
backlog. Admitted historical display remains separately authorized. A resumed or
changed period outside the actual period/profile grant SHALL require inclusion.

### C12 — Read-only, bounded coaching navigation

The coaching portfolio SHALL enumerate only admitted contexts, and detail/date
navigation SHALL only narrow existing authority. D24 SHALL add no raw drilldown,
export, delegation, coach target/source edits, cross-person totals, rankings,
automatic tasks/messages or compulsory reporting. Independent staff capabilities
SHALL require their explicit authorized context.

### C13 — Qualified zero and neutral states

Zero SHALL mean complete, current, permitted recorded evidence for the exact
comparison, including the applicable output floor. Unavailable, updating,
unshared, partial source, paused, expired and no-longer-authorized states SHALL
not collapse into zero or an attainment verdict. Hidden record/target existence
and exclusion counts SHALL not be disclosed in status, errors or timing.

### C14 — Coherent evidence and current release

Each result SHALL coherently bind applicable target-period revision, source
cohort/coverage, share/profile and current authorization. Relevant races SHALL
serialize or revalidate through the actual owner boundary before release. A
consistent old database snapshot, static field ceiling, supplied revision or cache
key SHALL NOT establish current authority. Changes that prevent qualification
SHALL yield a safe updating/unavailable result, not a mixed comparison.

### C15 — Trusted commands and structural database invariants

Tenant, actor, coach/subject attribution and authority SHALL derive from trusted
server context. Same-tenant typed keys/references, non-null bindings, valid
lifecycle/date states, immutable attribution and valid transitions SHALL prevent
retargeting. Counts/targets SHALL use exact nonnegative/positive integer semantics
as applicable, with overflow-safe qualified limits. Financial precision/currency
rules SHALL not be repurposed for activity. Routine removal SHALL not cascade
delete required lawful history.

### C16 — Effective grants and one fine-grained authority

Actual grants, all effective RLS policies, mutation old/new row behavior, views,
RPC/function ownership/execution, storage and privileged paths SHALL preserve
the same boundary. P12's canonical fine-grained decision SHALL sit behind coarse
tenant RLS; no second SQL policy engine or caller grant flags SHALL substitute.
Coach business mutations SHALL be denied through every route, with separately
qualified self-leave/preferences unaffected. An omitted `WITH CHECK` may inherit
`USING`; source inspection must evaluate effective behavior rather than syntax alone.

### C17 — Idempotent business effects and required evidence

Share changes SHALL atomically commit the authorized exact scope, expected head,
durable outcome and required audit/outbox evidence. Retrying the same accepted
intent SHALL recover that effect; changed meaning SHALL conflict. Recovery SHALL
recheck current rights and SHALL not restore narrowed/ended access. Notification
failure SHALL not undo a valid grant or falsely claim activation/stop failed.

### C18 — Cache, asynchronous and egress fencing

Cache identity SHALL include the exact tenant/active context/coach/subject/purpose
and qualified revision/generation bindings. Current release remains required.
Same-user tenant/hat/share changes, source restriction and revocation SHALL fence
late responses and invalidate affected protected state. Browser, realtime,
exports, errors, AI, logs and support paths SHALL not expose bypass projections.
No protected offline copy or unrestricted summary notification is introduced.

### C19 — Retention and audit are separate from access

Existing owners SHALL govern lawful retention, erasure/restriction, backups and
restoration. A retained fact or audit record SHALL not establish current coach
access. Audit SHALL preserve authorized actor/action/scope/revision/outcome and
required disclosure evidence without private bodies or raw target aliases in
ordinary telemetry. Restricted diagnostic access SHALL remain separate from the
coach's non-disclosing response.

### C20 — Bounded operations and qualified dependencies

Summary computation SHALL use qualified source predicates/indexes and bounded
period/portfolio workloads, not capped client pages or per-source fan-out. Publish
and test actual supported bounds before activation; reject unsupported requests
truthfully. A useful ordinary authorized summary SHALL be demonstrated before activation;
an always-unavailable shell SHALL NOT be presented as a delivered numeric feature.
Missing source authority, retention, producer or privacy qualification SHALL block that profile rather than substitute role elevation, inferred counts,
new infrastructure or manual database repair.

### C21 — Safe rollout and recovery

Classify the new output/profile and qualify existing source/P12 owners before
exposure. Policy widening SHALL follow ADR-0211's distinct-human change control;
routine sharing inside approved policy SHALL not acquire a second approval step.
Deploy compatible contracts/grants and negative tests before enabling the optional
module. No historic target auto-enrollment. Rollback/kill switches SHALL stop
release without deleting source/audit or restoring old grants; restored data
SHALL be reconciled before serving.

### C22 — Usable and accessible without pressure

Use existing shared Maia composition, one scope review/Save and independent Stop.
Canonical units, exact dates, recipient/context and durable outcome SHALL remain
clear with keyboard, touch, assistive technology, reflow, translation and weak
connections. No chart/color/hover/toast-only meaning, missing-log penalty,
leaderboard or inference of effort/relationship quality SHALL be introduced.

### C23 — Traceable proof and honest completion

Carry this decision and the C07 continuation contract into the glossary, later
authorized owner/ADR/OpenSpec/design/tasks/tickets, implementation tests and
release evidence without altering their authority levels. Record exact actual
producer/profile/bounds and executed results. Documentary checks and finite
counterexamples SHALL NOT be described as deployed source, RLS, UI or privacy
qualification. P01–P32 below are required future proofs, not tests already passed.
