# Q32 — one follow-up for a gift and commitment fulfillment

Research date: **October 18, 2026 UTC**. D31's corrected decision and required
amendments are explicitly ratified. **Q32 is unanswered.** This note identifies
one product choice; it does not merge source purposes, amend a formal owner
contract, create implementation tickets or establish runtime qualification.

## The single choice

Grace's newly received final gift completes her recorded fixed commitment to
Maya's ministry. That gift also independently qualifies for personal gift
follow-up—for example, it meets the organization's enabled D22 amount rule.
Both purposes are current, eligible, unhandled, permitted for Maya and compatible
with the same supporter/ministry and responsibility. This is an illustrative
design scenario, not reported ministry research or an implemented source producer.

**Should Maya handle the gift thank-you and commitment fulfillment together?**

- **A — One follow-up showing both purposes (recommended).** Maya reviews the
  gift and fulfilled commitment together and handles them once. Each purpose
  retains its own evidence and completion record.
- **B — Separate follow-ups.** Maya reviews and completes the gift thank-you and
  commitment-fulfillment follow-ups separately.

A reduces repeated handling when one personal action genuinely serves both
purposes. B keeps their working lifecycles visibly separate and avoids a new
joint-coverage contract, at the cost of two task dispositions for this case.
Neither choice requires two donor communications, treats a receipt as a personal
interaction, changes money or sends automatically. An actual interaction is
recorded once under its owner; exact task coverage must be deliberate and qualified.

## Why this remains a real product choice

- The [founder brief](phase28-founder-brief.md:165) and
  [program owner constraints](../../../prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md:74)
  already require native eligible gift follow-up and fulfillment celebration.
  Asking whether fulfillment should exist would reopen an adopted outcome.
- [D20-C01](phase28-d20-grouped-special-gift-follow-up-adversarial-review.md:177)
  explicitly keeps first-support, fulfillment, statutory receipt, care and other
  obligations distinct unless an owner qualifies shared coverage of an actual
  action. D20 selected grouping of compatible special gifts; it did not select
  combined gift-purpose and fulfillment-purpose handling.
- [D20-C02–C04](phase28-d20-grouped-special-gift-follow-up-adversarial-review.md:184)
  require compatible Tenant, exact typed subject, Legal Entity, Party, completion
  authority and responsibility; stable membership/source evidence stays distinct.
  “One current task” does not mean every open task for a supporter.
- [D20's shared-action boundary](phase28-d20-grouped-special-gift-follow-up-adversarial-review.md:483)
  permits one actual interaction to satisfy compatible obligations only with
  explicit qualified evidence. It preserves unrelated promises and receipts.
  Q32 asks whether to add the bounded product experience and source coverage for
  this specific combination, not whether a UI may hide two task rows.
- [ADR-0183](../../../adr/0183-source-owned-work-projects-into-one-shared-tasks-hub.md:622)
  distinguishes an independent human deliverable from source-owned business state.
  A completed thank-you/celebration cannot make the commitment financially fulfilled.
- [P16](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-16-pledges-recurring-commitments.md:1037)
  already owns fixed-pledge fulfillment meaning and separate recurring lifecycle.
  [Its conserved fold](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-16-pledges-recurring-commitments.md:1378)
  distinguishes fulfilled, ended/released and still expected amounts. Q32 supplies
  no replacement arithmetic, new financial authority or definition of fulfillment.

Independent financial and governance reviewers both identified this as an open
human-work-unit choice. Its engineering prerequisites are known owner obligations,
not questions the founder must solve.

**No reviewed source establishes the proposed joint-coverage semantics.** The

## Journey and counterexamples checked before asking

The ordinary donor journey is unaffected. Maya opens her existing task list.
Under A, the compatible follow-up could show **Thank Grace** with two clearly
identified purposes: **Gift follow-up** and **Commitment fulfilled**. This is
illustrative copy, subject to current identity/anonymity and source disclosure;
it does not select a final UI component, compulsory checkbox or extra modal.
Both underlying facts must be explainable without exposing unrelated gifts.

The distinguishing action is deliberate coverage of the reviewed purposes in
one ordinary handling flow. It is not merely expanding/collapsing two tasks or
automatically closing every linked obligation. If A is chosen, its answer review
must resolve source-purpose identity, shared task admission, reviewed coverage,
partial handling, late arrival, correction, races and recovery under D20/P9.

Counterexamples and inherited boundaries:

- A gift that pays one installment but not the complete applicable fixed
  commitment does not supply the fulfillment purpose. Unknown source is not zero.
- Ending/releasing a promise, reducing terms, reaching the last scheduled date
  or finishing an automatic recurring schedule is not the live fulfillment
  scenario in this question. No new financial milestone is inferred.
- The ordinary gift reason independently qualifies; gift fulfillment does not
  retroactively invent an additional-support, one-time-need or above-commitment
  reason. D19/D22/D29/D30/D31 keep their predicates.
- Different Parties, ministry/project scopes, Legal Entities, disclosure rules,
  responsibilities or completion authorities cannot be combined merely because
  a supporter name or date is shared.
- If one purpose was already handled, accepting the other does not reopen it or
  claim that the earlier action also covered an unseen later purpose.
- A later-arriving purpose cannot be silently absorbed into a reviewed set.
  Source lag does not require a second donor message; precise coverage is a
  source/task responsibility to resolve in answer review.
- A correction that invalidates fulfillment need not invalidate a genuinely
  received gift or completed human interaction. Preserve each purpose's evidence
  and independent work; do not erase or silently replay history.
- Maya can handle only the work she actually completed. A combined task cannot
  force a false claim about an unfinished purpose or hide residual work.
- A statutory receipt, financial correction, staff-only exception, first-support
  undertaking or unrelated promised action is not automatically included.
- Completing a task does not prove sending, conversation, consent, gift receipt
  or financial fulfillment. Existing communication and interaction owners remain.

## Frontier selection and status

Generic missed-recurring-payment automation was considered and rejected as the
next question. [P16 D8](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-16-pledges-recurring-commitments.md:887)
already makes a qualified terminal-miss notice informational and in-product only,
with no default donor-outreach task; its exact catalog activation gate remains.
[P16 G2–G3](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-16-pledges-recurring-commitments.md:1055)
owns source-qualified actions, freshness, optional enrollment and bounded policy.
Asking an unqualified “task for every miss?” would blur or reopen those decisions.

Q32 instead follows the explicit D20 reservation. It asks one choice whose
prerequisites are settled, with no dependency on a guessed financial classifier.
**No answer or cross-purpose permission has been selected.** Source qualification
and implementation remain unproved. No prior model/database probe was rerun,
and no Q33 is posed.
