# D32 — comparable products and clear combined follow-up

Research checked **October 18, 2026 UTC**. The founder selected combined gift
and fixed-commitment-fulfillment follow-up, with organization control to change
or turn off the combination. This evidence note does not ratify additional
engineering policy, establish current implementation or choose how a setting
change affects already accepted work. Only this file is authored by this review.

## What the evidence establishes

The closest documented patterns support contextual tasks, avoiding duplicate
tasks, distinct financial milestones and explicit acknowledgment state. **None
of the reviewed sources documents Core's proposed joint coverage of an eligible
gift-purpose and an independently qualified fixed-fulfillment purpose.** A
related-record link is context, not permission or proof of completion coverage.
Combining these two purposes is the founder's product choice; its exact meaning
must come from Core's owners.

Public product help was read, not authenticated product UI. No account, donor,
payment, message or task was created. Relative update labels below are preserved
as displayed; copyright and crawler dates are not treated as publication dates.
The TntConnect video was not watched. There is no measured usability, retention,
time-saving or fundraising result in this research.

## Smallest complete experience — recommendations

### Organization setting

Recommend one setting within the existing relevant organization settings:

**Gift and commitment follow-up**

- **Combine when both apply** — Show compatible gift and commitment-fulfillment
  follow-up together.
- **Keep separate** — Show and handle each follow-up separately.

Always-visible help: **“This changes how follow-ups are grouped. Both kinds of
follow-up remain available.”** The wording refers to eligible native work, not
a promise that every gift or commitment creates a task. Reuse the established
settings save pattern; no extra wizard or routine confirmation is justified by
these sources. A label such as “Turn off follow-up” would misstate the selected
control. If the existing switch pattern is preferable, label the switch
**“Combine gift and commitment follow-up”**, with the same help.

This is a recommendation for clear behavior-level customization. The evidence
does not justify amount rules, time windows, per-supporter overrides, editable
financial criteria or a general policy builder. It also does not determine the
initial stored default or authorize a new policy scope hierarchy.

The owner synthesis must state the effect on already combined, reviewed,
pending and handled work before finalizing saved-state help. Do not promise
“Existing follow-ups are unchanged” or “All current follow-ups are split” until
that behavior is chosen and qualified. Show the saved value only after durable
acceptance; an uncertain save is not proof the organization changed its setting.

### Missionary task

Recommend the existing **Follow up with Grace** row/detail, subject to current
identity and source disclosure. In the detail, make these two purposes visible:

- **Gift follow-up** — the exact permitted gift context and its qualifying reason.
- **Commitment fulfilled** — the exact permitted fixed commitment and its qualified
  fulfillment context.

The received gift amount and commitment total are different facts. Do not add
them into one apparent gift or display “2 gifts” when there is one gift and one
milestone. Where useful, a short sentence can connect them: **“This gift also
completed the recorded commitment.”** Use that sentence only when the financial
owner actually proves the relationship; shared supporter identity is insufficient.

Both included purposes and the action's scope must be apparent before completion.
An optional **Why this follow-up?** disclosure can explain qualified source facts,
but must not hide the second purpose, altered coverage or an unavailable state.
No audit ledger, mandatory note, source-calculation exercise or extra confirmation
is needed for the ordinary compatible pair.

Recommend **Finish both follow-ups** for that exact two-purpose case. The accepted
result can say **Both follow-ups finished**. It must not say a message was sent,
Grace was contacted or the commitment was financially completed by the click.
Actual contact/sending/logging remains deliberate under its existing owner.
In a larger D20 group, use the exact declared gift-and-milestone scope rather than
stretching “both” or counting heterogeneous purposes as gifts.

If Maya handled only one purpose, provide a secondary **Choose what to finish**
path through existing reviewed handling. Expose exact selectable purposes there;
do not force per-purpose checkboxes on every ordinary two-purpose visit. This
is a design recommendation, not a new task module or a claim that a visual
checkbox establishes source authority. Residual work remains truthful and usable.

### Change and recovery

The interface must distinguish a new purpose arriving, a reviewed source fact
changing, a purpose becoming unavailable and a purpose being conclusively
withdrawn. A late arrival cannot silently enlarge the accepted set. A correction
to fulfillment need not erase a valid gift-purpose or genuine human history.
An already handled purpose must not be reopened merely to create a tidy pair.

Use plain saved/pending/unavailable text and preserve drafts. After a lost
completion response, recover the existing result; do not imply the user should
contact the supporter again. After permission changes, reveal only currently
permitted results. These requirements come from Core ownership and current
authorization, not from the comparable products.

Keep mobile content stacked, essential scope readable without hover, keyboard
focus stable and status messages concise. Source facts unavailable to Maya must
not enter the browser under a collapsed disclosure. Do not use a combined row
as an excuse to expose a staff-only commitment or hidden source-purpose count.

## Strongest alternative and remaining evidence limits

Separate tasks remain a coherent organization choice: they make independent
purposes obvious and avoid a new joint-coverage path, while requiring separate
dispositions in this compatible case. Separation never requires two donor
messages. The selected combination can reduce repeated handling, but the research
does not quantify that benefit or prove that every organization prefers it.

Release evidence must still establish the actual source relationship, compatibility,
distinct handling coverage, setting-transition behavior, privacy and accessible
interaction. A concise prototype should test the ordinary pair, only-one-handled,
new arrival, fulfillment correction and organization Off cases. No interviews,
prototype sessions, usability success rate, actual SQL/provider proof or runtime
qualification were performed for this note. No prior evidence, model, test,
application, formal specification or external system was changed.
