## Final consistency disposition

Financial review accepted the whole-promise/current-history boundary after the
explicit restoration, amendment and identity clarifications. Governance accepted
the policy and preservation contract after lost-compatibility detachment and
empty gift-slot corrections. Security's four final amendments are included:
policy+intent atomicity, effective kill-switch/stale-worker fencing, atomic task
state with coverage and actual-time authority after waits. UX found no remaining
must-fix contradiction in the specified journey.

No independent review converts selected A into ratification of new amendments.
The final disposition remains **Accept with required amendments**. Organization-wide scope is now explicitly selected. Current unfinished-work
recomposition, activation defaults and once-per-continuing-pledge purpose remain
visible proposed amendments, not hidden engineering assumptions. The review is complete within the founder's research boundary;
formal owner integration and production qualification remain explicitly required
at their authorized phases.
