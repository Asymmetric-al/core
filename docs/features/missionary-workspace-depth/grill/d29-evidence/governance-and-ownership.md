# D29 governance and source ownership evidence

Research date: **October 17, 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. This is a focused repository
review for the selected Q29 A, not a formal owner amendment, runtime test,
ratification of the ensuing amendments, or activation evidence. The scope is
donor-stated additional support outside a specific one-time need or appeal.
Earlier source captures remain unchanged. No application, schema, provider,
formal specification, ticket or live-data operation was performed.

## Conclusion and selected actor clarification

**Accept with required amendments.** There is a legitimate additional-purpose
criterion here, separate from first support, amount, fulfillment and a computed
above-commitment result. The smallest permanent solution adds one qualified
purpose assertion and its admission command to the existing P28 stewardship
owner, with exact source references and the existing P9 task projection. It
does not need another financial classification, campaign, Request, task engine,
generic evidence platform or approval workflow.

The founder subsequently selected **“Authorized staff and missionaries when the
organization permits it (recommended).”** That actor-class choice is now settled.
It does not ratify every proposed constraint below or establish a working
permission. The repository does not presently grant a generic confirmation
power. The exact bounded P12 action, source scope and qualification still need
the proposed owner amendment and implementation proof; ordinary gift visibility,
task management, authorship, relationship, assignment and staff title remain
insufficient. Exact key names, storage layout and adapters are engineering
decisions, not questions for the founder.

“Confirmed” should mean an attributable, permitted source assertion of what
the donor actually communicated. It must not imply independent verification of
the donor's intentions, automatic trust in imported text, or a second approver
for every gift. [D21 C15](../phase28-d21-explicit-gift-follow-up-adversarial-review.md:287)
already rejects approval per gift. [ADR0211](../../../../adr/0211-distinct-human-approval-for-projection-widening.md:16)
governs actual projection-policy widening, not ordinary qualified fact recording.

## Recommended smallest source contract

These are candidate requirements for the parent review, not already ratified
capabilities or selected physical table names.

**G01 — Exact meaning.** A qualifying assertion SHALL state that the identified
donor communicated that the exact received gift, or identified relevant portion,
is additional support. It SHALL NOT mean merely one-time payment, unpledged,
unmatched, large, above prior average, associated with a campaign, or manually
called special. No named need or appeal is required for this criterion. A
known-absent commitment is not a prerequisite: the affirmative purpose is
independent of a mathematical comparison. Current or conflicting actual source
facts remain material; a cached incomplete commitment lookup is never evidence.
When the exact qualified live-source, donor-purpose, current eligibility and
scope conditions hold, P28 SHALL admit the native reason through the durable
D20 path. It is not merely an optional workflow or an invitation to add a manual
task.

**G02 — Separate authorities.** P28 SHALL own the purpose assertion and its current
qualification. P13 SHALL own actual received money, exact contribution/line
coverage, correction and dating; P14 SHALL own recognized supporter and permitted
follow-up route; P16 SHALL retain commitment and application truth. A P9/P27
interaction or existing permitted donor instruction may provide exact evidence
only through a qualified purpose-specific adapter. No source-name equivalence,
memo parser, generic import flag or read-model field establishes that adapter.
Posting, fulfillment and receipt processing SHALL not wait for a stewardship
purpose assertion.

**G03 — Minimal recorded facts.** The source SHALL retain the exact Tenant, Legal
Entity, typed ministry/project, canonical contribution and qualified relevant
coverage, attributed donor/speaker and represented capacity where applicable,
the precise additional-purpose assertion, actual statement time with honest
precision, source/provenance reference when one exists, trusted recording actor
and acceptance time, current revision/disposition, and stable gift-purpose
identity. It SHALL preserve unknown values rather than fabricate time, document,
conversation, donor identity or authority. Recording an honestly attested
conversation need not require an attachment, copied quotation, email account
connection, artificial Request or additional interaction log.

**G04 — Deliberate scoped assertion.** An explicitly registered purpose-recording
capability SHALL permit only the exact action within the actor's current Tenant,
subject and source scope. Gift read, ordinary task create/update, shared-context
authorship and role labels SHALL NOT substitute. Under the selected actor-class
choice, a qualified missionary or staff actor may record and confirm the
permitted donor statement when the organization grants the exact action. The
proposed ordinary path uses one deliberate command; no universal
staff-only restriction or second-person review is implied. The actor SHALL NOT
grant themself missing source-body, donor, Legal Entity or financial authority.
Who said it and who recorded it SHALL remain separate. An organization contact,
household member or DAF advisor is not automatically the legal donor or entitled
speaker for every relevant financial fact.

**G05 — Existing source references, not duplicated private evidence.** The
assertion SHALL be a separately authorized minimal operational fact. It SHALL
not quote, copy, reclassify, summarize or infer protected private message/care/CRM
content into a wider audience. If a lawful source cannot supply even the minimum
permitted purpose fact, D29 admission is unavailable for that path. Evidence
links SHALL independently authorize access; seeing “Additional support confirmed”
does not grant the underlying conversation. Newly authored eligible facts may
reuse an existing source where its exact owner contract fits, but a managed
partnership, appeal or shared interaction is not compulsory preparation.
An authorized person may record the minimum eligible fact from a donor statement
they directly received under the new purpose contract; the message channel alone
does not disqualify it. This permits the independently authorized fact, not a
general right to disclose the original message or to extract protected history.

**G06 — Truthful qualification and conflicts.** The command SHALL validate exact
current donor attribution, contribution and relevant coverage. Unknown,
contradictory, reversed or invalid source facts SHALL not become confirmed by
last-write-wins or an operator's generic approval. A material conflict requires
the exact owning correction path. Mere existence of a commitment or a lawful
application is not automatically a conflict: purpose and fulfillment have
different meanings. A D29 assertion SHALL never rewrite an application to make
the assertion appear consistent. An ordinary catch-up payment SHALL not qualify
merely because it is large or an operator wants a thank-you task.

**G07 — One accepted occurrence, recoverable effects.** Accepted assertion state,
required history, semantic command receipt and durable purpose-admission intent
SHALL commit together under the owning source transaction. Task admission SHALL
use the existing D20 current-task/member resolver and permanent effect identity.
Different request IDs, actors, reason revisions or future workflows SHALL not
create multiple effects for one covered gift-purpose. No external message send
or financial application occurs in this transaction.

**G08 — Delayed evidence and activation.** First valid purpose confirmation may
arrive after posting for an otherwise eligible originally-live, uncovered gift.
It SHALL preserve actual gift date, statement time and later acceptance time;
it SHALL not pretend a newly accepted purpose is new money. Originally-live
provenance and an explicit source activation boundary SHALL distinguish this
case from history-only intake/backfill or an old-gift reclassification sweep.
Historical imports, general criterion changes and duplicate affirmation SHALL
not resurrect handled or historical work. The source must not require the
purpose to have been known at posting time merely for implementation convenience.
This is the narrow proposed clarification of
[D20 C05/C09/C11](../phase28-d20-grouped-special-gift-follow-up-adversarial-review.md:216):
for D29 only, first qualified purpose affirmation for a gift already admitted to
the recorded prospective live cohort is delayed qualification of that same
unconsumed gift-purpose, not historical-only reclassification. Trusted original
source admission/provenance establishes cohort membership; a backdated gift
date or later confirmation/import timestamp cannot create it. This makes the
donor-explains-later journey testable without admitting a retrospective sweep.

**G09 — Correction and disappearance.** Correct/withdraw SHALL append an
attributable successor/disposition against the expected current assertion and
re-prove current authority. It SHALL update or withdraw only the affected
automatic reason/member, leaving independent reasons, promises, source financial
facts and completed human history intact. Source correction can restore the
same eligible, originally-live, still-unconsumed purpose through the existing
owner contract; changing a revision or reason code is not a new gift. A
recorder's later departure does not itself prove their accepted statement false;
new commands and readers use their own current authority. Where all remaining
qualification is lost, no stale cached reason may authorize new work.

**G10 — Permission and privacy throughout.** All commands and reads SHALL preserve
the same exact actor, tenant, source, purpose and field limits through jobs,
imports, direct database APIs, RPCs, replay, notification, task/group counts,
search, exports, coaching and AI. Required trusted identities and tenant-aware
references SHALL be non-null. Immutable source/scope/actor fields SHALL not be
retargetable through ordinary updates, even when both proposed targets are
separately writable. Generic task/metadata updates SHALL not mint qualification,
withdraw or forge its lineage. Use the one P12 resolver and database backstops;
do not build a competing permission engine in RLS.

**G11 — Retention with source ownership.** Purpose history, protected evidence
body, task coverage and technical logs SHALL retain separate owners and purposes.
Append-only history does not imply indefinite body retention. A qualified
minimal purpose fact can remain interpretable after a source body expires only
where its own source contract permits that independent fact to survive. Neither
body expiry nor recorder departure automatically rewrites accepted history;
current restriction, correction and lawful disposal still control future use.
No donor prose belongs in task titles, command telemetry or immutable financial
rows. Restore SHALL reapply restrictions/disposal and consumed effect identities
before readers or workers resume. ADR0031's email-copy periods SHALL not be
borrowed for this new source class.

**G12 — Focused UX and independence.** Explain “Additional support confirmed” as
the specific factual task reason, not as a donor score or an economic surplus.
Offer existing compatible Open follow-up or ordinary Add follow-up under current
rights. No purpose confirmation is required simply to create a manual task.
Show the actual accepted result and recover a lost response without retrying a
new business effect. A missing source or confirmation right SHALL not block
independently authorized ordinary work, money processing, first-support tasks
or an eligible D22 amount reason. No new top-level dashboard, universal checkbox,
rule builder, per-gift mandatory approval or compulsory reporting follows.

## Required ownership/proof handoff

Later authorized formalization should amend the exact P28 purpose contract and
its glossary; P12's narrow create/read/correct/restrict/admission powers and
projection floor; the selected P9/source evidence adapter if reused; P13/P14
read-only qualification/correction references; and P9 task member/effect linkage.
P16 requires no new application power. P27 needs no artificial Request or appeal
and retains its established shared-history boundary. Reconcile durable OpenSpec
and active change projections through their canonical owners; do not patch
generated artifacts independently. Keep native work independent of Workflow
Studio and leave unrelated E7 attribution/C-01 financial-correction questions
with their owners.

Minimum proof cases: authorized direct donor statement produces one eligible
task reason; the same small gift with no assertion does not; explicit purpose
with an existing commitment does not require a made-up absence; ordinary
catch-up/advance does not qualify by amount; protected/raw/imported text cannot
bypass confirmation; wrong speaker/tenant/entity/portion is rejected; no posted
financial field or P16 application changes; two concurrent confirmations and
lost-response retries preserve one effect; late originally-live confirmation
works while history-only acquisition does not; correction racing task handling
preserves the exact reviewed-set outcome; evidence access loss cannot leak a
body; source retention/restore preserves both disposal and consumed coverage;
independently permitted manual/first/amount work remains functional during a
D29-source outage. Qualified real-role and source-command tests are necessary;
reasoned cases or code-search absence are not their substitute.
