# D29 — explicitly additional support: primary research and UX

Research date: **October 17, 2026 UTC**. The founder selected Q29 A: explicitly
confirmed additional support may qualify for native personal follow-up without
a specific one-time need. The founder subsequently selected **authorized staff
and missionaries when the organization permits it** as the eligible confirming
actor classes. This selects the permission model, not blanket role access or a
live grant. This note supports the answer review; its remaining detailed
evidence, permission implementation and presentation amendments await explicit
ratification. No source rules, functionality or measured user findings are
claimed implemented. It does not settle every other special-gift criterion
reserved by D21.

## Smallest complete product

**Recommendation:** add one narrowly defined, source-owned additional-support
purpose path and its safe task reason. It is a factual attestation bound to an
exact received gift and eligible allocation, not a new amount category, general
gift editor, custom classifier or preference to generate arbitrary tasks.

Do not ask the donor to complete a new checkout question, create an appeal just
to qualify a gift, re-enter the donation, or paste a message into a task. No new
phone number, address, split-name form or financial date editor is needed.
Ordinary whole-gift confirmation references the authoritative amount without
re-entry. Where multiple eligible allocations exist, select the already-owned
relevant scope. A donor may also explicitly identify a positive additional
portion within that scope; offer an optional **Part of this gift** amount in the
fixed source currency for that case. This records exact purpose evidence, not a
new financial split, balance or ledger entry. Do not classify the whole gift
from evidence about one part or calculate the portion from a commitment or fees.
An explicit donor instruction such as **half of this gift** may resolve to the
exact source-currency amount only when exactly representable at the qualified
precision. Show that resolved amount for review. Do not round an unrepresentable
portion or introduce a percentage calculator or additional percentage field.

The strongest simpler alternative is **Add follow-up** using the ordinary task
form. It works when Maya wants personal work despite unknown or ineligible
automatic facts and remains necessary. It cannot replace the selected native
producer: doing so loses source corrections, exact purpose coverage and reliable
admission. The strongest narrower automatic alternative is Q29 B, a qualified
specific one-time need only; it excludes the selected general additional case
and still requires trustworthy attribution. Neither alternative is inherently
safer merely because a staff label or appeal name exists.

## Permission and confirmation recommendation

The source needs a current, explicitly scoped **record/confirm additional-support
purpose** capability distinct from gift mutation, task completion and private
message readership. Existing tenant role labels must not imply it. One suitably
authorized person can record and confirm the qualified statement in one deliberate
command; “confirmed” need not mean a second person approves it.

**Selected actor model:** authorized staff and missionaries may perform the
action when the organization permits it through the qualified P12 model for the
exact ministry/source scope, with the same evidence requirements and no financial
mutation. This supports the person who actually heard the donor without requiring
routine relaying or copying private messages. Staff or missionary status alone
never grants the action. A missionary without that permission retains ordinary
Add follow-up where independently authorized; no new approval inbox or compulsory
request workflow is introduced. The detailed capability mapping and source
implementation still require qualification, and the remaining amendments remain
unratified.

Do not confuse provenance with truth. Server-derived actor/time/source references
and a deliberate attestation are accountable evidence; they do not prove that a
person could not misremember or misstate a conversation. The owning contract must
state which donor-stated evidence is admissible, including an appropriately
attested verbal statement if that route is accepted. A generic unchecked/checked
“special” field, imported label or machine-parsed memo remains insufficient.

## Complete source-recorder journey

1. **Open the permitted exact gift.** From the ordinary received-gift detail,
   show donor/follow-up Party meaning, gift date, native currency and eligible
   ministry context. Clarify Legal Entity only where the permitted context needs
   disambiguation. Offer **Record additional support** only to someone
   with current source authority. Do not put a classification prompt on every
   gift or reveal restricted source evidence through a disabled form.
2. **Review a small form in context.** The gift and existing eligible allocation
   are read-only source context. Show the statement being confirmed:
   **The donor identified this gift or selected portion as additional support.**
   Capture the minimum
   qualified evidence route/reference the source needs. Link to an existing
   allowed evidence record when available; do not require an upload, new note,
   copied quotation or repeated donor identity. Actor and recorded time are
   server-derived, never editable form fields. A real statement's occurrence
   date is distinct from its recording date; do not guess missing dates or
   backdate financial receipt to make it fit. If the donor expressly named only
   a portion, reveal the optional amount field and show the whole eligible amount
   alongside it. Validate positivity, native currency precision and the source
   upper bound without rounding ambiguous values. An explicit fractional
   instruction may supply the reviewed amount only if its exact value is
   representable at that precision; no percentage-entry UI is required. An
   uncertain portion remains unconfirmed; the user must not invent an amount
   to enable Save.
3. **Make the effect clear once.** A short helper explains **This records the
   donor's stated purpose for personal follow-up. It does not change how the gift
   is applied or send a message.** The source command checks exact scope and
   qualified evidence before accepting. There is no preselected special flag,
   automatic inference, second approver by default or confirmation modal after
   this already reviewed save.
4. **Save deliberately.** Use **Record additional support** with a quiet Cancel action.
   Preserve entered source-safe form data on validation or network failure.
   Save is accepted only under current authority and current gift/evidence
   state. If the relevant source changed, refresh and review the actual change;
   do not silently substitute another gift or succeed with a partial scope.
5. **Show the actual result.** **Purpose recorded** is justified by source
   acceptance. It does not imply pending task work for a history-only assertion
   or already-handled gift. **Follow-up added** is justified only when the admitted
   task result is known. Show a calm **Follow-up is being prepared** only when the
   accepted result identifies an admitted, still-pending projection; do not
   offer a competing Create task button. Existing compatible authorized work
   can use **Open follow-up**. Unknown network outcome uses **Checking whether
   the purpose was recorded…**, then recovers the original operation.
6. **Correct at the source.** A permitted **Correct purpose** action uses the
   source's history-preserving correction path and short explanation of its
   actual effect. An unsupported reason disappears from pending native coverage
   through that owner; other qualified reasons and real human work remain.
   Neither correction nor Undo retracts a real message or deletes a gift.

If the donor statement arrived before processed money exists, do not create a
fictional received gift or qualify an unmatched future payment. Any already
supported promise/activity source keeps its own meaning. Once the exact gift
exists, the source can establish the match under its qualified rules. D29 does
not need a separate unmatched-purpose queue or donor-proof attachment system.

## Maya's ordinary task journey

Keep one relationship-oriented row in the existing Tasks Hub. Use a plain reason
instead of an unexplained Auto badge, trophy, score, unusually-generous label,
red urgency or a demand to write a thank-you report. The following is illustrative
copy; dates/amounts are display examples, not eligibility thresholds.

```text
Follow up with Grace
Maya's ministry · Additional support confirmed

Gifts covered by this follow-up
16 Oct 2026   USD 40.00   Additional support confirmed   View gift
17 Oct 2026   USD 25.00   Additional support confirmed   View gift

[Finish follow-up for 2 gifts]   [Not needed]
Record an interaction…

Finishing handles these 2 gifts. It does not send a message.
```

The exact set remains readable and stable while Maya acts. Ordinary groups need
no per-gift selection chore. She can contact Grace outside Asym, optionally log
the actual interaction, or deliberately use the existing permitted composer.
Additional-support qualification confers no communication consent or recipient
address and creates no automatic send, receipt or newsletter subscription.

If one gift has multiple valid reasons, show its factual reasons without creating
duplicate work or implying separate receipts. If unrelated eligible work arrives
mid-review, keep the reviewed set and action label stable. After handling two,
show **Follow-up finished for 2 gifts. 1 new gift remains.** Keep the remaining
work in the same current task under D20. A material change to selected source
facts requires review; an unrelated arrival must not gratuitously reject valid
work. Completed coverage never expands retroactively.

Source detail is optional and independently authorized. “View gift” is not a
permission bypass. A confirmed-purpose reason does not include donor message
text, internal notes, private location, evidence snippets or identity hidden by
the originating domain. Existing coach sharing does not automatically include
this reason, its gift list or evidence; any selected coaching summary remains
subject to its own approved-description and read-only contract.

## Visual, mobile and international behavior

Use the shared task row/detail pattern and a compact source form, composed from
existing Base UI primitives. Maia spacing, restrained separators, typography and
semantic tokens supply hierarchy: person/work first, ministry and reason next,
gift detail beneath. Avoid a second navigation destination, universal workflow
builder, novel status system or attention dashboard. Keep correction/history
details behind a clearly labeled permitted disclosure instead of crowding the
daily queue with IDs and source revisions.

On phones, stack gift rows so date, currency/amount, reason and action remain
readable without horizontal scrolling. Preserve the reviewed count near the
primary action even with a soft keyboard or long translated labels. No action
depends on hover, drag-and-drop or a precision icon. Follow the Core touch-target
guidance without shrinking visible focus or overlapping hit regions.

Use native labels and established Base UI focus behavior, semantic lists/tables
where appropriate, field-specific errors and an error summary when needed.
Announce whole outcome sentences politely. Keep successful handling and correction
outcomes in the detail/history, not solely a toast. New arrivals must not move
focus or reorder what Maya is reviewing. Test keyboard, screen reader, 320 CSS
pixels, zoom, high text scaling, RTL, long names and reduced motion.

Existing international supporter names are displayed as stored by their owner;
do not demand first/last name or postal address. Financial gift dates retain
their source meaning; source acceptance and human action timestamps use the
existing localized date/time presentation with a clear zone where material.
Display currency codes where symbols are ambiguous; never silently convert or
sum mixed currencies. Whole-gift confirmation uses the existing authoritative
amount. Only an explicitly donor-stated partial-purpose amount needs input;
show it as a portion of the eligible source amount, never as a financial edit.
The locale parser must reject ambiguous separators and unsupported precision
rather than silently coerce or round the assertion.

Low bandwidth must preserve the user's task/form context and distinguish saving,
accepted, projection pending and unknown. Do not declare offline edits recorded,
persist protected source bodies in a new browser store, or add a new offline
sync subsystem for this feature.

## Proof and remaining qualification

Before activation, representative staff and missionaries must be able to explain
which gift/portion is being confirmed, what evidence is required, why the task
exists, which gifts their action covers, and why finishing is not sending. Test
small explicitly additional gifts, ordinary catch-up, no commitment, same-date
same-amount gifts, split allocations, recognition routes, delayed receipt,
historical-only imports, source correction, unauthorized source evidence,
permission loss, two tabs, a third gift arriving, and an unknown save response.
No arbitrary successful-task count or aesthetic rating substitutes for these
outcomes. A materially wrong explanation in release testing blocks release of
the affected flow; the Missionary Workspace product/UX owner revises the copy or
interaction and repeats the relevant test without collecting private bodies.

The required native additional-purpose path must be implemented and qualified,
not indefinitely replaced by manual work. The exact admitted evidence routes,
record/confirm capability policy, source correction and retention contracts are
owner/design obligations. The founder selected staff and missionaries when the
organization permits; that selection must not be reported as a live grant or
ratification of all remaining detailed amendments.
No new infrastructure dependency, source table, formal spec, ticket, notification
subscription or runtime change is created by this research note.
