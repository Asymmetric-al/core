## Finding and smallest complete interpretation

Accept A with required amendments. Add one bounded P28 stewardship-purpose
source contract to the already selected D20 special-gift admission path. It
records an attributable donor-origin statement about an exact received gift or
portion. It neither edits financial facts nor infers intent from a payment
frequency, unmatched money, historical pattern, campaign label, note keyword,
recognition role or caller-supplied `special` flag.

An explicitly additional gift may also fulfill a separately made commitment.
P16 application alone neither proves nor disproves additional purpose. A
current authoritative instruction that the **same portion** is ordinary support,
catch-up or advance support can contradict the proposed assertion and requires
source resolution; P28 cannot resolve that by editing a financial application.

For mixed gifts, a source reference alone is sufficient only when it identifies
the whole stated purpose scope. A gift of 125 units to one ministry containing
25 units explicitly stated as additional needs an exact donor-stated portion.
The narrow complete solution permits that same-currency minor-unit annotation
as purpose evidence. An explicit expression such as “half this gift” may resolve
to an exact source-qualified amount without rounding; the donor need not have
spoken a literal number. Retain the original statement and its bound source
revision separately from current surviving eligible support. It is not a second
financial allocation, a new money total, an independently maintained remaining
balance or a P28 refund calculator.

## Precise source and lifecycle recommendations

The source record needs exact identities and provenance, not a generic
classification system. Its conceptual minimum is the source contribution and
eligible allocation/recognized-support scope; the typed ministry/project subject,
Tenant and Legal Entity; the attributable speaker and qualified capacity; the
original statement and occurrence precision/provenance; the confirming actor and
current authority; the whole scope or expressly quantified same-currency portion;
current acceptance/supersession and source revisions; and permanent semantic
purpose identity. This is a contract description, not a table/schema proposal.

The temporal recommendation is explicit new review judgment: D29's automatic
cohort consists of source-proven originally-live eligible received-support
occurrences whose durable source-live boundary belongs to the prospective D29
activation cohort. Every preactivation receipt is excluded from new D29
automatic admission, including receipts originally live under an older system.
It admits later genuine confirmation of those qualified cohort receipts,
not retrospective relabelling of the historical estate. There is no
arbitrary freshness duration in this note. Source proof, retention/currentness
and accepted coverage still limit eligibility. The source must resolve the
activation/receipt race from durable owner ordering, not worker arrival time.

## Material concerns and exact proposed clauses

### F01 — A stewardship label could become false financial authority

- **What:** A mutable posted `special` flag, generic imported tag or P28 purpose
  classification could change legal restrictions, money or fulfillment.
- **Why:** The three meanings—additional stewardship purpose, P13 accepted legal
  purpose, and P16 application instruction—have different owners and proof.
- **Severity:** Critical for money/source integrity.
- **Likelihood:** High unless the source boundary is explicit; the same word
  “purpose” is already used by the financial snapshot.
- **Evidence:** S02–S04, S09; P13:793–815 and P16:1007–1015 are exact boundaries.
- **Answer impact:** A remains valid; it requires a new bounded operational
  purpose contract rather than an extension of posted financial columns.
- **Permanent fix:** P28 operational evidence references exact financial owners;
  explicit qualification separates the meanings even if evidence is reused.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Additional-support purpose SHALL be a P28-owned
  stewardship fact over exact source identities. It SHALL NOT alter posted
  financial rows, legal accepted-purpose authority, designation, recognition,
  commitment application or payment instruction. Any reuse of evidence for those
  other purposes SHALL be independently accepted by the responsible owner.”
- **Proof:** Attempt purpose record/correction through ordinary and privileged
  writers; posted columns and financial/fulfillment results remain unchanged.

### F02 — Recorder, speaker and financial representative could be conflated

- **What:** Staff/missionary note permission or a donor relationship might be
  treated as authority to confirm additional purpose for another Party.
- **Why:** A statement author, platform recorder and authorized representative
  are distinct; a household, assistant, treasurer or DAF advisor does not acquire
  all financial powers through this feature.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High with a generic “Mark special” action.
- **Evidence:** S01, S10; Q29 expressly reserves actor qualification, P16:1164–1188
  separates the roles and :1209 restricts its own financial mutation surface.
- **Answer impact:** The subsequent founder answer selects authorized staff and
  missionaries when the organization permits the action. It does not ratify all
  detailed amendments or grant missionary financial mutation. P16's staff-only
  financial rule does not prohibit the separately selected P28 confirmation.
- **Permanent fix:** Qualify the selected narrow P28 confirmation/correction
  capability through P12; preserve exact ministry/Party scope and attributable
  speaker capacity. No new general permission engine.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Authorized staff and missionaries MAY accept or
  correct the assertion only when the organization permits the exact currently
  qualified P28 purpose-confirmation action for this source scope. Recording SHALL identify the
  donor-origin speaker and source-qualified capacity separately from the
  recorder. Note, task, roster, project-membership or financial-read access SHALL
  NOT substitute for either gate. This capability SHALL confer no P13/P14/P16
  mutation, payment or representative authority.”
- **Proof:** Exercise independent staff/missionary grants, wrong ministry,
  withdrawn recording authority, organization representative change and ordinary
  note access. Record the actor class as selected, without reporting the whole
  amended decision as ratified or the capability as deployed.

### F03 — Mixed gifts could be dropped or silently expanded to whole gifts

- **What:** Whole-gift-only evidence loses real mixed gifts; an unscoped amount
  or new P28 allocation ledger can count unrelated or already refunded money.
- **Why:** One posted support line may include both regular and additional
  portions. Recognition may also be header-scoped across several designations.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Medium–high; the source contracts explicitly support split
  designations and partial recognition, but no frequency estimate is available.
- **Evidence:** S04:784–823; S06:325–347; S12 C03–C04; S16 basis analysis.
- **Answer impact:** A needs a bounded portion representation; excluding all
  mixed gifts would silently narrow the selected useful journey.
- **Permanent fix:** Whole exact scope reference or donor-stated original
  positive minor-unit portion, including an express fraction resolvable exactly
  without rounding; separate source-owned current qualification. Keep monetary
  algebra in P13/P14. Reject unsupported cross-line attribution rather than guess.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “The assertion SHALL identify the entire qualified
  source scope or an explicitly donor-stated positive portion in that scope's
  currency and minor units. An express fraction MAY resolve to an exact
  source-qualified amount without rounding; no literal-number requirement
  follows. Bound the original assertion to its exact qualified source scope and
  revision. Separately prove positive surviving support currently attributable
  to that purpose; do not rewrite the original assertion or reject it merely
  because a later correction reduced current eligible support. The portion
  SHALL remain purpose evidence, never a financial allocation or independently
  maintained remaining balance. No whole-header expansion,
  currency conversion, proportional attribution, fee-cover inclusion or
  unrelated-ministry amount SHALL be inferred. Several portions or evidence
  revisions SHALL NOT multiply one gift-purpose member.”
- **Proof:** One 125-unit line with 25 additional and an identified correction
  leaving 15 additional; express half that resolves exactly versus one requiring
  rounding; mixed ministries; JPY/USD;
  full and partial P14 recognition; same gift with repeated evidence; two
  distinct equal-value gifts. A fixture is not qualification of the real fold.

### F04 — Commitment application could incorrectly veto or prove additional purpose

- **What:** “Applied to a commitment” may be used as a blanket disqualifier, or
  unmatched/above-current-commitment money may be treated as automatically extra.
- **Why:** An additional promise can itself be fulfilled by an additional gift;
  an amount exceeding today's plan may be ordinary catch-up/advance support.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High if eligibility is implemented as a balance comparison.
- **Evidence:** S09:1007–1031; S10:1164–1184; D20 C01 separates fulfillment work.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve A independently of P16 application status. Conflict
  detection must concern authoritative contradictory meaning on the same scope.
- **Permanent fix:** Consume financial applications as facts, never intent. Hold
  contradictory purpose for the owning source resolution without editing money.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “P16 application, lack of application or absence of
  a commitment SHALL neither establish nor by itself disqualify additional
  stewardship purpose. A current authoritative contradictory instruction about
  the same exact portion SHALL withhold that purpose until source-qualified
  resolution; P28 SHALL NOT override or repair financial instructions. Received
  money and unrelated valid follow-up reasons SHALL remain independently true.”
- **Proof:** Additional commitment legitimately fulfilled; ordinary installment
  unmatched; catch-up over plan; current contradictory same-portion instruction;
  unrelated instruction on another allocation must not block the whole gift.

### F05 — An intent or payment attempt could be mistaken for received support

- **What:** A pre-receipt statement, pending ACH, DAF recommendation or matching
  expectancy could create a thank-you task for money not received.
- **Why:** A source-purpose statement is not a financial posting or receipt.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Medium, especially when purpose evidence precedes payment.
- **Evidence:** S05; S10:1184; S12 C04; P14 roles distinguish expectancy/receipt.
- **Answer impact:** Accept prior evidence without turning it into an early task.
- **Permanent fix:** Bind the statement to an actual eligible source occurrence
  by owner-qualified identity, then admit the reason under current source policy.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “A pre-receipt statement MAY be retained as governed
  evidence of intent. Automatic follow-up SHALL require its explicit qualified
  binding to actual eligible received support; amount/date similarity, pending
  payment, pledge, DAF recommendation or expectancy SHALL NOT establish receipt.
  Existing source settlement/return-exposure policy SHALL remain authoritative.”
- **Proof:** Statement before payment, failed attempt, pending/posted/returned
  ACH, two similar gifts and later exact binding; no task before eligible receipt.

### F06 — Delayed confirmation and history import could collapse into one clock

- **What:** Barring all later confirmation loses genuine work; accepting every
  new confirmation timestamp creates historical task storms and replay.
- **Why:** Gift date, source-live receipt, evidence occurrence, confirmation and
  worker delivery answer different questions.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High with import/retry paths or a simple `created_at` predicate.
- **Evidence:** S12 C05; S14 C12–C13; S08:1128. The precise D29 activation cohort
  below is proposed judgment, not a pre-existing D29 source implementation.
- **Answer impact:** A remains useful with delayed donor confirmation; no catch-up
  campaign or historical source migration is silently selected.
- **Permanent fix:** Capture durable source-live eligibility at the qualified
  boundary and distinguish evidence acceptance from original receipt identity.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “D29 MAY first admit a later genuine donor-purpose
  confirmation for a source-proven originally-live eligible receipt within the
  prospective D29 activation cohort, established by the durable source-live
  received-support boundary, only while its gift-purpose is unconsumed.
  All preactivation receipts, including formerly live receipts under an older
  system, history-only imports, backfills and relabelling SHALL
  NOT become live merely because confirmation, import or worker time is recent.
  Original receipt, statement, confirmation and admission facts SHALL remain
  distinct. Replay/recovery SHALL preserve the same semantic occurrence.”
- **Proof:** Delayed confirmation of live gift versus identical historical import;
  receipt crossing activation; delayed dispatch; changed gift date; lost response;
  historical labels; already covered source; later source-qualified restoration.

### F07 — A partial refund or contrary statement could leave a false surviving reason

- **What:** The system could subtract refunds from ordinary support first, retain
  all extra support, apply a local minimum, or erase all unrelated follow-up.
- **Why:** A mixed-purpose refund lacks portion meaning unless its source proves
  it; a later donor correction may invalidate intent without changing money.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Medium; cases are directly supported by source correction
  contracts, but their production frequency has not been measured.
- **Evidence:** S04:790–817; S09:1027–1031; S13 C11. P16 uncertain-vector behavior
  illustrates the no-guess boundary, not permission to reuse its journal/case UI.
- **Answer impact:** A needs reason-specific correction, not a new finance ledger
  or a global pause on posted gifts and all other work.
- **Permanent fix:** Consume source-qualified surviving exact scope. Ambiguity
  withholds the affected reason; source correction can restore the same eligible
  unconsumed purpose. Preserve facts, promises, coverage and other valid reasons.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Correction SHALL requalify the exact additional
  scope using current owner facts. For a wholly additional qualified scope,
  source-proven positive surviving support MAY retain the reason without a new
  mixed-portion attribution. An unattributed partial reversal of mixed
  ordinary/additional support SHALL NOT imply which portion survived; that reason
  SHALL become unqualified until resolved. Qualified contradiction, full reversal
  or loss of the eligible scope SHALL withdraw only the invalid unhandled reason.
  Current source-qualified restoration MAY restore the same originally-live,
  still-unconsumed purpose with lineage. Human dispositions and independent
  undertakings SHALL remain intact; no correction SHALL invent a new occurrence.”
- **Proof:** Whole-extra partial return; mixed unidentified return; explicitly
  extra-only return; full reversal; donor contradiction without money change;
  corrected restoration; concurrent Done; another valid amount reason survives.

### F08 — More reasons could become more tasks or silently complete other purposes

- **What:** Additional purpose, first support, amount threshold and fulfillment
  could duplicate work, or one generic Done could clear unrelated obligations.
- **Why:** Reasons support a stable special-gift member; distinct purposes have
  distinct coverage authority. Manual tasks are not automatic proof of coverage.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** High if keyed by reason/evidence version rather than purpose.
- **Evidence:** S11; S12 C01–C05; S13 C06–C12; S14 C10; S15.
- **Answer impact:** D29 is an added qualification reason, not a new task engine
  or permission to merge every thank-you/receipt/fulfillment action.
- **Permanent fix:** Existing D20 membership/coverage and ADR-0183 independent
  task commands; material source-purpose revisions included in reviewed proof.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “D29 SHALL support the existing qualified
  contribution/Party/subject/special-follow-up-purpose member. Amount, additional
  purpose, allocations and evidence revisions SHALL NOT create duplicate members.
  Removal of one reason SHALL retain independently valid reasons. First-support,
  receipt and fulfillment coverage SHALL remain separate unless their owners
  explicitly qualify coverage of the same action. Done/Not needed SHALL cover
  only the exact reviewed set; no interaction, send or financial outcome follows.
  Generic manual completion SHALL NOT manufacture native coverage.”
- **Proof:** Both amount/additional reasons; one removed; first-support overlap;
  third gift arrival during review; purpose correction during review; completed
  manual task without scope; actual exact-work reconciliation; independent call.

### F09 — Recognition or relationship visibility could expose or contact the wrong person

- **What:** A sponsor gift could fan out to an advisor, spouse or church member;
  additional-purpose evidence might bypass anonymity or recipient restrictions.
- **Why:** Legal donor, recognized supporter, statement speaker and permitted
  follow-up recipient are not interchangeable.
- **Severity:** Critical for privacy/contact authority.
- **Likelihood:** Medium–high if a generic Party relationship query selects the
  recipient instead of the source-qualified route.
- **Evidence:** S06–S08, S10; P14:1167–1177 is an explicit visibility/contact wall.
- **Answer impact:** A adds no audience, church-member correspondence, household
  fan-out, donor-form consent, mailing-list enrollment or financial authority.
- **Permanent fix:** Current exact P14 recipient/recognition route and purpose
  disclosure; speaker capacity independently qualified. Ambiguity affects only
  that route/reason and does not undo correct financial posting.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Additional-purpose evidence SHALL confer no
  recipient, recognition, identity-disclosure or contact permission. DAF advisor
  and organization-representative statements SHALL use source-qualified capacity
  for this stewardship assertion without creating legal-donor, commitment or
  payment authority. Current anonymity and P14 recipient restrictions SHALL
  remain binding. No household expansion or church-member thank-you stream SHALL
  be inferred; actual communication SHALL separately pass its existing owner.”
- **Proof:** Anonymous advisor; sponsor known/advisor unknown; changed attribution;
  shared email/spouse; organization representative departure; visible church
  member; recognition correction; no hidden-identity details in task/audit copy.

### F10 — Proof retention could become indefinite private-message retention or false expiry

- **What:** A task could copy a donor's private message, retain it forever, lose
  accepted historical truth when a recorder departs, or accept unverifiable flags.
- **Why:** Current viewing rights, validity of accepted evidence and raw-body
  custody/retention are different lifecycles.
- **Severity:** High.
- **Likelihood:** Medium–high unless the new source contract defines the minimum
  retained provenance and safe task projection.
- **Evidence:** S02:52–64; S10:1186; S11:630–643. No inspected owner supplies a D29
  numeric retention period or general right to copy private source content.
- **Answer impact:** A needs auditable evidence without broadening CRM/note/email
  access or silently selecting a new retention duration.
- **Permanent fix:** Owner-qualified minimum structured attestation and restricted
  evidence references; authorized factual reason only in task; source retention
  policy before activation. Source revocation distinguished from body expiry.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Ordinary task projections and event envelopes SHALL
  carry only authorized bounded reason/source references, not protected statement
  bodies. The accepted fact SHALL retain source-qualified minimal provenance
  under an explicit lawful retention contract. Raw-body expiry or recorder
  departure SHALL NOT by itself be presented as donor retraction; continuing
  proof after disposal SHALL require owner-qualified retained evidence. Missing
  required proof SHALL be unavailable, never fabricated success.”
- **Proof:** Evidence body expired with/without qualified retained attestation;
  recorder revoked; viewer loses source rights; restricted D1 context; trace/log
  inspection; contradiction retained without retaining unnecessary prose.

### F11 — A convenient checkbox could conceal an unqualified producer or extra machinery

- **What:** A nice UI may ship with no source-qualified receipt/portion/actor path,
  or compensate with new registries, financial ledgers, queues and forced forms.
- **Why:** Current ratified intent and synthetic prior task probes do not prove
  D29's new evidence admission. One purpose does not justify a configurable rules
  builder, new donor checkout or P35 implementation dependency.
- **Severity:** High for activation; medium for maintenance burden.
- **Likelihood:** High if formalization/runtime gates are treated as already met.
- **Evidence:** S01:78–101; S02:87–90; S03:188–189; S11–S15.
- **Answer impact:** Accept the bounded product choice with real gates; neither
  claim shipped support nor reject A merely because target owners need work.
- **Permanent fix:** Formalize one P28 source purpose and the exact owner seams;
  reuse P9/D20 shared work and existing P3/P10/P12 access. Measure actual source
  completeness/lag and task drift after qualification, not invented budgets.
- **Exact proposed clause:** “Activation SHALL require the exact P28 purpose
  command and source/actor/portion/current-recipient qualifications, coherently
  bound to existing financial and shared-task owners. No generic special flag,
  parallel task ledger, financial ledger, automatic outreach, new donor intake or
  P35 prerequisite SHALL substitute for that qualification. Prior documentation
  and isolated fixtures SHALL NOT be represented as implementation or production
  evidence.”
- **Proof:** Trace real command through source/authorization/admission/task effect;
  correction race, retry and reconciliation with current owners; verify operator
  can see an honest unavailable state and continue unrelated legitimate work.
