# D29 actor clarification — selected

On October 17, 2026, the founder answered the single review clarification:

> **Question:** Who may confirm a donor’s explicitly stated additional-support
> purpose? This confirms the reason for follow-up; it does not change received
> money or apply a gift to a commitment.
>
> **Answer:** Authorized staff and missionaries when the organization permits it
> (recommended).

The actor class is now selected. A qualified staff member or missionary may
record and confirm the attributable purpose when the organization authorizes
the exact action and source scope. A role name, assignment, task permission or
gift visibility alone is not that authority. The action changes no financial
fact, application, communication consent or evidence-body permission.

One authorized deliberate save may both record and confirm; no routine second
approver is introduced. Current correction, privacy and source qualification
remain mandatory. The exact future capability/command contract must be qualified
through its existing P12/P28 owners; no production grant is created by this note.

The original A answer selected additional support without a required appeal or
one-time need. This clarification selects its potential confirming actors. It
does **not** separately ratify every required amendment in the
[D29 review](../phase28-d29-additional-gift-purpose-adversarial-review.md).
The main decision, Q29 brief, session register, supporting research and model
status are reconciled. No further product clarification blocks the completed
review; no Q30 or formal implementation work is initiated.
