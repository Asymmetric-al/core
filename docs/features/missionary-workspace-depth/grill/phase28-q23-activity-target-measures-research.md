# Q23 frontier — may optional activity targets include outreach attempts?

Research date: **October 17, 2026 Asia/Bangkok**. **Answered A.** The founder
selected separate optional outreach-attempt targets and subsequently confirmed
**supported activity types with personal customization** of names, amounts and
schedules. D22 remains ratified. The original frontier and alternatives below
are retained; the [completed D23 review](phase28-d23-activity-targets-adversarial-review.md)
records the corrected decision and required amendments, **explicitly ratified
on October 17, 2026, Asia/Bangkok**. No implementation or formal specification is
created.

## The one choice

Maya chooses to use an optional weekly activity target. She makes five calls to
five people. Two answer and have a conversation; the other three do not answer.

**Should Maya be able to choose a target for recorded outreach attempts, including
those that receive no response, separately from actual conversations or held
meetings?**

- **A — Allow separate outreach-attempt targets (recommended).** Maya may choose
  an outreach target that counts the five qualified recorded calls, while the
  conversation measure separately shows the two actual exchanges. She can choose
  a conversation/meeting target instead or use no activity target.
- **B — Limit initial targets to actual exchanges and held meetings.** The target
  measure counts the two qualifying conversations. The other attempts can remain
  useful permitted outreach history but do not count toward an activity target.

The five calls **include** the two that reached someone: these are not seven
activities. The figures illustrate meaning, not recommended targets or a required
weekly workload. The scenario is invented for the decision, not an interview.

**Recommendation: A.** It lets someone plan work within their control without
equating an unanswered call with a conversation or treating useful one-way care
as failure. B is the strongest simpler alternative: fewer target measures and
less opportunity for volume to dominate reflection. A therefore needs distinct,
plain labels and source facts, not a combined productivity score. The evidence
supports comparable patterns, not a measured benefit or a universal coaching method.

## Boundaries for either answer

1. **Only actual, qualified recorded facts count for their own meaning.** Opening
   a dialer/mail application, copying an address, scheduling a meeting or checking
   a task is not evidence that outreach occurred. A provider-accepted message is
   not proof of delivery, reading or reply. A manual source-qualified record remains
   useful; no transcript, provider integration or mandatory narrative is required.
2. **No conflation or duplicate activity.** Method, purpose and result remain
   distinguishable. One call can support a call-attempt measure and, when answered,
   an exchange measure without becoming two underlying calls. Two narratives,
   repeated saves, import or source corrections do not manufacture encounters.
   A newsletter or automated receipt is not a personal conversation.
3. **Voluntary participation remains intact.** No quotas, missing-log penalties,
   rankings, forced reporting, automatic next task or loss of ordinary workspace
   use. Recorded counts describe the eligible records, not all real activity or
   missionary effort. Unknown, unavailable, incomplete and unshared evidence must
   not become “zero activity” or a claim that nothing happened.
4. **No new disclosure entitlement.** Targets, activity facts, counts, dates,
   source links and derived trends retain current exact source/subject permissions.
   Choosing a target does not share private notes, message bodies, supporter names
   or history with staff or a coach. Whether this particular metric/history is
   admitted to a coaching share remains a separate qualification and unselected
   disclosure choice; existing D8–D11 rights are neither broadened nor removed.
5. **Keep the question bounded.** No financial-goal change, new activity store,
   general mailbox ingestion, automatic call observation, arbitrary formula
   builder or provider dependency is selected. Exact supported measures, source
   outcome mapping, period/date handling, corrections, deduplication and accessible
   labels need a coherent bounded review after the answer; they are not facts to
   infer from a competitor's report.

P9 owns qualified interactions and shared tasks; P6/P17 and D15 retain exact
communication outcomes; P27 owns the exchange/outreach meanings; P28 owns the
optional target experience. Reuse those sources rather than a second activity
ledger. This note proves only the documentary frontier and comparable patterns:
no actual Core metric, source adapter, authorization projection, coaching share,
mobile UI, user study or runtime qualification was exercised. No live settings,
supporters, providers, app code, formal owner documents or external messages changed.

## Selected answer and completed review

On October 17, 2026 Asia/Bangkok, the founder selected **A — Allow separate
outreach-attempt targets**, requiring deep research, substantial personal
customization and excellent simple UX. The follow-up explicitly selected
**supported activity types with personal customization**, not new activity types.

The [D23 review](phase28-d23-activity-targets-adversarial-review.md) returns
**Accept with required amendments** across all 22 categories. It proposes four
source-qualified measures, the existing P28 Goal Version activity arm, bounded
weekly/monthly/custom periods, exact source participation, non-additive counts,
truthful dates/coverage and deliberate adjustment/pause/resume. Personal targets
create no staff/coach/public disclosure, compulsory logging or automatic outreach.
C01–C24 and P01–P32 were proposed future obligations at initial review; they are
now explicitly ratified. Twenty-seven finite
counterexamples establish bounded calendar/cardinality failures, not actual Core
source/P12/UI/capacity qualification. The source, owner, database and UX evidence
retain those limits; no old probe was rerun. No Q24 was posed in the initial review.
