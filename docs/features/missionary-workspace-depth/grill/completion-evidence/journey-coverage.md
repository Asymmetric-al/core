# Phase 28 completion challenge — complete journeys and missing closure

Reviewed October 18, 2026, Asia/Bangkok, against Core
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` and the research working tree.
This is a fresh read-only document/source audit apart from this new evidence
file. No earlier program, model, probe or verifier was executed. No hosted
database, provider, browser or representative user journey was exercised.

## Corrected conclusion

**Accept the selected product direction with required completion amendments;
reject any interpretation that the end-to-end Phase 28 journey is complete.**
The prior synthesis correctly preserved qualification gates and six unratified
review packets. Its narrower observation that another feature preference was
not obvious is defensible. It is insufficient evidence that the decision
frontier is empty or that the essential journey has been assembled completely.

The material remaining work is mostly completion of already required journeys,
not another collection of optional features. Tenant activation and first use,
the ordinary missionary's ministry/context selection, and the joined daily
work loop need explicit composition. Required recurring-attention work still
needs an exact behavior contract; if its trigger, responsible role or human
purpose cannot be derived from accepted owner decisions, that is a real product
choice to return to the founder, not an implementation detail to guess.

The six earlier packets D1/D3/D6/D11/D12/D13 retain selected/reviewed status.
This review does not ratify them, publish a formal specification, or authorize
implementation or tickets. Explicit `/to-prd` and `/to-issues` remain required.

## Material concern register

Severity and likelihood are qualitative judgments about the proposed developing
specification, not measured incident probabilities. These concerns narrow the
closure claim; they do not invalidate the selected features.

### J01 — First use and tenant readiness are not assembled

- **What could go wrong:** staff believe an enabled setting means a usable
  feature; Maya arrives at an empty or blocked workspace and is sent through an
  optional guide instead of the real access/source repair path. Disabling an
  optional feature may be misread as a tenant-wide shutdown.
- **Why it matters:** discoverability, correct tenant configuration and a useful
  initial experience are essential parts of shipping the selected work.
- **Severity / likelihood:** medium–high / plausible; settings and readiness
  already span P12, P9, P16, P17, P21, P22 and P31.
- **Evidence:** post-D41 coverage contains no dedicated tenant activation/first-use
  row. D37 explicitly says its guide does not replace normal Home/work and
  requires no completion; D16 explicitly distinguishes no goal from lack of
  authority and unavailable policy. Those local states are useful but do not
  constitute the whole setup journey.
- **Effect:** adds a required composition and proof obligation, not new approval
  or a master workspace setting.
- **Permanent treatment / exact proposed addition:** **JC01** below.

### J02 — Ordinary missionary context navigation has no complete contract

- **What could go wrong:** the right person operates on the wrong ministry,
  an old deep link restores a revoked context, a newly selected subject inherits
  a former draft/action recipient, or no-assignment is presented as no gifts.
- **Why it matters:** the unit of authority and the user's chosen work context
  must agree before every action, not only on the coaching surface.
- **Severity / likelihood:** high / plausible; actual multi-context volume is
  unknown, but the brief and owners expressly permit distinct contexts.
- **Evidence:** current `resolveMissionaryPortalContext` selects one missionary
  row by tenant/profile with `.single()`; its snapshot then uses different legacy
  missionary/profile IDs for gifts, donors and tasks. This is static evidence
  that the reached implementation is not the qualified subject-selection
  contract; it is not proof of live cross-tenant access. D8 has an explicit coach
  portfolio, whereas the ordinary missionary entry/switch/resume path remains
  distributed among scope clauses.
- **Effect:** changes the journey-completeness assessment and requires the
  inherited subject boundaries to become a coherent UX; no shared-account or
  rights-union feature is introduced.
- **Permanent treatment / exact proposed addition:** **JC02**.

### J03 — Component-level task rules do not prove the complete daily loop

- **What could go wrong:** one actual call must be entered twice; task Done is
  mistaken for conversation or Request response; a successful source write is
  retried after the follow-up view fails; or completing one task leaves actively
  managed work without the required next action/owned exception.
- **Why it matters:** the founder specifically asks for minimal repeated entry
  and a complete referral-to-follow-up journey, not disconnected screens.
- **Severity / likelihood:** high / plausible if integration reuses legacy
  generic task paths without the source contract.
- **Evidence:** D19 already defines Mark done, optional Record interaction,
  Not needed, responsibility resolution and interrupted recovery. D14 C02/P01
  already require native appointment outcomes. P27 independently supplies
  Request/response and next-task-or-owned-exception meaning. The current task
  PATCH schema updates status/completed_at but has no qualified interaction,
  Request, source-purpose or next-work acceptance in that reached command.
  The gap is integration, not an absent product decision to permit logging.
- **Effect:** requires a joined acceptance/action map and proof; does not justify
  compulsory logging, a new task engine or completing all sources atomically
  when their owners require distinct effects.
- **Permanent treatment / exact proposed addition:** **JC03**.

### J04 — Remaining mandatory recurring attention has an unnamed human job

- **What could go wrong:** an implementer either creates tasks for every missed
  payment, suppresses all recurring attention, or gives Maya a task whose only
  useful repair belongs to the donor/payment staff. Each can contradict the
  selected no-noise case or leave a required outcome undelivered.
- **Why it matters:** a task needs a concrete source-qualified human action and
  honest completion, not only a status label.
- **Severity / likelihood:** high / plausible; the synthesis explicitly retains
  the exact purpose as unresolved.
- **Evidence:** founder brief native stewardship and P28 owner constraints
  require named recurring-attention tasks. D40 C01 excludes only its specific
  case. P16 defines separate current states and role-safe actions, including
  action needed to continue automatic gifts, but a display statement is not
  independently the selected P28 task-purpose contract.
- **Effect:** qualifies the prior empty-frontier claim. This is an unresolved
  behavior seam, potentially a product choice after exact owner research. Do
  not automatically label the trigger/assignee choice engineering-only.
- **Permanent treatment / exact proposed addition:** **JC04**. Ask a founder
  question only if this reconciliation exposes an actually unselected choice;
  do not re-ask whether already required native attention exists.

### J05 — A representative happy path masks essential negative/exit paths

- **What could go wrong:** implementation proves referral→gift→newsletter→coach
  but cannot finish useful work after a refusal, cancelled appointment, missing
  contact method, unknown commitment, source outage or denied/ended sharing.
- **Why it matters:** “essential journey” includes not obtaining a gift, not
  using coaching and not granting a communication right.
- **Severity / likelihood:** medium–high / plausible; the prior synthesis names
  alternatives but supplies no joined evidence matrix for them.
- **Evidence:** P27 preserves independent response and partnership lifecycle;
  D8 already specifies invitation rejection/self-leave; D7 already specifies
  exact-purpose unsubscribe; D19 already includes Not needed. These are covered
  requirements. The deficiency is treating named alternatives as passed journey
  evidence, or allowing a new composition to override them.
- **Effect:** adds whole-journey negative acceptance obligations. No new decline,
  unsubscribe, coach chat or relationship-closure feature is selected.
- **Permanent treatment / exact proposed addition:** **JC05–JC06**.

### J06 — Approval and runtime gates are visible but do not permit closure

- **What could go wrong:** “no Q42” is repeated as “Phase 28 complete,” six review
  recommendations become accepted defaults silently, or a user signs off on
  understanding and that is treated as authority to activate unqualified flows.
- **Why it matters:** the readiness claim, not just the implementation, must
  distinguish selected behavior, adopted amendments, formal reconciliation and
  observed proof.
- **Severity / likelihood:** high / demonstrated ambiguity in the user's
  completion challenge; no runtime incident is asserted.
- **Evidence:** post-D41 synthesis carefully disclaims full readiness but still
  calls for a shared-understanding checkpoint with an empty feature frontier;
  the session explicitly lists six unratified amendment packets and upstream
  policy/producer gates. No complete runtime or user study was performed here.
- **Effect:** narrows the closure language and requires status-based acceptance,
  not new feature scope or accidental blanket ratification.
- **Permanent treatment / exact proposed addition:** **JC07–JC08**.

## Exact proposed completion additions

These are research recommendations for the developing specification, not a new
formal specification or already ratified decision.

- **JC01 — Activation and first use.** The Phase 28 design SHALL include one
  compact cross-owner setup and first-use map. For each offered capability it
  SHALL identify the existing setting/permission owner, actual prerequisite,
  configured versus available state, responsible repair route, existing-data
  effect and independent baseline path. A first visit SHALL distinguish lack of
  authorized scope, no records, feature Off and temporary unavailability. The
  optional starting guide, target, coach, AI and external connection SHALL NOT
  become mandatory onboarding. No new master permission or settings engine is
  authorized by this requirement.
- **JC02 — Exact work context.** The ordinary missionary journey SHALL define
  zero, one and multiple currently permitted work contexts, visible subject and
  organization, deliberate navigation, deep-link admission and safe return to
  work. Switching SHALL re-prove access and prevent old responses, unsaved
  recipients, draft selections or queued commands from being rebound to the new
  context. Distinct Ministry Assignment, Support Assignment, Page and profile
  meanings SHALL remain intact; current state from one is not authority for the
  others. This is composition of existing rights, not a right to new contexts.
- **JC03 — One actual action, truthful results.** The composed daily journey
  SHALL show the source action for an ordinary task, appointment, eligible
  interaction and Request outcome; distinguish attempted, completed, cancelled,
  deferred and unknown outcomes; and identify which accepted effect satisfies
  which work. Optional interaction recording SHALL be reusable without duplicate
  entry or implied compulsory sharing. Actively managed work SHALL retain its
  inherited next-task-or-owned-exception rule; early unshared preparation SHALL
  not acquire that rule by accident. Partial success SHALL preserve and recover
  each accepted source effect instead of repeating it.
- **JC04 — Required recurring work.** Before declaring recurring stewardship
  behavior resolved, the owners SHALL publish a finite mapping from each
  intended native recurring-attention task to its exact source facts, human
  purpose, responsible role, current audience, task/effect identity, completion,
  correction and exclusion behavior. D40's continuing-schedule miss remains
  informational. If accepted owner intent does not determine a material trigger,
  role or obligation, the unresolved product choice SHALL be presented explicitly
  to the founder rather than inferred from a generic attention label.
- **JC05 — Negative paths remain complete.** The acceptance map SHALL include
  no qualifying gift, declined/deferred/withdrawn Request, cancelled/no-show
  appointment where the source supports that recorded outcome, missing or
  restricted contact method, unknown source state and changed responsibility.
  These paths SHALL retain useful permitted work without inventing a gift,
  conversation, donation pledge, contact permission or relationship ending.
- **JC06 — Exit proof.** Whole-journey proof SHALL include coach decline,
  cancelled/expired invite, self-leave, missionary Stop sharing, exact-purpose
  newsletter withdrawal, Page-entry Off, physical/email preference separation,
  departure and eligible return. It SHALL verify their distinct source effects
  across pending work, history, current reads and subsequent deliberate actions.
  Existing selected exit behavior is reused; no additional inbox, chat, settings
  matrix or blanket delete/revoke action is introduced.
- **JC07 — Honest completion states.** The coverage record SHALL separately mark
  selected product intent, amendment adoption, formal owner reconciliation,
  implemented source/UI capability and observed acceptance evidence. No single
  Covered label or count of decisions SHALL stand in for those states. The six
  earlier review packets retain their actual statuses until explicitly adopted.
- **JC08 — Closure test.** “Phase 28 complete” SHALL require the applicable
  canonical requirements, dependencies and acceptance evidence for the required
  journeys, not merely the absence of another feature preference. Before formal
  specification is authorized, the safe conclusion is that broad scope is
  selected while the named journey compositions, source-policy rulings, approval
  exceptions and proof remain open. Monitoring cannot substitute for these
  release-blocking obligations.

## What not to add

This challenge does not justify a new compulsory onboarding wizard, tenant-wide
workspace switch, shared login, ministry-union financial total, automatic inbox,
offline write replay, coach editor, generic automation builder, contact-sync
feed or complete incumbent-database migration. Existing optionality and excluded
scope remain intentional. The permanent correction is to join the selected
sources and journeys accurately, resolve actual policy choices, and prove the
result rather than prolong the interview with speculative features.

## Immediate synthesis recommendation

1. Replace any implication of complete journey closure with the corrected
   conclusion at the top of this note; retain D41's ratification unchanged.
2. Add explicit activation/first-use, ordinary missionary context and composed
   daily-loop rows to the completion map, using JC01–JC03 and J-P01–J-P09.
3. Resolve the named recurring-attention behavior and upstream policy gates in
   their owners. If the exact user obligation is not already decided, ask that
   concrete product question; do not assert an empty frontier in advance.
4. Preserve and separately reconcile the six amendment-adoption exceptions.
   Do not convert the user's completion question into approval of those packets.
5. Carry the already covered negative and exit paths into the integrated proof
   matrix, then formalize only when `/to-prd` is invoked. Runtime and real user
   evidence belong to the later authorized delivery, not a fabricated research
   claim or an excuse to remove the required outcome.
