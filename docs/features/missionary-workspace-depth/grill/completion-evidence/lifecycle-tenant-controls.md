# Completion challenge — lifecycle and tenant controls

Reviewed October 18, 2026 against Core 9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813 and the current research tree. This is a new, read-only source/research assessment. No earlier checker, model, probe or verifier was executed. No hosted permission, database, provider, browser or user journey was tested. Only this evidence note was written.

## Result and confidence

**The documents do not prove that the whole operational lifecycle is complete.** They do, however, contain unusually extensive individual lifecycle requirements. I found no evidence that another feature-selection question is necessary merely to handle departure, return, disablement or retries. Calling every unimplemented requirement an unanswered founder question would be inaccurate.

The material remaining acceptance gap is a **composed lifecycle qualification** across existing owners and surfaces. The previous coverage table maps feature choices to broad obligations; it does not supply a completed trace showing that one real actor can start, change context, lose authority, recover an accepted operation and continue independently valid work without disclosure or duplicate effects. The permanent amendment is a small, explicit cross-owner acceptance matrix and a source-owned control-effects inventory. It is not a new offboarding product, global settings engine, blanket cancellation policy or universal restore operation.

This conclusion narrows a completion claim. It does not by itself falsify the narrower statement that the inspected feature-selection frontier has no demonstrated new branch. Absence of a new branch is not exhaustive proof that every user journey has been specified or qualified.

## Material concerns

### LT01 — Feature coverage is weaker than complete lifecycle coverage

**What could go wrong and why it matters:** a team may read “no additional feature choice” or a completed feature table as authority to omit cross-owner behavior. A missionary could be successfully removed from one surface while queued work, retained coaching pages or recovery routes still use an obsolete context. Conversely, a broad shutdown could erase lawful source work or unnecessarily block an independent ministry.

**Severity:** High, potentially Critical for disclosure or forbidden outward effects. **Likelihood:** Plausible during integration; no incident frequency is known. **Evidence:** the [coverage synthesis](../phase28-post-d41-coverage-synthesis.md#selected-scope-complete-journey-and-remaining-treatment) expressly defines coverage as selected/inherited direction, while the [founder complete-journey requirement](../phase28-founder-brief.md:311) demands actual combined source/role/phone proof. The local D27/D28/D7 cases above exist but are not a demonstrated end-to-end run.

**Effect on the decision:** narrows the completion statement; does not invalidate the selected feature scope. **Permanent prevention:** adopt LC01–LC04 below as explicit composition requirements and retain their unchecked evidence status until exercised. No new founder feature choice is needed.

### LT02 — Optional controls have intentionally different consequences

**What could go wrong and why it matters:** generic UI or backend treatment of On/Off could revoke too much, preserve too much, replay old effects or falsely tell staff that all work stopped. The difference is real: D24 Off removes activity-module access, D33 Off stops only new reporter intake, D40 Off stops new/unpublished notices, and D30 Off leaves admitted source work intact. One shared switch component cannot establish shared business semantics.

**Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Plausible when independent slices are composed or settings are centralized. **Evidence:** explicit conflicting-by-design consequences in the table above. This is not a contradiction between decisions; it becomes a defect only if a consumer assumes one generic meaning.

**Effect on the decision:** requires an explicit inventory and cross-control acceptance, not another settings abstraction. **Permanent prevention:** LC02: document and expose each control's exact new-work, existing-work, read, queued-effect and re-enable consequences through the existing owner. Preserve current shared UI components. No founder choice is needed to enforce already selected meanings.

### LT03 — Current portal source cannot establish the promised multi-context experience

**What could go wrong and why it matters:** future work may reuse a singular profile-based portal as though it already resolves independently authorized ministry and role contexts. A functioning page for one missionary is insufficient proof of a dual-role staff member or coach with several organizations and assignments.

**Severity:** High for wrong-scope results or blocked legitimate work. **Likelihood:** Plausible reuse hazard; hosted behavior was not tested. **Fresh static evidence:** [resolveMissionaryPortalContext](../../../../../packages/api/src/missionary-portal/service.ts:154) returns one profile and one missionary row using profile/tenant filters and a singular query. [The roster reader](../../../../../packages/api/src/missionary-portal/donors.ts:493) also filters through the legacy profile-linked missionary column. Those observations do not demonstrate the exact P9/P12/Support Assignment contract, nor do they alone prove a production vulnerability. D8/D16/D24 specify broader qualified contexts.

**Effect on the decision:** an implementation qualification gap, not an unanswered feature choice. **Permanent prevention:** use the existing exact source/context resolver and test positive authorized contexts plus negative cross-context paths. Do not create another local role system or add access by broadening the legacy query. LC01 makes the whole experience falsifiable.

### LT04 — Offboarding examples from leading products can violate Core's accepted boundaries

**What could go wrong and why it matters:** adopting a convenient “transfer all work to admin” or same-email restoration convention would leak D1 preparation, launder protected details into tasks, or revive sharing/communication authority.

**Severity:** Critical for restricted context disclosure. **Likelihood:** Plausible pattern-copying risk, not observed implementation behavior. **Evidence:** fresh Asana, HubSpot and Atlassian documents below demonstrate broad transfer or restoration semantics; D3/D27/D28 and ADR0183 deliberately preserve narrower authority.

**Effect on the decision:** no additional feature is required. The permanent choice is already Core's source-owned continuity with current authorization. **Prevention:** LC03 and LC04 explicitly require both retained legitimate work and denied forbidden access. Do not add a global successor grant, generic offboarding workflow or automatic return of preparation.

## Exact small amendments proposed for the developing review

These are acceptance/design refinements, not formal OpenSpec publication or claims of implementation. They carry forward existing decisions rather than ratifying their currently unratified amendments.

**LC01 — Context entry and recovery composition.** The complete-journey acceptance record SHALL include an actor with two independently authorized ministry contexts, two organizations, and separately authorized staff/missionary or coach roles. The ordinary existing entry and context controls SHALL identify the effective organization, subject and role where consequential. A context change SHALL cancel or isolate obsolete presentation and reject late results. An already accepted operation remains accepted in its original source context and SHALL be recoverable only through current authority there; it SHALL NOT be re-created or displayed in a different context. Same-tenant different-subject isolation and positive legitimate work SHALL both be tested. No new switching product or permission union is implied.

**LC02 — Existing control-effects inventory.** Before a Phase 28 slice is called complete, its design/release record SHALL identify each exposed tenant/personal control, owning capability, initial/unconfigured state, exact acceptance boundary, consequences for new work, already accepted work, reads, queued outward effects and later re-enable. The ordinary control SHALL explain the material consequence in concise local copy. The inventory SHALL reference the existing source clauses rather than create a generic Off action or another configuration system. Tests SHALL include at least one pair of controls with different accepted consequences and prove independent modules remain usable.

**LC03 — Departure, remaining duties and return composition.** Using the existing identity, membership and source owners, qualification SHALL remove Maya's exact participation while private preparation, an accepted source duty, permitted coaching shares and previously authorized communication work exist. Current access ends according to the owning rule without waiting for reassignment. Accepted duties retain lawful history and a source-owned authorized resolution path; no guessed fallback recipient or new private-content access follows. Each unsubmitted outward effect is revalidated at its actual owner boundary; accepted or unknown external effects retain truthful evidence. Return SHALL distinguish temporary withholding from ended participation, require exact current admission and deliberate preparation selection where D27 applies, and preserve unselected deadlines and unrelated contexts.

**LC04 — Partial outcome and restore composition.** Qualification SHALL lose a response after authoritative acceptance, change context or current access, disable the relevant optional path, and replay the original intent. Recovery SHALL report only the currently permitted original outcome, never apply the business effect again or overwrite newer state. Restore and mixed-version rollout SHALL reconcile later revocation, suppression, current source corrections, consumed effects and disposition obligations before opening reads/jobs. Missing reconciliation authority withholds only the affected capability; independently valid work remains available. A finite set of source-owned cases is required, not a distributed global transaction.

**LC05 — Claim discipline.** The coverage conclusion SHALL distinguish selected direction, amendment ratification, exact owner reconciliation, implementation and actual complete-journey qualification. A named test with no execution evidence remains required, not passed. No additional feature choice found in a bounded audit SHALL NOT be rewritten as proof that no possible journey gap exists. D1/D3/D6/D11–D13 retain their recorded amendment statuses unless explicitly resolved.

## Recommended synthesis

1. Correct any statement implying complete operational qualification. Preserve the useful narrower result: the inspected feature choices are covered, with the exact earlier amendment-status exceptions visible.
2. Carry LC01–LC05 into the completion review as proposed acceptance/design refinements. They reuse the current owners and UI; no founder feature choice is required.
3. Reconcile exact source capability, actor, retained-work and outward-effect predicates during authorized formalization. Where an actual source policy remains absent, record that exact owner gate; do not invent a default in a generic lifecycle handler.
4. Require the concrete integrated cases and actual complete-journey proof before rollout. Trace each result to the source version, role, configuration and test environment used.
5. Preserve source-owned monitoring. Any confirmed cross-context disclosure, effect after a required denial, duplicate accepted business effect or restoration of forbidden content is an invariant breach: the owning source/P12 security operator contains the affected path, retains minimal evidence and requalifies it. No new arbitrary threshold or central incident product is needed.

**Founder choice needed:** none demonstrated by this lifecycle review. Explicit approval may still be needed for the six separately unratified amendment packets, and exact financial-owner gates remain recorded elsewhere. Neither should be silently converted into a new feature question or assumed accepted by this evidence note.
