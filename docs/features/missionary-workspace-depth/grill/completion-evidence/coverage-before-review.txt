## Why synthesis is the next step

The [founder brief](phase28-founder-brief.md:21) says to choose questions dynamically,
one meaningful decision at a time, rather than turn its coverage map into a
questionnaire. The [grilling skill](../../../ai/skills/grilling/SKILL.md) ends the
interview when the decision frontier is empty and calls for shared understanding
before acting. Formal publication still requires the explicit `/to-prd`
transition; tickets require `/to-issues`.

The roadmap's [expressly open Phase 28 product choices](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:4219)
now have selected answers: restricted prospects and sharing; coaching assurance,
summary scope and history; activity metrics; resources and regional discovery;
calendar and email; and incumbent-tool contact continuity. Real measured UX
budgets are evidence to obtain from representative journeys, not numbers for the
founder to guess.

Two independent brief/repository audits reached this conclusion. They checked
resources, geography, AI, calendar, households/churches, daily work and connections
against the selected decisions and P9/P27 boundaries. A separate owner audit
rechecked the remaining source conflicts. The root review reconciled their
findings against the actual decision register. This is a document audit, not a
new user study or a claim that every implementation detail is settled.

No new competitor survey is needed to decide whether already selected choices
are selected. The dated primary research in the individual decision packets,
including D41's comparisons, retains its stated scope and limitations. It does
not justify expanding the release simply to produce another interview question.

The P14/P9 discrepancy deserves explicit treatment because financial support and
relationship continuity are different facts. P14's roster v2 recognizes indirect
paths; P9's v1 edge has its separately versioned predicate. Preserve these existing
purpose-specific policies unless a coordinated owner amendment explicitly changes
them. No automatic edge upgrade follows from this audit. Native relationship work
must be composed through its independently permitted source; the financial read
model is not the contact directory. Its temporal bands do not establish retention,
access, partnership lifecycle, D19 first-support history completeness or P16
commitment health. Qualification must reconcile the current typed-commitment
predicate with historical-recency wording rather than silently dropping current
support based only on age. P14's financial field floor remains intact despite D5
native editing. Source owners must resolve exact consumer behavior in canonical
clauses and OpenSpec projections; a banner saying “follow the latest decision” is
inadequate.

financial temporal-policy ruling, not merely an adapter fix. Finding no new generic

## Declined artificial follow-on questions

- Mandatory guide completion would contradict D37's selected optional behavior.
- Automatic future digests would reopen D7's deliberate per-issue authorization.
- General inbox synchronization would expand D15 beyond selected personal sending.
- Full history/task migration would expand D17's bounded first-release intake.
- Another contact-feed option would reverse the newly ratified D41 choice.
- Whether to retain nonfinancial relationships or create required qualified tasks
  is already addressed by the brief/P27/source obligations. Exact task binding
  and queue presentation need engineering/design work, not an arbitrary A/B.
- A new coach editor/chat, reminder engine, AI assistant or per-contact setting
  needs demonstrated user need; it is not necessary to exhaust this coverage map.
