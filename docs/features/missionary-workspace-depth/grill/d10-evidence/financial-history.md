# D10 — financial meaning of earlier coaching-summary periods

**Subsequent status — October 16, 2026 UTC:** the founder explicitly ratified
the [D10 corrected decision and amendment](../phase28-d10-adversarial-review.md),
including C01–C20/P01–P28. The research below preserves its pre-ratification
reasoning and candidate language; only the reconciled main review defines the
ratified amendment. No runtime or source qualification is implied.

Research date: October 16, 2026 UTC. D8/D9 and their amendments are ratified.
The founder selected D10 A, allowing deliberately selected earlier support-summary
periods. This is targeted owner research and proposed review language, not D10
amendment ratification, an owner-spec change, implementation or production proof.
No lookback length, default, expiry, rolling access or future-period right is
selected here.

## Minimal permanent recommendation

Extend the existing D9 source profiles and D8 sharing command to admit an exact
reviewed set of earlier reporting periods. Use the same two financial meanings,
with an additional historical qualification for expectations:

1. **Gifts recorded for [earlier period] — updated [source time].** This remains
   D9's current-effective designated-money measure, before assessments, grouped
   by the current authoritative P7 gift-date fact. It answers what the qualified
   records now establish for that period. It does not answer what a coach, staff
   member or system knew at the period end.
2. **Support planned for [earlier period] — updated [source time].** This uses a
   P16-qualified historical expectation population: the dated terms, schedule
   epochs, occurrence-line snapshots and applicable lifecycle evidence for those
   scheduled dates, incorporating authorized factual corrections. It is neither
   today's active recurring total projected backward nor the remaining unfulfilled
   amount today. Later payment or routine ending of future support must not make
   an amount that was planned for the earlier period disappear from this metric.

The second definition is a bounded P16 historical metric amendment, not a claim
that the existing current-period endpoint already implements it. P16 must define
the treatment of suppression, effective donor changes, releases, disputed
authority, historical corrections and late-entered evidence. It must also retain
D9's certified non-overlap between recurring and linked fixed commitments.
If only a narrower historical population is qualified, offer it under its exact
label; keep an independently qualified received summary usable. Do not substitute
today's schedules, raw rows or an invented reconstruction.

For ordinary use, a compact period list/table with the exact dates, currency,
metric name and source currentness is sufficient. A chart is an optional
presentation of the same authorized cells; a line between missing points must
not manufacture observations. No frozen “as originally reported” comparison,
audit-event browser, old statement, donor drill-down, historical goal series or
new financial engine is necessary to satisfy D10.

**Selected earlier dates remain those dates.** Resolving a named choice into an
exact interval/set at review and saving that scope implements the selected
earlier-period choice. Reopening the view or reaching a new month does not shift
the selection backward or admit a new future period. A rolling profile would
authorize an evolving range and needs its own explicit qualified product and
sharing contract. This note does not select one. The access duration of the
fixed history is also independent and remains outside this choice.

## Eight material concern dimensions

Severity is potential harm. Likelihood is conditional design risk, not a measured
incident rate. Clauses below are proposed D10 amendments unless explicitly
identified as inherited.

### FH01 — Historical period versus historical knowledge

**High / likely if “history” is implemented by reopening old snapshots.**
D9 C04 and P7 **242–248/305** distinguish a current effective view from immutable
facts and official documents. A September amount viewed in October can differ
from the September screen without either source record being edited in place.
Calling the refreshed amount “what was received as of September 30” falsely
claims a historical knowledge cutoff.

**Clause:** “Earlier received summaries SHALL retain D9's current-effective
gift-date-cohort definition and expose their current source watermark. They
SHALL NOT be represented as frozen period-close, as-known-at, official statement
or previously seen values. Retained snapshots remain evidence/cache under their
owner and are not a competing current financial source.”

**Proofs:** an August gift first imported in October belongs to August; a later
source reversal reduces the effective August gift amount; an old cached number
cannot replace the current qualified fold merely because its period is old.
An as-known-at feature would need a separately qualified source cutoff across
money, dating, classification and schedule revisions, not a filter on row
creation timestamps. D10 does not require that additional product.

### FH02 — Today's commitment state can erase or fabricate the past plan

**High / likely if historical cards reuse a current active-commitment query.**
P16 **584–599**, **1567–1587**, **1621–1623** preserves terms/epochs and immutable
occurrence-line grain. A donor doubling support in October must not double the
August plan; cancelling future support cannot remove an August expectation.
A received installment is no longer unfulfilled but was still planned.

**Clause:** “Historical planned support SHALL derive from owner-certified
historically applicable dated expectation facts, not current active terms,
current unfulfilled capacity, today's monthly equivalent or current provider
subscription state. Normal later fulfillment, miss, retry or future lifecycle
change SHALL NOT erase the historical planned amount. Authorized factual
corrections apply only through the P16 historical metric contract.”

**Proofs:** amount/cadence/destination change; January-31 short-month recovery;
cancel after the selected period; pause spanning a scheduled date; skip before
claim; retry next month; late success proving a preclosure attempt; and one
fixed pledge linked to recurring support. Distinguish suppression effective
for the named date from a later cancellation of future dates.

**Required owner extension:** P16 must certify the exact historical inclusion
fold, including effective end/release and dispute/correction treatment. P16's
“Original/past/applied/money facts never change” (**1362**) preserves evidence;
it is not permission for P28 to ignore a valid source correction. A source-safe
unavailable state is preferable to an invented original-versus-final plan rule.

### FH03 — Late facts, inverses and dating corrections change different axes

**High / plausible with date-based cache invalidation alone.** P7 **248/305**
makes a date correction a separate successor without a money delta; P13
**1763** keeps that correction out of money postings. P16 **1027–1031** preserves
exact inverses and uncertainty rather than guessing allocations. P16 **1350**
allows evidence-backed past expected dates; entry time is not represented time.

**Clause:** “Historical summaries SHALL invalidate on every relevant owner
money, dating, expectation, allocation, classification and coverage revision.
Late intake SHALL retain the source represented date and proven historical
meaning. A date correction SHALL move the qualified amount between its source
date cohorts without creating another gift; a money inverse SHALL change the
effective source amount without inventing a gift date.”

**Proofs:** late imported gift; date corrected August→September; full and partial
refund; pending refund/dispute without finalized money effects; source
redesignation; ambiguous reversal; late entered historical commitment with and
without adequate source evidence. If a correction moves a fact outside the
granted range, update the permitted result without exposing its new hidden
period, subject, donor or correction details. No automatic donor recontact,
debt, reenrollment or revived collection follows.

### FH04 — Current identity or assignment is not historical allocation authority

**High / plausible when joins follow current membership.** P13 **811–815**,
**1896** explicitly keeps fund merges from repointing historical gifts. P16
**1567–1569**, **1621** pins destination at the applicable term/occurrence.
P21 **2852–2863** keeps Support Assignment and source financial facts distinct
from the P28 coaching goal/context. Current source permission is still required.

**Clause:** “Resolve each historical result through its exact current authorized
subject/profile and owner-qualified historical designation/allocation mapping.
Do not replace source attribution with a current missionary, household, team,
project membership, merge target or renamed display record. Legitimate source
corrections and approved alias resolution retain provenance and never transfer
a grant to another subject.”

**Proofs:** worker moves assignment; a project is renamed or merged; two subjects
share a contribution header; corrected designation changes one subject's amount;
coach newly gains a different subject; a retired source designation still has
eligible history. Scope each allocation once; do not sum overlapping subject
cards into portfolio cash. E7-dependent appeal history remains unavailable until
that exact owner conflict is resolved, without blocking unrelated history.

### FH05 — Calendar, currency and comparison baselines can silently drift

**High / plausible across settings changes and imported schedules.** P7 **246**
freezes the issuer's dating rules; P16 **497–531** freezes schedule zone/rules
per epoch and forbids guessing an imported zone. P16 **1152** supplies exact
local-window and currency semantics. P21 **3044–3055** does not synchronize goals.

**Clause:** “Every admitted historical period SHALL retain explicit boundaries,
source dating basis, governing calendar/timezone evidence, currency and metric
version. Preserve civil-date facts and historical epoch rules; do not recast them
in today's tenant/browser timezone. Cross-period comparisons require compatible
definitions and coverage. Neither historical goal versions, a ratio, FX total
nor a rolling benchmark follows from permission to read earlier summary cells.”

**Proofs:** issuer and giving timezones differ; tenant zone changed after the
period; DST and leap day; date-only offline gift; imported schedule lacking
zone evidence; weekly versus every-four-week cadence; two currencies; goal
changed midperiod; one partial month compared to a complete month. A source
qualification may admit an explicitly labeled comparison, but the dashboard
must not silently normalize these differences or call them changed effort.

### FH06 — Retention, coverage and an empty period are different facts

**High / likely if SUM-empty becomes zero or cache length becomes access policy.**
P16 **1152–1158**, **2530–2539** requires source-proved coverage and permits
rebuilding discarded dashboard caches from durable sources. Retained normalized
facts, raw evidence retention, legal hold and coach access each have different
owners. A retained snapshot is not proof of complete history.

**Clause:** “A historical zero requires current, complete admitted coverage for
that exact metric and period. Missing, partial, suppressed, unavailable or
unreconstructible history SHALL NOT be converted to zero. A removed cache MAY
be rebuilt only through the current qualified source; cache retention SHALL NOT
set coach scope or a new financial retention policy.”

**Proofs:** cache absent but source complete; source import begins midperiod;
partial import repaired later; retained source but no current read authority;
valid zero; legal-hold data; eliminated raw payload with sufficient normalized
source facts; insufficient historical schedule evidence. Show a safe preparing
or unavailable result when needed and keep other permitted periods useful.
Do not invent a retention extension, deletion rule or lookback duration.

### FH07 — A reviewed earlier range can silently become rolling future access

**High / likely if a saved “last N periods” UI expression is reevaluated forever.**
Q10 selects earlier periods with exact dates before save. D8/D9 require explicit
historical scope and current release checks. Period membership is a different
axis from whether access remains active today. Repeated financial releases also
create inference risk under ratified D9 C07.

**Clause:** “The accepted historical scope SHALL resolve to the exact reviewed
earlier dates/periods and admitted metric profile. Calendar rollover, cache
refresh, a newly imported older fact or a new source field SHALL NOT enlarge
that scope. Newly available facts inside that scope may refresh only under the
same current source meaning, coverage and disclosure checks. A moving range or
standing right to new future periods requires a separately explicit qualified
contract and deliberate sharing intent.”

**Proofs:** save August–September then reopen in October/November; late fact
dated within range vs outside range; profile adds a new field; source date moves
outside range; narrow/revoke while history loads; ended share is reenabled;
two overlapping authorized intervals expose a previously suppressed gift.
The mechanism must not freeze financial truth merely to freeze the date scope.
No date count, default, access expiry or mandatory reconfirmation cadence is
selected. Extending a scope uses the existing authorized sharing flow.

### FH08 — A historical coaching view can accidentally become a statement or write tool

**High / plausible when immutable snapshots are treated as account closes.**
P21 **37–42** preserves closed cycles and sends late facts to later occurrences;
P7 **242–244** separates official artifacts and live summaries. D9 is a
read-only gift/expectation projection, so it neither reopens financial closes
nor creates official corrected statements. Source effective corrections can
change D9 figures while leaving the original archived artifact intact.

**Clause:** “Historical coaching summaries SHALL remain source-qualified live
reads with clear represented dates and source currentness. They SHALL NOT create
or amend accounting closes, statements, receipts, commitments, collection,
goal history or source correction authority. Show corrected/current values and
safe completeness states without presenting a fiscal statement, frozen audit
snapshot or guaranteed historical record.”

**Proofs:** later refund changes D9 gift-date result while original P21 closed
statement remains unchanged; historical read has no writes; refresh during
source revision is consistent or explicitly updating; old response cannot
replace a newer scope/result; a missing point is neither zero nor a connected
interpolated chart value. Missionary/coach should be able to explain which dates
they are viewing, why a value may change, and why it need not equal an old
statement—without seeing private correction details or a technical event log.

## Owner work required before activation

- **P7/P13:** extend the already ratified D9 exact received producer to eligible
  earlier gift-date cohorts, pinning current dating and money cursors, correct
  allocation, source coverage and current disclosure. No new money/date authority.
- **P16:** qualify one bounded historical planned-support profile using existing
  authoritative versions and occurrence identities. Explicitly define historical
  suppression/release/dispute/correction and late-evidence handling, plus D9
  cross-kind non-overlap; prove current status cannot rewrite past meaning.
- **P28/P12 and source disclosure owners:** persist/review exact historical
  scope in the existing share, enforce current input/output admission and
  combined temporal inference rules, and make represented period distinct from
  source watermark and access duration.
- **P21/P7 document owners:** preserve their distinct immutable closed-cycle/
  statement behavior. They are not required dependencies for every D9/D10 gift
  summary, and this choice grants no balance or document access.

This research executed no application, financial source, hosted Supabase or
provider test. The separate mechanics probe receives already-qualified synthetic
facts and can test date membership/currentness/scope races; it cannot qualify
P7 dating, P13 money, P16 historical plan reconstruction, data completeness or
privacy. No general event-history service, bitemporal query engine, new financial
ledger, extra approval queue or perpetual audit dashboard is proposed.
