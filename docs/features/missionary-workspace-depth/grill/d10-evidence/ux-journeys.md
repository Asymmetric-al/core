All journeys and acceptance scenarios below are proposed, not implemented or run.

**Recommended smallest complete experience:** in the existing coaching-sharing screen, include earlier support periods deliberately, choose a permitted first reporting period, show the exact ending period and calendar dates, review the effective view, then Save once. The coach browses only those authorized periods inside the existing subject's support summary. A small period control and two honest amounts are enough; a report builder and two-calendar date-range picker are unnecessary.

**Important qualification:** a single “History starts” control with a moving “through the latest completed period” is an ongoing grant, not merely a selected past range. For the initially selected earlier-periods direction, this note recommends freezing the represented start/end periods at review/save, with a simple deliberate extension later. That is a recommended bounded implementation profile, not an already ratified range-progression decision. Fixed-start accumulation and rolling history remain different product policies; neither is silently selected here. No month count, initial inclusion default, access term or retention period is invented.

## Three different date policies: make the consequence explicit

| Policy                                                          | What is granted at Save                                                                                               | What happens when the next reporting period ends                                                                       | UX and privacy tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Frozen selected earlier range — recommended initial profile** | Concrete first and final periods, with exact represented dates.                                                       | Nothing is added merely because time passes. Extending the range is an explicit current-authority sharing change.      | Most predictable interpretation of selected earlier periods and easiest exact review. Ongoing review across new periods takes a small additional action; do not disguise that cost.                                                                  |
| **Fixed start with future accumulation**                        | An explicit standing rule that adds each newly eligible ended period after a fixed start, while access remains valid. | History grows. Its subject/modules remain fixed, but the represented dates expand under that originally approved rule. | Convenient for sustained coaching; larger future disclosure and potentially growing query/privacy scope. Needs a deliberately accepted progression rule, finite source limits and truthful preview. A start-only label does not supply that consent. |
| **Rolling set of recent periods**                               | A declared moving window under a defined calendar and count/profile.                                                  | A new period enters and an old one leaves.                                                                             | Keeps a short view, but automatically adds disclosure and removes reference context. It also introduces an unchosen count. Do not copy “last 6/12/13 months” or use an unexplained rolling preset.                                                   |

The second and third models are not inherently incorrect. They require different explicit product/source contracts and visible wording. This note recommends only the first as the bounded initial profile for the selected earlier-periods choice; formal ratification/qualification still belongs to the parent review. Do not ship a three-policy dropdown merely because the analysis distinguishes them.

**Is one start-period selector ending at the latest completed period good?** Yes, if “latest” is resolved to a concrete source-governed period during review and the exact end remains pinned through Save and later reads. Prefer **Earlier support periods — From [period], through [named period]** over **History starts**, which sounds ongoing. “Period ended” is a calendar fact, not a promise that finance has closed, finalized or completely imported its records.

For a fresh range, one first-period control plus an explicit proposed ending period is a good primary path. For an existing saved range, reopening the editor must retain the saved end; it must not reset it to today's latest ended period. If the admitted profile permits a shorter earlier interval, a secondary **Change end period** can reveal the same bounded period control for the end. Do not force someone to share newer periods merely to include an older one. No arbitrary day-range builder, noncontiguous cherry-picking interface or dynamic user formula follows from this recommendation.

## Three meanings of time, kept distinct

1. **Represented dates:** which source reporting periods the summary describes. Gifts follow the accepted gift-date basis; expected support follows qualified occurrence dates/historical meaning. Newly imported older gifts stay in their older represented period.
2. **Evaluation/currentness:** through which source facts/corrections a displayed number is calculated. Fixed represented dates do not freeze the numbers. A source-corrected past total is not necessarily what the coach would have seen at that past time. Historical planned support cannot be reconstructed silently from today's commitments.
3. **Access validity:** when this coach may open the approved view. The end of the represented period is not access expiry, and ending current access does not erase source financial history. D10 does not choose the access term or retention period.

Use the represented period beside the numbers, source currentness near the result or its explanation, and existing access terms in **View sharing**. Do not place an unlabeled **End date** beside the selector that could mean either financial coverage or permission expiry. Authoritative source period definitions—not the coach's device clock, locale, import timestamp or invitation date—govern the boundaries. Localization changes presentation without moving a source gift date.

## Proposed complete journey

All named months/dates below are illustrative for an already admitted calendar-month profile. They are neither a recommended lookback length nor a universal calendar choice. Other qualified period types must use their own names and exact boundaries.

### 1. Include and review earlier periods in the existing Sharing screen

```text
Hope Mission · Coaching                         Maya Chen
Sharing with Jordan Lee · Community Ministry

Support summary
[Current saved inclusion state]

[✓] Include earlier support periods
From [July 2026 ▾]
Through September 2026                          [Change end period]

Jordan will be able to view permitted support summaries for
1 July–30 September 2026.
Later periods are not added automatically.

[Review coaching view]
                                [Cancel] [Save sharing]
```

The checked value is a deliberate draft example, not an initial default. Only source-eligible period choices are offered; control options cannot be a census of hidden financial records. Selecting a period updates the draft and the visible exact-date summary, not the coach's access. The end is a concrete reviewed value, not a live relative expression. The optional end control is shown only if the qualified profile supports it; a concise finite alternative profile is also acceptable where exact source policy requires one.

The existing current-period summary remains separately scoped. A historical-range edit must show if it affects that current scope, rather than silently converting it to a fixed past range. Do not add the current period to the history selection merely because it is visible elsewhere. The review says which approved measures/currencies are represented and identifies safe unavailable/incomplete parts without claiming every cell is complete.

Preview comes from the same proposed authorized projection, limited also by the previewer's own authority. It must not show the coach's independent staff access or the missionary's broader records. Selecting a wider past range cannot reveal old check-ins, conversations, donor details or historical goal versions. Those are outside D10.

### 2. One Save, with a truthful source outcome

During submission show **Saving sharing…** and retain the exact reviewed range. Confirmed success: **“Earlier support periods shared: 1 July–30 September 2026.”** Persist the saved scope on the screen. Definite rejection: **“Sharing was not changed. [Safe reason.]”** Unknown/lost response: **“We couldn't confirm the sharing change.”** with **Check status**; do not blindly replay an older edit or show local success.

If organization limits, source scope or the saved sharing version changed, show **“Sharing options changed. Review the current dates before saving.”** Never silently clamp, extend or replace the user's selected dates and report the original selection as saved. If the calendar rolls over while the draft is open, the reviewed end stays unchanged. The user may deliberately choose a new eligible end, then review that exact addition.

### 3. Coach reads earlier support summaries

```text
Hope Mission · Coaching                         Jordan Lee
Maya Chen · Community Ministry                  View only

Support summary                                 [View sharing]
Earlier periods shared: 1 July–30 September 2026

[Previous shared period] [August 2026 ▾] [Next shared period]
1–31 August 2026 · USD

Online recurring planned in this period        USD [qualified amount]
Received support                               USD [qualified amount]
[Approved historical basis and source currentness]

[About these numbers]                           [Refresh]
```

Use the source-qualified labels from D9: the automatic-only planned subset must not become all expected support. The received and planned populations may differ, so period alignment does not itself authorize a paid-versus-due ratio. The coach selector filters within current authorization; it never changes or enlarges the sharing grant. Navigation does not request periods outside the allowed set or auto-jump to an unshared month.

At a boundary, the previous/next action is visibly unavailable with a short safe explanation such as **First shared period**. A searchable period control is justified only for a sufficiently large offered list; otherwise a short selection or simple previous/next is enough. On phones retain the full selected period/year and meaningful accessible button names. Selection must not send notices, alter sharing, or imply a new follow-up task.

A compact comparison table may help read multiple approved periods without repeated navigation, but its full multi-period output needs the same D9 inference qualification as a chart. A line chart is optional, not a condition for completing the job. Missing/withheld points are not zeros, and connecting them as a continuous known trend must not imply values the source cannot establish. Accessible alternatives expose exactly the permitted precision and periods, never hidden raw cells.

### 4. Change, extend or remove earlier periods

The missionary returns to the saved range. Moving the first period later narrows the range. A permitted end adjustment may narrow or extend it. Show a plain inline change summary, for example **“Adding October 2026”**, **“Removing July 2026”**, or the exact old/new intervals when more understandable. Selecting a different range is still a draft until Save; there is no new approval queue or report publication workflow.

For a deliberate extension after the next period ends:

```text
Earlier periods currently shared: 1 July–30 September 2026
[Include October 2026]

Proposed range: 1 July–31 October 2026
Adding: October 2026
Later periods will still require a sharing change.
                                [Cancel] [Save sharing]
```

The **Include** action prepares a draft, not an immediate permission change. Show it only when the new period and action are actually eligible; no fabricated date, outstanding-work badge or mandatory monthly task follows. If several periods have elapsed, present the exact proposed end and all added dates clearly rather than creating repeated dialogs. The user can retain the previous end.

Removing earlier periods returns a confirmed **“Earlier support periods removed from this coaching share.”** It preserves independently permitted current-period summaries and other coaching modules. It does not delete source records, revoke unrelated grants or recall information already observed. Current protected results, tabs, cache and browser Back must obey the narrowed scope; a stale in-flight response cannot restore a removed period. Preserve focus and route to an available safe view when the selected period disappears.

### 5. What happens next month?

Under the recommended frozen historical range, July–September remains July–September when October ends. The historical selector does not acquire October automatically. If a separate already-qualified current-period rule advances to November, it does so under that independent rule; D10 must neither create that rule nor freeze it accidentally. A previously viewed October current summary is not standing permission to serve October as browsable history later.

If the product instead explicitly chooses fixed-start accumulation, the initial review must say **“Share permitted periods from [date], including new periods as they end while this access remains active.”** It must show the concrete range available today, explain future additions, and qualify upper limits, changed source policy and accumulation/privacy/performance behavior before activation. With a rolling policy, explain both entry and removal: **“The available periods move forward; older periods leave this view.”** No such copy or control is offered unless that exact progression policy is adopted. Neither behavior renews or extends access expiry.

## Accessibility, clarity and scale

Keep `base-maia` and semantic tokens. Put the full exact date range outside truncated controls, with explicit years when a range crosses years. Field descriptions distinguish **period covered** from **access ends**. A form's date selection never auto-saves the sharing grant. Keyboard focus, popup Escape, selected option, inline errors and saved/unconfirmed status must be coherent; no toast-only result. Preserve phone/320px and 400% zoom reading order, touch targets, reduced motion and sticky-header focus visibility.

Use one bounded period list and the selected source-qualified summary; avoid loading all periods or donor rows to determine options in the browser. Keep historic evaluation/currentness separate from fetch time. Late responses from another period, subject, organization or scope version must not appear under the current label. A privacy-safe scope reduction should not break an unrelated qualified current view. These are proposed requirements, not measured performance or conformance results.
