## Useful direction to carry into the existing session

1. **A practical daily workspace.** Help missionaries find the right person,
   understand the next useful action, and finish work without repeatedly entering
   the same information. Preserve the shared CRM and source-owned Tasks Hub.
2. **Control that explains its consequences.** Put understandable help beside
   the relevant setting. Distinguish what the missionary chose, what the
   organization permits, and what a visitor can currently see. Public pages
   should not display dead controls or placeholders for disabled optional
   features; the authorized missionary still needs a clear way to understand
   and change the setting.
3. **Low-effort writing and supporter care.** Useful starting content, drafts,
   previews, explicit audiences and truthful outcomes matter more than copying
   a social platform. Publication, subscription and delivery remain different
   actions with their existing owners.
4. **Financial information people can trust.** Explain the goal, period,
   currency, source and freshness. Distinguish promises, received support and
   finance-confirmed balances; unavailable or incomplete data is not zero.
5. **Mobile usability and purposeful coaching.** Everyday work should be usable
   on a phone and resilient to interrupted connections. Coaching should help an
   invited missionary, with exact permissions and voluntary shared context.

These are interpretations of historical intent consistent with current
direction, not newly ratified requirements or claims that the runtime already
delivers them.

## Details deliberately not carried forward

**Obsolete stack prescriptions.** Twenty CRM is explicitly retired by
[ADR-0001](../../../adr/0001-asym-postgres-owns-crm-truth-twenty-retired.md).
Keycloak, WordPress, SendGrid, Unlayer, S3/CloudFront assumptions and proposed
QuickBooks/Xero synchronization in this attachment provide no authority to add
them. Existing compatible libraries mentioned in the document are not removed
either: necessity and compatibility are determined from current manifests and
owner contracts, not from being named in an old document. There are no package,
configuration or infrastructure changes from this intake.

**Unsafe shortcuts.** Reviewer absence does not approve publication. The current
tenant's qualified “Publish after checks” mode remains valid; it is different
from letting a required review expire into publication. A gift, follow, page
relationship or coach label grants neither private reading nor newsletter
consent. Reactions still need bounded abuse and duplicate protection. Giving
details, names and contact data retain their source-authorized disclosure limits.

**Speculative product machinery.** No duplicate social network, generic page
builder, custom moderation workflow, new notification inbox, email-export
pipeline, video-transcoding system, automatic milestone program or offline
replica follows from these examples. Existing required media, engagement,
communication, export and task capabilities remain with their current owners;
this is not a blanket removal of those capabilities.

**Invented precision.** The memo's 24-hour approval, 8–24-hour session, 30-day
donor, 50 MB/two-minute video, ten-post, thousand-donor and burst-load examples
are not accepted thresholds. Its “use best practices” responses are not evidence
of user demand, compliance, conversion benefit, performance or implementation.

## Consequences for the current decision and remaining coverage

- **D1–D5:** No reversal. Restricted preparation, deliberate sharing, no new
  continuity right, bounded archive and tenant-controlled ordinary corrections
  retain their recorded status and boundaries. The memo creates no new access,
  editing, export or recovery permission.
- **D6:** The emphasis on explained control and clean public presentation
  strengthens the proposed single Page signup control. It does not make entry
  Off mean unsubscribe, stop delivery, unpublish the Page or delete recipients.
  Historical reader notifications do not settle native signup confirmation,
  existing request migration, audience access or pending admission rules.
- **Remaining Phase 28 coverage:** Story reuse, digest/cadence scope, coaching,
  daily-work policy and qualified progress remain on the existing coverage map.
  This document supplies context, not their answers. Check simple public-page
  QR sharing against existing P22 sharing when that surface is considered; no
  separate founder questionnaire or feature commitment is needed now.
- **Terminology:** Public Page, Ministry Update, native newsletter subscription,
  supporter relationship, permitted financial view and coaching view remain
  distinct. “Feed” in this historical social-content memo also does not mean
  the separately governed external Missionary Support Feed projection.

No new glossary definition, ADR, formal specification, ticket or implementation
is justified by this intake. No D6 ratification is inferred. The document is
incorporated into the evidence register so subsequent questions can use its
useful intent without reopening settled boundaries or silently importing scope.
