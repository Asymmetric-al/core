# Q9 frontier: optional support-progress summaries for coaches

Research date: October 16, 2026 UTC. **Original question, now answered A.** The
founder selected optional support summaries and requested the completed
[D9 adversarial review](phase28-d9-adversarial-review.md). D8 and its amendments
are explicitly ratified. The founder also explicitly ratified D9 and its required
amendments on October 16, 2026 UTC. The original alternatives below remain question
provenance. Default inclusion, history and duration remain separate choices;
this research creates no financial access.

## The single product choice

**Should missionaries be able to include summary support progress in their
coach's view?**

- **A — Allow optional support summaries (recommended).** A missionary may
  deliberately share organization-permitted summaries of expected support from
  commitments and support actually received, clearly distinguished, alongside
  the goals they have chosen to share.
- **B — Keep those summaries out of the initial coaching view.** Retain shared
  goals, voluntary activity, discussion topics and agreed next steps; defer the
  native expected/received-support summary modules for coaches.

Illustrative scenario, not claimed user research: Maya and her coach want to
understand whether her support-raising plan is making progress. A lets them use
qualified source summaries instead of reconstructing amounts during each
conversation. B minimizes financial disclosure and integration scope, but gives
the coach less structured information about fundraising results.

Recommendation A is a product judgment supported by documented coaching work.
It does not establish measured demand, efficacy or a universal ministry method.

## Why this remains a real choice

- [Ratified D8](phase28-d8-adversarial-review.md) expressly leaves optional
  information modules separate. C10 sets truthful financial semantics when a
  module is permitted; it does not select a money-summary entitlement. C12
  supplies disclosure safeguards, not additional financial authority.
- [Retained owner constraints](../../../prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md)
  and the [roadmap](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md#phase-28-open-decisions)
  anticipate support progress and trends. B is an explicit narrower initial
  coaching disclosure scope, not a claim that Phase 28 has no financial-progress
  obligations. Either option preserves independently authorized missionary views.
- The [D8 dashboard evidence](d8-evidence/dashboard-truth.md) separates P28 goals,
  P16 expectations, P13 receipts, P14 recognition and P21 account facts. A monetary
  goal is already potentially sensitive; B should not be mislabeled entirely
  “nonfinancial.”
- [MPDX's coaching instructions](https://help.mpdx.org/article/510-for-mpd-coaches)
  and [reports guidance](https://help.mpdx.org/article/1253-mpdx-quick-reference-guide-reports),
  inspected October 16, 2026, document financial progress as part of coaching.
  Their broader display details and privacy claims are not Core authorization.
- [Tailored's services](https://www.tailored.fund/services) document personalized
  planning and practical fundraising support; the [D8 practice ledger](d8-evidence/coach-practice.md)
  retains dates, primary evidence and limits. Published services are not observed
  Asym user feedback.

## Boundaries already settled, without another question

The organization controls what can be shared; the missionary deliberately shares
within that ceiling. The coach remains read-only and sees only the exact
authorized subject in the active organization. No donor names, individual gifts,
contact details, private history, Field Account balances, compensation, exports
or cross-subject financial totals follow from summary eligibility.

“Summary” does not mean anonymous. Source-owned projection and inference checks
must qualify small groups, combinations and ratios before disclosure. Expected
and received support stay distinct, with correct currency, period and freshness.
Missing or unauthorized data cannot become zero. The UI must show the actual
sharing scope and meaningful unavailable states.

This question decides whether these native summary modules may be offered. It
does not select default inclusion, a history window, access duration, arbitrary
date-range filters, a custom field-permission builder or a different financial
source of truth. A trend necessarily contains history; its precise range belongs
after the information-scope choice. B neither prohibits a real-life conversation
nor licenses copying protected financial records into free text.
