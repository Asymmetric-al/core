# D21 evidence — giving history as context, not automatic qualification

Research date: **October 17, 2026 UTC**. The founder selected **A: explicit
qualifying facts only**. A gift being larger than historical gifts alone creates
no automatic task. Permitted history remains useful context; qualified native
special/above-commitment work and deliberate manual follow-up remain available.
This note recommends the simple experience for that decision. It selects no
remaining financial criteria, numerical threshold, lookback or source classifier.

This is public-document research and local source inspection, not an authenticated
competitor trial, user interview, financial qualification or implementation. No
application, dependency, live account, provider or donor data was changed.

## Minimal intended flow

**View the gift and its permitted history → open existing relevant follow-up or
deliberately add ordinary follow-up → act or defer → finish the personal task.**
A qualified automatic task enters that same native task experience with a clear
source reason. A history-only difference adds no task, badge, warning, ranking or
special inbox. No confirmation is needed merely to read history or create a
reversible ordinary task through the established owner.

The main recommendation is restraint in the ordinary screen. Do not place a
“Not special” or “No follow-up needed” label on every gift lacking an automatic
task. That absence is not a verdict on the gift or the relationship, and source
qualification may be unavailable or still being reconciled. A short explanation
of automatic task behavior belongs in contextual help when useful, rather than
repeated warning copy on every row.

Keep the form and history in the established surface. When returning from a task,
retain safe authorized filter/position context. Creating a manual task does not
require a second “Are you sure?” dialog. Extra details are optional; preserve
existing owner validation instead of inventing another task-type questionnaire.
Do not auto-insert financial facts into a free-text task description where later
source correction or permission revocation would leave an uncontrolled copy.

An optional explanation of the selected policy can say: **“Automatic follow-ups
use qualifying gift facts. A change from earlier giving alone does not create
one. You can add your own follow-up.”** Show this in appropriate help/context,
not as repetitive disclaimer copy. Do not state that explicit qualification is
implemented or that every currently absent task has been checked successfully.

### Manual work and subsequently qualified native work

Showing existing authorized work is helpful; deciding semantic equivalence is an
owner operation. The smallest honest design reuses current native coordination
and exact source-purpose coverage. It neither automatically merges all tasks for
Grace nor forces a second task when the same qualified deliverable already has
an eligible task/receipt. A genuinely different promise or contact purpose stays
distinct. If equivalence is unresolved, do not silently suppress the required
native occurrence or declare the manual task covers it. Preserve the manual work
and route necessary reconciliation through the existing owner, without adding a
mandatory missionary review queue.

A later source correction cannot erase an actual interaction or independently
accepted manual undertaking. Conversely, a human manually choosing a task title
such as “Thank for special gift” cannot authorize source qualification, financial
application, sending or automatic-purpose coverage. Unknown sender/provider
outcome remains D15's concern; task completion does not turn it into delivered.

## Alternatives and deliberate exclusions

The strongest alternative, B, is a clearly explained history-based review signal:
it could reveal useful changes among supporters without commitments. Its costs
include history completeness, currency/period interpretation, false-positive
attention and new source-purpose coordination. These are reasoned tradeoffs;
no opened primary source supplies measured benefit or error rates for this Core
case. Founder-selected A excludes that automatic attention product while keeping
the underlying history and human initiative useful.

A stricter alternative that hides history or removes contextual task creation
would reduce accidental inference but unnecessarily obstruct ordinary relationship
work. A generic disclaimer on every row is also unnecessary. A positive design
can simply present factual source data, readable history and deliberate actions.
Do not bring back B as a badge, sorted “priority donors” list, color heatmap,
inferred generosity score, suggested task or recommendation email. Nor should A
be broadened to commitments-only or used to disable required special-gift work.

## Mobile, accessibility and international presentation

Use a short readable list or a simple semantically marked table. A table's date,
amount/currency and meaning must stay associated with its row; a phone stacked
list preserves those same labels. Keep date/filter/task actions visible without
hover. Readable text identifies gift/task/interaction activity; color or icons
may support it but cannot be the only distinction. Dates use unambiguous localized
display and retain the source's date meaning. Viewer timezone affects presentation
of timestamps, not source financial chronology. Do not infer timezone from locale
or silently mix currencies in an average or total.

The existing Core touch guidance calls for 44-pixel touch targets; WCAG 2.2 AA's
target-size minimum is 24 CSS pixels with specified exceptions. Do not conflate
these. Preserve reflow, zoom, visible focus and stable reading order as history
loads or filters change. Retain a sensible focus return when the task form closes.
Visible labels must occur in accessible names for speech input. Long and RTL
names, multiple scripts and currencies must not hide the essential action or
financial meaning. Avoid Western-name assumptions.

Announce material filter results, task creation and unresolved save status using
appropriate semantics without moving focus unnecessarily. W3C's status-message
criterion applies to qualifying messages, not every changing row. Display
recovery and saved results persistently where useful; a disappearing toast is
not the only evidence. Slow/failed loading must not replace known facts with
false zeros. Maintain ordinary manual work when its own owner/permission permits,
even if an unrelated history/qualification view is temporarily unavailable.

## Eight-dimensional concerns, exact proposed clauses and proofs

Severity describes consequences; likelihood is a qualitative design assessment,
not observed frequency. The eight dimensions in each concern are explicit.
Clauses are proposed language for synthesis. Proofs are future acceptance checks;
none were executed against a UI or database in this research workstream.

### H01 — History inference returns through presentation

1. **Failure:** A comparison creates a badge, suggested task, urgency sort or
   notification even though automatic history-based tasks were rejected.
2. **Importance:** The same unqualified inference changes attention and donor treatment.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible if A is interpreted as merely disabling one writer.
5. **Evidence:** Selected Q21 A; E03 shows vendor intelligence features are
   separable from history; reused MPDX/Tnt docs do not qualify Core classifiers.
6. **Decision impact:** Preserve A across every derivative, not just task insertion.
7. **Permanent fix:** Explicit purpose boundary; source facts/context only.
8. **Exact proposed clause HUX01:** “Historical amount/pattern difference alone
   SHALL NOT generate automatic tasks, recommended work, anomaly labels, urgency
   ranking or notification attention. Permitted source history SHALL remain
   available as context without inferred generosity, commitment or donor intent.”

**Proof:** Change earlier gift amounts, load additional historical pages, import
history and change the displayed average. None alone creates/reprioritizes work
or alerts; ordinary history remains accessible within current permissions.

### H02 — No task is mistaken for no duty, no gift or no commitment

1. **Failure:** A filtered, delayed or failed projection renders “nothing to do,”
   “not special,” zero history or “no commitment.”
2. **Importance:** False certainty hides real qualified work and misstates finances.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Likely if a single empty-state component treats all causes alike.
5. **Evidence:** E04's distinct history bases; E05's distinct empty/error types;
   Q21 owner constraints on qualified absence and incomplete projection.
6. **Decision impact:** A excludes one inference, not required native work.
7. **Permanent fix:** Qualified empty/partial/updating/unavailable states with scoped copy.
8. **Exact proposed clause HUX02:** “Absent visible work SHALL NOT prove absence
   of a gift, commitment, source qualification or follow-up need. Empty, filtered,
   partial, updating and unavailable states SHALL remain distinct. A qualified
   native occurrence SHALL retain its admission/recovery path despite D21.”

**Proof:** Compare true empty, excluded filters, missing history, denied source,
known update and service error. No false zero/negative claim; admitted task intent
still materializes and history-only difference alone still does not admit one.

### H03 — Context becomes misleading financial evidence

1. **Failure:** A page average is labeled expected support; dates/currencies or
   recognition are mixed; an allocation is shown as the whole gift.
2. **Importance:** A visually factual chart can still imply false financial meaning.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible when reusing generic aggregate/chart components.
5. **Evidence:** E01/E04 and reused MPDX report distinguish fields and bases;
   founder brief explicitly separates history, expected support and recognition.
6. **Decision impact:** Keep useful history without selecting any new calculation.
7. **Permanent fix:** Owner-qualified period/currency/scope labels and data provenance.
8. **Exact proposed clause HUX03:** “Historical rows and summaries SHALL preserve
   their qualified source, subject, date, currency, amount basis and coverage.
   Historical averages SHALL NOT be represented as commitments, guaranteed income,
   automatic eligibility or complete lifetime history without the owning proof.”

**Proof:** Same-date gifts, partial import, split allocation, recognition versus
money, multi-currency, returned gift and locale/timezone change retain accurate
meaning. One page cannot establish lifetime counts or a historical baseline.

### H04 — Manual follow-up impersonates native coverage or duplicates it

1. **Failure:** A manual title/source link suppresses future native work; or a
   generic duplicate check merges different promises; or an equivalent action
   produces repetitive work despite qualified shared coverage.
2. **Importance:** Either necessary work disappears or the simple queue becomes noisy.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with contact-only or text-based duplicate matching.
5. **Evidence:** E01/E02 support contextual task entry, not equivalence; inspected
   writer lacks demonstrated source-purpose coverage; D19/D20 retain exact owners.
6. **Decision impact:** Preserve both manual initiative and required native work.
7. **Permanent fix:** Existing owner-qualified compatibility/coverage and authorized
   existing-task links, not a new duplicate wizard or second ledger.
8. **Exact proposed clause HUX04:** “Manual creation SHALL NOT establish native
   qualification or source-purpose coverage. Existing manual/native work SHALL
   coordinate only through qualified exact purpose, scope and outcome evidence.
   Contact, title, amount or date similarity SHALL NOT suppress or merge distinct
   work. Unknown equivalence SHALL NOT silently discard required native work.”

**Proof:** Create manual work before/after native admission; complete it, rename
it, retry it and add a different promise for the same supporter. Prove legitimate
shared coverage only with owner evidence and preserve distinct obligations.

### H05 — Helpful context leaks protected existence

1. **Failure:** “Existing task,” count, missing commitment explanation or history
   coverage reveals inaccessible records, staff activity or restricted amounts.
2. **Importance:** Permission-safe rows do not make derived explanations safe.
3. **Severity:** Critical.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible with broad dedupe/history queries and stale responses.
5. **Evidence:** D20 disclosure/hidden-residue contract; E05 generic permission
   guidance does not authorize disclosing the reason in Core.
6. **Decision impact:** Narrow projection to current permission, not underlying truth.
7. **Permanent fix:** Reauthorize every context, link, count, explanation and response.
8. **Exact proposed clause HUX05:** “History, absence claims, related-work hints,
   counts, source links and saved responses SHALL obey current disclosure rights.
   A task or contact link SHALL grant no additional financial, history or contact
   right. Hidden existence SHALL NOT be exposed by duplicate or unavailable copy.”

**Proof:** Hidden staff task, restricted commitment, anonymous gift, tenant switch
and access revoke while loading/saving yield no protected name/count/reason.
Known safe manual work remains available through its own current authority.

### H06 — Simple follow-up becomes reporting or implied outreach

1. **Failure:** Add follow-up requires justification, notes or donor rating;
   completion records a thank-you or source correction deletes human work.
2. **Importance:** Violates voluntary relationship work and honest task meaning.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible if vendor logging/automation semantics are copied.
5. **Evidence:** E02 distinct Add/Log paths; D19 independent-task contract and
   D20 exact coverage; E06's configurable milestones are not Core authority.
6. **Decision impact:** Keep A useful without a new review/reporting product.
7. **Permanent fix:** Compact deliberate creation, separate optional interaction,
   ordinary finish/defer/correction and retained independent undertakings.
8. **Exact proposed clause HUX06:** “Manual follow-up SHALL use ordinary shared
   tasks without mandatory narrative, rating or interaction recording. Creation
   or completion SHALL NOT send, record contact, change finance or certify special
   giving. Actual interaction and independent promises SHALL survive source
   correction under their owners; interaction audience SHALL be visible before save.”

**Proof:** Create/finish with no note or communication; cancel a phone handoff;
retain an actual call after gift correction. No automatic send, false interaction,
financial mutation or required coaching report is generated.

### H07 — Mobile and assistive technology lose fact/action context

1. **Failure:** Amount loses currency on phone, hover hides detail, changing rows
   move focus, icons alone identify type, or form/save feedback is inaccessible.
2. **Importance:** Users cannot make the same informed deliberate choice.
3. **Severity:** High for affected users.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible without dynamic manual checks.
5. **Evidence:** E03 text-label design; E07/E08 primary accessibility guidance;
   reused W3C criteria and current Core touch/UI contract.
6. **Decision impact:** A needs no new chart/table framework or mobile application.
7. **Permanent fix:** Shared semantic components, stable focus and visible labels.
8. **Exact proposed clause HUX07:** “History and contextual follow-up SHALL retain
   their scope, source meaning, labels and operability through mobile reflow,
   keyboard, screen reader, speech and touch. Essential facts/actions SHALL NOT
   rely solely on color, icons, hover or transient feedback. Material status
   messages SHALL be programmatically exposed without unnecessary focus changes.”

**Proof:** Narrow width, zoom, long/RTL names, multiple currencies, keyboard-only
Add/Open, screen-reader row context and focus restoration; new rows arrive during
review. Automated checks supplement rather than prove the whole experience.

### H08 — Network uncertainty repeats work or falsely confirms it

1. **Failure:** Creation commits but response is lost; retry creates another task;
   delayed history is labeled current or a failed save is optimistically confirmed.
2. **Importance:** Duplicate work and false confidence erode the calm queue.
3. **Severity:** High.
4. **Likelihood:** Plausible on ordinary weak mobile networks.
5. **Evidence:** D19/D20 receipt/recovery obligations; current manual writer is not
   evidence of qualified recovery; E05/E08 support truthful visible states only.
6. **Decision impact:** Preserve A with existing owner recovery, not offline machinery.
7. **Permanent fix:** Original-command reconciliation and explicit known/unknown UI.
8. **Exact proposed clause HUX08:** “An uncertain manual-creation outcome SHALL
   resolve through the original command before a competing creation is offered.
   The UI SHALL distinguish accepted, rejected and unknown outcomes and preserve
   safe authorized input/context. History loading failures SHALL NOT create zero
   facts, tasks or automatic communication retries.”

**Proof:** Drop the response after commit, double tap, reload, navigate and revoke
access during recovery. At most the qualified intended task exists; no guessed
success, unauthorized stale response, lost promise or send occurs.

## Qualification limits and useful future evaluation

No source establishes that a named donor's history is complete, a gift is special,
two tasks are equivalent, or the existing Core implementation meets these clauses.
Those proofs remain with P9/P12/P13/P14/P16/P28 and the shared task/communication
owners. No new financial rule is hidden in the proposed copy. Release should
prove that an explicit source-qualified task still appears, while merely changing
historical comparison data does not create attention anywhere.

A useful future qualitative check is to show one ordinary gift with a larger
historical amount, one independently qualified automatic follow-up, a partial
history view and one existing manual task. Ask participants what the facts mean,
why a task exists, how to add/open follow-up and whether finishing sends anything.
Include phone and assistive-technology journeys. Observe misunderstandings and
correct them; do not claim a universal sample-size rule or invented interview
results. This note proposes that evaluation, not evidence that it occurred.

Research verification is limited to structure, local links, clause presence and
whitespace. No application, source producer, database, provider, browser,
accessibility conformance or real-user test ran in this workstream.
