# D21 evidence — qualifying facts and financial truth

Research date: **2026-10-17, Asia/Bangkok**. The founder selected **A — Explicit
qualifying facts only**. Selection is not ratification of this review's proposed
clauses. D19 and D20 remain ratified. This is scoped owner research, not a formal
owner amendment, new criterion, implementation, financial query, or provider
qualification. Only this evidence note was authored; no executable test ran.

## Exact interpretation and recommended boundary

**Do not create additional automatic missionary follow-up whose deciding reason
is that this gift differs from the supporter's earlier giving.** A task labeled
“possible change worth reviewing” is included in that exclusion; avoiding a
confirmed-extra-support claim does not make the rejected automatic review purpose
acceptable. Adding the ordinary fact “a gift was received” to a history comparison
does not turn that comparison into an independently qualified special-gift reason.

“Explicit qualifying facts” does **not** mean raw, uncomputed data. P13's current
effective money fold, P14's recognition derivation, P16's authoritative
applications and permitted health evaluator, and D19's completeness-qualified
first-support conclusion remain their owners' valid facts. The distinction is
the accepted purpose and its qualified source contract, not whether SQL or code
calculated the value. Persisting an unsupported inference in a flag, score,
custom field, task reason, import label or workflow result cannot qualify it.

Required native special/above-commitment work remains required. It needs an exact
source-qualified criterion that establishes why this particular live gift is
eligible for that purpose. **The inspected sources do not yet settle a native
special-gift amount threshold, “staff marked special” record, blanket
non-recurring-gift rule, or historical anomaly formula.** D21 selects none of
them. Do not quietly substitute one, describe manual tasks as the completed
native feature, or permanently omit the required producer behind an indefinite
“qualification pending” label.

The remaining criterion decision and the source adapter's qualification are
different work. Product must settle any still-missing native special-gift
meaning; engineering must prove the exact approved predicate, evidence and
currentness. A known source fact such as received money or an applied amount is
an input, not automatically that complete predicate. This note records the
remaining boundary without asking another founder question or choosing its answer.
Even source-certified absence of a recorded commitment does not itself classify
every gift as special, extra or non-recurring. A missing row in the missionary's
visible list cannot establish that absence in the first place.

Permitted history remains useful context. Maya can deliberately create ordinary
independent follow-up under existing authority. That manual act is not a
financial classification, commitment application, native source admission, or
consumption of D20 gift-purpose coverage. Reuse of an existing compatible task
requires the shared owner's exact qualified command and evidence; matching a
title, description, donor or date is insufficient.

## Minimal source contract to carry forward

For each approved native criterion, require a named bounded producer contract
that binds the **actual contribution, exact relevant line coverage, typed ministry
subject, qualified follow-up Party/purpose, exact criterion and source revisions,
financial state, applicable commitment evidence and original live provenance**.
P13 owns money/corrections/allocation and exposes P7 dating facts; P14 owns
recognition; P16 owns commitment/application/attention truth. P28 owns purpose
qualification/admission. The shared task owner owns operational membership and
reviewed-set disposition/coverage. A generic payload field saying `qualified`
or a stored `special` bit is not the contract.

Retain current source invalidation and exact result evidence. P13's cursor alone
cannot certify unchanged P14 attribution or P16 terms/application. Source
posting remains independent of lagging recognition/task qualification. Missing
or ambiguous evidence holds only the affected admission; permitted received
facts, ordinary task work and independently qualified native purposes continue.
No unqualified claim that all special tasks work is allowed, and no blanket
financial shutdown follows from an unavailable special-gift predicate.

The source's accepted admission, required local evidence and projection intent
commit atomically; the existing shared task owner materializes membership
idempotently. Do not claim one cross-owner/provider transaction. A later source
correction updates/withdraws the generated requirement without a human Done;
qualified restoration of the same originally live unconsumed purpose retains
withdrawal/correction lineage even when the earlier withdrawal was correct at
its time. D20's current-group resolver and reviewed-set rules remain authoritative.
Policy-version changes and replay are not new gifts or new effect identities.

Keep the meaning of dates separate: the actual P7/P13 gift date, occurrence or
expectation date, recorded/imported time, event delivery, criterion activation,
qualification and task action time are not interchangeable. A delayed qualified
live occurrence may predate a closed task yet create newly uncovered current
work without changing its date or the closed receipt. Historical-only acquisition
does not become live because a policy now recognizes it or an event arrives today.

## Concerns — eight dimensions each

### QF21-01 — “Explicit” accidentally removes required native work

1. **Harm:** the flagship special/above-commitment feature disappears, becomes commitments-only, or is replaced with manual chores.
2. **Severity:** high product/operational regression.
3. **Likelihood:** high if a negative scope decision is implemented as blanket generator disablement.
4. **Cause:** confusing rejection of an additional history-only review purpose with rejection of native gift-driven work.
5. **Evidence:** S01; S02:16–31; S09 D20 C01; S12 roadmap:4837.
6. **Implication:** preserve the required producer and clearly expose its still-unsettled criterion/qualification obligation.
7. **Clause:** “D21 SHALL exclude automatic history-comparison-only missionary attention while preserving required source-qualified native special/above-commitment work. It SHALL NOT substitute commitments-only eligibility, manual-task availability, optional workflows or an indefinite placeholder for the native producer.”
8. **Proof:** an independently qualified special gift still admits D20 membership; a valid above-commitment occurrence still qualifies; required work runs without optional workflows; a history-only unusual gift creates no automatic task; unresolved criterion is not reported as implemented.

### QF21-02 — A saved flag launders the rejected inference

1. **Harm:** prohibited automatic history-only attention ships under a different field or label.
2. **Severity:** high decision-integrity/operational harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high if “explicit” is interpreted as “persisted” or “has a reason code.”
4. **Cause:** a comparison result is rebranded as source fact without an independent qualifying predicate.
5. **Evidence:** S02:14–30; S03:816–817; S06:1055–1068; S11 purpose separation.
6. **Implication:** review the deciding predicate/provenance, not just the output label.
7. **Clause:** “A stored flag, score, label, import field, custom property, workflow output or received-gift conjunct SHALL NOT convert a giving-history comparison into independently qualified special-gift evidence. The excluded automatic purpose includes uncertain-review wording and any indirect admission of the same intended attention.”
8. **Proof:** comparison→flag→task; comparison plus Received→task; AI summary→manual-looking auto-task; workflow renamed reason; ordinary source fold still permitted; no unused classifier or new enable toggle is introduced by A.

### QF21-03 — Required derived facts and existing suggestions are overblocked

1. **Harm:** first-support truth, lawful matching assistance or actual support health stops working.
2. **Severity:** high financial/operational correctness harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high if the implementation bans any historical query, derived number or suggestion.
4. **Cause:** confusing an excluded purpose with a forbidden data-processing technique.
5. **Evidence:** S03:816–817; S05:1015; S06:1055–1082; S09 D19 history; S11:171–180, 275–285.
6. **Implication:** scope the exclusion by exact purpose; preserve existing owner-qualified histories, projections and suggestions.
7. **Clause:** “D21 SHALL NOT prohibit P13 effective folds, P14 permitted recognition, D19 completeness-qualified firstness, P16 authoritative fulfillment/health or its explained Needs review matching suggestions. Suggestions SHALL still require owner authority before application. Existing separately governed staff analysis/review SHALL retain its own scope and SHALL not be copied into missionary ratings or the excluded P28 attention purpose.”
8. **Proof:** a prior gift prevents false D19 firstness; a history-assisted match suggestion remains actionable under P16; accepting it uses conserved authority; P16 health attention remains available; staff ratings stay staff-only; missionary unusual-history task remains absent.

### QF21-04 — Missing commitment or current excess becomes proof of extra support

1. **Harm:** a catch-up/advance/unmatched payment is mislabeled special or an old gift generates new thanks after a pledge reduction.
2. **Severity:** high relationship/data-integrity harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high with raw gift-minus-monthly-pledge comparisons.
4. **Cause:** treating missing visible data, current capacity, payment schedule and actual gift occurrence as equivalent.
5. **Evidence:** S05:968–1031; S06:1380–1393.
6. **Implication:** consume exact source-qualified comparison meaning; no generic subtraction or inferred no-commitment state.
7. **Clause:** “Missing or unmatched commitment evidence SHALL remain unknown for this comparison, not zero or extra support. Even owner-certified absence of a recorded commitment SHALL NOT itself classify a gift as special, extra or non-recurring. Applied above current commitment, expectation release, pledge reduction, pause/end, catch-up and advance allocation SHALL not independently manufacture a new special-gift occurrence. Any approved above-commitment criterion SHALL name its exact live gift, source application, comparison target, terms and currency.”
8. **Proof:** reduced pledge with unchanged gifts; two simultaneous commitments; one unmatched gift; proved no recorded commitment without another special predicate; partial application; future expectation coverage; recurring initial gift outside continuing series; authority-revoked application; ambiguous partial reversal.

### QF21-05 — New financial criteria are silently ratified

1. **Harm:** arbitrary thresholds or donor classifications govern work without the intended product decision.
2. **Severity:** high scope/financial-meaning harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high because familiar flags and vendor definitions look like ready-made facts.
4. **Cause:** borrowing P13 risk policy, P35 future configuration or an ordinary task/partner status as the missing predicate.
5. **Evidence:** S04:979–987; S12 DON-03:1233–1259 and MPD-01:2677–2703; S02:27–31.
6. **Implication:** retain a bounded unresolved criterion, distinct from engineering proof; do not create a general rules product.
7. **Clause:** “This decision SHALL NOT select an amount/multiplier/lookback, staff-marked-special field, non-recurring/partner-stage classifier, noncash conversion, or criterion-configuration permission. P13 posting-risk settings and later P35 recipes SHALL retain their own purposes. Any missing material native criterion SHALL be explicitly resolved through its owners before affected activation.”
8. **Proof:** tenant changes posting-risk amount with no stewardship effect; source missing special marker cannot invent one; ordinary task type thank_you does not admit a gift; P35 unavailable leaves qualified native work usable; document status distinguishes approved intent from unqualified implementation.

### QF21-06 — Money, recognition, recipient and scope collapse

1. **Harm:** wrong amount, duplicate gift, wrong person or hidden allocation is used to justify attention.
2. **Severity:** high; critical if restricted identity is disclosed.
3. **Likelihood:** high if a roster row, credit row or header total substitutes for exact source coverage.
4. **Cause:** treating legal donor, recognized supporter, follow-up recipient and ministry scope as interchangeable.
5. **Evidence:** S03:784–822; S07:325–449, 1052–1088; S08:1147–1177; S10.
6. **Implication:** keep D20's typed identity and exact recipient route; grouping never supplies missing eligibility.
7. **Clause:** “Qualification SHALL bind the exact business contribution, permitted relevant allocation, typed Tenant/Legal Entity ministry subject and source-qualified follow-up Party/purpose. Recognition SHALL not become money or contact authority. Currency and legal-versus-recognition meaning SHALL remain explicit; no mixed-currency total, household fan-out, hidden DAF inference or church-member correspondence is authorized.”
8. **Proof:** multiple same-subject lines remain one gift; split allocation includes a hidden destination; USD/JPY; fee cover; DAF sponsor/advisor; household credit; matched employee; church member with separate direct giving; anonymous recipient.

### QF21-07 — Source lag or correction fabricates current qualifying facts

1. **Harm:** work is admitted or completed using reversed money, obsolete attribution or superseded terms.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** high because P14 recognition may lag money and P16 has its own source revisions.
4. **Cause:** a persisted “qualified” result or single P13 cursor is treated as permanently sufficient.
5. **Evidence:** S03:792, 816–820; S05:983–995, 1027–1031; S07:345–375; S09 D20 C04/C07/C11.
6. **Implication:** maintain coherent qualification and current invalidation fences without coupling source posting to task availability.

2. **Severity:** high operational/relationship harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high if the consumer uses current eligibility, import time or new policy version as event identity.

7. **Clause:** “Historical-only import, backfill, reclassification or policy-version change SHALL not itself create new special-gift work. Preserve original live provenance for delayed delivery or qualified repair of an unconsumed occurrence. Do not alter gift dates or closed coverage receipts to fit arrival order. A new criterion version SHALL not mint a new effect identity or revive handled work.”
8. **Proof:** old import; repeated import; historical redesignation; lower threshold applied to old gifts; late originally live admission after another task closed; source-date correction; same gift requalified repeatedly; newer source restriction restored before replay.

3. **Likelihood:** medium/high when a broad data-unavailable fallback or privileged history scan is used.

8. **Proof:** direct qualified allocation works while unrelated campaign mapping is unresolved; correction request cannot bypass C-01; staff rating remains unavailable to missionary; restricted old history neither appears nor becomes a misleading zero; source access revoked during review or receipt recovery.

## Result and evidence limits

A is coherent with the accepted owners when narrowly applied to the additional
history-only attention purpose. Its strongest alternative remains a separate,
clearly uncertain comparison-review product that can notice changes for supporters
without commitments. That benefit does not prove its value, error rate or
source readiness, and the founder did not select it.

The permanent minimal path is to preserve owner-qualified native facts and
existing manual work, exclude the rejected automatic purpose at admission, and
finish the exact native criterion/producer contract through the existing owners.
There is no need for a new classifier, special-gift boolean, shadow ledger,
manual approval queue or generic rules engine to implement this choice.

All proofs above are required future qualification cases. The inspected current
task paths establish only their local behavior; accepted PRDs and OpenSpec prove
intent, not deployment. No actual financial qualifier, P12 enforcement, database
race, provider, migration, restoration or user-comprehension result is claimed.
