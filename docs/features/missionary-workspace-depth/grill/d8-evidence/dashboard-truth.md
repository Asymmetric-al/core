# D8 — coaching dashboard truth and disclosure boundaries

Research only, October 16, 2026 UTC. The founder selected outside coaches when
the Tenant permits them, using their own identity and separately authorized
read-only access. The Q8 research note is pre-answer provenance. **Eligibility
does not select financial modules, donor details, history, cross-subject totals
or new source grants.** This is the original review evidence. The founder
subsequently ratified [D8 and its required amendments](../phase28-d8-adversarial-review.md)
on October 16, 2026 UTC; its exact optional modules remain separate choices.
Ratification establishes neither a new financial grant nor implemented behavior.

## Minimal useful module recommendation

Start with a quiet list of independently authorized coaching contexts and one
context at a time. A missionary/project/ministry context is not automatically a
Support Assignment, participant roster or public Page. Resolve the exact owning
subject and any qualified binding; show its safe human label, not internal IDs.
Keep separate Tenants in separate current authorization contexts. A coach may
have several invited contexts without gaining organization-wide enumeration or
an aggregate financial portfolio.

| Proposed module                   | Exact meaning and owner                                                                                                     | Required limit                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deliberately shared goal          | P28 immutable Goal Version, purpose, currency where applicable, compatible period/cadence and start/end dates               | Never an Approved Support Plan, compensation target, balance or public-page denominator by inference. Show only independently authorized shared goal fields; no goal means no fabricated zero or missing-goal penalty.     |
| Voluntary progress context        | Qualified P9/P27 activity facts, separately chosen targets, eligible qualitative context                                    | Recorded activity is partial evidence. Count real qualified source occurrences once; no mandatory logging, diligence inference, rankings or copied private notes. The exact coaching audience/history needs qualification. |
| Agreed next steps                 | Read-only projection of independently deliberate, source-owned permitted next actions                                       | A coaching view cannot create/complete/reassign work, imply a promise or make an optional reflection mandatory. Source-required obligations keep their own accountability.                                                 |
| Optional recorded-support trend   | Exact P13/P21 permitted received-activity projection for one subject, currency and period, with source coverage/currentness | A separate optional module, not implied by coaching invitation. No donor rows or donor-level initials, no raw gift drilldown, recognition-as-cash, balances or spendability claim.                                         |
| Optional expected-support context | P16 authoritative commitment/occurrence aggregate and optional compatible P28 goal comparison                               | Separate from received support. Preserve lifecycle/attention, cadence/period, completeness and currentness. No donor identity, methods, decline reasons or operational recovery controls through this module.              |

The first three are the recommended minimal coaching product, still subject to
exact module/audience qualification. Optional amount-bearing modules require an
explicitly admitted finite profile and privacy proof; they are not silently
included by the invitation. A goal itself may disclose sensitive financial or
location context and needs its own field admission. This note does not invent
a new diary, coaching-note store or analytics builder: P9/P27 retain applicable
facts and P28 supplies only the qualified coaching projection.

Do not add Finance-confirmed balances, Balance Coverage, Reserve Position,
compensation, expenses, statements, payment methods, raw donor giving or a
participant roster to the default coaching view. P21 permits exact independently
authorized audiences for some financial modules; that is a separate source
decision, not proof that D8 grants those modules. Preserve an internal coach's
independent staff rights in their proper current context without unioning them
into the limited coaching view.

## Material concerns and proposed decision clauses

Severity describes the potential harm; likelihood is conditional design risk,
not measured production incidence. Each clause preserves source truth without
quietly expanding the founder's selected coach eligibility.

### DT01 — Eligibility or invitation must not become a financial grant

**Critical / plausible** if the dashboard reuses the missionary's full DTO or
acts as them. P12's four separate projection powers and P21's audience profile
are direct contrary evidence. **Clause:** “Each coaching module and field is
independently source-authorized for the coach's own current identity and exact
context. Invitation, staff title, missionary consent or participant membership
alone cannot disclose supporter, financial, roster, private-note or care data.”
The invitation preview must describe the effective limited view, not only say
read-only; read-only finance can still be excessive disclosure.

### DT02 — Separate subject cards must not become a duplicate financial total

**High / plausible** when a coach views spouses, a team, several missionaries or
projects funded by overlapping designation lines. P12 separates project summary
from coach progress; P14:2155–2159 scopes every amount/count before arithmetic;
P21:436–442 forbids authority from a binding. **Clause:** “Resolve each context
independently. Do not sum overlapping coached contexts, derive a team total or
deduplicate humans as a substitute for source allocation identity. A future
aggregate requires its own authorized subject, exact non-overlapping source
coverage and separately qualified summary projection.” Default recommendation:
separate contexts, no coach-wide money total or league table.

### DT03 — Received support, recognition, expectation and balance must stay separate

**Critical / likely** if a convenient loaded gift/credit/commitment array is
summed. P13 owns effective designation-line money; P14 applies MAX per
Party/header across recognition roles (`1929`), not a safe cross-person cash
sum. P16 `1101–1105` distinguishes posted receipt facts, processing and actual
occurrences. P21 `2854–2863` states the five independent authorities.
**Clause:** “Consume the exact owner-certified received-activity projection for
its declared purpose, tender/finality basis, subject and allocation. Do not sum
headers across splits, add recognition/household/DAF/church-member credit to cash,
count pledges or in-hand unposted gifts as received, or label recorded support as
spendable balance.” Corrections/refunds/returns follow source effective facts and
may reduce a historical trend; completed source history is not rewritten.

### DT04 — A goal percentage can reveal a hidden number or compare incompatible facts

**High / likely** if denominators are copied from `funding_goal`, a finance Plan
or a public progress card. P21 `3042–3055` requires explicit creation of a P28
Goal Version and no live synchronization; P22 `420–431` has its own page profile.
**Clause:** “A comparison pins the exact authorized Goal Version and compatible
numerator purpose, subject, currency, period, cadence, coverage and formula.
Missing/incompatible/nonpositive denominator or incomplete numerator cannot
produce a percentage. A ratio, remaining amount or trend is separately governed
derived disclosure, not a workaround for hidden amounts.” Sustaining monthly
support and a one-time project target remain different metrics; no fabricated
denominator, forced goal or automatic public update follows.

### DT05 — Currency and cadence shortcuts can produce materially false progress

**High / likely** with one converted headline or monthly-equivalent reuse.
P13:2025 forbids hardcoded two-decimal conversion. P16:1096–1126 separates dated
occurrences from exact rational monthly comparison; fixed-total pledges never
become sustaining monthly coverage. P21:3022–3029 forbids authoritative converted
balance totals. **Clause:** “Keep ISO-currency lanes explicit and use each
owner's exact arithmetic before display rounding. Do not sum unlike currencies
or imply an authoritative converted total. Scheduled-period amounts use source
occurrences; monthly equivalents are labeled planning comparisons. Do not
annualize a partial month or turn historical averages into guaranteed income.”
No FX/reporting conversion capability is added by D8.

### DT06 — Date range, watermark, coverage and unknown are different facts

**High / likely** with capped rows, current-page sums, asynchronous materialized
views or mixed data refreshes. P16:1096–1105 supplies tenant giving timezone and
explicit calendar-month/Next30days distinction. P27:147–154 forbids capped recency
and treats empty, filtered, incomplete, undated and unavailable separately.
P21:421–424/decision-log2976–2978 makes absence not zero.
**Clause:** “Each numeric module declares its period and exact source cutoff,
coverage and currentness. A known complete zero may display zero; unavailable,
unmanaged, unauthorized, partial or stale data must not. Compatible periods and
coverage are required for trends/comparisons. Display safe unavailable wording
without disclosing protected row existence; never invent healthy, no support,
Never contacted or inactivity from a failed or incomplete read.”

### DT07 — Aggregate-only and initials-only are not sufficient redaction

**Critical / plausible**, especially for one or few supporters, exact dates,
amounts, several coached contexts or changed filters. Brief:231 identifies this
explicitly. P14:1091–1111 forbids arithmetic shadows; P21:3012–3014 gates
enumeration/arithmetic before disclosure and :3031–3036 recognizes derivative
inference. **Clause:** “Coach privacy applies to values, counts, rates, totals,
timelines, filters, compare views, tooltips, downloads, caches and errors as well
as names. Do not expose donor initials, stable pseudonyms or exact donor-level
amount/time rows by default. Qualify the finite permitted summaries against
small-group and differencing attacks, including joint use of all authorized
contexts. If a safe useful release is not provable, withhold that module or use
an explicitly qualified coarser form; never silently exclude hidden contributors
and describe the remainder as complete total support.”
This does not select an arbitrary universal group-size threshold. A threshold
alone cannot prevent overlapping-query subtraction or knowledge from elsewhere.

### DT08 — Voluntary activity must not become a missionary performance score

**High / likely** when empty logs, percent-of-target or new-partner totals are
ranked. P27:17–25 explicitly forbids coercion and preserves independent duties;
:134–160 distinguishes actual exchange, recent outreach and copied narratives.
**Clause:** “Optional activity targets and coaching reflections remain deliberate.
Label metrics as recorded activity for their exact source meaning and coverage;
missing logs do not establish inactivity, diligence or failure. Count one actual
source occurrence once across surfaces/contributors; opening a dialer, sending a
newsletter, checking a task or receiving a gift is not a completed conversation.
No coach leaderboard, inferred performance grade, D28 donor rating or mandatory
logging gate follows from coaching.” A changed financial-support line is not a
lost human relationship or donor worth judgment.

### DT09 — Historical permission and current scope can change without changing source truth

**Critical / plausible** if a coach's old browser/cache retains donor-derived
facts after revocation or a new invitation silently reveals all prior history.
P12:2400–2412 and P27:277–284 apply current authority at result release, not only
calculation. **Clause:** “Pin the permitted historical window and exact source
scope for each coaching projection; invitation alone grants no earlier history.
Re-prove current authority before result release, refresh, export or recovery.
Cache and pagination identity include the relevant principal/context/source/
purpose/history/policy revisions. Revocation removes disclosure without deleting
financial facts or source obligations; reactivation does not silently restore
older history or erased/private bodies.” Exact history policy is a distinct
product choice still to settle; this note does not choose a date or duration.

### DT10 — Unresolved upstream attribution must remain local and visible

**High / plausible** if appeal progress is invented to fill a dashboard gap.
P13:456/1608 explicitly leaves E7 live-versus-frozen campaign attribution
unreconciled. :1731–1732 preserves the separate C-01 correction-approval gate.
**Clause:** “Do not qualify received-toward-appeal metrics until the exact P13
attribution ruling and producer are resolved. Mark only affected modules safely
unavailable; do not block independent goals or other qualified support facts.
Read-only coaching cannot manufacture a correction-approval policy or financial
write path.” No general Phase28 release gate is invented from an unrelated
unresolved source feature.

## Exact owner amendments and implementation qualifications

1. **P28/P12/P3/P10:** define the finite coach-module/field catalog, exact subject
   and history scopes, approved audience projections and all derivative/egress
   boundaries. Enabling external coaches does not itself widen those policies.
   Actual projection widening retains its existing governance.
2. **P28/P9/P27:** qualify voluntary goal/activity/next-step and eligible shared
   context projections without a new diary, private-content backfill, obligatory
   reporting or new task truth. Define precise activity labels and completeness.
3. **P13/P14/P16/P21:** add or qualify exact coach-purpose aggregate producers only
   for modules selected through an authorized finite profile. Reuse source
   effective facts, allocation identity, recognition distinctions, currency,
   periods and through-dates; do not expose a broader missionary/staff DTO and
   remove fields in the browser. P21 publication authority remains independent.
4. **P22:** consume only an independently authorized exact public metric where
   that is the actual displayed fact; do not reinterpret public progress as a
   private finance source or automatically publish a coaching goal.
5. **Cross-source release:** declare concrete numeric query/page/history/refresh/
   cache budgets and finite supported filters. Prove full-coverage source
   aggregates independently of paginated display rows. No arbitrary query/FX/
   cohort builder is needed for the minimal coaching job.

These are qualifications/amendments necessary for a selected module, not a
claim every optional module has already been chosen. Genuine remaining product
choices include the exact initial module audience, historical window and any
future amount/detail/export expansion. Resolve them explicitly in the review or
later single questions; do not turn row/page/time-budget engineering into a
series of founder microdecisions.

## Required proof cases

- Internal and external coach with the same exact grant get the same bounded
  projection; a staff coach cannot union staff financial data into that view.
- One principal coaching two Tenants, two missionaries sharing one Support
  Assignment, and a missionary/project with overlapping allocations gets no
  cross-context grant, leak, duplicated money total or implied project summary.
- Split contribution, DAF, church remittance, household credit, employer match,
  anonymous/restricted supporter, noncash value, provisional gift, refund,
  re-designation and partial fulfillment preserve the chosen source meaning.
- Zero/one/few supporters, overlapping ranges, changing counts, numerator plus
  percentage, known goal plus remaining amount, two cards and historical
  comparisons cannot reconstruct a forbidden person, amount or hidden row.
- JPY and differing currency exponents, parallel ISO currencies, weekly versus
  four-week/twice-monthly schedules, future starts, pauses, skips, fixed-total
  pledges and a late prior-period payment use the exact owner math and periods.
- Missing goal, incompatible Goal Version, actual zero, absent finance planning,
  permission denial, stale source, partial coverage, capped first page and failed
  refresh stay distinct without existence leaks or false zero/healthy claims.
- Duplicate narratives, one actual encounter on several surfaces, a sent thanks
  without a reply, date-only/undated events and corrected/restricted history
  produce truthful recorded-activity metrics without performance inference.
- Invite/revoke/history-change/cache-return races remove forbidden derivatives
  immediately at the disclosure boundary; source finance/history remains intact.
- Representative missionary and coach can explain what each number means,
  whether it is received or expected, its period/currency/currentness, what is
  shared, and that missing logs are not evidence of inactivity.
