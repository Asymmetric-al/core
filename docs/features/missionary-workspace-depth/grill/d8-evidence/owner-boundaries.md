# D8 — Coaching role, identity, scope and owner boundaries

Research evidence for the Phase 28 grill, October 16, 2026 UTC. This file records
owner intent, conflicts and proposed amendments. It is not a PRD, ADR, approved
capability manifest, implementation, deployment or access grant. The founder's
selected answer permits external coaches when the organization permits, using
their own identity with read-only access; the added direction requires a real
tenant coaching role and a useful dashboard spanning multiple missionaries and
projects. The exact modules, fields, history and duration were not selected by
that eligibility answer. This is the original review evidence; the founder
subsequently explicitly ratified [D8 and its required amendments](../phase28-d8-adversarial-review.md)
on October 16, 2026 UTC. D6 remains separately recorded. Optional modules, history
and duration remain open as stated in the ratified decision.

## Conclusion and smallest complete path

The choice is compatible with the accepted owners **with explicit amendments**.
It cannot be implemented truthfully by adding a navigation alias, impersonating a
missionary, creating a staff account, or adding a global `profiles.role = coach`
and treating that label as authority. Make Coach a first-class tenant product
role within the existing P12 membership, capability and resource-grant product.
Give that role a clear invitation, access-management and multi-subject dashboard
journey while preserving the single P12 policy decision point. The role starts
with only its qualified coaching read bundle; exact subject scope is a separate
grant. Creating or assigning the role does not mean all organization records.

The minimal permanent integration is:

1. P4 proves the coach's own Principal and the exact intended invitation binding.
2. P12 establishes a current tenant context and independently authorized role and
   resource grants. Eligibility policy, invitation, membership, grant, acceptance
   and delivery remain distinct facts, even if one simple UI coordinates them.
3. P28 supplies one coaching dashboard over the permitted source projections for
   the selected missionary ministry contexts and qualified projects. P9 retains
   canonical subject identity; P21/27 and other sources retain their facts.
4. P3/10/12 apply current field, purpose, classification, entity, history and
   revocation boundaries before enumeration, arithmetic or disclosure.
5. A coach can switch among authorized subjects within the current organization.
   Another organization's subjects are available only after a separately
   validated organization-context switch, not in a cross-tenant union.

This is an integration path, not a second ACL engine, project-management suite,
coach CRM, analytics platform, message inbox, or authorization service. Necessary
owner seams must be qualified; completion of every unrelated P12/P21 feature is
not a prerequisite. A permanently read-only role still needs safe account entry,
invitation outcome/recovery, subject discovery, current-access explanations and
revocation. It does not need edit rights to become a complete product role.

## Proposed exact corrected decision

> Organizations may permit missionaries with the appropriate source and tenant
> authority to invite eligible internal or external coaches. Every coach uses
> their own proved identity and current organization context. Phase 28 provides a
> first-class tenant Coach role, managed through the existing Phase 12 permission
> product, and one useful coaching dashboard for multiple specifically authorized
> missionary ministry contexts and qualified projects in that organization.
> The Coach role supplies only the qualified read-only coaching capabilities;
> each subject's scope, fields, purpose, history and current eligibility remain
> independently enforced. Role assignment, organization permission to use outside
> coaches, invitation, acceptance, ministry participation and source binding do
> not independently grant subject access. The normal guided invitation may
> coordinate their separately authorized owner commands without exposing their
> internal complexity to the user.
>
> Read-only coaching does not authorize changing another person's goals, notes,
> tasks, assignments, contact records, publication, newsletter, financial facts
> or permissions. Another independently authorized role remains available only
> through its proper current context and source action. No all-organization
> roster, automatic history, export, ranking, compulsory reporting or authority
> to invite further coaches follows. Module/field/history/duration choices still
> requiring founder decisions remain explicit; this answer does not silently
> select them. Existing source owners, current safety and immediate revocation
> apply throughout. Required owner amendments and runtime qualification precede
> activation; selecting this decision activates no account, grant or invitation.

The sentence on qualified missionary invitation preserves the selected A
journey. It must not be converted into a universal missionary permission or a
new mandatory staff-approval queue. Tenant policy and the existing grant
administration ceiling determine where a missionary can complete the bounded
invitation/grant path. The ordinary Coach read bundle is separate from the powers
used to establish it. Staff eligibility likewise gives no automatic coaching
rights. No default grant lifetime, historical lookback, onboarding password/MFA
rule or numeric assurance threshold is asserted here.

## Material concern register and exact proposed clauses

Likelihood is a qualitative assessment of the design ambiguity, not measured
production incidence. These findings are owner/contract release blockers for
the affected path; none asserts a currently exploitable implemented defect.

### OB01 — A role label can silently become broad or global access

- **What:** Treating the new role as a fixed global profile value, staff subrole
  or portal alias conflicts with the target tenant-context model.
- **Why it matters:** A coach in one organization can have a different ordinary
  role elsewhere; staff substitution inherits unrelated Mission Control scope.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High if the existing fixed role list
  is extended without the accepted owner model.
- **Evidence:** S05–S08; S09 distinguishes scoped read capabilities from role UI.
- **Answer impact:** Accept the real new role; reject global-label enforcement.
- **Permanent fix:** Explicitly reconcile the durable role vocabulary and P12
  human-role examples/registry/presets using one PDP and tenant membership spine.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC01:** “Coach is a first-class tenant role within
  Phase 12's existing grant product. Its label, route and global profile values
  never authorize. The role adds no principal class, all-organization scope,
  staff privilege or implicit union with another active membership.”

### OB02 — Organization eligibility and a delivered invitation can become grants

- **What:** “Organization permits” can be mistaken for role-wide visibility or
  power for every missionary to invite any coach to any subject. Accepted email
  can be mistaken for completed grant admission.
- **Why it matters:** A forwarded link, mismatched account, revoked inviter or
  changed policy could expose a subject despite invalid final authority.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High without the later P4 amendment.
- **Evidence:** S07–S10, especially P4:549–558 and P12:2393–2413.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve simple invitations; require independent current
  authority for each coordinated effect. No extra approval queue is inferred.
- **Permanent fix:** Reuse P4 invitation and P12 grant owners, final reproof,
  semantic idempotency and durable source/outbox boundaries.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC02:** “External-coach eligibility, invitation
  issue, intended-Principal acceptance, tenant membership and exact coaching
  grant are distinct owner facts. Invitation acceptance alone grants nothing.
  The guided flow admits each selected consequence only under its current owner
  and Phase 12 authority; failed, expired, forwarded, mismatched, replayed or
  revoked invitations cannot create access or erase unrelated valid facts.”

### OB03 — “Project” or “assignment” joins the wrong domain

- **What:** A dashboard may treat a Party, Ministry Assignment, Project, Support
  Assignment, Campaign, Designation or Page identifier as interchangeable.
- **Why it matters:** That can disclose another ministry, invent financial
  project ownership, or propagate grants when bindings or participants change.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High given existing overloaded terms.
- **Evidence:** S09:2347–2353; S11–S12.
- **Answer impact:** Keep both multiple missionaries and projects in scope;
  qualify exact typed sources instead of widening by display-name matching.
- **Permanent fix:** Source-qualified typed subject bindings with tenant/entity
  constraints, current owner adapters and explicit lifecycle handling.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC03:** “Each coaching subject has an exact typed
  source identity and same-scope owner binding. Ministry Assignment, Ministry
  Project, Support Assignment, Party, Campaign, Designation and Page remain
  distinct. Participant change, merge, retirement, successor or rebinding neither
  transfers coaching access/history nor manufactures a new project or finance
  source. Required multi-project views wait for their exact source qualification.”

### OB04 — A complete dashboard is interpreted as coach write permission

- **What:** Goals, check-ins and agreed actions are misread as authority for a
  coach to author shared notes, assign work, change goals or initiate messages.
- **Why it matters:** These are new product rights and can create compulsory
  supervision despite the selected read-only boundary.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** High in a broad “full-featured” expansion.
- **Evidence:** S02, S04, S09, S14.
- **Answer impact:** Keep the selected read-only role useful and complete;
  additional collaborative writes require their own later explicit decision.
- **Permanent fix:** Separate read capabilities from every source mutation and
  grant-management capability; validate server-side even when controls are hidden.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC04:** “Coach-only authority is read-only for the
  admitted coaching projection. It grants no shared-content, goal, task, contact,
  assignment, financial, publication, communication or permission mutation.
  Source-required duties and independently authorized other roles retain their
  own current authority; optional coaching cannot require logging, scores,
  rankings, quotas, missing-log penalties or automatic enrollment.”

### OB05 — Multi-subject summaries disclose excluded people or double count truth

- **What:** Fetch-all-then-hide, unrestricted totals or comparing tiny groups
  can leak private supporters, assignments, projects or amounts; overlapping
  subjects can produce false aggregate progress.
- **Why it matters:** Read-only is still disclosure; initials and exact money
  can identify people. Missing permission/history is not zero activity.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High for naive dashboard aggregation.
- **Evidence:** S02:231, S04:4179/4199, S13, S08:1638–1645.
- **Answer impact:** Multi-subject navigation is selected; cross-subject ranking,
  totals, donor detail and exports are not automatic consequences.
- **Permanent fix:** Apply source-qualified access and disclosure policy before
  enumeration/arithmetic. Keep currency, period, coverage and attribution honest;
  admit aggregate/comparison shapes independently where included.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC05:** “Dashboard rows, counts, search, trends,
  comparisons and derived values use only currently admitted source projections
  and apply the Phase 3/10/12 floor before selection or arithmetic. Aliases are
  not anonymization. Unavailable, incomplete, stale and zero are distinct. Shared
  underlying sources are not double-counted or silently combined across currency,
  period or entity; unauthorized subjects leave no enumerating residue.”

### OB06 — New coaches inherit history, private context or export rights

- **What:** Copying the missionary's screen grants all prior history or treats
  visibility as permission to download, quote or republish the underlying data.
- **Why it matters:** D1 private preparation and P27 private notes/care remain
  protected; newly allowed coaching does not change past intended audiences.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High without explicit history fields.
- **Evidence:** S02, S14, S15; D1–D5 source/access decisions remain distinct.
- **Answer impact:** No automatic history or export selected. Do not silently
  choose a historical default merely to complete the role.
- **Permanent fix:** Exact module/field/history manifest, separate export policy
  and current source audience enforcement across derivatives and recovery.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC06:** “A Coach grant exposes only its qualified
  module, field, subject, purpose and historical slice. It does not inherit a
  missionary's private preparation, general CRM notes, care, supporter roster,
  newsletter recipients or finance. Earlier shared context requires its explicit
  source-qualified history grant. Visibility grants neither export nor reuse;
  policy changes that widen disclosure follow ADR0211.”

### OB07 — Revocation or identity recovery transfers stale rights

- **What:** Role removal, organization disable, grant revocation, participant
  end and source safety block are collapsed; reactivation or rebinding revives
  expired access or loses independently valid rights.
- **Why it matters:** Cache, queued notifications, recovered results and late
  responses can continue disclosing after the exact basis ends.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High without explicit scope/epoch proof.
- **Evidence:** S07:1569–1587; S09:2400–2417; S10:560–565; S12–S14.
- **Answer impact:** Required safety, not a new continuity/retention privilege.
- **Permanent fix:** Reuse current P12/source epochs and scoped deny-first
  reconciliation at every relevant boundary; preserve lawful evidence separately.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC07:** “Revocation, expiry, tenant deactivation,
  source restriction and identity-transition fences stop subsequent protected
  reads, derived results and queued disclosures under the affected basis.
  Removing one basis preserves separately valid authority; global tenant-context
  deactivation fences that context. Reinvitation, account recovery, reactivation,
  merge or successor binding cannot resurrect or transfer ended grants/history.
  Lawful historical attribution remains source-owned; recall of previously read
  or lawfully exported material is not promised.”

### OB08 — External admission adds a new authentication path or false assurance

- **What:** A coach-specific password, shared missionary login, verified-email
  match, bearer dashboard link or unqualified social entry bypasses P4.
- **Why it matters:** The wrong existing Principal may receive new credentials
  or protected subject access before the application's later check.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** Medium; intended flow may otherwise
  appear to work in UI-only testing.
- **Evidence:** S10:186–200,227–235,272–278,541–558; S06–S07.
- **Answer impact:** External coaches are selected. Native identity qualification
  is a dependency, not authority to reduce scope to staff or invent a provider.
- **Permanent fix:** Reuse qualified shared Auth and exact owner gates; no claim
  that a newly selected assurance detail has already passed provider proof.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC08:** “Coaches authenticate as their own proved
  Principal through qualified Phase 4 entry/recovery. Invitations are not bearer
  access, email matching is not a grant or identity-attachment proof, and no
  donor claim, staff role, shared credential or new authentication provider is
  manufactured. Every affected native path satisfies the existing A10/G01 and
  current assurance gates before activation.”

### OB09 — Routine role use acquires an unnecessary approval engine

- **What:** ADR0211 is applied to every ordinary invitation/read, or a new
  coaching ACL product duplicates P12 groups, policy and audit.
- **Why it matters:** The founder's simple complete journey becomes operationally
  heavy; two definitions drift and revocation becomes inconsistent.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Medium in a safety-driven design.
- **Evidence:** S06, S08–S10, S14–S15.
- **Answer impact:** Accept necessary grant controls; reject generalized new
  approvals and per-coach permission matrix design by default.
- **Permanent fix:** One explicit standard coaching bundle and exact source
  grants, governed by existing administration and protected-grant rules. Distinct
  policy approval does not replace or multiply ordinary source actions.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC09:** “Reuse the existing Phase 12 registry,
  grants, floor, explanation and audit. ADR0211 governs projection-policy
  widening; ordinary qualified invitation, grant and read actions follow their
  existing source/P12 administrative rules without a new blanket staff review.
  New guarded or self-benefiting grants retain their applicable owner controls.
  No parallel policy engine, role builder or per-coach ACL product is introduced.”

### OB10 — Migration silently enrolls existing relationships or claims completion

- **What:** Existing coach relationship labels, project leadership, staff status
  or old profile rows are auto-converted into new broad access, or documents are
  presented as proof that the foundation is implemented.
- **Why it matters:** Silent grants are new rights. A new role can appear usable
  while identity, projection, revocation or source adapters remain missing.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** Medium to high without migration gates.
- **Evidence:** S01, S05–S12, S14; S02:313–325.
- **Answer impact:** Role/dashboard scope remains required; staged qualification
  is not a permanent feature deferral or automatic activation permission.
- **Permanent fix:** Inventory current evidence; preserve valid grants by source,
  quarantine ambiguous associations, and prove role-specific end-to-end entry,
  current access and revocation before enabling an affected cohort.
- **Exact proposed clause:** **OC10:** “Role installation, migration and rollback
  do not enroll people, mint subject grants or resend invitations from historical
  labels. Preserve independently valid current authority and historical actors;
  unresolved imported associations grant nothing. Each admitted role/subject
  path requires actual owner-service and database proof before activation.
  Documentation, synthetic tests and vendor claims are not shipped behavior.”

## Proof needed before activation

1. A coach-only external Principal can accept the intended invitation, enter the
   right organization and read two allowed missionary contexts and two qualified
   projects through actual owner services. No staff or donor fallback exists.
2. The same Principal's second tenant and unrelated staff/donor/missionary context
   cannot widen the first. A proper explicit switch proves that tenant's rights
   afresh. Late data from the former context cannot populate the current view.
3. A valid coach read succeeds while write, invite-management, grant-management,
   supporter, finance, private-note and export attempts fail where ungranted;
   independently valid actions in another proper context still work.
4. Forged subject type, tenant/entity mismatch, project/Page substitution,
   participant or Support binding succession and ambiguous imported relationship
   are denied without exposing hidden existence. Qualified sources remain useful.
5. Invitation acceptance and grant/policy revocation races exercise both valid
   serialized orders, including duplicate accept, mismatch, forwarded/replayed
   token, expired invite and failed delivery. Authority-free acceptance must be
   demonstrated, not inferred from successful authentication.
6. Revocation and source safety changes fence lists, aggregates, caches, streamed
   or recovered results, relevant notifications and exports before subsequent
   disclosure. Grant reactivation and identity recovery do not resurrect history.
7. Fields, history and aggregate cases include small populations, overlapping

## What this answer settles and what it does not

**Selected:** eligible external coaches as well as internal staff; own identity;
read-only coaching; a real tenant Coach product role; a dashboard for multiple
specifically authorized missionaries and projects. These are required outcomes,
not optional engineering suggestions.

**Inherited or engineering reconciliation:** existing P4 identity assurance,
P12 tenant/resource grants and current authorization, P3/10 field projection,
typed source bindings, explicit source history, invitation final reproof,
revocation, truthful states, protected audit, scoped migration and no duplicate
engines. The ordinary journey should expose simple choices and status rather
than asking the founder to select table structures or authorization algorithms.

**Still genuine product scope:** exact coaching modules and permitted detail;
what historical period newly invited coaches may see; intended grant duration;
any future coach-authored shared contribution or administrative right. D8 does
not answer these by saying “full-featured.” Some module boundaries may be settled
by an existing source's qualified projection, but broader rights or new history
cannot be inferred. Numeric assurance and operational gates require current
owner evidence, not a manufactured founder preference.

**Disposition:** Accept with required owner amendments OC01–OC10 and the scoped
qualification above. The selected role is meaningful and achievable through the
existing owners. Unqualified global role access, invitation-as-authority,
automatic historical access, all-tenant dashboards, coach writes or compulsory
reporting would change the founder's answer and must not be hidden as hardening.
