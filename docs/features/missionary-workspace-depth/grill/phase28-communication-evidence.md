## Main findings

1. **A complete native newsletter is already a requested Phase 28 outcome.** The current program contract requires it without Phase 32 external sync, SMS, optional workflows, or AI. Its composition belongs outside the P17 system-message catalog, while approved content/compiler/sender facilities and the sole P6 delivery spine remain shared owners. P17's older forward sentence assigning all newsletters to P32 must be read with the later scoped P28/P32 program amendment; it cannot silently delete the new missionary-native outcome.
2. **The public Request newsletter doorway still does not subscribe anybody.** P25 EX11–EX13 freeze this as a request for the missionary's separately managed newsletter. Native signup, recipient preferences for that newsletter, and automatic conversion of old requests are not authorized by this doorway. A new native signup is a genuine product/owner amendment choice; no historic request, gift, follow, reading or interaction can become opt-in by inference.
3. **The narrow guest-origin recipient projection already has an implementation home.** Live issue #1649 owns the complete minimum P28/P12 intended-recipient/detail capability and two-child readiness before accepting visitor intake. #1650 improves focused detail/Copy/return; it is not the missing upstream permission implementation. Future ticket work must reuse these allocations rather than create another Phase 28 inbox or projection ticket by title alone.
4. **An exact P6 producer gap remains explicit.** Current #897 says immutable preparation, sealed provider submission, ordered members and attempts lack exact implementation allocation and actual component proof. #888's occurrence compiler is not that producer. A sender facade or currently working Resend adapter does not resolve the gap. Affected provider paths remain gated; unrelated permitted workspace and manual relationship work need not stop.
5. **Three kinds of writing must stay understandable.** P9/P27 shared partnership context is voluntary working history for exact qualified collaborators; P22 Ministry Updates are versioned stories with independently admitted public/protected-reader releases; a newsletter is a separately approved recipient communication. Private Support/care/CRM material cannot be summarized or relabeled into either shared history or published content.

## The request-only newsletter contract, precisely

P25 `contracts/experience.md` EX11 permits an optional single Name and required editable Email, guest or signed-in, without donation/account/inbox proof. Name and address are supplied contact data, not verified identity. Public published form output is audience-invariant; private answers and results remain no-store. One accepted immutable occurrence plus its complete work plan precedes Request received. The same operation and meaning reconcile the same result; a deliberate changed-address submission is a new request and cannot recall the old one.

EX12 fixes one P23 Primary Outcome: action-qualified Verified Email Destination handoff, plus an independent missionary Information child. Frozen required email members each receive their own minimum actionable copy; visitor data does not choose sender, reply address, transport recipients, subject, headers, template, account or region. The visitor's supplied email is payload, not a transport destination. Email Primary completes only when every required frozen member has conclusive acceptance. Local availability remains independent of email success.

Local previews disclose generic safe purpose/time. Detail requires **the frozen intended human intersected with current exact same-Page/typed-subject/purpose, field and safety authority**. A guest origin does not require a donor/supporter relation; new assignee, spouse, editor, same-email account or generic missionary role does not inherit old bodies. Read/Archive/Copy is not handled, subscribed or enrolled. The view is not a new inbox/completion tracker or a supporter roster.

EX13 gives first preparation/attempt/handoff and first local availability an absolute acceptance-plus-seven-elapsed-days ceiling; normal work starts immediately. The new nonfinancial material class must use that acceptance anchor. Request body/detail access ends at acceptance plus thirty elapsed days or earlier restriction. CTA cannot outlive it, Recent sent copy is Off, and restore/read/archive cannot restart clocks. P6's existing twenty-four-hour physical disposal bound grants no extra read/decrypt/send authority. An expired provider idempotency window is not a license for another key or envelope.

EX16 explicitly requires the narrow P28/P12 extension before activating its Q25 consumer and preserves P32's no-automatic-external-enrollment exclusion. The active OpenSpec identity delta names this requirement and its positive/negative cases explicitly.

## Highest-leverage genuine product choices

These are discussion candidates, not a fixed questionnaire or accepted answers. The parent grill should select only one at a time after broader evidence and founder answers.

1. **How should a new supporter join a native missionary newsletter?** Ordinary situation: someone visits a public ministry page, wants emails and sees Request newsletter. The inherited doorway hands that request to the missionary without subscribing them. The choice is to retain this limited doorway for externally managed newsletters while native recipients enter through a separately qualified consent path, or explicitly amend the public owner journey to offer native signup when a ministry uses native mail. The latter improves the complete onboarding job but needs truthful topic/scope choice, contact proof policy, consent evidence, withdrawal, invalid-address repair and migration boundaries. Recommendation: do not reinterpret old requests; decide a deliberate native pathway only when its source/consent owner can be explained end to end. This decision changes P25/P23/P12/P3/P17/P6 contracts, not just a button label.
2. **Which low-effort story reuse belongs in the initial release?** Ordinary situation: a missionary has three approved short updates and wants one supporter email. Options include deliberate selection into a reviewable newsletter, draft-only digest preparation, or recipient-controlled scheduled digest delivery. The latter two add composition cadence, overlapping-purpose duplicate prevention, recipient preference and dispatch obligations. Recommendation: begin with deliberate selection of exact approved content into a separately reviewed newsletter; keep automatic digest/cadence proposals separate until founder value justifies their full owner contracts. This does not defer the complete native newsletter already requested.
3. **How much scheduling is genuinely needed?** One prepared newsletter for a chosen future time is different from recurring automated communication. Recommendation candidate: discuss deliberate one-off scheduled send with visible frozen content/audience and fresh narrowing checks, rather than accidentally adding an evergreen campaign engine. Exact scheduling edit/cancel and unknown-outcome behavior still require product decisions and owner qualification.
4. **What should a supporter actually receive for protected stories?** A full email, public story link, authenticated-story notice and print copy create different unavoidable disclosure. Recommendation: preserve P22's admitted public/supporter variants and owner-approved notification-only patterns; never label email secure based only on HTTPS, private access or a provider feature. Decide this with the restricted-worker scenarios and current P10 policies, not by merging all audiences into one preference.

Not founder questions: which service calls Resend, how semantic dedupe works, whether a timeout means success, whether a new assignee inherits old request bodies, or whether a gift implies opt-in. Those are already answered by adopted boundaries. Numeric query/throughput/provider limits require engineering qualification, not invented product preferences.
