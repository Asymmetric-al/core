# D15 — personal-account email: source, preparation and delivery owners

**Subsequent status — October 16, 2026 UTC:** the founder explicitly ratified the
[corrected D15 decision](../phase28-d15-connected-email-adversarial-review.md)
and its required amendments. That synthesis governs where this original research
contribution leaves options open or uses provisional wording. This note remains
evidence of the research performed; ratification proves no runtime capability.

Research date: October 16, 2026 UTC. **Research and proposed amendments only.**
The founder selected optional connected-account sending of individually authored
follow-up inside Asym, when the organization permits it. This note does not
declare the corrected decision ratified, amend formal contracts, establish an
implemented connector, or authorize a real send. Only this evidence file was
written by this pass.

**Recommendation: accept with required owner amendments.** The valuable job is
one deliberate individual message with truthful, automatically connected sending
evidence. A personal mailbox is not an exemption from contact restrictions, a
new CRM, a new inbox, or a second communication ledger. In particular, a
non-idempotent provider timeout can remain unresolved permanently. The product
must support that honest outcome rather than promise exactly-once external
delivery or offer an unsafe resend.

## Genuine conflicts and exact proposed owner clauses

Likelihood below means likelihood in a naïve implementation of the selected
feature, not an observed deployed incident.

### O01 — A connector silently becomes a second sender

**Critical / likely.** S04/S05/S14 currently admit Resend-specific sender and
credential topology. P31's registry is not authority to bypass those owners.
Keeping the existing generic “idempotency key required” signature while calling
Gmail/Graph elsewhere would create two send/recovery/history systems.

**D15-OC01:** “P28 SHALL register a bounded, human-authored individual-message
producer. P17 SHALL qualify its authored-content preparation and exact personal
sender/reply profile. P6 SHALL remain the sole recipient-intent, submission,
outcome and communication-history spine, including the only `sendEmail` external
writer. P31 SHALL own the qualified personal connection/grant lifecycle and
adapter implementation behind that spine. Amend the exact P6/P17/ADR-0029 and
OpenSpec topology for this lane only. Existing tenant-system and native-newsletter
Resend paths SHALL retain their current ownership and SHALL NOT fall back to a
personal account or vice versa.”

**Proof:** import/call-site enforcement catches direct SDK and fetch sends;
each provider attempt resolves to one source authorization, prepared artifact
and P6 event. Wrong provider/profile, unavailable connection and tenant/default
fallback tests emit no external work. A disabled personal connector leaves D7
and independently authorized manual/native work usable.

### O02 — Personal prose is forced through a system-message key

**High / likely.** P17:1093 excludes personal human messages; active OpenSpec's
uncataloged-message scenario is written broadly. D7 already requires a bounded
native authored lane. Neither fabricating a system-notice key nor treating human
prose as exempt from all preparation resolves this conflict.

**D15-OC02:** “Extend the qualified native authored-content lane with one
source-owned individual-message purpose family and explicit schema/profile.
The draft and deliberate send authorization are P28 source facts; the related
contact/relationship and genuine interaction remain P9/P27 facts. A typed
source binding identifies the exact authored revision, permitted recipient,
purpose, sender, reply posture and safety/retention profile. Human prose SHALL
NOT become a tenant-defined system-message contract or unrestricted recipient
DSL. Scope the catalog guard to its actual system-generated messages while
requiring separately registered and proved authored producers.”

**Proof:** arbitrary key, arbitrary HTML/header, raw fact bag, unknown source
profile and caller-injected recipient fail closed. Draft saving and previews
work without provider contact; no draft, approval or template action sends.
The real authoring job is exercised through the actual compiler, not a synthetic
system template standing in for a missing composer.

### O03 — A connected account is mistaken for permission or identity

**Critical / plausible.** A provider grants mailbox operations, not access to an
Asym Tenant, a supporter, a restricted prospect or another person's context.
One missionary may have several Tenant contexts. Similar address text or the
same provider login is not a cross-Tenant link or shared credential permission.

**D15-OC03:** “Every connect, compose, save, preview, authorize, decrypt, dispatch,
history read and recovery operation SHALL derive the exact current Tenant,
human principal/active assignment, purpose, source and capability through P12.
The personal connection binds the approved provider account/mailbox to that
human and Tenant purpose; knowing or controlling the mailbox grants no CRM
authority. A draft read or contact edit does not grant Send. Revoked source,
membership, grant, organization permission or connection blocks later unadmitted
work and result recovery. No service role, administrator, successor, spouse,
coach or support agent inherits private content or send authority.”

**Proof:** same human in two Tenants; wrong active hat; user-supplied tenant;
revocation after preview/authorization; stale queue and cached result; delegated
or shared mailbox presented as own account; operator/service bypass; return
after departure. Reconnection does not reactivate an ended authorization.

### O04 — “Individual” becomes a consent exception

**Critical / likely.** S14 explicitly preserves an absolute `do_not_contact`
floor and email-specific restrictions even for receipts. Connection authority,
manual authorship, a gift, referral, existing relationship or newsletter consent
cannot substitute for the actual permitted purpose/contact revision.

**D15-OC04:** “The source SHALL classify the exact personal-message purpose
through a closed owner-qualified policy, with its current contactability and
consent basis. Sender choice, user-entered labels and an AI/content guess cannot
turn solicitation into service mail. P6 SHALL re-prove canonical Party/contact
authority, `do_not_contact`, applicable `do_not_email`, suppression, purpose
withdrawal and safety at resolution and final submission admission. Unknown
authority blocks delivery. Authoring and inert preview remain separate from
contact. Topic-only newsletter withdrawal SHALL NOT automatically become
global email withdrawal; equally, an applicable withdrawal SHALL NOT be bypassed
by choosing another mailbox/provider or calling the message personal.”

**Proof:** every purpose against global stop, email stop, topic-only withdrawal,
address correction, known bad address and missing authority. Test an appeal
removed/closed or exact review invalidated between compose and dispatch.
No transaction/service dropdown, provider toggle or alternative account evades
the canonical verdict; blocked mail does not auto-launch mailto as a bypass.

### O05 — Provider suppression is either discarded or spread too widely

**High / plausible.** S07 distinguishes source consent/contact risk from
Resend connection-region suppression. A known complaint or broad contact stop
cannot disappear when transport changes, but a Resend-local transient/provider
block is not automatically a permanent global Gmail prohibition. Missing Gmail
bounce/complaint visibility is not a clean bill of health.

**D15-OC05:** “Retain one canonical source-owned consent/contactability verdict
and separately typed provider evidence bound to exact provider/account/grant,
destination revision and applicable evidence scope. An owner-qualified risk
fact may narrow all applicable contact; provider-local facts keep their proven
scope. New adapter evidence SHALL enter the existing typed boundary, never a
second consent flag. The UI SHALL say what is known without claiming complete
mailbox/provider suppression visibility. Automatic alternate-provider retry is
forbidden.”

**Proof:** source stop survives a provider change; Resend team/region evidence is
not misattributed to a different Tenant/account; address replacement does not
inherit verified possession, consent or a fabricated clean state; unrecognized
provider evidence quarantines without creating a new Party or guessing scope.

### O06 — Contact selection exposes or retargets the wrong person

**Critical / plausible.** P6's no-Party branch is an explicitly qualified source
authority, not arbitrary email input. D1's restricted preparation is not a grant
to send or to publish its details to shared staff CRM.

**D15-OC06:** “Select exactly one intended recipient from the qualified current
P9/P28 source projection and bind its exact contact revision at authorization.
The recommended initial individual profile contains one concrete recipient and
no implicit CC/BCC, spouse, household or group expansion. A restricted prospect
may be eligible only through an explicitly qualified purpose/contact authority;
do not require sharing merely to satisfy a legacy schema, create a fake Party,
or use the arbitrary-email fallback. Contact changes, merge/split, duplicate
resolution and relationship changes cannot silently retarget an authorized
message. Current checks may narrow, never substitute.”

**Proof:** hidden canonical match, shared mailbox, two contacts with the same
address, restricted prospect, wrong household, merge after preview, changed
email during preparation and malicious CC/header injection. Counts, suggestions,
errors, provider tags and history must not reveal a hidden match or recipient.
The one-recipient initial profile is review judgment, not an assertion that the
founder selected a complete CC/BCC/attachment inventory.

### O07 — Refreshing an OAuth token changes or freezes the wrong identity

**High / likely.** Existing P17 preparation pins a Resend credential revision.
Literal reuse could fail every ordinary access-token refresh; dropping the pin
could instead permit account replacement or revived revoked authority.

**D15-OC07:** “For the personal OAuth branch, freeze provider/account/mailbox,
exact permitted From/reply identity, connection generation and grant/permission
authority revision. Do not freeze bearer-token bytes as authored or provider
payload. The connection owner may resolve a refreshed short-lived credential
under that same still-current grant immediately before eligible I/O, after
current authority and scope reproof. Record only a nonsecret credential-version
reference on the attempt. Routine same-grant refresh does not create a new
message, sender, authorization or retry entitlement. Revocation, scope loss,
reconsent/replacement, account switch and sender/reply change are not ordinary
refresh and cannot silently alter the frozen authority.”

**Proof:** token expires between save/seal/eligible attempt; concurrent refresh;
same provider email with different immutable account; revoked/reconsented grant;
scope shrink; callback from a different Tenant; stale refresh response after
disconnect. Unknown earlier submission plus successful refresh remains unknown
and cannot resend. Existing Resend credential rules are not silently rewritten.

### O08 — Durable acceptance is mistaken for a completed send

**High / likely.** An application save, complete local plan release and external
provider outcome are different boundaries. One recipient does not remove the
complete-plan requirement or make a worker lease the cancellation boundary.

**D15-OC08:** “One explicit Send accepts an exact source authorization occurrence
and durable identifier-only handoff atomically, with an immutable expected
authored revision, recipient/contact, purpose, sender and usefulness limit.
Repeated browser submissions refer to that same source authorization even if a
transport request key differs. P6 compiles/releases the complete bounded plan
in one transaction before any member is dispatchable. Preserve distinct
Saved draft, Send requested, Prepared definitely unsubmitted, Submission may
have begun, Accepted, Definitely rejected and Outcome unknown meanings.
No client acknowledgment or local release alone means sent or delivered.”

**Proof:** double click with two HTTP keys, lost acknowledgment of source commit,
compiler rollback after parent/member insertion, stale draft revision, duplicate
worker and zero/blocked recipient. Accepted source work cannot disappear because
the wake-up/provider is unavailable. Pre-acceptance validation rejection cannot
be described as a successfully queued message.

### O09 — Gmail/Graph inherits Resend's safe replay window

**Critical / likely.** S06's bounded same-key Resend exceptions depend on a
documented provider contract. An internal unique key, Message-ID, Graph request
id, SDK retry setting or Sent-folder absence does not create such a contract.

**D15-OC09:** “Before the first external byte, atomically commit the exact sealed
provider attempt and permanent `Submission may have begun` fence. Crash after
that commit is conservatively uncertain even if no byte probably left. The
initial non-idempotent personal-provider profile SHALL permit no follow-up
send call for accepted or indeterminate work. Timeout, lost/malformed response,
unclassified error, contradictory evidence, expired lease and missing Sent
evidence cannot prove non-submission. Reconciliation may append exact authorized
evidence only; it never changes account, recipient, body, request meaning or
occurrence identity. A new attempt requires sufficient provider-specific proof
of definite rejection plus current source/consent/connection/utility proof.
Unknown is never repairable by a new key, fresh draft clone or alternate sender.”

This is an **at-most-one uncertain submission policy**, not a promise of
exactly-once external delivery or eventual success. A crash immediately after
admission can leave a message unknown without actual external transmission.
Where send-only authority cannot safely reconcile, say so; do not quietly widen
permissions to the full mailbox. Any later exact readback profile needs its own
purpose, scope, minimization and provider proof. Absence from a mailbox is not
proof of non-acceptance.

**Proof:** commit/crash before socket; timeout after provider acceptance; lost
response; 5xx; retrying SDK/proxy; stale worker; no provider message id; matching
Message-ID with ambiguous duplicates; empty/paginated/eventually consistent Sent
readback; reconnect/reconsent; restored queue. A documented definite `429`
rejection can be separately classified; it does not make an earlier timeout
retryable. No copied Resend “two follow-up calls” policy.

### O10 — Cancel, repair or edit promises recall or changes sealed content

**Critical / plausible.** P17:2380 locates the irreversible product boundary at
attempt admission before I/O, not provider acceptance or claim lease. A new
draft must not reopen the old occurrence. Cancellation or consent withdrawal
does not prove that a crossed external request was stopped.

**D15-OC10:** “Cancellation and source/consent/connection narrowing SHALL
serialize with final attempt admission. If narrowing wins, no new external
attempt is authorized. If attempt admission wins, preserve Submission may have
begun and its independent outcome; never promise recall. After narrowing, only
authorized evidence reconciliation may continue for the old attempt. Changing
content, recipient or sender requires stopping definitely unsubmitted old work
and making a fresh deliberate source authorization where eligible. Accepted or
indeterminate work cannot be replaced under a Retry/repair label. An
independently justified later communication is not a transport retry and must
not be manufactured to escape unresolved uncertainty.”

**Proof:** both commit orders of cancel/withdraw/revoke versus attempt; late
accepted response; consent re-enable; draft restore; edit while sending; failed
external stop; duplicate cancel; definite rejection after organization Off.
No “Canceled” success over admitted/unknown mail; no replay after re-enable.

### O11 — A private draft becomes permanent shared email content

**Critical / likely.** P6 is body-free. Current P17 readable-copy classes apply to
specified support-safe material, not arbitrary personal prose. D1/D26 do not
permit private-note laundering by placing it in an email or copying it to CRM.

**D15-OC11:** “Keep four separate records of meaning: restricted authored draft
and recovery revision under the qualified native content owner; immutable
restricted prepared execution material under P17; body-free sending/outcome
history under P6; and any separately eligible expiring readable copy. No body,
personalized subject, quoted reply, attachment, secret or raw header enters the
communication timeline, task, coach summary, audit label, search/export corpus,
telemetry or durable queue. Draft saving and deliberate sending do not share
the draft or authorize access to a provider Sent folder.”

“Before activation, assign the exact closed personal-message source retention,
abandoned-draft/revision, maximum useful dispatch and prepared-material profiles,
with finite values and purge/restore proof. These values are new review
judgments; do not inherit P17's financial thirty-day or staff seven-day classes,
Resend's idempotency window or an OAuth expiry. The initial personal lane SHALL
use `no_readable_copy`; no new author Recent-sent projection is needed for this
decision. Where the provider actually retained a Sent copy, the author can use
the external mailbox to see it. Asym SHALL NOT promise that uncertain mail has
such a copy, import that copy, or use it as its own private archive.”

“Send acceptance locks the authorized source revision while preserving its
restricted correction material during definitely unsubmitted work. A definite
pre-submission stop or provider-proved definite rejection may return that
existing source draft for correction after the old authorization is ended, or
retain the unchanged preparation for a separately eligible exact retry. Editing
cannot modify sealed bytes. Actual provider acceptance or an indeterminate
no-replay disposition closes the authorized source revision to a body-free
status, ends author-readable body/decrypt authority and triggers the same
bounded physical purge as linked execution material. This closes replay/body
authority, not the ability to append later truthful outcome evidence. Do not
retain the executed revision as a perpetually editable ‘draft’.”

“If later evidence proves rejection after authorized material has been purged,
preserve that improved outcome without restoring bytes from the provider,
backup, log or Recent copy. A person may compose new eligible work where the
source permits it, but the product cannot promise recovery of purged content.
An independently saved unsent draft remains subject to its actual authoring
purpose and finite abandoned-draft/revision policy, not an implicit Sent archive.”

These state transitions use P17's existing immediate denial and physical purge
within 24 hours; they do not invent a thirty-day personal-mail archive. The
source's exact finite unsent/abandoned-draft ceiling and maximum useful dispatch
interval still require a reviewed native-authored profile before activation.
No existing inspected owner class supplies those numeric values legitimately.

Draft/recovery data is legitimate source work, not another private-note system.
It must reuse the native authored lane and existing P3/P10/P12/P29 custody where
applicable. Ordinary draft editing may restart only the explicitly defined
draft-lifecycle clock; viewing, autosave retries, provider retry and restore
must not extend a sealed artifact or sent-copy deadline. This pass does not
invent numeric retention values as established facts. A finite profile remains
a concrete activation obligation rather than permission for unbounded storage.

**Proof:** sensitive marker in subject/body/quotes/link text/filename against all
secondary paths; expired drafts/revisions; two-editor conflict; restricted-party
reclassification; departure; changed audience; access revoked while response is
in flight. Test ciphertext, keys, caches, backups and deleted/restored state.
No app deletion claim implies deleting copies in either external mailbox.

### O12 — Retention destroys deduplication or restores sending authority

**Critical / plausible.** The existing P17 deadline rules assume a provider
idempotency window for some uncertain work. A no-replay profile must not keep
decryptable message material indefinitely awaiting evidence it cannot obtain.
Nor can an old backup prove that a newer attempt or revocation never happened.

**D15-OC12:** “The personal provider profile SHALL declare a no-replay material
posture explicitly. Acceptance, terminal rejection/no-send, privacy stop,
material expiry, or an indeterminate terminal no-replay outcome immediately
ends decrypt/adapter authority. Apply the existing bounded physical purge
discipline to ciphertext, wrapped keys and plaintext-capable caches. Retain only
the lawful body-free occurrence, identity/hash, authority, attempt and outcome
evidence required for history and duplicate prevention. Reconciliation after
purge uses qualified evidence, never provider-body refetch or a restored draft.”

“Restore SHALL quarantine external effects and protected reads until current
authority, revocations, retention and send evidence have been recovered or
reconciled. An older snapshot cannot guarantee recovery of later facts. Missing
evidence yields Recovery unknown and no new submission; backup age, missing
rows, manual reconciliation or restored credentials cannot reset occurrence or
grant generations.”

**Proof:** accepted/unknown before lost DB response; purge before late provider
evidence; old snapshot lacking attempt/revocation; lost key; stale worker holding
plaintext; restore of expired draft; lawful retention/erasure conflict. Preserve
truth without claiming deleted provider copies or resurrecting execution bytes.

### O13 — Sender/reply convenience exposes a private address or opens an inbox

**High / plausible.** Current P17 Reply-To is independently qualified; D7's
newsletter reply purpose is not a blanket personal reply destination. Own-account
sending naturally reveals a From identity and can generate externally retained
replies, but does not justify arbitrary aliases or inbound capture.

**D15-OC13:** “The review shows the actual qualified account/From and reply
destination the recipient will see. The initial personal lane uses the exact
proved mailbox identity and its qualified reply behavior; arbitrary display
name/address/header overrides, delegated send-as and shared mailboxes require
separate qualification. Provider-mutated or unsupported identity blocks safe
admission instead of silently substituting. Replies ordinarily arrive in that
external mailbox. D15 creates no inbound mirror, reply ingestion, Support ticket,
threading authority or private mailbox access for staff.”

**Proof:** restricted missionary alias versus legal name; provider alias
normalization; unusual/Unicode names; injected From/Reply-To; cross-Tenant
account; unmonitored/unsupported reply destination; tenant default accidentally
selected; reply does not create a Support record or shared encounter.

### O14 — Automatic send evidence becomes compulsory activity monitoring

**High / likely.** D15's avoided duplicate entry is a sending fact. It must not
increment genuine-exchange recency, complete a task/Request, subscribe someone,
or widen staff/coach history merely because a personal message passed through
Asym. S10 expressly separates those facts.

**D15-OC14:** “Record the one qualified sending/outreach fact automatically in
the existing source-linked P6/P9 projection without requiring duplicate manual
entry. Its current audience, purpose and historical scope remain independently
authorized; a shared contact is not shared correspondence. Sent, provider
accepted, delivered, opened, replied-to-link and actual human exchange remain
different. No score, quota, missing-log penalty, coaching enrollment, automatic
next action, task completion, appeal presentation or Request response follows
unless its exact source independently proves and accepts that meaning.”

**Proof:** sent thank-you with no reply expected; accepted/unknown mail;
automatic reply; later manually qualified real exchange; duplicate source link;
contact Share; new coach/staff assignment; grant removal; history correction or
merge. One message remains one occurrence without exposing body or private
existence through counts/recency/sort/filter.

## Minimal coherent lifecycle

1. **Connect for the chosen purpose.** Current organization permission and the
   missionary's exact Tenant context admit a qualified own-account grant. Show
   what it enables and what it does not prove; calendar authorization is separate.
2. **Compose/save.** The authorized source draft owns prose and current revision.
   Save acknowledgment means Saved only. Inert preview uses the same bounded
   preparation compiler and adds no send or real-recipient history.
3. **Authorize this message.** Review the exact recipient, content, actual
   sender/reply identity and current restrictions. Commit one durable source
   authorization plus its identifier-only handoff. Invalid/stale input returns
   actionable correction without pretending the request was accepted.
4. **Compile and prepare.** Release the complete P6 occurrence atomically;
   qualify and seal exactly one member's immutable content/delivery identity.
   Downstream transient failure cannot erase accepted intent, invent a provider
   result or change the reviewed message.
5. **Re-prove and admit the attempt.** Lock the source/intent/safety boundaries;
   check current source, recipient, consent, permissions and the pinned account
   grant; resolve an eligible current credential. Commit the permanent attempt
   before external I/O. Cancellation can win before that commit, not after it.
6. **Reduce evidence.** Exact success is provider acceptance with only the
   delivery certainty the provider actually supplies. Definite rejection uses
   its provider-specific closed retry posture. Ambiguous evidence stays unknown
   with no send retry, including after reconnect or a new HTTP key.
7. **Retain and close truthfully.** Material expires independently of body-free
   outcome history. Sharing, task completion, human exchange and consent have
   their own commands/owners. New authorization after a definite stop never
   resurrects old source tokens; unknown/accepted work is not replacement work.

The strongest clean initial profile is one deliberately sent individual message,
one proved own-account sender, no CC/BCC expansion, no batch/sequence, no automatic
follow-up, no general mailbox read, and evidence-only recovery after uncertainty.
This is a **recommended bounded profile**, not a claim the selection settled all
composer/media/scheduling details. Any attachment, template, quote, image,
