# D40 governance and source-owner review

Reviewed 2026-10-18 against Core `9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` and the existing Phase 28 research working tree. This is a new evidence note; no earlier evidence program was run or edited. The founder selected Q40 A, “Keep it informational.” This review proposes required clarifications; it does not ratify them, change owner specifications, qualify a provider, or activate a communication key.

## Finding and disposition

**Accept with required amendments.** A is consistent with the existing Phase 16 promise that a terminal missed occurrence remains visible and creates no automatic missionary outreach task. It must remain a narrow decision about a confirmed missed automatic occurrence whose normal future collection continues. It cannot remove separately qualified recurring-attention work, infer a next gift from missing data, or make truthful financial facts optional.

The principal additional decision needed for the requested tenant control is a bounded **optional missionary in-product notice** under the existing Phase 17 Delivery Plan. Its optionality is a proposed explicit owner amendment, not a property already established by the Reserved key. One organization-wide On/Off control suffices. Do not offer an automatic-task mode after the founder selected A, a per-missionary exception matrix, new miss-count thresholds, or a new notification engine.

Recommended Off semantics are deliberately small: stop new and not-yet-published optional notices; already published notices keep their ordinary read/archive/recent lifecycle. Source corrections and loss of access still act immediately. Re-enable applies only to later eligible source transitions, with no catch-up or revival. Explain the scope plainly in the control: **“New missed-gift notices”** and **“Turning this off stops new notices. Existing notices remain in history.”** This is more predictable and less costly than introducing a tenant switch that purges all presentation and subsequently needs a durable non-revival generation for each old item.

## Material conflicts and precise resolution

### G01 — Scope drift into disabling needed work

**Risk:** Developers interpret “informational” as suppressing every recurring attention transition, including a source-qualified action needed to continue collection. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible**, because the broad roadmap promises gift-event tasks while the narrow P16 missed-occurrence clause promises no automatic outreach work. This narrows the recorded answer; it does not invalidate A.

**Required wording:** “This decision applies only to a confirmed terminal missed automatic occurrence while the authoritative source still permits the normal future schedule. It creates no automatic review or outreach task. Other independently qualified required work and protected payment operations remain governed by their source contracts.”

Do not define “first miss” as a new counter. The question's one occurrence is illustrative; count alone is not a new trigger. Several past misses can remain facts while a separately qualified current action becomes necessary.

### G02 — Optionality invented from the existence of a Reserved key

**Risk:** A tenant switch is implemented against a communication key that has no Live contract, or it disables required donor messages along with missionary information. **Severity: high. Likelihood: likely under a generic switch**, supported by P16's explicit Reserved gate and P17's fixed-slot requiredness.

**Required wording:** “The existing `recurring_occurrence_missed_v1` contract SHALL declare a separately optional missionary in-product informational step. An authorized organization-wide plan editor MAY enable or disable only that step. The product default is On only after the exact producer, slot, role-safe recipient, publication, presentation/end rule and Live activation are qualified; an explicitly saved Off remains Off across upgrades. Neither setting changes donor/required steps, recurring source truth, permission, or task eligibility. Reserved/unsupported capability creates no notice or misleading operational setting.”

**Product judgment:** Default On preserves P16's promised eligible grouped notice after activation. There is no research evidence that most Asym tenants prefer it; review does not assert that claim.

### G03 — Off, queued work and historical revival

**Risk:** A plan pinned while On still publishes after staff choose Off; or Off removes records that come back unread after On; or re-enable floods old misses. **Severity: medium to high. Likelihood: plausible** with queued publication and cached projections. ADR-0026's general future-only plan editing does not automatically provide the extra current suppression rule.

**Required wording:** “Off SHALL prevent new and not-yet-published optional missionary notices at a current server-side publication gate. An immutable compiled plan is not edited; the optional member records the appropriate suppression outcome. Already published items retain their ordinary engagement, source correction, current-access and recent-history rules. Re-enable permits only later eligible source transitions, never a previously suppressed, expired, corrected or historical item, never a reset of unread/history clocks. Saving an identical setting is idempotent.”

This is an explicit limited amendment for the optional step's current publication gate. Prefer it to an Off operation that clears every historical presentation: the latter adds per-item irrevocable presentation-ending state and revival logic without improving source truth or reducing future noise. Existing published items remain archivable through the usual personal control. Required notice or source task history is unaffected.

### G03a — Exact informational presentation binding

The proposed Live missionary step SHALL bind the existing `presentation.information_30d_then_recent_90d@1` policy. It is All-only; read/archive/correction or the existing day-30 ceiling ends unread, and the existing day-90 ceiling ends recent presentation. Do not add a D40 retention interval, per-tenant timer, Needs attention placement or required acknowledgement. Its exact producer-owned source-applicability/end rule must be registered in the same activation generation; the decision invents no executable source-rule key or independently recomputed clock.

That rule must handle a corrected/superseded terminal miss by ending misleading unread and rendering current authorized corrected meaning without erasing durable evidence. A later successful occurrence does not delete the prior genuine miss. Later schedule changes do not rewrite the earlier miss into a new event and do not reset its presentation age. Historical notification copy must not retain an outdated next-gift assurance: the detail destination loads the current independently qualified source schedule, shows its current status/date or Updating as appropriate, and identifies the original occurrence separately. Current loss of source visibility removes presentation under ADR-0027 immediately. A new source-qualified required action may independently project its own exact attention/work; it is not manufactured from this item's unread or archive state.

**Severity: high; likelihood: plausible.** A copied upcoming date can outlive a schedule amendment and tell Maya a gift is still scheduled after cancellation. This changes the presentation/end contract, not the founder's informational choice.

### G04 — Misleading future assurance or source timing inference

**Risk:** A stale schedule, pending retry, voluntary skip, or provider subscription label produces “missed” or “next gift on…” as if known. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible**, especially with current prototype list math; source-level review must independently verify implementation.

**Required wording:** “Terminal Not received, last received and next scheduled facts SHALL come from the qualified P16/P13 role-safe projection and retain independent source currentness. Next scheduled is a plan, not a guarantee. Pending, Processing, Skip, Pause, no future occurrence, stale and unknown states SHALL not be substituted for the selected case. Incomplete source facts produce the corresponding honest state and suppress unsupported negative outreach.”

### G05 — New manual task mutates finance or silently suppresses future required work

**Risk:** Clicking Add follow-up marks the gift reviewed, creates fake donor contact, exposes provider details, duplicates an ordinary task, or future native work is suppressed because a similarly titled manual task exists. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible** across separate task/financial writers. D21 already supplies a qualified permanent solution.

**Required wording:** “Add follow-up SHALL reuse the ordinary task owner's deliberate create/recovery flow and permissions. It SHALL not write recurring state, received money, communication permission, activity completion, notification engagement, native task admission or coverage. Existing compatible work may be offered only by the owner's exact authorized contract; no title, person or amount heuristic merges work. Current financial detail loads from the source after authorization; protected details are not copied into task titles, notifications, exports or logs.”

### G06 — Configuration hides facts or changes financial policy

**Risk:** Tenant customization changes the definition of missed, amount, next date, or source totals; turning notices Off makes dashboards appear healthy. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible** with unrestricted presentation settings. P16 bounds tenant policy and platform surfaces require honest states.

**Required wording:** “Customization SHALL be one organization-wide optional notice choice plus existing authorized saved views and ordinary task controls. It SHALL not add tenant-authored financial rules, arbitrary thresholds, per-attempt alerts, tenant-specific status vocabularies, automatic task mode, donor email, or a new retention setting. Accurate authorized source visibility remains available under existing financial visibility controls; this notice choice neither widens access nor hides facts from an otherwise enabled source view.”

### G07 — Source/recipient grouping leaks hidden data

**Risk:** Cohort count, totals, title, amount or destination reveals hidden sibling lines or another recipient's support. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible** because collection cohorts and missionary authority have different grains. P16 explicitly says privacy before aggregates and no sibling facts.

**Required wording:** “P16 SHALL authorize and minimize constituent line facts before any group count, reason, total, notice, URL or task context. Grouping SHALL reuse the exact qualified communication meaning and recipient identity without widening that recipient's scope; zero and incomplete recipient proof SHALL not guess an audience.”

## Owner boundaries and integration order

1. Record the founder's A and the above precise amendments as proposed D40 research. It is not necessary to ask whether mandatory source truth can be turned off; that is already a governing boundary, not a meaningful product preference.
2. Preserve the no-automatic-task decision now. Qualify read-only P16 facts and ordinary existing manual tasks independently of future notice activation.
3. At authorized formalization, reconcile P16's missionary journey/D9/Template contract and P17's catalog/requiredness/manifest with the exact optional step and current Off gate. Update declared OpenSpec projections through their owners. No direct edit to these canonical owner files is authorized by this research phase.
4. Qualify current source DTOs, grants/RLS/service paths and exact manual task boundary. Financial source, comms, manual task and notification state must remain distinct.
5. Only then qualify/activate the exact notice slot and expose its existing-plan setting. Leave other Reserved keys blocked. A missing notice producer blocks that notice, not qualified factual source visibility.
6. Verify future-only enablement, Off versus publication races, zero notice/work while Reserved, unchanged source money/schedule, no historical catch-up, privacy before grouping, manual task retry recovery and retained independently qualified tasks before release.
