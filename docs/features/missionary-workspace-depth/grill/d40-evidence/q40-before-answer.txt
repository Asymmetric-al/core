## The next question

One scheduled automatic gift was not received, and its recovery window has
ended. The next normal gift is still scheduled. Maya can see the factual status.

**Should this situation also create an internal review task?**

- **A — Keep it informational (recommended).** Maya sees the missed gift and the
  next scheduled gift. She can deliberately add follow-up when useful.
- **B — Add an internal review task.** Maya reviews this missed gift once and
  decides whether further action is appropriate. Nothing contacts the supporter
  automatically.

This is one confirmed missed occurrence with a current, authoritative continuing
schedule. It is not an initial payment failure, recovery still in progress,
unsettled processing, stale or contradictory source data, voluntary Skip/Pause,
or a recurring arrangement that can no longer collect. Neither answer removes
other independently qualified attention work or grants contact permission.

## Recommendation and strongest alternative

Recommend **A** for this narrow case. It retains visible financial information
without requiring Maya to complete a review when the regular schedule already
continues. Existing deliberate task creation supplies a useful response when
she has additional relationship context. Avoiding an unnecessary obligation is
a product judgment, not measured evidence that all ministries prefer fewer
tasks. No selected answer is inferred from that recommendation.

**B** is a meaningful alternative: a tenant may want an accountable human review
of every confirmed missed occurrence even when future gifts remain scheduled.
Its purpose would be to review current permitted facts and decide whether any
further action is useful. It would not tell Maya to collect a debt or contact the
supporter. It would require an exact native task-purpose contract, source-owned
completion/correction, durable deduplication and current audience qualification.
Simply calling an outreach task a review does not satisfy those boundaries.

Two independent document reviewers found this an unresolved product seam and
challenged the proposed scope. Both favored A for this case after checking the
existing recurring requirements. That is adversarial document review, not
interviews, a usability test or proof of fundraising outcomes.

## User journey boundaries preserved under either answer

1. Maya encounters the permitted status within the existing recurring-support
   view, with occurrence amount/currency/date and the next ordinary scheduled
   gift when currently known. No separate failure inbox or technical retry
   details are introduced.
2. Language stays factual: a gift was not received. Do not blame the supporter,
   infer refusal, call the person lapsed, claim a balance is owed or imply an
   extra charge will occur.
3. Current source truth controls visible facts. If information is updating,
   the UI says so; it cannot present an uncertain next gift as confirmed.
4. A retains deliberate follow-up through existing workspace actions. B would
   require an exact review action in existing task presentation, without making
   financial correction, contact or message sending part of completion.
5. Current tenant, source and role permissions govern every view/action. Contact
   methods, provider diagnostics, private staff notes and hidden sibling lines
   do not become visible because a task or notice exists.
6. An informational notice, when its exact catalog contract is Live, retains
   the existing grouped in-product treatment. Reading it cannot complete work;
   a Reserved key cannot be bypassed by a new task-notification side channel.
7. Shared Base UI/base-maia components, clear text states, keyboard access,
   phone reflow and existing localization patterns remain the design baseline.
   No extra daily acknowledgement, generic automation builder or settings
   matrix is introduced by asking this question.

Tenant enablement/defaults, the precise action contract, correction behavior,
retry semantics and acceptance evidence must be reviewed against the selected
answer. They are not silently selected here. Existing tenant controls and
mandatory finance/source rules remain in force. A proposed review task must not
become a disguised donor-contact instruction or an override of those rules.
