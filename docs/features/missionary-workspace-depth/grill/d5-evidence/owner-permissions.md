# D5 owner and permission evidence

Research-only, 2026-10-16 UTC. Parent relayed the founder's selected A: include
direct missionary corrections of eligible ordinary shared contact details,
**with the Tenant deciding what missionaries can do/change through Tenant
settings**, and simple, permission-truthful UX. This note is not a formal
specification, owner amendment, implemented permission or field rollout.
No application, settings, accounts, grants, tickets or live records were changed.

## Recommendation and authority boundary

**Accept the capability through the existing P9 contact owner and P12 permission
product, composed with P3/P10/P11 field/source floors.** Tenant settings are a
presentation and administration entry to those governed sources, not a second
`missionaries_can_edit_contacts` authorization flag, role-string check, private
contact copy or per-contact invitation system. Register the exact bounded
action and eligible field/source purposes; expose only the current admitted
operations. One ordinary authorized Save needs no new staff correction queue
or per-save supervisory approval.

D5 selects the bounded product capability and Tenant control. It does not
automatically grant it to every existing missionary, select every contact field,
make the missionary staff, grant broad CRM browsing, establish new permission
levels, authorize self-administration, override platform safety, or derive edit
rights from authorship, D2 sharing, notification or D4 restoration. The exact
eligible contact predicate, field inventory, default grant/migration mapping and
settings-to-owner compilation remain required qualification, not facts already
proved by the answer.

## Material concerns and exact proposed clauses

Severity/likelihood are conditional design judgments, not observed production
vulnerabilities or measured incidence.

### O5-01 — Parallel settings or role-based fallback

**High; plausible** if an app flag or `role === missionary` independently allows
a save. It can disagree with the field ceiling, current grants or active Tenant.
**Evidence:** P3 A2–A5; P12 `:1542–1556`; P11 D1/D4.

**Clause:** “Tenant-configured missionary correction is administered through
the existing permission/policy owners and evaluated by the one current PDP.
The settings UI creates no parallel authorization source. A server-qualified
capability, exact eligible source/subject and permitted field/purpose are all
required. Do not promote missionaries to staff or add a broad CRM capability to
make the form work.”

**Proof:** Direct API, RPC, collection and privileged commands agree with the
rendered permissions; conflicting client flags/role labels confer nothing.

### O5-02 — A permission toggle lies about overlapping grants

**High; plausible** if removing one source is displayed as a universal deny, or
separate donor/staff/missionary hats are unioned. **Evidence:** P12 `:1544`,
`:1552–1556`, `:1593–1600`, `:1670`; P3 A5.

**Clause:** “Evaluate all applicable grant sources inside the one selected
current assignment, then apply the exact source/field floors. Removing one
grant does not remove independently surviving authority; a per-grant Off is not
a cross-grant DENY. An absolute Tenant field/surface restriction belongs in the
existing policy ceiling and follows its governance. Do not merge acting hats,
invent permission levels or use staff-only group machinery as an implicit
missionary grant. Explain actual effective permission and pending policy state.”

**Proof:** Overlapping grant paths, selected missionary versus staff context,
Tenant switch, record/field floor and a removed grant with a surviving valid
source all produce truthful server and UI outcomes.

### O5-03 — Tenant administration is confused with ordinary contact editing

**High; plausible** if any missionary/editor can change their own policy, or if
every contact Save is routed to an unnecessary approval queue.
**Evidence:** P12 `:1594,1599,1668–1672`; P3 `:238–265,288`; ADR0211 `:16–37`.

**Clause:** “Only a currently qualified Tenant administrator may change the
scoped grants/policy within their live administrative ceiling. An ordinary
authorized contact correction requires no new staff approval. Classify actual
projection-policy changes at their owner: widening requires ADR0211's distinct
human and current-base apply; narrow/neutral changes use the existing immediate
audited path. Ordinary grant administration follows its own P12 controls; do not
label every grant mutation a projection change or use that distinction to evade
an actual widening. Unknown field-policy direction remains widening.”

**Proof:** The same human switching hats cannot approve their own projection
widening. One-admin convenience does not waive ADR0211. Stale/pending policy
never enables the editor early. A permitted phone correction is one Save,
independent of financial C-01 or a new correction-request product.

### O5-04 — Shared, authored or visible becomes editable

**High; plausible** if D2 sharing, an old private assertion, gift-derived roster,
household membership or notification substitutes for current source authority.
**Evidence:** P3 `:90,107–108`; P9 `:278–288,661–679`; P12 `:1609–1633`; D2/D4.

**Clause:** “D5 admits only the exact ordinary contact correction capability
and fields within the missionary's currently eligible workspace scope. Viewing,
creating, sharing, archiving, restoring or authoring preparation does not by
itself grant canonical editing. Preserve independent existing facts and the
no-broad-CRM boundary. Editable implies currently readable for that exact field;
hidden fields cannot be changed blindly or exposed through validation.”

**Proof:** Party IDs, hidden canonical matches, another worker's relationship,
shared household data and caller-supplied owner/author fields do not widen the
action. Legitimate nonfinancial relationships must use a qualified source scope,
not a fabricated gift or arbitrary finance-roster shortcut.

### O5-05 — Generic contact CRUD changes identity, consent or fulfillment

**High; plausible** if all fields are treated as equivalent profile columns.
**Evidence:** field table above; P4 `:211–219`; P12 `:1624–1633`; IC10/IC12.

**Clause:** “Each exposed field uses its exact owner command, validation,
clearability, revision, verification and downstream-purpose rules. A contact
correction creates no login/claim/merge, possession proof, consent, subscription,
charge, new contact history or public/legal identity change. Preserve independent
Auth, legal, represented, billing, document and communication facts; never use a
fallback value or private mirror to hide a missing canonical source.”

**Proof:** Name/Phone false coupling, changed email claim preservation, shared
mailbox ambiguity, postal withdrawal races and absence versus unavailable source
are tested at their actual owners. Other fields stay unavailable until qualified;
an unrelated missing field producer need not block an independently qualified
ordinary correction.

### O5-06 — New grants or Tenant restrictions damage donor self-service

**High; plausible** if missionary field settings mutate a shared baseline or
claim record, or if source competition invents an actor hierarchy.
**Evidence:** P3 `:238,272,288,321`; P12 `:1557–1568`; P25 IC06/IC10/IC12.

**Clause:** “The Tenant's missionary correction policy does not implicitly
change the donor's independently qualified self-service, staff authority or
another surface's scope. Preserve contact and claim provenance with the actual
actor; do not impersonate the donor. Concurrent authorized corrections follow
the source's current revision/conflict rules, not an invented missionary-wins,
staff-wins or donor-wins overwrite hierarchy.”

**Proof:** Donor and missionary editing the same permitted contact race without
lost updates; each can recover only currently authorized results. Narrowing the
missionary slice leaves independent donor rights intact. Contact Clear does not
erase claim evidence, and a new contact email does not inherit verification.

### O5-07 — Default rollout silently widens access

**High; plausible** if D5 is interpreted as immediate permission for all existing
missionaries, new fields inherit editability, or legacy role flags become grant
evidence. **Evidence:** P3 A1/A5 and dormant override schema; P11 C1–C4; P12
`:1546,1595,1599`; D5's express Tenant control.

**Clause:** “Declare and qualify the exact registered action, eligible-source
predicate, field policy and Tenant administration path before activation.
Unconfigured/unclassified or unresolved authority grants no new edit. Reconcile
existing proved grants and reached clients/writers explicitly; do not revoke
independent valid rights or silently grant new rights from the feature decision,
legacy row presence, role label or new field. Any chosen initial template/default
and rollout widening follows the owning approval and migration rules.”

**Proof:** Existing Tenant with no newly qualified grant, Tenant with a valid
current equivalent grant, changed configuration, new/unclassified field and
mixed-version client are distinguished. D5 chooses no new default-on boolean;
fail-closed qualification does not require a second permanent feature flag.

### O5-08 — Permission-truthful UX diverges at acceptance or recovery

**High; plausible** if an open form uses old capabilities or success is inferred
from a local save despite partial source failure. **Evidence:** P9 B17; P12
`:1468,1619,1624–1633`; P25 IC10 `:93–97`, IC12 `:115–119`.

**Clause:** “Render only current permitted fields/actions with a concise shared
effect explanation. Readable-but-not-editable data remains a truthful summary;
hidden data stays absent without exposing its existence. Recheck exact current
authority and expected revisions before atomic acceptance; reject a partially
permitted multi-field request without writing allowed fields first. Recover the
same accepted result under current disclosure authority, distinguish refresh
failure, and preserve only still-permitted draft content after scope changes.”

**Proof:** Field narrowing and required-grant revocation race Save in both
orders; unrelated revocation with an independent valid source is not a false
denial. Audit failure accepts nothing. Lost response, stale source revision and
same-person role/Tenant change cannot overwrite newer data or reveal a formerly
allowed value. Audit contains the required minimized field/revision/actor/purpose
evidence, not raw phone/email/address values in generic logs.

## Coverage and limits

Read the cited scoped P3/P4/P9/P10/P11/P12 owner clauses, ADR0211, the P25 IC06–IC14
source extensions, current Q5 framing and D2–D4 decisions. Read root/general and
backend source guidelines and the Supabase skill; this pass changes no Supabase
schema/API and performs no provider, role or database proof. General RLS examples
cannot replace Core's current exact source/PDP contract. Runtime, detailed field
inventory, actual settings implementation and numeric workload qualification
remain unverified here. The findings must not be presented as a complete review
of every large source volume or proof that the intended permission product is
already deployed. Only this research file was written.
