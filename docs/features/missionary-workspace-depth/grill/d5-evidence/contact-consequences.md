# D5 evidence — consequences of correcting ordinary contact details

Research-only review of P4/P6/P9/P12/P17/P25 ownership. The parent reports the founder's D5 choice: **missionaries may make ordinary contact corrections directly within the capability allowed by the organization's settings**. That selects correction authority and does not make every contact-shaped field an interchangeable scalar. No application, root review, session, specification, tracker or live system was changed; no contact or communication was sent. The checked-in Q5 research still describes the earlier pending question; the later founder answer supplied by the parent governs this review.

## Decision consequence

D5 can promise a simple direct correction of **the exact ordinary contact fields the existing source owner and current Tenant policy admit**. It must not promise that a single Save changes login identity, proves possession, clears suppression, updates released documents or redirects already accepted communications. Those boundaries already belong to the named owners; they are not reasons to ask the founder to choose them again or to impose routine approval on every phone correction.

Use the existing P9 subtype/contact source and P12 field/purpose capability. Extend that source's admission to the accepted missionary correction scope; do not add a private authoritative contact mirror, a second contact-point model, a new permission engine, or a generic correction workflow. Tenant settings may narrow the admitted capability but cannot override identity, consent, safety or financial/document floors. Current source/proof qualification remains necessary before a particular field is enabled.

## Minimal lifecycle and user-facing consequences

1. Load only the source's current admitted contact values; show the ordinary shared effect concisely. Permission to view does not imply permission to edit. The current Tenant setting and exact capability govern both entry and acceptance.
2. Apply ordinary Name/Phone directly through the admitted source command. For email or mailing, compose the exact existing source behavior rather than pretending those fields have no downstream consequences. No phone proof, credential ceremony, reason essay or manager approval is added merely because the field can affect later communication.
3. Before submission, Cancel discards the draft. After submission, closing the UI does not cancel an in-flight commit; recover the same operation before repeating. Email proof requests, when actually required by the owner lane, retain their separate accepted/pending/superseded lifecycle.
4. Show a source-proved saved/no-op/conflict/unknown result. Contact saving can succeed while a relevant previously prepared mailing needs review. Report only an admitted, actual downstream status; do not leak protected communication/document inventory to explain a save.
5. Future communication re-proves exact contactability. Old accepted messages/documents stay historically honest, and no correction can promise recall of bytes already sent, downloaded or physically handed off.

Apply effects to the **exact changed source and purpose**, not a universal contact-row reset: a phone edit does not reset email claim/proof; a contact-name edit does not replace legal identity; a personal mailing withdrawal does not withdraw general consent or all independent destinations. Reuse source-owned safety guards/fences where required. This is implementation qualification under the accepted simple product action, not a new settings matrix for the founder.
