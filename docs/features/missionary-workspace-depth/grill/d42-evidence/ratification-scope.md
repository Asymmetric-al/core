## Recommended next single question

**Q43 — Finish a signup already started:**

> When Maya turns off **Show newsletter signup on this page**, should readers
> whose signup was already accepted still be able to finish confirming it?

**A — Let accepted signups finish (recommended).** They may confirm within the
original validity period, while current newsletter and recipient safety rules
still allow it. No new signup requests are accepted after Off.

**B — Stop pending signups too.** Turning the Page signup entry Off also prevents
those pending requests from completing. Readers must start again if signup is
offered later.

Under either answer, existing active subscriptions and unsubscribe access remain
unchanged. The question concerns only the Page-entry control, not broader
newsletter-purpose closure, applicable suppression, withdrawal, sender/send
authorization or removal of private-access rights. Those keep their own fences.

For A, “already accepted” means the native source durably accepted the original
request before Off. Opening an old form or starting a network request is not
enough. Unknown-response recovery must recover the original outcome rather than
manufacture a new acceptance. No read, retry, resend or toggle extends the
original validity or revives an expired/withdrawn request. The exact finite
period remains the subsequent separate decision.

This is a user-visible product choice: it determines whether the confirmation
link a reader reasonably expects to use still works and what staff understand
the entry toggle to mean. A aligns the control with its narrow stated purpose
and avoids stranding accepted work; B offers a stronger stop but requires clear
copy explaining its additional effect. Neither requires another settings panel,
per-reader exception, approval queue or automatic resubscription.
