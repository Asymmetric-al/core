the research working tree. **Q42 B is selected. Disposition: Accept with

## Exact corrected decision to record

> **B — Join using the reader's current verified account email.** When the
> organization permits this option, a signed-in reader deliberately choosing
> **Join newsletter** or **Rejoin newsletter** subscribes immediately to that
> named newsletter using the same current verified account address shown to
> them. The server validates the current Supabase identity and qualified
> verification evidence for that exact address. Signing in alone never
> subscribes anyone. Guests, other addresses and accounts without suitable
> verification evidence use the ordinary email-confirmation path, without
> needing an account.
>
> An already active identical subscription stays unchanged. A fresh permitted
> rejoin replaces only that newsletter's prior withdrawal; it cannot clear
> broader objections, complaints, hard bounces or other applicable restrictions.
> A retry of an older Join cannot undo a later withdrawal. Changing an account
> email never moves existing subscriptions automatically.
>
> Authorized staff have one organization-wide control, **Let signed-in readers
> use their verified email**. When it is Off, subsequent new signups and rejoins
> confirm by email. Existing subscriptions and their evidence are unchanged.
> Existing organization and Page controls govern whether native signup is
> offered at all. These controls do not unsubscribe readers or authorize sends.
>
> Both signup paths use one native subscription and consent authority. Signup
> grants no donor claim, CRM relationship, tenant membership, protected reading
> or other access. Personalized address and subscription feedback are private
> interaction data, outside shared public Page caches. Success means the source
> has accepted the subscription; it does not mean an email was delivered.

This preserves B's actual convenience. A permanent implementation that sends
confirmation to everyone because it never qualifies a reusable proof path does
**not** satisfy B. Conversely, `email_confirmed_at` alone does not satisfy it.

## Scope, alternatives and explicit supersession

The strongest alternative is email confirmation for every new signup/rejoin.
It has one proof ceremony and avoids evidence-reuse qualification, but adds an
inbox round trip even when the exact current account address has suitable proof.
B separates two facts: the new deliberate Join establishes newsletter-specific
intent; qualified account evidence establishes the address assurance. It does
not claim fresh inbox possession on every Join. This is a proportionate product
judgment, not a legal rule or a measured conversion claim.

B supersedes the **unadopted** universal-confirmation/no-verified-shortcut
recommendation in [D6 C05](phase28-d6-adversarial-review.md:346), including
dependent form/test wording for this branch. It retains active-identical no-op,
purpose-specific consent, ordinary guest confirmation and stronger restrictions.
[D7](phase28-d7-adversarial-review.md:13) expressly did not ratify D6. Ratified
[completeness C07](phase28-completeness-adversarial-review.md#c07--amendment-status-and-genuine-policy-gates)
preserves that distinction. This answer is not blanket D6 adoption.

The user's new tenant-control request also replaces Q42's earlier proposal to
add no confirmation-mode setting. One organization-wide narrowing option meets
that request; per-reader, missionary, project, provider and Page exceptions do
not have an evidenced need. The new-capability default is On only after the
native capability is deliberately enabled and technically qualified. Existing
stricter policy and Page-entry Off are preserved; missing policy is never On.

D6-C07's pending-confirmation-after-Page-Off policy and D6-C15's proposed
72-hour/24-hour timing and retention are **still separate, unadopted choices**.
No such number or default completion policy is adopted here. Completeness
C04/C05 remain ratified. E7/C-01 remain in their financial owning scope.

## The complete small user journey

### Organization setup and discoverability

Place **Let signed-in readers use their verified email** with existing newsletter
settings. Helper: “Readers who choose Join can subscribe with their current
verified account email. Other readers confirm by email.” Off helper: “New
signups and rejoins confirm by email. Existing subscriptions stay as they are.”
Use existing settings authority and a real saved result. Technical unavailability
is distinct from a staff-selected Off. Staff cannot self-certify a proof source.

Keep the existing capability and **Show newsletter signup on this page**
controls for availability. Explain once that hiding signup leaves existing
subscriptions and independently authorized sends unchanged. A broader source
shutdown, withdrawal and Page unpublishing retain their own meanings. No new
dashboard, approval queue or per-contact switch is needed.

The public ministry Page presents the named newsletter, public-safe organization
name and useful description. Promise cadence only when supported. Signup is
outside giving; neither gift entry nor a donation receipt implies consent.
Do not use a forced popup or an account modal. A custom domain without a valid
current session gets the guest form; no cross-domain cookie bridge is needed.

### Qualified signed-in reader

```text
Maya's newsletter
News from [public ministry name], from [organization].

Join using reader@example.org
[ Join newsletter ]
Use another email

You can unsubscribe from this newsletter at any time.
Privacy information
```

Use the existing Base UI, exact `base-maia`, Zinc semantic colors, spacing,
typography and focus patterns from [shared UI configuration](../../../../packages/ui/components.json).
The address is visible and wraps. Do not require email/name re-entry, an extra
checkbox, verification badge, password, second confirmation dialog or a wizard.
The one deliberate action records the instruction. The private resulting view
says **You're subscribed to Maya's newsletter**, shows the address and provides
the qualified unsubscribe/manage action. It promises no issue delivery.

**Use another email** opens the ordinary short form without signing out or
changing the account. If the user switches accounts through existing account
navigation, refresh the private signup context. Never silently substitute the
new account's address for the one approved in an already open form.

### Guest, other-address and unqualified-proof reader

Use required Email and optional single Name, one Join action, readable purpose
and withdrawal information. No account, password, postal address, phone or gift
is required. A known unqualified signed-in address can be prefilled with clear
“Confirm by email” guidance. An unavailable Auth service is not proof that an
instruction was accepted; retain a deliberate guest option and honest retry.

After durable public intake, use non-enumerating feedback such as **Signup
received** with “If a confirmation email is needed and can be sent, we'll send
you a link. Existing subscriptions stay unchanged.” A qualified private pending
receipt may say **Check your email to confirm**. Never expose an arbitrary
address's membership, suppression or donor status, or falsely assert that a
pending membership exists in every branch.

The email identifies this newsletter. Opening a link shows an explicit
confirmation action; GET, scanners and previews do not subscribe. Confirmation
works without login and without requiring the original device. Provide typo
correction and bounded reader-requested resend through the same source. Do not
silently enroll both addresses, reset lifetimes or turn this into Supabase login.

### Rejoin and edge-case feedback

Labels, errors and status messages must work by keyboard and screen reader,
without color-only or toast-only meaning. Preserve focus and entered values,
email autocomplete, one international-friendly optional name, 320 CSS pixel
reflow, zoom, long addresses, translated text and reduced motion. Use supported
international email handling; do not strip plus tags/dots or invent ASCII-only
validation. If the actual delivery lane lacks an address capability, explain
that limitation truthfully before accepting unsupported delivery. Dates in
history are localized; authoritative timestamps are UTC instants. Basic signup
must stay light on a poor mobile connection, without trackers or editor bundles.

The permanent path is one source-owned admission command through
[`packages/api`](../../../guides/architecture/data-access-boundary.md), using

donor settings Boolean, add browser table writes or send directly through a
provider. Replace misleading touched preference behavior with actual named
source results when integrating, preserving any independently governed donor
preferences.

Minimal invariants, without prematurely choosing table/function names:

1. At most one current membership per trusted tenant, exact newsletter purpose
   and canonical destination; different newsletters/tenants remain independent.
   Neither global email nor Auth subject alone is the membership key.
2. Active consent requires both deliberate purpose intent and qualifying address
   evidence. Current eligibility and delivery are separate facts. No absence of
   a suppression row can supply positive consent.
3. Tenant/purpose/destination/actor/proof/policy attribution comes from trusted
   server sources and cannot be changed through an allowed pending-row update.
4. Accepted intent, evidence, result and necessary identifier-only downstream
   intent commit together. Optional confirmation transport does not own consent.
5. New human rejoin can supersede ordinary withdrawal; old accepted/pending
   intent cannot. Active-identical operations do not manufacture new consent.
6. Auth/address/policy changes have a defined order relative to admission. They
   cannot substitute another destination or retrospectively rewrite history.
7. Optional Auth linkage is not a tenant access grant. Auth deletion cannot
   silently cascade away required consent/withdrawal evidence or transfer a
   subscription. Privacy owners govern minimization and eventual erasure.
8. No money amount, receipt, commitment, balance, supporter edge or fundraiser
   task is created by joining. No new financial data type or precision rule is
   needed for this decision.

## Material findings

Likelihood is conditional design risk without the prevention, not a numerical
incident estimate. Severity describes potential user/domain harm. The exact
normative text is in the cited C clauses; the linked P cases make it falsifiable.

### F01 — A confirmed flag is mistaken for mailbox evidence

**High severity; likely if the obvious flag-only implementation is used.**
Implicit/admin confirmation and arbitrary identity metadata can qualify an
unproved destination. This matters because B promises reuse of verified evidence,
not removal of address assurance. Evidence: local configuration, seeding and
current Supabase documentation. **Narrows B**, without rejecting it. Permanent
fix: P4-qualified provenance/current revision and a real ordinary mailbox proof
producer, with safe legacy fallback. **Exact change: C02–C04; P03–P07.**

### F02 — Public reader admission inherits the wrong tenant or privileged hat

**Critical severity; plausible.** Reusing the member-only context can exclude
legitimate readers, provision them as donors or admit/query another tenant's
data. Current Auth context and P12 public projection have different purposes.
**Clarifies B's authority.** Permanent fix: trusted public target resolution,
the certified source command and narrow self-Join; no role fabrication or
privileged impersonation of consent. **Exact change: C05/C13/C17; P08–P11/P28.**

### F03 — A UI Boolean or provider list becomes shadow consent truth

**High severity; likely if current settings/helper code is copied.** The
inspected local settings save does not persist a native subscription; the
suppression helper is negative-only. The UI can falsely report success or send
without positive purpose consent. **Changes integration requirements.** Use one
native authority, explicit recipient qualification and source-derived UI;
preserve independent preferences. **Exact change: C06/C12/C16; P01/P12/P29.**

### F04 — Rejoin or retry resurrects withdrawn or suppressed consent

**High severity; plausible.** A global subscribe upsert clears the wrong scope;
new transport keys replay stale intent, while permanent per-address dedupe blocks
legitimate fresh rejoin. Evidence: separate existing restriction owners, consent
cardinality, and the new bounded counterexample model. **Narrows rejoin and
changes lifecycle precision.** Use durable intent identity, withdrawal ordering,
fresh deliberate rejoin and active-identical no-op. **C10/C14/C16; P13–P18.**

### F05 — Current proof is checked before a later, unguarded write

**High severity; plausible under concurrent native Auth changes.** A completed
email change, logout, policy change or closure can invalidate the old branch
between `getUser` and commit. Matching A→B→A text hides changed proof. Direct
native Auth endpoints make a newsletter-only cache lock insufficient. **Adds
required acceptance semantics.** P4 must qualify the authoritative current-state
fence and its ordering with admission; no asynchronous-cache or HTTP-read
atomicity claim. **Exact change: C11/C15; P19–P23.**

### F06 — Convenience exposes identity, list membership or other organizations

**High severity; plausible.** Shared Page HTML, personalized RSC/cache responses,
raw unique-key errors, telemetry or a recovery lookup can reveal private reader
data. Current Page audience invariance and Supabase SSR cache warnings support
the concern. **Narrows rendering and disclosure.** Private request-scoped state,
non-enumerating public outcomes, safe errors and current recovery authority
prevent it. **Exact change: C05/C17/C19/C20; P24–P28/P38.**

### F07 — Partial success is presented as failure, pending or delivery

**Medium severity; likely on unreliable networks without explicit recovery.**
An accepted operation with a lost response may be repeated; an enqueue failure
may be mislabeled “email sent”; an Auth outage may trigger unsolicited fallback
mail. The current timer-success pattern and missing exact producer are evidence
of an integration obligation, not a production incident. **Changes feedback and
recovery requirements.** Atomic durable result, same-operation recovery and
separate delivery states. **Exact change: C09/C16/C18; P20/P29–P31.**

### F08 — Controls and steps obscure a simple deliberate choice

**Medium severity; likely if every technical exception becomes a setting.**
Ambiguous “Enable newsletters,” extra confirmation dialogs, required login,
disabled unexplained buttons and proof-age/provider rules create friction and
misconfiguration. Evidence: the selected user journey, D6/D7 distinct controls,
W3C requirements and bounded product comparisons. **Simplifies B.** One org
option plus existing entry controls, obvious address/other-address choice and
accessible truthful states. **Exact change: C07–C09/C18; P01/P02/P32–P35.**

### F09 — The shortcut silently expands another domain's authority

**High severity; plausible.** Generic PKCE success, native OTP, automatic social
linking, imported contacts or a provider callback could be treated as permission
to claim a donor, create an account, subscribe or send. P4 A10/G01, P25 request
scope and P6/P17 producer contracts explicitly disagree. **Narrows dependencies
and requires explicit owner reconciliation.** No new Auth system, OTP login
shortcut or provider subscriber master. **C03–C06/C16/C23; P06–P12/P29/P40.**

### F10 — Unbounded lookup, replay or confirmation work creates cost and delay

**Medium severity; plausible under concentrated public traffic.** Broad email
scans, N+1 proof reads, unbounded pending records or unrestricted resends can
degrade service and invite email bombing. No benchmark justifies a throughput
claim. **Adds bounded operating requirements, not new product settings.** Exact
indexed scope, bounded source queues and shared abuse controls, measured query
and lock behavior. **Exact change: C19/C21; P36/P37/P39.**

### F11 — Rollout, deletion or restore fabricates or loses consent history

**High severity; plausible during adoption or recovery.** A profile/email-based
backfill invents opt-in; Auth cascade deletion loses withdrawal evidence; old
writers bypass safeguards; rollback or restore reenables removed consent.
Evidence: current profile cascade, unqualified historical provenance and absence
of a qualified native migration. **Adds lifecycle safeguards.** Evidence-based
adoption, writer fencing, privacy-owned minimization and restore reconciliation.
**Exact change: C20/C22; P07/P38/P39.**

### F12 — A selected direction is advertised as ratified or working software

**High severity; likely if the session's many prior decisions are summarized
loosely.** It can silently adopt D6 Off/retention policy, copy a stale owner
constraint or mark runtime proof complete from a model. **Changes traceability
and readiness claims.** Clause-level supersession, separated selected/reviewed/
ratified/formal/implemented/proved states and exact production-shaped tests.
**Exact change: C23/C24; P40 and the full proof matrix.**

## Exact required amendments

The following language is ready to carry into the developing spec after its
applicable adoption/formalization step. SHALL denotes the recommended contract,
not a claim that current code already enforces it.

### C01 — Exact selected admission behavior

A new native newsletter signup or rejoin SHALL require deliberate intent for
one named newsletter. Where organization policy allows and C02–C05 qualify the
same current displayed account address, Join SHALL activate through the native
source without another mailbox-confirmation step. Other readers SHALL retain
the ordinary confirmation path without an account requirement. Login, donation,
follow, import and Page viewing SHALL NOT create subscriptions.

### C02 — Qualified reusable address evidence

Immediate admission SHALL require server-validated current Supabase principal
and session plus P4-accepted mailbox-verification provenance for the exact current
subject/address revision. `email_confirmed_at`, cached JWT email, `getSession`,
profile/CRM email, editable metadata, arbitrary linked identity, autoconfirm,
administrative marking or unknown imported proof alone SHALL NOT qualify. The
current authentication and credential-admission path SHALL independently satisfy
P4's applicable native/provider gates. Prior mailbox evidence SHALL NOT qualify
an otherwise unadmitted attached authenticator. Proof
reuse SHALL NOT be described as fresh mailbox possession or donor-claim proof.

### C03 — A real proof-producing path, owned once

Before B is enabled, the shared Auth owner SHALL qualify at least the ordinary
native mailbox-verification path and its exact challenge/destination/redemption
evidence. A generic successful PKCE callback SHALL NOT be treated as that proof.
Provider evidence MAY qualify only through its separately admitted P4 lane;
affected A10/G01 gates remain. Store only necessary proof/current-revision facts
in the existing application-owned private identity scope. Do not add newsletter
identity truth or custom objects in managed Auth schemas. Unknown historical
provenance SHALL use ordinary newsletter confirmation, not fabricated backfill.

### C04 — No identity or access elevation from newsletter signup

Newsletter Join/confirmation SHALL NOT create an Auth account/session, change
account email, link identities, upgrade account-verification assurance, claim a
Party/donor, infer a person match or grant protected access. Anonymous newsletter
confirmation SHALL use the non-auth exact-purpose source capability, not native
`signInWithOtp`/`verifyOtp`. No arbitrary proof-age limit or forced repeated
reauthentication is added by this decision; the qualified P4 policy governs.

### C05 — Trusted public target and narrow reader authority

The command SHALL resolve operational Tenant, Site/Page and exact newsletter
from the trusted published target contract, not posted tenant IDs or the reader's
profile tenant. The caller SHALL prove only the exact self-Join evidence needed;
no target-tenant workforce/donor membership is required or created. Public
projection remains read-only; mutation uses the qualified P23/P28 server source
command and existing service authority. Staff/view-as/impersonation SHALL NOT
manufacture a reader's deliberate instruction. No fifth P12 context or role
union is introduced.

### C06 — One subscription source and positive eligibility

Both paths SHALL converge on one native membership/consent authority under P3.
All touched preference displays and actions SHALL use its actual outcomes;
local booleans and delayed success displays are not consent. P6 eligibility
SHALL require positive exact-purpose membership and current independent
restrictions. Provider contacts/lists, negative-only suppression helpers,
P25 external requests and imports SHALL NOT become positive admission sources.
D7 issue authorization and frozen audience remain separate.

### C07 — One organization option and honest disablement

Authorized staff SHALL control **Let signed-in readers use their verified email**
once per organization. On permits only the qualified branch; Off sends subsequent
new signups/rejoins through ordinary confirmation. The recommended default is On
for newly and deliberately enabled, qualified native capability. Existing
stricter policy and Page Off SHALL survive rollout; missing/invalid policy is
not On. A saved change SHALL be versioned and checked at admission. It SHALL NOT
rewrite existing consent, activate pending work or replay old intent. Existing
source and Page availability controls retain their own effects. No per-reader,
project, missionary, Page or provider confirmation exceptions are added.

### C08 — One-action signed-in UI

The private signup interaction SHALL show the exact current address, named
newsletter, one **Join newsletter**/**Rejoin newsletter** action and **Use another
email**. It SHALL require no duplicate email/name input or second consent dialog
for the qualified branch. Other-address choice SHALL enter ordinary confirmation
without changing Auth. Active-identical membership SHALL be truthfully displayed
without new admission or confirmation. One action is measured from a ready,
qualified signup view, not from discovering or loading the Page.

### C09 — Guest and fallback remain first-class

The ordinary form SHALL require only Email, with optional single Name, clear
purpose and one submission action. It SHALL require no login, gift or account.
Known ineligible proof MAY use that explicit path with clear confirmation copy.
Unknown Auth/authorization failure SHALL NOT automatically send mail or claim
pending/active status; offer deliberate recovery or the ordinary form. Public
outcomes SHALL not enumerate membership, restrictions or CRM matches. Only a
qualified narrow receipt may disclose exact pending state.

### C10 — Rejoin and stronger restrictions

A fresh eligible reader instruction MAY replace only that newsletter/destination's
ordinary withdrawal, preserving its history. It SHALL NOT clear organization-wide
objection, complaint, hard bounce, person-level restriction or other current
safety constraint. Scope and remedy follow the owning restriction. An identical
active membership SHALL be a no-op. Rejoin SHALL need no staff ticket or second
dialog solely because it is a rejoin. Source-accepted consent, deliverability
and observed delivery SHALL remain distinct.

### C11 — Address and account lifecycle

The approved destination and current account-address revision SHALL match at
admission. Changed account/address requires review of the new displayed choice;
the command SHALL NOT silently substitute it. A→B→A SHALL not reuse retired
revision evidence merely because text matches. Subsequent account changes SHALL
not move subscriptions, rewrite evidence or grant new consent. Shared mailbox
proof SHALL not merge people. Account deletion SHALL follow C20/C22 rather than
cascading away required source history or assuming withdrawal of all independent
newsletter memberships.

### C12 — Database identity and legal states

The native source SHALL enforce non-null trusted tenant/purpose/destination
identity, tenant-aware relationships and at most one current membership per
exact scope. Optional Auth linkage SHALL have explicit null/delete behavior and
shall not be the only membership identity. Evidence SHALL bind the approved
destination/revision, intent, qualified proof reference, policy/disclosure
revision, trusted actor or guest capability and server acceptance instant.
Constraints and the single mutation boundary SHALL prevent active-without-proof,
scope transformation and duplicate durable effects. Corrections append meaning;
they do not rewrite past consent. Use source-owned supported email comparison;
no invented plus/dot equivalence or global-email person key.

### C13 — Effective grants, RLS and every privileged path

Browser roles SHALL have no raw positive-admission/proof/evidence mutation
capability. All APIs, workers and RPCs SHALL preserve old-row access, allowed
new state and immutable tenant/actor/purpose fields. Review effective grants,
`USING`, `WITH CHECK`, policy composition, views and function execution rights;
literal absence of `WITH CHECK` alone is not a finding. Privileged commands must
enforce the same source rules despite service-role RLS bypass. Definer functions
require narrow execute grants, fixed safe search paths and qualified objects.
No public proof table, subscriber Realtime feed, storage policy or raw constraint
error may leak membership. Supported state mutations need readback proof.

### C14 — Durable intent, withdrawal ordering and same-effect retry

Admission SHALL serialize on the exact consent aggregate and atomically record
the business effect, evidence, durable operation outcome and any required
identifier-only secondary intent. Retry SHALL recover the same effect without
reapplying it after newer withdrawal. A new HTTP key alone is not a new human
rejoin; a permanent per-address idempotency key SHALL not prevent a genuinely
new later instruction. Concurrent active-identical joins yield one membership.
Old pending confirmation and pre-withdrawal intent SHALL not resurrect consent.

### C15 — Currentness at a defined acceptance point

P4 and the native source SHALL qualify a defined authoritative acceptance point
ordering current session/address proof, source availability, current policy and
restrictions with admission. A change ordered before that point must prevent
use of the invalidated branch; a change after accepted admission does not
retroactively retarget its destination. A `getUser` request followed by an
unrelated write, asynchronous invalidation or a lock only on a newsletter cache
SHALL NOT be claimed to establish this order. The supported P4 adapter must
cover direct native Auth mutations and prove the actual race. No scattered
managed-schema dependency or new distributed transaction framework is prescribed.
An unqualified immediate path SHALL remain unavailable rather than fail open.

### C16 — Pending proof, dispatch and recovery

Qualified immediate Join SHALL require no redundant confirmation message. When
confirmation is needed, it SHALL be purpose/destination/generation-bound and
complete only through the explicit protected action; GET/scanners/opening do
not mutate. A later qualified Join SHALL converge with pending work and make
obsolete proof unable to revive consent. Required messages SHALL use P6/P17's
qualified durable seam, not direct provider calls. Lost responses recover the
same accepted operation under current authority. Success is source acceptance;
queued/sent/delivered remain separately evidenced states. Retry never invents a
welcome sequence or enlarges an accepted issue audience.

### C17 — Private interaction and public non-enumeration

Public Page content SHALL remain audience-invariant. Exact account email,
subscription state, receipts and session-dependent controls SHALL be private,
request-scoped and excluded from shared HTML/RSC/CDN caches, URLs, previews and
public analytics. Sign-out/account/host/context changes SHALL fence stale
responses and clear private UI data. Use shared clients per request, not shared
mutable session clients. Public results/errors/timing SHALL avoid address-state
enumeration. Address-keyed subscription status, manage/rejoin and recovery SHALL
require qualified current same-address proof or an exact still-authorized source
capability admitting that disclosure/action. An authenticated but unqualified
address alone SHALL NOT reveal prior membership. A guest intake receipt admits
only its own permitted operation result, not existing address-wide history or
management. No path reveals other tenant/list/CRM relationships.

### C18 — Accessible and honest feedback

The existing design system SHALL provide visible labels, field-associated errors,
keyboard/focus operation, inline announced status and adequate touch targets.
Test 320 CSS pixel reflow, zoom, long/localized text, international-friendly name
entry and actual supported email transport. Preserve safe inputs through errors;
never display timed success before commit. Low bandwidth/offline handling SHALL
offer honest deliberate retry, not automatic future enrollment. No color-only
status, disappearing-toast-only outcome, mandatory animation or new UI library.

### C19 — Abuse and capability boundaries

All consent-increasing actions SHALL use protected state-changing requests with
the existing origin/CSRF defenses, bounded validation and exact intent scope.
GET/prefetch and a valid session alone cannot increase consent. Confirmation
and recovery capabilities SHALL be narrowly scoped, expiring, non-enumerating
and protected at rest; do not log raw secrets. Resend and public intake SHALL
use qualified shared abuse/rate controls without account lockout or address
reservation that lets an attacker block a real reader indefinitely. An accessible
challenge, if necessary, follows established adaptive controls rather than being
mandatory on every Join. Login-free reduction-only unsubscribe remains usable.

### C20 — Evidence, retention and privacy

Durable source history SHALL distinguish intent, proof provenance/reference,
policy version, actor attribution, acceptance, withdrawal and correction from
technical logs and provider events. Retain only what the governing privacy/source
schedule permits; no indefinite raw-email/credential archive for dedupe. Exports,
backups, logs and generated messages SHALL honor the same privacy boundary.
Auth deletion, erasure and purge require explicit treatment of optional identity
links and the minimum lawful anti-resurrection evidence. D42 SHALL NOT adopt
D6-C15's proposed timing constants or invent a new verification-age policy.

### C21 — Bounded performance and operating ownership

Exact membership, policy, proof and recovery lookup SHALL use bounded indexed
paths; pending/dispatch work SHALL use bounded batches and fair tenant scope.
Avoid broad mailbox scans and per-row remote Auth checks in list rendering.
Record dataset sizes, concurrency, query/lock plans and measured latency against
an approved release budget. No throughput number is claimed by this review.
The monitoring table below supplies signals, thresholds, accountable owners and
responses; monitoring SHALL NOT substitute for permission or currentness proof.

### C22 — Safe adoption, mixed versions and rollback

Activation SHALL require the exact native producer, qualified proof lane,
effective grants and old-writer fencing. No donation, profile, provider contact,
external request, imported opt-in or confirmation timestamp alone SHALL be
backfilled as consent/proof. Preserve legitimate existing policy/effects and
classify unknown provenance without granting new assurance. New code/old schema
must fail closed; old code/new schema must not bypass admission invariants.
Rollback disables new affected admissions while preserving accepted history,
recovery and withdrawal. Restore SHALL reconcile later withdrawals/restrictions
before sends resume; reenable SHALL not replay old intent.

### C23 — Exact owner and artifact traceability

Formalization SHALL map D42 to P3/P4/P6/P12/P17/P22/P23/P25 and native P28 source
contracts, explicitly reconcile D6-C05 and P32-only handoff wording, and preserve
unrelated identity, financial, import and send rules. Use shared Auth/API/database
boundaries and current components. The glossary records purpose intent versus
address evidence, not table/API design. Later authorized tickets, tests and
release evidence SHALL link the exact adopted C/P clauses. No duplicated consent
resolver, premature ticket or implicit formal ADR change is authorized here.

### C24 — Preserve unresolved policy and actual completion state

Q42 B is selected; this review's amendments remain separately reviewed until
adopted. D6 pending-after-Page-Off and its remaining finite proof/retention policy
SHALL remain visible unresolved choices. No Q43 answer, blanket D6 adoption,
financial ruling, implementation or release success is inferred. `/to-prd` and
`/to-issues` remain the separate transitions. Documentation/model results SHALL
state their bounds and never substitute for deployed Auth/RLS, browser, provider,
migration, performance or representative-user proof.
