## Inherited boundaries

- A Tenant is the sending organization. A missionary is its user. Mission
  Control, missionary workspace, donor portal, and public Sites retain their
  distinct purposes. Asym Postgres owns CRM truth; Twenty is retired.
- Phase 9 owns Parties, Ministry Assignments, referrals, qualified interaction
  records, and shared tasks. Phase 27 owns continuing partnership work, Requests,
  Appeals, and stage meaning. Private notes and care records are not shared
  partnership updates. Participation in shared history remains voluntary.
- Ministry Assignment, participant membership, Support Assignment, responsible
  worker, login, public-page contributor, newsletter recipient, and coach are
  different concepts. None silently grants the others' permissions.
- Phases 13/14/16/21 own received contributions, recognition, commitments and
  fulfillment, and financial balances/plans respectively. A Phase 28 fundraising
  goal is not a balance or a finance Support Plan.
- Phase 6 owns recipient eligibility, consent, dispatch, and delivery evidence;
  Phase 17 owns its content/compiler responsibilities; Phase 22 owns Ministry
  Updates and specialized public-page operations. Their exact newsletter
  extensions still need qualification.
- The Phase 25 public newsletter request is a limited request/handoff. It is not
  subscription, identity verification, CRM prospect admission, or roster access.
  Its narrow P28/P12 recipient projection already has published source work.
- A stage, next task, recorded conversation, gift, newsletter preference, and
  published story have different meanings. Saving or checking one cannot
  manufacture the others.
