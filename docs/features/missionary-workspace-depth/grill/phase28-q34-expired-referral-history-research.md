## The single choice

Maya received a referral but did not pursue it before its follow-up window
expired. She has no other active supporter or referral relationship with that
person. She later wants to revisit the introduction. This is an illustrative
scenario, not an observed Core incident or a claim that every ministry uses
the same referral deadline.

**Should Maya still be able to find a limited record of that expired referral when the organization permits it?**

- **A — Limited expired-referral history (recommended).** Maya can recognize
  the referral in a clearly marked Expired entry. It shows only the minimal
  information approved for this purpose. Contact details and private referral
  context remain unavailable through this entry. Continuing the referral is a
  separate authorized decision.
- **B — Staff-assisted recovery.** The expired referral leaves Maya's
  workspace. Authorized staff retain the permitted source history and help if
  she later wants to revisit it.

Both preserve source history under its retention rules, respect current privacy
restrictions and leave independently authorized supporter access intact. Neither
restores contact permission, subscriptions, old tasks or former access. This
choice does not decide who may continue the referral, set an expiry duration,
create a new request queue or authorize indefinite retention.

A is recommended because a small, recognizable history entry can preserve useful
orientation without keeping the referral in current work. B is the strongest
simpler alternative: it retains the present active-relationship boundary and
puts recovery with authorized staff, at the cost of a handoff. The usability
benefit is a product judgment, not measured missionary preference.

## Why visibility came before continuation authority

The [founder brief](phase28-founder-brief.md:137) already requires referral
provenance, expiry, cannot-pursue dispositions and deliberate continuation after
expiry without contact permission. It does not select historical discovery or
the actor allowed to continue. The accepted
[workspace boundary](../../../prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md:143)
limits ordinary Party enumeration to an **active** supporter or referral
relationship. Therefore an expired referral cannot quietly become its own
standing access grant.

**Ratified A requires an explicit, bounded P9/P12 source-purpose amendment** allowing
the eligible missionary to discover only a qualified minimal expired-referral
projection despite the absence of an active relationship. The ordinary Party
list/detail/contact gate remains unchanged. Even a name, existence, count or
date needs current authority; "minimal" is not a classification exemption.
Organization permission alone cannot override source/privacy floors. The founder selected the limited-history direction; the exact
source/design amendments in D34 are now explicitly ratified as future requirements,
not live permission or formal owner-contract integration.

An actor-only question could be asked conditionally for an independently readable
referral, but it would not settle the loss of the last active
relationship. Independent governance and UX reviewers identified this dependency.
The root reviewer chose the visibility question first; missionary self-service
continuation versus staff continuation remains downstream. An independent active
supporter relationship likewise does not automatically reveal a protected old
referral body.

## Selected UX direction and remaining proof

Use existing shared list/history controls and the exact Base UI/base-maia design
language. Avoid another dashboard, inbox or permanent warning banner. Keep
current referrals prominent. Under selected A, a clearly named history filter can
show eligible Expired entries with approved identifying information and actual
dates where known. Do not invent a date, expose hidden counts, display the
referrer's private details, add a Call/Email action, link through to unauthorized
Party detail or imply that opening history has continued the referral.

Explain the limited view in ordinary language: **“This referral has expired.
Contact details are unavailable here.”** A currently permitted route to help or
continuation can be added only when its source/action is qualified; no inactive
button should promise an unbuilt path. Under B, do not leak a removed item's
identity through an empty-state explanation. Existing independently authorized
records continue to appear normally under either option.

Required downstream proof includes keyboard/screen-reader and phone usability,
international names and localized unambiguous dates; exact expired-only versus
independent-access cases; denial after current authority or retention ends;
redaction of labels, counts, search, caches and exports; preservation of current
work and history through correction/reassignment; and clear separation of
discovery, continuation and contact permission. These are qualification needs,
not completed runtime tests or a formal acceptance specification.
