## The single choice

Grace already supports Maya's existing ministry. She gives toward that ministry's
documented one-time vehicle repair need. The gift's connection to that need is
properly recorded and confirmed, but Grace has not described it as additional
support. No other automatic-follow-up reason applies in this illustrative case:
it is not first support for a new project, an amount-rule trigger, above-commitment
support or fulfillment of a recorded promise. This is a design example, not a
reported interview or a required ministry workflow.

**Should that confirmed one-time purpose independently create personal follow-up?**

- **A — Recognize gifts for a specific one-time need (recommended).** An eligible
  gift creates follow-up with a clear factual reason, such as **“Gift for vehicle
  repairs”** when that detail is permitted. It need not also be described as
  additional support.
- **B — Require another established qualifying reason.** The one-time purpose
  remains useful context. Additional-support and other qualified automatic
  reasons still apply; Maya can create manual follow-up when helpful.

A recognizes the purpose of the actual gift without requiring a statement about
the donor's broader giving intentions. B keeps automatic work narrower and avoids
another purpose criterion. Neither asks donors a new checkout question, equates
an appeal label with evidence, creates another task list or sends a message.

  accepts explicitly additional support without a named appeal or specific need,
  but expressly reserves the separate specific-one-time-need criterion. Its
  later ratification does not select this reserved branch.
- [D21-C03](phase28-d21-explicit-gift-follow-up-adversarial-review.md:187)
  requires the remaining material native criteria to be resolved; it does not
  replace them with permanent manual-only work or a generic special flag.
  [D21-C04](phase28-d21-explicit-gift-follow-up-adversarial-review.md:198)
  separates unknown commitment data, catch-up, advance coverage and genuine
  additional money. None answers this purpose-sufficiency question.
- The [founder brief](phase28-founder-brief.md:165) requires qualified native
  special-gift stewardship grounded in history and purpose. It does not say that
  every appeal gift, payment made once or Request response is special.
- [P27 REQ27-F83](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-27-donor-development/requests.md:818)
  prohibits task creation from mere membership, linking or a response. P28
  needs an exact qualified gift-purpose occurrence, not a reinterpretation of
  those events. [P27's owner reconciliation](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-27-donor-development/owner-reconciliation.md:31)
  keeps working lists, actual responses, received money and financial links
  independent.
- [E7](../../../prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-27-donor-development/owner-reconciliation.md:41)
  remains a financial temporal-attribution gate. This choice cannot settle
  campaign reporting, source-code remapping or historical gift attribution.

Keep D19 firstness, D20 grouping/coverage, D22 amount rules, D29 additional
purpose, current P12 authority and financial ownership fixed. A need in the
existing ministry is used deliberately: introducing a new project could create
independent first-support work and conceal the choice. A recorded promise fully
fulfilled by this gift would likewise conceal it behind a fulfillment reason.

## Journey and counterexamples checked before asking

The donor gives through the ordinary flow. Source owners retain the received
money and exact eligible purpose. If A is selected and those facts qualify, Maya
would see one plain reason in her existing compatible task, with authorized gift
details and D20's reviewed-group completion. The example reason is illustrative,
not approval to expose a private need title or build a new classifier.

- An ordinary recurring-support appeal is not a one-time need merely because its
  campaign has an end date or its payment was made once.
- A received gift for an explicitly identified one-time need can be distinct
  from a donor's statement that the gift is additional. Do not fabricate the
  latter from the former.
- A working-list link, internal appeal tag or default fund alone cannot prove
  the exact gift purpose. Request editing and gift visibility are not new
  confirmation permissions; D29's scoped capability cannot be silently widened.
- A mixed gift cannot be wholly classified from evidence about one portion.
  Exact source ownership, precision, privacy and correction rules remain required.
- A gift satisfying both D29 and a selected need-purpose reason still has one
  D20 business member, not duplicate follow-up or duplicate communication.
- A need being closed or funded does not erase received money, rewrite past
  purpose, create another gift or prove a communication occurred. Exact closure,
  correction, late evidence and admission behavior must be reconciled in answer
  review. The selected A now has exact ratified lifecycle rules in D30; no
  deployment qualification follows.
- Historical-only imports, relabeling and previously handled gifts cannot be
  used to flood the queue. Missing qualification is not proof of no support.
- Manual follow-up remains available under either choice without pretending to
  consume native gift-purpose coverage merely because titles match.
