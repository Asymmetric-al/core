# D28 — preparation retention: governance and ownership

Evidence date: **October 17, 2026 UTC**. Repository baseline:
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` plus the current working tree.
**A — Organization-set finite period** is selected. This research note records
owner facts, bounded source inspection and proposed requirements; it is not a
formal owner amendment, implementation, legal conclusion or release qualification.

## Findings that determine the design

1. **This is a real new source policy.** D3 expressly left departure deletion and
   retention duration unselected. Its C06 requires any missing material policy
   choice to return to the grill. D27 grants only bounded current resumption of
   still-eligible history, not a retention schedule. A now selects organization
   control; it does not retrospectively make every retained body readable.
2. **P9 must own the preparation schedule and disposition.** P12 owns who may
   administer the policy and who may access content. P28 presents and consumes
   those facts. Neither the financial records schedule nor Eve's timers owns
   this source. The separate human author, current actor, original assignment,
   current working inclusion, disclosure and records lifecycle must survive.
3. **There is no evidence-backed universal number.** Use an explicit
   **Unconfigured** state, one finite whole-day tenant selection within a
   source-qualified bounded profile, and no silent legal default, zero-as-Off,
   forever or unsupported arbitrary duration. Exact privacy floors/ceilings and
   operational bounds require the actual source's qualification. A borrowed
   vendor or financial number is not evidence.
4. **Keep ordinary policy changes prospective.** Each confirmed departure
   episode pins the applicable policy version, trustworthy trigger facts and
   deadline. A normal setting edit applies to departures whose authoritative effective
   instant is on or after the revision boundary, with that effect plainly stated.
   Event delivery or confirmation time does not select the revision. Existing episodes keep their known finite schedule. Mandatory
   erasure, source correction or changed governing constraints still require an
   owner-qualified affected-record transition; optional retroactive bulk
   extension/shortening is not necessary to deliver A.
5. **Return needs an explicit product ruling.** D27 says Resume does not reset
   retention. Keeping the departure deadline controlling after legitimate active
   reuse can surprise Maya with deletion; cancelling it on login retains every
   unselected entry. The root has asked the founder the precise tradeoff. This
   note does not choose it while that answer is pending. The recommended route is
   a deliberately accepted, source-owned ordinary-active retention admission for
   selected eligible work before expiry, preserving the old departure facts as
   history. It must be explicitly reconciled with D27, not hidden in a job.

## Minimal proposed lifecycle

| Event/state                                                    | Proposed handling and boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current participation, no confirmed departure                  | Ordinary source retention applies. D28 introduces no inactivity timer, forced archive or always-running second deadline.                                                                                                                           |
| Confirmed departure affecting exact preparation context        | End access through P12 immediately. Resolve and pin the applicable finite source policy using trusted departure facts. Preserve source identity and Active/Archived meaning.                                                                       |
| Delayed/backdated/corrected departure event                    | Preserve effective event time and recorded/correction time distinctly. Never substitute worker execution or `updated_at`. A newly discovered past-due body must enter qualified restricted review/disposition, not unreviewed instant destruction. |
| Before ordinary deadline, author still absent                  | Retained body remains under current restrictions. It is not staff-readable merely because the organization pays for storage or manages policy.                                                                                                     |
| Return/login before deadline without selected resumption       | Does not extend the body, stop every deadline or revive prior working selections. Current D27 history authority is still needed before discovery.                                                                                                  |
| Deliberate selected resumption before deadline                 | Founder clarification is pending. Recommended: a separately qualified source admission can move the same entry to ordinary active retention, preserving immutable prior schedule facts; clarify the composition with D27 and its effect to Maya.   |
| Author returns after ordinary deadline but purge is delayed    | Worker lag does not extend eligibility. No ordinary read/resume merely because bytes remain. Recovery would require a separately selected owner policy, not a race against the deletion worker.                                                    |
| Policy is edited                                               | Recommended default: prospective departures only. Preserve prior version/effect evidence and a plainly shown effective time. Do not silently rewrite existing deadlines.                                                                           |
| Mandatory lawful restriction/erasure or corrected source facts | Apply through the qualified source correction/disposition path to the affected records, even if their tenant preference remains longer. Preserve only independently required evidence.                                                             |
| Hold overlaps ordinary eligible period                         | Hold prevents disposal within its scope. It creates no new ordinary access and need not itself remove an independently valid ordinary access path.                                                                                                 |
| Ordinary deadline passes while hold remains                    | Ordinary access ends; required evidence remains restricted. A hold is not an extended return window. Hold review dates do not auto-release it.                                                                                                     |
| Hold is explicitly released                                    | Re-evaluate current schedule and restrictions; an already-expired body enters disposal, not a new retention period.                                                                                                                                |
| Physical deletion partly fails                                 | Keep access denied and record truthful pending/failed copy-class results; retry idempotently. Do not claim complete erasure merely because one SQL row or Storage object was removed.                                                              |
| Backup/restore or migration                                    | Reapply current restriction/erasure/suppression before reads or jobs reopen; preserve schedule provenance. Never restore old access, ended effects or a fresh retention clock.                                                                     |

The precise expiry boundary must be unambiguous in the later source contract:
use one trusted instant and deny ordinary reads/resumption at or after it. A
whole-day configuration needs one documented computation convention and an exact
displayed result; do not alternate calendar-day and elapsed-day meanings with
server or user timezone. This is an engineering precision requirement, not a
new request for the founder to choose timezone arithmetic.

## Default, bounds and existing-record treatment

**Recommended product strategy:** the organization selects one period for this
source, in a fixed unit with a clearly shown result. Default **Unconfigured**
must be a real not-ready state; it must not mean Off/forever, a guessed global
period or immediate deletion. A positive whole-day value is a proposed simple
unit, not evidence of a particular minimum or maximum. The actual finite range
must be supported by P9's qualified privacy/records profile and operational
limits. Do not present an unvalidated astronomical number as meaningfully finite.

D3-C06 requires a qualified disposition before preparation-source activation.
Where no independently valid existing baseline exists, missing configuration
blocks new activation of this affected source; it does not block ordinary shared
CRM, other sources or every missionary workspace feature. Existing records with
known schedules retain them. Ambiguous legacy records require a bounded,
restricted owner review and explicit qualified enrollment; importing current
`updated_at`, last login, email or today's policy as historical authority is
invalid. This review does not assert that such legacy preparation already exists.

Ordinary prospective changes are a defensible minimal implementation of tenant
control. Optional retrospective changes are a new higher-risk capability, not
an unavoidable consequence of A. If later selected, they require a bounded
impact set, current source constraints, expected-version fencing, truthful old
and new dates and an explicit effect review. Mandatory governing changes cannot
be ignored under the pretext of prospectivity. Their owner-qualified correction
path must not become an unrestricted tenant bulk-delete/extend tool.

Retention administrators need enough consequence information to act safely but
do not automatically gain a private inventory. For prospective changes, scope
and effective time normally explain the entire effect without naming people or
counting hidden entries. Any aggregate or existing-record impact disclosure must
itself pass P12/source authority; operational services may compute an exact impact
set without making that set visible to an unauthorized staff viewer.

## Product choices versus implementation obligations

**Selected now:** the organization controls a finite departure-retention period.
**Proposed economical amendments:** one policy per organization/source, explicit
Unconfigured state, prospective ordinary setting edits, no new private audience,
and source-owned versioned schedules. These are reviewable product judgments.

**Awaiting the root's pending founder clarification:** the treatment of a selected
entry deliberately resumed before expiry. Do not record either return outcome as
ratified until the answer is received. Other materially new policy choices—such
as a zero-retention opt-out, unrestricted retroactive changes, guaranteed recovery
grace, fixed default or cross-ministry transfer—are not silently selected.

**Engineering/source qualification:** exact keys and source event identity,
boundary arithmetic, constraints, authorization and grant census, concurrency,
minimal records evidence, bounded worker/retry/disposal adapters, backup restore,
privacy-approved numeric profile, migration and measured operation. Ordinary
engineering resolution is not a reason to ask the founder every implementation
detail. Conversely a new default or limit that changes retention entitlement is
not merely a parser choice.
