## Exact corrected decision to record

> **D28 — Organization-set retention for unshared preparation after departure.**
> Authorized staff may set one finite organization-wide period for this narrowly
> defined preparation source, within its qualified privacy and records rules.
> The setting controls how long eligible preparation may be kept for the
> original author's possible return; it grants nobody access to its contents.
>
> Use one clearly labeled duration with a fixed unit, concise help choosing the
> shortest justified period, and a plain explanation of what happens at expiry.
> There is no invented universal recommended number, hidden infinite default,
> per-person timer or generic retention-rule builder. A missing policy is an
> unresolved setup state, never permission to keep everything forever or delete
> everything immediately.
>
> A confirmed departure binds the exact original author, organization, ministry,
> source episode, effective departure time and policy revision. Ordinary edits
> to the organization setting apply to future departures. Existing episodes keep
> their recorded deadlines. Independently required erasure, lawful restrictions
> and source corrections still apply to existing records through their qualified
> owner; this is not a way to preserve an obsolete unlawful schedule.
>
> At an applicable departure deadline, ordinary discovery and access stop even if cleanup is
> delayed. Required held evidence remains restricted. Cleanup disposes only the
> qualified preparation and its actual derivatives, preserves separately governed
> facts and minimal scheduled evidence, and prevents backup restoration from
> re-exposing disposed content. Do not claim permanent destruction until the
> relevant copy outcomes have been verified.
>
> Maya sees the applicable policy and, where she is currently authorized to see
> an entry, its actual availability deadline. Her ordinary work remains simple.
> Staff see settings and permitted operational status, not private preparation
> inventories. No departure wizard, new recipient notice, successor access,
> restarted sharing or automatic export follows.

> **Selected return clarification:** a deliberate, authorized D27 Resume accepted
> **before** expiry admits only that same selected entry to the qualified
> current-work retention purpose and ends applicability of its departure
> schedule. Original departure/deadline history remains intact. Login,
> reactivation, viewing or resuming another entry does not preserve the rest.
> Expired, hold-only or purged content cannot enter this path.

**Selection versus remaining amendments:** the founder selected organization
control and explicitly answered the return clarification on October 17, 2026 UTC:
“Keep resumed preparation as current work under the normal retention rules; end
its departure deadline.” This narrow meaning now refines D27-C07. D27's current
text is reconciled; its original text and the initial D28 evidence are preserved
in the [clarification record](d28-evidence/return-clarification.md). This answer
does not ratify the remaining D28 amendments, choose a numerical period or qualify
an implementation.

The selected organization control is accepted in principle. The period's actual
number and supported bounds cannot be inferred from this answer. They require
the concrete source/privacy profile described below. That is a specific delivery
gate, not a substitute for selecting the product direction.

## Evidence, current behavior and ownership

Evidence labels matter: **repository intent** is an owning contract;
**current source** is inspected code, not deployed behavior; **external fact** is
directly read primary documentation; **judgment** is our proposed product/design
amendment; **unqualified** means actual runtime or participant evidence is absent.

- **Repository intent:** [D3-C06](phase28-d3-adversarial-review.md:249),
  [D4](phase28-d4-adversarial-review.md:58) and
  [D27-C07](phase28-d27-resume-preparation-adversarial-review.md#d27-c07--retention-holds-and-erasure)
  expressly reserve exact disposition. P9 owns preparation, P12 owns current
  administration/access, and P4 owns canonical identity. A policy administrator
  is not thereby a private-content reader. See the
  [governance evidence](d28-evidence/governance-and-ownership.md).
- **Scoped precedent:** [ADR-0038](../../../adr/0038-purpose-owned-records-schedules-and-verified-disposal.md)
  separates access, retention, hold and disposal and requires verified recovery.
  Its Phase 21 precision amendment explicitly rejects platform-wide semantic
  ownership. Its seven document obligations, financial periods, tenant extension
  model and grace rules are not silently imported into preparation.
- **Current source:** the inspected workspace has no qualified D28 preparation
  policy/source contract. Existing CRM notes have a different audience. Eve
  retention is a different source with different policy and external-delete
  behavior; it is not proof that preparation disposal is ready. Exact current
  source references and limits are in the
  [database review](d28-evidence/database-and-authorization.md).
- **Possible public-policy conflict:** [current policy configuration](../../../../apps/donor/openpolicy.ts:109)
  states periods for other named data classes and backups. Classification of P9
  preparation and the applicable MSA/DPA must be reconciled before activation.
  The [Phase 21 gate](../../../guides/features/openpolicy-legal-pages.md:164)
  is a scoped precedent, not a universal P9 owner. Departure, account closure,
  inactivity and an erasure request must not become synonyms.
- **External fact:** [HubSpot retention settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/privacy-and-consent/manage-data-retention-policy-settings),
  updated September 28, 2026, demonstrate authorized period configuration and
  explicit deletion consequences. Their inactivity triggers and numerical
  periods do not establish Asym's period or trigger.
- **External guidance:** the [ICO storage-limitation guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/)
  supports purpose-based, justifiable periods and early deletion where appropriate,
  rather than a universal duration. This is UK guidance, currently marked under
  review, not a global legal determination or permission to retain solely because
  future usefulness is imaginable.
- **Product judgment:** prospective setting changes, one duration field, no
  default number and proportionate review give the clearest bounded feature.
  An Asym-standard period was the strongest simpler alternative; it reduces
  configuration but cannot accommodate the tenant control the founder selected.

The [UX/primary evidence](d28-evidence/ux-and-primary-research.md) records additional
comparisons and accessibility sources. No ministry interview, authenticated

## The complete user journey

**Staff setup.** Place **Preparation after departure** in the existing authorized
organization settings, using Core's shared Maia/Base UI components and semantic
tokens. One compact card states what is covered and that setting the period does
not reveal private preparation. Show **Not configured** when no qualified tenant
choice exists. Do not use an On/Off switch for a policy whose absence would have
ambiguous retention consequences.

The duration label is **Keep unshared preparation for** with a whole-number
**days after departure** suffix. Use a fixed unit rather than days/months/years
toggles with unequal conversions. Show the supported limits from the exact
qualified source profile. Explain a day as a 24-hour duration; display resulting
deadlines in the reader's locale with an explicit time zone when relevant. Do not
silently reinterpret an entered day as a calendar month or a local midnight.

Suggested helper copy: **“Choose the shortest period your organization can
justify for someone returning to their ministry. Consider your actual return
needs, the sensitivity of the information and your records policy. This setting
does not keep financial records or give staff access to private preparation.”**
A small **How to choose** disclosure links the organization's applicable policy
or responsible role and source-linked guidance. It is not a mandatory checklist,
legal certification, new policy-document library or recommendation to retain
everything “just in case.” A qualified deployment may offer its approved
recommendation; without that evidence the field is not prefilled with a guess.

**Save and change.** Show the new period and effective time in a short inline
review, with **“Applies to departures taking effect after this change is saved.
Preparation already retained keeps its existing deadline.”** The saved result
shows the exact server-accepted effective instant; no future-date picker is needed. No private-record
count is needed. One deliberate **Save period** commits the reviewed revision.
Preserve input on failure, prevent lost-update overwrites, and recover an accepted
save after a lost response. Routine configuration does not need a second
approver, typed confirmation phrase or multi-page wizard unless independently
required by the actual P12 capability. Mandatory changes to existing records use
their qualified source correction process, not a hidden side effect of Save.

**Maya's ordinary work and departure.** Provide a quiet **How preparation is kept**
explanation in existing preparation settings/help. Confirmed departure revokes
access according to its source rules; the period does not grant a departed user
a grace login. Do not contact supporters, automatically export private content,
force a departure survey or invent a new email notification. Any existing
authorized departure communication retains its own owner and scope.

**Return.** D27's currently authorized **Earlier preparation** list remains the
entry point. Where disclosure is allowed, show the actual deadline and its
meaning before a consequential action. No inaccessible-record count, stale
cached preview, staff inventory or claim that missing data must have been deleted
is allowed. Explain the selected Resume behavior: **“Resuming keeps this
preparation as current work under your organization's normal retention rules.”**
Only selected eligible entries leave their departure schedule. Login alone never
admits all earlier preparation to current work.

**Expiry and support.** Use **No longer available to resume** when that is the
verified ordinary-access outcome. Reserve **Deleted from live preparation** or a
stronger copy-specific statement for verified disposal evidence. An access-denied
response does not reveal a protected record's existence, hold or contents.
Operational staff receive a compact permitted status and a repair action, not a
second record browser or a “restore everything” button.

**Accessibility and field conditions.** Use persistent labels, text alongside
status/color, keyboard-operable controls, focus-visible errors and a nonintrusive
save status. Keep the form single-column at narrow widths, preserve typed input
on slow or failed requests, and do not present offline saving as committed.
Dates and numbers are localized; policy identity and deadline arithmetic are not.
No display-name, address, language or time-zone assumption changes ownership or
expiry. Public/donor giving flows acquire no additional step.

## Exact required amendments

These are requirements proposed for the later authorized specification. The
organization-control choice and C07's return meaning are explicitly selected;
the remaining amendments await ratification. They select no live policy,
migration or new access grant.

### D28-C01 — One scoped organization control

The tenant MAY configure one finite ordinary post-departure period for the
qualified unshared-preparation source. Only a current P12-authorized policy
administrator MAY change it. The policy MUST NOT become a per-record timer,
department/ministry override hierarchy, generic rule builder or authority over
CRM, consent, finance, communications, care or coach grants. Retention
administration MUST NOT confer body, hidden-count or private-inventory access.

### D28-C02 — Qualified profile and honest unset state

The preparation owner MUST qualify covered bodies/derivatives, purpose, departure
trigger, finite supported duration bounds, controlling privacy/records limits,
holds, ordinary-work rules, evidence schedules and recovery before activation. It MUST distinguish the
ordinary-return cutoff from verified physical-disposal deadlines for each actual
copy class; a queue delay cannot extend an applicable privacy ceiling.
The UI MUST offer only that profile's supported positive whole-day choices; no
zero/blank/negative/overflow/forever encoding is accepted. No universal numerical
default is selected. With no configured choice or qualified existing baseline,
the affected new preparation capability MUST remain unactivated; independent CRM
work continues. Missing policy MUST neither purge legacy content nor retain it
indefinitely by convention. Existing unknown cohorts require bounded restricted
source-owner assessment with an owner and resolution deadline.

### D28-C03 — Purpose-based help and simple configuration

Show the policy's exact source scope, fixed unit, supported limits, selected
period, effective time and consequences in the existing settings surface. Help
MUST direct staff to justified continuity needs and governing obligations without
claiming a legally safe universal number. Policy guidance MUST remain distinct
from actual approved policy. One deliberate save and proportional error handling
MUST suffice; no content inspection, compelled reason for every ordinary save,
new approval queue or generic compliance wizard is introduced.

### D28-C04 — Prospective, versioned ordinary changes

Each accepted change MUST create immutable revision/effect evidence and change
the current setting atomically under expected-version and current-authority
checks. An ordinary Save is effective at its authoritative server acceptance
time; callers cannot backdate it. Exactly one revision governs each effective
instant; overlapping or retroactively inserted/reordered ordinary revisions are
forbidden. Equal-clock acceptance attempts must be ordered without ambiguity
before any affected departure admission; they cannot silently rewrite history.
Ordinary changes apply to source departures effective on or after that
revision's effective time; existing episodes retain their pinned revision and
deadline. The UI MUST state this before acceptance. No retroactive extension,
shortening or reset to the save time is implied. Mandatory source/legal/privacy
changes MUST use an independently qualified, auditable correction transition for
the affected records; obsolete invalid schedules cannot be grandfathered forever.

### D28-C05 — Authoritative departure and exact time

Only the source-authorized departure event for the relevant preparation ownership
context MAY start its episode. Preserve effective and observed timestamps,
durable source event identity, exact tenant/ministry/original-author binding and
the policy effective at that departure instant. A missing login, inactive tab,
archived preparation, grant suspension or departure from another scope MUST NOT
substitute. Duplicate or out-of-order delivery MUST NOT create a new clock.
Late/backdated events use their authoritative instant and validated historical
policy; missing/conflicting historical evidence enters restricted owner review,
not guessed deletion. A later genuine departure may schedule only preparation
previously admitted to that participation/current-work retention context. It
MUST NOT restart an unselected earlier entry or move its original deadline.
Deadline arithmetic MUST use checked UTC instants and the
fixed duration unit; departure-retained read eligibility is strictly before its
still-applicable deadline. A C07 admission ends that schedule’s applicability,
not its historical record.

### D28-C06 — Expiry controls reads independently of cleanup

At or after a still-applicable departure deadline, the ordinary retained body
MUST be unavailable to all
dependent discovery/read/resume paths even when the cleanup worker is late,
paused or failing. Current source/history authority remains independently
required before deadline. APIs, RPCs, views, search, counts, cache responses,
Realtime and result recovery MUST preserve the same boundary. No signed URL or
cache lifetime may outlast a relevant authorization/availability boundary.
Previously delivered copies cannot be recalled by deleting a server record; the
product MUST not promise that guarantee. After a valid C07 admission, the ended
departure deadline MUST NOT deny current-work reads or authorize deletion; current
source authority and normal current-work retention instead govern.

### D28-C07 — Return must have one explicit retention meaning

**Return meaning explicitly selected, refining D27-C07:** an authorized D27
Resume accepted strictly before the controlling deadline MUST atomically
admit only the selected same preparation to its qualified ordinary current-work
retention purpose and end applicability of that departure schedule. The original
departure instant, policy, deadline and admission history MUST remain truthful;
they are not reset, overwritten or used to mark information newly verified.

The source MUST reprove current original-author/history/purpose authority and
ordinary-work retention qualification. Missing qualification rejects the
admission without cancelling the existing obligation. Login, matching email,
reactivation, reading, archive/return alone or resuming a different entry MUST NOT
extend unselected preparation. D4 Return MAY compose this exact admission and
its existing archive transition with one clear action, as D27 already permits
composition. Resume of Active versus Archived work preserves D27 semantics.

Held-only, expired, erased or purged preparation MUST NOT be admitted. A hold that
merely prevents disposal before expiry does not by itself remove an independently
valid ordinary access path; any hold-specific use restriction still governs.
A later genuine departure schedules only work admitted to that later context,
without restarting unselected older episodes. The UI MUST explain **“Resuming
keeps this preparation as current work under your organization's normal retention
rules.”** No guaranteed permanence or automatically resumed sharing follows.

The founder explicitly selected this return meaning on October 17, 2026 UTC.
It is intended behavior, not proof of implementation or blanket ratification of
the remaining D28 amendments. The alternative—keeping the old deadline on resumed
work—is simpler mechanically but risks surprising loss of current work. The
initial finite model evaluated both branches before the clarification; its
original evidence remains unchanged.

### D28-C08 — Holds and required evidence remain separate

A hold MUST preserve only its qualified exact scope under restricted custody; it
MUST NOT create ordinary read or Resume authority. Its expiry/review date MUST
not automatically release it. Placement/release and irreversible disposition
MUST serialize at the same owning record boundary. Retention conflicts require
restricted records review with accountable resolution, not guessed deletion or
unbounded ordinary access. Minimal disposal/authorization evidence needs its own
qualified schedule; neither audit nor a hold justifies unlimited plaintext.

### D28-C09 — Source truth and database integrity

Asym Postgres MUST retain the preparation/policy/episode/disposition authority
behind `packages/api`. Use non-null tenant and source identities, tenant-aware
foreign keys, constrained positive durations and valid time/state combinations,
immutable origin/policy/trigger evidence, and uniqueness for the business effects.
At most one controlling ordinary departure episode per preparation is allowed;
multiple historical episodes may exist with their distinct original contexts. Current setting/revision changes must be atomic. Delete
behavior MUST preserve independently scheduled attribution/evidence and MUST NOT
cascade from auth/profile/tenant cleanup into unrelated domains or held records.
Retained readable content and disposition evidence are different payloads, not
one forever JSON snapshot. Money types and ledger mutations are out of scope.

### D28-C10 — Authorization across every path

Derive tenant, actor, administrative scope, policy authority, author and audit
attribution from validated server context and authoritative relationships. A
requested target ID is a selector to authorize, never proof. Base grants/RLS,
views/RPCs, worker credentials and storage access MUST preserve exact scope.
Policy/source mutations use the qualified command boundary, not unrestricted
browser CRUD. Explicitly verify old-row `USING` and resulting-row `WITH CHECK`
semantics, immutable tenant/author/source fields and legal state transitions;
no allowed update may move a row into another tenant or privilege tier. PostgreSQL
can reuse `USING` as an implicit check; omission alone is not proof of a bypass.
Function-owner, table-owner and BYPASSRLS behavior must be tested, not assumed
safe from an RLS flag or assumed unsafe solely from a definer label.

### D28-C11 — Atomic acceptance, concurrency and idempotency

Policy save, departure admission/correction, hold/disposition and any qualified
return transition MUST have explicit linearization points. Re-read authority,
expected source/policy revision, hold and current time after waits before
acceptance. Transaction-start time alone cannot prove the deadline still holds.
No stale worker, old client or prior policy snapshot may destroy now-ineligible
content. Effect identity MUST bind durable business scope, not merely one HTTP
request. Duplicate delivery returns the same accepted result under current
authorization; a lost response never demands a duplicate policy or source entry.

### D28-C12 — Verified, bounded disposal

For a database-only body, the source MUST atomically establish final eligibility,
remove the qualified ordinary payload and record minimal disposition evidence.
An actual external derivative requires a durable deletion-commit boundary,
idempotent exact-object operation and truthful reconciliation of ambiguous
outcomes. A short claim lease or its timeout is not proof a paused worker can no
longer delete. Hold/return acceptance MUST not falsely promise preservation after
an irreversible external deletion commitment. No generic storage service or new
copy is required where the actual preparation source has none. Purge jobs use
bounded indexed batches, per-tenant fairness and resumable progress, scheduled
to satisfy the qualified physical-disposal deadlines; errors never
widen access or report success before verification.

### D28-C13 — Derivatives, backups and recovery

Qualify the actual body/copy inventory: projections, search indexes, caches,
exports, storage objects, logs, backups and any generated material. No speculative
export/provider is introduced. A restore MUST replay complete forward-only
disposition, hold and revocation authority before readers or workers reopen;
that evidence cannot exist solely inside the older snapshot being restored.
Missing complete recovery evidence blocks reopening the affected source.
Backup expiry/overwrite and external-copy limits MUST be stated accurately.
Purged preparation MUST never reappear through identity merge, import, re-invite,
rollback or a larger future period.

### D28-C14 — Accurate UI, cache and accessibility

Use the journey above with Core's shared UI system. Show saved versus pending
policy state, still-applicable departure deadline and confirmed unavailable/disposal
state without exposing hidden content or holds. After accepted Resume, replace
the departure countdown with **“Kept under normal current-work retention rules.”**
An ended deadline may appear only as clearly labeled authorized history. Preserve input on conflict or
network failure and offer a current refresh/review action. Cache keys and
invalidation MUST include tenant/actor/scope and relevant policy/source/access
revisions; a same-account membership change or deadline passage is not caught
only by sign-out invalidation. Keyboard, screen reader, focus, reflow, error
association, localized dates/numbers and slow-network behavior need actual proof.
No donor checkout, public site or coach task receives an extra step.

### D28-C15 — Bounded operation and no unnecessary engine

Keep one duration setting, versioned source effects and the existing preparation
workflow. No retention workflow designer, per-entry exception timer, new
eDiscovery product, generic archive or vendor dependency is selected. Index
pending disposal by tenant, deadline and stable ID with plan/lock proof. Publish
an actual deployment profile for population, peak departures/expiry, batch size,
query/lock latency, retry and copy-completion budgets before activation. D27's
25-row list and 100-row request maximum remain its initial display profile;
they are not a source-record cap or measured capacity claim. Do not claim
scalability from a tiny state model.

### D28-C16 — Audit and operational truth

Keep durable, access-controlled policy revision, source episode, authority,
correction/return/disposition effect and copy-outcome evidence sufficient to
explain what happened. Separate it from technical logs and from private content.
Body text, contact identities and raw provider payloads MUST NOT become default
telemetry. Minimal opaque references still require scope and retention review.
Record effective, accepted and observed times accurately. Staff repair uses
qualified source commands; no direct SQL patch, invented success or silent
manual cleanup is the normal operating workflow.

### D28-C17 — Safe activation, migration and rollback

Qualify exact source/privacy/public-policy obligations first. Deploy constrained
source schema, access enforcement and compatible readers/writers before enabling
policy-driven disposal. Inventory legacy records without assuming departure,
author or policy from `updated_at`; unresolved cohorts stay restricted under
bounded owner review and no guessed purge. Prove dry-run selection, real negative
authorization, races, recovery and copy outcomes before a small qualified cohort.
Stopping cleanup MUST NOT restore expired access; stopping new admissions MUST
not discard existing disposition duties. After irreversible disposal, rollback
means compatible roll-forward/containment, never restoration of erased bodies or
an old reader that ignores deadlines. Reconcile public and contractual claims
for the actual P9 source; do not rewrite legal pages as a grill side effect.

### D28-C18 — Traceability and release evidence

Trace this answer and each applicable clause to the later authorized P9/P12 owner
amendment, glossary, canonical ADR/OpenSpec sources and generated projections,
design, tasks/tickets, implementation tests and release evidence. No current
ticket is claimed created or changed. Preserve initial question and prior
ratification evidence. Complete the proof obligations below with actual source,
database, service, UI and operations evidence before activation. A selected
answer, documentation check or synthetic model is not runtime qualification.

## Lifecycle and valid transitions

These are distinct facts, not a requirement to build one giant status enum.

| Axis/state                                                     | Valid next effect                                                                                                                | Forbidden shortcut                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Policy unconfigured                                            | Qualified authorized first revision; independent existing valid schedules continue                                               | Blank means forever, purge immediately or invent a historical baseline                     |
| Unsaved policy edit                                            | Cancel with no effect, or one currently authorized version-fenced save                                                           | UI optimism reports policy activation before durable acceptance                            |
| Accepted policy revision                                       | A later prospective revision; separately qualified mandatory correction where required                                           | Backdating/reordering history or undoing a saved revision by deleting its evidence         |
| Ordinary current preparation                                   | Confirmed relevant departure binds one exact episode and historical policy                                                       | Archive, inactivity or unrelated departure starts a timer                                  |
| Retained after departure, before deadline                      | Currently authorized discovery; deliberate selected-entry current-work admission under C07; required erasure/restriction; expiry | Old role, email, login, hold or viewing grants access or resets the deadline               |
| Current-work admission selected under C07                      | Same record under qualified active rules; a later genuine relevant departure may bind a later episode                            | Renew all earlier entries or treat resume as newly verified/shared work                    |
| Ordinary availability expired                                  | Restricted applicable hold/evidence custody, or verified due disposal                                                            | Purge lag, a longer future period, hold release or restore revives ordinary access         |
| Applicable hold                                                | Continue required restricted custody; explicit authorized release then current-rule re-evaluation                                | Auto-release on review date, new ordinary reader or fresh retention period                 |
| Queued/retryable disposal                                      | Reprove current source, deadline, hold and generation before destructive acceptance                                              | Queue claim or stale worker result is permanent deletion authority                         |
| Irreversible external deletion committed, if such copies exist | Reconcile actual outcome and truthful remaining copy obligations                                                                 | Clear fence on timeout, promise a later hold saved bytes, or retry a broader object set    |
| Qualified body disposal verified                               | Retain only independently scheduled minimal evidence; reconcile future restore                                                   | Rollback/reinvite/import resurrects disposed body or evidence is called a readable archive |

Original history remains truthful through corrections. A missed response can
recover an accepted result only under current authority. Cancelling an unsaved
form changes nothing; stopping a worker does not cancel accepted retention duties.
Physical deletion has no ordinary Undo. The source's independently qualified
correction process handles errors without manufacturing an earlier event or
recreating erased content.
