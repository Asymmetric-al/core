# D28 — organization-set preparation retention: primary research and UX

Research date: **October 17, 2026 UTC**. The founder selected Q28 A: an
organization-set finite period for unshared preparation after departure. This
is supporting research and proposed answer-review language, not a ratified
amendment, legal determination, implemented policy or new source permission.

The root's working design is one organization-wide period, with ordinary edits
applying prospectively to departures from the accepted policy's effective
instant. Existing departure episodes retain their accepted schedules. This is
a proposed amendment for the answer review, not a claim that the original A
already selected its temporal details. Mandatory erasure and qualified source
corrections remain with their own disposition boundary.

The founder clarification about a deliberate pre-expiry Resume and the current
work retention purpose remained unanswered when this note was written. This
review recommends a narrow proposed amendment: a currently authorized deliberate
Resume, accepted before expiry, admits only the selected same entry to its
qualified current-work retention purpose, ending applicability of its earlier
departure schedule while preserving that schedule as historical evidence.
Unselected entries keep their schedules. This is evaluated as a proposal for
ratification, not a founder-selected outcome or a change already made to D27.
D27's current no-reset clause must be reconciled explicitly if the amendment is
ratified; login, ordinary reading and expired/erased-body recovery acquire no
new effect.

## Evidence, limits and confidence

The sources below were freshly searched and directly opened for this review.
Dates are page-stated dates; retrieval in 2026 is not proof of a new product
release. No vendor account, ministry interview, current Core browser journey,
live database, accessible prototype or production workload was tested. Product
recommendations are identified separately from primary facts.

The evidence supports scoped configuration, clear consequences, proportionate
review and separation of retention from ordinary access. It supplies no valid
universal number of days for this private preparation. A CRM's general contact
retention period, nonprofit financial retention period or provider recycle bin
does not establish Asym's preparation purpose or required duration.

## Smallest useful configuration

**Product recommendation:** one organization-wide finite period for this bounded
preparation purpose, configured by authorized staff in the existing policy
surface. This avoids per-missionary overrides, per-record clocks editable in the
UI, a rule builder, custom formulas and duplicate retention settings elsewhere.

Use a plain label such as **Preparation after departure** and one field
**Keep unshared preparation for [number] days after departure**. The explanatory
line defines the governed departure scope once the source owner qualifies it;
do not silently reinterpret absence, inactivity, a role change or leaving one
unrelated project as that event. A short scope statement identifies private
preparation, rather than all contact, financial or account records.

Recommend **whole days** as the only editable unit initially. This is a simple
engineering/product convention, not a legal requirement. Avoid a Days/Months/
Years toggle when no user evidence requires it: calendar months differ in length
and conversions can change a deadline. If months later become a real requirement,
their calendar and month-end semantics need an explicit qualified contract; do
not silently convert each month to thirty days. The source must define the exact
day boundary/duration independently of display locale, daylight saving changes
or browser time. The UI displays the source-computed result.

### Help staff choose without inventing a legal recommendation

Keep the default field **unset**, not prefilled with a borrowed number. Brief
helper text can say: **Choose the shortest period that supports your
organization's expected return needs and its retention requirements.** An
optional expanded explanation points to the organization's existing policy
owner/process and explains the scope; it does not force an essay or a compliance
questionnaire for each save. Do not label a value Recommended, compliant or safe
unless an actual qualified source policy justifies that claim for this tenant.

There is no evidence-backed universal default, minimum useful return duration or
maximum ministry period in this review. Do not copy HubSpot's contact defaults,
a financial-record timer or a provider recovery interval. A missing setting
must not mean zero, permanent retention or a silent standard period. Before
this source capability is activated, the owner must qualify its actual finite
supported range, dispositions and missing-policy behavior; unrelated authorized
CRM/work must remain available. Missing policy is not permission to suspend
mandatory disposition of existing records.

**Decision level:** an organization chooses its own value because A is selected;
the founder need not select a universal numeric default that the product does
not use. Integer parsing, finite date arithmetic and rejection of unsupported
values are engineering/source qualification. A substantive imposed minimum or
maximum that changes the product's recovery opportunity needs justified product
agreement if evidence cannot resolve it. Do not disguise a speculative business
cap as an overflow check. The source/privacy owner must establish any applicable
constraint before enablement, and the UI must show its actual allowed range.

## Authorized staff journey

1. **Find the setting.** The current policy card shows the period, applicability,
   effective date and a clear Edit action. With no policy, show **Not configured**
   and an accurate setup action for authorized staff. A person without edit
   authority sees only the policy explanation they are entitled to see, not
   private bodies, hidden preparation counts or a disabled privileged form.
2. **Enter a finite period.** Use one labeled input with a visible unit and short
   helper. Validate required/whole-number/range failures inline in plain language,
   retaining the entered value. Reject blank, negative, fractional, overflow or
   unqualified values rather than rounding or treating them as Off. Zero is not
   a hidden immediate-erasure option. Show the actual source-supported bounds,
   not a guessed recommended value.
3. **Review the policy consequence once.** Show previous period, proposed period,
   and **Applies to departures effective after this change is saved. Existing
   departure schedules stay unchanged.** Save takes effect at its authoritative
   server acceptance instant, which the durable result supplies. Do not imply a
   clock based on event delivery or invent a scheduling picker to make the review
   concrete.
4. **Save deliberately.** **Save retention period** is sufficient for this
   prospective policy change. There is no bulk-delete action hidden behind Save,
   no list of private entries to inspect and no mandatory typing confirmation.
   If the current policy changed while editing, preserve the draft and show the
   current value; re-review the real difference before accepting a new change.
5. **Know it succeeded.** Show the accepted period and exact effective scope in
   the policy card, plus concise accessible feedback. Do not claim a click saved
   the policy or that an unknown-response failure deleted data. Recover the same
   accepted operation under current authority; a retry cannot apply a different
   policy or create a new effective episode accidentally.

The impact review is intentionally small because ordinary edits are prospective.
It does not need an affected-private-record count: the promise is that previously
accepted episode schedules are unchanged by this ordinary edit. Changing those
schedules for mandatory erasure or source correction uses its separately
qualified owner boundary, not an optional **Apply to existing records** checkbox.
Staff still cannot inspect hidden content to decide what to retain.

Operational policy history should show who changed the period, the accepted
value/version/effective instant and the result, under its own audience. It must
not copy note text or a person-by-person retained archive into a broad audit view.
Routine edits do not create a new support process or permission workflow.

## Maya's whole journey

### While currently working

Show the relevant organization policy in a calm, findable explanation near
preparation/settings or help. Do not place a warning badge on every prospect,
add a countdown to active ordinary work or imply that an old saved date alone
starts this departure period. A short explanation can say **Your organization
sets how long unshared preparation is kept after you leave. Retention does not
keep your access active.** Access, sharing and retention need distinct labels.

The policy information must be available without requiring Maya to initiate a
departure. Do not force a departure wizard, contact export or preparation cleanup
before revocation. No email or supporter notification is implied by displaying
this policy.

### After the actual governed departure

Ordinary access ends under its existing rules. Retained preparation is not a
new personal archive that Maya can keep reading after departure. Any already
authorized explanation uses the actual policy applicable to that departure
episode, not today's later organization setting. No new outbound retention
notice, private-content attachment or access-bearing link is created by D28.

### If she returns before the episode ends

D27 first establishes current identity/source/history authority, then lets Maya
review eligible earlier preparation and deliberately resume selected work.
Existing archive labels, historical facts and ordinary editor remain familiar.
Where lawful and useful, the entry can display **Return period ends [date/time]**
from its source episode. Avoid **Guaranteed available until** or a display date
rounded to midnight that overstates actual availability. Keep dates unambiguous
with year and an appropriate permitted display-zone label.

For the recommended proposed amendment, the successful result should say
**Resumed as current work. The earlier return-period deadline no longer applies
to this item.** This copy is conditional on ratification and actual successful
source admission; it is not a claim that the founder has answered the pending
clarification. The existing qualified current-work policy now governs, so do
not imply indefinite storage, erased history or guaranteed future access.
Preserve the original schedule in qualified history; do not present it as the
current deletion deadline. Preserve Active/Archived state unless an expressly
reviewed combined Return also changes it under D27. Login, ordinary reading or
grant restoration must not admit every old entry. Remaining unselected
preparation keeps its actual departure schedule and existing eligible-entry
deadline display.

### If she returns after eligibility has ended

Keep ordinary authorized current work usable. Use the neutral unavailable/empty
states below; never offer Create duplicate contact, Restore from backup or Ask
staff to read your notes as a recovery route. The source may keep minimal
evidence or held bytes under a different purpose without making the preparation
body readable. Do not make an expiration label reveal a protected old record.

### If the organization changes its period

Show the current organization rule accurately, but keep the accepted departure
episode's actual deadline on eligible earlier preparation. If they differ,
explain only where needed: **This preparation follows the period that applied
when you left.** A rule change is not a status change to every entry and does
not reset any clock. Do not show contradictory dates across the policy page,
earlier-preparation detail and operation result.

## Mobile, accessibility and visual quality

Keep the staff form as one readable setting card and the missionary experience
as a contextual explanation in existing prospect navigation. Use Maia spacing,
typography and semantic colors; no warning-heavy dashboard, chart, recovery
carousel or new component system is needed. Relevant errors and irreversible
consequences receive visual emphasis; normal retention is not a red-alert state.

- Keep labels, the numeric value and its unit together programmatically and
  visually. Provide a suitable mobile numeric keyboard while still validating
  on the server. A spinner alone is not a usable large-range input; accept direct
  entry and clear correction. Do not silently parse a locale decimal or separator
  into a different whole-day number.
- Form errors identify the field and how to correct it, using current actual
  bounds. Preserve valid input when navigating between edit and review. The
  source revalidates the accepted version and range at save.
- Review can be an inline state or a shared sheet/page consistent with the
  surrounding settings. Keyboard focus is visible and returns sensibly on close;
  no nested confirm-on-confirm dialog. A material result is announced without
  reading all policy prose into a live region.
- At 320 CSS pixels and zoom, keep the input, unit, old/new values, effective
  scope and action reachable without horizontal page scrolling. Long organization
  names, translated strings, RTL and enlarged text must not hide the unit or
  truncate the consequence.
- Display source-computed absolute dates/times with an allowed display zone;
  relative wording can supplement them. Browser locale, travel, DST and changing
  the display timezone do not change the deadline. No missionary location data
  is needed for this setting.
- Keep loading, empty, error and saved states distinct on slow connections.
  Authorized policy metadata is small; private body fetches are unnecessary for
  setup, review or operational diagnosis. Do not add an offline retained-content
  copy as a convenience.

## Alternatives, remaining qualification and disposition

The strongest rejected B is one qualified Asym-standard finite period. It reduces
setup and support differences, but does not provide the organization control now
selected. Keep its simplicity through one setting and clear defaults behavior;
do not compromise A by silently installing a universal value.

Applying every ordinary edit retroactively is a stronger flexibility alternative
than arbitrary per-record timers, but requires impact admission, changed-schedule
semantics and potentially irreversible removal of existing work. The proposed
prospective design keeps that out of this small feature. Independent mandatory
erasure and valid corrections are not disabled or delayed by this simplification.

Before release, the owner must qualify the source trigger, finite supported
range, period arithmetic, body/evidence/hold disposition, current-return
transition, restore behavior and operational handling of deletion failures.
Representative staff and returning missionaries must demonstrate understanding
of which period applies, what departure ends, what Resume does, and what cannot
be recovered. No interview results or numeric adoption/latency claims exist here.

The organization-set period can be reviewed without inventing a universal number.
The complete recommended amendment includes the selected-entry current-work
admission described above. The founder clarification remains unanswered, so this
recommendation must remain explicitly proposed pending ratification; no D27
authority is silently superseded. No Q29 or new product frontier is posed by this
note.

**Recommended disposition: Accept with required amendments.** One explicit finite
organization setting, current authority, prospective ordinary changes, truthful
episode dates and restrained consequence review make the selected control useful
without creating a policy engine, recovery promise or hidden reader.
