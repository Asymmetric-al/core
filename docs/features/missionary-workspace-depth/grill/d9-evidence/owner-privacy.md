# D9 — Summary authorization and privacy owner evidence

Research only, October 16, 2026 UTC. The founder selected optional,
organization-permitted expected-support and received-support summaries for
coaches. D8 and its amendments are ratified. This note preserves research leading
to the founder's explicit D9 ratification on October 16, 2026 UTC; the corrected
decision and C01–C22/P01–P30 in [D9](../phase28-d9-adversarial-review.md) identify
the accepted amendment. Candidate wording below remains supporting provenance.
This file does not amend an owner PRD/ADR/OpenSpec, grant
access, implement a producer or establish runtime qualification.

## Conclusion

**Accept with required amendments.** A useful authorized summary can be provided
without permitting the coach to read individual commitments, donors or gifts.
The permanent path is a small, versioned set of source-owned summary profiles
through the existing P3/P10/P12 boundary, with exact P13/P16/P21 provenance and
current D8 sharing authority. No dashboard-local money formula, second privacy
engine, custom cohort builder, new per-coach field matrix or universally invented
small-cell number is needed.

There are two different prohibitions to preserve:

1. A coach must not receive raw donor/commitment/gift SELECT merely because a
   trusted producer needs those inputs to produce an admitted summary.
2. A trusted producer must not aggregate every row available to its service
   credential and then claim that hiding names makes the total authorized.

The source owner must explicitly admit the input facts to the **exact coaching
summary purpose**, applying tenant, entity, subject, time, classification,
anonymity and other source restrictions before arithmetic. Only that post-floor
input projection may contribute. Separately, the coach must be authorized for
the resulting registered summary fields and disclosure profile. Input admission
for this purpose is not an unrestricted right for the coach to enumerate those
facts, and service credentials are not admission. If the existing owner contract
does not establish this summary-only path, qualify it through the explicit owner
amendment rather than pretending it already exists.

P12 already specifies a tenant-tunable small-cell N at the aggregate/query door.
D9 must consume that owner floor when applicable. It must neither invent a
universal N nor replace the inherited floor with “we have no k rule.” N is one
necessary control, not a proof of anonymity or resistance to comparisons.

## Minimal profile and authorization design

Recommend one understandable **Share support summaries** option with a plain
preview of the exact eligible information. The UI need not expose the source
producer, field registry, numerical privacy rule or capability matrix. It must
not imply that turning on a broad role or financial goal automatically selects
these modules for every coach. Expected and received remain distinguishable in
the preview, source evidence and output even if presented by one simple control.

A finite, versioned **summary disclosure profile** is a source contract and
review artifact, not a new policy engine. It binds:

- exact purpose, supported typed subject and owner adapter;
- registered expected/received output fields and source metric identifiers;
- allowable currencies, period families, history bounds, grouping and precision;
- source scope/inclusion and coverage semantics, current source cursor/version,
  applicable anonymity/classification and existing aggregate floor;
- the permitted combinations of totals, trends, goals, percentages and other
  simultaneously or sequentially disclosed values;
- safe omission/coarsening/unavailable behavior and current release checks.

Numeric periods, history range, granularity, N and precision are **not chosen
here**. Reuse an already qualified owner profile where it satisfies D9. Where
none exists, the source and disclosure owners must qualify the exact finite
profile before it becomes selectable. The founder's separate history decision
cannot be bypassed by using a source cache's retention length, P16's missionary
default month/12-month drill, the roadmap's 13-month trend, or a public Page's
already published percentage.

No arbitrary date range, donor filter, query predicate, sorting by a hidden
amount, cross-subject total or new subgroup is implicitly admitted. A necessary
future expansion is a named profile change with the same policy/source controls,
not another runtime expression language. Existing P12 small-cell configuration
stays with that owner; the coach does not tune it.

The source may continue to read canonical money facts for its own protected
production work. It may not return those raw facts to the coach, browser,
telemetry, preview payload, accessibility table or export. It must apply the
registered coaching-purpose input floor before computing a disclosed total.
There is no exception for a hidden HTML field or client-side redaction.

## Material concern register

Severity and likelihood are qualitative judgments about plausible designs, not
measured defect rates. All findings below are qualification blockers for their
affected summary path, rather than risks to accept through monitoring alone.

### OP01 — “Filter before aggregation” can mean two different unsafe things

- **What:** A producer either sums every privileged row or requires a coach to
  hold direct read access to each donor/gift record before a summary can work.
- **Why it matters:** The first leaks excluded facts; the second pressures the
  implementation to grant raw finance access or falsely reports no support.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High without an explicit producer seam.
- **Evidence:** S04, S07–S08, S10–S13.
- **Answer impact:** Keep selected summary-only access; clarify what admission
  occurs before arithmetic instead of expanding the Coach role.
- **Permanent fix:** Source-owned input admission for the exact summary purpose,
  plus independently admitted derived output under one P3/P10/P12 boundary.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC01:** “The trusted source producer SHALL apply
  current Tenant, Legal Entity, typed subject, source inclusion, period,
  classification, anonymity and purpose restrictions before arithmetic for the
  registered coaching summary. It SHALL aggregate only that admitted input
  projection. Summary admission SHALL NOT require or create the coach's raw
  donor/gift/commitment SELECT right; service credentials SHALL NOT substitute
  for admission. If the owner has not qualified this purpose, the summary is
  unavailable rather than over-granted or reconstructed from raw tables.”

### OP02 — A derived field is treated as less sensitive than its inputs

- **What:** A total, trend point, coverage percentage or classification label
  is considered automatically safe because individual donor rows are absent.
- **Why it matters:** Money and protected identity can be recovered from
  derived outputs; a generic dashboard field bypasses the source census.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High for an ad hoc summary endpoint.
- **Evidence:** S04–S05; S07:161,307; S08:86–104; S09–S10.
- **Answer impact:** New summary eligibility does not declassify its data.
- **Permanent fix:** Register every derived output, predicate and surface with
  inherited sensitivity, exact purpose and reviewed projection policy.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC02:** “Every coaching-summary field, ratio,
  trend point, label, predicate and supported serialization SHALL have an exact
  registered source meaning and disclosure classification. Derived data SHALL
  retain the applicable inherited sensitivity; absence of names does not
  declassify it. Unknown fields/purposes/profile versions fail closed. Any
  projection-policy widening follows ADR0211; normal reads and qualified sharing
  under that policy do not acquire a new blanket approval step.”

### OP03 — Adjacent periods and repeated releases reveal one person's changes

- **What:** Each period or refresh appears safe alone, while subtracting two
  periods, source revisions or corrected totals isolates a gift or commitment.
- **Why it matters:** A single new supporter, refund, ended commitment or
  backdated correction may become identifiable; a fixed cohort threshold does
  not prevent temporal differencing.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** Medium–High in small ministries.
- **Evidence:** S03:231; S07, S10:1664, S12:1264, S13:1150–1156; D8 S02.
- **Answer impact:** Retain useful summaries, but no arbitrary temporal drill
  or assumption that every newer source cursor is safe to expose immediately.
- **Permanent fix:** Qualify the finite profile's permitted period/revision
  combinations and apply current release checks on material source corrections.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC03:** “Disclosure qualification SHALL cover
  allowed adjacent/overlapping periods and repeated releases, including late
  posting, correction, refund, commitment change and historical reclassification.
  A formerly admitted profile/result SHALL not exempt a new source version from
  current source/privacy admission. Unsafe values and their reconstructing
  complements SHALL be withheld or source-qualified coarsened; no arbitrary
  period/query expansion or per-refresh raw-data fallback is permitted.”

### OP04 — Other visible outputs undo suppression

- **What:** A hidden amount is recovered from a goal and percentage, expected
  minus received variance, month and annual totals, overlapping ministry/project
  contexts, public progress or a shared narrative.
- **Why it matters:** Suppressing only the obvious card leaves the same fact
  observable elsewhere; several independently authorized subjects may overlap.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High if modules are checked in isolation.
- **Evidence:** S03, S07, S10, S12:1259–1264, S13:1152–1154.
- **Answer impact:** D9 grants no cross-subject rollup, ratio or hidden-series
  reconstruction. Existing subject navigation remains available.
- **Permanent fix:** Treat the admitted set of outputs/complements as one
  disclosure profile across reached representations and relevant owned surfaces.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC04:** “Privacy qualification SHALL evaluate
  the permitted combined outputs, including goals/percentages, differences,
  expected/received series, overlapping subjects and relevant source-published
  values. Withholding a protected amount SHALL also withhold or qualify every
  offered complement that reconstructs it. Separate authorized subject views
  SHALL not silently create portfolio totals, union hidden scope or authorize
  copying protected facts into narrative. No claim of safety against arbitrary
  external background knowledge or mathematical anonymity is made.”

### OP05 — A new arbitrary N replaces existing privacy governance

- **What:** The implementation invents a universal minimum donor count, or
  omits small-cell handling because D9 does not choose a number.
- **Why it matters:** Both bypass P12's accepted aggregate floor; N alone cannot
  address dominance, repeated observations, ratios or overlap. Excessive blanket
  suppression can also leave the selected product unusable without explanation.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** High if a single shortcut is adopted.
- **Evidence:** S10:1664; S12:1264; S13:1154; S03:231.
- **Answer impact:** Require exact existing-owner qualification, not a founder
  questionnaire about k or a new statistical platform.
- **Permanent fix:** Consume the existing qualified floor/profile and prove a
  useful positive case plus small-cell/differencing denials. Unknown safety denies.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC05:** “D9 SHALL consume the existing P12
  aggregate/query-predicate small-cell floor and its qualified tenant policy;
  it SHALL neither invent a universal N nor treat N alone as anonymization.
  Any admitted coarsening/precision rule is source-defined and labeled honestly,
  never fabricated money or unexplained random noise. When no safe useful profile
  is qualified, the affected module remains unavailable while unrelated coaching
  remains usable. No new general privacy/rules engine is introduced.”

### OP06 — Optional eligibility becomes default or historical financial access

- **What:** Selecting D9 turns summaries on for every existing coach, includes
  all cached periods, or restores old access when a tenant re-enables the option.
- **Why it matters:** The choice concerns eligible modules, not a blanket grant,
  prior-history release or policy-toggle revival.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High in a default-on migration.
- **Evidence:** S01, S02:443–485, S10–S11.
- **Answer impact:** Preserve deliberate missionary sharing under current
  organization and source authority; no unselected history/duration.
- **Permanent fix:** Add summary eligibility to existing D8 source sharing and
  P12 grants with exact module/profile/history scope and current preview.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC06:** “Summary eligibility SHALL not create
  grants, default inclusion, prior-history access, longer duration or migration
  enrollment. Exact sharing records identify the admitted summary profile and
  historical scope under current organization/source authority. A profile
  expansion SHALL not silently widen an existing sharing episode. Ending or
  disabling the affected episode fences dependent summaries; re-enable requires
  fresh qualified activation and cannot clear old fences through an unchanged
  role, preset or cache. Unrelated valid coaching grants remain usable.”

### OP07 — Correctly protected values have incorrect financial meaning

- **What:** A source-safe value is mislabeled as received, combined across
  currency/entity/period, or replaced by zero after privacy filtering.
- **Why it matters:** Privacy does not excuse financial misinformation. Summing
  only visible fragments can falsely claim complete coverage; processing, soft
  credit or plans are not posted receipts.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** High if coaching duplicates source math.
- **Evidence:** S12:1236–1264; S13:1086–1126,1150–1156; S14.
- **Answer impact:** Expected and received remain selected, distinct source
  summaries. Their exact metric meaning must be qualified by the owning source.
- **Permanent fix:** Use the existing metric registry, canonical effective fold
  and safe commitment summaries; retain source coverage and currentness.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC07:** “Expected/planned and received values
  SHALL retain exact owner metric identity, grain, currency, entity, period,
  version/currentness and coverage meaning. No local base-money SUM, cached
  writable counter, cross-currency total or unauthorized sibling-line residual
  may replace the source. Privacy exclusion SHALL not be presented as zero or
  complete support. If safe truthful coverage cannot be conveyed, return the
  affected module's uniform unavailable state. E7 or another unresolved owner
  ruling blocks only its affected metric, not unrelated qualified summaries.”

### OP08 — A safe number escapes through previews, caches or secondary channels

- **What:** A previously permitted result, preview, chart tooltip, accessibility
  table, debug trace, notification, export or late response outlives its scope.
- **Why it matters:** These paths can retain exact financial values after grant,
  identity, history or privacy restrictions change, or expose a wider source.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** High across asynchronous surfaces.
- **Evidence:** S02, S06–S11, S15.
- **Answer impact:** Screen-summary eligibility grants no export, print,
  notification-body money or new history. Legitimate current reads remain useful.
- **Permanent fix:** Reuse current owner/P12 release fences and partition caches
  by full exact context/profile/source authority; apply the same projection to
  all representations, with preview bounded by its requester's own authority.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC08:** “Every reached summary representation
  SHALL use the same current admitted profile, source version and exact
  principal/tenant/hat/subject/history authority. Late results, stored outcomes,
  previews and caches cannot bypass current release checks. A preview reveals
  only the sharing scope its requester may inspect, never the coach's separately
  broader rights. Visibility grants no export/print, email attachment or
  notification detail; those remain separately governed. Previously observed
  values cannot be recalled, but subsequent disclosure must stop.”

### OP09 — The reason for withholding becomes the disclosure

- **What:** “Hidden because only one donor,” exact suppressed-row counts,
  distinguished missing/denied endpoints or detailed logs reveal protected facts.
- **Why it matters:** No displayed amount is needed to identify a restricted
  supporter, assignment or changed gift.
- **Severity:** Critical. **Likelihood:** Medium–High during diagnostics/UX work.
- **Evidence:** S07:661–667; S09:441–449; S10:1649–1650,1664.
- **Answer impact:** Keep useful, truthful status without exposing internal
  suppression reasons. Permission to inspect a module is not audit privilege.
- **Permanent fix:** Existing uniform-denial contract, minimal source-safe UI
  states and separate authorized diagnostic evidence with no rendered amounts.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC09:** “Unavailable or withheld output SHALL
  not reveal protected contributor counts, identities, existence, exact hidden
  intervals or the reason a floor denied access. Apply the existing uniformity
  contract; source-safe freshness/availability may be explained only when
  independently disclosable. Detailed denial evidence remains in its authorized
  identifiers-only audit purpose, not user payloads, logs, telemetry or notices.
  Suppression never returns a fabricated zero.”

### OP10 — Formal complexity expands while actual assurance stays unproved

- **What:** D9 introduces configurable field expressions, arbitrary filters,
  noise budgets or a new warehouse, yet claims privacy from synthetic examples.
- **Why it matters:** The product becomes hard to maintain without establishing
  complete safe behavior or useful coach outcomes.
- **Severity:** High. **Likelihood:** Medium under broad analytics designs.
- **Evidence:** S04–S05, S07–S08, S13:1152; S03:313–325.
- **Answer impact:** Keep a finite source-qualified module/profile and existing
  owners. A small design still needs negative and meaningful positive proof.
- **Permanent fix:** Trace profile fields and predicates to existing source and
  privacy contracts; qualification at actual services/database plus real-user
  comprehension and measured supported workloads.
- **Exact proposed clause — PC10:** “Implement a finite versioned summary
  contract through the existing source metric registry and P3/P10/P12 resolver,
  not a second policy, analytics or privacy engine. Each selectable profile
  requires positive useful-summary, scope denial, inference, temporal correction,
  revocation and reached-surface proof at actual owner boundaries. Synthetic
  mechanics and documentation checks remain bounded evidence, not production
  privacy or usability qualification. No numeric budget or universal secrecy
  guarantee is invented.”

No finite fixture set proves safety against arbitrary external background
knowledge. The release claim must name the admitted source/disclosure profile,
the owned representations and combinations evaluated, the threat assumptions,
the observed results and their limits. If an unsafe inference remains reachable
through an offered output, suppress or narrow that affected profile rather than
moving the risk into monitoring or claiming that consent to coaching waived the
underlying donor/worker restrictions.
