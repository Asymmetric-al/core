# D9 — Optional coaching support summaries: benchmark and privacy evidence

Research date: **October 16, 2026 UTC**. Research and recommendations only. This
note does not ratify D9, grant access, change financial owners, select a history
window or establish that a coach-summary producer exists. The founder's answer
and the parent review remain authoritative for the product choice.

**Recommendation:** when optional support summaries are adopted, offer a small
number of separately authorized, source-qualified summaries that distinguish
commitment expectations from recorded receipts. Show the subject, currency,
period/basis and source currentness clearly. A coach should understand progress
without receiving donor records, a substitute account balance, an inferred
income promise or an unqualified performance score. A summary that cannot meet
its financial or disclosure contract should be unavailable, not approximated
into a reassuring number.

This recommendation is product judgment grounded in the evidence below. No
interviews, authenticated vendor accounts, live signups, sends, transactions,
production permission tests or observed coaching outcomes were performed.

## 2. What the evidence supports, and which owner still decides

The [founder brief](../phase28-founder-brief.md), especially its goals,
expectations and financial-visibility sections, already rejects shortcuts that
would collapse promises, receipts and available balances. The
[Q9 frontier note](../phase28-q9-coaching-support-summary-research.md) and
[D8 dashboard evidence](../d8-evidence/dashboard-truth.md) retain the exact access,
projection and unresolved-owner boundaries.

| Coach's question                           | Useful fact or context                                                                                                                    | Authoritative boundary; what must not be substituted                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| “What is the missionary working toward?”   | A deliberately shared Goal Version with its purpose, dates and currency when monetary.                                                    | P28 goal; not salary, an automatically adopted P21 Approved Support Plan or a newly inferred income requirement.                                                                                              |
| “What support is committed?”               | A qualified P16 commitment summary whose type, period and comparison basis are explicit.                                                  | Fixed-total pledge, actual scheduled occurrence and normalized recurring comparison are distinct. An observed monthly gift pattern or an optimistic forecast is not a new promise.                            |
| “What support has been received?”          | The exact owner-certified received-activity projection, with effective facts, subject/allocation scope, period, currency and currentness. | P13 contribution designation lines and corrections, with applicable P14 allocation/recognition rules. A provider attempt, recurring plan, unposted in-hand gift or recognition credit is not additional cash. |
| “Why does progress look different?”        | A separately permitted short explanation and sufficiently labeled source state.                                                           | A narrative is contextual self-report. It cannot silently change a financial record, reveal withheld donor facts or manufacture a recovery task for the coach.                                                |
| “Are we making progress toward this goal?” | A comparison only when its goal and measure have compatible meaning, currency, subject, period/cadence and disclosure scope.              | Dividing any visible number by any visible goal does not qualify a percentage. Do not compare an annual pledge total with one month's receipts.                                                               |
| “How much can be spent?”                   | Outside this optional-summary entitlement.                                                                                                | Finance-confirmed P21 balances, reserves, expenses and spendability remain separately governed. Donation sums cannot answer this question.                                                                    |

The distinction in B1/B2 is useful precisely because the labels are not universal:
one vendor's projected installments, another vendor's likely giving and a third
vendor's raised total can answer different questions. Core should name its own
qualified basis clearly, without imitating a familiar label that changes the
meaning. M1 and S1 also do not justify recalculating historical support with
today's exchange rate.

The inherited owner details remain as recorded in D8: P16 controls recurring
occurrences, monthly normalization and exclusions; P13/P14 control effective
money and attribution; P21 governs financial publication profiles; P12 governs
current scoped authority. P13 E7 temporal campaign attribution and C-01
correction approval remain unresolved owner gates where applicable. A dependent
metric must await its exact resolution. This is not a reason to block every
independent qualified summary, nor permission to settle either gate here.

## 3. Recommended lean presentation and strongest alternative

### Recommended source-based approach

The smallest useful direction is a **Support progress** area within the existing
coach subject view, containing only the independently chosen summaries that can
actually be disclosed. Do not create a new analytics application, raw gift
browser, query builder, financial ranking or bespoke coaching ledger.

Possible labels are **Expected support from commitments** and **Support
received**. They are examples, not a final field contract: if the source is a
monthly equivalent rather than scheduled support for a calendar period, say
that explicitly. For example, “Monthly equivalent of recurring commitments” is
more truthful than “Expected this month.” Do not use a single “Funded” figure to
blend both measures.

Each disclosed measure needs enough context to answer four ordinary questions:
which person/project and organization; what period and basis; which currency;
how current and complete the source is. Keep those facts visible close to the
value rather than hiding them exclusively in a tooltip. A concise explanatory
line may describe the financial meaning, while exact technical provenance stays
in the appropriate internal evidence surface. A fresh page request is not proof
that the source is up to date.

Offer same-currency, same-basis comparison only when the owner qualifies it.
Parallel currencies remain separate; currency codes supplement symbols. A
conversion is not required for a useful initial summary. If a later authorized
conversion is offered, its rate, as-of basis, rounding and financial meaning
need a source contract. This note selects none of them.

Missionary sharing controls should state what the coach will actually see, not
offer a broad “Share finances” switch that appears to include balances or donor
records. An eligible module can still be unavailable for a particular subject,
period or disclosure. Preview must use the coach's effective disclosure, not a
missionary's broader source view. Do not require the coach to submit a separate
approval request merely to view something already deliberately and validly
shared.

### Strongest alternative: narrative and goals without native money summaries

Continue with chosen goals, voluntary activity, discussion topics and agreed
next steps, optionally supplemented by a dated missionary explanation where
already permitted. This can express uncertainty, unusual support patterns and
practical priorities better than a number alone. It has less financial
integration and derivative-disclosure scope. It remains useful coaching and is
not accurately described as entirely nonfinancial when a monetary goal is shared.

Its tradeoff is manual reconstruction, inconsistent terminology and weaker
comparability. Self-reported “expected” amounts can mix hopes, donor intentions,
commitments and historic averages. A narrative must be visibly attributable and
must not masquerade as a ledger-derived receipt. Free text also does not remove
the duty to exclude protected donor details. This alternative is strongest when
qualified source projections are unavailable or the missionary prefers to share
context alone; it should not become a disguised requirement to copy forbidden
financial records.

The recommendation favors a small standardized summary plus optional context
when the product choice permits it. It does **not** recommend routine approvals,
a mandatory check-in schedule, coach edits, automatic sharing, automatic history
access, or automatic narrative generation. No selected history duration or
default inclusion can be inferred from this research.

## 5. Privacy: summary is a representation, not a guarantee

I1 supports assessing what the actual recipient can reasonably infer using the
released information and outside knowledge. I2 supports minimizing information
to the coaching purpose while retaining enough context for sound interpretation.
Neither source supplies a universal safe group size or an Asym entitlement.

The following are **synthetic threat examples**, not incidents observed in Core
or any vendor. They explain why removing names is not sufficient:

| Release pattern                                                          | Possible inference                                                              | Proposed mitigation direction, not a final algorithm                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One contributor in a period/currency lane                                | The total is that person's contribution when the coach knows who gave.          | Qualify the exact summary for that context; withhold when its disclosure is not permitted. Do not expose a hidden donor count as the explanation.                 |
| Total rises from 1,000 to 1,075 between two observations                 | A known unique intervening gift may be inferred as 75.                          | Review repeated releases, temporal deltas and visible prior values, not just each snapshot in isolation. A minimum count per snapshot is insufficient.            |
| Total plus a subtotal excluding one group                                | Subtraction exposes the omitted group's contribution.                           | Bound available combinations and derivatives; check complements before admitting filters or additional modules.                                                   |
| Goal of 1,000 and progress of 75%, while the underlying amount is hidden | The amount is recoverable as 750.                                               | Treat ratio, denominator, remaining amount and chart position as disclosures of their inputs. Hiding only the numeral is ineffective.                             |
| An extra currency lane, category, tooltip or chart point appears         | Presence or timing may reveal a contributor or event even without a number.     | Apply the same disclosure contract to structure, labels, counts, empty states, accessibility text and payloads.                                                   |
| Several project views overlap                                            | Differences can isolate a contribution or reveal a protected association.       | Evaluate authorized combinations across the actual subject views; no new portfolio total by default.                                                              |
| A value disappears with “anonymous donor excluded”                       | The explanation itself reveals protected donor existence.                       | Use recipient-safe unavailable copy; avoid explaining restricted counts or donor categories to an unauthorized reader.                                            |
| A revoked grant leaves cached historical figures visible                 | Earlier access persists through a derivative even after current authority ends. | Apply current source/grant rules to refreshed, cached and historical representations. Do not promise that a person forgets information already legitimately seen. |

Authorize the **exact aggregate projection and the contributions it may include**
before release. This does not mean a coach must receive permission to read each
underlying donor row: a separately qualified aggregate can legitimately have a
different access surface. Conversely, row access or a read-only UI does not by
itself qualify every aggregate or derivative. P2 explains technical row-policy
behavior, while the inference analysis here is a separate application judgment.

Keep the initial query surface bounded. Any additional dimension, period, trend,
ratio or comparison requires the same qualification; “only a chart” is not an
exception. Prefer withholding an unqualified value to inventing a noisy or
rounded financial fact. An owner-approved coarse representation may be useful,
but this research does not choose rounding, differential privacy, a minimum
count, suppression thresholds, retention or a new dependency.

## 7. Testable acceptance questions for later implementation

These are proposed validation cases; none was executed as a UI, SQL, permission
or finance test in this research.

1. Can a missionary and coach independently explain the difference between the
   displayed expectation, receipt and goal without assuming either is spendable
   money? Can they identify subject, currency, period and currentness on a phone?
2. Does a quarterly gift, annual gift, irregular pattern, skipped occurrence and
   paused commitment retain the correct source meaning without a fabricated
   overdue label? Is a monthly equivalent distinguishable from cash expected in
   the calendar month?
3. Does an undated fixed-total remainder stay out of an unsupported period
   forecast? Does a received but unmatched payment remain a receipt while its
   fulfillment state is unresolved?
4. Do refunds, corrections, duplicate imports, allocation splits, DAF recognition
   and overlapping project views preserve one authoritative financial amount
   without counting the same value twice?
5. Are USD, CAD and JPY distinct, correctly formatted and never added merely
   because a dashboard needs one total? Does a changed display exchange rate
   avoid silently rewriting a historical source fact?
6. Can a qualified actual zero be distinguished from no grant, no configured
   source, incomplete coverage, stale data and a privacy-withheld summary without
   exposing which hidden donor or gift caused withholding?
7. Can a recipient recover a protected amount using singleton groups, previous
   observations, complements, percentages, overlapping subjects, omitted points,
   network responses or accessibility labels? Test combinations as well as
   isolated widgets; do not treat one passing group-size case as proof.
8. Does the missionary preview match the coach's effective view, including when
   permission, source availability or sharing changes during the session? Does
   reopening or refreshing avoid restoring a revoked projection?
9. Does declining an optional summary leave useful coaching goals/context/actions
   available where separately shared, without a compliance warning, rank or
   inferred failure? Can a narrative be understood as contextual self-report?
10. Can readers use a text alternative and keyboard/mobile controls to understand
    any adopted chart without color-only currency distinctions or hover-only
    definitions? Exact UI/accessibility proof belongs to implementation, not a
    benchmark screenshot.

## 8. Remaining uncertainty

The evidence supports the utility of distinguishing commitments from receipts;
it does not quantify demand among Asym missionaries or prove improved outcomes.
No benchmark's real permission enforcement, refund propagation latency,
multi-currency accuracy, aggregation privacy or coaching workflow was tested in
an authenticated account. DonorElf's available help is particularly dated;
Sola's current public page is marketing rather than a formula specification.

Core still needs exact owner qualification for each proposed summary, including
source scope, period convention, currentness/coverage, effective corrections,
historical authority and derivative-disclosure behavior. Which summaries,
history and access duration the founder selects is distinct from that
engineering work. This note supplies evidence and candidate clauses only; it
does not claim a source producer, rollout, automatic conversion, universally safe
aggregate or real-time financial view exists.
