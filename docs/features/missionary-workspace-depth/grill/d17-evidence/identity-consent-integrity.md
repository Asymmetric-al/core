# D17 — contact-file identity, consent and data integrity

Research date: 2026-10-16. Research only. The founder selected missionary
self-service contact-file intake when the organization permits it; CSV/XLSX
files may come from different tools or ordinary spreadsheets. This note proposes
the exact bounded target profile. It does not implement an importer, approve a
new permission, upload a file, or change any canonical identity or consent.

## Recommended permanent path

Use the existing P30 transport/review/run identity and P29 custody, with a
qualified missionary reviewer/committer under P12 and a **P9-owned restricted
prospect-preparation target** under D1. This is reviewed contact intake: add
eligible supplied assertions, reuse the same previously accepted import target,
and show useful authorized duplicate/conflict outcomes. It is not a second CRM,
an alternate canonical Party writer or a universal migration of the exporting
tool's contact, household, note, gift and subscription model.

The minimum useful initial profile should **add/reuse, without bulk-updating
existing contact or preparation fields**. Already-present permitted contacts can be identified
or reused through their source owner without replacing their fields. A changed
value from a later file is a reviewed discrepancy, not the newest truth merely
because it arrived last. An authorized ordinary preparation correction or D5
canonical-contact correction remains available through its own command and
current exact field/subject permissions. Exact reimport convergence
means “the same admitted source record/effect,” not “every new upload may update
any person it resembles.” Broader bulk correction would need its own explicitly
qualified target action; it is unnecessary for this selected intake job.

Do not make every new row wait for a canonical identity decision. The accepted
D1 preparation seam can retain the uploader's permitted assertions without
publishing a Party, resolving a hidden match for them or changing canonical
facts. Its truthful outcome is **Added to your prospects**, not “new person
created in the organization CRM.” D2 sharing and canonical admission remain
later deliberate, independently qualified actions. This preserves a useful
ordinary import while the identity owner handles genuinely ambiguous identity.

## Material concerns and exact proposed clauses

Severity indicates potential harm; likelihood describes conditional design
risk, not an observed production incident. These are proposed D17 requirements.

### IC17-01 — File ownership becomes authority over a person or canonical field

**Critical / plausible.** D1, D5, P9:581–596 and P12 current scope govern every
accepted row, regardless of a CSV column called Owner, Staff, Missionary or ID.

**Clause:** “The importer SHALL derive the exact current Tenant, actor, target
purpose and source scope server-side. A file, source ID, claimed former owner,
relationship, staff role or successful upload SHALL confer no target authority.
This contact-intake profile SHALL add/reuse qualified restricted preparation and
SHALL NOT bulk-overwrite existing preparation or canonical P9 contact facts. Any later correction uses
the independently authorized source command and expected field revision.”

**Proof:** file has another missionary/tenant/Party ID; imported contact is
already independently visible to staff; missionary can import but not edit
canonical email; changed phone conflicts with a newer staff correction. No
whole-record upsert or “fill blanks” rule may bypass field permissions.

### IC17-02 — Email, name or phone causes a false identity link or merge

**Critical / likely if the importer uses `findOrCreate(email)`.** P4:153,185,
491–495; P25 IC06:57–59; CRM Core:61–64.

**Clause:** “Separate matching evidence from identity acceptance and permitted
action. Reuse the P4 owner's qualified matching normalization and confidence
rules; do not strip dot/plus aliases, infer identity from shared email/phone,
select the oldest match, auto-link a weak match or automatically merge. Exact
mailbox equality is not proof of one human, one household, one claim or one
subscription. Existing donor-specific uniqueness SHALL NOT become Party identity
or force a fabricated donor/duplicate Party.”

**Proof:** spouses share an address; church office mailbox used by several
people; twins/same name; household and person rows share contact details;
international phone lacks region; recycled address; unclaimed and claimed
records; Gmail dot/plus-looking strings; contact point appears in another Tenant.

### IC17-03 — Hidden matches leak through row results or counts

**Critical / plausible.** D1 and D2 result-authority discipline apply to preview,
commit, recovery, matching hints, totals, errors, timing and downloads.

**Clause:** “Qualify minimum candidate/result disclosure authority before any
existence-dependent branch. Hidden-existing and absent worlds SHALL not produce
distinguishable match counts, row disposition, target IDs, constraint messages,
timing classes or downstream notifications where that result is unauthorized.
Restricted assertion acceptance SHALL not claim canonical creation or sharing.
Do not create a second visible Party to evade a hidden match. A qualified
visible-record reuse path remains useful without requiring all new rows to enter
a universal staff review queue.”

**Proof:** paired absent/hidden datasets with identical submitted file and actor;
all preview/accepted/rejected/unchanged counts and downloadable errors; duplicate
constraint paths; per-row continuation and a one-row file; reimport and archived
records. Generic error wording is insufficient if one case succeeds and the
other rejects. A uniform denial that prevents all useful intake also fails the
positive product requirement.

### IC17-04 — External IDs escape their namespace or get trusted as Core IDs

**High / likely across multiple tools/accounts.** P30:4335; CRM Core:48–59
preserve source identity, not a global untyped string.

**Clause:** “Bind stable external identifiers to exact Tenant, source system,
source instance/account or dataset, source entity kind and the admitted target
binding. Preserve them as opaque strings under the source profile. A filename,
tool label, row number or user-supplied Core-looking UUID SHALL NOT select an
internal target or establish identity. An existing binding may be reused only
under current scope and its original source proof; a conflicting/recycled ID
requires source-owned resolution rather than reassignment.”

**Proof:** MPDX account A ID 42 versus account B ID 42; CSV label copied from
another user; person ID equals household ID; leading-zero identifier; spreadsheet
converted a long identifier to rounded/scientific text; same external ID points
to a different person later. No guessed recovery of information Excel discarded.

### IC17-05 — Reimport is either duplicate creation or a hidden overwrite

**High / likely under repeated files and interrupted runs.** P30:4331–4337,
4359,4373 requires exact repeat convergence and changed-target conflict.

**Clause:** “Maintain exact reviewed-plan/run/row lineage and accepted target
outcomes independent of filename and browser request keys. Replay of the same
accepted semantic row SHALL recover its current-authorized result without
another target effect. Changed bytes/mapping/meaning requires a new reviewed
plan. Where a stable source identity is absent, content fingerprints may detect
repeat evidence but SHALL NOT prove person identity; row position is not a
cross-file key. Report unresolved changed-file identity honestly, not a guarantee
of automatic deduplication.”

**Proof:** identical file renamed; reload after a lost commit response; rows
sorted; columns reordered with equivalent reviewed mapping; two identical-looking
rows representing different people; source ID removed; import repeated after a
contact correction, merge or archive. An exact repeated run is tractable even
when arbitrary edited spreadsheets cannot prove cross-file identity.

### IC17-06 — A household export manufactures people, grants or relationships

**High / likely with support-raising tool exports.** P9:330–351,400–415 and
P4:491–495 keep Party kinds and people distinct.

**Clause:** “Map through closed source/target profiles with minimum meaningful
assertions and any qualified reviewed source kind. Restricted intake may retain
an unsplit label and explicitly unresolved kind under its source contract; it
SHALL NOT pretend canonical Party-kind resolution has occurred. Do not split a couple's name,
invent a spouse, infer household membership from address/email, or convert an
organization contact into a donor. Household/org and relationship imports need
their qualified source commands; unsupported structures remain visible review
items rather than silently flattened records. No role/grant or ownership follows
from imported labels.”

**Proof:** `Sam & Lee`, one-name person, organization with human contact columns,
church household, several rows for members of one family, shared phone, blank
email, non-Latin names. A source preset can make ordinary cases quick; the user
does not need to design an identity algorithm or manually classify every clean
row when the reviewed file-level type is sufficient.

### IC17-07 — Bulk parsing damages names/contact data or clears better values

**High / likely with spreadsheet coercion and broad snapshots.** P9 B17 and
P25 IC10/IC11 govern exact name/phone/address purposes; D5 excludes broad writes.

**Clause:** “Preserve supported Unicode/contact display input and source
provenance; apply only the target's qualified normalization. No forced surname,
transliteration, locale-derived country/phone region, silent truncation or
multi-address extraction. Blank/missing/unmapped cells SHALL NOT be Clear commands
or default values. Reject or expose precision/encoding/type loss that prevents a
safe assertion; validate every accepted field before that row's source effect.”

**Proof:** `00123` postal code; phone extension/leading plus; accented and
single-character names; email cell containing two addresses or a formula result;
Excel date-coerced identifier; country absent; blank column in a reimport;
otherwise valid row whose second mapped field fails. Do not overwrite independent
legal names, issued documents, billing identity or released public labels.

### IC17-08 — Import manufactures consent, proof or newsletter membership

**Critical / likely if source subscriber columns are copied.** Founder brief:245,
P25 EX04:49–59 and IC06:59,87; P6:152–153; D6 independent enrollment/proof.

**Clause:** “An imported email, gift status, partner category, 'subscribed' flag,
verified column or prior provider audience SHALL NOT establish current mailbox
proof, native consent, newsletter membership, SMS permission, claim, login or
access. Positive preference migration requires a separately qualified exact
recipient/contact/source/list-purpose adoption contract; this contact-intake
profile has no such activation. Import SHALL send no welcome, confirmation,
newsletter, verification request or historical outreach.”

**Proof:** active Mailchimp subscriber; unsubscribed donor marked financial;
household subscription with two people; opted-in SMS field; legacy verified flag;
proof for an old contact revision; import repeated after withdrawal. D7/D15 send
authority is not created by D17, even if the actor can otherwise send messages.

### IC17-09 — Imported adverse evidence is discarded or becomes a global stop

**Critical / plausible in either direction.** P4:185 preserves most-restrictive
consent on governed merge; P6:571–585 scopes provider evidence; P25 EX04 preserves
current stops without unioning unrelated permissions or purposes.

**Clause:** “Preserve current canonical restrictions and clearly mapped imported
negative evidence with its original source, channel, list/purpose, contact and
certainty. Do not convert absence into consent, clear suppression on reimport,
or mutate global contact authority from an arbitrary file. The actual
consent/contact owner SHALL qualify the minimum applicable restriction. Target
acceptance with material adverse evidence SHALL atomically record that owner's
accepted scoped restriction/evidence and durable authority receipt, bound to
the exact Tenant, proposed target/recipient authority and contact revision,
source occurrence, channel/list-purpose, provenance and current revision. The
qualified P9/P28 recipient producer and P6/D15 gate SHALL consume that authority;
no parallel P28 consent flag or future unenforced hold is sufficient. If this
owner adapter, receipt or gate is not qualified, block the affected row before
target acceptance while allowing independently eligible rows to proceed.
Topic unsubscribe, provider-local bounce and global Never Ask remain distinct.”

**Proof:** newsletter-X unsubscribe with unrelated permitted communication;
global do-not-contact remains after an active-row reimport; bounce tied to old
address/provider; a forged restrictive flag targets an existing unrelated Party;
an adverse row cannot resolve its person safely. Preserve evidence and restrict
the relevant proposed source use without granting the uploader global suppression
write authority. A blocked row's file/staging evidence can remain available only
under its current custody/review policy; it is not an accepted contact with an
imagined later protection. Do not silently drop the column and declare it clean.

### IC17-10 — Historical labels create live duties, finance or enrollment

**High / likely with full competitor exports.** P30:4337,4353,4367;
P9 derived roles and ratified D1/P27 source separation.

**Clause:** “Accept only the finite contact-preparation target. Historical
status, last-contact, pledge, giving, referral, appeal, assignee or staff columns
SHALL NOT create money, commitments, completed interactions, P27 enrollment,
tasks, staff membership, coaching rights or current responsibility. Unsupported
facts are explicitly reported, not silently mapped to a native status. Historical
import provenance cannot become a prospective effect by changing a displayed
date or file mapping.”

**Proof:** MPDX partner-financial status; last-call date; total given; recurring
amount; former staff assignee; pending appointment; source “never ask” processed
only through IC17-09, not a cosmetic stage. No import-triggered notification or
first-gift/thank-you automation.

### IC17-11 — Departure, reassignment or merge revives another person's access

**Critical / plausible under durable batches.** P12 fire-time checks, P4:560–565
and ratified D3 deny invented successor access.

**Clause:** “Re-prove current actor/source/result authority at preview, commit,
each resumed acceptance and readback. Revocation/subject change fences work
whose required authority is lost; preserve already accepted source facts without
granting a former worker or replacement access. Reassignment, shared login,
merge/split or reimport SHALL NOT transfer restricted preparation, union private
histories or restore an ended grant. Independent surviving authority remains
valid under the existing resolver.”

**Proof:** actor leaves during a chunk; staff replaces missionary; same human
changes Tenant; merge occurs between plan and commit; prior importer may still
read a summary but no longer a target; file uploaded by departed staff is found
by another user. Possession of the file/run ID is not custody permission.

### IC17-12 — Archive/erasure/reversal is mistaken for delete-and-recreate

**High / likely with “undo import” or active-only matching.** P30:4343,4367;
D3/D4 exact lifecycle and retention; P4:185 reversible merge with limited shell
purge, not blanket Party deletion.

**Clause:** “Resolution SHALL consider relevant currently permitted archived
sources without auto-restoring or cloning them. Apply current erasure,
restriction and source tombstone rules before reimport/restoration; an old file
cannot resurrect erased content or stopped outreach. Reversal SHALL be source-
qualified, limited to the exact still-reversible accepted effects, and conflict
with later corrections, sharing, merge, independent references or required
history. Never promise universal batch Undo or delete a real Party to reverse
its import provenance.”

**Proof:** archived prospect reimported; prep was shared via D2; another actor
corrected a field; Party merged; original file contains later-erased details;
source row absent from next export; rollback after one chunk accepted. Missing
rows are not deletion instructions. Do not invent a retention period, permanent
raw-file archive or restored plaintext exemption.

## Required qualification, not new founder algorithm choices

1. P30 registers this exact restricted contact target and bounded missionary
   reviewer/committer, source-instance/record lineage, allowed actions and review
   outcomes. Source-specific templates/presets can map common exports, but all
   use the same P4/P9 commands and target field catalog.
2. P9/P4 qualify the D1 assertion-admission and optional visible-record reuse
   producer, including real successful new-contact intake and hidden/absent
   counterpart tests. Do not borrow the guest-gift attribution or claim-request
   purpose as generic admission authority.
3. P3/P6/P25 qualify the exact adverse-evidence mapping, atomic authority receipt
   and actual recipient/send gate before such target rows can be accepted.
   Existing restrictions survive every repeat, merge and contact change. No
   positive subscription migration is included by this contact-only decision.
4. Verify actual concurrency, source transactions and row lineage under resume,
   changed mapping, stale target, narrowed authority, duplicate source IDs and
   late commit. Couple each accepted row and its required source/run evidence;
   a completed upload or empty parser error list is not business acceptance.
5. Preserve the actual audience across the file, staging, preview, duplicates,
   annotated error download, counts, audit and result recovery. P30's ordinary
   staff surface does not entitle staff to all D1 source assertions.

No provider-specific dedupe algorithm, arbitrary similarity threshold,
bulk canonical updater, consent migration product, general household importer,
mandatory staff resolution queue, new role engine or automatic rollback is
needed to provide the selected useful upload → review → import journey. The
precise supported source profiles and qualified target actions must be visible
before upload/commit; “many tools” is not a claim that every export is lossless.

No executable tests were run for this evidence note. The proof cases above are
implementation/qualification obligations; another agent's mechanics evidence
must not be represented as P4 identity, P12 authority or consent certification.
