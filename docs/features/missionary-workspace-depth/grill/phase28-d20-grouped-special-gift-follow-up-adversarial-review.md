# D20 — One current follow-up for the reviewed special gifts

Review date: October 16, 2026 UTC. Repository baseline:
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813`, branch `work`.

**Disposition: Accept with required amendments; subsequently ratified.** The
founder has now explicitly ratified the corrected decision and required amendments,
as recorded below. This is a decision/research record, not a formal owner
amendment, OpenSpec change, implementation, ticket or release qualification.

The important distinction is small: **finish the gifts Maya reviewed, not whatever
happens to be in the task when she clicks.** One current task remains the useful
human work unit. Each gift retains its own identity, qualification and handled
evidence. This needs an exact completion contract, not a second task system.

## Exact corrected decision to record

> **A — One current follow-up.** Group compatible, source-qualified special-gift
> follow-up for the same supporter and exact ministry/project into one current
> shared Independent follow-up task. Clearly list the gifts covered by the action.
> Maya handles that reviewed set once; each gift keeps its own financial record,
> qualification and task-coverage history.
>
> Completion or Not needed applies only to the exact reviewed gift-purpose set
> under current permissions and responsibility. An unrelated new gift does not
> invalidate unchanged reviewed work and is never silently included. If it joins
> before completion, the same task stays open for the remainder. If the task has
> already finished, a genuinely new eligible gift can create the next current
> task. Completed evidence never expands retroactively.
>
> A material change to a reviewed gift, relevant purpose, responsibility or
> authority requires refresh and review before that command can succeed. It
> must not silently complete a different or partially substituted set. Recover
> an uncertain result through the original command receipt; do not repeat work
> against the latest group.
>
> Finishing the task sends no message, records no invented conversation, changes
> no gift, grants no access and requires no narrative report. Optional interaction
> recording and deliberate sending retain their existing owners and outcomes.
> A mistaken disposition has a qualified correction path that preserves one
> current task and history; it cannot recall communication or undo a gift.

## The complete simple user journey

The normal case uses the existing shared task row and detail sheet, existing
base-maia/Base UI components, typography and spacing. One primary action, a quiet
secondary action, and readable source details are enough. Do not build a separate
thank-you inbox, batch console, progress wizard or new design system.

Illustrative copy, with amounts supplied only by authorized source projections:

```text
Follow up with Grace
Maya's ministry · 2 special gifts

Gifts covered by this follow-up
12 Oct 2026    USD 100    [View gift]
15 Oct 2026    USD 150    [View gift]

[Finish follow-up for 2 gifts]   [Not needed]
Record an interaction…

Finishing marks follow-up for these 2 gifts as handled.
It does not send a message.
```

The amounts illustrate layout, not generosity thresholds. Use a permitted brief
reference when equal-date/equal-amount gifts need distinguishing. Show the precise
ministry and source reason; do not call Grace a new organizational donor or imply
these are all her gifts. Keep money secondary to the relationship action. No
combined total is needed; separate currencies can share human follow-up while
remaining separately labeled.

## Exact required decision and design clauses

These clauses are the ratified future contract. Physical tables, RPC names,
query plans and component placement remain design choices within existing owners.

**C01 — Qualified purpose, bounded choice.** Native grouping SHALL consume an
owner-qualified special-gift follow-up occurrence. It SHALL NOT decide eligibility
from an amount, visible roster, missing commitment, latest payment, recognition
row or financial risk threshold. D19 first-support, statutory receipt, care,
fulfillment and other distinct obligations SHALL remain distinct unless an
existing owner explicitly qualifies shared coverage of an actual action.

**C02 — Compatibility and one current task.** Grouping SHALL require the same
Tenant, exact typed P9 ministry/project subject and Legal Entity, qualified
follow-up Party, compatible purpose/completion authority and accountable
responsibility scope. The shared task owner SHALL enforce at most one current
compatible task. Assignee, public Page, name, first member or date window SHALL
NOT define a new obligation. Reassignment SHALL preserve purpose identity.

One-current SHALL mean compatible pending special-gift work, not every open
task for that supporter. A task retained solely for another independent
undertaking SHALL retain that work/history without automatically accepting new
special gifts. Admission may reuse it only when the owner still proves compatible
purpose/completion authority; otherwise resolve the one current special-gift task
without duplicating the independent undertaking.

**C03 — Stable member identity.** Each covered member SHALL be the qualified
business contribution occurrence/Party/subject/purpose, with canonical source
identity and provenance. Multiple allocation lines, recognition paths, reason
codes, revisions and transport deliveries SHALL NOT create multiple members for
that same effect. Distinct equal-value/date gifts SHALL remain distinct. Merges
and source aliases SHALL reconcile through their owners without rewriting old
attribution or replaying handled work.

**C04 — Separate source authority.** P13 SHALL own received money, corrections
and allocation and expose captured dating facts under P7's issuer/jurisdiction
dating policy and resolution; P14 recognition/recipient-route facts; P16 relevant
commitment/fulfillment comparisons; P9 subject and responsibility; P12 authority.
P28 SHALL own special-gift purpose admission. The existing shared task owner
SHALL own task membership and immutable reviewed-set disposition/coverage
receipts. No second P28 task or financial ledger SHALL be introduced. Qualification
SHALL bind all material source revisions coherently; one P13 cursor alone cannot
certify unchanged P14/P16 facts. Recognition/task lag SHALL NOT block money posting.

**C05 — Admission and recovery.** Qualified purpose admission and its durable
identifier-only projection intent SHALL commit atomically at the source command.
Shared-task admission SHALL atomically resolve the current compatible task and
deduplicated membership. Asynchronous materialization SHALL recover from durable
intent and semantic receipts, not event delivery alone. History-only imports,
backfills and historical repair SHALL NOT themselves admit new special-gift
work. Delivery or qualified repair of an originally live, unconsumed occurrence
SHALL remain distinguishable from historical acquisition and retain its lineage.

**C06 — Exact reviewed set.** Each disposition SHALL bind a nonempty exact set of
currently authorized member identities, relevant source versions, purpose,
responsibility and task-authority state, plus command identity and intended
outcome. The server SHALL validate this binding; caller IDs/hashes/counts do not
prove eligibility. Reviewed means deliberately presented, definite and inspectable
scope, not a claim that the system measured what Maya read or understood.

**C07 — Relevant concurrent change.** Acceptance SHALL re-prove current authority
and selected-source facts under the owning invalidation fences. A material
selected-member or relevant task/purpose/responsibility change SHALL reject the
entire reviewed disposition with no newly handled subset. Unrelated additions
outside the reviewed set SHALL neither be consumed nor alone invalidate it.
A generic global group-version equality check SHALL NOT replace that distinction.

**C08 — Atomic result and residual work.** The shared task command SHALL atomically
record exact per-member coverage, disposition receipt, actor/time and local task
state/history. If new or unresolved required membership remains, the same task
SHALL remain current; its result SHALL state only the reviewed outcome. It may
finish only when no unresolved required membership or independent obligation
remains. Unknown/hidden work SHALL NOT be inferred absent or silently handled.
Internal persistence, closure and residue SHALL NOT disclose hidden membership.
If no independently authorized actionable work remains for this viewer, remove
the task from that viewer's current queue without claiming underlying terminal
state or revealing hidden residue. The owner SHALL retain unresolved work and
reconciliation; no per-viewer task identity is introduced.

**C09 — Durable business idempotency.** Identical retry SHALL recover the original
outcome under current disclosure rights, never reevaluate the latest group.
Changed payload with the same key SHALL conflict. Different request keys,
workers or native/workflow paths SHALL NOT duplicate coverage of the same
gift-purpose effect. After a terminal task, or when an old task survives solely
for a different independent undertaking, a genuinely new eligible uncovered gift
may create the next current compatible task under C02; old covered members
SHALL NOT recur.

A delayed source-qualified originally live occurrence may have a gift date
before the earlier task closed; resolve its still-uncovered purpose into the
current compatible task, or create that task under C02, preserving its actual
source date and the closed receipt unchanged. C05 still excludes historical-only
acquisition; newly uncovered work does not mean a newly dated gift.

**C10 — Honest human completion.** Use ADR-0183's closed Independent follow-up
task contract. Done/Not needed SHALL record only the declared human task
disposition over the reviewed set. Neither SHALL invent interaction, send,
receipt, financial correction, enrollment, consent or visibility. No narrative
report SHALL be required. Not needed SHALL NOT permanently suppress future
distinct special gifts or borrow D19's lifetime-first scope.

**C11 — Source correction and independent promises.** Qualified correction SHALL
update/withdraw only the invalid automatic member/reason, preserving source and
human history and separately promised work/P27 next-action obligations. A stale
review SHALL refresh to remaining valid work, not remain blocked forever.
A qualified source correction may restore the same still-unconsumed originally
live gift-purpose under current eligibility, retaining withdrawal/correction
lineage; the earlier withdrawal need not have been erroneous. Replay, historical
reclassification or a criterion-version change alone SHALL NOT re-admit handled
or historical work.

**C12 — Conditional Undo.** Correcting the latest mistaken disposition SHALL
revalidate actor authority, member/source state, responsibility and intervening
handling. Restore only eligible covered members through the current-group owner:
reuse the same task when appropriate; if a compatible current task already
exists, restore there and preserve the original task and correction lineage.
Never reopen a second current copy. Conflicting handling or unsafe scope SHALL
return a truthful conflict and the existing qualified correction/handoff path.
Undo SHALL NOT alter money, actual interactions or sending; no general task-merge
framework or arbitrary new Undo expiry is required.

**C13 — Deliberate communication.** Contact actions SHALL use current authorized
contact channels and P6 eligibility. D15 sending SHALL retain separate deliberate
authorization, exact intended recipient/content and actual provider outcome.
Group changes SHALL NOT expand a draft or retry a message. Optional P9 interaction
recording SHALL show its audience and actual occurrence and cover only explicitly
named compatible work. A rejected task command SHALL NOT erase a real interaction.

**C14 — Tenant and disclosure invariants.** All task/member/source/receipt/history
relationships SHALL be tenant-aware, with non-null trusted Tenant/actor ownership,
composite reference checks and immutable scope fields. Subject, recipient and
Legal Entity compatibility SHALL be validated at the authoritative boundary.
Counts, source links, logs, notifications, exports, caches, Realtime and histories
SHALL enforce the same current disclosure limits. No all-tenant/organization
total, inaccessible member count or reason for restriction SHALL leak through a
group projection. Missing rights SHALL never become granted by grouping or handoff.

**C15 — Supabase enforcement.** Coarse tenant RLS SHALL backstop the governing P12
resolver and qualified commands; no competing SQL permission engine SHALL be
introduced. Effective grants, SELECT, UPDATE USING/WITH CHECK, INSERT checks,
views, function ownership/EXECUTE, RPCs, security-definer search paths and
service-role callers SHALL be qualified together. Browser/alternate generic
writers SHALL NOT mutate membership, trusted fields, coverage, contract kind or
audit history. Both old-row eligibility and new-row invariants SHALL hold. A
generic row update SHALL NOT transfer task responsibility/member coverage, spoof
an actor or relabel generated work; explicit owner-qualified handoff/correction
commands SHALL enforce current authority, trusted context and retained lineage.

**C16 — Data shape and retention.** Required identities, types, versions, status
combinations, unique member/effect and one-current-task constraints SHALL be
database-enforced wherever expressible. Cross-row decisions SHALL occur in one
qualified transaction with deterministic bounded locking. Source/correction and
disposition histories SHALL be append-only under their owners; mutable projections
SHALL be distinguishable. Deletes SHALL NOT cascade away gift/history evidence.
Avoid copying donor PII or money into generic metadata. Display money only from
qualified owner amounts/currency; no float, new arithmetic ledger or mixed sum.
Retention/anonymization SHALL follow existing owners and reconcile retained
references, backups and restores without recreating obligations.

**C17 — Explicit bounded review.** The ordinary small group SHALL show its gift
list and scoped action without per-gift selection ceremony. A quiet optional
Choose gifts action SHALL allow a deliberate nonempty subset when Maya handled
only some gifts; the included count/action SHALL update, with unselected gifts
remaining current. Larger review sets
SHALL be bounded, stable, inspectable and explicitly counted; page and action
scope SHALL never be confused. An explicit whole reviewed-set action may cover
its named N gifts across inspectable pages; merely loading a page SHALL NOT
select every matching gift. If resource limits require a smaller review set,
state N of M permitted gifts and leave the remainder in the same task. Failed or
partial loading SHALL NOT silently redefine the set. Limits and their boundary
behavior SHALL be release-qualified, not invented from an unmeasured large label.

**C18 — Truthful, accessible presentation.** Use the existing shared design
system, clear supporter/ministry/reason and source-qualified per-gift details.
Never imply organization-first giving, all giving, receipt issuance or message
delivery. Keep reviewed content stable during benign arrivals; announce scoped
success, conflict, uncertainty and residue accessibly without focus theft or
color dependence. Support mobile reflow, keyboard/screen reader, international
names, localized currency/dates and explicit time-zone interpretation. Clear
protected content on revocation/tenant switch and reject stale response generations.

**C19 — Privacy and minimization.** Grouping SHALL NOT infer an undisclosed DAF
advisor, turn every credit into a recipient, fan out to households/members or
override church-member correspondence exclusions. Store only necessary qualified
references/evidence. General logs, alerts, coach summaries, task titles, calendar
feeds and AI context SHALL NOT inherit gift details from this decision. Any
existing permitted derivative SHALL be separately minimized and authorized.

**C20 — Simple architecture and bounded work.** Extend existing source commands,
shared tasks, product receipts and recovery infrastructure. No new dependency,
configurable grouping/time-window engine, second task inbox, mandatory report,
automatic donor communication or cross-currency calculation is required. Index
tenant/scope/current membership and exact effect lookups; avoid financial-history
scans or N+1 requalification per rendered row. Resource bounds SHALL fail visibly
and preserve valid work rather than truncate coverage.

**C21 — Recovery and observability.** Durable business history SHALL record exact
members, relevant source versions, actor, disposition, correction lineage and
outcome separately from technical traces. Lost responses SHALL recover receipts;
failed secondary projection/notification SHALL retry its own effect, never redo
human completion. Operations SHALL have an existing authorized repair route for
unmaterialized, invalid, duplicate or unavailable work without direct database
editing. Monitoring SHALL follow the signals, thresholds, owners and responses
below; logs SHALL exclude unnecessary donor data.

**C22 — Safe migration and activation.** Enable grouping only after owner commands,
constraints, old-writer fencing, safe projections and receipt recovery are
qualified. Backfill source-qualified provenance without inferring coverage from
titles/status alone or replaying history. Collisions SHALL retain every old
outcome and independent obligation; unresolved cases SHALL be assigned through
existing exceptions. Mixed versions SHALL fail safely; disable new admission/
commands independently without reverting authoritative records or deleting new
receipts. Roll forward for written data; rehearse backup restore and replay.

**C23 — Verification and measurable release evidence.** P01–P30 below SHALL trace
to owner/OpenSpec requirements, design, implementation and release evidence when
those artifacts are authorized. Actual command, P12, migrations, browser, source
correction, accessibility, load and recovery proofs SHALL be required. The
isolated fixtures and desk research SHALL NOT be labeled production qualification.

**C24 — Settings and preserved boundaries.** This decision SHALL NOT add a tenant
switch for Independent versus source-controlled completion, an amount criterion,
grouping age window, new history right or mandatory interaction reporting.
Existing native/workflow purpose coordination and organization permissions SHALL
remain authoritative. The old dashboard document supplies context only; obsolete
stack/dependency instructions SHALL NOT be imported.

## Lifecycle and ownership synthesis

There are three distinct facts: source eligibility of a gift-purpose member,
whether that member's human work was handled, and whether its shared task still
has work. Do not encode all three in one boolean or task status.

| Transition                                                                                        | Valid effect and forbidden shortcut                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Qualified live occurrence → admitted member                                                       | Existing purpose identity joins one compatible current task, or creates the next current task. Source may commit before projection; durable intent repairs delay. No raw webhook/credit-row task creation.                                            |
| Pending member → reviewed                                                                         | Read current authorized exact scope and relevant versions. Review is not handling, source qualification or a new access grant.                                                                                                                        |
| Reviewed set → Done / Not needed                                                                  | Atomic exact outcome if current checks pass. Same task remains for residue; no update of “all current members.”                                                                                                                                       |
| Selected material change → conflict                                                               | No new human coverage from the rejected command. Source correction proceeds independently; refresh offers valid work. No silent substitute/skip.                                                                                                      |
| Only unrelated member arrives                                                                     | Existing reviewed set may finish; new member remains. No unnecessary global-head conflict or retroactive coverage expansion.                                                                                                                          |
| Last required work resolved → terminal task                                                       | Retain receipt/history. A later distinct eligible occurrence may start the next current task; old effects never recur from replay.                                                                                                                    |
| Source invalidates unhandled automatic reason                                                     | Qualified withdrawal preserves provenance and independent promises. Unknown/inaccessible is not automatic withdrawal and no hidden count is shown.                                                                                                    |
| Withdrawn originally live unconsumed member or mistaken latest disposition → qualified correction | Restore only through respective source/task owner, current proof and current-group resolver. Prior source withdrawal may have been correct at its time. Retain lineage; no arbitrary second open group, historical acquisition or communication undo. |
| Handoff/identity reconciliation                                                                   | Reconcile existing scope through owners, no new access or copied private notes. If collisions arise, exact effects/obligations survive; current authorized resolution cannot guess from display names.                                                |

An inactive/stale source projection must not leave falsely actionable work. The
owner can make that member unavailable and route unresolved qualification through
its existing exception path. Maya's authorized view must not disclose that hidden
members exist. Valid reviewed work can still finish where the owner can prove
its exact scope; neither the UI nor the command may claim a whole group is done
by counting only visible rows. Admission should avoid incompatible disclosure
contexts, and later policy changes require owner reconciliation rather than a
new task per viewer or permanently blocked queue.
If no authorized actionable residue remains, remove the viewer's current queue
projection and return the permitted scoped outcome. Do not expose an empty
still-open card, hidden remaining count or a false claim that the underlying
task is complete. Independently permitted historical receipts remain accessible
through their own authority.

One actual interaction can satisfy compatible obligations only with explicit
qualified evidence. Merely grouping gifts cannot close an unrelated first-support
task, partnership promise, receipt or financial exception. This is the scoped
extension to carry into P9/ADR-0183 and P28 later; their existing independent
task authority is preserved. Zendesk-style best-effort skips, finance batch
confirmations and generic SQL-only authorization are not imported over that
decision.

## Falsifiable acceptance and release proofs

**P01.** Two qualified distinct special gifts for the exact compatible scope
produce one current task with two distinguishable source references; one scoped
human outcome covers both, with no send/interaction/financial side effect.

**P02.** Same supporter in different tenants, entities, typed ministries,
responsibility scopes or incompatible purposes cannot be coalesced or disclosed.

**P03.** Multiple same-subject allocation/credit/reason/event rows of one gift
converge to one member; two equal-date/equal-amount gifts remain two members.

**P04.** Legal donor, DAF sponsor/advisor, household/member, organization contact
and recognized supporter retain owner-qualified distinct routes; no inferred
recipient or prohibited member correspondence is created.

**P05.** USD/JPY and split/fee-covered gifts show only permitted exact source
meaning and currencies; no hidden allocation, blended recognition or mixed sum
appears in UI, accessible text, notification, export or log.

**P06.** Coherent P13/P14/P16 qualification handles processing/settled/returned,
credit lag, advance/catch-up, commitment reduction and missing history without
inventing special-gift eligibility. A source cursor alone cannot bypass another
owner's material revision.

**P07.** Review G1/G2; admit G3; finish succeeds only for G1/G2, retains G3 in the
same task, and displays the exact successful outcome plus permitted residue.

**P08.** Finish G1/G2 before later G3 admission; G3 creates the next current task.
Race both orders and prove one current compatible task and no covered G3.
Also admit an originally live delayed G3 whose actual source date predates
closure: preserve its date and the closed receipt, and retain its uncovered work.

**P09.** Correct, reallocate or withdraw selected G1 before finish; the whole old
review rejects with no new handled member. Refresh can finish remaining valid
G2. Reverse race order preserves accepted human history then source correction.

**P10.** Change unrelated descriptive metadata or add unselected G3 without
changing relevant selected evidence; do not gratuitously reject valid completion.
Change selected eligibility/recipient/purpose and require refresh.

**P11.** Two tabs/keys attempt the same reviewed disposition concurrently; one
durable covered outcome exists. Conflicting outcome or changed payload returns
truthful already-handled/conflict, never duplicate history or an interaction.

**P12.** Lose the response after G1/G2 commit; admit G3; retry/reload recovers only
the original result and leaves G3 untouched. A key cannot be rebound to a new set.

**P13.** Concurrent admissions of distinct gifts share the one compatible task;
duplicate same-gift admissions from different transport/native/workflow paths
produce one semantic member across open and closed task generations.

**P14.** Revoke or hand off before acceptance/read; deny stale action and stale
disclosure. Reverse ordering retains the accepted pre-revocation outcome without
granting continued access. A→B→A responsibility does not validate an old review.

**P15.** Direct REST/table, RPC/view, privileged worker and alternative UI paths
cannot spoof Tenant/actor/source, move scope, clear generated provenance, mutate
coverage or bypass P12. Test both USING and WITH CHECK, grants and function owners
against the actual migrated schema, not only an administrator connection.

**P16.** Filtered member rows do not leave hidden totals, names, source links,
timing/detail errors, stale caches, Realtime payloads or old-tenant responses.
Unknown required work cannot be silently counted as zero.
Specifically finish visible G1 while inaccessible G2 remains: no empty card,
open-status clue, remaining-work indicator or receipt recovery may disclose G2,
while its owner's unresolved work is retained.

**P17.** Not needed applies to the reviewed gift-purpose set. Replay of those
gifts does not recur; a genuinely distinct later special gift remains eligible.
No lifetime-supporter suppression or D19 purpose collision occurs.

**P18.** Source withdrawal preserves an independently promised call and P27
next-action obligation. Merely reading/claiming/commenting does not fabricate
that promise. An owned exception remains actionable without financial corruption.
The surviving call cannot block or silently absorb a later incompatible
special-gift group. A source-qualified later restoration of the same originally
live unconsumed member retains lineage even if its prior withdrawal was correct;
historical reclassification or handled-member replay creates no new work.

**P19.** Latest eligible Done/Not needed Undo restores only its exact members;
with newer compatible work it retains one current task and old lineage. Unsafe
authority, intervening handling or source changes conflict. Actual interactions,
messages and money remain unchanged. This is not proved by the current fixture.

**P20.** Source alias/Party merge, subject correction and reassignment reconcile
duplicate semantic claims and independent obligations without exposing former
private notes, replaying handled gifts or guessing the surviving owner.

**P21.** Deliberate sending records actual D15/P6 outcomes; group arrivals cannot
expand draft scope or trigger retry. Optional interaction recording makes its
audience explicit, survives task conflict and never becomes mandatory reporting.

**P22.** Empty, loading, error, restricted and zero-remaining states are distinct.
Scoped success remains accessible and persistent; duplicate clicks, navigation,
refresh and low-bandwidth interruption recover the same result without lost work.

**P23.** At the declared page/review limits and one below/above each, the exact
set/count matches the action. Paging/reordering/partial load does not change it;
remaining work stays current. Keyboard and screen-reader users can inspect and
understand the same scope as pointer users.
Choose gifts can finish just G1 while G2 remains, without forcing selection
controls into the ordinary all-included journey.

**P24.** Test mobile reflow, 200% text, keyboard/focus, screen reader status,
contrast and target-size requirements; include long/RTL names, same-day gifts,
zero-decimal currencies and source dates versus localized action timestamps.

**P25.** Before release, conduct an initial qualitative session with at least
six actual missionaries/staff spanning new/experienced users and including at
least two primarily phone-based journeys; recruit assistive-technology users for
the accessibility journey. Every participant must correctly identify the covered
gifts and that Finish does not send. Any critical misunderstanding blocks release
and prompts redesign/retest. This is a proposed small qualitative gate, not a
statistically representative UX-performance claim.

**P26.** Inject failure before admission commit, after durable intent, during
projection, after disposition commit and before secondary notification. Replay
recovers the intended single effect through receipts and existing repair tools.

**P27.** Publish a release load profile: largest supported task/member counts,
review maximum, page size, concurrent tenants/commands, data skew, transaction

silent truncation. Product and platform owners sign the measured report before

**P28.** Old/new code-schema combinations cannot use generic completion/delete
or relabel group provenance. Provenance backfill, ambiguous legacy task outcomes,
collisions, activation and kill switches preserve historical evidence and assigned
exceptions. Revert UI without reverting accepted data; rehearse restore/replay.

**P29.** Retention/anonymization/export/backup-restore and secret/log inspections
preserve required evidence while minimizing protected facts. Authorized correction
is attributable; ordinary actors cannot rewrite attribution/history.

**P30.** Trace D20→glossary→owner amendments/ADRs→OpenSpec→design/tasks→authorized
tickets→implementation/tests→release evidence. Reconcile terms, task versus
