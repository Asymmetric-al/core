# D22 evidence — precise per-gift amount basis

Research date: **2026-10-17, Asia/Bangkok**. The founder selected optional tenant
amount-based follow-up and emphasized optional, customizable controls without
complexity. The precise basis and safeguards below are review recommendations,
not separately ratified financial policy or implementation. Only this research
note is authored. No financial/provider/database operation or runtime test ran.

## Recommended permanent contract

Use **one source-qualified amount for one business contribution, one permitted
follow-up Party and one exact typed ministry subject**, compared with the
organization's positive threshold in that same currency. Recommend the fixed,
plain-language comparator **“at least” (`>=`)**. No numerical default is supplied;
empty or Off is not a zero threshold. This is a fixed amount policy, not D21's
rejected comparison with the supporter's past giving.

The minimal source measure is the gift's **current effective monetary support
for this supporter and ministry**, excluding fee-cover and unrelated allocations,
before processor fees or P21 assessments. “Effective” includes owner-confirmed
refunds, returns, reversals and allocation corrections. Avoid labeling this
“net income,” “available support” or “net payout.” It is not a field-account
balance, receipt deductible amount, pledge, expectancy or noncash valuation.

Because the legal donor and recognized supporter can differ, use a **closed,
source-selected profile**, not an unlabeled amount that mixes vocabularies:

| Profile                     | Exact amount and owner                                                                                                                                                                                      | Required boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Direct legal support        | P13's sanctioned effective fold over each distinct eligible non-fee-cover monetary designation line of this contribution bound to this exact ministry subject, counted once.                                | The qualified follow-up Party is the legal-donor/supporter route; unrelated header lines never participate. Multiple relevant lines within the same gift are one source amount, not separate threshold tests.              |
| Eligible recognized support | P14's named source projection supplies the exact amount recognized through the permitted support-attribution route for this Party, contribution and ministry, backed by eligible effective monetary source. | Persist/display recognition vocabulary and via-route. Do not add this to legal giving or select arbitrary positive credit roles. P14 owns scope, role deduplication and attribution; P28 must not recreate its arithmetic. |

The second profile does not authorize any additional recognition recipients or
correspondence. An organization contact, solicitor, tribute honoree, derived
spouse, anonymous advisor or church member does not become a recipient merely
because a credit row has a value. The already-qualified P28/P14 purpose route
must permit the exact follow-up. Existing church-member correspondence exclusion
and hidden-recipient protections remain.

**A generic header amount or generic recognition total is insufficient.** For
example, a large sponsor/bulk contribution to the ministry must not trigger the
rule for every person credited with only a small portion. Likewise, a partial
header-scoped recognition amount spanning several designations/fee-cover cannot
be allocated by P28's own `MIN`, proportion or first-line guess. P14 must certify
the exact permitted supporter/ministry amount through its named interface.
Its MAX-per-(Party, header) rule prevents adding roles together; that does not
authorize an unrelated solicitor/contact role to supply an acknowledgment basis.
The profile must prove appropriate role and scope coverage, not merely read the
largest available credit. If exact attribution is unavailable, hold only that
affected route with an owned qualification outcome; keep money posting and other
independently qualified routes usable. This is a concrete bounded P14/P28 adapter
obligation, not a new attribution algorithm or an indefinite placeholder.

A single effective threshold for a currency applies to the qualified amount;
the caller or tenant does not choose header versus net/recognition/cash arithmetic.
“Customizable” permits the approved policy controls, not redefinition of financial
truth. The broader settings/permission contract is owned by the D22 governance
review. No per-donor thresholds, cumulative totals, lookback, percent-of-commitment,
currency conversion, priority ladder or general rules engine is inferred here.

## Money precision, identity and comparisons

One P13 header is one contribution and has one currency. Multiple allocations,
credits, criterion reasons, payment/webhook events and revisions are evidence,
not new gifts. Test the single owner-certified per-gift/Party/subject amount;
never add two gifts merely because they join the same D20 task. Employer match
and employee gift are separate received contributions; matching expectancy is
neither received money nor recognition.

Use exact integer minor units and the canonical currency exponent. The policy
input must reject unsupported precision/range instead of rounding into a different
qualification result. The actual current money boundary uses safe integers and
rejects precision loss; any later wider adapter needs its own qualification.
Do not run comparisons through binary floating-point major-unit values or a
lossy JSON number. Check finite/positive amount, exact currency and the supported
source profile before enabling a threshold. Canonical currency metadata alone
does not enable a payment or support profile.

A follow-up threshold is **not a charge or payout instruction**. Provider minimum,
maximum or amount-multiple constraints do not automatically govern an offline
or differently qualified source amount. Do not impose Stripe's representation
constraints on every threshold or silently reduce a three/four-decimal canonical
unit to two decimals. Conversely, metadata for a currency is not proof that the
amount rule has a qualified live source in that currency. Currency conversion,
processor settlement currency and donor presentment remain different facts.

## Captured policy and current financial truth

The proposed permanent policy lifecycle agreed for this review is:

1. **Capture the applicable policy at the authoritative originally-live source
   eligibility boundary.** This includes immutable policy identity/version,
   exact currency/threshold/comparator/basis and the activation generation. The
   source proves the ordering against policy changes; caller timestamps,
   original gift date, webhook arrival and delayed worker lookup do not prove it.
2. **Threshold edits are prospective.** An already captured, still-unadmitted
   eligible-live candidate in the same uninterrupted enabled generation retains
   its captured rule while source/recipient qualification finishes. An admitted
   reason retains its captured policy evidence. Neither silently switches to
   the latest threshold because materialization/reconciliation ran later.
3. **Off stops new admission and ends the generation for held/unadmitted
   candidates.** Already accepted exact amount reasons plus durable task intent
   survive and materialize once, including queued projections. Existing follow-ups
   remain. Re-enable starts a new generation and cannot revive earlier unadmitted
   candidates or sweep historical gifts.
4. **Current source truth and authority remain mandatory.** Current effective
   money, attribution, subject, recipient and permission can invalidate an
   accepted reason. Recheck its captured threshold against current qualified
   financial evidence; do not freeze the original amount as current truth.
   D20 correction, independent undertakings, actual interaction history and exact
   reviewed-set coverage remain authoritative. Turning Off alone is not a refund,
   source invalidation, blanket task cancellation or human Not needed outcome.

The UI must explain: **“Changes apply to new eligible gifts. Existing and queued
follow-ups remain.”** Threshold editing and Off have the distinct unadmitted
candidate semantics above; the source/command design must prove them, not hide
them behind an eventual settings cache.

The original source boundary must be defined for each qualified tender/profile.
ACH processing is not eligible received money. A gift that first reaches the
profile's approved received/support finality after enabling can be a new live
eligible occurrence; a previously eligible gift delivered late cannot borrow
the new policy. Do not decide this from an original gift date or choose a new
universal settlement rule. P13's existing provisional/support policy remains
authoritative. Recognition lag or repair preserves original source-live lineage
and policy capture; it does not block financial posting.

Source admission, its required local evidence and durable identifier-only task
intent commit together. The existing shared task owner controls operational
membership and reviewed-set disposition/coverage. Preserve only one intended
gift-purpose effect across native and later workflow routes. Several qualifying
reasons may explain that same member, but do not create several members or tasks.
Removing the amount reason cannot remove a still-valid first-support or other
distinct obligation. A source-qualified correction may restore an originally
live unconsumed previously admitted purpose under D20, including while Off,
after genuine source withdrawal and under current source/authority. This does
not revive a never-admitted candidate from an ended generation. No new effect
identity comes from policy version, amount crossing or replay.

## Concerns — eight dimensions each

### FB22-01 — Header or physical line substitutes for the qualified gift amount

1. **Harm:** unrelated ministry money triggers a task, or several same-subject lines never qualify despite one sufficiently large gift.
2. **Severity:** high financial/relationship correctness harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high if raw header totals or per-line predicates are convenient inputs.
4. **Cause:** confusing business contribution identity with its allocation evidence.
5. **Evidence:** S02:784, 791–823; S09; S10 D20 C03/C05.
6. **Implication:** compare one certified per-contribution/Party/subject amount, preserving exact allocation coverage.
7. **Clause:** “The amount criterion SHALL compare the owner-certified current effective relevant amount for one contribution, qualified Party and exact typed ministry subject. Distinct relevant lines SHALL be counted once within that gift; unrelated allocation and fee-cover SHALL be excluded. A header, line, credit or event identifier alone SHALL not supply the amount contract.”
8. **Proof:** two same-subject lines individually below but together at threshold; split gift above threshold only when unrelated lines are added; duplicate line join; hidden allocation; fee-cover alone crosses threshold; equal amounts on distinct contributions remain distinct.

### FB22-02 — A sponsor/batch gift or mixed recognition value triggers every person

1. **Harm:** a small attributed supporter is thanked for someone else's large gift, or prohibited recipients are inferred.
2. **Severity:** high, potentially critical for protected identity/contact disclosure.
3. **Likelihood:** high with header-based indirect gifts or generic positive-credit selection.
4. **Cause:** collapsing legal money, support attribution, role and recipient authority.
5. **Evidence:** S05; S06:432–468, 1058–1069; S07.
6. **Implication:** direct and eligible indirect profiles remain typed, source-selected and purpose-qualified; no mixed measure.
7. **Clause:** “Indirect qualification SHALL use the exact P14-certified permitted supporter/ministry amount for this contribution, labeled as recognition and never added to legal money. P28 SHALL NOT apportion partial header recognition, substitute a batch total, select the largest unrelated credit role, or infer a recipient. Ambiguous scope SHALL hold only the affected route through its owner.”
8. **Proof:** large batch with small employee attribution; DAF partial header credit across ministries; fee-cover in header; multiple roles; full-to-each spouse recognition without recipient entitlement; anonymous advisor; via-church member; legitimate exact DAF route still works.

### FB22-03 — Precision, FX or provider limits alter the intended threshold

1. **Harm:** boundary gifts qualify incorrectly or legitimate configured values are silently rounded/rejected under an unrelated payment rail.
2. **Severity:** high deterministic correctness harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high with floating major-unit arithmetic or universal two-decimal/provider assumptions.
4. **Cause:** treating policy comparison as payment creation or currency metadata as feature availability.
5. **Evidence:** S02:818; S03:1860–1864; S13.
6. **Implication:** exact supported source units and ranges; fixed understandable inclusive comparison; no FX/default amount.
7. **Clause:** “Thresholds SHALL be positive exact canonical minor-unit values in an explicitly supported source currency. Compare `qualified_amount_minor >= threshold_minor` without rounding or conversion. Reject unsupported precision/range. Empty/Off SHALL not mean zero. Provider charge/payout constraints SHALL not be universal policy bounds, and metadata SHALL not enable an unqualified currency route.”
8. **Proof:** below/equal/above by one minor unit; zero/negative/NaN/infinity; JPY and BHD precision; representable threshold differing from a provider charge multiple; safe-integer boundary; metadata-only currency; mixed currency; no default USD or inferred FX.

### FB22-04 — Current money is replaced by captured gross, payout or assessment value

1. **Harm:** reversed support remains eligible, or fees/assessments unpredictably change donor-care qualification.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** high if a convenient gross/net field is used without a named source measure.
4. **Cause:** confusing current effective gift allocation with original payment, deductible receipt value or spendable funds.
5. **Evidence:** S02:789–818; S03; S04; S08:1027–1031.
6. **Implication:** effective monetary gift support before fees/assessments, with current source correction, under the exact approved finality profile.
7. **Clause:** “The rule SHALL use current effective qualified monetary support, excluding fee-cover and before processor/P21 assessments. Processing, pledge/expectancy, noncash valuation, payout and account balance SHALL not substitute. Partial/full reversal or reallocation SHALL requalify the captured criterion through the owner; ambiguous correction coverage SHALL not be guessed.”
8. **Proof:** partial refund stays above versus drops below; full return; reallocation away; unchanged gift with increased assessment; fee-cover refund; ACH processing/success/provisional eligibility; check/NSF; noncash valuation; uncertain partial application correction.

### FB22-05 — Worker time chooses a new policy for an old live occurrence

1. **Harm:** retries, delayed recognition or enabling a rule create inconsistent/historical work.
2. **Severity:** high temporal/data-integrity harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high if evaluation simply reads latest settings when an event arrives.
4. **Cause:** no source-qualified policy capture and activation-generation ordering.
5. **Evidence:** S04 gift-date/finality; S10 D20 C05/C09/C11; S11 atomic source commands.
6. **Implication:** capture exact policy at authoritative original-live eligibility; retain version for held/admitted work as specified.
7. **Clause:** “Applicable policy SHALL be bound to the qualified original-live source boundary with provable ordering against activation/edit/Off. Gift date, arrival time and worker lookup SHALL not choose it. Within the same enabled generation, held candidates retain captured policy through qualification delay; numeric edits apply prospectively. Historical-only records SHALL not acquire a new policy by late delivery.”
8. **Proof:** source occurrence before/after edit; same clock instant with serialized ordering; delayed recognition; late webhook; stale worker; historical import after enable; previously processing gift first reaches approved eligibility after enable; lost event recovered without a new policy.

### FB22-06 — Off deletes accepted work or re-enable resurrects abandoned candidates

1. **Harm:** accepted tasks vanish, queued intent is lost, or old unadmitted gifts flood the queue after re-enable.
2. **Severity:** high operational/history harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high if Off is a bulk cancellation or mutable Boolean checked without admission lineage.
4. **Cause:** conflating source admission, candidate generation and asynchronous task materialization.
5. **Evidence:** S10 D20 C04/C05/C11; S11; explicitly recommended D22 lifecycle above.
6. **Implication:** Off stops new admissions, preserving accepted exact reasons/intents; ended generations remain ended.
7. **Clause:** “Off SHALL fence new amount-purpose admission and end the generation for held/unadmitted candidates. Already accepted exact reasons and durable task intents SHALL survive and materialize once under current source/authority. Re-enable SHALL not revive old unadmitted generations. Threshold edits and Off SHALL not silently record human Done/Not needed or erase other obligations.”
8. **Proof:** admission commits before Off and materialization follows; Off wins before admission; held candidate then Off/re-enable; threshold edit without Off; duplicate replay; another reason or deliberate undertaking survives; current source invalidation still withdraws only its affected requirement.

### FB22-07 — Customization expands into cumulative or overlapping policies

1. **Harm:** grouped small gifts qualify as one large gift, parallel criteria duplicate tasks, or unsupported rule combinations become a second policy engine.
2. **Severity:** high scope/data-integrity harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high if “fully customizable” is interpreted as unrestricted predicates or threshold tiers.
4. **Cause:** confusing allowed settings with financial measure/identity ownership.
5. **Evidence:** S01; S10 D20 C01–C04/C09; S12.
6. **Implication:** one organization-wide policy with explicit currency amounts, applied independently to each qualified source scope; no invented overrides or sums.
7. **Clause:** “D22 SHALL not infer cumulative giving, per-period thresholds, multiple-gift sums, percentages, donor-specific tiers or arbitrary Boolean rules. Resolve one governing policy through the approved settings owner. Several legitimate reasons for the same gift-purpose SHALL converge on one membership; policy/version/rule count SHALL not multiply the effect or suppress distinct first-support/other obligations.”
8. **Proof:** two below-threshold gifts in one D20 task remain below individually; employer/employee contributions are separate; overlapping amount/above-commitment reasons yield one same-purpose member; native/workflow duplication; competing configuration versions; no per-policy replay identity.

### FB22-08 — Amount qualification is mislabeled extra support or donor intent

1. **Harm:** ordinary installments are represented as exceptional generosity, above-commitment money or a new pledge.
2. **Severity:** high relationship/meaning harm.
3. **Likelihood:** high when all qualifying reasons share a generic “special/extra” explanation.
4. **Cause:** treating the amount policy as a financial or psychological classification.
5. **Evidence:** S01; S08:1005–1031, 1393; S12:1239; ratified D21.
6. **Implication:** explain the exact amount-based reason without asserting another meaning.
7. **Clause:** “A qualifying amount reason SHALL state the organization rule, exact permitted amount/currency and source meaning. It SHALL not claim extra support, generosity, unusual history, pledge fulfillment or donor instruction. Ordinary installments may meet this amount rule without changing P16 meaning. Commitment reduction without a new qualifying gift SHALL not create a new amount occurrence.”
8. **Proof:** normal annual/quarterly installment meets rule; catch-up/advance gift; no commitment; reduced pledge with unchanged money; user reviews legal versus recognized basis; D21 comparison alone still creates no attention; no automatic sending.

### FB22-09 — Current-source changes rewrite prior financial or human evidence

1. **Harm:** completed interactions disappear, old coverage expands or corrections repeatedly create thanks.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** high if eligibility Boolean doubles as task state/history.
4. **Cause:** conflating captured policy evidence, current source fold and immutable reviewed outcome.
5. **Evidence:** S02/S05 correction contracts; S10 D20 C07–C12; S11.
6. **Implication:** retain all three truths separately; current source changes use D20 exact reconciliation.
7. **Clause:** “Original policy/source admission evidence and actual human outcomes SHALL remain. Current source changes SHALL update or withdraw only the affected generated amount reason, preserving independent undertakings and other valid reasons. Restoring a qualified originally live unconsumed previously admitted purpose after genuine source withdrawal SHALL reuse its identity and lineage, including while Off under current source/authority; this SHALL NOT revive never-admitted candidates from ended generations. Reviewed material changes require refresh; handled coverage SHALL not recur from source/rule replay.”
8. **Proof:** refund before versus after Done; two reviewed gifts with one corrected; unrelated new gift remains residual; valid prior withdrawal later repaired; changed Party attribution; source date corrected; manual promised call retained; original accepted receipt remains truthful under later threshold changes.

### FB22-10 — Restricted amounts, source gaps or upstream gates are bypassed

1. **Harm:** threshold success exposes a hidden amount/recipient, or P28 bypasses financial ownership to make the rule work.
2. **Severity:** high, potentially critical for restricted ministry/donor disclosure.
3. **Likelihood:** medium/high if internal qualification is assumed to authorize its explanation.
4. **Cause:** collapsing source access, task audience, policy capability and financial correction authority.
5. **Evidence:** S06/S07 missionary restrictions; S09; S11 E7/C-01; S10 D20 current disclosure.
6. **Implication:** source profile and projection require real qualification; capability/configuration is no new access right.
7. **Clause:** “Current source/field/purpose authority SHALL govern qualification effects, amounts, counts, explanations and recovered receipts. A rule hit SHALL not disclose a protected amount or recipient. E7 SHALL gate only affected unresolved temporal association and C-01 only its governed correction path. Missing P14 attribution/basis or source finality SHALL not be repaired by guessed money, new financial writes or a fictional qualified flag.”
8. **Proof:** hidden split/source amount cannot be inferred from task presence; restricted DAF identity; stale authority; current receipt after access loss; direct qualified route continues when unrelated E7 association is blocked; no C-01 bypass; exact unavailable-route recovery without global financial shutdown.

## Proof and delivery limit

The direct and indirect measure profiles, their original-live policy capture,
permission/currentness fences and exact source exclusions must be qualified
before activation. No existing generic amount field or the current money parser
proves those contracts. The D22 database probe may seed an approved amount/basis
and test policy/admission mechanics; that cannot establish actual P13/P14 money,
recipient attribution, finality, source-time ordering or production authorization.

The recommended boundary supplies one precise per-gift comparator and finite
source profiles without a second ledger or a configurable financial formula.
Optional settings do not choose currency conversions, history comparison,
source policy, financial correction permission or additional recipient rights.
All proof cases above are future qualification obligations, not executed results.
