# D22 — Versioned amount policy, original live evidence and durable admission

Research only, **October 17, 2026 UTC / Asia/Bangkok**. The founder selected an optional tenant amount rule with understandable customization. The lifecycle and safeguards below are proposed review amendments, not a final schema, production implementation or newly ratified financial criterion.

**29/29 bounded PostgreSQL 17.11 outcomes passed, including seven real controlled two-session races and one expected unsafe rounding counterexample.** The container and anonymous volume were removed; absence was verified at `2026-10-17T04:21:12.848971+00:00` (`11:21:12 +07:00`). [Probe artifacts](postgres-probe/README.md) retain SQL, actual roles, commands, outputs, source/artifact hashes and cleanup. This is synthetic mechanism evidence, not Core, hosted Supabase, actual P12/financial qualification, privacy, user or load testing.

## The policy boundary the probe assumes

The minimal proposed comparator is **at least** (`>=`) a positive exact minor-unit threshold for the gift's admitted currency. No cross-tenant or currency default, FX, financial-risk threshold, donor-history comparison or source amount formula is invented. The financial owner must certify the exact business-gift, recipient, typed ministry/Legal Entity and eligible amount basis. The fixture receives a **prequalified subject amount**; it does not compute allocations, recognition, fees, assessments, installments, commitments or money.

The policy has two separate temporal concerns:

- **Threshold edit:** prospective rule selection. Original live source occurrence lineage resolves an immutable applicable policy revision. A delayed worker cannot substitute its latest policy. A held, never-admitted occurrence within the same uninterrupted enabled generation can qualify under its captured rule when its source evidence becomes sufficient. Current source/access still matters.
- **Off:** immediate fence on **new admission**, ending that enabled generation. Never-admitted or held old-generation candidates cannot be revived merely by turning the rule on again. **Already committed exact amount-reason admission plus durable task intent is existing work:** it remains and can materialize once after Off. Numeric edits and Off do not bulk-retire accepted reasons, erase history or disable other native/manual work.

This is the parent's reconciled proposed lifecycle. An earlier alternative—withdraw all unresolved amount reasons on Off—was rejected before the fixture was written. The user-facing control must say that existing/queued follow-ups remain. An accepted intent is not the same as a worker having observed a candidate or queued an unqualified event.

Current source correction, access loss, independently promised work and D20's precise handling remain authoritative. In particular, previously admitted but genuinely source-invalidated unconsumed work may later require qualified restoration under its captured rule **while Off**; that is repair of existing admitted work, not fresh admission. **The probe does not implement or prove this restoration, completed/Not needed repair, or the full task lifecycle.** It preserves the receipt and blocks materialization after a changed source revision, leaving qualified reconciliation explicit rather than forbidding it.

## Why this warranted a small new experiment

[D20's retained 38-outcome/nine-race probe](../d20-evidence/postgres-probe/README.md) seeds qualification and proves exact reviewed group mechanics. It does not contain policy editing, original-policy selection or enabled generations. The new falsifiable question is whether a late worker, policy Save, Off or source correction can change which rule governs an original occurrence or discard already accepted work. PostgreSQL's coercion of fractional values into declared numeric scale is also a concrete, independent amount-input hazard.

The new fixture therefore tests only policy/source binding and admission/materialization mechanics. It does not repeat D20 grouping, human completion, Undo, merge or communication matrices. No financial adapter or generic rule engine was implemented. No Core schema, application, dependency, live database, provider or runtime configuration changed.

Primary facts that govern the interpretation:

- PostgreSQL coerces a declared numeric scale **before** later checks inspect the stored value. `99.5::numeric(20,0)` becomes `100`, so a check that the stored number is integral does not reject the original fractional minor unit. The unsafe counterexample succeeds as predicted; a domain over unconstrained numeric rejects the fraction before any scale coercion. This is one mechanism, not a prescription to replace Core's approved money types/parser with unlimited numeric.
- NULL may satisfy a CHECK unless explicitly constrained. Enabled policy requires a non-null positive threshold; zero is not Off. The fixture tests fractional input, NaN, zero and missing amount. Its domain also rejects infinities and negative values, but those are not separately claimed as executed cases. Production must enforce its actual supported integer/range/exponent and input parser.
- Applicable omitted `WITH CHECK` inherits `USING`; the absence of written duplication is not a bypass. Coarse Tenant predicates cannot certify an amount basis, field permission or source chronology. Grants/columns, permissive policies, SELECT rights, functions and every privileged route must be qualified together.
- Definer execution uses its actual owner's rights; neither SECURITY DEFINER nor FORCE RLS is an unconditional bypass/safety claim. PUBLIC EXECUTE, owner/BYPASSRLS behavior and trusted search paths remain relevant. The fixture's executor is a separate nonsuperuser/non-BYPASS role, not a production permission resolver.
- Read Committed row locks can serialize conflicting transitions and re-read current heads after waiting. The exact rows and lock order must fit the real owners and workload. The toy per-currency policy head lock is not a production contention or throughput recommendation.

## Executed mechanism and its limits

The fixture has **seven nonsuperuser/non-BYPASSRLS roles** and **nine forced coarse-Tenant-RLS tables**. Table owner and bounded command executor cannot log in; actual command sessions use `policy_editor`, `source_owner`, `source_worker`, `other_tenant` and `policy_revoker`. SQL-login identity and a current gate row stand in for trusted context and an already-qualified permission result. They do not model Auth/JWT, P12 grants, exact actor hats or live NHI-owner ceilings.

Ordinary callers cannot write raw policy tables. The executor has bounded read/insert/column-update/sequence grants; `policy_revoker` can update its tenant's fixture gate. Immutable versions, occurrences and accepted intents have update/delete guards. Tenant/currency/source composite references, positive enabled thresholds and exact head CAS complement coarse RLS. Reference and immutability tests run as administrator where necessary; they do not prove a privacy-safe public error surface.

**Policy Save:** checks current fixture permission, locks the exact Tenant/currency head, verifies required expected revision, appends immutable policy version plus required audit and advances the head atomically. A required-audit fault rolls it all back. Two current editors with the same expected head serialize: one wins, one returns Stale. An unchanged effective Save creates no new version or audit. **The fixture does not implement durable policy-command idempotency/result receipts for a lost Save response.** Production must use its qualified existing command-result pattern; CAS and source-admission receipts alone do not prove that endpoint.

**Original occurrence capture:** a trusted source-owner command captures the then-applicable immutable policy reference while holding the head fence. This is an explicit stand-in for the real source-owned live/effective policy-selection boundary. It is not worker arrival time, donor gift date or a timestamp heuristic. Financial source qualification and the real mapping from occurrence to policy remain unproved. The source amount can be incomplete initially; its later ready version preserves the original binding.

**Admission:** current worker authority, current source version and current enabled generation are checked, but the amount comparison uses the captured policy revision. A lowered threshold cannot reclassify an older candidate; raising it cannot substitute a new rule for an originally captured candidate within the same uninterrupted enabled generation. Admission writes one immutable exact gift-purpose intent and required audit atomically. No ordinary negative-evaluation ledger/audit is added. A stored prior receipt remains historical acceptance, not current permission to materialize or disclose.

**Off versus accepted work:** Off-first denies fresh admission. Admission-first commits its durable intent; Off then occurs and later materialization succeeds exactly once. Off/on changes generation so old unadmitted candidates remain barred. Historical-origin source records are effect-dark. Accepted materialization bypasses the _new admission_ enablement gate but still checks current worker rights and material source revision. Source change blocks it for owner reconciliation. Actual corrected-source restoration and the full service/read authority remain unproved.

## Findings with all eight dimensions

Each proof below is a future production qualification, not an assertion that the toy established the financial/product boundary. Likelihood is a conditional engineering judgment.

### DB22-F1 — Processing time chooses a different policy

1. **Harm:** a delayed/replayed old gift newly qualifies after a lower threshold, or valid original work disappears after a raise.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** likely if workers simply read the latest setting.
4. **Cause:** no immutable original live occurrence-to-policy binding; gift date, arrival time or retry time stands in for source chronology.
5. **Evidence:** Q22 requires no historical task flood; D20 source/event identity distinction; actual cases 13–14 and races 24–25.
6. **Impact on A:** require prospective, source-owned policy meaning without a date-picker/rules framework.
7. **Exact clause:** “The original source-owned live occurrence SHALL resolve and preserve its applicable immutable policy revision and enabled-generation lineage. Later threshold edits SHALL apply prospectively and SHALL NOT reclassify that occurrence merely because a worker runs later. Current source evidence and authority SHALL still be re-proved. Gift date, import label and worker arrival SHALL NOT supply this boundary.”
8. **Production proof:** race edit with actual source acceptance in both orders; delay incomplete original qualification; lower/raise/revert policy and replay across time zones/imported dates. Verify actual owner lineage and unchanged effect identity, not just seeded revision IDs.

### DB22-F2 — Off is confused with deleting already accepted work

1. **Harm:** unadmitted old candidates revive on enable, or accepted required work vanishes because its worker was delayed.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible if one Boolean controls admission, materialization and all existing task states.
4. **Cause:** no distinction between candidate observation, committed exact admission+intent and delivered task; enabled generations are not fenced.
5. **Evidence:** parent/governance temporal reconciliation; ADR-0183 accepted intent; actual cases 16–20 and races 22–23.
6. **Impact on A:** Off stops new admissions; existing/queued accepted follow-ups remain. No bulk retirement, source mutation or hidden historical catch-up.
7. **Exact clause:** “Off SHALL fence new amount-rule admission immediately and end the unadmitted generation. Re-enable SHALL NOT revive never-admitted/held old-generation candidates. Already committed exact amount-reason admission and durable task intent SHALL survive Off and materialize once subject to current source/authority. Qualified restoration of genuinely invalidated, previously admitted unconsumed work remains existing-work repair under its captured rule, including while Off; ordinary replay SHALL NOT reopen human-handled coverage.”
8. **Production proof:** both Off/admission orders; worker downtime; enable/off/enable; source-invalidated then restored accepted work while Off; held-never-admitted comparison; human Done/Not needed; other reasons/promises. Restoration/human lifecycle is explicitly beyond this fixture.

### DB22-F3 — Threshold syntax admits a different monetary value

1. **Harm:** excess precision rounds a gift/policy across the boundary, currencies are mixed, or zero/null silently means enable/disable.
2. **Severity:** high.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible with floats, numeric scale coercion or generic form-number handling.
4. **Cause:** validating after coercion, hardcoded exponent, untyped scalar or source-header/recognition substitution.
5. **Evidence:** P13 exact minor-unit contract; fresh PostgreSQL numeric docs; actual unsafe case 1 and negative cases 2–6/29.
6. **Impact on A:** positive exact per-currency amount with plain at-least semantics; financial owner still certifies the actual subject/recipient amount.
7. **Exact clause:** “The threshold SHALL be a positive exact amount in an admitted explicit currency, parsed and range-checked through the existing currency-aware minor-unit boundary before lossy coercion. Enablement SHALL be independent of zero/null amount. Comparison SHALL use only the qualified same-currency source amount for the exact gift/recipient/subject basis, with no FX, rounding-up, mixed sum, hidden allocation or fee/assessment substitution.”
8. **Production proof:** exact/below/above one minor unit; excess precision, NaN/infinities, negative/zero/missing, unsafe integer/range; 0/2/3/4-exponent inputs; source allocation/recognition/fee cases. The toy does not prove parser/range or financial basis.

### DB22-F4 — Concurrent Save or a privileged caller changes the wrong policy

1. **Harm:** tenant misbinding, lost edits, forged attribution or rule changes without current rights.
2. **Severity:** critical for unauthorized/cross-Tenant effects; high for lost configuration.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible if reusing the inspected fixed-Tenant, role-only settings route or a raw JSON/table writer.
4. **Cause:** missing exact context/CAS/result receipt, ambient service privilege, or coarse RLS treated as field/business authorization.
5. **Evidence:** current settings route; P12; actual role/CAS/audit tests and race 21. The route is not claimed exploitable in a hosted project.
6. **Impact on A:** narrow current owner-authorized settings command; keep UI simple and safe response recovery.
7. **Exact clause:** “Policy read/change/enable/disable SHALL use exact current trusted Tenant/scope and owner-registered P12 authority, mandatory expected head and durable accepted result/audit. Replays SHALL recover the original command result under current disclosure rights; changed meaning conflicts. Generic table/RPC/import writers SHALL NOT forge policy, occurrence binding or admission. Coarse Tenant RLS, immutable references, grants/function ownership and proposed-row checks SHALL backstop, not replace, the one PDP.”
8. **Production proof:** two editors, policy revocation versus Save, lost Save response and same-key altered payload, current Tenant switch, direct/rest/service/view/RPC paths, all effective grants and policies. This probe proves CAS/audit failure, not complete settings replay or actual P12.

### DB22-F5 — Source correction is ignored because policy was once valid

1. **Harm:** old money/recipient evidence produces false current work, or retries erase a real earlier acceptance.
2. **Severity:** high/critical depending protected recognition.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible with an immutable policy reference mistaken for immutable eligibility.
4. **Cause:** qualifying only threshold/version, skipping current financial/recognition evidence and access; treating original receipt as current action permission.
5. **Evidence:** financial owner/current-source boundary; D20 correction lifecycle; cases 19–20 and races 26–27.
6. **Impact on A:** capture policy meaning while requalifying relevant current source/access; retain old outcome without silently authorizing new work.
7. **Exact clause:** “Captured policy evidence SHALL NOT freeze financial/recognition truth or confer continued access. Admission, materialization, correction and disclosure SHALL apply current relevant source and P12 fences. Original acceptance/coverage remains historical evidence. Owner-qualified withdrawal/restoration SHALL preserve lineage and independent promises; source changes SHALL NOT mint another gift-purpose identity or repeat human-handled work.”
8. **Production proof:** refunds, attribution correction, exact source revision races, NHI owner departure, source restoration while Off, receipt disclosure after revocation and all protected projections. The fixture's single source revision is not P13/P14/P16 coherence proof.

### DB22-F6 — A policy revision becomes a new effect or a second engine

1. **Harm:** duplicate tasks per criterion/version, historical backfill floods, or unnecessary scans/audits inflate cost and disclosure.
2. **Severity:** high for wrong work/privacy; medium for avoidable complexity.
3. **Likelihood:** plausible if policy version is part of business effect identity or every evaluation persists a donor classification.
4. **Cause:** confusion between explanatory policy evidence, stable gift-purpose effect and optional transport/workflow execution.
5. **Evidence:** D20 permanent per-gift coverage; ADR-0183 idempotency; no-op Save case 11, history case 15 and materialization replay case 28.
6. **Impact on A:** a bounded typed setting and source command, not a rules DSL, new task ledger or retroactive classifier.
7. **Exact clause:** “Policy revision, enabled generation, criterion label and transport key SHALL be evidence or execution fences, not a new intended gift-purpose identity. Compatible reasons SHALL converge through the existing shared task owner without duplicate work. Historical-only inputs remain effect-dark. No-op Save and ordinary nonqualifying evaluation SHALL not require a new business version/event or per-donor negative ledger; mandatory existing governance/security audit remains. No new rule engine or broad history scan is introduced.”
8. **Production proof:** repeated policy edits, same gift qualifying through multiple reasons/native/workflow paths, historical reimport/restore, no-op saves and materialization crashes. Preserve current D20 coverage and actual required audit without inventing a second registry.

## Release limits and verification

The probe demonstrates mechanism, not final tables or permission names. Its fake integer subject, static SQL-role gates, toy currency list and trusted source-owner command cannot certify legal entity, supporter, P13/P14 basis, source-live chronology or P12. Current full source/policy settings endpoints, browser parsing, human tasks/Undo, source restoration, privacy-safe errors/counts/logs, actual outbox/worker leases, production lock budgets, retention/restore and UI/a11y require real qualification.

D20's group fixture and D21's source-admission review remain inherited evidence and were not rerun or modified. The D22 run succeeded once; no failed run is hidden. Static verification checked actual role attributes, forced RLS/grants, all source/artifact/helper hashes, seven observed lock waits and cleanup. Formatting and local references were checked. No application test or hosted state claim is made.
