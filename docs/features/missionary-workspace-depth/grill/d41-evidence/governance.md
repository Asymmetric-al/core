# D41 — governance review of keeping contact work in Asym

Reviewed 2026-10-18 against Core `9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` and the Phase 28 research working tree. The founder selected **B — Keep contact work in Asym**: no additional external contact-update feed in this release; the separately governed giving feed retains its scope. This is an independent document review and proposed amendment record, not ratification, formal specification, implementation, runtime certification or provider authorization. No previous model, probe or verifier was executed; no old evidence was changed.

## Disposition and decisive amendment

**Accept with required amendments.** B is the strongest economical release decision on the evidence provided. Existing native correction and bounded inbound intake meet the selected workflow; an additional external contact-update family lacks a demonstrated Asym design-partner need. This is a founder-selected product scope and reasoned judgment, not a finding that external continuity is universally useless.

The critical amendment is **purpose precision**. “No contact-update feed” cannot mean “no contact-like information leaves Asym,” “no exports,” “all external integrations Off,” or “contact changes never affect an external projection.” P21's pre-existing giving/support field floor may include approved display identity and coarse city/state; D7 has qualified physical mailing exports; D15 has deliberate individual email; D17 has inbound CSV/XLSX intake. All retain their exact purpose, permissions and qualification. No new contact-sync toggle, dormant resource or vendor integration is added by B.

## Material concerns, impact and exact proposed prevention

Likelihood is qualitative reasoning for a careless implementation or wording; no production incident rate was measured.

### G01 — Blanket exclusion breaks established finance and communication purposes

**What/why:** “Keep all contacts in Asym” is interpreted as stripping permitted supporter display from the giving feed, disabling print labels or individual sending. Missionaries lose independently ratified workflows; finance projections become inconsistent. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible.** Evidence: P21 field floor, D7-C18, D15, D17 and ADR-0097. This narrows B's scope.

**Proposed clause:** “This release SHALL add no Phase 28 external relationship/contactability resource family, recurring contact-update feed, automatic contact export or writeback to fundraising tools. Existing finance-safe giving/support feed fields, qualified purpose-specific print/export and sending, D17 inbound intake and unrelated authorized integration purposes retain their own scope. None may be used as a substitute contact-maintenance feed.”

The phrase “automatic contact export” is scoped to the new contact-maintenance purpose; it is not a ban on independently required existing source projections.

### G02 — Customization silently reintroduces A

**What/why:** A disabled-by-default “Contact updates” switch, arbitrary field mapping, webhook recipe or hidden API family implements the option the founder rejected. Hidden code creates disclosure and maintenance risk even when ordinary UI looks quiet. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible**, given general integration/settings vocabulary. This changes customization requirements without changing B.

**Proposed clause:** “D41 SHALL create no contact-feed field family, subscription option, grant, destination mapping, outbox/queue, credentials, webhook, background worker, activation toggle, dormant feature or placeholder. Tenant control SHALL reuse the existing exact native contact permissions and independently qualified giving-feed controls. A new external contact purpose requires a later explicit product/owner decision; configuration, AI or workflow cannot add it by implication.”

This does not require deleting independent generic integration infrastructure. It requires no registration/admission for this unapproved purpose.

### G03 — Native correction is falsely described as globally synchronized or entirely isolated

**What/why:** “Saved everywhere” implies unsupported destination application; “saved only in Asym” promises that no approved giving field changed externally. Display-name/coarse-location corrections can legitimately update the existing P21 projection while email/phone/postal records remain unsynchronized. **Severity: medium. Likelihood: likely with simplified marketing copy.** Evidence: P21 floor and D5 source effects. This changes copy and acceptance wording.

**Proposed clause:** “Successful correction SHALL report authoritative native acceptance, such as ‘Saved in Asym.’ The giving connection SHALL describe exact approved categories and actual currentness; it SHALL NOT imply that complete external contact records are kept up to date. Contact-record update feeds are absent, while independently authorized source changes within the existing giving/support floor remain permitted.”

Show this distinction where a user inspects a currently qualified connection or asks about external continuity. Do not add a warning to every routine contact edit.

### G04 — Native editing and tenant settings become a new authority system

**What/why:** “Tenant customizable” becomes an app boolean that overrides denied field/source rules, or “turn it off” erases all native contact access. Protected data can leak and independently valid grants can be removed incorrectly. **Severity: critical. Likelihood: plausible.** Evidence: D5-C01–C11 and D1. B does not justify a new permission layer.

**Proposed clause:** “Native read/edit/share/intake/export/send authority SHALL continue to resolve through the existing exact P12/source-purpose boundary. Editing is allowed only for eligible viewed fields and current action scope; settings management does not grant record visibility. Revoking one grant does not override surviving independent valid authority. Disabling an action does not delete its source data, accepted history or independent read rights.”

### G05 — Valid giving credentials become a broad CRM extraction or mutation route

**What/why:** A token authorized for giving asks for contact fields, expands a resource, joins a canonical Party or pushes a contact update. Native UI omission alone does not constrain an API or worker. **Severity: critical. Likelihood: plausible** if generic CRM access is reused. Evidence: ADR-0097 closed resource/field/version/purpose scopes and CRM server authority.

**Proposed clause:** “Feed reads, queued egress, provider callbacks, resource selectors and serializers SHALL use the closed qualified existing family and field contract. Giving authorization SHALL not admit a P28 contact family or writeback. Unknown or unsupported resource/field requests SHALL fail before protected enumeration; no arbitrary CRM join, source-global ID or stale cursor grants access. Preserve tenant/entity/recipient/subject/destination/epoch and privacy-before-aggregation protections.”

No new contacts table, grants or policy is needed to enforce a family that is never registered. Qualify reached generic paths and forbid accidental broad fallback.

### G06 — D17 becomes a covert reverse-sync mechanism

**What/why:** Repeated external exports are reimported and silently overwrite native corrections or revive old restrictions/consent. The result is conflicting authority disguised as ordinary intake. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible during transition.** Evidence: D17-C04–C08, D5 conflict rules.

**Proposed clause:** “D17 remains the finite add/reuse contact-assertion profile. Re-import SHALL NOT become bulk contact update, automatic merge, consent authority, historical activity import or external conflict resolution. Changed bound assertions follow the existing discrepancy/ordinary source-correction path under current revision and authority; previously imported does not certify equality with the current native record.”

### G07 — B is treated as proof that no external copies or existing lanes exist

**What/why:** Staff promise old external contact data has been deleted or stop an independently authorized subscription while upgrading. Existing local databases, emails, files and backups can remain. **Severity: high. Likelihood: plausible.** Evidence: ADR-0097 revocation outcomes and P21 Stop sharing, D7/D15 copy limits.

**Proposed clause:** “This release-scope decision SHALL not claim downstream erasure, recall or verified absence of legacy copies. Preserve qualified existing giving subscriptions and accepted source/delivery history. Before changing any discovered contact egress, identify its actual purpose, authority and owner; quarantine only an unqualified affected path through its existing owner, without deleting source or destination records or disabling unrelated authorized channels. Stop sharing and reconnection retain P31's deny-first/new-grant semantics.”

A live unexpected contact-export path is an implementation/release concern requiring concrete inspection; its existence is not assumed here.

### G08 — Native correction is coupled to external outages

**What/why:** Native Save waits for giving-feed delivery or reports failure after the native transaction committed. Users retry and may overwrite newer contact data. **Severity: medium/high. Likelihood: plausible** with a broad synchronization abstraction. Evidence: D5 atomic source/retry boundaries; ADR-0097 separates source and disposable feed.

**Proposed clause:** “Native source acceptance, permission checks and recoverable receipt SHALL remain independent of external connection health. Qualified asynchronous effects use their own owner identity/recovery contract. An external delay cannot undo an accepted correction, trigger a duplicate native Save, or invent an unapproved contact delivery. Display meaningful connection delay only in its permitted affected context.”

### G09 — Scope exclusion turns into an indefinite unsupported-product promise

**What/why:** “Coming soon” contact controls, provider logos or stale roadmap tasks imply availability/commitment or require users to wait for external middleware. **Severity: medium. Likelihood: plausible.** Evidence: founder native outcome and strict provider certification.

**Proposed clause:** “The normal missionary journey SHALL expose no contact-sync setup, disabled toggle, provider conflict picker, export-and-reimport workaround or coming-soon promise. Native editing remains the complete selected workflow. Giving-tool controls appear only through their already qualified capability; unsupported providers or new MPDX organizations cannot be advertised as supported. This decision closes contact-feed scope for this release, not a promise or dependency for a later release.”

## Boundary-focused acceptance counterexamples

These are proposed outcomes for later independent tests, not executed test results.

1. Correct an allowed email in Asym: one accepted native correction and its source-required consequences; no new external contact-update event, subscription or worker. An unavailable giving connection does not block Save.
2. Correct an authorized display name: preserve lawful source effects and the exact existing giving/support projection if that subscription permits them; do not incorrectly reject the change under a blanket contact ban.
3. Give a provider credential valid for finance a contact-family/resource/field expansion or writeback request: deny before protected enumeration; a known connection does not supply authorization.
4. Import a CSV containing an old phone for a previously imported contact: preserve current native value and follow D17's reviewed discrepancy path. No automatic conflict winner.
5. Remove a native editing grant: fresh exact permission evaluation controls future writes; independent surviving read/edit grants remain effective. Existing history is not deleted.
6. Run authorized newsletter print preparation or D15 individual sending: its own purpose gates still operate; B neither disables it nor grants new permission.
7. Read the giving connection: show only its existing exact categories and evidence-backed currentness. Never say all contacts are synchronized or all external copies deleted.
8. Retry a native Save after a lost response: resolve the same native receipt under current rights, without waiting for a contact connector or producing any unapproved contact event.
9. Restore old data or upgrade schema/configuration: no dormant contact family becomes live, no private preparation is enriched into the giving feed, and independently explicit feed Off remains Off.
10. Request mixed-tenant/entity/recipient/subject fields or hidden anonymous identity through a giving endpoint: deny without count, cursor, group or error-side-channel leakage.
11. No qualified giving capability exists: D41 creates no generic integration card, pending connector, permission request or implied provider availability merely to offer customization.
12. No third-party tool is connected: native contact view/correction and qualified inbound intake remain complete and usable without configuring anything external.

## Interpretation of the remaining review categories

B creates no new store, transport, recurrence clock, subscription lifecycle, financial allocation or schema migration. Therefore no novel monetary-precision, deletion engine, queue-capacity or RLS implementation is justified by the excluded feature. That is a scoped absence of new design risk, not certification of existing code. The reached native/read/write/import/print/send/giving paths still need their existing source proofs. Testability should prove negative admission plus positive native usability and preserved independent purposes, rather than implement a complex external subsystem merely to test that it is disabled.

Lifecycle and idempotency risks are principally inherited native correction/import receipts and qualified giving subscriptions. External corrections continue through P21/P31 where already authorized; D41 adds no correction delivery lifecycle. Observability should record actual native acceptance/failure and existing feed outcomes, not audit every read or create empty contact-sync telemetry. Any discovered unapproved contact egress is a material zero-tolerance release defect owned by P31 with P9/P12; contain that route, preserve evidence and source data, and require proof before reopening. Routine native outcomes and giving health retain their existing owners and thresholds. No arbitrary latency, throughput or retention promise is invented by B.

2. Carry forward D1/D5/D17/P21/P31 exact qualification obligations; close the optional contact-family branch for this release. Do not leave a “choose contact sync default” question open.
3. At authorized formalization, propagate the exclusion and preserved owners into the proper source/traceability artifacts; do not amend ADR-0097 to pretend its future extension socket was removed forever.
4. During implementation qualification, verify no unapproved contact resource can be admitted through generic endpoints and prove native positive flows plus independent giving/print/send/import continuity. Do not create the excluded contact service or an artificial runtime switch.
5. If concrete future customer evidence later warrants a new contact-update purpose, conduct a separately scoped product and owner decision; no current ticket, placeholder or promise is required by this research.

No database, provider account, authenticated competitor installation, deployed API, current production subscription, load profile or end-user study was tested by this document reviewer. Root source and UX evidence must independently qualify those claims. The present decision is complete as a bounded release-scope choice; existing owner implementation obligations remain explicit and are not concealed as implementation success.
