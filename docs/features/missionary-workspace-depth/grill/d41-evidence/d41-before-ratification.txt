# D41 — keep contact work in Asym

Reviewed **October 18, 2026 (Asia/Bangkok)** against Core
`9cada8f3d8ab59db391d3fd20df77fcadeb09813` and its research working tree.
The founder selected **Q41 B — Keep contact work in Asym**, requested a clear,
tenant-controlled native experience, and requested every-category adversarial
review. **Disposition: Accept with required amendments.** The selected answer
is recorded; the corrected wording and amendments below are **reviewed, pending
explicit ratification**. No Q42 is posed in this answer review.

This is a completed research/design review under the
[founder boundary](phase28-founder-brief.md:21), not a formal PRD/OpenSpec,
application/schema change, integration activation or release certification.
No tickets, GitHub changes, live settings or external communications were made.
No earlier model, database probe or verification program was executed.

## Exact corrected decision to record

> **B — Keep contact work in Asym.** This release adds no Phase 28 external
> contact-update or contactability feed. Maya finds and maintains permitted
> contact information through the existing native workspace and authoritative
> contact owners. Ordinary work does not depend on an external fundraising tool.
>
> Authorized tenant administrators control missionary contact editing through
> the existing permission system and D5's eligible source/field rules. They can
> withhold that editing capability without deleting contact information or
> changing independently authorized viewing, staff work or other purposes.
> Current access determines whether Maya can edit, view or see nothing. There
> is no new contact-sync switch, dormant connector, permission engine or routine
> approval step.
>
> The separately governed giving feed retains its existing source-owned scope,
> safe field floor, default-Off setup, current authorization, qualification and
> stop-sharing rules. D41 neither widens nor removes those fields. A source
> correction may still affect an already authorized giving projection under its
> own contract; this is not synchronization of external contact records.
>
> Existing independently governed intake, communications, exports and other
> source consequences retain their own permissions and limits. Contact editing
> grants none of them. Saving updates only the selected eligible facts, preserves
> other values and required history, and reports accepted, conflicted or unknown
> outcomes honestly. No external contact-refresh obligation, automatic message
> or second contact database is introduced.

The amendment narrows ambiguous phrases such as “keep contact work in Asym” and
“turn this off”; it does not reintroduce option A. The excluded feed is a release
scope decision, not a disabled feature waiting for a tenant toggle. The useful
tenant controls already exist in the accepted D5/P12 and P21/P31 contracts.

visually separate from giving information, notes and activity without duplicating
the person. Use the existing shared Base UI/base-maia components, Zinc semantic
tokens, typography and spacing from
[the UI contract](../../../../packages/ui/AGENTS.md). A short readable detail
section and one qualified Edit action are sufficient; no sync dashboard,
integration warning banner or contact score is needed.

## Ownership, invariants and database review

| Authoritative fact                               | Owner and invariant                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contact identity and eligible corrected values   | P4/P9 native sources; one authoritative correction, with no provider-owned winner or shadow contact store. D1 preparation is distinct and retains its audience.      |
| Effective view/edit/admin capability             | P12 with source/field floors from existing owners; actor and active tenant assignment come from trusted context. A contact relationship is not an edit/export grant. |
| Gift/support facts and external safe field floor | P13/P14/P16/P21 according to their existing contracts. Contact Save cannot manufacture money, consent, legal donor identity or support history.                      |
| Giving subscription and delivery evidence        | P31 under ADR-0097; no new P28 contact resource is admitted. A disposable projection is not source authority.                                                        |
| Import, communication and protected bytes        | P30/D17 intake, P6/P17 dispatch, P29 custody as applicable. D41 neither grants nor cancels these independent purposes.                                               |

The selected no-build scope needs **no new table, enum, nullable flag, feed
cursor, scheduled job, mapping language or retention period**. Review native
correction using D5's existing boundary rather than treating absence of a new
feed as proof that native storage is safe:

- **Keys and relationships:** enforce tenant-aware references and the exact
  allowed source/assignment scope. Shared phones/emails are not unique person
  identity. Never derive ownership from a caller's contact ID or external ID.
- **Mutation constraints:** current old-row access and valid resulting-row
  state must both hold. Tenant, actor, author, owner, approval and audit fields
  are server-derived or immutable for this action. A contact edit cannot move
  the row into another tenant/owner/classification or turn hidden data visible.
- **Grants and RLS:** test actual grants, SELECT/UPDATE policies, permissive
  unions, inherited checks, view execution mode, functions/RPCs and service-role
  paths. Browser denial does not qualify a privileged endpoint; a UI button is
  not authorization. Missing/filtered targets are not successful saves.
- **Field/null semantics:** omitted means unchanged; Clear is explicit and
  source-valid. Reject the whole selected action if a submitted field is
  forbidden/invalid. Do not convert missing/redacted display defaults into
  authoritative values. Preserve international strings and field-specific proof.
- **History and deletion:** mutable current contact facts and governed immutable
  correction/authority evidence are distinct. No new append-only contact ledger
  is warranted. Do not cascade a correction/removal into gifts, tasks, private
  preparation or financial history. Existing retention/erasure rules apply to
  source history, logs, caches, exports and backups.
- **Money and dates:** no financial amount, currency, schedule or date type is
  added or changed. Preserve exact existing financial units and histories; no
  contact row may overwrite them. Audit instants use trusted source time.
- **Indexes and capacity:** use existing scoped lookup/permission paths and
  bounded native pagination. Validate actual plans and supported tenant cohorts
  when the source action is implemented; no new speculative index or universal
  latency/volume claim is justified here.
- **Migration/backfill:** D41 requires no contact copying/backfill. Native D5
  cutover still must preserve retained data, prevent old broad writers from
  bypassing new checks and keep permitted edits functional. Rollback cannot
  restore unsafe browser grants or a retired external CRM.

The meaningful native lifecycle remains: view → authorized edit draft → source
acceptance, rejection/conflict, or unknown outcome → current permitted result.
Cancelling before submission abandons the draft; closing after submission does
not prove cancellation. A true no-op creates no changed-value or interaction
history. Accepted corrections retain their identity; delayed/repeated responses
must not overwrite newer state. Permission changes are a separate lifecycle,
and giving-feed publication/revocation is a third. D41 adds no fourth state
machine and cannot make one axis impersonate another.

## Exact required amendments

These are proposed review clauses for the later authorized spec/design, not an
assertion of implementation or a new formal specification.

- **C01 — Bounded exclusion.** This release SHALL add no P28 external
  contact-update/contactability resource, subscription option, serializer or
  producer. Native contact work SHALL remain independent of that absent feature.
  Any later addition requires a separate explicit product/owner decision and
  ADR-0097 qualification; no dormant tenant toggle SHALL bypass it.
- **C02 — Preserve existing giving meaning.** D41 SHALL neither widen nor remove
  the already approved P21 financial projection or field floor. Its current
  corrections, authorization, anonymity and P31 delivery rules SHALL remain
  effective. Existing safe identity-field changes SHALL NOT be called contact
  synchronization or used to infer permission for new contact methods. Giving
  credentials/cursors SHALL not authorize contact-resource/field expansion, direct
  CRM enrichment or writeback; unsupported requests SHALL fail before protected
  enumeration while valid giving requests remain usable.
- **C03 — Preserve independent purposes.** D17/P30 inbound contact intake,
  qualified communications/print/export actions and unrelated owner contracts
  SHALL retain their existing scope. D41 SHALL grant no new export, send,
  subscription, consent, identity or financial power, and SHALL not create an
  automatic export workaround or external-refresh task. Repeated D17 intake SHALL
  not overwrite native corrections, merge people or restore consent; bound-row
  discrepancies retain the existing reviewed source-correction path.
- **C04 — Reuse tenant permissions.** Tenant administration SHALL reuse D5/P12
  capability and eligible field/source controls, including current configured
  versus effective/pending state. No new D41 master flag, per-contact ACL or
  routine staff approval SHALL be added. Removing a grant SHALL preserve other
  independently valid rights; missing authority SHALL not manufacture a grant.
- **C05 — One qualified native correction.** Native correction SHALL satisfy
  D5's complete actor/source/field inventory and preserve the active roster-safety
  contract. Only selected eligible changes SHALL be submitted; Clear, omitted,
  no-op, validation and owner-specific proof effects SHALL remain distinct.
  Unrelated fields and lawful history SHALL remain unchanged.
- **C06 — Trusted authorization and resulting state.** All reached server,
  browser/Data API, RPC/view and privileged paths SHALL enforce exact current
  tenant/assignment/source/purpose/field authority. Trusted server context SHALL
  establish attribution. Effective old-row and resulting-row constraints SHALL
  prevent cross-scope reassignment, protected-field promotion and audit spoofing.
- **C07 — Atomicity, conflicts and recovery.** The existing source boundary SHALL
  atomically accept the qualified correction and required evidence, fence relevant
  concurrent source/permission changes and recover the same durable intent.
  Retries SHALL not repeat accepted effects or overwrite later corrections;
  uncertain responses SHALL remain uncertain until reconciled. Readback obeys
  current access. No new generic transaction/retry framework is required by D41.
- **C08 — Honest native UI.** The existing detail/editor SHALL show eligible
  editing, read-only access, loading, temporary unavailability, validation,
  conflict, accepted and unresolved states distinctly. One ordinary Save SHALL
  suffice for the qualified correction. Read revocation or tenant changes SHALL
  clear affected presentation/drafts and fence late responses.
- **C09 — Accurate connection copy.** A qualified giving connection SHALL show
  its exact shared scope and truthful preparation/fetch/application state under
  P21/P31. Native contact screens SHALL not imply pending external synchronization.
  No blanket “nothing leaves Asym,” guaranteed erasure, or fictitious Connected
  state SHALL be presented. Explain D41's limit only where relevant.
- **C10 — Accessibility and international use.** Shared base-maia primitives and
  semantic tokens SHALL provide labeled controls, visible focus, keyboard/phone
  access, useful field errors, status announcements and narrow-screen reflow.
  Qualified source rules SHALL preserve international names/phones/addresses;
  no unrelated required email or hardcoded country SHALL be introduced.
- **C11 — Privacy and retention.** Native/contact/feed visibility SHALL remain
  purpose-specific across lists, counts, searches, caches, logs, exports and
  diagnostics. Private preparation, hidden identities and donor contactability
  SHALL not be inferred or copied through the giving feed. Existing source
  retention/erasure rules SHALL apply; D41 adds no permanent snapshot or backup.
- **C12 — Compatibility and safe rollout.** No D41 data backfill or blanket
  permission/feed revocation SHALL occur. Before the native correction release,
  qualify retained records, old clients/writers, new source checks and actual
  permissions. Preserve valid use, stop only unsafe paths and roll forward
  without restoring retired CRM or unsafe grants. Inventory any discovered
  legacy external-contact path under its owner before changing it.
- **C13 — Bounded native performance.** Native list/detail/correction SHALL not
  wait on a contact provider or perform new feed scans/fan-out. Existing pagination
  and complete reachability SHALL be preserved; actual authorization/query and
  phone/network budgets SHALL be measured for supported cohorts before release.
- **C14 — Accountable evidence.** Source correction history, policy administration,
  security decisions and P31 delivery evidence SHALL retain their separate
  meanings. Correlation/outcome diagnostics SHALL be permission-scoped and
  content-minimized; no full-contact dumps or synthetic sync backlog are allowed.
- **C15 — Qualification and traceability.** The later authorized spec/design SHALL
  trace D41 and P01–P30 below through D5/P9/P12 and P21/P31 owners, real positive
  and negative tests, retained-data rollout and release evidence. An earlier
  probe, passing document check or static settings page SHALL not certify runtime.
- **C16 — Minimal scope.** D41 SHALL add no provider, dependency, new permission
  service, generic sync engine, second CRM, AI classifier, contact-value score or
  separate missionary data-entry system. Unreached platform risks SHALL not be
  turned into new requirements merely because external contact sync is excluded.

1. **Before recording the reviewed answer:** settle the release scope, preserve
   the giving floor and independent purposes, and define tenant customization as
   existing native permissions. The corrected decision and C01–C04/C09 do this.
   No unresolved product answer is needed to retain B. Exact amendments remain
   pending the founder's explicit ratification.
2. **At the later authorized specification/design transition:** integrate the
   bounded exclusion with D5/P9/P12 and P21/P31, trace C01–C16/P01–P30 and the
   accepted terminology, and preserve D17/communications and existing owners.
   Do not create a new ADR solely to restate ADR-0097's non-admission rule.
   Formal publication and tickets still require `/to-prd` and `/to-issues`.
3. **Require implementation safeguards:** qualify the native field inventory,
   real permissions/grants/RLS, selected-field writer, source conflict/retry
   boundary and privacy-safe consumer states. Current static gaps belong to
   this native correction delivery; they do not justify building A. Preserve
   real allowed edits as well as denials.
4. **Before releasing the affected native/connection presentation:** demonstrate
   positive/negative authority and concurrency tests, retained-data/mixed-version
   compatibility, current connection-state evidence and accessible mobile use.
   Users must correctly identify where a correction is saved and what an existing
   giving connection does. Any observed wrong access/sync/erasure interpretation
   is a design blocker to fix and retest, not a warning to ignore.
5. **Monitor only after those gates pass:** use the named signals below. No
   monitoring substitutes for permission, source-integrity or privacy proof.
