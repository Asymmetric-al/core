Other synthesis resolutions preserved:

- P28 owns source-purpose qualification/admission and local durable intent;
  the existing shared task owner owns operational membership and reviewed-set
  disposition/coverage receipts. No duplicate P28 completion ledger.
- G3 arriving before G1/G2 finish remains on the **same** current task. Completing
  and recreating the task merely because G3 arrived was rejected. G3 arriving
  after actual closure may create the next current task.
- Selected material change rejects the whole reviewed command; unselected new
  arrivals do not invalidate it. Finance/Zendesk best-effort skips are not adopted.
- Explicit full-N scope can be inspected through stable pages without forcing
  page-by-page completion. A bounded chunk is a declared smaller scope, not a
  silent limit. “Included” is the correct pre-action label; loading is not proof
  that a user has read or understood each item.
- Conditional Undo uses current-group resolution and lineage; no second current
  task or general task-merge engine. Both simple Undo and successor restoration
  remain **unproved** by the PostgreSQL fixture.
- The database reviewer confirmed **38/38 outcomes and nine actual races**, not
  actual source/P12/Core/hosted behavior. Source-withdrawal reconciliation,
  independent obligations and Undo are explicitly absent from that fixture.
- No current external product is claimed to prove Asym's exact race semantics.
  Accessibility minimums, recommended larger touch targets and qualitative
  recruitment gates remain correctly distinguished.
