# D2 evidence: optional notice after sharing a contact

Research-only owner review. The parent reports the founder's direction: **Share makes the contact generally visible to staff in the same organization's CRM, with an optional notification about availability/follow-up, without unnecessary complexity.** The founder then explicitly chose **Choose staff to notify (recommended; avoids an all-staff alert)**. Named staff recipients are therefore confirmed; notification channel and default-off presentation below are recommendations, not attributed founder selections. This file is not a formal specification or implementation ticket. No application, OpenSpec, GitHub or live system was changed; no message was sent.

## Recommended simple experience

**Make available to staff remains the main action.** Offer one initially empty **Notify staff (optional)** selector for one or several currently eligible staff members from the existing permitted organization directory. No selection means no notice; a separate checkbox is unnecessary. The recommendation is one **in-product Information notification** per selected person, with a short safe label such as **Contact available in CRM** and **View contact** opening the current permitted CRM record.

This recommendation deliberately separates **everyone's ordinary CRM availability** from **who receives a notification**. It does not notify every staff member, send email, assign responsibility, create a task, contact the person, subscribe anyone, or share private early notes. The optional notice is Off by default for each sharing action; no standing preference, team-routing builder, channel matrix, reminder schedule, read-receipt report or new inbox is needed.

The selected-recipient approach follows the founder's explicit clarification. In-product delivery and an initially empty selector are the recommended minimal treatment, not a claim that the founder selected email or another channel. No all-staff broadcast or team selector is needed. Internal availability notification is distinct from the P25 public **Request newsletter** handoff and from actual supporter newsletters.

**Information** fits the accepted meaning: a contact is now available for authorized work, with no newly imposed duty. P17 places Information in **All**, not **Needs attention**. Read or Archive changes only the recipient's engagement. If the user wants someone to be responsible for follow-up, use an independently deliberate P9/source-owned task or responsibility action; a notification cannot silently become that assignment.

## Acceptance, delivery and recovery without a complicated flow

The UI needs truthful distinct results, not extra user steps:

- **Shared** means the source accepted the contact's exact visibility transition. It remains true if subsequent notice publication or refresh fails.
- **Notification requested** means the selected notice's source request and durable dispatch intent were accepted. It does not mean an item appeared, was read, or that follow-up will happen.
- **Notification available** means the source-qualified local item was committed for that recipient. It is not email sent/delivered or a read receipt.
- **Before acceptance: correction needed** means reviewed fields, the expected source revision or a selected recipient is invalid/stale. Preserve the proposed action for correction; do not claim Shared or Notification requested, silently drop a selected recipient or substitute another person/channel.
- **After acceptance: shared; notification pending/unavailable** preserves the successful share when later local materialization or presentation fails. Offer qualified same-notice recovery; never undo sharing or claim the item is available when it is not.

At acceptance, the source must retain enough immutable operation/result evidence to distinguish those outcomes. First validate the exact reviewed shared fields/current revision and all deliberately selected staff recipients. If the user selected notification, its exact bounded request and identifier-only dispatch intent become part of the command's accepted meaning. Atomically commit the share, required source audit and that durable optional request before reporting **Shared / Notification requested**; a browser callback, best-effort after-response call or in-memory queue cannot supply that guarantee. P6 then admits the complete initial resolved recipient/step set all-before-any: no recipient item is claimable from a partial plan. A required local persistence failure leaves no accepted command; a lost response reconciles the same operation instead of submitting another share.

This upfront validation is not a dependency on optional provider delivery. For the recommended local-only notice, no provider is involved. If the optional local source contract is unavailable before starting, leave ordinary Share available and make the optional notice unavailable with a truthful explanation. Do not accept a selected option and quietly discard it. If a selected recipient becomes stale before acceptance, return actionable correction so the user can fix the selection or deliberately share without the option.

No distributed transaction should wait for local notification delivery or external I/O. After the share is accepted, a delayed worker, failed view refresh or missed invalidation cannot roll it back. Retry repairs the same accepted notice occurrence and only its residual eligible recipients; it cannot share again, create a new audience, assign work, or generate another unread item. A changed recipient selection after acceptance is new deliberate meaning and cannot reuse the old operation identity. A general resend/re-notify feature is not proposed here.

The source freezes the selected eligible recipients for the accepted notification occurrence. Before acceptance, stale or unauthorized selection is rejected for correction, not silently accepted, dropped or replaced. After acceptance and complete initial plan release, current authority can suppress a recipient's unstarted notice individually, and the rest remain independent. Later staff joining, changing assignment or gaining permission does not receive historical unread notices. Current tenant/recipient/source proof also governs result recovery; old success does not authorize disclosure of old contact details to a now-revoked viewer.

Use the existing information presentation policy and its exact key-specific source end. Its thirty-day unread and ninety-day recent-history ceilings are P17 defaults for Information, not new D2 waiting periods; a source restriction, privacy stop, deletion or shorter source retention ends access earlier. Do not borrow P25 newsletter request clocks, optional-staff email material clocks or a marketing unsubscribe policy for this local internal event.

## Present capability and exact gaps

**Owner contract exists; the exact contact-sharing notification capability is not established.** P17 contains the intended local Information model and staff notification center, but its `REAL vs FORWARD` section explicitly distinguishes those future contracts from current code. Its deferred inventory does not name a qualified contact-shared producer. P9 has no inspected contact-sharing notification source meaning/resolver. A new approved bounded source binding is therefore needed, not an invented generic notification key or a second notification store.

The parallel runtime workstream reports no contact-shared producer or qualified P6 local Information recipient path in the inspected app/API code. It found a static admin notification dropdown, a donor/campaign-oriented `notification_queue` scaffold, narrower financial approval recipients and Eve operator notifications. These are **delegated runtime findings**, not paths inspected independently in this file; consult [current-runtime.md](./current-runtime.md) for exact lines and limits. No claim is made that a remote deployment has no uninspected worker.

The minimum exact dependencies are: qualified P9/P28 share-transition/source command and immutable occurrence; P12 current staff/source/recipient admission; P3/P10 field/purpose/safety projection; P17 new exact optional Information semantic binding and safe local presentation/end rule; P6 complete intent/recipient slots/local event/availability and permanent dedupe; existing shared dispatch/claim/recovery execution where needed. Exact API names or a new issue allocation are not chosen here. Full P34 workflows, P32 newsletter sync, a Resend connection, SMS and AI are not prerequisites for the recommended local option.

## Suggested decision wording

> Sharing makes the admitted contact details available in the same organization's ordinary staff CRM, subject to the platform's existing safety and field restrictions. Sharing does not expose private early notes or grant unrelated financial or history access. The missionary may optionally let selected eligible staff know through a simple in-product notice; the staff selector starts empty. The notice provides information and a link, not a task or assignment. Sharing remains successful if notification cannot complete, and recovery never repeats the sharing action or sends an unintended message.

Named staff selection in this wording is founder-confirmed. In-product channel and empty initial selection remain recommendations for the simplest ordinary flow; do not attribute them as an explicit email/channel selection. They do not create an extra approval ritual or reopen the already answered recipient question.
