# D2 owner review: make the contact available in the organization CRM

Research only. Reviewed 2026-10-15 America/Los_Angeles against checkout
`9cada8f3d`. The new founder direction is that sharing makes the contact generally
available to staff across the organization to see and follow up, with an optional
notification to selected staff. The founder clarified that notification is not
an all-staff broadcast. Notification selection does not select the contact's
staff audience. This simplifies the earlier possible selected-collaborator model.
No formal owner amendment, implementation, ticket or live change is made here.

The recipient choice is settled: Maya chooses which staff to notify. A quiet
in-product notification is a channel recommendation, not a founder-selected
delivery channel or an already-qualified producer. Do not infer email, push,
an all-staff broadcast or a staff task from that choice.

## Conclusion

Use one deliberate **Share with the organization** action that admits the
selected contact facts through P9's ordinary canonical Party/contact boundary.
After acceptance, staff find and use that contact in the normal CRM under its
existing permission and safety rules. Do not create a contact-specific staff
collaborator list, invitation/acceptance flow, per-contact staff access grants,
shared-contact table or second staff inbox.

“All staff” describes organization-wide ordinary CRM availability. It must not
be silently reinterpreted as only selected collaborators. It also does not
override a hidden/protected-person floor, revoked access, sensitive field rule,
financial/care boundary or contact restriction. Those are inherited platform
constraints, not new obstacles added to this choice. P12 still evaluates the
actual staff member's current capabilities and source scope, rather than a
literal `role == staff` bypass.

D1's bounded restricted preparation remains proportionate **as preparation**:
it protects new unshared assertions without requiring every canonical CRM reader
to understand private provisional people. D2 makes its exit much simpler:
admit/reuse the ordinary contact; retain the private preparation and source
lineage under their own restrictions; use the canonical source for subsequent
shared contact reads/changes. No ongoing synchronization between independent
editable contact masters is needed. Do not keep a growing private CRM once the
contact has been admitted.

This is the recommended conceptual owner design, not proof that this exact
P9/P12 prospect-admission producer is accepted or implemented. It needs the
coordinated owner extension already identified by D1. P9 ordinary staff create
and edit contracts establish the destination and its owners; they do not
automatically authorize a missionary to call every staff command.

## What becomes shared, and what does not

**D2 admits the contact.** A normal clear case can create/reuse the exact P9
Party/subtype and accept the contact facts deliberately included in the share
action. The person is a contact, not necessarily a donor, login user, supporter,
committed partner or cultivated relationship. Share needs no fake donation,
financial amount, household, Ministry Assignment, Support Assignment or claim.
Party identity remains Tenant-scoped; do not invent Legal Entity ownership for
the Party just because later ministry work has an exact Legal Entity.

**D2 does not publish Maya's private notebook.** Earlier notes, private activity,
referrer identity, personal impressions, care/Support facts and attachments do
not become shared just because the contact does. D1's preparation assertions
that are deliberately accepted as ordinary contact facts are distinguishable
from narrative private history. If Maya later writes an eligible new staff
handoff or shared partnership update, it uses its qualified source; do not
auto-generate it by summarizing old private text.

**D2 does not expose staff internals back to Maya.** The accepted result may give
her the qualified contact/submission outcome and her permitted relationship
view. It does not make her staff or reveal the contact's other ministries,
giving, care, assignments, private notes or historical shared entries.

**D2 permits staff follow-up through ordinary source actions.** Visibility allows
staff with the ordinary applicable powers to take appropriate work; it does not
assign every viewer a task, choose a lead, create a required next-action duty,
advance a stage, record a completed conversation or enroll P27 cultivation.
Existing lead/task/Request ownership remains unchanged. Actual contact still
requires current channel/purpose eligibility; “staff may follow up” cannot
subscribe the person to newsletters or override Never Ask/do-not-contact.

The conceptual command consumes the exact current preparation revision, the
deliberately selected eligible contact facts, current trusted actor/Tenant
context and stable operation identity. It uses source-qualified matching and
canonical admission, rather than caller-supplied authority or a raw upsert by
email. A general create/update permission cannot be assumed for the missionary;
the bounded share/admission capability must be qualified through P12.

The ordinary interface can show a brief summary of the contact being shared and
exclude private notes by construction. “Exact selected facts” does not require
a field-by-field permission wizard or a collaborator ceremony. Optional staff
notification recipients are the only recipient selection D2 calls for.

Where ordinary identity/contact admission, preparation outcome, required audit
and notification intent are local source effects, use a coherent atomic source
acceptance. External notification submission and delivery are later separate
facts. Do not invent a distributed approval workflow, recipient acceptance
ceremony or mandatory review queue for clear ordinary entries.

## Identity and hidden-match qualification remain material

The simple workflow does not make matching trivial:

- Same name, phone or church mailbox is evidence, not identity or consent.
- A hidden Party cannot become ordinary staff-visible because another person
  typed its email and pressed Share. Creating an extra visible Party as a
  workaround would defeat the original protection.
- A hidden duplicate warning, different failure path or constraint result can
  disclose existence. “Generic error” is insufficient if observable success
  versus rejection still reveals the hidden difference.
- P27 P03 supplies an accepted discipline for exact result authority and
  indistinguishable bounded defer/deny where that authority is absent. It does
  not itself qualify the new P9 prospect-admission producer.

The permanent admission contract must check the caller's **minimum admission
and result-disclosure authority before taking an existence-dependent branch**.
If that minimum is absent, absent and hidden-existing candidates follow the same
bounded defer/deny path; do not first query for a hidden match and normalize only
the returned wording. An already authorized visible identity or an independently
qualified candidate/result scope can support ordinary usable admission. Neither
a broad create capability nor the assertion that Maya knows this person proves
that result authority; no grant overrides an applicable P10 hidden-identity
floor. Internal identity-resolution service authority also does not grant the
requesting human its result visibility.

P4's `account_claim_requests` (`phase-04-identity-account-claiming-foundation.md:
239–245`) permits an owner-specific unresolved request with own-requester access.
That demonstrates the ability to retain one's submission without inventing a
resolved donor, not an approved prospect result contract. Its exact claim
possession/reveal policy and staff audience cannot be borrowed. Likewise P4
`attributeGuestGift` (`:208–211`) has a constant-shape, nonrevealing financial
attribution purpose; it does not authorize generic contact creation or an
honest “now available to staff” result for a missionary.

Therefore do not promise universal instant sharing of every hidden/ambiguous
case. Qualify the exact P4/P9/P12 admission and result-disclosure seam before
enabling that lane. This does not require a generic human-resolution team or
per-contact approval; clear permitted cases should remain one ordinary action.
If a specific guarantee is unavailable, block the affected admission lane while
preserving restricted preparation and other independently safe work. Do not
substitute a duplicate CRM record or expose the reason to avoid the limitation.

This is a producer qualification requirement, not a permanent no-release rule.
The owner must prove both the ordinary successful new-contact journey and its
hidden-identity counterpart for the exact admitted actor/source/result scope.
A proposed implementation that uniformly denies every new contact may satisfy
one negative test while failing the product; one that instantly accepts absent
but reveals blocked hidden matches fails privacy. If qualification exposes an
unavoidable product-policy conflict, bring that exact failed guarantee back
rather than invent a hidden-record disclosure exception. Acceptance of a
source-owned admission request, where such a lane is qualified, remains distinct
from completed canonical admission and must not be labelled “shared.”

## Proposed clauses for the later authorized owner reconciliation

These are engineering/product-reconciliation proposals, not an approved formal
specification or new grant. The founder's organization-wide staff audience and
optional-notification direction are recorded separately from these mechanisms.

1. **Ordinary staff availability:** “Deliberate sharing admits the contact into
   the sending organization's ordinary CRM. Staff do not require individual
   contact invitations or collaborator membership. Current P3/P10/P12 field,
   source, safety and purpose restrictions still apply; sharing does not
   override them.”
2. **One source:** “P9 accepts/reuses canonical Party and contact facts through
   its source commands, using P4 identity repair where needed. Restricted
   preparation retains its original provenance and private context; it is not
   a second editable canonical contact, shared list or portfolio.”
3. **Exact admission:** “Only the exact selected eligible contact facts are
   admitted. A missionary's bounded sharing authority creates no general staff
   role, global contact-edit power, claim, verification or financial authority.
   Existing canonical facts are changed only through independently authorized
   source rules.”
4. **Private history:** “Contact sharing does not publish, copy, summarize or
   relabel earlier private notes, attachments or activity. Newly authored shared
   context uses its exact qualified source and audience.”
5. **Identity safety:** “Same-Tenant identity resolution preserves provenance,
   treats low-confidence matches as unresolved and never auto-merges. Hidden
   identity/work, constraint diagnostics and reuse results are disclosed only
   through their qualified result authority. Privacy does not justify a
   duplicate canonical contact or cultivation enrollment.”
6. **Follow-up meaning:** “General staff availability permits ordinary
   authorized follow-up actions without creating an assignment, task, actual
   interaction, cultivation enrollment, stage, Request, gift or commitment.
   Current consent and contact restrictions govern every consequential action.”
7. **Optional notification:** “Sharing may independently request a qualified
   internal notification to the staff deliberately selected for attention,
   rather than broadcasting to all staff. Notification selection or delivery
   does not change
   CRM availability, add permissions, assign work or prove the contact was
   reviewed. A failed notification cannot undo an accepted share. No donor-
   facing communication follows from this option.”
8. **Safe acceptance/recovery:** “Source admission uses expected revisions,
   exact scoped operation identity, current authority and coherent required
   audit/result evidence. Repeated delivery cannot duplicate contact admission
   or the same requested notification. A changed payload cannot reuse the same
   operation; lost response reconciles the actual prior result.”
9. **No per-entry policy review:** “Ordinary sharing operates under a previously
   qualified source/policy. The command does not rewrite field-policy rows or
   manufacture contact-specific staff grants. Actual projection-policy widening
   still follows ADR0211; clear ordinary sharing does not acquire a routine
   supervisor approval or recipient-acceptance requirement.”

## Proof needed at the real owner seams

- New contact appears in ordinary staff CRM list/search/detail after accepted
  sharing, with no collaborator setup. Ordinary staff excluded by no specific
  safety/permission restriction can find it and perform their permitted
  follow-up; a narrow chosen-recipient implementation would fail D2.
- Private unique markers in notes, private referrer context, attachments,
  timeline/audit payloads and notification drafts remain absent from staff
  projections. Shared contact facts and protected history have distinct traces.
- Existing same Party is reused without changing unrelated canonical facts;
  conflicting assertions, same-name people and shared mailboxes use qualified
  resolution. No fake donor/ministry or consent is introduced.
- Pair hidden-existing/absent datasets across results, timing, counts,
  constraint errors, notifications and repair routes. Source proof must cover
  the actual admission result—not merely a redacted detail screen. Verify the
  minimum-result-authority gate occurs before existence-dependent behavior,
  including permission-denied and request-accepted versus contact-shared states;
  also prove a useful ordinary new-contact success case rather than denying all
  new people to pass the negative test.
- Concurrent share commands, retries, stale preparation edits, identity merge,
  access revocation and lost response converge on the correct source outcome.
  Accepted notification intent is not repeated by replay or a failed refresh.
- Notification off, failed, suppressed or recipient-revoked leaves canonical
  admission truthful. Contact sharing alone creates no assignment, task, P27
  work, stage change, contact consent or outward message.
- The missionary sees only their permitted share result/context. The staff
  receiving visibility never grants missionary access to unrelated staff,
  financial, care or other-ministry records in the reverse direction.

## What D2 settles and what it does not

D2 settles the ordinary sharing destination: organization CRM availability for
staff generally, with optional attention to deliberately selected staff. It
removes the need for a
selected-collaborator access model for ordinary shared contacts.

It does not select an unrestricted “every employee can read every sensitive
field” policy, mandatory staff assignment, private-history publication,
automatic sharing on an event, canonical conflict-resolution authority,
newsletter enrollment, exceptional access to unshared preparation or a
departure/retention policy. Optional notification still needs its exact source
recipient/channel/outcome contract; notification recipients are attention
recipients, not a newly restrictive staff visibility list.

“Unshare” is not automatically approved by D2. Once canonical facts are admitted
and staff have used them, deleting the preparation cannot withdraw independent
CRM truth, erase accepted work or promise recall of already seen information.
If the product later offers correction/withdrawal/restriction, it must use the
existing source owners and accurately describe its effects. Do not invent that
lifecycle merely to make a two-state toggle symmetrical.
