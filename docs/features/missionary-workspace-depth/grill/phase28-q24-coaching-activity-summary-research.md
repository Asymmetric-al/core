## The one choice

Maya has chosen personal activity targets. Her workspace shows **5 recorded calls
against a target of 8**, and **2 conversations against a target of 3**, for a clearly
identified period. Jordan is her invited, read-only coach.

**Should Maya be able to deliberately share an eligible activity-versus-target
summary with Jordan?**

- **A — Allow optional activity summaries (recommended).** When the organization
  permits it, Maya deliberately includes the eligible measures, chosen target
  quantities and recorded counts for clearly shown periods. Jordan sees a
  read-only summary without supporter names, message bodies or individual activity
  records. Choosing a target alone does not share it.
- **B — Keep activity numbers personal initially.** Do not offer this quantitative
  coaching module in the first release. Coaching continues with voluntary
  qualitative check-ins and other independently shared information, including
  the already-selected optional support summaries.

**Recommendation: A.** It connects Maya's chosen plan to a useful coaching
conversation while leaving her in control. B reduces numeric disclosure and
potential pressure, but expressly narrows the roadmap's intended first-release
activity-versus-target coaching capability. Both keep qualitative check-ins and
ordinary coaching; neither permits compulsory reporting or a performance score.

The figures and scenario are illustrative, not recommended quantities or evidence
from a real missionary interview. Five calls include the two conversations; these
are not seven activities. Recorded counts are not a claim about all actual work.

## Original boundaries, now reviewed after A selection

1. **One optional module in the existing sharing review.** Use the existing
   organization ceiling, deliberate missionary inclusion and invited coach
   relationship. Do not create another coaching invitation, role or access system.
   Neither target creation nor accepting an invitation automatically adds it.
2. **Preview the exact permitted meaning.** Show canonical measures, quantities,
   recorded counts and exact dates/scope. Personal target labels may contain
   private context; they are not automatically shareable. Source coverage and
   currentness remain truthful. The product must not promise the coach every
   number Maya can see personally.
3. **No donor-level access by inference.** Supporter names, message bodies,
   private notes/care and individual activity history remain outside this module.
   Aggregate-only access must not require raw source-row grants or an enabled
   View activity action. Small counts, dates and comparisons still require
   qualified disclosure; removing names is not an anonymity guarantee.
4. **Keep purposes distinct.** A chosen quantity is Maya's optional planning
   denominator; the count is eligible recorded evidence. Missing/unshared history
   is not zero actual work. No ranking, effort inference, compulsory report,
   automatic task, coach editing, recipient contact or financial effect follows.
5. **Do not smuggle in history or wider comparison.** D10 financial history does
   not authorize activity history. Exact dates, future updates, source corrections,
   grant duration/revocation, cross-period inference and multi-context composition
   need a bounded review after the answer. Existing D8–D11 rights are preserved;
   no new historical/future-period entitlement is inferred here.
6. **Reuse the real owners.** P28 Goal Versions own target meaning; P9/P27/P6/P17
   retain actual activity/outcome/participation; P3/P10/P12 own the permitted
   projection and current action. No copied coaching activity ledger, new source
   access role, shadow permission model or vendor is justified.

The strongest alternative is genuinely personal numeric planning with voluntary
newly authored qualitative context for coaching. It avoids disclosing counts, but
cannot deliver the same transparent activity-versus-target conversation. If B is
selected, record the roadmap narrowing explicitly rather than leaving a promised
first-release module silently unimplemented.
