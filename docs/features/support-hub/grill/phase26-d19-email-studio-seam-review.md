## Inputs and outputs at the seam

### 1. Intake commands

The reviewer supplies a permitted action, exact source/expected version and authorized destination choice where applicable. The server derives tenant, actual acting profile/membership, capability, decision time and current source gates. The intake owner commits the decision and durable canonical-admission obligation; its output is an attributable pending/blocked/admitted result. It supplies no outgoing subject/body, address, template, From, Reply-To or Resend credential.

Release is not a promise that no email can follow: actual admission may satisfy D13's already approved confirmation rules. The UI's existing conditional line is accurate: “The usual new-request confirmation may follow if this message is eligible.” Dismissal has no sender notification. Receiving retries never pass through an outgoing template just to look like communication work.

### 2. In-product review attention

D19 supplies only the closed source episode/condition, source revision and applicability, independently authorized recipient-role inputs, typed safe facts, typed product destination and bounded occurrence identity permitted by its qualified binding. Phase 17 resolves the exact contract/presentation and recipient authority through the existing registry; Phase 6 records the local occurrence. Do not mint a generic support-notification key, accept raw template IDs, or borrow the existing system-message delivery-repair key for intake review merely because both require attention.

This is a required owner qualification already stated in D19-R23/P32, not a request to reconsider whether attention exists. During later authorized specification/implementation, register and prove the exact bounded meaning(s), source-actionable end rule, occurrence/recipient identities and safe fact/destination schema before activation. This review deliberately supplies no unratified physical key or API name.

Use `presentation.source_actionable_then_recent_90d@1` under the existing Phase 17 contract. Reading clears unread/badge state only; required active attention cannot be archived away. Source resolution ends actionable treatment. Authorized recent presentation then follows the existing ninety-day ceiling, shortened by source/privacy/access restrictions; it retains only a minimized safe projection, never the held body or a body snippet. Expiry/revocation is enforced on reads even if cleanup is late. Staff must not be told the mail was reviewed merely because an attention card was read.

D19's grouping by tenant/inbox/condition is necessary but not sufficient: the inherited contract also separates recipient role, privacy boundary, meaning and genuine source episode. Current eligibility is re-proved for list/count/search/render/destination. An oversight recipient may receive qualified coverage/health information without message-body or hidden-inbox access. New meaningful source transitions create their proper new occurrence, rather than mutating old attention to renew its lifetime or unread status.

Review expiry and the later missed-review obligation are different source conditions. When held content expires, its release action becomes inert. If it expired without a decision, the source-owned operational review remains actionable until its typed cause/response is recorded under D19-R28. Completing that obligation neither revives content nor rewrites the original miss. Dismissed-and-later-expired mail does not fabricate this obligation. This prevents a forever-actionable expired-mail card and a false “resolved” condition caused by a notification click.

### 3. Automatic confirmation after admission

D13 remains the sole business producer for eligible New request confirmation. Its original qualified first provider receipt anchors `confirmation_utility_ends_at`; actual preparation/dispatch must remain strictly before original receipt plus fifteen minutes and satisfy every inherited gate. Review decision, release, retrieval retry, corrected destination, policy edit and return from dismissal do not restart it or create another confirmation. A late release can admit legitimate Support work while sending no stale confirmation.

Email Studio provides the distinct qualified **Support request received** meaning/template and publication/preparation contract. Inbox settings retain Preview/Edit in Email Studio with a contextual return route. Editing/publishing that template sends nothing, approves no incoming message, activates no inbox, changes no source eligibility and does not replay previous inputs. The content confirms receipt only; it cannot truthfully claim human review, target attainment, a refund, receipt correction or a response-time promise.

### 4. Human work after admission

Once genuinely admitted, normal Support conversation authoring uses D18 My replies and Shared library. The same Email Studio source is consumed, not synchronized into another library. Exact eligible structured wording is copied into the current authorized draft; recipient data remains draft/preparation context rather than generic shared preview content. My Save, Shared Publish, Insert and Replace/Undo remain their separate effects.

Human replies are outside the system-message catalog but inside the shared P17 validator/compiler/presentation/preparation contract qualified by D4/D18. P17 exact ready preparation precedes Support's atomic local reply/work admission; the resulting durable dispatch goes through P6 and tenant Resend. Notes use shared structured validation without external preparation/P6 send/Resend. Intake-reviewer responsibility confers neither replying access nor library maintainer/publication rights.

### 5. Resend receiving versus sending

The provider name is shared; the authorities and effects are not. Receiving verifies its exact qualified tenant/provider connection and accepted-source evidence before payload retrieval/replay. Phase 6 outbound obtains the current secret from the exact owner behind its frozen safe delivery profile, re-proves send authority and handles provider evidence at its actual specificity. Review workers cannot reuse receiving credentials or a mailbox address as outbound authority, nor fall back to a platform account.

Phase 6 records one canonical communication effect per admitted recipient copy linked to the shared submission where D2/D4 group semantics apply. It never sends the whole To/Cc group once per member. An inbound review decision and a local staff attention event do not become a donor communication occurrence. Unknown provider outcome stays unknown until qualified evidence resolves it; review actions cannot rekey or rerender an already possible send.
