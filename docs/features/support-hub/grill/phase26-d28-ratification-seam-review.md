## Exact accepted invitation controls

Off is the initial state. When deliberately enabled, the starting random selection is **25%**, with an integer control from **1–100%**. Each qualifying completion candidate freezes its setting; the single original opportunity records one unbiased persisted draw at maturity. Existing candidates do not silently adopt later percentage changes. There is no reroll, replacement sampling, historical catch-up, per-handler invitation choice or exact-quota promise.

The quiet interval is **24 elapsed hours** after a genuine completion. The send-by is **due_at + 24 elapsed hours**. Current merged-work checks preserve one current chance without erasing independent original histories. A reopening before sampling can invalidate that timer; after sampling it cannot revive a selected result or create another draw. No response endings and other ineligible handling remain excluded by their actual source meaning, not by a generic Resolved-row test.

The independent tenant-mailbox gap is **30 elapsed days**. Concurrent candidates serialize at the real endpoint/admission boundary. A still-permitted or in-flight provider crossing holds the guard. A different genuine opportunity may pass only after P6 qualifies that no old future/in-flight crossing remains and 30 days have elapsed from its latest possible crossing bound. Irreducible historical uncertainty does not suppress a mailbox forever, and a timeout cannot release it early. This never permits replay or replacement of the old effect.

Off and inbox removal immediately fence all new and definitely-unsubmitted invitations. Possible/accepted submission remains truthful and reconciles. Already issued response and preference actions keep their own original lifetimes unless their applicable privacy/safety authority revokes them. Off→On cannot restore a backlog, renew credentials or clear a recorded P3 opt-out. These controls do not make D5's local ending a mail sender or block an ordinary authorized Support reply.

## Two protected actions in one message

The accepted contract accurately reflects the governing P17 sources. Its [catalog definition has finite plural actions](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-17-system-messages-template-management.md#L568), while the reference producer input is still [singular at line 2280](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-17-system-messages-template-management.md#L2280) and the executable binding has [one action issuer field](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-17-system-message-executable-manifest.md#L773). D28 explicitly qualifies its fixed named pair through the adapter/DTO/manifest, protected presentation and immutable prepared evidence. It does not misrepresent this as already implemented arbitrary multi-action support.

The **Give feedback** action and the **Stop feedback invitations** action have separate issuers, purposes, recipient bindings, credentials, expiry/revocation/Used states and session handling. A primary response token cannot operate the secondary preference, or vice versa. Both use the one existing generic D6 doorway: inert/non-enumerating selector GET/HEAD, fragment verifier handling, deliberate same-origin Continue POST, then the appropriate explicit action POST. No rating is selected or written by opening a link, and the generic first page reveals no protected tenant/action/resource fact. There is no alternate survey URL protocol, score-in-URL, auto-POST or account-creation requirement.

The fixed form asks the neutral satisfaction question with three equally weighted labelled choices, no default selection and an optional plain-text comment bounded to 2,000 Unicode scalar values. A requester edits before Submit; one final logical response is admitted. Same-payload retry returns its receipt, changed replay cannot overwrite Used authority, and a forwarded/used link does not redisplay prior private comments. A valid response is neither provider acceptance evidence nor verified CRM speech.

P17's Party/contact-only action wording receives only the narrow qualified no-Party feedback/preference branches needed here. No private/financial action, portal resource, transcript, CRM record or identity verification is granted. The exact fixed instrument and locale remain distinct from editable professional invitation prose.
