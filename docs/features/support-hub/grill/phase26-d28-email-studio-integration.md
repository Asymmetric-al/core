## Preparation and submission sequence

1. Support qualifies the mature sampled opportunity, recipient, current policy/source/topology and finite utility. It never assumes a known CRM email or a Resolved row is enough.
2. P3 resolves actual current feedback contactability and owner endpoint key. A missing no-Party preference/action qualification blocks readiness; a transactional label cannot bypass it.
3. Support registers the exact response issuance; its frozen expiry is issued_at +14 elapsed days or an earlier owner ceiling. Response authority is inactive until the associated effect may have submitted. Distinct P3 preference issuance has a proposed 90-elapsed-day fixed lifetime from its own issue time, bounded by its own current contact/privacy authority. Its selector/verifier/session purpose is independent. Rating Used/expiry, conversation reopen/content expiry/deletion and ordinary Off do not revoke it. It can only stop feedback contact, never read private data, restore a preference or increase contact. A used response receipt cannot reconstruct/reissue the preference URL from purged P17 material; the original email’s footer remains independently usable. This is a bounded product judgment, not a norm. The grant cannot be a raw URL or reused response authority. The 90 days expires only the preference credential, never the resulting recorded opt-out. The P3 preference persists under its independently authorized lifecycle; policy Off→On, CRM merges, key rotation and new invitations cannot clear it or opt anyone back in.
4. P17 compiles the immutable purpose-qualified message and all action/material dependencies. Its optional-feedback prepared-material ceiling is no later than source send-by and earlier source/action/privacy limits. No live CMS or recipient rerender occurs at dispatch. The governed Reply-To and D1/D25 correlation keep an ordinary reply connected to the original permitted Support context; source unavailability follows existing safe intake, never reconstructs a transcript or infers a Party by email. A human email reply is distinct from the protected response form and may legitimately reopen work under D1.
5. Immediately before P6 crosses into possible submission, current Support policy/source/inbox/topology fences and the owner endpoint reservation must still permit the exact intent. Existing source-fence-before-intent locking is preserved; the new gate must be explicit conformance, not P6 reverse-reading arbitrary Support business state.
6. Possible submission consumes the opportunity and mailbox guard, even if the provider outcome is unknown. Off after that boundary cannot recall it. P6 reconciles the same permanent effect and frozen request; no blind retry/new key. Definitely-unsubmitted work expires at send-by, with no catch-up.
7. Qualified provider outcomes trigger P17's existing finality/material disposal. The canonical source email/response has its own finite content custody. Recent sent copy excludes action targets/verifiers/remote references and cannot be a resend archive.

P6 may reconcile uncertainty beyond delivery utility; it may not start a fresh optional send after utility expires. A response through an issued link is distinct evidence and cannot rewrite provider acceptance. The 30-day contact guard treats active possible crossings conservatively; hidden ambiguity does not free a slot early. Qualify a finite P6 latest-possible-crossing bound: after no future/in-flight crossing can occur and 30 days have elapsed from that bound, different genuine work may qualify despite irreducibly unknown historical outcome. Same-effect dedupe remains; no permanent endpoint block solely because provider history is unknown.
