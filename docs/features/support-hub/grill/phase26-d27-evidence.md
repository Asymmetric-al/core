## Independent review and reconciliation

Three independent reviewers checked source/data/publication, the entire staff/curator UX, and Tiptap/P17/P6 ownership. Their final actual-document passes are appended to the linked reports. Root reconciled the consequential disagreements and omissions:

1. **Stable source proof:** current published/slug/null-Site code is not a qualified shortcut around the full owner contracts.
2. **Public-by-link search:** selected Shared-by-link body text needs complete staff-purpose lookup while ordinary public discovery remains unchanged. A public-search first-page filter would be wrong.
3. **Passive preview and exact insertion:** no forms/widgets or full CMS editor; Link selected text preserves staff wording and stale selection cannot redirect an insertion.
4. **Redirects:** same-Page/Site/exact-locale qualified continuity may preserve old href. Different-Page public redirection is not inherited curation approval.
5. **Restoration split:** Removed finder entries stay removed; safe already-copied links may requalify after current owner public/safety proof. Fenced or terminal prepared effects do not automatically revive.
6. **Byte custody:** immutable message meaning/effect identity does not mean indefinite prepared-envelope storage; P17 qualified-acceptance purge and body-free reconciliation remain authoritative.
7. **Honest UX states:** incomplete search coverage is shown to staff; removal copy promises only that it does not change publication/links, not that they are currently available. A failed Guidance insertion cannot certify that a reply was not sent by another action/tab.

These are required amendments within A, not scope expansions. The root clauses, companion lifecycle and proof groups now express them consistently. They strengthen the accepted Email Studio seam rather than introduce another sending or content platform.
