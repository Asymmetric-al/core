## Decisions, inferences and tested assumptions

- **Verified repository fact:** owner rules and source gaps above. **Verified vendor fact:** behavior within cited current edition/docs, with dates and limitations preserved.
- **Reasonable inference:** a short optional form and separate contact gap reduce avoidable repeated asks; the actual response/satisfaction effect in Asym is unmeasured.
- **Product judgments:**25% enabled preset; fixed 24h quiet interval;30 day mailbox gap;24h post-due delivery utility;14 day frozen response issuance;90 day independent stop-feedback preference authority; one three-choice question; one final response; no reminders;20 response operating caution and performance qualification fixtures. Only 24h has a direct Zendesk default precedent; none is presented as universal legal/empirical optimum.
- **User-established facts:** tenants support donor care and other legitimate staff/missionary needs; the user wants random sampling and full invitation Off. Actual tenant volumes, jurisdiction-specific contact basis and field usability are not known.
- **Source/owner decisions:** no-Party action and feedback-specific contactability are mandatory explicit qualifications before On, not unresolved choices handed to a developer.

[Reference experiments](phase26-d28-reference-experiments.json) run 118 synthetic checks. They exhaust all 101 percentage thresholds 0–100 across 10,000 possible draw values, test 25% edges, durable retry predicates, modeled current gates and a negative control where two preview-only workers both pass a cooldown. In 10,000 synthetic 20-opportunity trials at 25%, mean selected was 4.9962 against expectation 5. With a purely hypothetical 20% response probability,20 opportunities at 25% invitation probability have about 35.85% chance of receiving no responses. That hypothetical rate is not a donor-care estimate; it demonstrates why a low-volume tenant may rationally select a higher percentage without overriding the gap.

These are reference predicates/arithmetic, not actual SQL locking, production randomness, provider, browser or respondent tests. Independent reviews plus [documentary validation](phase26-d28-validation.json) verify consistency/preservation/source pins, not a deployed safe feature. P01–P40 remain required and unexecuted at this grooming stage.
