## Permanent algorithm and lifecycle

The root's proposed product settings are **Off by default**, **25% initially when enabled**, editable integer **1–100%**, selected inbox scope, **24 hours of quiet completed work**, **30 days between invitations to the same qualified mailbox within the tenant**, **14-day original issued-link lifetime**, and a **send-by cutoff 24 hours after the quiet-period due instant**. These numbers are product judgments, not universal vendor or legal requirements. Percentage is a selection probability; recipient limits and later source failures can reduce actual sends further.

1. A genuine qualifying completion while the policy is On creates a **completion-generation candidate**. It freezes the actual completion evidence, inbox/scope, policy revision/percentage and due instant. No random draw or recipient outreach occurs yet. Historical work is not backfilled by enabling the policy.
2. Reopening before the 24-hour maturity invalidates that generation's timer. A later genuine completion can create a new quiet candidate because no final opportunity has yet been sampled. Generic edits, guide use, labels or repeated same-state writes cannot reset its clock.
3. At quiet maturity, re-prove neutral human-help completion, current scope/contactability/source safety, current handling and absence of an already consumed opportunity. Materialize at most **one feedback opportunity per original conversation lifetime**. A delayed worker uses frozen source/policy evidence and cannot send after the fixed send-by cutoff.
4. Persist one server-generated uniform integer draw in `[0, 9999]` under the unique opportunity key. Selection is `draw < percentage * 100`. The caller never supplies the draw or changes the frozen percentage. A duplicate/racing worker reads the committed result. Selected, not selected and later cancelled/suppressed are durable dispositions; no retry, policy edit, reopen or merge rerolls them.
5. A selected opportunity passes current recipient/cooldown and prepared-message gates. The **tenant + qualified canonical mailbox** contact guard is checked atomically at the existing submission-possible boundary, including current reservations by another eligible conversation. A cooldown-blocked opportunity is skipped; it is not parked for a surprise email thirty days later.
6. P17 prepares the exact invitation and registered feedback action; Support/P6 admit and submit through their existing permanent identities and current fences. At possible submission, reserve the mailbox gap and establish the issued-invitation cohort. Unknown provider success keeps that reservation; only qualified proof of definite non-submission can release an unconsumed reservation. A network timeout is not that proof.
7. The recipient edits locally before deliberate **Submit**. Admit **one final logical response per invitation**. Exact same-payload retry returns its receipt; a changed payload conflicts with a privacy-safe already-submitted outcome. No bearer score replacement, public echo of a prior comment or staff score editing is introduced. A separately authorized privacy/correction action has its own attribution and never pretends to be the respondent.

This distinction between temporary completion candidates and final sampled opportunities avoids both premature consumption on a short reopen and repeated random chances after sampling. It adds no campaign/list builder, survey DSL, telephony, new CRM or independent queue.

## Material findings and exact clauses

Severity/likelihood are conditional engineering judgments rather than measured incident rates. Each finding changes or narrows the selected A and has an independently testable correction.

### FD01 — Stable sampling, not worker randomness

**Concern: Yes — High / High.** Retrying `random() < percentage` gradually selects everyone; policy-version keys and merges can likewise create new chances. This invalidates the intended percentage and may contact skipped requesters.

> Persist candidate generations separately from final opportunities. An opportunity is uniquely owned by tenant and immutable original-conversation identity, not current root, Party, policy version, worker run or HTTP request. Commit the unbiased draw, frozen percentage, source eligibility evidence, disposition, audit and continuation atomically. Draws outside the integer range or caller-controlled draws/percentages are rejected. One opportunity cannot be sampled twice.

**Proof:** 1/25/100 percent boundary draws; duplicate worker and lost response; percentage change before/after maturity; reopen before maturity versus after draw; policy Off/On and deployment/hash-version change never produce a second opportunity.

### FD02 — Full Off is an immediate sending control

**Concern: Yes — High / High.** An Off toggle may only stop new cron jobs while queued invites continue; re-enabling may release a hidden backlog.

> Off controls **Automatic invitations**: stop new candidates/opportunities and immediately fence all definitely-unsubmitted invitations in its scope. Cancel their pending timers/continuations; re-enable never revives them, backfills old completions or sends delayed remainders/reminders. Already issued or possibly submitted invitations keep their original response window unless separately revoked for current source/privacy/safety reasons. Explain that existing links remain usable beside the Off control and its effect review; retain historical responses under their actual custody.

Ordinary percentage changes apply only to new completion candidates. Inbox-scope removal is restrictive for outstanding scoped candidates/invites; expanding scope creates no old-work backfill. Old policy bytes and outcomes remain historical.

**Proof:** Off races timer maturity, preparation, P6 dispatch and response submission; before/after the existing submission boundary have correct outcomes. Off cannot recall possible external mail; re-enable does not restore cancelled credentials or expired authority.

### FD03 — Neutral completion and finite utility

**Concern: Yes — High / High.** Every Resolved row, a D26 manual brief or an old cleanup batch may become an invitation. Work can reopen during the delay, or a late worker can blast obsolete requests.

> Use genuine source-owned human-help completion and admitted email evidence, not generic timestamps, inferred sentiment, guide use, expected praise or resolution count. D5 No response, duplicate/continuation/correction endings, unreviewed current recovery, still-owed Support work, manual-only no-email records and ambiguous required source evidence do not become successful help. No newly qualified external email endpoint is invented. Recheck current source at maturity, preparation/admission and submission. Due/send-by instants are materialized UTC facts; late past-cutoff work expires rather than accumulating.

**Proof:** No response, only acknowledgment/system mail, unsent reply, reopened work, moved scope, delayed processing and manual-only input. Real human handling need not prove a successful financial outcome or a satisfied requester; its exact source eligibility remains neutral.

### FD04 — One qualified recipient, no hidden primary Party

**Concern: Yes — Critical / High.** Selecting the first related Party, CRM primary email, all reply-all participants or every merged sender can disclose or multiply outreach. D9 intentionally has no primary-Party convention.

> The source resolver must yield one exact currently qualified external recipient authority from actual admitted correspondence. No list-order/assignee/CRM-email heuristic or automatic recipient fanout is permitted. If the source facts cannot uniquely qualify a recipient for this feedback purpose, record a safe exclusion instead of asking staff at every closure or guessing. Preserve Party/contact versus no-Party exclusive branches and source revisions. Possession of a feedback invitation proves only its narrow capability, not the human writer's CRM identity or organizational representation.

A verified delivery endpoint is not necessarily one human; shared mailboxes remain supported only under the honest invitation-possession attribution. Public response pages expose no private conversation, participant list, CRM identity, gift details or prior comments.

**Proof:** no Party, multiple related Parties, shared address, CC/reply-all, represented organization, recipient correction/removal, forwarded link, source merge and current permission/contactability loss.

### FD05 — Shared contactability and mailbox gap

**Concern: Yes — Critical / High.** A feedback-specific opt-out table or Party-keyed cooldown bypasses existing suppression or contacts the same mailbox repeatedly through different identities.

> Reuse P3/P6 current consent, contactability and `email_suppressions` under the exact feedback purpose; tenant enablement is not recipient permission. No-Party cannot bypass absolute contact floors or ambiguity in required owner evidence. Do not label optional feedback required transactional mail to evade an opt-out. Use an owner-qualified tenant-scoped opaque canonical-mailbox key for the thirty-day gate across inboxes and Party/no-Party branches; do not guess provider aliases, remove dots/plus suffixes or use current Party ID as the mailbox key.

The contact-key/suppression owner must support a no-Party endpoint without creating CRM identity. Durable feedback history stores references, not a second plaintext address list. Keys remain potentially identifying and require access/retention/rotation rules; do not introduce an unreviewed permanent email hash warehouse.

**Proof:** two sampled conversations with the same endpoint race; two inboxes/Party records share a mailbox; ambiguity, opt-out, hard suppression or provider block appears after prepare; confirmed no-submit versus uncertain submit preserves the right gap and no second provider call.

### FD06 — Sampling does not replace throughput control

**Concern: Yes — High / Medium.** Twenty-five percent of a large batch is still a burst; a worker outage can release many due messages at once.

> Existing P6 tenant/provider pacing, fairness and actual send budgets govern feedback, with ordinary/required operational mail protected according to their owners. D28 adds no competing provider-rate limiter or percentage-based quota. Bound candidate scans and concurrent work; respect due/send-by expiry and no-backfill rules. Show selection chance honestly, not “exactly 25 of every 100 people” or a guaranteed volume cap.

**Proof:** high-volume tenant, many tenants sharing infrastructure, delayed worker, 100% selection, provider throttling and finite send-by expiration without starving receipt/help delivery or dumping a backlog.

### FD07 — D10 merges preserve identity and stop duplicate outreach

**Concern: Yes — High / Medium.** A mutable root key can reissue mail after Merge/Undo, while flattening history can erase two real invitations.

> Preserve original candidate/opportunity/invitation IDs and historical decisions. Merge/Undo alone creates no candidate, opportunity, draw or email. Current merged-work membership is an additional guarded dedupe check: if independent pending opportunities join, retain one deterministic eligible candidate and suppress the rest; if any member invitation may already have submitted, suppress remaining pending invitations. Two messages submitted before merge remain two truthful historical facts; never recall, erase or rewrite them. Separation cannot revive suppressed work or reset consumed original opportunities.

Use existing current topology/authorization guards and a complete bounded component query. No forever-merged pseudo-CRM identity, component hash as permanent key or per-message duplicate guess is added.

**Proof:** merge before maturity, two sampled pending opportunities, one or two possible submissions, Undo afterward, topology race and current-root cache lag. Historical reporting never recalculates old event counts from today's forest.

### FD08 — Protected response action needs an explicit no-Party contract

**Concern: Yes — Critical / High.** Ordinary links, URL parameters, scanners or unrelated sessions may mutate a rating or expose private context. Current P17 protected-action wording has a Party/contact assumption that delivery's no-Party branch alone does not resolve.

> Register D28's narrow feedback producer, invitation recipient-authority branch, action purpose/postcondition, source fence, original issue/expiry, revocation, one-response replay semantics, assurance and recovery route in the existing P17 protected-action contract. Explicitly reconcile the no-Party action branch across P17/manifest/OpenSpec/P6 before activation. Reuse the fixed shared selector + fragment verifier doorway; no second token/login/redirect mechanism. GET/HEAD/scanner/prefetch is inert. Deliberate same-origin POST re-proves current issuance/source authority, verifier, expiry, privacy/safety and one-response state before committing.

No forced donor account or fake Party is needed for the narrow feedback act. Tokens/PII never enter path/query, logs, analytics, P17 Recent sent copy or public caches. Same-origin/CSRF/Origin checks, code-owned return route and P17 exact no-store/referrer controls remain required.

**Proof:** scanner and image fetch, forged/cross-tenant selector, missing verifier, expired/revoked token, forwarded link, simultaneous submit, wrong payload retry, host/open-redirect attack and mixed-version verifier rotation.

### FD09 — One final response and durable attention

**Concern: Yes — High / High.** Duplicate submit inflates counts, bearer update lets another mailbox user overwrite a score, or committed critical feedback is lost before staff attention is queued.

> One invitation admits one final logical response with trusted acceptance time, exact question/version/scale and bounded validated score/comment. Exact same-payload retry returns the original privacy-safe receipt; a changed payload cannot overwrite it. Public terminal pages do not echo prior private comment/rating. Staff cannot edit the respondent's score or speak as that respondent. Actual privacy/correction actions retain separate actor/reason/source evidence. Response plus required source-owned review/attention intent commit atomically; a notification failure cannot discard the response or its review obligation.

Do not automatically convert ratings to Resolved/Open, a CRM note, a new customer ticket or successful-help evidence. Any follow-up is the existing currently authorized Support action based on review, without a second workflow platform.

**Proof:** double click, timeout, competing different payloads, abuse input, review-worker failure, deletion/correction after receipt, browser Back and no previous-response disclosure.

### FD10 — Fences must cover Off and source changes without eventual fanout

**Concern: Yes — Critical / Medium.** Cancelling every queued invite asynchronously leaves a window after Off/reopen/revocation where an old intent crosses the provider boundary.

> Qualify D28's current tenant/inbox sending authority epoch plus exact opportunity/source state through the existing producer/material and P6 submission contract. Off/scope removal and source invalidation deny at the authoritative boundary independently of eventual cancellation cleanup. Preserve the existing fence-before-intent ordering and semantic slot; no worker may reinterpret old frozen input or invent a new key after ambiguous external success. Percentage-only policy changes neither revoke nor reroll existing frozen candidates.

This is a required narrow integration extension, not permission to add another queue or ask P6 to infer Support business meaning. The final response action has its own source/expiry authority: Off stops invitations but does not break already issued, still-valid links.

**Proof:** every ordering of Off/reopen/privacy change against preparation, intent admission, submission-possible and response acceptance; exactly one old effect or safe suppression, never a blind second send.

### FD11 — Constraints, RLS and actual command authority

**Concern: Yes — Critical / High if existing CRUD is copied.** Caller-supplied actor, percentage, source, respondent or state fields can bypass sampling, forge feedback or cross tenants.

> Policy versions, candidate generations, opportunity, invitation, response, mailbox reservation and control/audit relationships use non-null tenant and actual owner-compatible identifiers. Composite local FKs bind each child to the same tenant/source; Support conversation IDs retain their actual TEXT semantics. Enforce policy percentage bounds, valid lifecycle/time order, unique original opportunity, one logical invitation per opportunity, unique final response per invitation, and immutable draw/source/publication/question/actor facts. Transport/Party/no-Party authority follows the existing exclusive arc; no arbitrary recipient JSON bag.

> Derive policy actor, source actor, tenant, draw, timestamps, respondent capability and audit attribution from trusted context. Expose purpose-qualified commands/projections; raw control/audit/address/credential tables are not ordinary browser-readable or writable. Review effective SELECT/USING/WITH CHECK, column grants, permissive-policy OR, views, functions/RPC execute/search paths and service jobs together. Existing broad Support staff CRUD is not a security baseline for this source. PostgreSQL's implicit USING reuse means missing explicit WITH CHECK alone is not a finding.

**Proof:** positive and negative API/Data API/view/RPC/service paths; scope-changing update; forged score/draw/actor; duplicate source effects; wrong-parent child; direct audit mutation; credential reads; hidden constraint-error inference; actual transaction isolation under contention.

### FD12 — Reporting denominators must describe the real sampled flow

**Concern: Yes — High / High.** A rate can exceed 100%, merge can improve results retrospectively or missing/declined responses can be reported as satisfaction.

> Keep separately defined cohorts/counts: matured neutrally eligible opportunities, sampled selected/not selected, current contact-limited/cancelled/expired/suppressed, invitations issued at submission-possible, confirmed accepted, submission uncertain and unique responses. Define response rate as responded invitations divided by the same issued-invitation cohort, showing uncertainty separately; do not divide all responses by only confirmed provider acceptance. A valid response may arrive while P6 remains indeterminate. Neither issued nor accepted means delivered/read.

Freeze actual policy percentage, inbox/source, time basis and question version used for each event; current merge roots/handlers cannot rewrite historical facts. Cohort selection, source access, totals, drilldowns and exports use current joint permissions before counts. No-response is absence of a submitted rating, not negative/positive sentiment. Low/voluntary samples and eligibility bias remain explicit; no staff ranking, effort or donor loyalty fact is inferred.

**Proof:** response with indeterminate send; zero denominator; all selected but cooldown-blocked; changing percentages/questions/inboxes; current merge/Undo; privacy withholding and lack of access; unique numerator despite retries.

### FD13 — Privacy, retention and CRM remain source-owned

**Concern: Yes — Critical / Medium.** Feedback comments or address keys become a permanent shadow transcript/CRM sentiment profile, or a response revives expired case content.

> Support owns feedback source/response and permitted review facts. D9/D26 exposes them through the same canonical currently authorized Support detail; no Party creation, relationship fanout, duplicated CRM sentiment field, Activity/last-contact or financial effect is caused by sampling/response. A feedback response is not a fake email/P6 inbound event. Declare finite custody separately for candidates/opportunity controls, active/revoked action material, submitted score/comment, mailbox-gap keys, consent/suppression references, prepared provider material, source review, aggregates/audit and backup/restore barriers. Retain minimal consumed-opportunity/tombstone evidence while the original source can otherwise be re-enrolled; dispose it only under a contract that cannot restore old opportunity authority. Keep a mailbox reservation/key only as long as its contact-prevention purpose and actual audit/records rules require. P17 acceptance purge and Recent-copy exclusions remain unchanged. **Proof:** redaction/expiry during an issued response window; token disposal versus submitted record; original conversation disposal; mailbox key rotation/deletion without cooldown bypass; CRM merge/unlink/revocation; restore after Off/revocation/expiry; no feedback corpus enters D27-C Staff guides, public search or AI.

### FD14 — Additive activation, no surprise migration

**Concern: Yes — High / High.** New workers may interpret old resolved records as eligible or old serializers may lose no-Party/action/fence semantics.

> Qualify source eligibility/policy controls, P3 recipient/contact key/suppression, P17 action/no-Party/preparation and P6 occurrence/fence/pacing before activation. Default Off; enablement has a reviewed effective point and no historical backfill. Deploy compatible readers/writers and direct-grant restrictions first. Old/new schema or unknown contract generations fail at the affected boundary; pending accepted effects reconcile under original semantics. Roll forward after new source data unless rollback proves all state/authority/tombstone rules. No raw provider testing or live invitations are performed during grooming.

**Proof:** Off legacy install, policy enable after years of history, mixed versions, duplicate delivery/replay, interrupted purge and compatible kill switch. Verify source counts and hashes; no fake imported historical consent, draw or respondent identity.

## Required evidence and operating response

All FD01–FD14 proof groups remain required and unexecuted. Test actual Supabase grants/RLS and concurrent transactions, not only application mocks. PostgreSQL documents Read Committed anomalies and serialization retry requirements; an application pre-check without a locked/conditional unique effect is insufficient. [PostgreSQL isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html), [policy semantics](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html). The existing one-ticket invitation behavior in HubSpot is relevant comparative evidence for bounded repeat contact, not proof that its CES model or timings are right for Asym. [HubSpot support surveys](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/customer-feedback/how-do-i-send-a-customer-support-survey).

No correctness gap is deferred to monitoring. After proof: one duplicate/rerolled opportunity → Support source owner fences affected issuance and reconciles original identities; one invite crossing a current Off/contact/privacy denial → P3/Support/P6 owners stop that sending path and prove the race repair; one private response/token leak → security/source owner contains it and requalifies; any mailbox-gap violation → contactability/delivery owner reconciles the reservation boundary; any missing committed response review intent → Support owner recovers durable attention; first breach of the registered send-by/purge/resource budget → platform/source owner applies bounded recovery, with expired work never released as a backlog. These signals require named owners and tested thresholds in the root's final controls.

**Final synthesis:** keep the owner separation and simple settings, settle candidate versus sampled opportunity, exact mailbox/no-Party action authority and current Off/source fencing first; capture the stated lifecycle, one final response, reporting cohorts, finite custody and end-to-end proofs in the future authorized specification. This is a complete proposed data direction, not proof the system already implements it. The root's final requirement package resolves any remaining exploratory alternatives; nothing here selects a second CRM, generic survey builder, new message provider or AI analysis.
