## Independent synthesis and adopted corrections

Three independent reviewers produced the [authorization/data/CRM review](phase26-d19-auth-data-review.md), [lifecycle review](phase26-d19-lifecycle-review.md) and [UX research/review](phase26-d19-ux-research.md), then pressure-tested the root's complete R01–R30/P01–P42/O01–O08 draft. All recommended accepting A with required amendments. Their raw proposals remain as historical evidence; the root's corrected decision governs where synthesis differs.

Adopted corrections include stable input admission identity across cancel/re-release; current deciding-actor authority at deferred admission; global identity versus tenant membership distinction; read-only CRM lookup versus actual admitted correspondence; conditional D13 confirmation copy; pending **Cancel release** versus dismissed **Return to review**; separate pending-release and technical views; historical Dismissed versus expired content; an operational completion path for missed-expiry attention; qualified schedule variants rather than arbitrary huge finite periods; a separate bounded unknown-receipt recovery class; and Phase26 semantic ownership with qualified records/private-byte execution.

The root deliberately chooses a **48-hour** approaching-expiry indicator rather than the lifecycle review's initial 24-hour suggestion. It leaves more time before irreversible ordinary expiry, particularly across a day boundary, while producing one grouped condition rather than more notification types. This is an explicit product judgment. Ordinary review attention remains 24 elapsed hours, including weekends. The initial 24-hour suggestion in the independent report is superseded, not a second rule.

Other numeric proposals are a 14-day ordinary schedule, separate at-most14-day normal restricted timing-recovery access with 24-hour review, 25-row default/50-row maximum and bulk cap, and five-minute stuck-release detection. These are bounded product/operational decisions awaiting ratification, not claimed universal best practices. Actual external custody horizons, permitted schedule variants and deployment execution budgets require owner qualification before activation. There is no new predicted-backlog system: O07 compares recorded scheduling/budget evidence to an actual active deadline. Final residual checking also made every durably accepted decision immutable (including dismissed/canceled decisions) and made missing scheduling evidence an O07 trigger, so an unscheduled obligation cannot evade a deadline comparison.
