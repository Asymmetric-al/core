## Final response-order correction verified

The independent runtime review correctly identified one material interaction between the allowed control mutation and snapshot-only read. A combined response carries two differently ordered facts: its control acknowledgement and an optional peer projection. A single latest-request-wins gate over that whole response could discard the valid new control window, or could reapply older peer names while processing the acknowledgement.

The **actual data contract's snapshot paragraph**, **actual runtime orchestration paragraph**, and **P07** now explicitly separate the two acceptance paths. A later snapshot request may supersede the older response's peer projection without losing its valid control sequence/window. Processing an acknowledgement cannot overwrite a newer peer projection. Tenant/source/real-session/instance changes still invalidate both paths. P07 requires both response orders and no stale UI overwrite; P08/P22 additionally cover changed context and delayed responses. **Resolved without another protocol, receipt store or new server authority.**

## Authorization and database interpretation checked

- The design does not treat private topic possession or successful channel join as ongoing permission. Current source/session qualification remains necessary at control, snapshot and fanout admission, with explicit admission linearization rather than an impossible database/network atomic recall promise.
- The model uses regular finite application-schema state and avoids custom objects in Supabase's managed `realtime` schema. Provider `realtime.messages` receive policies remain a separately tested boundary; browsers have no publishing path on awareness topics.
- The PostgreSQL review concerns **effective** old/new row conditions and actual grants. It does not repeat the incorrect blanket claim that omission of an explicit `WITH CHECK` is itself a vulnerability: applicable PostgreSQL policy semantics can reuse `USING`. Service-role access still needs the closed current authorization boundary.
- The fresh-input frontier, observer deadline, composing deadline, control window and local display trust each have a different purpose. A snapshot/reconciliation read cannot prolong any of them. Fixed-window expiry and unknown-ID rejection prevent delayed traffic from recreating cleaned-up activity.
- The bounded current snapshot is derived display state. It cannot mutate Support work, refresh conversation business activity, grant CRM access, invoke P17 preparation, dispatch through P6 or replace D1/D4 collision admission and durable send idempotency.

## Proportionality and numerical claims

The proposed 5/10-second combined loop, 15-second report/liveness bounds, 30-second observer/input bounds and 12-second display trust are coherent as one declared starting profile. The runtime honestly includes authorization/read costs, request serialization, transport uncertainty and current peer paint in its latency obligation. The 1,000-millisecond healthy-connected p95 target is **not measured Core behavior** and does not excuse excluding ordinary in-flight-control delay from the fixture.

The source/context and response caps are finite complete-result boundaries, not truncation permissions. Exceeding a bound degrades awareness without blocking qualified drafts or replies. The source/context limit applies to underlying eligible instance contributions, independently of the two-name visual presentation. Operational limits do not grant visibility into otherwise excluded staff or sources.

No additional generic presence, preferences, notification, workflow, CRM or durable event platform is justified by this feature. The needed actual-session and shared rate-limiting seams are stated as owner qualification requirements, not claimed to be already supplied by the legacy Support helpers.
