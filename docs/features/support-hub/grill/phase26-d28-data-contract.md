# D28 — Sampling, response, Supabase and lifecycle contract

**Fully founder-ratified D28 data/authorization/lifecycle amendments; 13 September 2026.** The [32 decision clauses](phase26-d28-adversarial-review.md) govern. This document describes the permanent logical contract; table names and implementation code are not frozen. The [independent data review](phase26-d28-data-review.md) and [25 source pins](phase26-d28-data-source-evidence.json) distinguish current source from intended behavior.

## Current behavior versus intended path

Current Support collections/report aggregations do not qualify a feedback collector. Existing broad staff/admin route gates, email fallback resolution and authenticated CRUD/RLS cannot be inherited as proof of new policy, sampled draw, contact guard or public response safety. Existing text IDs remain valid. The new contract must preserve actual P6 semantic occurrences/source fences and P17 sealed output rather than adding a survey queue.

P6 already describes a no-Party email recipient branch, while P17 protected actions still name exact Party/contact authority. D28 explicitly qualifies only the narrow Support-feedback response branch and P3 feedback preference branch. Neither public possession of a token nor an email-address match proves a Party's identity, relationship, staff role or right to private records.

Tenant foreign keys must include or otherwise enforce the tenant component all the way through inbox, original, candidate, opportunity, effect, response and review. Nullability is explicit: a qualified no-Party delivery has null Party/contact fields and a required no-Party source reference; a Party/contact branch requires its actual refs. Historical actors may reference retained opaque actor attribution without preserving an active user login. Rating enums and integer percentage/draw checks have database constraints. No money field, floating currency value, gift amount or donor-value selector is added.

Service-role use is not evidence of authorization and must reprove the same owner context. Public feedback is a narrow command endpoint, never anonymous direct-table insert.

## Exact sampling population and algorithm

The user-facing percentage is a probability among matured eligible original-conversation opportunities. It is not a percentage of unique donors, all Support records or each worker's conversations. One donor may have multiple independent conversations; one mailbox may represent several people; one person may use aliases. Core must not invent identity resolution to convert this into a person survey.

At a genuine eligible ordinary completion while On, atomically record a candidate and frozen percentage/current source generation. Scheduler maturity at 24h checks current source state and qualification. Current absence of proof excludes safely; it is not filled from CRM or AI. A late worker may process only before due+24h and uses the frozen candidate percentage. It must not pretend it ran at due or reset the deadline. Reopened candidates before maturity are invalid; only a new genuine completion can create another candidate while no opportunity exists.

Before any draw, lock current D10 topology and examine all original opportunity dispositions. Among multiple unsampled eligible candidates, choose earliest genuine completion then stable original ID. Any current-component prior opportunity—including Not selected—prevents a second chance from another original. Record immutable linked-suppression for eligible losing originals, with no draw and a required original-opportunity reference; never key identity to a mutable root. Two independent draws committed before merge remain historical; Undo cannot revive suppressed origins. At mature admission for the chosen eligible representative, generate one unbiased cryptographic draw in 0–9999, commit the original opportunity, draw/outcome/evidence and continuation in one transaction. Example 25% selects 0–2499;2500–9999 is Not selected. At 100%, every eligible opportunity is selected but current contactability/cooldown/gates still apply. A duplicate unique-key loser retrieves the admitted decision. Repeated job IDs, policy updates, old callbacks, merge/Undo and restore never generate another draw.

After sampling, any relevant reopen/new input before submission cancels the pending invitation generation; a later completion cannot resurrect it or generate a new opportunity. Cooldown-blocked selected work is **Skipped**, not scheduled for 30 days later. A skipped/not-selected/cancelled original remains consumed. This biases the eventual sent population toward first eligible completed opportunities and away from repeat contact; report that limit, rather than claim all interactions are equally represented.

Sampling by itself does not cap burst size. P6's existing pacing and tenant/provider fairness protect required/service mail; D28 adds no competing throttle. At 25%, one mailbox with 10 independent eligible opportunities has about 94.37% chance of at least one selection before contact protection. This exact arithmetic explains why sampling and the 30 day guard are separate, not why a particular donor will receive a survey.

P3’s independent stop-feedback preference issuance uses a proposed 90-elapsed-day fixed lifetime from its own issue time, shortened by its own source/contact/privacy ceiling. It remains valid across response Used/14-day expiry, Support content expiry/deletion and policy Off; it cannot read records or opt a person back in. Its grant/key/session scope is independent of the response, and both descriptors must be qualified in the closed P17 pair. The 90 days expires only the preference credential, never the resulting recorded opt-out. The P3 preference persists under its independently authorized lifecycle; policy Off→On, CRM merges, key rotation and new invitations cannot clear it or opt anyone back in. The 30 day instant is measured from source-owned submission-may-have-begun, not local UI click, template save, provider-delivery timestamp or response. Known non-submission can release an exact reservation; an actual attempted effect remains one opportunity. P6 must qualify a finite latest-possible-crossing bound and proof that no old permitted/in-flight crossing remains. Until then, hold the contact guard. After that proof and 30 days from that bound, a new genuinely independent opportunity may pass even if the old outcome remains indeterminate. Retain same-effect dedupe/history under their own finite custody; unknown historical success cannot hold a mailbox or contact-key record indefinitely. Do not release early from a timeout. A delayed definitive provider event does not renew the 30 days by its arrival time.

## Response and reporting integrity

The fixed neutral instrument is a versioned Support fact. Invitation text may be localized/professionally edited within P17 bounds, but cannot change the rating scale. Store the one final response, not a chain of public overwrites. A changed retry conflicts; a separate privacy/correction path records its own actor and reason. No submitted comment is echoed to someone reopening a forwarded bearer link. A response can arrive while the provider outcome is still indeterminate; do not falsely mark it accepted by the provider.

Define **issued invitations** as distinct invitation effects whose submission may have begun. The displayed response fraction is **unique responded issued invitations / issued invitations in that same cohort**, with counts and inclusion dates. Accepted, rejected/failed, indeterminate and delivered evidence remain separate facts beneath that cohort. This definition cannot exceed 100% solely because a response precedes provider acceptance evidence. Excluded/redacted ratings have explicit denominator treatment; no staff mutation secretly makes the score improve. Satisfaction share, when shown, is Satisfied / all currently includable submitted ratings with that denominator visible. Current filters authorize source rows before counts/snippets/exports.

Use both explicit invitation-cohort reporting and separate received-date response browsing. A cohort becomes complete only after all its original response windows have ended or been validly terminated. Delayed receipt processing must use actual accepted response occurrence/receipt, not silently slide results into another cohort. Small samples are shown as counts; the 20 response/28 day operating caution is not a statistical guarantee. No response equals missing feedback, not neutral or satisfied. Do not weight or infer donations/agent performance from these samples.

## Privacy inventory and activation gate

The complete release inventory must name purpose, floor, ceiling, restriction/hold behavior, deletion/restore proof and owner for: policy/audit revisions; candidates and skipped decisions; opportunity/effect tombstones; recipient control keys; response action digests/sessions; independent preference tokens/preferences; rating/comment/question version; review/correction/exclusion history; P17 sealed bytes; actual P6 communication facts; search/report caches; exports; notifications; backups and technical telemetry.

Rating/comment have their own feedback-review purpose from server submission. The old conversation’s Resolved timestamp cannot end that new purpose. Qualified Mark reviewed records its purpose end unless a genuinely continuing existing follow-up needs the source; D17’s active-purpose review safeguards and the qualified finite interval then apply, with earlier owner ceilings. Routine views, links or corrections cannot renew expired authority; an unexamined pending review cannot become an indefinite archive. Test a new response to old Resolved work under a short finite interval. They never reopen work, reset older source clocks or renew an expired transcript. Ratings/attribution/control metadata are personal or linkable, not automatically anonymous; retain only within their explicit owner purpose. Minimal tombstones can prevent replay only under their finite qualified source/control custody; when a whole original source legitimately disappears, no recreation/backfill may rebuild a new opportunity from old events. P3 suppression can outlive the comment where the actual contact-control purpose requires it, with restricted opaque references; it is not a free indefinite analytics store.

No new universal numerical retention period is invented for Support bodies. If the tenant has not qualified a finite D17 policy and actual metadata/control inventory, feedback cannot be enabled. P17 send-by/material ceilings and D6 action expiry are narrower independent limits, not content retention defaults. Holds can preserve required bytes in restricted custody while ordinary display expires. Exports/backups/derived data follow the same restriction/restore invariants rather than generic DELETE cascades.

They use no donor data. A serial reference model does not prove actual PostgreSQL locks/RLS, production RNG, P6 effects, browser security or usability. P01–P40 in the main review are the falsifiable actual release obligations; none is marked executed by these experiments. The test workload budgets are prospective qualification criteria, not measured scalability claims.

The bound concerns submission crossing, not eventual email delivery or successful provider-outcome reconciliation. Source send-by, bounded attempt deadline, fenced workers and no permitted future retry establish the closed window; late callbacks never renew its latest possible crossing bound. Qualify this exact proof in P6 before activation.
