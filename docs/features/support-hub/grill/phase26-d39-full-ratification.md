## Exact accepted decision

Support provides **personal read/unread** over currently permitted conversation content. Each staff member's cue is independent; it indicates familiarity or a deliberate revisit intent, not team responsibility, comprehension, complete historical inspection, attachment inspection, delivery, response approval or completion of a CRM/giving/care action. Ordinary opening of a deliberately selected, visible foreground reader acknowledges the exact authorized content snapshot only after the required current region is successfully and safely presented. Existing contextual **Mark read** and **Mark unread** actions let staff control their own orientation without new settings or routine confirmation.

Unfamiliar eligible content authored by others is unread on demand, including retained history and newly permitted history. No personal marker is fabricated for empty or self-only sources. No tenant-wide enrollment/read-all baseline, new unread total, new filter/folder or staff-by-source fanout is created. Explicit Mark unread may flag currently readable eligible own content for revisit; it creates no deadline or work obligation. Wholly inaccessible, expired or excluded-only sources give no phantom marker or hidden-activity hint.

The strongest alternative meaning was a shared team cue, which signals that somebody looked but cannot tell the current worker what is new to them. The strongest smaller scope was no ordinary read cue, retaining work/notification tools while sacrificing individual catch-up. Those alternatives are not additional tenant modes. No reader roster, last-seen history, sender pixel, requester receipt, staff analytics or provider mailbox synchronization is accepted.

## Accepted content and reader journey

Qualified requester contributions, first admitted human staff briefs, native human Internal notes and another worker's locally admitted human Replies qualify once through trusted source provenance. Queued/failed/unknown delivery does not erase the fact that a colleague's admitted body may be new; later delivery callbacks do not make it new twice. Trusted actual author attribution may suppress self-generated automatic unread; email resemblance, assignment, CRM relationships and caller JSON cannot prove identity. This is not a new human-versus-AI detector.

Drafts/autosaves, composing presence, generated system rows, acknowledgments, note corrections, redaction/expiry, upload/scan progress, work/assignment/reminder/follow changes, merge and provider callbacks do not create ordinary reading contributions. Actual direct attention, required review and source work remain independently governed. Own posting never silently covers older unread content by others.

The experience uses Core's **Base UI and exact base-maia** controls, semantic tokens, restrained dot/title emphasis and accessible **Unread for you** text. The dot is decorative, not a tiny toggle; contextual labelled actions work on keyboard and touch. Keep reading state separate from work/priority and D33 Compact/Full presentation. No per-acknowledgment toast, pulse, sound, dwell setting or confirmation dialog is introduced. Real focus, contrast, target-size, reflow/zoom, localization/Thai/RTL/IME, low-bandwidth and assistive-technology qualification remains required.

Prefetch, list previews, default selection, roving focus, API success, hidden/background panes, notification receipt or Tiptap events do not count as reading. The canonical reader must distinguish successful body, valid empty/file-only contribution, unsupported/invalid content, unavailable source and rendering failure. The existing silent blank wrapper is not readiness. Permitted file metadata may support ordinary orientation without promising a preview/download or actual file inspection. Valid older repeated-history folds do not require expansion of every message.

A complete authorized **combined** reader snapshot may acknowledge its exact original/visibility cutoff vector once the latest eligible combined region is safely rendered. Older bodies may remain folded, paginated or offscreen; there is no separate scroll chore for every merged original. Missing/unproved/inaccessible coverage is not guessed into the vector. Narrower original/search views acknowledge only their represented scope. Merge/Undo retains each original's state, and newly merged or later-arriving content cannot inherit an old snapshot's Read.

New content stays unread during older-history/search reading or active composition, including when it appears visibly beside the editor. A quiet **New activity** action deliberately returns to current reading without scroll jump, row resorting, caret/selection/IME loss or changes to Reply/Note drafts. An inactive saved draft does not block normal reading. Deliberate return cannot mint a new activation merely to override a later manual Mark unread.

## Accepted Supabase ownership, concurrency and recovery

Support owns sparse state by trusted real staff owner, tenant and immutable original conversation. Actual UUID tenant/auth/profile relationships and TEXT Support IDs remain distinct. Same-tenant unique relationships, constrained exact cut/epoch values and immutable scope/owner/incarnation protect current state; generic updates cannot transfer authority or choose future cutoffs. Deleting personal state cannot cascade into messages, CRM, notification engagement or delivery evidence. No financial fields are added.

The source supplies committed per-original eligible-content ordering with coherent snapshot/cut acquisition and source-local serialized admission. Bare nextval, email dates, generic timestamps or UUID ordering cannot establish a safe read-through boundary. H acknowledgments cannot cover H+1, and a reserved lower ordinal cannot commit unseen behind an acknowledged cut. Actual transactions, row-lock ordering, conflicts and retries require real database proof.

For existing already-committed qualified history, initialize a complete gated **source-control** baseline before personal markers/acknowledgments are enabled. Stable control ordinals do not claim to reconstruct historical commit order, change real IDs/times/provenance or emit new content/D15 attention/D17 clock events. Subsequent first eligibility serializes above the baseline. This differs from the rejected per-person enrollment/read-all cutoff; old unfamiliar content remains unread on demand.

Current source authorization supplies the smallest bounded existing visibility/grant-episode coverage needed to preserve acknowledged retained content while exposing newly permitted old content. Homogeneous sources need one cut; actual heterogeneous visibility may need a compact vector. An opaque hash alone cannot distinguish expansion from contraction. Unproved scope stays honestly unavailable/needs-current-view; do not reset everything, fabricate Read, create new ACL groups or build a permission-history/readership platform. This owner contract must be qualified before activation.

Manual actions compare and advance a personal intent epoch; automatic acknowledgments only extend valid cuts under their original deliberate-view activation. Mark unread survives old tabs, background refetch/rerender and ordinary focus changes. A new deliberate opening after that intent or an explicit current Mark read may clear it. New arrivals and own posting do not. Multi-original commands bind the exact offered authorized set atomically with deterministic locks; a later source is not swept in.

Unknown outcomes refresh current authorized state, without blindly repeating old intent under a fresh epoch. Sparse current state, bounded opaque context and incarnation/generation fences are sufficient; no permanent per-click result ledger, P6 prepared artifact, outbox or offline replay queue is added. Cleanup/regrant/restore cannot reset epoch-zero and admit stale requests. HTTP cancellation is not rollback. Cue failure remains truthful and does not block otherwise authorized draft/post/send/CRM/work operations.

`packages/api` commands and approved `packages/database` read projections preserve trusted context. Current self ownership **and** source authority apply through grants, SELECT/USING/WITH CHECK, immutable columns, effective policy unions, RPC EXECUTE/search_path/owner, views, Storage references, caches, result lookups and privileged service paths. Ordinary administrators cannot browse coworkers' state. Context tokens remain bounded, opaque and integrity protected; base64 signing alone cannot conceal hidden ordinals, original IDs or coverage. Realtime may optionally invalidate minimal private projections, but join/JWT authorization caching is not continuous source authorization or reading truth.

Offboarding does not transfer state to an assignee, replacement employee or reused email.
