For example, Maya and Daniel independently open the same donor-care exchange and start preparing an answer. The already-required send guard handles a material committed update. The present choice concerns whether they receive an earlier coordination cue, while both drafts remain private. This is an illustrative overlap, not a measured claim about ministry staffing or duplicate-reply frequency.

A targets avoidable duplicate effort with a small cue and a restrained privacy footprint. B is a legitimate richer coordination model, not inherently unsafe; C is a legitimate smaller product, not permission to remove collision protection. ## Accepted constraints and the D15 privacy distinction

**D15-R01's exact boundary matters:** it excludes a **read-surveillance roster**. D15-R02 also forbids automatic following on viewing, replying, notes or assignment. Q34 cannot silently turn transient viewing into a persistent list of who saw a message, a staff reading history, a follow subscription or management evidence.

A composing-only cue avoids introducing passive-viewer disclosure, while still needing explicit current identity/source authorization. B would require an explicit narrow clarification/amendment in its selected-answer review: transient current-conversation viewing metadata for immediate coordination may be shown, while read receipts/history, last-seen staff logs, monitoring dashboards and broad rosters remain excluded. Do not simply declare that the old exclusion already authorizes B. Nor should B be unfairly presented as a surveillance product; its strongest permissible form is minimal, current and nonhistorical.

The inherited boundaries across all options are:

- **Advisory, never a lock.** Presence is not send authority, proof of review, current-version validation, exclusive claim or a reason to disable the only safe ordinary workflow when the signal service fails. D1/D4's admission checks remain authoritative.
- **Private drafts stay private.** No draft body, snippet, recipients, files or unpublished note text is shared by an activity signal. This is not permission to impersonate another author, take over a draft or co-edit it.
- **Real current identity and source scope.** Use trusted authenticated tenant/member identity and current source disclosure rules. Matching email, assignment, a linked CRM Party or access to one source in a merged component does not authorize every teammate/source disclosure.
- **No handling or receiving side effects.** D6 says viewing/composing/sending does not claim a conversation; D7 separates coverage from browser presence. A live cue does not change assignment, Receive new assignments, workload, coverage, reminder, work status or underlying business completion.
- **No read/diligence claim.** A current viewer/composer indication is not proof that anyone read a message, accepted an assignment, sent a response or completed a donor-care task. No historical reporting, staff ranking, elapsed-effort metric or attention ledger is implied.
- **Same Support reader through CRM.** Preserve current authorized conversation/source, draft and return context. Do not create a CRM activity, second record, duplicate roster or broader access merely because the entry surface is CRM.
- **Email Studio/P6 unchanged.** A transient cue prepares or sends nothing, changes no signature/recipient/body and creates no email purpose or notification family. P17 owns actual authorized preparation; P6 owns delivery and communication evidence.
- **No passive noise expansion.** The choice does not add notification sounds, desktop/mobile push, digest, email or roster-wide announcements. Existing directed/follow attention remains governed by its own accepted owner.
