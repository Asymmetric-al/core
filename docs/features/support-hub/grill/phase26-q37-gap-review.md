## Three genuinely different discovery policies

**Question:** After leaving a conversation, where should a staff member find their own unfinished saved Support replies?

| Option                                    | Concrete experience                                                                                                                                                                                          | Benefit                                                                                                                                                       | Real tradeoff                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — A private My drafts view**          | A named Support entry lists the current person's eligible saved replies across accessible Support inboxes; opening one resumes the same authorized draft in its conversation.                                | A stable, complete place to return to unfinished writing without remembering an inbox, assignee or work status. Familiar scanning and mobile full-list space. | Adds one navigation destination and an explicit private-draft read projection. It must remain a personal writing view, not another team queue or status.             |
| **B — My draft filter in existing views** | Personal row indicators and a My draft filter operate inside the ordinary visible inbox/view scope, with the existing explicit broader-scope path available.                                                 | Reuses familiar lists, filters and saved-view machinery with the least new navigation. This is the strongest smaller alternative.                             | Existing status/inbox filters can hide older unfinished replies until scope is widened. The user must understand that the filtered list may not be all their drafts. |
| **C — A Resume draft picker**             | A compact persistent entry opens a searchable picker over all eligible own saved replies and resumes one in the existing reader, retaining return context. It is not restricted to only the most recent few. | Fast switching while staying near the current conversation; no new primary list destination.                                                                  | Less room for scanning many drafts, long subjects and surrounding work context; pagination, keyboard, narrow-screen and accessibility behavior require care.         |

All three can satisfy durable restoration. They differ in discovery and scanning, not saving quality, access, private/shared ownership or delivery semantics. They are alternative product policies, not three new tenant-configurable modes. A is the single recommendation because leaving and later finding an unfinished reply is already inherent in the accepted navigation/draft journey. It is a product judgment; no measured Asym draft volume, staff habit or universal vendor standard is claimed. Independent current vendor/UX research accompanies the final question.

## Invariants every option retains

1. Only the current actor's currently permitted draft/source metadata is discoverable in the current tenant. Assignment, following, related CRM records and composing awareness do not expose another person's writing, draft count or existence. Losing source access wins over a saved reference or stale query response.
2. Use one canonical draft and the existing Support source/owner command. The finder is an expendable authorized projection. It is not an Email Studio draft library, conversation status, saved-view membership table, CRM interaction, scheduled-send folder or team workload.
3. Opening a draft restores its exact target, explicit audience, staged action, signature and quote dependencies subject to current qualification. New input, inbox movement, merge/unmerge, changed CRM record and changed defaults cannot silently readdress it. Draft discovery does not solve or bypass collision checks.
4. Saved, unsaved, failed save, pending outcome, queued/accepted send and delivered are distinct. A personal finder must not call unconfirmed local material Saved or treat an unresolved send as an ordinary safe-to-send-again draft. The actual selected-answer review must resolve display/recovery details without a second delivery state machine.
5. No body-content search/indexing, cross-tenant union, administrator draft review, forwarding/approval workflow, persistent posted-note edit drafts or generic personal-work platform is introduced. Search can use currently authorized safe metadata; exact fields/limits/ordering and representation permissions require explicit qualification after selection.
6. Support owns the private human draft, its source and authoritative save/admission. Email Studio/P17 supplies governed content/schema/preparation; P6 owns actual dispatch/reconciliation. Listing, opening or filtering drafts sends no mail, prepares no outgoing message, publishes no template and creates no CRM duplicate. Eventual deliberate Send retains D4/D36's complete-message boundaries.
7. Base UI/base-maia, shared list/picker primitives, meaningful labels, source-qualified badges/counts, same-author privacy, return focus, mobile reading width and low-bandwidth recovery remain required. The question chooses no final cap, new persistence table, retention period, autosave cadence or config hierarchy.
