## Technical evidence and decisions

[PostgreSQL datetime documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/datatype-datetime.html) distinguishes UTC instants from retained original zone information. Store the zone/calendar provenance separately. [PostgreSQL RLS](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html), [constraints](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-constraints.html) and [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security) support separate grants, row policies and privileged authorization. Do not copy a generic user_id policy as Core's complete tenant/resource model or a now-relative check onto legitimately overdue rows.

The calendar rule is an explicit Asym judgment: count the union of real instants belonging to the pinned local schedule. An independent [Python zoneinfo](https://docs.python.org/3/library/zoneinfo.html) research oracle verifies eight minute-aligned examples, including gaps/folds, overlapping intervals, holidays, overnight splits and a real zero service interval. See [the results](phase26-d14-calendar-examples.json). It deliberately enumerates minutes for transparent small examples; production must use bounded interval arithmetic. The oracle is not a proposed product dependency or a runtime implementation proof.

[Resend sent-event documentation](https://resend.com/docs/webhooks/emails/sent) and [retrieval](https://resend.com/docs/api-reference/emails/retrieve-email) provide provider evidence, not a guarantee of a human reading a message. P6 qualifies its exact timestamp/member meaning. The proposed conservative acceptance-interval treatment is Core reasoning for missing precision: use qualified bounds rather than backdate retries, force uncertain periods into a date bucket or call accepted mail delivered. No new provider call is authorized by D14.

## Independent findings adopted into the root decision

Three independent reviewers examined data/CRM/reporting, lifecycle/provider/calendar and vendor/UX. The root reconciled their alternatives rather than treating their separate drafts as competing specifications. Material adopted corrections are:

1. Optional First/Next per inbox with blank durations, explicit time basis, no invented universal hours or resolution target.
2. Neutral due/overdue hierarchy and no Due soon threshold, green coverage claim or recurring target alerts.
3. Exact original/correspondent periods: several senders do not inherit one another's elapsed time; no CRM identity inference.
4. One collecting segment plus sealed transferred work and pending-response candidates, not one unfinished target per root/lane.
5. Immutable reviewed reply coverage with actual accepted recipients; another CC, unseen input or related record cannot clear an obligation.
6. Pending-send candidate Next deadlines prevent a short successor target hiding behind a long First target.
7. P6 acceptance-time/interval proof and accepted-versus-delivered distinction; no first-POST backdating or false success.
8. Precise local-instant-union calendar semantics, versioned clocks and explicit missing/zero differences.
9. Source-time policy revisions for delayed eligible input; enable/Off cutoff differs from an ordinary revision edit.
10. Off explicitly covers its configuration family's unfinished targets across current homes; preserve misses and disclose restricted impact safely.
11. Exact withdrawal/disposition cutoff lets late acceptance establish the correct prior result without reviving later mail or restoring status.
12. No reply needed ends only selected response obligations; partial-prefix and non-prefix behavior preserve other input and age.
13. Persistent Undo corrects current mistaken dispositions without stale snapshot/timer restoration or re-enabling independently Off targets.
14. One due→Open review preserves a valid later reminder, overrides deferral, engages existing coverage and does not repeat after review.
15. D10 projection and D12 exact current-home continuation preserve original obligations; later old-route input does not silently expand transferred scope.
16. Raw read/write protection, tenant-aware constraints and server summaries replace browser-calculated authority/body-heavy reporting.
17. Current all-age work, targets-due outcomes and completed-wait durations use separate cohorts and explicit units.
18. Uncertain candidate/cohort membership remains visible outside scored denominators; exact-timed samples exclude bounded timing explicitly.
19. One email ending several waits is labelled completed waits, not several emails; median/p90/sample conventions are precise.
20. Mixed certainty preserves confirmed overdue attention while retaining other uncertainty; current assignee is not historical responder credit.
21. Logical First/Next sequencing uses trusted Core human-send/input admission validated by P6 acceptance; fulfilled reply work is separate from uncertain historical scoring/cohort membership and never remains owed solely to compute a metric.
