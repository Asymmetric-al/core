## Why the first candidate is genuinely unmade

- [D2-R03 and R04](phase26-d2-adversarial-review.md#d2-r03--target-and-eligible-audience) settle exact target/audience and stable drafts. An incoming email, CRM change or assignment cannot silently retarget existing work. Quoted-context review is already a consequence of deliberate retargeting, but a default quotation amount is not specified there.
- [D33-R18](phase26-d33-adversarial-review.md#d33-r18--email-studio-and-rich-text-seam) explicitly separates Compact/Full reading from outgoing quoted-body inclusion, recipients, headers, signature, attachments and prepared snapshots. Therefore choosing A/B/C is not changing the ratified reading preference.
- [D23-R11–R14](phase26-d23-adversarial-review.md#d23-r11--one-visible-managed-block-in-the-normal-reply-composer) govern the managed signature and complete structured draft. The warning against deleting quoted previous signatures preserves authored/source meaning; it does not require automatic quotation of earlier messages.
- [D16-R14](phase26-d16-adversarial-review.md#d16-r14--drafts-prepared-bytes-and-irreversible-send-boundary) already governs source-derived quote/file dependencies and restriction before send. [D17-R19](phase26-d17-adversarial-review.md#d17-r19--prepared-material-and-the-send-race) applies source expiry to quoted material. These anticipate quotations and govern their safety without selecting automatic inclusion.
- The roadmap's inbound quoted-reply-stripping language is already reconciled by D33-R26 as non-destructive derived presentation. It is not an instruction to strip or append outbound context.

This is a bounded positive finding from the inspected relevant decisions and interfaces. It is not a claim that a repository-wide absence search proves no external integration ever appends quoted text.

## The actual question and three fair alternatives

**When a staff member replies to a Support email, how much earlier email text should be included automatically?**

| Option                                         | Starting reply                                                                                                                                          | Main benefit                                                                                                     | Real tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — New reply only; quote deliberately**     | The new answer and governed signature/footer. Staff can deliberately include a currently permitted source quotation when useful.                        | Clean messages, little repeated text and deliberate context selection when the audience changes.                 | A person who needs earlier wording included must take an extra deliberate step. Recipients using fragmented mail views may have less immediate context.                                            |
| **B — Quote the message being answered**       | The new answer plus the qualified content of the exact D2 reply-target message. Staff can inspect/remove that quote before sending.                     | The specific question is immediately available alongside the answer without selecting a quote each time.         | Repeats text on many routine replies and must distinguish the chosen source from any nested earlier history. It can be less compact on mobile.                                                     |
| **C — Include qualified conversation history** | The new answer plus a bounded, reviewable set of earlier external correspondence eligible for that exact outgoing audience. Staff can reduce/remove it. | More context in one email, useful when recipients cannot easily reconstruct the exchange in their own mail view. | More reading and larger body/provenance scope, with greater effort to make source selection, recipient changes, omissions and size boundaries understandable. It is not the entire staff timeline. |

All three share the same current disclosure/source controls and deliberate removal/review. B/C are not unsafe strawmen; their benefit is less effort to carry context. A is recommended as the most proportionate default for the accepted Asym Support flow, where exact source/audience review and direct, uncluttered answers already matter. This is a **product judgment**, not a claim of measured Asym adoption or a universal vendor standard. Vendor workflow research is being assembled independently for the actual question.

“New reply only” does not mean Reply to sender. D2's Reply all default and personal audience setting remain independent. It also does not remove signatures/required footer, change threading, prevent staff from explaining context in their own words or introduce a plain-text-only editor.

## Boundaries every answer retains

1. The exact D2 target remains stable; “message being answered” never changes to the latest message at Send. Newly received mail or a merged component cannot silently widen the quotation.
2. Read permission is not recipient-disclosure authority. Internal notes, Staff guides, private CRM/member-care data and restricted source material do not become external history just because a staff reader can see them. Existing ordinary authored-content controls still apply; do not promise impossible universal clipboard/retyping detection.
3. A quotation is body content, not a recipient/identity/representation grant, external forwarding operation, attachment acquisition, file-preview export or business action. D9/D26 provide the same qualified composer through CRM without copying authoritative CRM state.
4. Source-selected quotations preserve qualified provenance and current D16/D17 restrictions through preparation. Sent bytes remain the already-admitted effect; redaction cannot recall them. An ordinary quote/default change never rewrites a past send.
5. P17/Email Studio compiles the complete reviewed structured body, managed signature, permitted dependencies and actual plain text; Support selects/authorizes the source context and admits the reply; P6 dispatches/reconciles sealed material. No new template family, arbitrary live history token, independent mailer or provider-side dynamic rendering follows from this question.
6. D33 reading folds are expendable display state and cannot select or truncate outgoing content. D22 search hits and D32 preview bytes are not quoted-body authorities. D35 current/earlier internal-note revisions remain internal.
7. No policy storage hierarchy, exact message/byte cap, nested-quote algorithm, signature stripping, raw-message converter, transcript exporter, source-node schema or grace/default preference is selected now. The founder's answer gets the full lifecycle, security, UX and proof review before any such necessary detail is recorded.
