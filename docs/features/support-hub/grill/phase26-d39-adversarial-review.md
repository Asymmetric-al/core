# D39 — Personal read/unread: corrected decision and adversarial review

**Current ratification — 15 September 2026:** The founder fully ratifies D39 A and all amendments, additions, adjustments, changes, updates and incorporated corrections. D1–D39 and every amendment are fully ratified. The [full ratification](phase26-d39-full-ratification.md) incorporates this entire record and supporting prose. Earlier proposed/pending/intent-only and no-Q40 wording below is historical, not a repeat approval gate. Original evidence and unexecuted-runtime limits remain unchanged.

**15 September 2026. Disposition: Accept with required amendments.** The founder selected A; the detailed amendments below are proposed for ratification. D1–D38 and all their amendments remain fully ratified. Q39 is answered at intent level; no Q40 is opened. This is exploratory grill documentation, not a formal specification or permission to implement.

Personal read/unread is a useful, familiar orientation aid. Keep its experience small: new-to-you content is emphasized, deliberately opening the current conversation clears your own cue, and **Mark unread** lets you leave a personal revisit marker. Another person's reading changes nothing for you. Work status continues to say what the team owes.

The required amendments prevent false Read from a failed renderer, premature fetch/prefetch, a late database commit, newly permitted old content, or an old tab undoing a deliberate Mark unread. They preserve ordinary one-open catch-up for combined conversations without per-message inspection chores or colleague surveillance.

## Evidence and judgment

The [evidence register](phase26-d39-evidence.md), [current source/PR snapshot](phase26-d39-source-evidence.json), [24 source pins and primary database references](phase26-d39-supabase-evidence.json), [vendor research](phase26-d39-ux-research.md) and [actual reader probe](phase26-d39-reader-probe.json) distinguish evidence classes. Likelihood below is qualitative engineering judgment, not measured Asym incident frequency. No ministry workflow or user-study result is invented.

Verified current behavior is weaker than the selected policy. The Support migration stores one conversation-level `unread_count`; its adapter increments that shared value and the table/board render a dot from it. This is not a personal reading model. The source-backed renderer probe also finds that malformed/unknown rich-document input can become an empty wrapper without throwing to its caller. A mounted or successfully loaded pane therefore cannot prove successful content presentation. Four synthetic rendering fixtures were run; no D39 database, hosted permissions, browser interaction or production performance was tested.

External products support the selected direction, not every detail of this design. Front, HubSpot Service Hub and Zoho document personal read behavior; Quo supplies a genuine shared-team alternative. Front distinguishes selection from actual-message-open timing. Asym should take the latter direction and require a qualified successful reader result, based on its own source failure. [Front read/unread](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2164), [Front preferences](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2033), [HubSpot help desk](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/help-desk/manage-tickets-in-help-desk), [Zoho ticket actions](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/desk/ticket-management/actions-in-tickets/articles/user-actions-in-the-ticket-detail-page), [Quo conversation status](https://support.quo.com/core-concepts/inboxes/conversation-status).

## Exact corrected decision to record

The following R01–R26 clauses, together with the complete [UX/reader contract](phase26-d39-reader-tiptap-contract.md), [Supabase C01–C16](phase26-d39-supabase-contract.md), [lifecycle L01–L16](phase26-d39-lifecycle-seam-review.md), [proof P01–P30 and controls O01–O05](phase26-d39-proof-and-operations.md), and incorporated review corrections form one proposed decision. Companion examples explain these requirements; they do not expand the product into a readership, analytics or workflow platform.

### R01 — Personal meaning

Support SHALL expose read/unread as the current staff member's personal orientation state over currently permitted conversation content. One person's action SHALL NOT change another person's cue. Read does not mean understood, handled, delivered, inspected in full, approved for sending, or completed in CRM/giving/care. No colleague names, reader roster, last-seen timestamp, requester receipt or employee reading report is added.

### R02 — Honest initial state

When no personal coverage exists, a source with currently eligible other-authored content SHALL be Unread on demand, including retained history and newly permitted history. An empty or self-only source SHALL have no automatic unread cue. Do not invent historical reads, introduce an enrollment cutoff, create staff-by-conversation state on arrival, send retrospective alerts, or turn unfamiliar history into a work obligation. No aggregate unread count/badge is added.

### R03 — Eligible contributions

First-qualified requester input, an admitted human staff context brief, a published native human Internal note, and another staff member's locally admitted external Reply SHALL qualify once under the owning source's provenance and safe-read rules. An already admitted Reply can be new content while its delivery remains Queued, failed or unknown. Later acceptance/delivery does not make it new a second time. Native trusted actor attribution alone identifies self-authorship; email resemblance, CRM relationship, assignment or caller JSON cannot establish it. This requires no speculative detector of whether every inbound sentence was typed by a human: use the existing qualified requester/contribution class and distinguish system-generated events by trusted provenance.

### R04 — Excluded events

Draft saves, composing presence, generated system notes, automatic confirmations, upload/scan progress, assignment/work changes, reminders, optional Following, note edits, redaction/expiry, merges and provider callbacks SHALL NOT create ordinary unread contributions. Existing direct attention and work obligations remain independently governed. Own posting SHALL NOT advance coverage over unseen contributions by others. No source body or attachment is admitted merely to make an unread cue possible.

### R05 — Source ordering and coherent snapshots

The source owner SHALL allocate a committed per-original reading order atomically with first content eligibility, using a source-local serialized admission protocol. A bare sequence, timestamp, email Date, generic updated_at or UUID order is insufficient. Content represented by the snapshot and its offered cutoff SHALL come from one coherent committed view. An acknowledgment of H SHALL never cover a newly committed H+1. A previously reserved lower ordinal SHALL NOT commit later behind an acknowledged cutoff. Source locks must follow a documented deterministic order and bounded transaction protocol, with retry behavior qualified on the actual database. Existing already-committed content may receive stable read-control ordering through R23's gated source baseline before any personal coverage; this neither reconstructs historical commit order nor creates new source content.

### R06 — Authorization coverage without a new permission system

Personal coverage SHALL distinguish acknowledged permitted content from newly admitted visibility, even when newly visible content has an older ordinal. Preserve cuts for proven retained coverage on contraction; do not reset the whole source on every permission hash change. Reference existing source visibility/grant episodes, using one cutoff in the ordinary homogeneous case and the smallest bounded vector only where actual visibility requires it. The source authorization owner must prove containment/expansion. An uncomparable hash or missing coverage produces a truthful unavailable/needs-current-view result, not fabricated Read or a blanket Unread reset. Implementing this narrow owner contract is a preactivation prerequisite; do not create new ACL groups, a policy-history platform or per-message readership ledger.

### R07 — What automatically marks read

Automatic acknowledgment SHALL follow a deliberate opening of the actual selected, visible foreground conversation reader, after successful current-authorized data qualification and safe rendering of its latest eligible combined region. Fetch success, prefetch, list preview, default selection, roving focus, hidden pane, background refresh, notification receipt or editor focus cannot qualify. No dwell timer, percentage-read calculation or all-paragraph inspection is required. The product claim is orientation to the current conversation, not human comprehension or attachment inspection.

### R08 — Combined conversations and historical reading

A normal combined reader MAY acknowledge its exact complete authorized original/visibility cutoff vector after the latest eligible combined region renders successfully. Older bodies may stay folded, paginated or offscreen; users need not visit every original's head separately. Missing, unproved, inaccessible or incompatible scope cannot be guessed into that vector. A narrower original/search reader acknowledges only its represented scope. State stays attached to original sources across merge and Undo; a new original added after the snapshot never inherits Read. Multi-original commands apply the bounded exact set atomically or refresh on conflict.

### R09 — New activity while working

When someone is reading older history, following a search match or actively composing, newly arrived content SHALL remain unread until qualified viewing or explicit Mark read covers it. Active composition suppresses automatic catch-up even when new content renders nearby in the visible latest region. A deliberate return to current reading or **New activity** may qualify its presented snapshot under the still-valid activation; it cannot mint a fresh epoch merely to bypass a newer manual-unread choice. The mere presence of an inactive saved draft does not prevent normal reading. Preserve scroll anchor, keyboard focus, text selection, IME composition, recipients and both Reply/Note drafts. Do not reorder rows merely because a personal cue changes. Ordinary new content at the already selected latest region may qualify only after that new snapshot is safely presented under the still-valid view activation and the user is not actively composing.

### R10 — Simple manual actions

Use the existing conversation/row **More actions** menu for the state-appropriate **Mark read** or **Mark unread** command; retain text labels on touch and keyboard access. No routine confirmation or separate reading settings are needed. Mark read covers only the server-offered authorized displayed snapshot, not future content or a whole inbox. Mark unread is a personal revisit marker over current eligible readable human content, including one's own content when deliberately chosen. Empty, wholly expired, inaccessible or excluded-only sources provide nothing to mark and do not create phantom personal rows. No new bulk action, filter or unread folder is selected.

### R11 — Respect deliberate Mark unread

Manual commands SHALL compare and advance a personal intent epoch. Automatic acknowledgments only extend valid cuts under their original deliberate-view activation epoch. Mark unread made while the conversation is open SHALL survive that view's later rendering, old tabs, refreshes and ordinary focus changes. Only a new deliberate conversation opening after that choice, or a current explicit Mark read, may clear it. New arrivals and the user's own posting do not clear the override. This needs no editor lock, colleague roster or presence lease.

### R12 — Minimal conditional state and recovery

Use sparse current personal state and bounded context/generation fences, not a permanent receipt per reading click. A stale manual command cannot reverse newer intent; a lost response requires a current-authorized refresh, not blind reapplication under a freshly fetched epoch. Automatic retries remain bound to the original owner/source/cut/intent. Cleanup/regrant/restore SHALL fence old generations before state can be recreated, preventing epoch-zero ABA. No deferred offline queue may silently replay old reading intent later. Canceling an HTTP request is not database rollback.

### R13 — Trusted identifiers and database invariants

Derive actual UUID tenant/auth/profile identity from trusted Core context; preserve TEXT original Support IDs. A same-tenant unique owner/original identity SHALL prevent duplicate active states. Tenant, owner, original, incarnation and author attribution are immutable through generic updates. Use constrained cutoffs/epochs and a closed coverage representation; preserve exact large integers across serialization. A client cannot choose a future cutoff or another owner. Personal cleanup cannot cascade into source content, CRM, P17 notifications or delivery evidence. No money field or arithmetic is introduced.

### R14 — Grants, RLS and opaque contexts

Route personal commands through `packages/api` and approved `packages/database` read facilities. Current self ownership AND source read authority SHALL protect the relation, projections, result lookups and all privileged paths. Check grants, SELECT, USING, resulting-row WITH CHECK, column immutability, permissive-policy unions, RPC EXECUTE/search_path/actual owner, invoker views, Storage references and service-role paths. Ordinary staff/admin rights do not grant others' personal state. Tokens bind the exact owner/source/coverage/topology/epoch and remain bounded, opaque and integrity protected; a signed but merely base64-readable payload must not reveal hidden source IDs, ordinals or coverage. Tokens are context, not access grants.

### R15 — Reader and Tiptap boundary

The canonical safe reader SHALL distinguish successful nonempty body, valid empty/file-only content, restricted/unavailable content and invalid rendering. The current empty-wrapper-on-error behavior cannot trigger automatic Read. Validate source documents before lossy normalization; use the existing qualified canonical document and viewer profiles. Reading state SHALL NOT be a Tiptap document field, `onUpdate`/`onFocus` side effect, editor extension, autosave write or collaboration document. Do not remount/rewrite the editor when a cue changes. File-only content may qualify through permitted ready metadata without opening file bytes; failure of required current presentation remains truthful.

### R16 — Visual and accessible experience

Use Core's Base UI and exact base-maia theme, semantic tokens, existing row/menu patterns and restrained emphasis. An unread dot plus appropriate subject weight may aid scanning, but expose **Unread for you** or equivalent accessible text; color and a hover tooltip cannot be the only meaning. Keep reading emphasis distinct from status/urgency and leave typography readable in Compact and Full. Menus must be keyboard/touch accessible with reliable focus return; no attention-grabbing animation or announcement on every background acknowledgment. Qualify reflow, zoom, contrast, reduced motion, Thai/RTL/international text, low bandwidth and assistive technology through the actual journey.

### R17 — Truthful failure and cross-device refresh

Reading-state failure SHALL NOT block otherwise authorized writing, posting, sending, CRM navigation or work handling. A pending/failed state must not settle into false Read; retain only currently lawful confirmed state and offer a small Retry where useful. Coalesce updates and refetch current authority on appropriate view/foreground navigation. Cross-device state converges through qualified refresh; no unsupported instantaneous-sync promise. Optional Realtime may carry minimal private invalidation, never bodies, a colleague read roster or read authority. Its join/JWT policy cache is not a per-message authorization check.

### R18 — One Support source across CRM

A genuinely authorized Support reader opened from CRM SHALL use the same real person's original-source state and preserve return context. CRM record/list opening alone does not mark Support read. Requester, participant, authenticated staff owner, CRM Party/contact, represented organization, assignee and CRM owner remain different concepts. Shared email, linking, unlinking, Party merge, assignment or conversation membership grants no additional Support/CRM/giving/care access and does not transfer reading state. No duplicate CRM body, Party, Activity, synchronization or business mutation is created by viewing/marking.

### R19 — Email Studio, P17 and P6 seam

Support owns eligible source content and personal orientation. P17/Email Studio continues to own its governed reusable authoring, whole-message preparation and notification presentation/engagement; P6 owns actual communication/delivery and reconciliation. Viewing or marking SHALL create no Email Studio draft/publication/prepared material, dispatch, tracking pixel, provider read synchronization or recipient read receipt. It SHALL NOT read/archive related P17 notifications, change Follow membership or satisfy source-result/response/Send review. A real typed notification-click engagement remains with its existing owner; subsequent qualified Support presentation may separately update D39. D35 posted-note history, D36 quotations and D37/D38 drafts remain unchanged.

### R20 — Privacy, retention and departures

Markers, cutoffs, hidden coverage references and operational times are potentially sensitive personal metadata. Inventory their purpose, retention and cleanup before activation. Ordinary user/admin exports, CRM history, public/missionary/donor APIs, global search and employee reports SHALL exclude them. Restriction/expiry removes ineligible contributors without fabricating new activity or renewing native source clocks. Offboarding never transfers markers to a new worker or a reused address. Minimum rejection fences may outlive active UI state only for a declared bounded purpose; restore must reinstate source/privacy/control barriers before serving old data. Previously disclosed bytes cannot be recalled by changing a dot.

### R21 — Query and workload discipline

Compute one private boolean/known-state projection using bounded conversation rows, sparse owner/original cuts and indexed authorized eligible-content existence checks. No bodies, editor instances, staff-by-message fanout or whole-tenant scan solely to decorate a row. Authorization and eligibility precede pagination/aggregate conclusions. Coalesce identical acknowledgments and avoid writes when coverage is already sufficient. Record actual source/page/visibility cardinalities, active staff, concurrency, query plans, lock durations and p50/p95 under the existing qualified list contract before release; no invented scale or latency claim and no arbitrary new limit frozen by this decision.

### R22 — Minimal useful observability

Measure aggregate technical read/command failures, latency and typed conflicts with body-free protected correlation. Normal successful view/acknowledgment/manual-state operations SHALL NOT generate a durable actor-plus-source-plus-timestamp activity stream that reconstructs employee viewing. This applies to application logs, gateway paths and traces, not just product analytics: avoid principal-correlated resource paths, view tokens and success payloads. Current state is not a public history journal. Narrow existing security events may retain necessary attribution under their own controls. No bodies, filenames, addresses, tokens or raw hidden ordinals in telemetry. O01–O05 define residual signals/thresholds/owners/responses; authorization or false-read correctness is a release gate, not merely a monitor.

### R23 — Source-first rollout and compatibility

Qualify source provenance/order/visibility before private-state commands and their UI. Existing qualified content predating reading ordinals SHALL be captured as a complete already-committed source baseline under a gated source generation before personal markers/acknowledgments are enabled. Assign stable control ordinals without claiming to recover historical commit order, changing original content IDs/times/provenance, or emitting new content, D15 attention or D17 clock events. Serialize all subsequent first eligibility above that baseline; prove live publication cannot slip behind it. This is a one-time source-order initialization, not a per-user enrollment cutoff or fabricated read history. Do not backfill per-person state from shared `unread_count`, assignee, email, last-update time or provider status. Existing history uses R02 on demand. Retire the old shared-dot dependency for this cue; preserve any separately justified existing data under its actual owner. Mixed versions cannot bypass epochs, offer future cuts, strip coverage or revive retired state. A kill switch may stop the optional cue/commands while retaining current intent/fences and independent work. Roll-forward and restore preserve restrictions and exact original identity; do not roll back to misleading shared-read semantics after new personal state exists.

### R24 — Deliberate scope limits

This decision adds a personal cue and two contextual actions, not a new help desk, CRM, workflow engine, knowledge base, AI feature, tracking system, count/filter catalogue, offline vault or editor platform. It does not authorize new message intake, external attachments, refunds, receipt reissues, contact changes or disclosure. Reuse existing capabilities only where their semantics and authority actually fit. Do not preserve the current weak global count merely for visual consistency.

### R25 — Governance and traceability

Keep D39's selected intent/proposed details and the existing ratified decisions explicit in the feature glossary, exploratory ADR0039, ledger and handoff. Preserve historical records. On later explicit formalization, trace these clauses into authorized OpenSpec/design/tasks/tickets and release evidence, reconciling the stale Twenty/synchronization wording in `crm-core` against governing ADR0001 rather than reviving a retired CRM backing. Do not alter governing formal specs or publish tickets during this grill. Source absence and a renderer probe are not deployed-role proof.

### R26 — Completion and release proof

P01–P30 SHALL be satisfied on the actual selected API/database/browser stack before activation, with real authorization, concurrent-session, migration, accessibility and production-shaped evidence. The owner coverage contract, numerical budgets, retention inventory and accountable operating assignments are prerequisites, not unknowns silently deferred to production. All 30 runtime groups remain required and unexecuted in this grooming session. Documentary checks and the four source-backed renderer fixtures are reported at their actual evidence level. No claim of perfect UX is made without intended-staff journey validation and correction.

## Ruthless synthesis and order of work

**Resolved before recording this proposed answer:** personal meaning; on-demand absent-state baseline; eligible/excluded activity; successful actual-view trigger; combined-snapshot catch-up without per-original chores; contextual manual controls; manual-intent precedence; original-source ownership; CRM/Email Studio/P17/P6 separation. These decisions are now exact. The source probe disproves pane-load equivalence, and sequence/grant/tab traces disprove the simplest unsafe implementations.

**Capture during later authorized formalization:** R01–R26, complete companion contracts, one glossary term/feature ADR, actual owner of the narrow source-coverage primitive, retention/control inventory, numeric shared workload limits and accountability. Resolve stale CRM prose against ADR0001. Do not create formal specs or issues during this grill, and do not invent a ministry use case to justify extra features.

**Require in implementation, in dependency order:** first source provenance/commit ordering/current permission coverage; then self-only sparse state and bounded conditional commands; then safe viewer outcomes and one stable personal cue/menu; then CRM entry/cross-device refresh and source-first migration. Qualify actual grants/RLS/concurrency, reader focus/mobile/AT and production-shaped plans before enabling. No paid editor/Realtime service or separate read-receipt platform is selected.

**Monitor only after those gates pass:** O01–O05 cover remaining real-world privacy incidents, technical failures, false-read/lost-intent defects, performance regression and repeated UX friction with explicit thresholds, owner roles and responses.
