# D29 — Contact first, with optional contextual guidance

**13 September 2026. Founder selected A: relevant public guidance beside Contact support, with information optional and contact easy.** This independent report supplies research, proposed exact UX amendments and proof for the root's complete adversarial review. Amendments are not yet fully ratified. No runtime, public page, inbox, provider, DNS, GitHub or formal specification was changed.

**Disposition: accept with required amendments.** The strongest design is a contact-first Help entry, a short ordinary contact form and the tenant's approved direct email route, with a small curated list of relevant public guides beside or below them. The person can ignore all guidance. Reading must not lose an entered message, alter the contact's recipients or create work. Public content, form acceptance, Support work and actual email continue through their existing owners.

The root synthesis selects **zero to three guide references per qualified Help entry**, with no search box, topics tree, paging or Browse all help. Three is a proposed compactness bound, not a universal optimum; the earlier exploratory five-item suggestion is superseded. Zero is fully valid: contact alone is useful. Record the bound as product judgment rather than measured tenant demand. Do not fill missing slots with generic popular content. The same synthesis selects an upload-free Contact form with email/message required, optional unparsed name, a 10,000-Unicode-scalar message bound, 200-scalar name bound and 256 KiB raw-request ceiling; these are proposed implementation safety/product bounds, not industry standards.

## Proposed clean requester experience

### 1. Open Help and see a real contact route

Use the product's existing **Help** entry and normal public **Contact support** link where appropriate. **Help opens the Contact form directly**, without a preliminary chooser. Do not add a floating animated bubble or proactive interruption solely for this feature. The panel/page heading names the tenant's approved public Support team. Contact is primary; optional **Helpful guides** are visually secondary.

On wider screens, form and compact guide list can sit beside each other using shared layout components. On narrow screens, contact comes first and guides follow; do not put guide cards ahead of the form or require horizontal tabs to reveal it. With no eligible guides, omit the guide region without a “knowledge base empty” warning. The contact entry remains a complete product experience.

Show the currently approved, monitored public email address as readable/selectable text with **Email the team** and a copy affordance where useful. A `mailto:` link alone is insufficient when the person has no local mail client. If the tenant actually offers an approved phone contact, display its country code and real hours/time zone as an optional route. No new calling/callback service, fabricated phone number, private worker mobile or missionary office location is introduced. Existing public contact information comes from its owner rather than a second editable Support copy.

### 2. Ask for only what permits ordinary help

Qualify this minimal purpose matrix with the existing public-form owner:

| Field                | Proposed role                                                            | Required guard                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Email address**    | Required ordinary reply destination, with “We'll reply to this address.” | Owner-normalized syntax/transport support, paste/autofill, visible check/edit; no requirement to sign in or retype it twice. Entering an address does not prove identity or opt into another purpose. |
| **How can we help?** | Required plain-text message in this upload-free form                     | Clear safe bounded input; no mandatory subject/topic/category/department. The intake owner derives any internal title through a qualified mapping, not a free-text mail-header merge.                 |
| **Name (optional)**  | One unparsed display name only if the team has a demonstrated use        | Do not split first/last, require a surname, title, Latin alphabet or organization. Leaving it empty is legitimate. Omit entirely if unnecessary.                                                      |

No donor ID, gift amount, project selection, phone, account creation, department selector or “Have you tried…” field is required for this ordinary contact. Questions needed for a consequential owner action belong to that later qualified action; public Help is not an identity verification form. An ordinary message may mention an organization or another person without forcing a CRM association.

Use a native textarea, not a rich editor, because this form asks for a short plain message. User-selected Tiptap remains the shared editor for qualified Support rich-text authoring; it is not necessary for email address, curation or this text-only field. Public guide content continues to be authored in Web Studio's actual editor rather than another Support editor. No screenshot capture, file upload or payment fields are added. A quiet note can explain that files may be sent through the actual qualified email route; do not automatically attach browser content or insert form text into a mailto URL.

### 3. Prefill safely and transparently

In an authenticated app-owned Help experience, current trusted actor context may supply their own ordinary reply address/name where the owning source permits it. Show **“We'll reply to [address]”** or the actual populated field; permit correction under the intake purpose. A different reply address remains a declared endpoint, not a change to the signed-in identity, CRM preference or proof of control.

Never prefill from the first linked Party, represented organization's address, a person whose CRM record the staff member happens to be viewing, raw query parameters or a stale browser identify payload. Keep requester, authenticated actor, represented Party and chosen reply endpoint distinct. Anonymous public CMS output must not vary with donor role or private cookies; authenticated prefill belongs to the app-owned boundary, not a new public CMS audience.

The draft/prefill is bound to current tenant, surface and actor/session. On successful submission clear its private fields; on sign-out, tenant switch or actor change discard the old identity/draft without showing it to the next person. Ordinary same-context recoverable errors retain the text. Do not implement cross-device or permanent anonymous drafts simply to support this flow.

### 4. Consult a guide without losing work

The guide list contains current owner-qualified public titles, optional concise public summaries and canonical links. It is deliberately curated, not a personalized recommendation based on message text. No query, contact address, giving record or form content is sent to a public search service. No Internal Staff guide, draft, preview, Shared-by-link Page or wrong-locale substitute appears.

Use a simple link-safe public projection rather than a miniature complete website renderer. The root selects plain canonical guide links with optional qualified source summaries. Show **“Open guide — opens in a new tab”** through a normal anchor with `noopener` and `noreferrer`; the current contact form stays open and the link remains usable without the JavaScript enhancement. Do not add a passive-reader panel, scrape arbitrary public HTML, execute page forms inside Help or strip content while claiming full fidelity. The link can open through ordinary browser controls and the person can return to the unchanged current form.

Do not promise persistence across browser termination, full reload or arbitrary navigation unless the existing draft owner actually qualifies it. Preserve the active form during the supported optional-guide path and make deliberate discard/navigation clear. This is a proportionate supported journey, not a new persistent draft platform.

Reading creates no Support request, CRM Activity, resolution, acknowledgment or feedback invitation. Do not ask “Did this solve it?” as a gate before contact. After returning, **Send message** remains the primary action with the entered text intact.

### 5. Send once and understand the result

Use one explicit **Send message** action. Do not disable it because the person has not opened a guide or chosen a topic. Validate required fields and actual limits using the released purpose on the server; show concise linked errors beside fields and at the top on submit. Preserve every valid entered field. Do not issue a privacy/consent checkbox merely because a vendor does; the actual applicable purpose's notice/consent contract governs.

At admission, the existing owner atomically records the released form occurrence, one Primary Outcome and required durable continuation. Only then may the interface say **“Your message has been received.”** This on-screen receipt is mandatory. Explain the actual email reply path without promising a response duration unsupported by staffed policy. A provider acceptance is not a staff reply; a browser click is not durable receipt. An optional **P23 Visitor Email Confirmation** is a separate P17/P6 effect, default Off for new forms; it is not the D13 incoming-email acknowledgment and its failure cannot erase accepted work. If enabled, its exact owner contract qualifies the shared 24-hour courtesy guard and 15-minute form-admission utility. D28 is not triggered by this form submission.

An uncertain network response says **“Checking whether your message was received…”** and reconciles the original submission receipt. It does not invite the user to immediately email the same message or press a new-key retry. If the same request was admitted, return its existing success. If definitely not admitted and the temporary failure clears, retry through the same qualified operation. Private text and receipt authority must not be exposed through query strings or public tracking.

After receipt, keep a durable visible success region with the safe reply address/ordinary continuation instructions permitted by the source. Do not dump their message into a public success URL, reveal staff routing or offer a new requester message archive. A user needing to add information uses the existing email continuation or a separately deliberate new message, not an accidental duplicate caused by browser refresh.

### 6. Handle failure without inventing a route

| Situation                                           | Required experience                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

No broader search or other-tenant fallback.                                                                                                                                                                                    |

Do not render the entire Help entry as unavailable.                                                                                                                                            |
| Contact adapter unready before admission            | Explain that the form is temporarily unavailable and show the actual current qualified alternative, if one exists. Preserve text safely in the active session. Never claim Received or pick an arbitrary inbox.                                                         |
| Form admission outcome uncertain                    | Reconcile original receipt and say checking; do not encourage a second independent channel while the original may already exist.                                                                                                                                        |
| Accepted work has downstream email trouble          | Preserve accepted work and route repair to its actual owner. The requester-facing receipt remains honest about what was received, not future email success.                                                                                                             |
| JavaScript enhancement blocked                      | Public contact address/link and normal page navigation remain available. A no-JavaScript form uses the same qualified admission/abuse/CSRF contract if supported; otherwise truthful alternative contact, not a new weaker endpoint.                                    |
| CAPTCHA/abuse challenge needed                      | Use the platform's qualified proportional protection, accessible alternative and retained text. No visual puzzle is made universally mandatory by this decision. No contact route can bypass required abuse policy.                                                     |

Expose only the existing qualified recovery information and notify the responsible owner through an authorized path. Readiness must prevent publishing a broken required Help experience in the first place. |

“Always easy to contact” requires a genuinely working configured route; copy cannot manufacture it. D29 must require route readiness and operational repair while being truthful during unavoidable outages. Phone or email alternatives are shown only when they are real, appropriately public and allowed—not because every failure needs a new channel.

## Complete maintainer journey

1. **Reach one management area.** From the qualified Help entry settings, a permitted maintainer sees the current public presentation and links to its actual owners. This is not D27's staff-guide publication surface. An inbox reviewer or CRM owner does not automatically gain CMS publication or public-route authority.
2. **Choose exact context and contact.** Use a code-qualified Help entry/surface identity with the exact tenant/Site/locale mapping. Confirm the current released Contact form/Primary Outcome and approved public contact routes. Do not let a visitor choose recipients or an administrator create a wildcard script targeting private donor properties.
3. **Select up to the adopted small bound of guides.** Search may be used by the authorized maintainer inside the existing content picker; this does not add requester search. Show only current source-qualified publicly listed Pages in the exact scope. Store stable references and display order, not copied article bodies or guessed URLs. Include clear title/locale/publication availability and source-owner edit links. Selecting a D27 staff favorite is not public publication.
4. **Preview as the requester.** Show contact first, actual source-qualified guide titles and current language. Separate unpublished configuration preview from actual released public state. No private account persona simulator, real submission or message is generated. Mobile preview must expose if guides push contact off screen.
5. **Save/publish through the owner.** Preserve drafts during validation and cross-owner navigation. Save does not publish unless the actual owner contract says so; show Draft/Published/Unpublished changes accurately. When a Site release is required, use that release boundary rather than instant Support-side override. One effect receipt and expected revision prevent duplicate publication or lost update.
6. **Maintain source truth.** Editing the public guide occurs in Web Studio. The Help selection follows current allowed source state and canonical identity. Unpublishing, switching to Shared-by-link, changing locale availability or safety withdrawal removes the public suggestion immediately according to source fences; it does not leave a stale title/summary. Record unavailable selection to the maintainer without exposing it to public visitors.
7. **Remove/reorder safely.** Removing from Help changes discovery in this entry, not the guide's publication or other placements. Reorder with keyboard-accessible controls as well as any drag handle. Removing all guides yields contact-only; it must not disable Help or require placeholder content. Do not silently fill empty slots with new sources.
8. **Change/retire contact routes.** Revalidate the actual destination owner and publish the new generation. Preserve the accepted occurrence's frozen old routing/evidence; future submissions use the new release. No accepted work is lost or mirrored. If the current required route is invalid, visibly assign repair; do not redirect old pending work to whatever new mailbox is selected.
9. **Handle maintainer departure or rollback.** Tenant ownership survives a staff departure. Current authorized oversight can repair ownership without widening source access. Disabling optional guides leaves contact; restoring configuration rechecks every current source/route rather than resurrecting withdrawn content. No direct database cleanup should be needed for routine maintenance.

## Completion limits and rollout signals

This completes the independent proposed journey and maintenance research. It does not establish visual polish, browser compatibility, email reliability or ease of use by testing the actual Asym implementation; none exists from this grooming work. The root's full review must reconcile exact field/purpose/route and source-presentation contracts before ratification, then later implementation must prove the scenarios above.
