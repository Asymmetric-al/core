## The same record from both surfaces

Support-first and CRM-first entry use one shared form and one Support-owned admission command. CRM can visibly stage an authorized Related record and an exact existing encounter reference; the reviewed request, links and local effects admit together. Every applicable manual/email request and resolved history participates in D9's complete current-authorized CRM Support view. Its row opens the same full qualified Support detail inline or through the full-page fallback, with preserved navigation/drafts. No CRM-local composer, source-body mirror, second ticket or independent send/resolve endpoint.

CRM owns Parties and relationships. Support owns work, its brief and relevance associations. An independently recorded encounter stays with its actual owner. Shared display does not transfer editing or disclosure authority, grant access to either domain, or manufacture a Communication event/last-contact update.

## Saving is not contacting the requester

The exact form help is **No email will be sent to the requester**. This is deliberately narrower than a promise that no staff notification can ever occur. A separately qualified assignment/attention event retains its existing P6 local/optional-email contract. It does not become native requester correspondence or create an email reply target. There is no new requester acknowledgement, staff blast, producer, message-template key, Role Layout or provider-template mirror.

The initial brief uses the accepted staff-context Tiptap profile and Support custody. Where already-qualified reusable staff wording is used, D18's content-only purpose/source controls still apply; reading or inserting wording cannot assign work, publish content or send mail. D24's curated external reply-and-work shortcuts do not execute on manual intake. There is no automatic conversion of the brief, notes, CRM data or original encounter into reusable Email Studio content.

## Manual work setup and later email setup

A qualified manager can create a named work inbox with access/coverage without fake inbound, From or Reply-To values. If none is available, Record a request offers a contextual setup or safe administrator handoff while retaining the permitted draft. This is the explicit accepted extension to D25's email-first Add inbox assumption.

Configure email subsequently uses **Use an existing Support address** or **Create a new Support address** with equal prominence. D25's same connection, receiving-purpose credentials, authenticated ingress, candidate custody, managed-destination proof and conditional email activation remain intact. A work-ready flag does not mean an email route, sender, credential or external destination has been verified. This adds no third receiving method or provider platform.

## First email from a manual request

Start email thread requires a deliberate qualified recipient selection because there is no received-email trigger. Authorized CRM contact lookup may help, but a Related Party, its current preferred email, assignee or observed callback number is not an instruction to address someone. The source brief and private files stay internal unless a distinct currently authorized public composition incorporates permissible information through its proper rules.

The author reviews audience, actual From/Reply-To, wording, signature and any current handling action. P17 prepares one immutable qualified message; Support admits it; P6 dispatches/reconciles that effect. Draft, queued, accepted, delivered and read remain different states. Possibly submitted mail must not be rekeyed or replayed to achieve apparent success.

D14 remains exact: manual save creates no incoming-email target. A genuinely accepted Start email can establish Next for later eligible incoming correspondence at the qualified send-intent boundary. Unknown acceptance retains candidate First/Next interpretations and the earliest applicable possible deadline, labeled Reply timing needs confirmation. Definite rejection discards only invalid candidates. No retrospective phone-note timestamp manufactures response credit.

## Lifetimes and correction

The first admitted manual brief joins D17's human-content activity set once at trusted recording/admission time. Reported encounter time is separate. Ordinary edits, CRM links, list refreshes and provider callbacks do not renew retention. D22 reuses the eligible Staff context brief source kind; this does not expand into CRM/global/AI search. All actual source restrictions and derivatives remain bounded by D16/D17, with no copied CRM Note archive.

All D12 unused-tracking exclusions remain before the additional standalone created-in-error no-obligation/current-home test. Legitimately used work cannot be relabeled just because it is complete. Existing canonical Support TEXT IDs remain stable; no rekeying is needed for this origin. Source, Party and work correction retain their actual owner evidence and present-day authorization.
