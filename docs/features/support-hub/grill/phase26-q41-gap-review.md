## Candidates rejected after actual source review

**Inbox retirement:** D25-R19 explicitly distinguishes pausing ordinary intake from stopping SMTP, shutting down tenant mail or closing work. Retirement already reviews old routes, unresolved custody, pending/prepared/indeterminate sends, outside changes and safe reconnect. Reasking this would reopen accepted effects.

**Participant, move or external-forwarding expansion:** D2's recipient/audience contract, D9–D12 original/relationship rules and D25 identity/routing seam already cover the chosen continuation journey. An old checklist's phrase “remaining participant/move questions” does not identify a still-unsettled concrete outcome. External forwarding is a possible additional capability, but no demonstrated unmet Asym need was found here. It must not be manufactured from an email-client feature list.

**General automation:** Current legacy configuration is not product authority. The actual roadmap and D1/D6/D19/D24 keep Phase34 the sole configurable vocabulary. The future exact sender predicate selected in D40 does not overturn that boundary. Implementation cleanup remains required.

**AI:** [Platform product intent](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/openspec/specs/platform-product-intent/spec.md#L241) correctly makes AI a core product direction with bounded human authority. D24-R25/R26 explicitly accepts future governed discovery/draft/maintenance assistance and research while excluding current model/UI/index/automatic effects. Preserve that stage decision; do not claim AI is outside Asym or add unvalidated AI merely to keep the interview going.

**Print/export, send-later, extra channels or custom fields:** These might be useful in a demonstrated future workflow, but no inspected current need requires them to finish the selected Phase26 outcome. Existing file access, reminders, CRM owner actions and source-safe navigation already solve the established portions. Competitor parity alone is insufficient authority.

**Legacy data migration:** The source still has two modules and insufficient send/state paths. That is a real implementation problem. D1-R14 already gives the governing disposition rule; current production-use inventory and verified mappings cannot be replaced by a hypothetical product preference. This audit made no live-data query and does not claim every legacy record can be converted.

## Integrated journey and ownership closure

A requester can reach the tenant through the accepted email/help entry, have input qualify into either ordinary work or accountable held review, and receive a human reply through exact P17 preparation and P6 dispatch. Staff can own/reassign work, ask for bounded help, use permitted CRM context without re-entry, work with private Reply/Note drafts, safely read/file/quote relevant source, and complete or continue the genuine obligation. Late input, delivery failure, source change, absence, revocation or restored data returns through its actual owner rather than disappearing behind a status or folder.

The same underlying source/work facts appear across Support and authorized CRM; no duplicate CRM, universal workflow model or provider-mailbox truth is introduced. P17/Email Studio owns governed content/authoring/preparation and separate notification presentation/engagement; P6 owns actual communication/evidence. Support owns its work, source context and selected personal state; intake owns receiving/policy/admission; privacy/records, giving, tasks and care retain their authority. The [platform surface](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/openspec/specs/platform-surfaces/spec.md#L13) and [shared/server boundary](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/openspec/specs/platform-boundaries/spec.md#L13) contracts support this as one Mission Control feature within Asym.

This is a documentary dependency/coverage check of the combined journey. It is not an end-to-end execution or a claim of perfect UI, deployed safeguards, current provider readiness or zero undiscovered implementation defects. A later contradiction found during formalization or actual proof must be corrected explicitly rather than ignored because the interview is closed.
