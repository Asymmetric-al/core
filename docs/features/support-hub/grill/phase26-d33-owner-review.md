## Selective primary research

**The concrete inline-answer hazard is documented.** Zendesk describes partially inline replies appearing beneath an expansion ellipsis, while completely inline replies display in full; it also documents limitations for inline images. This supports cautious interpretation and easy expansion, not copying its delimiter/ingestion behavior into Asym. D33 needs no outbound delimiter or Email Studio template change. [Zendesk delimiter and inline-reply behavior](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408821456922-Adding-and-removing-the-delimiter-from-email-notifications)

**Low-effort full reading is a legitimate workflow.** Front's article, edited9 September2025, provides Expand all/Collapse all because repeated individual expansion adds effort. Its Find article, edited19 March2026, distinguishes conversation-content Find from broader search. These support an obvious full-reading escape and exact-match navigation; neither proves a particular segmentation algorithm, Compact default or Asym performance benefit. [Expand all messages](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2416), [Find in Front](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2415)

**Disclosure has a simple established interaction.** W3C specifies a button that toggles a content section with Enter/Space and truthful expanded state. Use visible labels such as Show quoted history/Show repeated signature rather than a bare ellipsis. This does not require a new widget or a modal. [W3C disclosure pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/)

The founder's C adds maintained personal preference despite A being smaller. That is a valid explicit product choice. B, always Full by default, remains the strongest simpler algorithmic alternative; it avoids fold interpretation but adds repetition. The useful compromise is C with only two values, initially Compact, and conservative uncertainty behavior. No measured tenant corpus establishes a detector accuracy rate, universal signature convention, preferred default or time saving; those claims need actual release/staff evidence.

## Required owner and interpretation amendments

Severity is potential impact of an underspecified implementation; likelihood is qualitative, not measured incident frequency. Each finding completes/narrows C and has a permanent exact correction.

### O33-01 — One canonical source, reversible presentation

**Material concern: high severity, plausible.** Two stored Compact/Full bodies or destructive ingestion stripping can lose inline answers and create contradictory history/search/exports.

**Exact language:** “Compact and Full render the same currently permitted qualified message source. Compact applies a versioned display segment map identifying source regions; it does not store or write a replacement message body. Full removes display folds only. The roadmap's quoted-reply stripping is reconciled as non-destructive derived presentation and cannot authorize deletion of admitted source content. Source correction/expiry remains exclusively D16/D17.”

The map may be rebuilt, discarded or cached within the source's custody; it is never a write authority or an archival copy. Do not reconstruct an expired/missing message from a previous quote, P17 prepared material or today's signature publication.

### O33-02 — Quote markers are candidates, not proof

**Material concern: high severity, likely with real email.** Top/bottom/interleaved replies, forwarded-only evidence and custom email markup can place new information inside a blockquote or after an apparent reply separator.

**Exact language:** “Automatically fold only a complete, conservatively bounded repeated quote region whose full represented meaning matches currently authorized earlier material in the same qualified conversation/context. Quote tags, CSS classes, leading `>` characters, delimiters, content length and vendor parser labels alone are insufficient. A changed, interleaved, first/only forwarded, unsupported or uncertain region remains open. If comparison/segmentation cannot be completed within its limits, display the qualified full source.”

This need not infer author intent or prove that repeated text is unimportant. Repetition can still matter, so clear in-place expansion and Full are always available. Prefer leaving one changed quote block fully open to building a word-level diff/fold system that can hide part of a new answer. Do not fold the entire message into an empty/signature-only-looking bubble.

### O33-03 — Meaning-aware equality, not search normalization

**Material concern: high severity, plausible.** A link can retain its label while changing its destination; strikethrough, numbers, images or table positions can change meaning without changing extracted text. Casefolded, whitespace-flattened or truncated hashes can falsely match.

**Exact language:** “Repeated-region equality is a separate versioned rendering contract, not D22's search normal form. Compare the complete qualified region, preserving meaningful text, Unicode distinctions, block/list/table structure, marks, link destinations and qualified asset identity/representation. Normalize only declared presentation-equivalent quote wrappers/prefixes. Unknown attributes, unsupported structure, changed meaning or incomplete comparison prevents automatic folding.”

Do not use generic `extractPlainText`, regex tag removal, source text prefixes, similarity percentages or an AI classifier as proof. If visible introduction/header text is excluded from the repeat comparison, keep it visibly represented outside the fold or qualify its complete equivalence; do not discard a changed sender/date/contact detail as wrapper noise. Meaningfully changed emphasis or other unqualified differences conservatively stay open.

### O33-04 — Signatures require their own narrow evidence

**Material concern: high severity, plausible.** Signature-shaped closings can contain a new phone number, role, privacy instruction or the only reply. Re-reading current profile/template data can rewrite historical identity.

**Exact language:** “Keep first, unique, changed and uncertain signatures/closings visible. A retained outbound managed signature may use its preserved D23 slot/source provenance and exact rendered content to identify a repeated region. Incoming structural signature markers identify candidates only; complete repeated-content proof and a qualified boundary are still required. Unmarked/unsupported closings remain open. Do not infer identity from matching email or signature text.”

Comparison uses historical admitted content that is currently eligible, never today's profile or Saved Section. Signature-only/quote-only messages must still communicate their actual currently eligible content rather than collapsing to an apparently empty response. A signature folding action is not D23 Hide for this reply or a persisted instruction to omit future signatures.

### O33-05 — Current access before comparison and expansion

**Material concern: critical severity, possible.** Matching against a hidden merged source can leak its existence; an old segment map or expansion response can reveal expired content after a move, permission change or redaction.

**Exact language:** “Tenant/actor/current source purpose and disclosure scope are derived from trusted context for segmentation inputs, source reads, cached maps, expansion and search navigation. Comparison cannot consult an unauthorized earlier source or reveal its existence. Segment references bind the current source revision and rendering-policy generation; stale maps/responses are rejected or rebuilt from currently permitted content. A fold is never an authorization boundary.”

If an earlier comparison source is no longer eligible, do not render a special hidden-source hint; leave the current message's own authorized representation open as appropriate. Current restriction applies to source content, derived maps/text/HTML and client state, including delayed responses and reconnect. Already received bytes cannot be remotely recalled.

### O33-06 — Search reaches the real source

**Material concern: high severity, likely without integration.** DOM-only Find misses collapsed/unmounted text; target offsets can drift after source change; stale excerpts can reveal removed content or highlight a different nearby message.

**Exact language:** “D22 retains its full current-eligible source population and item/phrase boundaries in both modes. Display-folded text remains searchable where D22 permits it. Exact-match navigation loads and expands the necessary current source region even beyond the mounted page, without changing the saved default. The highlight maps to actual source grapheme/structure boundaries; stale/restricted matches show truthful unavailability, not substituted text.”

The source-aware navigation path is the authoritative Find behavior. `hiddenUntilFound` or browser Find can only supplement already mounted eligible content. A find match must not remain inside a closed fold; clearing search must not unexpectedly collapse a section the user deliberately opened. Content forbidden for indexing remains excluded even if a different direct-read purpose can display it.

### O33-07 — Tiptap/static rendering failures stay truthful

**Material concern: high severity, concrete current-source hazard.** Generic JSON parsing accepts unsupported documents, render exceptions become blank messages, or HTML fallback makes blocked images/links active.

**Exact language:** “Apply the existing D23 canonical purpose/schema/semantic and safe-static rendering contract before segmentation. Segment typed safe nodes or their qualified display representation; do not cut arbitrary HTML strings or parse raw MIME into the app DOM. If segmentation fails, show qualified Full. If source rendering itself fails, show an explicit current-authority-aware unavailable/repair state or a separately qualified equivalent safe-text view, never a successful empty message or raw HTML fallback.”

A collapsed panel is not a network defense: use the source-qualified image/link/asset policy in every mode. No contentError normalization is autosaved over history. No live editor is mounted simply to read a source, and no broad Tiptap/DOMPurify upgrade is implied by this requirement.

### O33-08 — Only two preference values; no policy authority

**Material concern: medium severity, plausible.** A tenant default, persistent source state or generic preference engine can be accidentally introduced around one personal display choice. Late loading/saves can collapse text while somebody is reading.

**Exact language:** “The saved personal reading default contains only Compact or Full, with Compact when no choice has been saved. It is distinct from the effective temporary mode of the current conversation and from per-region disclosure state. Save/reset is deliberate and truthfully acknowledged; temporary expansion/search/navigation does not change the saved preference. Another user, source work action or inbox assignment cannot set it indirectly.”

Use the qualified shared personal-preference mechanism and trusted actor/tenant scope, not a new generic policy engine or a body-bearing browser archive. The root's data contract should settle the exact scope, conflict/retry and reset rules. Preserve the current conversation's deliberate mode/focus/anchor when a saved default is refreshed elsewhere; do not auto-collapse it on a late preference response. Missing/failed preference lookup must not masquerade as a successfully saved Compact choice or block access to eligible messages.

### O33-09 — Shared components with exact valid state

**Material concern: medium severity, concrete API mismatch risk.** Copying a modern-looking Radix example can create an empty/two-selected mode or break focus and disclosure semantics in Core's Base UI wrapper.

**Exact language:** “Reuse Core's exact shared Maia/Base UI Collapsible and reading-mode control. The effective mode always has exactly one valid value; a toggle-off cannot leave no mode. Independent disclosure buttons expose truthful expanded state and meaningful labels and preserve keyboard, focus, RTL and touch behavior. Use the actual controlled array-valued Base UI API, `render` composition and semantic tokens; no Radix `type`/`asChild` assumptions, alternate preset or app-local primitive.”

Do not make a clickable message card a giant disclosure trigger containing links/buttons. Focused content must not be silently removed by mode/default refresh; deliberate collapse restores focus predictably. Avoid nested repetitive fold controls, automatic scroll jumps and height animations. Mobile/zoom and assistive-technology proof apply to the real shared composition, not just the primitive's existence.

### O33-10 — Preserve P17/P6 and managed signature behavior

**Material concern: high severity, plausible.** A reading preference can be incorrectly reused as an outgoing-body serializer or signature omission preference, changing what recipients receive.

**Exact language:** “Reading mode, segment maps and expansion are absent from P17 canonical authoring/preparation and P6 immutable transport material. They do not alter reply targeting, quoted outgoing content, recipients, signature slots, required P17 footer content, published wording, template/catalog keys or delivery history. No new outbound delimiter, message family, automatic reply, notification or provider operation follows from reading.”

P17 retains governed authoring/presentation and immutable source-qualified preparation; Support admits the actual reviewed effect and P6 dispatches/reconciles it. Routine signature publication changes do not rewrite historic display content. D18/D23/D24 live draft insertion/refresh/omission actions remain distinct deliberate operations with their existing safeguards.

### O33-11 — Attachments and CRM keep independent actions

**Material concern: high severity, plausible.** Folding a footer can hide the only current attachment card, auto-open a file or copy content into CRM to preserve context.

**Exact language:** “D32 attachment cards and source-state indicators remain discoverable for their original message independently of a folded body region. Preview and Download original retain current qualification and deliberate action; expanding a quote fetches no attachment bytes merely because they are referenced. The same Support detail through CRM preserves draft, audience, list/search, source anchor and return context with current joint permission.”

No party/profile copy, title/subject rewrite, activity/task, financial/care mutation, business completion or additional requester surface is created. A quoted sender, participant, CRM relationship, assignee or matching signature proves no identity or access. D10 merge/Undo and D12 related sources preserve original lineage.

### O33-12 — Bounded processing and migration

**Material concern: high severity, plausible under long/hostile threads.** Pairwise whole-history comparison, catastrophic regex, unbounded DOM mounting or stale projection caches can turn a cosmetic feature into a performance or disclosure failure.

**Exact language:** “Segmentation is bounded per current source and qualified comparison context, with declared input/node/depth/candidate/work/time limits and deterministic profile/version identity. Use qualified source lineage/candidate lookup rather than all-pairs global transcript comparison. Exhausted/unknown comparison leaves content open. Full is a display mode over bounded source loading, not a command to fetch or mount every message/attachment at once.”

Do not introduce a new stored vector/text corpus, external parser service, AI classifier or worker workflow solely for folding. Cache only bounded derived maps within current source custody; invalidate on content/restriction/render-policy changes and clear source-scoped overrides across actor/tenant context. Unknown segment/profile versions fall back to qualified Full, never hide content using guessed offsets. Inventory actual provider/import/read paths before activating Compact; source already lost cannot be fabricated or silently recovered from another owner's retained copy. A kill switch can disable folding while preserving Full, source restrictions, drafts and other Support work.

## Falsifiable acceptance proof

These are required proof obligations for the root synthesis, not executed product tests:

1. **Conservation:** expanding every fold produces the exact same current safe source representation, order, links/assets and block semantics as Full; neither operation writes source/prepared/send/CRM state.
2. **Inline novelty:** top/bottom/interleaved/partial-inline replies, first-only forwarded material, changed numbers/phone/address/role, and a new answer inside quoted HTML remain visible. Changing only an href, strikethrough or an unqualified meaningful mark prevents a false repeated match.
3. **Conservative regions:** unknown quote/signature markers, malformed nesting, Unicode/bidi, provider normalization changes and comparison-budget exhaustion remain Full/visible. Signature-only/quote-only messages are not presented as empty. No word-level partial fold hides an inline answer.
4. **Historical signatures:** same retained signature can fold with valid provenance; changed or first signature stays visible; today's profile/publication cannot replace an admitted copy. Reader toggles never invoke draft omission/refresh or remove required footer content.
5. **Authorization races:** revoke/move/reclassify/redact/expire/merge/Undo between map generation, fetch, expansion and highlight. Forbidden earlier sources do not affect visible labels/counts/folding; delayed old data cannot repopulate restricted content.
6. **D22:** a permitted quoted match in a folded, unmounted message loads the exact current region and becomes visible; incorrect source revision is unavailable rather than substituted. Search boundaries remain unchanged; display collapse does not become index exclusion.
7. **Preference/interaction:** initial Compact, saved Full, explicit temporary mode, one-region expansion, save/reset failure, concurrent/late updates, tenant/user switch and return from CRM/attachment preview produce truthful stable state. ToggleGroup never leaves zero/two active modes. No other staff view or current work record changes.
8. **Renderer safety:** unsupported canonical JSON and static-render failure yield explicit failure/qualified equivalent fallback. External images/scripts/URLs are not activated by mode changes; malformed HTML is not interpolated into controls. Local generic helper shortcomings are not inherited as successful empty messages.
9. **Accessibility:** labelled buttons/expanded state, keyboard/Space/Enter, focus after collapse and search, logical reading order, long names/RTL, touch, 200% zoom, responsive chrome and reduced motion pass on supported real browsers/assistive technologies. No DOM-only Find completeness claim.
10. **Scale/recovery:** long/nested/repeated/hostile sources meet declared budgets and remain usable with bounded history loading. Unknown/stale map versions fall back safely; source policy purge clears all derived representations; no logs/preferences become body archives.
11. **Studio/CRM/attachment non-effects:** verify zero P17/P6 template/preparation/send changes, zero live Tiptap draft/signature/recipient/work-intent changes, no auto-file reads, no new P18/P29 file derivative and no copied CRM/financial/task history from every reading action and failure.
12. **Actual release compatibility:** verify the implemented shared wrappers against pinned Base UI/Tiptap/parser contracts, mixed deployments and current safe source profiles; measure a representative email corpus and staff reading tasks. Source research and static inspection alone are not a claim of detector accuracy or effortless UX.

## Synthesis

**Accept with required amendments.** Record C with initially Compact and a single personal Compact/Full choice. The mandatory narrowing is conservative reversible segmentation over one current safe source, leaving uncertain/changed regions open. Source and display lifetime, D22 exact navigation, historic signatures and the no-P17/P6-effects seam must be part of the recorded answer, not left to heuristics during implementation.

The least complex path reuses qualified source rendering/projection and existing shared UI/preference machinery, adds a bounded display map and controlled disclosures, and explicitly reconciles the old stripping wording. Current generic rendering weaknesses must be addressed at their existing owner seam before Compact activation. Build no replacement editor, email delimiter system, transcript store, generic preference policy engine or AI detector. Remaining numerical limits and real compatibility/fidelity/accessibility/load evidence belong to the root's release contract; they cannot be replaced by an untested claim of perfect recognition.
