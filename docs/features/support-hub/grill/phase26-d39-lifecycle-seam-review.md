## Exact proposed clauses

### L01 — Private personal orientation

“Ordinary conversation read/unread belongs to the current real staff member in the current tenant. It is not shared with teammates, exposed as a reader roster, copied to a CRM property, or synchronized with a provider mailbox. The indicator says that qualified content lacks the person's recorded viewing coverage, or that they deliberately marked it unread. It never proves comprehension, complete historical review, attachment inspection or requester receipt.”

Keep shared Open/Waiting/Resolved, assignment, follow-up, reply targets, failure recovery and directed attention authoritative. A personal unread cue is not a task, priority, service commitment or instruction that every accessible conversation must be read. No unread total, red urgency badge, personal-work dashboard or manager activity report is added by this decision.

### L02 — Simple absent-marker baseline

“With no personal coverage marker, compute Unread on demand if currently permitted eligible content authored by another actor exists. This includes retained historical or newly accessible content. Self-only and empty originals create no automatic unread cue. Do not fabricate a historical Read event, copy the old shared unread_count, create a tenant activation cutoff, or fan every existing message/conversation out to every staff member.”

The product meaning is **not yet recorded viewed for you**, not newly received today. Old history does not create new notifications, work, deadlines or obligation debt. Normal work views and the absence of an aggregate badge limit noise. Existing personal coverage, once established, survives ordinary rereads and source grouping under its current lawful scope. This is a deliberate product judgment favoring simple honest absence over a complex prospective baseline that silently treats unviewed history as read. A first-use explanation may clarify the meaning in existing help; no compulsory onboarding or Mark all read workflow is needed.

### L03 — Closed eligible human-content set

“Eligible ordinary reading activity consists of a newly admitted human request contribution (qualified inbound email or contact-form input), the first admitted human Staff context brief, a newly published native-human Internal note, or another staff member's locally admitted human external Reply. Each retains its real original source and first content-admission identity. Content must be currently permitted and safely representable to the current reader.”

A locally admitted human reply may honestly be Queued, failed or delivery-unknown while its body is new readable content. Later provider acceptance/delivery callbacks do not generate another reading event; D15's accepted-send optional-follow eligibility remains distinct. Self-authored activity is excluded from automatic unread only with a trusted actual human-author mapping. Assignment, public signature, matched email, imported sender display name or worker identity cannot prove self authorship. An external message that merely matches the staff member's email is not automatically a trusted self-authored Support operation.

Do not include private drafts, autosaves, system/automatic acknowledgments, scan/upload progress, delivery callbacks, following changes, work/assignment/label/priority/merge events, routine note/brief edits, redaction or expiry as new ordinary unread content. Those owners keep their genuine state/attention cues. An independently actionable owner result remains discoverable through its established Support need/attention, not by turning every domain event into a conversation message. Unqualified legacy/generated content is not promoted to native-human evidence merely because it displays as a note.

### L04 — Content ordering reflects committed admission

“Use a source-owned committed content frontier, advanced with the corresponding canonical source admission in one transaction. The body's current authorized snapshot and its cutoff must be coherent. Allocate ordering through the actual source serialization/row-lock protocol, not email Date, wall-clock equality, generic updated_at, provider callback arrival or a standalone sequence allocated before commit.”

For retained legacy content that predates these reading ordinals, first gate the original against personal-marker activation, capture its complete already-committed qualified baseline under the source owner protocol, and assign stable internal read-control ordinals before personal markers can acknowledge it. Preserve actual content IDs, original times and existing display chronology; these ordinal labels do not reconstruct historical commit order, admit new content, fabricate Read or emit D15/D17/native events. Qualify concurrent live intake/admission through the same source gate and durable owner custody; future first eligibility is serialized above that complete baseline, including late genuinely new contributions regardless of their old supplied dates.

Counterexample: transaction A reserves ordinal100 and pauses; B reserves101 and commits; a viewer acknowledges101; A then commits100. A naive `<=101` read-through silently marks unseen A as read. The required source protocol makes this impossible: a later committed contribution cannot appear behind an acknowledged committed cutoff. Scope ordering to originals/current owners, not a new global event bus, per-keystroke stream or recipient fanout. Keep raw source ordinals, visibility episode references and cutoff vectors server-side; a client uses an opaque, scope-bound integrity-protected snapshot context under the current owner checks. A clear base64-signed payload is not opaque if hidden sequence gaps or scope identities remain readable. Caller-supplied future cutoffs or forged coverage cannot establish Read.

### L05 — Authorization coverage accompanies the cutoff

“A read-through cutoff is valid only for the exact original and visibility/authority coverage represented when it was acknowledged. Current source authority supplies a bounded control-only coverage basis over existing visibility/grant episodes. It must prove current eligible scope unchanged/subset, or identify newly eligible coverage. Preserve acknowledged cutoffs for retained coverage; new or regranted coverage starts without such coverage, so an older previously hidden contribution cannot inherit Read merely because its ordinal is below an old cutoff.”

Contraction, redaction and expiry remove ineligible contributors; they do not reset retained read coverage and create phantom unread on already-viewed surviving content. Where the owner cannot safely prove coverage relation, show a truthful unavailable/needs-current-view reading state rather than invent Read, reveal hidden activity, or reset everything to Unread. A fresh qualified current view can establish coverage anew without claiming prior reading. This can use a compact original/scope cutoff vector if the qualified owner requires it; no new arbitrary ACL groups, message-body copy, per-message reader ledger or product-wide permission-history service is authorized. The actual Supabase contract must demonstrate this with Core's existing source/permission mechanisms.

### L06 — Qualifying automatic view, without surveillance

“Automatic acknowledgment requires the deliberately selected conversation's actual foreground, visible, focused reader, a successfully validated and rendered current eligible latest-content region, and the exact server-qualified represented original/coverage cutoffs. API load, prefetch, list snippet, hidden pane, background refresh, notification delivery, Tiptap focus/save or a renderer wrapper with blank error content is insufficient.”

No dwell timer, word-by-word/read-duration measurement, per-message full-viewport requirement or persistent scroll log is needed. A long newest message can qualify once its meaningful reader region is presented; this is orientation, not evidence that every word was consumed. D33's legitimate folds do not require expanding repeated quotes or every old message. Qualified attachment-only content may present its real metadata/readiness without requiring preview/download; no file-inspection assertion follows.

A successful normal combined-reader open may acknowledge its complete server-qualified represented original/visibility cutoff vector once the latest eligible combined region is safely rendered. Older folded or paginated bodies need not all be mounted or individually viewed; that would impose unnecessary per-source reading chores. Included original scope means present in the qualified reader snapshot coverage, not every body visible in the viewport. Missing, truncated, unproved or inaccessible source coverage is excluded rather than guessed. A historical search jump or scrolling older history does not automatically acknowledge newer unrepresented material. If a later update arrives while the person is away from the current latest region or actively composing, preserve their position and show the existing quiet New activity/return-to-latest path. Active composition suppresses new-arrival automatic acknowledgment even when the latest message remains visible. An inactive saved or dirty draft alone does not block normal reading. Deliberately returning to the reader or selecting New activity may qualify actual latest-region presentation under the existing still-valid activation; it does not create a new activation epoch that bypasses a manual Mark unread. A later current rendered snapshot can acknowledge only its represented scope, and a new arrival after that cutoff remains unread. Loading errors/unavailable source cannot clear the cue or be called Read.

### L07 — Merged originals remain independent

“Keep reading coverage on original sources. D10 current handling combines their currently permitted contributions for one displayed conversation; Merge/Undo never copies, collapses or invents reading coverage. An automatic combined view acknowledges the exact complete authorized original/visibility cutoff vector in its successful reader snapshot when the latest eligible combined region is presented. This may include B whose older bodies are folded, paginated or offscreen; it is orientation to combined context, not proof of viewing each original body. Showing A alone with missing or unproved B coverage cannot acknowledge B, and hidden/inaccessible contributions remain excluded.”

Separate retained source cutoffs make Undo meaningful. Read A plus unread B initially makes the combined view unread. A normal qualified A+B snapshot opened at its latest eligible combined region can acknowledge both represented cutoffs without visiting B separately; an A-only or incomplete snapshot cannot. Explicit Mark read remains a deliberate way to acknowledge its exact current authorized snapshot. On Undo, A/B retain their own history; no root-level flag backfills all children. Any explicit component command binds exact authorized original vector and topology. Newly merged C is not silently swept in by a delayed command. Inaccessible original names/counts/activity do not leak through an aggregate dot; a narrower inspection does not attest to broader scope.

### L08 — Explicit Mark read

“Offer an ordinary personal Mark read action using the exact currently displayed server-qualified authorized conversation/component snapshot and its cutoff vector. It may acknowledge that reviewed scope without pretending every body/file was viewed. It changes only the current person's orientation state. An arriving H+1 contribution after displayed cutoffH remains unread even if the action commits later.”

Do not substitute latest head at acknowledgment time, mark all tenant/inbox content, include sources absent from the displayed authorized scope, or reset another person's state. Deliberate Mark read may clear the applicable manual-unread intent under its current expected epoch. It does not approve sending, certify reply coverage or mark unrelated notification groups read.

### L09 — Explicit Mark unread and view activation epoch

“Mark unread is a deliberate personal revisit marker for currently qualified readable human content, including the person's own authored content when they intentionally choose it. Empty, expired or excluded-only sources provide no content to mark and do not silently create a phantom row. The action has no deadline, work transition, shared reminder, Follow or notification effect.”

Advance the person's manual-intent epoch/control generation under a conditional command. Every automatic acknowledgment is bound to the epoch of its original deliberate view activation. Already-open tabs, ordinary focus/visibility changes, background fetches and refreshed content cannot acquire a new activation simply to immediately clear the marker. A **new deliberate conversation open** after the epoch, or explicit Mark read, can clear it with current source coverage. Preserve the marker during the existing view so Mark unread is not instantly undone. No global lease, staff lock or reader roster is required.

Bind the manual marker to its current eligible source/coverage applicability. A restriction/expiry can end inapplicable retained-content marking without fabricating a read or reviving bytes. It is not an indefinite hidden bookmark store. Source metadata/custody and minimal control evidence remain independently owner-governed.

### L10 — Small current-state command protocol

“Manual read/unread commands bind real tenant/owner, exact original/coverage/topology snapshot, current personal control generation and expected manual epoch. A stale opposite command cannot overwrite a newer deliberate choice. Automatic acknowledgments merge only valid nondecreasing represented cutoffs within their accepted epoch; they do not increment the manual epoch on every view.”

Duplicate/stale manual commands reconcile or reject with current permitted state; an unknown outcome first refreshes authoritative state and never blindly reapplies the old intent with a newly fetched epoch. A status failure is not proof that the mark was canceled or failed. Current state, source generations and bounded control evidence are sufficient; do not copy P6's permanent per-email occurrence ledger into every reading click. Cleanup/restore must not reset a control generation so an old command can recreate the marker. Require current-generation commands rather than update-as-create after retirement. No user-facing read-history journal, per-message last-seen timestamps or cross-user audit roster is created.

### L11 — Honest failures without blocking ordinary work

“Reading-state read/write/loading failures remain separate from conversation body loading, draft saving, Add note, Send review and work commands. Preserve a truthful last confirmed state only where current authorization permits; label unavailable/pending rather than inventing Read/Unread or zero. A stale response cannot cross actor/tenant/source/coverage or manual-epoch changes.”

A person may continue otherwise authorized Support/CRM work when the personal cue service is unavailable. No unread-clear transaction is a prerequisite for a valid Send, owner-result review, note publication or resolution; those owners perform their own real guards. Optimistic cue feedback can be temporary but cannot remain falsely confirmed after failure. Coalesce refresh/foreground sync using existing bounded read facilities, with no constant polling or body broadcast. Personal reading changes never reset Tiptap content, clear drafts, consume notes, change recipients, reapply shortcuts or move the reader unexpectedly.

### L12 — P17 notification engagement and actual work stay separate

“D39 personal conversation reading is not D15 Following, P17 notification-group engagement, D11 result review, D14 response coverage or D3 shared work. Viewing/Mark read/unread does not automatically read/archive all associated notifications, cancel direct attention, Follow/Stop, end an obligation, open/resolve a conversation, change assignment or satisfy a response target.”

An actual existing typed notification-click engagement rule remains with its owner, but D39 introduces no broad conversation-ID linking heuristic. A notification deep link may lead to a qualified conversation view; only that subsequent actual presentation can qualify D39 coverage. Conversely, notification email acceptance/open pixels do not establish staff source viewing. D15's7/30/90-day classes and P6 occurrence IDs are not reading-state retention or cursor keys. Routine corrections, expiry and delivery events do not manufacture fresh conversation unread debt.

### L13 — Email Studio and P6 seam

“Support owns the personal orientation state and exact eligible original-source content coverage. P17 continues to own governed reusable content, whole-message preparation and notification presentation/engagement; P6 continues actual communication/delivery and immutable reconciliation. Reading creates no Email Studio draft/publication/prepared artifact, provider read receipt, delivery correction or new notification.”

A Queued human reply may be new readable content while its P6 state remains Queued; read status cannot be used as acceptance or delivery evidence. Self authoring or later provider callbacks never acknowledge other content implicitly. D23/D36 external signatures/quotations, D37 Reply and D38 Note drafts, and D35 posted-note history stay independent. No provider mailbox sync, tracking pixel, sender fallback or employee-reading notification is added.

### L14 — CRM and privacy continuity

“An authorized Support reader reached through CRM uses the same real actor/source personal state and preserves the user's return context. Opening a CRM record/list alone grants no viewing coverage or Support access. Party merge, shared email, relationship, assignment or record ownership does not transfer personal markers or expose another person's reading.”

Markers, coverage metadata and boolean cues are sensitive. Apply actual tenant/owner/source authority before list predicates, read results, state commands, caches, optional realtime, export and diagnostics. No colleague/manager read roster, timestamps, throughput or duration statistics. Exceptional security/records custody is narrowly owned and audited; ordinary administrative status is not a personal-reading browser. D16/D17 restrictions/expiry remove ineligible content/coverage without erasing independent CRM or sent-message history or fabricating user engagement.

### L15 — Migration, source scope and retirement

“Do not backfill personal reading from old unread_count, assignee, provider status, message author's address or source last-update time. Existing unvisited other-authored content follows L02 on demand. Before that original becomes eligible for personal markers, capture its complete already-committed baseline and assign stable internal reading ordinals under L04, without claiming to recover historical commit order. Future first eligibility is admitted above the baseline. No source IDs/times/native clocks change, and no migration emits new activity, notifications, D15/D17 events, work or per-person fanout. Install qualified source ordering/coverage and private marker commands/readers before replacing the shared dot.”

Source publication, visibility changes, manual control generations and current marker updates share a proven ordering/fencing discipline. Retire the misleading shared-counter display/write dependency for the personal cue; keep independently valid aggregate/source data with its own owner. Mixed-version code cannot acknowledge hidden scope, ignore manual epochs or recreate retired state. A stop can disable the cue/updates while retaining current source restrictions, actual work, drafts and communication recovery. Roll-forward/restore preserves negative source/control barriers before serving markers. Actual retention and minimization for marker/coverage/control data are a release inventory requirement; there is no eternal per-click ledger.

### L16 — Proof, performance and observability

“Use one metadata-only private projection, sparse current personal markers and bounded source coverage rather than staff-by-message fanout or copied bodies. Complete source authorization and visibility semantics precede pagination or a boolean existence test. Load no draft/message bodies solely to decorate a list, and subscribe to no per-person keystroke stream.”

Prove actual production-shaped source/permission/query plans, source-cardinality limits and mixed-original coverage before launch. Technical metrics may measure aggregate state-request failures/latency and command conflicts with minimized protected correlation; they may not become employee-reading analytics. Any confirmed hidden-activity leak, cross-user mutation, stale manual reversal, source-gap false Read or lost incoming contribution is a release blocker/containment event. Source/code/document checks do not substitute for real DB/RLS/browser/AT tests.
