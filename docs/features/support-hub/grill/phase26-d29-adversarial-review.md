## Exact corrected decision

> Help opens a clear **Contact [tenant]** experience with the message form already available. A person does not have to search, read an article, choose a department, talk to a bot, sign in or prove a CRM match before asking for ordinary help. The form asks for a reply email and message, with an optional unsplit name. A published monitored tenant email is visible and copyable, and a published phone contact may be offered when the tenant actually provides it. These alternate methods do not create a call, request or delivery record merely by being clicked.
>
> Up to three deliberately selected, currently eligible public guides may accompany the contact experience. They are optional information, visually secondary to contact. Opening a clearly labelled guide link preserves the contact draft. No guidance or a guide-only failure leaves contact usable. Internal Staff guides, Shared by link Pages, private CRM/giving context and unqualified language/site content are excluded from public discovery. A includes no new public library search, help-center destination, live chat, telephony integration, AI or mandatory self-service.
>
> Public pages use P23's released public presentation/form owners. Authenticated apps use a narrowly qualified app consumer of the same purpose/admission contract, with separate current tenant/principal and optional owner-authorized context. A genuine form occurrence produces exactly one recoverable Support intake outcome and a truthful browser receipt, without inventing an incoming email, staff-authored note or duplicate CRM activity. First replies and subsequent email use the existing governed Support/P17/P6 paths with exact source/recipient authority.
>
> Browser receipt and optional email confirmation are separate. A new Help form starts with visitor email confirmation Off; an authorized tenant may enable P23's single optional acknowledgement child only through its separately qualified web-Contact source/recipient/Email Studio contract. D13's receiving-email trigger is not reused. The existing automatic-receipt courtesy and safety gates must cover both source families without blocking accepted intake or human replies. Guide use never triggers D28 feedback or marks Support work resolved.

## Exact required amendments

### D29-R01 Purpose, priority and alternatives

Contact is the primary job of this Help entry; guidance is optional. A is preferable to B, contact with ordinary pages separate, when current relevant guides can save an avoidable round trip. If no useful guidance exists, A provides B's complete contact journey. C, A plus a public browsable/searchable library, is the strongest broader alternative for a substantial maintained corpus; no evidence establishes that every Asym tenant needs it. Do not add C's library/search, a public version of D27 Staff guides, AI or other channels merely for competitor parity.

### D29-R02 Direct entry and clear contact affordance

The existing qualified Help/Contact entry SHALL open **Contact [tenant public name]** with the form available immediately, not a search-first, department-first or channel-choice gate. Keep the entry consistent within each actual surface and preserve the user's return location. A full-page semantic route is always available; an app may enhance it with its existing accessible panel/sheet pattern without making the enhancement a prerequisite. On narrow screens contact precedes optional guides. No “Have you tried the articles?” step, forced article rating, countdown, empty bot conversation or unearned online indicator.

### D29-R03 Minimal replyable form

Qualify one narrow ordinary-inquiry Purpose Profile, not claimed to exist already: **Email for our reply** and **How can we help?** are required; **Name (optional)** is one unsplit field. No mandatory subject, surname, donor/account number, organization, phone, topic, department, address, screenshot, login, marketing consent or checkbox asserting articles were tried. This Help form is text-only and payment/upload-free under P23. Tenants may adjust qualified labels, explanatory copy and locale, but may not turn this standard entry into a questionnaire or change field meaning. Other P23 form purposes retain their own broader lawful customization.

### D29-R04 Validation and bounded input

Message is nonblank plain text, up to 10,000 Unicode scalar values after CRLF/CR normalization to LF; optional name is up to 200 scalars. Preserve international names, combining characters, punctuation and line breaks; no Western name splitting or English-only rules. Validate one reply mailbox through the current qualified P6/contactability parser and its actual supported length/encoding contract; no silent alias folding, typo “fix,” CRM replacement or claim that syntax proves ownership. Enforce a 256 KiB raw request-body ceiling before expensive parsing, plus the exact released envelope/field limits. These are proposed product bounds, not industry norms. Show visible labels, explain the optional field and keep entered answers on recoverable validation error. Client hints cannot narrow legitimate server-supported mailbox input or substitute for server validation.

### D29-R05 Public direct contact methods

The tenant deliberately publishes a monitored email for ordinary contact; managed Support addresses reference their qualified D25 route/public identity rather than staff/admin/login/billing email. Show selectable address text and optional Copy email enhancement alongside the mailto link. Offer phone only from deliberately published tenant contact data with a current accountable maintainer; display an international dialable number and readable formatting. Do not require a phone service or infer staffing, 24/7 availability, callback, geographic reach or live presence. A click only opens the person's external client; it proves no call/email or Support request. Published display data cannot establish P23 Verified Email Destination authority.

### D29-R06 Small contextual guide selection

Each qualified Help entry may select zero to three exact public Page references in a deliberate order. Use current source-owned title and optional safe summary with a canonical source-qualified destination; no copied article body or editable shadow content. Context means an explicit owner-approved page/task/entry key, not donor value, role-based public content, private records, referrer text, browsing history, URL query or AI inference. No dynamic “popular” list, mandatory search, fallback to unrelated articles or tenant-authored matching rules. Reject a fourth selection at configuration rather than silently dropping it at runtime.

### D29-R07 Public eligibility and withdrawal

Public discovery requires current exact Tenant/environment/Site/locale/public-generation and **Listed** source eligibility, source safety and valid canonical destination. D27's staff finder allowing Shared by link does not grant requester discovery. Internal guide titles/body/history/search and draft/preview content never enter this population. A source withdrawal/reach/locale change removes its offer without substituting another guide; restoring source eligibility may restore an unchanged active selection, but cannot recreate a deliberately removed selection. Unknown proof is unavailable, not a reason to broaden scope or use a stale cache.

### D29-R08 Source-owned presentation and optional failure containment

Public placements belong to P23's released Page intent. Explicitly qualify a bounded **Optional Help guides** semantic leaf over at most three Page refs, its renderer/contract version and prequalified empty/unavailable presentation. P23's initial Article dynamic-source catalog is not already a Page-help qualification. D1 still releases one complete generation; the leaf's current adverse/source admission may suppress ineligible items without rewriting saved selection, inserting replacements or changing the contact route. The contact form/direct-contact closure is a required separate dependency; a guide-only failure suppresses only the optional leaf. Unsafe whole-Page/base/renderer/form authority is not bypassed by serving a partial generation; an independent safe Contact route is usable only under its own current qualification. This is explicit P23 conformance, not arbitrary runtime template patching.

### D29-R09 Safe reading and return

Present guides as clear links, with a concise visible/screen-reader indication that **Open guide** opens a new tab. Use ordinary user-activated anchors with noopener/no-referrer protection; no on-load popup, miniature renderer of arbitrary CMS forms/embeds or automatic article query. The contact form stays intact in its current page. Browser-default tab/window controls remain available. A source link can explain how to use an authenticated owner action, but the guide grants no receipt, giving, identity or care authority. No guide click creates a ticket, “resolved” event, deflection claim, feedback invitation or CRM contact history.

### D29-R10 Separate public and authenticated app consumers

The public adapter derives scope from trusted public host/route/current P23 generation and exact released form. The app adapter derives current portal/app tenant/principal, approved entry and exact purpose/route binding through its owner. Qualify both against one common Contact purpose/occurrence/Support handoff contract; no second submissions ledger. A portal host is not a public Site; a browser-supplied Site/tenant/form ID cannot establish scope. App guidance references use qualified published public content without personalizing CMS body/list output. Public and app releases have their own receipts/readiness; do not claim a cross-owner atomic publish or silently read an unreleased mutable “latest” configuration.

### D29-R11 Prefill and shared-device safety

An app may visibly prefill the current actual requester's authorized self-contact information; never default to a represented organization's, linked donor's or CRM owner's address. Email remains reviewable/editable and is a submitted response endpoint, not a CRM mutation or verified identity assertion. Public anonymous content stays auth-invariant. Bind draft/prefill to current tenant, entry and local principal/session context; clear the full draft on successful receipt, deliberate logout or tenant/account change. On context-only permission/revision loss, remove unqualified context references and source-derived prefill while preserving only still-permitted visitor-authored text for deliberate requalification or reviewed contact without account details. No persistent browser storage, device-ID history, tracking identity or hidden autosave for this form. During a recoverable same-context failure preserve local answers. Session expiry must offer qualified reauthentication or deliberate ordinary contact without private account context; never silently retarget a private draft to another actor or submit stale record references.

### D29-R12 Optional authorized context

App-origin Help may present one concise **About [permitted context]** summary of its actual owner-qualified context reference, removable before submit. It is optional to ordinary contact. Server rechecks current authorization, tenant and source revision when accepting it; a plain hidden field, route parameter or matching email is insufficient. Invalid context requires a clear retry/requalification or deliberate **Send without account details** path preserving permitted visitor-authored text; never quietly attach another record. No private names, financial facts, processor IDs, screenshots or raw URLs are copied into public guide selection, search, mailto, analytics or the P23 public presentation. Linking context grants no additional staff access.

### D29-R13 One genuine form intake outcome

This Support Help profile requires P23's certified Support handoff as its exactly one Primary Outcome. P23's other email-only outcome remains valid elsewhere but is not a hidden fallback or a second simultaneous master. One authoritative acceptance transaction commits the immutable form occurrence, exact released route, source-owned Support work/acceptance receipt, optional child intents and required dispatch requests before **Received**, or commits none. Support acceptance responsibility is durable at that boundary. If conversation materialization is deferred, accepted pending intake is discoverable to its currently authorized Support/recovery owner and recovers the same occurrence exactly once with its original admission clocks, including after a route change. Later materialization failure is visible recovery, not lost work. An Inngest event or external email alone is not acceptance. Browser answers cannot choose recipients, inbox, actor, template, header or route.

### D29-R14 Honest form-origin source and first reply

Represent the submitted text as a native **form-origin requester message** with its own original source/occurrence and trusted admission time. It is not inbound RFC email, a staff-recorded D26 brief or an internal note. Do not create fake provider receipt, Message-ID, From header, inbound delivery or human actor fields to fit current email-only defaults. A replyable endpoint can exist without a Party. Staff see the actual submitted endpoint and any separately qualified context in the normal canonical conversation, with current source/recipient checks. The first human reply uses a qualified D4/P17 form-response recipient basis and actual outgoing email lineage. It may continue a real acknowledgement thread already established; otherwise it starts the first outgoing thread. Subsequent actual email uses D1/D2/D25 correlation, not subject/email guessing.

### D29-R15 Duplicate, retry and uncertain acceptance

Use the form owner's existing replay/idempotency mechanism, qualified end to end to permanent Support intake effect. One intent with identical semantic input returns the same privacy-safe receipt; changed input under that intent conflicts. Double clicks, refresh, lost responses and delayed dispatch cannot create another original. Received status/recovery is scoped to the exact protected browser occurrence capability/current app authority, exposes no message or CRM record and never becomes an enumerable public ticket lookup. On ambiguous network outcome show **Checking whether your message was received** and reconcile the same intent. Direct contact alternatives remain visible, but do not automatically send the draft through another channel. A person deliberately sending a separate email may create another genuine source; D10 reviewed duplicate handling applies, not silent cross-channel merge.

### D29-R16 Clear receipt and no false promises

After durable acceptance, show **Your message has been received** and the accurate next step: the tenant will handle it through its qualified Support process, with replies using the provided email when permitted. Display a safe reference if the existing occurrence contract qualifies it; no reference entry is required to keep conversing. Do not say delivered, read, assigned, solved, refund completed or promise a deadline from an internal target. A phone/email link click shows no Received state. A failure before acceptance clearly says it was not received; unknown remains unknown. Confirmation is persistent and accessible, not toast-only. Accepted work survives optional guide, rendering, acknowledgement, notification and executor failures.

### D29-R17 Optional visitor email confirmation

New standard Help forms start with visitor email confirmation **Off**. An authorized tenant may enable the existing P23 zero-or-one Visitor Acknowledgement child, subject to a newly qualified web-Contact source/recipient/P17 publication profile. Browser receipt needs no email readiness. D13 remains receiving-email-only; form admission does not invoke it as a second confirmation. The web profile may reuse neutral wording through valid authoring/copy and an explicitly compatible publication, not relabel an email-only contract. No arbitrary answer/body/context echo, CC, personal data disclosure, fundraising, workflow branch or automatic knowledge answer is added. Like D13-R17, the web confirmation’s Recent sent copy is Off-only: one body-free Automatic confirmation event with actual state, no permanent generated body/subject in support_messages, and synthetic publication preview labelled Template used rather than an actual sent copy.

### D29-R18 Confirmation timing and shared courtesy

If enabled, the web confirmation is eligible only for its exact accepted new form occurrence and submitted response endpoint under current form/recipient/abuse/contactability authority. Its utility ends 15 minutes after trusted form admission, with no intentional delay or clock renewal. Extend the qualified automatic-receipt courtesy control to share one tenant/mailbox 24-hour bucket across D13 receiving-email and web-form receipt families; per-source-key buckets must not permit duplicates. Human reply takes precedence when it wins the current shared gate. Preserve D13-R15’s full rule: a qualifying public human reply for the same current request and exact confirmation recipient, admitted after confirmation submission may have begun, also ends every additional confirmation provider-call/decrypt authority, including otherwise permitted indeterminate same-key follow-ups. Reconcile already in-flight outcomes without recall; a later staff send failure never revives that confirmation. Current Off/revocation/readiness/source/route fences stop definitely-unsubmitted work; possible submission follows P6 reconciliation, never blind retry/rekey. Suppression never rejects the primary request or blocks a human reply. New source and expiry semantics must be qualified across P23/P17/P6 before enabling this child.

### D29-R19 Work targets, feedback and histories

Explicitly qualify D14 for replyable form-origin input: a genuine owed first reply starts at trusted form acceptance/admission under the existing source/calendar policy, not a fabricated receiving-provider date or delayed worker timestamp. Human reply coverage and acceptance still govern fulfillment/First/Next; browser receipts and automated confirmations earn no human reply credit. D28 can consider genuinely completed form-origin Support only after the actual qualified human public email correspondence and all existing sampling/source/recipient guards. Mere form submission, acknowledgement or guide use is not feedback eligibility. A form occurrence is not a fake P6 inbound email; actual outgoing messages contribute once through the shared spine.

### D29-R20 CRM continuity and authorization

Guest requester, authenticated user, submitted mailbox, message participant, CRM Party, represented organization, worker and CRM owner remain distinct. No exact-email auto-create/link, implied identity verification or transcript permission. Qualified app context and deliberate later D9 linking may relate the actual Support source to CRM under current permissions. CRM Communications → Support opens the same canonical detail; the Overview remains its existing limited preview. Link/unlink/Party merge changes current authorized navigation, not original authorship/intake/recipient provenance. No duplicated Activity/body/last-contact/financial outcome is inferred from form or guide use. Refunds, receipts, recurring gifts, contact changes and sensitive care actions retain owner authorization and audit.

### D29-R21 Supabase grants, RLS and constraints

Derive tenant/environment, public/app source binding, authenticated actor when present, route, source identity, timestamps and audit from trusted server context. Guests are represented honestly without fake authenticated users. Enforce tenant-aware parent relationships, valid exclusive producer branches, one occurrence/primary effect, immutable accepted source/route/actor fields and valid state/time combinations. Preserve current owner-compatible text IDs. No direct anonymous writes/reads to Support, form-control, routing, audit or private-content tables. Qualify grants, SELECT, USING/WITH CHECK, column writes, invoker/definer views/RPCs, fixed search_path and service jobs together; a permitted row cannot be reparented or transformed into a forbidden state. Existing broad Support CRUD is not proof. Money tables/types are unchanged.

### D29-R22 Privacy, source retention and safe rendering

P23 occurrence is minimal delivery/recovery truth; Support owns the requester content after certified handoff, with references rather than a second readable CMS inbox. Qualify the retained envelope, replay material, selected refs, app context, request source, review/audit and every actual secondary copy under finite owner purpose/ceilings and D16/D17 restriction/disposal/restore rules. New form content has its own trusted admission; it cannot renew old transcripts through a later link/merge. Public submitted-page/error/receipt responses and app private context are no-store and no-referrer under their owner contract; static public Help content may use its qualified cache without embedding visitor answers. Escape untrusted text; no raw HTML/Tiptap/URL unfurl or automatic AI consumption. Logs/metrics/analytics/provider tags never contain answers, raw endpoints, private refs or replay secrets.

### D29-R23 Proportionate abuse protection

Reuse P23's trusted origin/generation/schema checks, CSRF/replay controls, bounded parser/request/field limits, tenant-fair multi-dimensional rate limits, backpressure and accessible risk-based challenges. No mandatory puzzle for every person or hidden trap that classifies assistive technology/slow typing as abuse by itself. Required retention/classification/safety floors still apply to hostile free text. If acceptance is denied, show an honest state and independently qualified published alternatives without revealing accounts or internal routes. A displayed alternate never bypasses its channel's actual safety/intake policy; anonymous emails and challenges do not grant CRM authority. No bot/spam score is called proof of a human or mission need.

### D29-R24 Maintenance without a new platform

Provide one understandable **Requester help** management entry showing the actual public/app placements, contact route, exact locale, selected guides, readiness, preview and current owner actions. Public Page/form/copy/phone/email publication delegates to Web Studio/P23; app-entry mappings delegate to the app's qualified presentation owner; Support routing and Email Studio editing remain at their sources. Do not claim one shared atomic release or duplicate their editors. Choose/reorder/remove at most three current public references, explain why an item is ineligible and show no-match/zero-guidance honestly. Save drafts and publish/activate are distinct; source edits do not mutate old accepted requests. No freeform URL rules, generic taxonomy, public/privacy toggle or automatic promotion from D27 staff curation.

### D29-R25 Contact readiness and failure recovery

Require a qualified normal contact path and published monitored email before exposing this standard Help entry; phone is optional. A guide failure cannot disable a still-qualified form/direct route. If the form cannot accept, say so, preserve safe local visitor-authored text and expose only still-current separately approved email/phone alternatives; no invented fallback address, platform inbox, another tenant or stale CMS preview. Whole-scope identity/host/locale/source ambiguity fails safely rather than displaying someone else's contact. Accepted requests remain recoverable when providers/executors fail. Managers see the exact affected owner/action and can repair without direct database changes or resetting idempotency.

### D29-R26 Accessibility and low-bandwidth behavior

Use existing Core semantic design tokens/Base UI ownership for app surfaces and P23's approved public presentation, with native semantic form submission and no-JavaScript path. Visible labels, optional-field text, descriptive links, proper autocomplete/input purpose, keyboard operation, error summary linked to fields, retained answers and persistent receipt are required. No placeholder-only labels, color/emoji-only status, hover-only contact or focus-stealing suggestions. Prove 200% zoom, narrow mobile, long translations, RTL/bidi, keyboard and named screen readers. A JS/network failure must not strand the ordinary form or use a weaker authority protocol. Do not promise offline submission or hide a send queue in browser storage.

### D29-R27 Performance, measurement and operating bounds

No new full-corpus search, behavioral analytics or article-rating subsystem. Resolve at most three selected Page refs in bounded owner queries, with contact rendered independently of optional guide latency and current authorization before content/counts. Later source-read performance must be qualified on an explicit nonproduction workload: at least 100 simultaneous Help loads and 20 competing retries of one form intent across two tenants; contact first usable p95 ≤2 seconds under a documented network/server fixture, zero duplicate primary effects and zero cross-scope disclosures. These are release targets, not measured capacity claims. Technical counts distinguish attempted/accepted/rejected/unknown/primary recovery and optional delivery; article visits are not solved/deflected/satisfied or CRM activity.
