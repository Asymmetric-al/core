## Final reconciliations after independent QA

The independent reports below retain their source findings and proposed clauses. The final D5 review resolves these differences and controls the now-ratified decision:

1. **Canonical meaning is an ending reason.** Earlier reports sometimes say outcome/disposition. No response is the recorded reason for ending this follow-up episode, separate from confirmed customer/business outcomes. It does not mean no prior human messages. Acknowledgement/partial input invalidates old review, but a clearly unanswered part can remain the reviewed basis without mandatory extra chasing. Explicit refusal/inability/withdrawal or a new owed clarification is not silently classified as absence of response.
2. **There must have been a real request.** A solely drafted or definitely-unsubmitted request cannot qualify. An actual conveyed or possibly submitted request may be reviewed honestly without proof of human reading. Fully reviewed irrecoverable uncertainty need not impose eternal work, but outstanding/actionable recovery and actual promises remain obligations. An optional final courtesy reply may be queued after a previously qualified wait; it is not the sole first request used to manufacture No response.
3. **Awareness is exact, not an invisible email.** The compact staff review explicitly shows No email will be sent and the actual reminder effect. Requester correspondence is an optional deliberate human reply through D4, with prior promised updates still owed. No claim that an unmessaged requester knows about the internal ending is made. Mandatory final notices were considered and rejected as a universal default; no hidden closing/survey sender is added.
4. **Dismissal semantics change at submission.** Before submission Cancel/Escape/dismiss commits nothing. After submission, closing UI is not cancellation; durable pending/unknown results reconcile under the same identity. Current-state feedback persists independently of the modal/toast.
5. **The combined action is an explicit D5 extension of D4.** Send and end follow-up carries the No response reason/review basis visibly. Selecting another ordinary action clears incompatible metadata; ordinary Send and resolve never inherits No response. Opening the optional draft does not end work. Local-only ending preserves drafts; combined admission may retire only the exact admitted reply draft, preserving newer edits/notes/recovery.
6. **Historical correction cannot mutate current work.** It binds the expected episode revision, preserves endedAt and original evidence, and records correction lineage. After a reopen or later ending it cannot restore Resolved, assignment or timers. Newly exposed current obligations cause D3 review rather than silent history rewriting.
7. **Report findings are conditional on their meaning.** A current-assignee cohort and creation-based duration can be legitimate when labelled. They are not historical actor credit or the same population as an ending-date count. The executed probe exposes those differences; it does not claim every existing cohort is a defect.
8. **Monitoring does not fabricate legacy evidence.** Missing actor/reason/source alerts apply to newly admitted qualified D5 endings; legitimate legacy reason-not-recorded records remain explicit. Operational thresholds are proposed accountable triggers, not measured rates or contract SLAs.
9. **No lifecycle model was executed.** The five-check probe calls actual pure reporting source. Lifecycle counterexamples are paper analysis and later acceptance criteria; they are not an executable proof of the new command, SQL, RLS, provider or UI.

#### Root decision refinements

1. Name **No response** as an ending reason, not evidence of no prior human messages, deliberate neglect or a confirmed problem outcome. The currently still-needed response is absent at a reviewed point. Partial input/acknowledgements invalidate old review but do not force another chaser merely to requalify the same remaining request. Explicit refusal/inability/withdrawal or a new request for clarification requires its actual handling and is not silently classified No response.
2. Keep one staff action and one simple review surface with short consequences; no checkbox pretending to prove every free-text promise, no long mandatory narrative and no new blocker/task graph. Known authoritative guards plus accountable human review are the proportional control.
3. Optional final correspondence stages D4's exact typed send-plus-ending intent. Opening that draft must not end follow-up early. New specific questions/promises are work; an invitation to reply later with the already requested information can accompany an ending without creating a fresh chase promise.
4. Preserve work history and ending reason, but do not retain bodies forever to make old threads resumable. Normal no-response ending imposes no final lock/deletion. Later valid input uses the retained qualified conversation link; where owner retention has removed linkage, safe new intake must not resurrect private data or silently drop the person.
5. No ordinary End action creates P6 outbound evidence or a duplicate CRM communication. It is a Support work event. An actual optional reply has the D1/D2/P6 per-recipient communication effect once. Read-only authorized CRM projections may reference the same Support fact without becoming its write authority.

All refinements are proposed D5 amendments for founder ratification, not completed runtime safeguards. Unknown activation proof is listed explicitly in the final review.

#### Findings and exact permanent clauses

##### F1 — Ending eligibility must be deliberate and bounded

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood conditional on ordinary incomplete requests and staff pressure to clear queues. Effect: narrows B.** An operator could label a bounced question, an unreviewed new answer or an outstanding promised Finance check “No response,” hiding actual work. Conversely, requiring every external system to be terminal or every delivery ambiguity to disappear would create perpetual Support work. D3-R01/R02/R08/R12 distinguishes actual obligations from independent owner facts.

**Proposed clause:** “An authorized worker may end follow-up as No response after reviewing the relevant unanswered request, admitted participants, available delivery evidence and actual remaining Support commitments. Requested input needed to proceed is still missing, and staff deliberately ends further permitted pursuit. No required Support step, promised follow-up or actionable/unreviewed recovery may be hidden by this choice. An unrelated independent owner process does not by itself keep Support open. Missing or restricted owner evidence is not completion evidence; retain a safe indication and any actual review obligation.”

“Use current authorized evidence and staff intent, not last-message direction, zero message count, elapsed days, CRM email equality or AI classification as permission. Explicit withdrawal/refusal or a relevant new substantive response must not be blindly called No response. Review the meaning of an acknowledgement or incomplete answer rather than treating any mailbox event as either proven resolution or automatic disqualification forever.”

“A due reminder may already have moved genuine requester-blocked work to Open for review. Eligibility depends on the reviewed obligation, not the literal current Waiting label; no Open→Waiting→Resolved detour is required. Waiting on our side with an active required blocker is ineligible. Merely relabelling it Waiting for requester cannot bypass that check.”

No mandatory duration, follow-up count, promise-detection NLP, task graph or new admin approval role follows. The server enforces authoritative known blockers and review/version requirements; staff supplies judgment that cannot honestly be inferred from unstructured correspondence.

##### F2 — Preserve delivery uncertainty without imposing endless waiting

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood normal email failure/ambiguity. Effect: narrows B.** No response is not proof of successful contact or human reading. A reviewed irrecoverable uncertainty is also not necessarily an actionable recovery obligation forever. D1/D2 distinguish submission and member evidence; D3 distinguishes fresh required recovery from already-reviewed facts.

**Proposed clause:** “The ending records observed missing response and staff judgment without asserting that a person read or ignored a successfully delivered question. Draft, queued, failed, suppressed and indeterminate mail remain truthful separate facts. Actionable or unreviewed delivery recovery must be addressed before ending; already-reviewed irrecoverable uncertainty may remain when no Support commitment is owed. Preserve its provenance and uncertainty rather than changing it to delivered. A later fresh actionable delivery/owner result creates Open review under D3.”

Native group evidence remains member-qualified: successful delivery to a copied person cannot establish delivery to the person whose input is needed; nor should ending require reading/delivery by every irrelevant copy. No new provider state, synthetic Party, open-tracking requirement or fallback send is introduced.

##### F3 — One command must commit reason, episode and timer effects

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood high under the current setter if extended superficially. Effect: requires inherited D3 implementation plus D5 purpose.** Current setter can stamp Resolve repeatedly and has no episode, eligibility, version or atomic audit. Separate end and audit requests permit an ended conversation with missing reason/history. A browser-only guard can be bypassed by macro, bulk, direct DML or a stale client.

**Proposed clause:** “Use the canonical server-owned Support work command with typed end-follow-up cause and No response disposition. Derive tenant/current actor, authorize the current conversation, and check the reviewed work/conversation and materially relevant evidence revisions. Atomically persist current work change, unique episode identity, reason, trusted actor/time, before/after evidence, current reminder cancellation/generation and any required durable recovery intent. Rejection or conflict commits none of these effects. No response ending alone creates no email or communication event.”

“Same command identity and unchanged canonical input returns its original admitted outcome, with current authorized conversation state separately. Different input under the same identity conflicts. An already-ended episode is not a new ending: a repeated No response action cannot advance endedAt or create a second episode. Requesting a different reason on an already Resolved episode requires explicit correction, not a fresh Resolve or silent overwrite.”

“Keep exact bulk IDs and reviewed scope; enforce all rules per item. Return durable per-item applied/unchanged/stale/denied/failed/indeterminate outcomes. Do not imply all-or-nothing across separately committed conversations, repeat successful endings on retry, or apply the choice to newly matching rows.”

No transaction spans a mail provider or independent owner process. Where owner facts can change after review, use qualified source evidence/revisions and D3's causal review of new relevant results, rather than inventing distributed atomicity or locking every CRM record.

##### F4 — Grants, RLS, relationship integrity and actor attribution must enforce the same boundary

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood conditional on available direct writes or copied service patterns. Effect: required prevention; inherited D3 debt, not a new tenant permission product.** RLS exists and both UPDATE clauses scope old/new rows correctly to tenant/staff. But authenticated DML includes history updates/deletes, and a same-tenant permitted audit row can name any existing global profile ID. Those checks do not enforce command-only mutation, real actor, immutable episodes or reason/state combinations. Broad staff scope is itself intentional MVP.

**Proposed clause:** “Keep RLS enabled. Preserve tenant-aware logical relationships from conversation to current ending, ending to its conversation, correction to its specific episode, and command/reminder evidence to the same tenant and subject. Enforce non-null valid work meaning, known disposition for newly admitted ending episodes, unique business-effect identity and monotonic revisions. If the current Resolved projection references an active ending, it must reference that conversation's current episode; a reopened conversation cannot expose an old ending as its current outcome. Historical endings remain available separately.”

“Revoke client mutations that bypass work/episode/correction/history invariants. Approved client reads retain current scope; any remaining allowed mutations need both old-row USING and new-row WITH CHECK and immutable ownership checks. Remove permissive bypass policies/grants rather than adding a stricter policy alongside them. Service-role and RPC paths independently authorize subject, actor and cause; use restricted execution privileges and a safe search path. Internal command/identity hashes are not a new public client table.”

“Derive real actor user/profile from trusted authenticated context; do not accept caller-selected actor, use assignee, infer by email or assume userId equals profileId. Delegated service execution preserves the initiating human separately from execution actor; a timer cannot masquerade as the staff review required by B. Preserve permitted historical attribution under owner retention rules without requiring live membership forever or fabricating a profile for a system actor.”

Physical tables/enums/RPC names are intentionally not fixed. The database must prevent contradictory pairs and cross-conversation references, not rely on JSON conventions. Amount/currency columns are not introduced, so no new money precision decision arises. Conversation/body/attachment retention is not selected; ending cannot delete or cascade away required evidence. Current audit ON DELETE CASCADE and SET NULL behavior require the already-ratified retention/attribution reconciliation before reuse as permanent episode history.

##### F5 — Reopen, correction and Undo are distinct outcomes

**Material concern: yes. Severity high for lost work; medium for misleading history. Likelihood normal retries, mistaken classification and late replies. Effect: precise refinement.** A new reply after ending legitimately creates new work; it does not prove the old ending was dishonest. A reason correction changes classification of the same ending, not when or how often it ended. Current mutable resolvedAt cannot express either safely.

**Proposed clause:** “A first-time admitted relevant human reply reopens through D3 and preserves the original ending episode, even when its sender timestamp is older. Automatic/quarantined/unrelated/duplicate intake does not act as that reply. End-before-reply leaves new Open work; reply-before-end invalidates stale review. The expired/cancelled reminder generation cannot reopen or re-end later work.”

“Correcting an ending outcome appends correction evidence against that exact episode, with actual correction actor/time, original/new classification and expected episode revision. It does not create a new resolution episode, move original endedAt, rewrite the original event or force current work back to Resolved. After a later reopen, historical correction remains historical. If the correction reveals an actual outstanding current obligation, create/reconcile the required Open review; cosmetic reclassification cannot hide it.”

“Undo of a mistaken ending is a new conditional authorized correction/reopen, not history deletion. Exact restoration is offered only while the affected current work/reminder still permits it under D3. Restore any eligible cancelled reminder with a new generation; if now due, make follow-up actionable. Newer input or work makes stale exact Undo unavailable. Distinguish ordinary reopening for new work from a correction stating the earlier ending itself was mistaken.”

Reporting must state whether it uses original event facts or corrected classifications as of report generation. A correction is attributable to the original episode/date while retaining correction evidence; it is never counted as another ending. The full taxonomy of withdrawal, duplicates and other endings is not introduced by this clause.

##### F6 — Historical reports need an explicit event and cohort contract

**Material concern: yes. Severity medium; likelihood demonstrated in current pure source. Effect: required truthful reporting; no new analytics platform.** Actual-source probe results:

1. Current September resolution count is1; after a fixture applying the verified reopen shape (`resolvedAt=null`) it becomes0.
2. Replacing an August ending timestamp with September moves the prior August count1→0.
3. Reassigning the same ended record from A to B moves historical agent count from A to B.
4. A conversation created in August and ended in September yields September ending count1 but September duration result0, because those metrics use different cohorts.
5. The current `[from,to)` period boundary correctly includes the lower and excludes the upper instant.

These show source behavior, not a deployed incident. Current-assignee filtering and creation cohorts can be legitimate if explicitly named; they are not evidence about who ended the conversation or endings occurring during the selected period.

**Proposed clause:** “Current work counts use current state. Historical endings use durable episode identity, endedAt and typed disposition; reopening or reassignment cannot erase or invent an episode. No response is shown distinctly and never silently included in a metric claiming verified problem resolution or first-contact success. Ordinary Resolved is also completion of Support work, not automatic evidence of donor confirmation.”

“For each exposed metric/export, define the population and period: conversations created, ending episodes occurring, or current backlog; actor-at-ending versus current assignee; original versus corrected classification; calendar versus business hours; tenant/viewer time-zone conversion and lower-inclusive/upper-exclusive bounds. Do not put incompatible cohorts under labels implying the same denominator. Correcting a reason changes the qualified classification, not the event date or episode cardinality.”

“Apply authorized scope and outcome/date predicates before pagination; use complete database aggregation or bounded exhaustive processing, not the capped2,000-row list. Choose indexes from actual tenant/conversation/endedAt/disposition access paths and measure representative skew; do not add speculative sharding or a search platform. Current labels/CRM associations are not historical attribution unless the report explicitly describes current association.”

Negative result0 can mean no eligible observations; it must not suggest instantaneous success. Do not invent past No response reasons from age, last-message direction, current CRM data or arbitrary JSON tags.

##### F7 — Preserve owning-domain authority and optional-message boundaries

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood conditional on handoff and courtesy mail. Effect: preserves existing scope.** Contribution actions and their notification/recovery obligations have their own permission and audit paths. Support closing can neither certify payment outcome nor dismiss owner attention. Existing CRM query links and a generic source string are not a qualified Support handoff.

**Proposed clause:** “Read relevant owner context under its own permissions; retain qualified same-tenant references and outcome projections, not copied financial/CRM truth. No response changes no Party, owner, refund, receipt, recurring gift, approval or shared owner task. Required owner recovery remains visible through its owning flow; separate independent owner work does not force artificial Support dependency. A missing Support source/reference seam must be qualified through the owner contract rather than mislabelled as a CRM-originated action.”

“Ending alone sends nothing. An optional human final reply follows D1/D2/D4 exact audience and ready-preparation rules. If a UI offers a combined Send-and-end-No-response operation, both effects must be visibly approved, the exact reply fully validated/compiled/prepared before local admission, and message/work/reason/episode/reminder/dispatch intent admitted atomically. A mere template insertion cannot end work; a later send failure creates qualified review instead of pretending the ending never occurred. Separately executed Send and End remain separately honest commands, not falsely atomic.”

This does not itself add a D4 composer option, require a final courtesy message, authorize an automatic notice or broaden Email Studio catalog/retention. D4's seven-day execution-material ceiling is neither a no-response deadline nor conversation retention.

##### F8 — Fence legacy writers and preserve unknown history

**Material concern: yes. Severity high if old writers remain; likelihood predictable mixed-version rollout. Effect: required sequencing.** Current status setter, macros, browser collection shapes and numeric auto-resolve settings cannot preserve new reason/history/version invariants. Parallel `support` is a separate legacy module, not proof it currently updates Support Hub's table; inventory it and consolidate/retire according to Phase26 rather than inventing a demonstrated shared writer.

**Proposed clause:** “Introduce compatible reader/history/command contracts before enabling No response. Inventory actual writers and fence every path that could bypass the new cause/version/history rules, including direct table grants, old API clients, macro set-status and any activated automation. Disable/retire the misleading legacy automatic setting for this staff-controlled policy without running stored14day/zero/null values as new semantics.”

“Migrate known timestamps as qualified legacy observations, not reconstructed ending episodes with invented reason/actor. Legacy unknown outcome remains visibly unknown where history is reported; it need not be a fifth normal work state. Prevent old clients from overwriting a current reason or erasing episodes on reopen. Kill switches stop new unsafe endings while preserving reads, admitted-command reconciliation, inbound/delivery review and due-work recovery. Roll forward a compatible repair after new history exists; rollback cannot restore the bypassing writer or drop the evidence.”

#### Consolidated exact decision

“Support Hub permits authorized staff to deliberately end further follow-up on a reviewed unanswered request when no actual required Support commitment or actionable/unreviewed recovery remains. Record No response visibly as the reason for the existing Resolved work state and in its durable ending episode; this does not claim successful contact, a solved problem or completion of an owner-domain action. Use the canonical authorized, versioned and idempotent Support command to atomically record state, reason, attribution, history and applicable reminder cancellation. Ending sends no message. Newly admitted relevant input or fresh actionable evidence returns work to Open under D3, preserving the ending. Repeating an ending does not restamp it; a correction appends evidence to the same episode without manufacturing another resolution. Fully reviewed irrecoverable uncertainty is retained truthfully and does not create indefinite work by itself. No automatic deadline, fifth work state, new obligation graph or mandatory chase sequence is introduced.”

#### Exact recommended clauses

##### L1 — One explicit reason on a real ending episode

“End follow-up — No response is an authorized staff decision to complete the current Support follow-up obligation because clearly requested needed input remains unavailable after current review. The work state becomes Resolved with that explicit episode disposition. It is not a fifth work state, a delivery/read assertion, requester blame, a withdrawal, confirmed problem resolution or completion of an owner-domain action. Ordinary Resolve does not silently assign this reason.”

“Staff-facing current state may read Resolved · No response while that resolution episode is current. The supporting explanation is ‘Follow-up ended; the needed information was not provided.’ Historical replies remain visible. The label must never imply there were zero earlier messages.”

##### L2 — Review the current request, not an arbitrary last message

“The review identifies the existing clear outstanding request and the remaining needed input using the authorized conversation context and stable message/context references. It includes the latest relevant admitted input, relevant delivery/recovery evidence and current Support commitments. A last outbound timestamp, current status, original requester identity, latest internal note or generic updated_at is insufficient authority.”

“Use existing permitted messages/notes and the admitted review revision; do not create a mandatory question taxonomy, task graph, field-by-field requester form or another CRM record. Do not require copying sensitive content into a new disposition field when stable context references suffice. The actor's judgment and review basis are auditable; the database cannot prove the meaning of freeform prose.”

##### L3 — Partial responses trigger review without forcing another chase

“Any first-time admitted relevant human input invalidates an older ending approval and creates D3 Open review, whether it supplies a full answer, a partial answer, an acknowledgement or a new question. After review, an unchanged still-unanswered portion of an already clear request may remain the follow-up basis. A new outbound question is not mandatory merely because someone said ‘thanks, I will look’ or answered only part of the request.”

“Preserve the new input and record/reuse the remaining need in existing context. A complete answer ends that no-response basis. An explicit inability/refusal to provide the requested input, withdrawal, or a new clarification the team owes must not be rewritten as silence. If a new required fact was never actually requested, it is not an unanswered old request. The appropriate ordinary work action may still be available under D3; D5 does not require a new catalog of other outcome codes.”

This deliberately treats No response as missing needed input after reviewed participation, not ‘no human bytes ever arrived.’ Explanatory copy such as No further response can clarify a partial-participation history without creating a second canonical outcome. No AI/regex policy automatically decides that an answer is insufficient or changes work from text.

##### L4 — Admitted participants and machine/forwarded input

“Requester input can come from an admitted person seeking help other than the original CRM-linked address. A legitimate partner/team representative's contribution may satisfy ordinary information needs without proving authority to perform a protected owner action. Matching email, quoted From lines, forwarded text or possession of a thread token alone cannot grant that authority.”

“Machine OOO/acknowledgements do not satisfy the human information request or automatically resolve, reopen or reschedule work. Their safe admitted content may inform the human review, including an advertised return date. Unclassified/quarantined/ambiguous intake that may contain relevant work must remain visible to the proper reviewer under D1/D3; it cannot be silently interpreted as no response. This does not open a second intake engine or require every spam item to block every conversation.”

##### L5 — End only this Support obligation

“Ending cannot hide a current substantive Support action, an actual promise to follow through, or a required unreviewed/actionable recovery step. A known owner process matters when Support still owes obtaining, communicating or following through on its result. An independent pending owner-domain process with no remaining Support commitment does not force this conversation to stay open. Ending neither cancels nor completes that process.”

“Known structured obligations and review fences are enforced at the owner boundary. Staff reviews ordinary freeform commitments; do not claim a status command can automatically discover every promise or introduce a generic obligation engine solely for this feature. Loss of access to context required for a decision requires an appropriately authorized review, without revealing restricted content.”

##### L6 — Truthful delivery review, not endless unprovable work

“Sending a question, a provider acceptance or mail-server delivery does not prove human receipt or understanding. A queued, failed, suppressed or unknown outcome cannot be relabelled delivered to support No response. Review the actual current contact history and recovery obligation. Outstanding unreviewed/actionable recovery blocks ending; already-reviewed irrecoverable uncertainty with no remaining safe Support action does not automatically create eternal work. Preserve that uncertainty and the actor's truthful rationale.”

“A newly queued first request is not by itself evidence of a period of absent needed input. A known unsubmitted sole request has not established that the needed input was sought from the person. Do not use the no-response label to disguise that fact. An optional final courtesy message queued after a previously qualified period of follow-up is a different case and may remain pending while the existing obligation ends. No read receipt, blanket delivered-event prerequisite, fixed chase count or fixed number of days is added by D5.”

Qualification caveat: a required request may be evidenced by existing authorized conversation context rather than only a Resend webhook. This is not a mandate to build a new phone/SMS channel or accept unverifiable imported statements as protected-action authority.

##### L7 — Fresh conditional admission, normal D3 timer effects

“End uses the existing canonical conditional transition with current tenant, actor, authorization, reviewed conversation/input/work version and stable command identity. The ending, episode disposition, cause/review references, timer cancellation and durable effects commit atomically. If current input or obligations changed, return the authorized fresh state/conflict without recording an ending. Same-identity replay reconciles the same result; changed input conflicts.”

“A due reminder may already have made the conversation Open; that does not make No response unavailable after review. Successful ending cancels that current reminder under D3. A cancelled/older timer or delayed earlier command result cannot reopen or overwrite the ending. Conversely, newly admitted relevant human input or genuinely fresh actionable adverse/awaited-owner evidence opens review under D3, while preserving the earlier reason. Work state and provider truth do not overwrite each other.”

##### L8 — Optional final correspondence is explicit and consistent

“D5 adds no automatic ending notice. Staff may use D4's qualified composer to send a final courteous message. Merely leaving the door open—‘Reply here whenever you have the information’—does not create a proactive follow-up promise. A new required question, deadline promise, promised chase or uncompleted required action is inconsistent with claiming no current obligation remains; staff must choose the appropriate work intent.”

“This consistency is part of the explicit staff review and any known typed owner fences, not a new text classifier that secretly overrides D4. If a combined Send-and-end convenience is offered, its visible action carries the No response disposition and uses D4's ready preparation and one atomic local reply/work admission. Preparation/validation failure creates neither effect. After admission, queued/unknown/adverse delivery remains honest and recoverable; later failure causes at most its fresh D3 review effect, not replay of the ending. Do not implement a compound action as separate browser Send and status requests.”

“If that combined convenience is not offered, keep the default ending entirely local and the existing composer behavior unchanged. Do not silently expand the D4 After sending selector merely by changing the meaning of its existing Resolved item. Any adopted interaction must be explicit in the final D5 blueprint.”

##### L9 — Current versus previous No response stays honest

“The disposition belongs to the resolution episode. Reopening removes the current Resolved · No response presentation because the conversation is now Open; it does not erase history. A summary that displays the previous reason must label it Previous ending or equivalent with its date. A later ordinary resolution does not inherit No response from a conversation-level ‘last reason’ field. Repeat Resolve on the already ended episode does not manufacture a new reason, timestamp or episode.”

“Correct a mistaken historical reason through the existing authorized append-only correction lineage, not by overwriting the old evidence or reopening/re-resolving solely to edit a label. Current snapshots and reports can be derived; the historical reason is not a write authority for work. CRM activity projects permitted references without duplicate communication effects or unrestricted body copies.”

##### L10 — Quiet awareness and exact scope of reporting

“No response is visible in the current resolved detail and relevant list/history view, with a short accessible explanation and a discoverable authorized reopen/correction route. It is not conveyed only by a toast, icon, color or raw internal code. Show the actual current status after a racing event, not an earlier optimistic ending. Keep selected detail/draft/context through filter changes; finishing work is not implicit navigation away.”

“A count labelled completed Support episodes may include this ending, separately distinguishable by disposition. No response must not be counted as confirmed problem solution, satisfaction, owner completion or first-contact solution. Neither an ordinary unlabeled Resolve nor a historical unknown reason automatically proves those stronger outcomes either. Do not build a complete outcome analytics product for this one reason; preserve the minimal evidence now so later reporting can be correct.”

##### L11 — Individual review first, no bulk shortcut

“The initial No response action is an individual conversation review. Existing generic bulk Resolve, macros, API writes or old auto-resolve settings may not manufacture a No response outcome or bypass D3/D5. This does not remove unrelated bulk actions.”

“A future explicitly accepted bulk No response operation would need per-item current eligibility, actor authorization, reviewed context/revision and an affected-item result/reconciliation path; one checkbox over a truncated queue does not demonstrate individual review. Do not introduce this capability or a mandatory blanket confirmation flow for all ordinary Resolve operations through D5.”

This is a proportionate scope recommendation requiring inclusion in D5's ratified amendments if adopted. It is not a claim that every manual multi-item action is inherently unsafe or that the user separately forbade bulk actions.

##### L12 — Preserve histories through rollout and old writers

“Add compatible episode/disposition readers and canonical guarded writers before enabling the new action. Old generic status/macro/bulk writes cannot erase or invent the reason. Legacy resolved records without trustworthy reason remain unspecified/unknown; never infer No response or solved-problem success from age, no recent message or `resolved_at` alone. No migration sends reminders, activates a seeded 14-day policy or rewrites provider evidence.”

“D5 reason/history retention follows the existing owner policy, not permanent body duplication. Reply tokens/RFC reference routing and safe late intake obey D1; ending alone does not expire the email continuation path or demand a fresh ticket. No business effect is undone by rolling back a label.”

## Independent staff/requester awareness and UX review

### D5 — Awareness when staff end unanswered follow-up

Independent bounded UX/product review, checked 10 September 2026. Founder selected B: staff may explicitly end eligible follow-up after review, recording No response. D1–D4 are ratified; **the amendments proposed here are not yet ratified**. Baseline supplied by root remains `7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd`. No implementation, provider action or canonical document change was performed.

#### Recommended policy

**Make staff awareness mandatory and requester communication deliberate.** Ending follow-up must be an explicit, attributable action with a visible No response outcome, a clear reminder consequence and truthful current-state feedback. The action sends no email on its own. Offer a useful final human reply through the existing composer; if selected, its reply admission and No response resolution are one D4 operation. Never send a second automatic closure notice behind that reply.

This handles “users aware” in two distinct ways:

- Staff must understand what they are choosing before it happens and be able to see who ended follow-up and why afterward.
- The requester must retain normal email continuation and receive honest wording in any selected final reply. The product cannot claim the requester was notified when no email was chosen, or that they read a delivered email.

**The strongest alternative is a required final notice for every No response outcome.** It makes the end of active follow-up explicit to every reachable requester and reduces inconsistent staff messaging. Its cost is mandatory extra correspondence, including after an already clear question or earlier final follow-up. It introduces a delivery dependency and risks contradictory closure/receipt notices. No Asym evidence establishes that every case benefits from another email. Prefer an optional, clearly offered human reply, with a prior genuinely unanswered request and no unfulfilled our-side promise as eligibility gates. Do not reinterpret B as authorization to run a notification sequence.

**A hidden automatic notice is weaker than either choice.** Staff cannot reliably explain the action, preview the actual D2 audience, or avoid duplicates. A generic “issue solved” email is false when the issue is still unknown. A mandatory modal or explanation on every ordinary Resolve would also be unnecessary. D5 needs one proportionate review surface only for this distinct end-follow-up outcome.

#### Proposed concrete interaction contract

##### 1. The user enters an explicit, narrow review

Expose **End follow-up…** in the existing completion/action affordance, not as a fifth status. Selecting it opens a compact review surface and performs no mutation or send. Use the existing anchored popover/dialog patterns; a narrow-screen rendering must keep the same meaning and fit the supported viewport without horizontal scroll. Do not add this review to every ordinary Resolve action.

Show this content, using only authorized current context:

> **End follow-up — no response**  
> We’ll stop planned follow-up on this request. A later reply will return it to the team.  
> **Awaiting:** the referenced request for information, with an accessible link to that message.  
> **Reminder:** the current follow-up will be cancelled, if one exists.  
> **No email will be sent.**

Use the real existing unanswered request, not a fabricated summary. Its text may be opened in place rather than duplicated into permanent metadata. An unavailable/restricted reference shows the truthful permitted state. Do not make staff retype a paragraph already visible in the thread, add a discretionary score, or fill a generic closure questionnaire. The fixed No response outcome is explicit; additional contextual notes remain optional and private.

Offer **End follow-up** and **Write a final reply** with **Cancel**/Escape. These are alternatives before execution, not sequential requirements. The dialog's initial focus is on its heading/introductory context, not an automatically activated completion control. End follow-up runs the reviewed local command with outcome and reminder effect. Write a final reply only opens/focuses the existing composer and stages the optional combined action described below. It has no immediate work or provider effect.

This extra review step is justified by the ambiguity of ending an unanswered obligation. It replaces surprise with a short explanation and an optional route to communicate. It should not become a generic “Are you sure?” modal followed by a second confirmation or mandatory email ritual.

##### 2. A final reply is optional and uses D4's one final action

**Write a final reply** opens the public reply draft in the same conversation, preserving any existing reply text and the independent internal-note draft. It preserves the D2 target/audience rather than guessing a new CRM address or collecting historical CCs. It stages **End follow-up — no response** in D4's **After sending** selector and changes the visible button to **Send and end follow-up**. A short adjacent summary identifies the No response outcome and reminder cancellation. No email or work mutation occurs while staging.

This is a contextual D5 outcome of Resolved, not a new default work status or sticky personal preference. Returning the After sending choice to **Keep current status** returns the visible button to **Send reply** and removes the staged end-follow-up effect. Choosing a different permitted work outcome clears incompatible staged No response metadata. Restoring this exact draft preserves its explicit choice; a new reply still starts with ordinary Send reply. The keyboard follows the visible staged action under D4's guards.

Optional wording may be offered through the existing qualified saved-reply mechanism. Do not silently replace a draft or generate/send a notice just because the action was opened. For a **synthetic** gift-date example:

> We still need the date of the gift to look into this. We won’t send further follow-ups for now, but you can reply here when you’re ready and our team can pick it up.

Staff edit this to the real request. No “solved,” “successfully completed,” accusation of ignoring staff, countdown threat, ticket-number requirement, portal link or compulsory new request is needed. The phrase promises ordinary future contact, not perpetual retention of every old message or approval of a protected action. A genuinely required disclosure/verification still uses its owner's flow.

When staff press **Send and end follow-up**, one reviewed command binds D2 audience/content, D5 reason and D3 resolution/reminder effect. Known invalidity of either local component admits neither. There is no **Send now, then separately mark No response** implementation. External delivery remains asynchronous; a later failed final email can cause D3 Open recovery without pretending the earlier admitted work decision did not occur. Retrying delivery never replays an old work decision or sends a second closure notice.

##### 3. End without email: truthful result and recoverable correction

After accepted local completion, keep the selected conversation and safe draft/CRM context visible. The actual work status becomes Resolved, with a compact secondary outcome **Follow-up ended · No response**. History records the actor, time, reason and applicable unanswered-request reference under the owner retention rules. Do not create a second CRM communication event for an email that was never sent.

Brief feedback: **Follow-up ended. No email sent.** Where D3 permits conditional correction, offer **Undo** as an accessible command. The durable outcome and a valid correction/reopen path remain reachable in the conversation after any transient feedback disappears. Correction is a new authorized event; it does not erase history, recall mail, cancel a financial action, or resurrect an obsolete timer generation.

If the conversation no longer matches a list filter, update counts/membership truthfully but retain the selected detail and explicit Next navigation per D3. Do not inject a nonmatching row, jump to another conversation or use toast dismissal as a navigation trigger.

##### 4. Send and end: show both outcomes

After combined admission, use **Reply queued · Follow-up ended** (or the actual permitted dispatch state), not “Requester notified,” “Delivered” or “Problem solved.” The message row carries its delivery evidence and authorized per-recipient exceptions. The history carries the work reason. They are correlated facts, not one status badge.

If a new human reply or other independently admitted D3 event has already reopened work, show current Open state. A delayed completion receipt can say **End of follow-up recorded; a new reply needs review** if both facts are known. It must not overwrite the newer state with Resolved. Keep former No response as dated history, not an active assertion that nobody answered.

If there was a lost response, preserve **Checking outcome** / pending recovery and reconcile the original command. Do not offer a fresh duplicate Send as an error-recovery shortcut. If a preflight blocker appears, preserve the permitted draft, exact audience and staged action, state the blocker near the action and provide its real next step.

##### 5. Awareness stays appropriate to the audience

Other authorized staff see the outcome in the same conversation/history when they return; already-visible data refreshes through the existing projection mechanism. Reuse existing owner/assignment notification policy where it genuinely applies. Do not email every agent, create another notification center or duplicate audit rows solely because someone ends follow-up.

The donor, missionary or other requester continues in ordinary email. If no final reply was chosen, the product must not claim external notification. If one was queued/delivered, it must not claim reading or agreement. An email reply later is admitted through D1 and reopens under D3; end-follow-up does not lock the thread, force a new ticket, delete content or become a financial/privacy retention action.

#### Precise amendments proposed for the root decision

1. **“Ending follow-up records a staff work outcome; it does not imply that the requester’s problem was solved, that they agreed, or that a notification was sent. Resolved and No response remain distinct state/reason facts.”**
2. **“A clearly named end-follow-up review shows its unanswered-request context, reminder consequence and whether an email will be sent. Opening it or choosing the optional composition route performs no work mutation, reply admission or external send; authorized read/preflight checks remain allowed.”**
3. **“No automated closure email, CSAT or other external communication is triggered by this outcome. Staff may deliberately compose a final reply. Selecting that route stages D4’s current-draft combined send and explicit No response resolution; final admission is atomic for local effects and asynchronous for external delivery.”**
4. **“All resolution entry points, including generic status controls, macros, bulk and keyboard paths, enforce the same No response eligibility/outcome rules where the remaining obligation is unanswered requester input. A different control cannot hide the obligation by omitting its reason.”**
5. **“Outcome feedback distinguishes the original command receipt, current work state and actual delivery evidence. It remains discoverable beyond a transient toast, preserves current context, and offers only a presently valid conditional correction under D3.”**
6. **“A later admitted relevant reply reopens normal work without erasing the historical outcome or requiring a new account, portal or ticket. Ending follow-up grants no new CRM/disclosure/action authority and cancels no owner-domain obligation.”**

#### Acceptance scenarios

1. Opening/closing End follow-up review performs no send, work transition, timer mutation or audit claim of completion. Cancel/Escape restores focus and leaves both drafts intact.
2. A screen-reader/keyboard/touch user can understand the fixed No response reason, referenced unanswered request, timer consequence and no-email consequence before choosing the explicit action. Labels remain clear in long translations/RTL and supported narrow views.
3. End follow-up without email admits one eligible work outcome, cancels only the current reminder under D3, produces no communication send intent and leaves a discoverable actor/time/outcome record. Repeat activation/retry cannot append another episode.
4. Write final reply only stages the current draft. Existing text, attachments, internal-note draft and D2 recipients survive. Closing the composer does not end work. A new draft does not inherit the selected work action.
5. Changing After sending back to Keep current status removes the staged reason/effect. Visible button, screen-reader name and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter match the actual staged command. Picker Enter, IME and key repeat do not submit accidentally.
6. Send and end follow-up has one approval/durable local admission for content/audience/work reason/reminder. Missing input, access loss, invalid outcome or a stale review admits neither local effect. No Send-then-PATCH sequence or hidden extra closing email exists.
7. Known prior request delivery failure/uncertainty, new quarantined/relevant input or an unfulfilled our-side Support promise cannot become No response. The UI gives the permitted next step without revealing protected context.
8. Inbound-first rejects stale completion; completion-first followed by inbound shows Open while preserving former No response history. A late success response cannot regress the UI. Conditional Undo cannot overwrite intervening work or recall a chosen final email.
9. Final reply queued/failed/delivered/member-partial states remain separate from end-follow-up outcome. Staff never sees a claim that an unselected, unsent or merely queued notice reached/read by the requester.
10. After successful completion, filter counts are accurate, the selected detail remains stable, the history/correction path is available and no other conversation opens implicitly. Background projection refresh does not steal focus.
11. Generic Resolve/status/macros/bulk/API/keyboard paths cannot bypass the same outcome eligibility and privacy restrictions. Tests must use the actual authoritative boundary, not just component mocks.
12. A requester can later reply normally. Their historical record, protected owner obligations and CRM authority have not been changed by ending Support follow-up. No artificial “new ticket required” email or unreviewed audience expansion occurs.
