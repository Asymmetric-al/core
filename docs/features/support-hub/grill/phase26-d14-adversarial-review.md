# Phase 26 D14 — Internal reply targets

11 September 2026. **Founder selected A. Historical disposition: Accept with required amendments. D14 and its complete amendment package are fully founder-ratified, 11 September 2026.** D1–D13 remain fully ratified and unchanged. This is a complete grooming decision and its required implementation proof, not a formal specification, implemented target engine or production-readiness claim.

The strongest simpler alternative is the already-ratified shared queues and deliberate reminders with accurate reports. That avoids policy administration, but relies more on staff noticing each aging response. Optional internal targets add timely guidance without a public response promise. The evidence supports that product shape, not a universal ministry deadline or every feature of an enterprise help desk.

The [UX and reporting blueprint](phase26-d14-reply-targets-ux.md), [evidence and independent corrections](phase26-d14-evidence.md), [four actual-source observations](phase26-d14-source-probe.json), [eight calendar research examples](phase26-d14-calendar-examples.json) and [documentation validation](phase26-d14-validation.json) complete this record.

## Exact corrected decision

> Support Hub provides optional, tenant-configured **First reply** and **Next reply** targets per inbox. An administrator deliberately enters one or both durations and chooses **Service hours** using a qualified calendar, or **Elapsed time**. New and migrated configurations start unconfigured; no universal deadline or seeded resolution target is imposed. Ordinary edits apply prospectively; turning off a policy explicitly withdraws unfinished targets created by it while preserving misses, source work and history.
>
> A target measures a source-specific response owed to a particular reply correspondent. It never creates a CRM identity, chooses an email recipient, merges separate requests or substitutes for a follow-up reminder. Repeated input from the same correspondent keeps the oldest unanswered start; another correspondent does not inherit that person's elapsed wait. One actual human reply can satisfy only its reviewed source coverage and qualified actual recipients. Drafts, queued mail, automatic confirmations and internal notes cannot earn response credit.
>
> Show one neutral **Reply due [time]** or **Reply overdue** indication for the earliest applicable current work, with detail only when useful. No Due soon threshold, green On track claim, resolution deadline, priority matrix, recurring target-alert emails, automatic target reassignment or public response promise is introduced. Reaching a still-owed target creates one Open review under a narrow D3 extension, preserving a valid later follow-up reminder and normal coverage/authorization rules. It does not repeatedly reopen work after staff review.
>
> Support owns reply obligations, policy application, source coverage and derived target outcomes. Qualified P6 evidence owns whether the exact human reply was accepted for delivery, and when that is known; delivery to a mailbox is separate. Calendar and policy versions, original input times and current work-home lineage remain explicit through handoffs, merge/Undo and D12 continuation. Pending provider evidence never becomes a fabricated success or a certain miss.
>
> Reports separately show current all-age waiting work, results for targets due in a selected period, and durations for replies completed in a selected period. They preserve first/next distinctions, unknown evidence, unanswered overdue work, exclusions, corrections and denominator meaning. CRM navigation reuses the same authorized Support projection and actual P6 facts without copied ownership, restricted-context leakage or duplicate communication history.

The following clauses are the complete required amendments. Proposed identifiers describe logical contracts; existing owners and suitable tables/components are reused rather than requiring a new engine or a fixed physical schema.

### D14-R01 — Scope, necessity and terminology

Deliver optional internal reply guidance plus truthful operational reporting. A reply target is a measured expectation for a particular response; a D3 reminder is a chosen review/follow-up time; a D11 task due date belongs to the task owner; resolution describes Support's actual ending. None substitutes for another. No resolution timer, public commitment, automatic update cadence, customer-tier entitlement, donation-value priority, personal target, custom policy hierarchy, AI quality grader or generic rule builder is needed for this decision. Ordinary staff promises remain governed by D3/D5. The current schema's mandatory resolution duration is a compatibility gap to repair, not a product requirement.

### D14-R02 — Complete simple configuration

One current configuration family per inbox has optional First reply and Next reply durations; at least one must be configured before enabling. Defaults are Off/unconfigured and blank durations, including migrated legacy seeds. An administrator chooses positive whole-minute durations using hours/minutes controls; no ambiguous days unit. Conversion is exact, rejects NaN/infinity/zero/negative/fractional minutes/overflow, and fits the existing positive PostgreSQL integer-minute range; do not arbitrarily round a requested duration. A duration is not a claim of staff coverage. Explicit Service hours requires the qualified calendar; Elapsed time counts continuously, including nights/weekends. Missing/loading/inaccessible calendar never silently selects elapsed time. Use one staged Save/Cancel and expected revision, with a calculation preview and no real test email. The full UI is in the blueprint.

### D14-R03 — Versioned activation, edits and Off

Publish immutable policy revisions with trusted effective intervals, and a separate enable-episode boundary. Bind the source-time applicable revision and time basis to each new collecting reply period using the qualified original receipt and inbox-scope provenance; use its already captured bundle when present. An ordinary duration/calendar revision must not make delayed eligible input untargeted merely because the latest revision is newer. Truly pre-enable or Off-period input remains untargeted. If the historical source/policy binding cannot be proved, retain visible applicability-unknown review rather than guessing. Persist each original input's applicable policy bundle for a later proven successor split. Existing periods keep their rules across ordinary duration/calendar edits. Untargeted or withdrawn current periods do not become targeted through re-enable, another message in the same unresolved burst, a move or a retry; a genuinely new period can qualify.

**Turn off [Inbox] reply targets** is an explicit stop of new target applicability and withdrawal of unfinished targets created by that configuration family, across its revisions and current work homes. It does not close the underlying response period, cancel reminders, resolve work, restore a prior wait or clear a known miss. Review that scope before admission; show only authorized impact rows/counts and never a false zero when cross-inbox detail is restricted. A destination inbox with its own configuration Off may still contain pending targets created under another inbox's policy; explain that accurately. Already known pre-stop misses remain misses; before-due withdrawals are visible exclusions. Same-family Off, input, due evaluation and fulfillment use a current control fence so races have one auditable result. Re-enable never resurrects withdrawn target applicability. Record the exact target-applicability cutoff and cause for Off/withdrawal/No reply needed/valid ending. Late acceptance proved before that cutoff can establish the historical response result; acceptance after it remains real mail but does not revive the target. An interval crossing the cutoff is applicability/timing unknown. Resolve equality with a qualified common logical-order fence where available; otherwise retain uncertainty. Callback arrival order cannot decide this result.

### D14-R04 — Qualified calendar ownership and revision

Qualify the existing Support service-hours capability at the shared API owner, with reusable immutable schedule revisions and typed consumers; do not invent an app-local timezone calculator or whole-product calendar platform. A service calendar identifies an IANA zone, local weekly intervals, dated closures/overrides and its qualified timezone-data/calculation version. Split overnight intervals into their actual local dates; holidays/overrides apply to each affected local date. Normalize overlaps and reject malformed, contradictory or wholly unavailable schedules. Calendar names and previews obey current permissions.

D14 binds the calendar revision and materialized due instant for an existing period. Ordinary edits and timezone-data upgrades affect future calculations; they do not silently improve past performance or move pending deadlines. A proven source/calculation defect is corrected through an audited revision and reviewed repair, preserving the prior interpretation. D13's optional hours wording still uses its own current-fact checks; frozen internal target history does not authorize stale public hours content. A calendar's existence or elapsed-time mode never proves someone is staffed.

### D14-R05 — Precise clock arithmetic and display

For Service hours, count actual elapsed duration over the union of real instants whose local calendar representation belongs to the selected half-open working intervals. Nonexistent DST clock time contributes nothing; both real occurrences of an included repeated local interval count; gaps between repeated short intervals do not. Overlapping intervals never double-count. The due instant is the earliest instant at which the configured duration has accumulated from the original start. Elapsed mode adds that duration directly. Store UTC instants plus policy/calendar/zone/calculator provenance; timestamptz alone does not retain the originating zone.

Use bounded interval arithmetic and calendar-indexed computation, not minute-by-minute production loops. Reject unrepresentable combinations or retain visible timing-unavailable recovery when source-time/calendar evidence cannot produce a finite due instant; never invent now, zero, infinity or a distant sentinel. An unresolved target is due at equality; a qualified reply at the exact due instant is on time. Compare full source precision before display rounding. Viewer timezone changes display, not the instant. Show exact dates/zones when useful and distinguish service duration from elapsed duration. Zero service minutes outside hours is a real possible result, distinct from no data. The eight research examples specify DST/overlap/holiday behavior without pretending that oracle is production code.

### D14-R06 — Qualified input, original age and admission

Only first-time admitted relevant human Support email creates or contributes to a reply period. Detected automated, malicious, quarantined/unqualified, unrelated and replayed input does not. Preserve its immutable original receipt identity/time, trusted admission order and actual original conversation; email Date, updated_at, current CRM data and new tracking rows cannot reset age. On qualified hold release, requester wait starts at the original trusted receipt, with intake delay separately visible; applicability still requires the original policy-generation cutoff. Unknown time produces unscored visible review rather than an apparently fresh deadline. This does not alter D13's much shorter automatic-confirmation eligibility.

Source intake/work creation, reply contribution and durable target-evaluation outbox commit atomically at the owner boundary. A calendar or target failure cannot prevent D1 recoverable intake. An explicit staff-origin conversation or first outgoing email alone does not create an incoming-response target. D12 citations/referrals are not new received messages.

### D14-R07 — Reply correspondent and First/Next meaning

Maintain a stable source-scoped reply-correspondent identity for each original conversation, based on qualified correspondence/response-route authority, never an inferred CRM person, household or organization. Passive CC membership does not create a target; a participant's own qualified input can. A different sender gets their own start instead of inheriting another person's nearly expired deadline. Shared mailboxes remain mailbox correspondence scopes, not proof of a single person. No global address matching, plus-alias collapsing, Party merge or preferred-email change silently combines scopes.

First/Next follows the logical source admission sequence, not provider-webhook arrival order or proof of receipt/read. A qualified D4 human public send establishes a boundary at its immutable Core send-intent admission position, once P6 proves acceptance for the relevant correspondent. Input admitted after that boundary uses Next; input admitted before it uses First unless an earlier qualified boundary already exists. An input is satisfied only by explicit coverage, regardless of boundary position. Keep original provider receipt time for waiting duration while using trusted Core admission order for this sequence. Queued intent alone establishes no confirmed boundary; unresolved acceptance retains candidate interpretations and definite rejection establishes none. A genuinely accepted human Start email thread in a staff-origin original can establish Next for later incoming work, without satisfying that future period in advance. No reply needed, automated mail and internal notes do not establish human correspondence. A transferred original's target kind and source history are preserved. Ambiguous prior acceptance retains both permitted interpretations under R10 rather than choosing according to webhook arrival order. Reports count First/Next target instances explicitly; several periods ended without a human reply can be First-kind instances without inventing several actual first replies.

### D14-R08 — Collecting periods, bursts and explicit reply coverage

Allow at most one collecting segment per original/correspondent, with explicitly sealed transferred segments and unresolved response-coverage candidates where necessary. Do not impose one unfinished target per current merged root or even per original/correspondent: D12-transferred input and later native input can remain owed in different homes. Contributions in an ordinary same-correspondent unanswered burst retain the oldest outstanding start; a new message cannot extend that due time.

The existing human send review/admission captures an immutable reply-coverage receipt: exact permitted original/correspondent contributions or qualified sequence cutoffs, current home/topology/source revisions and the actual intended recipient mapping. It proposes coverage only for input the actor has reviewed in the current authorized work; a mere related link, unseen message or shared Party is insufficient. One human reply can cover multiple eligible scopes only with that explicit reviewed mapping and the actual accepted P6 recipients. Coverage never expands the email audience. A reply only to B cannot clear A. Recipient-route correction is an explicit source-authorized change preserving original waiting time and evidence, not a CRM preference lookup or target reset.

Do not add a new routine send modal: use D2/D4's existing reviewed source/audience and collision boundary, with target details showing what the approved reply can cover when needed. A stale source/recipient/topology change requires the existing review; the target module cannot override the send decision.

### D14-R09 — What satisfies a reply and what it proves

Fulfillment requires a genuine human-approved public Support response with qualified P6 **accepted for delivery** evidence for the exact actual recipient/member and reviewed source coverage. A useful answer, clarification question or substantive progress update can count; it does not have to resolve the underlying issue. Empty/draft/queued mail, automated confirmations/answers, internal notes, unrelated outgoing mail, a status click or completion of an owner task cannot count. Software proves the authorized human-reply classification and coverage, not semantic helpfulness through an AI scorer or body-length heuristic; quality remains a staff/review responsibility.

Use the P6-owned acceptance occurrence time when qualified. If an API success proves acceptance without its exact time, retain a conservative possible-acceptance interval bounded by the relevant server-call/evidence times. Earlier ambiguous calls may widen it; a definite earlier rejection does not. On time requires the entire possible acceptance interval to be on/before due; late requires the entire interval to be after due. A straddling interval is timing unknown until stronger evidence narrows it. Once all contributions have qualified accepted-response coverage, the reply obligation is fulfilled even if acceptance timing, historical First/Next attribution or report-cohort membership remains uncertain. That uncertainty is reporting/evidence work; it cannot keep the fulfilled period in Replies needing attention or reopen Support solely to score it. Candidate deadlines create live reply attention only for still-unanswered contributions. A separate genuinely actionable delivery problem still follows D3. Never backdate a later successful retry to the first attempted POST. Mailbox Delivered/read is separate and cannot be inferred from this metric.

A later genuine bounce does not erase a real timely submission; it creates prominent D3 delivery recovery and remains visible beside the metric. Evidence correcting supposed acceptance to pre-send suppression/rejection/wrong-member mapping does correct the derived fulfillment. No P6 event is fabricated to improve the score.

### D14-R10 — Pending sends, later input and late proof

Ordinary send preparation freezes coverage but does not erase the collecting obligation. Later input remains unconsumed. When qualified acceptance covers a complete oldest contribution prefix, atomically close that covered period and form the successor from the oldest uncovered input, preserving that input's receipt time and pinned policy bundle. Partial coverage cannot erase an uncovered older contribution or reset its deadline. Definite send rejection leaves the original owed period/start in force. Multiple sends reconcile exact coverage; a late callback for already covered input cannot consume newer input or create another completion.

While acceptance or First/Next interpretation is unresolved, preserve candidate successor boundaries and the earliest applicable possible deadline, labelled **Reply timing needs confirmation**, never a scored success or certain breach. Example: First is 24 hours, Next is one hour; input at 09:51 after a 09:50 send with unknown acceptance may need a 10:51 response. Keeping only tomorrow's First deadline would hide it. Retain the candidate even when First is disabled and Next enabled. On qualified acceptance finalize the applicable successor; on definite rejection discard only the invalid candidate split, keeping older work and delivery recovery. Source evidence revisions can correct derived interpretation, not rewrite message history, send again or restore an old D3 state.

Provide an explicit, current-revision **No reply needed** disposition for the exact reviewed outstanding contributions, for cases such as an acknowledgment while internal work continues. Preserve already-confirmed misses for the disposed portion. It cannot settle a known outstanding promised reply or unreviewed actionable recovery merely to clear a target. Use current source/obligation review, not text classification. New input after the reviewed cutoff remains owed.

An eligible D3 Resolve can end remaining no-longer-owed reply periods in the same approved command rather than force a redundant second action. No response, continued elsewhere and created in error retain their distinct D5/D12 meanings. D12 transferred work follows R15 rather than being ended for reporting. Before-due no-reply/withdrawal is an explicit exclusion, never met; after a known miss that miss remains alongside the eventual disposition. A source-owned correction proving mistaken eligibility/time can revise results with audit; ordinary late cleanup cannot improve the denominator. Persistent Undo no reply needed reverses only the still-current mistaken disposition for the exact still-unfulfilled contribution set, through a new authorized correction and contribution/source fences. Preserve original age/policy, reconcile with the current collecting period and create current review with a new generation, without duplicate active periods or restoring old status/reminder snapshots. Already accepted-covered contributions are a no-op; newer input is untouched. Independent Off/withdrawal remains effective, so restoring reply work does not re-enable its stopped target. A valid restored target uses its original due/history interpretation, with any resulting miss/correction explicit. Undo never cancels admitted mail.

### D14-R12 — Due review: narrow D3 extension

After staff explicitly review and choose a valid plan, do not repeatedly force Open for the same overdue period; the overdue fact remains visible until the reply/disposition is settled. Independent new source or due events can create new review normally. No reply needed may settle this exact reply-review obligation without ending unrelated work. Off/late proof/correction does not automatically restore a previous wait.

D7 coverage and D8 handoff restrictions respond to the resulting qualified Open work under their existing rules; D6 workload truth also remains accurate. This is not a new target-specific escalation. If a delayed worker has not yet committed the Open transition, authorized due queries still reveal the actionable target, with the actual current status and pending review truth; they do not wait for a tag to make work discoverable or pretend a transition already happened.
