# Q34 — Advisory live teammate awareness: independent UX research

**13 September 2026. D1–D33 are founder-ratified. Q34 is unanswered.** The next proposed choice concerns an advisory live cue before another staff member has committed a reply, note or material update. It does not reopen mandatory stale-send protection, private drafts, assignment, coverage or notification policy.

## Genuine scope gap

[D1](phase26-d1-adversarial-review.md), [D2](phase26-d2-adversarial-review.md) and [D4](phase26-d4-adversarial-review.md) require current authorization, private stable drafts and a collision check that stops stale sending until relevant changes are reviewed. Those requirements are already accepted. An advisory cue could additionally reduce wasted parallel effort before anyone commits an update, but its absence cannot weaken the actual send gate.

[D6](phase26-d6-adversarial-review.md) and [D7-R01](phase26-d7-adversarial-review.md) distinguish actual assignment/coverage from browser presence. An open tab cannot enroll someone for assignments, start/end coverage or prove that work is handled. [D15](phase26-d15-adversarial-review.md) already defines following, in-product Information and optional email updates; a new notification center or optional-following policy would duplicate accepted work.

Current scoped searches of `apps/admin/features/support-hub` and `packages/api/src/admin/support-hub` found no qualified live teammate-presence/composition implementation. The occurrences of typing in the inspected UI concern ordinary input/shortcut behavior, not a server-authorized presence contract. This supports a remaining choice; it does not prove every unrelated platform capability is absent. The independent scope reviewer and parent agree that this narrow pre-update advisory topic is the legitimate gap.

The question should be: **Before anyone saves a reply or note, what live teammate activity should Support show?** It must not be framed as whether to prevent duplicate or stale sends—every option retains that mandatory protection.

## Three actual alternatives

| Option                                                  | Staff experience                                                                                                                                                                                            | Strongest advantage                                                                                        | Real tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Show when a teammate is composing — recommended** | A quiet, currently authorized cue indicates that another teammate is actively composing in the same qualified conversation context. It discloses no draft text or keystrokes. Passive reading is not shown. | Helps avoid starting a competing reply/note while exposing the smallest relevant live activity signal.     | Does not show someone who is only reading or researching; some parallel effort can still occur. Needs trustworthy scope, stale-state expiry and honest unavailable behavior.                                                                |
| **B — Show when teammates are viewing or composing**    | Distinct transient cues show current readers and current composers, without draft content or historical read tracking.                                                                                      | Gives earlier awareness: a colleague may be investigating before writing, so others can coordinate sooner. | More passive activity is exposed, with more visual noise and ambiguity about whether an open page represents work. Requires explicit reconciliation with D15's read-surveillance exclusion and the same reliable source/privacy boundaries. |
| **C — No live activity cues**                           | Staff use existing assignment, work views, committed updates and the mandatory collision check. No new presence signal appears.                                                                             | Simplest and most private; avoids another ephemeral state stream and the false confidence it can create.   | Two staff may spend time investigating or drafting before a committed update triggers protection. Assignment and explicit communication remain their coordination tools.                                                                    |

These are competing current product scopes, not three new tenant settings. A does not include B's passive-viewer list. B does not include collaborative editing, a historical read roster or activity analytics. C preserves the same stale-send block and draft recovery; it is not a choice to allow unsafe duplicate sending.

**Single best recommendation: A.** It addresses the most direct avoidable cost—someone else has already started composing—without turning passive reading into a routine visible signal. This is a proportional product judgment, not proof that Asym teams have a measured collision rate or that a live cue guarantees coordination. B is legitimate for teams that benefit from earlier investigation awareness; C remains strong when the extra infrastructure and distraction are not justified.

## Credible reported friction, separately labelled

An [Intercom community report dated 30 April 2026](https://community.intercom.com/ideas/fix-teammate-presence-it-s-causing-duplicate-work-14456) describes presence disappearing while the person still considers themselves to be working, leading to parallel effort. It asks for a signal to persist while a ticket remains open. A [separate request from 1 October 2025 with August 2026 replies](https://community.intercom.com/ideas/feature-request-real-time-agent-presence-indicator-12772) reports layout/multiple-tab inconsistency and asks for clearer viewing/composing cues.

These are self-selected reports, not confirmed current defects across all editions or measured incidence. The April report's request for an open-tab signal also conflicts with the official FAQ's actively-viewing description; that is a real expectations problem, not proof that one side is technically wrong. Asym should state its chosen meaning and avoid treating absence as proof that no work is happening. Do not copy requests for live draft previews merely because they appear alongside presence feedback.

A [Zendesk community request dated 20 December 2023](https://community.zendesk.com/ideas/guided-mode-collision-detection-sans-light-agents-2775) reports that light-agent viewing affected guided work selection. Its present status was not established. It nevertheless illustrates why presence should not silently become assignment, availability or a queue lock. The accepted Asym owner boundaries already reject that coupling.

No nonprofit source or tenant study reviewed here establishes how often donor-care, ministry administration or missionary-support staff investigate the same conversation at once. Examples remain illustrative. A staff team sharing a work queue is a known product context; specific frequency, financial stakes or private-care workflows must not be invented to justify B.

## D15 privacy reconciliation

D15-R01 expressly says no **read-surveillance roster** or management leaderboard is introduced, and D15-R22 protects personal interest metadata from a default roster, follower count or staff-read history. That is not silently superseded by vendor presence behavior.

A composing-only advisory cue avoids creating a passive read roster. It still needs exact current audience/identity/privacy qualification; the option is not prior permission to broadcast staff activity broadly. C adds no live-activity disclosure.

B is presented only as **transient current awareness**, not historical reading, time-on-ticket, last-seen, duration or management reporting. Selecting B would require explicitly qualifying that narrow current-awareness addition and reconciling it with D15, while preserving the prohibition on a surveillance/history product. Q34 research does not assume D15 already authorizes B or quietly erase its exclusion. If the governing final interpretation forbids even that bounded current-view signal, B must be restated as an explicit proposed amendment rather than described as existing allowed behavior.

## What the recommended A journey would mean

1. **Open the same permitted Support conversation.** Staff retain current assignment/work context, draft, source basis and CRM return path. Reading a record alone does not publish a composing cue or change Follow/notification preferences.
2. **Start composing.** A narrowly qualified current composing signal may appear to other staff who are themselves authorized for that exact context and any disclosed staff identity. It contains activity state, not the text, selected recipients, attachment names, note contents or keystroke stream. Exact signal timing and whether reply/note modes share one neutral wording are design qualification after selection, not preselected in this question.
3. **See the cue without interruption.** A restrained text-led indicator near the composer/reading context explains that a teammate is composing. It does not move Send, steal focus, pop a modal, reserve the conversation or force the staff member to wait. Names/avatars follow actual permitted staff-directory identity, not requester/CRM/public-signature guessing.
4. **Coordinate deliberately if useful.** Staff may continue their own permitted work or use existing team communication/assignment controls. The cue neither transfers responsibility nor offers a new takeover, draft handoff, shared editor or chat surface.
5. **Commit through the normal gate.** A relevant committed update still triggers the mandatory current-version/source/collision review. Presence cannot authorize bypassing it; a missing cue cannot authorize send. Current source truth, not a realtime subscription or avatar, decides admission.
6. **Leave or lose connection.** Active state must expire/clear and reauthorize on return; abandoned tabs cannot remain a permanent claim. If awareness is unavailable or unknown, the UI must not claim that nobody else is working. Reading/composing recovery and the existing collision check remain usable without a functioning presence channel.

B adds an earlier viewing signal and distinct view/compose meaning to that journey. C omits the advisory signal while preserving all normal work and committed-update protections. None sends requester notifications, displays customer typing, modifies P17 Email Studio preparation/P6 delivery, changes CRM facts or opens a new external channel.

## Material risks for the selected answer's later adversarial review

These concerns make the question meaningful but do not preselect a heartbeat, expiry duration, simultaneous-user limit, broadcast provider or implementation schema.

| Risk                                            | Severity / likelihood                         | Required boundary to carry forward                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Cue confused with send safety                   | High / plausible                              | Every option retains the authoritative collision gate. Presence is advisory and must never become a lock, grant, bypass or proof that no other work exists.                                            |
| Stale or missing signal                         | Medium / plausible on mobile/network changes  | Define current activity/unknown/expiry behavior and test multi-tab, tab-hidden, offline, crash/reconnect and delayed events. No permanent open-tab claim or false nobody-working state.                |
| Draft/privacy exposure                          | High / plausible if a vendor bundle is copied | Share no body, keystrokes, recipients or private draft assets. Prove current source and staff-identity disclosure independently; no automatic draft transfer on assignment.                            |
| Broad or historical surveillance                | High / plausible if B is underspecified       | Preserve D15 exclusions; no history, duration, productivity scores, follower roster or broader CRM/global presence list. Any B addition must be explicitly reconciled.                                 |
| Presence becomes workload authority             | High / plausible hidden coupling              | Preserve D6/D7 receiving/coverage and actual assignment. Viewing/composing cannot change status, routing, workload eligibility, reminders or ownership.                                                |
| Cross-tenant/source cache or broadcast leak     | High / plausible without a qualified contract | Partition current identity/context, authorize both publishers and observers, expire membership and invalidate on moves/merges/permission changes. Same email/CRM link is not membership.               |
| Noise/accessibility and mistaken interpretation | Medium / plausible                            | Use readable state names and keyboard/touch-accessible identity, not color/hover alone. Do not place a flashing typing animation in every list row or force realtime announcements of every heartbeat. |
| Scope inflation                                 | Medium / plausible                            | Add no shared drafting, typing transcript, requester chat, notification matrix, lock/takeover system or general presence platform solely for this cue.                                                 |

The source comparisons make B credible, and the privacy/uncertainty costs make A's narrower scope preferable. C is the honest no-add alternative, retaining all already-ratified safety and work coordination. These tradeoffs should appear visibly in the actual question instead of providing only the recommendation.

## Final research disposition

**Proceed with Q34 and recommend A — composing-only advisory cues.** The gap is genuine and narrower than notifications, draft ownership or collision correctness. All required safeguards above are inherited boundaries or matters to specify after the choice; no D34 answer, numeric policy, new setting or technical provider is selected by this artifact.

Current documentation and scoped source were checked. No live Asym presence implementation, database/realtime authorization, supported capacity, detector accuracy, UX task improvement or accessibility result was demonstrated. No code, ticket, formal specification, external state or real message was changed.
