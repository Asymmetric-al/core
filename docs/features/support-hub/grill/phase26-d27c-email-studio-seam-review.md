## Internal consultation has no implicit messaging effect

The Internal tab serves only currently permitted, published common Support instructions. It exposes no Insert into reply, Insert into note, Copy article, automatic citation, attach-guide or Convert to Saved reply/Public action. A worker can read instructions beside the existing conversation, open the full authenticated guide where useful, navigate to a real business-owner surface and return. Reading does not add a note, generate a requester reply, create a communication/CRM event, mark a checklist complete or fulfill a reply target.

D18 Saved notes remain available for repeatable note wording. Staff may write an actual note about observations or work performed through its existing authorized command. D27-C adds no native guide-body/reference insertion or second message-retention graph. An authenticated guide URL is navigation, not a bearer grant: its destination rechecks the current actor, tenant and source permission, without revealing a hidden title/body to an unauthorized reader.

The same experience works through D26's canonical Support detail in CRM, with current joint permissions and preserved context. Guidance can also be consulted from Support without inventing a conversation. Neither route grants access to restricted financial, care, missionary or CRM records. Instructions explain a workflow; its owning domain still validates the actual record, actor, approvals and action. A support resolution or prose instruction cannot certify that a refund, receipt or contact change happened.

Manual work remains independent of email readiness. Consulting guidance cannot require a requester address, sender or receiving connection, and it sends no requester email. A later deliberate Start email thread still follows D26's explicit manual-origin recipient path and D25/P17 qualifications. Existing independently qualified staff-attention communication retains its own D12/P6 producer and recipient rules; D27-C adds no new notification or review campaign.

## Trusted purpose and clipboard limits

D27C-R08/P07 explicitly preserve D23's original-input requirement. The complete original canonical JSON and trusted source-purpose envelope are validated **before** generic Tiptap/ProseMirror normalization and again at the applicable source command. Purpose comes from the stored source and qualified command, not a caller-supplied `internal` attribute. Unknown or incompatible structure is rejected visibly without replacing valid retained content with a stripped autosave. The restricted text/link profile does not silently inherit generic image, embed, variable or executable-checklist support.

Known internal-purpose content cannot be made externally publishable by a controlled generic import, metadata spoof or purpose switch. Existing D18/D23 restrictions continue to govern actual known source copies and message authoring. That does not require an internal-guide email material family or permanent correlation of every sentence with every guide.

The guarantee remains honest: normal selection, operating-system clipboard transfer, screenshots and manually retyped information cannot be comprehensively controlled by removing an Insert button or using Tiptap. The product promises current source access, clear distinct actions, no sanctioned internal insertion/conversion route, and enforcement where internal purpose/source is known. It does not claim global URL policing, perfect classification or complete prevention of human disclosure. Ordinary editor safeguards and actual owning-domain authorization remain necessary.

## Public links keep the accepted preparation and safety path

Public still offers safe source preview followed by deliberate Insert link or Link selected text. The copied visible wording/href and minimal trusted source provenance remain in the actual draft. D18 reuse, D23 managed signatures and the original D27 source checks still apply. Nothing is sent by previewing, inserting, publishing a Page or publishing a Staff guide.

For an actual email, P17 qualifies the source before preparing the complete approved reply. Support admits the exact ready preparation; P6 uses current qualified local safety fences at its atomic provider crossing. There is no CMS/source/URL fetch, redirect traversal, source substitution or rendering after seal. Actual Resend delivery remains P6's responsibility; the no-fetch rule does not prohibit the intended provider I/O.

The accepted lifecycle distinctions remain intact:

- Ordinary Page edits update future finder content without rewriting old drafts or prepared/sent bytes. Removing Public selection affects finder offers, not publication.
- An unchanged href may remain qualified through owner-proved same-Page, same-Site, exact-locale route continuity. A different-Page redirect does not inherit curation approval; new Guidance use/preparation requires deliberate source reselection.
- Loss of public eligibility or unsafe destination without qualified continuity stops new public-use qualification and fences the exact definitely-unsubmitted dependent effects. Dangerous withdrawal also preserves applicable known-material restrictions.
- A current adverse fence before submission prevents crossing; possible submission first requires exact reconciliation without claiming recall or issuing replacement mail.
- Safe copied links can requalify after current public/safety restoration even while their finder selection remains Removed. Fenced/terminal prepared effects never automatically revive.

An Internal lookup failure, Staff guide archive or Staff guide deletion creates no dependency on these email effects. A public-source qualification failure remains limited to the affected source/preparation; it does not become a whole-Support CMS-uptime gate. Unknown proof remains distinguishable from known unpublication.

## Staff publication is distinct from message publication and records custody

Current active-tenant **Support-read authority** defines the initial common Staff guide audience. This is not every tenant staff/admin account, CRM reader, matching address, inbox participant or role name. Read, candidate/history authoring, Publish to staff, source-protection release, deletion and maintainer assignment remain explicit current capabilities. D19 held-incoming-email review and D18 saved-wording maintenance grant none by implication, and inbox-scoped curation cannot silently become tenant-wide publication.

An authorized person may hold both author and publisher capabilities; no extra two-person approval floor was invented. Actual applicable governance remains enforced. Source-protection authority remains independent: an ordinary publisher, new candidate, Restore or new revision cannot clear an active safety restriction. The same person can hold both permissions when the real policy permits it.

Save draft affects the authorized candidate only. Publish to staff atomically releases one validated immutable revision to the common audience, with current head, attribution, receipt and required continuation. Existing published content stays available during ordinary candidate editing until replacement publication or a current stop. Restore creates a reviewable candidate and preserves existing candidate/unsaved work unless replacement is deliberately accepted. Archive stops ordinary serving; Delete archived content rechecks current authority, records retention and holds before actual disposal. User acknowledgement is not hold-release permission.

D27C-R23 and the data custody matrix require an explicit owner policy and tested final disposition before activation for current publications, candidates, superseded/archived/deleted content, private indexes/previews and minimal body-free evidence. Ongoing generic instructions can retain their continuing source purpose; abandoned candidates and old revisions do not gain accidental indefinite storage. No universal legal duration, new hold builder or conversation-expiry rule is imposed on guide sources. A source hold can preserve required bytes in restricted custody while ordinary serving remains denied.

Actual Support content still follows D16/D17. Consultation, source publication, CRM linking and restored guidance do not renew conversation clocks. P17 sealed provider-request material remains a separate bounded execution class: qualified acceptance with member mapping removes adapter/decrypt authority and triggers its purge; later evidence is body-free. Recent sent copy still excludes every URL/action target and internal provenance. Neither copy becomes an archive of guide bodies or a way to reconstruct disposed content. Restore must reapply current restrictions/deletion before reads resume.
