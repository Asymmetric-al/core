## Current primary research

The [full vendor research](phase26-d40-ux-research.md) rechecks Front, Help Scout, HubSpot, Zoho and Zendesk workflows with dates/editions where established. Strongest evidence: distinct current versus future actions, documented unintended requester/contact consequences, current versus backlog recovery, and managed review. No general help-desk feature parity, volume, ministry workflow or measured effectiveness is inferred. Kustomer/Freshdesk/Intercom and nonprofit marketing did not establish a stronger direct owner/matching contract for this selected narrow question; absence from the final comparison is not a claim of feature absence.

Source-specific adoption is disciplined:

- Front's current-conversation action versus sender control supports separate verbs; its connected-mailbox synchronization is not Asym's receiving architecture. [Front](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2251).
- Help Scout's learned future filtering and warning about legitimate automated mail demonstrate the cost of implicit coupling; its purge window/report exclusion is not a Core policy. [Help Scout](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/307-manage-spam-and-unwanted-email).
- HubSpot's separate policy settings and later legitimate-thread return are useful journey evidence; its CRM deletion and automatic new-ticket consequences are not adopted. [HubSpot policy](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/inbox/manage-your-allow-and-deny-list).
- Zoho's contact-level effects extend across tickets, duplicate contacts and access. Reject those cascades; a matched address is not a CRM identity. [Zoho](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/desk/contacts-and-accounts/articles/managing-spam-contacts-in-zoho-desk).
- Zendesk documents accidental requester suspension from spam marking, while its separate action page contains contradictory recovery wording. Use the documented footgun, not an invented recovery guarantee. [Zendesk troubleshooting](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408832769306-What-does-Detected-as-spam-mean).

Official cautions are credible failure-pattern evidence, not an incidence study. Illustrative staff/address examples remain synthetic; no representative nonprofit user feedback was fabricated. Selected B's value is the founder's stated scope, weighed against A's smaller path, not a claim that every tenant has high repeat-spam volume.

An omitted UPDATE WITH CHECK can inherit USING; SECURITY DEFINER uses actual owner rights, not a universal bypass. The evidence corrects overbroad skill shorthand without editing the skills. [Supabase API](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/api/securing-your-api), [PostgreSQL policy](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html), [function](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createfunction.html).
