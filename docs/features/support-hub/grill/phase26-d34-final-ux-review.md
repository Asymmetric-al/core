## Timing reconciliation

The parent's combined final proposed profile supersedes the simpler exploratory proposal in the research report. It consists of leading dispatch when control state is ready; one five-second fast or ten-second quiet combined control/snapshot loop; a fifteen-second control window and composing-liveness bound; a thirty-second observer lease and local/input frontier; and twelve-second maximum snapshot trust from request start. Those values describe different clocks, not contradictory variants of one timeout.

The data contract explicitly acknowledges that a delayed but still-valid fresh-input report is bounded self-report uncertainty. It does not claim to know the physical time of the person's last keystroke, and a renewal/read cannot continually extend the last-input frontier. The observer maps absolute expiry conservatively and clears outdated claims rather than assigning fresh trust on response arrival. These details support an honest recent-composition cue without changing its advisory status.

The healthy connected p95 one-second target is a required measured outcome, not a guarantee established by a five-second polling cadence or broker HTTP202. Transition hints improve the normal path; bounded snapshots repair lost hints; source and send work remain independent when awareness degrades. The actual test fixture must establish whether the target and supported envelope are achieved.

## Complete journey synthesis

Staff can enter through Support or CRM, notice a quiet cue, begin an independent private draft, pause or use draft controls, inspect a file, return, submit through the normal source/collision gate and recover from awareness failure. The cue cannot force waiting, expose an internal note's draft or imply that a refund/receipt/contact action completed. Nothing in the new state changes the ordinary draft, recipient, signature, work-intent or provider outcome contract.

The interface deliberately avoids two common failure patterns from the research: a live cue bundled with automatic draft sharing, and an absent indicator presented as a reliable statement that nobody is working. Current composer names, unknown availability and private drafts remain distinct. Passive reading is still outside selected A.

The declared behavior on window inactivity or preview suspension is narrower than indefinite open-tab awareness. That is a coherent product choice, not a defect to fix by adding a viewing state. Likewise, the inactivity grace smooths normal pauses without claiming an ongoing reservation. The explanation **Shows recent drafting activity. Drafts stay private.** is consistent with the actual technical bounds and does not add a mandatory warning flow.
