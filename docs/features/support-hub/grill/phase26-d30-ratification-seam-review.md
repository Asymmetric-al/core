## Accepted scope and final corrections

**The fixed 3+4 composition is intact.** Work now is All unfinished, Unassigned and Replies needing attention, measured as distinct currently authorized continuing conversations. Open and both Waiting states, deferred Open and qualified handling/review distinctions are preserved. Unassigned is a subset; reply-period groups overlap and cannot be added as extra workload. Fulfilled-but-unscored timing evidence is not an outstanding reply. Results consists of Reply targets, Reply times, Request labels and Feedback, each retaining its own source population and unit.

**Scope controls remain deliberately separate.** Work now alone has Currently handled in, default All accessible inboxes or one exact currently qualified inbox. Results has period/zone and the persistent explanation that Work now filters do not apply. There is no global inbox selector, invented common historical dimension or silent fallback from invalid selected scope to All. Full-report navigation carries period/zone; a metric click carries its exact native cohort/kind/evaluation descriptor. Current rights are checked again at use; an updated evaluation is labelled rather than pretending a timestamp is a replayable historical snapshot.

**Retired does not mean automatically unavailable or newly authorized.** Final R05–R06, data scope and UX explicitly preserve still-owner-qualified retained work/history and selected references, with a Retired qualifier where that metadata can be disclosed. Today's active-inbox catalog cannot erase a historical report population. Missing, forbidden or otherwise no-longer-qualified scope is unavailable and cannot widen. This accepted correction supersedes the earlier final review's unqualified shorthand about retired selection being unavailable; no archive-access capability is added.

**Existing D3 obligations stay discoverable.** Final R04/P04 and UX now include Follow-ups and Delivery recovery alongside Intake review, Handoff review and Feedback review under **Other work and review queues — all dates**, outside both overview control scopes. These are existing source-owned destinations, not a combined problems count or new queue. A fulfilled reply or zero reply-attention cannot hide an actionable later bounce. A processed or cleared reminder field cannot hide the actual pending D3 review obligation. No report value certifies those independent duties complete, and no hidden source count or P17 attention state is copied.

**Exact report facts remain accepted.** D14 target results preserve finalized target-instance grain, First/Next, assessed/finalized coverage, confirmed unanswered misses, exclusions, unknowns and separate potential targets. Reply times preserves completed-wait grain and exact median/nearest-rank p90, with the fixed preview explicitly **Elapsed time**; qualified service-hours detail remains in the full report. D21 uses the whole current component's earliest qualified original start before period filtering, current labels, complete N/L/U, overlapping counts, archived applied labels, stable top-five ordering and separate Start date unconfirmed evidence. D28 uses the source-qualified first submission-may-have-begun issued cohort and only its related responses as of evaluation, with current source/custody exclusions, separate rating denominator and open-window meaning. Provider-indeterminate issued effects are not dropped. Off retains authorized history and independent all-age review; no-history, unknown, error and Off are different facts.

**Source and CRM history remain truthful.** D26 staff-recorded work and D29 form-origin requests retain genuine source identities, no-Party possibilities and their qualified clocks.
