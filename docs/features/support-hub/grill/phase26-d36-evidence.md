## Vendor and user-experience evidence

The [complete UX research](phase26-d36-ux-research.md) records current official Zendesk, Zoho Desk, HubSpot Service Hub, Kustomer, Freshdesk/Omni, Intercom, Front, Apple and Teamwork evidence with applicability and tradeoffs. Consequential uses:

- **Adopt direct, contextual insertion.** Teamwork documents a per-message Quote Message command; Zoho's public demonstration visually places the quote inside the existing answer editor. Old Teamwork screenshots support the pattern, not a contemporary visual skin. Asym adopts placement and familiar editing, not their automatic history or adjacent forwarding actions.
- **Adopt deliberate excerpts as an accelerator.** Apple documents selected-versus-all quotation. Asym retains a labelled non-selection path because a precision gesture alone would exclude or frustrate some users.
- **Adopt inspect/edit/remove.** Front and Intercom document content control. Asym adds the source-bound attribution and lifecycle rules required by its actual architecture; vendor controls do not prove those internals.
- **Reject visual fold as inclusion authority.** Zendesk can still send collapsed quoted history; Freshdesk's SDK illustrates UI lifecycle sensitivity. Asym's canonical document decides inclusion, independently of reader state.
- **Reject implied universal default.** Kustomer documents current-only versus history behavior with channel-specific differences. The selected A is a founder/product judgment, not industry unanimity.
- **Preserve CRM consistency.** HubSpot's documented related context and reported differences between composition surfaces highlight continuity. Asym uses its own authoritative source and composer rather than synchronizing two products.
- **Reject historical-file acquisition.** Front documents repeated history/file accumulation. Asym quotes text and leaves actual file admission separate; it does not copy vendor count limits.

The research includes actual browser inspection of Zoho's official animated quote example and Teamwork's official menu screenshot, and browser verification of Kustomer's April2026 article. No private helpdesk account was opened and no remote media was republished. These inspections verify depicted layout only, not runtime usability/security of either vendor or Asym.

Four qualified self-selected community reports in the UX research concern hidden/unedited historical content and cross-surface context. Some are historical and one is Fin/AI-specific. Treat them as failure hypotheses, not prevalence, a current universal limitation or donor-care field research. No nonprofit-specific evidence establishes a different quoting default or a need to reproduce commercial helpdesk scope. Actual intended staff usability tasks remain P28.
