## Exact proposed amendments

### H-O01 — Contact remains a complete independent path

**Material concern: yes; high severity, high likelihood if guidance is treated as a prerequisite.** Stale, missing or inaccessible content could block someone from reaching the tenant or imply that reading an article closed the issue.

> Contact support SHALL be directly reachable in the first Help view without searching, reading, rating, signing in or declaring whether a guide helped. Public guide choices remain optional. No qualified guide, source outage or missing language removes the independently qualified contact path. Reading/navigation creates no Support record, reply target, D28 invitation, preference or resolved outcome. The selected scope adds no public library/search product, chat, AI assistant or requester conversation archive.

**Proof:** With zero guides, source outage, stale source and every narrow/mobile/keyboard state, contact remains usable; no manufactured article or hidden mandatory step appears.

### H-O02 — Preserve the P23 purpose, route and single outcome

**Material concern: yes; critical severity, medium likelihood.** A Help-specific `sendEmail` endpoint could bypass the form's sensitivity/consent rules, create duplicate owners or acknowledge work that was never durably accepted.

> The Contact form SHALL consume a qualified versioned Contact Purpose Profile and released Route Plan through the existing public-form/owner command. It admits one exact Primary Outcome: a certified Support handoff or one action-qualified Verified Email Destination. Staff notifications and zero-or-one Visitor Acknowledgement remain independent optional child effects. Browser input, placement, subject, selected guide, query string and mail header never choose recipients, outcome adapter, template, sender, redirect or sensitivity.

For the Support outcome, the certified adapter establishes a genuine requester form occurrence and recoverable Support work using a stable original identity. It does not create both an email-only handoff and a Support conversation as competing truth. An email-only Primary Outcome does not fabricate a Support ticket; the work owner is the declared governed destination. No automatic route switch occurs after timeout or downstream failure.

**Proof:** Same form replay creates one occurrence/outcome; template failure cannot erase the Primary work; changed tenant/route/generation/payload conflicts or requalifies under the owner rather than rewriting a prior acceptance.

### H-O03 — Browser receipt means durable acceptance, not delivery

**Material concern: yes; high severity, high likelihood.** A success page can be shown before durable work exists, or a failed optional acknowledgement can cause a requester to submit again.

> Before returning Received, atomically record the immutable form occurrence, exact released schema/Route Plan, Primary work item, child intents and their durable dispatch requests, or record none. The browser receipt is not a P17 message and must not await email rendering/provider delivery. Its wording SHALL reflect what was actually accepted, without claiming a staff member read it, an inbox received email, a conversation was created before that outcome is proved, or a response deadline exists.

Email-only Primary completion remains distinct: all frozen required destination members must be transport-accepted before that handoff is marked complete, and later adverse evidence raises its qualified attention. The browser can truthfully acknowledge durable receipt while that delivery work is pending. P23 Form health is redacted recovery, not another staff content inbox.

**Proof:** Crash before/after transaction, outbox delay, one failed notification, partial required-member delivery and optional acknowledgement failure all show truthful noncontradictory receipt/outcome states.

### H-O04 — No fabricated email; qualify any visitor acknowledgement

**Material concern: yes; critical severity, high likelihood if D13 is reused by name alone.** A form could invent provider-receipt evidence, send two auto-confirmations or turn user-supplied email text into proven identity.

> Opening Help and browser Received SHALL NOT send email. An enabled Visitor Acknowledgement uses the existing P23 zero-or-one child slot and a separately qualified Contact-form source/recipient/P17 publication. D13's email-only occurrence and original-From rules SHALL NOT be invoked by creating a synthetic incoming message, RFC header or provider receipt. D13 remains unchanged unless its exact source/recipient meaning is explicitly amended through a successor-qualified contract; no silent key reuse is allowed.

The least surprising permanent path is a specific neutral **Contact request received** P17 meaning for the qualified form occurrence, reusing eligible presentation/wording where appropriate. It confirms durable receipt, not a verified identity, successful fix, gift acknowledgement or staff reply. Its only response endpoint comes from the profile's explicit one-address contact field/current qualified context and remains labelled unverified where applicable. Browser answers cannot change From/Reply-To/CC/BCC, template or arbitrary email subject. No raw free-text answers, sensitive context or `all_fields` merge is echoed in the visitor message.

The Route Plan is the one acknowledgement configuration authority; there is no second competing Help auto-reply toggle or duplicate P23+D13 sender. Its source utility starts from trusted form admission, not a fabricated provider receipt. Register its finite utility/material class, consent/contactability, abuse gates, human-reply precedence, actual monitored return route and outcome history before activation. Acknowledgement failure/suppression never discards accepted work. Existing inbox D13 enablement does not by itself authorize form acknowledgements.

**Proof:** Native email intake still creates only the qualified D13 occurrence; a form creates only its declared form child; retries, fallback email delivery, duplicate webhook and a later human reply never generate another acknowledgement. The original form source and true provider evidence remain distinguishable.

### H-O05 — Counter mail abuse and loops at the real source

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** A public form can be abused to send receipts to arbitrary victims or to a Support/automatic-return address; a forged original From cannot supply email-origin trust.

> Qualify the form's own abuse/contactability posture, not an imitation of inbound-email authentication. Use the existing purpose/generation/replay/size limits, source and endpoint rate/courtesy controls, honeypot/timing signals and accessible risk-based challenge path. A syntactically valid contact address or captcha result does not prove the person's identity or authority. Qualified automated receipt content is minimal and cannot relay visitor text. Feedback/marketing enrollment and broad CRM contact creation never follow from submitting Help.

Registered auto-generated delivery semantics and current receiving anti-loop/suppression rules apply to resulting mail. Own sending/return/ingress addresses, automatic delivery reports, loop chains and abuse conditions cannot create new confirmation cycles. Staff notification members are independently resolved/frozen with no auto-CC of the requester or disclosure of recipients to one another. Missing proof suppresses the optional mail while preserving recoverable Primary work when intake was actually accepted.

**Proof:** Submit own-inbox/bounce/automated addresses, replay from many endpoints, forged browser recipients and subsequent bounce/auto-responses; no reflection loop, extra recipient or duplicate source occurs. No required live provider proof is replaced by a subject-string heuristic.

### H-O06 — Retry without changing the destination or multiplying requests

**Material concern: yes; high severity, high likelihood on mobile connections.** A timeout followed by automatic email fallback could create both a Support request and an unrelated mail handoff.

> Retain the exact form operation identity, released schema/route and payload digest through native submit/refresh/retry. After ambiguous success, recover the same privacy-safe receipt before creating a new operation. Keep answers and show checking/unknown honestly; do not claim failure solely from timeout or auto-resubmit with a fresh key. A stale form/schema result cannot silently remap answers or switch Primary Outcome.

Direct email/phone alternatives may remain visible, but using them is the requester's deliberate separate contact, never an automatic fallback for an uncertain submitted form. Explain possible duplicate contact when uncertainty is known without disabling access to human help. If a source was definitely not admitted, the same recovery/approved alternate path is available; do not create two acknowledged primaries or auto-merge by matching email/subject. A source receipt grants no general request-history or message-body access.

**Proof:** Lost success response, repeat POST, tab retry, stale schema, pending provider outcome and deliberate separate email yield exactly the intended source records with clear uncertainty and no hidden resend.

### H-O07 — Public contact details are published presentation, not delivery authority

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Personal admin/billing contact information could be disclosed, or a public mailto address treated as a certified delivery destination.

> Display only tenant-authorized, deliberately published public Help contact channels, with a qualified existing source reference when a managed Support inbox supplies the address. An optional email or phone is a public-contact presentation fact, not an arbitrary administrator/login/billing/personnel record. Its publication owner is accountable for its purpose and currentness. Do not invent hours, 24/7 availability, live-chat presence, response targets or “online now” from application status or an internal reply target.

P23 **Verified Email Destination** is a separate operational delivery object: same tenant, accountable owner, qualified action/notification purpose, current active/verified status, monitoring attestation, sensitivity ceiling and frozen members. A displayed contact address or `mailto:` link satisfies none of those rules by itself. Conversely, a private verified destination cannot automatically be published as contact information. No new global contact directory or independent CRM profile is needed; qualify the minimum existing/public-Help source rather than guessing.

`mailto:` and `tel:` are deliberate native navigation. A click does not prove email sent, call placed, Support work accepted or CRM contact. Do not prefill sensitive context, processor identifiers or hidden form answers into mailto URLs. Show accurate complete contact text with safe native actions and international formatting; no forced personal-phone disclosure.

**Proof:** Private staff details and recipient membership never enter public HTML; changing a display contact does not mutate email routing; mail/call clicks add no false communication event; missing channels have truthful fallback/readiness.

### H-O08 — Public Site routing and app Help context stay distinct

**Material concern: yes; critical severity, medium likelihood.** A portal Help entry could leak private gift/represented-person context into public HTML/URLs or use the public Site as account authority.

> Public Help/form routes derive tenant/environment/Site/exact locale and released generation from the trusted P23/P24 route owner. Authenticated Help belongs to the app and its current portal host/brand/tenant/subject; it SHALL NOT pretend the portal host is a public Site or personalize an invariant CMS public generation. Qualify a narrow authenticated-app consumer of the same Contact purpose/route/admission semantics where needed, not a second form engine or raw direct-to-Support writer.

If the app navigates to a public Site instead, use the exact source-issued Site/locale URL and do not export private context in query strings, fragments, referrers, prefilled mail URLs or cached Page props. An explicit permitted context reference can accompany a deliberate first-party intake only after current server verification of actor/subject/record/purpose. The user can review the allowed context; screenshots, processor IDs, whole giving history and arbitrary browser fields are not required substitutes. Entry context does not grant support workers access to the referenced CRM/financial source.

Missing source/language cannot silently fall back to another tenant/Site/default locale. Portal authentication/credential routes do not redirect through Help Pages. Return navigation preserves current permitted location and draft without granting source access.

**Proof:** Signed-out/expired session, represented subject, changed active tenant, another Site, exact-locale failure, copied link, malformed host and lost context all preserve the right owner or safe direct-contact path without private egress.

### H-O09 — Real web-request origin and later Support continuation

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Treating a form as an incoming email or staff brief would make source attribution, reply audience, reply targets, feedback eligibility and retention misleading.

> A certified form-to-Support adapter SHALL preserve its real requester web-form origin, immutable form occurrence and declared contact/consent/context facts. It does not manufacture RFC Message-ID, email From authentication, provider receipt or staff-authored narrative. Later human email uses the qualified source-recipient/audience and existing P17/D4/P6 path; a form contact field is a response endpoint, not verified CRM identity or an email-thread header.

D13 automatic email confirmation, D14 email reply targets and D28's email-correspondence eligibility cannot be triggered merely by labelling the form “email.” Any intended web-source extension must be explicit in that owner's contract and evidence. Ordinary requester input remains a real request even where a particular email-derived metric is not applicable. No hidden target/SLA or survey promise is added by Help. A real subsequent email continuation keeps its actual D1/D2/D25 correlation and source evidence; public form replay never pretends to be a continuation.

**Proof:** First form/first staff reply/subsequent real incoming reply, correction, merge/reopen, no-Party requester and source expiry produce honest source/recipient/target/feedback facts without duplicate authoritative CRM state.

### H-O10 — Fit the editor to the task and preserve custody

**Material concern: yes; medium severity, high likelihood of unnecessary complexity.** Forcing a rich editor onto a brief public message can weaken no-JS operation, add mobile friction and imply support for attachments or markup not admitted by the source.

> The short requester Contact form SHALL use semantic native/shared name/email inputs, a plain message textarea, associated labels and authoritative server validation. It retains P23's no-JavaScript submit/recovery path. It does not expose Tiptap, uploads, arbitrary rich HTML, embedded forms or paid editing services simply because staff replies use Tiptap. Existing D23 Tiptap profiles remain appropriate for staff replies/notes/reusable wording/signatures; public guide rendering remains the qualified static public reader.

Known plain input is safely rendered as plain content, not interpreted as executable HTML or a template. Keep the original form source and admitted owner content under their declared finite purpose, restrictions and correction policy; the form recovery envelope does not become a permanent second transcript. No public draft autosave/history or raw field data in logs, analytics, URLs, notification bodies and workflow events. Declared source context is minimized before each egress. Post-acceptance edits, retries, guide clicks, provider callbacks and CRM linking cannot renew content or recreate disposed originals.

**Proof:** No-JS/mobile/keyboard/AT submission and recovery, pasted HTML, long Unicode names/messages, field errors and expired/restricted retained content work without lost answers, script execution, covert copies or a private-data public cache.

## Final owner synthesis

There are three separate receipts/contacts to make understandable: **browser Received** confirms the committed form occurrence; **Primary Outcome** is the certified Support work or verified-email handoff; **Visitor Acknowledgement** is an optional separately governed email. The permanent implementation reuses P23/P17/P6 for those facts. It does not turn a Form Submission Occurrence into provider evidence, make a mailto click into accepted work, or regard a public contact address as a Verified Email Destination.

First qualify the public/app Help context and Contact Purpose/Route adapter, then the single-outcome atomic source and recovery receipt. Preserve the existing public-guidance eligibility and immediate contact even when guidance is absent. If visitor acknowledgement is enabled, qualify its web source/recipient/P17 meaning, finite utility, abuse/contactability and actual return-thread path before any email can send. Keep browser success independent of that optional delivery. Source-aware staff/CRM continuation, finite custody and real no-JS/authorization/concurrency/provider proof complete the seam.

The review does not require another global workflow/form builder, a second CMS or CRM, public Help library, new provider, media service, AI, requester archive or unconditional visitor auto-email. Exact root synthesis governs whether its optional acknowledgement is exposed/enabled and records any intended adjacent web-source qualification explicitly. The sound existing owner contracts are reused; the real missing source/recipient qualifications are named instead of declared complete by interface similarity.

All validation described here is read-only repository and primary-document research. No form, domain, email destination, P17 publication, acknowledgement, database/RLS, provider, browser/assistive-technology or staff/requester journey was executed. The proof requirements above must be demonstrated by the eventual implementation; current planning/source evidence is not release certification.

## Root synthesis choices and required source qualification

The root has selected the following concrete amendments for the D29 proposal. They resolve the exploratory acknowledgement/profile choices above without expanding the selected A scope; they are not yet a claim of founder ratification or implementation.

1. **Direct contact first, small replyable form.** The requester form is text-only with required email and message, optional name, and the semantic native/no-JavaScript route. No upload or Tiptap is added to this short public task. Public email/phone navigation remains an independently deliberate contact action and creates no automatic work record.
2. **Browser Received is mandatory and mail-independent.** It follows the atomic P23 occurrence/Primary work acceptance. A visitor email is optional and **Off by default for new forms**; this does not change D13's ratified New requests recommendation during incoming-email inbox setup.
3. **One optional P23 child, a new web Contact profile.** The Route Plan's zero-or-one Visitor Acknowledgement slot owns the setting. A qualified new web Contact P17 source/recipient profile governs its email. D13 wording may be reused as canonical authoring input only through publication explicitly valid for the new profile; an email-only key/publication cannot be relabelled into new authority. No P23+D13 double acknowledgement, browser-controlled sender or synthetic incoming-email event exists.
4. **Fifteen-minute original utility, one cross-source courtesy guard.** The form acknowledgement's authority ends 15 elapsed minutes after trusted form admission, shortened by earlier current source/privacy/contactability limits. Bind that absolute deadline into its qualified prepared-material posture; delayed preparation/retry cannot restart it. The existing D13 tenant/mailbox 24-hour courtesy control is shared across these automatic receipt source families, not partitioned by template/key to admit two messages. Original form/email occurrence uniqueness remains separate. Human reply wins the current gate; applicable Off/form-route/source revisions fence definitely-unsubmitted work through the existing P6 path.
5. **Real web origin and first-reply authority.** The certified Support adapter must explicitly qualify a form-response recipient basis for D4/P17: the form's declared response endpoint under current source/contactability rules, reviewed in the ordinary human composer, with no automatic CC or identity/CRM claim. If a qualified automatic acknowledgement already established an actual outbound mail thread, a human reply can continue it. Otherwise the first outgoing message establishes native lineage. Neither route fabricates an incoming From/RFC Message-ID or treats a provider resource ID as an RFC message identity. Subsequent real email follows D1/D2/D25.
6. **Explicit adjacent metric eligibility.** D29 qualifies a web-source D14 initial reply clock starting at trusted qualified form admission under the existing target/calendar/pause rules. It does not claim provider receipt or email intake. D28 may include this genuine requester form work only after actual human public email to its qualified respondent is accepted, with all other eligibility, quiet-window, sampling, contactability and privacy rules preserved. Browser Received, an automatic acknowledgement or a guide click grants no human-response credit or feedback invitation by itself.

No conflict with the reviewed owner architecture was found in these choices. They need explicit propagation into the P23 adapter/profile, D4/P17 first-reply recipient contract, D13 shared courtesy-control application, D14 source-clock definition and D28 qualifying source population during the later authorized specification stage. These are narrow source/consumer qualifications, not a second form engine, new survey effect or alternative transport.

Required combined proof: anonymous/no-Party and authenticated-context form → one durable Support outcome → optional Off acknowledgement; enabled web acknowledgement inside its original fifteen-minute window → shared cross-source 24-hour contention with incoming-email confirmation; human reply before/after acknowledgement crossing → one honest native lineage; duplicate POST/ambiguous receipt → no new original; first web-source target → actual accepted human reply → only then potential D28 eligibility. Source expiry, route/policy change, suppressed recipient and published-template mismatch must fail the affected optional effect without losing accepted work.
