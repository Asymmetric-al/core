## Independent challenge incorporated

1. Narrow the question to optional ongoing following; P17 already owns center, channels and presentation.
2. Preserve D11/D12 required attention in every option; do not claim all generic assignment/mention contracts are already live or settled.
3. Prefer A without pretending vendors agree; Help Scout/Front establish C and Intercom establishes a credible B posture.
4. Preserve explicit Stop intent independently of fresh direct attention. Direct mentions do not need to become hidden renewed subscriptions under A.
5. Reject follower-based access grants, requester recipient changes, CRM relation propagation and a duplicate personal status machine.
6. Preserve exact original-source follow scope through merges, later merged constituents, Undo and related continuations; no automatic follower union.
7. Distinguish conversation following from D11's delegated-work interest and its Stop following this work action.
8. Require a finite useful source-event set and P17 purpose-preserving grouping; never equate every webhook/timer/CRM update with follower activity.
9. Keep self-generated events, overlapping reasons, concurrent stop/revocation, source ending/reopening and mixed-version migration in the selected answer's full review. No exact schema, stable notice key, retry delay or channel default is selected prematurely.
