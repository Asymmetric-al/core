## Exact corrected decision

> Support Hub provides deliberate, personal **Follow conversation** and **Stop following conversation**, plus a native **Following** filter in its existing conversation views. Staff choose ongoing interest without taking responsibility. Assignment, replying, notes, mentions, viewing, CRM linking and related work never silently enroll someone; administrators cannot subscribe colleagues on their behalf.
>
> Following binds the exact reviewed original conversation sources. It persists through ordinary resolution and assignment changes while the same staff authority remains valid. Merging does not expand an existing follow to new originals; Undo partitions current interests; a related continuation does not inherit them. The interface makes partial following understandable and offers deliberate inclusion of currently visible threads.
>
> Qualified human input, accepted human replies, published internal notes and deliberate human endings/reopenings produce optional following information. Routine assignment/Waiting/label changes, timer ticks, internal reply-target warnings, delivery callbacks and unrelated CRM activity remain quiet. Actual source duties and direct attention are independently governed. Stop affects optional following, not Support work, reminders, notification history, external recipients or completed business actions.
>
> Support owns source activity and following intent. Auth/identity owns staff identity and access; P17 owns shared notification presentation, preferences and approved content; P6 owns complete communication occurrences, dispatch and delivery evidence. Updates use the existing Information policy. Email is optional and starts Off for this meaning, using a qualified Email Studio template and Resend through P17/P6. No provider or CRM synchronization is introduced.
>
> Every action, occurrence, recipient and read retains tenant, source and personal-generation boundaries. The shared activity fence and each recipient's following applicability must be enforced independently, so stopping one follow does not cancel other recipients. The full bounded recipient set is released all-before-any through P6; retries cannot select newcomers or re-followed generations. Intake and actual work remain recoverable independently of notifications.

The exact required clauses below resolve the selection. Logical identifiers describe contracts; physical table names and function names are not prematurely mandated.

### D15-R01 — Necessity, alternatives and domain scope

Provide optional ongoing staff interest in a particular Support conversation. The strongest smaller alternative is directed attention plus existing views/search; it requires more remembering or repeated mentions when a colleague wants the eventual outcome. Automatic following after participation saves one action but introduces implicit enrollment and extra stop rules. A is the chosen posture. No measured ministry demand, notification-volume improvement or satisfaction gain is claimed from vendor availability alone.

Keep following, direct mention, Support handling, delegated-work interest, email audience, CRM relevance and notification engagement distinct. D11's Stop following this work ends a conversation's interest in a delegated action; Stop following conversation ends one staff member's optional conversation updates. Neither substitutes for the other. No generic subscription platform, public follower feature, personal conversation status machine, customer-wide following, read-surveillance roster, AI relevance grader or management leaderboard is introduced.

### D15-R02 — Personal identity and voluntary enrollment

Follow and personal channel changes derive the authenticated staff principal, active tenant, exact staff recipient/role authority and actor from trusted server context. Qualify the existing identity-owner mapping; matching an email, selecting the first Support agent or accepting caller-controlled agentId is not acting authority. A requester, message participant, CRM Party, authenticated user, Support worker and record owner remain distinct.

Only the person enrolls their own following. No automatic Follow on reply, note, mention, assignment, creation, viewing, import, CRM link or team membership. No colleague/admin Add follower control or cross-tenant preference is offered. Qualified privacy/access administration may end unsafe authority without impersonating a user's voluntary Stop. Tenant/role navigation changes visible context and cache scope; it does not itself terminate valid follows in another tenant. A genuine loss of the bound staff/source authority follows R05.

### D15-R03 — Exact original-source scope

A normal Follow captures the currently reviewed, authorized original conversation source. On a merged view it captures the explicit currently reviewed source set, never every present or future source that may later resolve to that root. The server verifies submitted scope against its authorized projection and relevant topology revision; caller IDs cannot widen it. Existing active follows in that set retain their start/generation; only newly included sources receive new following generations.

Use personal per-original relationships and exact operation scope, not one mutable follower array on a root or a copied subscription graph. Current component projection may display one row for several original follows. Current access filters names, counts and scope choices before presentation. **Following selected threads** describes a partial disclosed scope; **Follow all current threads** deliberately includes the permitted reviewed set. Neither phrase claims knowledge of hidden originals or grants access. Newly merged sources never enroll by implication.

### D15-R04 — Explicit commands and reliable outcomes

Commands express desired Follow/Stop state, a durable operation identity, expected personal generation and the exact relevant topology/authority scope. They are not blind toggles. Exact retry returns the recorded result; changed input or a stale opposite operation reconciles rather than reversing a newer decision. A delayed Stop cannot cancel a later explicit re-follow. A timed-out Follow is pending until its outcome is recovered; UI optimism is not authority.

Atomically sample the source activity admission frontier when starting each generation. Do not reject a safe Follow merely because an ordinary new message arrived before the click committed; that activity may correctly precede enrollment. Refresh is required for changed followed scope/topology or authority, not an unrelated composer collision. Stop affects only the reviewed current self-owned generations; a concurrent newly followed or newly merged source is not silently swept into it. A repeat desired state is a no-op with a truthful receipt, not another notification or history event.

### D15-R05 — Stop, re-follow, access loss and ordinary endings

Follow persists across Open, both Waiting categories, ordinary Resolve, manual reassignment, coverage and inbox moves while exact source/staff authority remains valid. It is not a duty and does not need an active task. A later genuinely new permitted contribution can therefore update a follower of a resolved conversation. Following a currently resolved conversation is allowed and future-only; it does not replay its history.

Stop ends new optional selection and unsent authorization for those generations. It also ends unread treatment for already-available follow-only information through an explicit source-applicability correction, without inventing a read. Authorized recent history remains under its original P17 limits. Already-authorized in-flight/accepted email cannot be recalled. Stop never changes shared status, reminder, reply coverage, assignment, delegated work or a separately qualified direct/required attention reason. Re-follow starts a new generation/frontier and never restores old unread debt, canceled deliveries or stopped generations.

Confirmed loss of the relevant staff/source access, account retirement or source retirement closes only affected generations and removes active/recent presentation under ADR0027. Later regrant requires a new deliberate Follow and cannot transfer old role-scoped notices. An authorization outage or indeterminate identity lookup fails closed temporarily; it is not proof to permanently end intent. Stop remains available through a safe self-owned cleanup command without disclosing an inaccessible source. A source-scope erasure/correction applies the owning retention/privacy policy; it does not fabricate another request or user.
