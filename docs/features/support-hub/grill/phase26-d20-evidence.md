## Independent synthesis and adopted corrections

Three independent reviews assessed data/auth/CRM, lifecycle/query behavior and UX/vendor patterns, then pressure-tested the root's complete draft. All recommended **Accept with required amendments**. Adopted refinements include:

- Platform named-view reuse; explicit Support-only Shared activation and startup/pin adaptation without CRM permission leakage.
- Versioned default baselines so saved deltas cannot change silently when defaults change.
- Stable active ID/revision, metadata-only rename, no update-as-create and changed-payload idempotency conflicts.
- Creator-only initial pin and truthful saved-but-not-pinned recovery; archive/restore generation guards against old pin revival.
- Source-owned D14 due sorting/nulls, D15 original-follow scope and proper distinction from other explicit current-root filters.
- Result query consistency while facet options remain an independently authorized catalog/contextual query.
- Fixed CRM record scope until explicit full-workspace navigation; Support-specific name-namespace compatibility.
- Protected bounded temporary query-state references within the shared capability, not raw sensitive URLs, bearer grants or another platform.
- Direct Shared creation/maintenance vocabulary rather than a misleading publication state.

Raw independent proposals remain evidence, not competing authority; the corrected main/data/UX contracts govern when wording differs. The final bounded data review found no remaining material contradiction after these corrections. No new external side effect or feature expansion was needed.
