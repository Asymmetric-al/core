### Exact boundary retained by F01

The finite guard concerns whether an old effect may still **cross into external submission**. It does not require proof that an accepted email was delivered, read or replied to, or that all historical provider events can be recovered. A delayed delivery event or callback arrival cannot renew the interval. The P6 qualification must establish the crossing bound from the actual fenced submission/attempt authority and in-flight closure; merely waiting for a timeout is insufficient. Same-effect idempotency and historical uncertainty remain governed independently after a new, genuinely distinct opportunity becomes eligible.

### Exact boundary retained by F02

A terminal linked-suppression disposition is not a fabricated failed random draw. Its database branch has no draw and references the opportunity that already supplies the current component's chance. It cannot later change into a sampled branch. A sampled opportunity remains tied to an immutable original conversation, not a mutable merge root or CRM Party. This preserves both history and the current-component contact restraint without retroactively rewriting independent pre-merge samples.

## Other consequential seams checked

- **Candidate versus opportunity:** Completion creates a generation-bound candidate with its percentage frozen then. A reopen before maturity invalidates that timer; later genuine completion may start another timer while no opportunity exists. Sampling occurs only at quiet maturity. After a committed draw, reopening, cancellation, policy change and merge/Undo cannot produce another chance. Due plus 24 hours is a finite send-by boundary, not a backlog promise.
- **Off:** Current Off is an authoritative submission fence, not an eventually processed cancellation list. It stops new candidates and definitely-unsubmitted invitations. Previously issued response links retain their original response window unless a distinct safety/privacy restriction applies. On creates no revival, reroll, catch-up or automatic reminder.
- **Recipient and contactability:** A shared mailbox, participant email or CRM association is not proof of an individual donor or represented organization's authority. The exact authorized endpoint is qualified without a first-contact match, CC fan-out, alias guessing or automatic Party creation. P3 owns the purpose preference; no Support-only shadow consent state is introduced.
- **Email Studio/P6:** D28 needs an explicitly qualified optional invitation purpose, no-Party response/preference authority and a closed named protected-action pair. The current singular producer binding is expressly identified as requiring an owner amendment. Publishing presentation does not enable policy or create an invitation. P17 freezes qualified presentation/action dependencies; P6 retains one permanent effect and immutable provider request. There is no live CMS rendering, survey mailer, ordinary signature survey injection or generic workflow dependency.
- **Response and reporting:** One final logical response is admitted atomically with Used authority and review intent. Identical retries return the same receipt; changed-payload retries do not overwrite it. No public prior-comment echo or staff score editing appears. Issued-cohort conversion includes effects whose submission may have begun, including indeterminate provider outcomes, so a valid response cannot produce a numerator outside the denominator. Provider acceptance remains a distinct fact.
- **Support–CRM continuity:** Feedback appears through the same D9/D26 canonical Support detail under both current domain permissions. Linking, unlinking and Party merging do not change response authorship, sample authority, contact preferences or business completion. No donor score, duplicate CRM Activity body or giving mutation is created.
- **Schema and authorization:** The package preserves actual Support text IDs, tenant-aware owner relationships, one lifetime opportunity/effect/response, trusted server attribution and exclusive recipient branches. It does not claim broad current CRUD or RLS qualifies the future workflow. The data contract correctly notes PostgreSQL's implicit USING fallback when WITH CHECK is omitted, while still requiring effective old/new-state authorization and immutable fields across commands, RLS, grants, views and privileged jobs.
- **Custody and rollout:** Comments, ratings, prepared material, action credentials, sessions, preference state, opaque contact guards, receipts and derived projections have distinct purposes and disposal boundaries. Restore must replay restrictions before serving. New functionality starts Off; current source/owner qualification, additive schema, old-writer fencing and actual negative/concurrency/accessibility evidence are prerequisites to activation. They are implementation proof obligations, not unresolved product choices being deferred to an operator.
