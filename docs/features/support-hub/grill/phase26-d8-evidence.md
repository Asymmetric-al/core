## Reconciled independent corrections

Three independent reviews covered source/DB/authorization, vendor/UX, and lifecycle/owner/mail counterexamples. Their intermediate reports are not coequal alternative specifications; the root review and exact D8-R01–R20 are the reconciled amendment package.

1. **One saved inbox choice:** reject user/tenant inheritance and a UI-only departure override. Saved policy covers owner events from multiple routes without a new offboarding engine.
2. **First durable Support admission binds policy:** no Support policy/fan-out work inside the security transaction; no mid-pagination mix. Existing reviews cannot be released by later Save.
3. **Current invalidity and Shared release differ:** deny access and record invalid handling independently; pending C/paused/missing-cover work is not ordinary claimable Shared just because its assignee is null.
4. **Pause keeps its meaning:** mandatory denial/invalidation/discovery continues, autonomous A release holds; qualified manual A handoff remains direct. C requires the actual reviewer outcome.
5. **Review governs assignment only:** no new Send/note/Resolve approval. Review chooses and performs the disposition in one command rather than Approve followed by Assign.
6. **Dormant C is preserved:** legitimate resolution removes actionable work pressure but preserves unreviewed binding. Reopening/moving cannot launder C; explicit No handoff needed can settle a completed-work review.
7. **Fresh episode versus new content:** later genuine reopened work may get a fresh current-policy pursuit; an ordinary message revision while the same handoff is pending keeps its captured mode. Unrelated governance epochs are not duplicate losses.
8. **No handback and no stolen authorship:** restoration may obsolete uncommitted invalidation, but never reclaims committed assignments. Actual author revocation may block provider I/O; original reply effect remains immutable and recoverable.
9. **Shared tasks, one PDP, one mutation:** derived handoff/task views are not new owner systems; current compatibility/helpers/claims do not prove the required future authority.
10. **Honest evidence:** five source-helper checks and existing mocked/source tests have bounded meaning. No deployed security, transaction, vendor-account, mail or real-usability result is claimed.
