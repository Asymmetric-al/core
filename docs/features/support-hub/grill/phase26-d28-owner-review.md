# D28 — Feedback invitations, response authority and Email Studio

**13 September 2026. A selected: optional automatic feedback, Off by default, with an adjustable random sampling percentage.** These are proposed amendments for the current adversarial review, not an implemented feature or already ratified detailed contract. D1–D27-C remain ratified. No formal specification, runtime, database, provider, DNS, inbox, message or GitHub change is made here.

**Independent disposition: accept with required amendments.** Use one narrowly coded Support feedback policy, one qualified invitation/action producer, the existing P17/Email Studio preparation boundary and P6 delivery spine. Do not make the D5 ending command itself a sender, reuse a saved reply/signature/ordinary Guidance URL as a survey, or wait for a Phase 34 rule/form builder.

## Major owner qualifications that must be explicit

1. **New invitation meaning:** Email Studio needs one separately registered optional Support feedback invitation contract, neutral template meaning, action descriptor, compatible existing presentation role, exact locale and bounded material posture. The proposed display name is **Support feedback invitation**; an eventual code key must be registered through P17's existing catalog process rather than accepted from tenant input.
2. **Narrow no-Party action authority:** P6 has qualified no-Party delivery branches, but current P17 Protected Actions explicitly requires an exact tenant Party/contact authority. D28 must amend that owner contract narrowly for an invitation-bound Support feedback recipient authority. An unknown/unlinked requester must not require a fabricated CRM Party, and a separate survey bearer-link framework is unnecessary.
3. **Feedback contactability:** P3 owns the shared consent/contactability chokepoint. A feedback-only opt-out, including no-Party endpoints, needs a narrow registered purpose/contactability extension. It must not become a competing Support preference flag, a generic marketing-subscription change, or an exception to do-not-contact/provider suppression.

All three are implementation qualification requirements in the selected decision. None is already proved by the current code or by a vendor offering CSAT.

## Exact proposed owner clauses

### F-O01 — Optional policy and neutral source eligibility

**Material concern: yes; high severity, high likelihood without separation.** A status change could contact people who were not helped, bypass existing ending review or silently change the meaning of D5.

> Feedback invitations SHALL be a separate tenant-enabled Support policy, Off by default, over explicitly qualified inbox scope. D5's local ending remains message-free. The feedback producer considers only a current reviewed completed human-handled opportunity with qualified actual correspondence and one permitted intended respondent. No response endings, unused/cancelled/duplicate tracking, purely administrative closure, automatic acknowledgement, internal-note-only handling, a guide view or an unsubmitted human reply do not establish completed-help invitation eligibility. No automatic historical backfill, retroactive enablement or staff-selected per-case send is introduced.

The current requester's status as donor, staff member, missionary or unauthenticated person is not itself a different survey rule. Actual qualified source/audience facts matter. Work completion is not proof of satisfaction or a successful financial result. Known sensitive/restricted source exclusions come from real policy, not a handler's guess about whether a rating will be favorable.

**Proof:** Off, newly enabled, old completions, D5 No response, administrative resolution, only-automatic handling and later human correspondence produce the exact eligible/ineligible states without changing the ending command's effects.

### F-O02 — Sampling is stable selection, not a percentage promise

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Repeated evaluations or setting changes could redraw until a desired conversation is invited, while staff might read 25% as exactly one invitation in each four requests.

> Use the root's proposed initial **25%** sampling value with a separate Off state and a bounded integer percentage control. Draw/derive and durably record one unbiased selection outcome for the stable feedback opportunity using a qualified server-owned method. Record the applicable policy/sample version. Retry, reopening, reclassification, policy edits and restored jobs SHALL NOT redraw the same opportunity or convert an earlier excluded cohort into a backfill blast. Percentage changes affect new opportunities. The UI explains approximate random coverage, not a quota or guaranteed sample size.

Sample eligibility and sample outcome are separate from actual invitation admission/provider outcome. No message content, expected score, donor value, protected relationship or handler preference controls the draw. Do not expose a “try again” or manual include action that undermines the selected automatic policy. Statistical aggregation must preserve eligible, selected, suppressed, submitted/unknown and responded populations; no-response is never a negative rating.

**Proof:** Replays yield the same sample; 0/Off and 100% boundaries are unambiguous; edits cannot redraw; provider failure does not change sampled status or falsely increase invited counts.

### F-O03 — A real recipient authority, including no-Party requesters

**Material concern: yes; critical severity, medium likelihood.** The first To address, assignee, related CRM Party or current contact record could be chosen as respondent without evidence, or a forwarded survey could impersonate that person.

> The source producer SHALL bind exactly one qualified intended feedback recipient from the actual human Support correspondence and current source authority. Never send to every To/Cc member or infer the respondent from the first endpoint/related Party, assignee or matching email. Ambiguous multi-party/group cases remain ineligible for this automatic scope unless the source contract independently proves one recipient; the policy cannot guess. No staff per-case recipient picker is added merely to force eligibility.

> Extend the existing P17 tenant action descriptor narrowly with a **Support feedback invitation recipient authority** for the qualified no-Party endpoint/source revision. It authorizes only the specified invitation response and scoped feedback preference effect, never account login, CRM access, contact verification or another protected action. Tenant/environment, feedback opportunity/issuance, intended endpoint-authority revision and source/safety fences remain server-owned. This branch does not make every P17 action no-Party-capable.

The response is accurately attributed as feedback submitted through that invitation. Mailbox possession, a forwarded link and a shared address do not identify which human responded or prove a CRM Party's opinion. A later CRM link, merge or address change cannot silently rebind the original invitation or rewrite attribution.

**Proof:** No-Party flows work without fake records; group/ambiguous cases are safely omitted; cross-tenant/source substitution fails; forwarding allows at most the intended limited response action and reveals no private conversation.

### F-O04 — Email Studio owns the invitation, not the response

**Material concern: yes; high severity, high likelihood if ordinary snippets or signature ratings are reused.** A reusable URL or template could bypass issuance, recipient binding, language meaning, neutral questioning and duplicate guards.

> Register one new governed optional **Support feedback invitation** meaning in P17, with the Support producer, exact recipient branches, approved fixed response instrument/version, locale contract, action descriptor, material class, source applicability and current contactability requirements. P17 owns invitation wording/presentation/validation and immutable preparation. Support owns question/response semantics, issuance, eligibility, expiry/revocation and response admission; P6 owns actual dispatch and evidence. Use existing compatible Brand Kit/Role Layout and one neutral invitation action, not a new feedback Layout Role or a personal human signature.

Invitation wording can be professionally authored within its purpose, but cannot imply that the request was successfully fixed, solicit giving, expose transcript/subject/CRM/private guide facts, change score labels/meaning secretly or create additional questions. No rating-changing GET links, preselected rating in the URL, case attachment, tracking pixel or raw tenant-authored action target is allowed. Template previews use synthetic non-redeemable action material. Publishing/editing invitation wording sends nothing and does not activate the tenant policy.

**Proof:** Exact invitation locale/instrument and action are frozen; the source template cannot leak protected context, create an arbitrary action or make preview tokens redeemable. D18/D23/D27-C remain separate sources.

### F-O05 — Existing D6 doorway, deliberate response submission

**Material concern: yes; high severity, documented scanner risk.** GET/prefetch can invent ratings or leak conversation context; a separate short survey URL could bypass Core's existing authority contract.

> Reuse the one fixed D6 protected doorway. The URL carries only the random nonsecret selector; the independent verifier remains in the fragment. Selector-only GET/HEAD is generic, inert and non-enumerating. The first-party fragment script strips the fragment and prepares, but never auto-submits, the same-origin form. The requester's deliberate Continue POST validates current issuance/source/recipient authority and creates only the existing short-lived non-authorizing landing session. Then present the narrow feedback form; its deliberate Submit feedback POST re-proves current authority and atomically records the response.

The earlier idea of a feedback-specific form directly selected by GET is rejected: current P17 explicitly forbids revealing the action/tenant/resource from a selector-only request. The existing generic doorway adds a deliberate step but avoids a new link protocol or unrecorded privacy exception. No portal account, OTP, sign-in or privileged source access is required for this narrowly qualified non-financial response branch. No transcript, requester identity or case subject is revealed on the landing or response form. The feedback form uses the exact approved instrument and accessible labels; it does not accept unrestricted tenant HTML.

Preserve existing CSRF, exact trusted origin/path, Fetch Metadata, fragment handling, cookie, no-referrer, cache/CSP, rate-limit and non-enumeration requirements. No cross-site analytics, AI request, live attachment, automatic POST or open-redirect target is added. Reject a submit with no deliberate valid rating; scanners and previews do not create a response or consume the grant by GET.

**Proof:** GET/HEAD/scanner requests change no rating; missing/stripped/modified verifier, cross-origin POST, wrong scope, expired session and token replay fail safely; supported mail clients preserve the fragment without unsafe fallback.

### F-O06 — Quiet delay, delivery utility and response expiry are different clocks

**Material concern: yes; high severity, high likelihood if a single expiry field is reused.** An outage can send stale requests long after resolution, or a delivery event can extend a credential after P17 sealed it.

The root's proposed defaults are **24 elapsed hours of quiet after the qualified completion**, **30 elapsed days between possible feedback invitations to the same delivery endpoint within the tenant**, and a **14-day response authority window**. These are product defaults, not universal legal or vendor best-practice constants.

> Eligibility SHALL be re-proved when the delay becomes due and again at admission/submission. Relevant new work/input invalidates the exact completion basis. Issue the feedback action only during qualified preparation after the quiet delay; freeze its absolute expiry from the issuance time, shortened by any earlier source/privacy authority. Neither delivery, opening, retry, response-page refresh nor policy changes extend it. Show the real expiry date rather than promising fourteen days after receipt.

The invitation also needs a finite source-owned delivery utility distinct from response expiry and prepared-material custody. This review recommends **send-by 24 hours after the scheduled due instant** (at most 48 hours after that qualifying completion), then expire definitely-unsubmitted work without catch-up. The root must record the final value explicitly. The new optional-feedback prepared-material class cannot outlive that utility or action/source bounds, accidentally inherit required donor mail's 30-day ceiling, or reuse D13's unrelated 15-minute confirmation window.

**Proof:** UTC/DST boundaries, delayed workers, source reopening, credential outage and late provider events cannot renew an invitation, prolong material custody or create a stale catch-up send.

### F-O07 — Cooldown and duplicate protection survive uncertain delivery

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Two simultaneously completed requests can both pass a read-only cooldown check; a lost provider response can release the cooldown and trigger another invitation.

> Limit each conversation's stable invitation lineage and use one tenant-scoped recipient-endpoint frequency guard. Atomically reserve/check the recipient slot and current exact source/policy fence before the provider crossing. Pending reservation, definitely-unsubmitted cancellation and submission-may-have-begun are distinct states. A possible or accepted submission consumes the repeat guard even if delivery is unknown or no response follows. A definitively unsubmitted canceled reservation may be released only by its actual owning state/version; an ambiguous worker timeout is not that proof.

An endpoint-based keyed/minimized correlation may support contact-frequency protection without asserting that equal addresses are the same person. It must live under the qualified contactability/frequency owner, remain tenant/environment scoped and survive valid key/schema migrations without erasing active exclusions. Do not copy plaintext email into permanent event/audit tables or infer a CRM merge. An address change/rekey cannot grant permission to contact a new person or bypass an existing known suppression.

Reopening must cancel the exact pending completion generation without redrawing selection or granting another invitation. Any later eligible unsent opportunity must preserve the stable sample/history, have a genuinely current reviewed source basis and obey the single-lineage/repeat contract; a cosmetic close/reopen loop cannot manufacture additional sends.

**Proof:** Concurrent conversations, duplicate jobs, lost responses, partial local success, old reservations and contact/key changes produce at most the permitted invitation and preserve unknown state honestly.

### F-O08 — Off and source changes use exact fences

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Disabling the feature could stop only future scheduling while queued invitations still send, or unexpectedly throw away responses from people already asked.

> Turning Off SHALL stop new opportunities and all definitely-unsubmitted invitations in the affected policy scope through the existing producer/safety fence. It SHALL NOT recall mail, rewrite possible/accepted outcomes, clear cooldown evidence or silently revoke already issued response links. Those links retain their original expiry unless a separate applicable privacy/security/source revocation removes authority. Re-enabling starts from new qualified opportunities, not a backfill/replay of the Off interval.

Reopening, newly admitted actionable input, no-longer-valid recipient/contactability, tenant removal and source restriction similarly invalidate only the exact affected unstarted invitation. Off/reopen before submission linearization prevents the send; possible submission first reconciles. Later adverse source state must not make a new provider key or substitute another sender/account. The ordinary reply, D5 ending, manual work and other valid P6 purposes remain independently available.

**Proof:** Race Off/reopen/opt-out against scheduled work, preparation and provider crossing; received links still have the documented response posture; unrelated Support and provider evidence remain intact.

### F-O09 — Scoped opt-out through P3, not a second consent system

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** A feedback opt-out could be ignored for no-Party requesters, erase required receipt eligibility, or become a second contradictory CRM preference.

> Qualify a narrow P3-owned **Support feedback invitations** contactability purpose, including the appropriate no-Party endpoint-authority branch. Every invitation obeys current global contact restrictions, this purpose's preference, source privacy eligibility and provider suppression. Tenant enablement and an earlier Support exchange are not by themselves recipient-consent or provider-suppression proof. Present a clear way to stop future feedback invitations without requiring a CRM account or changing giving receipts, ordinary authorized human support, or unrelated subscriptions.

Its actual withdrawal command is scoped, idempotent and bound to the intended tenant/endpoint/purpose authority. Recipient preference changes create no rating and need not delete an already submitted response. P17's visible unsubscribe/header requirements follow the registered purpose and applicable classification; no caller/template builds headers or abuses required-message exceptions. Declare that classification with the shared owner before activation, rather than assuming every service survey is legally identical to marketing or to a required receipt.

**Proof:** Party/no-Party, global opt-out, feedback-only stop, shared addresses, revoked endpoint and provider suppression all affect exactly their intended contact scope; forged unsubscribe cannot change another tenant or contact purpose.

### F-O10 — Response receipt and attribution are their own facts

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** A bearer response might be stored as verified CRM speech, provider acceptance or a new email, while retry creates duplicate ratings.

> Admit one logical feedback response under the invitation/response source command, with trusted invitation, instrument version, rating, optional bounded plain-text comment, timestamp and minimal attribution. The simplest recommended contract is one final submission per invitation: edits remain free before Submit; identical replay returns the same receipt, while changed replay conflicts. Staff cannot rewrite the submitted score. Any later user-edit feature requires an explicit repeatable/successor action and response-version contract rather than silently replaying a Used D6 action.

Accept only after the invitation has durably reached Submission may have begun or a stronger compatible state, preventing pre-send authoring/preview material from being response authority. A fast valid response while provider outcome remains unknown is independent response evidence; it does not fabricate a transport acceptance event. Forwarded/shared-mailbox attribution stays honest. No email address, CRM Party or free-text author name is accepted from the form as identity authority.

Comments are untrusted text, not HTML, commands, AI instructions or automatic complaint/identity evidence. No file upload, link expansion or private source preview is needed. The public result is a neutral receipt without replaying another person's prior comment or exposing case context.

**Proof:** Duplicate/changed submits, two holders of a forwarded link, possible-send uncertainty, expired/revoked authority and malicious comments yield one authorized source outcome without false identity or email evidence.

### F-O11 — Feedback review without automatic business consequences

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** A low score/comment could automatically reopen work, email an apology, create a duplicate CRM score or certify a problem exists.

> Feedback belongs to the Support response source and is available through the same currently authorized Support detail reached from CRM. Use the qualified existing staff attention/review capability for feedback that requires human consideration; no new workflow builder or automatic requester message is introduced. A reviewer may deliberately choose ordinary follow-up through the actual source/work/reply owner. A response alone SHALL NOT reopen/resolve the conversation, create a new ticket, change assignment, send an apology, alter giving/identity records or create a CRM sentiment/loyalty/performance score.

Ordinary email replies to the invitation may enter through actual D1/D2 intake and be handled as real correspondence. That is different from submitting a rating. No false message/last-contact event, D14 reply credit or verified completion is generated by form submission or review. The support producer must declare accountable attention ownership before activation, not leave negative actionable comments in an unowned hidden record.

**Proof:** Form response, ordinary email reply and explicit staff follow-up retain distinct events/authority; inaccessible CRM/source data is not revealed through a rating or attention count.

### F-O12 — Custody, production proof and rollout

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Survey tokens/comments or preserved invitation history could become another permanent transcript store; a feature flag might skip new source compatibility.

> Classify policy/sample history, opportunity/cooldown records, issuance verifier/session, response/comment, derived reports and audit separately under their real owners, with declared bounded retention/final disposition and restore barriers before activation. Feedback is not a copy of the conversation. No transcript/subject/private guide/body is stored in the invitation record or public form. Response collection does not renew D17 source content. Redaction/deletion and source safety apply to retained comments and projections; an audit reason cannot preserve erased text.

P17 prepared material purges under its exact acceptance/finality/deadline rules; Recent sent copy still excludes every action URL/verifier and internal provenance. Provider reconciliation after purge is body-free. Report selected, attempted/unknown, accepted where proved and responded facts separately; silence, tracking and a response token are not staff-performance evidence or proof of delivery/reading.

Deploy qualified producer/action/recipient/preference contracts and tolerant constrained readers before enabling the Off-by-default setting. Prove actual same-origin browser/scanner, no-Party/current contactability, database/RLS, concurrent cooldown, issuance expiry, privacy/restore and P6 provider-boundary behavior. Kill controls stop only the affected optional invitation/response capability while preserving histories and possible-send reconciliation. Do not roll back to writers that drop source purpose, lose suppression or restore disposed tokens/comments.

## Final synthesis and proof boundary

The chosen A requires explicit owner work; it is not just another email checkbox. First settle source eligibility, one respondent authority, stable sampling/frequency and Off/reopen semantics. Qualify the new P17 invitation and narrow no-Party D6/P3 branches. Then add source-owned issuance/response and scoped contactability, connect existing preparation/admission/dispatch, and complete the small accessible response/review journey. Shared source primitives and exact fixed policy are sufficient; Phase 34/AI/general survey building is unnecessary.

The root's **25% / 24-hour quiet / 30-day repeat gap / 14-day response window** are proposed product defaults. This review additionally recommends a **24-hour post-due invitation utility** and **one final submission**; these remain synthesis inputs until the root records the final answer. The response expiry is fixed from issuance before preparation, not silently extended on provider delivery. Existing D6's generic Continue step is retained; the earlier lower-friction alternative would conflict with the current selector privacy rule and is rejected.

Required proof must cover: Off/no backfill; stable sample/replay and percentage changes; no-Party/ambiguous group authority; protected-route scanners and supported mail clients; exact language/instrument and synthetic previews; finite delivery/response clocks; concurrent endpoint reservations; uncertain provider success; Off/reopen/opt-out ordering; source expiry/deletion; repeat/forged response posts; safe comments; no false CRM/message/work effects; accountable review; and body-free purge/restore/mixed-version behavior. No database, provider, browser, accessibility, capacity or actual recipient experiment was executed for D28 in this review. Source and primary-document checks justify the proposal; they do not qualify its future implementation.
