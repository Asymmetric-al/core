## Independent question review and recommendation

The data and lifecycle reviews confirmed that neither D10 nor D11 chooses this product capability. They highlighted original-message custody, current-root granularity, prepared replies and current work/assistance scope. The vendor review confirmed that message movement, a related conversation, and a new outward email are different actions. All three reviews favored two meaningful alternatives rather than an artificial third option repeating D11.

**Recommend B as an optional capability:** staff may create a related independently handled conversation for genuinely separate requester work. Keep A available and ordinary when one coordinated exchange is clearer. This is a product judgment, not a universal best-practice claim. It avoids forcing independent issues into one current status/reminder while allowing the requester to communicate naturally.

The strongest case for A is lower coordination and one outward thread. B earns its additional complexity only when independent handling or follow-up is useful. Its cost must be visible in the founder question: independently responding may create a second donor email thread. Creation alone sends nothing; staff should do the organization without requiring a resubmission.

## Boundaries for the selected answer's full adversarial review

1. Preserve one actual incoming email and its historical time/audience/evidence, including when it contains two topics. Context references or staff summaries are not new received messages.
2. Reconcile D10's original-source/current-root and D2 reply-affinity rules explicitly. A new related work item cannot secretly rebind an ordinary reply or broaden current handling.
3. Establish independent outward continuation deliberately through existing Email Studio/P17/P6/Resend contracts; a subject edit alone is insufficient. Keep already-admitted/possibly-submitted effects under their original identity.
4. Keep replies to old routes serviceable. No silent topic inference/fan-out, duplicated inbound events or requirement that donors resend to the correct thread.
5. Reconcile current D3 work/reminders, historical completion and actual input time; avoid false closure, reset service clocks or dropped owner-result review.
6. Requalify current D6–D8 assignment/access/coverage and D11 source interests without cloning tasks or replaying new-intake automation.
7. Keep CRM relevance, actual correspondence and source permissions distinct; linking new work does not automatically create/associate a Party or grant a transcript audience.
8. Provide a safe understandable correction after later mail/work. Do not promise snapshot rollback or assume D10 Merge is always a truthful inverse.
9. Prove atomicity/idempotency, original-source races, browser/collision/keyboard behavior, accessible navigation, mixed-version rollout and visible recovery before activation.

These are review obligations and existing invariant boundaries, not accepted new D12 requirements or a premature implementation plan. The founder chooses the product capability first. Then its full review settles a concrete permanent behavior and explicitly identifies any amendment needed to earlier decisions.
