## Important reconciliations verified

1. **Combined snapshot versus per-original body inspection.** The earlier stricter idea was explicitly replaced before integration. All current contracts distinguish represented authorized scope from whether every old body is mounted. This preserves normal one-open catch-up without inventing coverage for a source missing from the snapshot.
2. **Content newness versus delivered email.** A colleague's locally admitted reply can be unread even while queued; D15 accepted-send following and P6 provider evidence retain their own timing. Reading does not certify delivery and delivery does not reset reading.
3. **Private orientation versus a business audit ledger.** Current epochs/cutoffs and bounded rejection/context controls protect intent. They do not justify permanent per-click receipts or successful view telemetry that reconstructs staff reading. Security evidence remains its own restricted purpose.
4. **Missing personal state versus known Read.** Absence is not silently converted to Read by activation. Current eligible other-content can be Unread without a stored personal row, aggregate alert or work obligation.
5. **Read availability versus action authority.** A cue failure cannot block valid work. A successfully cleared cue cannot authorize sending, resolve work, acknowledge a task or expose restricted CRM content.
