## The next decision

**How much should the staff queue distinguish conversations that are waiting on someone?**

Imagine Sarah asks about a receipt. In one conversation, Maria needs Sarah to clarify which gift she means. In another, Sarah has already supplied the information and Maria is waiting for an authorized finance colleague to check the receipt. Both requests remain unfinished, but the next necessary input comes from different places. These are illustrative test scenarios grounded in the already-discussed owner-domain boundaries, not a claim about measured ministry workload.

This choice decides what a colleague can understand from the inbox without opening each conversation. It also affects future assignment, follow-up and reporting. It does not decide a database enum, SLA pause rule, automatic closure period or Send-and-close default.

| Option                                     | Staff work labels                                             | Benefit                                                                                                                       | Cost                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — One general Waiting status**         | Open · Waiting · Resolved                                     | Three clear choices distinguish actionable work, unfinished waits and completed support.                                      | Staff need conversation context or a note to learn whom the team is waiting for; the queue cannot filter that distinction as a work fact. |
| **B — Two kinds of waiting — recommended** | Open · Waiting for requester · Waiting on our side · Resolved | The inbox distinguishes needed requester input from a next step the ministry must obtain from a colleague or outside service. | One additional choice; the boundary between active work and a genuine wait must be explained clearly.                                     |
| **C — Waiting only for requester input**   | Open · Waiting for requester · Resolved                       | Three choices distinguish requester input from everything the tenant still owes.                                              | Work blocked on a colleague/service remains Open alongside immediately actionable work; notes and reminders explain that distinction.     |

A minimal Open/Resolved model with reminders was also considered; it can work for small teams, but offers less explicit information about unfinished waits than the three choices presented here. All three presented choices are credible alternatives. None means abandoning follow-up, hiding accepted messages, removing collaboration, or requiring the requester to use a portal. A customizable enterprise status system is unnecessary for this decision and is not offered as a weak extra option.

## Recommendation and precise meaning

**Recommend B.** A key Asym requirement is coherent support-to-CRM and owner-domain handoff. The queue should make it easy to distinguish “we need the person's input” from “our side still needs to obtain an answer.” One extra visible choice provides that distinction without a new workflow engine, configuration hierarchy or task framework.

- **Open:** Support has a useful next action to take now, whether replying, investigating or arranging a handoff. It need not already be assigned to a person; assignment is separate.
- **Waiting for requester:** the next necessary input is awaited from the person or admitted participants seeking help. This is not restricted to the original CRM-linked contact. A copied church representative or other admitted participant may supply it under D1/D2's message/identity boundaries.
- **Waiting on our side:** Support has sought the next necessary input from a colleague, an owning-domain process or an outside service and is awaiting that result. The tenant remains responsible for obtaining it and following through. This does not claim an outside provider belongs to the tenant, forbid progress updates, or let staff label ordinary actionable work as waiting.
- **Resolved:** Support's obligation for this request is complete; no promised or required support next step is being hidden. This does not prove a refund, receipt correction or CRM action completed. It also does not require a donor to send a courtesy confirmation merely to clear ordinary completed support work.

These are proposed staff-facing meanings, not a commitment to four physical database enum values. Nor should a three-state alternative quietly acquire a mandatory hidden waiting-reason taxonomy: A and B have genuinely different visible granularity.

The strongest objection to B is that one Waiting status may be sufficient for small teams and requires less categorization. One independent reviewer preferred A; another preferred C, which keeps all tenant-side work Open and reserves Waiting for requester input. Both are credible simpler designs. No measured Asym support-volume or task-study evidence proves the extra distinction is necessary for every tenant. B is the recommended product judgment because permission-aware handoffs are already central to Asym's support scope, and confusing a requester obligation with a ministry obligation is a practical handoff problem. The founder can choose A or C without abandoning the existing safety requirements. B adds a visible distinction between work Support can act on now and work for which it is awaiting a colleague/service result.

## Boundaries that apply across the choices

**Work status is not assignment, unread state, delivery state or an owning-domain action.** Waiting retains Support accountability. Unassigned unfinished work remains visible in an explicit unassigned queue. A support assignment does not grant access to finance, CRM or care records. A receipt correction, refund or contact update remains authorized and performed by its own domain; Support may retain a permitted link and follow-up responsibility without creating a second authoritative workflow.

**Snooze is a return-time action in these options.** It need not answer who holds the next input. A promised follow-up must not disappear, but this question does not choose a mandatory reminder for every wait, durations, timezone defaults, office hours or SLA pause policies. Exact wake-up behavior remains governed by the existing timed-snooze/reopen requirement and subsequent lifecycle design, not a blind copy of one vendor's settings.

**Relevant admitted replies bring work back to staff attention.** Participant/thread admission remains a dependency, but it need not block deciding work-state meaning. Duplicate mail, auto-responders, quarantine entries and unrelated messages do not become human work transitions merely because they contain a thread reference. D2's pinned draft target and audience remain stable even when new activity requires review.

**Sending is not proof that someone owes an answer.** A progress update may leave work Open; a request for missing information may justify Waiting; a final answer may complete Support's obligation. A queued or bounced question is not proof that the requester received it. Delivery problems must remain visible and actionable under D1/D2, independent of the chosen work label.

**Status changes do not silently send messages.** This choice adds no portal, mandatory donor acknowledgement, automated closing email, cross-team access grant or implicit CRM mutation. Optional courtesy questions after a complete answer are not the same as missing information required to finish work.

**History and races still matter.** An incoming reply, manual resolution and a delayed snooze timer must not overwrite one another blindly. A move must preserve the relevant work facts; a stale timer must not reopen later-resolved or re-snoozed work. The existing authoritative transition/audit and D1/D2 concurrency requirements apply; this document does not pretend they are implemented.

## Pressure test of the recommendation

| Challenge                                             | Required interpretation or consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An acknowledgement says “I am investigating”          | The outbound direction alone cannot set Waiting for requester. Support still has a next step, so the work remains Open.                                                                                                                           |
| A third party is outside the ministry                 | Waiting on our side means the tenant owns obtaining the result, not that the third party is a tenant member. The label grants no access or CRM identity.                                                                                          |
| A copied participant can answer the question          | Requester-side waiting is not hardwired to one historical contact email; admitted participants follow D1/D2.                                                                                                                                      |
| Staff can do other useful work while awaiting Finance | Do not mark the conversation wholly blocked merely because one external step is pending. Open remains appropriate while Support has a useful next action.                                                                                         |
| Both requester and finance inputs are outstanding     | A single work label cannot express every dependency. This requires a clear primary-next-step rule during the answer's adversarial review, with notes/authorized links preserving context; it is not justification for a generic dependency graph. |
| A user marks Waiting to clear the inbox               | Waiting must remain visible as unfinished work with accountable follow-through; reminders cannot be its only means of discovery.                                                                                                                  |
| A late delivery failure arrives after resolution      | Failure recovery must be visible and actionable. The exact status transition follows causal owner evidence, not a universal rule that every callback reopens the conversation.                                                                    |
| No reply arrives                                      | Q3 does not authorize arbitrary automatic resolution. Unresponsive, duplicate, withdrawn and actually solved requests have different outcome meanings; closure/disposition policy remains explicitly unchosen.                                    |
| The current Pending data lacks a reason               | Never infer a past requester/our-side wait from the latest email direction. A later migration needs evidence-preserving handling of ambiguity.                                                                                                    |
| A worker merely changes the status                    | The draft, its recipient audience, assignment and protected-owner state do not silently change.                                                                                                                                                   |

These are pre-question challenges, not a substitute for the full adversarial review after the founder answers. They show why B needs precise meanings and why A and C remain strong alternatives.

## Scope of the answer and next proof

Selecting an option chooses staff-visible work granularity and its meaning. It does not select physical storage, configurable status builders, no-response closure, default Send actions, reminder durations, SLA enforcement or multi-party task graphs. Those are distinct unresolved decisions or implementation proof obligations and remain in the session coverage record.

The founder has answered B; the completed D3 review now supplies its independent adversarial findings and fully ratified amendments. In particular, verify same-tenant authorization and stale transition behavior; honest work/delivery/owner-state separation; outstanding follow-up visibility; reminder/incoming-message races; current-data migration; and staff understanding of Open versus each Waiting label. No new glossary terms or ADR are treated as ratified merely because this question has been prepared.

The research is complete enough to pose this choice: current source exposes a real semantic gap, the strongest alternatives have primary support-product precedent, the cost of extra granularity is explicit, and the remaining unknown is the founder's desired product behavior rather than a discoverable fact.
