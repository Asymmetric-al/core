## Required retirement correction

**Severity: High. Likelihood: plausible when an inbox/address is retired with retained work/history. Effect: narrows an overbroad restriction without changing A.**

The reviewed R05/P05 and the corresponding UX scenario classify every retired selected inbox as unavailable. [D20-R18](phase26-d20-adversarial-review.md) instead permits a retired resource to remain a qualified predicate when its owner preserves exact meaning and disclosure. [D25-R19](phase26-d25-adversarial-review.md) distinguishes retirement of new use from preserved accepted custody, historical evidence and necessary continuation/reconciliation. Retirement alone is not proof that all remaining work or report access is invalid.

If a reader uses the active inbox catalog as its source population, a still-permitted unfinished request or historical target/invitation can disappear. This would contradict the all-age current-work requirement and the original-source historical reporting contracts. It would also make an overview silently different from its qualified full report.

**Exact correction supplied to the root:**

> Replace the blanket retired condition with “missing, forbidden, or no-longer-qualified selected scope.” A retired reference remains usable only where its owner preserves meaningful current work/report access; label that state appropriately. An unusable reference must never become All or a successful empty result.
>
> Do not filter historical populations through today’s active-inbox catalog. Retain retired source references only where their owner preserves meaningful current report access. This does not create archive permission, restore retired routing, or grant a new authority tier.

**Required proof:** Retire a source that still has owner-qualified unfinished work and a retained target/issued invitation. The currently authorized Now and historical populations retain their correct contributions. Then remove the relevant access or source qualification: the exact selected scope becomes unavailable rather than widening; forbidden contributions disappear under current owner rules. No route is reactivated and no new archive authority is inferred.

## Specific boundary checks

The four historical blocks are explicitly separate **Reply targets**, **Reply times**, **Request labels** and **Feedback**. Elapsed time is a clear fixed-preview choice; source-qualified service-hour analysis remains in the full report. No new formula replaces D14. The source-qualified first submission-may-have-begun occurrence defines the issued feedback cohort in the data contract; callback arrival, confirmed acceptance and response-received dates do not substitute for it.

The current work summaries include waiting/deferred states. Unassigned remains a source-qualified subset, not a missing-hydration heuristic or a Claim grant. Reply attention counts distinct continuing conversations with still-owed contributions; a fulfilled reply with uncertain scoring is not owed work. The declared groups can overlap. Delivery recovery remains reachable even when reply attention is zero.

“All accessible/reportable” is an authorization scope, not a claim that failed, capped or incomplete source data is complete. The explicit incomplete/unavailable states and body-free complete server queries preserve that distinction. Unknown metadata cannot become unlabelled work, a raw ID, or a favorable denominator.

The new **1–366 local-date** overview bound, **60-second foreground** automatic refresh, **120-second stale** presentation threshold, **50-row** native-detail cap and **top-five** label preview are declared product/query choices. The documents do not present them as vendor facts, legal rules, retention periods or measured capacity. Full reports retain their own qualified range behavior; source caps never truncate totals. Current permission/withdrawal checks are independent of these presentation timings.

The proposed workload fixture and p95 targets are explicitly prospective and record the necessary units, workload and conditions. They are not claimed current performance evidence. The fixed page needs neither a warehouse nor a generic dashboard builder to meet them; owner-approved read models remain conditional on authorization, correctness and lifecycle qualification.

Email Studio remains the governed authoring/preparation owner. Overview opens no editor, prepares no mail and creates no new email family. Any future separately authorized scheduled report would have a qualified Phase 33 run/audience, P17 preparation and P6 delivery; current screen access never grants permission to email a snapshot. The existing CRM and giving/care command boundaries remain intact.
