## Material change from the September 12 context

The earlier Q28 evidence describes D27's completed public-guidance decision. That is now historical context: [D27-C](phase26-d27c-adversarial-review.md) adds a separately owned Internal Staff guide source and explicit staff publication while preserving the Public half. The resumed question should identify this revision, without erasing the earlier question or treating C's Guidance selection as a feedback answer.

The new internal owner does **not** change the feedback options:

- Reading, writing, publishing, archiving or restoring a Staff guide creates no requester feedback invitation, rating, evidence of completed human handling or P17 message dependency.
- Internal guide text, titles and access must not enter a requester invitation, public response page, selection rule, AI score or CRM sentiment field merely because Support staff can read them.
- Guide maintainership/publication authority is not automatically feedback-policy or send authority. An internal instruction may explain an eventual approved policy; editing that instruction cannot change the policy.
- Existing D5 local-only ending remains effect-free. A requires separate explicit tenant enablement and neutral correspondence/work eligibility. Neither expected praise nor the use of a guide establishes eligibility or success.

## The same three alternatives remain credible

| Option                                                        | Distinction and practical tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Optional automatic feedback invitations — Recommended** | A tenant deliberately enables a separate policy for eligible completed human-handled Support work. Consistent opportunities and less recurring staff effort; requires careful outward-contact eligibility and lifecycle. Off by default. |
| **B — Staff chooses when to ask**                             | An authorized worker deliberately initiates each supported invitation. More situational discretion; extra staff work and less consistent coverage. It still needs a real safe invitation/response contract.                              |
| **C — Ordinary feedback, without a dedicated rating flow**    | No structured invitation/rating capability is added. Lowest contact/build burden; ordinary replies remain useful, but operational metrics and silence are not a structured requester assessment.                                         |

A remains the best product judgment because it reduces one source of handler-selected coverage and repeated staff effort while allowing tenants not to enable further contact. It does not remove eligibility or voluntary-response bias, prove universal ministry demand, justify small-sample staff rankings or select a mandatory survey. B and C remain valid alternatives with the stated tradeoffs.

The existing Q28 wording already explicitly avoids positive-outcome eligibility, distinguishes completed human handling from a verified fix, and preserves recipient-consent/identity limits. Retain those points. Support would own the qualified feedback purpose and response facts; P17/Email Studio would own governed invitation wording/action material/preparation; P6 would own delivery and reconciliation. Neither Public links nor Internal guides substitute for that new contract. CRM continues to expose permitted Support context without a duplicate score/identity owner.
