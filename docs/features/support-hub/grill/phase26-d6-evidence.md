## Independent challenges adopted in the final package

The separate working reports remain in the session's `work/` folder as intermediate evidence. The following are the reconciled permanent decisions; alternative exploratory wording in intermediate drafts is superseded by D6-R01–R20.

1. **Future-only versus live pending policy:** pinning old pending work indefinitely creates stale rules and an extra Apply engine. Publish to disclosed new plus still-pending automatic work, with a current-policy commit fence and stable initial source. No historical Shared/backlog sweep is allowed.
2. **Coverage versus another role:** no qualified current coverage-steward authority was found. Use the same named Support people/team operational association; require human coverage before activation without inventing an extra owner field or forcing people On.
3. **Team-preservation bypass:** a person-only update must validate the effective preserved responsible team too. Recording the actual team alone is insufficient. Manual authorization does not imply automatic-pool membership.
4. **Shared default versus result:** Shared with an explicit automatic rule is not globally off. Only an evaluated Shared result is released; Pause holds automatic results, including those under a Shared default. The rule/default distinction appears in settings and proof.
5. **Tenant-wide versus inbox Save:** person receiving/limits are read-only summaries in the inbox editor, with a separately scoped management save. Self receiving and inbox Pause are immediate independent commands; Cancel cannot imply they were undone.
6. **Cap overclaim:** current/reopened/manual work may exceed an automatic gate. Count complete tenant Open including deferral; waits stay visible. No count mismatch is excused by this overage exception.
7. **Elapsed-wait trap:** initial age rules only concern still-pending automatic sources and their original elapsed UTC age, including Pause. They cannot schedule later adoption of an intentional Shared result or reset age on Save/retry.
8. **No unnecessary fourth mode:** one-person automatic pools cover fixed-person distribution with identical safety. The final package consistently names exactly three modes.
9. **Honest source readiness:** actual current selector/history/settings findings stay distinct from intended future contracts; the six-source-check result and 24 paper ordering cases are not production proof.
