## Protocol and Resend evidence

[RFC3834](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3834.html), August 2004, distinguishes responder classes. Its service-responder allowance supports a precisely specified From-address contract; Reply-To is unsuitable as an automatic recipient shortcut. Its loop/recipient/automatic-marking guidance informs R04/R05/R10. The Asym design explicitly documents the ordinary-subject continuity tradeoff rather than pretending to follow the RFC's automatic-subject SHOULD literally. This is protocol-backed product reasoning, not proof that a signed webhook identifies a human author.

[Resend receiving](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/receiving/introduction) and [received-email retrieval](https://resend.com/docs/api-reference/emails/retrieve-received-email) expose received source/headers and retrieval. The retrieved schema is not a promise of a trustworthy authentication-results boundary. An activation test must prove provenance for the particular inbound path; no arbitrary header parsing or borrowed CRM match fills that gap.

[Resend threading guidance](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/receiving/reply-to-emails) supports original RFC IDs in reply headers and normal subjects. The [8 July 2026 actual Message-ID change](https://resend.com/changelog/message-id-for-sent-emails) adds actual RFC `message_id` to sent-email evidence. The [send API](https://resend.com/docs/api-reference/emails/send-email) supports custom headers; the shared Core wrapper inspected here still needs the corresponding qualified field and semantic result mapping. A [sent event](https://resend.com/docs/webhooks/emails/sent) is not proof of mailbox delivery. These distinctions are required in P6 and UI.

[Resend idempotency](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys) lasts 24 hours; it is secondary transport protection, not permanent business dedupe. The new 15-minute dispatch utility and 24-hour recipient courtesy cap are separate Asym choices with different purposes. The former prevents stale mail, the latter repeated reassurance; neither guarantees delivery or authorizes another request key. ADR0032 remains the stronger recovery boundary.

**Source correction:** the Core guide and `consent.ts` comment claiming Resend has no transactional suppression list are stale. The dated [8 January 2026 suppression visibility announcement](https://resend.com/changelog/email-suppression-visibility) establishes provider suppression and its distinct outcome before this session. The currently opened [suppression guide](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/email-suppressions) describes team-wide application; a search-index excerpt returned older per-region wording. The opened page, not the stale excerpt, informs the comparison. Keep ADR0029's exact connection/region identity and qualify actual account behavior; do not assume another tenant/account/region is clear or expose a provider-team list to arbitrary tenant staff.

The current [managed suppression changelog](https://resend.com/changelog/suppression-list-support) and [webhook examples](https://resend.com/docs/webhooks/emails/suppressed) contain October/November 2026 example timestamps and do not establish a reliable earlier launch date for every management capability. This review does not depend on installing or exercising those newer management APIs. Native suppression evidence remains a necessary separate fact, and D13 never automatically unsuppresses an address.

[WCAG2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) supports visible focus, semantic controls, status announcements, contrast and operable targets. The blueprint's restrained service-email presentation is an Asym design choice; real screen-reader/mobile/RTL/email-client verification remains required, rather than a claim that a pretty mockup is accessible.

## Independent findings adopted

The independent data/CRM, lifecycle/protocol and vendor/UX reviews were synthesized into the root decision. The material corrections incorporated are:

1. Durable initial-intake authority instead of the transient adapter `created` flag, including recoverable outbox independent of template success.
2. Narrow new P17 automatic document/profile/material class, distinct from human, financial and optional staff classes.
3. One qualified original From service audience; no human Reply-all/Reply-To/quoted-forward/CRM inference.
4. Immutable original provider receipt time plus a 15-minute source deadline, with no replay/hydration/hold/move reset.
5. Twenty-four-hour tenant/mailbox courtesy cap consumed only at final provider crossing, with honest shared-mailbox/second-request tradeoff and no delayed catch-up.
6. Human-first suppression limited to an actual public human reply to the same qualified recipient; CC-only mail, drafts and presence do not qualify.
7. Automatic generated content never enters the permanent human Support-body exception; visible body-free P6 history and Off-only Recent copy.
8. Owner-resolved no-Party contactability instead of first matching donor; provider suppression distinct from Core consent/manual/bounce facts.
9. Explicit adapter-owned `auto-replied` exception, actual RFC IDs and subject-attribution tradeoff, with provider canary proof.
10. Single confirmation producer despite competing normal/holiday/new-ticket mechanisms seen in vendors; Email Studio owns wording, not triggers/audience/work.
11. Protected decision/budget/control/audit raw reads as well as writes denied; current-PDP projections and scoped RLS remain mandatory.
12. Compact mode/readiness/template/sender UX, quiet system history, no forced portal, personal staff identity, force-send control or duplicate body archive.
13. A prospective activation generation prevents mail received while Off being adopted merely because delayed processing occurs after enablement/mode change.
14. Off/Pause and a later recipient-relevant human dispatch stop further automatic provider calls even for an indeterminate envelope; preserve reconciliation rather than false cancellation.
15. Optional calendar content is revalidated in every mode, independent of outside-hours eligibility.
16. Fresh qualified adverse delivery/contactability evidence still creates D3 review when current Support action is actually required; optional-notice expiry alone does not.
17. Use the existing Service message Role Layout, and require actual recipient/member Party attribution for Activity rather than a Related-only link.
18. Manual forwarding does not authenticate the quoted donor, but its outer sender may themselves be a legitimate requester. UI describes dispatch attempts/caps, not guaranteed receipt or arrival order.

The full source reviews are working evidence, not independently adopted contradictory specifications. Root clauses reconcile their suggestions and are now fully ratified. Any later corrections discovered in the final independent pass are recorded in this same complete package before final delivery.
