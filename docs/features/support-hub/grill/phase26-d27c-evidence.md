## Final independent reconciliation

Three independent reviews checked the actual root review and both companion documents. Their final appendices record the resolved findings:

1. Use a bounded operational Staff guide owner; do not repurpose CMS public audience/drafts, P17 wording or the existing demo screen.
2. Choose common current-tenant Support readers and separate author/publisher/protection authority. Reject new per-inbox ACL modes, automatic privilege from D19 and another curation switch after internal publication.
3. Use one fixed language per guide and deliberately authored translations; no new translation-family synchronization. Keep Internal consultation-only rather than adding a typed message reference family.
4. Validate original canonical JSON/trusted purpose **before** Tiptap normalization. The actual generic parser observation makes this necessary.
5. Preserve candidate and unsaved work on restore/archive/delete unless replacement/discard is explicitly reviewed.
6. Keep safety restriction independent from ordinary Archive/Publish. Restoring or republishing a revision cannot release it through weaker authority.
7. Re-prove applicable retention/holds at irreversible disposal and declare each content class's custody/final disposition before activation. No confirmation or source-history feature grants indefinite retention.

These corrections do not add another product surface or approval bureaucracy. They close specific sources of lost work, disclosure and false authority in the simplest source lifecycle that satisfies the revised request.
