# D27-C — Public and internal staff guidance

**Fully founder-ratified, 13 September 2026. D27-C is the current Guidance decision; D1–D27 and all adopted amendments are accepted.** The substantive answer below is preserved; its earlier pending/paused statements describe the answer stage. The full ratification appended below governs current status. Q28 requester-feedback resumes unanswered. This remains exploratory grooming, not formal specification, implementation or release proof.

The strongest solution is **one Guidance surface with Public and Internal tabs, backed by different owners**. Public keeps selected Web Studio Pages and deliberate link insertion. Internal adds a small, maintained Support-owned Staff guide source for consultation by current tenant Support readers. Internal content is never inserted into replies or notes by the finder. This is a complete bounded source and maintenance journey, not a general company wiki, private CRM vault or new workflow platform.

The [UX blueprint](phase26-d27c-ux-blueprint.md), [source/data/Email Studio contract](phase26-d27c-data-contract.md), [evidence](phase26-d27c-evidence.md) and independent [data](phase26-d27c-data-review.md), [UX](phase26-d27c-ux-research.md) and [owner](phase26-d27c-owner-review.md) reviews form the full proposed revision. Exact requirements below govern ambiguities in exploratory research alternatives. No formal specification, implementation, tickets, real messages or production changes are authorized by this record.

## Corrected decision

> Phase 26 Guidance provides distinct Public and Internal tabs within the same authorized Support experience, including the canonical detail opened from CRM. Public sources retain D27's qualified CMS Page selection, safe preview and deliberate source-aware link insertion. Internal Staff guides are reusable, non-case-specific Support instructions owned in Core's operational domain, with a current tenant Support-reader audience, separately authorized authoring/publication, versioned drafts and explicit Publish to staff. Internal use is consultation-only. Its body, title, search, history and deep links remain private and never enter public search, public CMS, email preparation or AI retrieval merely because staff can read them. This revision includes D27C-R01–R30, all category resolutions, journeys, source contracts and required proof below.

## Exact amendments

### D27C-R01 Revision and bounded need

C SHALL replace D27's public-only coverage exclusion while retaining all unchanged public controls. Q28 feedback remains unselected. Staff guides SHALL contain reusable instructions for handling Support work, not customer/case histories, member-care records, sensitive missionary identities or locations, credentials, live financial facts or a general ministry knowledge platform. An example such as “how staff handle a receipt question” is a test fixture, not a claim about every tenant's policy or permission to perform a receipt action.

### D27C-R02 One surface, explicit source types

Guidance SHALL have two equally legible tabs, **Public** and **Internal**, with no mixed All-results mode. A fresh conversation opens Public; an explicitly selected tab may be remembered within that conversation's current working session. Switching tabs preserves the entered query and return state without changing the reply/note purpose. The source discriminator is a closed owner-defined type, not a client-controlled visibility field that converts private content into public content.

### D27C-R03 Public behavior is preserved

Public SHALL retain D27-R02–R30 as applicable: exact tenant/Site/Page/locale proof; selected Listed or Shared-by-link public variants; full eligible title/summary/body search; passive preview; deliberate Insert link/Link selected text; frozen wording/href and trusted provenance; same-source route continuity; withdrawal/preparation/P6 fencing; finite retention; complete authorization and compatible rollout. Shared-by-link is public, never the Internal audience. C creates no internal-to-public conversion shortcut.

### D27C-R04 Internal source ownership

Support SHALL own Staff guide identity, validated structured content, draft, published revision, lifecycle and private search in operational storage through `packages/api`. It SHALL NOT use CMS drafts, private editor previews, an authenticated variation of a public Page, P17 Saved Sections, source-code agent instructions or fixture data as this authority. A qualified existing owner may be reused if subsequently proven equivalent; the current inspected contracts do not provide one. Reuse editor/search primitives and platform capabilities without copying owner state or extending the CMS public audience.

### D27C-R05 Audience and restricted data

The initial internal audience SHALL be **Support staff**, meaning current active-tenant Support-read authority resolved through P12, not all tenant staff/admins, CRM readers, matching emails, inbox membership alone or a navigation alias. Staff guides are common Support instructions; no per-guide/inbox/private-person ACL builder is added. If content requires narrower financial, care, HR, security or missionary-record access, retain it in its qualified owner and use an authorized navigation path rather than copying it into common guidance. A label or author assertion cannot certify arbitrary prose is free of restricted data.

### D27C-R06 Reader, author and publisher authority

Reading published guides, reading/editing candidate content, publishing to the common Support audience, withdrawing/deleting and assigning a maintainer SHALL be explicit current capabilities. Existing P12/Support content-management authority with tenant oversight supplies them; no new role enum or implicit privilege from D19 intake review, assignment, source visibility or D18 saved wording is allowed. A publisher may also be the author when authorized; no mandatory second person is invented. Any actually applicable governance requirement remains enforced. Inbox-only curation authority cannot publish to a wider tenant Support audience.

### D27C-R07 Minimal content structure

A Staff guide SHALL have stable identity, a fixed canonical language, clear action-oriented title, short summary, validated structured body and accountable maintainer. One guide is one language; a translation is a deliberately authored separate guide with its own publication, not an automatic fallback or synchronized body. Start with headings, paragraphs, emphasis, bullet/numbered lists and descriptive links. No new attachment/upload/media service, executable checklist, live CRM variables, recipient tokens, embeds, scripts or automated actions is introduced. Authorized owner links may lead to relevant files or product actions without importing their protected content.

### D27C-R08 Tiptap purpose boundary

Use D23's shared Tiptap architecture with a registered **Staff guide** document purpose and the restricted R07 schema. Common editor code is not shared publication or email authority. Server commands SHALL validate the complete original canonical JSON and trusted source-purpose envelope, schema/version, content bounds, links and source restrictions BEFORE generic Tiptap/ProseMirror parsing or normalization, as well as before accepting or publishing. Source purpose derives from the stored source and qualified command, never a user-supplied node attribute. Unknown protected nodes/attributes or unsupported versions SHALL fail visibly rather than be silently discarded and saved. Hiding toolbar buttons or passing generic Tiptap parsing is not sufficient validation. No new paid collaboration/AI service or editor upgrade is implied.

### D27C-R09 Draft and publication

Save draft SHALL save only the authorized working candidate; it SHALL NOT publish or alter what ordinary readers see. Each guide has at most one current candidate and one current published head, with generation-based concurrency. A published guide may show **Unpublished changes** while its previous published revision remains readable. Publish to staff SHALL show the exact candidate/preview, source purpose, common audience and meaningful diff, then atomically admit an immutable published revision, trusted actor/time, current head, audit/receipt and required derived-data continuation. Publication itself includes the guide in Internal; no second Add-to-guidance switch is required.

### D27C-R10 Edit, restore, archive and delete

Edits create/update a candidate against an expected generation. Discard changes leaves the published head unchanged. Archive immediately makes ordinary search/read unavailable without destroying authorized management history; a known unsafe guide can use the same immediate withdrawal boundary with an explicit reason. Restore or restore an old revision SHALL create a reviewable candidate, never silently republish. Delete archived content requires current qualified authority and explicit irreversible impact acknowledgement, removes owned body/title/summary/candidates/revisions/derived content through a durable deletion process, and retains only permitted minimal body-free audit/tombstone evidence. Unfinished deletion is visible and cannot revive content. Ordinary archive and source safety restriction are independent controls: a safety restriction persists across revisions, candidates, Restore and Publish until its authorized owner admits a current reviewed release. Ordinary publication authority, a reason label or a new revision cannot clear it. Restore must preserve an existing candidate/unsaved work unless staff explicitly review and accept its replacement; archive/delete while editing likewise require a clear save/discard and effect decision, never silent loss. Deletion SHALL re-prove applicable records retention or hold; it can stop ordinary serving while required content stays in restricted custody. Acknowledgement cannot override custody, and completion states what was actually disposed.

### D27C-R11 Maintenance without a second workflow

Manage guidance SHALL distinguish Public selection management from Internal authoring/publication. Internal management exposes Draft, Published, Unpublished changes, Archived and deletion progress with clear actions; it does not create a generic review queue, social comments, progress tracker or an automatic Support ticket. Maintainer departure SHALL not delete tenant-owned guides/history; tenant oversight can assign a current eligible maintainer. Show the actual last publication and maintainer, not a fabricated review date from any edit. Content corrections use existing authorized staff-work capabilities where needed; no mandatory recurring review interval is invented.

### D27C-R12 Find and read Internal

Internal SHALL search only currently authorized, currently published Staff guide titles, summaries and body text, with title/summary priority and stable tie-break. A blank query provides a paginated, predictable title list. A language filter appears when useful; there are no nested folders, mandatory tags, public Site filter or custom relevance controls. Public Site scope is preserved for return to Public but cannot silently filter Internal. No automatic query extraction from private conversations or persistent raw-query history is allowed. Empty, no-match, incomplete/updating and unavailable states are distinct.

### D27C-R13 Consultation-only means no message insertion

Internal preview SHALL display **Internal — for Support staff** and readable published content, with an authorized full-page reading path when needed. It SHALL NOT offer Insert into reply, Insert into note, Copy article, automatic citation, conversion to Saved reply or a purpose switch. Existing manually authored replies/notes remain available. Known internal-purpose structured content SHALL not be accepted as externally publishable wording through client metadata stripping or a generic import path. No claim is made that Asym prevents screenshots, ordinary operating-system clipboard use or manual retyping of visible information.

### D27C-R14 Reference navigation and action authority

An authenticated Staff guide link SHALL reauthorize the current actor/tenant/source on every read, never act as a bearer grant or reveal a title/body to an unauthorized recipient. Links to another guide or business owner are navigation only; the target independently authorizes its content/actions. Public/external links do not leak private guide/conversation context through referrers, tracking or auto-preview fetches. Reading a procedure, checking a conceptual step or opening a domain does not perform/approve a refund, reissue a receipt, change a contact or certify identity. Current owning-domain controls prevail over stale procedural wording.

### D27C-R15 Support and CRM continuity

The same Guidance reader/finder serves Support detail and the qualified detail opened through CRM; one draft/context and current joint permissions remain. Guidance may also be consulted from the Support surface without creating a conversation. Staff author/maintain guides from its existing management area without manufacturing a ticket. Lookup/reading creates no Party, association, CRM Activity, last-contact event, note, staff-performance metric or resolution fact. Requester, participant, represented organization, actor, handler, CRM owner and guide maintainer remain distinct.

### D27C-R16 Authoritative mutation and idempotency

Create/save/publish/discard/archive/restore/delete/maintainer changes SHALL use one current-authorized owner command with expected generation and durable idempotency receipt tied to the effect and payload. Head transition, actor/time, audit and required continuation SHALL be atomic. Lost response retries consult the receipt; key reuse with a different payload conflicts. Concurrent publication/withdrawal/deletion and stale saves cannot overwrite newer intent, recreate deleted content or publish an older candidate. Long-lived browser authority or an old JWT claim cannot substitute for current capability at admission.

### D27C-R17 Database invariants

Operational guide/candidate/revision/head relationships SHALL be tenant-aware and constrained to the same guide identity and fixed language. Non-null tenant/source/purpose, allowed state/head combinations, at most one current candidate/head, unique revision identity, valid schema generation and protected immutable publication facts SHALL be enforced through constraints and the authoritative command. Tenant/actor/owner/approval/time/publication fields derive from trusted server context. Author deletion SHALL not cascade away tenant-owned content. CMS public references remain owner-validated soft references; Staff guides do not introduce cross-schema FKs or a shared visibility table that changes source ownership.

### D27C-R18 Grants, RLS and privileged paths

Application checks plus effective grants/RLS/column privileges SHALL independently protect published reads, draft/history reads and writes. Verify existing-row USING and resulting-row WITH CHECK behavior, including Postgres's implicit reuse rules; omission of explicit syntax alone is not a vulnerability finding. A permitted write cannot move tenant, source purpose, owner or publication state into a forbidden condition. Views, RPCs/functions, storage, exported projections and service-role jobs SHALL preserve the same authorization. No public/anonymous role gets internal content or a service key; privileged execution is not permission by itself.

### D27C-R19 Private derived data

Internal title, summary, snippets, counts, current/history bodies and links are protected data. Its search index and caches SHALL remain operational/private and never join the public P23 index, sitemap, public API, requester-facing knowledge source or AI corpus. Reuse shared indexing/query code only behind separate purpose/authorization contracts. Exact current tenant, membership, source/publication/safety generation and appropriate scope qualify every exposed result/count/snippet before paging. Public search SHALL expose no hidden internal-match count. Unknown current proof fails closed for that source class without substituting a wider query.

### D27C-R20 Temporal and permission correctness

Publication, archive, deletion and membership revocation SHALL gate new reads immediately at the current owner boundary, independently of eventual index/cache purge. Late favorable events cannot revive a withdrawn/deleted head or broaden audience. An open reader receiving invalidation SHALL clear no-longer-authorized content and show an accurate unavailable/access state; the system cannot recall content already seen. A newly published revision is signalled without scrolling/replacing text mid-reading; reload deliberately obtains the new current version. No offline/private-body downloads or durable browser cache are added.

### D27C-R21 Failures, completeness and recovery

One tab/source failure SHALL not take down the other tab, the conversation or unrelated message preparation. Search/preview requests are bounded and obsolete responses ignored. Incomplete indexing is disclosed rather than a false No matches. Current heads deny stale adverse data even if a worker is delayed. Existing durable continuation/receipt infrastructure supports replay, deletion purge and repair; normal maintenance must not require SQL surgery. Authoring shows Saved, Saving, Unsaved/conflict accurately; a failed autosave cannot claim a draft or publication exists.

### D27C-R22 Email Studio and P6 seam

Public Guidance links retain D27's existing P17 source-aware wording/preparation and local P6 submission fences; no CMS/URL I/O occurs at dispatch. Staff guides are consultation sources, not a new P17 material, signature or message-reference family. Internal availability SHALL not block or re-render ordinary replies and SHALL not cause source-body copying into a preparation. D18 reusable message wording, D23 signatures, D19 held-mail review and D26 manual-origin work/CRM histories retain separate roles. All existing purpose validation, custody, actual audience authorization and source restrictions still apply to any real message.

### D27C-R23 Retention and safe correction

Before activation, current published content, candidates, superseded revisions, archived/deleted bodies, derived data and body-free evidence SHALL each have a declared owner retention/disposal rule and tested final disposition, including applicable holds and restore behavior; no accidental indefinite source-history or backup exception is permitted. Reusable Staff guide source content follows its declared knowledge-source lifecycle, not D17's conversation expiry clock; this is not permission to store case/CRM data indefinitely. Published/archived revisions exist for controlled maintenance until authorized deletion or applicable owner retention removes them. Deletion/withdrawal must include derived indexes/caches and preserve restrictive tombstones through restores before serving resumes. Historical body-free evidence is not a hidden body store. Actual Support content, P17 provider-request purge and Recent sent copy URL/provenance exclusions remain unchanged. No restoration can reconstruct disposed content from backups or a projection outside its allowed recovery contract.

### D27C-R24 Simple accessible UI

Use Core's base-maia/Base UI/shared tokens and existing auxiliary detail surface, not a fourth permanent pane or an app-local component system. Both tabs have clear text labels and visible focus; Internal has a persistent purpose label and no disabled send affordance pretending insertion is possible. Preserve query, filters, scroll and draft on Back/Escape/close; use a one-stack narrow-screen flow and normal full-page fallback. Qualify keyboard/screen reader, meaningful headings/link labels, non-color status, RTL/IME, zoom/reflow, reduced motion and low-bandwidth text-first use. Permissions are explained without disclosing inaccessible titles or counts.

### D27C-R25 Performance and limits

Use bounded indexed tenant/current-head queries, private operational full-text search and batch authorization; avoid N+1 source requests and filtering after a first-page cap. Register byte/node/depth/text/query/page-result bounds and the production-shaped capacity profile before activation. Measure body search, stale-event handling, publication and purge at each declared maximum and one beyond with safe rejection/degradation. No universal article count, arbitrary review duration or new external search vendor is frozen without evidence. Shared primitives do not require a shared public/private result corpus.

### D27C-R26 Audit and operating ownership

Durable guide history records trusted actor, guide/revision/generation, command/effect, publication/withdrawal/restore/deletion disposition and allowed reason without raw body snapshots in technical logs. Technical telemetry distinguishes query failure, stale head, authorization denial, save conflict and purge lag using safe IDs. A guide correction requiring follow-up may use the existing authorized shared staff-work owner with a qualified guide reference; it is not automatically a customer ticket. Tenant oversight owns orphaned/obsolete guides; platform owners own current access, propagation and deletion failures. R30's named controls must exist before monitoring is relied upon.

### D27C-R27 Migration and replacement of the demo

The current `/support/knowledge` demo/read model and “attach to replies” promise SHALL be replaced or retired when the real capability activates. Fixture summaries are not source records, seeded official instructions or evidence that ACL/publication/search is ready. Legacy/demo links resolve only through explicit qualified migration mapping; otherwise show a neutral unavailable state, never an all-guides fallback or guessed title match. New staff-guide authoring/publishing and separate reader/index controls precede UI activation. No mass import of conversations, CMS drafts, Saved notes or external content into guides is authorized.

### D27C-R28 Compatible rollout and future change

Use additive operational schema/document versions and owner APIs; preserve independent Payload migration authority. Old readers/writers SHALL reject unsupported guide purpose/schema instead of dropping constraints. A kill switch can stop Internal publication/lookup while preserving existing Public and ordinary Support behavior, without defeating mandatory safety/deletion fences. Roll forward after new guide content is written unless rollback compatibility is proven. Restricted internal audiences, media authoring, general wiki functions and AI remain separate researched extensions, not speculative abstractions or hidden dependencies of C.

### D27C-R29 Traceability and proof

Record this as D27-C's scope revision and retain D27-A's original ratification and Q28 question/evidence unchanged. Link the exact supersession map below into ADR0027/glossary/session records; no ADR0028 or feedback choice is created. Later authorized formal OpenSpec/design/tasks/tickets/release evidence SHALL trace D27C-R01–R30 and P01–P40, together with unchanged D27 public proof. All actual runtime, DB/RLS, publication/race, browser/AT, usability and capacity groups remain required and unexecuted. Source probes/documentary checks establish only their stated observations.

### D27C-R30 Residual monitoring and usability acceptance

O01–O06 define signals, thresholds, owners and responses. Missing authorization, secret-source separation, valid publication, actual content limits or named accountability blocks release; monitoring cannot substitute. Representative staff must find the right public/internal source, distinguish shareable from staff-only, preserve their draft, perform an independently authorized owner handoff, and maintain/withdraw a guide without accidental publication, disclosure or facilitator help. Record observed issues and correct them; no mockup or vendor feature proves the experience perfect.
