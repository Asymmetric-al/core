## Decisions and consequences that a short ratification must not lose

The accepted experience is one quiet private cue plus labelled **Mark read** and **Mark unread** in the existing conversation actions. It adds no unread-count burden, folders, filters, settings, bulk actions, teammate roster, requester receipt or employee-reading analytics. Compact/Full remains presentation, and qualifying a combined conversation does not require scrolling every original source head into view.

Absent personal state means currently authorized other-authored eligible human content is unread on demand, including old or newly permitted history. Empty, excluded-only and self-only sources do not gain automatic unread. Another person's locally admitted human Reply can be eligible while Queued; subsequent acceptance/delivery is not another contribution. Drafts, own posting, system/generated content, edits/redaction, expiry, assignment, merge and delivery callbacks cannot manufacture new unread or acknowledge unseen others.

The durable model is sparse per trusted real staff owner, tenant and immutable original Support source. Source-committed ordering, coherent exact offered snapshots, current visibility/grant coverage and minimal personal intent/incarnation fences preserve arrivals and deliberate choices. No shared-counter relabelling, per-message reader ledger, per-arrival staff fanout, CRM copy or generic authorization-history platform is authorized. Hidden older content becoming newly permitted must not inherit Read; contraction/redaction must not make already covered remaining content unread. Unknown coverage stays honest rather than guessing a cutoff.

Automatic acknowledgment requires a deliberate foreground reader opening and successfully presented latest eligible combined region under an exact authorized source vector. Prefetch, hidden panes, default selection, editor events, malformed empty rendering and notifications alone do not qualify. Older folded/paginated bodies need not all mount. Newly arriving content while actively composing remains unread even when the latest region is visible; an inactive saved draft does not block ordinary reading. Returning to New activity cannot defeat a newer manual-unread epoch. Manual choices use bounded conditional current-state recovery, with no permanent per-click receipt ledger or blind reapplication after ambiguous success.

F02's source-only baseline is mandatory before personal markers for legacy sources: gate relevant writers and marker admission, capture complete already committed eligible content, assign stable internal ordering, and serialize later eligibility above it. It does not claim historical commit order, change source IDs/times/provenance, create new D15/D17 events, mark anybody read or fan out personal records. Actual migration and concurrent-session proof remains required.

Owner/tenant/source authority must hold across grants, SELECT, USING, WITH CHECK, immutable fields, RPCs/views/definer privileges, service paths, caches, tokens and optional Realtime. Raw hidden ordinals and coverage vectors remain server-side. Realtime is optional invalidation; join/JWT policy caching is not per-message authorization or proof of reading. The skill clarifications about implicit WITH CHECK and actual SECURITY DEFINER owner rights remain part of the evidence, not changes to the skills or governing specifications.

Reading-state errors do not block independently authorized drafting, posting, sending, CRM work or actual support handling. State changes do not remount/reset Tiptap, overwrite documents, alter recipients, consume drafts or lose caret/IME/return context. Current silent reader failure requires an explicit safe-render outcome before acknowledgment can be reliable. Privacy includes markers and control metadata, finite owner-defined retention and bounded rejection fences; ordinary successful application/gateway/trace events cannot reconstruct an actor–source–time reading history.
