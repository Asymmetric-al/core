# Automatic Support content expiry after work ends

**Historical disposition: Accept with required amendments. D17 and every adopted amendment are fully founder-ratified, 11 September 2026.** The founder selected A and fully ratified the complete package. D1–D16 remain fully ratified and unchanged. This is a finished grooming decision and required proof, not a formal specification, deployed retention engine or legal-compliance certification.

The [policy and CRM UX](phase26-d17-retention-ux.md), [source/vendor/legal evidence](phase26-d17-evidence.md), [retained-data inventory](phase26-d17-data-inventory.md) and [documentation validation](phase26-d17-validation.json) form the complete record.

## Exact corrected decision

> Support Hub automatically expires ordinary conversation content after its genuine Support purpose has ended and the tenant's approved retention interval has elapsed. A tenant has one explicitly activated finite default for ordinary message bodies and Support-owned files. The administrator reviews the policy's scope, existing-history effects and timing before activation or change. No universal numeric period is preselected.
>
> Expiry removes ordinary access to the affected original content and its governed representations. It does not delete the conversation's permitted structural facts, actual communication evidence, CRM identity, giving records, independent tasks or required official documents. Those records retain their own authorization, purpose and retention policies. CRM and Support show the same current source availability without a copied transcript or a new interaction.
>
> Retention uses genuine work-ending evidence and first durable admission of new native human content, not generic update timestamps or delayed delivery callbacks. Original sources retain their identity through merge, Undo, linking and continuation. A later message starts new retained content; it cannot resurrect expired content. Policy changes and ordinary reopening affect only content whose retention authority has not already ended.
>
> A valid hold can preserve required bytes in restricted owner custody while ordinary Support access still expires. Active-purpose reviews prevent indefinitely Open work becoming an unexamined archive. Actual mandatory disposal and earlier owner ceilings always apply. D17 supplies a narrow routine-expiry eligibility command using D16's restriction, cleanup and restore contracts; it does not broaden the manual redaction tool or build a generic retention platform.

## Required decision clauses

### D17-R01 — Necessity, alternatives and product boundary

A is the chosen ordinary model: preserve context while staff handle the request, then perform predictable disposal without a recurring approval queue for every ordinary item. B, qualified review before routine disposal, is the strongest conservative alternative where continuing value is routinely uncertain, but creates recurrent workload. C, individual message-age expiry during active work, bounds old content more directly but can fragment a legitimate conversation. Neither vendor availability nor an illustrative ministry scenario establishes measured demand, frequency or satisfaction gains.

Support remains a surface within Asym. D17 does not create a second CRM, organization-wide records manager, scanner, public message archive, knowledge base, automatic summary-before-delete feature or enterprise rule builder. It settles ordinary Support retention and its required integration, not a universal time period for all Asym data.

### D17-R02 — One finite tenant default

Provide one tenant-level **Support content retention** policy for ordinary Support bodies, published note content and Support-owned attachment occurrences. No ordinary per-inbox, per-person, per-conversation Never switch, separate attachment clock or arbitrary conditions builder at launch. A different legally governed source class or official artifact keeps its own actual owner policy; it is not an implicit inbox override. Routing, team membership, related Parties and merge topology cannot select a more lenient policy.

The duration field is blank until configured: positive whole-number quantity plus an explicit Days, Months or Years unit. No guessed recommended period, forever, zero, negative, fractional, NaN or infinity. Reject quantities that cannot be represented exactly in the bounded integer contract or whose calculated deadlines overflow the authoritative timestamp type; UI and API share these bounds. The approved owner policy must substantiate the duration and comply with applicable maximums/requirements. This is a maximum ordinary post-purpose content lifetime, not a guaranteed minimum or an extension of shorter P6/P17/source limits.

Live activation of this retention capability requires a qualified finite policy and actual owner inventory. Missing legacy configuration is an owned migration/custody exception, not proof of compliance or permission to lose new intake. Preserve D1 recoverable intake while the responsible owner resolves it; do not silently disconnect a working inbox or auto-delete unclassified history.

### D17-R03 — Policy authority without transcript access

Use Core's current tenant administration capabilities for retention policy management and a separately qualified complete, body-free policy-impact view. The actor, tenant, authorization and activation time derive from trusted server context. Ordinary Support assignees/followers, CRM owners and people with redaction permission do not automatically receive policy publication authority.

A policy administrator may be authorized to govern the whole declared tenant Support scope without reading every transcript. Such authority must be explicit, and aggregate impact access must be qualified as its own administrative purpose. Samples and drill-downs still require current source permission. Never disclose hidden names, subjects, Parties, legal matters or filenames to make an impact preview convenient. A partial personal inbox view cannot be represented as a complete tenant preview. Privileged automation acts as the source owner under the approved policy, not by impersonating the publishing administrator or treating their past body access as permanent authorization.

### D17-R04 — Stable original content and active-purpose eligibility

Retirement binds a bounded content generation/frontier of one original Support source. It never deletes a changing merged-root query result or the entire conversation row. A generation covers all Support-owned content admitted through that frontier, including automatic confirmations and generated/system-note content and their files/representations, with original identities and monotonic admission ordering. Human content is the limited renewal class, not the only content subject to expiry. Typed content-free control facts retain their separate policy. Pre-qualified intake/quarantine material remains with its actual owner and shorter limits. A generation is not a new CRM person or necessarily a new conversation each time content expires.

Normal eligibility requires ended Support work plus absence of a qualified continuing Support need for that original content. D3 Open and both Waiting categories remain unfinished. D10 merged-away status does not prove finished purpose while the duplicate-work component still handles the issue. D12 Continued endings and D11 delegated work require their exact source-interest/obligation checks. A Finance task merely existing, a peer-related conversation, Party link, follower or citation is neither an indefinite hold nor disposal authority. Retain only the explicit source scope genuinely needed by ongoing work; no recursive whole-CRM retention graph.

### D17-R05 — Precise inactivity anchor

For an unexpired content generation, the ordinary anchor is the later of its latest genuine relevant Support-purpose ending and its latest first durable admission of native human content. Count only qualified human incoming content becoming Support-ready, a human reply admitted for sending through the qualified Core command, and a newly published human internal note. Count the event once. Draft typing, unsent preparation alone, automatic confirmations, generated/system notes, note edits, redaction, expiry, delivery callbacks, imports of duplicates, reads, assignments, labels, links, merge/Undo and repeat identical Resolve do not move the anchor.

Do not wait for or use P6 provider acceptance as the retention activity clock: deliberately admitted reply content can exist while delivery is unknown. P6 actual send/delivery evidence stays separate and truthful. Provider/email Date, backdated user text and worker execution time cannot replace first qualified source admission. A genuinely new human note on ended work is new content and updates inactivity; no AI semantic test tries to decide whether it was meaningful. Oldest-content purpose review and mandatory owner ceilings address repeated chatter retaining older context.

If continuing purpose ends later, record that real ending once and use its time; repeated status commands are no-ops for this clock. A status-only correction or reopen–resolve cycle without a new qualified purpose preserves the prior anchor; mechanically cycling status is not new work evidence. If note editing is supported, saving edits keeps that content's existing deadline and must not imply a renewed lifetime. Legacy dates that cannot prove the anchor remain explicitly unknown and require bounded reconciliation, not created_at guessing, a fresh retention lifetime on migration or immediate destruction.

### D17-R06 — Duration arithmetic and displayed dates

Compute from immutable UTC instants with explicit duration semantics. Days mean elapsed 24-hour days added on the instant timeline, including across daylight-saving changes. Months and Years use the policy's frozen IANA timezone, initially taken from the tenant setting and visibly reviewed at activation. Convert the original anchor to that zone, add the whole calendar quantity directly, preserve local time-of-day and clamp an unavailable destination day to that month's last day. Years equals twelve calendar months, not 365 elapsed days. Always calculate directly from the anchor, not by repeatedly adding one month. Thus 31 January in the policy timezone plus two months ends on 31 March in that timezone; 29 February plus one year ends on 28 February in a non-leap year.

Resolve a nonexistent destination local time by shifting forward by the gap; choose the earlier instant if the destination local time occurs twice. Requalify the shared D14 timezone/calculation primitives for these exact calendar operations; business-hours arithmetic alone is not proof. Test the behavior explicitly rather than assuming a library name or its default offset preference. No Temporal dependency or new calendar platform is mandated.

Store quantity/unit, frozen zone, timezone-data/calculator evidence, policy revision, authoritative anchor and materialized UTC deadline. Display exact localized examples and the policy calendar zone. Existing materialized deadlines remain authoritative across timezone-data upgrades; replay/restore must retain sufficient pins/evidence or use the recorded results, not silently recalculate under a new runtime. Adopting another tenant zone or calculator interpretation is an explicit reviewed policy revision with the same E/d rules; viewer or unrelated tenant setting changes do not move deadlines. Use the same qualified arithmetic for purpose-review intervals. Business hours, holidays and staffing calendars do not pause retention. Earlier actual owner obligations may end availability sooner; this policy cannot override them.

### D17-R07 — Purpose review for long-lived content

Use the same configured interval to require an accountable review when the oldest ordinarily available, unexpired content in the current generation has reached that age while ongoing purpose prevents ordinary expiry or repeated new content keeps renewing the inactivity anchor. Exclude already-expired bytes held under D16 restricted custody. This oldest-content review clock is independent of labels, new chatter, reopening loops and last_updated. No new arbitrary secondary period is required.

Maintain one source-scoped review obligation, grouped through existing staff work/records capabilities. The qualified owner records a current continuing need and next review no later than the interval added directly to that review's trusted admission instant, ends actual work when appropriate, or places required evidence with its proper records owner. Policy shortening reevaluates pending reviews without resetting oldest-content age; newly due reviews become due at E. Lengthening cannot erase a prior missed-review fact. A shorter actual owner deadline/review rule wins. Overdue review produces owned attention; it does not auto-Resolve, fabricate lack of need, silently erase active evidence or grant indefinite retention. A mere self-assigned Never/Keep switch is unavailable. Purpose decisions must be attributable and source-specific without pasting the content into the review task.

### D17-R08 — Draft, review and publish policy

Editing produces an inert draft. **Review changes** obtains a server-calculated impact snapshot for the exact tenant scope, candidate duration/calendar basis, current active policy revision and declared immediate effective-time semantics. Display old/new values and any zone/calculator-basis change, existing-plus-future applicability, the fact that some existing content can expire immediately, counts with exact units, exclusions/unknown classes and calculation time. Use original histories, native messages (including source-owned system content) and attachment occurrences as distinct units; current merged rows and unique physical blobs are different units.

The final **Activate policy** or **Apply changes** is approval of that explicit ongoing rule and complete authorized scope, not a per-message selection manifest. Counts are a dated snapshot and may change as work changes; a changing count alone is not a new scope. Revalidate actual eligibility at execution. Policy, scope, authority, calculation semantics or omitted legacy-class changes require fresh review; a failed/incomplete calculation is not zero. No all-target-ID manifest or body download is required just to publish a policy. For unsafe/unclassified scope, block that scope's destructive activation and assign the exception rather than silently omit it.

### D17-R09 — Immediate effective time and existing history

One confirmed publication atomically records an immutable policy version, trusted actor, complete scope, prior-version comparison and authoritative effective instant E. It applies to existing and future **unexpired** ordinary content in that scope; this is stated before confirmation. There is no hidden trash period, automatic seven-day extension, undocumented grandfathering or silent prospective-only exclusion. Content newly overdue under activation/shortening expires at E, never retroactively before the policy existed.

A stale concurrent settings edit conflicts rather than overwrites another published revision. Exact retry returns the original activation receipt and current status. A timeout is reconciled, not resubmitted as a new policy. Old revisions remain immutable evidence. Metadata display may update, but changing a period means publishing a new version. Setting the same effective policy again is a no-op, not a retention reset.

### D17-R10 — Policy changes cannot reverse elapsed authority

Let d be the source's already-authoritative expiry deadline. An authorized policy change or qualifying new work admitted at E strictly before d may affect future eligibility/deadline. At E equal to or after d, the old content has expired even if no worker has yet nulled a body or deleted an object. Process that expired frontier before admitting new content or applying an extension; it cannot be rescued by worker lag, policy lengthening, reopening, an inbox move or Undo.

Conversely, an extension validly effective before d must be honored even when a due-date projection still contains the old deadline. A stale worker must not destroy content using the old version. Evaluate policy-version history, source events and purpose state at the relevant authority boundary; a current-value-only setting plus best-effort mass row updates is insufficient. Current source authority may be computed lazily or projected, but every read/mutation/disposal must qualify it against the actual effective revisions. No giant cross-tenant transaction is required.

### D17-R11 — Bounded pause and operational controls

Provide an exceptional **Pause future expiry until…** to a qualified policy owner, with a finite end instant, content-free reason and impact review. It is an explicit bounded policy revision and cannot override an earlier mandatory disposal or owner safety ceiling. It affects only content not already expired. Normal source access restrictions, accepted cleanup obligations, required physical disposal and incident response continue. There is no permanent Off/Never mode for an active retention policy.

At the approved pause end, recompute against original anchors and current work: still-eligible overdue content expires then, not one new full interval later. The pause review explains this automatic resumption and the expected backlog. An earlier deliberate Resume is a reviewed policy change. Extending a pause requires current authority and another finite reviewed decision; it is not a silent scheduler retry. An infrastructure kill switch that stops work execution is different: it does not extend logical content authority, conceal deadline failures or disable source/restore barriers.

### D17-R12 — Logical expiry before physical cleanup

At the effective deadline, ordinary read, search-membership, download, preparation, export and new dispatch authority for that expired frontier ends. Due workers materialize one content-free expiry receipt, current source restriction and identifier-only owner cleanup obligations through a guarded source transaction. Reads and competing writes also enforce elapsed authority when workers lag. Missing owner/revision proof fails closed for affected content without declaring physical deletion complete.

Use a unique durable effect identity tied to tenant, original source and expired content generation/frontier, not policy revision alone or a cron run. Duplicate/replayed jobs reconcile the same effect and cannot sweep in messages admitted after the frontier. Later automatic/system content cannot create a fresh retention interval on an already-ended expired source: apply its existing source cutoff or independently qualified owner class, retaining only permitted typed facts when payload authority is absent. Atomically preserve expiry/policy evidence and D16 restriction/outbox obligations; perform external cleanup outside the transaction. A transaction failure, lost response or partial downstream failure has a truthful recoverable result. Never restore ordinary content because cleanup failed.

### D17-R13 — Exact retained-data allowlist

The [data inventory](phase26-d17-data-inventory.md) is normative grooming language. Expire Support-owned body text/HTML/editor data, private-note bodies, obsolete source titles/subjects/snippets/summaries, selected-generation file appearances and derived text, unsafe filename/URL/header/address snapshots, parsed intake/MIME copies and affected managed previews/exports. Unknown or free-form fields are content until their owner explicitly classifies and permits a narrower retained fact. No automatic summary or export-before-delete recreates the archive.

Preserve only separately permitted typed facts/control: original/message identities, source lineage/frontiers, actual communication channel/direction/time/outcome and authority references, genuine work transitions, D14 coverage/timing/uncertainty, qualified Party relevance and necessary routing/dedupe/restore barriers. Each has an owner, purpose, access rule and actual retention/anonymization horizon. A skeleton or body-free fact is still potentially identifying; neither is forever by default. Retained structural fields must not secretly contain arbitrary user-authored strings.

Never implement expiry as DELETE conversation or inbox. Current cascades can destroy messages, assignment history and audit. Keep the minimal factual skeleton only while justified; later owner-authorized retirement handles its dependencies and obligations safely. No new money column, amount precision change, CRM synchronization or financial mutation is introduced.

### D17-R16 — Merge, continuation and content generations

A current shared issue can provide an actual purpose dependency; ending/releasing it updates the appropriate original's purpose evidence once. Undo after expiry cannot return old payload, titles, files, tokens or previews.

Native content newly written on continuing B does not become new native content on A merely because A is linked. If B actually needed A's original evidence, its qualified source-purpose interest—not copied text or topology alone—governs that dependency. Owner-record preservation stays distinct.

### D17-R17 — Holds, required records and actual legal limits

A genuine ongoing Support purpose is evaluated separately. Unknown preservation checks block destruction and create an owned exception; they do not authorize unlimited ordinary access or a made-up legal conclusion.

Hold/admission/disposal transitions share current owner guards. A valid hold arriving after logical expiry but before physical disposal can preserve remaining bytes without restoring ordinary use. It cannot recover bytes already destroyed. Before deleting a file that is the sole required record, qualify its proper owner custody; do not assume every receipt-related attachment must be kept or that every attachment is disposable.

Follow D16's US applicability assessment and current primary evidence. No claim that all nonprofits are CCPA-covered/exempt, every private health sentence is HIPAA, every email must be kept seven years, or ordinary expiry completes a formal rights request/incident response. Actual notice, contract, preservation and deletion duties remain owner-qualified. Policy lengthening cannot silently authorize an incompatible new use or override a promised/legal limit. The finite tenant field is configured from those facts; no missing numeric global default remains to be guessed.

## Individual adversarial category review

All severities and likelihoods below assess realistic omission paths, not measured incident rates. Critical means potentially serious confidentiality or irreversible-record loss; High means consequential data/security/work harm; Medium means material usability, maintenance or operating cost. Each concern states its decision effect and exact permanent correction.

**Material concern: Yes. Severity Medium; likelihood plausible.** Manual cleanup can be forgotten; automatic removal can erase context still needed. This **narrows A** to ended-purpose ordinary content, with B/C honestly compared. **Permanent fix/exact language:** R01–R04 require one approved finite default, genuine purpose and no extra platform. P01/P02/P42 verify the actual ordinary journey and scope.
