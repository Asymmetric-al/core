# Q29 — Help discovery for requesters

**13 September 2026. D1–D28 and all adopted amendments are fully ratified. Q29 is unanswered.** This is the next researched product choice, not a new accepted requirement or a reopening of the staff Guidance decision.

## The decision

**When someone opens the tenant's Help entry, how much help discovery should Phase 26 provide alongside direct contact?**

For example, Sarah is on Giving history and cannot find the statement she needs. The tenant has a current public guide explaining where statements are available. Sarah should be able to consult that guide or contact the team immediately, with the permitted context preserved. This is an illustrative donor-care scenario, not a claim that every tenant has that guide or the same statement policy.

D27-C settled how **staff** find public and internal guidance while handling work. It did not add a requester-facing Help finder or public Help center. D1 already requires direct ordinary contact without mandatory searching, article reading, sign-in or a My messages archive. Those promises remain true for all three choices.

## Three actual options

| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Relevant public guidance beside Contact support** **(recommended)** | The existing qualified Help entry offers a small curated set of currently public, discoverable guidance relevant to that entry, with **Contact support** immediately available. Sarah can read the statement guide or go straight to contact. Reuses published Web Studio content and the existing Support intake path. | Tenants must maintain the selected guidance and its applicability. This is a contextual entry, not a full searchable article catalog. It should not pretend to have an answer when none is qualified.       |
| **B — Direct contact, with ordinary public pages kept separate**          | Help opens the existing qualified contact path. Tenants can still publish FAQ/help pages through Web Studio and link them normally, but Phase 26 adds no contextual guidance panel.                                                                                                                 | Fewest new product concepts and maintenance obligations. Strongest simpler alternative when a tenant has little useful help content or most questions require personal review.  | People may contact staff for questions an existing guide could answer, or have to find that guide elsewhere. The direct contact path still needs its complete context, accessibility and recovery behavior. |
| **C — Contextual guides plus a public Help library**                      | Everything in A, plus a tenant-branded destination with topics, article browsing and search over qualified published content. People can open it independently or choose **Browse all help** from contextual Help. Contact remains available throughout.                                            | Supports both immediate task help and broader exploration of a substantial maintained library, without forcing a separate detour.                                               | Adds public organization, search/navigation, locale coverage and ongoing curation. These are alternative Phase 26 scopes, not three modes to build. B does not remove required Help intake or ban normal CMS pages. C does not add a second CMS, private staff-guide access, chatbot, AI search, live chat or a requester conversation archive.

## Single best recommendation: A

A best fits the immediate journey: offer relevant help where someone is already asking for it and keep the human route obvious. The existing staff/public guidance work gives it a sound content foundation, while tenant/site/language and public discovery still need the correct consumer qualification. It keeps the contact experience complete even for tenants with no maintained library.

The strongest argument for B is that empty or stale suggestions can be worse than a straightforward contact form. A should therefore fall back to direct contact without placeholders or a “no articles” obstacle. C includes A’s contextual guides and contact path; it adds an optional library destination rather than replacing that entry. The strongest argument for C is a large, coherent public library used repeatedly by donors, staff or missionaries. That need would justify the extra discovery/maintenance surface; vendor feature availability alone does not establish it for Asym.

[Help Scout's current Beacon modes](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1296-work-with-beacon-modes), updated 3 September 2026, make this tradeoff concrete: Neutral leaves contact accessible while articles are available; Ask First leads with contact. Its forced Self Service mode requires interaction with content before contact appears. Asym should preserve the optional help/contact pattern and reject that mandatory gate under D1. This is evidence of actual product behavior, not measured proof of an Asym satisfaction improvement.

## Boundaries that all choices preserve

- **Correct audience and owner:** only source-qualified public discovery content can be offered to requesters. Internal Staff guides, CRM facts and conversations do not enter the finder. D27 staff search can include Shared by link pages; that does not automatically authorize those pages for public suggestion/search. Public discovery follows Web Studio's Listed/Shared by link intent and current Site/locale/route authority.
- **No forced detour:** contact remains directly reachable without searching, rating an article, logging in or claiming that the guide solved the issue. An article click creates no ticket, completion, feedback invitation or contact preference.
- **Preserved work/context:** using a guide must not lose the contact draft or unnecessarily repeat information. Only explicitly allowed context accompanies a deliberate Support intake; public guidance must not inspect private giving/care details to generate suggestions.
- **Current public content:** unavailable/withdrawn/wrong-locale guidance cannot be filled with an unqualified fallback. Missing guidance leaves contact usable. Reading an article does not authorize an underlying receipt, refund, recurring-gift or identity action.
- **Coherent platform roles:** Web Studio owns public content and public presentation; Support owns accepted work and staff continuation. Email Studio/P17 owns actual governed confirmation/reply wording and preparation, and P6 owns delivery. Opening Help or reading guidance sends no email. D13/D28 triggers remain separate and fully ratified.

The selected answer will receive its complete adversarial review and exact UX/owner/data amendments. This question deliberately does not preselect taxonomy depth, page layout, source schema, article-count limits, search implementation or additional analytics.
