## One deliberate authoring and delivery path

1. **Find and preview.** Staff manually opens Guidance from the current Support conversation, including the same detail reached through CRM. An explicit query and visible Site/language scope use the qualified public owner. No conversation text or CRM information is automatically sent as a search query.
2. **Insert a link.** Support rechecks current source, target draft, purpose, access and caret/selection. The single editor transaction stores frozen human-readable text/href with minimal trusted source/locale/destination provenance. It inserts no Page body and creates no external or workflow effect.
3. **Edit or reuse.** Normal text editing, selected-text linking, Undo/Redo, native copy and recovery preserve the existing known-source rules. D18 can save eligible generic wording containing the link under its actual authoring/publication authority and qualify it at use. Neither a raw pasted attribute nor a client flag creates source approval. Changing href removes the selected-Guidance assertion for the new destination while applicable restrictions on known source-derived material remain. Arbitrarily manually retyped URLs stay ordinary links; the decision does not create a global URL registry or claim universal detection.
4. **Prepare.** For a real external reply, P17 validates the canonical document and current exact public-purpose source qualification, then compiles matching accessible HTML/plain text. It freezes the link bytes, signature, recipient, sender/return route and the complete existing presentation/dependency set. Internal source IDs, safety receipts and authorization tokens are not emitted as email markup. No raw HTML or separately editable HTML/text source becomes authoritative.
5. **Admit and deliver.** Support admits the exact ready preparation under current work/send authority. P6 checks its current qualified local source/material fences at the existing atomic submission boundary and uses the sealed provider request. The prohibition on post-seal network lookup concerns CMS/source/URL resolution; the actual qualified Resend delivery remains P6's responsibility. Provider uncertainty is reconciled against the same effect, never retried as a freshly rendered reply with a new key.

Internal notes use their existing internal document and Support admission path. They do not become external email merely because they contain a public link. Existing independently qualified staff-attention communication remains under its own D12/P6 producer and recipient contract; D27 adds no notification. Likewise, D26 manual work stays usable without an email-ready inbox, requester address or receiving connection. Any later **Start email thread** still follows its explicit recipient branch and D25/P17 email readiness. The private intake brief is not extended into a new Guidance authoring surface or automatically copied into outgoing mail.

No new system-message key, per-inbox Role Layout, Brand Kit, provider template, direct Support mailer, campaign, automatic confirmation, AI runtime or workflow engine is added. D13 automatic confirmation and D24 staged reply-and-work shortcuts retain their own sources and admission rules. A Guidance insertion cannot execute a shortcut or satisfy a D14 reply target.

## Source changes do not all have the same effect

| Event                                                                                                | Exact accepted consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Routine Page title/body edit                                                                         | Future finder results/previews use current public content. Existing wording/href and prepared bytes are not rewritten; Support does not review each Page revision again.                                                                                                                                      |
| Remove from guidance                                                                                 | Stops future finder offers. It does not unpublish the Page or retroactively prohibit otherwise safe copied/D18 wording.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Public route change                                                                                  | An unchanged old href may qualify only through owner-proved same-Page, same-Site, exact-locale continuity. A different-Page redirect may be valid navigation but cannot inherit the previous source's curation approval; deliberate reselection is required for new Guidance use/preparation.                 |
| Ordinary unpublish, loss of public eligibility or destination authority without qualified continuity | Stops new source qualification/preparation and fences the exact definitely-unsubmitted dependent messages under the source owner's no-new-public-use disposition and P17's existing mechanism.                                                                                                                |
| Dangerous privacy/security withdrawal                                                                | Also preserves applicable known-material restrictions. Removing a source marker or replaying Undo cannot declassify known copied material.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Withdrawal races with provider crossing                                                              | Adverse fence first prevents crossing. Submission may have begun first preserves possible success and exact reconciliation; it does not promise recall.                                                                                                                                                       |
| CMS outage or unknown source proof                                                                   | Shows truthful unavailable/checking/partial state and holds the affected new qualification/preparation only. It is not labelled a known unpublish and does not block unrelated Support composition or ordinary mail. Sealed sends use the existing local material contract, not a live CMS availability gate. |
| Qualified source restoration                                                                         | Current safety release/public proof may restore an Active finder entry. Removed entries remain Removed. A safe existing copied link may requalify without restoring its finder entry. Fenced/terminal prepared effects never auto-revive; actual P17 recovery/successor and authorized admission rules apply. |

The narrow new material family must be implemented and qualified at its source and consuming boundaries before activation, including current adverse-first fencing, schema/version compatibility and recovery. This is an explicit accepted owner extension, not a claim that today's Page reader or compiler already implements it. Mixed-version readers cannot silently drop provenance or treat an unknown family as ordinary unrestricted material. A finder kill switch does not switch off existing safety fences. No perpetual domain reservation arises merely because an already sent link may exist in someone else's mailbox.

## Retention and history stay separate

The article and its public revisions remain CMS-owned. Selection history retains minimal owner/actor/transition facts; derived search and previews remain disposable projections. Draft and reusable content follow D18/D23 and their source restrictions. Admitted Support content follows D16/D17. Those classes must not be collapsed into the lifetime of provider-bound delivery material.

P17 sealed preparation and provider-request material exist only for their bounded execution purpose. Exact acceptance with qualified member mapping removes adapter/decrypt authority and starts the existing purge path; later reconciliation uses safe IDs/hashes and body-free evidence. Terminal no-send, exhausted/forbidden retry, the indeterminate idempotency-window boundary or the earlier frozen deadline also retain their existing effects. Immutable content meaning and effect identity do not permit indefinite byte custody.

P17 **Recent sent copy** is a distinct optional, purpose-limited projection and still excludes every URL/action target, remote reference and internal provenance. D27 does not make that projection a searchable or dispatchable archive of Guidance links. The actual Support message can retain eligible link content only under its own finite source policy. Neither current article content nor a saved preview, backup, provider history or old provenance record can reconstruct disposed message content. Ordinary Page edits, link insertion, a CRM association and source restoration do not renew conversation retention.
