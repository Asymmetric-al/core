## Concrete qualification cases to retain

These are concrete readings of already accepted proof groups, not newly adopted product policy:

1. **Optional guide fails while the form is valid:** withdraw one of three guides, lose its current proof, or fail its renderer. Preserve the qualified form and published alternate contact. If the whole public base is unsafe, do not use the guide-only exception to serve another tenant's or stale contact. **R07/R08/R25, P03/P04/P36.**
2. **Acknowledgement enabled but provider unavailable:** the genuine Support Primary Outcome remains independent of the optional child. Persist its already-defined safe outcome/recovery responsibility without saying mail was sent, changing the Primary Outcome or needing a second requester submission. A structurally invalid primary route still cannot report Received. **R13/R16/R17/R25, P18/P27/P36.**
3. **Same requester uses both channels:** two distinct genuine sources may exist; one shared mailbox courtesy guard prevents duplicate automatic receipts. Intent replay does not create another source, but a deliberately separate email is not silently merged. Provider24-hour key retention does not become the product dedupe lifetime. **R15/R18, P17/P26/P28/P29.**
4. **Human reply after unknown acknowledgement submission:** terminate all further acknowledgement call/decrypt authority, including a nominally allowed same-key indeterminate follow-up; reconcile already-in-flight evidence only. Do not revive it when human delivery later fails. **R18/P29.**
5. **First staff reply to an edited app email:** show the actual submitted endpoint; recheck contactability/source without treating self-prefill or linked context as email ownership. Do not echo protected context or perform the requested business action merely because the form mentions it. No fake incoming RFC record or CRM preferred-address substitution. **R11/R12/R14/R20, P12/P15/P25/P33.**
6. **Route/template/locale changes after receipt:** recover the same accepted primary source and original clocks; new public/app release affects future occurrences through its owner. Already sealed effects keep immutable material, while current adverse fences still stop unsafe unsubmitted work. No late renderer or new idempotency key. **R10/R13/R15/R18/R28, P16/P17/P29/P35/P43.**
7. **Restore after content disposal:** preserve minimal authorized control/evidence and suppress revoked body/token/material resurrection before ordinary serve/retry. Neither occurrence recovery nor Template used preview is a removed-content archive. **R22/R28, P27/P34/P43.**

## Email Studio seam remains sufficient

The requester form itself stays a native plain-text semantic form. It does not require Tiptap, an email editor, account creation, a marketing opt-in or a prior guide interaction. Staff-rich authoring continues through its already qualified Tiptap profiles. P23 owns contact definition/occurrence/route; Support owns genuine form requester content and work; P17 qualifies/authors/freezes actual messages; P6 alone submits/reconciles them; P3/source authority supplies permitted contactability. No browser-controlled template, sender, header, recipient, Reply-To or purpose is accepted.

The contact display address and optional phone are separately tenant-published monitored contact facts, not P23 Verified Email Destination authority and not private administrator/profile data. A click proves neither receipt nor delivery/call completion. The same canonical source appears through authorized CRM navigation without a duplicate note/body/Activity/last-contact record. Browser receipt, auto acknowledgement and guide use satisfy neither D14 human response nor D28 feedback eligibility; an actual qualified human public reply remains required.
