The recommended audience is one clearly labelled **Support staff** audience for generic operational instructions. This means current readers of the Support capability in the exact active tenant, through P12—not every tenant staff account, administrator role, CRM reader or holder of an inbox email address. No demonstrated requirement justifies a new per-guide ACL builder. Inbox categories or applicability are not access grants. If a subject needs restricted knowledge, its actual protected owner and existing qualification remain authoritative; the common Staff guide is not the place to copy that material.

This is a deliberate product boundary, not a claim that all real ministry knowledge can safely be shared with all Support workers. General procedures can explain how to request a Finance action without containing private gift data, credentials, restricted identities or case details. Source permissions still decide who can perform the action. A guide's internal label alone is neither proof of safe prose nor a clearance grant.

## Material findings and exact proposed clauses

Severity/likelihood below are conditional engineering judgments, not measured incidents. Each clause narrows or changes the original public-only decision while preserving its public-owner safeguards.

### DC01 — Close the source discriminant and ownership

**Material concern: Yes. High / High.** A polymorphic URL field, private CMS query or Saved Section masquerading as a guide creates competing truth and permits public/internal confusion. Evidence S01/S04/G01/G02/G05.

> Guidance SHALL consume a closed source union: qualified public Page references under D27's existing CMS/public owner, and operational Staff guides under Support. Staff guide identity/content, exact locale variants, draft/publication heads, archive and safety state are owned by Support through `packages/api`. Do not reuse a Page ID, public URL, Saved Section ID, inbox name or role as Staff guide identity. No CMS audience toggle, copied public-article archive, internal body in the public reader, or live message-fragment graph is added.

**Proof:** equal numeric/string/slug-like source values across families and tenants cannot collide; requesting an internal source through a public endpoint fails uniformly; deleting a CMS Page does not cascade into Staff guides; a common UI cannot switch source interpretation by client metadata.

### DC02 — One explicit common audience, with actual capability boundaries

**Material concern: Yes. High / High.** “Internal” is easily implemented as all staff/admin or reused inbox membership. Publishing into a common audience may exceed an inbox-scoped curator's authority. G03/G04; S02 shows why current role checks are insufficient.

> The common Staff guide audience SHALL be displayed as Support staff and enforced by the current P12 active-tenant Support-reader capability plus applicable classification/purpose floor. Tenant-wide Support content authoring/publication authority is required to create/edit/release common guides; inbox-only curation or CMS publishing does not confer it. Draft read/edit, release, archive/restore and safety administration remain separately authorized actions under the existing capability registry. Role names and same-email identities authorize none of them. No per-guide user/group ACL or inbox-scoped publication mode is introduced by this revision.

This does not require a mandatory second approver for ordinary guidance: a person with the existing qualified edit and publish authorities may perform both unless an applicable existing policy requires separation. Tenant oversight does not imply unrestricted private-case access.

**Proof:** authorized Support reader can read current guides; tenant staff without Support read, CRM-only reader, unrelated CMS editor, unauthenticated caller and other-tenant member cannot; inbox-only curator cannot publish common guidance; current role/grant loss wins over stale UI/JWT metadata.

### DC03 — Purpose and classification are explicit, not magical content detection

**Material concern: Yes. Critical / Medium.** Private conversation or restricted identity data could be copied into a broadly readable guide and then enter indexes/logs. G04 and D16/D17/D18 govern known-source restrictions.

> Staff guides SHALL contain reusable, non-case-specific instructions appropriate for the common Support staff audience. Do not automatically create guides from conversations, CRM records, attachments, staff notes or private CMS drafts. Known typed/copied material retains its owning classification and restrictions; a source that cannot be disclosed to the guide's complete audience cannot be published there. Guide title, summary, body, history, filenames and source metadata receive classification and current authorization, not only the body. Credentials, protected case records and restricted identities stay with their owning domains.

Human-authored prose still requires accountable content review and a correction/withdrawal path. No schema, checkbox or absent AI warning proves that arbitrary text contains no sensitive information. Do not add automatic AI mining, secret scanning or a generic DLP platform under this decision.

**Proof:** known restricted source/copy cannot be relabelled internal and published; source withdrawal wins over cached derived text; raw queries/body do not appear in telemetry; current classifiers/floors cannot be bypassed through search predicates or preview.

### DC04 — Save is not Publish to staff

**Material concern: Yes. High / High.** Autosave-live edits, mutable published bodies or concurrent replacement can expose unfinished instructions and make it impossible to know what staff read. S01 has no actual lifecycle; G05 provides useful immutable-revision precedent without transferring owner identity.

> A Staff guide SHALL have a stable tenant-owned identity, exact independently authored locale variants, a private working candidate and at most one current published revision per variant. Saving the candidate never changes reader content. Publish to staff reviews and validates the exact candidate, then atomically advances its published head with trusted author/publisher/time, expected candidate/head revisions, audit, receipt and required derived-data work. Published revisions are immutable. Another editor's newer work cannot be silently overwritten. Readers continue seeing the prior qualified publication while a newer candidate is edited.

Use the existing Tiptap/common authoring primitives with a bounded Staff guide document profile. Do not convert CMS articles or P17 message publications into this source. A new role enum, enterprise editorial workflow, collaboration server or general workflow engine is unnecessary.

**Proof:** autosave versus reader view; concurrent edits/publishes; publish after permission loss; client-forged published pointer; validation failure leaves prior good publication; lost response resolves one exact receipt and never double-publishes.

### DC05 — Withdrawal, archive and restoration cannot silently revive instructions

**Material concern: Yes. High / Medium.** A restored database/candidate or old publish job can resurrect obsolete or restricted guidance; deletion may destroy useful control evidence.

> Ordinary Archive SHALL stop normal Staff guide discovery and current instruction serving while retaining only permitted authoring/history evidence under its owner schedule. Qualified managers can inspect permitted history; ordinary readers receive an appropriate unavailable presentation without a sensitive existence oracle. Restore returns to a currently authorized reviewable candidate; it does not silently republish an old revision. Safety restriction is independent, immediate for all current serving and known derivatives, and cannot be removed by ordinary Archive/Restore or stale events. Do not automatically fall back to an older publication after a withdrawal.

This is distinct from D27 public-reference removal: removing a selected public Page does not unpublish that Page or revoke otherwise safe copied public links. A Staff guide is an owned instruction source, not merely a selection of an independently live public source.

**Proof:** archive while another tab edits; stale Publish after Archive; archive/restore across locale variants; dangerous withdrawal with a warm cache; ordinary restore cannot release safety restriction; author departure does not delete shared history.

### DC06 — Database invariants and trusted mutations

**Material concern: Yes. Critical / High if S03 is copied.** Broad CRUD can rewrite release state, actor attribution, tenant ownership or audit. A valid old row can be updated into an invalid scope.

> Operational guide, variant, revision, draft, head and control relationships SHALL use actual owner-compatible IDs, non-null tenant qualification and composite tenant-aware keys/FKs where relationships are local. Enforce one logical variant per guide/exact locale, one current published head referencing a revision of that same tenant/guide/locale, valid states, immutable accepted revision/control attribution and restricted delete behavior. Actor, tenant, audience, publisher, accepted time and safety fields derive from trusted server context. No ordinary raw UPDATE can publish, change source/tenant, erase audit or widen scope. Staff departure cannot cascade-delete tenant-owned guidance.

Verify table/column grants, effective RLS SELECT/USING/WITH CHECK, views, function/RPC execute authority/search paths, service-role paths and storage together. PostgreSQL can reuse USING as the effective WITH CHECK when omitted; omission alone is not a vulnerability. Multiple permissive policies combine with OR, so a new narrower policy cannot repair retained broad staff CRUD by itself. Public schema storage does not mean public audience. P12 owner predicates govern; no new sibling PDP.

**Proof:** positive/negative direct Data API and command tests; forged tenant/actor/publisher; wrong-guide or wrong-locale revision pointer; client audit mutation; service path missing current authority; hidden-target uniqueness/FK errors do not disclose protected data.

### DC07 — Exact language meaning without publication coupling to Sites

**Material concern: Yes. Medium / Medium.** Reusing public Site locale as internal audience or copying translations automatically can change meaning and create needless website dependencies.

> Each Staff guide variant SHALL name its actual content language using the qualified shared locale vocabulary. Publication of one variant does not publish or create others; no inferred translation or silent language substitution. Internal guide identity does not require a public Site, domain, DNS state or public locale release. UI locale and content language remain distinct. Show available permitted variants deliberately; source edits do not claim translated variants were reviewed or updated.

**Proof:** public Site outage does not disable an independent internal guide; exact Thai/Spanish/English variants remain distinct; switching app locale never changes the selected source silently; missing variant is not silently replaced with a default.

### DC08 — One finder, two qualified search populations

**Material concern: Yes. Critical / High.** Internal text can leak into the public index or disappear behind a public API's population/row limit. ACL filtering after ranking/counts leaks existence and omits valid matches.

> The Internal finder SHALL search current eligible Staff guide titles, summaries and published body text through the Support owner, over the complete currently authorized population before ranking, snippets, counts or pagination. Drafts, superseded versions, archived/restricted guides and other tenants are excluded. Internal search documents remain derived and inaccessible through public search. Reuse proven Postgres/full-text and normalization primitives where appropriate; do not create a new search vendor, crawler or vector/AI corpus. Public Guidance preserves all D27 exact-source/Shared-by-link/public-discovery rules independently.

Use separate visible Public/Internal source choices rather than mixing incomparable ranks or having a mode label silently change the email purpose. Source owners can share a common result DTO with a required source discriminant, but never a common permissive reader. Internal publication can directly curate its guide into the Internal list; do not add a redundant internal selection switch on top of Publish to staff.

**Proof:** body-only result beyond every API cap; no private count/snippet in Public mode; hidden draft title absent from suggestions; deterministic cursor/ties; no N+1 source checks; Unicode and declared language semantics; incomplete coverage says incomplete, never false zero.

### DC09 — Current serving and derived-data repair

**Material concern: Yes. High / High.** A stale cache, delayed search update or old client response can display withdrawn instructions after permission loss.

> Current source head/state, tenant, reader-purpose authority and applicable safety/governance epoch SHALL qualify every favorable guide list/detail/preview/search response. Cache keys and cursors bind their actual source family/scope/locale/current-control semantics. Invalidating a tag is not authorization. Adverse control wins before derived cleanup; stale favorable updates cannot restore it. In the local Staff guide owner, prefer indexing the current qualified published revision within its own bounded write/query model; no new background queue is required merely to copy guide text into another authority. When asynchronous derivation is used, persist and reconcile its continuation under existing infrastructure.

Do not persist internal guide bodies or queries in unprotected browser storage, public caches, URL query parameters, analytics or error reports. A delayed response from a prior tenant/source tab cannot replace the current authorized result. Unknown authority is unavailable, not a stale-success fallback.

**Proof:** worker/cache lag, tenant switch, grants-epoch change, stale cursor and source restore; archived body absent before physical purge; source-specific failure does not block the other family or ordinary Support work.

### DC10 — Consultation is not an external disclosure action

**Material concern: Yes. Critical / High.** S01's “attach to replies” wording would turn internal guidance into public message material. D18/P17 source restrictions and G04 remain governing.

> Internal Guidance SHALL be for staff consultation and preserve the existing conversation/draft/mode. It provides no article-body Insert into reply, public sharing action, public link token, Copy article into reply or Internal-to-Public toggle. It must not inject internal title/body/URL/provenance into external email, signatures, Reply Saved Sections or public CMS content. The P17/external validator rejects known internal-reference material regardless of UI mode, Undo or client metadata. The public Guidance branch retains its existing deliberate link insertion.

A typed **internal-note reference** can be qualified as a narrow extension if the root synthesis chooses it: it must be structurally internal-only, carry source identity rather than copied body, resolve current authorized metadata on read, and remain rejected by external serialization and Reply conversion. No copied guide text or grant comes from the note. Without that complete contract, consultation alone is sufficient to fulfill “find internal guidance”; do not pretend a plain URL supplies the safeguards. This report does not decide that optional insertion action for the founder independently.

**Proof:** Reply/Internal note switching, Undo, saved wording, clipboard metadata, old serializers and export cannot make known internal material external; browser manual retyping remains outside any claim of universal prevention. Public-page links continue to work.

### DC11 — CRM, source links and actual actions retain their owners

**Material concern: Yes. High / Medium.** A guide might become proof of refund authority, a second CRM note history or a way to obtain restricted record context.

> Guidance through CRM SHALL use D9/D26's same currently authorized Support detail and current guide authorization. Reading guidance creates no Party/link/merge, Activity, last contact, assignment, work completion or giving action. An ordinary instruction can describe the existing Finance/CRM workflow; opening or performing it reauthorizes through its owner. Embedded safe resource links confer no access. Do not copy real case/financial/member-care content into a guide to avoid those checks or rebuild that context inside the guide reader.

**Proof:** CRM-only viewer denied internal guidance as appropriate; Support reader without refund authority can read a generic process but cannot refund; unknown/shared-email requester has no inferred Party; same guide through CRM and Support has one source/history.

### DC12 — Retention, attribution and minimal evidence

**Material concern: Yes. High / Medium.** Retaining every body version/query forever or applying conversation expiry to generic guides creates wrong custody and restore behavior.

> Active generic Staff guides, working candidates, superseded/archived revisions, control/audit receipts and derived previews/indexes SHALL have declared owner purposes and finite inactive-data schedules before activation. D17 conversation expiry does not become the guide's storage clock, and guide lookup does not renew any conversation lifetime. Preserve only justified actor/control/version evidence; no raw before/after body or query archive in audit. Deletion, redaction/withdrawal, account departure, exports and restore respect their actual source/records authority and current barriers. Immutability is not indefinite readable retention.

Use existing records infrastructure where qualified; do not copy a generated-document numerical schedule or build another records product. Safe attribution uses actual stable authenticated identity and permitted display projection, not mutable email or legal-name fallback.

**Proof:** editor departure, removed membership, expired revision, body-free receipt reconciliation, backup restore after withdrawal, no rehydration from search/preview/audit, valid finite export custody without public release.

### DC13 — Additive migration and clear prototype retirement

**Material concern: Yes. High / High.** Old fixture screens, raw CRUD and public-reader fallbacks can survive behind a new polished UI; rollback may silently drop the new internal-only source discriminant.

> Activate C only after its operational source, P12 grants/floor, draft/release lifecycle, complete private search and safe UI/serializer paths are qualified. Replace or retire the fixture-backed Support Knowledge source and its internal-playbook-to-reply claim. Do not seed live guides from fixture text, all CMS drafts, case notes or P17 sources. Any evidenced import creates an inactive reviewed candidate with exact source restrictions, never automatic common publication. Unknown source/document versions fail safely; old writers cannot drop internal provenance. A family-specific kill switch stops new internal use without taking down qualified public Guidance or normal Support.

Migrate operational and Payload schemas only through their own owners; there is no Payload migration for the new operational source merely because a public finder shares its UI. Roll forward after new source records exist unless old code is proven compatible with the same access/safety rules.

**Proof:** old writer/new source and new reader/old schema; unsupported copied reference; feature disabled while old route still exists; deterministic nonpublishing import; failed activation preserves existing public Guidance and D1–D26 work.

Numerical capacity/latency/custody budgets must be named and tested in the authorized design/release plan, not inferred from vendor examples.

All database/RLS/PDP, concurrency, search, migration, browser, assistive-technology and staff-usability proofs remain unexecuted. Source pins and this independent review establish the present evidence and proposed permanent contracts only.
