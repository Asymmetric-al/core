# D27 — Ratified Guidance and Email Studio integration

**Fully founder-ratified, 12 September 2026.** This consolidates the accepted seam from D27-R01–R30 and the existing D18/D23/D26/P17/P6 contracts. It does not add a new decision, email provider, renderer, template system, source owner or runtime implementation. The [full ratified review](phase26-d27-adversarial-review.md), [data contract](phase26-d27-data-contract.md) and [independent seam check](phase26-d27-email-studio-seam-review.md) are read together.

Staff can start from a Support conversation or open the same conversation from the CRM's authorized Support view. They deliberately open Guidance, search within current public selections, inspect a safe passive preview, choose Insert link or Link selected text, then return to their unchanged reply or internal note. Search/preview/insertion does not change recipients, purpose, signature, assignment, work status or the meaning of a pending business action.

Internal notes remain structurally internal and never enter P6 because they contain a public link. Staff-recorded requests without email remain valid D26 work; Guidance does not invent an inbound sender, recipient or external message. Starting email later keeps D26's explicit audience and P17/P6 qualification.

## Source qualification and representation

The source must have an exact qualified tenant/Site/Page/locale identity, current public-generation/safety and public-purpose destination. A published boolean, editor preview URL, matching title, successful HTTP request or staff CMS permission is insufficient. Current develop's null-Site mapper and mutable Page lookup are known implementation gaps, not an alternate permanent contract.

A selected Shared-by-link public Page can participate in the staff finder without entering public search or sitemap. Its eligible body text must be searchable in the complete staff-purpose selected corpus through the shared owner. The preview shows only safe public projection content and permitted media; no form, giving widget, script or embed executes inside the staff session.

A Guidance link contains frozen visible text and destination plus the minimal trusted occurrence/source qualification needed by the existing document and preparation contract. It contains no copied article body, private CRM context, recipient identity or administrative token. External HTML/plain text renders an ordinary accessible link. The public source may change later; historical message content is not rebuilt from its latest title or body.

## Tiptap, reusable replies and edit behavior

At a caret, Insert link adds the source's readable title. For ordinary selected text, Link selected text preserves staff wording. Stale or protected selections require a valid insertion position rather than silent replacement or insertion into another draft. One insertion is one normal undoable editor action, not an implicit send or work shortcut.

Text edits retain applicable source provenance. Destination edits remove the assertion that the new URL is the selected Guidance source, while known source-derived material restrictions survive under D18/D23 rules. Client HTML attributes cannot manufacture approval. Undo/Redo, copy, saved replies, native paste and draft recovery must not strip known safety restrictions. Arbitrary manually retyped URLs remain ordinary links; D27 does not promise global URL policing.

Saving a Guidance link inside existing reusable wording follows that owner's current private/shared and purpose rules. No new Guidance-specific template library, inbox folder hierarchy or source-edit workflow is introduced. Routine source editing does not require a new Support approval for every revision.

Late favorable events cannot override current source/selection/safety generations. Removing a picker item and unpublishing a Page must be different visible actions with consequences owned by their respective surface. Guidance does not claim control over a public URL's lifetime after delivery.

## Preparation, admission and provider crossing

1. Support qualifies the chosen source and current draft/purpose/access before insertion. Source provenance is admitted through a trusted boundary, not caller-supplied assertions.
2. P17 qualifies the actual source occurrence, destination, audience/sender/signature and rendering when preparing the whole reply. Its existing preparation owns the minimal Guidance source-material dependency and current safety relationship. There is no second Guidance preparation or signature compiler.
3. Support admits that exact prepared reply with current work/audience/actor authorization. Existing ordinary D4/D26 reply and work-intent semantics remain unchanged.
4. P6 checks current qualified local material/safety fences at the existing atomic submission boundary. If the adverse fence wins while definitely unsubmitted, do not submit. If submission may have occurred first, reconcile the exact effect/attempt; do not recreate the message, rerender, rekey or retry as a new send.
5. Dispatch never fetches a CMS Page, follows a URL, selects a replacement source or rebuilds a document after sealing. Failure of optional Guidance lookup does not make all Support unavailable.

The owner-qualified local safety contract must converge adverse source dispositions before new public-use effects can cross its authoritative fence. Merely deleting an eventually consistent search row is not a sending safeguard. This extends existing P17/P23 mechanisms narrowly; it does not add a generic content-revocation platform or second queue.

## Retention, attribution and proof

Immutable content meaning and effect identity do not authorize indefinite byte custody. P17 retains its qualified-acceptance purge/finality rule for sealed provider-request material and uses body-free evidence for later reconciliation. Actual draft/message/source/audit copies each follow their existing owner retention. D17 cannot be bypassed through a Guidance occurrence, cached preview, export or source-history reconstruction.

P17's optional **Recent sent copy** is a separate projection that continues to exclude URLs/action targets, remote references and internal provenance. It does not become an archive or resend source for Guidance links. Eligible links in the actual Support message follow that message's own finite retention. Source edits, link insertion, CRM association or source restoration do not restart the conversation's retention clock. Separately qualified staff attention remains an existing owner contract; Guidance consultation itself creates no new notification event.

Curation records trusted actor, time, exact source, transition/generation and receipt without copied public article or private conversation bodies. Operational selection commands use constrained tenant-aware relationships, current capabilities, expected generation and durable idempotency receipts. Public/CMS soft references do not become hard cross-schema SQL ownership. Technical traces use safe IDs and dispositions; they do not require raw private queries, rendered messages or secret URLs.
