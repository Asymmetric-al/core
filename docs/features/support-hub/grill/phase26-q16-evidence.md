## Independent question challenge incorporated

1. Select a substantive unchosen behavior rather than only relocating the already-required quarantine queue.
2. Offer A/B/C as different ordinary correction postures; preserve mandatory containment, owner retention, holds and irreversible erasure under all.
3. Keep B strong: whole affected body or file redaction is simpler and can preserve the rest of the conversation, unlike deleting a ticket/person.
4. Bound C to permitted retained originals, explicitly label masking as non-erasure and early-access evidence as emerging. Do not turn accidental passwords into a revealable secret vault.
5. Use an unrelated private paragraph/wrong attachment example so the options are not framed as permission to retain prohibited secrets.
6. Preserve source-aware cleanup through CRM, notifications, prepared replies, search and merge/Undo; a visual overlay is insufficient.
7. Distinguish pre-commit Cancel/Unmark from irreversible completed removal, and distinguish managed cleanup evidence from external-copy recall.
8. Defer exact permission roles, mapping/cleanup mechanisms and retention numbers to the selected answer's complete research/review; no unchosen schema, legal conclusion, AI scanner or platform DSAR project is introduced.
