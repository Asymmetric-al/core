# Conversation text and attachment-filename search

**D22 and every adopted amendment are fully founder-ratified, 12 September 2026. Historical disposition: Accept with required amendments.** D1–D21 remain fully ratified. The complete accepted package and explicit Email Studio seam are recorded below as grooming authority; no formal specification or implementation is claimed.

Support search should find the actual discussion and explain its matches through a compact, familiar interface. It must not search every readable object indiscriminately, concatenate unrelated records into a synthetic match, or preserve sensitive copies beyond their source authority. The permanent path is one source-qualified keyword-search contract, original-source identity/revision, complete server queries and a small set of existing filters. Future search consumes that boundary after separate qualification; no AI, vectors or attachment extraction is built now.

The [UX](phase26-d22-search-ux.md), [source/query/lifecycle contract](phase26-d22-data-contract.md), [evidence and independent synthesis](phase26-d22-evidence.md), three independent reviews and [validation](phase26-d22-validation.json) form the full record.

## Corrected decision clauses

### D22-R01 — Closed current coverage and explicit future intent

Provide keyword search over eligible Support conversation details, admitted message and internal-note text, and attachment filenames. Details include permitted Support references/IDs, subjects and Support-owned requester/participant display details; a qualified D12 staff brief is Staff context. Include admitted human and automated messages only with their truthful provenance. Search no attachment contents, OCR, image/audio interpretation, semantic/AI answers, embeddings, external AI provider or global CRM corpus now. The founder's future broader/AI intent is recorded, not activated. Do not add disabled AI chrome, speculative vector columns or a parallel future-search framework.

### D22-R02 — Index eligibility is separate from read permission

A source must be durably admitted, currently available, allowed to be indexed for this Support purpose, and currently readable by the actor under the exact source/field scope. Phase10 restricted-tier exclusions and P9 member-care/finance-provenance indexing exclusions are governing floors. A clearance-holder's direct read is not permission to index that material or scan it through a fallback. Apply policy classifications, not a guessed sensitive-keyword blacklist that rejects ordinary donor-care discussion. Private drafts, unadmitted prepared candidates, raw headers/MIME/provider evidence, D19-held intake, Email Studio reusable-library sources and P6/P17 Recent copies are excluded. ADR0031 expressly forbids turning Recent copies into a search corpus. No source means no fallback to an older readable copy.

### D22-R03 — Truthful typed source items

Search one original source item at a time: one subject/reference/detail field, one allowed participant-name or address field, one admitted message, one internal note, one Staff context brief, or one attachment-name item. Keep stable tenant/original-conversation/source-kind/source-ID/revision and current owner availability. An admitted queued, failed or uncertain outgoing reply can be eligible but is never called Sent/Delivered without P6 evidence. Internal note and Automated message remain distinct from human correspondence. Staff context may exist with zero messages; its citations do not recursively index the cited conversation. Label names are already covered by D21's explicit label filter, not a reason to copy catalog/CRM fields into every source item.

### D22-R04 — Plain literal matching with phrases

The new Support text predicate requires every entered unquoted fragment and quoted phrase within the same eligible source item; ordinary unquoted fragments may appear in any order. A fragment matches literally within normalized visible text, including partial words: receipt can match receipts. Preserve punctuation and stopwords; no stemming, accent stripping, spelling correction, synonym expansion, implicit OR/NOT, regex or wildcard language. Names/addresses in another field cannot supply a missing query fragment. Use a separate qualified Person or email filter with content search when staff want that explicit cross-field condition.

Normalize source/query text through the same versioned Unicode NFC, full casefold and whitespace rules; preserve a map back to displayed source text. Straight double quotes and paired typographic double quotes delimit an exact phrase after whitespace normalization. Apostrophes are literal. The companion contract defines escaping, incomplete quotes and literal operators. Invalid syntax is an actionable input error, not No matches or a silently broadened query. Query bounds are 256 Unicode scalar values after outer trim, at most 16 nonempty fragments/phrases and at most 1,024 UTF-8 bytes; these are explicit product bounds, not a vendor optimum. A nonblank single-character query is valid but may need narrower scope under the server query budget. Never drop short/stopword-only queries silently.

### D22-R05 — Match only source-visible text, without invented adjacency

Use the owning domain's qualified visible-text projection, not JSON-stringified message bodies, raw HTML attributes, scripts/styles, hidden transport fields or a merged transcript. Current permitted quoted content within a message may match under that source's own custody; repetition does not increase relevance. Redacted/hidden/expired spans create hard boundaries: a literal fragment or phrase cannot cross them. Unquoted fragments may occur in separate eligible visible runs of the same source item, but no excluded text contributes. Rendered placeholders such as Content removed are UI state, not searchable source text. Chunking may accelerate candidate retrieval but cannot change item/phrase boundaries or lose a match across a storage chunk boundary.

### D22-R06 — Preserve visible context and simple filters

Retain the existing inbox/view/CRM/report scope when searching. Keep Status, Assignee and Labels as compact familiar controls; other already-qualified D20 Following/reply-attention criteria remain under Filters. Add a compact Search in refinement for All searchable content, Details, Messages, Internal notes, Staff context or Attachment names; All is the ordinary new-search coverage. Person or email narrows permitted Support participant/detail facts, not an arbitrary CRM property builder. Show active scope/refinement chips; use D20 AND-across-facets and D21 Any/All/No labels semantics. No second filter builder, date wizard or raw operator syntax is required. Do not import D21 report dates into ordinary root createdAt filtering.

In a Support list, **Search all my accessible Support conversations** is an explicit action: keep entered query and Search in; remove ordinary inbox/view/status/assignee/label/person/following/attention filters, clearly describing that consequence. Permission/source exclusions remain. In a CRM record or report, broader search first uses explicit Open in Support Hub with preserved return context; never silently remove the anchor or exact report predicate. An exact reference obeys the same visible scope—do not jump to an out-of-scope result automatically.

### D22-R07 — Predictable updates, sorting and clearing

Retain a deliberate existing saved/user sort. A new unconfigured text search uses Best match: exact full Support reference/ID matches first, then eligible matches by original source admission/creation time descending, unknown time last, with deterministic source/root-ID ties. The best eligible source item represents each continuing conversation; no popularity/frequency or unavailable-source ranking contribution. Explain the simple ordering; it is not semantic AI relevance. A query-local automatic search order does not overwrite the underlying queue/view sort. Clearing text restores that underlying order unless the user deliberately changed it, while retaining current filters. Clear search, Clear filters and D20 Reset view remain distinct.

Use the existing 200ms input debounce as an initial interaction setting, with cancellation/generation checks, IME-composition handling and Enter/Search to run the current valid query immediately. This is a tuning choice, not a latency promise. Distinguish input being edited, the executed query and displayed results. New results cannot move focus, substitute the selected conversation or retarget an action under a pointer. Show quiet Updating/Results changed treatment; preserve the opened conversation and draft. Blank query returns the qualified filtered list; malformed/over-budget input does not silently run blank All.

With blank text, Search in retains its preference but is dormant: it is not a has-message/note/file filter. Clear filters resets Search in to All searchable content and removes ordinary status/assignee/label/person/following/attention refinements while retaining text, current inbox boundary, the active view's identity as Modified where applicable, and mandatory CRM/report context. Reset view remains the distinct D20 command. The exact-reference ranking tier applies only when the entire parsed query is one atom equal to a complete permitted reference/ID; one fragment inside a larger query does not receive that tier.

### D22-R08 — Explain the result and open the right source

Show one authorized continuing-conversation row under D10, with current subject/handler/work context and a concise matching excerpt or filename, source kind and truthful source time/state. Never show inaccessible-match counts or synthetic text assembled across sources. A best-match preview is a navigation aid, not a new Support message. Opening a result targets the exact original source/revision within the current allowed topology, preserves list position/query/return context and rechecks current access. Filename matches locate attachment metadata; they never download/open bytes automatically. If a match changed, was removed or became forbidden, show current source-safe unavailability and refresh options, not the old excerpt or a nearby unrelated match presented as the same evidence.

### D22-R09 — Find within a conversation

Provide a visible Find in conversation entry and qualified keyboard shortcut without hijacking typing or ordinary browser shortcuts. It uses the same literal/phrase and owner-eligibility contract, limited to current permitted original sources in that conversation/context. Match navigation reaches eligible retained messages beyond the mounted page; DOM-only find is not the product's whole-conversation feature. Show current match position/next/previous only when that count is complete; preserve access and source revision at navigation. A CRM-anchored Find stays limited to its qualified source basis; opening full Support handling is explicit. Index-forbidden sources remain excluded even if directly readable elsewhere.

Empty Find clears highlights and disables match navigation; it does not treat every item as a match.

### D22-R10 — Source authorization before matching and aggregation

Trusted server context resolves tenant, actual actor/profile, current membership and purpose. Apply the D9 CRM/source basis or report constraint, indexing eligibility, current source revision and field/attachment-metadata authority before any source can contribute a hit, snippet, rank, facet, aggregate or count. Seeing a conversation shell, assignment, follow or related Party grants none of these. Establish valid contributing source hits first, then D10 continuing-root projection/deduplication, ordering and pagination. Return no raw body/index rows to the browser for filtering. Searchability never proves identity, representation, receipt issuance, consent or completion of a giving/care action.

### D22-R11 — One source-owned derived projection

Asym Support owns original conversation/source truth; its search projection is rebuildable and has no independent write, retention or authorization power. Extend the qualified shared API/list/named-query machinery with a closed Support search subject and versioned source/query contract. Use Asym PostgreSQL candidate retrieval and exact verification behind that boundary; qualify the P9 pg_trgm approach for this subject where suitable. P9's CRM body-search deferral and Cmd-K budgets remain CRM-specific; no global CRM body search is enabled. Current intended pg_trgm architecture is not proof that an extension/index is already deployed. No new external engine, full taxonomy/search platform or provider dependency is required for D22.

### D22-R12 — Database, RLS and privileged paths

Projection identity and references are tenant-aware and source-kind constrained, with immutable original endpoints, source/projection/schema revisions, purpose/eligibility state and minimal source ordering/freshness facts. Derive actor, tenant, source admission and eligibility from trusted owners, not a caller body. Enforce same-tenant relationships and valid non-null states/revisions through constraints and one qualified projection writer. If heterogeneous sources need a tagged reference, each allowed source kind must have a verified endpoint-integrity boundary; no unconstrained arbitrary record_type/id reference or polymorphic data leak.

Projection text is private server-side derived content; revoke unneeded raw anonymous/authenticated reads and writes. Prove query RPC/view/function execution grants, invoker/definer choice, fixed search_path, USING and WITH CHECK, table owner/BYPASSRLS, service-role/worker and raw Data API paths. Existing broad staff message reads are not a safe substitute. Parameterize queries and escape LIKE metacharacters when LIKE is used; no caller SQL, regex or raw tsquery execution. Tenant/source permission predicates must hold for both existing rows and proposed projection updates, and old permissive policies must not OR unsafe access back open.

### D22-R13 — Durable indexing without source-write coupling

Source admission/update/restriction commits the necessary durable projection intent or equivalent discoverable revision gap atomically with its owner event. Index workers reread current owner data, act idempotently on original source identity/revision, and never let older or out-of-order work replace a newer projection or revive a withdrawn source. Jobs carry identifiers, not long-lived body copies. Index failure does not undo admitted mail/notes, stop valid replying or pretend the source never arrived. Creation of a source is not proof search is complete: expose truthful indexing readiness. Use existing durable-work and reconciliation capabilities, not a new job service or one polling timer per conversation.

### D22-R14 — Current restriction wins before asynchronous purge

D16 redaction, D17 expiry, source correction, privacy reclassification and access changes fence serving immediately at their authoritative admission boundary. A stale index can contribute no hit, rank, count, snippet, filename or match reason while physical purge catches up. Projection updates cannot extend source availability. Revoke queued/cached results and revalidate at navigation; no rendered app can recall content already observed or independently copied, but Asym must stop serving it. Remove forbidden source text/derived index material through its actual owner purge process, including backups/restore barriers; search history/logs are not an alternate archive. No direct-scan fallback over index-forbidden or expired sources.

### D22-R15 — Complete queries and honest limits

Apply complete eligible predicates before result limits/counts/page, eliminating the current 2,000-before-filter and unpaged hydration assumptions. Indexed candidates are not authoritative matches until the exact contract verifies them; candidate truncation cannot become a complete zero. Use bounded keyset pages and stable ties, with query/normalizer/coverage version, scope and ordering bound into opaque current-authorized continuation. Root topology or source changes may alter live results; detect invalid continuation/visible changes, deduplicate known rows and offer deliberate refresh rather than claim a historical snapshot. Do not hold indefinite DB snapshots or preserve result bodies for stable pagination.

Only display an exact total when the complete authorized query and coverage justify it; otherwise show page/progress and an explicit incomplete/unavailable result state. No per-filter/per-source counts by default. Timeout, index backlog, unreadied source generation and failed projection are different from No matches. Supported source units that exceed a processing budget remain a visible coverage/recovery condition, not an undocumented unsearchable tail. Excluded source types and inaccessible data are outside declared coverage, without revealing hidden-match counts.

### D22-R16 — Bounded performance and exact matching

Index qualified normalized source text and tenant/source keys as justified by measured plans. Literal substring semantics can use escaped LIKE/position verification with pg_trgm candidates; do not substitute fuzzy similarity or English tsvector stopword/stemming semantics. Trigram-poor short/punctuation queries can require scans: apply authorized structured scope first, enforce registered execution/resource limits and return a clear Refine search outcome if the budget is exhausted, never fake zero. Segmentation/chunking must preserve source and redaction boundaries; retrieve candidate parent items then verify all terms/phrases against the same qualified item. No all-terms-in-one-chunk shortcut or cross-chunk phrase loss.

Before activation publish and test a finite Support search profile: normalized-input expansion, eligible source/segment size, snippets, pages, row/query time, concurrency, per-tenant fairness, cancellation, index/recovery/freshness deadlines and cache/query-state retention. The 256-scalar/16-atom/1,024-byte input bounds do not imply those source/index limits. Do not import P9 Cmd-K's p95 target or a vendor's result/comment caps as an unmeasured Support SLA. Existing source records above a profile boundary require honest qualification, not silent clipping. No payload embeddings or attachment parsing to improve performance.

### D22-R17 — Safe excerpts and accessible emphasis

Generate bounded excerpts from the current qualified display projection, escape text and add only trusted highlight markup. Never render raw source HTML or trust PostgreSQL ts_headline as an XSS sanitizer. Highlight only actual verified matches, mapping normalization/casefold expansion back to original visible grapheme boundaries; preserve punctuation and meaningful whitespace. Do not highlight private removed spans or fabricate adjacency across them. Long matches may need multiple clearly separated excerpts from the same source, not a false continuous quote. Untrusted links/attachments remain inert until their separately qualified actions. Announce meaningful state changes without reading entire private excerpts into global live regions.

### D22-R18 — Sensitive query state and client isolation

Reuse D20's bounded authenticated query-state reference for sensitive text/criteria in navigation and saved contexts; no plaintext query in share URLs, telemetry, referrers or cross-user recent-search lists by default. A handle is not a bearer access grant. Current authorization and owner lifetime govern handle resolution, not knowledge of its value. Bind caches and responses to tenant/principal/acting scope/source purpose, query/coverage/version and current authority. A late response after logout, tenant switch, scope change or revocation cannot populate the new UI. Invalid/expired handles show unavailable/re-enter-search rather than blank All. Do not add a new recent-search product, session-replay capture or analytics corpus of raw queries.

### D22-R19 — D20 saved meaning and D21 report boundaries

Save search through the existing shared named-view contract, explicitly recording query, Search in source coverage and matching/normalization version. New coverage defaults never silently broaden existing details-only saved queries. Migrate supported legacy meaning faithfully under an explicit legacy version or require reviewed upgrade; no approximate reinterpretation of old substring/concatenation semantics as the new predicate. Known ambiguous old source meaning becomes bounded owned reconciliation. My privacy, Shared audience/field-use checks and versioned defaults remain D20's rules. D21 report drilldown retains its exact cohort and source query; it is not translated to ordinary root createdAt or silently made a saved search. Future AI mode cannot reinterpret a stored keyword query automatically.

### D22-R20 — CRM and source continuity

D9 anchors the CRM record and qualified original-source basis. A Party linked through original B does not gain A's historical message matches through merge. Intersect permitted relevant sources before D10 grouping/ranking; projected results remain attributable. D12 related conversations stay independent. Open in Support Hub explicitly enters separately qualified broader handling, preserving return context. Requester, participant, authenticated actor, represented organization, CRM Party, support handler and CRM owner remain distinct; searching an address creates none of those links. No support-side copy of CRM identity, giving facts, care notes or CRM search index is introduced. Source association changes, Party merge/delete/archive and permission changes require current requalification, not silent search-result reassignment.

### D22-R21 — Filename discovery is not byte access

Index only attachment names whose Support/file owner allows that metadata in the declared source context. An allowed name may be discoverable even when a file is unsupported for preview; a blocked/held name is not eligible merely because bytes exist. Store no signed URL as searchable text and make no byte download/provider fetch during search. Opening/downloading uses the current original attachment, immutable byte version and separate source/storage/malware/access checks. Filename edits/removal and parent-message restrictions invalidate old name matches. Finding a filename never proves that its contents were searched, are safe, or may be opened. Phase29 retains storage/access/hygiene mechanics; D22 does not implement text extraction.

### D22-R22 — Email Studio, Resend and business actions

Search is a read capability over qualified admitted Support sources. It neither publishes Email Studio wording, creates a template variable, prepares mail, changes recipients, follows, labels, creates CRM interaction/last-contact history nor calls Resend/provider search. P6/P17 Recent and private prepared material are never used to fill missing source text. Searching an admitted queued reply does not imply it was sent. An actual later Reply or Note rechecks its current source/draft/recipient purpose: P17 ready preparation→Support atomic admission→P6 tenant Resend for external replies; Notes remain non-deliverable. Search outage cannot block otherwise qualified handling, and a hit never authorizes a CRM/giving/care mutation or business completion.

### D22-R23 — Future broader/AI search reuses the boundary

Preserve typed original source identities, eligible projection/schema revisions, query-mode/coverage semantics, match provenance and a current-authorized source retrieval contract as the extension seam. Future attachment content and AI search are separately designed and reviewed with Phase29/40 and existing shared AI connection/capability/egress/invocation/suggestion owners. They must inherit indexing exclusions, current retrieval checks, source disposal and citation/provenance requirements. No generic plugin framework, embeddings, extraction jobs, external prompts, autonomous actions, provider keys or AI placeholders are added to D22. This records the founder's direction without charging current users for an unused feature or claiming future compliance/readiness.

### D22-R24 — Observability without a second content store

Technical metrics/traces record safe query class/version, scope identifiers where permitted, latency/cancellation, eligible projection freshness and repair codes; avoid raw query/source/filename text and sensitive snippet payloads. Durable source/index lifecycle receipts establish which revision was projected or withdrawn; they are distinct from customer-visible conversation history and security read audit. Reuse existing required sensitive-read/export audit when a permitted operation qualifies; index-forbidden content stays excluded rather than becoming an audited indexing exception. No search itself fabricates a communication or last-contact event. O01–O05 below name concrete signals and accountable responses.

### D22-R25 — Migration, rollout and recovery

Inventory actual searchable-source stores, old list/message endpoints, raw browser/fixture paths and saved definitions before activation. Add projection/source revision/coverage contracts and indexes compatibly; seed only currently admitted index-eligible sources. Backfill carries original source identity and current policy, not current-time admission or invented historical sent state. Gate source kinds/tenants until coverage and privacy proofs pass. Fence old body-leaking reads, raw-query URL serializers and incomplete pre-filter paths. Do not keep permanent parallel search engines or competing write authority; all supported query versions run through the single qualified owner, and unsafe or unsupported legacy meanings require reviewed upgrade under R19. Stop controls disable unsafe search while preserving source work, current privacy and owner navigation. After new restrictions/versioned queries exist, rollback cannot re-enable old unsafe projections or reinterpret saved meaning; roll forward and rebuild only from current authorized sources. Restored backups must apply withdrawal/revision barriers before serving.

### D22-R26 — Proof and stage discipline

Trace D22-R01–R26 and P01–P40 through selected answer, glossary, feature ADR, eventual OpenSpec/design/tasks/issues, implementation and release evidence. Broadening beyond A requires an explicit future scope decision, not a dependency convenience. The current review includes source inspection and limited synthetic matcher experiments only; real SQL/RLS/indexing/privacy/concurrency/browser/accessibility/capacity proof is required before activation. No Q23, formal specification, implementation, tickets, external mutation or real messages are inferred. D1–D21 are preserved; the complete D22 amendments await ratification.
