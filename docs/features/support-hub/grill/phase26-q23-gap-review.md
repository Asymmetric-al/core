## Why this question is next

The session coverage ledger leaves consequential macros/signatures open after D18. D1 separated actual actor, assignment and approved signature. D4 fixed presentation/preparation/sending ownership and required a restrained signature/frame. D18 expressly deferred a new signature policy rather than smuggling it into reusable replies. Therefore the unresolved product choice is who manages the signature body and what the normal reply shows, not whether to build a separate signature service.

Attachments, draft collisions, held intake, labels and search already have substantial accepted safeguards. Reopening them now without a new decision dependency would repeat earlier work. Signature governance is visible in every human reply and has concrete current source seams to reconcile; it is a bounded next decision, not a claim that every other Phase26 gap is closed.

## Boundary audit

The recommended option cannot change From/Reply-To, choose the coordinator as actor, create a CRM Party, disclose arbitrary staff/CRM fields, send an acknowledgement, credit a reply target or mutate a business record. Signatures have no authority to authenticate a person. Internal notes retain local authorship rather than a customer-facing footer. D13 automated mail and D18 reusable fragments retain their existing distinct roles.

Tenant-scoped consumer references, immutable approved presentation revisions, actual server-derived actor, profile disclosure qualification, exact draft audience, authoring versus publication versus sending capabilities, defaults that survive staff departure, concurrent default changes, retirement, retained sent history and restore fences are required topics in the selected answer's full review. Do not prematurely freeze physical tables, arbitrary quotas, SLA thresholds or a new global role in this question.

The option table presents three genuinely different ownership/default strategies. A's team-only exception does not add C's personal-authoring system. There is no automatic rollout, hidden all-three implementation or accepted Q23 answer. This source-based challenge supports asking the question; it is not 23-category acceptance proof.
