**Recommendation: ask whether staff may directly record a bounded Support request received outside email. Recommend A — Record Support follow-up directly.** The competing choices should be B — Use CRM notes/tasks for now, and C — Record the interaction first, then open Support follow-up. They differ in the required first action and whether a shared Support work item is available, not in which transport provider to add.

## Why this is genuinely unresolved

D12 permits a new related conversation only from one explicitly reviewed admitted incoming email. It already establishes truthful staff-created work, a brief and source references, zero native mail until correspondence occurs, and Create being separate from Send. It does **not** grant a general standalone phone/in-person intake action. Its provenance and initial trigger requirement must not be silently weakened by relabeling a new button. [D12-R01–R02, lines 21–31](phase26-d12-adversarial-review.md).

The coverage ledger explicitly leaves phone notes and knowledge use unresolved. The roadmap describes Phase 26 primarily as inbound email work, so adding manually recorded off-email follow-up is a real product scope decision, not an inevitable implementation detail. Conversely, the roadmap does not establish that every staff encounter needs a ticket. [Coverage ledger, line 793](phase26-grill-log.md), [Phase 26 scope, lines 2895–2959](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md#L2895).

The strongest concrete example is illustrative, not a claimed measured ministry frequency: a donor calls about an unresolved receipt issue. The worker needs another authorized team member to investigate and intends to follow up. Recording an ongoing Support request should not require inventing an inbound email, making a CRM person merely to unlock the form, or recording the same call a second time. A completed courtesy call with no Support follow-up stays ordinary relationship activity. D11 remains the path for a bounded internal contribution to existing Support work.

## Actual A/B/C fork

**Question:** When a phone call or in-person conversation creates a request that needs continuing Support follow-up, where should staff begin?

| Option                                                            | Actual journey                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Benefit                                                                                                                                                     | Cost / limitation                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Record Support follow-up directly — recommended**           | In Support, record the request and its source, choose the responsible inbox and follow-up plan, and save without sending. If the encounter already has an authorized owner record, reference it. Relevant CRM context can launch the same action with permitted context preserved. | Puts shared work where staff handle it, avoids a compulsory intermediate record, and can serve a legitimate requester who has no CRM Party or usable email. | Requires a narrow new staff-recorded origin, accurate source/time presentation and a qualified shared-history seam. It must not grow into a separate all-purpose call log.                                                             |
| **B — Use CRM notes and shared tasks for now**                    | Record the encounter in its qualified relationship/records owner and use the shared task capability for follow-up. Do not add standalone off-email Support creation in this phase.                                                                                                 | Smallest scope; preserves email-centered Support and uses domain-owned activity/task workflows.                                                             | These requests do not receive the same shared Support work/status experience. Staff must work across the appropriate existing surfaces; a task is not a substitute for requester conversation management when that is the actual need. |
| **C — Record the interaction first, then open Support follow-up** | Begin with the qualified shared interaction journey. When Support work is needed, explicitly create linked Support follow-up from that one existing source.                                                                                                                        | Establishes the encounter in one shared history before creating work; useful where relationship logging is already the staff's standard starting point.     | Adds an obligatory intermediate step and depends on a complete interaction owner, including how to record legitimate unlinked requesters. Current Party-keyed CRM activity cannot simply be presumed to provide that.                  |

These are alternatives for the default product contract, not three mandatory tenant configuration modes. All three preserve appropriate CRM access, no automatic Party creation and no automatic email merely because staff saved work. A can offer a contextual entry from CRM without becoming C: C requires the interaction to be recorded first; A permits direct Support capture and reuses an existing source when present.

For A, the principle to carry into the selected-answer review is **one source fact, shared authorized references/projections, distinct Support work**. If an encounter is already recorded, creating Support work must not clone its body into a second independent call log. If Support first records the request, later CRM visibility should derive from that authoritative source and actual links, rather than dual-writing a second editable encounter. The minimal Support request brief and the historical call are distinct meanings; do not pretend that a brief is a verbatim transcript or complete relationship log. This does not yet select a physical table, new generic event platform or automatic association policy.

## Boundaries to review after the founder chooses

1. **D12 and identity:** explicitly qualify a new bounded origin rather than fabricating an incoming trigger. Unknown requester, represented person and staff recorder remain distinct. Matching a name/phone/email is not identity or authority. Do not require a Party merely to record legitimate Support work.
2. **D14 measurement:** D14-R06 says only first-time admitted relevant human Support email creates/contributes to a reply period; staff-origin creation alone does not. Keep that meaning unless a separately justified amendment is selected. Existing D3 promises/reminders can govern manual follow-up without inventing an email reply target or crediting a phone note as sent mail.
3. **D16/D17 records:** classify the manual brief and any original encounter source under their actual owner. Do not evade Support expiry by duplicating text into CRM Notes, or let Support expiry destroy an independently owned required interaction record. D17-R04/R05's content and clock meanings require an explicit compatibility check for any newly admitted origin.
4. **D4/D13/P17/P6:** saving staff-recorded work sends nothing and creates no eligible inbound acknowledgement. A later real email uses explicit reviewed audience, qualified Email Studio preparation/signature and tenant Resend delivery; no fictional incoming message, notification-only mail or caller-controlled provider send.
5. **Scope:** this question adds no dialer, recording, transcript, call center, SMS, chat, offline device sync or AI. A resolved courtesy interaction does not automatically become Support work. A financial/care action still belongs to its actual owner.

These are candidate-review guardrails, not newly ratified Q26 clauses. The chosen answer should receive the requested complete adversarial review before final acceptance.
