## Material finding found and resolved

**F-D31-O1 — Due reminder must retain D7 coverage evaluation.** The first UX text said assignment did not change at due. That was a material, high-impact contradiction: ratified D7-R03 expressly evaluates valid due reminders, and its required coverage-evaluation intent commits with D3 due-created Open work. A covered absent worker's conversation can then transfer under the existing guarded assignment path. A direct timer reassignment and a qualified D7 consequence are different effects.

**Resolved in the actual final files.** D31-R09 now says due processing does not directly select an assignee but retains applicable D7 coverage, including when the scheduling worker has left. R12 explicitly commits required coverage-evaluation intent with due-created Open work and delegates any transfer to the guarded assignment path. The UX Due and failure journey carries the same distinction, and P14 includes the due/coverage/scheduler-departure case. No new assignment mode, personal alarm or notification was added.
