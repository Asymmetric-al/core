# Personal and shared Support reply library

**Historical disposition: Accept with required amendments. D18 and every adopted amendment are fully founder-ratified, 11 September 2026.** The founder selected B—My replies plus a curated shared library and fully ratified its complete amendments. D1–D17 remain fully ratified and unchanged. This is the finished grooming decision and required proof, not a formal specification or implemented library.

The [complete user journeys and starter examples](phase26-d18-library-ux.md), [data and CRM contract](phase26-d18-data-contract.md), [primary evidence and independent synthesis](phase26-d18-evidence.md) and [record validation](phase26-d18-validation.json) accompany this decision.

## Exact corrected decision

> Support Hub offers **My replies** and a curated **Shared replies** library through the existing Email Studio authoring owner. My items are tenant-governed reusable wording available for their author's ordinary use; Shared items are deliberately published for their declared authorized audience. Personal save is not publication or sending. Shared publication follows existing proportionate review, including protected and tenant-elevated requirements, without another Support approval platform.
>
> The library supports short phrases, complete answers, instructions and internal-note structures with bounded rich text, approved reusable assets, typed current-context values, explicit one-use fill-in fields and authored language variants. Reply and Internal note are separate insertion purposes. Length, tone and formatting do not create new message types or layouts. One searchable, scope-aware picker, favorites, recent selections, optional simple categories and explicit preview/Insert/Replace make the right wording easy to find without hidden actions.
>
> Insertion copies an exact eligible revision into the current draft; it never sends, changes recipients or work, creates CRM facts, or rewrites other drafts/history. CRM facts and protected actions remain owned and authorized by their domains. Saving, sharing or importing wording cannot create a shadow archive of conversation content or bypass D16/D17 restrictions. Actual sending continues through D4, Phase 17 preparation and Phase 6's tenant-qualified Resend connection.
>
> Shared stewardship outlives its creator. Personal access, deliberate contribution, concurrent edits, archive, safety restriction, staff departure and import/export have explicit source-aware lifecycles. Ordinary archive stops new selection; unsafe-source restrictions can stop affected preparation or dispatch through existing owner fences. These are distinct effects, with current permissions, immutable evidence, safe recovery and no retroactive rewriting.

## Required decision clauses

### D18-R01 — Scope and strongest alternative

Adopt B as the ordinary launch model. A—curated Shared only—is the strongest simpler alternative: less duplication and fewer custody states, at the cost of personal reuse and maintainer dependence. C—broader collaborative Shared publishing—reduces contribution handoffs but increases the group affected by ordinary edits and additions. No measured ministry study proves B universally superior; B is a deliberate product judgment balancing personal writing and maintained guidance.

Complete this bounded library, not an enterprise help-desk suite. Reuse the already accepted Saved reply wording concept. No new CRM, knowledge base, AI suggestion/translation system, marketing designer, business-action macro builder, general form engine or three-mode tenant configuration is selected.

### D18-R02 — One authoring owner and two scopes

Qualify a narrow Saved Section specialization within Email Studio's canonical structured authoring capability. A logical item has one tenant, one immutable identity and exactly one scope: personal with one tenant-membership custodian, or shared with tenant stewardship and no individual ownership dependency. Creator/editor attribution is separate. No global-account personal library, implicit cross-tenant portability, second raw-HTML store, provider template authority, new Layout Role or system-message catalog key.

Scope is not a generic editable null owner field. Promotion creates a distinct destination-owned shared candidate with provenance; it does not mutate the personal item into a different audience. Item identity, content revision, language variant, usage purpose and presentation dependencies remain distinct. The same shared item is managed from Email Studio and consumed in Support, not synchronized between duplicate stores.

### D18-R03 — Rights and the meaning of personal

Separate ordinary use, own-item maintenance, shared-content maintenance/publication, and exceptional personal-custody administration using Core capabilities. Shared maintainers do not automatically read others' My replies or their source conversations. My replies is personal ordinary use inside this tenant, not a secrecy promise against specifically authorized tenant records/security custody. Exceptional custody requires its explicit tenant-scoped capability, purpose, current authorization and audit; no hidden supervisor inheritance or routine export-all-personal button from shared-edit rights.

Save to My replies commits a validated immutable authoring revision usable only by its current authorized custodian. It is not publication and grants no template dispatch authority; the tenant's another-reviewer-for-every-publication setting does not add a reviewer to this save or normal human composition. This narrow distinction cannot lower an actual protected publication floor: personal items admit only qualified reusable human wording, not protected system/artifact/layout sources or recipient-specific protected material stripped of provenance. Current insertion, content-safety, preparation and Send/Add note checks still apply. Membership/permission loss stops new unauthorized reads and use, including cached clients.

### D18-R04 — Reply versus Internal note, not dozens of template types

Each item declares one insertion purpose: **Reply** or **Internal note**. Both may live in My or Shared. The picker filters by the active composer purpose and labels it clearly. It never switches composer mode; an internal-note item cannot be fetched through an external-reply insertion path. A deliberate separately reviewed conversion creates another appropriate candidate; an unsafe body is not made public by toggling a label.

Changing mode after insertion also does not authorize disclosure of internal-only wording. Preserve separate Reply and Internal note drafts. External preparation rejects retained internal-only item provenance, including content recovered through Replace/Undo; do not silently carry the note body into Reply, relabel it or strip its source. Any deliberate conversion requires owner-qualified content/audience review. Internal notes reuse the shared authoring validator but never enter external email preparation, P6 dispatch, Resend or the external prepared-material lifetime. Their Add note command owns admission.

Full answers, short phrases, greetings, closings, information requests, updates, instructions and handoff/checklist wording use the same bounded content model. Their length/tone are content choices, not workflow types. No stored mention activates notifications during save/preview; a note template uses safe text or fill-ins, and any later real mention must be explicitly selected through the existing current-user authorization path. Saving/inserting adds neither a note nor a D11 delegated action by itself.

### D18-R05 — Useful styles with one visual system

Use P17's qualified semantic paragraphs, emphasis, headings, lists, safe links and approved images, plus reusable files under R12. Quoted wording may be plain text within that vocabulary; no new blockquote or general-table authoring is selected. Protected domain-owned tables remain only in their already qualified owner context. Keep actual HTML and plain-text output consistent. Full responses remain body content: no embedded From/To/subject, outer email document, duplicated brand wrapper, executable script/CSS, remote font, tracking pixel or live nested snippet reference.

Use concise plain-language copy as the starter style, with optional warm, formal, concise, detailed or translated authored alternatives. These are titles/wording, not a tone-generation engine or mandatory matrix of every variant. Shared presentation, sender identity and signature/framing remain with D4/P17; do not invent a separate Support signature policy or duplicate a signature on every inserted fragment. Email Studio tools retain base-maia and accessible outline parity. Formatting must remain usable with images blocked, narrow screens, long names, RTL text and real plain text.

### D18-R06 — Creation and editing without harvesting a case

Offer New saved reply from the picker and the same library management surface. Start clean with title, purpose, language and content; default an ordinary author's new item to My replies. Scope and recipient visibility are visible before saving. Allow an optional search alias and category; do not require administrative metadata before a useful personal save. Preserve drafts, errors and confirmed save state.

Do not automatically save a sent message, a whole reply draft, conversation history, live donor values, attachments or hidden links as reusable material. Provide no one-click Save conversation as template. Deliberate reusable authoring can preserve approved unresolved typed fields; known source-derived material keeps its dependency restrictions and must pass generalized-content review before the owner admits it as reusable. Unsupported opaque source mappings fail closed for that copy, not for all ordinary composition. A warning or AI guess is not proof of anonymization. Freely typed misleading prose cannot be perfectly detected; guidance and actual domain-action boundaries remain necessary.

### D18-R07 — Typed context values and CRM are separate authorities

Expose only fields qualified by the actual Reply or Internal note content contract. Resolve values server-side from the current tenant, exact conversation/audience and permitted source. Label the authenticated replying author separately from the Support assignee; an unassigned queue is not a person. Do not split international display names at the first space, choose the first To recipient or first related Party, treat matching email as identity, or guess represented-organization authority.

Missing/ambiguous/unauthorized required values block preparation with a precise repair path. Optional neutral wording must be explicit; use a safe greeting rather than fabricated names. A no-Party reply remains possible through D1. Shared authoring previews use synthetic data; actual context appears only in the authorized current draft. D2 visible groups use one audience-safe body. Display permission is not permission to disclose every CRM value. No blank CRM property is filled as a side effect, and a snippet cannot assert receipt/refund completion as a protected fact without its owner's actual evidence.

### D18-R08 — Explicit one-use fill-in fields

Qualify a narrow **Fill-in field** authoring node through the shared compiler: plain-language label/instruction, required or optional, and safe bounded text input. Values belong only to the current insertion/draft. They are not CRM properties, query expressions, executable braces or live cross-draft values. A required field has no silent example/default; an optional default is explicit reusable wording. After insertion, fields display clearly in the draft and keyboard order; the author can complete them or remove the associated field/wording deliberately.

Unresolved required fields block Send or Add note at the authoritative command, not only in the client. Whitespace is not completion. Human literal brace text remains ordinary text. A resolved value is escaped once as text, never recursively interpreted as variables, mentions or markup; links/assets use their separate qualified tools. Repeated appearances of one field in an item share that insertion's value, while another insertion receives fresh identity and no old answers. Deleting/changing an input invalidates its prior review. Values cannot update the library, another draft, an authoritative CRM fact or imply a business action. This requires a narrow P17 authoring/input qualification, not a generic forms platform.

### D18-R09 — Explicit language and honest translation freshness

An item may contain independently authored language variants under the same logical identity and purpose. Each variant has an exact canonical locale, explicit content revision and availability. Reuse the owning locale validation and structured RTL/bidi support; do not introduce a second locale registry or infer a person's language from nationality, name or email. A current explicitly selected draft language can filter the picker. Otherwise show the tenant's configured authoring default clearly, with the language control available.

Missing target-language content is visible: offer other available languages for deliberate selection or a normal authored reply; never silently send a different language or machine translation. Changing UI locale does not replace inserted wording. Record a translation's declared reviewed source revision when supplied; a source change can mark linked translations Needs review in management without silently rewriting or automatically withdrawing still-safe approved wording. Actual safety revocation remains a separate enforceable fence. Display freshness only where relevant. A language variant is not a new publication of every other language or a mandatory all-locale campaign rollout.

### D18-R10 — Find the right item without a noisy library

One searchable picker supports Shared, My and Favorites filters, a small current-purpose recent-selection area, and optional single-level categories. Categories organize content and are not permission boundaries or live nested snippets. Shared category maintenance follows shared rights; personal categories/preferences belong to the current tenant/custodian. Deleting a category leaves items uncategorized rather than deleting them. Reuse a shared item directly as a favorite instead of forcing personal copies of official wording.

Search authorized titles, descriptions, aliases and reusable body text with Unicode-aware literal matching through the shared search capability. Resolve tenant, purpose, source eligibility, locale and ACL before membership, results, counts, autocomplete or ranking. Do not scan only the first page or capped current conversation list. Exact alias/title matches precede ordinary matches; scope and locale disambiguate duplicates. Aliases are search hints, never record identity or executable expansion. They do not silently shadow a Shared item with a personal match. A slash/keyboard command opens the same picker; it never auto-sends, opens on ordinary slash-containing text indiscriminately, or bypasses explicit choice. Search ranking stays stable during keyboard navigation; late results cannot move focus to another item.

Favorites and recent IDs grant no access and keep no body/hidden title after revocation. Management can include authorized Archived items; ordinary use excludes them. Empty, no matches, wrong-language, unavailable and denied states are distinct without exposing hidden assets. No automatic folder hierarchy, AI ranking, shortcut collision oracle or cross-tenant popularity index.

### D18-R11 — Safe insertion, combination and replacement

Preview the exact currently eligible item revision and assets before or alongside selection, then insert at a stable current caret/selection. A complete answer still uses Insert by default; replacing existing content is a distinct Replace reply/Replace note action with local Undo. Do not require a modal preview for every simple insertion. Switching purpose, target, tenant or draft while a result is loading cannot apply it to the wrong editor; stale bookmarks/versions require reapply without losing typed text.

Copy the selected structured content with immutable provenance, fresh fill-in occurrence IDs and asset appearances in one coherent draft edit. Multiple insertions can combine a greeting, instructions and closing. Repeated exact file references do not duplicate already present attachments; Undo removes only text/fields/appearances introduced by that insertion, never a preexisting file or later independent work. Undo cannot restore restricted content or convert internal-only provenance to Reply; restore only currently permitted material and preserve unrelated authored work. No live saved-item reference remains in the body. New library edits do not alter restored drafts. Actual source restrictions still govern retained dependency material, preparation and dispatch. Review any changed source facts/audience under D4; do not replace text behind the author's back.

### D18-R12 — Governed reusable files and images

Support static reusable attachments such as approved general guidance through Email Studio's governed asset owner, with immutable version references, filename/type/size, scan/readiness, author permission, reuse purpose and current source availability. Insertion and actual sending both validate compatibility and audience disclosure. Images require suitable alternative text unless genuinely decorative; plain-text output preserves the useful linked meaning. No external image fetch, iframe, remote executable content or hotlinked private-case asset is admitted as trusted library material.

Actual receipts, statements, identity proofs, care records, expiring action links and donor-uploaded documents do not become general attachments by saving a pointer. Use the owning action/document flow in the current authorized conversation. Updating a library file produces a new revision; old pins follow their valid owner lifecycle, while a safety-revoked version stops new unsafe use. Respect the stricter of source, library, prepared-message and provider limits. Publish numeric size/count/total-byte limits from the qualified shared contract before activation, and prove exact boundaries; do not copy a vendor's arbitrary file budget or claim unlimited capacity.

### D18-R13 — Shared publication and audience

Shared items have explicit authorized-use scope: all eligible Support staff in the tenant or selected existing qualified Support teams. This scope is an actual asset authorization rule, not merely a From-channel filter. Shared maintenance applies to the actor's explicit asset-management scope and does not grant any CRM/conversation access. A title or aggregate count cannot leak out of that scope. No arbitrary sharing with email addresses or cross-tenant users.

Shared editors create an unpublished candidate, review exact wording/purpose/locale/fields/assets/scope, and Publish through the owning immutable publication boundary. Ordinary qualified self-publication stays simple. Existing protected and tenant-stricter review apply; a Support-local permission or personal copy cannot bypass them. Widening scope requires a reviewed publication. Failed validation or stale concurrent publication leaves the previous complete valid shared version active. Saving a draft does not update colleagues' picker. Publishing sends no mail, changes no work and creates no CRM interaction.

The effective item audience governs all old revisions, deep links, favorites, previews and new insertion/preparation. A historical audience snapshot is provenance, not enduring access. Audience narrowing takes effect through a current authorization revision; it differs from routine Archive and preserves D4's treatment of admitted or possibly-sent envelopes. Widening an item audience reviews every currently exposed locale/asset in that scope; a valid single-language candidate cannot authorize unseen incompatible variants.

### D18-R14 — Deliberate contribution, not implicit promotion

Offer to Shared creates an immutable contribution snapshot of an exact eligible personal revision and declared proposed audience. The author sees what will become visible to qualified maintainers. Those maintainers gain only the offered candidate, not the source conversation or all personal items. A later personal edit neither changes the offer nor updates Shared. An authorized maintainer may edit a separate candidate and publish through R13 with truthful provenance; accepting an existing candidate against another shared item requires an explicit target and current-head comparison.

Before exposing an offered file/image to maintainers or publishing it to a broader audience, re-prove exact reuse/disclosure authority and establish a destination-owned governed occurrence/reference. Never hotlink Shared to the author's personal file area. Valid Shared assets survive ordinary source-item deletion/departure under their own custody while true safety/source restrictions still propagate. No physical byte copy is mandated. An invalid asset prevents exposure of the affected candidate; do not create a partly shared body/file result.

Use existing Email Studio review/task attention, with an accountable maintainer or qualified shared work home. No new notification campaign, approval engine or content-bearing task copy. Pending contributions may be withdrawn before publication; concurrent publish/withdraw is conditional and resolves one ordered result. Once published, withdrawing the offer does not unpublish the independent Shared item; use its owner action. Declined/withdrawn offers stop pending work and obey asset retention. Identical retry creates one contribution; deliberately offering revised wording is a new reviewed snapshot, with duplicate hints rather than silent merging. Known source safety restrictions propagate to affected copies even though ordinary edits do not.

### D18-R15 — Archive, restore and ordinary edits

Draft, saved/published usable revision, Archived, and safety-restricted are distinct facts. **Archive** stops new picker selection/insertion without making routine maintenance an emergency block on otherwise safe already-inserted drafts or prepared messages. Shared editors can archive within scope; authors can archive My items. The action needs clear effect wording and safe correction, not typed confirmation for every item. Archiving must not require the broken body still to compile.

Restore returns an archived item to use only after current ownership, scope, schema/assets and publication rules are satisfied; it never simply resurrects a safety-restricted source. Restoring an older content version creates a reviewed new candidate with attribution, not in-place history editing. Editing content always creates a new revision. Published/usable versions are immutable; cosmetic metadata changes cannot secretly modify an existing prepared body. Ordinary library retirement, item disposal and dangerous-source revocation are not interchangeable APIs.

### D18-R16 — Safety restriction, deletion and source correction

Unsafe or unauthorized content uses existing owner quarantine/restriction and D16-style active-use/cleanup obligations for affected material. Safety changes can stop new reads/insertion/preparation and qualifying definitely-unsubmitted work; in-flight/unknown provider effects remain D4/P6 reconciliation, not recall or a replacement send. Apply the owner's correctly declared quarantine effect rather than treating every archive as an all-envelope incident.

Do not offer generic hard Delete as a shortcut around source history, contributions or prepared references. Permanent disposal follows the actual asset owner's policy, restrictions and retention, preserves minimum necessary provenance/control evidence, and never cascades through sent messages, independent CRM records or official files. Wrong cleanup evidence is corrected append-only; content already disposed is not restored. Exports, previews, search, logs and backups/restore obey current source boundaries. No implicit forever archive or Reveal bypass for personal items.

### D18-R17 — Departure and tenant custody

Shared replies stay tenant-managed when a creator changes role or leaves. Attribution can retain a minimized former-author reference under the identity owner's policy; it does not retain login/access rights. Personal use stops on membership/capability loss. Personal items are neither published to Shared nor deleted automatically by an agent-parent cascade. Their actual tenant authoring retention/custody determines later disposition; no universal permanent retention or guessed grace period.

An explicit qualified custody action may inspect, restrict, export or prepare a new permitted shared candidate only within the existing records/security administrative purpose and audit. Ordinary shared-edit permission cannot silently claim a departed person's entire library. Reactivation/recovery rechecks actual current identity/membership and source lifetime; a newly invited person with the same email does not inherit another person's My replies. Open contributions already deliberately offered remain governed by their own scope and maintainer, not dependent on the author staying logged in.

### D18-R18 — Retention of library content and its copies

Generic reusable library wording has an authoring purpose and owner policy distinct from an individual conversation's D17 clock. That does not create an exemption for known case-derived content. Establish each actual library, draft, offer, asset, preference, audit and recovery class's owner, permitted use, visibility, horizon and disposal outcome before activation. An unsent insertion remains draft material under its qualified draft lifetime and inherited source restrictions; it is not a new admitted Support original or a retention-renewal event. Once the reply or note is actually admitted, its Support content follows D17. P17 prepared material and P6/owner-qualified Recent copies retain their separate tighter ceilings. Ordinary library edits are not retention events for old conversations.

Known source-derived content or files cannot lose their restriction by copying, editing, offering, importing, exporting or renaming an item. Keep the minimum source dependency evidence required for correction and expiry; do not retain the original secret in audit. Human generalization must produce valid reusable content under the owner review; opaque lineage does not justify automatic extraction. Pasted literals can require human correction, so do not claim perfect semantic DLP or build an AI archive scanner to justify B.

### D18-R19 — CRM integration and cross-surface continuity

Requester, participant, authenticated author, assignee, related Party, represented organization and CRM owner remain distinct. D9 allows several related records without a main designation; insertion cannot select a first record as the authoritative personalization source. Resolving ambiguity belongs in the current authorized reply context, with neutral wording available. Requesting a receipt, initiating a refund or updating contact data uses the real owner action, with context-preserving navigation and its audit/approval; words saying done never perform or prove it.

The same authorized Support composer opened from CRM sees the same eligible library and exact draft. Returning preserves context without exposing hidden CRM data to the library editor. Shared editing, personal custody and item use grant no additional giving/care/missionary access. Save, favorite, insert, offer and publish produce no communication event or fresh donor interaction. Actual send adds only P6's proper deduplicated communication evidence; the final message retains permitted source revision attribution without a second canonical body. Ordinary contact merges/changes cannot rewrite old attribution or covertly choose a new recipient.

Tenant/source/purpose identity, accepted actor and audit fields derive from trusted context. Scope changes use the explicit owner command, never an allowed update that turns a private row into Shared by nulling owner. No sentinel string stands in for a nullable owner namespace.

Titles and optional aliases may collide without granting access or selecting a hidden target; they are not authoritative keys. Validate locale/schema/state combinations, positive revision ordering, bounded data sizes and typed timestamps. Published source and durable business history are append-only except authorized source-content disposal with protected minimal evidence. Explicit delete behavior preserves surviving consumers and required recovery; no cascading staff deletion of shared truth.

Replace or reconcile broad existing grants/policies; adding a restrictive-looking permissive policy alongside an allowing policy does not narrow their OR-combined result. Views/functions/RPCs, source files, storage, exports and privileged service-role paths require equivalent authority; current broad staff DML is insufficient. All business writes live at the qualified packages/api owner boundary. Recheck revocation at use, publication, preparation and dispatch, not just picker load. Cached projections, client-supplied actor/scope and generic JSON metadata never become write authority.

### D18-R21 — Concurrency and durable effect identity

## Individual adversarial category review

Severity measures consequence if the specified boundary fails; likelihood is qualitative, not measured production frequency. A material design concern does not claim an exploit is deployed. Each exact fix below refers to binding clauses and proof groups in this record.

### 1. Problem validity, necessity, and alternatives

**Material concern: Yes. Severity Medium; likelihood plausible.** “Everything a team needs” can turn wording reuse into a workflow platform. A shared-only library is simpler and may be enough for some teams; no Asym usage study proves demand for every feature. This **narrows B, not rejects it**. **Permanent fix/exact language:** R01/R04/R05/R10/R28 require one bounded personal/shared authoring model, explicit purposes and useful discovery while excluding speculative engines. P01/P02/P46 prove the intended boundary.
