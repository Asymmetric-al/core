## Independent synthesis and conflicts resolved

Three independent reviewers covered data/identity/lifecycle, Tiptap/schema/runtime and professional UX/vendor behavior. The root reconciled their concrete findings into the exact clauses and companions:

- Replaced the initial possible per-inbox presentation/live fragment approach with a narrow P17 published Saved Section purpose, copied into the canonical managed slot. Existing Service message Layout/Brand Kit remain intact.
- Chose exact publication binding rather than automatic latest-version fan-out. Publish is independent; deliberate bounded Use updates selected inboxes atomically, and Publish and use reports partial success truthfully.
- Qualified public identity at the membership/profile owner, not the generic displayName endpoint; public name/optional title and approved team contacts form the current narrow vocabulary.
- Adopted strict original-JSON validation before Tiptap parsing, because both primary-source behavior and the installed experiment show why toolbar/enableContentCheck alone are insufficient.
- Preserved Hide on Refresh and the pinned copy on Restore, with current safety checks. Actual sending-inbox/locale changes requalify the slot and show the result before approval; assignment/routing alone does not impersonate or reselect.
- Defined ordinary archive-in-use as reviewed replacement plus atomic archive, with deliberate bounded prior rebindings if the full set exceeds the operation limit. Safety restriction remains independent of replacement/manager availability.
- Kept static rendering failure explicit and source-aware rather than empty success, with correct block/text boundaries.

The independent final-pass appendices identify their checked snapshots and accepted corrections. Fresh reviews completed this turn; the prior Q23 usage-limit note remains historical and is not carried forward as a present review limitation. No independent reviewer or root ran live RLS, mailbox, browser/AT, capacity or provider-send tests.
