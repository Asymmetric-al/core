# D22: Conversation search and Email Studio ownership

**Fully founder-ratified, 12 September 2026.** This addendum makes D22-R02/R03/R22 and the existing D4/D13/D18 owner contracts explicit. It adds no template, search corpus, notification entitlement, capability grant or implementation authority. The full [D22 record](phase26-d22-adversarial-review.md), [source contract](phase26-d22-data-contract.md) and [D4 preparation contract](phase26-d4-adversarial-review.md) govern.

## One source of truth at each step

P17 is an authoring/preparation owner within Asym, not an external CRM to synchronize. Support does not add a second template library, compiler, Brand Kit, credential store or CRM identity. Current scaffolding remains subject to these intended contracts; this document does not claim those seams are fully implemented.

## The user journey

A staff member searches an authorized inbox/view or CRM-context Support list. Query matching uses eligible current original Support sources. A reusable reply used in a past message contributes only through the admitted source's currently permitted text, not through a live join to today's library version. Updating or archiving the reusable source does not rewrite historical correspondence. Redaction/expiry/source restrictions still remove forbidden historical content from serving and governed derivatives.

Opening a result rechecks access and navigates to the actual source. It neither starts a draft nor selects recipients. If the person deliberately replies, D18 can insert eligible wording into their current draft through the same qualified authoring seam. Support validates actual audience and disclosure, P17 prepares the exact approved content/presentation, Support admits the intent, and Phase6 delivers through the tenant's qualified Resend connection. Already admitted queued/failed/uncertain content may be searchable under D22, but its delivery status remains truthful.

The internal-note path remains local and non-deliverable. Notes do not call external preparation or Resend merely because their wording originated in an eligible shared library. Search results, saved views, labels, follows or CRM associations never supply automatic audience authority.

## Templates and messages

**No new Email Studio template is required for search.** A successful search, zero matches, Refine search, result click, Find, index catch-up or ordinary query failure sends no email. D13's existing **Support request received** template retains its own producer and eligibility window; searching or recovering an index cannot renew it or release D19-held intake.

Human replies remain outside the finite system-message template catalog under D4. They consume P17's bounded content/presentation preparation; they do not acquire a made-up system template key or provider-hosted template mirror. Phase6 sends the sealed prepared HTML and real plain text using Resend, without mutable template rendering at dispatch. Source/preparation/connection revision and frozen audience remain part of the exact admitted effect.

## Failure, privacy and recovery

Email Studio or Resend unavailability does not prevent an otherwise authorized search. An indexing outage cannot block otherwise valid source admission: commit durable projection intent or a discoverable gap and show truthful coverage. Searching a failed outgoing reply does not retry delivery. Selecting its existing recovery action requires that action's current ownership and exact-effect checks.

P6/P17 Recent is explicitly excluded by ADR0031. Neither a unavailable source nor a broken index can justify reading a retained Recent/provider copy. Rebuilding rereads current eligible source revisions; no template expansion, provider archive fetch or new recipient lookup is part of repair. Source deletion/redaction/expiry and restore fencing apply to index text, snippets and cache contributions. Published reusable content, drafts, admitted messages, prepared artifacts and delivery evidence have separate owner lifetimes; no signature/template/library reference extends D17's body clock.

D22 O01–O05 concern real source/search failures. Where an existing owner contract already qualifies staff attention, its source-owned local presentation remains separate from an email entitlement. A qualified no-artifact local occurrence uses `prepared.none@1`/local `available`, without a Resend call or invented provider state. Reading attention is not fixing the source/index problem.
