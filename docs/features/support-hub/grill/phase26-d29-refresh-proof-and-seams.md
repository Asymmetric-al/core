## The acceptance boundary

The existing rule is strict: **one Primary Outcome, certified Support handoff**. Admission creates the exact occurrence, released route, durable Support responsibility/receipt, permitted optional child intents and required dispatch obligations atomically, or none. Only that durable primary acceptance permits **Received**. A standalone CMS row, transient task/event or provider call does not suffice.

If the conversation row is materialized later, the accepted pending intake must be visible to the authorized recovery owner and materialize the same original source once. Original admission clocks and the accepted route remain stable even if a later release changes the destination. Reconciliation of an already accepted intent is not a new routing decision.

Use durable business-effect idempotency. Same intent and same canonical semantic input return the same privacy-safe outcome; changed semantic input conflicts. An ambiguous response triggers same-intent checking, not a new key, independent email fallback or duplicate submission. A separately and deliberately sent email is a separate genuine source; any later duplicate association follows reviewed source rules rather than automatic email-equality merging.

Browser receipt is independent of an optional acknowledgement. A transient optional child/provider outage cannot invalidate a sound primary acceptance or silently drop required durable recovery obligations. An actually invalid form/primary source or route remains fail-closed. This distinction is already required by R13/R16/R17/R25 and P18/P27/P36; the refresh adds no weaker fallback.

## Supabase/PostgreSQL and data invariants retained

- Use actual owner ID types and tenant-aware keys/relationships. Every source/occurrence/primary/child association must agree on its trusted tenant and exclusive producer branch. Nullability distinguishes a genuine guest actor from a trusted authenticated actor; never manufacture a staff author or CRM identity for a guest.
- Trusted server context derives tenant, actor, purpose, source branch and admitted route. Submitted reply email/message/name are untrusted content, not actor or role claims. Preserve accepted attribution and route evidence rather than accepting mutable caller fields.
- Exclusive public versus app source combinations and unique durable effects must be enforced at the database/authoritative command boundary. Do not rely on a component convention or a client-hidden input to make invalid states impossible.
- Scrutinize effective grants, RLS old-row `USING`, new-row `WITH CHECK`, column privileges, unique/check/foreign-key/delete behavior, RPCs/views/definer functions, storage and privileged service paths together. An allowed update must not transform an allowed row into a forbidden source/actor/tenant state. UPDATE/ALL omitted `WITH CHECK` can inherit `USING`; missing syntax alone is not the identified defect. [PostgreSQL17 policy semantics](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html), [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security).
- No anonymous direct table DML/read or service-role shortcut may bypass the certified Contact command. Receipt lookup is narrowly scoped and privacy-safe; authentication to a CRM record alone cannot read an unrelated intake's body/metadata.
- Retain newline normalization and Unicode-scalar field limits separately from raw encoded-envelope bytes. The accepted 10,000/200-scalar and 256 KiB limits require actual native/enhanced encoding-boundary tests; they are product limits, not vendor capacity guarantees.
- Keep minimal authoritative receipt/recovery evidence separate from finite content custody under D16/D17. Erasure, retention, archival, revocation and backup restoration must not resurrect private answers, templates, tokens or hidden context. No attachment/money schema is introduced by this text-only intake.
- Source and authorization predicates need appropriate actual query plans/indexes under the declared two-tenant and skewed-load fixtures. Do not invent speculative aggregate tables, global caches or a separate contact ledger.

## Email Studio seam in full

**Browser receipt:** belongs to the Contact/Support acceptance journey. It is immediately persistent after durable primary acceptance and exists even when email confirmation is Off or unavailable. It is not a P17 system message, delivery event, human reply or CRM activity.

**Optional Visitor Acknowledgement:** reuse P23's zero-or-one child. The standard web-Contact profile starts **Off**. Its sole enable/disable authority is the P23 Contact form / Route Plan Confirmation setting; Email Studio publication alone does not enable it. Enabling it requires an explicitly qualified **web-Contact source, recipient and P17 profile**; reusing D13's incoming-email source key or a generic conversation-created hook is forbidden. Copying wording does not transfer source/recipient authority. The submitted endpoint may have no CRM Party, so its owner-qualified no-Party recipient branch must be real rather than fabricated.

**P17 / Email Studio owns** approved wording, presentation, allowed personalization, sender/Reply-To authority and immutable prepared material through its closed manifest/profile contract. The content is a concise acknowledgement; it does not echo the visitor's answer, private context, CRM details or credentials, add CC, promote fundraising or introduce AI. Maintenance links to the real Email Studio entry rather than exposing a second template editor.

**P6 owns** the one qualified send/retry/reconciliation path. A provider's idempotency window is not the lifetime of Core occurrence/effect identity. Resend currently documents a 24-hour key window; D29's separate 24-hour courtesy policy is a product guard across sources, not a provider guarantee. [Resend idempotency](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys).

**Utility and courtesy:** preserve the original form-admission **15-minute** acknowledgement utility and a common tenant/qualified-mailbox **24-hour** courtesy guard across D13 incoming email and D29 web contact. Source, route, form, privacy and contactability fences remain current. Delayed materialization/retry never restarts the utility clock or creates a new courtesy identity.

**Full human precedence:** D13-R15 applies both before and after acknowledgement might have submitted. A later qualifying human reply for the **same current request and exact confirmation recipient** ends all further acknowledgement provider-call/decrypt authority, including a nominally possible same-key follow-up. Already in-flight facts reconcile without claiming recall. Human delivery failure does not revive the acknowledgement. This is a business-effect guard, not a UI check.

**History and copies:** Recent sent copy remains **Off-only** for this automatic profile. Show a body-free **Automatic confirmation** event with its actual outcome. **Template used** is a synthetic preview, not a retained generated subject/body. Protected execution material follows its finite custody/disposal contract. Never insert a permanent automatic body as a Support message or CRM note merely to make the timeline look full.

**First human reply:** Support owns the actual requester source, state and response endpoint; P17/D4 must qualify a genuine form-response branch and real outgoing lineage. Continue an actual compatible acknowledgement thread if established; otherwise start the first genuine outbound email. Never invent an incoming provider ID or RFC Message-ID. Later real incoming replies use existing D1/D2/D25 correlation. A different submitted endpoint does not silently update CRM or grant disclosure rights.

**Service and feedback semantics:** D14's first owed reply starts from actual form admission, not worker materialization. Guides and automatic receipts earn no human-response credit. D28 feedback requires actual qualifying human public correspondence and every other eligibility guard. Support closing does not prove a giving or care action completed.
