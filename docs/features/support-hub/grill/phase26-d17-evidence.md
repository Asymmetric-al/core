## Legal and technical qualification

[FTC minimization guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business) supports documented justified retention. [IRS exempt-organization guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/eo-operational-requirements-recordkeeping-requirements-for-exempt-organizations) supports preserving required underlying records, not every Support copy. [Current CPPA law/regulations](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/) and the ratified [D16 US assessment](phase26-d16-us-legal-and-provider-evidence.md) preserve actual applicability and exceptions. No new tenant-specific legal fact, mandatory global period or compliance certification was established. Existing source obligations and the tenant's approved purpose determine real settings and retained-class horizons.

[PostgreSQL 17 row-security documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html) distinguishes grants, existing rows, resulting-row checks and privileged bypass. The local major 17 configuration is not proof of production engine or effective policies. All grants, functions, service paths and storage need the actual negative tests specified in D17.

[TC39 timezone documentation](https://tc39.es/proposal-temporal/docs/timezone.html) and [ZonedDateTime arithmetic](https://tc39.es/proposal-temporal/docs/zoneddatetime.html#add) explain calendar versus elapsed arithmetic and ambiguous local times. The selected design uses explicit gap-forward/earlier-overlap resolution for calendar Months/Years in a frozen tenant IANA zone, with materialized UTC deadlines. Days use elapsed time: a calendar-day add helper can yield 23 or 25 hours and is not the selected Days contract. These references do not require installing Temporal or establish that an existing Core helper implements the exact semantics.

[W3C error prevention](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/error-prevention-legal-financial-data.html) and [status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html) support meaningful review/confirmation and accessible async outcomes. They do not prove a rendered UI or require typed phrases/dual approval for every setting. The complete UX has actual keyboard/AT/mobile and comprehension proof obligations.
