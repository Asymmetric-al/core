## Final independent corrections that are included in ratification

**F01 — Control ACK and projection separation:** preserve the actual data/runtime rule and **P07**. An older-started valid control response's accepted sequence/window is processed independently from its superseded embedded peer snapshot. Conversely, accepting an ACK cannot replace a newer peer projection. Both paths retain current context/session/instance checks. This avoids losing the next valid window or reviving stale display through ordinary permitted concurrency.

**F02 — Telemetry versus protected backup retention:** preserve **P29/P34** and R20. Logs, traces, errors, ordinary exports and rate-limit diagnostics omit draft/name/raw-topic content. Protected platform backups may retain only the declared minimal technical rows under their actual retention/access policy; they are not a staff history and cannot revive activity after restore. Row cleanup does not promise immediate deletion from backups. No unlogged-storage requirement is introduced.

## Exact glossary definitions

**Composing cue:** A brief indication that another currently authorized staff member has recently worked on a private reply or note for the permitted Support source. Avoid: draft preview, work reservation, verified continuous typing.

**Composition episode:** A period of a staff member's recent deliberate reply or note work, ending when they stop, leave or are no longer currently eligible to compose. Avoid: shared draft, conversation ownership, proof of sending.

**Composing awareness availability:** Whether the current reader can obtain sufficiently current permitted composition cues. Avoid: staff availability, absence of other work, sending permission.
