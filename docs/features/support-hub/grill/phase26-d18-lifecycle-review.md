## Finding and recommendation

**Accept with required amendments.** B supplies useful personal reusable phrasing while preserving maintained team guidance. A, shared-only, is the strongest simpler alternative: it avoids personal duplication and departure custody but requires all durable reuse to go through maintainers. The additional personal scope is justified by the founder's chosen flexible working experience. C broad shared publication would trade faster collaboration for greater scope leakage and churn; it is not required for B.

The permanent answer is one qualified Email Studio Saved Sections owner model with personal or shared availability, immutable saved/published revisions, governed destination copies and the existing proportional publication policy. It is not a new template type in the system-message catalog, a macro/workflow engine, a CRM data store, a global user notebook or live synchronized fragments.

## Exact proposed clauses for the root synthesis

### L01 — One qualified owner with separate use and publication meanings

“Saved reply wording is an Email Studio authoring asset with tenant ownership and an explicit My or Shared availability scope. My use/edit is scoped to its current authorized tenant principal; Shared availability uses named owner-defined access and publishing capabilities. Scope and principal are derived by the server; nullable owner fields, staff assignment, CRM ownership and supplied IDs never confer access. A saved reply is eligible wording, not a sent message or a live system-message contract. Qualification reuses the canonical bounded structured document/compiler and existing owner publication policy without adding a catalog key, layout role or parallel HTML renderer.”

### L02 — Personal save and shared publication

“My authoring saves a validated immutable reusable revision and advances the actor's current usable head atomically. Invalid autosaved work may remain a labelled draft but cannot replace the last valid usable revision. Personal saving is neither shared publication nor permission to send; D4 revalidates inserted content and the actual audience. Shared editing uses existing Draft, Needs review, Changes requested and Published semantics as applicable. Ordinary authorized publishers may self-publish after the existing validation/diff/impact gate; protected or tenant-stricter publication needs the existing independent review. A Support-only second approval for every ordinary edit is prohibited.”

Potential qualification detail: a personal item is an authoring input, not an independently published protected system artifact. Content carrying owner-protected semantics must not use the personal scope to evade those constraints. Personal free prose remains subject to the same restrictions as a deliberately typed human reply, not a new requirement to publish each human sentence.

### L03 — Snapshot contribution, no live personal dependency

“Offer to Shared copies one exact validated personal revision and its eligible generalized assets into a distinct destination-owned candidate and records the source revision. It does not publish or mutate the personal source. A contributor sees the exact proposed wording and destination scope before offering. The authorized maintainer reviews that snapshot; later personal edits do not alter it or invalidate it merely because the personal head moved. Offer updates are explicit new candidate revisions and stale any review. Request changes and withdraw before publication are ordinary owner lifecycle transitions; publish-versus-withdraw serializes. After publication, the contributor cannot unpublish by deleting or withdrawing the personal item. The publicized shared item remains tenant-managed when the author leaves.”

Duplicate retry uses one durable contribution identity and exact source/destination hash; changed payload conflicts, deliberately offering a changed candidate is new. Publication may occur against an existing shared item only after deliberate target choice, expected head check and before/after review. Never silently deduplicate by identical title/body, replace another author's asset, or keep personal/shared copies synchronized.

### L04 — Maintain common guidance without encouraging clones

“Use the current Shared item directly for maintained common instructions and keep brief stylistic personal wording separately. Personal variants are independent copies and never called live inheritance. When a personal item was deliberately copied from Shared, retain safe origin revision and surface a quiet update-available clue in management/preview, not repeated interruption on every send. An explicit update shows the difference, preserves personal modifications and never overwrites the item automatically. Names, copy counts and update availability are returned only while their underlying scope remains authorized.”

This is a modest optional convenience; parent may omit the update clue if it would introduce a graph. The essential clause is no hidden fan-out and no false “up to date” claim. Do not build general three-way semantic text merging or policy drift detection.

### L05 — Exact insertion and undo

“One picker searches eligible current revisions and exposes My/Shared, intended audience mode and language. Preview pins the exact item/revision; insert re-proves its current eligibility. A newly published ordinary revision is not silently substituted after the user selected another preview. Insert at the current selection/caret without replacing unrelated draft content or recipients/status. The author may deliberately insert multiple short sections. Replacing the whole body is separately labelled and Undo restores only the affected draft state while respecting newer safety/source restrictions. Undo is never a send recall, source archive restore, or rollback of another author's concurrent edits.”

If caret/selection changes while the picker is open, apply to its tracked current document position or show the actual insertion target; do not replace a stale text range in a newer draft. Complete body versus short section is descriptive guidance, not separate engines or layouts.

### L06 — Complete but bounded content forms

“Support reusable content may be short phrases, multi-paragraph explanations, structured steps, request-for-information wording, safe next-action instructions, and approved general reference material, all using the same bounded semantic document. Tone variants and locales are explicit authored choices, not runtime AI rewrites. A complete reply body still cannot replace sender framing, subject, audience, reply routing, legal/financial facts or protected actions. No arbitrary executable expressions, record paths, remote-fetch blocks, embedded forms, custom HTML/CSS, or actions on insertion are admitted.”

If internal-note reuse is adopted, it needs an explicit internal-only usage class in the same library, and mode-filtered insertion: no note inserted into Reply, no Reply template silently switches to Internal note, no asset may contain both external and internal payload and choose by client boolean. An external-safe item may be copied into an internal note deliberately without implying external disclosure authority. The parent should decide whether internal-note wording is earned scope; it is not proved by today's D4 external human-reply contract.

### L07 — Locale revisions are explicit, never mixed or machine-certified

“Each locale variant has exact structured content, canonical locale/direction, revision and the relevant validation/review. Updating one language does not assert all translations are updated. Group variants under one conceptual saved reply only where the owner model can identify their relationships without shared live text. The picker makes the selected language visible and lets the author choose another; a different language is not silently substituted or auto-translated. Language readiness and the recipient's preference are distinct. Required current CRM fact resolution remains D4's exact-audience operation, not a library preview with real recipient data.”

No inheritance of user interface language as proof of recipient language. Bare “English” text with placeholder syntax must never be silently classified as universally locale-neutral. Plain text, RTL/mixed script, international names, Unicode search and long text are proof requirements, not new display settings.

### L08 — Ordinary retirement differs from safety restriction

“Archive removes the item from new selection, shortcut resolution, favorites/recent results and new reuse, with an explicit authorized restore path. Archiving does not delete inserted text, prepared messages or history and does not pretend to recall mail. Restoration re-proves current schema, scope, assets and safety before making a compatible revision selectable. Ordinary revision replacement leaves existing pinned drafts intact. Where the owner's retirement policy disallows new preparation from the archived revision, show a precise repair choice; do not silently replace it. A real safety restriction uses the existing P17 quarantine effect and D16 source restrictions; it can block affected unsubmitted content without rewriting accepted or indeterminate mail.”

Important simplification choice for parent: use Archive as removal from discoverability/selection while a separate safety restriction governs dangerous already-inserted content. Avoid unexplained blocking of safely authored drafts merely because a maintainer tidied the library; if parent chooses archive blocks all new preparations, label that impact before archive and provide a clean re-author path rather than silently changing pins. Whichever owner-qualified policy is selected must be exact, not left implementation-dependent.

### L09 — Permission loss, departure and administrative custody

“Every library read, preview, insert, edit, sharing, publication and asset retrieval re-proves current tenant and scope authority. Permission loss immediately stops unauthorized new use and evicts previews/search/favorite hints on the next authorized operation or refresh; a cached item is not authority. D4 independently re-proves send authority and source-dependent disclosure at preparation/dispatch. Already inserted ordinary safe authored text is not erased solely because a personal owner leaves or library browsing permission changes; current source/revocation/asset rules govern its safe use. Real recipient material cannot be laundered through this rule.”

“Offboarding disables personal availability; it does not publish, transfer to another tenant, null ownership into Shared, or cascade-delete needed provenance. Existing tenant records policy names the accountable custodian, permitted administrative review capability, retention horizon and final disposal. A custodian may deliberately create a reviewed Shared candidate from eligible material; this is not blanket permission for supervisors to browse others' My replies. Reinstatement does not silently restore disposed material, override a restriction, or assume a deleted membership is the same principal assignment.”

Personal belongs to current tenant work, not secret personal storage. A common management capability must not imply public export of all personal content. Explicitly qualify any administrative visibility separately from ordinary shared maintenance.

### L10 — No transcript laundering

“New saved reply starts with clean generalized wording, synthetic fixtures and safe typed variables. Do not save a conversation, sent reply, rendered CRM values, protected link or uploaded case file into the reusable library by one click. Any future Save selection shortcut requires exact source lineage, generalized destination content, owner-authorized retention and D16/D17 correction/expiry behavior before activation. Removing a provenance link, retitling content or moving My to Shared does not turn restricted source material into permanent general content.”

No need for perfect AI/regex redaction. The reliable bounded launch is deliberate clean authoring, safe placeholder controls and generalized reference assets. Manual independent retyping is not claimed to be detectable from lineage; human report/correction and owner privacy handling cover that residual risk. Source-dependent quotes preserve their authority and cannot remain usable after expiry just because copied into a draft.

### L11 — Governed assets and realistic attachment behavior

“Only qualified tenant-owned general reusable assets may be associated with the wording, with allowed usage, immutable asset revision and current safety/retention constraints. Actual donor receipts, financial exports, recipient-specific files, protected action tokens and conversation uploads are ineligible library reference assets. The library preview uses inert safe rendering and synthetic context. Insertion reviews eligible attachment occurrences; it cannot silently add confidential files or reuse an expired URL. Sharing creates destination-governed asset references/copies under current authority, not hotlinks into the author's personal area. Changing or deleting the personal source cannot break a published Shared asset. Physical byte deduplication cannot widen access or conflate owner retention.”

The UI should list a small readable attachment summary in preview and composer, preserve filename safety, report blocked assets next to the affected item, and allow insertion of safe wording without an optional unavailable attachment only through an explicit choice. Required attachments never disappear silently. No new library cloud drive or PDF editor is necessary.

### L12 — Shortcuts/preferences are pointers, not authority or separate copies

“Favorites, recents and optional shortcut aliases point to eligible stable items in the current tenant/user scope. They never store a second full body. Renaming a title does not silently rebind an existing shortcut to another item. Duplicate visible aliases require explicit disambiguation or a declared server-enforced scope rule; never silently prefer a personal override to shared policy text. Deleted, archived, restricted or inaccessible targets disappear or produce a neutral unavailable state without displaying stale snippets. Changing tenant cannot carry usable pointers or cached personal content across tenants.”

An optional slash-search workflow should open the same picker rather than run automatically while typing ordinary syntax, URLs or non-English input. No keyboard binding may hijack screen-reader/IME/browser behavior. No silent expansion/send when a complete alias is typed and space/Enter means ordinary writing.

### L13 — Atomic changes, recovery and idempotency

“All content-head and publication transitions use expected revision and current scope/capability checks at the single owner mutation boundary. A stale write returns compare/reload/save-a-new-copy without clobbering either person's text. Duplicate Save/Offer/Publish/Archive intent with identical input reconciles the existing effect; changed input conflicts. The server atomically records the immutable revision/current head/required history and any durable secondary work. A post-commit lost response is reconciled by identity, not retried with a new asset ID. Failed sharing or asset qualification exposes no partial Shared item. Short database transactions use consistent resource ordering and bounded retry of fully aborted transactions, not locks held while a human reviews.”

Missing picker connectivity must not block an otherwise-qualified plain reply. Failed library rendering or an unavailable selected asset is a content-specific issue; it must not silently use cached HTML, select another template, or rerender sealed mail. Saved draft recovery preserves current source restrictions and no durable browser archive of sensitive content is introduced.

### L14 — Portability, migration and retirement

“Reuse the governed P17 native package/conversion lane for eligible generalized sources and assets. No ad hoc CSV/full-personal-library export or provider-template copy path is introduced. Export explicitly scopes eligible personal/shared material under current capability and retention; import creates destination drafts with new owner authority and explicit My/Shared destination, not automatic publication, approvals, source principal impersonation or cross-tenant live sync. Unsafe foreign content remains isolated with truthful conversion/loss and purge evidence.”

“Inventory current support_canned_responses and every route/hook/macro caller. Legacy nullable owner, raw text/HTML, agent cascade and shortcut uniqueness are not translated mechanically into trusted personal assets. Preserve legacy evidence under bounded read-only custody, derive ownership only from reliable records or require authorized disposition, convert safe content through the shared compiler into unpublished qualified candidates, and fence old writers before exposing the new lifecycle. Mixed-version code must reject unsupported new scope/revision states rather than defaulting them to Shared. Rollback retains immutable evidence and must not reactivate the old permissive path.”

### L15 — Low-noise maintenance and honest metrics

“Each Shared item has accountable current stewardship, visible last reviewed/updated facts and a direct report-an-issue or propose-improvement path within existing owner attention. Usage means a clearly defined insertion or actual send attribution, never both silently; edited text and multiple sections do not imply exact verbatim use or answer quality. Aggregate only authorized information and avoid personal staff ranking. Maintenance should expose stale/broken/unused items in the existing library, not add another inbox, reminder engine, automatic publication bot or optimization dashboard without demonstrated need.”

## Follow-up clarification requested by parent: personal save, global review and modes

The parent candidate includes Reply and Internal note as one declared mode per item, and a narrow owner-qualified Fill-in field whose missing required value blocks Send or Add note without a CRM write. This is coherent if library insertion itself never switches the current composer mode and the shared human-content preparation contract is qualified for each mode; complete body, phrase and steps remain content forms rather than new artifact types.

**Exact publication boundary:** “Save to My replies commits a server-validated immutable authoring revision usable only by its current authorized author in that tenant. It is not a publication and grants no live template or dispatch authority, so the tenant's another-reviewer-for-every-publication setting does not add a reviewer to this save or to ordinary human composition. Publish to Shared is an owner publication and obeys the existing standard/protected/tenant-stricter floor. My items admit only qualified reusable human wording; they cannot contain or impersonate protected artifact/system-message/layout sources, recipient-specific protected actions/facts, or known protected dependency material stripped of its provenance. Copying a protected source into My cannot lower its floor or authorize prohibited content. All insertion/preparation/send and source-safety fences still apply.”

This separates publication policy from composition capability. It does not assert perfect semantic detection of freely retyped misleading prose; those remain ordinary human-composition content risks governed by the owning operational/legal truth, qualified controls and correction process. The decision must not make My replies the back door for executing a template that the author could not publish.
