**12 September 2026. Founder selected C, extending the ratified public Guidance scope to public and internal staff guidance.** The exact amendments below are recommendations for the selected-answer review, not already ratified additions. The earlier D27 public-source contract remains the baseline except where this explicit scope revision changes it. Q28 feedback is unanswered and paused. **Independent disposition: accept C with required amendments.** Keep the public half of D27 intact. Add a narrowly owned, maintained **Internal guide** source for staff consultation in the same working context. Internal guidance is not another kind of canned reply, a private Web Studio Page or an executable procedure.

## Strongest permanent approach

Support owns internal procedural guidance as tenant operational content, using shared rich-text/editor, identity/access, revisions, audit, asset and search mechanisms where qualified. It is a bounded source capability available through the existing Guidance surface. Public guidance continues through the P23/P24 published-content owner. Email Studio remains the owner of reusable message wording, signatures, purpose-qualified message validation, publication and preparation; it does not become the publisher of every internal procedure merely because both surfaces use Tiptap.

For this scope, internal guides should be **consultation-only**. Staff reads the current permitted procedure, returns to the same conversation and uses the real owning-domain controls to do the work. There is no Insert into reply, Insert into note, Quote article, attach guide, automatic citation, completion checklist, linked case-progress record or one-click conversion to a public Page/Saved reply. D18's existing Saved notes still handle repeatable note wording. A worker can record what actually happened through the existing note command without copying the procedure into every case.

An internal-guide reference in a note is not necessary to satisfy C's stated need. Adding a first-class reference would require current source authorization on every historical note render, title/disclosure rules, source-version versus current-version semantics, source retirement/deletion handling and a new document compatibility path. That may later be justified by a demonstrated traceability need; it should not be included merely because references are technically possible. No per-conversation “guide used” record or compliance attestation is required just to read guidance.

## Exact recommended amendments

### C-O01 — Two source purposes, one working context

**Material concern: yes; high severity, high likelihood without an explicit split.** Calling all results “articles” and giving them the same insert action risks sending a staff procedure or pretending a CMS draft is approved internal content.

> Guidance SHALL distinguish Public guidance from Internal guides by source kind and permitted action, not merely color or a user-editable display tag. Public guidance retains all applicable ratified D27 public eligibility/link/withdrawal requirements. An Internal guide is a tenant operational Support source for permitted staff consultation, never a public Page, Saved reply, Saved note, signature or executable workflow. Changing source kind in place SHALL NOT be allowed. A separately authored public Page or reusable wording candidate requires its actual owner's deliberate publication and disclosure review; no automatic conversion is provided.

**Proof:** Equal titles and the same human author do not merge identities, actions, audience or source authority. Internal source material never reaches the public reader, public search/sitemap, public Guidance insertion, signature publication or requester-visible outputs.

### C-O02 — Name the internal owner without a generic knowledge platform

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Reusing CMS draft access or Email Studio Saved Sections would leave source currentness, permission and lifecycle under the wrong authority.

> Support SHALL own the logical internal guide, declared staff audience, exact locale, draft/current published revision, lifecycle, author/publisher attribution and safe source history. Use shared Core mechanisms for identity, rich authoring, audit, search and qualified assets; source business rules stay at one operational owning boundary. Do not introduce a second CRM, cross-schema body synchronization, authenticated public-CMS variant, external knowledge connector, AI index or generic document/workflow platform.

**Proof:** Source authorization works independently of a currently open conversation and is preserved across both Support and CRM entry. Shared implementation reuse does not create two write authorities or an unqualified CMS backdoor.

### C-O03 — Consultation-only is complete for the chosen need

**Material concern: yes; medium severity, high likelihood of unnecessary expansion.** Inserting procedure bodies/references into every case adds clutter, historical permission dependencies and apparent compliance evidence without a demonstrated need.

> Internal guides SHALL be readable in the same Guidance context with a clear Internal — staff guidance indication and a preserved return path. No native insert, quote, attach, automatic note citation, per-case checklist or guide-completion action is included. Public sources retain their qualified Insert link action. D18 Saved notes remain available for reusable note wording; actual notes record the worker's relevant observations/actions through their existing command. Reading guidance SHALL NOT create a conversation event, add an audit claim of procedure compliance, satisfy a reply target or advance work.

An ordinary authenticated navigation link to a guide, where the product exposes its own source detail, grants no access and carries no capability token or copied content. This is not a new embeddable note-reference feature. A future first-class reference must separately earn its source/history/disclosure semantics; it is not needed to complete this selected consultation scope.

**Proof:** A user can read an internal procedure, preserve a half-written reply, perform an independently authorized owner action and return without any inserted internal body, new note or manufactured completed step.

### C-O04 — Draft save and deliberate internal publication

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Autosaving an unfinished correction directly over the staff-visible procedure may cause someone to act on partial or contradictory instructions.

> Creating/editing SHALL produce a private authorized authoring candidate. Autosave/Save draft persists a revision-checked checkpoint; it does not release content to ordinary guide readers. Publish internally deliberately makes one validated complete revision available to its declared current audience. An existing published revision remains available while a replacement draft is edited, unless current source safety requires restriction. Publication checks expected head, tenant/source identity, audience, locale, allowed structure and assets, then atomically records current publication and attributable outcome. Ambiguous save/publication resolves the durable receipt; stale clients cannot overwrite newer work.

Use the existing P12 capability framework with deliberately qualified guide read/maintain/publish scopes and tenant oversight. Inbox assignment, held-mail review, CRM ownership, Email Studio asset access and broad “staff” status do not grant these rights by implication. One authorized maintainer may author and publish ordinary guidance; additional review applies only when an actual configured policy requires it. Do not invent a two-person rule merely because a generic guide describes a money-related workflow. Source safety restriction and membership revocation must remain possible without waiting for a staffing replacement or review quorum.

**Proof:** Autosaved but unpublished text is absent from ordinary results, snippets and open reader refresh; two publishers cannot both replace the same head. Publisher offboarding removes authority without destroying valid shared sources or history.

### C-O05 — Internal audience and current reads

**Material concern: yes; critical severity, medium likelihood.** A worker's case access does not imply permission to read every internal procedure, and an internal title or count may itself disclose sensitive operations.

> Declare the guide's staff audience through the existing qualified tenant/team/capability model. Source read authority SHALL govern list membership, search terms/snippets/counts, direct navigation, body/assets, retained revisions and exports before exposure. The current conversation, linked CRM Party, support handler and source author are not substitute access grants. Audience narrowing/security restriction immediately denies later reads and invalidates relevant projections/client state; old publication or membership snapshots are not lasting authority. Do not expose hidden guide names through an unavailable-source message.

Generic support guidance must not become a repository of live donor, missionary, care, credential or case data. Author from generic/synthetic content. Any actual source-owned sensitive material requires its separately qualified owning purpose and access; “Internal” alone is not that permission. The permanent initial contract need not add ad hoc per-record sharing or ABAC expressions.

**Proof:** Same-tenant unauthorized staff and revoked members cannot see hidden hits or cached bodies; permitted staff sees the same current source through both surfaces. Another person's CRM access is not granted by the guide.

### C-O06 — Shared Tiptap, distinct internal source profile

**Material concern: yes; high severity, high likelihood if toolbar restrictions are treated as enforcement.** Unknown nodes can be stripped during parsing, while visually hidden features remain schema-admissible.

> Reuse D23's shared Tiptap infrastructure with a code-owned Internal guide semantic profile and original-JSON validation at its source command. Start with useful headings, paragraphs, emphasis, ordinary lists and safely validated informational navigation. A procedure checklist is written guidance, not persisted per-case progress. Any qualified static image uses its actual private asset owner; no raw HTML, scripts, forms, embeds, arbitrary remote fetches, template variables, live CRM values, mentions-as-notifications or workflow commands are enabled by the guide editor. Unknown source schema/attributes fail the affected writable path without stripped autosave; migration produces a reviewable new candidate.

The source reader uses safe static rendering. Neither CMS article conversion nor a live editor is required to consult a guide. Email Studio's server compiler remains authoritative for actual message preparation; the internal guide source is not a new P17 template/publication purpose just because it shares semantic editor primitives.

**Proof:** Unsupported internal canonical material survives for authorized repair rather than becoming apparently valid stripped content. Copying a guide's JSON into a Reply schema cannot admit internal source metadata, executable controls or protected values.

### C-O07 — Disclosure prevention with honest limits

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Removing an Insert button reduces a common mistake but cannot guarantee that a person never types internal information into an external reply.

> Known internal-only source material and recognized Internal guide references SHALL remain non-disclosable through external Reply, shared Reply wording, signature, preview/test/preparation, portable publication or requester export paths. Preserve existing D18/D23 controlled copy/paste, purpose and server validation where an actual source is known; changing mode, display label, a client flag or stripping metadata within a controlled Asym operation is not declassification. Consultation-only adds no native guide-copy/insert route and no internal-guide provider material family. It does not require inspecting every arbitrary URL or detecting every independently retyped sentence.

Do not disable ordinary selection, accessibility tools or browser copy and call that a confidentiality boundary. Plain-text clipboard transfer through another application, screenshots or deliberate retyping can lose provenance; no complete prevention claim is justified. The real guarantees are current source authorization, clear internal/external actions, no sanctioned internal export into reply, rejection of recognized internal material, source-safe established copy paths and actual owner permission at consequential actions. A narrowly recognizable app-owned internal-route purpose can be denied by existing external-link validation without creating a global URL registry.

**Proof:** Native controlled copies/Undo cannot launder a known internal source into a Reply; external HTML/plain text and query/referrer telemetry contain no internal guide identifiers/title/body/token. Separately test the documented provenance-loss limitation rather than claiming perfect DLP.

### C-O08 — Guidance explains actions; owning domains authorize them

**Material concern: yes; critical severity, medium likelihood.** “Follow these steps to issue a refund” could be confused with a permission grant or completed action, especially after role changes.

> Internal procedure text SHALL NOT authorize or execute CRM, giving, identity, financial, missionary, care, routing or support work mutations. Ordinary safe navigation may open the actual owner surface with preserved context; the destination independently proves current tenant/record/actor/approval authority. Opening a link is navigation, never a side effect on GET, an executable macro or proof a step was completed. Guide content may describe the process but cannot substitute for the current code-owned requirements, approvals, protected facts or source result. No live action buttons, preauthorized recipient/data payloads or embedded workflow interpreter are added.

**Proof:** A user may read a general procedure but still be denied a particular record/action; no source role or guide publication lifts that denial. Reading/linking guidance creates no receipt/refund/correction, last-contact credit, completed task or automatic requester email.

### C-O09 — Publication, archive and safety have distinct lifecycles

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** Routine retirement could be mistaken for erasure, or a restored draft could silently become a valid current procedure.

> Preserve draft, published current revision, ordinary Archived state and current safety/access restriction as distinct facts. Archive removes the guide from ordinary use without deleting required provenance; management can inspect eligible retained revisions under its own rights with clear archived/noncurrent labels. Restore requires current ownership, audience, source/schema/asset validation and deliberate new publication where needed. Restoring a prior revision creates a new reviewed candidate; it never removes intervening history or revives revoked access/disposed bytes. Ordinary editorial changes use new revisions. An open reader must distinguish its loaded revision from a newer current source without silently changing instructions mid-task; adverse access/safety changes take precedence over this continuity.

Consultation alone creates no prepared delivery, so internal-guide retirement/safety withdrawal cannot globally fence Support email. Existing actual known copies, if admitted through an independently qualified existing source-copy path, retain that path's D16/D17/P17 restriction and cleanup requirements. Public D27 source references in an email retain their already ratified source/publication/continuity/fence semantics; internal guide availability does not become another prerequisite for them.

**Proof:** Ordinary archive, material withdrawal, audience narrowing, source restore and stale favorable events have different truthful results. No old internal publication, body cache or editor recovery overwrites current restriction; unrelated public replies continue.

### C-O10 — Minimized retention and operational proof

**Material concern: yes; high severity, medium likelihood.** An internal knowledge source could become a permanent cache of incident/case data or turn every guide read into surveillance.

> Active generic internal guidance may have a continuing documented source purpose. Draft, superseded, archived, deleted, asset, index/cache and audit classes require declared finite retention/final disposition under their real owners before activation; immutability is not indefinite readable retention. Guide content is not governed by one conversation's idle timer, and consulting it does not renew D17 conversation content. Minimal body-free source change/audit facts are distinct from ordinary reads, raw queries and article bodies. Reuse existing privileged/export/security auditing; no mandatory per-read staff activity metric, certification event or copied guide transcript is introduced.

No universal retention number is established by vendor behavior. Actual class limits and restore barriers must be registered and tested before activation, including safe disposal of abandoned authoring/assets and prevention of restored restricted content. P17 sealed execution material, Recent-copy URL exclusions and body-free provider reconciliation remain unchanged. Q28 feedback is not answered by inventing guide ratings, deflection tracking or a new maintenance survey in this review.

**Proof:** Restores reapply current owner restriction/deletion before exposure; source correction affects every known retained source derivative without reconstructing it from logs. Actual guides remain usable without indefinite authoring debris or permanent case-content copies.
