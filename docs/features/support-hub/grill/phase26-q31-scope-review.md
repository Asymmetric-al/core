## Proposed actual question and three fair options

**Question:** When staff set a follow-up reminder, how should Asym help them choose when it comes back?

All options retain the same single shared reminder, visible exact date/time/timezone, custom exact-time capability, current authorization and D3 lifecycle. These are three alternative picker scopes, not three tenant modes to build.

| Option                                             | What staff receive                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Strongest benefit                                                                                                                     | Main tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — A small consistent menu, plus Custom**       | A short product-maintained set of common time choices, each showing its actual resolved date/time, with Custom for a precise promise or unusual schedule. The same applicable choice experience is used across qualified reminder entry points. | Fast ordinary use with no personal setup or preset administration, while exact scheduling remains available.                          | A common menu will not perfectly match every worker's recurring schedule; those cases use Custom.                                                                                                                            |
| **B — Choose the exact date and time every time**  | Open the compact shared date/time picker directly, with the actual timezone and result clearly shown. No preset menu or saved-preset management.                                                                                                | Maximum explicitness and the smallest ongoing configuration surface; useful when reminders usually follow a particular promised time. | Repeated routine scheduling takes more date/time interaction than a useful quick choice.                                                                                                                                     |
| **C — Common choices plus personal saved choices** | Start with useful common options; staff may create, name, edit and remove their own reusable time choices through the qualified preference/control owner. Each use still resolves visibly to one shared conversation reminder.                  | Better fit for recurring shifts or follow-up rhythms without forcing one person's preferred menu on teammates.                        | Adds discoverability, save/edit/reset, lifecycle, compatibility and preference semantics that A does not need. Personal picker preferences must not become private reminders or alter already-scheduled shared due instants. |

**Single recommendation: A.** It completes a frequent existing control with low interaction cost and little maintenance. Custom remains the complete escape hatch; C is credible if repeated real staff use shows that the common choices are systematically inconvenient. No Asym study currently establishes that demand. B remains the strongest simpler implementation, but makes all ordinary scheduling pay the exact-entry cost.

Do not preselect a morning hour, precise quick-choice list/count, business-day skipping, personal-versus-tenant timezone precedence or reusable-preset limit in the question. Those are concrete consequences of the selected answer's full review. No natural-language parser, recurrence, automatic reminder on every Waiting change, new Send-and-remind action, AI scheduling or multi-reminder system is implied by A.

## Seams that remain unchanged

- **Support/D3:** owns the current shared reminder, validated intended due instant, work effects, generation/cancellation and auditable outcome. Shared time controls help choose an instant; they do not own Support work.
- **Workflow owner:** executes/reconciles the already-qualified due intent under current source authority; a menu option does not create a competing scheduler or Phase 34 rules engine.
- **CRM:** the same authorized Support detail and reminder are reachable through the accepted CRM journey. No copied CRM task, Party matching, finance/care update or last-contact event follows from selecting a time.
- **Email Studio/P17/P6:** choosing, saving, changing or clearing a reminder does not author, prepare, queue or send requester email. Existing qualified staff attention keeps its owner policy; the picker creates no new email family, recipient or schedule-to-email authority.
- **D14/D28/D30:** reminder choice does not pause targets, change feedback timing, rewrite report dates or remove overdue work from its authoritative review population.
