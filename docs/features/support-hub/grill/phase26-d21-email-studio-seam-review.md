## Exact clarification language for the ratification record

1. **Labels are not Email Studio content.** “Support owns its one flat tenant catalog and original-conversation memberships. Email Studio owns reusable wording and qualified presentation/preparation. Curating a shared vocabulary does not import Email Studio's Saved Sections, content schema, publication lifecycle, review permissions or template bindings. Ordinary label settings contain no template picker, Publish or Send control.” This restates D21-R02/R03/R07/R19 and preserves the [canonical message boundary](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0030-canonical-message-document-and-presentation-dependencies.md#L18).

2. **Ordinary operations stay quiet and content insertion stays content-only.** “Create, Edit, Apply, Remove, Archive and Restore cause only their explicit authorized metadata outcomes and minimized owner history. They do not publish wording, compose/send mail, change recipients, establish following or produce routine P6 communication history. D18 wording insertion cannot execute old macro label/status/assignment actions. D15 routine label changes remain quiet, and D10 merge/Undo does not generate synthetic label events.” This is D21-R10–R12/R19/R21, not removal of independently authorized ordinary conversation actions.

3. **The list is not a mail audience.** “Label-based search, D20 views, the D21 current-label report, drilldown and governed result export never infer a send audience or recipient authority. Opening a conversation from any of those surfaces merely changes navigation. Current conversation/source/recipient authorization is re-proved by the actual action owner. Labels do not establish requester identity, consent, proof of a refund, receipt issuance, priority, service target or restricted classification.” This restates R04/R15–R19. No label variable or new message-fact binding is admitted merely because catalog text exists.

4. **Actual email retains the normal sequence.** “When staff deliberately send an authorized reply, eligible D18 wording is copied into the current Support draft; P17 supplies the exact ready preparation before Support's atomic admission; P6 owns the sealed delivery envelope and actual evidence through the qualified tenant Resend connection. Label catalog state never substitutes for those authorities, selects another sender/provider account or rerenders already prepared material. A group is not resent once per member.” Human replies remain outside the finite system-message catalog while using its qualified authoring/preparation foundations. [Prepared-message ADR0032](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0032-immutable-prepared-message-and-whole-message-recovery.md#L19), [P6 A17/A18](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-06-shared-communication-event-model.md#L175), [tenant-owned Resend ADR0029](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0029-tenant-owned-resend-and-composed-delivery-identities.md#L18).

5. **The automatic acknowledgement remains separate.** “D13's Support request received template, original source eligibility and original fifteen-minute utility ceiling remain unchanged. Applying a label, repairing its catalog, restoring it or inserting saved wording cannot admit held input, restart the clock, confirm an identity or send an acknowledgement.” D19 intake authority is unchanged. No new Email Studio template is needed for label operations.

6. **Optional metadata outages stay local.** “An isolated catalog/picker/report failure or unknown label mutation outcome is visible beside that affected control. It does not disable an otherwise valid Reply, internal note or Resolve, discard a draft, clear existing chips to No labels or cause delayed label changes to replay under another tenant. Conversely, actual source/permission/preparation uncertainty still blocks its own consequential action. A label failure is neither a reason to bypass messaging safeguards nor a prerequisite to every conversation command.” This follows R01/R06/R10/R20 and the UX's explicit independent Reply/Resolve behavior. No P17 renderer or Resend readiness check is required just to manage ordinary labels.

7. **Operational attention requires a real independent source obligation.** “Use existing qualified operations and source-attention contracts for actual failures in O01–O05. Do not create per-label notice keys, reminders for optional sparse coverage or notifications for successful edits. If a qualified local occurrence is required, its actual source owner supplies the bounded episode, role-safe facts, destination, applicability/end and idempotency; P17 resolves presentation and P6 records local available with prepared.none@1. It creates no external artifact, provider ID or Resend call. Reading attention does not repair the underlying failure.” Existing [notification ADR0027](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0027-one-notification-presentation-and-engagement-model.md#L18) and [no-artifact posture](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0032-immutable-prepared-message-and-whole-message-recovery.md#L61) apply; this does not independently authorize new operational notification coverage.

8. **Metadata has its own qualified privacy lifetime.** “Label text and old names can be sensitive even when bodies are unavailable. D21 configuration, membership, change-history and receipt evidence use their actual finite owner purposes/horizons; they do not copy D17's ordinary body clock or assume permanence. Known source-derived metadata, copies, search/report results and notification previews remain subject to current restriction and disposal. Label edits do not renew content or metadata lifetime. Privacy disposal may end name reservation without allowing ID reuse or retargeting old references. No copying metadata into a draft, saved reply, general log or audit label creates a new independent retention authority.” This is the accepted D21-R05/R14/R21 and D18/D16/D17 copy-custody contract.

9. **Label permissions remain independent in both directions.** “A label curator need not be a reply author or Email Studio publisher; a publisher, Shared-view maintainer, assigned worker or intake reviewer does not thereby gain catalog maintenance. Source label application, catalog management, CRM disclosure and export remain separately authorized. A renamed label or archived catalog does not itself revoke a valid draft/send intent; the actual source/privacy/message owner can still invalidate its own dependent work.” This prevents an incidental metadata revision from becoming a new dispatch fence or a generic authority escalation.

## Proof mapping and limits

These clarifications add no proof-count inflation. Existing D21 P01/P02/P03 establish optionality and ownership; P12/P17/P25 establish capability and CRM separation; P18/P19/P22 establish topology and clock/notification invariants; P24/P26/P30/P34 establish source privacy, history, failure and restoration; P27 proves no publication/send/follow/recipient/confirmation/automation side effect. P33 closes old macro/direct-writer bypasses. D18's exact preparation/notes/P6 proof remains inherited, not replaced by label tests.

An implementation test should include an isolated label-service failure while a fully qualified draft can still send normally, and a separate source-permission failure where the same send is correctly denied. A no-op/replayed label operation creates neither duplicate metadata history nor communication history. If an existing qualified local repair occurrence is used, prove its source-only episode, local-only materialization and current role-safe end state instead of testing an invented label notification.

This review inspected accepted documentary and repository contracts, not a running compiler, API, database, provider, browser, migration or assistive-technology session. It introduces no new template, publication review, notification channel, provider dependency, role, CRM write or automation. The root ratification record should preserve those limits while marking the complete D21 package accepted.
