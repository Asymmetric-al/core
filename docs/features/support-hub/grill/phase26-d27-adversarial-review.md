# D27 — Find approved public guidance in Support

**Fully founder-ratified, 12 September 2026. D1–D27 and every adopted amendment are accepted.** The substantive review below is preserved from the answer stage; its earlier proposed/pending statements are historical. The full ratification appended below governs current status. This remains exploratory grooming, not formal specification, implementation or release proof.

The useful addition is a small, maintained **Guidance** finder inside the existing Support conversation. Staff can find a public page selected by their tenant, read a safe preview and deliberately insert its link into their current reply or internal note. The same experience works when that conversation is opened from CRM. Web Studio owns the page; Support owns its inclusion in the finder; Email Studio/P17 owns message preparation; P6 owns delivery.

This is stronger than B, maintaining links only in saved replies, when staff need to search current source guidance without reproducing links and descriptions across wording variants. B remains an adequate no-build alternative for a very small library. C, adding internal procedures, solves a different confidentiality and content-ownership problem and is not justified by this answer. A does not require another help center, private knowledge repository, AI, article quotation, public search redesign or generic workflow engine.

The user-established need is donor care for organizations supporting missionaries and mission projects. A request for instructions on finding a receipt is an **illustrative** journey, not verified research that every tenant has that exact page or workflow. Sharing instructions never proves a receipt was generated, a gift changed, identity verified or the Support obligation completed.

The [evidence report](phase26-d27-evidence.md) distinguishes current source, accepted planning in open PRs, external documented behavior, judgments and remaining release proof. The [UX blueprint](phase26-d27-ux-blueprint.md), [data and owner contract](phase26-d27-data-contract.md), and independent [data](phase26-d27-data-review.md), [UX](phase26-d27-ux-research.md) and [owner](phase26-d27-owner-review.md) reviews are part of this proposed package. The exact clauses below govern any ambiguity in exploratory alternatives in those research reports.

## Corrected decision to record

> Phase 26 provides a manually opened Guidance finder for tenant-curated references to currently qualified public Web Studio Pages. It uses the shared public-content owner, exact tenant/Site/Page/locale identity, current public-generation and destination proof, and one flat Support selection set. Staff preview safe public content and deliberately insert a frozen, source-qualified link into the existing Tiptap reply or internal-note draft. Support does not copy or publish articles. The same authorized detail and draft are used through CRM. Source changes, curation changes, preparation and sending have distinct effects; new public-use restrictions fence exact unsubmitted dependent messages through existing P17/P6 mechanisms. No live CMS fetch occurs at dispatch. The full requirements D27-R01–R30, category resolutions, UX/data contracts and required proof below accompany A.

## Exact amendments

### D27-R01 Purpose and scope

Guidance SHALL help authorized staff find selected public instructional Pages and share links while handling Support work. It SHALL NOT introduce internal guidance, AI retrieval/generation, automatic conversation-derived queries, article quotation, a public self-service surface, a second CMS, or new business actions. Future AI requires a separate researched decision and may consume only subsequently qualified owner contracts; no speculative vector store or agent framework is built now.

### D27-R02 Source identity and authority

Each selection SHALL reference the exact operational tenant, CMS owner namespace, Site, stable Page and locale lineage using their existing owner-defined identifier types. Environment isolation SHALL be preserved. Slugs, titles, login hosts, email domains, matching email addresses and stringified CMS tenant IDs SHALL NOT establish these identities. Operational-to-CMS references SHALL be validated soft references; no cross-schema FK or operational article-body copy is permitted.

### D27-R03 Current public eligibility

Selection, editorial read permission, a draft preview URL and a Payload published flag SHALL NOT individually certify public eligibility. The source owner SHALL supply current proof for the exact Page/Site/locale, public generation, public-purpose route, primary-domain authority and applicable safety state. Unknown proof SHALL be unavailable for new selection/insertion/preparation, with a truthful retry state. Public reads SHALL use the qualified public bridge even for privileged staff and SHALL NOT fall back to an editorial reader.

### D27-R04 Public reach and language

Both Listed publicly and owner-qualified Shared by link — public Page variants MAY be deliberately selected. Selection SHALL NOT add the latter to public search, navigation or sitemap, or represent them as private. Each Site/locale variant SHALL be selected explicitly. No implicit translation, language fallback, unrelated-site substitution or new translation admission is allowed. Existing owner-qualified same-source language alternatives remain deliberate, visibly language-labelled choices.

### D27-R05 One curated set and permissions

Use one flat tenant selection set. Existing P12/current Support shared-content management capabilities with tenant oversight SHALL govern adding/removing selections; no new role enum, inbox copies, approval chain or folder hierarchy is introduced. Ordinary authorized Support staff MAY consult the active public set; source-reference discovery and CMS editing/publication retain independent owner permissions. Curation SHALL NOT grant CMS write access or access to another tenant's administrative catalog.

### D27-R06 Selection lifecycle

Selection is Active or Removed, with immutable transition history and a current generation. Available, Unavailable and Checking are derived public-eligibility presentations, not client-writable publication states. Remove from guidance SHALL affect future finder offers, not unpublish the Page or revoke otherwise safe links already composed. Republication MAY restore an Active selection only after current qualification; it SHALL NOT restore a Removed selection. Restoring a CMS draft is not republication. Deliberate re-add uses the same stable selection identity and a new generation.

### D27-R07 Search contract

The finder SHALL search current selected eligible Page titles, summaries and the owner's eligible public textual content, in that relevance order with a stable tie-break. It SHALL reuse the qualified P23 search/read projection and current-proof checks, extending the shared owner interface only where necessary. It SHALL NOT use the latest Updates list, crawl URLs, filter an already capped public search page, or create a second authoritative text corpus. Body search is part of this chosen contract: an implementation lacking it SHALL NOT advertise complete Guidance search or activate the complete feature. Source content/keywords remain source-owned; no parallel Support alias taxonomy is added. The shared owner query SHALL cover the complete selected staff-purpose population, including eligible textual content of explicitly selected Shared-by-link variants, without admitting those variants to public discovery.

### D27-R08 Simple visible scope

The finder SHALL open manually with an empty query and a bounded browsable list. Provide Search guidance plus Language and Site filters only where meaningful choices exist. Initial scope MAY use an explicitly resolved conversation language/site; otherwise display All languages/All sites. Do not infer language or representation from names, addresses or CRM relationships. Visible chips, independent clearing, clear sorting and distinct empty/error states SHALL prevent hidden narrowing or silent broadening. Do not persist raw queries in shared views, URLs, analytics or durable search history.

### D27-R09 Passive preview

Preview SHALL use the source owner's safe public projection, show title, Site, language and public destination, and provide Open public page. It SHALL NOT mount a full interactive site, execute scripts/forms/donation widgets, fetch arbitrary embeds or load editor-only data inside staff context. Unsupported meaningful sections SHALL be identified with an explicit public-page action rather than silently omitted. Approved static media SHALL use owner delivery/privacy rules and lazy loading. Preview content is derived, never an editable Support article.

### D27-R10 Deliberate insertion

Insert link SHALL add the current human-readable source title at a valid caret. For an ordinary link-eligible text selection, the action SHALL read Link selected text and preserve the selected wording. It SHALL NOT silently replace selected text with the article title. A stale or unsupported selection SHALL return staff to a valid draft position without losing their text. One command inserts one source-qualified link; opening or previewing never inserts or sends.

### D27-R11 Draft and surface continuity

Guidance SHALL preserve the exact conversation, current private draft, Reply/Internal note purpose, recipients, cursor, signature and work state through preview, navigation, cancellation and retry. It uses D23's existing Tiptap profiles and D26's same inline/full-page Support detail through CRM. A read-only user MAY consult but cannot create or mutate a draft. No duplicate CRM composer, hidden second draft or insertion into a newly focused conversation is permitted. The intake brief is not extended with a new Guidance authoring workflow by this decision.

### D27-R12 Link representation and provenance

A Guidance occurrence SHALL contain frozen visible text/href plus minimal trusted source/selection/locale/destination qualification provenance in the existing typed document/dependency contract. No article body, requester data or private CMS record is embedded. External HTML/plain text SHALL contain ordinary accessible links without internal provenance or authorization tokens. Editing link text preserves applicable provenance. Changing its destination SHALL remove the assertion that the new URL is selected Guidance while retaining existing D18 restrictions on known source-derived material; client metadata is never proof.

### D27-R13 Editing, reuse and undo

Insert, Link selected text, Undo/Redo, copy, saved wording, draft recovery and normal document edits SHALL preserve applicable D18/D23 provenance and purpose rules. Saving a Guidance link in reusable wording SHALL use the existing wording owner and requalification at use, with no article clone. A removed finder selection does not retroactively prohibit safe saved wording. A source withdrawal cannot be bypassed by native Undo, source-metadata stripping or changing only link text. Arbitrarily manually retyped URLs remain ordinary links; this does not claim global detection or policing.

### D27-R14 Ordinary edits and route continuity

Routine Page title/body edits SHALL update future finder previews but SHALL NOT rewrite composed or sent wording/hrefs or require per-revision Support approval. Newly inserted links use the current source-owned canonical destination. An unchanged older href MAY remain usable only through owner-qualified same-Page, same-Site, exact-locale public-route continuity. A valid redirect to a different Page does not transfer the Guidance selection to that Page. New Guidance use/preparation then requires deliberate source reselection; no HTTP-200, title match or redirect-following heuristic substitutes for owner proof.

### D27-R15 Withdrawal and restoration

Unpublish, loss of public eligibility, unsafe destination or domain release without qualified same-source continuity SHALL stop new qualification and fence exact definitely-unsubmitted dependent sends through the owner's no-new-public-use disposition and existing P17 Revoke unsubmitted/safety epochs. Dangerous privacy/security withdrawal SHALL additionally preserve applicable known material restrictions. Already accepted or possibly submitted messages SHALL preserve immutable effect identity/content meaning and reconciliation under each owner's actual retention, including P17 purge of sealed provider-request material on qualified acceptance and body-free later evidence; no recall or indefinite byte custody is promised. Finder restoration requires current owner safety release/public proof and an Active selection. Existing copied/D18 links from a Removed selection MAY requalify from current same-source public/safety proof without restoring the finder selection. No restoration automatically revives a fenced or terminal prepared effect: current P17 recovery/successor preparation and authorized admission rules apply, never a stale favorable event.

### D27-R16 Email Studio and P6 seam

P17/Email Studio SHALL own source-aware wording/preparation, purpose profiles, signature placement, render validation, immutable prepared bytes and material/dependency fences; Support SHALL own draft/work authorization and send admission; P6 SHALL own transport/submission evidence/reconciliation. Add only the narrow Guidance-reference dependency family required by R12–R15. Source qualification occurs before preparation; admission/dispatch use current qualified local fences and existing atomic submission boundaries, with no CMS/URL/network fetch or rerender after sealing. Internal notes remain internal and do not enter P6.

### D27-R17 CRM and business boundaries

Opening Guidance, previewing a Page or inserting a link SHALL NOT create/link/merge a CRM Party, add a CRM Activity or last-contact event, change a financial record, satisfy identity verification or resolve a Support request. D9/D26 qualified CRM links and current joint authorization govern viewing the conversation. Requester, participant, authenticated user, Party, represented organization, handler and CRM owner remain distinct. Giving/contact/missionary actions remain deliberate owning-domain commands; guidance may explain them but cannot perform or certify them.

### D27-R18 Atomic curation and idempotency

One authorized owner command SHALL atomically admit the selection transition, trusted actor/time, expected generation, durable receipt, audit and required secondary-effect continuation. Concurrent adds cannot create duplicate active references; stale edits/removals/re-adds cannot overwrite newer intent. Retry keys bind the durable curation effect and payload digest, not one HTTP attempt. Source facts are qualified through their owner; no distributed transaction or dual write to Payload is introduced. After ambiguous success, resolve the command receipt before retrying.

### D27-R19 Database and authorization enforcement

Non-null tenant/source identity, typed relationships, uniqueness, valid states and protected control fields SHALL be enforced through constraints and the single authoritative mutation boundary. Tenant-aware operational FKs, indexes and restricted delete behavior SHALL protect local relationships; owner-defined soft references protect the CMS seam. Grants, RLS, column privileges, views, RPCs, service paths and storage delivery SHALL enforce the same capability boundary. Both existing-row USING and resulting-row WITH CHECK semantics SHALL be verified; a permitted update cannot move a row into another tenant/source/approval state. Actor, tenant, approval, owner and audit fields derive from trusted context. No service key reaches clients.

### D27-R20 Derived data and privacy

Search documents, caches, excerpts and preview state SHALL be explicitly derived and disposable under source generation/safety and selection generations. Current qualified eligibility SHALL filter hits, counts and snippets before exposure; a shared cache key cannot omit tenant/environment/site/locale/selection/authorization scope. No private message, attachment name or CRM fact is sent automatically as a source query, copied to CMS, embedded in an outbound guidance URL or retained in technical logs. D17 and owner retention govern message-derived provenance; it cannot become an indefinite second transcript store.

### D27-R21 Failures and partial availability

Show No guidance selected, No matches and Guidance temporarily unavailable as distinct states. An unknown source SHALL NOT be labelled unpublished. Failed/unknown qualification SHALL hold only affected references/preparations, not all Support composition or unaffected replies. Favorable indexing lag may omit a newly eligible item. When coverage is incomplete or updating, staff results SHALL disclose that state; No matches is shown only for a complete qualified query. Curators additionally receive per-source repair/status detail. Adverse states cannot rely on eventual purge alone. Recovery/replay SHALL use existing P23/P17 durable work and current owner heads, with no direct database repair as the normal procedure.

### D27-R22 Maintenance journey

Manage guidance SHALL expose source identity, current public eligibility, Site/language and the last curator transition, with Add to guidance and Remove from guidance. Edit in Web Studio and publication controls belong to Web Studio and require its permissions. No inline duplicate article editor is provided. Source content can improve without routine Support reapproval; curators remain accountable for suitability and obsolete selections. If no suitable Page exists, preserve the Support draft and use the existing authorized CMS work path; never auto-create a ticket, publish a conversation or copy its private content into a content request.

### D27-R23 Accessible, coherent presentation

Use Core's shared base-maia/Base UI components, semantic tokens and existing detail/context patterns. Guidance SHALL be keyboard and screen-reader operable with visible focus, correct dialog/list semantics, named actions, meaningful link text, non-color state labels and announced results without focus theft. Narrow screens use one layered finder/preview with Back returning to query, scroll and draft; Escape closes only the current layer. Reflow, zoom, RTL, IME, reduced motion and low-bandwidth behavior SHALL be qualified. No drag-only action, hover-only preview or new workspace navigation is required.

### D27-R24 Performance and completeness

Use indexed tenant/selection/source queries, owner search ranking, batched current-head checks and bounded keyset pagination. Evaluate the selected eligible corpus before pagination/counts; no client-side cap filtering or N+1 owner requests. The release capacity profile SHALL name tenants, Pages per tenant, locales, selected corpus size, text sizes, concurrent searches, update/withdrawal rate, payload bounds and latency budgets; prove at each declared limit and one beyond with explicit bounded rejection/degradation. No fixed universal page count or new search provider is justified without measurements. Core/P23's existing capacity and convergence contracts remain the starting point.

### D27-R25 History and diagnostics

Curation history SHALL record stable source references, actor, trusted time, transition/generation, reason for consequential changes, receipt and outcome without article/message bodies. Technical traces SHALL separately record safe correlation IDs, source-proof disposition, cache/index generation, denial class and P17/P6 effect state. Staff see actionable reason/retry/source-owner navigation; tenant oversight sees unavailable selections. No raw query, secret link token, message content or private preview is required to diagnose failure.

### D27-R26 Additive rollout and compatibility

Qualify the public owner extension, stable source mapping/search, selection command and Guidance document dependency before enabling the UI. Migrate operational and Payload schemas only through their own owners. Old readers/writers SHALL neither drop provenance nor interpret unknown dependency families as safe; fence unsupported versions. Backfills use source evidence and a deterministic report, never URL/title identity guessing or auto-selection of all published Pages. A finder kill switch disables new use while existing prepared messages keep their P17/P6 safety contracts. Roll forward after new documents exist unless old code is proven compatible; do not delete new fields to simulate rollback.

### D27-R27 Explicit dependency limits

The current shared reader is a seam, not complete proof of P23/P24 stable Site/locale/domain/search behavior. Activation SHALL depend on the named qualified contracts, not on an open PR number or a marketing feature. Use one Payload public implementation and the shared API interface, not a Support-only Local API shortcut, cross-product synchronization or URL scraper. Tiptap remains the Support editor; the CMS's editor/schema is not replaced or converted by D27. No provider upgrade, package installation or paid AI feature is selected here.

### D27-R28 Proof and traceability

Every clause SHALL map to its owning formal requirement/design/task and user/domain outcome proof when the founder later authorizes those stages. The proof matrix below is required and unexecuted; source probes and documentary checks do not substitute for runtime, DB/RLS, accessibility or staff usability evidence. D1–D26 terminology, state, delivery, retention and CRM invariants SHALL remain unchanged except the explicit narrow source-reference extension above. No new tickets or formal OpenSpec are created by this grooming record.

### D27-R29 Monitoring is bounded

Only the residual signals O01–O06 below may be monitored after the associated prevention/proof exists. Each has a named owning team/capability, threshold and response. A missing owner, unregistered capacity budget, unauthorized result or missing safety fence is a release blocker, not something to monitor into correctness. Monitoring SHALL not require recording private queries or customer text.

### D27-R30 Product validation and exclusions

Before release, representative tenant staff SHALL complete the ordinary finder, correct-language choice, linked-text insertion, CRM return, source removal and unavailable-source recovery journeys without facilitator intervention or unauthorized side effects. Record task outcomes and confusion, not a claim of perfection from a mockup. The exact public-content inventory and observed demand across tenants remain empirical release inputs; they do not prevent recording this bounded direction. Staff may still write a helpful answer or use existing saved replies when guidance is absent.
