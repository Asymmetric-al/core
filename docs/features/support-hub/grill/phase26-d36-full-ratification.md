## Accepted decision

New human Support email replies start with the new answer and governed signature/footer without automatically appended earlier text. **Add quote** beside the exact reply context and **Quote message** on an eligible source insert deliberate, source-qualified context directly into the existing editor. **Quote selected text** is an accelerator; exceptional **Choose excerpt** offers accessible smaller-source selection when precision selection is unsuitable or a whole body cannot fit. The ordinary insertion remains one action, with no mandatory picker, confirmation, second editor or history setting.

The quote begins expanded and readable. Staff can edit, trim, move, remove and Undo naturally while the protected source identity remains truthful. Changes to meaning-bearing canonical content—including text/order, meaningful list/table relationships and link destinations—produce recipient-visible **Edited excerpt from…**. Only qualified presentation-only formatting is exempt; exact semantic Undo can restore the original attribution. The complete accepted policy is [D36-R01–R26](phase26-d36-adversarial-review.md), incorporated in full with its exact corrected decision, all23 category outcomes, evidence qualifications, alternatives, consequences and ordered synthesis.

## Exact Email Studio, Support and delivery responsibilities

**Support owns the human reply and source context.** It selects the original source message/revision and exact excerpt, authenticates and derives the real actor/tenant, qualifies current source read/use and disclosure to the explicit audience, binds the private draft/target/recipients and source dependencies, and admits the reviewed reply through its existing command. Quotation never silently adds an audience, retargets Reply, changes work, creates a Party/contact or updates CRM/giving truth.

**Email Studio/P17 owns the shared governed authoring and complete-message preparation capability.** Its code-owned canonical Reply profile, compiler, approved presentation/signature/footer and immutable preparation produce the whole reviewed message. It receives Support-qualified source context; it does not become a conversation-history owner, automatically fetch a transcript, publish a human reply as a system-message template or create a new Layout Role/library/message family. Literal quoted braces, @names and prior source tokens stay inert. A generic formatting blockquote is not source-provenance authority.

**P6 owns actual delivery and evidence.** The exact current sender/connection/audience authority, permanent intent identity, sealed provider envelope, permissible retry, outcome reconciliation and communication history remain P6 responsibilities under ADR0032. No mutable provider template or later adapter inserts history, files, headers, signatures or substituted CRM facts outside the reviewed canonical message. A possibly submitted envelope is reconciled under its original identity, never rerendered, cleaned, rekeyed or blindly resent after a quote is removed.

**Preparation and admission remain separate from draft editing.** Adding, trimming, removing or saving a quote creates no P6 send, new message intent, CRM Activity, note publication, resolution or staff notification. D4's ready preparation and atomic local admission remain required before dispatch authority. A newly unsafe source stops affected future materialization/decryption/dispatch through current owner fences. It cannot rewrite a delivered message or make an unknown provider outcome known.

**Shared capabilities retain their own limits.** D18 owns reusable wording insertion conventions; D23 owns the managed signature and purpose profiles; D32 owns file preview/acquisition rules; D33 owns staff reading layout; D34 owns private composing awareness; D35 owns note correction/history. None of these controls decides outgoing quotation. D29 Contact confirmation and D15 staff attention are not triggered by quote editing. Email Studio publication does not enable a Support quoting policy or alter an existing draft.

**CRM continuity is canonical and permission-aware.** Authorized CRM entry uses the same source reader, private draft, quotation command and delivery result, preserving return context. CRM association/ownership does not confer Support source or protected-record rights. A requester, participant, staff principal, CRM person, represented organization and assignee remain distinct. Quoting receipt/refund/contact-change language is not authorization for, or evidence of, the owning business action.

## Full accepted UX and safety detail

The existing editor keeps readable Maia styling, semantic Base UI tokens and stable focus/scroll. Each deliberate insertion uses one original source and one undoable operation at a valid remembered block boundary; fallback is the end of authored body before the managed signature. It does not replace highlighted authored text. Same-operation replay/double activation produces one insertion; a genuinely new action may add another. There is no arbitrary one-quote cap beyond the qualified shared profile.

Whole eligible source content may contain nested quotations or embedded signature/address text. Its safe representation is reviewed honestly, not shortened by an unqualified newest-paragraph parser or D33 fold. Source-selected text must have exact revision/range mapping, Unicode/grapheme and repeated-occurrence correctness. Choose excerpt uses Start here, optional Extend through here, then Add excerpt or Cancel in the current source reader, without a thread-wide picker or separate editor. Indivisible meaningful structures and individually unsupported/oversized blocks get truthful limits; no hidden truncation or empty success.

Text quotation acquires no historical attachment, inline image, remote resource, tracking pixel or private action/storage URL. Existing file admission stays separately authorized. Collapsed quotation, when supported, remains labelled included in email and can be inspected. No routine confirmation is required for add/remove/send; material audience expansion or changed identity invokes one source-qualified review, satisfied by the existing exact D2 review where applicable. Removing recipients ordinarily does not add another acknowledgment. Quoting earlier content never retargets Reply; explicit retarget/topology changes revalidate without silently replacing the quoted body.

Draft extraction is cancelable and leaves typing usable. Late results cannot revive removed quotes or land in another draft, purpose, tenant or account. Send cannot silently omit a still-pending intended quote: finish or cancel it. D4's save/version/conflict/result recovery owns private persistence; body and dependency manifest are one effect. Reply/Note remain separate purposes, and D35 posted-note edits do not acquire a persistent quote-draft workflow. Forbidden content clears from controlled editors/caches/Undo under current privacy rules, while reliably separable safe authored work is retained.

Exact original source identity and known derived dependencies survive supported transformations. Caller JSON/DOM/HTML/hidden attributes cannot manufacture tenant, actor, source attribution, disclosure approval or lineage. RLS/grants, old/result update checks, immutable columns, tenant-aware endpoints, views/RPC/definer/service-role paths and storage all require actual closure, including old broad writer paths. This does not impose staff ACLs or CRM accounts on legitimate external email correspondents.

D16/D17 source restrictions and earliest applicable execution deadlines remain authoritative across controlled quotations. Copying content does not renew its source. Independently authored/received quoted messages remain separate evidence with qualified owner correction; no global text-matching erase cascade or remote recall promise. Native reply admission retains its own D17 clock without renewing a linked original. P17/P6 preparation and dispatch claims serialize correctly with source stops; already admitted/possibly submitted evidence stays truthful.

## Accepted glossary

**Support reply quotation:** A deliberately included excerpt of an eligible earlier external email within a new Support reply, attributed to its source and distinct from the current reply target and recipients.

**Edited excerpt:** A Support reply quotation whose meaning-bearing content has changed after insertion, visibly identified as adapted from its source rather than presented as that sender's unchanged message.

The two definitions in the scoped glossary remain unchanged. No next-question term, preference, draft-discovery policy or ADR0037 is selected by D36 ratification.
