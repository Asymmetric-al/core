# Staff-recorded Support requests and complete CRM access

**D26 and every adopted amendment are fully founder-ratified, 12 September 2026. Historical disposition: Accept with required amendments.** A, complete CRM access, all 30 clauses, 23 category findings, full UX/data/Email Studio contracts, four terms and required proof are accepted. D1–D26 are fully ratified as grooming, not implemented behavior or formal specification.

The [two complete journeys](phase26-d26-ux-blueprint.md), [Supabase/data/CRM contract](phase26-d26-data-contract.md), [source and research evidence](phase26-d26-evidence.md), independent reviews and required proof form one complete decision package. “Support ticket” in the founder's request means the existing canonical **Support conversation/request**, not a second ticket entity.

## Corrected decision

> Authorized staff may directly record a phone or in-person request that requires continuing Support work, from Support Hub or an authorized CRM context, using one shared form and one Support-owned admission command. Creation records a concise, visibly staff-authored request brief, current handling and explicitly reviewed source/CRM references; it sends no requester message and requires neither a fabricated email nor a CRM Party. Every currently relevant and permitted Support conversation—email-origin or staff-recorded, unfinished or resolved—is discoverable through the relevant CRM record's Support conversations view and opens the same fully capable, currently authorized Support detail, inline where practical with a full-page fallback. Support owns work and its brief; CRM owns Parties and relationships; existing encounter records are referenced, not cloned; Email Studio/P17 and P6 retain actual email preparation and delivery. Joint authorization, one authoritative source, accurate clocks, finite retention, atomic admission, durable retry and complete permission-filtered queries govern both paths. Ordinary relationship activity, general tasks, finance and restricted care remain with their actual owners.

## Necessity and strongest alternative

A addresses a real product gap: a received request may need shared Support handling without an incoming email. The founder establishes the intended need; no measured call frequency or universal ministry practice is claimed. An illustrative receipt discrepancy needs investigation and follow-up, while the receipt domain still performs any actual correction. A completed courtesy call does not become Support merely to populate a report.

The strongest simpler alternative is B: qualified CRM notes plus shared tasks. That remains appropriate for ordinary relationship follow-up. It is weaker for this chosen need because channel alone would determine whether staff get the shared Support queue and work model. C—record an interaction first—remains useful when an existing encounter is already captured, but a universal prerequisite would require a qualified general interaction owner and an unidentified-requester path that current Core source does not establish. A therefore reuses existing sources when available without requiring a duplicate entry or pulling the whole Phase28 workspace forward.

Help Scout documents phone notes saved without email; HubSpot distinguishes manual ticket creation from actual conversation association and separately supports activity-plus-task work. Zoho demonstrates an embedded support-detail journey inside CRM. These are evidence for deliberate recording and coherent navigation, not authority to import their customer creation, synchronization, permissions or data model. [Help Scout](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/12-create-a-new-conversation), [HubSpot manual tickets](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/help-desk/create-respond-to-tickets-in-help-desk), [HubSpot activities](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/manually-log-activities-on-records). Detailed edition/date/limitation comparisons are in the [independent UX research](phase26-d26-ux-research.md).

## Exact decision clauses

### D26-R01 — Bounded new origin, one canonical work record

Allow deliberate staff recording of a received phone/in-person request needing continuing Support handling. Extend the staff-created/no-native-mail model explicitly; D12's required incoming-email trigger does not already authorize this origin. Reuse the same canonical conversation identity, D3 work states and owning commands. No second ticket, generic call log, automatic all-calls intake, telephony, audio recording, transcription, SMS/chat, device-offline synchronization or AI is added. Updates to existing work use that work; ordinary relationship cultivation, general tasks and restricted care retain their owners.

### D26-R02 — Every applicable CRM record has complete Support access

Apply D9 discovery to all accepted origins and work states. For each requested Party, select active explicit relevance OR independently qualified actual correspondence, apply current joint visibility, then deduplicate by tenant/current D10 conversation root before counting or paging. Both reasons may justify one row. No mandatory Party, primary link, household/organization/beneficiary fan-out or inferred linkage from a phone/name/email match. Unlinked work remains valid and serviceable in Support with a clear No linked CRM record state; it becomes discoverable on a Party after a qualified association, not before. “Every” means every applicable authorized record through the complete view, not every tenant record on every Party.

### D26-R03 — Distinguish the people and authoritative facts

The staff recorder, person who reported the request, requester, represented person/organization, observed contact endpoint, CRM Party, assigned worker and CRM owner remain separate meanings. A reported name or callback number is unverified Support context, not a new authoritative contact or proof of identity/representation. Resolve the actual actor through trusted Auth/profile/tenant membership/Support identity; do not derive authorship from an email match, current assignee or caller-supplied author field. CRM contact corrections and consequential giving/care actions use the owning domain's current permissions, validation and approvals.

### D26-R04 — Support-first entry without a forced CRM detour

Use **Record a request** in the normal Support entry area, distinct from Send email and D12 Create related conversation. Preserve tenant and any currently suitable inbox context. A focused form captures the request, Phone/In person source, responsible inbox and optional known context. Show existing relevant work when it is already discoverable within current authority; do not force an exhaustive CRM search or disclose inaccessible matches. Related records are optional peers. No fake email, placeholder Party or automatic Create contact shortcut is needed to unlock Save.

### D26-R05 — CRM-first entry is the same command with reviewed context

The CRM Overview/Communications Support section offers **Record a request** only where both creation and current Party-reference permissions allow it. Open the same form with the current Party visibly staged as a removable Related record and the originating return context preserved. From an actual encounter record, also stage its exact permitted reference; never copy the whole activity body or secretly broaden relevance. Admit the request and all reviewed links together. If a link/source becomes invalid, reject preserving permitted input or offer an explicit revised Create without that link; no silent partial success that leaves the request missing from the originating CRM record. No caller URL parameter is authority.

### D26-R06 — Small form, truthful saving and private drafts

Use the D23-qualified Tiptap staff-context profile for a concise brief, with ordinary inputs for short title/source/inbox and shared D9 selectors for context. Allow authorized existing-source reuse without requiring the person to retype it; any new brief states the Support need, not a second authoritative encounter transcript. Optional occurrence details stay collapsed until needed. The primary action is **Record request**, with **No email will be sent to the requester** nearby. Cancel before admission has no work effect. Finite actor/tenant-scoped draft custody uses existing Support draft primitives; no raw browser-local permanent archive, ambient submit shortcut or automatic admission on blur. Unknown save outcomes reconcile the same operation before Saved is shown. Changes of tenant, permission or entry context cannot silently retarget an unsaved draft.

### D26-R07 — Open work with deliberate, qualified handling

Creation starts D3 Open: the request needs attention now. Use an explicitly chosen/currently qualified inbox; Shared handling is the default for a new standalone request, with an ordinary visible choice of eligible team/person/Assign to me. Do not silently assign the recorder, follow on their behalf, inherit another conversation's handler or run D6 automatic first-email routing. Optional follow-up reminders are deliberate D3 choices, not copied from a CRM task or source call. Respect D7/D8 eligibility and review controls. Invalid staged handling produces an actionable correction rather than a silent fallback. Existing owner tasks are referenced or bound through D11 when needed, not duplicated automatically. Existing qualified staff assignment/attention notifications retain D12/P6's actual source, recipient, preference and delivery rules; no new requester notice or all-team blast is introduced, and optional staff-email failure cannot block manual work admission.

### D26-R08 — Manual handling and email readiness are independent

Manual admission needs the inbox's current work acceptance, classification, accountable handling and actor authorization. It does not require a public email address, qualified MX route, receiving credential or P17 sender. The same inbox can accept qualified manual work while its email path is not ready; no third email-setup branch or new provider product is implied. Archived/retired work scope cannot receive new work; an email-only pause does not itself prohibit an otherwise authorized manual action. Conversely manual admission cannot activate receiving, bypass D19 email review or authorize outgoing mail. D25 email configuration and actual P17/P6 readiness remain independently visible and enforced.

An authorized inbox manager can establish a named Support work inbox with its required access/coverage before or without configuring email. If no eligible inbox exists, Record a request offers contextual inbox setup or a safe administrator handoff while preserving its permitted draft. **Configure email** is a separate action using D25's same two equal paths. This explicitly extends D25's email-first Add inbox assumption; it adds no third receiving method. Qualify current mail-shaped inbox schema/DTOs so manual-ready work requires no dummy inbound/From/Reply-To address, and an undifferentiated active flag cannot confer mail readiness.

### D26-R09 — Truthful staff source and absent native correspondence

Admit one typed staff-recorded source/brief with stable origin identity and protected provenance. Show **Recorded by [staff] · Phone/In person** as staff context, never a caller-message bubble, claimed transcript or fabricated provider receipt. Native first/last incoming/external message times and direction remain absent, native message counts zero, until real correspondence exists. The request title is staff-authored content, not an email subject until an actual outgoing subject is reviewed. Qualify schema, DTOs, validators, lists, search, exports, reports, automation guards and legacy readers together; no `now()` or literal `"null"` fallback manufactures a message/contact. Origin stays stable after later email occurs.

### D26-R10 — Recorded time and reported occurrence are different clocks

Trusted admission records who saved and when, in UTC with original precision, and cannot be backdated by the caller. Optional when-received information is separately marked staff-reported, with its supplied precision and timezone meaning. A date-only or unknown time must not become invented midnight or verified provider time; future encounter claims are rejected/corrected rather than creating a scheduled intake. Editing reported details is an attributable correction, never a rewrite of recording time, service target, audit occurrence or financial evidence. Display localized dates and relevant zones; record ordering and retention use their stated trusted clocks.

### D26-R11 — One source fact, references rather than duplicate encounter bodies

Support owns the request brief/work; an independently recorded CRM/relationship encounter stays with its actual owner. Reference that exact source under a closed typed owner contract and joint source permissions. Do not turn the current legacy `donor_activities` table, a generic timeline row, `contact_ref` JSON or a polymorphic free-form ID into a new shared interaction authority. Support-first capture may later be exposed through qualified Support projections; it is not mirrored into a second editable CRM call note. A minimized Support brief can describe the distinct unresolved need while referencing the source, but cannot copy restricted source material or create a retention escape. No generic encounter platform or Phase28-wide dependency is required for this narrow capability.

### D26-R12 — CRM placement and a complete, quiet list

Preserve D9's separate Support conversations section; the existing financial “support” summary is not this feature. Overview retains up to three currently accessible unfinished conversations and a visible View conversations link, including when the preview is empty. Communications → Support conversations is a complete permission-filtered paginated view, default Unfinished with an obvious All option for resolved/history. Include manual and email origins without requiring a P6 event. Show permitted title, work status, handling, current inbox and provenance; no stored transcript excerpts are required. Denied/hidden rows contribute no count or locked-card hint. Loading, empty, unavailable and content-expired are distinct authorized states.

### D26-R13 — Narrow zero-message ordering amendment

For a conversation with real external correspondence, retain D9's latest actual external-message occurrence and stable canonical-ID tie-break. For staff-created work with no native external correspondence, use its original trusted recorded/created time as the explicit fallback, label it **Recorded**, and show **No email yet** where helpful. Never call that Last message. This narrowly extends D9 for manual and existing D12 staff-origin work; it is not generic last-activity ordering. Notes, link changes, labels, assignment, read time and provider callbacks do not move the row as new correspondence. Reported call time cannot game recency. If historical creation evidence is unknown, preserve an explicitly unknown sortable class, not `now`.

### D26-R14 — View and work inline through the same Support detail

A CRM row opens the same canonical Support detail within the shared record/navigation shell when space permits; narrow screens and direct/refresh links have an ordinary full-page route. Provide Open in Support Hub and contextual return without losing permitted list filters/scroll/selection or the user's separate private draft. Avoid stacked modal editors. Reuse the actual Support thread/source cards, qualified attachments, notes, collision protection, assignment, work controls and reply composer; no reduced copied transcript or second CRM reply/resolve endpoint. Current read-only/action permissions are known before edit, rechecked at command admission, and explained without exposing protected targets. CRM and Support failure/loading boundaries remain independent.

### D26-R15 — Joint permissions precede all content and discovery

Enforce current tenant, Party reference/read, Support conversation/source and field/classification policy before rows, counts, previews, snippets, attachments, search, export, prefetch, streams or mutations. An inbox assignment, relevance association or conversation participant never grants CRM, giving, missionary or care access. Apply the same policy to both entry points, deep links, cached responses and privileged workers; never use a connector administrator's viewing lens. Current capabilities govern edits, not a broad staff role or stale JWT alone. On access loss, remove forbidden content/drafts from the visible context through existing policy while retaining only safe recovery/navigation; no revealing “one hidden request” badge.

### D26-R16 — Actual CRM history remains actual history

Manual recording, context links and inline viewing create no requester-correspondence/P6 email event, ordinary copied CRM Activity marker, note or last-contact update. Independently qualified staff attention retains its actual producer, recipient, template, P6 event and optional-email dedupe contract; it is not a conversation message from the requester. D9's Support summary supplies discovery independently of actual Communication history. Real later correspondence uses P6's existing source/member evidence and one read-composed CRM timeline with dedupe and qualified attribution. Existing independent encounter history remains visible only through its owner. Record Support creation/correction/link outcomes in the qualified Support business/audit history; no duplicate authoritative timeline store or bidirectional Support↔CRM synchronization.

### D26-R17 — Later email is a deliberate first-thread operation

Reuse D12's explicit Start email thread semantics with a new manual-origin recipient path: there is no incoming trigger from which to infer recipients. Staff deliberately selects a qualified endpoint under current contactability and disclosure rules; optional authorized CRM lookup is convenience, not identity proof or automatic Related-Parties expansion. Preserve D2 reviewed audience, real new lineage, actual From/Reply-To and D23 signature. The internal brief and source attachments are not automatically copied into a public draft. P17 prepares, Support admits and P6 dispatches/reconciles one exact effect. Missing email leaves manual work usable; missing mail readiness yields a clear repair path without losing the request. Uncertain send never becomes a blind new send.

### D26-R18 — Work promises and email targets retain their meanings

Manual creation, reported phone occurrence, a note or a claimed callback does not create/fulfill a D14 email reply target. D3 reminders and actual work promises govern manual follow-up. Preserve D14's existing nuance: a genuinely accepted human Start email thread in a staff-origin original establishes Next for later eligible incoming correspondence. A draft, queued effect or private note establishes no confirmed boundary. Unknown first-send acceptance retains D14-R07/R10's candidate First/Next interpretations and earliest applicable possible deadline, labelled Reply timing needs confirmation; it must not force First or score a success/breach. Definite rejection removes only invalid candidates. Native email receipt and target-age rules continue unchanged when actual mail arrives. Resolve and callback notation do not prove receipt correction, refund, recurring-gift change, contact update, delivery or requester satisfaction.

### D26-R19 — Later incoming mail and duplicate work are deliberate

Replies on a real qualified outbound lineage join under existing source routing. A separately arriving new email with no proved lineage must not auto-attach to a manual request based on name, number, subject, recent proximity or current CRM email. Show currently authorized relevant work to support explicit D10 merge or D12 related-work judgment; do not manufacture original-mail provenance. One call may reveal multiple independent needs and two workers may record distinct requests, so content similarity is not an authoritative uniqueness key. Deduplicate retries by durable operation identity, not people or text.

### D26-R20 — Correct, cancel and preserve responsibility

Before admission, Cancel discards the draft through its finite custody policy. After admission, edits are current authorized corrections with immutable original actor/time and versioned source availability; an ordinary “Undo save” cannot delete real work. All D12 unused-tracking guards remain: no subsequent independent activity, native/admitted/possibly submitted mail, external/owner effects, active merge participation, additional dependent continuation/referral or protected draft being discarded. Only when those guards pass, extend created-in-error cancellation for a standalone source with the additional requirement that no genuine obligation remains, or the exact remaining work has a durably accepted authorized current home. A legitimately used/completed request cannot be reclassified as created in error merely because nothing remains owed. There is no original email conversation to invent or silently rehome into. Use a distinct non-success ending and protected retirement control with audit; do not inflate solved metrics, destroy source evidence or cancel an independent encounter/task. Otherwise use truthful current work/context correction and retain actionable obligations. Merge/Undo, reopening and later input retain D3/D10 semantics and never revive expired content.

### D26-R21 — Links, merges and independent source lifecycle

Use D9's current conditional add/remove/correction/Undo and Party merge/repoint provenance. Linking/unlinking changes relevance only, not recipients or source retention. Removing the sole relevance basis removes the CRM row; independent qualified correspondence keeps one row with its truthful reason. Party merges dedupe discoverability without merging Support work; unmerge uses actual provenance, not today's email. Source deletion/archival/revocation gives a safe unavailable reference, not copied fallback text or a cascade that erases request/work/audit. Keep a qualified owner repair path when an erroneous link becomes unreadable.

### D26-R22 — Finite content retention includes the new brief once

Explicitly extend D17's human-content eligibility to the first admitted staff-recorded request brief, using trusted admission as its content occurrence. The brief/title and equivalent JSON/text/HTML/search/draft/attachment derivatives remain Support-owned content with D16 restrictions and D17 finite purpose. Ordinary edits, CRM linking, projection refreshes and backdated occurrence correction do not reset the retention anchor; actual later eligible new notes/replies/input follow D17. Expiry/redaction removes ineligible representations from both surfaces and recovery paths without erasing independently owned CRM/financial evidence. Metadata and history remain only within their own minimal finite purpose; a view or permanent CRM note is not a backup archive.

### D26-R23 — Reuse existing search and saved-view semantics

D22 already permits an eligible Staff context brief with zero messages. Add this admitted origin to that existing kind and its current source/visibility/expiry rules, not to a new CRM/phone/AI index. Simple D20 filters and counts use the same complete source-qualified query; no browser filtering of loaded rows. Existing saved predicates retain their defined meaning and version. No broad last-activity filter, source reinterpretation or silent global-search expansion is implied. Ordinary search excludes private drafts, restricted source payloads and expired content; a permitted retained request row is not permission to resurrect its old brief.

### D26-R24 — One guarded local admission and durable retry

One Support-owned transaction admits the original work identity, brief/source provenance, current handling/reminder choices, exact reviewed D9 links/source references, audit, durable receipt and required identifier-only invalidation/outbox intent. Recheck current authority, canonical targets and relevant revisions at that boundary. A rejected source/link/handling state writes no silent subset. Keep network/provider I/O outside. Use a tenant-scoped durable operation identity and semantic hash covering the reviewed effects; identical retries reconcile the original outcome and latest state separately, changed meaning conflicts. After cancellation/merge/correction, replay cannot resurrect the old request. Current permission also governs receipt reads. Two tabs cannot independently admit one shared draft occurrence twice.

### D26-R25 — Supabase schema and constraints encode the actual invariants

Use non-null tenant/original request/source IDs, same-tenant composite foreign keys, immutable creation fields, consistent origin/provenance shape, and constraints distinguishing absent correspondence from true message facts. D9 relevance retains at most one active resolved-Party association per original/current mapping under its merge contract; source citations do not become a second identity spine. Required brief/source history cannot disappear through cascade delete. Preserve existing canonical identifier types and values, including Support TEXT IDs; use native UUID only where that owning identity is UUID. Do not rekey Support to implement this origin. Use UTC instants/appropriate date types, bounded validated Tiptap JSON plus derived text, explicit nullability and enums/checks consistent with the accepted model. Cross-row authorization, current-root, source-use and work-acceptance invariants use the single guarded owner boundary rather than mutable cross-table CHECK expressions. No new money field, float, receipt ledger or giving precision change is introduced.

### D26-R26 — Grants, RLS, columns and privileged paths agree

Preserve Core's tenant ENABLE/FORCE posture, least-privilege role/table/column/function grants and single current authorization policy. Protect source, provenance, operation, link and audit tables from raw browser read/write; compound conversation creation and protected state changes are server-command-only. Inventory/fence generic direct INSERT/UPDATE and old service writers, not only the new route. Evaluate effective RLS old-row USING and resulting-row WITH CHECK, SELECT/RETURNING behavior, multiple policies, views, RPCs, storage and definer/service-role/NHI paths. Supabase default-grant changes do not revoke old explicit grants. Invoker views obey the invoking role's privileges; they are not enough when the role itself bypasses RLS. Harden required definer code with exact execute grants, safe search_path and the same actor/resource gate. No caller-controlled tenant/author/approval/role may transform an allowed row into a forbidden state.

### D26-R27 — Complete, bounded queries and current caches

CRM list membership comes from indexed tenant/Party/relevance/source joins, current visibility and D10 canonical dedupe before keyset paging/counts. Qualify beyond the current configured Data API max_rows=1000, Support cap2000 and CRM activity cap50; those source numbers are limitations to test, not accepted product limits. Index actual reverse predicates and cursor ordering, bound payload/links/assets/transactions through registered owner limits and measure plans with skewed tenant sizes. Reuse current read-model/collection conventions; no N+1 transcript hydration or unbounded all-tenant prefetch. Tenant/actor/Party/access generation/filter/cursor scope fences caches and late responses. After create, the returned authorized identity and targeted canonical read reconcile CRM discovery; projection delay never reports a false failed save or encourages duplicate creation.

### D26-R28 — Failure, history and operational recovery are first-class

Distinguish validation rejection, permission change, save pending/unknown, admitted work, projection unavailable, mail-not-ready and actual send failure. Durable admission/audit/continuation prevent committed work without a recoverable discovery path. Optional CRM section failure isolates that section, not the whole CRM record; Support remains authoritative. Business history records recorder/origin/current corrections/actual actions; security audit covers access and denied privileged attempts; technical logs use safe IDs/revisions/error classes without brief/contact payloads. Reconciliation and repair use owner commands, not direct database edits or repeated imports. O01–O06 below provide named signals, thresholds, owners and responses after prevention.

### D26-R29 — Rollout preserves old evidence and stops unsafe writers

First qualify the shared source/authorization/command/query contracts, then additive origin/nullable-mail/read changes and both UI entries, then inactive legacy conversion and writer fences, then actual release proof. Do not relabel email-only history, infer manual sources from notes/phone fields, or backfill fictitious message timestamps/Party links. Mixed-version deployment must prevent old readers from inventing mail and old CRUD from bypassing new constraints. A kill switch stops new manual admissions while existing requests, CRM discovery and admitted effects remain safely readable/recoverable. Roll forward or use compatible readers after new-origin data exists; reverting to an email-only model is not a safe rollback. No production migration, provider change or formal spec is performed in this grooming turn.

### D26-R30 — Coherent UI and independently testable traceability

Use Core base-maia, Base UI, semantic tokens and shared components; one responsive detail/form, labelled fields, keyboard/focus restoration, clear errors/status, no hover-only actions or required drag. Preserve long/international names, accessible reflow, locale/timezone meaning and input under low-bandwidth retry. Validate complete Support-first/CRM-first/CRM-view-only/correction and later-email tasks with representative staff and assistive technology; no claim of perfect UX or compliance comes from a wireframe. Carry D26, the exact shared-owner amendments, four glossary terms, P01–P50 and operational controls into the later authorized ADR/OpenSpec/design/tasks/tickets/tests/release chain. “Ticket” introduces no new aggregate. AI augmentation/search remains future research under existing governance, with no model egress or action authority here.
