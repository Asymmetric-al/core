# Selective redaction: US legal and provider boundaries

Selective permanent redaction is an appropriate narrowly scoped correction tool. It is not, by itself, a complete privacy-rights process, a retention policy, an incident response, or proof of legal compliance. This assessment supports [D16's proposed decision](phase26-d16-adversarial-review.md), checked against public primary sources on 11 September 2026. It does not establish the facts needed to determine every tenant's legal obligations.

## The proportionate US approach

Use the same safe removal mechanism for all eligible Support content. Keep legal applicability, retention exceptions, authenticated privacy requests and actual preservation instructions with the existing responsible privacy/records owner. Ordinary staff should not answer a legal questionnaire for each mistaken paragraph. A known preservation restriction or unavailable policy check changes that particular operation's outcome; it does not justify indefinite retention of all conversations.

The necessary deployment evidence is a short owner-approved record of applicable jurisdictions and roles, relevant data classes, retention/disposal rules, actual holds, and provider/backup capabilities. No new legal-rules engine, universal deletion period, universal two-person approval, compulsory reason essay, or separate enterprise compliance product is required by this decision.

Their absence does not prevent recording the chosen safe product behavior; it prevents claiming a particular operation or deployment is legally cleared.

## Payment-card standards and exposed credentials

[PCI SSC FAQ1157](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/1157/) describes unsolicited card data in unintended channels and warns against propagating it in replies. [FAQ1533](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/1533/) addresses prohibited retention of sensitive authentication data after authorization even when encrypted. These are payment-security standards, not a claim that PCI DSS itself is a general US statute. Their actual contractual/scope applicability must be respected.

For Asym, never request card details through Support; route payment entry through the qualified giving owner. A credential/card-data incident calls for containment and the appropriate owner response, including credential rotation when appropriate. Redaction is not proof that a disclosed credential is safe again. Do not transmit the secret to a new scanner or copy it into an incident title. A preservation-versus-disposal conflict is handled by qualified records/security authority; an ordinary “keep for audit” option cannot authorize prohibited storage.

## Resend is a provider boundary, not a universal erase API

[Resend's security page](https://resend.com/security), [DPA](https://resend.com/legal/dpa), and [email-retention pricing documentation](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/what-is-resend-pricing) describe security, retained message content and plan-dependent retention. The public security page describes seven-day provider backups; the DPA includes an account-termination disposal period. Neither fact proves that one Support redaction immediately removes every provider copy. Account deletion is not an acceptable per-message cleanup strategy.

No verified per-message selective purge contract, current tenant plan, signed processing terms, support-confirmed deletion mechanism, or actual provider deletion was obtained in this review. P6's provider owner must qualify scoped deletion/restriction, retained evidence, deadlines and acknowledgments for the actual deployment. A request submitted to a provider is not completed deletion. An unsupported API must not be invented, and an entire tenant account must never be deleted to remove one message.

Ordinary Support removal sends no automatic requester/follower email. If a separate authorized service or privacy response is needed, it uses the existing P17 Email Studio template path and P6 Resend dispatch. Do not include removed text, filenames, attachments or private evidence in that response. A message already accepted by an external email system cannot be recalled by editing Core.

## Required language for the decision

> Permanent redaction removes the selected Support-owned material and its governed active representations, while retaining only permitted body-free facts. It has no ordinary reveal or Undo. A valid preservation duty may instead require restricted owner custody; that outcome is never labelled erased. Deferred provider or backup disposal is separately tracked under the applicable owner policy and legal deadlines, and restored data must pass the current restriction barrier before any read, export, replay or send. External messages and independent downloads cannot be recalled by this operation. Formal privacy requests and incident obligations remain independently owned and are not completed merely by redacting a Support message.

The policy deliberately avoids automatic global scanning, all-message lifetime holds, per-redaction legal advice, generic retention configuration, and universal two-person approval. Qualified authority, a precise preview, one meaningful confirmation, source-safe containment and verifiable owner cleanup are the permanent minimum.
