## Main finding

B is coherent only as **two independent actions with distinct authority and outcomes**: a recoverable disposition of currently reviewed non-support correspondence, and a separately chosen future-input hold that feeds D19. Neither can be implemented by changing a conversation to Resolved, adding a label, deleting a contact, mutating a provider mailbox or turning on the legacy automation builder. Existing D14 No reply needed, D17 source-purpose retention, D19 review/admission and D10 original-source commands supply most of the required machinery after qualification.

A remains the strongest smaller alternative; B's added work is now selected by the founder. The permanent path must therefore provide complete hold creation, current coverage, false-positive review and Stop behavior. It cannot merely add a checkbox and defer recovery.

## Exact proposed lifecycle clauses

### L01 — Two separate effects

“Mark unwanted changes only the exact reviewed admitted-source disposition. Hold future email changes only a separately reviewed, owner-qualified receiving-policy scope. Each has its own current authority, reviewed context, durable command identity, result and correction path. One action never implicitly enables, stops or rolls back the other. A failure of one does not rewrite the other's already-admitted result.”

The optional hold can be initiated from one qualified admitted incoming email or a currently authorized qualified D19 held input, with exact observed From and receiving-inbox evidence and separate hold-management authority. Creating it never requires first releasing a held input or marking the whole conversation unwanted. The current held input keeps its existing gates and admission decision. This is essential when a real request contains an unrelated later message: staff must not misclassify legitimate work merely to reach the hold control. D19 held-before-admission Dismiss remains a different command. Current non-email/error paths retain their accepted owners; a manual or web-only address is not a qualified email occurrence.

### L02 — Disposition is not a fifth work status

“Keep ordinary Support work's four D3 meanings. An Unwanted original records a current non-support disposition and preserves all earlier work/source evidence; it does not manufacture Resolved, No response, successful service or a fifth ordinary-work status. Ordinary-work membership and current source disposition are explicit, not inferred from an optional label. While the entire current component is set aside, do not display a dormant Open/Waiting value as an active obligation or count it as ordinary unfinished work.”

Preserve original contributors rather than storing a mutable-root-only flag. Current handling resolves the exact original set under D10. A component with newly ordinary/legitimate work is visible as ordinary work; an all-Unwanted component remains set aside. Physical schema names, nullable fields and enums belong to later implementation design; the valid product combinations and one authoritative command boundary do not.

### L03 — Whole-component eligibility and mixed content

“The new current Mark capability applies to ordinary admitted email-origin sources, including their notes and replies. It does not replace D26 created-in-error correction for staff-recorded requests or D29 form-safety handling. Mark unwanted admits only after complete current authorization and review of every included original in the selected current component, confirming the material is genuinely non-support correspondence and that no legitimate current or historical request is being hidden. Reject wholesale marking of mixed legitimate/unwanted content, uncertain source coverage, missing required owner evidence, known promised work or actionable unreviewed recovery. A complaint, criticism, persistent legitimate inquiry, sensitive case or difficult requester is not nuisance for this purpose.”

Use D14's exact-input No reply needed for a reviewed irrelevant contribution while real work continues. Use D10's current reviewed Undo for an actual mistaken duplicate merge and D9/D16 for their own link/security corrections. Do not add a general message-splitting, transcript-reparenting or arbitrary per-message classification editor solely for D40. No access failure proves absence of an obligation. A pending hold or existing CRM association does not establish eligibility to mark the source unwanted.

### L04 — One current conditional set-aside admission

“Admit the exact designation contributors, source/work/topology revision, trusted actor, approved applicable No reply needed effects, legitimate source-purpose ending where proved, before/after history, durable command result and required owner continuations atomically. Retire the current ordinary-work reminder only under the same reviewed no-remaining-obligation judgment, preserving its actual cancellation evidence. Do not loop over standalone HTTP commands or perform provider I/O in the transaction.”

Any relevant new contribution, owner outcome, work commitment, topology change or active human-send admission that invalidates the review must cause a current conflict before the effect. Preserve the source and all personal drafts. A private autosave alone does not create a business obligation or give another worker access to the draft. The action does not reassign work, cancel an independent task, fabricate a human reply, restart a target, edit source text or replace an existing email intent.

### L05 — Restore means current review, not rollback

“Restore is a new authorized correction of the still-current unwanted disposition over its exact currently reviewed original scope. It returns the relevant current work home to Open for review, preserves classification history and requalifies current handling/coverage. It does not restore stale Waiting, an old assignee, an expired timer, old recipients, an obsolete send approval or revoked access. No automatic reply, feedback candidate, target success or retention renewal follows merely from Restore.”

When the same command also reverses its own still-current mistaken D14 No reply needed disposition, reuse D14's exact contribution correction: preserve original age/policy and independent Off, leave already-covered or newer contributions alone, and show actual resulting due/review state. A classification that has already been superseded by new work yields current state, not a second successful restore. Actual expired/restricted content cannot be restored through a disposition command.

### L06 — New input, continuation and topology

“Every new input retains its original accepted-input/recipient occurrence and native original source. The future-mail hold is evaluated by its receiving-policy owner before ordinary admission; a held input is not appended to the conversation, not a personal unread contribution, not a D15 human update and not ordinary reply work. Once a genuinely new relevant human input is actually admitted through D1/D19, atomically clear the applicable original's current unwanted treatment and bring its current handling component into Open review. Do not leave new legitimate work hidden because the prior conversation was unwanted.”

Previously admitted input/replayed webhook is not new work. Automated, unrelated, blocked or unqualified input follows its actual admission/review owner rather than forcing ordinary reopening. For an A/B merged component, native B input cannot erase A's original classification history or fabricate a second B admission; current work projection must expose B's review at the current home. Missing topology/source proof becomes owned recovery, not a guessed root.

Require Restore and ordinary current eligibility before a new Merge includes an unwanted component. Existing D10 Undo remains available under its actual current review and preserves original dispositions. An all-Unwanted result of Undo does not automatically become Open or obtain a new purpose/expiry anchor merely because detached ordinary work normally defaults to Open review. A result containing legitimate ordinary work remains visible and gets its actual current plan. No Undo moves messages or reinstates canceled effects.

### L07 — The optional hold belongs to qualified receiving policy

“Bind a future hold to one qualified observed incoming mailbox and the actual tenant/receiving-inbox occurrence under the canonical intake owner, with explicit reviewed scope and current management authority. The current handling inbox after a Move is not proof of the original receiving inbox. A Reply-To, display name, current CRM email, guessed forwarded original, Party or domain is not substituted for that observed source.”

Use the intake owner's qualified mailbox equality; never invent local-part lowercasing, dot/plus removal or alias/household equivalence. Do not infer that this proves who sent the mail. Preserve separate D19/tenant/security gates and exact recipient occurrences when one input reaches more than one qualified inbox. No all-inbox, domain, contact or cross-tenant expansion is implicit. Scope/readiness failure leaves current handling usable and the unqualified hold unavailable; it does not create a Support-local blocklist or activate the Phase34 builder.

### L08 — Held inputs and Stop retain their own lifecycle

“Each qualifying future match enters existing D19 review with one source-owned hold reason and accountable current coverage. Reviewers can Release or Dismiss only that exact retained input under all current gates. A one-input Release explicitly discharges its current reviewable hold reason for its one durable admission effect; it does not disable the future hold, certify identity, bypass another safety gate or create permanent sender trust.”

Define future at first durable canonical input/receiving-occurrence acceptance. The intake owner serializes Enable/Stop with that acceptance and records the effective policy decision once, including the absent-rule insertion race. An input accepted before Enable is not retroactively captured; an input first accepted while the exact hold is effective keeps that held reason after Stop. Later hydration, routing recovery, webhook retries or Stop/restart cannot substitute worker-time policy. Initially unresolved receiving scope uses the retained original acceptance/policy evidence when resolved; missing proof remains owned pending recovery. Current non-overridable safety is still rechecked at actual admission. Already-admitted messages remain admitted. Repeated transport/worker execution reconciles the same outcome rather than re-holding or admitting twice. Release approval binds current relevant gate generations; an obsolete approval cannot bypass a newly applicable independent restriction. Stop holding changes future policy qualification only; it does not release, dismiss, purge, renew the deadline of or restore previously held inputs. Existing D19 queue/history and finite custody remain the only review store.

### L09 — Human sends, automatic mail and adverse recovery

“Mark unwanted must not silently cancel an already-admitted human reply or hide its actionable recovery. Before marking, reconcile any active human send/admission whose result or remaining positive execution authority conflicts with setting the source aside. Use only an already-qualified owner cancellation if available; D40 does not invent cancellable Send. Preserve the draft/operation identity while unknown. A reviewed historical unknown outcome with no remaining positive effect or required recovery is not an eternal blocker.”

A stale Send review predating Mark cannot cross local admission afterward. If human admission wins first, Mark re-evaluates that actual source/send state instead of pretending it canceled it. If an independently qualified late failure/result creates a real Support review obligation after marking, end only the now-inapplicable current exclusion and make that valid source/current work home visible for Open review through the normal owner boundary. Preserve all classification and communication history; Unwanted cannot suppress required recovery. This owner result does not fabricate an incoming message, native-human content admission, retention renewal or personal unread event. Duplicate or already-reviewed callbacks do not repeatedly reopen.

Current unwanted classification removes new submission eligibility for optional source-derived confirmations/feedback that are definitely unsubmitted, using their existing source/preparation/P6 guards. The separate future-mail hold is an inbound gate, never an outbound recipient suppression or P6 denylist. A held input creates no ordinary message or automatic confirmation while held; after its actual qualified one-input Release/admission, normal D13 eligibility still uses the original receipt-based utility window, and an active future hold does not independently block a valid human reply or later genuine owner-qualified feedback. P6 dispatching remains the actual external I/O boundary. Already or possibly submitted material retains truthful evidence and permitted reconciliation; no recall, fresh key, altered payload, split envelope or replacement success is fabricated. A source change does not clear an unrelated recipient or required owner-purpose event. Reporting/cleanup cannot send an explanatory email to make the transition feel complete.

## Required proof additions

These group with the root [P01–P40 requirements](phase26-d40-proof-and-operations.md); all actual runtime proof remains unexecuted.

1. **Current-only effects:** Mark/Restore and Hold/Stop execute and fail independently, with truthful committed/unknown/rejected outcomes and no cross-effect rollback. Hold creation from an authorized qualified D19 held input requires no forced Release and changes none of that input's existing gates.
2. **Eligibility:** Whole reviewed email-origin nuisance may be marked; genuine historical/current Support, mixed originals, known commitments, unresolved required recovery and missing complete scope cannot be hidden. Non-email created-in-error/form paths retain their owners.
3. **Work meaning:** Unwanted is not a fifth status or a successful resolution. Original messages, old work/reminder decisions, real communication and known reply misses survive.
4. **Original projection:** Fully unwanted components leave ordinary work; B-only genuine reentry exposes current B review while preserving A designation. Merge requires Restore; Undo preserves exact original state with no mechanical Open or new clock.
5. **Current Restore:** Restore opens current review and reverses only its still-current exact No reply needed contributions, respecting accepted coverage, newer input, original age/policy and independent Off. No stale snapshot of assignment, timer or recipients is restored.
6. **Intake timing:** Actual separately scheduled database transactions cover first acceptance before/after Enable/Stop, absent-policy creation, deferred hydration and initially unresolved receiving scope. The outcome follows recorded acceptance-time policy rather than retries or sender dates.
7. **Single Release:** Active hold plus exact one-input Release, cancel/re-release, worker retries and independent new safety gates produce one canonical outcome. Stop/restart cannot reinterpret old input or mass-release backlog.
8. **Scope identity:** Receiving A moved to handling B; multiple receiving occurrences; two tenants/inboxes sharing the same mailbox; shared/forwarded/ambiguous From; local-part/plus/domain rules; creator departure and inbox recreate never broaden or retarget policy.
9. **Send race:** Exercise Mark before/after ready preparation, local human admission, external dispatch, possible success and required newly actionable recovery after a valid Mark. Reentry ends only the now-inapplicable current exclusion and opens the valid current source/work home for review without erasing classification history or fabricating native-human/retention/read activity. Prove no silent human cancellation, extra dispatch/rekey, fictitious recall, hidden active obligation or repeated reopen from duplicate/already-reviewed evidence.
10. **Optional mail:** Current unwanted source designation gates its own optional confirmations/feedback before new dispatch. A held input contributes no ordinary confirmation while held; actual qualified Release/admission reuses original D13 timing and its true gate exception. Active future holding never becomes an outbound suppression, including for ordinary human replies or later genuinely eligible feedback. Already/possibly submitted P6 evidence and unrelated required owner purposes remain intact.
11. **Private work:** Both draft purposes survive current classification changes and uncertain results without sharing bodies, consuming drafts or overwriting newer state. Reply/new business work requires Restore; lawful explanation notes and own-note corrections do not auto-Restore.
