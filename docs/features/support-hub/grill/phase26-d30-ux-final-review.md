## Initial disposition: one material clarification required

**Finding UX-F01 — Keep D3 follow-up and delivery recovery directly discoverable. Severity: High; likelihood: plausible if the listed navigation is treated as exhaustive.**

The actual R04 and blueprint list Intake review, Handoff review and Feedback review as independent all-date destinations, but do not explicitly preserve D3's existing due/follow-up and actionable delivery-recovery entry points. The three Now summaries are not equivalent to those paths. In particular, a human reply may fulfil its response obligation and leave Replies needing attention while a later bounce still needs delivery recovery. A reminder's authoritative due-review transition can also clear its due value without proving the underlying review complete.

The consequence is a superficially quiet overview that makes real existing work harder to find. This does not invalidate A or require a new metric, queue, Problems union or notification family. Ordinary existing navigation is sufficient if it is explicitly retained and tested on this surface.

**Exact proposed correction to R04/UX:**

> Existing D3 due/follow-up and actionable delivery-recovery destinations remain directly discoverable from Overview through their qualified existing navigation. Current reply-attention or a zero summary never certifies these obligations complete. Do not invent a new combined queue or copy provider/attention state into Overview; each destination retains its owning scope and access.

**Required proof extension to P04:** a fulfilled reply followed by an actionable bounce remains reachable for recovery, and a due-review transition remains reachable under its owning work rules even after the reminder value clears. Neither inherits an unrelated historical date or broadens current authorization.

This finding was sent to the root for exact correction. Final disposition will be appended after rereading the actual changed clauses.
