## User research and accessibility

[NN/g's Contact Us guidelines](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/contact-us-pages/), **18 August 2019**, report qualitative research with **20 business professionals and 40 corporate websites**. Hidden direct methods and obstructive flows caused reported frustration. This supports discoverability and low friction; it does not establish ministry-specific prevalence, a conversion percentage or a requirement to staff phone/chat and publish office locations. The HTML article was read. The larger PDF failed to fetch and is not claimed as reviewed.

Current [WCAG2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and its [reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html), [focus-obstruction](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html), [target-size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html), and [form-notification](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/notifications/) guidance ground **D29-X01** and existing error/receipt requirements. Text resizing is distinct from reflow; author-created obstruction is distinct from a browser keyboard; the product's44-pixel primary-action goal is distinct from the24-pixel AA minimum/exceptions. The exact amendment and three proof groups are recorded once in the [decision](phase26-d29-refresh-adversarial-review.md) and [proof ledger](phase26-d29-refresh-proof-and-seams.md).

No Asym participant study or rendered contact-flow usability test ran. The blueprint's six journeys are reasoned design/test scenarios. Current D29's P44 moderated research remains a required release proof; no percentage satisfaction or task-time benefit is invented.
