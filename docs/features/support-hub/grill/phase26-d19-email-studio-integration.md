# Email Studio and intake review: the ratified boundary

**Fully founder-ratified D19 integration record, 12 September 2026.** This addendum makes the accepted D19/D13/D18 and governing Phase17/Phase6 responsibilities explicit. It introduces no new product choice, channel, template key or implementation authority. The [full ratified D19 decision](phase26-d19-adversarial-review.md), [D18 integration record](phase26-d18-email-studio-integration.md) and [independent seam review](phase26-d19-email-studio-seam-review.md) supply the complete supporting contracts.

Email Studio owns governed authoring, publication, presentation and qualified preparation. Support owns intake decisions, source conditions, current drafts and work intent. Phase6 owns the common communication spine, including local in-product occurrences and actual external dispatch. Resend provides the qualified receiving and external email transport capabilities. CRM and every business domain retain their authoritative facts and actions.

## In-product attention is a qualified local contract

D19-R23/P32 already require an exact bounded source meaning before activation. For each enabled meaning, qualify its real producer, source episode and applicability, recipient-role authority, safe fact/destination schema, occurrence identity, correction/end rule and P17/P6 binding. Do not borrow an unrelated delivery-repair key or mint a generic support-notification template as a shortcut. Exact registry/API names remain implementation design details, not new founder decisions.

The inherited `presentation.source_actionable_then_recent_90d@1` policy keeps required work actionable after a notification is read; reading changes engagement, not source completion. Source resolution ends actionable treatment. Authorized recent presentation follows the existing 90-day ceiling, shortened by source/privacy/access restrictions. It retains only the permitted minimized projection, never the held body/snippet or a stale working release link. Access and elapsed restrictions are enforced on reads even if cleanup is delayed. [Notification ADR0027](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0027-one-notification-presentation-and-engagement-model.md#L18).

Grouping respects tenant, inbox and condition **plus** the inherited recipient-role, privacy, meaning and genuine episode boundaries. Oversight may receive qualified coverage health without message content. New meaningful source transitions receive proper new occurrences; retries, read state or reassignment cannot keep renewing an old item.

At content expiry, release/recovery authority ends. If expiry occurred without a business disposition, the separate source-owned operational review remains actionable until its typed cause/response or qualified no-further-action conclusion is recorded. That completion preserves the miss and does not restore bytes. Deliberately dismissed content later expiring does not create a missed-review obligation. This distinction prevents an expired-mail card from becoming permanently unresolvable.

P6 local materialization uses the no-artifact contract. It must not invent external preparation, a provider ID, send/delivery state or Resend traffic just to record an in-product notification. [Prepared-message ADR0032](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0032-immutable-prepared-message-and-whole-message-recovery.md#L19), [P6 common seam](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-06-shared-communication-event-model.md#L175).

## Actual email after admission

Release is not an email operation, but actual admission may satisfy D13's already accepted confirmation rules. Keep the conditional UI copy: **“The usual new-request confirmation may follow if this message is eligible.”** Original receipt plus 15 minutes remains the strict utility end; no release, retry, template edit, corrected destination or return from dismissal restarts it. All recipient, suppression, courtesy, source and connection controls remain intact. A late release may admit valid Support work while producing no stale confirmation.

The automatic confirmation has a distinct qualified **Support request received** Email Studio template. Inbox settings retain contextual Preview/Edit in Email Studio. Publishing wording sends nothing, admits no held input, activates no inbox, changes no eligibility and replays no historical occurrence. The message confirms receipt; it cannot claim human review, achieved reply targets, completed refunds or any other unproved business result. [Ratified D13](phase26-d13-adversarial-review.md).

Human replies use D18's My/Shared source and current Support draft. P17's exact ready preparation precedes the atomic Support reply/work admission; P6 owns dispatch and evidence at actual recipient-copy/submission specificity. A whole To/Cc group is never sent once per member. Notes remain structurally separate. Library save/publication, current draft insertion and sending retain their different permissions and outcomes. [Ratified D18 integration](phase26-d18-email-studio-integration.md).

The provider name does not collapse receiving and sending authority. Receiving needs its qualified source/connection and retrieval capability. Outbound consumes the exact proved owner connection and frozen delivery identity; a send-only credential does not establish retrieval powers, and receiving credentials do not confer outbound authority. No global/platform fallback account or request-level sender override is permitted. No production workflow shells out to the Resend CLI. [Tenant-owned Resend ADR0029](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0029-tenant-owned-resend-and-composed-delivery-identities.md#L18).

## Implementation qualification and proof

The [independent review's ten-row qualification register](phase26-d19-email-studio-seam-review.md) maps receiving authority, current reviewer/access/publication separation, exact source-attention meaning, local-only P6 materialization, source/presentation lifetimes, D13 confirmation, D18 human authoring, immutable dispatch recovery, copy custody and writer/CRM cutover to their accepted owners and proof groups. These are applications of already ratified requirements, not additional open product options.

D19 defaults authorize **in-product staff attention**, not reviewer email, push, body digests or dismissal notices to senders. No unused email template should appear in Intake review settings. Any separately authorized future email channel would need its own exact bounded source/template/material contract and P6/tenant Resend delivery. It cannot be introduced by a queue worker choosing an HTML template.

No formal specification, implementation, live provider/database proof, inbox connection or real message is created here. D19's 42 required proof groups remain required and unexecuted; documentation acceptance is distinct from production readiness.
