[Complete ratified D23](phase26-d23-adversarial-review.md) and [Email Studio seam](phase26-d23-email-studio-integration.md) govern current acceptance.

The relevant comparisons are shared-mailbox help desks. No nonprofit-specific field studies were located or relied on to claim a signature convention unique to ministries. Kustomer/Intercom/Freshdesk are not forced into this narrow comparison; five products establish the actual alternatives without broadening the scope. No assertion of industry consensus or universally optimal design follows from vendor availability.

[W3C's labels guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/labels-or-instructions.html) supports clear visible input labels with enough guidance without clutter. [Reflow guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) supports usable narrow/zoomed layouts. These are authoritative accessibility explanations, not evidence that a proposed screen is already accessible or beautiful. Staff testing and email-client qualification remain necessary after the decision.

The exact [Q23 alternatives and recommendation](phase26-q23-reply-signatures.md) remain unanswered. This research does not ratify A, alter D22 or implement any of the alternatives.
