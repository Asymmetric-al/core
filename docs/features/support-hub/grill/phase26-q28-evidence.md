## Current external evidence and disagreements

The [vendor research](phase26-q28-vendor-research.md) contains the detailed workflow comparisons, page dates, edition caveats and constraints. The root also directly retrieved the following primary pages. Product docs show supported behavior, not proven Asym demand or vendor internals.

- [Front's CSAT feature](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2255), **7 August 2026**, current Professional-or-above with legacy caveats: separate signature, manual and automatic approaches make A/B real alternatives. Automatic repeated-request suppression is useful evidence; using editable tags as authoritative idempotency or adding a rating to every signature is not adopted. Its [24 February 2026 metric definition](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2399) differs from the newer main page's teammate attribution. That conflict is disclosed rather than turned into an Asym formula.
- [Zendesk solved-ticket recipe](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408887007386-Workflow-How-to-send-satisfaction-surveys-when-tickets-get-solved), **7 August 2026**, documents a delayed default and an immediate alternative guarded by Unoffered. Its [standard automations](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408835051546-About-the-standard-Support-automations), **1 May 2026**, identifies Suite Growth+ or Support Professional/Enterprise for enabling ratings. Adopt explicit policy/previous-invitation reasoning, not its exact 24-hour delay, closed-state model or platform role vocabulary.
- [Help Scout email ratings](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/386-gather-feedback-with-satisfaction-ratings), directly retrieved **9 September 2026** revision: per-inbox enablement adds ratings to each reply. It requires a final Send on the landing page before saving. The latter supports a deliberate response journey; the former measures a different unit and adds reply/signature noise. Its ten-day window and removal policy are not Asym decisions.
- [HubSpot customer support survey](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/customer-feedback/how-do-i-send-a-customer-support-survey), **20 June 2026**, Service Hub Professional/Enterprise with an assigned Service seat, is **CES**, measuring effort. It is not the same as [HubSpot CSAT](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/customer-feedback/create-and-send-customer-satisfaction-surveys). The useful comparison concerns reviewed activation, eligible ticket state and duplicate invitations; it does not select a seven-point scale or CRM campaign cohort for Asym.
- [Intercom conversation ratings](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/7872853-measure-customer-satisfaction-with-conversation-ratings) and [Workflow FAQ](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8826728-workflows-faqs), accessed **12 September 2026**, have pathway-dependent eligibility/reclosure guidance. Their group-conversation and repeated-closure constraints demonstrate why Asym must define actual recipient and invitation identity. No universal Intercom rule, arbitrary recency threshold or timer is inferred across the two paths.

The consequences recommended from these comparisons are selective: a short voluntary response, deliberate final submission, no every-reply signature decoration, transparent enablement, current eligibility checks, durable duplicate prevention and trustworthy pending/submission states. These are product judgments requiring selected-answer proof, not claims that a vendor implementation guarantees safety.

## CRM comparison without importing synchronization

[Help Scout's Salesforce integration](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/700-salesforce), retrieved 12 September 2026, is available with Help Scout Plus/Pro and documented Salesforce editions/API access. It distinguishes a view of conversations in CRM from optional writes of new-conversation/new-rating events, and offers email-based profile lookup/sync. This is useful evidence that viewing and recording are different effects.

For Asym, use the existing authorized canonical Support detail from CRM. Do not import cross-vendor email matching, automatic lead creation, duplicate rating events, profile sync or a second score on the Party. A reply to a feedback invitation may come through a shared or forwarded mailbox; possession of its response link cannot certify CRM identity or authority over the underlying case. The feedback owner must expose only qualified records and effects; CRM's own context and business commands remain separately authorized.

## Why A wins, and where it does not

**Product judgment: A is strongest as an optional tenant-enabled policy.** It gives eligible requesters a more consistent opportunity to respond and removes a recurring staff decision. B offers excellent situational discretion but can miss requests or favor cases expected to receive positive ratings; it still requires the same secure response/retention core. C is a fully valid no-build choice when ordinary relationship feedback is sufficient and further invitations would not drive useful action.

A does not eliminate selection or nonresponse bias. Only eligible people are invited, only some respond, and dissatisfaction may concern the result rather than staff conduct. [AAPOR's best-practices guidance](https://aapor.org/standards-and-ethics/best-practices/) supports neutral questions, explicit recruitment/population and transparent response limitations. Therefore do not claim representative satisfaction, infer ratings from silence or AI, compare small samples as staff performance rankings, or import commercial response targets. No Asym field study currently establishes universal demand, optimal frequency or an improvement in donor retention.

No source proves that all support-related feedback mail has the same communication classification. Actual purpose/content, applicable preferences and qualified recipient policy must be preserved and reviewed for the selected answer. The broader legal distinctions cited by the vendor report are not a blanket legal determination for ministries. There is no justification here for new marketing consent, fundraising appeals, survey reminders or third-party tracking.

## Questions deliberately not bundled into this choice

The founder is choosing scope and invitation initiation only. The selected-answer review must settle the exact feedback question/scale, timing, neutral eligible endings, recipient/cardinality/identity limits, repeat/change/expiry semantics, policy activation/backfill/disablement, reopen/merge/delivery races, safe response submission, retention/correction/export, access through CRM and accountable attention to feedback. These are necessary elaborations after A or B, not presumed settings or accepted defaults now. If C is chosen, no dedicated response model, token route or new score is built.

A/B need a proper purpose-owned invitation/response lifecycle and P17/P6 message qualification. They do not need the entire Phase 34 forms/workflow product, a satisfaction score on every signature, a private knowledge store or AI analysis. A response-page visit must not itself commit a rating; a public page must not reveal conversation content or create an account just to collect one brief assessment.
