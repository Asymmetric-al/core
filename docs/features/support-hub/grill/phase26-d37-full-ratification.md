## Exact recorded decision

Support provides a stable, private **My drafts** entry for the current staff member's saved external Reply drafts across currently accessible Support inboxes, independent of ordinary conversation work-status filters. Rows show permitted conversation metadata and authoritative save time, with no draft-body preview or body indexing. Opening a row restores the same exact source-owned draft and current qualified context in the existing Tiptap composer. Quiet bounded autosave and explicit save recovery preserve one atomic draft bundle; current authorization, optimistic concurrency, cancellation and durable operation identities prevent loss, exposure or duplicate sends. Discard is explicit and conditional; send consumes the exact draft through existing Support/P17/P6 admission and immutable delivery. CRM uses the same authorized source. R01–R28 and all linked contracts are the complete accepted decision.

The whole journey is **write → save quietly → find in My drafts → resume the same composer → Send or deliberately Discard**. This is one small personal destination over one canonical private draft owner. It is not a new inbox, shared status, CRM record, reusable Email Studio template or email outbox.

## Accepted experience and limits

- Keep My drafts in Support work navigation even when empty. No sidebar count, urgency badge, forced landing, dashboard tile, additional picker or global draft-filter product. Direct return to a known conversation still resumes the same eligible draft.
- Include all eligible own saved external Reply drafts across accessible inboxes and ordinary Open, Waiting and Resolved work states. Current source privacy, retention and actual draft lifecycle determine eligibility. One row is a draft, not a CRM person or merged handling root.
- Use metadata-only compact rows, **Search conversation titles**, a separate authorized Inbox filter, Refresh and Load more. Requester context is display-only. Preserve D22 source/index eligibility; no private-body corpus, recipient search or unrestricted CRM lookup. No Send or bulk destructive actions in the finder.
- Order by last accepted meaningful author edit with a stable identity tie-breaker. Reads, no-ops and housekeeping do not bump recency. Do not move rows under the user's focus. **20 rows initially, maximum 50 per request** are accepted interface/query bounds; neither is a total population limit. Keyset pagination must reach the full eligible population without an exact count.
- Opening or focusing an empty Reply, hydration, selection and normal signature seeding create no phantom draft. The first meaningful authored bundle change may create one. A valid incomplete draft can save before it is ready to send. An existing draft emptied by its author remains until deliberate Discard or accepted Send.
- Autosave after **750 ms of quiet**, with an attempt by **5 seconds** during eligible continuous input, is accepted scheduling behavior for a foreground, online, authorized, valid-schema, non-IME-composing editor with no unresolved earlier save. It is not a network acknowledgment or crash-durability promise. Coalesce work and allow one in-flight save per active controller; provide Save now and Retry save.
- **Saved** means the newest eligible whole bundle has an exact durable acknowledgment. An old acknowledgment cannot mark newer typing saved or replace it. Restore the exact source, audience, target, signature, quote, file manifest and staged plan in the same existing composer; do not flash defaults or silently adopt a current CRM address.
- Preserve list/search/scroll context on supported same-session return. Cross-device recovery promises acknowledged content/context, not identical caret coordinates. Mobile displays source context before raising the keyboard. Use existing Base UI and base-maia tokens, accessible messages and focus, 320 CSS-pixel reflow, 200% text resize, 400% zoom-equivalent reflow, international names/RTL/Thai/IME and reduced-motion qualification. The 44 CSS-pixel touch target is an Asym product target, distinct from WCAG's 24 CSS-pixel minimum and exceptions.

## Accepted safety and recovery

The private draft has a trusted tenant, real staff owner, stable immutable origin and current revision. At most one active external Reply draft exists per tenant/owner/original conversation. Actual TEXT source IDs and UUID identity relationships remain distinct. Owner, actor, tenant and source authority come from trusted platform context, never caller-controlled agent names or email matching. A source assignment, conversation membership, CRM relationship or ordinary administrator role does not reveal another person's private draft.

**Draft origin, selected reply source and current handling root are separate facts.** An A-origin draft may explicitly target a B message under D2/D10; its private slot stays anchored to A while the admitted reply retains B-source affinity. Do not rehome, copy or merge private drafts. Two own slots deliberately targeting B remain subject to the existing component collision review. Merge Undo and access changes require current destination/source review.

Save the canonical document and the complete semantic bundle atomically, with revision checks and durable operation identity. Validate original canonical structure before Tiptap normalization can strip unsupported content. Same-person tab/device conflicts preserve permitted local work and offer deliberate review; no blind overwrite, automatic merge, CRDT service or shared drafting lock. Reads, no-op saves and hydration do not manufacture edits.

Unknown create/save/discard results recover the **original immutable operation**, with late-admission fencing before definite nonacceptance can be asserted. An absent lookup or canceled HTTP request is not proof that nothing happened. A scoped body-free operation handle survives supported navigation; this creates no second private-content ledger.

Ordinary navigation flushes settled committed editor/IME state and continues after confirmed save without a modal. Known failure offers Retry, Stay or informed Leave. **An unresolved save must not trap navigation:** an explicit Leave explains that the earlier save may still finish and the newest unacknowledged changes may be lost. It neither cancels an admitted effect nor authorizes a replacement Send. Pending browser File bytes require retaining a qualified transfer, waiting or explicit Leave without the unfinished attachment even when text is Saved. Combine text/file/unknown-save consequences in one guard; no offline body/file vault. Browser close/crash protection is best-effort. Current privacy loss or account/tenant change clears forbidden local content and Undo.

Discard has one clear confirmation and conditionally terminates the exact reviewed revision/generation. It removes only that draft and its draft-only pending changes; the conversation and published messages remain. Old saves cannot resurrect it. No trash/Undo/restore product, timer or bulk discard is added. Draft attachment occurrence cleanup respects the actual asset owner. Discard cannot cancel a possibly admitted Send.

Actual Send requalifies the newest exact bundle and atomically consumes its draft generation through D4 admission. Denial before admission retains the active draft. Admitted queued/failed/unknown dispatch is handled through its existing intent and is never recreated as a fresh draft for blind retransmission.
