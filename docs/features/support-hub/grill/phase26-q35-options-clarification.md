# Q35 — Explicit comparison of note-correction policies

**14 September 2026. Q35 remains unanswered. D1–D34 and every amendment remain fully ratified.** This clarifies presentation of the existing question; it changes no accepted decision, policy, timing window or implementation authority.

The decision concerns how staff correct an already-posted internal Support note. Example: Maya wrote that a donor requested a Tuesday callback, then notices the donor actually said Thursday. A later change of the donor's plans would instead be a new update in every option.

| Policy                                         | Concrete result                                                                                                                                            | Benefit                                                                      | Cost                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Author edits with visible history**      | Maya corrects Tuesday to Thursday in her own note. The note keeps its original author/time, shows Edited and offers currently permitted earlier revisions. | Clear current information and easy corrections with traceable changes.       | Requires governed revision history and conflict handling. Earlier readers/notifications cannot be recalled.       |
| **B — Linked corrections only**                | Tuesday remains in the original note; a new correction says Thursday. Both entries visibly link together.                                                  | Strong explicit chronology; no ordinary in-place revision of posted content. | More entries, including for small errors; correction links must prevent acting on stale text.                     |
| **C — Brief editing window, then corrections** | Maya can use A briefly after posting; after that, she uses B.                                                                                              | Immediate typo repair while stabilizing older notes.                         | Adds a timer and two journeys, including interruptions or saves near expiry. No grace duration has been selected. |

**Single recommendation: A.** Permit the original author with current source/edit permission to correct their own note, with visible qualified history. It keeps day-to-day donor-care records readable while making changes attributable. It introduces no administrator override to rewrite another author's statement. New events still receive new notes, and material corrections can use existing deliberate staff attention.

The recommendation is a product judgment, not unanimous vendor practice. [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8838326-conversations-faqs) documents author-only editing with an Edited marker; [HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/help-desk/create-edit-and-manage-notes-in-help-desk) documents inline edits and a property-history panel. Neither proves the full proposed Asym revision contract. [Zendesk](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408885807258-Can-I-edit-recall-or-delete-comments-in-my-tickets) does not permit ordinary comment editing, making B a serious alternative. [Help Scout](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/32-edit-threads-and-notes) permits note edits but does not preserve original note text; that history tradeoff is not adopted in A. These primary pages were reopened on14 September2026.

Every policy retains D16 privacy redaction and D17 expiry, including old revisions and correction links. Ordinary edits do not resend donor email, renew retention or change CRM/financial facts. Canonical Support notes remain Support-owned when viewed through CRM. P17/P6 retain any independently authorized staff-notification preparation/delivery; routine edits are not new optional alerts. D26 recorded request briefs, incoming/sent messages and other owners' records are outside this question.
