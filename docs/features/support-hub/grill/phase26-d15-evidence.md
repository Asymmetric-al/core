5. The imported canonical preference query key is static and lacks tenant/viewer/role components. This does not by itself prove cross-user cache disclosure.

## Independent findings adopted

Three independent reviews covered lifecycle/source scope, current data/authorization/P6-P17 contracts, and current vendors/UX. Final QA challenged the written clauses and blueprint. The adopted synthesis is:

1. Preserve deliberate self-only enrollment; no automatic or administrator-added followers.
2. Distinguish personal interest, direct attention, work, email audience, CRM identity and engagement.
3. Bind original-source generations instead of root booleans or follower arrays.
4. Make partial following and inclusion of currently shown threads explicit without hidden counts.
5. Use desired-state commands and persistent Stop/Follow again, with old retries unable to reverse newer intent.
6. Separate confirmed access loss from an authorization outage; regrant cannot revive old notice history.
7. Limit activity to four meaningful human/source classes and keep routine churn/technical events quiet.
8. Suppress optional self-events only through trusted human authorship; send confirmation remains D4/P6-owned.
9. Retain D10 actual note/reply affinity and partition original interests through Undo.
10. Retain D12's no-inheritance rule even when another conversation fulfills transferred D14 reply work.
11. Deduplicate only declared purpose/channel-equivalent effects; retain separate required meanings.
12. Use P17 Information, existing engagement and current-authorized history; do not invent a personal Support status machine.
13. Explicitly qualify the missing per-member applicability guard; one person's Stop cannot close the common activity fence.
14. Separate source/outbox commit from first notification admission, keeping intake independent of recipient enumeration.
15. Preserve whole bounded all-before-any compilation, including zero members and immutable released membership; do not fake per-recipient occurrences.
16. Bind optional email to historical channel-enable generations and current guards so Off/On cannot email old activity.
17. Freeze source usefulness independently of actual P6 availability; delayed first projection/sealing cannot renew freshness.
18. Add the narrow new optional informational staff-material class, preserving the existing immutable class scope.
19. Provide a canonical generic Email Studio pointer with no copied sensitive content and a personal email default Off.
20. Email links are typed authenticated navigation; GET/scanners never Stop or execute business actions.
21. Use a native Following filter, All statuses and source-relevant ordering without message-body scans.
22. Repair the actual caller-selected preference/identity seams before reusing them for personal authority.
23. Scope caches/counts before pagination and distinguish the static query-key observation from a proven cache exploit.
24. Use correct action/menu versus pressed-toggle semantics and require real accessibility proof.
25. Record nine actionable owner controls and a measured finite compiler envelope as activation evidence, not invented scale claims.
26. Separate unread and recent-history cutoffs so read/archive/Stop do not erase permitted history.
27. Distinguish compiled/prepared email from actual dispatch: unstarted effects still honor Stop; sent/in-flight mail cannot be recalled.

These are reconciled requirements in [R01–R26](phase26-d15-adversarial-review.md), not wholesale adoption of every independent suggestion or vendor feature. The exact owner prerequisites, channel/source freshness policies and UI wording are proposed amendments to A. Their rationale and costs are recorded rather than hidden as implementation details.
