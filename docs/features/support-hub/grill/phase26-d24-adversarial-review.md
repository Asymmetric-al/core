# Curated reply-and-work shortcuts

**D24 and every adopted amendment are fully founder-ratified, 12 September 2026. Historical disposition: Accept with required amendments.** C, the complete 30-clause/23-category review, UX/data/Email Studio/CRM/Resend contracts, four terms and required proof are accepted. Future AI remains a governed research direction. D1–D24 are fully ratified as grooming, not implemented behavior or formal specification.

The corrected model is a **reviewed reply draft with a small, visible set of pending Support changes**. A shortcut prepares it; one final guarded admission commits the message and its permitted local changes. It is neither an execution loop nor a second template, CRM or workflow platform. The [UX](phase26-d24-shortcut-ux.md), [owner/data/admission contract](phase26-d24-data-contract.md), [evidence and independent synthesis](phase26-d24-evidence.md), three independent reviews and two isolated current-source probes form the complete record.

## Corrected decision clauses

### D24-R01 — Accept C as one curated human-reply capability

Provide shared curated reply-and-work shortcuts for one current authorized Support conversation's external Reply draft. A shortcut supplies a suggested reply and permitted Support changes for deliberate review. Selecting, previewing, editing or saving it never sends or changes live work. No personal shortcut library, action-only execution mode, internal-note-and-work mode, bulk reply, new conversation/intake creator or automatic invocation is added. D18 My/Shared wording and normal individual controls remain available.

A, content-free Support action shortcuts, remains the strongest narrower alternative; B, existing controls plus saved wording, is the strongest no-build alternative. C is selected because it can make recurring reply-and-follow-through combinations coherent. This is founder product direction supported by documented help-desk patterns, not measured Asym demand, time savings or proof every tenant uses the same combination. A shortcut that adds no useful combination can remain ordinary D18 wording.

### D24-R02 — Ministry fit without invented business authority

Serve the founder's target organizations that receive donor requests while supporting missionaries and mission projects. Let each tenant curate its actual wording, language and support practices. Illustrations such as asking for receipt details or explaining next steps on a giving request are examples, not seeded departments, observed tenant frequencies or permission to infer sensitive ministry facts.

Support can coordinate an owning-domain action and communicate about its verified outcome; it cannot make a shortcut perform or certify a refund, receipt correction, recurring-gift change, allocation, CRM contact update or member-care action. A requester's email match is not identity or representation proof. Unknown/unlinked requesters can use the D1 qualified no-Party reply path. Do not create a CRM person to fill a template or require a donor relationship to receive ordinary help.

### D24-R03 — A fixed form preset, not a parallel workflow interpreter

Support owns a versioned preset for its existing human reply-admission form, referencing P17/D18 wording and a closed flat desired-state patch. It does not own an ordered action program. Reuse the shared code-owned command descriptors and domain semantics; if that narrow owner seam is missing, qualify it rather than add a second configurable vocabulary or synchronize registries later.

Phase34 retains the sole configurable trigger/condition/action vocabulary and workflow-run state. D24 introduces no trigger, event enrollment, branch, loop, wait, schedule, arbitrary HTTP/script/SQL, connector call, task engine or rule evaluator. It does not forward-gate on the whole Phase34 product. This explicitly reconciles the roadmap boundary: the only executor here is the existing D4 human-authorized reply admission extended for the fixed allowed fields. Future automation cannot invoke this human authority under a forged actor; any automatic message requires its own qualified producer contract.

### D24-R04 — One exact shared wording dependency and immutable shortcut revision

Each shortcut has one tenant/stable identity, plain internal name, optional description/when-to-use guidance, authorized use scope, immutable active revision and editable candidate lineage. Its active revision references one exact eligible published **Shared D18 Reply** wording source/variant policy, plus the fixed Support patch. It contains no independently editable reply HTML/text or embedded workflow code. Personal My replies cannot be exposed through a shared shortcut; deliberate contribution first follows D18.

Activation requires suitable shared wording and at least one declared non-Keep Support directive. Runtime may find some directives already satisfied; report those as unchanged, not failure or fabricated activity. All directives becoming unnecessary does not justify hidden mutation: show that the final reply has no work changes. Removing the entire pending plan deliberately returns the draft to normal reply semantics while preserving actual text provenance. No invisible fallback to a different wording revision or source is allowed.

### D24-R05 — Closed initial Support patch

The initial patch permits exactly these dimensions: one D3 final work choice (Keep current status/Open/Waiting for requester/Waiting on our side/Resolved); at most one D6 handling choice over current responsible team/person (Keep, explicit compatible team/person, Me, or Shared/unassigned); and unique, disjoint Add-label and Remove-label sets referencing the curated D21 catalog. Keep means no requested change, not a stale captured value. No action order, toggles, repeated writes to the same dimension or contradictory label sets exist.

Me resolves to the current actual responder. On already assigned work it is reviewed reassignment, not a claim operation allowed to steal a concurrent claim. Preserve the current team unless explicitly changed; validate the resulting team/person relationship. D6 manual eligibility applies even outside an automatic pool or above its advisory automatic limit. Do not run routing rules, reset receiving controls or waive D7/D8 human-control/review fences.

No new priority, snooze/reminder preset, inbox move, conversation merge, related-conversation creation, note/mention, delegated task, follower, recipient/subject/thread/sender/signature change, safety disposition, retention/redaction, CRM/giving/care action or external effect is permitted. D3 status changes retain their existing reminder consequences; the shortcut invents no independent reminder policy. The selected action list replaces the legacy macro enum as accepted scope.

### D24-R06 — Use, curation, publication and action authority remain distinct

Use current Core capabilities and existing qualified inbox/team scope to separate shortcut use, shortcut maintenance/activation, P17 shared-wording authoring/publication and actual source mutations. A shortcut's audience is only a discoverability/use restriction; it grants no source, CRM, asset or assignment permission. Effective availability intersects shortcut scope, current conversation context, D18 source/asset audience and the actor's actual action authority.

A curator can maintain reusable metadata without general access to donor conversations. A source publisher does not become a Support administrator. Ordinary qualified Support configuration activation needs no new mandatory second-reviewer workflow; content publication retains P17's actual protected and tenant-stricter rules. Widening scope requires current authority and revalidation of all exposed variants/assets; do not silently widen P17 content audience or use an old audience snapshot as current access.

### D24-R07 — Find and preview without executing

Expose a labelled **Reply shortcuts** entry within the existing composer insertion/action area, distinct from content-only **Saved replies**. Use a compact searchable picker with understandable names, concise use guidance and current scoped availability; a person may browse or preview without changing their draft. Avoid forced folder syntax, dozens of seeded definitions, a second global search page, automatic selection, popularity-driven changes or AI chrome.

Preview shows the selected wording variant, introduced files/fill-ins and concrete pending status/handling/label outcomes in the current authorized context. It distinguishes available, incompatible, missing required input and unavailable dependencies without exposing forbidden titles or records. A staff member sees what will be prepared before selecting **Use shortcut**. Keyboard selection inside the picker never sends; cancellation preserves the draft and focus.

### D24-R08 — Stage a coherent private draft, with no live Support effect

Using a shortcut copies its exact qualified wording into the current canonical Tiptap Reply draft under D18 insertion rules and stages the patch in the same logical private-draft revision. Record shortcut/wording revision, insertion mode, actual source/asset provenance and relevant current context. No live label, assignment, status, note, message intent or provider effect occurs at this point. Saving/restoring the private draft preserves this meaning and never executes it.

The work patch is typed draft metadata, not executable Tiptap nodes or text that can be copied/pasted into another message. The source remains a normal editable human reply; typed variables/fill-ins and assets obey D18/D23. One draft can have at most one staged shortcut plan. Do not stack macros, deduce an action sequence from button order or silently merge conflicting presets.

### D24-R09 — Keep pending changes visible beside the final action

Show a compact **With this reply** summary beside the composer action area. It names the actual pending status, handling and labels, distinguishing Keep, requested change, unchanged and blocked. Pending values do not masquerade as live conversation fields. Staff may edit or remove permitted pending directives through the same qualified controls without editing the shared shortcut; modifications remain local and recorded in final intent.

Preserve D4's explicit primary labels: Send reply, Send and open, Send and wait for requester, Send and wait on our side, or Send and resolve. Where labels/handling are also staged, visibly and programmatically associate the With this reply summary/count with that action so it cannot appear to send only. The phrase Send and apply describes the composite operation, not a replacement generic button that hides the status. Native header controls retain their separate immediate semantics; their changes trigger current revalidation rather than silently rewrite the pending plan.

### D24-R10 — Change, detach, Undo and mode switching cannot leave a hidden plan

**Change shortcut** presents an explicit choice. Default **Keep current reply; replace pending changes** preserves the existing body, attachments, signature and real wording provenance; the new shortcut's suggested wording/files are not inserted. Record kept-current mode rather than falsely crediting the new source with that text. Optional **Replace reply with new wording** uses D18's explicit reviewed whole-body replacement and safe asset/Undo rules; no fragile attempt to find previously inserted paragraphs after editing.

**Remove shortcut, keep reply** detaches pending shortcut work only. It preserves current human wording and its source restrictions. Undo/Redo that crosses a shortcut staging/replacement boundary restores the prior draft and pending plan coherently, or conservatively disarms affected pending work with a clear explanation if exact restoration cannot be proved. Ordinary Undo of later typing does not needlessly discard a visible valid plan. No text-only Undo may leave a hidden Resolve/assignment action armed, and no Redo may rearm without current eligibility.

Reply and Internal note remain separate drafts. Switching to Note never carries shortcut actions or external audience into Add note. Returning restores only the permitted Reply draft/plan; it does not execute it. Conversation/tenant/account changes fence stale responses and drafts. Detaching a plan is not permission to send restricted copied wording.

### D24-R11 — Honest wording, values, files and signatures

P17/D18 owns canonical structured wording, typed fields, one-use fill-ins, language, assets, publication and safe copy-in. D23 owns the managed signature behavior. A shortcut cannot override the selected signature, external identity, visible recipients or shared frame. HTML and real plain text are compiled from the final canonical document, not independent macro body fields or repeated brace-string substitution.

Required unresolved fill-ins or forbidden values block preparation; optional omission/fallback is the existing typed contract, not sample data or empty-string invention. Do not infer identity, language, donor status or protected disclosure from an email match. A common group reply must be valid for every admitted recipient and preserve D2's visible-group rules. Source updates do not silently repersonalize or overwrite the draft.

Introduced assets use exact qualified D18 occurrences and current readiness/disclosure. Repeated references do not duplicate existing attachments; cancellation/Undo removes only introduced appearances, never preexisting or later independent work. Internal task/care files and official receipts cannot become reusable assets merely by being attached to a shortcut. Protected outcome confirmations require their actual owner-qualified facts/purpose; this feature cannot prove arbitrary natural-language assertions true or perform the action they describe.

### D24-R12 — One atomic reply-and-work admission

Extend the existing D4 server-authorized local admission to the fixed desired-state patch. First obtain durable ready P17 preparation for the exact reviewed body/audience/signature/assets/locale. Then one conditional Asym database transaction claims that preparation and commits the Support message intent, final D3 work/reminder effects, D6 handling, D21 label effects, trusted before/after history, shortcut-use receipt and durable dispatch/recovery intent. No request loop of separately committed mutations implements this capability.

Validate the complete resulting state and current owner preconditions in one unit of work. Failure, stale review or denied required component before admission commits none of these effects and preserves the private draft. Unchanged directives are explicit no-ops, not skipped unavailable actions. No stop-on-error option or compensating best-effort rollback of the current runner is the permanent fix. P17 preparation staged before admission has no dispatch authority until claimed; orphaned candidates follow owner cleanup. No transaction spans Resend I/O.

### D24-R13 — Canonical work and handling truth outrank shortcut convenience

D3 defines the four work meanings. A courtesy update does not establish Waiting; an unsent draft does not contact a requester. Waiting on our side requires a real outstanding input already sought; a shortcut does not create the missing task/request. Open precedence, mixed waits, reminder consequences and current unread/review obligations stay intact. Resolve must pass D3/D5/D11 obligations, including required owner-result review; an independently linked process need not be terminal when Support can legitimately finish.

D6/D7/D8 assignment and handling controls remain authoritative. Shortcut use does not waive a required handling review or current access fence, select a CRM owner, take back absence coverage or force a new automatic assignment. D7 Keep is a runtime coverage decision, not a reusable shortcut instruction. D8 review-first blocks handling changes until the separate review, not otherwise-authorized reply/status/label work when no handling change is requested. D8 A can be settled by an explicit eligible individual manual assignment even when automatic release is paused or Shared lacks independent coverage. Do not impose automatic-pool limits on a valid manual assignment. A changed assignee is not the reply author. All affected original label contributors and their current source permissions follow D10/D21; root-only membership edits or hidden-source mutation are invalid. D5 No response remains its separate explicit ending review and evidence; it is not a hidden shortcut status preset.

### D24-R14 — Review binds current context and resulting intent

Bind the reviewed shortcut revision, actual final document, typed patch, conversation/original-source topology, relevant work/handling/label/membership generations, actual actor, complete admitted audience and P17 preparation dependencies. A different conversation, late reply, merge/Undo, permission change, source restriction or target departure can make a formerly valid preview stale. Refresh current permitted context and require review of changed meaning; never auto-rebase Resolve or silently drop a failed directive.

Use current source/field checks before previews, target choices, counts, snippets and final admission. A result arriving after navigating from one donor to another cannot populate the new draft. Preserve unrelated typed work and distinguish the original command outcome from today's state. Routine metadata edits that do not affect the admitted meaning need not create artificial collisions; security/control and actual relevant revisions must.

### D24-R15 — Durable retries and provider evidence stay separate

The durable business identity is the reviewed reply-admission intent, with an immutable semantic hash including its final patch and provenance. Same identity/same input reconciles the same result; changed input conflicts. A new deliberate reply is new intent even if wording is identical. Lost responses reconcile before another send is enabled. Request/provider transport keys are not permanent business identity.

After local admission, the work effects have happened and the reply is Queued, not Delivered. Resend failure, suppression, uncertainty or a later adverse event does not roll back or replay old labels/handling/status. D4/P6 reconciles the original sealed effect; D3 creates fresh causally appropriate review when needed under current handling. No new sender/account, rekey, mutable template, repeated whole-group send or blind resend. Counterpart domains' actual action status remains independent.

### D24-R16 — Create and maintain through a focused Support form

Provide **Support settings → Reply shortcuts**, with Active, Drafts and Archived views plus a Needs attention filter. Each entry shows name, eligible scope, wording version/language, compact work summary and safe maintenance status. Use plain searchable metadata, restrained columns and current permissions; no graph canvas, nested folder grammar or mandatory tags. Tenant Support configuration maintainers are accountable; creator departure cannot orphan tenant custody or impose a new global role.

New/duplicate creates an unpublished configuration candidate with a clear name, optional when-to-use guidance, qualified use scope, a selected published Shared Reply and the fixed work fields. Incomplete but structurally safe drafts can be saved with actionable validation; they cannot activate. **Create/Edit wording in Email Studio** opens the same owning source with preserved return context. No second Tiptap body editor or raw HTML field appears in the shortcut form, and no Save conversation as shortcut harvests live donor/care material. Reusable wording contribution first follows D18 generalization and publication.

### D24-R17 — Content publication and shortcut activation are distinct

P17 publishes the exact wording candidate under its own permissions/review. Support saves, reviews and activates an exact shortcut configuration revision only after source purpose/scope/variants/assets and target/schema compatibility are proven. **Review and activate** exposes wording, use scope and resulting work together, with before/after for edits. Activation is not sending, task completion or content publication, and no new universal second reviewer is required.

If one contextual action helps publish wording then activate the shortcut, preserve separate truthful results and durable reconciliation. Published wording with failed activation is not an Active shortcut; prior valid configuration stays active. Activation compares the expected active head and commits revision/control/history atomically. Selecting a new published wording revision is deliberate; no mutable latest reference or source save silently changes all shortcuts or existing staged drafts.

### D24-R18 — Keep updates and dependency repair understandable

Ordinary edits create a candidate; activating a new revision affects future selections. Valid already staged copies/plans remain pinned unless deliberately changed, subject to current restrictions and command preconditions. Show **Newer wording available** separately from **Needs attention**; old still-eligible content is not broken merely because a new version exists. Review a wording/target/scope update before activation and preserve actual locale/fallback meaning without silently dropping a translation.

Ineligible label operations, departed targets, inaccessible wording, unavailable assets or incompatible schema produce precise maintenance reasons. Label eligibility is operation-specific: Add requires current active catalog eligibility; Remove may target exact currently authorized existing memberships of an ordinarily archived label. Archive alone does not invalidate that permitted Remove. Missing or restricted labels retain their current owner gates. Do not silently substitute an assignee, recreate a label, widen a source audience or resolve a broken reference by title/email. A current use can be blocked even if the health projection is stale; direct source gates remain authoritative. Repair uses existing owner navigation and exact references, not database surgery. No routine mass email or repeated banner on every conversation announces a library edit.

### D24-R19 — Archive and safety restriction must work when the shortcut is broken

Ordinary **Archive** stops new selection and activation without requiring the broken wording, target or action values to resolve or compile. It preserves already staged otherwise valid revisions under their owner policy and is not an emergency revocation. **Restore** rechecks current eligibility and never silently restores old active dependencies or safety-restricted material. An older shortcut revision can become a new reviewed candidate; history is not edited in place.

Actual safety/authorization withdrawal fences current reads, staging and qualifying preparation/admission immediately, with the existing definitely-unsubmitted versus may-have-submitted rules. It does not recall sent email. Tenant source/configuration custody survives maintainer departure; same-email accounts inherit no access. Routine hard Delete is not a shortcut around retained dependencies, source evidence or cleanup. Finite records policy owns actual disposal and safe attribution minimization.

### D24-R20 — Database, RLS and the complete mutation surface

Asym Postgres remains authoritative through packages/api. Enforce same-tenant source/publication/variant, shortcut revision, scope, draft, conversation, team/member, label contributor and preparation/receipt relationships with actual endpoint types and suitable composite constraints. Active revisions are immutable; one current active head and at most one staged plan per draft hold. Typed patch structure, noncontradictory labels, valid state/nullability, revision monotonicity and idempotency uniqueness are enforced at the authoritative boundary and in constraints where appropriate.

Tenant, actual user/profile/member, author, reviewer/control status and audit attribution derive from trusted current server context. A caller can propose permitted fields and expected revisions, not move a row across tenant/purpose/custody, publish by setting a flag or forge a human execution identity. Creation and update differ; a missing update target is not recreated by upsert.

Review grants, direct collection/REST writers, effective SELECT/USING/WITH CHECK, additional permissive policies, views, RPC execute grants, fixed definer search paths, storage and service/worker code together. PostgreSQL can derive WITH CHECK from USING; prove actual old/new-row and column invariants rather than assuming the absence of syntax is a vulnerability. Privileged clients independently enforce the same capabilities. Fence all legacy macro, automation, import/fixture and client-history writers that bypass the compound owner boundary.

### D24-R21 — Cross-surface CRM continuity and source minimization

Requester, admitted participant, authenticated responder, public responder name, tenant membership, CRM Party, represented organization, Support coordinator and CRM owner remain distinct. The same shortcut journey may be opened in an authorized CRM-context Support conversation, preserving return context and draft; that context neither changes its recipient audience nor exposes unrelated merged originals or restricted CRM/care/giving fields.

Read only owner-authorized context needed for the actual reply. CRM/giving/care changes and protected documents use their existing owner commands, validation, approvals and history; Support can navigate or coordinate through D11 but the shortcut does not include these actions. Resolving Support does not certify their completion. Only actual admitted correspondence contributes to its permitted Support/CRM history projection. Selecting, previewing, saving, activating or archiving a shortcut creates no Party, interaction or false last-contact timestamp.

### D24-R22 — Retention, privacy and portability preserve actual provenance

Shortcut configuration/revisions, source wording/assets, private stage copies, prepared/admitted messages, command/use receipts, caches, maintenance feedback and audit have separate finite owner purposes. No shortcut receipt stores a second full reply body, resolved donor facts, raw search query or secret. D16/D17/D22 restrictions and restore barriers cover known derivatives. Template source is not a fallback for expired correspondence; Recent is never a search corpus. Detaching/removing a plan preserves actual copied-content restrictions and cannot declassify it.

Reuse qualified owner import/export where available, without adding a Support-specific format. Export only currently authorized configuration and eligible source references/content through their owner; omit real recipient fills, credentials, private drafts, permissions and activation authority. Imports create destination-owned inactive candidates with explicit source remapping and validation, never active cross-tenant recipes or automatically created labels/people. If the owner export/import seam is not activated, provide clear unavailable behavior rather than invent a new transfer system for D24.

### D24-R23 — Useful maintenance reporting with exact meaning

Provide a small curator view of current health, last recorded use and usage in a selected period, using minimal durable admission receipts. **Shortcut-assisted reply** means one locally admitted human reply retaining one staged shortcut association and its actual final patch/provenance. Count once per admission intent, including explicitly unchanged directives; exclude picker views, selection, canceled/detached plans, preflight failures and provider retries. Kept-current wording mode remains distinct from inserted/replaced source, without claiming uninserted wording was used. Queued use is not delivery or successful resolution.

Scope before aggregation and drilldown. Period uses half-open admission timestamps in the visible reporting timezone; current permission changes govern accessible results. Merge/Undo groups display without duplicating original admission facts. Exact same-evaluation drilldown/export preserves that receipt cohort, not conversation creation date or today's status. Incomplete retained evidence is Unknown/partial, not zero/Never. No usage tags pollute D21, no per-agent leaderboard, invented time-saved metric, auto-retirement threshold or AI success score. Optional specific feedback uses existing owner repair attention and safe metadata, not a new case-body feedback archive.

### D24-R24 — Accessible and fast in the actual staff journey

Preserve Core base-maia/Base UI and shared Tiptap purpose profiles. Clear labels distinguish Saved replies, Reply shortcuts, With this reply, current values and pending values. Support keyboard/touch/screen-reader use, semantic field summaries, focus return, IME, conditional errors and equivalent accessible editing. No hover-only disclosure, color-only state, drag-only action ordering, noisy countdown or automatic navigation to another conversation. Mobile preserves every declared effect and final approval; it cannot silently insert only text while discarding the plan.

Use tenant/principal/context-generation-scoped draft recovery and current source gates. Offline or uncertain save shows Not saved/Pending recovery; reconnection never sends or applies automatically. Register concrete source/document/asset/label-set/lookup/transaction/compile/query bounds and tested concurrency before activation. Validate before expensive work, use complete scoped queries and appropriate FK/lookup indexes, and prove exact boundaries and production-shaped skew. Reuse static reads and selective editor subscriptions; no second editor runtime or whole-CRM fetch per keystroke. Do not invent universal vendor quotas or latency guarantees.

### D24-R25 — Future AI is an explicit direction, not current scope

Record future AI augmentation for discovery, context-appropriate draft assistance and maintenance candidates. Do not add an AI button, vector index, model/provider call, background transcript mining, Tiptap AI service, automatic translation, generated action language or service execution now. The useful foundation is already needed by the manual product: typed immutable sources/presets, exact staged intent, current eligibility, minimal outcome evidence and the same reviewed admission boundary.

Any later AI capability consumes ADR0099's purpose-routed tenant AI foundation and Phase40's product boundary, not a Support-only model router or copied CRM. ADR0035 Eve concerns GitHub-session engineering orchestration and is not authority for processing customer support data. The shared product foundation owns tenant provider connections and credential revisions, exact purpose/capability/model/region/data-use/budget bindings, classification- and authorization-gated minimum-data AI Egress Manifests, invocation evidence and non-authoritative suggestion versions. Restricted/care/security/high-risk contexts retain the governing remote-egress prohibition; no silent provider/model/purpose fallback is permitted. Retrieval/processing permission, tenant/source restrictions, provider retention and actual actor authority must be separately qualified. Read permission is not automatically permission to send data to a model. D22's indexing exclusions cannot be bypassed by calling a new index an AI assistant. Human acceptance still invokes the owning typed command; neither AI output nor its egress manifest confers write authority.

### D24-R26 — Research the AI progression before enabling it

The recommended future research sequence is: evaluate useful candidate tasks and consent/data scope on qualified synthetic/redacted data; test suggesting only currently eligible active shortcut revisions; then evaluate explicitly requested wording adaptations and unpublished maintenance candidates if they outperform the manual baseline without weakening safety. These are research hypotheses, not accepted launch features or a commitment to a particular model.

Future output is untrusted data constrained to the same typed fields and authorized source references. Incoming messages, attachments, template text and tool results cannot instruct the system to widen access, run a command or publish. Suggestions must be reviewable/dismissible with no silent selection, and human final body/actions remain visible. No AI auto-send, activation, publication, assignment, CRM/giving mutation or training from live conversations is authorized here. Similarity/confidence or user feedback is not permission or proof of truth. No operational or governance review is waived by generated wording.

Before later activation, the owning phase must set falsifiable domain/safety/usability criteria, authorized datasets, source-citation/abstention behavior, prompt-injection/privacy tests, cost/latency limits, model/prompt/version trace, retention controls, rollout/kill switch and accountable incident response. Compare wrong-shortcut/incorrect-action/unsupported-claim rates and time to a correct reviewed reply against manual work; clicks or adoption alone are insufficient. Keep raw sensitive prompts/output out of generic logs and prevent cross-tenant learning/feedback authority. Human public responder identity cannot disguise a future autonomous actor.

### D24-R27 — Failure visibility and accountable operations

Differentiate missing/ineligible source, incomplete fill-in, invalid resulting work, stale context, save/preparation failure, denied admission, admitted Queued reply and delivery uncertainty. Preserve permitted work and provide the exact next safe action. Shortcut health is a projection; it never overrides current authoritative eligibility. Plain reply and unrelated inbox work remain available when their actual dependencies are valid; do not fallback to stale cached HTML, a different wording source or a direct sender.

Audit curated source changes, activation/archive and actual admission separately from technical traces and security incidents. Use stable safe source/revision/command/cause identifiers and current responsible owner; do not log bodies or raw PII merely to debug a preset. Required source/dispatch/repair intent is durable and idempotent with the owning effect. P17/P6 existing local attention can represent qualified real repair, without a new shortcut-notification template or broad subscriber blast. Reading a notice is not repair.

### D24-R28 — Migrate and roll out one permanent path

Inventory current macros, canned content references, priorities/snooze/note effects, client/server runners, mutable action arrays, broad writers and all entry surfaces. Convert only eligible generic wording through D18/P17 and only fixed permitted fields into inactive reviewed shortcut candidates. Unsupported or uncertain combinations remain read-only for explicit remediation; do not silently drop a step and activate a weaker operation. Preserve actual source IDs/provenance/history and explicit unknowns, not historical false outcomes.

Add schema/owner contracts and compatible readers first; qualify P17 preparation and shared in-transaction primitives; implement one staged draft/compound admission; fence legacy writers and automatic paths; migrate and validate; then activate qualified scopes. Mixed old/new deployments cannot expose the new composer over old sequential mutations. Stop controls prevent new staging/admission while preserving safe draft/history and admitted-envelope reconciliation. Rollback/restore cannot reenable raw HTML, broad permissions, old loop semantics or withdrawn source. Use reviewed roll-forward when new data cannot be safely understood by old code.

### D24-R29 — Precise proof and traceability

Carry D24-R01–R30 into the glossary/ADR and these companion contracts, then only through later explicitly authorized OpenSpec/design/tasks/tickets and implementation/release evidence. P01–P50 below prove positive, negative, source, permission, concurrency, migration, accessibility and cross-owner outcomes. No GitHub issues or formal specification are created now. The two isolated probes establish four current-source counterexamples/observations, not a proof of the proposed transaction, renderer, browser or provider path.

### D24-R30 — The resulting bounded product

The complete capability is one curated shared Reply preset, one exact eligible source, one visible staged plan and one human final admission, with understandable creation, activation, repair, update, archival and scoped maintenance evidence. Preserve D18 wording, D23 signatures/Tiptap, D3 work, D6 handling, D21 labels, P17 preparation and P6 Resend as their actual owners. No second CRM, workflow framework, public portal, ticket hierarchy, AI platform, message catalog or financial engine is introduced to make the shortcut feel seamless.
