# D40 — Recoverable unwanted handling and optional future-mail holds

**Current ratification — 15 September 2026:** The founder fully ratifies D40 B and every amendment, addition, adjustment, change, update and incorporated correction. D1–D40 and all amendments are fully ratified. The [full ratification](phase26-d40-full-ratification.md) incorporates this entire record and all supporting prose. Earlier proposed/pending/intent-only and no-Q41 wording below is historical, not a repeat approval gate. Original evidence and unexecuted-runtime limits remain unchanged.

**15 September 2026. Disposition: Accept with required amendments.** The founder selected B; the complete detailed amendments below are proposed for ratification. D1–D39 and all their amendments remain fully ratified. Q40 is answered at intent level; no Q41 is opened. This is exploratory grill documentation, not formal specification or implementation authority.

B can be manageable without becoming a spam-management platform. Give staff four plainly distinct actions: **Mark unwanted**, **Restore**, **Hold future email for review**, and **Stop holding**. Marking affects current correspondence. A hold affects matching future intake. Restore does not stop a hold; stopping a hold does not release its backlog. The normal journey needs no rule builder, contact block, secondary CRM or compulsory future-mail choice.

## Exact corrected decision

R01–R30 and all substantive companion data/lifecycle/UX prose, incorporated corrections, [P01–P40 and O01–O06](phase26-d40-proof-and-operations.md) form one proposed decision. Numbered clauses below reconcile exploratory shorthand; prior accepted owner boundaries remain authoritative.

### R01 — Two independent capabilities

Support SHALL provide recoverable current unwanted handling and an optional separately chosen future-mail hold. Mark unwanted does not create a rule; Hold does not require marking the current conversation. Restore does not stop a rule; Stop holding does not release existing held inputs. No command deletes a CRM Party, changes consent/login, sends mail or grants new source access.

### R02 — A classification outside ordinary work

Unwanted SHALL be an original-source designation, not a fifth D3 work status, deleted message, successful resolution or requester reputation. Preserve prior source/work events. An all-Unwanted component presents an Unwanted banner instead of a misleading active Open/Waiting badge. A component with any ordinary original or genuine owed work stays visible in ordinary work; retained unwanted originals remain available as authorized history rather than disappearing from the combined reader. Historical outcomes are not rewritten. The projection has one canonical source, not a copied spam conversation.

### R03 — Exact eligible current scope

Mark unwanted applies to ordinary admitted email-origin sources in this selected journey, including their legitimate source history of notes and replies. It SHALL require an authorized explicit review that the bounded current source/component has no legitimate Support obligation to hide. It does not replace D26's staff-recorded created-in-error correction or D29 form-safety owners. Outstanding promises, assistance, actionable owner results or actual delivery recovery cannot be erased. A difficult complaint or donor problem is not unwanted. A mixed legitimate/unwanted conversation cannot be sidelined wholesale. Use existing exact-input No reply needed or legitimate D10/D12 corrections where applicable; do not invent message reparenting, duplicate records or an AI legitimacy scorer.

### R04 — One authoritative Mark command

The command SHALL bind exact originals, current topology/content/work revisions and actor authority. Atomically record the designation, immutable decision/result and qualified changes to current work projections. It may apply D14's exact reviewed No reply needed disposition to truly unowed contributions and retire only their inapplicable deferral/review effects; preserve genuine obligations, original ages and known misses. No redundant second click is required merely to perform those same reviewed effects. Do not append a successful resolution, fabricated reply, feedback trigger or new human-content event.

### R05 — Restore means current review

Restore SHALL remove only the applicable current mistaken designation and return the permitted source to **Open for review** under current handling/coverage authority. It is a new correction, not rollback to stale Waiting, assignment or reminder snapshots. Reverse only still-current mistaken D14 no-reply dispositions under that owner's existing correction rules; accepted-covered inputs, independent Off decisions, newer content and historical deadlines remain intact. A newly admitted relevant human input also returns its valid source to review; held-before-admission mail does not do so until genuine release/admission. Restore alone creates no incoming message, feedback opportunity or new retention lifetime.

### R06 — Originals, merges and topology

Persist designation and correction on actual original sources. A component-wide action requires exact permitted scope and eligibility for every affected original, with atomic application or conflict—not hidden partial success. A new merge involving Unwanted work requires lawful Restore first; D10 Undo remains available when otherwise permitted and preserves each original's actual designation/history. An all-Unwanted resulting component does not automatically become Open or acquire retention activity merely because ordinary D10 detachment normally creates review. A new relevant contribution changes its own valid original and current work-home projection atomically, making actual work visible without erasing the other originals' classification history. A later unrelated or newly merged original never inherits an old action. Future holding may be initiated from one qualified inbound message in mixed work without misclassifying the whole conversation.

### R07 — Drafts, notes and pending sends

Private Reply/Note drafts remain private and may save/discard while their actual custody allows. They neither block Mark by mere existence nor disappear with it. Public Reply or creating new actual Support/owner work requires Restore and normal current review first. An authorized Internal note or own-note correction may document the decision without auto-restoring work; D38 admission/mentions and D17 native-note clocks still apply. Mark SHALL NOT silently cancel or hide admitted human mail. Outstanding submission/dispatch/unknown actionable effects require their existing reconciliation or explicit permitted cancellation first; a reviewed historical uncertainty with no remaining effect must not become an eternal blocker. A newly actionable owner result or delivery failure after a valid Mark makes its valid current original/work home visible for Open review, ending only the now-inapplicable current exclusion while preserving designation history. Duplicate or already-reviewed evidence cannot repeatedly reopen work. This source-owned transition creates no fabricated human-content admission or D39 unread event.

### R08 — Current-action UX and recovery location

Offer Mark unwanted in existing conversation actions with one concise review of scope, effects and actual recoverability; future holding is a separate optional action, never a prechecked add-on. Keep a built-in **Unwanted** inbox view and a persistent **Restore** action on the same authorized conversation. Reuse the list/reader and Base UI/base-maia shell. Preserve position, focus, dirty drafts and mobile navigation; show meaningful pending/error outcomes rather than vanishing a row before durable acceptance. No permanent-delete button or new trash timer is added.

### R09 — Exact receiving inbox and observed mailbox

A hold SHALL target one tenant, one immutable qualified **receiving inbox** and one exact qualified observed sender-mailbox key. Current handling inbox, a hardcoded default, Reply-To, envelope return path, CRM preferred email, display name or quoted forward is not a substitute. Contextual creation may start from an actual admitted inbound message or a currently authorized qualified D19-held input, always with independent policy authority and the same proven receiving/mailbox pair. Staff must not release an input merely to configure a future hold; creating the rule changes neither that existing input's recorded decision nor its gates. A conversation shortcut may prefill only an unambiguous eligible pair. Show the actual sender address and receiving inbox explicitly. Ambiguous/unsupported evidence withholds the shortcut rather than guessing. Manual/web-only context cannot fabricate email evidence. No domain wildcard, plus/dot alias collapse, all-inbox scope or arbitrary sender registry is selected.

### R10 — Straightforward hold management

Use **Hold future email for review** as the deliberate command and **Sender holds** within the actual receiving inbox's existing settings for management. Show Active/Stopped, exact address/inbox, actor/start or stop evidence and a source-authorized route to related held work. Policy permission does not grant message-body/held-count access. Start/Stop are explicit actions; immutable scope is not silently edited. Restarting a stopped pair is a fresh qualified activation, not resurrected approval. No rule builder, administrator approval queue, second settings hierarchy or compulsory expiry picker is added.

### R11 — Canonical intake policy owns holding

Implement the bounded sender-hold predicate in the canonical intake owner and shared `packages/api` boundary, with one authoritative policy store/read model. This is new typed capability qualification, not a claim that current recipient-route CRUD already implements it. No Support-local matcher, second sender identity system or configurable automation engine is authorized. Phase34 retains general rule vocabulary; the narrow owner integration must be captured and proved before activation. Runtime unavailability is an owned pending/processing condition, never permission to silently ignore the hold.

### R12 — Define future by durable acceptance

Enable/Stop SHALL serialize with the canonical first durable accepted input/policy decision, not email Date, retry time or delayed Support admission. Record the effective policy revision/decision on the original receiving occurrence. Inputs accepted before Enable are not retrospectively captured; later matching inputs are held. Stop changes later first-accepted input only. Deferred hydration, repeated webhooks and routing recovery reuse the original effective decision. Initially unresolved receiving scope must be evaluated from retained original acceptance/policy evidence when resolved, not whatever policy a worker happens to see later. Current non-overridable safety and source authority are rechecked independently.

### R13 — Qualified parsing and limited trust

The intake owner SHALL parse and validate the actual supported single From mailbox and define versioned exact equality before rule creation/matching. Preserve local-part distinctions unless its qualified protocol explicitly proves equivalence; normalize domain semantics under that same supported contract. Preserve plus tags and do not infer aliases or CRM personhood. Multiple/malformed/unsupported From evidence is not reduced to the first convenient string. A valid signed provider event or domain check proves only its actual technical evidence; it does not certify the person's identity or authorize disclosure. Reject self/receiving infrastructure loop targets under existing owner rules, not a broad ban on legitimate role-based ministry addresses.

### R14 — Reuse Needs review and single-input Release

Matching future inputs SHALL remain in the canonical D19 held-intake/review lifecycle with a clear **Sender hold** reason and the actual inbox's qualified coverage, safety gates, content schedule and age. No ordinary message, acknowledgment, reply target, D39 unread contribution or CSAT opportunity is created by holding. D19 **Release** is a one-input decision/override for that exact applicable hold gate; it neither disables the policy nor licenses other held mail. Preserve all other gates and generation/actor checks, and prevent retries from reholding or duplicating an already qualified release. Dismiss retains its existing one-input meaning.

### R15 — Stop holding without a backlog surprise

Stop holding SHALL affect only the exact currently reviewed policy activation under expected revision/current authority. It does not restore the old unwanted conversation, release/dismiss/delete held messages, change other inboxes or allowlist the sender. Show **Existing held messages still need review** with an authorized link. Releasing an item does not stop the hold; restoring the conversation does not stop it either. Avoid a combined stop-and-release or bulk recovery product. A stale stop cannot disable a later restarted activation.

### R16 — Honest failures and separate operation results

Mark, Restore, Start hold and Stop hold SHALL have separate durable outcomes and retry identity. If Mark succeeds and optional hold creation fails, show both facts; never imply a combined rollback or success. Lost responses reconcile the same operation and currently permitted result before admitting a conflicting retry. Preserve work and clear errors without repeated modal/toast loops. A processing failure holds unresolved input visibly for qualified operators; it is not “not matched,” released, dropped or safe. Independent ordinary work remains available where its owners permit it.

### R17 — Database shape and invariants

Use actual UUID tenant/auth/profile relationships and TEXT Support original/inbox IDs as qualified by current schema. Same-tenant keys bind originals, receiving occurrences, policies and decisions; one active hold per exact tenant/receiving-inbox/mailbox key. Scope, actor, source and generations are trusted/immutable, with closed typed states, nonnegative exact revisions and uniqueness for durable business effects. No caller-selected audit time, author, owner or future epoch. Disposition/hold history is append-only under its declared custody; deleting a policy/state cannot cascade into source content, held evidence, CRM or communication. No money field is introduced.

### R18 — Operation-specific authorization and RLS

Separate current conversation handling, Restore, hold management and D19 review/release authority within Core's actual permission system. Assignment, generic staff/admin status or a CRM link grants none by implication. Derive actor/tenant/current membership server-side and recheck source/action authority at commit and replay/result access. Qualify grants, SELECT, USING, resulting-row WITH CHECK, immutable columns, policy unions, views, RPC EXECUTE/search_path/actual owner and every privileged service/Storage/cache/export/Realtime route. RLS enabled with broad grants alone is not this boundary; no ordinary contact operation edits intake policy.

### R19 — Atomicity, replay and concurrency

Mark/Restore and hold Enable/Stop require current source/policy generation checks, deterministic lock order and one committed decision/result with required owner obligations. Input acceptance, hold effectiveness, release exceptions and canonical admission share an actual serialized/fenced protocol. Duplicates cannot create multiple policies, notes, target changes, admissions or notices; distinct legitimate operations are not deduplicated by similar text. Revoke/move/expiry/merge and pending command races must fail or reconcile safely. Current-generation barriers survive cleanup/restore; absence of a result or canceled HTTP is not proof of noncommit.

### R20 — Inbox changes, coverage and departed staff

The hold follows its immutable receiving-inbox identity, not a moved conversation's handler or a renamed address label. A recreated inbox has a new identity and inherits nothing. Before Start/restart, require current eligible review coverage and finite custody for matched inputs; no unsupported active hold is advertised. Security revocation/offboarding always proceeds and raises D19's owned coverage exception rather than granting access or blocking revocation. Holds are tenant policy, not transferred personal drafts; the departed creator is retained only under permitted audit purpose. Rebinding receiving routes must show/requalify affected policy scope, never silently broaden it.

### R21 — CRM integration and real identity

CRM-hosted Support uses the same authorized originals, designation and intake policy references with preserved return context. A requester, message participant, authenticated worker, CRM Party, represented organization, assignee and record owner remain distinct. Linking/unlinking, shared email, Party merge/deletion or contact changes do not retarget rules, reclassify other conversations, duplicate Activity, suspend login, unsubscribe or alter giving/member-care authority. CRM context reads and any independently requested owner action keep their actual permissions/validation/history. A source address is an operational match, not a second CRM identity.

### R22 — Email Studio, P17, P6 and current communication

Support owns current handling and source eligibility; canonical intake owns holds; P17/Email Studio owns governed content/preparation and separate notification engagement; P6 owns admitted delivery/recovery. Mark/Restore/hold changes send no requester notice, publish no template and create no provider spam training, mailbox-state sync or read receipt. Current Unwanted designation removes new submission authority for its own ineligible optional confirmation/feedback material. A future-mail hold is an inbound per-occurrence gate, not an outbound or consent block on every conversation with that address: held-before-admission input creates no mail, and a qualified D19 release may regain only its normal D13 purpose eligibility within the original fifteen-minute window and other rules. Independently valid ordinary outbound work is unaffected by an active sender hold. Already or possibly submitted P6 evidence remains truthful and cannot be recalled/rekeyed. Human pending sends use R07's owner recovery. D38 notes/mentions and D15 optional Following remain separate; no cleanup action mass-reads notifications or fabricates communication completion.

### R23 — Retention and honest Restore availability

D16/D17 and D19 remain the source/held-content custody owners. Mark records a genuine purpose ending only when actually proved; correcting falsely active work or mark/restore cycling cannot create a new lifetime. Show the owner's actual recoverability/deadline before consequential action, including content already due for expiry; never promise Restore for expired/restricted bytes or invent a trash grace period. New genuine Internal notes retain their existing D17 native-content rule. Active hold policy metadata has its own operational purpose/finite post-stop history; it does not retain the triggering body forever. Restore obeys current restriction/expiry/holds, and backups cannot revive negative controls or deleted evidence.

### R24 — Reporting and attention preserve facts

Keep current unwanted population, review decisions, input holds/releases, real resolutions, missed targets and feedback eligibility distinct. Designating unwanted is not successful service or a new D28 opportunity; a correction cannot erase valid historic misses, invitations or denominator evidence to improve scores. D14's independently proved mistaken-eligibility/time correction remains available with its own audit; this designation is not that proof. D19 held backlog retains original receipt age and existing 24-hour routine-review/48-hour expiry attention; policy changes/retries do not reset it. Use existing grouped source-actionable attention with current authorization, not per-message emails or staff rankings. Retain lawful D15 Follow intent; Mark/Restore are housekeeping corrections, not E4 successful-end notices or new follow enrollment. Actual E1/E2/E3 content and direct mentions retain their separate producers. Mark/Restore alone does not create D39 unread activity; an actual qualified new human contribution does.

### R25 — Lists, search and recoverability

Amend D20 ordinary work views explicitly to exclude currently Unwanted material while its dedicated view remains discoverable under current access. Do not silently change the meaning of work-status predicates or treat an ordinary label as policy. The built-in Unwanted view lists only current all-Unwanted components. Mixed components remain in ordinary work and retain each original designation in their authorized history. D22 admitted-content search can still find unwanted original matches, with that original clearly labelled and the current component work state accurate; open the same source and applicable Restore path, not a duplicated unwanted component. No new body store or search engine. Held inputs remain outside ordinary conversation search under D19. Source privacy/expiry precedes hits, counts, pagination and snippets. Keep missing/partial results distinct from an empty list.

### R26 — Bounded efficient implementation

Reuse existing paged metadata lists; for the new Unwanted and Sender holds management projections use 25 initial rows and at most 50 per request, with stable cursors and no total-population truncation. These are chosen consistent UI bounds, not measured capacity. Index exact active policy lookup, receiving occurrence/effective decision, original designation and authorized review queries. No scan of all senders, bodies or CRM records on every arrival. Record actual burst/skew, tenant/inbox sizes, policy counts, lock/query p50/p95 and backlog capacity before activation. No promised instant processing or universal sender-rate budget is copied from vendors.

### R27 — Useful audit and manageable operations

Keep immutable business/security attribution for Mark/Restore/Enable/Stop/Release, exact scope/generation/result and required current owner actions, separate from technical traces. Record aggregate technical failures, conflicts and latency without bodies, full addresses in ordinary logs, tokens, filenames or hidden-source metadata. Policy managers may see authorized operational address data without message-body authority; aggregate held counts still need their own current source permission. O01–O06 provide named residual signals, thresholds, responsible roles and responses. No manual DB repair, tribal-knowledge cleanup or hidden unowned review queue is an acceptable normal operating path.

### R28 — Source-first migration and safe rollout

Add qualified canonical control identities/constraints/decision history and retire conflicting route/review writers before enabling commands or UI. Do not reinterpret old recipient routes, spam-like labels, requester addresses, shared counters or current status as approved holds/designations. Unknown legacy provenance stays owned and restricted for reconciliation, not silently blocked or deleted. Mixed versions cannot bypass current generation/policy snapshots or revive stopped rules. Separate optional UI/policy-write stops from required intake safety, held recovery and P6 reconciliation; rollback must honor accepted effects and negative controls. Qualify disposable database migrations and live-path compatibility before activation.

### R29 — Prove the complete journey

P01–P40 SHALL verify actual DB/RLS/current roles, multi-session races, parser/matching, source policy timing, pending sends, drafts/notes, restore/expiry, receiving-route changes, CRM and reporting outcomes. Real keyboard/touch/AT/IME, Compact/Full, low-bandwidth, 320 CSS-pixel reflow, text zoom, Thai/RTL, reduced motion and Core target/contrast requirements apply to the same Base UI/base-maia journey. Intended-staff formative testing and corrections are required; a source helper probe is not UI or production proof. Numerical capacity budgets, actual custody inventory and accountable operating assignments are preactivation gates. All 40 runtime groups remain required and unexecuted here.

### R30 — Traceability and disciplined scope

Record B as selected with detailed amendments proposed in the glossary, exploratory ADR0040, ledger and handoff, preserving D1–D39 and historical comparisons. Later explicit formalization must trace each clause into authorized OpenSpec/design/tasks/issues/tests/release evidence and reconcile stale Twenty/synchronization prose against ADR0001's native CRM authority. No new provider, AI classifier, global blocklist, generic rules/approval engine, bulk block/release, arbitrary contact search, separate CRM or privacy-export product is selected. The complete correction is B with bounded explicit actions, not a return to A disguised as a missing implementation dependency.
