## Reconciled direction and remaining selected-answer work

A combines optional personal efficiency with maintained shared meaning. B reduces configuration when staff duties are uniform. C is a credible no-build alternative if required built-ins already cover repeated work. All preserve temporary search/filtering. They are launch choices, not three new engines or modes.

Under the recommended A, the later complete review must make criteria, personal/shared audience, authorized current results, pins and shared editing clearly distinct. It must test invalid/deleted filter targets, current-viewer identities, revocation, definition privacy, source expiry, admission/merge effects, offboarding, concurrent saves, persistence, mobile and complete queries. Those are identified consequences for the founder's eventual answer, not silently ratified design details now.

The D19 Email Studio seam is explicitly documented in its [ratified addendum](phase26-d19-email-studio-integration.md). Q20 introduces no Email Studio publication, template, preparation, Resend operation, recipient audience or CRM contact event. Shared reusable wording and shared reusable filters have similar plain-language organization but different owning capabilities; importing one engine into the other would create unnecessary coupling.
