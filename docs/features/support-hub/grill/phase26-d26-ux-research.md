# D26 UX research — record Support work and use it through CRM

Researched 12 September 2026. Founder selected **A — Record Support follow-up directly** and explicitly requires both manually recorded and email-origin Support requests to be accessible and viewable through their relevant CRM records. **Recommended disposition: Accept with required amendments.** This independent artifact proposes the complete experience and exact corrections for the root's adversarial synthesis. No D27 question, implementation, formal specification, provider action or message is introduced.

The product should have **one Support request, one qualified detail experience, and both entry points**. Staff working in CRM should be able to open the actual request, understand its history and act within their permissions without leaving their place. Staff working in Support should be able to record a phone/in-person request directly, identify relevant existing records when known, and find that same request from CRM. This does not require a copied CRM transcript, mandatory person creation, unrestricted CRM permissions or a second help desk.

## Governing fit and what changes

[D9 and its complete CRM blueprint](phase26-d9-crm-ux-blueprint.md) already require a CRM Overview preview, a full **Communications → Support conversations** list, explicit relevance versus genuine correspondence, shared canonical detail, preserved context and current authorization. Its exact accepted overview limit is three unfinished rows; its list offers Unfinished and All. Those intentional placements remain. The founder's D26 clarification makes this a required complete journey for **every eligible source**, not a manual-only add-on or a small summary that merely directs staff elsewhere.

[D12](phase26-d12-adversarial-review.md) already distinguishes staff-created work from native email, but its creation action requires a selected admitted incoming email. D26 adds the separately qualified manual source; it must not disguise phone notes as received email or quietly broaden D12's original trigger. [D9](phase26-d9-adversarial-review.md) continues to allow zero or several peer Related records and prohibits mandatory Party creation or correspondence inferred from a context link. [D2/D4](phase26-d4-adversarial-review.md), [D14](phase26-d14-adversarial-review.md), [D16/D17](phase26-d17-adversarial-review.md), [D23](phase26-d23-adversarial-review.md) and [D25](phase26-d25-adversarial-review.md) retain audience/send, email-only reply-target, content lifecycle, Tiptap and transport/readiness authority unless the root records a specific necessary amendment.

Core's current source baseline is `7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd`, refreshed by the root and independent data reviewer. The prior [Q26 gap review](phase26-q26-gap-review.md) establishes that existing donor activity writes and a “Log a note, call, or meeting” placeholder are not a qualified general interaction service. Phase28's shared-interaction guardrail prevents dual ownership but does not require pulling its whole product into D26.

Canonical `docs/ai/rules/frontend.md`, `packages/ui/AGENTS.md`, `packages/ui/components.json`, accessibility-review and testing guidance were read. Use the exact **base-maia / Base UI / shared semantic tokens** and existing app navigation/state conventions. Reuse D23's bounded Tiptap profiles for accepted rich-text staff briefs and notes; titles, names, source selectors and dates remain ordinary controls. Read-only histories use the safe static renderer. This is researched UX, not a rendered or tested interface.

## The complete CRM access contract

“Every Support request is accessible through its relevant CRM” should mean:

> Every manual or email-origin Support request with a currently valid CRM relevance or qualified correspondence basis appears once in that CRM record's Support conversations view to a staff member authorized to see it. Opening the row presents the same current Support detail and permitted actions within the CRM journey, with a working full-page fallback. No origin, absence of native email, closed status or client-side page cap silently excludes an otherwise eligible request.

It does **not** mean every employee, donor, household member or organization contact may see all requests. “Relevant” is a real D9 explicit link or independently qualified source relationship, not inferred membership in a household, church, employer or missionary-support graph. A legitimate request without a known Party remains accessible in Support; it cannot appear on an invented CRM record. Provide a clear current linkage state and the normal authorized linking path when identity/relevance becomes known.

This is a coverage requirement, not a new all-tenant CRM inbox. If the founder later wants a CRM-wide aggregate of unlinked Support requests, that is a separate navigation/query scope to qualify; do not silently add it or claim D9 already contains it. The relevant record must not show “No requests” when its Support query is unavailable.

## Journey 1 — staff begin in Support

1. **Choose Record Support request from the normal Support actions.** It is visibly different from Send email and D12 Create related conversation. It records an actual request received by phone or in person that needs continuing Support handling. Routine relationship activity stays in its owner; no question forces every call to become a ticket.
2. **Capture only what the next worker needs.** A short title, useful staff brief, how the request was received and an eligible inbox are the core information. Preserve actual recorder/server-recorded time separately from reported occurrence time. Optional observed name/contact details can remain unverified. Existing rich-text staff-brief fields use the D23 Tiptap profile and safe assets, not arbitrary HTML. Avoid mandatory contact, email, category, priority, case hierarchy, project, custom workflow or call-duration fields.
3. **Review relevant existing work without blocking capture.** If a known Party or specific source provides qualified context, show a compact set of relevant accessible unfinished work with View. Staff may update the existing request rather than create another. No-match, incomplete search and unavailable source are different states. Similar words are not automatic duplicate proof, and an unrelated household member's work is not a suggestion merely because the graph connects them.
4. **Add known CRM context deliberately.** The shared D9 selector can stage existing people, households or organizations, all peers. Show the exact selected set under Related records and explain: “This request will be visible in Support conversations on these records.” Add the existing clarification that it does not add email recipients or communication history. Unknown/ambiguous identity can remain unlinked; no Create contact shortcut is smuggled into this selector.
5. **Review the work destination and save.** Use the current qualified manual-work and D6 handling controls, without silently assigning the creator or enabling optional following. **Create request** records the request and any explicitly reviewed relevance links together through one guarded command. If a required selected target becomes invalid, show the current safe conflict and preserve permitted input; do not succeed with an unexplained missing CRM link. A deliberate later retry without that link is a different reviewed choice.
6. **Open the canonical request immediately.** The first card says **Staff-recorded request · Phone** or **In person**, with actual recorder and reported/recorded times where available. It is neutral staff context, not a received-email bubble or a verbatim caller statement. “Request created. No email sent.” is useful success feedback. Show **No email yet** when native correspondence is absent, not zero-duration response success.
7. **Move to CRM when useful.** Open a related record in its shared CRM shell. Its Overview and Communications view show this same request, including its current work state. Opening it from CRM returns the same detail and the worker's existing private draft; there is no duplicate note editor, request copy or alternate status endpoint.
8. **Follow up through the actual owner.** Internal coordination, reminders and work status use existing Support contracts. If email is appropriate and current sender/recipient readiness permits, **Start email thread** opens the existing D2/D4 composer with an explicitly reviewed audience and genuine new lineage. No email comes from the act of recording, linking or opening CRM, and a callback phone number is not an email address.

The manual source should not inherit a blanket “email disconnected, cannot record work” gate. It still requires actual Support authority, classification, accountable handling and source custody. P17/P6 readiness controls later mail independently. The root's data contract must explicitly reconcile this seam with D25 rather than make the UI silently bypass it.

## Journey 2 — staff begin in CRM

1. **Open the relevant person, household or organization.** Use the ordinary Party shell and existing permissions. The Overview preview shows up to three accessible unfinished Support conversations and **View conversations**. The full Communications → Support conversations list includes manual and email-origin work. Its **Unfinished** default is explicit; **All** includes permitted resolved history.
2. **Check existing work or choose Record Support request.** Place the creation action with Support conversations, not a generic Activity button that pretends every saved request is an interaction. It is available only with current Support-create and selected-context rights. A read-only CRM user can still inspect what they are entitled to read without being shown an editing surface that fails only at the end.
3. **Reuse the same creation form.** The current Party is staged visibly as a Related record and can be removed. It is context, not an automatically proven caller, recipient or main record. A person speaking for an organization is recorded honestly in the brief/observed request context; the chosen CRM organization does not become the human requester. Other relevant records can be added using the same selector.
4. **Preserve source without copying its history.** If launched from a qualified existing interaction, retain that exact owner reference and permitted useful context. Do not copy all past notes, attachments or emails into the new brief, and do not require a second interaction when there is already a usable owner source. A restricted source cannot be made generally visible through a fresh staff summary without actual sharing authority.
5. **Create once, with the reviewed CRM relationship.** The same server-owned operation commits Support work plus the selected context facts and necessary durable projection intent. After confirmed creation, immediately show the canonical detail with its originating CRM context; the existing CRM row reconciles from the authoritative receipt/read. If a projection is pending, show that truth and the actual created request rather than “No requests” or an invitation to recreate it.
6. **Work inline without losing the record.** On a wide screen, show the shared Support detail in the CRM's established detail region/panel, with a clear request title, current work state, source/history and normal permitted controls. Keep a visible Back to [record] or close action and an Open full page action. The whole detail must remain usable; do not squeeze full email composition into a tiny popover or make staff jump to a second app for ordinary authorized notes/attachments.
7. **Return predictably.** Close/Back restores the CRM Party, selected tab/filter, list position and focus to the initiating row or the next logical item if it no longer exists. Refresh, a copied direct URL and opening in a new tab resolve the same canonical request with an authorized full-page fallback. Return context is safe navigation metadata, not a bearer authorization or duplicate draft store.

When a CRM-first user deliberately removes the originating Related record before creating, explain that the request will not be listed on that record solely from this action. Do not silently restore the removed link to satisfy a perceived universal-visibility requirement.

## Journey 3 — view an email-origin request through CRM

1. An actual admitted email's qualified correspondence basis, or a deliberate D9 relevance link, makes the request eligible for the relevant CRM list. Staff do not need to re-enter it as a manual request or log the same email again.
2. The CRM row shows current Support subject/work/inbox/handling, its source-origin presentation and the reason it appears: **Correspondence**, **Related context**, or both. It never implies every linked Party wrote the email or received all replies.
3. Open the row to the same canonical detail used by Support. Messages, internal notes, files, D16 redaction and D17 unavailable content follow current source permissions in both places. CRM does not keep a second unredacted transcript when Support content expires.
4. Permitted note, assignment, status, recipient review, saved wording, signature and send actions are the same commands and controls. Email Studio/P6/Resend preparation, collision handling and possibly-submitted recovery do not change when the composer was reached through CRM.
5. CRM Activity → Communication remains actual source-owned P6 history. A manual brief, context link or phone update never pretends to be an email event, and a later email does not retroactively turn the original phone request into email-origin intake.

Both origins use the same list query, paging, row component and detail route. An origin filter may be an ordinary explicit filter where qualified, but the default cannot hide one origin or require separate Phone and Email subsections just to see complete Support work.

## List clarity, order and current state

Preserve D9's three-row unfinished Overview preview and full paginated Communications list. **No unfinished Support conversations** keeps View conversations available; **No visible Support conversations** is not a claim about inaccessible work. Show partial/error/retry states locally without blanking the rest of the CRM record.

The narrow ordering amendment is: use the existing true latest external-correspondence occurrence when native mail exists, otherwise use the original staff request-created time as an explicit zero-mail fallback, with a stable canonical-request tie-break. The row displays **Recorded [time] · No email yet** for that fallback, never **Last message [time]**. It is a Support list ordering basis, not a last-contact or reply-target fact. Notes, link edits, assignment, provider delivery updates and CRM metadata edits do not promote a request as newly contacted. Any broader Support-activity sort would need explicit event semantics; it is not necessary for this decision.

Counts use the same permission-filtered, canonical deduplicated set as the list before pagination. A request visible through both a Party link and correspondence appears once; merging Parties or Support roots does not multiply it. No hidden totals, global contact count, first-page filter, stale membership cache or unloaded-source inference supplies the count. Current authorization applies to the subject and preview itself, not only opening the full body.

## A restrained interface that fits Asym

Use the existing CRM shell and shared detail layout rather than add a new CRM top-level tab or an all-purpose request dashboard. On desktop the record context and request detail can coexist at usable width; on narrow screens the detail becomes the same full-page experience with a clear Back action. Avoid drawer-inside-drawer stacks when a worker opens a related CRM record from a Support detail already inside CRM: replace the active presentation through the shared navigation stack and preserve a single understandable return path.

The reading order is request title/work state, source card, relevant history, then normal action/composer region with contextual details accessible. Read-only users get usable history and source status; unavailable actions are absent or explained through the existing capability pattern before they type. Essential meaning does not rely on color, an icon tooltip, hover or a disappearing toast. Long international names, organization names and addresses remain readable; record type and safe owner-approved disambiguation distinguish matches without exposing sensitive location or financial data.

Use Base UI behavior-heavy primitives, native labels and actual button/link semantics. The complex create form follows Core's TanStack Form/Zod pattern; the simple list filters stay appropriate shared/native controls. Safe read models use the established collections/query paths and server-owned business logic. Navigation follows Core's current route shell/instant-navigation contract; no bespoke global state store or cloned shadcn library is needed.

Keyboard users can open the list row, read source details, use the same commands, expand related records and return to the initiating row. Focus should not jump when a background projection refreshes. Dialog/panel closure respects permitted draft preservation and actual unsaved-work handling. Touch targets use the shared tokens; the drawer/full-page alternative must survive text zoom and mobile keyboard opening. Async status is announced once per meaningful change, not every polling tick. Reduced motion uses the existing global baseline. Generic UI timer advice does not pause actual domain deadlines, retention or proof expiry when a browser tab is hidden.

## Proof required before the experience is considered complete

The following are user/domain outcomes to test after implementation authorization. None was executed by this research.

1. Record a phone request from Support with no Party/email, then add a permitted Party; find and fully view the same request from that Party's CRM without a fake message or duplicated interaction.
2. Create from a person and separately from an organization; preserve the staged explicit link, observed caller distinction, exact inbox/work and return position. An invalidated required link fails safely without an invisible success.
3. Open an admitted email-origin request from CRM, inspect permitted messages/files/notes, edit a private draft, visit a giving owner record and return; no duplicate editor, recipient mutation, lost text or unauthorized business action occurs.
4. Find both manual and email-origin work in the same Unfinished/All lists beyond one page, with no hidden count leaks and truthful zero-mail ordering. Resolve manual work without falsely crediting an email reply.
5. Test ambiguous/shared names and emails, representing another person, restricted care context, missing source, merged/deleted Party and reused contact endpoint. No guessed relationship or authorization appears.
6. Remove a context-only link and a link with independent correspondence. Test current conditional Undo and genuine attribution correction. The correct rows disappear or remain for the right reason, while permitted work/drafts survive.
