## Independent synthesis and resolved option disagreement

Independent scope and vendor reviews both preferred scheduled ordinary disposal. A third review pressure-tested the options. An earlier candidate made separate earlier attachment expiry its own alternative, but that can be configured within an automatic schedule; presenting it beside A without qualification would overlap. The final question instead compares three complete defaults: A work-ended automatic expiry, B review at that same ordinary threshold, C individual content age even while work continues.

C is a reasoned minimization alternative, not a claim that every compared vendor provides exactly this active-conversation behavior. B is a legitimate judgment/control alternative, not permission to keep content indefinitely. A's advantages are a product judgment about continuity and recurring workload, conditional on actual policy and source safeguards. Exact policy structure, units/durations and optional attachment exceptions remain unselected.

Required question-stage corrections incorporated:

1. Keep D16 fully ratified; distinguish routine eligibility from selective correction/disposal machinery.
2. Choose the explicit roadmap gap rather than repeat settled safeguards or presume a knowledge product is necessary.
3. Treat A/B/C as mutually exclusive ordinary defaults; do not imply all three must ship as tenant settings.
4. No keep-forever/no-policy alternative or borrowed 7/30-day Recent-copy interval.
5. Preserve current source identity through merge/Undo and avoid raw updated_at clocks.
6. A needs accountable treatment for long-open/repeatedly reopened work; B needs owned, timely review. Neither overrides mandatory disposal.
7. C can fragment active context; its exceptions must be qualified, not a keep-everything workaround.
8. Resolve eligibility of source material supporting ongoing continuation/delegated work; mere links are neither disposal authority nor indefinite holds, and a merged-away source is not automatically finished work.
9. Preserve permitted P6/CRM facts without blanket metadata-forever or actual business-record deletion.
10. Admin changes require impact/scope preview, explicit activation and handling of existing history; A is not silent destructive onboarding, and routine staff expiry stays quiet.
11. Expose the Phase6 reserved-job dependency explicitly. Pull forward only the selected narrow Support owner capability, with no generic DSAR/retention engine presumed.
12. Retain D16's irreversible source, read/search/download/send, provider/backup and restore safeguards under every option.
