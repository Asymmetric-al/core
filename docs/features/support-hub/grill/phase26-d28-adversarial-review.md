## Exact corrected decision

> Support Hub offers optional, tenant-enabled automatic feedback invitations after eligible human Support work has remained completed for 24 elapsed hours. Automatic invitations start Off. The enabled starting setting samples 25% of eligible opportunities; the tenant may choose an integer percentage from 1 through 100. Selection is a durable random chance, not an exact quota or a percentage of all donors. A separate tenant-wide minimum gap of 30 elapsed days between invitations to the same qualified recipient mailbox applies even at 100%. There are no reminders, automatic thank-you emails, or per-reply survey signatures.
>
> Each original conversation can have at most one sampled opportunity and one invitation effect. Reopening before sampling cancels that completion's timer; a later genuine completion may start a fresh quiet window. Once sampled, retries, reopening, merging, edits and percentage changes never reroll it. Current merged-work checks additionally prevent pending duplicate invitations. One source-qualified external recipient is selected from actual human correspondence; ambiguous recipients, manual-only work without qualified email, No response endings and restricted/ineligible work are excluded without guessing a CRM identity.
>
> Off stops new candidates and all definitely-unsubmitted invitations immediately through an authoritative sending gate. A submission that may already have begun is reconciled; it cannot be recalled by a setting. Already issued response links remain usable until their original expiry unless independently revoked for safety or privacy. Turning invitations back on creates no historical catch-up batch.
>
> A short, neutral invitation leads through the existing protected-action doorway to one labelled three-choice satisfaction question and an optional plain-text comment. Nothing is scored by opening a link. The requester reviews and explicitly submits once, without an account or a request for data Asym already knows. The resulting feedback is bound to the invitation, not proof of the submitter's CRM identity. Feedback is visible through the same currently authorized Support record in CRM. It does not update giving, contact details, consent for other purposes, work status, agent performance scores or a second CRM history.
>
> Support owns feedback eligibility, sampling, review and response records; shared contactability owns suppression; Email Studio/P17 owns governed invitation content and protected presentation; the existing producer action protocol owns the qualified handoff; P6 owns transport and delivery evidence. Required no-Party feedback-action/contactability amendments must be qualified before activation. Future AI may be separately researched; no AI selection, scoring, drafting or action authority is added now.

## Required decision language

### D28-R01 Purpose and strongest alternatives

The demonstrated founder need is a modest, optional sample of feedback without repeatedly contacting everyone. A solves recurring staff selection effort better than B, a manually chosen invitation per case, and avoids B's additional opportunity for staff to select likely positive responses. C, ordinary unsolicited replies and operational reports without a rating flow, remains the smallest alternative and is represented operationally by leaving automatic invitations Off. Those alternatives do not become three runtime modes. Do not claim survey demand, response rates, donor preferences or representativeness have already been measured in Asym tenants.

### D28-R02 Small policy surface

Provide one tenant-owned Automatic invitations policy, default Off, with selected qualified inboxes and a validated integer percentage 1–100 (enabled preset 25). Display fixed 24-hour quiet delay, 30-day mailbox gap and no reminders as plain-language guarantees. Zero is Off, not a hidden second mode; reject fractions, NaN, negative and over-100 values on the server. Percentage-only edits affect newly created completion candidates; existing candidates retain their frozen percentage. Off and inbox removal are immediately adverse current gates. No conditions builder, campaigns, per-agent opt-in, sentiment exceptions, donation-tier targeting or exact invitation quota.

### D28-R03 Enablement and authority

Only a separately qualified tenant feedback-policy manager may enable or change the policy; D19 intake reviewer or D27 guide publisher status alone grants nothing. Require a named accountable tenant Support feedback owner and a qualified review route with coverage. Current scoped Support readers may see feedback for conversations they can read; feedback review and privacy redaction are separately qualified mutations. Inbox scope, transport readiness, P3 contactability, P17 purpose/action readiness, finite data custody and release proof must all pass before On. Disabled prerequisites have a clear owner action; a saved draft is not Active.

### D28-R04 Eligibility is source-owned

A genuine ordinary Resolved completion can create a generation-bound candidate while the policy and inbox are enabled. At maturity, current source facts must still show completed ordinary work, an admitted external human request, qualified actual human email correspondence with that recipient, and a currently qualified recipient/send basis. A known accepted human reply establishes only transport evidence, not human receipt or satisfaction. Unresolved submission, missing authority, No response endings, spam/auto-only intake, drafts, internal-note-only work, restricted care content, suppressed endpoints and manual-only work lacking actual qualified email do not establish eligibility. A caller-supplied closure intent is not completion evidence: still-owed Support commitments and actionable unreviewed recovery block it under the existing owner rules. No arbitrary minimum message count or AI judgment substitutes for these facts. Exclusions and the email-only population limit are reported honestly.

### D28-R05 Candidate, opportunity and quiet interval

Completion creates a candidate with original conversation identity, exact resolution generation, frozen policy revision/percentage and due_at = genuine completion admission time + 24 elapsed hours. Reopening or relevant new admitted input before maturity invalidates that generation; later genuinely completed work can start a new candidate if no opportunity has been sampled. At mature admission, atomically create the one durable opportunity per original conversation and its sampling decision. After a draw, reopening or relevant new input cancels every definitely-unsubmitted invitation from that generation; the original opportunity stays consumed and later completion cannot resurrect the selected result. Old callbacks cannot sample a newer generation. Do not backdate a draw or apply a worker's processing-time percentage to an older candidate.

### D28-R06 Random selection without repeated chances

Generate one unbiased server-side cryptographically secure integer draw in [0, 9999] at opportunity admission, persist it with frozen percent and algorithm version, and select iff draw < percent × 100. A linked-suppression disposition instead has no local draw and a required same-tenant source-opportunity reference; the exclusive branch cannot be changed later. The unique original opportunity key makes concurrent creation/retries return the same decision. Neither client randomness, worker RNG retries, mutable tags, every-Nth closures, permanently selected mailbox cohorts nor a new percentage may replace it. Selected is not sent: later current safety/contact limits may reduce actual invitations. Do not compensate by drawing replacements or silently increasing the percentage.

### D28-R07 One justified recipient

Bind one exact external endpoint through current source-owned recipient authority from the actual request/correspondence. Do not use the first CRM match, first linked Party, assigned worker, represented organization, current CRM email or every To/Cc participant. Unknown/unlinked people remain eligible only through the explicitly qualified no-Party endpoint branch; no fake Party is created. Multiple plausible recipients, shared/on-behalf ambiguity or changed authority are skipped with a safe reason. Do not prompt staff to select a likely positive recipient at closure. A mailbox can be shared; the response therefore represents whoever possessed that invitation, not verified speech by a particular human.

### D28-R08 Independent frequency protection

At the final submission admission, atomically reserve the tenant + owner-normalized opaque recipient endpoint across all included inboxes and all current D10-linked originals. Require at least 2,592,000 elapsed seconds since prior submission-may-have-begun. A still-permitted or in-flight crossing holds the endpoint guard. Once P6 proves no old future/in-flight crossing is possible, a different genuine opportunity may pass after 30 days from the latest possible crossing bound even if the historical provider outcome remains indeterminate. Historical uncertainty alone is not a permanent mailbox suppression; no timeout releases the guard early. Qualify that finite crossing bound in P6 before activation. Concurrent readers of an old cooldown cannot both submit. A definitely-unsubmitted cancelled reservation may be released under its exact generation; possible submission consumes the opportunity and gap. Do not strip plus-addressing, guess alias equivalence or join mailboxes by a CRM merge. This protects a mailbox, not a provable unique donor across all aliases or tenants.

### D28-R09 Current gates and full invitation Off

Off fences every new or definitely-unsubmitted optional invitation without waiting for cancellation fan-out. Clear queued presentation through idempotent cleanup, but the current policy/source epoch is authoritative at the P6 boundary. Scope removal applies the same rule for that inbox. Existing submitted/possibly-submitted evidence remains truthful; issued response authority retains its original expiry unless its own privacy/safety source is revoked. The Off UI explains both facts. Off→On never resurrects cancelled generations or samples old completed history. Ordinary Support replies, intake and owner work remain available independently. A human reply to the invitation uses D1/D25’s qualified source/thread correlation and current intake; it is actual correspondence, not a rating submission. If original content is unavailable, existing source-safe intake applies without resurrection or email-only CRM matching.

### D28-R10 Finite usefulness and exact time

An invitation's send-by is its candidate due_at + 24 elapsed hours. Do not start a definitely-unsubmitted effect at or after send-by. Any permitted recovery of the same effect follows existing P6 rules inside that window; no new idempotency identity or new send after utility expires. Mint the feedback action only for qualifying preparation after due; expires_at is no later than issued_at + 14 elapsed days and any earlier source/privacy ceiling. Show the actual expiry date after authorized handoff; do not promise 14 days after delivery. Provider callbacks, rerender, policy edits, clock-zone changes and retries cannot renew these limits. All comparisons use trusted UTC instants; UI formats the relevant locale/time zone and elapsed-day wording avoids DST ambiguity.

### D28-R11 Merge, Undo and historical conservation

Keep immutable original conversation/opportunity/effect identities. Before sampling and submission, serialize against current D10 topology. If multiple unsampled candidates belong to one current component, select one neutrally eligible representative by earliest genuine completion instant then stable original ID and admit only its draw. Any prior component opportunity, including Not selected, prevents another original supplying a new chance. Record an immutable linked-suppression disposition for each otherwise eligible losing original, referencing that original opportunity without a fresh draw; Undo cannot revive it. Two independently committed pre-merge draws remain historical facts. A component with a prior possible/submitted invitation suppresses pending duplicates. If independent pending invitations merge, retain one deterministically by earliest genuine completion instant then original stable ID and suppress others. Two invitations submitted before their conversations merged remain two historical facts; no history rewrite or recall fiction. Undo alone creates no invitation, restores no cancelled generation and grants no new draw. CRM Party merging is distinct from Support merging and cannot create or transfer sampling authority.

### D28-R12 Separate email effect and Email Studio purpose

Feedback is a separately registered optional Support invitation purpose and effect, never a rating link automatically appended to every reply/signature or an implicit D4/D5 send. P17 governs its structured wording, locale, team identity, layout/signature, fixed protected CTA and immutable preparation. Suppressing feedback must never reject an ordinary human Support reply. Qualify the no-Party action branch explicitly against P17's current Party/contact wording; existing public guidance URLs do not qualify a feedback action. P6 continues to own transport and ambiguous outcome recovery. No second queue, survey sender, raw HTML template engine or Phase 34 generic workflow prerequisite.

### D28-R13 Preferences and minimization

Honor P3's absolute do_not_contact, applicable email contactability and actual provider suppression; qualify a narrow feedback-invitation purpose opt-out for Party and no-Party endpoints in that shared owner. Offer “Stop feedback invitations” through a distinct protected preference command, not a marketing unsubscribe or a Support-only consent flag. Its proposed fixed validity is 90 elapsed days from its own issuance, shortened only by its own current P3 contact/privacy authority. Rating Used/14-day expiry, Support reopen/content expiry/deletion and ordinary policy Off never consume or revoke this restriction-only authority. A separate selector/verifier/purpose binds the same qualified endpoint; it cannot read data or increase contact. After its own expiry, generic recovery includes the existing qualified ordinary contact/privacy path; there is no secret-bearing URL fallback or new unsolicited replacement email. This 90-day value is a bounded product judgment, not an industry or legal requirement. The 90 days expires only the preference credential, never the resulting recorded opt-out. The P3 preference persists under its independently authorized lifecycle; policy Off→On, CRM merges, key rotation and new invitations cannot clear it or opt anyone back in. It suppresses future feedback across the tenant; ordinary service/contact rules remain their own owners. No subscription or marketing consent is inferred from participation, a reply, donor status or tenant enablement. Do not classify every survey universally as transactional/legal without purpose/jurisdiction qualification. Keep the invitation free of appeals, giving amounts, missionary locations and case details.

### D28-R14 Scanner-resistant requester entry

Qualify a closed named response/preference descriptor pair in P17’s manifest/adapter, with separate issuer/purpose/recipient/expiry/revocation/Used state and both pins in prepared evidence; the existing plural action catalog does not prove the current singular producer API already supports the pair. Use the existing single D6/P17 selector + independent fragment verifier doorway exactly: generic inert GET/HEAD, deliberate same-origin Continue POST, short-lived non-authorizing landing session, then explicit Submit feedback POST. The first page resolves no protected tenant/action/resource fact from the selector. No score in URLs, GET vote, auto-POST, account creation, raw token logging, tracking pixels or alternate bearer-link protocol. Reprove tenant/environment, recipient-authorization epoch, purpose, source, expiry/revocation and expected state at every relevant action. Submission authority begins only once the corresponding P6 effect may have submitted, never during editor preview.

### D28-R15 Short neutral response

Use one fixed, versioned and reviewed localized question: “How satisfied are you with the support you received?” Choices are Dissatisfied, Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied, Satisfied, with no default selection and equal visual weight. Add optional plain-text “What would you like us to know?” limited to 2,000 Unicode scalar values, no attachments or rich HTML. Explain that authorized staff may read feedback, it is not anonymous, and the form is not an urgent-help channel; do not promise a reply. Rating is required to submit; comment is never required, including for a negative rating. No branching questionnaire, incentive, NPS/CES conflation or satisfaction preselection.

### D28-R16 One response and honest correction

Allow editing/review before Submit. Atomically admit one logical response with rating, optional comment, exact question version, source invitation and durable receipt. An identical retry returns that privacy-safe receipt; a changed replay conflicts and cannot replace a Used protected action. Do not redisplay a previous comment or transcript through a reused/forwarded link. Staff cannot change the requester's score. A later correction/deletion request uses the existing qualified Support/privacy owner path, with separately attributed correction or redaction and honest report exclusion where applicable; it does not secretly rewrite what was submitted. A valid bearer response is not provider acceptance evidence or proof of who answered.

### D28-R17 Accessible low-bandwidth experience

Use Core's shared semantic UI styles, native labels/fieldset/radio controls, visible focus, keyboard completion, explicit inline error plus error summary and persistent success. No emoji/color-only scale or auto-advance. Preserve in-memory form input across recoverable failures; do not persist private comments in localStorage/service-worker caches. Narrow mobile is one column, long translations/RTL wrap, 200% zoom remains operable, and network uncertainty shows checking/same-receipt recovery rather than urging another invite. The shared doorway's no-resource CSP remains exact; a no-JavaScript client receives truthful generic recovery rather than a weaker query-token fallback.

### D28-R18 Staff access and review journey

Support Reports → Feedback is the primary owner view, with date and permitted inbox filters, a compact invitation funnel, rating distribution and a paginated response list. Conversation detail displays one Feedback section with current invitation state, rating/comment when authorized and link to source history. A named permitted review queue covers every submitted response, not only negative ratings; low ratings/comments can be prioritized within it. An idempotent Mark reviewed records reviewer/time without claiming resolution or automatically sending an apology. Any needed follow-up uses existing D3/D4/D26 workflows and current recipient/owner authorization. No new ticket/task is created solely for a score.

### D28-R19 CRM continuity and domain boundaries

The D9/D26 canonical Support record remains reachable inline/full-page through CRM Communications → Support and the authorized Overview preview. Feedback appears on that same detail under both current CRM-context and Support permissions; a CRM link or record owner grants no extra access. Requester, participant, authenticated user, Party, represented organization, support worker and CRM owner stay distinct. Link/unlink/Party merge may change authorized navigation, never submission attribution or feedback ownership. No duplicate CRM Activity/comment body, current-contact score, auto-created contact, synced directory, giving update, receipt/refund claim or last-contact mutation follows from survey participation. Resolving Support and a satisfied score do not complete business actions.

### D28-R20 Source of truth and custody

Support owns policy/candidate/opportunity/response/review facts; the source owner owns recipient authority and opaque contact keys; P3 owns contactability/preferences; P17 owns prepared presentation/material; P6 owns intent/submission/delivery evidence; CRM/giving owners remain unchanged. Derived funnel/cache/search/CRM panels never become write authorities. Distinct immutable origins survive merge/unlink and source restrictions. Feedback rating/comment have their own Support review purpose starting at server submission. Unreviewed feedback is not already purpose-ended by the older conversation’s Resolved state. Qualified review records that purpose end unless genuine continuing ordinary follow-up still needs the source; existing D17 active-purpose reviews, finite interval and earlier owner ceilings govern it. Review/read/link/correction events do not renew expired authority or restart another transcript clock. Retained body-free receipt counts do not preserve expired ratings/comments or imply their continuing availability. Earlier D16 privacy restrictions and qualified holds/disposal always apply.

### D28-R21 Supabase authorization invariants

Every tenant-owned record has non-null trusted tenant identity and tenant-aware relationships; current text IDs are preserved rather than casually migrating to UUID. Composite foreign keys or equivalent enforced owner constraints forbid cross-tenant conversation/inbox/opportunity/effect/response links. Unique original opportunity, one effect per opportunity and one response per invitation are database-enforced. Caller input cannot choose tenant, actor, author, approval, sampled draw, recipient proof or reviewed-by fields. Direct broad CRUD must not transform a private/foreign row, reparent an immutable source, revive a terminal state or edit an accepted response. Test grants, SELECT, USING, WITH CHECK, invoker/definer views/RPCs and privileged jobs as one boundary; do not assert existing broad Support RLS already meets it.

### D28-R22 Atomic commands and idempotency

Use the existing canonical command/outbox and P6 recovery boundaries. Candidate creation, opportunity draw, intent/effect linkage and required durable secondary intent each commit with their authoritative receipts. Final gate ordering includes current policy/source/topology and endpoint reservation without reversing existing P6 locks. Response admission, Used transition, review obligation and audit references commit atomically; notifications/projections run idempotently from durable intent. Changed input under a reused permanent command key conflicts. After an authoritative write with a lost response, reconcile that receipt; never infer failure from a network timeout. Expiration/revocation and response races have explicit winner/terminal behavior.

### D28-R23 Private storage, logs and disposal

Store addresses only in their existing authoritative or encrypted finite delivery custody, not in durable feedback metadata/URLs. Endpoint cooldown and preference lookup use shared owner opaque versioned keys; no public raw-email hashes or client-supplied normalization. Comments and question/freeform renderings never enter technical logs, analytics, notification payloads or public tokens. P17 exact prepared-material/finality disposal remains tighter than ordinary content retention; Recent sent copy excludes action targets. Inventory each comment, rating, source metadata, public session/digest, contact guard, export, projection, backup and audit class with its finite purpose floor/ceiling and hold behavior before activation. Redaction/expiry invalidate all ordinary representations and restore replays suppression before serving. No anonymous-forever archive is implied by pseudonymization.

### D28-R24 Honest measurement

Report completion candidates/basic exclusions, matured eligible opportunities, selected/not selected, post-selection suppression reasons, submission-may-have-begun, accepted/unknown/failed/delivered evidence and submitted responses as distinct units. Invitation cohort dates are explicit; response-date browsing is distinct from cohort conversion. Preserve original effect counts after merges and show currently permitted original inbox/cohort provenance. Define issued as distinct invitation effects whose submission may have begun. Response fraction is unique responded-issued invitations divided by that same issued cohort, including indeterminate submissions; it never divides all responses only by provider-confirmed acceptances. Present counts and distribution of the three choices; if showing satisfied-share, define Satisfied / all non-excluded submitted ratings and show its denominator. No response is not positive or negative. Sampling is random among qualified opportunities, not all donors or unique people; frequency/eligibility exclusions and voluntary response mean no representativeness or margin-of-error claim. Do not offer employee leaderboards or cross-vendor benchmark comparison.

### D28-R25 Scope and dependencies

No external survey provider, synchronization directory, attachments, branch builder, reminders, auto-thank-you email, auto-reopening, bulk invitation action, chat/SMS surveys, public feedback archive, AI classification/sentiment/search or generic campaign/workflow engine is included. Public/Internal Guidance continues to help staff consult/share permitted material; its access/publication does not trigger invitations or feed private content into them. Future AI can consume only separately qualified owner contracts under new research/ratification, never treat survey text as instructions or gain owner-action authority.

### D28-R26 Maintainability and operations

Policy managers can inspect readiness, scoped inboxes, selected/suppressed reasons and upcoming due candidates without editing sampling history. Staff review the actual comments and use existing ordinary follow-up when justified. If the accountable reviewer loses access, route body-free attention to tenant oversight and require an eligible replacement; do not broaden inbox/CRM access. Shared-key/provider/readiness failures pause new submission through the existing repair path. Operators may retry only the same eligible effect or acknowledge terminal failure, never rekey old invitations. Percent changes are accountable experiments, not automatic response-rate optimization.

### D28-R27 Bounds and scalability proof

Use bounded keyset pages of at most 50 records, tenant/inbox/status/due and original/effect endpoint indexes aligned to actual plans, shared endpoint serialization and tenant-fair bounded workers. Do not aggregate only a client-loaded first page or queue one job per recipient for every policy toggle. Release qualification must document actual supported volume and prove at least 100,000 retained opportunity records in one tenant, 10,000 due candidates in a recovery batch and 20 contending workers, with current authorization and skew. These are proposed test fixtures, not measured customer size or production capacity claims. Require first-page feedback list/funnel p 95 ≤2 seconds under the declared fixture and zero duplicate effects; report environment/query plans and enforce tighter existing budgets where applicable.

### D28-R28 Rollout and compatibility

Add schema and narrow capabilities/readers first with policy Off; qualify P3/P17/P6 producer seams, privacy inventory, protected landing and worker cancellation before any tenant activation. Do not backfill invitations, draw outcomes, verified recipients or invented response history. New writers must fail readiness on old schema; old broad writers/routes cannot bypass new invariants. Maintain compatible read-only history across feature disablement. A kill switch blocks new admission while reconciliation/response privacy/disposal continue. Roll forward after new source data exists; do not drop tables or restore an old permissive reader as rollback. Restore suppression and current epochs before workers/links become active.

### D28-R29 Acceptance and release evidence

Carry R01–R32 and P01–P40 below into later authorized OpenSpec/design/tasks/tickets/tests/release evidence with stable cross-references and exact changed owner contracts. Grooming does not create those artifacts or mark their proof complete. Execute real RLS/role/concurrency/merge/provider/public-route/accessibility tests against current code when implemented. Research and synthetic reference experiments establish a design basis, not production correctness, measured usability or legal certification.

### D28-R30 Safe correction and residual uncertainty

Decline/opt-out does not negatively score the requester or change giving eligibility. Feedback may be unfair, misattributed through a shared mailbox or malicious; staff review it as unverified feedback, cannot secretly improve the score, and use existing auditable privacy/abuse exclusion with reason and denominator impact. Excluded comments/ratings are not erased merely to improve reports. No arbitrary fundraising claim, confidential case detail, promised outcome or named personal responder is auto-inserted into the invitation. Qualify tenant-selected public team identity and an actual monitored reply route.
