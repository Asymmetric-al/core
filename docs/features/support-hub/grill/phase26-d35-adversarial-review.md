# D35 — Edit your own internal note, with visible history

**Current status — full ratification, 14 September 2026:** D35 A and every amendment, addition, adjustment, change and final correction in this complete package are fully founder-ratified. This includes all 26 clauses, 23 categories, two glossary definitions, 40 required release groups and six controls. D1–D35 including D29-X01 remain fully ratified. Earlier proposed/pending/no-Q36 language below is historical. The [full ratification](phase26-d35-full-ratification.md) is incorporated in full; actual runtime proof remains required and unexecuted.

**14 September 2026. Disposition: Accept with required amendments.** The founder selected **A**, explicitly prioritizing a simple, beautiful and understandable editing experience. The selected policy is recorded; the complete safeguards below are the proposed amendment package, not silently treated as fully ratified. **D1–D34, including D29-X01, remain fully ratified.** Q35 is answered at intent level. No Q36, formal specification, implementation tickets or product implementation is opened.

The permanent design is straightforward: **Edit → Save changes or Cancel → Edited / View history**. One current note stays in its original place. Its author can correct the text while qualified readers can inspect permitted earlier versions. There is no autosave, required explanation, edit timer, administrator rewriting another author, restoration button or collaboration platform.

## Exact corrected decision

Adopt author-only correction of ordinary published human-authored internal Support notes, under current source/edit authority, using the existing qualified Internal note editor. Keep original identity, author and publication time; save a new source-owned revision and change the current head atomically. Show Edited and deliberate, read-only, permission-aware history. A correction is not a newly published note, human reply, business action, new retention lifetime or automatic notification. Preserve D16/D17 privacy across every version and D15/P17/P6 source/material fences. CRM accesses the same canonical Support note. The following26clauses and the linked contracts are the complete corrected decision.

## Required clauses

### D35-R01 — Scope and strongest alternative

Allow the original human author to edit their own ordinary posted internal Support note while that exact note/source and actor remain eligible. There is no arbitrary grace period. Incoming mail, outgoing email, system/AI events, other people's statements, CRM-owned notes and D26 staff-recorded request briefs retain their own contracts. New events belong in new notes. The strongest alternative, linked corrections only, preserves explicit chronology but adds entries for minor mistakes; it is not built alongside A as a new policy mode. Existing ordinary new-note creation remains available for a clarification or new material.

### D35-R02 — Local, discoverable editing

Offer **Edit note** in the note's accessible actions menu to its eligible author. Preserve the Internal note label and replace only that note's readable body with the qualified editor. Use **Save changes** and **Cancel**, in that order of importance. No hover-only action, forced modal stack, required edit reason, autosave, new setting or timer. Keep the original note position, separate Reply/Note draft, reader scroll and return focus. Only one posted-note editor is active in the current reader at a time; ordinary switching preserves intent through R08 rather than silently dropping changes.

### D35-R03 — Readable history

After a real successful change, retain original author/posting time and show **Edited** with an accessible edit time and **View history** action. History is a single read-only disclosure over this exact note: current version, Original only when true original-content provenance is qualified, a clearly labelled Recorded baseline for an observed legacy baseline, and earlier permitted saved versions with attribution/times. Initially fetch at most20 permitted metadata rows and one selected version body; use keyset pagination for more. No eager full-body archive, diff editor, Restore button, revision branching, new history export or per-revision comments. A reader sees only currently permitted versions and metadata; forbidden existence/counts are not leaked by gaps or placeholders. Display an unavailable/expired state only when its control facts themselves are permitted.

### D35-R04 — Correction versus new work

Correcting a typo, transcription or phrasing keeps the note in place. A changed circumstance, new action or new supporting material belongs in a fresh ordinary note. Explain this briefly near the editing affordance/help; do not require a reason code, classifier, AI judgment or universal confirmation before Save. A material correction that someone must act on can use existing deliberate staff attention through its qualified owner. An Edited marker does not claim that previous readers or recipients saw the correction.

### D35-R05 — Real authorship and current authority

The server derives the real authenticated principal, tenant and current staff/source authority; immutable original authorship and positive native-human publication provenance must be independently trustworthy. A row labelled note may actually be generated/system activity. Require current read and edit eligibility plus original-human-author equality at the atomic mutation. A matching email, current agent display JSON, role, assignment, CRM record link, API key or D34 cue is not authorship proof. No routine administrator override, service-worker impersonation or reassignment of note authorship. A disabled/revoked actor cannot save using a formerly valid editor session. History viewers require current note/source and version disclosure authority, not edit permission or mere CRM access.

### D35-R06 — Qualified canonical content

Reuse D23's Internal note canonical Tiptap profile and safe static renderer. Validate original persisted JSON and incoming full candidate against the exact code-owned schema/purpose, allowed attributes/nodes/marks, source references and depth/size/count limits **before** parser normalization. Derive HTML/text through the owner compiler; caller renderings are not write authority. Missing or incompatible original structure is a visible read-only migration/repair state, not successful blank content or permission to strip unknown fields. The exact profile and limits must be qualified before activation; D35 creates no separate numeric content allowance. Do not accept raw arbitrary HTML, fetch pasted external assets or execute content. A valid no-change candidate is handled under R09.

### D35-R07 — Practical correction profile, files and mentions

Edit prose, supported formatting and ordinary safe links. Deliberate deletion or movement may remove an existing typed mention or inline image appearance, including by deleting its paragraph; this is different from a parser silently losing unknown content. Submitted typed references must be a validated subset of the loaded current base's allowed references; retained target/source/purpose attributes cannot be changed. Undo within the unsaved editor may restore a still-allowed base reference. No new or retargeted notifying mention, asset acquisition, file removal/replacement or file-membership change is part of this correction command. Existing file occurrences/bytes remain unchanged and visible under **Existing files**. Explain that removing an image from the text does not remove the file from the note/history. Use the existing new-note action for new files/attention; D16 handles actual sensitive-content removal. Plain @name text is not a mention or permission grant. Mount, typing, paste, reference changes, history, Save and Cancel must never invoke an insertion-time activity callback or the ordinary addPrivateNote path.

### D35-R08 — Private draft, cancellation and navigation

Edits remain private local working state until Save. Cancel before submission discards only this edit and restores the current permitted note; unchanged Cancel needs no prompt. Dirty navigation, opening another edit or deliberately leaving the reader uses the existing small discard/keep-editing guard; do not create persistent multi-note draft storage or an offline queue. Background refresh must not overwrite the local candidate. Privacy/source access loss, real logout/account/tenant change or incompatible topology invalidates and clears forbidden local bodies regardless of a dirty-navigation prompt. Browser/OS tab loss is not promised recoverable. Browser close warnings are best-effort, not data guarantees.

### D35-R09 — Truthful save and no-op

Save deliberately shows an in-place saving state; do not replace the readable note with an unconfirmed candidate, falsely mark Edited or drop the candidate on a known failure. On success, show the authoritative current result and return focus without jumping chronology. Canonically unchanged content creates no revision, edit timestamp, activity or invalidation event. Every admitted Save, including No changes, records a minimal immutable operation binding/result with no body or diff. Do not use raw string equality alone when the qualified canonical representation treats content as equivalent. Empty or invalid content follows the existing qualified note profile; do not silently delete the note or add a new required field.

### D35-R10 — Conditional save and real concurrency

Save binds the exact original note/source, current base revision and relevant permission/privacy/content-generation/topology fences. Recheck them atomically. At most one competing candidate against the same base can advance the head; stale saves cannot silently win, rebase or restore superseded content. On conflict, show **This note changed** with the currently permitted saved version and **Your unsaved changes**. Let the author review the current version, deliberately continue from that new base and reapply their correction; there is no one-click overwrite, automatic merge or generic diff platform. A privacy/authorization conflict instead clears or withholds forbidden content. Multiple devices and D34 awareness do not weaken this guard.

### D35-R11 — Durable effect identity and unknown results

Bind one opaque operation identity to trusted tenant/actor/note, base/fences and canonical desired content. Same operation and same meaning produce the same minimal durable result; changed meaning conflicts. Do not rely on transport retries, short TTLs or a per-tab boolean. After a lost response, show **Checking whether your changes were saved** and use a read-only current-authorized outcome lookup. Unknown is not failed, cancelled or permission to mint another operation. Close during a possibly accepted save may stop waiting but cannot claim rollback; returning reconciles rather than resending. Current source/privacy rules govern even a replay response. Outcome evidence is content-free/minimized and follows its owner retention; old-body equality hashes/tokens cannot become a permanent private archive. Retain the minimal operation binding or equivalent durable rejection fence until the note is irreversibly non-editable and restore/replay cannot re-enable that identity. Response-cache or material expiry never releases the operation key. Accepted effect identity cannot be reused to create a second effect after cache expiry.

### D35-R12 — One source-owned revision model

Retain one stable Support note identity, immutable original tenant/source/human-author/publication provenance, a source-owned chain of content revisions and exactly one current revision pointer while content is available. Each admitted edit adds one next revision, updates that pointer, records truthful minimal edit attribution and any required source-invalidation obligations in one authoritative transaction, or none. Use actual owner-compatible TEXT IDs and tenant-aware relationships; do not assume UUIDs because a UI emits one. Revision identity/order/provenance do not allow arbitrary body overwrite. Retained content is separately purgeable under D16/D17; immutable history is not undeletable sensitive bytes. A compatibility current-body projection is derived, never a second write authority or second CRM note. Note edits do not bump conversation last-human activity, unread/new-note signals, reply clocks, status, assignment or retention anchors.

### D35-R13 — Database, grants and privileged paths

Constrain same-tenant note/revision/current-head/operation references, unique revision order and unique business operation, required original provenance, valid lifecycle combinations and deliberate delete behavior. The current pointer must belong to its own note; a permitted update cannot reparent it or change actor/source ownership. Index actual tenant/note/order/source predicates and prove plans. Qualify effective SELECT/USING/WITH CHECK, column privileges, views/RPCs, invoker/definer functions with fixed safe search path, storage and service-role paths together. No generic browser CRUD or worker can bypass the command. An omitted UPDATE/ALL WITH CHECK may inherit USING in PostgreSQL; the issue is effective enforcement, not syntax absence. Minimized audit/receipt facts and content bodies have explicit separate access/retention. No money schema or external CRM synchronization is added.

### D35-R14 — Privacy dominates every version

D16's actual source restrictions/redaction apply to current body, old revisions, edit drafts, history reads, typed references, file appearances, previews, search, exports, prepared source-dependent notices and restored backups. A body-only edit is not a sensitive-data-removal tool. A redacted span in the current body does not prove a safe mapping to earlier revisions. Where the owner cannot qualify a safe earlier representation, restrict the affected prior revision body and known derivatives as a whole, retaining only permitted body-free evidence. Do not fuzzy-match across versions, remove only the first matching span or build a new semantic redaction engine. Do not offer history as a way to recover removed content, or silently expand a current redaction to another person's unrelated source. A history row may show only a permitted body-free unavailable fact; neither old text, forbidden author metadata, counts nor filenames leak. Source-aware corrective disposal uses existing owners and does not require routine manual SQL. Authorization loss is not merely a network-error fallback to cached content.

### D35-R15 — Retention and temporal truth

All revisions remain bound to the note's original source/content generation and existing D17 deadline. Saving, looking at history, linking through CRM or reapplying text never resets the anchor or renews an old source. Expired content is unavailable even if deletion workers lag. No last-N eviction substitutes for the accepted finite retention policy, and no unlimited-history promise bypasses it. Body-free edit/control history and operation receipts retain only their qualified minimal purposes and ceilings. Policy change, paused disposal, merge/Undo, archive and restored backups retain original source ordering and current adverse fences. A truly new ordinary note retains its genuine admission semantics; it cannot recreate forbidden old material.

### D35-R16 — Email Studio, attention and external copies

A routine note edit is not a new note publication, @mention dispatch, follower alert, donor email, P17 preparation or P6 resend. Preserve D15's quiet correction behavior and current-source-safe previews. Already prepared/sent communication material and actual delivery facts are not overwritten by editing a source. Where changed/redacted content invalidates outstanding source-dependent notification material, enforce the existing adverse fence/correction/disposal rules before further rendering/decrypt/send; do not blanket-delete valid body-free historical events or falsely claim recall. Independently qualified new-note or deliberate staff attention uses existing P17/P6 owners and can have its normal effects. D29 confirmation, D23 outbound signature/wording, recipients and financial messages are unaffected.

### D35-R17 — CRM continuity without inherited rights

CRM displays/opens the same current-authorized canonical Support note; one successful edit is visible through that source without duplicate Activity, copied body, two-way sync, CRM last-contact mutation or duplicate notifications. Preserve the selected record/list and return context. Linking/unlinking, merged constituent sources, Party merges, reassignment and permission changes must requalify the original note/source rather than inherit access from the visible CRM record or merged root. CRM people/organizations/relationships and giving/receipts/refunds/recurring/contact/member-care actions remain owned and authorized elsewhere. A changed note sentence does not perform, approve or establish completion of those actions.

### D35-R18 — Current reading and search

Ordinary timeline, D22 search/Find and snippets use the current permitted note head. Historical content is accessed only through deliberate qualified note history, not silently added to global/search/AI corpora. Revalidate stale indexed matches and counts before disclosure; a removed term cannot surface from an old revision as a current hit. Required projection invalidation/recovery is durably tied to the source change; repair does not mint a new edit. While an index is not current, apply D22's truthful currentness behavior rather than false completeness. D33 reading preference remains presentation only and does not save, restore or alter a revision.

### D35-R19 — Existing D34 awareness, explicitly qualified

Deliberate editing of a posted human note may use the existing D34 neutral composing controller only after this producer's exact note/original-source and edit-authority binding is qualified. It cannot reuse a Reply composer instance for another constituent source. Switching between edit and Reply/Note preserves private drafts and stops/replaces the technical context under D34. Retain the same label, privacy, timing, expiry and stop-on-save/cancel rules; no new presence service/schema, edit-mode disclosure, draft sharing, history viewing signal or lock. D34 outage never prevents an independently qualified save. Read/history/focus alone is not composing.

### D35-R20 — Accessible and polished presentation

Use exact shared Base UI/base-maia, Maia/Zinc semantic tokens and the qualified Internal note editor/static renderer. Keep a calm note card, readable body and stable action placement; do not copy hardcoded legacy amber styling as authority. Visible labels and text states, accessible menu/history controls, deliberate focus return, linked validation errors and coalesced status announcements are required. Test200% text resizing,320CSS-pixel reflow/400% zoom, long international names/RTL, contrast, keyboard/screen readers and mobile virtual keyboards. Apply WCAG2.4.11 author-created focus-obstruction and2.5.8 target-size/exception requirements, with44CSS-pixel standalone-primary touch targets as a product goal. No hover-only timestamps, color-only Edited signal, destructive Escape surprise or unnecessary motion. Use existing motion/reduced-motion behavior.

### D35-R21 — Bounded performance and fair work

Load one active editor and one selected historical body, never one editor per revision. History pages are capped at20 permitted metadata rows with stable keyset cursors bound to current source/authorization and a stable viewed frontier; newer edits do not silently reorder the open history list. Make currentness visible and let the reader refresh deliberately. No full-history count or body preload is required. In qualification, use two tenants, a200-revision retained note, bodies at the exact profile's ordinary and allowed maxima,100 concurrent authorized readers and20 same-base competing saves/retries. Require zero duplicate heads/effects/cross-scope disclosure and healthy-fixture p95≤2seconds for confirmed Save and selected-history-body readiness. Record hardware/data/profile/network conditions; these are proposed acceptance targets, not measured capacity. Existing actor/tenant-fair admission limits/backpressure apply, with bounded failure preserving permitted drafts and never deleting history to control load.

### D35-R22 — Legacy and safe activation

Current note creation/rendering does not establish trustworthy authorship or a revision capability. Inventory actual writers/author provenance/content shapes first. Current generated activity can share the ordinary note endpoint; note type or a text prefix cannot establish native-human provenance. Only qualified legacy authorship may be migrated to editable provenance; never infer it from today's email or display JSON. Preserve one honest observed baseline version without inventing prior revisions or original creation evidence; label it Recorded baseline unless true original-content provenance is independently qualified. Unqualified old content stays readable where permitted with a clear editing-unavailable explanation and ordinary new-note/D16 paths under their own rights. Qualify the canonical editor cohort, parser/renderers, source model, constraints/grants and old-writer fences before activating Edit. Mixed readers use only a derived compatible current body; old writers cannot overwrite heads. Disable new edits without losing accepted revision/outcome recovery or privacy enforcement. Rollback after new revisions cannot restore permissive writers, discard accepted history or resurrect erased bytes; use a compatible roll-forward path.

### D35-R23 — Truthful audit and repair

Record trusted actor, note/source, operation, predecessor/successor identities, actual time and minimal outcome in a typed business edit event; do not store body diffs, free-form reasons, endpoints, private filenames or candidate JSON in audit/log/metric tags. Technical traces diagnose save/read/invalidation failures separately from durable business history. Privacy-safe operators can reconcile the original operation and source projections without impersonating the author, fabricating a note or direct SQL. D16 disposal/restore controls govern affected payloads; a successful edit does not erase previously valid communication evidence.

### D35-R24 — Simple defaults and explicit exclusions

Ship one author-edit policy, not a tenant policy builder. No grace timer, required reason, restore/compare suite, branching/real-time co-editing, Pro document-history service, persistent multi-note drafts, history export, new AI retrieval/action path, general CRM note platform or bulk edit is justified by this need. New attachment/mention acquisition remains ordinary new-note work; supported current text/reference removal remains natural under R07. This is simplification at the right boundary, not permission to omit source/version/privacy checks.

### D35-R25 — Proof and operating gates

The [proof and operations ledger](phase26-d35-proof-and-operations.md) supplies precise positive/negative/boundary/source/concurrency/CRM/migration/accessibility/user-journey outcomes and named operating controls. They are required before activation, not optional monitoring substitutes. Source inspection, synthetic examples and document review are not actual SQL/RLS, provider, browser/AT, performance or participant proof. Reconcile every finding into these clauses and the final data/editor/UX contracts before recording the answer.

### D35-R26 — Recording and terminology

Record A as selected founder intent and this complete corrected decision as **Accept with required amendments**, with detailed amendments proposed pending the founder's full ratification. Add only the narrow feature ADR0035 and two glossary definitions for Internal note correction and Internal note history. Preserve all prior decisions, evidence and the source distinction between notes, briefs and messages. Carry accepted wording and proof into future explicitly authorized OpenSpec/design/tasks/tickets/implementation/release evidence; do not create those artifacts during grooming or ask Q36 before this decision is settled.
