## Independent question review and synthesis

Three independent reviews—Core/data, lifecycle and vendor/UX—agree Q14 is explicitly unresolved and recommend A with the following refinements:

1. Ask the launch capability level first, not a database field, timer duration or default SLA matrix.
2. A means optional internal guidance plus reporting. Existing reminders and shared responsibility remain; an internal target is not an extra status or task.
3. Describe B fairly: active queue management, aging visibility and deliberate reminders already help staff. Its tradeoff is greater reliance on human monitoring, not an absence of care.
4. C is product-managed requester-visible commitments, not an automatic legal contract, penalties or enterprise entitlements. It needs explicit public-content authority beyond D13's receipt-only fact wall. Existing deliberate staff promises remain governed under all choices.
5. No automated confirmation, staff note or owner-task update gets human-reply credit. Qualified first/subsequent reply meaning, recipient/source scope and provider uncertainty are consequences of the selected answer's review.
6. Do not inherit snooze bypass, mutable historic clocks, tag-only authority or current source default numbers. Preserve source age, actual outcomes, permissions and D3/D10/D12 history.
7. Prefer quiet due/overdue guidance in the existing queue/detail hierarchy; do not assume repeated alerts, countdown animation, escalations or a second notification engine.
8. A/B/C all require trustworthy data and reporting; B is not permission to retain current UTC/elapsed-time inaccuracies. C does not promise when another domain's business action completes.

**Recommendation: A.** It adds timely staff guidance to the shared email workflow without requiring public promises or every staff member to set a separate reminder for each unanswered request. This is a proportionate product judgment, not measured ministry research or a claim that all vendors behave alike. B remains the strongest simpler alternative, and C remains available for an explicitly demonstrated public-commitment need.
