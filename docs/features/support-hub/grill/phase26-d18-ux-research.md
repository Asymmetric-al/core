# D18 reusable Support replies: independent UX and vendor review

**Independent review input:** The [final D18 decision](phase26-d18-adversarial-review.md), [UX](phase26-d18-library-ux.md) and [data contract](phase26-d18-data-contract.md) govern adopted amendments. Candidate publication, format, lifetime and other alternatives below are preserved review history, not competing final requirements. In particular, My Save is nonpublication authoring; Internal notes have separate drafts and no email preparation; new table/blockquote authoring is excluded. Full D18 amendments remain proposed pending ratification.

Independent research input for the founder-selected **B — My replies plus a curated shared library**. Checked 11 September 2026. This is a candidate product design for the parent review to reconcile, not a ratified amendment or runtime implementation. The founder audience, Phase 26 grooming scope and Markdown research format were established, so no additional clarification was necessary. Root and shared-UI instructions, grill-with-docs, grilling, domain-modeling, accessibility-review, P17 authoring/publication/locale/Saved Sections, D4 and Q18 evidence were read. Parent owns source refresh, overall schema review and final recording.

## Recommendation

Accept B with required amendments. Deliver a complete everyday writing tool: one contextual searchable picker; My replies and Shared replies; concise previews; personal favorites; optional simple organization; complete answers and small reusable passages; explicit reply-versus-internal-note compatibility; safe fields; supported rich/plain-text rendering; a clear authoring and shared-contribution lifecycle; repair, retirement and offboarding. Keep it inside Email Studio's owner and D4's composer. A new macro engine, a standalone knowledge base, a tone-generation system, multiple libraries per channel or automatically synchronized personal clones are not earned by this need.

The strongest simpler alternative remains shared-only A. B adds ownership and lifecycle work, but makes personal phrasing reusable without turning every small preference into maintained team policy. This is product judgment supported by relevant mechanisms in mature products, not a measured claim that every ministry needs personal variants.

## A bounded, complete content inventory

**Reply wording is one content family.** Length and writing purpose should not create new renderers, catalog keys or Layout Roles. The author can write a single phrase, a paragraph, a complete answer or a structured set of steps using the qualified P17 vocabulary. A separate explicit **Use in: Reply / Internal note** field determines compatibility. Note wording cannot appear as an eligible external reply or switch the composer mode. If wording is useful for both audiences, publish independently reviewed variants rather than assuming internal content is externally safe.

| Useful form                                  | Intended use                                                                      | Important boundary                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Greeting, thanks, empathy or closing passage | Add personal warmth around a tailored answer.                                     | Prefer a known salutation or a neutral greeting. No automatic names, pronouns or religious style assumptions. Do not duplicate the shared signature/footer.                                    |
| Complete common answer                       | Repeated instructions about an established process.                               | “Complete” still means editable draft wording, not automatic send or forced replacement.                                                                                                       |
| Clarifying request                           | Ask for the minimum missing information.                                          | No reusable request for passwords, complete payment credentials or unnecessary personal information.                                                                                           |
| Step-by-step guidance                        | Numbered instructions, informational links and explanatory image.                 | Must remain meaningful in plain text, with images blocked and on mobile. No image-only instructions.                                                                                           |
| Progress update or next-step explanation     | Clearly tell a requester what staff are doing or what is expected next.           | Do not automatically change Waiting status, reminders or targets. Do not promise a deadline from a guessed SLA.                                                                                |
| Verified outcome explanation                 | Explain a source-owned completed action.                                          | A receipt/refund/contact-change statement cannot substitute for the owning domain's facts, authorization or official artifact. Do not ship unverified outcome examples as ready team guidance. |
| Internal handoff or investigation outline    | Reusable headings/questions in a staff-only note.                                 | Adds no delegation, task, owner, notification or state effect. D11 remains the actual action handoff.                                                                                          |
| Language or wording variant                  | A separately maintained answer in a supported locale or concise/expanded wording. | Explicit language, current validation and publication. No “professional/friendly” transformation engine or combinatorial tone matrix is necessary.                                             |

Illustrative starter content may include generic thanks, a neutral clarification outline and empty internal handoff headings. It must be synthetic and opt-in editable draft content, not prepublished claims about a ministry's policies. Receipt, refund, member-care and security examples should be qualification fixtures with no real people/data, not a feature mandate or universal practice.

### Style and rendering

Use the existing structured editor's paragraphs, emphasis, headings where justified, ordered/unordered lists, safe informational links and qualified tenant images. D4's compact Service-message presentation and Email Studio Brand Kit remain the visual source. Do not add per-snippet fonts, button styles, full promotional layouts, arbitrary HTML/CSS, raw Markdown execution, general tables, GIF/video embeds or tracking pixels. Literal supported Unicode characters are text; no extra emoji/tone engine is required. Plain text is generated from the same canonical document, never an independently drifting source.

Authoring guidance should encourage clear names, a direct answer first, short paragraphs, one action per step, descriptive link text, considerate neutral language, and no false urgency or unsupported promise. Phrasing can be warm or concise without locking a tenant into one voice. Broad protected-content labels must not turn every mention of “receipt” into a mandatory second-person review: P17's actual applicability and narrow truth-core evaluation govern.

### Fields and fill-ins

Expose **Insert information** for approved source-owned typed facts and a separately labelled **Ask me to fill in** for one-use draft values. These are distinct: editing a one-use value never edits CRM. A fill-in has a human label/instruction, required/optional status, type/size/encoding contract and explicit default or omission semantics. It appears as an identifiable field, not a yellow-highlighted `[TODO]` convention. Unresolved required fields block Send or Add note with an in-context repair link. Required material must not silently disappear when an optional paragraph is omitted.

P17 already defines typed indivisible variable nodes, but the reviewed text does not establish a manual-entry source contract. This is a **narrow required owner extension** to qualify through P17, not a claim that it already exists, and not a generic form/condition builder. A phrase requiring no variables must remain usable without a new form. Unknown senders use neutral language; multiple recipients/related records require exact safe binding. No automatic name splitting, no whole record selectors, no expressions or CRM writes.

## Concrete ordinary journey

An illustrative staff member, Maya, opens a receipt-question conversation. She needs general instructions, not a new official receipt. The existing **Use template** action opens a compact search surface above the composer. Its title is **Saved replies**; the secondary description distinguishes **Shared replies** and **My replies**. There is no navigation away from the conversation and no second Support editor.

1. **Find.** Search title, short description, optional search aliases and eligible plain-text content. Match across the complete currently authorized library. Prefer title/alias matches, then description/body matches; expose why an ambiguous result differs through visible scope, locale and purpose. An exact short code can expedite selection but can never silently select a personal item over similarly named shared guidance. Search is case-insensitive and Unicode-aware; do not impose ASCII names or require staff to remember capitalization. Distinguish no results from failed or partial retrieval.
2. **Narrow only when needed.** Start with eligible items for current composer mode and explicit current message locale, with easy All/Shared/My controls. Show Favorites first when the query is empty, followed by recent eligible use; typed searches remain relevance-first. Scope/locale filters are visible and easily cleared. A reply that cannot be used because of a current context requirement should have a safe explanatory unavailable state when its existence is authorized, not disappear mysteriously. Unauthorized items, titles, counts and previews do not appear.
3. **Preview.** Each result shows title, concise purpose/description, Shared/My and a language marker when relevant. On focus or selection show complete content, included files, required fill-ins and any “Use when” guidance. Guidance is clearly outside the outgoing body. A keyboard or touch user can open the same preview; no hover-only step. Small passages remain one simple insertion. Longer or asset-bearing content needs inspectable preview, not another mandatory modal on every use.
4. **Insert.** Selecting Insert copies the exact currently qualified revision at the preserved caret/selection. The editor receives one undoable insertion. Explicit Replace reply remains secondary and undoable under D4. Multiple phrases may be inserted. Opening the picker is not a draft mutation; Escape preserves previous text/caret. A stale editor selection is recovered visibly rather than overwriting new typing. Files appear visibly in the attachment area; cannot be hidden only in template metadata.
5. **Personalize.** Maya fills missing one-use values, edits wording, and checks actual audience. Source facts and owned links follow D4 current permission/recipient proof. No saved reply changes recipients, subject, From, work status or CRM data.
6. **Send.** The current named D4 action still owns the decision. Library use does not add publication to each ordinary message or bypass shared preparation/Resend. A library outage leaves qualified plain drafting available; unavailable library assets are not silently stripped from an already chosen draft.

### My replies, curation and team maintenance

**Create:** New reply opens the shared Email Studio authoring experience in context, defaulting to My replies for an ordinary author. Required inputs are a meaningful internal title, intended composer use, explicit locale and content. Optional description, folder and short code stay under More details. Publishing rights determine whether Shared is available; UI hiding never replaces server authorization. An ordinary eligible personal save should combine validation and making the saved reply available through the existing standard publication path. Unsupported/protected content keeps the existing P17 floor rather than inventing private bypasses.

**Share:** Offer for sharing creates a deliberate candidate copy of one reviewed revision into the shared owner. The submitter sees who can review that candidate before submission; their other personal items stay unchanged and undisclosed. Shared maintainers can use existing Draft/Needs review/Changes requested/Published states. Someone with standard shared publication rights may publish directly after existing checks. There is no mandatory waiting queue or additional approval layer for routine qualified edits. A submitted personal reply is not silently switched to Shared, and changes afterward do not mutate the shared candidate. A withdrawn candidate becomes unavailable for publication through that request; retained audit does not grant ordinary body access indefinitely.

**Keep common guidance current:** Favorites point to a shared identity, not cloned text. New insertion uses its current eligible published revision. Personal duplication is deliberate, marked with source provenance, and never synchronized automatically. A source update can be shown in the personal asset's management detail as a comparison opportunity; do not repeatedly interrupt unrelated replies or auto-merge wording. Personal items must not be advertised as official current guidance.

**Organize:** Provide one optional flat folder/category within My and Shared namespaces, with Unfiled. This earns simple team/topic browsing without another taxonomy project. One item has one organizational folder at launch; moving it is not sharing or permission change. Folder deletion moves eligible items to Unfiled after clear impact acknowledgement, never deletes replies. Reuse existing Email Studio organization if it already supplies this exact behavior instead of adding another tree. Favorites remain independent user+tenant references. No shared global favorite order, numbered-title convention, nested folders or per-person sharing lists are necessary for launch.

**Maintain:** In Email Studio, show Title, scope/use/language, publication/availability, last substantive update and responsible maintainer. Offer exact revision history/diff, revise, duplicate, offer for sharing, retire and restore-as-new-candidate where authorized. Normal revision preserves the currently published version until the next approved publication succeeds. Retirement is a real source lifecycle action, reversible through current qualification; do not use a dummy team or deletion as temporary unavailability. Quarantine remains distinct and cannot be reversed by ordinary restore or duplicate. Retiring an item stops new insertion, not previously sent history; D4/P17 safety fences still govern stale or prepared content.

**Departures:** Shared content outlives its original author under current tenant stewardship. Personal items lose ordinary use when their owner loses authorization. No automatic sharing, hard cascade deletion, new personal owner inferred from reassigned conversations, or cross-tenant transfer. Qualified governance custody can preserve/review eligible items with the actual authority; it is not “private means anyone with admin role can browse.” Reinstatement rechecks current asset/person/tenant/safety state and never undoes prior explicit retirement or disposal.

## Assets, locale and retention

Reusable files are valid only as separately qualified library assets suitable for repeated distribution. A tenant-owned public guide can be an asset; an individual donor's receipt or a temporary signed download cannot. Previews list filenames, type, size and audience suitability. Insertion pins the actual eligible revision and rechecks sender/audience at preparation. No silent link-to-attachment conversion, revoked URL reuse, duplicate byte custody or stripping failed files while leaving “see attached” wording. Preserve the P17 safe image vocabulary; do not add arbitrary legacy embedded media.

The simplest reliable creation path starts in generic authoring. **Save this conversation as a reply is not a harmless convenience.** If provided, it must explicitly select only staff-authored reusable wording, exclude headers/quoted thread/attachments and resolved recipient values by default, retain honest source provenance, and qualify generalization/custody before publication. Scrubbing detected emails alone cannot prove independence. A safer launch boundary is a clean reusable draft plus deliberate eligible authoring-asset duplication, rather than auto-saving a sent/transcript body. Do not describe that bound as a temporary workaround: it is a deliberate permanent scope choice until a source-safe extraction contract is justified.

Known source-derived copies remain subject to D16/D17 restrictions; deletion from a conversation is not bypassed by putting it in My replies. A generic independent reusable asset follows its own P17 retention policy. Usage/favorites/search metadata must not retain recipient facts or bodies, and request query strings must not become analytics payloads. Deleted/expired asset revisions disappear from ordinary previews and exports; necessary history is a separately governed minimal record.

Locale is the message language, not the author's UI language or a guessed nationality. Reuse P17's canonical supported locale and capability/validation model. Show an existing approved language variant explicitly; do not silently machine-translate, adopt Kustomer's live nesting or patch individual fields from different versions. If the preferred language is unavailable, show alternatives with their actual language and permit deliberate selection if the applicable contract allows it. Mixed-language group recipients do not yield invented personalized fan-out. Updating one wording revision does not claim corresponding translations remain current; mark affected variants for the existing governed review and preserve the last valid publication only where still eligible.

## Accessibility, mobile and low-bandwidth acceptance

Use exact Core base-maia, shared Base UI primitives and semantic tokens. [W3C combobox guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/combobox/) supports manual option selection, Escape without unintended change and preserving native text editing. [Character-key-shortcut guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/character-key-shortcuts.html) informs scope/remapping/disable behavior; [target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) informs shared touch targets. A custom ARIA implementation is not required merely because the picker is important.

- The visible button and optional slash entry use the same authorized selection path. Slash recognizes an explicit composer command context, not URLs, fractions, pasted text or IME composition. Escape dismisses; Tab navigates normally. It never acts as an unconditional text-expansion trigger.
- Focus restores to the editor and saved insertion point. Enter selects/inserts only while the picker owns focus; it cannot also trigger Send. No key-repeat or repeated click creates duplicate insertions/admissions.
- On mobile, use the same authorized library and semantic controls in a full-height sheet/panel with a reachable search, preview and Insert action. The virtual keyboard cannot obscure required fields or the insertion action. No horizontal table or hover-only metadata is necessary for ordinary use.
- Long translated names, mixed-script text, RTL, large text, zoom/reflow, images-blocked and plain-text outputs must remain readable. Staff notes must retain their explicit internal label.
- Loading, empty, invalid filter, permission loss, failed search, stale revision, missing field, unsafe asset and offline states differ. Never show “no replies” for a failed request. Lazy-load large previews/assets, preserve drafts and do not maintain a secret offline personal-library copy.
- Validate actual keyboard/focus, screen-reader announcements, touch and low-bandwidth behavior; axe alone is insufficient. No live UI or usability evidence was executed in this review.

## Completion and proof recommendations

Resolve the owner extension for personal scope, internal-note compatibility and one-use fill-ins before presenting D18 as implementation-ready. Record no new field/layout/permission enum as existing runtime truth. Reuse P17 standard publication, source safety, canonical document, asset, locale, revision and retention behavior; qualify the narrower Support insertion boundary through D4.

Required proof cases should include: two same-titled shared/personal items; shortcut collision; case/Unicode/RTL search; unauthorized count/body prevention; archived/quarantined/stale source; accessible complete preview; caret loss during network delay; Insert/Replace and single Undo; multiple passages; unresolved optional/required fields; unknown and multi-recipient requester context; multiple related CRM records; no incidental CRM update; image/attachment revocation; no source-retention bypass; author departure and stewardship; exact shared submission revision; concurrent edit/publish/retire; mobile same-library parity; low bandwidth; unsupported locale and stale translations; graceful library outage with qualified plain drafting; no provider call on Save/Publish/Insert; source-use telemetry against actual admission.

Do not claim implementation, browser proof, production capacity, legal compliance, ministry prevalence or productivity gains from this report. Numeric item, field, file and rate bounds must follow qualified shared contracts and measured production-shaped performance rather than a vendor's plan limits. This input earns a clean, complete authoring/reuse journey, not additional channels, AI assistance, campaigns, a knowledge base, bulk sends or a generic approval platform.
