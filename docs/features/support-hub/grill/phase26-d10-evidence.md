Adopt that distinction, not its distributed workflow infrastructure: Core can atomically change its narrow Support relationship and current work in its existing database, followed by durable derived refresh. A generic event-sourcing/saga platform is unnecessary.

## Reconciled independent findings

The three reviews converged on preserved original conversations, a narrow active-parent forest, one composed staff history, persistent current-preview Undo and source-aware CRM. The root incorporated these specific corrections:

- Undo ends one exact active edge, including non-latest nested merges; old URLs cannot use permanent cached redirects as authority.
- One current-outcome Undo algorithm avoids a brittle clean-versus-dirty rollback classifier. It creates current work/reminder/assignment plans and preserves later facts.
- A still-active combined reminder carries component scope, not a generic promise ledger. Undo explicitly chooses follow-up for applicable results with fresh generations; valid Waiting remains Waiting, while Open with future reminder requires explicit compound intent.
- Independent pending financial work does not itself force Support Open. Outstanding Support promises and required unreviewed owner outcomes remain protected.
- Collision protection covers relevant changes from every constituent source, including drafts opened through different original URLs.
- An already effectively related Party/label Add is unchanged; it cannot secretly create a new root-origin association. Remove and its Undo target the actual contributing origins and current delta.
- Current priority is reconciled from the two current work components, not old dormant priorities; subject, label origin and service-time evidence have explicit rules without an all-fields wizard.
- Same-current-inbox launch scope avoids hidden routing/coverage moves, while current compound/source authorization prevents access expansion. Different source email addresses do not automatically establish or forbid duplication.
- Merge and Undo activate together. Disabling new merges retains original routing, existing current work, authorized Undo and repair.

Each is reflected in D10-R01–R25 and P01–P39. Working independent reports are intermediate evidence; the root review/blueprint resolves their alternatives. The complete resulting requirements and amendments are now explicitly founder-ratified as D10; D1–D9 remain preserved.
