## Recommended next decision

**How should staff categorize conversations beyond their inbox and work status?**

Recommend **A — Optional labels from a curated Support list**. Staff can apply useful established labels, while qualified maintainers control the vocabulary. Support remains usable without labelling every conversation.

The complete alternatives are:

| Option                                                 | Practical meaning                                                                                                                                                                             | Benefit                                                                                | Cost                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Optional curated labels**                        | Staff choose one or more existing Support labels when useful; qualified maintainers create and maintain them. Resolve does not require a label.                                               | Consistent labels make D20 views useful without adding a step to every conversation.   | Some work stays unlabelled, and a genuinely new topic may need a maintainer to add a term.                                 |
| **B — Optional labels staff can create while working** | Qualified handlers can deliberately create a new Support label from the conversation and use existing labels. Resolve does not require a label.                                               | Fast response to new needs without a separate maintainer handoff.                      | Similar terms and spelling variants fragment views and reporting, increasing cleanup and ambiguity.                        |
| **C — At least one curated label before Resolve**      | Staff choose from a maintained Support list; the normal Resolve action requires at least one applicable label. Intake, reading, replying and normal unfinished work do not require one first. | More complete classification of resolved work for teams that depend on topic coverage. | A close-time step and pressure to use generic or inaccurate labels merely to finish. Coverage is not evidence of accuracy. |

These are three launch models, not an instruction to ship three tenant modes. C does not add a primary-topic column, a required intake form, an exclusive single-category model or automated classification. A and C may allow several labels; totals by label consequently need not be an exclusive partition of conversations.

Illustrative example, not a claim about ministry workload: a church emails about a receipt correction. The team has a `Receipt question` Support label. Alex can apply it and later retrieve that work through a D20 view. Under A Alex may resolve a straightforward unrelated question without inventing a category. Under B Alex may create `Missing receipt` while working, raising the question whether it is genuinely different from `Receipt question`. Under C Alex must choose an applicable curated label before Resolve, even if the response itself is already complete.

**Strongest counterargument to A:** if the tenant actually relies on a complete record of resolved request topics, optional labels leave a biased sample. C would better support that need, provided the list is concise and staff can classify accurately. No Asym user study, reporting mandate or measured topic-coverage need was found that justifies making classification a universal completion gate. Therefore A is the best current product judgment. B is strongest when vocabulary genuinely changes quickly and curatorial latency is more costly than cleanup.

## Why this is genuinely unresolved

- The current D20 catalog explicitly includes **existing labels**, and settles `Any selected` / `All selected` / `No labels`, reference identity and complete authorized query behavior. It does not settle who may create labels, whether ordinary staff may invent them inline, or whether resolution requires one.
- D3 settles work status and purposeful resolution. It explicitly avoids a mandatory blocker taxonomy. A new universal topic requirement must therefore be a deliberate founder choice, not smuggled into the meaning of Resolved.
- D15 keeps ordinary label edits quiet; Q21 cannot silently add follower notifications.
- The existing Support label schema and CRUD show this is a real current product seam. Existing implementation is evidence of scaffolding, not proof that the current permissions, deletion or reporting consequences are correct.
- Broader conversation full-text search remains a separate legitimate gap. D20 explicitly does not expand search to new text, attachment, CRM or AI sources. It is not necessary to resolve that independent product scope before choosing label governance.

## Current primary comparisons

**Front — Understanding tags, edited 1 July 2026.** Shared tags are maintained by company admins or users with workspace permissions; their application does not share the conversation. The conversation picker supports search and creation. Front also documents required-tagging rules before move/archive. Adopt the separation between vocabulary maintenance, application and access; evaluate mandatory completion as a real alternative. Do not import private tag folders, company/workspace nesting, mailbox synchronization or label-triggered routing into Asym merely because Front supports them. Company tags are Enterprise; those edition-specific capabilities are not an Asym requirement. [Source](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2100).

**Zendesk — Working with ticket tags, current at 12 September 2026 retrieval; an absolute article-edit date was not confirmed on the opened page.** Staff can create a previously nonexistent tag while entering tags, subject to relevant custom-role permissions. Tags support retrieval/views but also feed Zendesk rules. This is evidence that B is practical, while its free-creation model requires vocabulary discipline. The source's literal text, suggestion delay and vendor limits do not become Asym data/storage/UX requirements. [Source](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408835059482-Working-with-ticket-tags).

**Intercom — historical tagging-permissions announcement, 3 September 2020.** Explicit conversation-tag create/edit/archive permissions were introduced to constrain uncontrolled vocabulary growth. This is historical rationale, not sufficient evidence of current plan availability or precise present permissions. The current custom-role page is dated 1 May 2026 and makes custom roles Expert-plan only. Root's primary research should verify the current conversation-tag help page before relying on detailed current behavior. [Historical announcement](https://www.intercom.com/changes/en/2217-prevent-your-conversation-tags-from-getting-unmanageable-with-new-tagging-permissions), [current role documentation](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/3659500-manage-permissions-effortlessly-with-custom-roles).

Vendor interfaces demonstrate patterns and tradeoffs; they do not prove which classification mandate ministries need. No prevalence, satisfaction lift, arbitrary label cap or compliance claim should be invented.

## Non-negotiable explanation beside all three options

1. These are **Support conversation labels**. Applying one does not tag a person, household, organization, gift or other CRM record, establish identity, prove representation or change a related Party's properties.
2. `Confidential`, `Urgent` or `Refund complete` typed as a label cannot grant security classification, alter D14 targets, establish giving completion or create a routing rule. Those facts retain their actual owners. Avoid examples that encourage labels to masquerade as them.
3. Curators are qualified maintainers through existing Core capabilities, not automatically all administrators or a new all-powerful role. Applying an approved term and changing the shared vocabulary are separate acts.
4. Label edits do not Send, add recipients, publish Email Studio content, dispatch Resend, subscribe followers, resolve work or create CRM interaction history. Existing separately governed actions remain independently available.
5. Multiple conversations may match multiple labels; label counts are not mutually exclusive topic totals. A label is optional human description under A/B, not measured truth about every request.
6. Preserve D20 stable IDs, reference meanings, current-source permissions, complete queries and safe lifecycle; no label name reuse can silently retarget a saved view.

## Challenge to the proposed question

- Do not ask “Do you want labels?” without acknowledging D20 already uses the current label capability.
- Do not offer a no-label option as if it preserves D20 unchanged.
- Do not repeat My/Shared library options or invent private personal labels merely to mirror D18/D20.
- Do not describe A as inherently superior “best practice.” It trades completeness for less interruption and vocabulary consistency.
- Do not describe C as mandatory on intake or as classification proof. It is a late, explicit resolution gate and can produce inaccurate catch-all choices.
- Do not require a request/approval workflow just to propose a missing term before a demonstrated need. A modest maintained list can use existing maintainer collaboration.
- Do not freeze table shape, global versus scoped vocabulary visibility, retention, cleanup limits, colors, statuses or automatic actions in the question. Those require selected-answer source qualification and review.

**Independent conclusion:** Q21 is necessary and distinct. Present all three practical options visibly, recommend A, and wait for one answer before the exhaustive selected-answer review. The unselected alternatives remain research evidence only.
