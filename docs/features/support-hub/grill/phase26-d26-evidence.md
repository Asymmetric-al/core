## Independent findings reconciled into the permanent path

[Data review](phase26-d26-data-review.md) supplied 14 material findings, 21 source pins and all 23 categories. [UX review](phase26-d26-ux-research.md) covered both entries, existing-email viewing,14 maintenance cases and source-backed pitfalls. [Owner review](phase26-d26-owner-review.md) supplied 17 scoped proposed clauses. Root synthesis incorporated the findings into one 30-clause contract, not parallel competing requirements.

The final challenge produced these adopted corrections:

- **Reachable manual-only setup:** a manager can establish a named work inbox/access/coverage before email; Record a request preserves drafts through setup/handoff. Configure email remains D25's two paths. This explicitly amends D25's email-first setup assumption and mail-shaped inbox data.
- **Stable identifiers:** retain existing Support TEXT IDs; no rekey migration disguised as a UUID best practice.
- **Unused cancellation only:** every D12 unused-tracking exclusion remains before the additional standalone obligation/current-home check. Completed/used work cannot be reclassified to improve reports.
- **Uncertain first email:** retain D14 candidate First/Next interpretations and earliest applicable possible deadline until acceptance is resolved.
- **Qualified staff attention:** no requester email or fictional mail event from manual save; independently qualified existing staff assignment/attention keeps its own source/recipient/policy and never makes optional staff email a work-admission prerequisite.

The chosen path is therefore complete for grooming: direct bounded capture; all-origin authorized CRM discovery and shared inline detail; one source/ref; explicit manual work readiness; accurate native-mail/recorded clocks; exact later-email behavior; finite retention/search; atomic guarded admission; compatible rollout. The precise corrected decision and acceptance language is in R01–R30/P01–P50, not left for the founder to reconstruct.
