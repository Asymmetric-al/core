## Exact recommended contract

Each concern below identifies the risk and decision effect; the quoted text is the permanent amendment. Severity/likelihood assess an unamended or naive implementation, not a claimed production incident.

### O26-01 — Narrow new origin, truthful source

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood high if generic conversation insertion is reused.** A phone brief could become a fictitious incoming message or a generic CRM call log. This narrows A's implementation.

> Authorized staff may record an actual phone or in-person request that requires continuing Support work. Admit one stable original Support conversation with explicit staff-recorded origin, trusted recorder/admission evidence and a bounded request brief. The brief is staff context, not the requester's authored message, an email, a transcript or proof that Asym placed a call. Native correspondence counts and first/last mail timestamps remain absent until real correspondence exists. Ordinary completed relationship interactions, general staff tasks and restricted care stay in their proper owning surface.

The new origin extends D12's source union; it does not invent an incoming-email reference to pass an existing constraint. Proof: manual creation with no Party/email produces usable work, zero mail/recipient events and truthful source presentation through both entries.

### O26-02 — Original interaction and request brief have separate meanings

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood medium/high.** A second editable call log would diverge or preserve content after its owner removed it. Making a full interaction engine mandatory would overbuild A.

> If an authorized original interaction already exists, reference its stable owner identity/revision and capture only the needed Support request brief. Do not clone the interaction body, participants or attachments into another authoritative encounter record. If Support is the first capture, its staff-recorded request/source is authoritative for that bounded request; do not dual-write an encounter into CRM Notes merely to make a timeline look complete. Later CRM visibility uses qualified references and projections. A brief is not a complete relationship log or verbatim transcript. Exact ownership and lifetime follow the source kind, not the launch screen.

Multiple legitimate independent requests may arise from one interaction. Neither interaction ID alone nor person/date/summary text is a universal uniqueness key. One requested effect has one command identity; independent work remains possible. Proof: existing-interaction-to-Support journey leaves one encounter, one appropriate Support source and no shadow body; two independent issues are not incorrectly collapsed.

### O26-03 — Every relevant record is reachable through CRM

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood high if only an email timeline or summary card is reused.** Manual and resolved work could disappear from CRM, or the founder's access requirement could be misread as unrestricted access.

> Every authorized relevant Support conversation, regardless of email or staff-recorded origin, is discoverable and viewable through the relevant CRM record's existing Support conversations capability. Preserve D9's compact Overview preview and provide its complete paginated All view for resolved history; the preview limit is not a record limit. Open and read the same canonical current Support detail while preserving the CRM shell/context, with current joint CRM/Support/source permissions, original source provenance and meaningful return context. A token Activity marker or a link requiring reconstruction of the request elsewhere is insufficient; reuse the canonical component/read command rather than a second CRM message store. Merge grouping deduplicates displayed work without losing authorized original history. Context-only relevance is visibly distinct from actual correspondence.

> Unknown or unlinked requesters do not require a fake Party. Their work remains usable in Support and becomes discoverable through CRM when a legitimate explicit link or independently qualified correspondence basis exists. Viewing CRM does not confer restricted Support, care, financial or attachment access. No inaccessible title, excerpt, count or relationship topology leaks through the CRM entry. Expired content remains unavailable even where permitted structural history is still visible.

This requirement concerns relevant Support work, not exposing every secret, configuration item or reusable library record through every CRM person. Proof: manual/no-mail, email, mixed, resolved and merged fixtures appear under the appropriate authorized basis; permission-negative fixtures expose neither content nor hidden-record counts.

### O26-04 — One creation command from either surface

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood medium.** CRM and Support forms could admit different states, context or duplicates.

> Support and authorized CRM/interaction entry invoke the same current manual-request command and focused form. A CRM action explicitly labeled for the current Party may stage that requested D9 relevance link visibly for review, subject to both domains' current authority; staff may remove it before admission. Support entry infers no Party from text, phone or email. The launch surface never becomes source identity or independent write authority. Preserve the form, originating record/list state and return location; permission loss clears inaccessible context without losing permissible recovery information.

Proof: identical reviewed inputs from both entries produce the same source/work/authorization behavior; a CRM reader without Support-create authority cannot create or inspect it; a Support creator without CRM access cannot attach or discover the hidden Party.

### O26-05 — Qualified manual work does not need fake mail readiness

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood high if D25's email setup flag gates all Support work.** A receiving outage or unconfigured email account would prevent staff recording an actual obligation; weakening email gates would leak sends.

> Manual admission requires a qualified Support work destination, current create/handling/source-release authority, valid accountable coverage and applicable records policy. It does not require a fabricated receiving address, external mailbox challenge, Resend key or production-email Ready state merely to save non-email work. D25's email route lifecycle remains unchanged. Where an inbox is not eligible for manual work for a real security/custody/coverage reason, expose that reason rather than equating it with email transport failure. Every later actual email separately re-proves the complete P17/P6/D25 route, account, sender and recipient gates.

This is an explicit operation-specific readiness seam, not a universal Active bypass. Proof: phone-only work persists during an outgoing provider outage with proper internal handling; first email remains blocked and its draft recoverable until mail readiness passes.

### O26-06 — Private staging and one deliberate admission

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood medium.** Autosave could publish a brief, send a message, change assignments or leak into CRM/search before submission.

> The draft stages only the selected source, minimal brief, destination and reviewed optional existing controls. Editing/selecting/preparing does not create a conversation, Party association, message, assignment, reminder, label, use receipt or provider intent. Persist drafts only under the existing bounded protected draft owner; no generic browser storage or CRM-note autosave becomes authority. The primary admission action states that no email will be sent. Cancel before admission creates no Support work and does not delete an independently existing source interaction.

Use ordinary inputs for title, reported time, source and target selection; the accepted bounded Tiptap Staff context/note profile edits the brief. A toolbar is not schema validation. Existing safe schema/parser/rendering/paste/version rules remain; no new rich-text model or signature insertion applies to this internal source. Proof: unsupported nodes and unsafe pasted content cannot be admitted or silently downgraded into changed protected meaning; drafts stay absent from normal CRM/search/reporting.

### O26-07 — Initial work is explicit and accountable

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood medium.** A default waiting state, auto-assignment or duplicate task could hide the very follow-up being recorded.

> New manual follow-up begins Open because Support owes work. Show the reviewed qualified inbox and handling choice; do not automatically assign the recorder, rerun email first-intake routing or copy stale absence/Keep/coverage state. Existing D3 reminders and D6–D8 handling remain available through their owner commands; explicit Open-plus-reminder can represent a reviewed later follow-up. No duplicate CRM task is generated solely because Support work exists. Waiting requires the actual D3 blocker, and Resolve cannot certify a giving/CRM/care action or conceal an outstanding promise.

An invalid target or prefill fails before admission without silently selecting another owner. Proof: no-self-assignment, unavailable handler, Shared handling, real reminder and subsequent work transitions preserve accepted meanings from either entry.

### O26-08 — Atomic creation and stable retries

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood high under lost responses/concurrency.** Separate source/link/work writes can leave missing or duplicate obligations.

> One current-authorized conditional transaction admits the original conversation, classified brief/source references, reviewed initial work/handling/reminder and optional D9 links, actual actor/audit evidence, durable command receipt and required identifier-only continuation. Known rejection admits none. Caller input cannot choose tenant, recorder identity, provider provenance, authorization result or creation time. Use one durable operation identity and immutable reviewed meaning; exact retries reconcile the original result, changed input conflicts, and later current state is read separately. A lost response never creates a second request or replaces a newer state with an old receipt.

No provider or remote source mutation is performed inside this local transaction. Existing external interaction success is not rolled back if Support creation fails. Proof: failpoints at every write, two tabs, stale destination/link permission, replay after correction and partial read failure preserve one attributable outcome.

### O26-09 — Reported occurrence is not trusted admission order

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood high for late entry.** Backdating could manipulate retention, elapsed work or contact metrics; forcing a precise time could invent evidence.

> Retain reported encounter occurrence and its actual precision/zone/source separately from immutable staff admission/order and work-transition times. Allow honestly unknown or date-only occurrence where the user lacks a precise time; do not fabricate midnight or now as a proven encounter instant. Corrections are attributable source revisions, not edits to trusted creation/audit order. Reject a future encounter as completed manual intake; future planned work belongs to the existing task/reminder journey. Late entry is visible and does not rewrite native mail timestamps, provider history or last-contact truth.

No universal call-response SLA, duration or phone productivity score is introduced. New-work counts, any reported occurrence view and existing email counts have named different units. Proof: late/date-only/unknown occurrence, DST/localization and corrected reported time leave actual admission and D14/retention anchors correct.

### O26-10 — Later first email needs its own audience

**Material concern: yes. Severity critical; likelihood medium if D12's trigger logic is reused.** Manual work has no incoming email from which to derive recipients; CRM relevance could accidentally become an audience.

> Before native correspondence, the external action is Start email thread, distinct from recording the request. Manual source has no email-trigger-derived recipient default. Staff deliberately choose a current qualified audience; permitted CRM contact data may supply reviewed candidates but neither relevance links nor reported caller information automatically selects recipients or proves contactability/representation/consent. An absent Party remains valid. Do not union related records, inferred household members or future preferred addresses into the audience. Never auto-copy the private brief or source attachments into public content.

> Use D2/D4/D12's qualified first-thread mode: a meaningful reviewed subject, no fictitious old In-Reply-To/References, the original work's stable source identity and registered qualified return-route correlation before provider I/O. P17 supplies eligible wording/signature/presentation and immutable preparation; P6 dispatches through the exact tenant Resend owner. Body, internal note or signature text cannot select From/Reply-To or bypass current route proof. Source restriction or recipient change requires current review, not silent substitution.

D12-R11's incoming-trigger recipient-basis rule therefore gains a manual-origin branch; its original behavior remains for email-derived related work. Proof: no-address work is saveable, initial audience remains empty until reviewed, wrong Party/represented-person cases cannot disclose, and actual first mail has independent provider/RFC lineage.

### O26-11 — Preserve D14 First/Next and delivery truth

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood medium.** Teams could receive false response credit or hide a later short Next target.

> Manual creation, a published brief, an internal note, CRM linking and a phone outcome create or satisfy no D14 incoming-email target. A genuinely accepted human Start email thread can establish the existing D14 Next boundary for later qualified incoming mail. Queued/indeterminate/definitely rejected sends retain D14's existing uncertainty/coverage rules; do not simply force First because the original began manually. Actual admission, P6 acceptance and delivery remain different facts. A phone follow-up does not become a sent email or an AI-judged response.

Proof: manual-only original has zero target instances; accepted first email followed by qualified input uses Next; failed/unknown first send and reordered acceptance evidence preserve the permitted interpretation and real obligations.

### O26-12 — Explicit retention coverage for the new brief

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood high if the new source is omitted from disposal.** The brief may remain forever, be deleted using a caller-reported old date, or renew older content repeatedly through edits.

> A first published D26 staff-recorded request brief is classified Support-owned human source content and participates once in D17's trusted first-admission activity/retention semantics. It remains Staff context with no invented native correspondence. Include its body/editor data, title, reported identity details, permitted file appearances and derivatives in the owning content frontier and finite policy. Use trusted admission and genuine work-purpose ending; reported encounter time does not backdate the disposal clock. Edits, links, CRM projections, metadata changes and retries do not renew it. Source-owner corrections/redaction/expiry apply before serving either surface.

The R05 activity allowlist must name this origin explicitly. Where an original interaction is independently owned, its policy remains independent; Support reference alone neither extends nor destroys it. If an authorized source-derived brief carries a stricter disclosure/retention floor, enforce it. Expired bytes cannot return through a copied note, search result, Recent copy or new email draft. Proof: manual-only resolved work expires correctly; edits do not postpone expiry; late entry does not cause immediate deletion; reopen at/after elapsed authority cannot resurrect old context.

### O26-13 — Search and CRM detail use the existing source contract

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood medium.** New briefs could be invisible in Support search or indexed through CRM without the required source restrictions.

> Register admitted D26 briefs in D22's existing Staff context source kind with stable tenant/original/source/revision and truthful staff provenance. Reuse its literal matching, current source eligibility, search authorization, excerpts, invalidation and bounded pagination. Index neither private drafts nor every cited interaction's content. Adding a related Party does not expand a brief's indexing permission. CRM opens the same canonical source/detail under current permissions rather than maintaining another searchable copy.

The complete CRM Support list can include records with zero native messages; show Recorded time/source where appropriate without labeling it Last message or Sent. Retained structural shells after expiry show permitted status/reference and clear content availability, not old subject/snippet snapshots. Proof: authorized same query finds admitted staff context from the proper work surface; denied, expired and non-indexable sources yield neither matches nor counts in either route.

### O26-14 — Existing work, subsequent mail and genuine duplicates

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood high in mixed-channel service.** A caller update and later email could create parallel obligations or get merged by unsafe identity guesses.

> When the reported request is already handled in authorized Support work, offer that work and record only needed new staff context under its owner rather than silently create another request. The staff member explicitly reviews the resulting D3 work/reminder needs; saving a note is not automatic resolution or fulfillment of an owner action. New relevant work must remain Open/actionable or have its actually valid reviewed plan. No general automatic classification of free text decides a work transition.

> Later genuine email preserves its actual original receipt and source identity. Qualified reply-route evidence may attach it under D1/D2; matching name, phone, Party, email address or subject alone cannot choose or merge a conversation. If it arrives as separate work, use deliberate D10 Merge only for a genuine duplicate with current authority and reviewed obligations. D12 independent related work remains available for genuinely different issues. Manual origin remains historical after native email arrives; topology never rewrites it as email-origin intake.

Proof: two independent requests from one caller survive; an email plus manual duplicate can be reviewed and merged without duplicate P6 events; later old-route replies preserve source affinity and obligations after merge/Undo.

### O26-15 — Correct and cancel without an invented original work home

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood medium.** Reusing D12 cancellation can discard an obligation because a standalone manual request has no original email conversation to reopen.

> Cancel before admission simply abandons the private draft. After admission, Review creation may use D12's qualified unused-created-in-error retirement only when its existing no-subsequent-activity/effect/dependency constraints pass and either no real request remains owed or its obligation is durably accepted into a current authorized work home. A standalone manual origin has no implicit fallback source conversation. If actual work remains without a valid home, keep it actionable and use current correction; do not delete it or manufacture a source conversation. Created-in-error is excluded from successful-resolution/intake success metrics and preserves permitted administrative evidence.

Incorrect occurrence/brief/link/handling are repaired through the proper owner with current revisions; no universal Undo can recall mail, roll back a refund or erase an independently recorded call. Native/possibly submitted mail, later staff work, merge participation or other protected dependencies require ordinary current correction rather than unused cancellation. Proof: accidental no-obligation creation may retire; real owed work cannot vanish; replay returns corrected historical receipt, never a new live request.

### O26-16 — Cross-surface authorization survives lifecycle changes

**Material concern: yes. Severity critical; likelihood medium.** CRM context may become a side door after Party merge, unlink, staff departure or redaction.

> Every CRM Support row/detail, source link, action, search result, asset and count evaluates current tenant/resource/source policy. A Party merge may recompute legitimate discovery under its owner but grants no access, requester identity, recipient expansion or transferred Support ownership. Unlink removes context-only discovery while independent qualified correspondence remains. Changing employment/email ownership does not reattribute old manual reports or mail. Revoked access withdraws affected cached content from both entries; stale projections cannot restore it. D16/D17 restrictions beat asynchronous cleanup.

Proof: CRM-only, Support-only, jointly authorized, care-restricted and revoked identities each get exactly their allowed projection; merging/unlinking/deleting a Party cannot duplicate history or restore a removed association through a delayed projector.

### O26-17 — Email Studio, shortcuts and future AI remain consumers

**Material concern: yes. Severity high; likelihood medium if every text editor or action shortcut is treated as a sender.** Saving the brief could invoke publication, provider delivery, source mining or hidden work macros.

> Email Studio does not own or publish the encounter/request brief. Shared Tiptap authoring components do not transfer ownership. P17 becomes a preparation owner only for a deliberate actual email, preserving D18 wording and D23 managed signature/public-responder rules. D24 reply-and-work shortcuts operate only in an existing authorized reply draft and cannot create a manual request, invoke capture, extract its private brief or act from form submission. No new system-message key, template runtime, Role Layout, provider-template sync, AI call or generic workflow vocabulary is introduced.

A later permitted shortcut still stages text/work and uses existing admission, identity, guard and idempotency rules. Saving manual work does not trigger D13 incoming acknowledgement or give automatic staff email permission. Required source-qualified in-product work discovery remains available if notifications fail; any actual staff email needs its own already-qualified producer/recipient/Email Studio/P6 contract. Proof: create paths yield no P17 preparation/provider send/shortcut-use event, while later deliberate valid email consumes the established seam once.
