## What is known, inferred and decided

**Founder evidence:** the user explicitly wants contextual information to be optional and reaching the tenant to be easy. Earlier ratified decisions establish email continuation, one canonical Support conversation, current CRM authorization, shared sending ownership, public/internal guidance boundaries and no requester message archive. This is sufficient evidence for a contact-first entry. It does not establish demand for a new searchable public library, chat, a bot or a form builder.

**Verified repository facts:** the current Core source and pinned planning objects below have been read. Existing email-shaped Support rows and broad staff policies are not proof of certified form intake. The current FAQ contains hard-coded organization/financial claims that must not become tenant truth. P23 already defines an authoritative public form occurrence with exactly one primary outcome and optional notification children. Public and authenticated app surfaces have different scope authorities.

**Reasonable inference:** for a person who already knows they need help, mandatory article interaction and extra routing questions add work. Contextual references can help someone who wants guidance without making them choose a second destination. Neither inference proves a numerical contact reduction, satisfaction increase or response-time improvement.

**Product judgment:** contact form immediately visible; two required fields; optional unsplit name; deliberately published email; phone only when genuinely available; up to three optional guides. The numerical input/load/monitoring bounds are proposed acceptance controls, not vendor norms or measured platform capacity. A guide opens as a labelled normal link in a separate tab to preserve the draft without building an embedded CMS renderer.

**Unresolved empirical evidence:** actual tenant corpus size, locale coverage, requester device/assistive-technology mix, reliable telephone staffing and measured completion/performance baselines are not known. They are not silently assumed. Their owners and release proofs are explicit in P38–P44 and O04/O06; no unknown grants permission to publish an unstaffed channel, private context or an unqualified form. There is no remaining unanswered policy choice inside this proposed package.

## Usability and nonprofit evidence

[NN/g's Contact Us study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/contact-us-pages/) reports qualitative research with **20 business professionals across 40 corporate websites**, published 18 August 2019. Participants valued visible contact options and disliked hidden channels or obstructive forms. This supports visible email and immediate contact. It is older B2B research, not a 2026 ministry usability study; it does not justify mandatory phone, physical location or live staffing for every tenant.

[GOV.UK's form structure guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/form-structure), [names pattern](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/names/) and [email pattern](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/email-addresses/) support asking only for necessary information, flexible names, visible labels and clear use of a reply address. A two-field inquiry does not need the multi-page question sequence used by longer government services. [Progressive enhancement guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/technology/using-progressive-enhancement), updated 27 September 2024, supports resilient HTML-first contact; Core's P23 contract independently requires the secure native/no-JS form path.

[W3C form instructions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/instructions/) and [notifications](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/notifications/) inform visible labels, linked errors and a persistent receipt. [Redundant Entry](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/redundant-entry.html) supports avoiding unnecessary re-entry, while [Consistent Help](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/consistent-help.html) concerns consistent relative placement of repeated help. Neither standard grants identity authority or requires a staffed channel on every page. [H83](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/html/H83.html) illustrates indicating user-requested new tabs; our choice still needs browser/assistive-technology proof and is not the only compliant technique.

[MDN autocomplete](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Attributes/autocomplete) describes useful browser input hints; these cannot establish identity or override current authorization. [MDN email inputs](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/input/email) documents browser validation limits. The actual owner-qualified mailbox grammar remains authoritative; do not silently reject supported addresses through narrower hints or turn email syntax into CRM verification.

Actual nonprofit examples include [American Red Cross FAQ](https://www.redcross.org/faq.html), [Compassion FAQ](https://www.compassion.com/sponsor-a-child/faq/) and [contact](https://www.compassion.com/about-us/contact/), and [charity: water contact](https://support.charitywater.org/hc/en-us/articles/205703905-How-can-I-contact-you), whose article showed 10 August 2026. They demonstrate coexistence of information and human contact. Compassion's extracted page does not prove the complete behavior of its embedded form. These pages do not establish Asym tenant roles, contact staffing, required form fields, phone availability or financial policies. No live donor record, request or real contact was used.

No credible Asym-specific user dataset was available. Vendor feature documentation, qualitative user research and nonprofit examples have different evidentiary weight. No conversion lift, reduced workload, improved satisfaction, successful deflection or representative donor preference is asserted. Moderated requester/maintainer journeys remain P44 release evidence, including guests, on-behalf use, mobile/low bandwidth, international names, translation and current CRM restrictions.

## Supabase, authorization and finite proof

Current [Supabase RLS documentation](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security) supports checking grants and row policies together and recognizes privileged service paths. A guest is not the same thing as an anonymous signed-in Supabase user. [PostgreSQL 17 CREATE POLICY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createpolicy.html) documents that an omitted WITH CHECK can use the USING predicate in applicable policies. Therefore this review requires proof of effective old/new-row authority; it does not incorrectly report every omitted WITH CHECK as a vulnerability.

The official [Supabase changelog](https://supabase.com/changelog.md) was retrieved through read-only HTTPS after the web reader rejected its Markdown MIME type. Current notices, including future Management API log changes, extension pinning and Realtime/self-hosted changes, do not establish a deployed version or require an unrelated infrastructure change for this grooming decision. No database connection, migration, advisor scan, provider action or production query was performed.

The executed [reference examples](phase26-d29-reference-experiments.json) contain **24 finite synthetic checks**: selected-source scope/reach and withdrawal, empty-guide contact continuity, fourth-ref rejection, Unicode boundaries, encoded request size, idempotent intent examples, exclusive primary/zero-or-one acknowledgement cardinality and actor/tenant draft clearing. They test a small explanatory model, not Core code. They are useful for detecting a contradiction in a proposed contract, not proving concurrency, SQL/RLS, P17/P6 or a working browser journey.

The encoding experiment materially changed the proposed body cap: 10,000 four-byte message scalars plus 200 name scalars and a bounded synthetic envelope use **126,677 URL-encoded bytes**, within **256 KiB = 262,144 bytes**. A 64 KiB raw-body cap could reject otherwise legal non-ASCII input. The fixture is not a proof of every real envelope; the actual release must enforce both semantic and raw limits. HTML maxlength measures UTF-16 code units, so it must not silently halve this scalar-based limit for emoji. CRLF normalization, combining text, boundary bytes and actual mailbox support still need P08 product proof.

The three-guide limit, 10,000/200 scalar limits, 256 KiB ceiling, p95 two-second contact target and explicit 100-load/20-retry/two-tenant workload are prospective judgments. Existing D13 courtesy and timing are carried into the new qualified source deliberately, not presented as general email best practices. All **44 release proof groups remain required and unexecuted**. Independent final data, UX and owner reviews establish documentary consistency only.
