## Whole journey and scope

- **Immediate ordinary contact is preserved.** Help opens the form; there is no required search, article, rating, account, department, bot or channel-selection step. Email and message are required, name is optional and unsplit. Direct methods are visible and the narrow-screen order places contact before guides.
- **Guide use remains optional and bounded.** Zero to three exact Listed public references use source-owned titles/destinations. No public library/search, Internal or Shared-by-link discovery, automatic filler, copied article body or embedded article browser appears. Removing the last guide leaves a complete contact experience.
- **Reading and return remain honest.** A labelled ordinary new-tab link preserves the active contact page during normal navigation. The blueprint now explicitly avoids promising recovery after a browser/OS discards the tab. It introduces no persistent browser storage, offline queue or hidden resend.
- **Requester identity and optional context are distinct.** Actual-requester self-prefill is visible/editable. A represented organization or linked CRM record does not supply the reply endpoint by default. Context-only loss removes forbidden context/prefill while preserving only permitted visitor-authored text for deliberate continuation; real logout/account/tenant change clears the entire managed draft.
- **Failure states remain differentiated.** Known field rejection, uncertain acceptance, delayed materialization, optional guide/acknowledgement failure and unsafe whole-scope failure do not share a misleading generic success. Same-intent reconciliation does not rekey or send a second channel. Native/no-JavaScript submission retains the same authority, validation and durable outcome contract.
- **Staff and CRM continuation remain coherent.** Native form-origin content is not fabricated RFC email or a staff note. Current authorized CRM Communications → Support opens the canonical detail. No copied Activity, automatic Party creation, email-as-identity assertion or grant through assignment/linking is added. Consequential giving/identity/care actions remain at their owners.
- **The Email Studio seam is complete.** Browser Received follows durable primary acceptance and is independent of email. The optional web acknowledgement starts Off, needs its own qualified source/recipient/profile and retains the existing finite utility, common courtesy and full human-precedence rules. Governed wording/preparation belongs to P17; delivery/reconciliation belongs to P6. There is no duplicate editor/switch, permanent automatic-body copy or implicit D13 incoming-email trigger.
- **Maintenance remains an owner-based journey.** Requester help exposes actual placement, source, route and readiness, then navigates to qualified owners. Synthetic preview and routing check are side-effect dark. Public/app releases remain distinct; source removal, last-guide removal, route changes and maintainer departure have explicit recovery without routine SQL or an added rules platform.
- **Visual and international behavior is bounded.** App Base UI/base-maia and public presentation ownership remain separate. Existing tokens, clear labels, restrained hierarchy and reduced motion are preserved. Accurate published phone hours do not create mandatory phone staffing or a new service calendar. The refresh does not add a public Tiptap editor or attachments merely because staff Support uses them elsewhere.
