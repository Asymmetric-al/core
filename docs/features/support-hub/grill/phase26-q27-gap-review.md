## One practical A/B/C question

**When staff need a how-to or policy while handling a Support request, what guidance should they be able to find directly in Support?**

| Option                                                         | Concrete journey                                                                                                                                                                              | Strength                                                                                                                                                                 | Tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Find approved public guidance in Support — recommended** | From the current request/composer, find suitable qualified published tenant guidance, preview its public meaning and deliberately insert a link. Keep the request and private draft in place. | Reuses Web Studio publication and reduces page switching without introducing an internal knowledge product. Link insertion leaves content with its authoritative source. | Depends on genuinely useful published guidance and a qualified lookup/selection seam; it does not supply internal-only operating instructions.                                                                                                      |
| **B — Keep saved replies and manual links**                    | Continue using D18 wording/files and ordinary link insertion; staff opens the relevant published page or owner resource when needed.                                                          | Smallest scope, with no new guidance discovery or maintenance surface. It can be enough for teams whose repeated answers fit the curated reply library.                  | Staff must know where the resource lives and find/check its link separately. There is no dedicated guidance lookup inside the request.                                                                                                              |
| **C — Find public and internal staff guidance**                | The same working context supports public references and separately permission-qualified staff-only instructions, clearly differentiated before any public insertion.                          | Supports staff who need both a requester-facing explanation and an internal process/checklist while handling a request.                                                  | Adds a real internal source ownership, authoring/currentness, audience, retrieval and egress lifecycle that current Core evidence does not establish. It cannot be implemented by treating CMS drafts or every readable file as approved knowledge. |

These are three alternative scope choices, not three tenant modes to implement. A concerns selected qualified published guidance, not indiscriminately searching every ministry update, campaign page or other public item. Exact source selection/curation and maintenance should be resolved in the chosen answer's adversarial review rather than freezing a new taxonomy or separate editor at question time.

A provides the strongest proportionate next step: make useful existing public guidance easier to find and link, retain B's simple ordinary manual path, and avoid requiring the larger internal knowledge lifecycle before the basic use case works. C remains a serious option when staff procedure lookup is part of the intended need; it is not represented as inherently unsafe or unnecessary.
