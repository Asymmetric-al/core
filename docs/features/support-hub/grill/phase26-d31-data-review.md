## Permanent contract recommended to the synthesis

Use four common choices — **In 1 hour**, **In 4 hours**, **Tomorrow at this time**, and **In 1 week at this time** — plus **Custom**. These values are a proposed product choice, not a discovered universal ministry schedule. No universal morning hour, next-business-day rule, holiday skipping, recurrence, saved personal menu or automatic reminder on waiting is needed.

The picker starts in the current tenant's validated `default_timezone`, an actual owned field. Missing/invalid configuration produces a visible UTC fallback and an appropriate configuration issue; it does not block all work. An explicit per-use IANA zone override is available for every choice. A browser zone may help a person locate their zone but does not silently replace the chosen zone, establish identity/location or become the time authority. Editing an existing reminder initially preserves its recorded instant and zone; absent legacy zone provenance is shown honestly rather than inferred from today's settings.

Quick choices derive from one actual trusted server reference instant. Elapsed and calendar arithmetic are different:

1. **In 1/4 hours:** add exactly 3,600/14,400 elapsed seconds, then round the resulting target upward to the next whole minute, with an already exact minute unchanged. Display that resulting date/time/zone. The rounding adds less than 60 seconds and never schedules earlier than the named duration.
2. **Tomorrow / In 1 week at this time:** take the reference's actual local calendar date and displayed HH:mm in the selected zone, add one/seven local dates, and resolve that wall time in the same zone. Seconds are zero. Do not round the reference into the next day first. Calendar choices can differ from 24/168 elapsed hours across offset transitions.
3. **Custom:** choose an explicit local date, time and zone, then resolve it to one unambiguous instant. A new Custom form may show today's selected-zone date with time blank; do not secretly select an hour. An explicit carried candidate or existing reminder retains its value.

After selecting a choice, the candidate is an exact displayed instant. Preserve it while the person reviews and on retries. Do not rerun the relative recipe at commit, on focus, on reconnect or after tenant settings change. No 60-second hard expiry, durable preview table, preview job, reservation lock or viewing-attestation protocol is justified. A valid future absolute candidate is still useful after a minute. A stale relative caption can use anchored wording or the absolute date; offer deliberate **Refresh choices**. New current authorization/state checks still run at admission. A time that has become past must be corrected, never silently postponed.

The representational range may be ISO calendar years 0001–9999, with both selected local values and serialized instants supported by the qualified stack. This is a finite interchange/UI bound, not a promise of an uninterrupted multi-millennial provider run, a 365-day business limit or a retention exception. Newly selected time precision is whole minutes; existing accepted legacy seconds are not rounded merely by opening/editing/displaying a reminder.

The current D3 reminder remains the sole authority. Scheduling/replacement/cancellation admits the exact current component, expected material work/reminder/topology scope, trusted actor, semantic operation identity, intended instant and required history/dispatch intent atomically. Worker execution uses the exact admitted generation and trusted due comparison. No menu interaction creates requester email, a second CRM task or a new target/feedback policy.

## Material findings and exact proposed clauses

Severity is potential impact; likelihood is conditional on an unamended implementation, not a measured incident rate. Each finding narrows or hardens A without rejecting its direction.

### DC01 — Calendar words and elapsed durations cannot share one arithmetic recipe

**Material: Yes. High / demonstrated current mismatch.** S01 uses +16h for Tomorrow morning; fixed elapsed arithmetic also fails local-date promises across offset changes. Rounding a late-night reference upward before determining tomorrow can accidentally add two dates.

> “Resolve duration choices by adding their exact elapsed seconds to the trusted reference and then ceiling the resulting instant to minute precision. Resolve calendar choices from the actual reference-local date and displayed HH:mm before rounding, add the declared one/seven calendar dates, and explicitly disambiguate that local result. At 23:59:59 on 13 September, Tomorrow at this time is 14 September at 23:59 in the selected zone, not 15 September at midnight. Show the exact result before admission.”

**Proof:** Midnight boundary; exact-minute reference; duration rounding below 60 seconds; 23-hour and 25-hour local days; one-week local arithmetic; fractional-hour zones. No silent weekday/holiday adjustment.

### DC02 — Timezone is scheduling context, not identity, calendar policy or a live binding

**Material: Yes. High / plausible distributed staff use.** S11 gives a tenant setting but no qualified general staff preference. Browser, report, SLA and missionary-location zones have different meanings.

> “Initialize from the current tenant's server-qualified default timezone, with visible UTC fallback if absent/invalid and an owned configuration diagnostic. Permit deliberate one-use IANA override. Never infer a person or permission from the zone, import a restricted missionary location, or substitute an SLA/report zone. The chosen zone and input provenance explain the accepted instant; subsequent tenant/device/viewer zone changes do not reschedule it. Opening an existing reminder preserves its accepted instant and recorded zone; unknown old zone remains unknown.”

**Proof:** Tenant change, invalid zone setting, explicit remote-worker override, accepted reminder followed by tenant-zone edit, legacy missing-zone value, current permission loss. An unrelated profile setting is not created by use.

### DC03 — DST ambiguity must be surfaced before the instant becomes authoritative

**Material: Yes. High / realistic boundary, infrequent per affected locale.** A local wall time can map to zero or two instants. Temporal's default compatible behavior chooses a value; that is not proof the staff member intended the adjustment.

> “Require one unambiguous local-to-instant result. For a nonexistent time, preserve the user's input and show the exact proposed valid alternative for explicit acceptance or editing; do not silently normalize it. For repeated time, expose the two offsets/instances with understandable labels and require a choice. If a zone's rules change before new admission so the reviewed mapping no longer agrees, show the changed result and require review. Existing accepted reminders retain their instant unless explicitly rescheduled.”

**Proof:** Gap, overlap, 30-minute offset transition and unsupported zone; exact instant/offset displayed and stored; new zone-rule version cannot silently alter an accepted reminder. A calendar error does not schedule through a generic string parser.

### DC04 — A selected instant should not drift while the person reviews or retries

**Material: Yes. High / likely with relative options and slow connections.** Browser-clock arithmetic in S01/S02 and a recipe re-run at admission can differ from what was displayed. A short hard preview TTL would add needless interaction to valid exact-time choices.

> “Obtain a trusted reference without trusting the browser clock. Unselected offers display their reference-derived result; once selected, preserve one exact absolute candidate until explicit editing/refresh. Do not auto-change choices under hover/focus or recompute the selected due time at save/retry. An aged relative caption uses its anchored or absolute meaning. Revalidate current authority, scope, supported mapping and future time at admission. A now-past candidate requires correction with input retained; no silent move to tomorrow or plus-one-hour. No preview reservation, persistent preview record or viewing-attestation system is required.”

**Proof:** Device clock ±12 hours; chooser open through midnight; tab sleep/reconnect; settings/tzdb update; due crosses while request waits for locks; failure returns the same editable candidate. A 61-second-old but valid absolute candidate is not rejected merely for age.

### DC05 — Finite canonical time validation belongs in the server command

**Material: Yes. High / directly observed weak input validation.** S03 accepts arbitrary nonempty text; PostgreSQL supports infinity and broad formats, while S15's JavaScript converter has narrower assumptions.

> “Use a closed input contract for actual ISO local date, minute time, validated IANA zone and explicit resolution, yielding one finite canonical instant. Reject ambiguous numeric date strings, offset-less instant claims, infinity, nonfinite numbers, invalid dates, unsupported years/offsets and coercion-dependent values. New custom/quick choices support the declared 0001–9999 interchange range with both local and UTC representations valid; reject an offset conversion that exits it. Newly selected time is minute-precision. Do not round already-accepted legacy instants on read or unchanged edit. New admission requires due_at greater than trusted current time sampled at the serialized mutation boundary; existing due work naturally has due_at less than or equal to now.”

**Proof:** Empty/whitespace/garbage, `infinity`, nonexistent date, year 0000/10000, UTC boundary crossing, missing zone, numeric 01/02/03, minute precision, and retained legacy seconds. Do not implement a CHECK constraint `due_at > now()`; finite/type/state constraints are durable, future-ness is admission validation.

### DC07 — Retry reconciliation must precede new-admission future-time checks

> “Reconcile an existing semantic command identity before interpreting past time or advanced generation as a new Schedule failure. Changed semantic input with the same identity conflicts. Only a genuinely new admission applies current future-time validation. **Proof:** Lost save response → due fires → retry; lost cancel response → a colleague creates a new reminder → retry old cancel; changed payload/key reuse; same reminder/receipt after merge/current access change with permission-safe recovery output.

### DC08 — Durable execution must survive executor limits and stale workers

> “Product reminder identity/generation/due instant remain authoritative beyond any workflow run or log lifetime. A bounded indexed product scan must discover current reminders whose due execution or handoff is missing, including already-dispatched runs that later fail. Far-future due values remain product-owned and enter finite supported executor horizons through qualified recovery; do not keep an endless chain of steps in one run or clamp the user's date to the provider limit. At execution, re-resolve current tenant/component/generation/state and compare due <= trusted now atomically. Early attempts remain pending; cancelled/superseded/applied generations do nothing. **Proof:** Crash before/after local commit and dispatch, loss after dispatch acknowledgement, run expiry, duplicate wake, early wake, far-future value outside current plan horizon, executor downgrade/disable/restore, and a full queue beyond a single scan page. ## Logical data and admission shape

This does not freeze a physical table or API field spelling. Reuse D3's current reminder plus transition/command evidence and the existing shared workflow ledger.

| Fact                                | Owner and invariant                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current component/reminder identity | Support current topology and D3; one current shared reminder per continuing component, same-tenant references.                                                                                                |
| Intended due instant                | Finite canonical timestamptz-compatible instant. Current work generation determines applicability; being past does not make an admitted active row invalid.                                                   |
| Chosen zone and input provenance    | Minimal explanatory data: validated zone, quick-choice kind or Custom, actual local date/minute/offset choice when necessary. Not identity, permission, a recurring expression or a live settings dependency. |
| Human scheduling/cancelling actor   | Trusted authenticated profile/current capability at admission; not assignee, email match or supplied actor ID.                                                                                                |
| Due execution actor                 | Explicit qualified system cause and source generation, not a fabricated human or a nil UUID copied into a profile FK. Original human loss of access does not erase genuine work.                              |
| Expected versions                   | Material work/reminder/component/authority control, sufficient to reject conflicting overwrite without using incidental `updated_at` as a universal mutex.                                                    |
| Durable command outcome             | Semantic operation identity, exact normalized payload/result and current-state response separated. Same payload retry reconciles; changed payload conflicts.                                                  |
| Dispatch/recovery intent            | Same local admission, tenant/source/generation-bound identifier-only handoff; executor acknowledgement is not proof due work was applied.                                                                     |
| Historical time evidence            | Original scheduled due, admitted time and actual applied time distinct. Zone/display change or retry cannot rewrite occurrence/history.                                                                       |

For **new admission**, acquire/recheck the required current source guards, sample trusted time after relevant lock waits, validate the reviewed candidate and atomically write the accepted effects. PostgreSQL `now()` reflects transaction start, so it must not silently become a fresh post-wait validation time. For **an already admitted retry**, first recover the compatible original receipt under current disclosure rules; do not run a new schedule validation against the old due time.

The common quick-choice list is code-owned configuration with stable semantic keys. A localized label is not the key, and changing a label/order cannot change accepted times. No user/tenant preset persistence is needed for A. A future personal menu would require its own owner/lifecycle and would still select a shared reminder, but it is not part of D31 A.
