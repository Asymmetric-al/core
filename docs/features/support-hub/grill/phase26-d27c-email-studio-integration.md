## Public path into an actual reply

Staff manually open Public, search qualified selected Page titles/summaries/body, inspect a passive public preview and deliberately insert a link at a valid caret or link selected text. The exact public source, Site/language, generation, destination and current safety are qualified; publication flags, private editor preview, mutable titles or matching URLs do not supply that proof. No private conversation query is automatically sent to the source.

The resulting ordinary accessible link carries frozen visible wording/href and minimal trusted source occurrence metadata in the existing document/preparation contract. It carries no article body or private record data. Text/href edits, Undo, recovery, native recognized copy and D18 reuse keep their accepted purpose/provenance behavior; changing a destination removes the assertion that the new URL is selected Guidance. This is not a universal URL detection or clipboard-policing mechanism.

P17 qualifies and seals the whole actual reply with the existing signature/audience/rendering and narrow Public Guidance material dependency. Support admits that exact reply; P6 submits it under current local material/safety fences. Routine source edits and selection removal do not rewrite composed/prepared/sent content. Same-Page/Site/exact-locale owner continuity may qualify an unchanged older href; a different Page or reclaimed domain cannot inherit approval. Public source withdrawal fences exact definitely-unsubmitted dependents. Possible prior submission reconciles without recall, rerender, rekey or a blind fresh send.

Current public/safety restoration can requalify a safe previously copied link even when its finder selection remains Removed. It does not re-add the selection or automatically revive a fenced/terminal preparation. Current P17 recovery/successor and admission rules remain. An Internal source failure never becomes a condition on these Public link effects.

## Internal consultation creates no message material

Staff choose Internal, read currently published instructions under current tenant Support-read authority, then continue normal work or navigate to an independently authorized owner surface. The reader has no Insert into reply/note, Copy article, Quote section, automatic citation, public conversion or message-purpose switch. An authenticated full-page guide link checks the current source and actor; it is not an access grant and reveals no denied title/body.

The guide is not sent as an attachment, source snippet, reusable reply or prepared message. Therefore it adds **no internal-reference P17 material family, no internal-message retention graph and no guide-availability dispatch dependency**. Merely reading/publishing a guide also creates no staff/customer notification, feedback invitation, work completion or CRM history event. Separately qualified existing staff-attention behavior retains its original owner contract.

D18 Saved notes are still legitimate reusable note wording, distinct from reference procedures. D19's designated inbox reviewers review held incoming work; that designation is not Staff guide publication authority. D26 staff-recorded work remains valid without email/Party and gains no invented requester or message from guide use. Its same authorized CRM detail and explicit later-email path remain intact.

Known internal-purpose documents/imports fail inappropriate external publication boundaries. Validate the original canonical JSON and trusted stored source/command purpose **before** generic Tiptap/ProseMirror normalization can drop unknown attributes. A hidden toolbar or caller-supplied `internal` label is not a protection. The application does not claim to prevent every screenshot, ordinary OS clipboard copy or manually retyped sentence of visible text.

## Source publication and protection

Internal authoring has one current candidate and one current published head. Save draft/autosave changes only the candidate; readers keep the current publication. Publish to staff is a deliberate, current-authorized, versioned command admitting the exact reviewed revision, audit/receipt and required continuation. Publication also places the guide in Internal, without a second selection switch.

Archive stops ordinary serving/search and leaves permitted management custody. Restore, including an older revision, creates a candidate and never silently publishes or replaces current saved/unsaved draft work. Replacement/discard requires a clear reviewed intent. Ordinary archive and safety restriction are independent controls: unresolved protection survives candidates, revisions, restore and ordinary Publish until its actual owner releases it. The same actor may hold the needed capabilities when permitted; no new two-person floor or global review platform is invented.

Delete archived content must re-prove current permission, source generation and applicable retention/hold at the irreversible boundary. It may deny serving while required content stays in restricted custody. Completion reports actual allowed disposal, not just a submitted deletion request. Maintainer departure does not cascade-delete tenant-owned content or history. Existing authorized staff work may handle an actual correction need without creating a customer ticket automatically.

## Retention and independent publication histories

Internal source candidates, current/superseded publications, archived/deleted bodies, derived caches/indexes and body-free evidence each require an explicit owner custody/disposal rule before activation. Reusable instructions do not borrow D17's conversation clock, and reusable purpose does not justify accidental indefinite retention. Applicable records holds and restrictive restore/tombstone behavior prevail over user acknowledgement or ordinary publication authority.

Actual Support message content remains under D16/D17. P17 sealed provider-request material retains its qualified-acceptance purge/finality and body-free later reconciliation. Immutable effect identity/content meaning is not permission to store all bytes forever. **Recent sent copy** continues excluding URLs/action targets, remote references and internal provenance; it is not an archive/resend source for either guidance class. Guide reading, editing, publication, restoration or CRM navigation cannot restart conversation retention or reconstruct disposed content.

Internal titles, body, snippets, counts, history and links remain private operational data, never public CMS/search/API/sitemap/AI content. Public and Internal share UI conventions and safe primitives, not a corpus or read authority. Current source/access/safety heads gate results before paging/counts/snippets, independent of eventual projection purge. Unknown/failed Internal lookup affects that source path rather than unrelated composition or sending.
