# Support retention: what expires and what remains

Ordinary Support content expires under the tenant's approved ended-work policy. Useful facts remain only where their actual owner permits them. This inventory is part of [D17's fully ratified amendment package](phase26-d17-adversarial-review.md); D1–D16 remain ratified. It is a logical ownership contract, not a new physical schema or claim that current tables already enforce it.

## What a CRM user sees

When D9's qualified relevance/correspondence and joint authorization still permit a row, CRM Communications can show:

```text
Support conversation #482
Resolved · Last human message 10 September 2026
Content expired under your retention policy
View permitted history
```

This is an illustrative semantic layout, not a rendered component. Only permitted facts are shown. Do not retain a sensitive subject as its title, add a new Activity event, use expiry as a fresh last-contact date, or expose a source whose discovery authority was withdrawn. An existing Finance receipt is reached through Finance's authorized navigation; the expired Support attachment does not automatically redirect to it.

If new qualified mail later continues this source, the current conversation can contain readable new messages and an older content-expired marker. A source-level generic expired badge must not hide the fact that new content is available. The old message's Reply/Reply all control cannot rebuild lost audience evidence from CRM. New incoming mail supplies a new qualified response audience; deliberate new outbound remains a separate reviewed action.

## Database invariants

The current conversation table combines work state and unsafe display snapshots; current parent deletion cascades into messages, assignments and audit. The permanent design must use content availability and a protected minimal skeleton, not generic parent DELETE. [Support schema](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/supabase/migrations/20260515025814_support_hub_core_modules.sql#L198).

Require one tenant-aware authoritative source identity; validated message/file/frontier relationships; unique policy activation and expiry-effect identities; immutable published policy/effect evidence; trusted actors/times; and controlled delete behavior that preserves unresolved cleanup/custody/barriers. Expired payload fields and serving authority cannot be restored through generic row updates or new policy versions. Logical payload-availability constraints should make an expired generation with a readable current payload impossible through the ordinary mutation/read boundary.

Current source threading queries raw message-header JSON and then sender/subject. Those are observed legacy paths, not endorsed D1 identity behavior. Separate justified correlation from raw payload before enabling expiry; never preserve the entire header blob or unsafe fallback merely to keep current code working. [Current lookup](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/packages/api/src/admin/support-hub/adapter/supabase.ts#L1371).

Keep minimum retained-class policy records: owner, permitted purpose, visibility, applicable horizon, final disposal/anonymization outcome and replay/restore dependencies. Actual values must be qualified before the affected deployment. Source barriers outlive the maximum permitted recovery path they constrain; a shorter ordinary staff-visible history horizon cannot remove the service-only protection prematurely. These obligations are not a universal indefinite archive.

The endpoint policy must obey the current P6 supersession of its older durable-recipient-email wording. [P6 endpoint amendment](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-06-shared-communication-event-model.md#L226). Broader authority remains Asym-owned CRM truth and body-free communication history. [CRM ADR0001](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0001-asym-postgres-owns-crm-truth-twenty-retired.md#L60), [ADR0031](https://github.com/Asymmetric-al/core/blob/7abd2c11ffd4ed70c6775c4fd6f51c996e4350dd/docs/adr/0031-body-free-history-with-expiring-recent-copy.md#L18).

## Privacy and records rationale

FTC guidance supports minimizing unnecessary data and documenting justified retention. IRS guidance requires appropriate supporting records; it does not establish a single lifetime for every Support email. Preserve the owning facts and actual required record rather than all support-side copies. [FTC guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business), [IRS exempt-organization recordkeeping](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/eo-operational-requirements-recordkeeping-requirements-for-exempt-organizations).

D16's [US legal/provider assessment](phase26-d16-us-legal-and-provider-evidence.md) remains qualified and ratified. Applicable law, actual holds and processor contracts govern real deployments; no universal nonprofit exemption, numerical retention period or legal certification is inferred. Every class above must have real owner evidence before Live activation. The inventory is complete as a design contract; no database, storage or provider deletion was executed here.
