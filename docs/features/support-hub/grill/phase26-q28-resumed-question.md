# Q28 — Requester feedback after Support help

**13 September 2026. D1–D27, including every D27-C public/internal Guidance amendment, are fully ratified. Q28 is unanswered.** This resumes the feedback question paused during the Guidance revision. It is about asking requesters how the help went, not choosing public versus internal guidance again.

**How, if at all, should Support invite a brief assessment from the person it helped?**

For example, Elena contacts Donor care for help finding a receipt. Staff assist her and finish the Support work. Asym can record actual correspondence and response timing, but those facts do not establish whether Elena found the help useful. She can always reply normally; the decision is whether to add a dedicated feedback opportunity and what initiates it. This is an illustration, not proof that every ministry needs surveys or follows an identical receipt workflow.

| Option                                                        | What the tenant, staff and requester experience                                                                                                                                                                 | Strongest benefit                                                                                                                        | Main tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — Optional automatic feedback invitations — Recommended** | A tenant explicitly enables a qualified feedback policy. Eligible completed human-handled Support work can generate a short invitation without staff choosing each case. The feature remains Off until enabled. | More consistent opportunities to respond, less recurring staff effort, and less handler-selected invitation coverage.                    | Adds optional outward contact and a real invitation/response lifecycle. Current recipient eligibility, preferences, reopening, repeat prevention and honest interpretation need careful design. Responses are still voluntary and potentially biased. |
| **B — Staff chooses when to ask**                             | Authorized staff deliberately initiate a feedback invitation for a particular conversation. No completion-triggered invitation runs.                                                                            | Best control over timing and context for unusual or sensitive exchanges.                                                                 | Adds a recurring action; requests may be forgotten or selectively sent. It still requires secure response handling, delivery, retention and clear reporting.                                                                                          |
| **C — Ordinary feedback without a dedicated rating flow**     | Add no structured rating invitation or response page in Phase 26. Requesters continue replying with thanks, concerns or questions; staff use those conversations and existing operational reports.              | Least additional contact and the smallest product/maintenance scope; credible where direct relationships already supply useful feedback. | No consistent structured requester assessment. Silence, reply speed or a resolved status cannot stand in for satisfaction.                                                                                                                            |

These are alternative scope/initiation choices, not three modes that must all be built. Ordinary Support replies remain available with every option. No option implies an employee league table, donor loyalty score, mandatory signature survey, NPS campaign, fundraising appeal or AI-inferred sentiment.

**My single best recommendation is A, Off until deliberately enabled by the tenant.** It provides a consistent opportunity to hear from eligible requesters without making each worker remember or choose whom to ask. The policy must use neutral facts about handling/correspondence, never whether staff expect a positive response. Completed human handling is not a verified fix or a satisfied requester. C remains a valid lower-contact operating preference, which A's explicit enablement respects; B is strongest when discretionary asking is the intended model and its selective coverage is understood.

## Evidence behind the comparison

The 13 September refresh found no material vendor change that alters these alternatives. [Front](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2255), updated 7 August 2026, documents manual, automatic and signature approaches. [Zendesk](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408887007386-Workflow-How-to-send-satisfaction-surveys-when-tickets-get-solved), updated 7 August 2026, documents a completion-based invitation with a prior-invitation guard. [Help Scout](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/386-gather-feedback-with-satisfaction-ratings), updated 9 September 2026, instead rates each enabled email reply and requires a final submission on the response page. These demonstrate different initiation/measurement units, not an Asym scale or schedule to copy.

The [fresh research](phase26-q28-resumed-research.md) preserves plan/date caveats and the unresolved discrepancy in Front's teammate-attribution documentation. HubSpot's support survey is CES, not interchangeable CSAT. The recommendation does not depend on a vendor's scoring formula, exact delay or unverified claim about ministry demand.

## What remains unchanged

**D5's ending command alone sends nothing and enrolls nobody.** A would add a separately and explicitly enabled policy with its own qualified effect; B would require a deliberate authorized invitation. Neither guide reading/publication nor clicking Resolve alone proves eligibility or successful help.

**Guidance and feedback have different owners and authority.** D27-C Staff guides remain consultation-only operational instructions. Reader/author/publisher powers do not grant feedback-policy management, access to feedback responses or permission to contact a requester. Internal guides never become invitation wording, a response store, public search or an AI source. Public Guidance links remain their qualified source links, not survey actions.

**Email Studio remains the messaging owner.** If A or B is selected, Support must own feedback purpose/eligibility/response, Email Studio/P17 must own governed invitation wording/action material and immutable preparation, and P6 must own delivery/submission/reconciliation. Existing signatures, saved replies and ordinary URLs do not already provide that feedback lifecycle. A bounded policy need not create or depend on a generic Phase 34 forms/workflow builder.

**CRM continuity uses the same authorized Support detail.** A feedback response must not create a new Party, duplicate sentiment score/history, last-contact fact, business permission or financial action. A shared/forwarded mailbox link does not prove which CRM person responded and must not expose private conversation content. Operational timings, explicit feedback and inferred sentiment stay distinct; nonresponse and small voluntary samples are not reliable staff rankings.

The selected answer's full review will settle the exact question/scale, delay, neutral ending/recipient eligibility, invitation cardinality, repeat/response-change/expiry rules, policy disablement/backfill, reopen/merge/submission races, privacy/retention and accountable follow-up. None is preselected here. Selecting C means no dedicated feedback lifecycle is added.
