## Root synthesis and evidence limits

**C — Present both paths equally** is the revised single recommendation after the founder requested actual visible options and deeper reconsideration. A is strongest with a known suitable existing address, and B with a known new program. The evidence does not justify assuming either starting point for all tenants.
