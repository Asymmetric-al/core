## Concrete safe journey

Maya opens an authorized request from CRM, chooses existing approved wording in the Support composer and edits it. D34 reports only her current authenticated composition intent, qualified for the exact source. Arun can see “Maya Chen is composing” from the same eligible source in Support, but cannot see her wording, recipients, note/reply mode, chosen asset, signature or cursor.

Maya reviews and submits through the existing Support/P17/P6 contract. Only successful reply/note admission ends the cue as an admission event; D34 does not author a Send command, bypass updated-source review or assert provider success. If delivery later fails, the real delivery path exposes it and preserves retry/ambiguity truth. If Arun also submits, D1/D4 still resolve actual concurrency. This example describes a proposed journey, not a verified ministry interaction or current runtime.

The same activity can accompany a deliberate signature/wording insert, but automated metadata refresh, template hydration, draft restore or background AI output does not imply human composition. Notes stay internal and use the same neutral awareness label. A future AI feature must not use the cue to impersonate Maya or obtain her private draft through a hidden integration.

## Required negative assertions

Starting, renewing, stopping, losing or disabling D34 must create **zero** P17 preparation/send review changes, P6 notifications/dispatches, CRM interactions/tasks/contacts, support assignment/status/coverage/Follow transitions, D14 timing mutations, D24 work-plan completions, D33 preference writes or file/public-content actions. Existing draft updates caused by the user's real typing remain legitimate under their owner; the awareness observer adds no duplicate effect.

No cue fact is synchronized back to a CRM person record or stored as a communication. No Email Studio, public asset bucket, workflow queue or AI message is used as the cue transport. Sharing one Core platform means coherent navigation/current authorization and owner-qualified task completion, not unrestricted field visibility or circular synchronization.
