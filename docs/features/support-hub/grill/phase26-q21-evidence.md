## Why labels are the next unresolved choice

D20 deliberately consumes existing labels and settles Any selected, All selected, No labels, stable references and authorized complete queries. It does not decide who may create the vocabulary or make categorization compulsory. D3 purposeful resolution and D15 quiet routine edits remain accepted. Therefore Q21 is a distinct choice rather than reopening saved views, assignment or reply targets.

The no-new-capability alternative is to keep the existing flat label concept and establish its smallest workable policy. A does that with optional use and curated terms. B trades catalog discipline for rapid inline creation. C trades a completion step for coverage. Building a hierarchical taxonomy, automatic classifier, primary-topic field or generic workflow engine would answer additional questions that are not necessary here.

Broader conversation search scope is also a real future gap, but need not be settled first. No source, attachment, CRM-text or AI search expansion is inferred from the label choice.

## Current comparative evidence and what fits

The [independent primary-source register](phase26-q21-vendor-research.md) records nine relevant comparison groups, source dates/edition qualifications, contradictory claims and reported feedback. These vendors are selected for directly relevant mechanisms; marketing breadth is not an Asym feature checklist.

- **Intercom:** currently documents that staff without Can manage tags can still apply conversation tags. This directly supports A's division of ordinary use and vocabulary stewardship. Its visible Conversation tags section and searchable Add tag control support discovery without a separate administration journey for every application. Subscription eligibility is not inferred from the generic permission page. [Permissions](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/176-teammate-permissions-how-to-control-workspace-access), [conversation workflow](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6604447-keep-track-of-support-requests-and-bugs-by-tagging-conversations).
- **Front:** September 2026 new permissions separate create/edit/nest, archive/restore and apply/remove; granular custom permission sets are Enterprise/legacy Scale. Its tag lifecycle distinguishes archival from deletion. Adopt the separation and evaluate safe retirement; omit scope/nesting machinery that does not meet an Asym need. [Permissions](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4645696), [lifecycle](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2100).
- **Zendesk:** current instructions permit a nonexistent tag to be created by typing and pressing Enter, subject to applicable role constraints. Exact formatting variants can fragment retrieval/rules. B therefore has both a real speed advantage and a documented mechanism for inconsistency. Do not copy silent unsupported-character removal or limited suggestions. [Ticket tags](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408835059482-Working-with-ticket-tags).
- **Front compulsory tagging:** Professional+ offers a curated selection before applicable move/archive. Specific FAQ exclusions contradict the rule-library's broad complete-coverage wording: mobile, automatic archive and brand-new Send & archive bypass enforcement. C remains credible, but a mandatory Asym policy must be explicit and enforceable across its actual mutation paths. [Detailed rule behavior](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2106), [broader library claim](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2114).
- **HubSpot:** July 2026 object-tag documentation describes configured criteria-derived badges for supported objects including Tickets, with edition-dependent pipeline scope. This is a different model from manual conversation labels; copying it would duplicate existing status/target facts and introduce another rules surface. [Object tags](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/object-settings/color-coded-object-tags).

Front/Intercom archival and Zendesk field/tag coupling provide concrete lifecycle cautions. Archive preserving history is a candidate, not permission to retain source-restricted data indefinitely or repurpose a name across history. Neither vendor behavior nor UI appearance establishes its undocumented internal architecture.

## Independent challenge and resulting judgment

The [gap challenge](phase26-q21-gap-review.md) identifies the best argument against A: optional labels cannot produce an unbiased complete topic census. C would be stronger if Asym had a demonstrated reporting obligation that depended on universal resolved-topic coverage. User feedback in Intercom's community reports this demand and the friction of inaccurate keyword automation, but is neither prevalence data nor evidence that Asym ministries share it. B would be stronger if real catalog-change latency repeatedly prevented useful categorization.

No representative Asym study, staffing measurement, categorical-reporting mandate or label-frequency analysis was found. **Recommendation A is a product judgment under those known limits**, supported by practical documented patterns and the narrower scope of Support Hub. Do not claim a universally best policy or perfect measured UX. The founder's selection is the next evidence needed; the selected-answer review then resolves exact permissions, safe catalog lifecycle, migration/concurrency and testable interaction requirements without presuming runtime correctness.

For A, the proposed usable minimum is a visible Labels control, searchable eligible terms with plain-language meaning, stable identity, optional application, a maintainer destination and safe retirement. No special approval queue, deep taxonomy, mandatory close-time selection or new email template is needed merely to curate a small vocabulary. Reports must expose unlabelled coverage and overlapping categories. Labels never establish identity, relationship authority, access, targets or a business outcome.

The [D20 Email Studio seam](phase26-d20-email-studio-integration.md) stays fully accepted: configuration is separate from reusable wording, authoring/preparation and tenant Resend delivery. A/B/C add no implicit communication, publishing, audience or provider operation. No Q21 glossary term or ADR0021 is accepted in this evidence record.
