Support may retain optional explicit links to existing same-tenant CRM people, households and organizations as peers. These links mean relevance to the conversation, not sender identity, recipient membership, representation, CRM relationship, ownership or action authority. There is no main designation, first-record authority, mandatory classification or automatic Party creation. Typed business-record references remain with their owners and do not automatically expand this conversation through the CRM graph.

## Consequential boundaries

Support owns a narrow conversation-to-Party relevance fact with canonical tenant-aware endpoints, active uniqueness, current control/provenance and protected durable audit. Each read and mutation proves current Support and Party policy for the exact purpose/resource. Raw browser association/control/audit reads and writes are denied; tenant-only RLS is insufficient to protect hidden linkage. A qualified API/read contract exposes only permitted projections. No JSON identity bag, provider-mapping reuse, shadow customer or copied CRM truth is introduced.

Bounded Add selected commits exact links and evidence together. Retry reconciles the original receipt separately from current state; stale Add/Remove cannot resurrect or erase later generations. Undo reverses only the actual still-current delta, never pre-existing links or a whole historical set. Undo Remove is a fresh permitted generation/time. Owner merge/unmerge preserves origins, deduplicates survivors and respects subsequent edits without merging conversations or guessing mappings from current email.

CRM shows one current authorized Support conversations row when a Party has explicit active related context or independently qualified actual source-message correspondence. Retain both reasons when applicable and deduplicate before paging/counts. Overview previews up to three unfinished rows; View conversations opens the existing Communications capability with a visible Unfinished/All choice. The empty preview says No unfinished Support conversations and retains navigation. Financial support remains separate. Canonical Support detail and the existing CRM shell preserve private drafts, source record and return position.

Activity's P6 Communication branch remains actual source-owned history. Context Add/Remove generates no communication event, ordinary Activity marker, CRM note, contact metric or historical audience fan-out. Qualified association audit supplies link/change evidence. Related-only discovery can disappear on unlink while genuine correspondence remains. Owner attribution correction revises current discovery/Activity under current provenance without rewriting actual endpoints, intent, consent or delivery, creating replacement events, or letting an old projector restore retracted attribution.

A proven common Support message may display as one read group without erasing P6 recipient-copy events or outcomes. Qualify visibility, deduplicate canonical events, group proven source lineage, then paginate groups. Fixed source time/lineage cursors and complete permitted member outcomes prevent page-boundary duplicates and false Delivered labels. Unproved legacy evidence remains individual canonical events. Link/control/delivery-update times are not new contact occurrences.

Linking conveys no private CRM, giving, missionary or member-care access. It cannot choose a refund target, alter prepared Email Studio/Resend material or recipients, transfer assignment or complete a business action. Current owner retention, redaction and permission changes govern projections and payloads. Support projection failure degrades locally without blanking otherwise allowed CRM facts.

## Why this tradeoff

One main Party is smaller but cannot retain all request-specific context. Main-plus-related retains orientation at the cost of an extra classification with no demonstrated authority requirement. Peer relevance retains useful context with less ceremony. The actual sender and explicit action targets provide communication/task orientation instead of inventing a primary.

Copying thread history onto related records is misleading; a separate link-time Activity marker adds noise where Overview/Communications and qualified audit already answer discovery and provenance. Use shared record ownership, not a separate support-to-CRM synchronization system. Existing coarse JSON/notes/donor DTOs do not prove the intended owner boundaries. Accepted Asym-owned CRM ADR0001 wins over stale Twenty OpenSpec wording; reconcile formal text during the later authorized stage.
