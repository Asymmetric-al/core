# Explicit no-response ending and honest awareness

**Status:** Fully founder-ratified, Phase 26 D5, 10 September 2026, including every amendment, adjustment, change and update. This includes D5-R01–R16, P01–P18, all 23 categories, the full awareness blueprint and adopted evidence corrections. D1–D4 remain fully ratified. This is a grooming ADR, not a formal specification or implemented behavior.

## Decision

Authorized staff may deliberately end otherwise eligible follow-up when clearly requested needed input remains absent at a current reviewed point and no actual required Support commitment or actionable unreviewed recovery remains. Use the existing Resolved work state and record No response as that episode's ending reason. This is not proof of no prior messages, successful contact, a fixed problem or completion of another domain's work.

The compact End follow-up review identifies the current need and actual reminder effect, explains that future relevant input can return to review, and explicitly says No email will be sent. Ending alone sends nothing. Optional Write final reply stages the D5-qualified Send and end follow-up action through D4's exact ready preparation and atomic local admission. It does not end work early, change future Send defaults or permit a second automatic closing notice.

## Why this tradeoff

Indefinite waiting is conservative but leaves some no-action work active. A configured timed policy can reduce repeated review but adds eligibility, message, clock and exception costs without demonstrated Asym need. An explicit human ending is proportionate if it preserves commitments, truthful evidence and a visible reason. Requiring a closing email every time would improve consistency of notification but also add unnecessary correspondence and delivery dependence; the chosen optional human path preserves a promised update as an actual obligation.

## Invariants and corrections

Partial answers and acknowledgements invalidate old review but may leave a clearly unanswered part of the existing request without forcing another chaser. Refusal/inability/withdrawal or a new owed clarification cannot be silently treated as no response. A solely drafted or definitely-unsubmitted request cannot qualify; reviewed possibly submitted uncertainty remains honest and does not impose eternal waiting.

One authorized conditional command derives tenant/actor and commits work, episode/reason, history, reminder cancellation and required durable intents together. RLS/grants, service roles, views/RPCs/storage and old macro/bulk/direct writers must preserve that boundary. Known owner facts and accountable human review do not require a promise parser or new task graph.

Reopening keeps past history and clears the past reason from current active-work presentation. Repeated ending is replay/no-op. Historical reason correction binds that episode revision, preserves original endedAt/evidence and cannot re-close or change current work/assignment/reminders; newly exposed obligations create D3 review. Undo is conditional new history, never mail recall or owner-action reversal.

No response is an ending reason, not confirmed success. Metrics distinguish units/cohorts and preserve episode attribution through reopen/reassignment, including explicit legacy unknowns. A local ending is Support history, not another P6 outbound communication or CRM authority. Owner permissions/retention and normal email continuation remain independent.
