# Email continuation with owner-authorized actions

**Status:** Explicitly founder-ratified with all review amendments, Phase 26 D1,
10 September 2026.
This records product direction and inherited safeguards, not an implementation
specification, completed runtime behavior, or permission to activate real inboxes.

## Decision

Phase 26 provides ordinary email continuation with the tenant's support team.
Donors, missionaries, church representatives and other legitimate requesters can
ask for help and read and reply to the ordinary answer without creating an Asym
account, signing in, opening a portal, completing a CRM link, entering a ticket
number, or passing through mandatory self-service. Qualified website/portal Help
entry points retain permitted context. Phase 26 includes no requester My messages
archive. The reply identifies the tenant and uses its governed, activated and
monitored return route.

Email participation, a matched address, a Party link, a thread reference and a
support assignment do not grant identity, representation, protected-record access
or authority to change money. Protected disclosures and consequential actions use
the existing owner's minimally sufficient qualified flow, which may support a
guest, and preserve context. Ordinary conversation does not need that verification
merely because a later action might. Support resolution never proves an owner
action completed.

Staff work in one canonical support conversation with accurate actor attribution,
explicit recipients, structurally private notes, recoverable drafts, safe concurrent
reply decisions and truthful delivery/recovery evidence. Support reads permitted
CRM context and invokes owner commands; it creates no second CRM, synchronization
loop, financial authority, or generic workflow platform. Phase 17 prepares the
bounded human-authored reply; Phase 6 dispatches and records each admitted recipient
copy once, without duplicate capture at the Support hook and send seam.

## Why this choice

The founder chose A to make talking to the tenant feel normal and effortless.
An optional authenticated archive would improve centralized requester history,
but adds a separate historical-disclosure surface and is not required for that
chosen outcome. It remains outside this phase; future access would require its
own explicit authorization rather than automatically exposing old correspondence.
A plain shared mailbox also fails to establish the requested collaboration,
CRM continuity and recovery guarantees. The permanent path is ordinary email
backed by dependable, integrated staff operations.

policy, retention schedule, SLA number, or new automation/AI/channel capability.

before real activation: owner authorization and database enforcement, durable
business-effect idempotency, collision checks, body/attachment recovery, sender
and receiving qualification, note isolation, and accessible staff/requester journeys.
Vendor documentation and passing helper tests are not deployment proof.
