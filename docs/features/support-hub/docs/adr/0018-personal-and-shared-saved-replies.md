## Decision and tradeoff

Accept B with the complete fully ratified D18-R01–R28, P01–P50, all 23 category outcomes, full UX/starter examples/data contract/evidence, every adopted independent correction, five ratified glossary definitions and seven operating controls. ## Complete product and ownership boundary

One scope-aware picker supports complete answers and phrases, instructions, typed owner information, one-use required/optional fill-ins, approved reusable assets, authored language variants, favorites/recent IDs and optional flat categories. Reply and Internal note are independently qualified purposes; later mode switching/Undo cannot carry internal-only provenance into external mail. Notes reuse validation, not P6/email preparation. Ordinary phrasing/tone/length is content, not new Layout Roles or workflow kinds. No general table/blockquote authoring or live nested snippets.

Immutable copy-on-insert preserves drafts and provenance without changing recipients, subject, work, CRM or sent history. Fill-in values belong to one draft insertion and never update CRM/library. Current owner values resolve exact authorized participants/Parties; no first-recipient/related-Party or name-splitting inference. Real financial/document/care mutations remain with their owner. D4/P17/P6 and tenant-qualified Resend own actual external sending.

Shared contribution freezes a reviewed independent candidate with destination-governed file occurrences. Personal edits/deletion/departure do not mutate Shared or break valid Shared assets; real source restrictions propagate. Current audience narrowing covers old revisions/deep links/use, while widening reviews all exposed locale/asset scope. Archive stops new selection, not safe old drafts; safety restriction/disposal and actual provider uncertainty retain their own fences. Tenant custody is explicit and audited, never an implicit grant to shared editors or same-email replacement accounts.

Unsent inserted material remains a draft with source restrictions; admitted replies/notes follow D17. Generic reusable wording has its own actual authoring purpose/retention, not permission to retain a source transcript. Clean creation, no automatic conversation harvest, known-copy D16/D17 propagation, safe native import/export and restore barriers prevent shadow archives. Trusted tenant/member fields, protected heads, grants/RLS USING/WITH CHECK, complete search, safe caches and legacy-writer fencing are activation requirements.
