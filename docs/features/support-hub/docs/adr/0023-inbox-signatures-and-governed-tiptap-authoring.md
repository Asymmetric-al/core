### Accepted durable data, lifecycle and privacy invariants

Asym Postgres and packages/api own authorized mutations. Tenant-aware endpoint constraints, immutable source/publication/copy identity, valid purpose/state combinations and one effective selection per inbox prevent shadow ownership. Actual actor/tenant/audit/publication facts are server-derived. Separate use, authoring, publication/review, inbox binding, public-name maintenance and reply admission capabilities; inspect direct grants, effective RLS USING/WITH CHECK, mutation columns, views/RPCs/definers/service workers and storage together. PostgreSQL's effective-check semantics, not an explicit-clause checkbox alone, determine correctness. Retire broad legacy and raw-HTML writers rather than place a safe-looking editor over them.

Autosave/create/copy/publish/apply/refresh/hide/archive/restore/public-name operations retain owner-specific durable command identity and expected revisions. Replays reconcile original outcomes and cannot resurrect an old default or overwrite newer work. Publication/head evidence and binding/history changes are atomic at their owners; secondary work is idempotent and source-aware. No transaction surrounds provider I/O, and no generic workflow engine is introduced to make these commands safe.

Routine Archive stops new source selection/copy and requires explicit compatible replacement/posture for currently bound inboxes, comparing the complete affected set and revisions atomically. If that set exceeds the registered command bound, authorized managers may deliberately rebind bounded subsets while the source remains active and archive against the complete remainder. Never truncate or report incomplete archival as success. An ordinary source editor without affected-inbox rights cannot silently rebind them. Safety restriction remains independently available without compiling the broken source or waiting for manager coverage. Restore rechecks current scope/schema/assets/privacy and never rebinds old inboxes automatically.

Shared source custody survives staff departure; same-email reuse transfers no identity or ownership. Signature/public-name/source/draft/prepared/admitted/audit/receipt lifetimes remain distinct finite owner purposes. D16/D17/D22 withdrawal and restore barriers apply to current and known derived copies. Today's signature or public profile never replaces historical admitted text or supplies a Recent-copy search fallback. Configuration/draft edits do not change CRM identity, contact history, giving/care outcomes, assignment, reply targets, labels, follows or conversation timestamps. Actual admitted correspondence alone contributes to its qualified history and metrics.
