# ADR-0013: Qualified new-request confirmation through Email Studio and Resend

**Status: Fully founder-ratified, 11 September 2026.** The founder selected A on 11 September 2026 and requested the complete modern-practice, Resend/Email Studio and adversarial review. D1–D12 remain fully founder-ratified. This is exploratory Phase 26 grooming, not formal implementation authority.

## Context

Normal email support has no mandatory portal status page. A short automatic receipt confirmation can reassure a requester, but indiscriminate new-row triggers, inherited Reply-all, duplicate notification engines and stale retries can send misleading or unsafe mail. Core already assigns content/preparation to P17/Email Studio, actual communication and transport to P6, and authoritative CRM facts to their owners. Current raw-template/Support adapters do not prove the intended qualified path.

## Decision

Accept the selected A with the complete fully ratified D13-R01–R26, D13-P01–P38, all 23 category findings, full template/UX/evidence and operational responses in the linked review. New inbox setup recommends New requests; administrators may choose Outside service hours or Off. Saved configuration and qualified Ready state differ; existing inboxes/history are not automatically enabled.

Support owns one immutable original qualified new-intake confirmation decision and recoverable outbox, independent of template/provider success. A server-timed eligibility generation prevents pre-enable or mode-changed delayed inputs from being adopted. One qualified original From mailbox receives the narrowly defined service response; human Reply-all/Reply-To/CC/CRM matching do not confer recipient authority. Automated/unsafe/ambiguous input cannot trigger a response. Ordinary continuations, reopens, merge/undo, internal work and staff-created related conversations are not new triggers.

Use one new qualified P17 Support service-confirmation meaning, an Email Studio **Support request received** starter, the existing **Service message** Role Layout and canonical structured publication/compiler/dependencies. No provider-template or raw-HTML authority, additional editor, automation builder, AI/knowledge answer, marketing content or second CRM. The exact template confirms receipt and normal reply continuation with visible automatic attribution; no human-read, deadline or business-completion promise.

Dispatch promptly without deliberate delay, strictly before qualified original provider receipt plus 15 minutes. Admit at most one new confirmation per tenant/qualified mailbox in a rolling 24 hours, consuming the courtesy slot at final provider crossing; a second legitimate request still reaches staff. These are explicit proposed Asym limits, not vendor SLAs. Header/time/provenance qualification, current contactability and provider/account budgets precede every permitted call. A human public reply to the same recipient in the same handled request wins if it crosses first; a later qualifying human dispatch also ends further automatic calls. Off/Pause/source revocation preserve in-flight truth and reconciliation but end further send authority.

The tenant's composed Resend connection/sender/monitored original return route is frozen through P17/P6. Adapter-owned auto-replied/thread headers and real RFC Message-ID are qualified separately from Resend resource IDs. ADR0032 permanent occurrence/envelope semantics and its two-follow-up-call ceiling remain unchanged, further bounded by the 15-minute source utility. No post-crossing rerender, rekey, fallback, changed account or expired replay is permitted. The CLI supports synthetic diagnostics and is not the product sender.

Automatic history uses one P6 occurrence and original Support link, labelled **Automatic confirmation** with actual outcomes. Recent sent copy is Off-only; no generated body is permanently copied into human Support message storage. Approved-design preview is synthetic, not the actual sent copy. Source and CRM authorization intersect; no Party or fake correspondence is created. Activity requires actual recipient/member Party attribution. Admission/success/expected no-send do not affect work or human-response metrics; genuinely actionable adverse evidence still follows D3 review.

Protected source/decision/intent/budget/control/audit data denies raw reads and writes to callers, with tenant-aware constraints and owner-filtered PDP projections. Implement source/adapter/contactability qualification and additive writer/reader rollout before explicit activation; keep intake, ordinary staff replies and reconciliation working when this purpose is stopped.

## Consequences and complete record

The tradeoff is one extra automatic email for eligible new requests; source/freshness/courtesy/human-reply gates can skip it. The 24-hour cap includes shared mailboxes and requests to other inboxes in the same tenant, without treating them as the same person or request. Requester work is preserved regardless. Privacy and recovery remain bounded without an enterprise workflow engine.
