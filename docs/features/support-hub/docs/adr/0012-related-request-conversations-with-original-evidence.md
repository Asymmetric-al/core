## Why this shape

One coordinated conversation remains simpler and appropriate when one handler/work plan can give a coherent answer. D11 handles an internal specialist step; D10 handles duplicate requests. Independent requester work sometimes needs its own follow-up, but vendor-style message movement, a child-ticket hierarchy or copying a transcript would compromise source history and correction. A narrow related-work relation plus qualified source references gives independent work without another CRM or generic workflow engine.

New staff-origin work has no native messages/times/direction until real correspondence occurs. Its creation time and trigger's actual received time are distinct. The existing inbound creation/mapping helpers cannot be reused unchanged: they stamp inbound dates and can fabricate present-time hydration for absent dates. New source kinds require their own protected compound admission through the existing owner boundary.
