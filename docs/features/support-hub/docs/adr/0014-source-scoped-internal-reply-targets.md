# ADR-0014: Source-scoped internal reply targets

**Status: Fully founder-ratified, 11 September 2026.** The founder selected A—Internal reply targets on 11 September 2026 and requested the complete target, quiet guidance, reporting, UX and adversarial review. D1–D13 remain fully ratified. This is exploratory grooming authority, not a formal specification or implementation.

## Context

Shared queues and deliberate reminders are useful, but optional internal reply targets can make waiting responses easier to prioritize. Current SLA fields, seeded defaults, browser clocks and reports do not establish correct source/recipient/timing/history semantics. A global first-response flag or one timer on a merged conversation would mis-handle several correspondents, pending email evidence and continued work. Copying public SLA/enterprise rule features would exceed the selected need.

## Decision

Accept A with the complete fully ratified D14-R01–R30, D14-P01–P55, all 23 category findings, full UX/reporting/evidence, twenty-one adopted independent corrections and nine operational responses in the linked record. Configure optional First/Next reply targets per inbox with deliberately chosen positive hours/minutes and explicit qualified Service hours or Elapsed time. No seeded deadline, resolution timer, Due soon threshold, priority matrix, public promise, per-target notification family or second workflow engine is introduced.

Support owns original/correspondent reply periods, immutable source contributions and reviewed coverage, pinned policy/calendar bundles and current reply-work-home receipts. At most one segment per original/correspondent is collecting; sealed transferred work and unresolved coverage candidates can coexist. Same-correspondent bursts keep oldest unanswered age, different correspondents get their own starts, and no scope implies CRM identity or recipient authority. Only actual reviewed human reply coverage and P6 accepted-recipient evidence fulfill an obligation. Logical First/Next sequencing uses trusted Core human-send/input admission validated by P6 acceptance; receipt time remains the duration start. Unknown send outcomes retain conservative candidate deadlines and cannot silently hide a short next-reply target.

P6 owns acceptance/delivery evidence and corrections, with conservative time intervals where exact acceptance is unavailable. Accepted-for-delivery is not delivered/read. Fulfilled coverage ends reply-owed work even if historical scoring remains uncertain; genuine delivery recovery still follows D3. No module sends mail to repair a metric, and D13 automatic confirmation earns no human response credit.

Count service time over the union of actual instants in the pinned local schedule with IANA zone and qualified timezone/calculator revision. Gaps contribute no nonexistent time, repeated included intervals both count and overlaps do not double-count. Freeze existing deadlines across ordinary edits; bind delayed input to its applicable source-time revision rather than the latest setting. Off explicitly withdraws unfinished applicability created by its configuration family across homes while retaining known misses and exact cutoffs. Before/after cutoff acceptance and uncertain cohort membership remain auditable; no replay or new source age is invented.

Display one neutral Reply due/Reply overdue cue, with confirmed-overdue attention precedence and progressive uncertainty/source detail. At target due, a narrow D3 extension creates one Open review while preserving a valid later reminder and overriding deferral for visibility. Existing D7/D8 coverage/restrictions apply. An explicitly reviewed valid plan prevents repeated reopening for that same miss. No reply needed is a current exact-input disposition, independent of target enablement; it preserves other work and cannot hide promises or turn a late cleanup into success. Persistent Undo is a new source-fenced correction, not snapshot restoration or re-enablement of Off targets.

D10 merge/Undo projects current original obligations without resetting history. D12 exact whole-obligation continuation changes the current work home and seals transferred input; later native old-route input remains local until explicitly referred/transferred. A B-source reply can fulfill transferred A work only through live reviewed coverage and actual recipients, while remaining one genuine B-source P6 communication. Cancellation rehomes still-owed work before retirement.

Reports separate current all-age waiting work, finalized targets due in a period, potential unconfirmed target membership, and waiting periods ended by a reply in a period. They name units/denominators, preserve open misses, exclusions, uncertainty and actual source/custody/responder dimensions, and use median/p90 with exact measured sample counts. One email can end several waits; no-data is not zero/100%, and favorable completed durations do not hide unanswered work. Current source/PDP and CRM authorization apply before count, pagination, export or navigation; target changes create no fake CRM correspondence or business completion.

Use protected tenant-aware owner tables/constraints, raw SELECT/DML denial with authorized projections, durable conditional commands/outboxes, indexed due queries and bounded interval arithmetic. Qualify old writers, source evidence, live P6/member and calendar semantics before additive tenant-gated activation; preserve current visibility/recovery under stop/rollback. No body-heavy report hydration, new content archive or unrelated product ownership is introduced.
