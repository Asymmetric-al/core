### 1.1 Is automatic merge permitted anywhere?

**No accepted identity-owner source permits automatic merge of Parties/donors.** Evidence:

- ADR-0216 rejects "Automatic or cross-Tenant merge ... because match confidence is not human judgment or same-Tenant source authority" (`docs/adr/0216-non-destructive-source-governed-replayable-donor-merge.md:54-55`).
- Phase 4 A9: "Merge is non-destructive, reversible, replayable, **never automatic**" (`phase-04...md:185`); user story 30 (`:107`); dedupe "never auto-merges" (`:222`); acceptance "merges never ... auto-run" (`:399-400`); "merge-suggestion automation" out of scope (`:369`).
- Phase 1 matrix: "Asym; merges never auto-run" (`phase-01-source-of-truth-ownership-matrix.md:48`).
- CONTEXT.md "Non-destructive merge / golden record": _Avoid_ "automatic merge" (`CONTEXT.md:1623-1628`).
- Phase 9 out-of-scope: destructive merge deferred, replaced by the P4 non-destructive contract (`phase-09...md:989-991`).
- Phase 25 identity contract: an identity attached elsewhere "is a conflict, never automatic transfer, merge or deletion" (`phase-25.../contracts/identity.md:47`).
- P21/P22 amendments in Phase 4: Party merge never unions grants or transfers assignments (`phase-04...md:491-495, 522-525, 560-565`).

**Contrary (older) text:** `owner-constraints.md:366-372` ("match-confidence review queue (auto-merge high band only, human review middle band)") and P28 OA-01's reference to "high-band auto-merge and stable-ID bulk-update rules" of ordinary staff import (`phase-28.../owner-reconciliation.md:47-48`; echoed `owner-constraints.md:349-351`). The newer roadmap chapter rewrites it as "Any automated merge must already be permitted by the identity owner" (`roadmap.md:4351`) — and the identity owner (P4/ADR-0216) does not permit it. Also note `openspec/contracts/crm-core.md:125-127` only says _low-confidence_ matches MUST NOT auto-merge; it is silent on high-confidence, which a reader could mis-take as permission. Unrelated: ADR-0033 "Eve strict auto-merge" concerns GitHub PR merges, not CRM identity (`docs/adr/0033-eve-strict-auto-merge-policy.md:14-16`).

### 1.2 Exactly how ADR-0216 constrains Phase 30

From `docs/adr/0216-non-destructive-source-governed-replayable-donor-merge.md`:

1. Merge is **staff-deliberate, same-Tenant, field-by-field golden record** (`:17-18`). An import row cannot itself be the merge decision; at most the plan may _queue_ a candidate for the P4 workbench (`roadmap.md:4349` lists "queue for merge").
2. **Consent combines to the most restrictive state, including later restrictions** (`:18-19`). An import resolving to an existing Party cannot loosen consent (consistent with P28 REQ28-R22, `relationships.md:417-426`).
3. **Tombstone + replayable `merge_operations`** (`:19-21`). Phase 30 reversal must not delete a real Party after merge (`roadmap.md:4371`; `owner-constraints.md:384-385` Planning Center hazard). Import undo must defer to P4 governed Undo, not row deletion.
4. **Only source-approved mutable identity/CRM references are repaired**; frozen contribution/legal-donor facts, issued receipts/statements, posted accounting rows, original commitment-owner snapshots and **provenance** remain unchanged (`:24-30`). Import batch provenance on a merged-away record must survive; imported historic gifts keep their frozen legal donor.
5. **Owner transfer is never a merge consequence** (`:32-36`) — an import cannot "fix" a commitment owner by merging.
6. **Undo is governed, not an unconditional inverse**; it checks current source revisions and later restrictions (`:38-40`). A batch undo that crosses a merge must go through governed Undo and reject unsafe restoration.
7. **Unknown references block unsafe repair or purge** (`:41`); shell purge only after complete source census (`:41-44`). Import-created references in tables not yet in the census would block purge — Phase 30 targets must register their reference families with P4.
8. **Automatic or cross-Tenant merge rejected** (`:54-55`).
9. Status caveat: "records accepted target intent ... not installed storage, implementation, qualified Undo or activation evidence" (`:5-6`).

### 1.3 Email-as-identity and claim rules an importer must respect

- Imported email is **unverified contact data**, never claim, possession or access: "Staff capture stores an **unverified contact email** on the donor and creates no login" (`phase-04...md:152`); `CONTEXT.md:1608-1614` (Claim: _Avoid_ "email match as ownership").
- Migration of claim/contact proof "only from trustworthy provenance, not equal email, current Auth verification, last activity ... Unknown/ambiguous data stays unresolved without leaking another Party candidate" (`phase-25.../contracts/identity.md:149`); Phase 4 §D: "migrations classify known proof provenance and leave ambiguous records unresolved rather than backfilling claim from equal emails" (`phase-04...md:250-253`).
- Ratified key: "partial unique on `lower(email) WHERE merged_into_donor_id IS NULL AND email IS NOT NULL` ('one unclaimed donor per email per tenant')" plus one canonical email-normalization function (`phase-04...md:268`). This collides with shared couple/household emails in legacy files (see discrepancies). P9 A7 keeps `donors.email` as "the claiming/consent key" and rejects contact normalization now (`phase-09...md:396-397`); multiple emails/phones per person (`contact_points`) remain reserved (`phase-04...md:357`).
- Every spouse/teammate "retains a distinct Party and principal. Party merge never treats a relationship or shared email as proof of one login" (`phase-04...md:491-495`); "Household membership, job title, shared email ... grant no access" (`phase-25.../contracts/identity.md:19`).
- Legacy invitations for imported donors are separate, staff-issued, branded, single-use; "Bulk reserved" (`phase-04...md:243, 368`; `CONTEXT.md:1616-1621`). An import never sends invitations (`roadmap.md:4365` "no ... account grant").
- Directory/staff identity: imports "do not create staff membership, reactivate revoked grants or merge principals by email" (`roadmap.md:4381`).

## 2. External / source ID model

### 2.1 What is specified

There is **no single accepted general external/source-ID model** for Phase 30 staff imports. What exists is a set of partial, overlapping rules:

1. **Provider-link principle (ratified):** "a provider may _execute_ an action ... Asym links the provider object by ID (the `crm_record_links` pattern, generalized to Stripe/Mailchimp links). A provider ID is a **link, not an identity** — losing or re-pointing it must never change who a record _is_ or what money _happened_." (`phase-01-source-of-truth-ownership-matrix.md:106-113`; `CONTEXT.md:2784-2789` "Provider link (record link)"). OpenSpec: "A provider identifier is stored ... it is treated as a reference AND Asym Postgres remains the authoritative record" (`openspec/specs/crm-core/spec.md:169-174`).
2. **Link metadata (ratified, OpenSpec binding contract):** "each link MUST preserve the source concept, source id, tenant, confidence, status, verification state, and repair metadata" (`openspec/contracts/crm-core.md:122-124`, referenced by `openspec/specs/crm-core/spec.md:38-42`).
3. **Imported/legacy provenance carrier (ratified, Phase 9):** "Imported/legacy provenance is a Phase 30 concern carried by `crm_record_links`, never a party_kind or status" (`phase-09...md:846-847`); External IDs/provider links record-shell socket "(P30/P31, over `crm_record_links`)" (`phase-09...md:433-434`); "Full import tool -> Phase 30 (External-IDs socket reserved; provider-link registry pairs with Phase 31)" (`phase-09...md:986`).
4. **Entity-type enum scope (ratified, Phase 4):** `crm_link_entity_type` remains for "generalized provider links only"; household/organization/... values "added only if/when a provider link actually needs them" (`phase-04...md:266`).
5. **Roadmap Phase 30 obligation (adopted scope):** "Preserve external IDs, source system, batch identity, occurrence and recorded times, historical/cutover disposition and coverage. Reimport converges on the same permitted target identity" (`roadmap.md:4363`); "Stable external/source IDs on all importable records ... Every imported financial root also pins exact Tenant, Legal Entity, currency, source system, and stable external ID. None is inferred from Site, provider, or a default entity" (`owner-constraints.md:373-377`).
6. **The only explicit scope tuple (ratified, P28 D17 profile only):** "Qualified external IDs bind Tenant, exact target/workspace, source instance, entity kind and established source binding. Reordered/changed files without stable qualified IDs require authorized review; no perfect deduplication claim" (`phase-28.../relationships.md:400`; AC28-R21-02 `:407`). Also: file hash/row number/tool name "alone is never person identity" (`:406`).
7. **Collection items (ratified, Phase 11):** `party_collection_items` carries an `item_external_id` column (`phase-11-custom-fields-custom-collections.md:137`) — the only target-level external-ID column designed in an owner PRD. Its scope/uniqueness rules are not specified.
8. **Owner-specific source identity (ratified elsewhere):** Phase 21 owns its own source identities and mapping admissibility for openings and card imports; "General fuzzy matching or auto-merge is forbidden for Phase 21 D17 financial identity" (`owner-constraints.md:398-403`); Phase 27: "Imports, merges and missing metadata SHALL NOT confer rule-managed status" (`phase-27-donor-development/assignments.md:112-113`).

**Not specified anywhere I found:** a general (non-D17) tuple for (Tenant, source system, source _instance_, entity type, _purpose_) uniqueness; whether one source ID may bind to several Asym records (e.g., a SiteStacker "household" row that becomes household Party + two person Parties); what happens to a binding when its target is merged (the P4 A9 re-point list includes `crm_record_links` — `phase-04...md:185` — so bindings follow the survivor, but uniqueness collisions after re-point are not addressed); and whether the "purpose" of a link (provider execution vs import provenance vs reconciliation) is a column.

### 2.2 What is implemented (integrated source on develop `629cb71dc`)

- `public.crm_record_links` exists but is **Twenty-shaped**: `crm_provider TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'twenty'`, `twenty_object_name TEXT NOT NULL`, `twenty_record_id TEXT NOT NULL`, `asym_entity_type crm_link_entity_type`, `asym_entity_id TEXT`, `relationship_type DEFAULT 'represents'`, `link_status` (active/suspected_duplicate/merged/archived/rejected), `confidence NUMERIC(5,4)`, `verified_at/by`, `metadata JSONB` (`supabase/migrations/20260508000413_crm_identity_mapping.sql:73-90`); unique indexes keyed on `(tenant_id, crm_provider, ..., twenty_object_name, twenty_record_id, ...)` (`:95-121`). RLS enabled (not FORCE in this file), staff select/insert policies (`:200-218`).
- The Twenty-only CHECK was dropped and the table re-commented as "Tenant-scoped leftover provider links ... Historical Twenty record ids may remain as retired references; they are not CRM truth" (`supabase/migrations/20260818010000_retire_twenty_crm_runtime.sql:21-34`). Prod/dev had 0 rows on 2026-08-18 (`:1-8`).
- Enum gained `household`, `organization` (`supabase/migrations/20261024120000_crm_link_entity_type_party_values.sql:15-16`), but "no table, trigger or link-write path uses the new labels yet" (`:5-7`); TS mirror `packages/api/src/crm/identity/concepts.ts` with parity test (`:10-13`).
- **No** `source_system`, `source_instance`, `external_id`, `legacy_id`, `source_record_id`, `import_batch`/`import_run` columns exist in any other migration (targeted grep of `supabase/migrations`, 2026-10-31, zero hits outside the link migration). In `packages/api/src`, `sourceSystem` occurs only as a display label ("Asym CRM", "Asym finance summary") in `admin/crm/relationships/model.ts:62-520`.
- `donation_crm_links` exists (constraint dropped in the retirement migration, `20260818010000:27-31`) — a gift-to-CRM link, Twenty-era.

**Conclusion:** the ratified carrier (`crm_record_links`) is integrated only as a renamed-in-comment Twenty table; it has no generalized source-system/instance column. Generalizing it (or adding a separate import-binding store) is unratified schema work. ADR-0001 and the retirement design say do not "drop generalized command/merge/link records or reinterpret historical identifiers as live Twenty ownership" (`openspec/changes/complete-twenty-crm-retirement/design.md:25-28`).

## 3. Party, household, organization, church, relationship, assignment, interaction semantics

### 3.1 Party kinds (Phase 9, ratified; storage not integrated)

- "`party_kind` is TEXT+CHECK over `{person, household, org}` with `'group'` reserved-not-built; canonical value name is **`person`** (not `individual`). Church is **not** a kind: `kind='org'` + `org_type in {church, school, foundation, business, daf_sponsor, partner, agency, ...}`" (`phase-09...md:330-338`; `CONTEXT.md:2204-2211`).
- Shared-PK subtypes: a subtype row IS its party (`phase-09...md:330-332`; `CONTEXT.md:2500-2504`).
- `donors.party_id` NOT NULL composite FK "born correct" (no backfill); `donors.type`, `donors.organization`, `donors.spouse`, `donors.notes` "never carried"; `donors.missionary_id` dropped (`phase-09...md:340-351, 770-773`). Integrated reality: the legacy flat `donors` table still has `type DEFAULT 'individual'`, `organization`, `spouse`, `notes`, `address`/`work_address` JSONB etc. (`supabase/migrations/20250101000000_init_schema.sql:75-97`). No `parties`/`households`/`org` subtype tables exist; only the inert `persons` anchor + nullable `person_id` on missionaries/profiles (`supabase/migrations/20261025110000_person_anchor.sql:36-117`). Party spine issue #570 is OPEN (gh, 2026-10-31).
- Importer consequences: a source "type = Individual/Organization/Church/Household" column maps to `party_kind` + `org_type`, never to a status or free-text column; lifecycle is `active|inactive|archived|deceased` with `merged` read from the tombstone (`phase-09...md:759-762`); "Imported/legacy provenance ... never a party_kind or status" (`:846-847`); no new inline contact columns on parties (migration lint) (`:763-765`).
- D17 profile: "no universal email/location/Party-kind requirement" (`roadmap.md:4355`; `relationships.md:363`).

### 3.2 Households and couples

- `household` IS a party_kind; `household_members` grandfathered "for that one kind forever (receipt-compliance construct — NOT the template)" (`phase-09...md:400-407`). Household membership is a **derived** edge (view), not a stored relationship row (`:829`). Phase 4: households are "a separate _party_ entity (never an account that absorbs people)" (`phase-04...md:366`).
- Spouse is a person-to-person stored edge (`phase-09...md:350, 833`), not a column.
- "Every spouse or teammate retains a distinct Party and principal" (`phase-04...md:491`); "Household membership ... shared email ... grant no access" (`phase-25.../contracts/identity.md:19`); a `household_members` row grants zero portal visibility (`phase-09...md:920`).
- D17: "Do not split couples, infer a country, fabricate lost digits or call imported assertions verified" (`relationships.md:385`; AC28-R20-04 `:390`).
- **How a source couple/household row should map is not ratified for staff import.** Candidate mappings (household Party + 2 person Parties + 2 membership rows + spouse edge vs one person Party with a couple display label) are an open product choice. Constraint: the integrated `donors_tenant_email_normalized_uidx` UNIQUE `(tenant_id, email_normalized)` (`supabase/migrations/20261024140000_identity_email_normalizer_and_donor_keys.sql:162-164`) means two donor rows in a Tenant cannot hold the same normalized email — a shared couple email can sit on at most one donor row. The PRD's intended partial key ("WHERE merged_into_donor_id IS NULL", `phase-04...md:268`) is not what is installed: the migration comment says "The email key counts merged donors too (Q7); merge execution (#512) owns that case" (`20261024140000:19-21`).

### 3.3 Relationships

- One canonical, time-bound, never-hard-deleted edge per relationship; partial unique on active `(from, to, type)`; no mirrored pairs (`phase-09...md:353-367`; `CONTEXT.md:2506-2512`).
- Seeded type catalog; "derived kinds are rejected from the stored catalog by **CHECK + API rejection + test**"; types edited "via migrations/seed only" in P9 (staff catalog UI is P11/12) (`phase-09...md:369-376`). An importer cannot invent relationship types; unknown types need mapping to seeded types or quarantine.
- Derived (never importable as edges): supports (from the giving ledger, `supports_policy_v1`), household membership, org contacts (`phase-09...md:378-386, 826-830`). A legacy "supporter of missionary X" column is evidence for gifts/commitments, not an edge.
- Gift-level facts are never party edges (DAF recommendation is P14 territory) (`phase-09...md:844`); advocacy, events/trips, team/region are deferred to other phases (`:839-843`).
- Merge: edges re-point, active collisions deduped, self-edges eliminated, recorded in `merge_operations` (`phase-09...md:409-415`).
- Relationship role lives on the edge, never a party-role table (`CONTEXT.md:2520-2524`).

### 3.4 Assignments (staff responsibility)

- Staff-assignment family = stored edges (`donor_rep`/`regional_rep`/`church_relations`/`mobilizer`), N role-qualified assignees + one primary; duplicate active role-qualified edge forbidden (`phase-09...md:358-367, 493-510`; `openspec/contracts/crm-core.md:58-60`).
- "Manual choices, deliberate unassignment and unknown imported provenance remain protected" (`phase-09...md:505-506`); "Imports, merges and missing metadata SHALL NOT confer rule-managed status" (`phase-27-donor-development/assignments.md:112-113`); "Unknown imported provenance remains protected until deliberately qualified ... Backfills are resumable/idempotent and cause no transfers, notifications or access changes" (`assignments.md:315-318`); "Imports/backdates preserve occurred-at precision separately from recorded-at and do not invent past rule effects" (`:251-252`).
- Assignment grants no access (`openspec/contracts/crm-core.md:60`); Ministry Assignment and Support Assignment memberships are separate owner facts that merge never unions (`phase-04...md:491-495, 560-565`).

### 3.5 Interactions / notes / activity

- P9 owns interaction/encounter identity and source records; P27 owns development meaning (`phase-27-donor-development/context.md:7`). "two narratives/retries/imports count one encounter" (AC27-C12-01, `context.md:164`). Recorded personal exchange excludes outreach-only and record maintenance (`CONTEXT.md:2648-2655`).
- Imported history is not shareable content: "Existing private notes, imported history ... SHALL NOT be automatically copied, relabeled, migrated or summarized into this lane" (`context.md:31`); P9 safety tier: the D26 exception "cannot relabel, import, migrate or summarize protected notes into shared content" (`phase-09...md:924-926`).
- Notes are party-keyed with `standard|restricted` visibility (`phase-09...md:786-797`); `member_care_private_notes` stays RLS-revoked and excluded from timeline (`:444-446, 913-914`). Integrated: `crm_notes` table exists (`supabase/migrations/20260818000000_crm_notes.sql`) and `createMissionControlCrmNote` (`packages/api/src/admin/crm/notes/service.ts:167`).
- Activity events carry `actor_kind CHECK (human|system)` (`phase-09...md:799`); the timeline is composed at read, never persisted (`:577-579`).

### 3.6 Restricted preparation and hidden-match non-disclosure

- Restricted start / restricted preparation: organization-owned early prospect preparation not visible to relationship staff before deliberate sharing; "It is not a fake Party, second personal CRM or supporter enrollment" (`CONTEXT.md:2213-2224`; `owner-reconciliation.md:26-31`).
- Self-import target: "P30's closed D17 self-import profile admits the permitted missionary as reviewer and committer only for their own restricted-preparation target, through P9/P4 commands and current P12 grants ... ordinary staff import, high-band auto-merge and stable-ID bulk-update rules cannot widen this profile" (`owner-reconciliation.md:42-50`).
- Non-disclosure: "Apply authorization and safe-field projection before enumeration, search, counts, ordering, paging, serialization, recovery, export ... Hidden and absent subjects must not form an existence oracle" (`relationships.md:11`); "Hidden matches cannot alter disclosed counts, suggestions, detail links, result shape or repair routes into an existence signal" (`:66`; AC28-R03-02 `:73`); "Hidden matches do not leak through candidates, accepted/reused/unchanged outcomes, downloads or target references" (`:404`; AC28-R21-04 `:409`). ## 5. Sensitive-data and permission rules for source rows, previews, counts, error downloads and match candidates

### 5.1 Integrated permission floor

- `public.field_policies` (static ceiling) exists with five surfaces: `mission_control`, `donor`, `missionary`, `public`, `export` (`supabase/migrations/20261005050728_field_policy_foundation.sql:8-21, 44-49`; counts by grep 2026-10-31). **No `import` surface row exists**; per ADR-0209 and Phase 3 A1, an unregistered surface fails closed ("absent rows deny", `20261005050728:21`; `docs/adr/0209-static-field-policies-and-subtract-only-projection.md:16-20`; `phase-03-minimum-permission-role-scoped-projection-foundation.md:104, 112`).
- Integrated staff capabilities are coarse: `admin.dashboard.access`, `admin.crm.manage` ("Create and edit people, households and CRM records"), `admin.gifts.manage`, `admin.reports.view`, plus contributions.* and permissions.* (`packages/auth/permissions.ts:10-14`; `packages/auth/capabilities/registry.ts:21-123`). **No import, merge, dedupe, custom-field (`classify:manage`) or security-clearance capability exists.**

### 5.2 Ratified rules (not implemented unless stated)

- **One PDP, subtract-only:** every read/write/export goes through `resolveProjection`; "The resolver gates filter/sort/group-by _inputs_ too" (P11 C4 `:163`); Phase 3 A2/A4 (`phase-03...md:105-107`).
- **Import plans are protected artifacts (Web Studio contract, but the only OpenSpec text naming import plans):** "Private export artifacts, import plans ... SHALL each require current purpose-specific read/action capability and exact Tenant/resource scope before enumeration, count, signed retrieval or diagnostic composition. Every export download SHALL reauthorize and record a receipt." (`openspec/contracts/identity-and-access.md:350-354`). Phase 23 separates `import check` and `import commit` capabilities (`openspec/contracts/identity-and-access.md:418`).
- **Import role never widens target authority:** "possessing an artifact digest, operation ID, manifest ID, Field Account role, QBO/Xero connection, or Phase 30 import role never widens it" (`phase-12-full-role-permission-configuration.md:2249`; also `:2327`). P28: "parsing or upload permission cannot authorize arbitrary target commands" (`owner-constraints.md:337-338`).
- **Non-human principals / background jobs:** "A single-Tenant NHI MUST remain bounded by its own grants intersected live with its required human owner's current resolved capabilities ... Deferred NHI/operator work MUST ... re-prove current identity, owner ceiling or operator grant, scope, purpose and capability at fire time" (`openspec/contracts/identity-and-access.md:218-220`); P12 principal class (3) NHI: required human owner, live intersection, single-tenant, force-disabled when owner leaves (`phase-12...md:1552`); "A worker or Inngest function may execute an exact already-authorized audit export only after fire-time reauthorization" (`phase-12...md:480-482`); system schedulers "use separately registered service-principal capabilities ... never a staff-role shortcut" (`phase-12...md:27-29`). For Phase 30's Inngest batches this means each chunk re-proves the initiating authority at fire time (an inference from these general rules; no Phase 30-specific ratification).
- **Restricted-tier people:** `security_level in {standard, sensitive, restricted, high_risk}` (`phase-10-sensitive-data-safety.md:96`); "an API/import that sets a restricted level without an alias is **rejected**" (`:97`, test `:174`); "dedupe/merge of restricted-tier parties is **clearance-gated** (name-matching needs the legal name)" and merge takes the strictest `security_level` (`:122`); legal name/real country/location/photo live in `party_restricted` (`:99`); locked-room fields redacted from telemetry/logs/support tooling/demo seeds (`:102`). P12: "Restricted-worker records are non-exportable by default ... bulk contact/export across a restricted set is blocked outright" (`phase-12...md:1641`); hard-deny restricted read/export budget (`:1663`).
- **Member care / restricted notes:** `member_care_private_notes` RLS-revoked; excluded from search index and snippets (`phase-09...md:444-446, 568-571`).
- **Export governance applies to egress files:** `csvSafeCell` formula-injection neutralization + consent gate + identifiers-only audit (`phase-03...md:71, 109`); P11 governed CSV (`:181`). Annotated error downloads re-emit source values, so they are an egress; no ratified rule states that explicitly for Phase 30 (inference).
- **Mapping assistance boundary:** "imported PII never leaves its governed boundary merely to improve mapping"; later AI uses Phase 40's suggestion ledger and human-commit contract (`owner-constraints.md:359-363`).
- **Counts and non-disclosure:** P28 rules (hidden matches cannot alter counts/suggestions/result shape; outcomes/downloads disclose no hidden record; counts "describe rows, never automatically unique people") (`relationships.md:11, 66, 404, 435`). P12/P33 small-cell suppression is named for reporting (`phase-12...md:13`).
- **Identity facts in import:** imported email/phone are unverified contact data; "A contact number is not login/MFA/recovery enrollment, SMS consent, verified possession or a channel preference" (`phase-25.../contracts/identity.md:95`); imported subscribed/active flags grant no opt-in (`relationships.md:417`); historic phones and provider subscription flags are "evidence to classify, not automatic SMS consent" (`roadmap.md:4381`).

### 5.3 Unspecified for staff import

Who may (a) upload/see raw source rows, (b) see previews containing existing-record values, (c) see match candidates that include restricted-tier or care-adjacent parties, (d) download annotated error files, and (e) see aggregate counts that could reveal hidden records — none is ratified for general staff profiles. The D17 profile answers these only for a missionary's own restricted preparation.

## 8. Discrepancies and contradictions

1. **Auto-merge.** `owner-constraints.md:366-372` ("auto-merge high band only") and P28 OA-01 (`owner-reconciliation.md:47-48`, echoed `owner-constraints.md:349-351`) presume a staff-import high-band auto-merge. ADR-0216 (`:54-55`), Phase 4 A9/stories/acceptance (`phase-04...md:107, 185, 222, 399-400`), Phase 1 (`:48`) and CONTEXT.md (`:1628`) forbid automatic merge. The roadmap rewrite (`roadmap.md:4351`) defers to the identity owner, which forbids it. Handling: treat ADR-0216 as governing; record an owner-constraints/OA-01 wording correction as a Phase 30 grill outcome; do not design a high-band auto-merge.
2. **OpenSpec weaker than ADR.** `openspec/contracts/crm-core.md:125-127` bans auto-merge only for _low-confidence_ matches; it does not encode "never automatic" for high confidence. Handling: Phase 30 spec should cite ADR-0216 explicitly; consider an OpenSpec delta through the P4 owner.
3. **Email uniqueness vs PRD.** PRD: partial unique "WHERE merged_into_donor_id IS NULL AND email IS NOT NULL" (`phase-04...md:268`). Installed: unconditional `UNIQUE (tenant_id, email_normalized) WHERE email_normalized IS NOT NULL` (`20261024140000...sql:162-164`), "counts merged donors too (Q7)" (`:19-21`). Consequence for import: a couple sharing one email cannot both carry it on donor rows; a merged-away donor's email still blocks. Handling: P4 owner decision before Party/donor import; importer must surface conflicts, not silently drop or reassign emails.
4. **Merge storage naming.** PRD/ADR name `merge_operations`, `merged_into_donor_id`, `merged_into_party_id` (`phase-04...md:254, 262`; `phase-09...md:414`); integrated schema has two other stores: Twenty-shaped `crm_merge_candidates` and prototype `donor_merge_candidates`/`donor_merge_redirects`/`donor_merge_audit` (with `actor_type` 'agent') (section 7). Handling: Phase 30 must consume whatever P4 #512/#513 actually ships; it must not write either current store directly.
5. **Provider-link table shape.** Ratified "generalized" `crm_record_links` carrier (`phase-01...md:106-113`; `phase-09...md:846-847`) vs integrated Twenty-column table (`20260508000413...sql:73-90`).
