## 1. Phase 30 baseline text on mapping assistance (published roadmap + retained owner obligation)

- Published roadmap, Phase 30 "Upload and mapping": "Mapping assistance may propose matches, but the authorized reviewer determines the accepted plan: staff for staff import profiles, or the permitted missionary for their own qualified D17 restricted-preparation profile without a routine second reviewer. Optional AI is not a prerequisite." (`docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/roadmap.md:4347`). Label: published spec (roadmap chapter), summarising owner intent.
- Published roadmap, Phase 30 open decisions: "Choose importer build-versus-adopt, the first supported source export profiles, mapping-assistance data handling, ..." (`roadmap.md:4409`). So the data-handling question is explicitly open, not decided.
- Retained owner obligation (P30 "Import wizard"): "deterministic mapping suggestions with tenant-confirmed remembered mappings ... A later AI enhancement must use Phase 40's suggestion ledger, classification, evidence, and human-commit contract; imported PII never leaves its governed boundary merely to improve mapping. Build-vs-embed is decided at grooming." (`docs/prds/program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md:356-363`, key sentence `:360-362`). Label: ratified owner intent (retained obligation).
- Reading note. The owner text makes three separable statements: (a) the v1 baseline is deterministic suggestions plus tenant-confirmed remembered mappings; (b) AI is a _later enhancement_; (c) when it comes, it must use "Phase 40's suggestion ledger, classification, evidence, and human-commit contract". Statement (c) names a _contract shape_ attributed to Phase 40; it does not by itself say whether that shape can only be consumed after Phase 40 ships. Sections 2-4 show most of that shape is not owned only by Phase 40.

### 3.3 What the precedents establish

1. A feature phase that sits _before_ Phase 40 can admit a new closed feature purpose on the D10 foundation (D18 did exactly this, and Phase 28 is an earlier phase than Phase 40).
2. The admission vehicle is a founder-ratified decision in the consuming phase, followed by dated owner amendments: D10/ADR-0099 purpose registration, Phase 10 egress amendment (minimum-data manifest contents and exclusions), Phase 12 separate capabilities, Phase 3 purpose projection, Phase 1 matrix row where meaning is new. D16 shows the complete set; D18 shows ratification plus publication with owner reconciliation deferred to "before activation".
3. The consuming domain owns suggestion meaning and acceptance; D10 owns provider/credential/binding/egress/invocation truth; Phase 10 owns the egress decision.
4. No precedent required Phase 40's workbench. D18 said so in terms (`d18 review:75-76,215-216`).

## 4. Constraints that bind any Phase 30 mapping purpose (scope matters)

### 4.1 D17 C12: an absolute ban, but only for the D17 self-import profile

- Text: "**C12 — Bounded inert file parsing.** Admit only the qualified CSV/data-only XLSX profile. ... No source content enters external AI or an unqualified processor." (`docs/features/missionary-workspace-depth/grill/phase28-d17-contact-import-adversarial-review.md:278-285`). Ratified 2026-10-16 with C01-C24 (`d17 review:6-12`).
- D17 also fixes the mapping method for that profile: "Review what Asym understood. Suggest mappings deterministically and show real sample values." (`d17 review:128-129`).
- Scope: C12 opens with "Admit only the qualified CSV/data-only XLSX profile", i.e. the D17 missionary self-import profile. The Phase 30 owner obligation keeps it separate: "Other import profiles retain their existing rules" (`owner-constraints.md:352-353`), and the roadmap says the D17 reviewer/target is "already selected ... Other target profiles retain their own unresolved decisions" (`roadmap.md:4411-4414`).
- "Source content" plainly covers headers as well as cell values: headers are bytes of the uploaded file. So for the D17 profile even a header-only external call is barred. Reopening that needs a new owner ruling that amends D17, not a Phase 30 grooming choice.
- Carry-over check: the published P28 requirement REQ28-R25 cites "D17 C12/C22" as its decision basis (`phase-28-missionary-workspace-depth/relationships.md:471`) but its text restates only the inert-parsing limits. A bounded grep of the Phase 28 package, owner-constraints and openspec for "external AI" / "unqualified processor" found no restatement outside the D17 review itself. The clause still binds as ratified owner intent; the published spec just does not repeat it word for word. Label: ratified owner intent; not restated in published spec text.

### 4.2 Owner obligation "merely to improve mapping": narrower than a total ban

- "imported PII never leaves its governed boundary merely to improve mapping" (`owner-constraints.md:361-362`). The word "merely" bans PII egress _whose only justification is better mapping_. The clause does not on its face ban non-PII metadata (Asym's own field-catalog labels, types, or a header string with no personal data). Whether a header string can be treated as non-PII is a classification question for Phase 10/Phase 3, not something Phase 30 can assume (headers sometimes carry names, a data row mistaken for a header, or ministry-sensitive labels).
- Phase 11 already treats "AI/LLM context" as one of 26 per-field egress doors; "Any unregistered egress → strictest (`internal`) → omitted" (`docs/prds/sitestacker-parity/phase-11-custom-fields-custom-collections.md:159`). So _cell values_ of catalogued fields could reach a model only where that field's AI door is open. Field definitions themselves are catalog metadata; I found no explicit ruling on whether tenant custom-field _labels_ are AI-egress-eligible (see Unverified).

### 4.3 ADR-0173 item 15 already anticipates AI mapping suggestions, with human review

- ADR-0173 "Accepted (founder-ratified Phase 23 D29 B-prime-R, 2026-08-24)" (`docs/adr/0173-governed-content-portability-and-private-draft-imports.md:3`); "Phase 30 owns the reusable migration workbench" (`0173:19,30,42`).
- Item 15: "Exact adapter-known mappings are collapsed as **Matched**. Deterministic aliases may be suggested; heuristic/AI/fuzzy mappings are labelled **Suggested — review required**, show representative source values, and require a human decision. ... Ordinary staff never select arbitrary database fields or match keys." (`0173:153-160`).
- Reading: ratified owner intent for the content-portability path _contemplates_ AI mapping suggestions as a labelled, human-decided class. It sets the UX/acceptance rule, not the egress rule; it does not say what data a model may see. "Show representative source values" is a display to the human reviewer, not permission to send values to a model.

### 4.4 Phase 10 / Phase 40 untrusted-content and restricted-data rules

- Restricted, high-risk, care and security content "remains ineligible for remote model egress under the current ruling" (`phase-10-...md:324-326`); restricted tier excluded from "any future AI context" (`phase-10-...md:194`).
- Phase 40 roadmap: "Inbound email, forms, imported text and source files are untrusted content, not instructions granting an agent authority. Source retrieval and any external model egress must respect current permission, purpose and data-processing rules." (`roadmap.md:5290`). A mapping purpose would inherit this: source headers are untrusted model input; the output is a suggestion only.
- D10 hard limits apply unchanged: certified adapters only, no arbitrary endpoint/model, no tenant-authored system prompt, no silent fallback, no tools/browsing/retrieval, strict structured output, manual path always complete (`phase-21-...decision-log.md:3383-3390,3413-3445`; `0099:47-60`).

## 6. Is there a dependency cycle? (fact, not product choice)

There is no hard cycle under either available reading of `owner-constraints.md:360-362`.

- **Literal reading** ("A _later_ AI enhancement must use _Phase 40's_ suggestion ledger"): Phase 30 v1 ships deterministic mapping (allowed and expected: `owner-constraints.md:357-358`; "Optional AI is not a prerequisite", `roadmap.md:4347`). Phase 40 then starts after Phase 30 (`roadmap.md:5266`). A Phase 30 AI increment follows Phase 40. That is a two-step sequence, not a cycle. A cycle would exist only if AI were a precondition of Phase 30 completion, and the roadmap rules that out.
- **Contract-shape reading** (Phase 40's "ledger, classification, evidence, and human-commit contract" means the shared suggestion contract): most of that contract is already ratified outside Phase 40 (sections 2.1-2.5). Phase 40's own obligation sends new purposes through "the D10 code-owned registry" and leaves "suggestion interpretation and acceptance commands" with feature domains (`owner-constraints.md:1385-1391`). D18 admitted a new purpose in an earlier phase and said Phase 40 "supplies coordinated suggestion contracts, not a requirement to deliver its entire staff workbench first" (`d18 review:75-76`). On this reading a Phase 30 mapping purpose needs the D10 foundation plus its owner amendments, not Phase 40.
- What _is_ real: (a) every AI option depends on the unbuilt D10 foundation (section 5), which is not among Phase 30's starting dependencies (Phases 9, 13, 11, 29, 4, 3: `roadmap.md:4340`); (b) the owner text uses "later" and names Phase 40, so choosing the contract-shape reading for v1 needs an explicit owner ruling. Implementers cannot just assume it.
- So the r01/r09 framing ("AI must wait for Phase 40's ledger, and Phase 40 depends on Phase 30") overstates the dependency. The founder should not be told the choice is constrained by a cycle.

## 7. The decision as it actually stands (separating facts from founder choices)

### 7.1 Already settled (facts; no founder choice needed)

1. D17 self-import profile: deterministic mapping only; no source content (headers included) to external AI or an unqualified processor (`d17 review:128-129,285`). Not reopened by Phase 30 (`roadmap.md:4411-4414`).
2. Any AI in any profile is optional; the manual/deterministic path must be complete without it (`roadmap.md:4347`; `0099:58-61`; `phase-21-...decision-log.md:3438-3445`).
3. Any AI mapping output is a non-authoritative suggestion labelled "Suggested — review required", decided by the authorized reviewer, and accepted through Phase 30's own plan/commit command with fresh authorization (`0173:153-160`; `roadmap.md:4347`; `CONTEXT.md:6107-6112`; `owner-constraints.md:1376-1384`).
4. If AI is used, it runs only through the D10 foundation: tenant-owned Provider Connection, write-only Credential Revision, a closed code-owned Phase 30 purpose, a capability-certified Binding Version, a minimum-data Egress Manifest, Invocation Evidence; no Eve, no vendor-side model on the vendor's own keys, no arbitrary endpoint, no silent fallback (`phase-01-...md:66,397-401`; `0099:37-80`; `phase-21-...decision-log.md:3346-3390`; `d18 review:450`).
5. Restricted/high-risk/care/security content never goes to a remote model (`phase-10-...md:324-326`); imported text is untrusted input (`roadmap.md:5290`).
6. Imported PII may not leave "merely to improve mapping" (`owner-constraints.md:361-362`). Sending sample cell values from a contact file to improve mapping is therefore barred under current owner text.
7. Admission recipe, if a purpose is wanted: founder-ratified Phase 30 decision, then dated amendments to D10/ADR-0099 (purpose registration and certification profile), Phase 10 (egress manifest contents and exclusions), Phase 12 (separate activate/invoke/accept capabilities), Phase 3 (purpose projection), and a Phase 1 row if new meaning is created (precedents in section 3).

### 7.2 Genuine founder choices

1. **v1 posture.** Keep v1 deterministic-only (aliases, adapter-known exact matches, tenant-confirmed remembered mappings) and defer AI to a later increment, _or_ ratify a Phase 30 mapping-assistance purpose on the D10 foundation as an optional, default-Off increment. The second option interprets the "later ... Phase 40's" wording as D18 did, so it needs an explicit owner ruling.
2. **Data scope, if a purpose is admitted.** (a) Asym field-catalog metadata plus source header strings only; or (b) also representative sample values. Option (b) conflicts with the "merely to improve mapping" PII clause for contact data and would need an owner amendment. Option (a) still needs a Phase 10 ruling on whether header strings and tenant custom-field labels are eligible egress, since headers can contain personal or ministry-sensitive text.
3. **Which profiles may use it.** Staff profiles only (D17 is already excluded). Possibly limit to specific source families, for example content-portability adapters, where ADR-0173 item 15 already contemplates AI suggestions.
4. **Vendor-embedded ML (ties to build-vs-adopt).** If an embedded importer is adopted, its built-in AI/ML mapping (vendor keys, vendor retention, cross-customer learning) does not fit D10's tenant-owned, certified-adapter model. The choice is to disable it, or to require the vendor to be certified as a D10 adapter on a tenant connection. Under current owner text, the vendor's own AI cannot simply be turned on. This is an inference: the Phase 1 row makes the shared foundation the only owner of AI connections, bindings, egress and invocation and forbids "arbitrary endpoint/model" (`phase-01-...md:66`), and D10 bounds providers to "currently capability-certified adapters" (`phase-21-...decision-log.md:3384-3385`). No text names embedded-importer AI specifically. Vendor behaviour facts are in r09 §2.2 / r00:773.
5. **Who funds and sequences the D10 foundation build.** If AI is wanted in or near Phase 30 v1, the foundation must exist first. Phase 21 is its ratified owner, but it is unbuilt. ## Resolution

Resolved on the facts; one genuine founder ruling remains.

- **No cycle.** The ratified shared AI execution foundation (Phase 1 matrix row 66 and prose 397-401; Phase 21 D10 / ADR-0099; Phase 10 D10 egress amendment) owns Provider Connections, Credential Revisions, closed Feature Purposes, capability-certified Binding Versions, Egress Manifests and Invocation Evidence "for this and later product domains". Phase 40 "later consumes rather than replaces" it, and its own obligation sends new purposes through "the D10 code-owned registry". The shared AI Suggestion Version contract (owner-domain acceptance, human commit) is already ratified outside Phase 40. Phase 28 D18 (ratified 2026-10-16) admitted a _new_ purpose before Phase 40 and stated that no Phase 40 workbench dependency is introduced. So Phase 30 AI does not depend on Phase 40. Even on the literal reading it is a later increment after Phase 40, not a cycle.
- **Already fixed:** D17 self-import stays deterministic with no source content, headers included, to external AI (C12). Every AI output is a "Suggested — review required" suggestion committed through Phase 30's own plan with fresh authority. Any AI must run on D10, never Eve or a vendor's own keys. Restricted content never leaves. PII may not leave "merely to improve mapping". The manual path is always complete.
- **Real founder choice** (replacing the false-cycle framing): _For staff import profiles, should Phase 30 ratify an optional, default-Off mapping-assistance purpose on the D10 foundation that sends only Asym field-catalog metadata and source header strings, with human commit (subject to a Phase 10 egress ruling and D10/P10/P12/P3 owner amendments, on the D16/D18 pattern)? Or should v1 stay deterministic-only, with AI deferred to a later increment?_ Either way the D10 foundation is unbuilt, so any AI option also needs a sequencing decision for that build.
