**Findings.** Configuration-only copies are the cheap default; copies with records are opt-in, capped and priced. Every vendor documents a gap in its default-deny: Salesforce Contact emails stay real, Microsoft plug-ins and scheduled flows keep running, HubSpot sends from production IPs, Stripe test mode shares live settings. Promotion is usually impossible (Shopify forbids it; Salesforce and Atlassian move changes, not the copy); Microsoft is the exception.

## 2. Preventing "wrong environment" mistakes

- **Persistent top banner naming the copy:** Salesforce ribbon, Stripe and HubSpot banners (VD, §1). Stripe makes it the safety signal: no banner, assume live effects.
- **Distinct host:** Salesforce `--SandboxName.sandbox.`, Zendesk sandbox subdomain, Atlassian admin-chosen subdomain, Stripe `/test/` path (S).
- **Public pages gated:** a Shopify dev store's password page cannot be removed (VD).
- **Admins add their own cues:** browser extensions add sandbox headers and red production borders to Salesforce (S: chromewebstore.google.com items ifmhcncdlalfeogfoabkgojlfffdimpo, bhnfbnhpmnfacebccnjjoeecgmnbhjam). _U:_ native cues are not always enough.
- **Accessibility (VP):** WCAG 1.4.1 "Color is not used as the only visual means of conveying information" (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html); WCAG 2.4.2 "Page titles help users identify and distinguish different pages" (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/page-titled.html). No vendor documents banner accessibility. _U:_ use banner text plus a page-title prefix, not a colour bar alone.

## 3. Deletion and backups

- **Supabase** (VD https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups):
  - **Daily backups:** Pro keeps the last 7 days, Team 14, Enterprise "up to 30 days".
  - **PITR:** retention of 7, 14 or 28 days. "By default, we back up WAL files at two-minute intervals." "If you enable PITR, we will no longer take Daily Backups."
  - **What deletes backups:** "When you delete a project, we permanently remove all associated data, including any backups stored in S3."
  - **Storage files:** "Database backups do not include objects you store via the Storage API".
  - No per-backup or per-row purge is documented.
  - _U:_ rehearsal-tenant rows deleted inside the shared project stay in backups until retention ends.
- **Supabase branching** (VD https://supabase.com/docs/guides/deployment/branching): "By default, new branches do not start with any data … This is meant to better protect your sensitive production data."
- **ICO** (S; ico.org.uk 403): erase from "backup systems as well as live systems"; acceptable if "put beyond use", the backup "simply held on your systems until it is replaced in line with an established schedule". Practitioners add: re-erase on restore (S, ICAEW).
- **EDPB** (VP https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-identifies-challenges-hindering-the-full-implementation-of-the-right-to-erasure_en, 18 Feb 2026): its 2025 enforcement action found problems with "the deletion of personal data in the context of back-ups". Summaries say controllers often leave backups until overwritten (S, Cott Group).
- **Deletion is not instant elsewhere:** Microsoft keeps deleted environments recoverable for seven days (VD).
- **Proof practice (S):** a "Data Deletion Certificate documenting completion of logical deletion from active systems", backups expiring later (preview.thinksmartinc.com); Freshworks and ServiceNow listings publish production deletion within 24 hours and backup expiry within 7 or 14 days (slack.com marketplace). Certificates cover active systems and state backup expiry separately.

## 5. Shared-identifier hazards

**Incidents.**

- **Copy of a production database:** "I was doing some software testing on a copy of the production database and accidentally sent out thousands of emails to customers." The addresses had not been scrubbed. S (Steve Jones, 2022-03-02): https://www.sqlservercentral.com/editorials/the-test-mistake
- **HBO Max, 18 June 2021:** "Integration Test Email #1" went to "a portion of our HBO Max mailing list" (test template, production list; not a copy). S https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/technology/hbo-max-emailed-a-mysterious-integration-test-to-all-subscribers/
- **Atlassian/Okta:** partner emails were "inadvertently uploaded to the internal sandbox environment, triggering the email invitations". S (snippet; 403) https://community.developer.atlassian.com/t/what-is-this-okta-email/97545
- **Not found:** any public postmortem of real charges or webhooks from a copy (evidence gap, not absence).

**Documented hazards and mitigations.**

- **Salesforce** (VD): "some sandboxes can be set to generate emails—even to donors"; Contacts are not rewritten. A Marketing Cloud Next option to discard all sandbox email is S only (Medium, Spring '26).
- **Microsoft** (VD copy-environment): "A mailbox can't be synced with two different environments"; Yammer posts duplicate; HTTP-triggered flows get "a new HTTP URL" and source flows should be disabled; connection references "require new connections".
- **Dropped or rewritten identifiers** (VD): Atlassian skips webhooks and OAuth tokens; Oracle skips endpoint URLs; HubSpot leaves integrations unconnected; Zendesk rewrites user emails to `@example.com`.

**Pattern:** hazards come from copied outward-pointing identifiers (addresses, credentials, callback URLs, tokens, mailboxes). Vendors drop or rewrite them and require explicit reconnection.

## Common pattern

1. Configuration copied by default; records on request, capped and priced.
2. Created locked: admin-only, outward effects off, outward identifiers dropped or rewritten; re-enabling is explicit. Gaps exist, so controls are layered.
3. Persistent named banner, distinct host, public pages gated.
4. A refresh replaces the copy; 1–5 record-bearing copies; no promotion (Microsoft excepted).
5. Regulators: authorise each copy, log copying and use, protect like production, delete after testing; no count or day limits.
6. Live deletion is fast; backups age out on schedule; proof covers active systems and states backup expiry.

## Implications for Asym's rehearsal copy (U unless cited)

1. **Scope:** copy the ministry's configuration, not its live records, then commit the practice import (Stripe, HubSpot, Microsoft configuration-only patterns). Copy live records only if matching against them is being rehearsed, and tell staff.
2. **Born locked, fail closed:** one tenant rehearsal state checked at every outward boundary (email, payment, webhook, public giving page, donor login, scheduled jobs). Microsoft shows a flag alone leaks; each send, charge and call site needs its own deny check and test.
3. **Don't copy outward identifiers** (Atlassian, Oracle): no Stripe connection or keys, no fallback to the platform live key (r1), no webhook endpoints, OAuth tokens or custom domains.
4. **Email** (HubSpot model): deliver only to the ministry's staff users, so staff see their own receipts; discard and log the rest. Salesforce's Contact trap shows rewriting staff addresses alone is not enough.
5. **Identification:** always-on text banner with name and expiry, distinct host or path, page-title prefix, not colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1, 2.4.2). Donor-facing pages unreachable (Shopify).
6. **Access:** ministry admins only by default (Microsoft admin mode, Stripe Private); invitations explicit and logged.
7. **Count:** one copy per ministry, replaced by each new practice commit (Salesforce/Atlassian refresh, Zendesk 1–2).
8. **Lifetime:** fixed short expiry, advance warnings, automatic destruction (Microsoft schedule); extension explicit and logged.
9. **No promotion:** the copy never becomes live; the real switch re-commits into live (Shopify, Salesforce), keeping Q1/Q2 intact.
10. **Deletion proof:** the receipt states live rows were deleted at time T and backups containing them expire by T plus plan retention (7, 14 or 30 days, or the PITR window), held beyond use until then. Any restore re-deletes rehearsal tenants; Storage files are deleted separately; never promise immediate erasure from backups.
11. **Authorisation and logging:** a named ministry admin creates the copy with a stated purpose; creation and access are logged (EDPS, ISO 8.33, NIST SA-3(2), MoJ).
