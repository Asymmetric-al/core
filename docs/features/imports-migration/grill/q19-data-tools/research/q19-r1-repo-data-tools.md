## 5. Who may see raw source rows and error output (owner text)

1. **Restricted-data floor (ratified).** Uncleared viewers, helpers and Asym: headers, counts and validation summaries only; restricted people by alias (DL:208-212). Previews and value tables only to people allowed to see them; fund names only to cleared members (DL:545-548). The file starts restricted under Phase 10/12 (Q2 cl. 5(e), DL:83).
2. **Clearance is Phase 10/12's.** "Security Clearance" is a per-person capability, never baked into a template (`phase-12:1495`); its admin surface is "Mission Control → Settings → Security" (`phase-10:149`). Restricted reads, exports and prints are logged (`phase-10:75`); bulk restricted export is hard-stopped (`phase-12:1531`); export power raises an alert (`phase-12:1498`).
3. **Asym never previews or downloads files** (DL:218), and Asym staff AI never processes error output (DL:553).
4. **Error output form (ratified for staff profiles).** Waiting rows are an on-demand text-typed spreadsheet, never stored (DL:830); reversal results lists render on demand under the viewer's permissions (DL:1039; Q18 review E37 shows restricted people only to permitted viewers, `q18-adversarial-review.md:127`). The raw export is never sent back (DL:822).
5. **Is reading raw rows or downloading error output a separate permission?** No ratified text says so. Evidence for separation:
   - D29 makes "artifact download" independently authorizable from preparation, plan view and commit (P23-DL:7884-7892), ratified for CMS only.
   - The Q1 adversarial review's spec requirements list "Separate capabilities for planning and running an import, reading raw rows and error files, committing ... and signing off" (`q01-switch-model/q01-adversarial-review.md:2132`; skeptic ruling `:623`). These are review recommendations; the ratified Q1 text (DL:19-61) and Q5 text (DL:186-235) do not adopt them.
   - ADR-0212 makes export a separate, stricter decision than visibility (`0212:15-20`). Whether a Waiting-row or results download is an "export" under it is unstated.
   - The Q5 review assigns "Raw bytes and preview or download permissions" to Phase 29 (`q05-adversarial-review.md:158`) and enforces the floor "at the Phase 10 floor and the Data Tools projection" (`:170`). Phase 3 requires Phase 30 import-session mechanics to have their own projections (`phase-03:614-616`).
   - For the D17 profile, #2083 puts "previews, error files and recovery material" in private P29 custody and requires "current export permission" for correction-artifact export (#2083 body, AC28-R26-01, AC28-R26-04).

## 7. What is genuinely still open for F7

The settled clauses fix the floor, the Asym boundary, the reversal page, error-output form, matching outcomes and Gift-file landing. These product choices remain:

1. **Area or no area.** One dedicated Mission Control area (created by Phase 30, later shared with Phase 40 per RM:5260), versus nesting under Admin/System or a Settings area, versus no hub with only in-context entries. Ratified text already uses "Data Tools preparation capabilities" (DL:205) and the roadmap names the surface (RM:4335), but nothing fixes an area.
2. **Label and sidebar group.** "Data Tools" (roadmap, Q5) versus "Imports" (Phase 15:216) or another staff label; which sidebar group (today Modules / Tools / System, `mc-shell.tsx:88-118`). Any choice must update L1 and L2, the tile catalog and the Eve allowlist, and decide whether to delete L3 and L4.
3. **Inside the area.** How the single Switch (Switch page with its ratified lists) and everyday imports sit together: for example a Switch page plus an imports list, each import with one page that carries preview, plan review, commit, results and Prepare reversal (Q18 review's "Import page", `q18-adversarial-review.md:207`). Also where remembered mappings, templates, retention notices and Verify your copy, and the Created-by-Switch list sit, and whether the steps form one saved stepper (D29's five-step precedent) or separate pages.
4. **In-context entry points.** Whether "Import people" on the People list (`/crm/people`) and "Upload a Gift file" on Contributions or the Phase 15 batch list launch into Data Tools, or imports start only in Data Tools. Ratified elsewhere and not open: CMS in Web Studio, missionary self-import in the workspace, Field Account opening in Finance → Field Accounts, church remittances on Phase 14's screen.
5. **Capability set and defaults.** Beyond the ratified decision-level capabilities (S1), O52's four and Phase 15's batch permission, decide:
   - whether there is a view or follow-status capability;
   - what "Data Tools preparation" covers (upload, mapping, planning, practice runs);
   - whether **reading source values and downloading Waiting rows or results lists** is separate from preparation (D29 and the Q1 review say separate; nothing ratified);
   - whether narrowing a file needs more than clearance;
   - which Switch decisions share one "Switch decision capability" (DL:252, :270) and which are separate;
   - default holders under Phase 12's seeded templates, which today have no import template (`phase-12:1595`), and whether any of these are "sensitive" for just-in-time or recertification.
6. **Nav visibility.** Which capability shows the entry, and what staff without it see: nothing, or status and counts (D29 lets non-preparers follow status, P23-DL:7887-7889). Today the sidebar shows everything and the search list filters by role names (§3.2).
7. **Downloads as exports.** Whether Waiting-row spreadsheets, results lists and annotated templates are governed exports under ADR-0212 and Phase 12 "export power". If so, how restricted rows are treated in them (aliased, excluded or counted).
8. **Match review presentation.** Placement is settled: uncertain matches are reviewed in the plan, in-file duplicates listed before commit, real duplicates go to Phase 4 after commit (S9, S10). Still open:
   - whether staff match review shows, hides or counts candidates the reviewer cannot see (D17 has a rule, RM:4359-4360; staff profiles do not);
   - how post-commit pairs reach Phase 4: an import-filed candidate (O6) or Phase 4's own scan. This needs Phase 4 agreement (DL:1290 note).
9. **Run attention.** Whether the area's run list is the only place runs needing a person appear, or Phase 30 also registers a Tasks Hub source projection (ADR-0183). Also where import-specific operator health appears (Data Tools versus a Phase 8 catalog addition). This is O11's surface half.
10. **Uncleared view control.** Where the "uncleared view" that S3 requires is switched on, and whether it applies across the whole area.
