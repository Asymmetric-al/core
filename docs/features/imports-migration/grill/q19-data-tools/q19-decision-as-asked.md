## Option B as proposed

1. **A Data Tools entry in the sidebar, next to Admin.**
2. **Switch.** Shown only while a ministry has one. It is a checklist of steps with status and who's doing what:
   - readiness;
   - practice runs, mapping and match review;
   - reconciliation and sign-off;
   - go-live;
   - tail backfills and close.

   It becomes a read-only record once closed.

3. **Imports.** One list of every People import and Gift file: who, when, counts, status and the reversal deadline.
4. **One page per import, covering its whole life:**
   - upload, mapping, and preview with match review;
   - commit and results;
   - Prepare reversal.

   It is saved at every step and survives a closed browser.

5. **Saved mappings.**
6. **Shortcuts.**
   - "Import people" on the People list and "Upload a Gift file" in Contributions open the same flow.
   - Gift batches appear in Contributions and link back to their import.
   - Imports that need someone also appear in the Tasks Hub.
