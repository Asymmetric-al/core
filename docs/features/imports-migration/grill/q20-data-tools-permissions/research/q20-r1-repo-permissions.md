## 0. Findings in brief

1. **Already ratified.** Decisions belong to holders of each decision's capability, and money commits also need the money owner's capability (DL:198). One person may hold everything (DL:199), and no Asym identity decides (DL:200). There is a "Switch decision capability" (DL:197, :254, :272). Helpers get "Data Tools preparation capabilities", ending at Switch closed (DL:207). O52 names four everyday capabilities (DL:620). Reversal has its own capability (DL:996), and so does history correction (DL:725). Legal-hold capabilities exist (DL:848; O74). Source values are visible only to people allowed to see them, and only after a cleared member narrows the file (DL:211-213, :550-552). Downloads are on demand, text-typed and never stored (DL:836, :1045, :1154). The navigation entry comes from one server-side capability decision (DL:1209).
2. **Not ratified, and left to Q20** (DL:1215-1220):
   - who sees the entry, values and downloads;
   - other people's drafts;
   - restricted match candidates;
   - who may start a Switch.

   Also unsettled:
   - the exact capability map (Q5R:366 sends it to F7);
   - what a person who cannot act sees;
   - whether the restricted-by-default floor covers everyday imports.

3. **Phase 12 constrains the shape.** Capabilities are the only enforcement unit (P12:1542, :1466). A sensitive capability can never sit in View or Manage (P12:1599). Clearance is never baked into a seed (P12:1495, :1595). Export power raises an alert (P12:1498). Bulk restricted export is a hard stop (P12:1531, :1663). Blocked and nonexistent look identical (P12:1507, :1650). No seeded template covers imports (P12:1595). The owner phase mints exact keys and Phase 12 enforces them (P12:20-22). The Phase 21 D17 precedent forbids collapsing distinct import powers into one "import" capability, but lets one actor hold the whole flow by default in small tenants (P12:2219-2226).
4. **The closest precedents for downloads both treat them as governed exports or separately authorized:**
   - Phase 28 D17: "Correction artifacts require current export policy" (P28R:503; #2083 AC28-R26-04).
   - Phase 23 D29: "artifact download" is independently authorizable, and every download re-proves capability (P23-DL:7884-7887, :7948-7950).
5. **Restricted match candidates have no staff-profile rule.** D17's rule is "without exposing inaccessible candidates" (RM:4359-4360). Phase 28 adds that hidden matches cannot change counts (P28R:66). The owner texts disagree on what an uncleared viewer sees: the alias (P10:97, :120; P12:1640; DL:213), nothing (P12:1638; P10:69), or a count (Q18R:127, which conflicts with small-cell suppression at P12:1664).
6. **Code today.** One role gate admits every `staff`, `admin` and `super_admin` (`apps/admin/lib/admin-access.ts:57-75`). "All staff subroles have full Mission Control admin access" (`packages/auth/permissions.ts:37-51`). The registry has no import, clearance or export atom (`packages/auth/capabilities/registry.ts:20-129`). The resolver is unwired (r3 V15). Tenant overrides are dormant (`effective-capabilities.ts:48-49`, `:157`). No grant product exists, so a tenant admin cannot grant anything today. Contributions derives its flag from role names (`packages/api/src/admin/contribution-operations/permissions.ts:25-40`, `:267-287`). #2281 is still OPEN with defaults that conflict with Q5 (DL:1485).

### 3.2 What this means for Q20 (analysis, not a decision)

- **Raw rows have no alias.** A source file carries legal names. The alias is a property of an Asym Party (P10:97), so Q5's "only by alias" can apply only after a row is matched. That is why the file starts restricted and uncleared people get "headers, counts and validation summaries only" (DL:211-213; Q5R:48, BR3).
- **Showing a candidate by alias links the two names.** A staff People-import row in a legal name that matches a restricted Party would be shown next to that Party's alias, tying legal name to alias for an uncleared reviewer. Q17 forbids that for links (DL:949). Phase 10 gates name-matching on clearance (P10:122).
- **Hiding the candidate and creating a new person is worse.** If the row "creates new", it adds a standard-tier Party under the restricted worker's legal name, with no alias. That is a second, unprotected record, against P10:97 and C2 (no automatic merge). A hidden match therefore has to **hold** the row, not create from it. _(Inference, but it follows directly from P10:97 and DL:601.)_
- **"Count them otherwise"** (Q18R:127) conflicts with Phase 12 small-cell suppression (P12:1664) and Phase 28's "cannot alter disclosed counts" (P28R:66). Q20 needs one rule for counts in Review, results and downloads.

## 8. Open choices for Q20

Each item is unsettled by ratified text. Constraints already fixed are cited so options stay inside them.

1. **Capability granularity.**
   - **Already fixed:**
     - one capability per decision (DL:198);
     - a Switch decision capability (DL:197);
     - Data Tools preparation capabilities (DL:207);
     - O52's four everyday capabilities (DL:620);
     - reversal (DL:996), history correction (DL:725), legal hold (O74), tier tightening (DL:756);
     - Phase 15's batch capability (DL:620).
   - **Phase 12 rule:** distinct powers must not collapse into "one import … capability" (P12:2219-2223).
   - **Choice:** one Data Tools capability plus O52's atoms, or a small explicit set.

     | Candidate atom        | What it covers                                                     |
     | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
     | follow                | see the list and status                                            |
     | prepare               | upload, map, plan, practice                                        |
     | view source values    |                                                                    |
     | download problem rows |                                                                    |
     | run People import     | O52                                                                |
     | Replace               | O52                                                                |
     | clear values          | O52                                                                |
     | reverse               | O52                                                                |
     | Switch decision       | one atom, or split as Q1R:623 suggests (commit and sign-off apart) |
     | narrow                | may need more than clearance (r1 §7 item 5)                        |

2. **Default holders.** Phase 12 seeds have no import template, and Owner is a stack of specific manage caps (P12:1595). Options:
   - Owner only;
   - Owner plus a new seeded template;
   - add Data Tools atoms to Advancement or Finance;
   - one actor holds the whole flow by default (P12:2223-2225; DL:199; P23-DL:7890).

   Also decide which atoms are `sensitive`. Sensitive atoms can then only sit at Admin or be explicitly granted, with JIT and recertification (P12:1494, :1599, :1661). A strong candidate: viewing restricted source values and downloads, since export power alerts (P12:1498).

3. **Viewing source values versus preparing.**
   - **Already fixed:** values only to "people allowed to see them" and after narrowing (DL:550-551); fund names to cleared members only (DL:552); Asym and helpers map columns without values (DL:551).
   - **Choice:** do values follow clearance plus narrowing alone, so any preparer sees values once a file is narrowed? Or is there a separate "view source values" atom, as Q1R:623 and D29's split suggest?
   - **Consequence:** fixing cells in Asym (DL:1131) and value mapping (DL:546) require seeing values.
4. **Downloads as governed exports.**
   - **Choice:** are Waiting-row, problem-row and results downloads exports under Phase 3 A6 and ADR-0212, needing a current export permission (the P28R:503 precedent) and a Phase 12 export-power alert (P12:1498)? Or are they governed by the same atom as viewing values?
   - **Either way, decide:**
     - per-download reauthorization and an audit receipt (IAC:353-354; P23-DL:7948-7950);
     - delivery through actor-bound grants (O64);
     - **what restricted rows become** in a download: excluded and reported uniformly, or excluded silently. Aliasing raw rows is impossible (§3.2), and counting conflicts with P12:1664 and P28R:66;
     - a hard stop if the file would be a bulk restricted export (P12:1531).
5. **Other people's drafts and imports.**
   - **Already fixed:** the Imports list shows "started by" and filters by person, and is "the guaranteed way back to any import" (DL:1093, :1098-1099). A draft is protected "so two people never overwrite each other" (IMCTX:376-378; Q19R E11).
   - **Options:**
     - every holder of the kind's capability sees and edits every draft;
     - everyone sees, but only the starter or a decision holder edits;
     - own drafts only, plus Switch decision holders.
   - **Note:** an import's name defaults to the file name, which can carry personal data (Q19R:301). Other people's import names are a disclosure surface.
6. **Gift file gate.** Phase 15's `finance:record_contribution` alone (DL:620; P15:792), or that plus a Data Tools atom? Posting stays Phase 15 in both cases.
7. **Restricted match candidates in staff imports.** Restricted-tier matches "always go to review" (DL:601), and no staff-profile display rule exists (§3). Options:
   - **A.** Hidden, and the row is held as "needs a cleared reviewer" with no candidate shown and no count difference (the D17/P28 pattern: RM:4359-4360, P28R:66);
   - **B.** Shown by alias (DL:213), which links legal name to alias (§3.2; DL:949);
   - **C.** Counted (Q18R:127), which conflicts with P12:1664.

   Any option must forbid creating a new person from such a row (§3.2).

8. **Who may start a Switch.** The lead defaults to the creator (DL:197), so creating is a distinct act. Options:
   - the Switch decision capability;
   - a separate "start a Switch" atom;
   - Owner only.

   Interacting gates: Switches are not offered until the Practice environment is Approved (DL:286); the Q2 LATER gates (DL:83); one Switch per source (DL:116); a kind switch for the ministry (DL:1211). Also decide who turns an import kind on (DL:1211-1212 says only "switched on").

9. **Navigation visibility rule.** One server-side decision, never role names (DL:1209), and only once a kind is on (DL:1211). Options for which atoms show the entry:
   - any Data Tools atom (including follow);
   - preparers only;
   - also the Switch lead, who must see Q17's weekly list (DL:979) but whose designation "grants nothing" (DL:197).

   Q19R:181 says the entry is absent without a capability.

10. **What someone who cannot act sees.** Precedents:
    - D29 non-preparers request, follow status and review results (P23-DL:7887-7889);
    - Q18R:207 "Others see the deadline and who can act";
    - Q19R E26 "Permission page naming who can help";
    - Phase 12 stories 19, 20 and 22 (P12:1504-1507).

    Choices:
    - whether a follow or view atom exists;
    - whether a deep link to an import the person cannot see returns a permission page or a uniform not-found (P12:1507; Q19R:637 E25 not-found for another ministry's import);
    - whether there is a "Request access" path (P12:1505).

11. **Scope of the restricted-by-default floor.** Q2 cl.5(e) and Q5 cl.4 speak of "a real export" in a Switch (DL:83, :211). Q11 cl.4 applies Q5's visibility to previews generally (DL:549-551). Q12 is silent. Decide whether everyday People imports and Gift files start restricted and need narrowing.
12. **The uncleared view.** Q5 requires the ministry to use "the uncleared view" when screen-sharing (DL:205). Decide:
    - whether a cleared person can switch into it;
    - where the switch lives;
    - whether it covers all of Data Tools.
13. **Helpers and Asym outside the Switch.**
    - Asym help access is "for one Switch" (DL:216), so it presumably never covers everyday imports. Confirm this.
    - Helper capabilities "end at Switch closed" (DL:207). Decide whether everyday-import helpers exist, and with what expiry.
    - Both depend on O27. Today no grant product exists (§7).
14. **Prepare reversal versus confirm.** Can a non-holder press Prepare reversal (DL:996, "Pressing it changes nothing")? Or is the button visible only to holders (Q18R:207; ADR-0173 cl.29 "Reversal has separate capability", Q18R:14)?
15. **Audit of reads and downloads.** Restricted reads and exports log `view|export|print` with reason and origin (P10:75, :141). Decide whether Data Tools previews and downloads write to that log, ask for a reason, and appear in My Access.
