## 1. Asym's identity model

**Owner model (ratified).**

- **Party** is the supertype: "every person, household, or organization (including churches) is one party", `party_kind` person / household / org, `group` reserved; church is `org` + `org_type` (C:2204-2210; P9:330-338). A subtype row IS its party (C:2500-2504).
- **Household is a Party kind and a group, not an account.** "a household is a group of persons, never an account that absorbs them" (P7:236); P4 reserves households "as a separate _party_ entity (never an account that absorbs people)" (P4:366). `household_members` is "grandfathered … (receipt-compliance construct — NOT the template)" (P9:400-404). Membership is a **derived** edge, never a stored relationship row (P9:829; C:2506-2510).
- **Spouse** is a stored person↔person family edge (P9:834); free-text `donors.spouse`, `donors.type`, `donors.organization` are "never carried" (P9:349-351, :772-773).
- **Household shape:** `households` has "primary/head, address-of-record, include-in-name/greeting toggles"; `household_members` has role (spouse/partner/child), `started_at`/`ended_at`, include flags (P7:290-291). **Published** #570 (OPEN) adds: "The household is a grouping, not a donor of record on its own gifts"; "A person may belong to zero or one active household at a time"; "a household never owns a gift's `donor_id`"; Samson's 2026-10-24 amendment removes the address column ("The address of record resolves through `head_party_id`") (#570 body lines 10-11, 20, 30).
- **Head of household** exists only as the household's primary/head reference. No owner text gives it any legal, consent or claim meaning.
- **Names and salutations:** B17 name is "one unsplit Unicode contact/display value; never infer given/family parts, require a surname" (P9:661-664); `parties.display_name` is write-through (P9:760). Beyond the two include-in-name/greeting toggles, no owner text defines household naming, addressee ("Mr. & Mrs. John Smith") or formal/informal salutation. The root glossary has **no** Household, Organization, Org contact, Spouse or Head-of-household entry (grep of `C`).
- **Donor record** is a permanent organization-scoped record, claimed or unclaimed (C:1577-1580). `donors.party_id` is NOT NULL from the first migration (P9:340-342).

**Built (implemented).** None of the party spine exists. `donors` is still the flat table with `name`, `email`, `type DEFAULT 'individual'`, `organization`, `spouse TEXT` (`supabase/migrations/20250101000000_init_schema.sql:62-97`). `persons` is an inert anchor that "nothing reads or writes" (`20261025110000_person_anchor.sql:3-4`). `crm_link_entity_type` gained `household` and `organization` with no writer (`20261024120000_crm_link_entity_type_party_values.sql:5-7, 15-16`). No `parties`, `households`, `household_members`, `org_contacts`, `crm_relationships` or `contact_points` table exists in any migration. Issues #570 (party spine), #571 (credits), #512 (merge), #513 (dedupe scan), #509 (claim), #510 (legacy invitations) and #583 (portal, "household joint view") are all OPEN (gh, 2026-11-02).

**Answer to "Party, group or relationship?"** A household is a Party (an identity record you can open), whose membership is a derived grouping. It is not a relationship row, and not a legal donor. Two owner texts still let something household-like own money facts: P16 lets a household be a **Commitment Party** "only when explicit household intent was captured" (P16:1182; C:4331-4333), and P7/P19 allow a "**source-proved joint-donor Party**" as Statement Subject (P7:46-49; `openspec/contracts/statement-operations.md:18-19`). "Source-proved joint donor" is defined nowhere (grep of `docs/`, `openspec/`).

## 2. Matching, merge and email uniqueness

- **No automatic merge** (ratified): ADR-0216 `:17-22`, `:54-55`; C2 (DL:441). Merge is staff-deliberate, same-Tenant, non-destructive, replayable (P4:185; C:1623-1627). D1: `owner-constraints.md:366-372` still says "auto-merge high band only"; ADR-0216 governs (DL:470).
- **Phase 4 match bands** (ratified): dedupe scan is "exact-email=high / fuzzy=low … never auto-merges" (P4:222). Guest gifts attribute to a matching donor; "a single clean match may attribute … ambiguity goes to staff review" (P4:153 rule 6; story P4:69). C:1602-1606: "An ambiguous match goes to review."
- **Installed scorer** (implemented): email 70, phone 20, name 10, org 15; ≥90 is `high`/`link_candidate` (`packages/api/src/crm/mapping/duplicates.ts:58-95`). Two spouses sharing an email and a home phone score **90, high**. `resolveGuestDonorMatch` attaches a gift on any exact email match unless two records tie (`packages/api/src/public-giving/donor-match.ts:110-125`). No runtime caller was found (exported only, `public-giving/index.ts:52`).
- **Import match review** (ratified): match-to-existing on uncommitted rows is plan review; post-commit duplicate pairs go to Phase 4 (D19, DL:500). Q1 clause 4: gifts made in Asym before final import "resolved in match review with no automatic merge" (DL:40). P13 D.6 bypasses review "unless donor-match or allocation is ambiguous" (P13:1039).
- **Phase 28 D17 precedent** (ratified for the missionary self-import only): "Names, email, phones and shared addresses are evidence, never universal person keys or automatic merge instructions" (P28rel:64, AC28-R03-01 :72); "Do not split couples" (P28rel:385, AC28-R20-04 :390); review item P05: "Shared family/church email … multiple same-email canonical records never auto-merge or choose a hidden winner" (`docs/features/missionary-workspace-depth/grill/phase28-d17-contact-import-adversarial-review.md:682`). C03 there: "an ambiguous couple/family label may remain unsplit and unresolved" (:194-196).
- **Email uniqueness, everywhere it exists:**
  - `donors`: partial unique `(tenant_id, email_normalized) WHERE email_normalized IS NOT NULL` (implemented, `20261024140000_identity_email_normalizer_and_donor_keys.sql:162-164`). It "counts merged donors too" (:19-21; `docs/security/identity-email-normalization.md:81-90`). Any number of NULL emails is allowed (:72-77). The P4 PRD intended a partial key excluding merged donors (P4:268), so installed and ratified differ.
  - P9 A7: `donors.email` "stays the claiming/consent key"; multiple emails per person (`contact_points`) remain reserved, not built (P9:393-397; P4:357).
  - **Auth**: "The selected single Supabase project has a global-unique email constraint"; separate logins for one email are rejected (ADR-0214 `:12-14`, `:40-41`). So one email is one login across the platform.
  - `email_suppressions` is address-keyed, unique `(tenant_id, email, suppression_type)` (implemented, `20260402090000_resend_email_foundation_backfill.sql:86-95`, `:143-144`); `donors.do_not_contact` / `do_not_email` are per donor (`20260214090000_foundation_1_schema.sql:378-379`).
  - SiteStacker: "This must be unique as Site Stacker does not allow duplicate emails" (r07:97).
- **Shared email at claim** (ratified): a claim needs fresh proof of the intended mailbox, and "A newly matching email cannot replace a different person's established claim" (C:1608-1614). "Every spouse or teammate retains a distinct Party and principal. Party merge never treats a relationship or shared email as proof of one login" (P4:491-493); "Shared spouse/team credentials and impersonation are forbidden" (P4:546). "Household membership, job title, shared email … grant no access" (P25id:19); no detection of "unknowable mailbox recycling" is promised (P25id:23). #509 (published) rejects shared email and household as reveal authority. _(inference)_ Only the one donor row holding a shared email can ever be claimed through it; the other spouse needs their own address, and whoever reads the mailbox controls the claim.

## 3. Money and documents for couples and households

- **One legal donor per gift** (ratified): "exactly one hard-credit legal donor" (P7:121, A8 :234; C:4440-4442), frozen at acceptance and unchanged by merge (C:1636-1640; P13:225). #570 (published) bars a household as a gift's donor.
- **Soft credit** is recognition-only, never receipted or summed (C:4445-4448). Recognition may be "both-spouses-full" (C:4456-4460; P14:419).
- **Household recognition is derived at read** from time-bounded membership "evaluated against the gift date" and labelled **Household support**, "never merged into personal legal giving"; explicit `household` credit rows exist only for exceptions (P14:1164-1180, :400). DAF letters naming two advisors of one household attribute "the **household**: one credit row, one thank-you" (P14:588-590).
- **Statements** (ratified and published): the Statement Subject is "the exact Phase 7 legal donor or source-proved joint donor"; Phase 19 "cannot choose or merge it" (C:4778-4782). "Household membership, surname, address, email, current relationship … MUST NOT create or change a Statement Subject … MUST NOT provide … combine-household override" (`statement-operations.md:26-35`). Combined household tax statements are out of scope (P19:113); "Shared destinations never merge items" (P19:350, :1272). Household recognition may appear only in the optional, off-by-default **Support overview — Not a tax document** (C:4905-4908; P14:28-40). P7's controlling amendment retires "households roll up" and "one joint statement" language (P7:72-75); Phase 25 marks #583's "household joint statements" as superseded (P25 `decision-log.md:116`).
- **Receipts:** the US annual goes to the "Exact source-owned legal contributor" (P18M:129-130). Canadian cash receipts need the "true donor name/address" (P18M:177). No owner text says whether a receipt may name two people (US or CRA). _(inference: whether CRA accepts joint spousal names is a counsel or Phase 18 question; nothing in the repo answers it.)_
- **Recurring and pledges:** each commitment has "exactly one non-null Commitment Party" (person, household or org) (P16:447). "A household owns only when explicit household intent was captured; membership, marriage, shared address, or shared email grants nothing" (P16:1182). A recurring-giving group is never inferred ("_Avoid_: inferred household bundle", C:3007-3012). Payer, representative and service contact are separate facts (P16:458; C:4335-4341).
- **Phase 30 reviews** (proposed): "Household gift (joint card): One payer per arrangement. The email goes to the payer of record" (`q08-tail-closure/q08-adversarial-review.md:76`). A shared household email where "the person who reads it is not the cardholder" binds the link to the arrangement (`q01-switch-model/research/q01-r6-ux-and-ministry-needs.md:336`). "One spouse moves to Asym, the other stays on the tail: each arrangement is judged separately" (`q07-…/q07-adversarial-review.md:72`).
- **Switch year:** SiteStacker statements were per household and Asym's are per legal donor, so totals differ for couples; the Q4 "different total" sentence covers it (`q04-…/review/q04-v2-ux-ops-review.md:149`; r1:447 X17).

## 5. Organizations as donors

- **Owner model** (ratified): the org Party carries an org profile (EIN, legal name, `org_type`) and **org contacts** ("the human signer(s) … soft-credited") (P7:236, :292-293). The org-contact edge is derived from `org_contacts` (P9:830, :768-769).
- **Money:** an org can be a legal donor. Church remittance hard-credits the church, with soft credit for members (P13:1090-1092). An employer match is the company's own receipted gift (P7:240). The DAF sponsor is the legal donor (C:4482-4486). "An organization owns its own promise while representatives, signers, treasurers, remitters, and contacts may turn over independently" (P16:1182). A treasurer title is not permission (C:4335-4337). Represented giving appears in the portal only through separate explicit grants (P25id:11-17; P25 `decision-log.md:54`).
- _(inference)_ The donor email key covers every donor row, of any kind, so an org donor and its contact person (for example a church office address) cannot both hold one email either.
- **SiteStacker:** organizations are CRM **Groups** (`*_is_group`; Historic Giving `donor_type` person/group). Groups used as committees or teams are not organizations, and Person and Group ID spaces may overlap (r07:105, :129; r1:182).

## 8. Conflicts and gaps that force a choice

1. **A shared email versus the installed key.** SiteStacker already forced one email per Person, but template sources may carry the same email on two people. Asym can store it on only one donor row (§2), and that email is also the claim key and the only login. Nothing says who keeps it. The importer "must surface conflicts, not silently drop or reassign" (r02:269, proposed).
2. **One combined-name row ("John & Mary Smith").** Splitting it conflicts with B17's unsplit name (P9:661-664) and the D17 "do not split couples" precedent. Keeping it creates one Person, so one legal donor named for two people, no spouse edge, and per-person consent that covers two humans. Splitting needs two targets per source ID (§7).
3. **Whose gift.** SiteStacker gives each gift a Person, so its history converges. Household-unit template sources have no legal-donor landing: #570 bars households, and "source-proved joint donor" is undefined.
4. **Household as identity or grouping.** Settled in owner text as a grouping Party (§1). Open: whether SiteStacker's auto households of one become Asym household Parties.
5. **Shared-mailbox restrictions.** A person-level Q9 block on a shared address names one Person. The other spouse at the same address is unaddressed, while provider suppression is address-wide anyway (P17:2148).

## What is settled

- No automatic merge from any band, email, phone or source ID (ADR-0216; C2; P4:222; P28rel:64).
- Every spouse is a distinct Party and login (P4:491-493, :541-547). Shared email, household membership and marriage grant no access, claim, recipient authority, commitment ownership or legal-donor status (P25id:19; P16:1182; P17:3963; `statement-operations.md:26-29`).
- A household is a Party kind that groups persons, with zero or one active household per person, and it never owns a gift (P7:236; P9:400-404; #570, published).
- One hard-credit legal donor per gift, frozen at acceptance (P7:234; C:1636-1640). Spousal and household recognition is derived and labelled, never a receipt or official total (P14:1164-1180).
- Official statements are per legal donor: no household combination, and shared destinations never merge (P19:113, :350; C:4778-4793). The switch-year total difference is covered by Q4.
- Imported email is unverified contact data, never claim or login (P4:152; C:1583-1588). Imports grant no account (`roadmap.md:4365`).
- Restrictions import first. Address-level blocks are bounce and cleaned; person-level blocks are opt-out, do-not-email and complaint. A deceased spouse never blocks the survivor (DL:364-377, :425).
- Every record family needs a stable source ID, and identity rests on Source Record Bindings, never digests (DL:41, :92; P30C:83-85).
- Household membership never subscribes anyone to a newsletter (P28com:600).

## Gaps and hazards (ranked)

1. **Wrong legal donor through email attribution** (High; implemented, unwired). P4 attaches a guest gift on a clean email match (P4:69, :153), and `donor-match.ts:110-125` attaches on exact email. Because the unique key allows only one row per email, the "two records with same email → review" guard (:118-125) can never fire. After a switch, a spouse giving with the shared email becomes the other spouse's legal gift and receipt, and it stays frozen after any merge (C:1636-1640).
2. **False high-confidence duplicates** (High). Spouses with a shared email and phone score 90, `high` (`duplicates.ts:58-95`). P4 bands exact email as high (P4:222). A match-review UI would present real couples as the same person; nothing merges automatically, but review bias is strong.
3. **Import commits fail or drop data on shared email** (High). The second row violates `donors_tenant_email_normalized_uidx`, and merged rows still hold their email (`identity-email-normalization.md:81-90`). This needs a decision before any Parties commit (Q1 kit).
4. **Binding cardinality is unsettled** (High). P30C defines one target per binding. The integrated `crm_record_links` is Twenty-shaped, staff-writable until #2295, and lets one ID bind many targets (g2:137, :370-380). SiteStacker household identity is a column on Person rows, not a record, so a household Party's binding needs its own record family keyed on `household_id`/`household_external_id`. Person and Group IDs may collide (r1:182).
5. **"Source-proved joint donor" is undefined** while #570 bars household donors and P16 allows household Commitment Parties (Medium). Template sources with household giving units have no lawful target.
6. **No home for additional emails** (Medium). `contact_points` is reserved and not built (P4:357; P9:393-397), yet SiteStacker holds many emails per person (r1:184). A spouse's own address may exist only as an "additional email". Unless Phase 9/4 builds a target, it can only be evidence or a visible exclusion.
7. **Joint-name receipts** (Medium). CRA needs the "true donor name" (P18M:177). One Person named "John & Mary Smith" would print both names on an official receipt. No owner or counsel ruling exists.
8. **Shared-mailbox suppression scoping** (Medium). Q9 person-level blocks attach to one Person, and Mailchimp exports are rows per address. Implemented suppression is address-keyed while `do_not_email` is per donor (§2).
9. **Household mail de-duplication and salutation undefined** (Low-Medium). P28 cites an "owner household deduplication" no owner defines, and Phase 32 lists it as open (`roadmap.md:4615`). No glossary terms exist for Household, Organization, Spouse or Head.
10. **SiteStacker households with no head or two heads, placeholder emails (`none@…`, `+alias`), and logins shared by couples** (Low-Medium; r1:183, :201, :437). Placeholders must not become contact data.
11. **Minors as household members** (Low-Medium). Children appear as household Person records (r1:450 X20; P7:291 role "child"). They need Phase 10 classification before import.
12. **Different statement subjects across the switch** (Low). Already covered by Q4's "different total" sentence (q04-v2:149).
