## 1. Acknowledgment timing norms

- **Burk, _Donor-Centered Fundraising_:** "Ninety-three percent of the individuals Burk studied said they would" "definitely or probably give again" if thanked "promptly" and "in a personal way", with a later "meaningful report"; "64 percent would give a larger gift", "74 percent would continue to give indefinitely." Secondary (Simone Joyaux, NPQ, 2009-11-04): https://nonprofitquarterly.org/unraveling-development-thank-your-donors-and-thank-them-again
- **Board-call test (Burk via Bloomerang):** board members who called "within 24 hours saw an average gift of 39% more than those not called." Verified-vendor-doc (Claire Axelrad, updated 2026-09-14): https://bloomerang.com/blog/ask-an-expert-how-often-should-we-thank-monthly-donors-and-how. Other write-ups say 48 hours, so sources disagree on the window (secondary).
- **"48 hours / 4x":** Bloomerang says donors are "4x more likely" to give again if thanked within 48 hours. Its pages credit McConkey-Johnston, Guidestar or Tom Ahern, and I found no primary study. Secondary. The current Bloomerang "do all donors need a thank you within 48 hours" page gives only "Timeline (e.g., Within 48 hours)", "Say thank you immediately", and "Even a $20 online gift warrants an email acknowledgment". Verified-vendor-doc (Melaina Chromy, modified 2026-09-18): https://bloomerang.com/blog/ask-an-expert-do-all-donors-need-a-thank-you-within-48-hours
- **Bloomerang call data** (about 1.9M donors): first-time retention 33% with no call, 41.24% with one, 58.21% with more. Secondary (snippet).

**Recurring donors: thank per gift or periodically?**

- Axelrad: "You can never thank monthly donors too much"; "Thank them at least monthly by varying your methods"; "No, a quarterly thank-you falls short." Verified-vendor-doc (URL above).
- Virtuous (Megan Donahue, 2019-03-12): one customer's donors get "a receipt and a thank-you note" "every month when that gift hits". It also reports one agency's view that "reminding those monthly donors that they're giving every month increases the likelihood that they'll cancel the gift". Boudreau disagrees ("I just resist this very intently"). Verified-vendor-doc: https://www.virtuous.org/blog/how-often-should-you-thank-recurring-donors/
- Practitioners split between per-gift thanks and one annual receipt plus a few touches. Secondary (DonorSnap; Blackbaud community).

**Late or duplicate thank-yous**

- CanadaHelps calls duplicates "the most common (and most damaging) data quality issue" and says "Nothing erodes donor trust faster than receiving the same appeal twice". It also warns of "two different thank-you letters with different gift totals." Verified-vendor-doc (Philip Manzano, 2026-04-21): https://www.canadahelps.org/en/charity-life/canadahelps-ensemble/data-checkup-5-steps-canadian-charities-should-take/
- No controlled study found on weeks-late thank-yous; Burk's effect depends on promptness.
- **Canadian tax receipts:** CRA allows a replacement receipt only if it carries "the serial number of the original receipt" and "a statement that it replaces the original receipt". Verified-primary: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/charities/operating-a-registered-charity/issuing-receipts/correcting-replacing-official-donation-receipts.html. My reading that a second, independent receipt for the same gift does not fit this regime is unverified.

## 3. Missionary support-raising norms

- **MPDX (Cru)** notification types: "Partner started giving", "Partner missed a gift", "Partner gave a Special Gift", "Partner recontinued giving", "Partner gave less than commitment", "Partner gave a larger gift than commitment", and a flag for partners with no thank-you note logged in the past year. Each can be delivered as a push, an email, or "a task for the associated contact will be added to your Tasks list." Verified-vendor-doc: https://help.mpdx.org/article/1199-notifications
- MPDX source code labels `StoppedGiving` as "Missed a gift" and `ThankPartnerOncePerYear` as "No thank you note logged in the past year". Late status falls into "0-30 days late", "30-60 days late" or "60+ days late", and "Donations should only be late if the current date exceeds pledgeStartDate + the frequency window." Verified-primary (open source): https://github.com/CruGlobal/mpdx-react/blob/main/src/components/Layouts/Primary/TopBar/Items/NotificationMenu/Item/Item.tsx; …/ContactPartnershipStatus/ContactLateStatusLabel/getDonationLateStatus.ts
- **TntWare DonorHub** "delivers donation information from an organization's donation system to many popular MPD tools used by their staff". "Your missionary staff will be notified of new gifts directly within their MPD tool." "This way they can thank their partners more quickly." Verified-vendor-doc: https://www.tntware.com/
- SEND International says DonorHub "synchronizes daily" with MPDX or TntConnect. Secondary (snippet).
- **DonorElf:** "New Donor" fires when "A person gives their first gift", with a thank-you note as the suggested action. "Stopped Giving" fires when "A recurring donor is 90 days behind in giving their next expected gift". "Late Donor" uses a 10-day window. Secondary (snippet; fetch 404).
- **World Gospel Mission** tells missionaries to note "Whether this is a repeat gift or a first-time gift" when writing thanks. Verified-vendor-doc (Kateland Vernon, 2020-01-15): https://wgm.org/story/say-thank-you
- Agencies usually issue the official receipt; World Team's default is "a yearly tax receipt". Secondary.

## 4. Lapse detection

- **Bloomerang:** a lapsed donor "hasn't given again within the timeframe you use to define 'active' giving"; "Common definitions are 12 months (short), 18 months (moderate), and 24 months (long)." Verified-vendor-doc: https://bloomerang.com/nonprofit-glossary/lapsed-donor
- **NPSP Status Automation:** "if a donor doesn't make a payment for 60 days the recurring donation can automatically move to Lapsed status, then move to Closed status if the donor doesn't make a payment in 90 days." Verified-vendor-doc: https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/opportunity-settings-in-nonprofit-success-pack/manage-recurring-donations-npsp
- **Blackbaud CRM:** "Define recurring gifts as "Lapsed" when they have a certain number of "Past due" installments" (the example uses two). Also, "any recurring gift included in an open automatic processing batch is automatically excluded" from the update. Verified-vendor-doc: https://webfiles-sc1.blackbaud.com/files/support/helpfiles/crm/uk/40/Content/tkUpdateRecurringGiftStatuses.html
- **Virtuous analytics:** missed payments are rows "where `GIFT_KEY` is null", meaning "payment expected but not received". "Typical lag is under 4 hours." Verified-vendor-doc: https://docs.virtuous.org/analytics/reference/recurring-giving
- **Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud:** lapse is set by a count of consecutive unpaid transactions, and an "unpaid transaction within the grace period is considered a gift transaction". Secondary (403).
- **False-alert precedent (MPDX):** gifts that include the card-fee top-up trigger "Partner gave a larger gift than commitment". The workaround is to edit the commitment by hand. Verified-vendor-doc: https://help.mpdx.org/article/1085-adjusting-a-recurring-gift-amount-to-reflect-the-eft-credit-card-processing-fee
- **No vendor addresses backfills that arrive weeks or months late.** Unverified inference: every rule keyed to an expected date (MPDX, DonorElf 10/90 days, NPSP 60/90 days, Blackbaud past-due) would flag a legacy-charged partner as late or stopped between backfills. When the batch lands, it would clear the flag or fire "recontinued" or "started giving".

## 5. Transactional vs marketing email

**United States (CAN-SPAM)**

- The FTC says the Act covers email whose primary purpose is "the commercial advertisement or promotion of a commercial product or service". Transactional or relationship content "facilitates an already agreed-upon transaction or updates a customer about an ongoing transaction". Those messages "are otherwise exempt from most provisions" but "may not contain false or misleading routing information." Verified-primary: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- Bloomerang: "the CAN-SPAM Act exempts "transactional or relationship messages"", and donors "would fall within the transactional relationship-type". Secondary (vendor opinion): https://bloomerang.com/blog/guide-when-your-nonprofit-can-and-cannot-send-an-email. Whether nonprofit appeals are "commercial" at all is unsettled (secondary).

**Canada (CASL)**

- A commercial electronic message (CEM) is one whose purpose is "to encourage participation in a commercial activity" (s.1(2)). Consent is not required for a message that solely "facilitates, completes or confirms a commercial transaction" or reports on an ongoing "subscription, membership, account" (s.6(6)). The identification and unsubscribe rules of s.6(2) still apply. Verified-primary: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/page-1.html
- **Implied consent:** "a donation or gift made by the person to whom the message is sent" "within the two-year period immediately before the day on which the message was sent" (s.10(13)). Verified-primary: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/page-2.html
- **Registered-charity exclusion:** applies to a message "sent by or on behalf of a registered charity" where "the message has as its primary purpose raising funds for the charity" (ECPR s.3(g)). Verified-primary: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2013-221/page-1.html
- Charity Law Group: "A pure appeal for donations with no purchase involved is generally not a commercial electronic message." Secondary (Dov Goldberg, updated 2026-10-07): https://www.charitylawgroup.ca/charity-law-questions/casl-for-charities
- **Thank-you from the new system for a gift the old system charged:** no source addresses this. Unverified inference, not legal advice: the sender of record is still the ministry, so changing software does not reset consent. Each legacy-tail gift renews the two-year CASL window. A pure thank-you is probably not a CEM and is a transactional message under CAN-SPAM. The risk is to stewardship (duplicates, confusion), not consent. Adding appeals or welcome-series content moves a message toward marketing rules.

## 6. Duplicate communications during migrations

- **WeGive:** "Before running a data import or syncing historical data through an integration, pause your triggered (auto-trigger) messages", because "imported records — like historical donations — can fire live messages". Admins can also turn off "Create engagement events for data synced via integrations", and should wait until the import "has fully finished processing" before re-enabling messages. Verified-vendor-doc: https://help.wegive.com/articles/1271594711-disable-triggered-messages-before-a-data-import
- **Bloomerang:** imports dated within the last 30 days can enter donation automations (§2). "Imports are largely irreversible so we always recommend running a test file of the first five rows." During a recurring transfer, the old provider keeps processing until a "definitive STOP date on which they'll no longer process." Verified-vendor-doc: https://help.bloomerang.com/en/articles/13382796-how-do-i-transfer-recurring-donor-data-to-bloomerang-fundraising-from-another-provider
- **Fundraise Up:** "No notification is required, so the migration can go largely unnoticed by your supporters". It tells customers to check "'Plan cancellation' or 'Donation failed' emails in your legacy platform" before cancelling plans. Verified-vendor-doc: https://fundraiseup.com/docs/migration-faq/
- **DonorDock:** "Keep your previous system active through the transition period"; "Configure core automations (thank-yous, donation receipts, stewardship journeys) before launch." Verified-vendor-doc: https://www.donordock.com/articles/nonprofit-crm-migration-checklist
- **Chargebee:** "will not trigger any webhooks or send email notifications to customers during import" (verified in q06-r2 §1, not re-fetched).
- No vendor guidance covers a 12-month legacy tail fed by periodic backfills.

## Common pattern

1. **Imports are silent to donors by default, or vendors tell admins to make them silent.** Examples: Planning Center sends no receipts for imports and runs no automations on in-progress batches; Givebutter sends no dedication email; Chargebee sends no notifications; WeGive says to pause triggered messages.
2. **Where silence is not the default, the burden falls on the admin.** Bloomerang's 30-day window and its First Ever Donation re-entry are the documented failure mode.
3. **Acknowledgement is a per-gift status, separate from whether the gift counts in totals.** "Already acknowledged elsewhere" is a recognised value: NPSP's "alternative method", Raiser's Edge NXT integration defaults, the DonorPerfect "neither" option, and Fundraise Up setting Neon's "Has been acknowledged".
4. **Missionary tools run a separate, staff-facing channel.** It covers new, started, missed and special gifts and creates thank-you tasks (MPDX, DonorHub, DonorElf), and missionaries use it to write personal thanks.
5. **Lapse rules assume timely data.** Grace windows are measured in hours to weeks, not monthly backfills.

## Implications for (a)/(b)/(c)

- **(a) Silent history** matches the dominant import pattern. The legacy system has already thanked and receipted these gifts, so an Asym thank-you would be a duplicate. Because it would land a week to a month after the charge, it would also miss the promptness that Burk's effect depends on. Gaps: missionaries lose the started-giving and new-partner cue they use to thank people personally. Lapse and late logic also needs to know that these plans are expected to arrive through legacy backfills, or Asym will raise false missed-gift signals (unverified inference).
- **(b) Normal donor workflows** runs against every migration source found (WeGive, the Bloomerang 30-day hazard). It risks duplicate and late thanks, and a duplicate tax receipt (CRA allows only marked replacements). It would also start first-gift or welcome series for long-standing donors. Consent is probably not the obstacle; donor trust is (CanadaHelps).
- **(c) Silent to donors, visible to missionaries and staff** matches mission-agency practice: the agency receipts, the missionary thanks personally, and MPD tools notify. It only works if staff signals understand backfills. "Started giving" or "new supporter" must not fire for donors with legacy history. Missed-gift alerts should be held while a legacy plan is active. Alerts that arrive weeks late cannot support a prompt thank-you, so a backfill digest is likely better than per-gift alerts (unverified). The MPDX fee false-alert shows how data artefacts become missionary noise.
