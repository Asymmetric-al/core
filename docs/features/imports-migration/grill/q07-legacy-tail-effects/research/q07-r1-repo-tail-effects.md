## 2. Coverage and lapse logic

- C22 (DL:288, R/P); Coverage start "first gift, lapsed, first support stay blocked" (CTX:73-75). Coverage is defined only as a **start** date. No term covers the tail's moving **covered-through** date (last backfill).
- No person-level lapsed label: FS:52-53 "SHALL NOT label… 'lapsed'"; OC:1148-1149 "contribution inactivity, not a… lapsed state"; P16:1068 stale sources produce "**Status updating**, suppress negative outreach… Unknown never defaults to healthy, lapsed, zero, or received" (R).
- **Lag exposure.** P16 review intervals for manual/external expectation evidence: "24 hours for a proved structured feed; structured-feed overrides are bounded from 5 minutes through 7 days" (P16:1072, R). A monthly (or weekly plus review) backfill exceeds the maximum, so an external tail expectation would enter review between backfills unless the planned tail is projected separately. P16 already moves "quarantined future support… to a qualified attention bucket" (P16:1316); Q1R4 G15 (Pr): "every SiteStacker recurring gift could look at-risk on day one". A DON-13 cohort frozen just before a backfill would include donors whose gifts are pending.
- **Owner wording for incomplete external coverage:** P25F:33 distinguishes "known incomplete external coverage, source Updating" (R, donor portal); P16:1068 "Status updating"; RM:4643 "through-date". O17's "given through previous system, last updated {date}" (DL:348) is **Pr** and appears in no owner document. Q1AR:758 (Pr) proposed it: "never as lapsed, missed or needing attention solely because of catch-up lag."
- **Code (I):** missionary status is a stored, staff/missionary-editable label, not computed: `packages/api/src/missionary-portal/donors.ts:174-192` maps unknown to "Active". "Needs Attention" = At Risk or Lapsed (`apps/missionary/app/donors/donors-list-model.ts:21-22`). Admin CRM "at risk" = active pledge with past `next_payment_date` (`packages/api/src/admin/crm/detail/service.ts:187-198`). Nothing recomputes on insert, so backfill lag cannot flip a label today.

## 3. Timing

- Cadence: "fixed cadence" chosen per Switch, tail ≤12 months by default (DL:36, R); the value is not set. Q1AR:33 (Pr) adds "a final backfill after the dispute and return windows for the last legacy charge"; Q1AR:1008 (Pr) monitor threshold "cadence plus 7 days"; Q1AR:1342 (Pr) manifests span "Switch Boundary minus 120 days to the export time".
- Lateness: a charge arrives after up to one cadence period, plus export, review and match review ("a fact with no exact binding match waits in match review", DL:36). PayPal tail gifts come "only from the ministry's PayPal reports" (DL:38). Refund and chargeback outcomes can follow months later.
- **No staleness limit anywhere** (P7, P27, P28, P35, CAT). CAT `clock_policy` "three business days" (CAT:1173) is response time, not gift age. Closest: "delayed delivery never causes the system to pretend the event occurred now" (WL:55); "processing time is never a new gift date or activation event" (FS:102-103); "A source event marked historical cannot become prospective by changing a displayed timestamp" (RM:4395).

## 4. Duplication risk

What the old system and processors send during the tail:

- SiteStacker "Contribution Receipt", "sent… as soon as the donor submits" (Q4R4:27, verified-vendor-doc); **per recurring charge: unverified** (Q4R4:28). Q1R2:218 says Mastercard rules require Receipt and Recurring Scheduling Confirmation/Updated on.
- Contributions Declined with `updatePaymentURL`; a later fix re-charges after the switch (Q1R2:184-185, :360).
- Spark "Recurring Schedule Reminder" pre-billing (Q1R2:219).
- **Spark New Contribution automations:** "Send message to Donor… Thank you", "Send message to Campaign Owner… New Offline Donation to Your Campaign", "Add Missionary Given To relation" (Q2R1:210). Attributes include "Is First Time Donation", "Is First/Last Recurring" (Q1R1:46).
- Gateway receipts (PaymentSpring `send_receipt`; Stripe) and PayPal notices are outside the ministry's control (Q1R2:220-221; DL:37).

Would duplicate: any Asym per-gift thank-you (vs Contribution Receipt and Spark thank-you); MPD-01 or first-support tasks (vs Spark campaign-owner notice); DON-08 / P17 refund notices (vs processor notices); tribute digests. Clause 7 keeps receipts and recurring messages on and turns "appeal and record-changing automations" off (DL:37); **Spark gift-thank-you and missionary-notice automations are in neither list.**

- **Code (I):** gift reads filter only settled status (`packages/api/src/missionary-portal/service.ts:197-201`); `donations.source` is hidden from missionaries (`supabase/migrations/20261005050728_field_policy_foundation.sql:185-189`); dashboard alerts cover tasks and donor count only (`packages/missionary/components/dashboard-view.ts:87-102`); tasks are never auto-generated (`missionary-portal/tasks.ts:93`). ## 6. Handover moment

- Formal cutover "requires proof the old executor stopped plus applicable fresh IDs and collection authorization" (P16:1314). It creates a "fresh group/line/auth/binding" (P16:2020) with a donor step (P16:1242) (R). Predecessor/successor links exist (P16:1985) but no rule says downstream consumers treat a successor as continuing.
- **Messages:** UI shows "Success + next scheduled gift" (P16:554). "Recurring arrangement created/confirmed" is "Deferred unless exact producer contract exists" (P17C:357); P25F:85 keeps "initial confirmations". No handover or welcome message is defined in P16 or P17.
- **First gift / new donor:** the first Asym-processed charge is not first where pre-boundary history is imported (FS:271-273; CAT:1163). Exception: a donor whose whole history is tail charges, if those charges are absent or late. DON-09's trigger ("new authorized commitment", CAT:1461) **would match the fresh handover line**; only "orientation only if not already owned by source" (CAT:1463) restrains it. P25X:77 forbids a persisted "new-donor/welcome-completed flag".

## 7. Consent

- C10 (DL:276) cites RM:4381, which is **SMS-only** ("not automatic SMS consent"). General support: "verification and contact import alone provide no consent" (`docs/adr/0170-…md:129`); "Donation alone is not subscription consent" (CAT:3291); "never invents opt-in from a gift" (P25X:49). Q3 consent columns are "evidence only, never consent" (DL:83).
- **Transactional vs marketing:** shipped gate classes are only `"transactional" | "marketing"` (`packages/email/types.ts:17`). Receipts and correction notices are transactional; unknown types default to marketing (`packages/api/src/email/consent.ts:24-35`). P14:541: "transactional/relational classes bypass marketing opt-outs but never `do_not_contact`". P6/P9/P17 never classify a donor thank-you; Phase 35 sends are "consent-gated" (OC:1139-1140).
- So a thank-you classed transactional/relational needs no marketing consent; a stewardship/marketing class needs consent Asym does not hold for imported donors.
- **Adverse-fact lag:** old-system unsubscribes reach Asym only at the next backfill, restrict-only (DL:36). Asym-recorded opt-outs are "pending in legacy systems" (DL:42). An Asym thank-you between backfills could reach someone who opted out in the old system.

## 8. Current code (verifies q04-r3 §7)

Confirmed, with three corrections:

- **Triggers:** only currency normalization on `donations` (`20261003051630_currency_foundation.sql:221-222`); no AFTER trigger, pg_net or pg_cron on gift tables.
- **Inngest:** seven functions (`packages/api/src/workflows/serve.ts:23-31`); none sends, tasks or notifies.
- **Stripe `payment_intent.succeeded`:** writes a `staged_gifts` row with `receipt_status: "pending"` (`packages/api/src/stripe/webhooks.ts:289-321`; `giving/staged-gifts.ts:350-354`). `invoice.paid` only bumps pledge counters (`stripe/recurring.ts:254-265`).
- **Receipts are staff-initiated:** `admin/contributions/staged-gifts.ts:288`, plus a **third path**, the single or bulk `resend_receipt` action (`contribution-operations/dependencies.ts:27-28`), with no `source` check (also Q1AR:419).
- **Insert paths:** `begin_donation_saga` is not the only writer; uncalled `atomic_create_donation_with_audit` (`20260223170000_atomic_rpc_and_donation_saga.sql:799`) also inserts.
- **Origin markers:** only `donations.source` (default `'direct'`), read solely by the staff grid (`admin/contributions/model.ts:218-253`).
- **Not found:** thank-you/welcome/first-gift email, missionary notification, tribute send, LTV/LYBUNT/retention.

## Gaps and conflicts

1. **No carrier binds tail facts to any suppression key.** Candidates: `historical=true` (WR:25, P), import entry-origin (P14:1487-1490, R), entry method `import`/`legacy` (C21), "live/history cohort" (FS:102), Recorded outside Asym (Phase 7 receipt fact, "not a contribution status", CTX:136). Phase 13 has no event-level origin flag; O1/O13 are Pr. A tail gift is post-boundary, so "historical" is semantically wrong for it. Reusing the Phase 7 fact for Phase 28/35 gating needs owner amendment (C3).
2. **"Backfill" name collision.** Studio darkness lifts when "a bounded backfill request was reviewed" (WR:25; WSA-021; RM:4395). A reviewed Legacy Tail Backfill could be misread as that authorization. Q1AR:417's "Approving a catch-up plan approves data only" (Pr) is not in ratified clause 6.
3. **No Asym donor thank-you exists to make "eligible".** F5's real choice is about Phase 35 recipes, Phase 28 tasks and tributes, plus whether to create a new acknowledgment meaning.
4. **Covered-through gap.** C22 and Coverage start handle missing old history, not lagging tail data. P16:1072's 7-day maximum conflicts with any monthly cadence. O17 is unanswered, and DL:144 keeps missionary views unchanged.
5. **Spark gift-triggered thank-you and missionary-notice automations** have no Q1 clause 7 disposition.
6. **Per-charge SiteStacker recurring receipt unverified** (Q4R4:28), yet clause 7 assumes "SiteStacker alone receipts what it charges".
7. **Handover** has no message set; DON-09 would treat the fresh line as new.
8. **Refund notices:** P17M:800 leaves notice-requiredness to the producing command; O13 does not say tail reversals need none.
9. **C10 citation** (RM:4381) is SMS-scoped; email thank-you consent class is undefined.
10. P7:339 still permits receipting imported gifts until O22 lands; live `resend_receipt` has no origin check. Whether backfills carry tributes or soft credits is unstated. DL's O2 cite `:1183` should be CAT:1163.
