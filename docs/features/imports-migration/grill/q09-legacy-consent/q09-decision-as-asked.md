# Q9 — Legacy consent and suppression evidence: decision as asked and chosen

Founder answer (2026-11-02): **"B. Bring the evidence across. Research modern best pratices and make sure we are doing it correctly and will work well."**

## Options as asked

- **A. Unsubscribes only; everyone re-confirms.** Every block is imported, nobody keeps any permission, and every donor must opt in again.
- **B. Bring the evidence across (chosen).** Donors keep receiving what they received before unless they opted out. Nobody is added to anything new. Proof is required only where the law demands it.
- **C. Trust the old system's flags.** Old subscription flags become Asym permissions as they are.

## Option B as proposed (six parts; ★ = amends earlier decisions or another phase's rule)

1. **Unsubscribes first, always.** Every opt-out, do-not-contact, do-not-solicit, do-not-mail, do-not-call, deceased marker, hard bounce and spam complaint from the old system and its email provider is imported before anything else as a lasting block. Each block keeps its original scope (all channels if unknown), source and date. A row whose block cannot be recorded stays blocked (Phase 6 rule, D17 C08). A blank means unknown, never permission.
2. ★ **The ministry's email provider is part of the switch** (amends Q1 clauses 1 and 12). If the ministry uses Mailchimp or similar, its unsubscribed, cleaned and complaint lists, freshly exported at the final import, are a required source. That audience joins Q1's "pending in legacy systems" list, so opt-outs recorded in Asym are applied there while it is still in use.
3. ★ **Permission carried over from evidence** (amends Q3 clause 3 "evidence only, never consent"). An imported subscription becomes the matching Asym email permission only through a recorded mapping: the old list maps to an Asym list, the evidence is kept (list, source, date, any form wording), and the Switch lead attests to it. Donors keep what they had and are never added to new lists. By jurisdiction: US carried over unless opted out; Canada carried over under the registered-charity fundraising exemption or the 2-year gift rule (recomputed from gift dates) or with express-consent evidence; UK/EU only with real consent evidence, otherwise one re-confirmation request. SMS unchanged: exact-number evidence only.
4. ★ **Missionary newsletters carry over the same way if the ministry ran them in the old system** (amends Phase 28's Join rule for migrated lists only). Missionaries' own outside Mailchimp lists stay theirs (Q1 clause 7).
5. **A sending gate.** No newsletter, update or appeal goes to imported donors until Phase 12's permission check (#680) exists. First sends go to recent givers and engaged readers before the full list.
6. **Deceased status.** Asym gains a deceased status that blocks all contact.
