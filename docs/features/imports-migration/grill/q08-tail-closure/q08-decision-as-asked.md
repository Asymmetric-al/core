# Q8 — Moving tail donors and closing the tail: decision as asked and chosen

Founder answer (2026-11-02): **"A. End with notice. do a final check before done. we need the best possible UX and UI for the user and tenant. Think through the whole journey. we need to follow modern best pratices here."** The founder then required a full adversarial review before recording.

## Options as asked

- **A. End with notice (chosen)** — donors not moved by the cap have their gift ended by the ministry with a dated notice and a one-click restart link.
- **B. Keep running where possible** — PayPal and gateway schedules keep charging indefinitely; SiteStacker-run gifts must still move or end. Needs an ongoing owner intake that does not exist.
- **C. Extend until everyone moves** — the tail stays open; old system stays licensed.

## Option A as proposed (six parts; ★ = amends earlier decisions or sets donor-facing policy)

1. ★ **Default at the cap: end with notice.** Arrangements not moved are ended by the ministry; the donor gets a dated notice at least 30 days before the last legacy charge, with a one-click restart link. "Keep running as external" stays available only as a recorded exception once an owner intake exists (O13), never for SiteStacker-run gifts.
2. **A standard move campaign** (owned by Phase 16/17 move messages, O12). Week 1, on the day the old system stops taking new gifts: every tail donor gets an invitation with a prefilled one-click confirmation (or a re-entry link where card details could not be copied). Weekly reminders for weeks 2–5. A "not yet moved" list for staff and each supporter's missionary for personal follow-up. Then the end notice. Do-not-contact donors get no campaign emails; staff handle them personally. The card-copy request goes to the old processor on switch day.
3. ★ **Moves never double-charge** (amends Q1 clause 8). A moved gift's first Asym charge falls on the next due date after the old arrangement has provably stopped. PayPal: the donor confirms in Asym, the ministry cancels the PayPal subscription, and Asym's first charge follows the proof it stopped.
4. ★ **Closing the tail.** The tail closes when every arrangement has moved or ended, or at the cap. After the last legacy charge, batches carry only refunds and disputes (from processor reports) until 120 days (card) or 60 days (bank debit) after that charge; amends Q1's "no batch after closure" by making that window part of the tail. SiteStacker can be switched off once its last charge has run.
5. ★ **Weekly batches by default** (amends Q7 clause 4). Weekly; the ministry may choose every two weeks; a batch with nothing to review is approved in one step; staleness judged on the covered-through date, which Phase 16 accepts for these arrangements.
6. **Attrition is measured.** The first switch records how many donors moved, when and through which path, to recalibrate the 12-month cap and the campaign.
