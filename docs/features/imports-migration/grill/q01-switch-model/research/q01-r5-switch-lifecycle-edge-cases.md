## 3. End-to-end lifecycle of one planned switch (PROPOSED)

### 3.1 Three identities and one boundary (the backbone)

| Concept                        | Definition (proposed)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Why                                                                                                                                                                                      | Basis                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Switch**                     | One ministry-level record: scope (Tenant; source system and instance), state, named owners, go/no-go criteria, boundary, snapshot manifests, runs, reconciliation, acceptance, late-tail window, closure evidence.                                                                | Groups everything a ministry signs off; lets a second attempt supersede an aborted one without losing evidence.                                                                          | A2 "batch identity", A5 "import-run evidence"; K6                                      |
| **Switch Boundary**            | One instant per source family (v1: the same instant for all families), stored as UTC instant + tenant-declared IANA zone + civil date/time, interpreted **half-open**: source occurrences strictly before the instant are pre-switch history; at or after it are late-tail facts. | Every row is classified once, at plan approval, against a recorded fact, not a displayed date (A2, A3, `roadmap.md:4395` "cannot become prospective by changing a displayed timestamp"). | A24 (half-open per source family); A23 (tenant IANA giving timezone, `phase-16-…:112`) |

**Boundary time zone and date-only facts (proposed).** SiteStacker's export time zone is undocumented (unverified), and Historic Giving and many grid columns may be date-only (r07 template columns; unverified for grids). Recommend a **date-aligned boundary** (00:00 local on the switch day in the declared zone). Then every date-only fact classifies unambiguously (date < switch day → history), and only time-stamped facts on the switch day itself need care. Any row whose timestamp zone cannot be established and falls within one day of the boundary goes to review rather than being guessed. Basis: A24 half-open boundary; A26 (avoid "mutable cutoff"); inferred.

### 3.2 Switch states (proposed)

`draft` → `readiness_blocked` | `ready` → `rehearsing` (repeatable) → `scheduled` (go/no-go criteria and boundary proposed) → `frozen` (boundary recorded; ministry freeze attested) → `snapshot_received` → `snapshot_accepted` | `snapshot_rejected` (re-export) → `importing` → `reconciling` → `accepted` (target owners and ministry sign-off) → `live` (Asym authoritative for in-scope families) → `tail_open` → `tail_closed` → `decommissioned`.

Abort branches: `aborted_before_commit` (from any state before `importing`); `aborted_after_commit` (from `importing`/`reconciling`/`accepted`, before `live`); after `live` there is **no abort**, only roll-forward correction (§3.13). A later attempt creates a new Switch that `supersedes` the aborted one.

Terminal visibility: O11 (`decision-log.md`) says post-handoff run failures are invisible today; every state and every run needs a visible terminal state and a cause-owned next action (`roadmap.md:4389` "cause-owned next action rather than a generic failed batch").

### 3.5 Stage S2 — Scheduling and go/no-go criteria

| Item                                 | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Timing guidance                      | Avoid year-end giving season, books close and staff vacations (MS Learn: "Try to pick a time when your business is less busy… Don't do the cutover when you have to close your books or report your results. Consider holidays and vacations, too", https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/guidance/implementation-guide/prepare-go-live-cutover-strategy, accessed 2026-11-01, verified-vendor-doc).
