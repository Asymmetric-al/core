# Q04-R4: External verification of the switch-year tax-document approach (Option A)

_Read-only web research. It extends `q04-r2-tax-rules-and-vendor-practice.md` and does not repeat it. Every URL was accessed on 2026-11-02. This is not legal advice._

Labels:

- **verified-primary**: a statute or tax-authority page, fetched and the quote checked.
- **verified-vendor-doc**: the vendor's own page, fetched and the quote checked.
- **secondary**: a third-party page, or a search snippet whose page was not fetched.
- **unverified**: not found, or my inference.

The fetch tool caps each quote at about 125 characters, so long sentences appear in fragments.

## Findings that bear on Option A

1. **SiteStacker's year-end statement is its tax document.** Its template says "This letter will serve as your receipt for tax purposes" and certifies that no goods or services were received. No SiteStacker article shows that the per-gift email carries that certification. So Part 1's "skip the old system's year-end statement" should not be the default for SiteStacker.
2. **The Statements report is an add-on in SiteStacker.** The article says to email support to add it to an installation. Nothing on the training site covers Canada, CRA receipts or receipt numbers.
3. **Receipt data in exports is common but means different things.** In Bloomerang, a "receipt" email does not mark a gift as acknowledged. Receipt numbers appear mainly in Raiser's Edge NXT, DonorPerfect, Little Green Light and Virtuous.
4. **No vendor found labels each gift with the system that issues its tax document.** Fundraise Up is the closest match. It shows imported CRM gifts in its donor portal, says it "cannot issue receipts for them", and links to the receipt the CRM issued.
5. **December is very large.** M+R reports 37% of 2025 online revenue in December and 4% on 31 December. No neutral source mandates a 1 January switch.
6. **Canadian source documents are kept for six years.** Copies of receipts are kept for two years, but the CRA checklist keeps "source documents" for six. The CRA says nothing on serial-number prefixes or changing software.

Recommendation (inferred): keep receipt status as **optional provenance**, mapped per template, with "unknown" as the default. If every "unknown" gift becomes its own checklist item, nearly every import will be flagged. Let the ministry attest once per batch instead.

## 4. Seasonality and timing

- **M+R Benchmarks 2026 (2025 data).** "In 2025, nonprofits received 37% of all online revenue in December alone." "The last week of the year accounted for 10%". "The last day of the year accounted for 4% of annual revenue." GivingTuesday fell in December in 2025, which inflates the December figure. Source: 2026.mrbenchmarks.com/fundraising/, **verified-vendor-doc** (industry study).
- **Blackbaud Institute (2025 data).** December was "roughly 18% of annual giving" across all channels, and Q4 was "More than 36%". Source: finviz.com/news/338352, **secondary**.
- **Statement deadlines.** CRA suggests receipts by 28 February. US practice is 31 January (r2, verified-primary).
- **Migration timing guidance.** No neutral source recommends 1 January.
  - Pushpay marketing: "January is the perfect time to think about switching" (grow.pushpay.com/lp-demo-start-strong.html, verified-vendor-doc).
  - DonorDock: "Build in a buffer around any major fundraising deadlines, year-end campaigns" (verified-vendor-doc).
  - Mpire: "Migrating during campaign season can affect donations and donor retention". It also cites a case in which "Thousands of donors did not receive tax receipt confirmations during year-end giving" (verified-vendor-doc, consultant blog).
  - Virtuous: align the switch with "your software renewal date and your budget cycle" (verified-vendor-doc).
- **Inferred: a 1 January switch does not prevent a split year when there is a Legacy Recurring Tail.** Tail gifts the old system charges after 1 January make that new year a split year, and the old system must still document them. The timing advice should say so.

## 5. Canada

- **More than one receipt per donor per year.** CRA: "A registered charity may issue receipts periodically through the year" or, for cash, "issue one cumulative receipt for the year" (when-should-a-charity-issue-a-receipt.html, page date 2018-11-01, **verified-primary**). No CRA text limits how many receipts a donor may receive in a year; the limit is per gift (r2). The proposed pattern is consistent with the CRA's periodic-receipt rule (application **inferred**): an old-system receipt for pre-switch and tail gifts, plus an Asym receipt for Asym gifts.
- **Serial numbers.** "Each receipt must have its own unique serial number" (sample-official-donation-receipts.html, 2021-12-01, **verified-primary**). Searches of canada.ca found nothing on prefixes, numbering formats or changing receipting software (**unverified / not found**).
  - CPS-014 mentions "sequential numbering" only in examples, not as a rule (**verified-primary**, 2017-09-22). Little Green Light's claim that the law "requires … sequential receipt numbers" goes beyond the CRA text found.
  - Charity Law Group: "Charities design their own numbering systems" and "The system doesn't matter as long as each receipt gets a unique number" (**secondary**).
  - Inferred: the receipts belong to the charity, so numbers must be unique across the charity, not per software. A distinct prefix is a sound way to achieve that. Record the switch-over numbers so an auditor can explain any gap.
- **Retention.**
  - Receipt copies: "two years from the end of the calendar year that you received the donations". Cancelled or reissued receipts: two years after the year of that action. Records must be kept in an "electronically readable format" at the Canadian address on file. With a third party, "you are still responsible" (books-records.html, 2025-09-08, **verified-primary**).
  - CRA checklist: "Financial statements, source documents, and copies of T3010 returns" are kept **six years** from the end of the last tax year they relate to (keeping-adequate-books-records.html, 2025-10-14, **verified-primary**).
  - **Change to Part 8:** keep the old system's gift and deposit records for six years, as well as its receipt copies for two years.
- **Electronic copies.** "the computer-stored receipt can be considered a duplicate for purposes of the Act" (CPS-014, **verified-primary**). T4033 was not fetched.

## 6. United States

- **No duty to send a year-end statement.** Confirmed in r2 (Pub 1771: "incurs no penalty").
- **An informational summary still counts as a record.** IRC §170(f)(17) allows any monetary gift to be substantiated by "a bank record or a written communication from the donee … showing the name of the donee organization, the date of the contribution, and the amount" (law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/170, **verified-primary**). So a ministry-branded summary with those details is a valid record for gifts under $250, whatever its disclaimer says. **Inferred:** say "not your official year-end acknowledgment; gifts marked X are acknowledged by {old system}" rather than "not a tax document", which is arguably inaccurate. The summary is harmless as long as amounts and dates are correct. Even so, a donor who treats it as the CWA for a gift of $250 or more, when the old system never sent one, has a _Durden_-type problem (r2). Make sure the old system's document is actually issued. No IRS guidance on informational summaries was found.
- **Donor notice wording.**
  - Church notices:
    - CyLife (r2).
    - VCC: "we transferred your January - October TOTAL for each Fund into Subsplash as one transaction for each Fund", on 20 Dec 2024 (vcc.nucleus.church/subsplash, verified church page).
    - St. Mike's: history from the old platform "might not show… it will show up on your annual statement" (secondary; PDF unreadable).
  - Vendor templates:
    - Nucleus: "you may need to set it up once more in the new one"; "Our previous online giving system will close on September 30" (verified-vendor-doc).
    - ChurchTrac: "We are switching to a new giving platform that will make giving easier" (verified-vendor-doc).
  - No IRS guidance on transition notices was found.

## 7. Donor-experience evidence

- **Kalos Church (Overflow case study).** It switched in June: "I had maybe 3 or 4 issues out of 400 donors" (overflow.co, **verified-vendor-doc**, marketing).
- **Planning Center.** Frames the problem as churches' worry about donors "receiving split year-end statements". Its remedy is to import history and "send a single year-end statement" (planningcenter.com/blog/2026/07/…, **verified-vendor-doc**).
- **Failed acknowledgments after a CRM migration** (Mpire, §4).
- **No forum threads or quantitative study** on mid-year switch complaints were found (**unverified**).
- **Mitigations observed:**
  - Import history and issue a single statement (Planning Center, Givebutter).
  - Tell donors to "keep both" statements (CyLife).
  - Consolidate pre-switch totals into one line per fund (VCC).
  - Hide imports from the donor portal until validated (Fundraise Up), or keep them off statements until validated (Virtuous, r2).
  - Run staged re-setup campaigns for recurring gifts (Nucleus, ChurchTrac).
