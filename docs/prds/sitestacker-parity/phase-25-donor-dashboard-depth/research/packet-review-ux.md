## Required corrections

### 1. Main scope exclusion must preserve the explicitly required message contract

`main.md` Out of Scope excludes “new message families” without qualification. EX12 explicitly requires one new qualified Newsletter request received semantic contract/two-step generation, and EX08 explicitly expands the donor presentation of thirteen existing meanings. The broad exclusion can be misread as blocking required implementation.

Use **“additional message families beyond the finite EX08 donor catalog, EX12 newsletter-request contract and independently qualified inherited required notices.”** Keep automatic export-ready notifications and a new messaging inbox out of scope. No new product choice is necessary.

### 2. Ordinary GET safety must not outlaw the qualified OAuth callback

EX02 says GET/HEAD cannot redeem proof. IC03 correctly states that an ordinary validated OAuth callback GET may exchange its code after state/PKCE/nonce/request checks and must not inherit emailed-scanner semantics. These are different protocols.

Narrow EX02's first sentence to **ordinary navigation/content GET/HEAD and emailed-action doorway/scanner behavior**. Add: **“The separately qualified OAuth callback exchange described in IC03 remains permitted after its native protocol and request checks; this is not a side effect of merely reading a portal page.”** Preserve the strict fragment/explicit-POST email proof and no unsolicited GET business effect. US25-U06's narrower no-read/consent/message/financial-effect acceptance does not need expansion into a new Auth test story.
