## Exact corrected decision to record

> Provide an optional, bookmarkable ministry overview inside the existing private donor portal. It composes a currently safe ministry context, a bounded preview of independently permitted Ministry Updates and a separate, precisely scoped giving section. It uses one exact P22 Page and immutable typed subject as the context anchor, through a narrowly extended P22-owned private association read; no universal Ministry entity or inferred designation-to-content join is introduced. Financial scope is one explicitly identified, owner-qualified current Page Giving Binding, not all funds ever associated with a ministry. Current human reading rights and personal/represented financial rights remain independent. Use a calm shared Maia presentation, up to three admitted Update previews and up to two matching source-defined Current recurring arrangements, preserving exact line states, complete source-qualified material context and clear links to the existing full journeys. Ordinary previews honor Hide. Existing direct task links remain direct. The overview creates no total, impact claim, balance, new financial command, enrollment, notification, copied feed or private CMS authority.

### A1 — Make the ministry association exact, with honest financial scope

The narrow permanent solution is a **private association descriptor returned by the existing P22 owner**, keyed to one Page, its immutable typed subject and compatible source versions. This is an explicit required owner-contract extension: the currently inspected code does not implement it. It is a projection over existing owner records, not a new durable portal registry.

The descriptor identifies one exact current D7 Designation binding for the financial context and one independently qualified D3 source/purpose binding for reading. Current means the owner's applicable association/configuration, **not eligibility to receive a new donation**: a disabled giving destination can still have authorized History and recurring records. An older gift or arrangement whose financial binding differs keeps its existing exact History/detail destination. It is never silently continued in the new fund. No historical-overview mode, picker or binding-history union is added. The person may separately open an independently qualified current overview, whose exact fund label makes that new context clear.

Never auto-union Page binding history, choose the first Page for a fund, infer the relationship from names/authors, or call a one-fund view all of the donor's giving to the ministry. Where the owner cannot establish a complete association, preserve the independent existing record/reader and omit the optional combined entry. This is proper source qualification, not a name-matching fallback. All available History remains reachable.

### A2 — One page, independently authorized facts

P12 resolves the authenticated human and current server-validated portal context. Reading is admitted for that human and the exact source/purpose/release. Financial data is admitted for the exact personal or represented legal donor, issuer/currency/field/history scope and action. The ministry label/media have their own current disclosure floor. Each proof is independent; a gift, follow, public Page, spouse, shared email or represented organization grants none of the others.

A revoked financial grant removes its section without changing the human's Updates preferences. A withdrawn public Page does not erase authorized historical gifts. An unavailable or unsafe identity cannot be reconstructed from a gift label, URL, metadata or cached editorial content. A source-safe generic unavailable destination may retain ordinary donor navigation without revealing the denied association.

### A3 — Make the overview small, readable and useful

Use an ordinary full page in the established donor shell. Keep the logical order **context → Ministry Updates → Your giving**, with simple section links for quick mobile/keyboard access. Use up to **three** source-ordered Update previews and up to **two** real Current recurring-arrangement previews; full reading, all arrangements, History and exact management remain their existing owner journeys. These are reviewed product defaults, not empirically optimal counts.

Use a neutral **Recurring giving** label within the financial section. Current group membership does not mean every matching line is active: a matching ended line remains Ended even when another permitted sibling keeps its real group Current. Display no group-as-ministry amount, derived ministry total, copied annual card, extra latest-gift card, chart, support-health score or causal impact. Precise task links remain direct. No new contextual donation CTA is required; ordinary already-authorized giving navigation remains available.

### A4 — Reuse the existing owners and prove the reached paths

Put the composite read orchestration in `packages/api`, with thin donor routes and shared `@asym/ui` composition. P22 supplies identity/association and canonical released content through its owning service; P13/P16/P12 supply financial matching and authority. Reuse approved Query/collection adapters as appropriate. No browser raw-table join, mutable donor counter, legacy `partners` visibility or CMS preview becomes the source.

Opening, refreshing, following links or pressing Back has no business mutation. Changes remain in the already-ratified preference/financial journeys, with their own authorization, concurrency and idempotency. Complete the narrowly required owner/projection/navigation contracts and target tests before enabling the overview. Missing predecessor functionality is recorded as adoption work, not disguised as an existing capability.

### Association and rebind contract

Resolve the exact Page origin where available. A financial designation alone cannot identify a unique Page. If an explicit owner association admits several distinct destinations, offer a bounded deliberate selection only after the person asks for an overview, with safe labels and source-qualified continuation; never expose inaccessible candidate names/counts. If safe choices cannot be distinguished, do not guess. Existing financial/reading tasks stay available.

The descriptor pins applicable Tenant/Legal Entity/environment, exact Page/typed subject, current selected D7 binding/Designation, current permitted D3 source/purpose set, locale, source generations and relevant authorization/safety epochs. It also supplies safe navigation descriptors for History, recurring detail/continuation and full reading. Those descriptors are revalidated at their destination; they are not bearer capabilities.

**History and recurring matching use exactly the descriptor's selected Designation.** Copy should identify that actual giving destination, for example “Giving to River Community Fund.” A current fund can contain old gifts; a current-binding scope is not a current-year date filter. Older records for a different binding remain in their exact financial destinations, with ordinary All available History available. Do not say “All your giving to River Community” unless a separate existing owner proves that exact complete population; this review does not create such an aggregate/population. The regular All available History route remains discoverable for other gifts.

A D7 successor does not move old gifts, rewrite the old binding or reinterpret a bookmarked financial origin as the new Give target. A source association change while a composed request is outstanding invalidates the obsolete composite. Return a typed `context_changed` state, discard incompatible results and resolve a newly coherent current context with visible changed scope before using its financial links. Existing exact old financial links remain with their source records and current financial authorization; they are not upgraded to the current fund. No historical overview mode or browser union of past/current bindings is allowed.

P22 public reach alone neither grants nor denies private finance/content. Retirement can make the combined association unavailable while authorized gifts remain in History. The private descriptor may continue only with independently current private source, safe-label and association proof; it cannot replay a withdrawn public editorial body or stale feed binding. No new retirement-specific donor identity or copied history store is created.

### Financial and reading completeness

The financial section is a precisely labeled **view into records**, not an aggregate. A USD100 gift split USD40 here/USD60 elsewhere remains one gift where its parent is independently admitted. Matching USD40 does not permit showing an otherwise forbidden parent or sibling. Charitable fee cover, corrections and refunds remain source-defined in History; Q23's fee-inclusive whole-contribution headline is not reused here.

Established recurring lines match the Designation in their **current effective terms**. Future changes or pending replacement proposals do not move the match or replace the current amount/cadence. A source-admitted `pending_activation` arrangement with no running terms may use its accepted pending descriptor, visibly Pending rather than Active. Unaccepted/rejected drafts never create a preview. A live line moved to another Designation is not current support here merely because its older terms matched; former terms remain in explicitly historical owning detail.

Qualify the full permitted set before previewing. Current/Past is Q22's real arrangement classification, including paused, pending and source-defined residual states. Then match allowed lines to the selected Designation. Keep the real parent, exact matching line states and source-required material information. Displaying two arrangements is not evidence that there are only two, that everything is active, or that no work needs attention. A scoped complete-recurring destination must actually apply the matching descriptor; a broadly labeled Recurring giving link is not falsely presented as filtered.

Updates use the exact owner-certified binding, human-reader audience projection, canonical ordering and Q07 Hide before the three-preview limit. A Page with several sources is not one publisher preference. The full reader must accept that same owner-qualified context; do not implement a client-side author/name union. One canonical Update is not duplicated because several binding members can place it. No hidden-post title/count/thumbnail teaser is returned.

## Data, database and currentness safeguards

**Minimum boundary:** the app requests an opaque exact context; the server authenticates, resolves the verified host/environment/Tenant and validates the requested financial subject/action. P22 produces current association/label proofs; P13/P16 and P22 D11 execute their own scoped reads. All browser-visible payloads are allowlisted per field. Subject/actor/tenant/owner/author/audit attribution are never accepted as authority from caller input.

Use independent typed section outcomes: loading is UI-only; admitted data, certified empty/not-applicable, retryable unavailable, denied and context-changed are distinct server meanings. A JSON object missing a required collection is invalid, not certified empty. Include only the source watermarks needed to reject mixed/obsolete results. There is no promise of one cross-domain atomic business snapshot: each section is a currently admitted projection with its own watermark. A shared association change invalidates all dependent sections; one transient financial read failure does not invalidate independent reading.

**Schema/relationship discipline:** use owner-native IDs and full Tenant/Legal Entity/environment keys. Page→typed subject and binding→Page/Designation/source members require source-enforced references, correct nullability and immutable released versions/current-head rules. A caller cannot mutate tenant/subject/source/key fields to transform a permitted row into another owner's row. No new overview money column, balance, user↔ministry join table, array of follower IDs or nullable catch-all “ministry_id” is introduced. Historical source records follow their existing retention and append-only corrections; Page withdrawal does not cascade-delete contributions or authorizations.

**P12 authorization split:** retain the sole fine-grained Policy Decision Point (PDP) for human/Party/Legal Entity/purpose/field/source decisions and **coarse trusted-Tenant RLS** for tenant isolation. Do not copy role/capability or person-specific policy expressions into RLS as a competing permission engine. Protected raw source tables must not have a browser/API path around the PDP merely because coarse Tenant RLS passes; restrict direct grants/egress accordingly. Existing legacy identity/super-admin/`USING (true)` migration paths are predecessor evidence, not target certification.

**Grants/RLS/views/functions:** explicitly test the actual reached tables, grants and functions, not a synthetic table with similar names. The donor overview is read-only; no donor INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE grant is needed on Page bindings, releases or financial records for this feature. For existing source mutation paths, inspect UPDATE's `USING` and `WITH CHECK` plus SELECT policy, immutable-key enforcement and tenant-aware references. Check all permissive policies together. Invoker-safe views preserve caller RLS where appropriate; any definer/service-role path instead needs restricted invocation and the same explicit current source authorization, controlled search path and field projection. Default grants, user-editable metadata and service credentials are never a substitute. RLS does not provide column or business-purpose admission automatically. [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security), [PostgreSQL row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-rowsecurity.html).

**All channels:** private media, RPC/REST routes, browser collections, server rendering, refresh and continuation must preserve the same authorization. A PostgREST pre-request hook does not cover Storage or Realtime. Protected media is admitted through the existing media owner, not raw bucket or cached public URL substitution. No new anonymous Data API surface or raw realtime subscriber is required. [Supabase API security](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/api/securing-your-api).

**Cache/races:** reuse the existing Query/approved collection layer with exact context-qualified keys, consumed AbortSignal and a monotonic client context/response guard. Key fields include the human reader, exact financial subject/scope, verified Tenant/environment and Page/binding/locale/projection generations as applicable. Clear/invalidate affected sensitive memory on logout, principal/tenant/subject change and current adverse events. Cancellation alone does not prove an obsolete response cannot render. TanStack Suspense query hooks have cancellation limitations; React/Next streaming boundaries are not permission to assume those hooks consume cancellation like an ordinary signal-aware query. Existing Next.js private/browser-memory caching is not current authorization. Sensitive data must be reauthorized before re-exposure on navigation, focus or restored page state; a stale-time timer cannot extend access. [TanStack cancellation](https://tanstack.com/query/latest/docs/framework/react/guides/query-cancellation).

Read filtering and admission precede limits. Use owner-supported indexed predicates/keyset continuation, not fetch-all plus client matching. Query must seek at most the requested admitted preview set plus bounded owner continuation/completeness work; SQL effort still needs plans and representative load proof. Three/two rendered previews do not prove a bounded authorization scan. No per-post/per-arrangement network fan-out, all-history totals or broad identity lookup is needed. Query owns request state; existing DB/Table/Store/Virtual roles remain on their approved surfaces, with no parallel store or unnecessary new instance here.

## Individual adversarial category review

Severity is the credible consequence if the concern is implemented incorrectly. Likelihood is qualitative reasoning, not a production incident rate. “Material concern” includes a necessary guarded implementation requirement; it does not claim the proposed code has shipped or been exploited. Each C clause below is exact required execution language.

### C01 — Problem validity, necessity, and alternatives

**Material concern: Yes. Severity: Medium. Likelihood: Plausible.** A new overview could repeat screens and make known tasks slower. The roadmap and founder require ministry context, but Q02/Q10 already provide filtered tasks; B remains the strongest cheaper alternative. This narrows A to deliberate exploration, rather than invalidating it. **Required language:** “The overview SHALL be optional and contextual, preserve exact task links, and add no mandatory directory/chooser or additional step before an existing task. Its purpose is to connect admitted reading and giving, not reproduce their full screens.” **Proof:** T01/T17 compare direct and exploratory journeys.

### C02 — Brittleness

**Material concern: Yes. Severity: High. Likelihood: Likely if legacy association fallbacks are reused.** Fund-first/general-fund fallback and assumed one-to-one Page mapping can select the wrong content or lose historical giving when bindings change. Actual model probes and D3/D7 show these assumptions are unsupported. This changes the execution model. **Required language:** “Resolve the exact versioned P22 private association under A1; names, authors, first matches and current Give targets SHALL NOT infer historical financial or content scope. Ambiguity/absence SHALL preserve legitimate existing tasks without substitution.” **Proof:** T02/T03/T10.

### C03 — Technical debt

**Material concern: Yes. Severity: High. Likelihood: Plausible, supported by multiple predecessor readers/models.** A feature-local composite or copied CMS/feed creates permanent dual authority and inconsistent corrections. The source has separate cached History and dashboard paths; a convenient existing reader is not automatically the reached correct seam. This requires convergence at the owning boundary. **Required language:** “P25 SHALL orchestrate existing P22/P13/P16/P12 projections through packages/api and approved adapters; remove or stop using reached legacy fallbacks at cutover. No copied posts, portal mapping registry, mutable balance or app-local primitive fork.” **Proof:** T04/T15/T18.

### C04 — Edge cases

**Material concern: Yes. Severity: High. Likelihood: Expected at ordinary product scale.** Past-only giving, non-donor readers, renamed/retired Page, multiple associations, hidden sources, read-only representation and accepted ACH can otherwise become false empty/error/active states. D7 lifecycle, Q07/Q16/Q22 and actual status defaults establish the risk. A remains valid with the full state matrix. **Required language:** “Implement J13–J18 and the state table using certified owner outcomes; missing/failed data SHALL NOT mean zero, no giving, Active/monthly, restored Updates or a donation obligation.” **Proof:** T05–T12/T16.

### C05 — Footguns

**Material concern: Yes. Severity: High. Likelihood: Plausible.** A broad Manage button, clickable amount, Page-wide preference switch or generic Give fallback could trigger the wrong journey or couple hiding to billing. Source group/line contracts and the Patreon Block/payment contrast make the danger concrete. This narrows actions to existing exact navigation. **Required language:** “No view visit changes money, preferences or enrollment. Every financial link SHALL identify an authorized exact line/detail and revalidate at destination; no Manage all, default retry/restart, hidden-feed reveal or destination substitution.” **Proof:** T01/T07/T13.

### C06 — Tenant safety

**Material concern: Yes. Severity: Critical. Likelihood: Plausible if broad context/cache keys are reused.** Same labels/IDs in different environments, Sites or financial subjects can mix private facts. Current weak query keys and source scope requirements are evidence, not proof of an incident. A needs strict independent scoping. **Required language:** “Resolve the verified host/Tenant/Legal Entity/environment and each principal/subject server-side; every descriptor, source query, cache, continuation and link SHALL preserve its complete applicable scope and reject cross-scope substitutions. Never combine personal and represented giving.” **Proof:** T04/T05/T09/T14.

### C07 — Database, RLS, and authorization safety
