# Phase 25 — Grooming terminology

These distinctions record the founder's 7 September 2026 clarification. This is a local grooming glossary, not an amendment to the repository's canonical CONTEXT.md or a formal specification.

**Ministry Update** — The canonical Asym post described by the existing content glossary. It is not a missionary newsletter or an email merely because a notification can refer to it. Existing release, audience and access meaning remains unchanged.

**Missionary newsletter** — A newsletter whose mailing list and sending are managed by the missionary. The donor portal does not claim current subscription control or enrollment evidence for that separately managed list.

**Newsletter request** — A person's request to receive a named missionary's newsletter, using the supplied contact details. Recording or forwarding the request does not establish that the person was added to the mailing list, that a newsletter was sent, or that it arrived.

**Ministry Update email preference** — A person's choice about eligible Asym emails notifying them of new Ministry Update posts. It is separate from a missionary newsletter request, content access and proof of delivery.

**Hide from my Updates** — The requested personal ability to leave a ministry's posts out of the ordinary donor reading list. It is not deleting posts, revoking access or ending financial support. Question07 A now explicitly keeps the email control independent. The reviewed exact canonical source scope, ordinary-list/direct-link and restore rules are ratified in Question07 C01–C22.

**Organization communications** — Messages controlled by the tenant ministry organization, with each message's own purpose and permitted preference rules. This does not mean the separate organization whose giving a person may help manage.

**Canonical publishing source for a preference** — The one owner-certified source and purpose controlling an Update and its donor preference across Page placements and coauthor presentations. It is not the latest author, label or Page. This source-owner refinement is ratified in Question07 C01–C22; no new canonical registry/table is presumed and implementation remains separately qualified.

**Receipts & statements destination** — The donor's dedicated entrance to current authorized official documents and separately identified qualified informational documents, selected in Question08. It is not a new archive, issuer, renderer or source of financial truth. The reviewed overview/default and C01–C22 are explicitly founder-ratified on 7 September; implementation remains separately qualified.

**Document availability** — The owner's truthful current retrieval state for an actual logical document in the viewer's permitted scope. It is distinct from a supported generation purpose, a retained object, successful payment, delivery status or proof of human reading.

**Annual document group** — Phase19's disposable yearly presentation of permitted official documents followed by an optional qualified Support overview. It does not merge legal subjects, create exactly one document per year, or define the layout of every individual receipt.

**Current document during correction** — The source-designated currently valid artifact. A pending or failed successor does not automatically invalidate a still-valid predecessor; source withdrawal does. These are existing owner distinctions, not a new portal lifecycle.

**Recurring change proposal** — Unaccepted desired changes to exact currently authorized recurring terms. It is not a saved instruction, accepted command, new schedule, provider setup outcome or permission. Q09's reviewed execution is explicitly founder-ratified on7 September; target implementation remains separately qualified.

**Collapse an edit section** — Hide its controls while preserving its proposal and visible proposed/error summary. It does not remove a requested change or accept it.

**Undo a proposed change** — Remove that field's unaccepted edit, leaving other proposed edits intact. It does not write an old source value, reverse an accepted command, detach a saved method or cancel giving.

**Exact recurring review** — The current owner-generated before/after and consequence view bound to the whole proposal, actor/scope, source revisions and required authority. Relevant changes require a fresh review; purely opening/closing sections does not change terms.

**All available history** — The currently authorized and source-admitted giving record without a default date cutoff. Selected in Q10. It is not a claim of complete lifetime records, a retention guarantee or an all-at-once download.

**History filter** — A donor's temporary choice narrowing the permitted record by source-qualified facts. It does not change giving, access, communication preferences or document availability. Q10's exact filter execution is explicitly founder-ratified on 7 September; implementation remains separately qualified.

**Gift amount (ratified Q10 filter label)** — The existing source-owned original supported-gift amount, excluding separately recorded fee cover and before refunds. It differs from actual charged total, deductible amount and the amount allocated to a selected fund. Pre-posting records use an owner-qualified Requested amount distinction; no received gift is invented.

**Matched fund amount** — The permitted portion of a recognizable gift assigned to the selected source designation. It does not relabel the full gift as support for that fund or reveal a hidden sibling/parent amount.

**History continuation** — Access to the next or previous permitted part of the same qualified record query. It does not confer access, prove complete lifetime coverage or freeze a person's permissions.

**Reading anchor** — The recognizable gift and position a donor was reading before opening details or changing display. It is not a new financial identity or evidence that a person read a gift.

**Sign-in email** — The mutable address a person enters to identify their account in the email-first sign-in journey. The address is not itself a secret/authenticator or immutable person identity; the required verification supplies proof. The stable account/Party bindings and current permissions remain separate.

**Contact email with this organization** — The person's contact information held in this Tenant's source-owned donor/Party context, where that owner permits editing. It is distinct from the sign-in credential and from the consent/security rules deciding a particular message's delivery. Q11's corrected execution and A1–A5 owner amendments were explicitly ratified on 7 September; implementation remains separately qualified. This is not a new contact model or propagation rule.

**Established claim proof (ratified Q11 owner amendment)** — Accepted identity-owner evidence binding the stable account/profile to its donor/Party. Distinct from verification of the current organization contact address; a contact edit neither transfers this binding nor erases valid historical proof.

**Contact email revision (ratified Q11 owner amendment)** — The owner's monotonic version of the exact current organization-contact address and its purpose-bound proof. Returning to an old string does not return to the old revision or inherit its verification.

**Guided email-change request (ratified Q11 owner amendment)** — Bounded, immutable accepted intent for explicitly selected email uses, with exact actor/scope/revisions/proof correlations and independently evidenced owner results. It is not email truth, a claim/merge command or a generic workflow engine.

**Finish later (ratified Q11 execution)** — Leave a provider-initiated pending change without canceling it. Remaining valid deliberate proof may complete it until qualified expiry/supersession. Draft discard and post-initiation provider cancellation are different operations.

**Retained finishing session (ratified Q11 execution)** — The exact verified session bound to the same account and request on the device where confirmation finishes, retained while the Auth owner retires the principal's other existing sessions. It is not necessarily the browser that started the request.

**Current Home need (ratified Q12 execution)** — A currently authorized presentation of an exact owner-admitted donor step or required continuing state. It is not a new task, an unread notification, every available action or an independent completion record.

**Required continuing presentation (Q12 clarification of existing ownership)** — An admitted owner or Live notification contract can require current presentation until its exact source-end predicate, even after an Apply action disappears. This includes ordinary source-actionable Attention as well as Urgent/safety. It neither creates another donor obligation nor a third notification policy.

**More actions (ratified Q12 execution)** — Deliberately reveal the next bounded current needs in the same Home section. It does not mark anything read/resolved, promise an exact total, create a notification center or make already loaded rows complete history.

**Current-need ordering time (ratified Q12 execution)** — An owner-certified time when the present need became actionable, used only for stable ordering within comparable presentation peers. It is not a deadline, urgency, last retry, unread age or a browser-generated event.

**Routine recurring receipt-email preference (Q13 ratified execution)** — An authorized donor's standing choice about eligible routine receipt emails for current and future recurring giving to the same legal issuer and receipt-owning legal donor. It does not decide receipt issuance, marketing consent or independently required notices.

**Receipt-email scope (Q13 ratified execution)** — The exact organization/Tenant, legal issuer and receipt-owning legal donor whose routine receipt emails a choice affects. The acting person or delivery contact is not automatically that legal donor.

**Routine email not requested (Q13 ratified execution)** — The receipt owner's decision that an otherwise qualifying routine email is not requested under the donor's applicable choice. It is distinct from a document not being issued, an email blocked after admission, a provider suppression, delivery or reading.

**Social sign-in (Q14 ratified direction)** — Using a qualified Google, Apple or Facebook identity to authenticate to the shared account. It does not itself grant a Tenant membership, claim giving, merge a Party or change donor communications.

**Connected sign-in method (Q14 ratified direction)** — A qualified credential identity attached to the person's shared Auth account. Connecting or removing it can affect account access across surfaces; Tenant donor records remain separately authorized.

**Offered sign-in method (Q14 ratified direction)** — A qualified sign-in choice presented on a Tenant's entry surface. Offering or hiding it is distinct from an enforced authentication-method or assurance policy.

**Previous giving values (Q15 ratified execution)** — Source-certified accepted gift amount per occurrence, exact currency and full frequency that were effective at the canceled arrangement's cancellation boundary. These are editable suggestions, not the last payment, a future never-effective change or renewed authority.

**Restart giving (existing owner meaning; Q15 presentation)** — Begin a freshly authorized recurring gift related to canceled history. The old agreement remains canceled; paused Resume and ended/completed fixed promises retain their different meanings.

**Repeated restart entry (Q15 ratified execution)** — Return to the same source-owned unresolved or accepted restart operation/result rather than create a duplicate. It does not prohibit a deliberately separate new gift or create a permanent one-successor-per-predecessor rule.

**Preferred saved method for new gifts (Q16 ratified execution)** — One person's explicitly chosen starting saved method in a qualified giving/payment context. It is not a Stripe billing default, payment authorization, mandate, backup charge permission or a change to existing recurring giving.

**Pending new-gift preference (Q16 ratified execution)** — A bounded explicitly requested preference waiting for its exact method/setup to qualify, while the prior confirmed choice remains. It can expire, be superseded or be withdrawn without deleting the method or stopping its verification.

**Preference intent revision (Q16 ratified execution)** — The source's monotonic ordering of accepted explicit preference decisions and matching semantic invalidations. It differs from row revision so unrelated cleanup neither revives old intent nor cancels a valid newer request.

**Preference-request deadline (Q16 ratified execution)** — The absolute product deadline for conditional automatic preference application, measured from the accepted request. It is distinct from provider verification expiry and financial authorization validity; later valid saving/manual preference can remain available.

**All notifications (Q17 ratified execution)** — The current viewer's authorized, currently presentable donor notification set. It is not all gifts/emails/posts, unlimited records, a cross-Tenant feed or every Home need.

**Notification preview (Q17 ratified presentation)** — A bounded first view of canonical attention groups leading to the same full center. A display limit does not delete notices or end an obligation.

**Human recipient versus giving subject (Q17 clarification for owner qualification)** — The person receiving and engaging with a notice is distinct from the legal donor or organization the notice concerns. Shared represented authority does not create shared private read state.

**Informational unread ending (existing ADR0027/P17 policy; Q17 clarification)** — Unread eligibility ends at the earliest qualifying read/archive/correction or original30-day deadline. Archive restore does not restart it, and source history is not rewritten as human reading.

**Pause ending mode (Q18 ratified execution)** — The donor's instruction for this pause: return automatically on a chosen civil date, or stay paused until the donor authorizes return. It is not a saved personal preference, a schedule re-anchor or a new financial authorization by itself.

**Pause end versus next scheduled gift (Q18 clarification)** — The pause end is the exclusive suppression boundary. The next gift is the first original-grid occurrence still eligible under all current source guards. These dates can differ; an indefinite pause supplies no speculative next date.

**Pause scheduled (Q18 ratified presentation)** — A derived description of an accepted future pause. An already activated ongoing arrangement remains ongoing until the boundary. Pending activation, current payment outcome and external-control progress remain separate facts.

**Accepted pending-activation pause (Q18 ratified owner amendment)** — Protective future-giving instruction recorded after recurring terms are accepted while initial activation proof remains incomplete. It neither recalls the initial payment nor allows future execution before all activation and current pause/control guards pass.

**Same-day eligible occurrence (Q18 ratified owner clarification)** — An existing ordinary occurrence whose owner-certified window and other authorization/control conditions still permit collection today. Later clock time alone does not prove expiry; naming it in a Resume review never creates an extra initial gift or revives closed work.

**Final horizon during pause (Q18 ratified owner amendment)** — The original last eligible giving boundary still fences new collection during pause or activation. Its end projection preserves prior cancellation/supersession and required external-stop warnings; earlier submitted money can complete independently.

**Known-empty personal Home (Q19 ratified execution)** — Neutral Home in an already established authorized personal portal context where current qualified reads show no available financial records or required current action. It is a presentation condition, not proof of first-ever giving, a persisted new-donor status, an unresolved claim, an access grant or a failed/pending read.
