# Recurring giving, saved payment methods and fixed pledges

This normative contract synthesizes the ratified Q03, Q06, Q09, Q15, Q16, Q18, Q22 and Q26 decisions and final Wallet Remove clarification. SHALL/SHALL NOT are requirements. Product approval is not source, provider or release qualification. No additional product choice, provider upgrade or new authority is implied. The accompanying stories and trace register identify individual acceptance obligations and inherited proof.
