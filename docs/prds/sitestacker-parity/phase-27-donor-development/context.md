# Phase 27 supporting specification — shared context, exchange recency, optional financial analysis and meeting preparation

This volume specifies the ratified D26–D28 product meaning and the inherited deterministic briefing, church-context and shared UX obligations. It is a normative supporting volume for the authorized Phase 27 specification. It does not assert implementation, live authorization, activation, observed usability or release qualification. Requirement and acceptance identifiers are stable for integration with the parent specification.

## Ownership and explicit reconciliation

- P9 owns interaction/encounter identity and source records; P27 owns development meaning; P28 consumes its narrowly permitted, voluntary missionary projection. Imported/private/care content does not become shared.

## Requirements and falsifiable acceptance

### REQ27-C01 — Voluntary missionary contribution and independent obligations

Shared partnership history SHALL remain useful when a missionary chooses not to contribute. Reading or writing SHALL NOT require logging, reports, quotas, check-ins, supervisory approval, missing-log penalties, performance rankings or inferred diligence; SHALL NOT assign staff status; and SHALL NOT admit, reopen or advance managed work or enroll coaching. Goals, invited read-only coaching and qualitative check-ins remain separately deliberate, qualified assistance. Tenant configuration SHALL NOT turn this shared-history lane into mandatory missionary reporting. This is a product boundary, not a statement about employment status or every agency's policies. Accepted source work, commitments, gift handling and safety obligations keep their own accountability and lifecycle; an update neither creates, completes nor waives them. Personal follow-through is available only through a separately deliberate qualified source action.

Acceptance:

- **AC27-C01-01:** A missionary can decline to write and continue ordinary permitted use without penalty, required stage/task, activity report or coaching enrollment; staff cannot configure such coercion through this lane.
- **AC27-C01-02:** Saving or reading an update leaves managed-work admission, pause/closure, source obligations and staff status unchanged; a gift handoff cannot be completed by a note.
- **AC27-C01-03:** An independently selected personal follow-up is created once through its source. Its failure or cancellation neither fails nor duplicates an already accepted update.

Trace: D26/R01, D26/R02, D26/owner-amendments-3-4, D27/R08.

### REQ27-C02 — New eligible shared content and truthful private alternatives

Existing private notes, imported history, personal messages, staff-only CRM activity, Support/member-care content, sensitive location information and restricted finance SHALL NOT be automatically copied, relabeled, migrated or summarized into this lane. Unclassified content SHALL NOT default to shared. An existing qualified private-note action SHALL be separate and state its real audience; neither Internal Notes nor restricted implies author-only. An unavailable private destination SHALL NOT fall back to shared storage. No personal-journal or attachment-storage product is introduced.

Acceptance:

- **AC27-C02-01:** Existing private/imported/internal/care/location-sensitive/financial fixtures remain protected after activation, import, refresh and shared-history migration.
- **AC27-C02-02:** The private destination discloses its actual reader scope, and its absence leaves a clear unavailable/cancel option with no shared save.
- **AC27-C02-03:** A newly authored eligible update can be saved directly in its qualified shared context without a repeated sharing checkbox or supervisory approval.

### REQ27-C03 — Exact ongoing audience and historical access

Shared SHALL mean the current qualified audience and historical-access scope of the exact tenant, partnership, source and purpose, not all staff, all missionaries or everyone who can view the same church. Composer and saved detail SHALL explain that scope before input and after saving, including possible authorized future collaborators, without exposing a restricted roster. A new collaborator reads earlier history only through an explicit source/history-qualified grant. Assignment, appeal links, Party merge, role title or church visibility grants nothing. Revocation SHALL stop future source/derived reads, exports and result recovery without erasing lawful authorship or promising recall of material already read or lawfully exported. Intended-audience failure SHALL NOT select another audience. Reuse ongoing source audience semantics; do not create frozen per-update recipient lists.

Acceptance:

- **AC27-C03-01:** Two ministries sharing one church and two tenants cannot see or write each other’s updates or protected audience metadata.
- **AC27-C03-02:** A new assignee gains no earlier history from assignment alone; an independently history-authorized collaborator sees only the qualified earlier slice with the audience explanation.
- **AC27-C03-03:** Revocation before acceptance blocks the write; revocation after acceptance blocks subsequent unauthorized body/result disclosure while preserving accepted history. Both serialized orders are demonstrated.
- **AC27-C03-04:** Users can identify the exact partnership and permitted audience before saving; a changed or unavailable audience does not silently broaden the destination or disclose restricted names.
