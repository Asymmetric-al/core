# Phase 27 — continuing partnership work and stage configuration

This volume specifies continuing cultivation work, responsibility, deliberate entry, pause/review/close/reopen, organization-approved stage configuration and the meaning of recorded exchanges. It preserves source ownership and current authority. It describes required behavior, not a claim that the implementation, real-user testing or production qualification has already passed.

## Implementation decisions and acceptance

The requirement IDs are stable trace targets. Each acceptance criterion states an observable source or user outcome; implementation evidence must use the confirmed browser, source/API, effective database-role and controlled-concurrency seams where appropriate.

## REQ27-P01 — Continuing work belongs to an identified relationship purpose

Decision trace: D1; D2 exact decision and F1–F2; D3 C02/C10–C11.

An ordinary missionary-support cultivation enrollment SHALL represent continuing work for one canonical supporter Party, one identified organization-owned Ministry Assignment and one admitted relationship purpose. The enrollment SHALL retain the Ministry Assignment's exact Tenant and Legal Entity and a compatible same-Tenant Party; it SHALL NOT invent Legal Entity ownership for a Party whose owner is Tenant-scoped. Ministry identity SHALL be independent of current people, logins, households, Pages, Designations, Support Assignments or current giving. A ministry may have zero, one or several participants. Required identified-ministry references SHALL be real eligible references, never null, a sentinel or a guessed profile mapping.

Successive asks, annual renewal and stewardship SHALL use the continuing enrollment unless genuinely different future work requires an admitted separate purpose. New staff, a new year, changed currency, display labels, translation, stage list or public surface SHALL NOT create a new ordinary identity. Participation in prayer or other nonfinancial work SHALL remain meaningful without giving. Enrollment SHALL express internal development intent and SHALL NOT claim the supporter has agreed to partnership, prayer, giving or contact. Other admitted agency/church/project purposes SHALL use their own qualified source contracts; they SHALL NOT require a dummy Ministry Assignment. Authorized Party/referral/task work SHALL remain available before a ministry is identified.

### AC27-P01-01

**Given** a Party and eligible ministry with no participant, Support Binding, gift, email address or public Page, **when** authorized staff deliberately start ordinary work, **then** the identified relationship can be recorded without inventing any missing financial, public or participation fact.

### AC27-P01-02

**Given** one enrollment for a ministry with successive asks and annual renewal, **when** the year, responsible worker, ministry display name or stage list changes, **then** the ordinary enrollment identity and earlier work remain; no new enrollment or fresh supporter assent is inferred.

### AC27-P01-03

**Given** a worker participates in two ministries and a church has a named committee contact, **when** staff select a support relationship, **then** the actual Party and exact ministry are distinguishable; neither the worker login nor church contact silently substitutes for the selected subject.

### AC27-P01-04

**Given** a relationship is not yet attributable to an identified ministry, **when** staff record an introduction or authorized task, **then** the useful Party/referral/task record is accepted through its owner without creating a cultivation enrollment or dummy ministry.

### AC27-P01-05

**Given** two Tenants or two Legal Entities have similarly named ministries, **when** a command supplies a mismatched or hidden reference, **then** the command produces no cross-scope relationship or restricted identifying result; legitimate explicitly authorized multi-entity work uses each exact admitted scope.

An independently due request, different owner, year, currency, privacy need, stage preset, surface or mutable label SHALL NOT satisfy that test. Ordinary work SHALL NOT require an explanation essay, configuration wizard, purpose catalog or manager approval.

Each request's response, partner instructions, obligation and applicable source restriction SHALL remain independently interpretable. A declined request or stopped financial support SHALL NOT fail or close all development work; prayer SHALL NOT become a preliminary financial stage. Dedupe and reuse SHALL use canonical meaning and the admitted lifecycle, not text or random request identifiers. Paused work SHALL NOT free a duplicate slot. Genuine return after closure SHALL follow REQ27-P12. Party repair and collision handling SHALL use the existing identity/source owner's qualified commands and retain original facts, actors, obligations and accepted effects; no automatic history/grant union or invented second purpose may evade a conflict.

### AC27-P02-01

### AC27-P02-02

### AC27-P02-03

### AC27-P02-04

### AC27-P02-05

**Given** a Party correction would cause two ordinary enrollments to collide, **when** the existing source owner processes the correction, **then** no history, grants or accepted effects are silently unioned and no fake purpose is created; unresolved meaning remains explicit for qualified correction.

## REQ27-P03 — Deliberate contextual admission and safe reuse

Decision trace: D4 exact decision; C01/C04/C05/C09–C11/C14/C19/C22; D3 C07.

New cultivation work SHALL begin by deliberate authorized human action. A gift, referral, score, import arrival, recorded note or contact alone SHALL NOT admit work. Where known context supplies the Party, ministry, purpose and actual intended work, one clearly worded contextual start/reuse action MAY record the expressly included referral/request/work through their owners. The UI SHALL collect only missing or materially changed facts and SHALL explain the consequence before submission; no duplicate enrollment ceremony, supporter login, gift or optional Studio setup is required.

Reuse SHALL preserve existing stage, responsibility, instructions and obligations. Additional requested work SHALL have its own expressly included meaning, authority and durable effect identity. Current create/reuse/result-metadata authority SHALL be independently qualified. Where minimum admission observability is absent, hidden-existing and absent cases SHALL yield the same bounded denial/defer behavior before existence-dependent results. Metadata-only reuse SHALL grant no contents, history or edit access. The UI SHALL never recommend changing purpose, ending work or creating a duplicate to work around a restriction.

Admission SHALL NOT send communications, grant contact permission, create or redirect financial facts, publish content or stop independently required financial/service effects. Qualified financial synchronization and receipts SHALL remain possible without cultivation admission.

### AC27-P03-01

**Given** known authorized Party/ministry/purpose/work context exists, **when** staff expressly start cultivation while recording the included referral or ask, **then** one understandable action accepts the specifically included source effects without asking for known information again.

### AC27-P03-02

**Given** a gift, score change, imported contact or introduction-only note arrives, **when** the source fact is processed, **then** no new enrollment is admitted; separately qualified financial/service facts and existing Party/task work remain usable.

### AC27-P03-03

**Given** an ordinary enrollment already contains stages, owner and several open asks, **when** a new start operation resolves to permitted reuse, **then** those existing facts remain unchanged and only separately requested authorized work is added.

### AC27-P03-04

**Given** paired fixtures differ only in whether a forbidden ordinary enrollment exists, **when** a caller lacking minimum admission-result authority attempts entry, **then** observable availability, bounded response, counts and errors do not expose the hidden difference or admit a workaround duplicate.

### AC27-P03-05

**Given** a caller has limited reuse metadata authority but not history/edit authority, **when** the command resolves existing work, **then** only that admitted metadata is returned and no edit, history, missionary or finance access is created.

## REQ27-P04 — Finite reviewed group intent and recoverable member outcomes

The review SHALL distinguish selected page from all explicitly supported results and display honest currently authorized scope/counts. Later filter edits, matches, page eviction, name changes or browser navigation SHALL NOT enlarge or silently substitute the set. Capture only required identity/provenance; do not hold a database transaction open during human review. Duplicate selected identities SHALL not multiply work.

Execution MAY continue through the existing durable executor after the browser closes, while retaining original authenticated human intent and the exact original validated Tenant/context. Every consequential member action SHALL revalidate current source eligibility, authority and material revisions. A later selected Tenant SHALL NOT retarget queued work. Each accepted member SHALL atomically commit source state, attributable history, receipt and required local work or owned actionable exception; members SHALL be independently recoverable. Group progress SHALL be derived from source receipts and SHALL distinguish accepted, reused, unaccepted and unresolved outcomes. Queued or dispatched SHALL NOT mean accepted. Loss of the first response SHALL be recoverable using the same stable command identity.

Cancellation SHALL stop only unadmitted work according to the source-defined race ordering. It SHALL NOT delete accepted or reused work, erase later changes or claim a global Undo. Repair SHALL retain independent successes, reconcile ambiguous outcomes first and retry only the same still-eligible intent. Limits for selection count, payload bytes, transaction/query duration, concurrency and result pagination SHALL be measured and recorded before activation; oversize work SHALL be rejected or visibly split, never silently truncated.

### AC27-P04-02

### AC27-P04-03

### AC27-P04-04

### AC27-P04-05

**Given** cancellation and acceptance contend for the same unadmitted member, **when** each valid serialization order is exercised, **then** accepted work survives if acceptance wins; cancellation blocks later admission if cancellation wins; no accepted history is rolled back.

### AC27-P04-06

**Given** a Tenant switch, permission revocation, source retirement or material source change occurs after review, **when** a deferred member executes or its result arrives, **then** original context remains fixed and current constraints decide the member outcome; stale output cannot populate the new context.

### AC27-P04-07

**Given** a group exceeds a qualified numeric capacity boundary, **when** staff submit it, **then** the system explains a rejection or explicit partition; it neither truncates membership nor starts an unreviewed replacement operation.

## REQ27-P05 — Visible, eligible partnership responsibility

Decision trace: D5 founder answer and boundaries; D2 F6; D3 C17; D6 R03–R04.

The starting responsible person SHALL preferentially be the clear established currently eligible lead for the actual ministry partnership. When that source evidence is absent or ambiguous, staff SHALL make an authorized choice. A safe unambiguous default SHALL be visible and changeable without a redundant step. Creator, overall Party representative, ministry participant, role label, name, friendship or display order alone SHALL NOT prove the lead.

Partnership responsibility SHALL remain distinct from the Party's overall representative and from individual request/task ownership; one person may hold several responsibilities. Defaults SHALL not establish standing automatic reassignment policy. Changes to Party representation or ministry personnel SHALL NOT silently transfer cultivation, child work or private history. Responsibility SHALL use the qualified existing assignment/shared-work source and SHALL not establish a shadow portfolio or access grant. Every unfinished obligation SHALL retain qualified accountability on departure or handoff; deny revoked access first. Where no eligible existing person or queue is qualified, preserve an authorized visible needs-assignment exception rather than guessing a queue, notifying a former person or declaring completion.

### AC27-P05-01

**Given** a partnership has one established currently eligible lead, **when** staff start or review responsibility, **then** that lead is visibly prefilled and changeable without a separate confirmation; the unrelated creator/Party representative is not substituted.

### AC27-P05-02

**Given** the partnership lead is unknown or several candidates are equally plausible, **when** staff start work, **then** the UI requests an authorized choice and does not use first result, administrator status or ministry membership as proof.

### AC27-P05-03

**Given** the Party representative changes while a partnership and requests have different owners, **when** that assignment change is accepted, **then** partnership and child responsibility do not silently transfer; separately qualified handoff remains possible.

### AC27-P05-04

**Given** the current lead departs and no existing eligible assignee or queue remains, **when** access is revoked, **then** revocation applies immediately and unresolved work is visible to its qualified repair owner; no private payload reaches an invented or former recipient.

### AC27-P05-05

**Given** a user becomes responsible for partnership or task work, **when** the source assignment is accepted, **then** no independent read, financial, consent, publication or missionary grant is created.

## REQ27-P06 — Source changes and corrections preserve real intent and history

Decision trace: D2 exact decision/F1/F4/F6; D3 C04/C14; D7 R10/R12; D8.

Routine source metadata, participant, name, login or display changes SHALL NOT reset cultivation or create a donor-wide review. An unchanged ministry identity SHALL NOT imply supporter acceptance of replacement people or a changed purpose. Review SHALL be limited to source-evidenced affected future work: source ineligibility; materially different purpose/ministry; conflict with recorded partner restrictions; changes to people actually represented in an open ask, invitation or promised follow-up; or loss of an eligible obligation owner. Review SHALL itself grant, send or transfer nothing. Independently applicable restrictions SHALL take effect immediately.

Mistaken-reference correction, source-certified same-real-world identity repair and genuinely different future work SHALL remain distinct. A qualified expected-version correction SHALL preserve the original reference, actor, reason, revision and history; it SHALL not rewrite presented/accepted asks, encounters, financial facts, supporter intent or prior effects. Owners SHALL correct their own downstream facts. Genuine ministry, purpose, Tenant or Legal Entity change SHALL require explicit future disposition, not silent reassociation. Missing source qualification SHALL block automatic repair without making honest draft mistakes permanently unrepairable. This requirement SHALL NOT introduce speculative ministry succession/merge machinery, generic historical Undo or permission union.

### AC27-P06-01

**Given** ministry name or participant metadata changes without changing source eligibility or represented future work, **when** source updates propagate, **then** history remains and no blanket supporter review, new journey, task or consent inference occurs.

### AC27-P06-02

**Given** an open invitation names a person who leaves the ministry, **when** the authoritative source change is accepted, **then** only the affected future invitation/follow-through is presented for permitted review; replacement-person supporter allegiance is not inferred.

### AC27-P06-03

**Given** a draft relationship points to the wrong source and authorized correction is qualified, **when** staff correct it with the expected revision, **then** the current association is corrected with original reference, trusted actor and reason preserved.

### AC27-P06-04

**Given** an ask has already been presented or an encounter/gift accepted, **when** staff correct the enrollment reference, **then** those accepted source facts remain attributable to their original context; required downstream corrections use their owners.

### AC27-P06-05

**Given** two distinct same-name ministries or different Tenant/Legal Entity scopes are involved, **when** a move or source repair is proposed, **then** it cannot silently merge or retarget history; genuinely different future work uses explicit permitted disposition.

### AC27-P06-06

Native work SHALL function without optional Studio and without a parallel task, queue or notification system.

### AC27-P07-02

### AC27-P07-04

## REQ27-P08 — Deliberate pause and truthful internal review occurrences

Decision trace: D6 exact decision/R01–R02/R08–R10; D7 R07; D21.

A deliberate temporary pause SHALL preserve the partnership and create one current internal review obligation with a staff-chosen review date. The source SHALL maintain one task identity for that review occurrence and at most one active task row per qualified recipient/role/surface. Reuse requires the same obligation, not merely a matching person or date, and SHALL NOT repurpose a promised call or another task. Missing response, elapsed time, gift, score, travel or task expiry SHALL not pause, resume or close work automatically.

At an actual review, staff SHALL be able to keep work on hold with a deliberately chosen successor review date, resume appropriate work, or close when REQ27-P11 readiness is satisfied. Reviewed-and-continue SHALL atomically end the completed review occurrence and establish its successor. Rescheduling the current review SHALL change its date without creating a completed-review fact. Resume/end SHALL explicitly account for open work. If no further meaningful work is intended, the closure action SHALL be available under its authority; an undated indefinite hold SHALL not masquerade as a dated pause. Due/overdue SHALL describe internal review work, never a supporter or missionary performance judgment. Paused stage/list edits follow REQ27-P29 and SHALL not satisfy the review.

### AC27-P08-01

**Given** authorized staff choose a date and pause current work, **when** the same save is repeated, **then** one current review occurrence and at most one source task identity are retained; changed meaning under the same key conflicts.

### AC27-P08-02

**Given** an actual review concludes that work should remain paused, **when** staff choose Keep on hold and a successor date, **then** the old review is Completed and a new current occurrence with the chosen date exists atomically.

### AC27-P08-03

**Given** staff need a different date without conducting the review, **when** they reschedule the current occurrence, **then** the schedule changes with attributable history and no completed-review fact is created.

### AC27-P08-04

**Given** the due date arrives or a notification is read/archived or a gift is received, **when** the external or engagement event is processed, **then** cultivation remains paused, the review remains unresolved, and no automatic donor contact or source completion occurs.

### AC27-P08-05

**Given** a review action resumes or closes work, **when** the qualified source command commits, **then** affected obligations are explicitly accounted for; unrelated tasks, gifts, commitments, consent and public content remain unchanged.

### AC27-P08-06

**Given** staff intend meaningful future work but do not choose a date, **when** they attempt the dated-pause action, **then** a clear date requirement is shown without fabricating a cadence, donor action or completed review.

## REQ27-P09 — Review dates retain their calendar meaning

## REQ27-P11 — Closure requires complete bounded readiness and preserves independent work

Decision trace: D7 exact decision/R01–R15/AC01–AC16; D6 R08.

Close SHALL mean a deliberate decision that no meaningful further development/stewardship work is intended for the exact Party/ministry/purpose enrollment. A missing date, filtered empty task view, declined ask, absent gift/response, departing worker or score SHALL not establish closure. Actual prayer/practical follow-through is meaningful work; a continuing relationship alone need not block closure. Staff SHALL understand finished work versus dated pause versus an independently pending request.

Before closure, the source SHALL obtain complete bounded readiness from the exact owners of affected obligations, including relevant promises, pending asks and source-owned work beyond the visible page. Unknown, unavailable, filtered or incomplete coverage SHALL not mean empty. Blocking work SHALL be fulfilled or receive a qualified owner disposition. Legitimate independent obligations MAY survive only with continuing authority, accountability and discoverability; moving the same unfinished development work to another person SHALL not make it finished. No blanket linked-task cancellation is permitted.

Authority to close, read minimum readiness results and inspect history/blocker details SHALL remain separate. Where minimum readiness-result authority is absent, protected-obligation-present/absent cases SHALL not reveal their difference through action availability or response. Closure and each affected work-creating writer SHALL share a qualified source fence: a newly accepted blocking obligation forces reevaluation; accepted closure blocks incompatible new work. Closure, history, receipt and required local intent SHALL commit atomically with source-defined current facts. Normal closure time SHALL be trusted accepted time, not caller backdating or future dating.

An actual Review → End SHALL record review Completed; direct closure that makes a future review unnecessary SHALL record No longer required. Already admitted attention ends applicability under its existing presentation policy without fictitious read or recall. Closed work SHALL leave authoritative current-work views/counts while remaining findable in permitted history; reporting SHALL preserve original closed periods. Closure SHALL not change Party/ministry lifecycle, observed stage, ask response, assignment portfolio, financial facts, consent, contact history or public content. No new donor-facing flow, mandatory essay, extra approval or unsafe generic Undo is introduced.

### AC27-P11-01

**Given** staff inspect cases of declined ask, ongoing promised prayer follow-through, continuing giving without further development work and a deliberate temporary pause, **when** they choose a source action, **then** the UI distinguishes these meanings and prevents treating missing date or visible emptiness alone as completed work.

### AC27-P11-02

**Given** a relevant promise exists outside the first page or is protected from the actor, **when** closure readiness is evaluated, **then** complete owner evidence determines readiness; permitted minimal outcomes disclose only authorized information and unknown checks cannot pass as empty.

### AC27-P11-03

**Given** an independent obligation has valid continuing authority and responsibility, **when** closure disposes the development-specific work, **then** the independent obligation stays findable and actionable; reassigning the same continuing development work does not falsely satisfy readiness.

### AC27-P11-04

**Given** close races new work, review changes or authority revocation through any admitted writer, **when** real transactions exercise both orders, **then** no accepted combination strands required work or permits stale authority; closure and the incompatible new work cannot both pass separate stale prechecks.

### AC27-P11-05

**Given** a review is actually performed before End, or a future review is made unnecessary by direct Close, **when** the action is accepted, **then** the first outcome is Completed and the second No longer required; unrelated task reasons survive and queued obsolete attention cannot newly release.
