# Phase 27 — Coordinated owner amendments and qualification gates

This volume records the exact accepted owner changes carried into the formal specification. It is intended behavior, not a declaration that predecessor APIs, permissions, tables, jobs or screens are already qualified. ## P9 — Role-aware staff assignments (D30)

For the existing staff-assignment family only, active source identity distinguishes the exact role-qualified relationship within Tenant. The same staff person and Party may have distinct legitimate roles; one role may have multiple staff. A duplicate of the same active staff/Party/role-qualified source edge remains forbidden. Preserve one primary in the existing shared header contract independently. A transfer targets one exact existing responsibility/edge lineage; it cannot overwrite all roles or manufacture a second portfolio store. Other relationship families retain their existing identity and constraints. Role, policy provenance, manual protection and primary effects are source-owned and historical changes remain attributable.

P27 owns the bounded policy and consumes P9 commands. Reviewed existing transfers are the default. Tenant-enabled Auto applies only to source-proved rule-managed responsibility; manual, intentionally unassigned and unknown imported provenance remain protected. Save draft is inert, activation is prospective, current-population application is finite and deliberate. First proved-match selection, unknown higher-priority criteria, ineligible selected target, causal events, policy/protection/revocation races and independently owned handoffs follow the full assignment volume. No access or financial succession follows.

## P9/P3/P12/P28 — Narrow shared interaction exception (D26)

General donor/Party notes, private CRM activity and scores remain prohibited in missionary projections. Only newly authored, sharing-eligible development-interaction context from the qualified P9 source may be exposed for an exact partnership with its current audience, allowed fields, purpose and explicit historical-access scope. P27 owns its development meaning. This exception cannot relabel, migrate, import, quote or summarize protected notes into shared content. Care and Support protected facts retain their owners.

P12 registers and qualifies exact create/read/history/correct/restrict capabilities; a missionary can hold the bounded workspace capability without becoming staff. Any projection-policy widening follows ADR0211's distinct-human approval. Once that source/policy is qualified, ordinary eligible updates require no supervisory approval or fresh policy-widening decision. Current audience applies to every read, derivative and recovered result. New collaborators receive earlier history only through an explicit qualified historical-access grant; assignment or linking alone grants nothing. Removal/revocation applies to subsequent reads and result recovery; source retention/correction still governs lawful history.

Reading or contributing context does not admit, reopen or advance managed partnership work. Its next-action-or-owned-exception floor applies to deliberately admitted active work and its responsible owner, not every missionary interaction. Updates cannot complete actual source obligations. Shared history remains voluntary and cannot impose logs, quotas, missing-log penalties, rankings or automatic coaching enrollment. Invited read-only coaching, goals and qualitative check-ins remain separately deliberate qualified assistance; tenant configuration cannot turn this feature into compulsory missionary reporting. Actual accepted work and legal/financial duties remain independent.

## P15/P12/P13/P16 — Gifts awaiting finance (D25)

P15 SHALL support a bounded operational report/intake for an explicitly reported actual offline gift needing handling or finance entry. Preserve trusted Tenant/recording actor/time, attributed receipt/handling assertion and its time precision, reported holder, current accountable next actor or owned unresolved exception, permitted source context and exact lineage. It is not independently verified custody and needs no invented default signature, image or second approver.

Unknown donor, financial Legal Entity, amount, tender detail or intended allocation may remain unknown in a qualified restricted operational envelope outside a valid financial batch. Do not invent a Party, anonymous legal donor, USD default, Legal Entity or noncash valuation. It creates no money, accepted value, tax date, financial suspense, deposit, receipt, acknowledgment, fulfillment or communication authority. Every actual financial root retains complete required source scope. Reuse existing incomplete intake where qualified; already-valid entry has no compulsory preliminary report or new precursor table.

Reporting/read/correct/routing powers are separate P12 capabilities. A reporter cannot create a donor, post/correct money, allocate fulfillment, issue a receipt or read financial detail without independent authority. A qualified minimal completion projection may be visible without the whole finance record. Finance continues or exactly reconciles the same intake through its existing batch/commit boundary; it does not request duplicate entry. Preserve lineage across drafts/batches/corrections and owner-qualified split/carry-forward. Duplicate reports retain both origins; equal amount/name/check number is not an automatic match.

Only exact current source evidence resolves the covered recording work; partial outcomes resolve only covered items. Recover/link an already-posted result without reposting. Recording and real physical handling can finish in either order and each keeps its own source predicate. Explicit dispositions handle mistakes, duplicates, returns, loss, disputes and rejection without inventing money outcomes. Draft cleanup, appeal closure, elapsed age or staff departure cannot erase unresolved handling; genuinely resolved work does not become perpetual tasks. Use qualified existing P15/shared work with stable occurrence identity, current action/recipient ceiling and existing internal attention. No outward donor reminder follows from reporting.

Qualify reporting, restricted reads, correction/routing, lineage, recovery and retained history together before this narrow lane activates. Do not require every future finance, accounting or Studio feature first. Financial posting remains the existing single money door; ADR0206 keeps all posted financial rows immutable. D23 Request review does not settle posted-correction policy C-01.

Native work stays independent of optional workflows and no speculative outward cadence is introduced.
