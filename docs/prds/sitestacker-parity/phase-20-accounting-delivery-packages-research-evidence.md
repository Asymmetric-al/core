# Phase 20 Capability-Certified Accounting Delivery Packages — Research Evidence

**Decision candidate:** D16
**Status:** Ratified as Phase 20 D16 and adversarially hardened
**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Scope:** The staff-mediated QuickBooks Online and Xero delivery lane only

This dossier preserves the research and adversarial reasoning for the Phase 20
decision about provider-import packages. It is not the Phase 20 PRD,
implementation specification, issue set, or authorization to build.

## Executive conclusion

Option C-prime is viable only with one precise meaning of customization:

- tenants control the accounting outcome prospectively through the already
  ratified D4-D8 policy, Posting Profile, mapping, and provider Carrier Plan
  authorities; and
- Asym controls the provider file mechanics that must be exact for the current
  QuickBooks Online or Xero importer.

An arbitrary spreadsheet designer would not provide more reliable tenant
control. It would turn every tenant into an untested integration, duplicate the
D4-D8 accounting configuration, and make the word “compatible” indefensible.

The hardened product is one immutable, Legal-Entity-, destination-, and
delivery-lane-pinned **Accounting Delivery Package** compiled from one frozen
Provider Delivery Plan. It presents one logical package page, one direct
download when there is one import file, ordered direct downloads when there are
several, and an optional download-all bundle. Download, provider staging,
provider finalization, staff testimony, exact readback, and reconciliation
remain separate truths.

No package is called **Ready to import** unless:

1. the applicable D7 or D8 destination Capability Certificate is current;
2. an Asym-owned Import Surface Conformance Record proves the exact provider,
   country or region, subscription capability, importer, current template,
   limits, serializer, and recovery behavior; and
3. the immutable generated bytes and digests match the frozen Provider
   Delivery Plan and pass balance, coverage, and privacy checks.

This does not certify that the staff member has provider-side permission at
that moment, that the import succeeded, that Xero drafts were posted, that
QuickBooks records remain unchanged, that the tenant's accountant approved a
new policy, or that final bank reconciliation occurred.

## Existing Phase 20 authorities

D16 is a delivery projection. It does not create another accounting authority.

- **D2** owns one immutable Accounting Release, artifact-always evidence, and
  mutually exclusive direct and staff-mediated delivery lanes.
- **D3** owns Legal Entity and exact Accounting Destination identity.
- **D4** owns Accounting Posting Intent, Canonical Accounting Effect, source
  coverage, and accountant-confirmed semantic policy.
- **D5** owns the prospective Posting Profile, source-purpose provider-native
  recipe, grain, and grouping.
- **D6** owns Designation-to-Accounting-Reporting-Target resolution and exact
  mapping coverage.
- **D7/D8** own QBO/Xero Carrier Plans, provider reporting visibility, and
  destination capability evidence.
- **D11** owns permissible posting-period treatment and append-only
  compensating corrections.
- **D12** owns cadence and the atomic Accounting Release fence.
- **D13** owns cause-authoritative Accounting Exception Cases.
- **D14** owns destination-pinned provider authorization and connection
  lifecycle.
- **D15** owns direct-delivery capacity and makes the staff-mediated lane an
  explicit pre-freeze choice, never a silent fallback.

The package may display these choices and deep-link to their owning setup. It
may not let staff edit accounts, grain, dates, tax, tracking, representation,
or destination on one frozen package.

## Current QuickBooks Online evidence

Intuit's current import guidance shows that QuickBooks Online does not have one
stable universal CSV contract:

- The current [common import guidance](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/import-export-data-files/common-questions-importing-data-quickbooks-online/L4OYJRFdj_US_en_US)
  lists many importable record families, but says QuickBooks imports only
  record-specific fields, directs users to the current in-product sample, and
  limits ordinary spreadsheets to fewer than 1,000 rows.
- The [journal-entry importer](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/import-export-data-files/import-journal-entries-quickbooks-online/L4tQBwbs7_US_en_US)
  requires journal number, date, account name, description, debit, and credit;
  existing accounts; `Parent: Subaccount` paths; vendor or customer names for
  A/P or A/R; and enabled Class or Location tracking when those fields are
  used.
- Duplicate journal-number warnings depend on a tenant preference. They are not
  a durable idempotency contract.
- The [QBO sales-receipt workflow](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/sales-receipts/create-send-sales-receipts-quickbooks-online/L9vG2pbTQ_US_en_US)
  documents an Advanced batch-import path, while regional documentation and
  field requirements differ.
- [Spreadsheet Sync](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/accounting-bookkeeping/spreadsheet-sync-faq/L3FiuAq13_US_en_US)
  is a separate Advanced or Accountant workflow with its own permissions,
  application, templates, validation, and concurrency behavior. It is not a
  universal upload format.
- Class tracking is subscription- and settings-dependent; current
  [class guidance](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/class-list/create-manage-classes-quickbooks-online/L1QzEOUxM_US_en_US)
  limits creation to Plus or Advanced administrators.

Consequences:

- A file cannot be certified merely because it has balanced debit and credit
  columns.
- Certification must bind country or region, subscription capability,
  importer route, current sample or schema, required settings, reference
  paths, permissions, and row or transaction limits.
- Accounts, customers, vendors, items, classes, and locations must be resolved
  before package generation. Release-time auto-creation or list overwrite is a
  footgun and is prohibited.
- QBO bank-transaction uploads belong to D10 bank evidence. They are not
  Accounting Delivery Packages.
- Manual re-import is not a safe retry. This is an inference from the absence
  of a durable file-import idempotency contract and from preference-dependent
  duplicate warnings.

## Current Xero evidence

Xero also uses record-specific import surfaces:

- Current [Xero import guidance](https://central.xero.com/s/article/Import-data-in-to-Xero)
  states that each record type has a specific supported file format.
- The standard [manual-journal workflow](https://central.xero.com/s/article/Add-import-and-post-manual-journals-UK)
  creates draft journals, requires Advisor or Standard + reports access,
  accepts up to 300 lines per template, and supports base-currency manual
  journals only.
- Manual-journal lines support at most two tracking category pairs, matching
  the provider's tracking constraints.
- Xero is rolling out a modernized manual-journal interface from June 2026,
  including terminology and navigation changes, while preserving import
  semantics. See [Xero's current interface-change notice](https://central.xero.com/s/article/About-the-changes-to-manual-journals-in-Xero-US).
- [Bills and credit notes](https://central.xero.com/s/article/Import-bills-and-credit-notes)
  import as drafts. Exact contact, account, tax, currency, and tracking values
  matter; some flows can create contacts or untracked items or update contact
  data if staff select provider options.
- The [Manual Journals API](https://developer.xero.com/documentation/api/accounting/manualjournals/)
  has capabilities not represented by the ordinary CSV importer, including
  provider IDs, exact readback, source URL, status, and attachment workflows.

Consequences:

- Xero's ordinary journal package must be described as creating **draft
  journals**, not posted general-ledger results.
- Imported, posted or approved, and independently verified are separate
  milestones.
- D5's prohibition on a silent Manual Journal fallback still governs. A
  manual-journal package cannot replace a Receive Money, Spend Money, Payment,
  clearing, or bank flow when the resulting Accounting Effect would differ.
- Bills and invoices are available only when the source intent is a genuine
  payable or receivable and the D5 recipe selects them. They are not format
  preferences.
- Automatic contact, item, account, tracking, or tax-rate creation is
  prohibited during delivery.
- Xero bank-statement imports remain D10 bank evidence. Generating one from an
  expected Accounting Effect would fabricate bank truth.
- The Conversion Toolbox is a migration surface, not a routine Phase 20
  delivery lane.
- Manual re-import must be treated as capable of creating duplicates because
  the standard importer documents no durable external idempotency key.

## Comparable-product evidence

The useful modern pattern is broad but bounded configuration:

- [Ramp custom accounting CSV](https://support.ramp.com/custom-accounting-csv-exports)
  lets customers show, hide, rename, reorder, and hard-code supported fields,
  while preserving required semantic columns for each export shape.
- [Ramp Universal CSV](https://support.ramp.com/universal-csv-overview/)
  combines imported accounting fields, required coding, review, export
  history, and bounded reset behavior. It demonstrates why a useful import
  workspace must show readiness and history, but its generic-provider model
  should not override QBO/Xero's exact schemas.
- Ramp's [synced-state guidance](https://support.ramp.com/marking-transactions-as-synced)
  explicitly distinguishes preview-only downloads from committed workflow
  actions. Asym must be stricter: a committed download still does not prove
  provider import.
- [Bloomerang's current QBO guidance](https://help.bloomerang.com/en/articles/13382604-quickbooks-online-integration-with-bloomerang-fundraising)
  warns that overlapping integrations create duplicate data.
- [Virtuous QBO guidance](https://support.virtuous.org/hc/en-us/articles/360051317172-How-Do-I-Sync-Data-With-Quickbooks-Online)
  exposes mapping, rollup, duplicate-number, backlog, and failed-transaction
  hazards.
- [CiviCRM accounting batches](https://docs.civicrm.org/user/en/latest/contributions/accounting-integration/)
  distinguish open, closed, and exported work and make exported batches
  immutable.

Asym should borrow guided mapping, exact preview, export history, and immutable
evidence. It should not copy generic CSV programming, unsafe “mark synced”
semantics, or whole-backlog retry behavior.

## Product contract

### Accounting Delivery Package

An Accounting Delivery Package is one immutable staff-mediated delivery
projection for one Accounting Release. It is bound to:

- Tenant;
- Legal Entity;
- exact QBO realm or Xero organization and environment;
- selected staff-mediated delivery lane;
- currency and accounting date;
- frozen D4-D8 and D11 authority versions;
- Provider Delivery Plan;
- current destination Capability Certificate;
- Import Surface Conformance Record;
- compiler and serializer versions; and
- package and part digests.

The package does not become contribution, settlement, bank, accounting-policy,
provider, or reconciliation truth.

### Import Surface Conformance Record

This is a narrow Asym-owned evidence record, not another tenant configuration,
certificate platform, or state machine. It records:

- provider and environment;
- country or region and applicable locale;
- provider subscription or capability tier;
- importer name, navigation surface, object type, and expected post-import
  state;
- current sample or template fingerprint and provider documentation version;
- required provider-side role and enabled settings;
- required, optional, and prohibited fields;
- exact date, number, sign, decimal, quoting, encoding, and line-ending rules;
- transaction, row, file-size, and logical-record limits;
- list-reference and parent-path rules;
- duplicate, partial-success, undo, and error behavior;
- certified compiler and serializer versions;
- production-shaped fixture evidence;
- verification time, expiry, and quarantine reason; and
- exact recovery constraints.

It must not be called a new D15 Certified Execution Envelope. D15 owns direct
provider capacity; D16 owns conformance of staff-import files.

### Destination evidence

A production package described as **Ready to import** requires the active D7 or
D8 live destination certificate. Staff-mediated means that Asym does not write
the release to QBO or Xero. It does not mean Asym guesses the destination's
accounts, tracking, tax, currency, plan, lock dates, or capabilities.

The provider connection may be used for discovery and exact readback while the
release remains in the staff-mediated delivery lane. Where provider OAuth is
not granular, Asym must enforce the no-write boundary locally.

Without live destination evidence, Asym may retain the immutable Accounting
Evidence Artifact and show a clearly labelled provider-formatted preview. It
must not cross the release fence or say **Ready to import**.

## Package composition and UX

One logical package does not mean one mandatory ZIP.

- When one import file is sufficient, the primary action directly downloads
  that file.
- When several files are required, the package page provides clearly numbered,
  ordered direct downloads and an optional **Download all (.zip)** action.
- Instructions, destination, control totals, and outcome capture remain in the
  accessible application page. They are not hidden inside a README.
- Technical manifests, hashes, compiler versions, and raw evidence remain
  available under **Technical evidence**.
- The Accounting Evidence Artifact remains independently archived and linked.

Every part has:

- deterministic human-readable filename;
- stable short release and part reference;
- object or record family;
- expected transaction and row counts;
- debit and credit control totals;
- source-coverage digest;
- byte digest;
- required import order;
- expected provider state after import; and
- provider-specific next step.

Parts split only at complete, independently valid provider-operation
boundaries. A logical journal, bill, receipt, or deposit is never cut apart to
satisfy a row limit. If no certified partition exists, the package is blocked
before release.

### Routine three-step workspace

1. **Review what will land in QuickBooks or Xero**
   - exact Legal Entity and provider organization;
   - period, currency, total, and short release reference;
   - provider object type and expected provider state;
   - source, account, dimension, tax, and control totals;
   - exact and summarized designation coverage;
   - file count and import order;
   - privacy consequence; and
   - one concise warning or blocker when needed.
2. **Download the ordered import file or files**
   - one primary action;
   - byte-identical re-download;
   - current provider instructions;
   - persistent warning that import is not idempotent; and
   - no claim that download means delivery.
3. **Record what happened**
   - everything imported;
   - imported as drafts, where applicable;
   - imported and finalized;
   - some items imported;
   - nothing imported; or
   - uncertain.

Provider readback may add exact independent evidence. It never overwrites what
staff reported.

### Accessibility contract

- Use semantic headings, lists, buttons, links, fieldsets, legends, labels, and
  data tables.
- Keep visual, DOM, reading, and keyboard order aligned.
- Give icon-only and file actions explicit accessible names.
- Preserve focus after downloads and move focus to a linked error summary after
  validation failure.
- Associate every field error and instruction programmatically.
- Announce package-generation completion, material status changes, and outcome
  submission politely without notification storms.
- Never communicate destination risk, partial success, or draft-versus-posted
  state through color, icon, motion, or toast alone.
- Reflow at narrow widths and 200% zoom; use card alternatives for dense
  tables without hiding evidence.
- Mobile staff may review and download. The UI may recommend desktop for the
  provider's importer but may not make the evidence inaccessible.

These requirements align with WCAG guidance for
[status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages),
[error identification](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/error-identification),
[focus order](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-order.html), and
[reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html).

## Outcome truth and recovery

The design never persists one overloaded `exported` or `synced` boolean.
Separate append-only evidence supports these derived user-facing states:

- package ready;
- downloaded;
- staff reported imported or provider-staged;
- provider draft;
- provider posted or approved;
- partial;
- outcome unknown;
- independently verified exact;
- verification mismatch; and
- later provider drift.

For Xero, **Imported as drafts** and **Posted** are always distinct. For QBO,
provider-specific copy describes the actual importer result rather than using a
generic “posted” label.

Re-download returns the exact original bytes. It does not make re-import safe.
Because ordinary file import has no durable Asym-controlled idempotency key,
**re-import is never retry**.

Partial or uncertain results:

- preserve successful or proved provider work;
- identify the exact file, provider operation, or transaction family involved;
- open or update the D13 cause-owned Accounting Exception Case;
- require exact provider inspection or credible staff evidence before another
  write;
- generate a recovery package only for work proved not to have been imported;
- retain lineage to the original immutable package and release; and
- use D11 compensating releases for posted errors or corrections.

A provider-template change never silently regenerates a historical download.
A replacement package requires D2 proof-gated recovery, proof that unsafe
duplicate posting cannot occur, a new immutable conformance record and digest,
and retention of the original package.

There is no generic **Undo import**. Provider drafts may be deleted under the
provider's rules; posted or approved records are corrected through provider and
D11 append-only accounting semantics.

## Security and privacy

[OWASP CSV Injection guidance](https://owasp.org/www-community/attacks/CSV_Injection)
states that formula-leading values can execute in spreadsheet software and
that no universal sanitization works for every spreadsheet and downstream
consumer.

Therefore:

- provider import cells use typed, allow-listed facts and system-generated
  PII-safe labels;
- arbitrary donor, missionary, care, staff, memo, or user-entered text does
  not enter machine import files;
- generated references cannot begin with formula control characters;
- provider-specific quoting and escaping are certified without adding
  characters that change provider meaning;
- machine import files and human-readable spreadsheet reports use separate
  safety contracts;
- a modified file is no longer represented as Asym-certified;
- packages use private storage, authorization on every request, short-lived
  download grants, and byte digests;
- download and outcome permissions are Tenant-, Legal-Entity-, destination-,
  and purpose-scoped;
- logs and metrics exclude secrets, raw files, unrestricted provider data, and
  donor PII; and
- optional provider error or screenshot uploads are allow-listed, size-limited,
  scanned, privately stored, and authorized.

This follows OWASP's
[authorization guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html)
and [file-upload guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/File_Upload_Cheat_Sheet.html).

## Production proof gates

No provider/import-surface recipe is production-ready until all applicable
evidence passes.

### Compiler and package proof

- Golden byte fixtures for every certified provider, country or region,
  importer, object type, schema, and locale.
- Exact headers, order, quoting, encoding, line endings, date and decimal
  formats, signs, continuation grouping, and deterministic filenames.
- Zero, one, typical, maximum, and maximum-plus-one transactions and rows.
- One logical transaction larger than a provider file limit.
- Stable ordering and byte-identical re-generation.
- Debit equals credit and complete source/mapping coverage.
- Control totals by account, dimension, tax, currency, file, and provider
  operation.
- Unicode, commas, quotes, newlines, formula-leading characters, and long
  provider names.

### Provider proof

- Import the exact generated bytes through the current supported QBO or Xero
  UI in controlled destination fixtures.
- Verify expected provider objects, drafts, accounts, dimensions, tax,
  currency, dates, references, and rounding.
- Finalize drafts where applicable and compare the provider accounting effect
  with the Canonical Accounting Effect.
- Exercise current provider roles, subscription capabilities, enabled
  settings, close or lock dates, inactive references, region changes, and plan
  downgrade.
- Verify partial success, duplicate attempt, edited file, wrong destination,
  unknown outcome, and exact recovery of only proved-unimported work.
- Confirm that routine recipes never fall back to journals, bank statements, or
  other semantically different imports.

### Security, scale, accessibility, and operations

- Tenant, Legal Entity, destination, environment, storage, cache, and download
  negative-isolation tests.
- Formula-injection and privacy tests for every machine and human artifact.
- Expired link, revoked role, modified file, malicious optional upload, and
  redacted logging tests.
- 100, 1,000, and 10,000 source facts, 1,200 and 10,000 Designations, maximum
  provider parts, concurrent tenants, restart, and storage-failure tests.
- Keyboard, screen reader, focus, status-announcement, error-summary, contrast,
  zoom/reflow, touch, and reduced-motion verification.
- Bookkeeper usability proof that representative staff can identify the exact
  destination and expected result, import ordered parts, distinguish draft
  from posted and staff-reported from verified, record a partial outcome, and
  avoid unsafe re-import without assistance.
- Named provider-contract, security, certification, support, and incident
  owners; drift canaries; quarantine and kill switches; and rehearsed
  duplicate, partial-import, wrong-destination, and template-change runbooks.

## Ruthless synthesis

The permanent path is:

1. keep accounting choice in D4-D8 and make package configuration prospective;
2. define the exact closed QBO/Xero import-surface matrix and narrow conformance
   record;
3. refuse any staff-import recipe that cannot preserve the active Provider
   Delivery Plan;
4. generate one immutable package with deterministic complete-operation parts
   and artifact-always control evidence;
5. make the ordinary staff workflow review, download, and record outcome;
6. preserve downloaded, staged, finalized, verified, partial, and unknown truth
   separately;
7. recover only work proved not to have been imported; and
8. ship a provider recipe only after current provider-UI, security, scale,
   accessibility, and bookkeeper-usability evidence passes.

## Independent provider review

An independent review against current Intuit documentation confirms the
proposed QBO boundary and adds one necessary qualification:

- Intuit's [common import guidance](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/import-export-data-files/common-questions-importing-data-quickbooks-online/L4OYJRFdj_US_en_US)
  lists many importable records, generally requires list data before
  transactions, limits ordinary spreadsheets to fewer than 1,000 rows, and
  directs users to the current record-specific sample. That matrix does not
  prove that every importer is available in every country, subscription, or
  staged QBO interface.
- The [journal-entry importer](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/import-export-data-files/import-journal-entries-quickbooks-online/L4tQBwbs7_US_en_US)
  depends on existing accounts, exact parent/subaccount paths, A/P or A/R
  names, enabled Class or Location settings, field mapping, and
  preference-dependent duplicate-number warnings. A balanced file is
  therefore necessary but not sufficient evidence of safe importability.
- QBO's [manual bank-upload workflow](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/import-transactions/manually-upload-transactions-quickbooks-online/L0rE9OXBz_US_en_US)
  creates bank-feed candidates for matching and categorization. It is not an
  exact Accounting Release posting surface and remains excluded under D10.
- Intuit's API-level [`requestid` contract](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/learn/learn-basic-field-definitions)
  and [per-operation fault model](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/develop/troubleshooting/error-codes)
  apply to direct API delivery, not browser-mediated CSV import. They cannot
  make a downloaded file idempotent or prove that staff imported it.

**Necessary correction:** a QBO package must not become **Ready to import**
from a generic QBO product label, a provider-plan check, or public
documentation alone. Its Import Surface Conformance Record must prove the
exact destination's country or region, subscription capability, enabled
settings, importer route, current in-product sample fingerprint, reference
behavior, and production-shaped fixture. Public Intuit documentation does not
establish atomic rollback or one uniform partial-success contract across all
manual importers; until a specific certified surface proves otherwise, Asym
must treat manual import as non-idempotent, potentially partial, and
unverified without exact readback. This last recovery rule is an explicit
product inference from the documented provider behavior, not an Intuit claim.

This review supported the founder's subsequent ratification of D16 and does not
change D2, D4-D8, or D13-D15. It supports the accepted split: tenants own
accounting meaning through the ratified authorities, while Asym owns and
certifies QBO file mechanics.
