# Phase 24 D36 — Prospective Save and Explicit Current-Work Application

**Status:** Founder choice pressure-tested and recorded as Reserved  
**Decision:** How a saved Website work-recovery policy may be deliberately
applied to Needs-assignment occurrences already current at the policy cutover  
**Review date:** 2026-08-29  
**Scope:** D35 Tenant recovery policy, current recovery-routing generations,
shared Tasks Hub projections, personal engagement, and reusable bulk-operation
mechanics

## Final disposition

**Accept Option 1 with required amendments.**

The choice fits D29, D35, ADR-0027, ADR-0183, and Core’s platform boundaries
only when “apply” is a second durable Website operation—not a settings-side
checkbox, browser loop, generic task edit, one giant database transaction, or
Inngest-owned workflow.

The prospective save succeeds independently. Existing work keeps its current
applied policy/routing basis until separately reviewed and changed. The
current-work operation creates per-occurrence differential successor routing
under fresh expected heads. It never changes Needs assignment, correction
ownership, access, content, publication, Giving, or finance.

## Exact corrected decision

> Saving **Returned Website work** SHALL append one immutable D35 Tenant policy
> revision, advance its expected current head, establish one authoritative
> cutover fence, and return one durable policy receipt. The save is complete and
> prospective by itself. It governs recovery occurrences whose authoritative
> Needs-assignment generation begins after that cutover and SHALL NOT mutate,
> reroute, notify, mark, re-engage, or remove any occurrence already current.
>
> The save result SHALL say both consequences plainly: the saved setting applies
> to new returned Website work, and current Needs-assignment work is unchanged.
> Failure, cancellation, staleness, or abandonment of any later current-work
> operation never rolls back or makes ambiguous the completed save.
>
> Before save, helper copy SHALL explain: **This setting applies to new returned
> work. Existing Needs assignment work and coordinator tasks will not change
> until someone separately applies it to current work.**
>
> When current work may differ and the actor has a distinct current-work
> application capability, Core SHALL show an **Update current work** section
> with **Review current-work impact**. It SHALL NOT auto-open another modal,
> hide current effects inside **Save**, require the follow-up step, imply an
> unauthorized count, or nag when staff deliberately choose **Not now**.
>
> **Review current-work impact** SHALL request a fresh server-computed,
> permission-safe impact bound to the exact Tenant/environment, D35 purpose,
> saved target policy head, policy cutover, current actor authorization,
> source/routing heads, resolver versions, and a code-owned cohort contract.
> D37 now defines that contract as the complete compatible pre-cutover Tenant
> cohort proved from authoritative source heads and a closed producer/version
> catalog under a distinct Tenant-wide application capability.
>
> The preview SHALL show only consequences the actor is currently authorized to
> know: safe Website-item and personal-assignment units, new personal
> assignments, assignments preserved with no new unread, assignments that would
> end because coordinator responsibility changed, and visibly unchanged or
> changed work. It SHALL never reveal restricted work, hidden counts, Sites,
> locales, D30/D34 content, source identity, sibling engagement, per-person
> qualification, or coverage through differences, timing, errors, or totals.
> The surface always says it shows only information the actor may view.
>
> Website-item counts and personal-assignment counts SHALL remain separate; Core
> never collapses them into “changes.” Approximate counts SHALL NOT appear in a
> consequence-bearing action. A numeric action label may appear only when the
> server proves the count exact, complete, current, and fully disclosable.
>
> The stable review title is **Apply this setting to current Needs assignment
> work?** The ordinary primary action is **Apply setting to current work**. When
> the target is `source_lane_only`, consequence copy and the primary action
> SHALL instead say **Use Needs assignment only for current work** and explain
> that personal coordinator assignments end while source work remains.
>
> The review surface itself is the confirmation. It SHALL present the target
> setting, added/preserved/ended personal-assignment consequences, unchanged
> source/access/public effects, and current reproof before the primary action.
> Core adds no nested confirmation, destructive styling by default,
> type-to-confirm phrase, remembered consent, “don't ask again,” countdown, or
> timeout pressure.
>
> On confirmation, one trusted server command SHALL recompute impact rather than
> trust a client list, recipient set, count, preview body, occurrence ID, actor,
> Tenant, or hidden identifier. The submitted opaque impact identity/digest and
> expected heads must still match. A materially changed policy, authority,
> cohort, recipient impact, or meaning returns **Coordinator settings or current
> work changed. Nothing was applied. Review the updated impact.** The completed
> prospective save remains intact.
>
> A valid command SHALL append one immutable product-owned **Current-work policy
> application** header plus normalized occurrence-member/result records. Trusted
> server state derives Tenant, environment, purpose, target policy head, cutover,
> actor Party, authorization context, cohort-contract version, semantic command
> identity, impact digest, and time. Caller-controlled attribution, membership,
> result, count, end reason, or completion is rejected or ignored.
>
> One application is one user decision but not one unbounded cross-backlog
> transaction. Every captured occurrence SHALL process atomically under its own
> exact source, Needs-assignment, routing, policy, authorization, action-scope,
> and recipient-resolution heads. One member failure never corrupts another or
> the source lane.
>
> Every accepted member SHALL converge to exactly one durable terminal outcome:
> `applied`, `already_current`, `source_changed`,
> `target_policy_superseded`, `not_current`, `not_authorized`,
> `recipient_resolution_indeterminate`, or `execution_failed`. Header/member
> totals conserve the accepted cohort; no member disappears, and in-progress
> state is never reported as applied.
>
> For a still-current member, Core SHALL use D35’s complete/zero/indeterminate
> resolver and append one differential successor recovery-routing generation
> under the existing source occurrence and shared recovery-task identity. It
> compares stable Party, recipient purpose, task identity, and exact
> Source-action scope—not order, display name, profile row, URL, or generic task
> assignee.
>
> A coordinator admitted before and after remains one continuing recipient:
> assignment identity, creation history, engagement lineage, and read/unread
> state are preserved; no new item, badge, notification, or assignment event is
> fabricated. A newly admitted, currently authorized recipient alone receives
> one fresh personal assignment and unread engagement. A removed recipient ends
> with typed code `coordinator_policy_changed` and user-facing **Coordinator
> responsibility changed**, never Completed, Read, Dismissed, Reassigned
> correction work, Source resolved, or Access revoked.
>
> Applying `source_lane_only` ends applicable personal coordinator assignments
> under that route-derived result while every current Needs-assignment source row
> and independently authorized source action remains intact. Applying named
> coordinators never assigns the correction to those coordinators and never
> performs or completes correction work.
>
> Work entering Needs assignment after the policy cutover already uses the saved
> policy and is `already_current`; it never receives a duplicate task or unread
> reset. Older work assigned, ended, superseded, made inapplicable, or recurrent
> before member commit becomes `source_changed` or `not_current` and receives
> no stale projection. Recurrence creates a successor occurrence governed by
> the then-current policy.
>
> Before every occurrence commit, the target policy head must still be current.
> If a later valid save supersedes it, every uncommitted member stops as
> `target_policy_superseded`. Already committed successor generations remain
> truthful history and are never rolled back. Staff correct remaining mixed
> current work only through the newer saved policy and a new explicit
> current-work application.
>
> Transient execution may retry only within the same immutable application,
> member, target policy, source/routing heads, and durable semantic key. Retry is
> code-owned and bounded. After terminal `recipient_resolution_indeterminate`
> or `execution_failed`, the member cannot release a delayed task; an authorized
> actor must obtain fresh impact and invoke a successor application. Exact
> same-key replay returns the original receipt; changed meaning conflicts.
>
> Application progress/result SHALL be route-addressable and resumable after
> navigation, reload, or lost response. The UI distinguishes checking, ready,
> already aligned, accepted/in progress, applied, safe skip, superseded,
> unverified, projection delayed, and terminal failure with durable in-page
> status and current safe counts. Toasts may supplement but never replace this
> surface. Closing it does not cancel committed work; no misleading one-click
> Undo appears after any member commits.
>
> Preview, application, member, routing, task, and engagement reads SHALL apply
> current Tenant/purpose authorization and query ceilings. Access loss removes
> protected presentation without changing body-free audit. Events, logs, caches,
> search, analytics, AI, exports, channels, and Inngest contain only
> schema-versioned Tenant-safe identifiers and result codes—not D30/D34 bodies,
> source detail, roster snapshots, or hidden impact data.
>
> Inngest, if used, may claim and chunk already accepted application members and
> reconcile identifier-only results. It never owns the cutover, cohort, preview,
> policy, authorization, routing, task, engagement, idempotency, terminality,
> rollback, or source lane. A synchronous executor and Inngest SHALL share one
> product command; there are never two mutation paths.
>
> D36 creates no correction assignment, source lifecycle change, CMS/public
> mutation, publication, Giving/finance effect, email, bell item, SMS, push,
> digest, reminder, date, SLA, escalation, claim, performance score, Site
> override, generic task mutation/bulk API, or cross-source/Mobilize application.
> Later sources reuse only mechanics after their own cohort/privacy/action
> decision.

## Problem validity, necessity, and strongest alternative

The decision solves a real workflow: a Tenant may add coordinators because a
current ownerless backlog already exists. Future-only behavior forces repeated
manual recovery, while automatic retroactivity turns an ordinary settings save
into hidden private fan-out.

The strongest alternative is **future occurrences only**. It has the smallest
mutation and audit surface. It loses because there is then no coherent,
low-friction way to repair current accountability after a deliberate policy
change.

Option 1 wins only because save and apply stay independent, consequences are
reviewed, each occurrence changes under exact current heads, and the source lane
remains complete through every result.

## Current modern-practice review

- [HubSpot workflow publication](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/create-workflows)
  explicitly asks whether to enroll records that already meet criteria or only
  future records. This supports deliberate existing-versus-future effects.
  HubSpot’s displayed existing-contact number may be an estimate; Core does not
  import approximate counts into consequence-bearing actions.
- [HubSpot manual enrollment](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manually-enroll-objects-into-workflows)
  separates workflow editing from enrollment authority. Core likewise requires
  a current-work capability distinct from policy editing.
- [Microsoft Dynamics routing rules](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/create-rules-automatically-route-cases)
  requires an explicit action to apply routing rules to existing cases rather
  than treating activation as silent retroactivity.
- [Jira bulk editing](https://support.atlassian.com/jira-software-cloud/docs/edit-multiple-issues/)
  separates selection, operation, review, confirmation, and acknowledgement,
  and requires relevant permissions. Core adopts review/confirm and per-member
  authority while rejecting generic task-owned field mutation.
- [Salesforce mass transfer](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=platform.admin_transfer.htm&language=en_US)
  requires explicit transfer permission and sharing access for records the
  actor does not own. Core likewise treats backlog application as governed.
- [Contentful Entry Tasks](https://www.contentful.com/developers/docs/references/content-management-api/entry-tasks/)
  warns that task assignment does not prove entry-read access. D36 therefore
  re-proves every personal recipient and action.
- [RFC 9110 `9.2.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-9.2.2)
  limits automatic retry after an uncertain failure to idempotent requests or
  cases where the client can determine the original request did not apply.
  D36 uses durable receipts before offering another apply.
- [W3C confirmation technique G168](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Techniques/general/G168)
  says confirmation should identify the selected action and consequences when
  an effect is not readily reversible. D36’s impact surface supplies that
  information; a nested dialog adds no safety.
- [W3C status-message guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/status-messages.html)
  requires programmatically determinable outcome feedback without forcing
  focus. D36 keeps durable status and does not rely on a toast.
- [OWASP authorization guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html)
  requires least privilege, default deny, and authorization on every request.
- [Inngest idempotency guidance](https://www.inngest.com/docs/guides/handling-idempotency)
  states application effects must be idempotent and event-ID dedupe is bounded
  to 24 hours. Durable uniqueness remains in Asym Postgres.

**Best-practice conclusion:** deliberate existing-versus-future treatment,
fresh consequence review, exact authorization, per-member atomic effects,
truthful partial results, and durable feedback are modern proven patterns. No
source establishes one universal batch size or nonprofit preference. D37
records the complete compatible Tenant cohort; production evidence must decide
measured execution budgets.

## Domain model, ownership, and invariants

### Canonical concepts

**Policy cutover** is the immutable ordering fence written by a successful D35
policy save. It distinguishes recovery occurrences governed prospectively by
the new head from occurrences already current under an earlier applied policy
basis. It is an internal invariant, not staff vocabulary.

**Current-work policy application** is one explicit, auditable Website
operational command applying one saved D35 policy head to a code-owned cohort of
recovery occurrences already in Needs assignment at that cutover. It owns
application intent, membership, progress, and result—not source work, policy
truth, task completion, engagement, or authorization.

**Differential recovery-routing generation** is the immutable per-occurrence
successor that preserves continuing coordinator assignment/engagement, admits
new qualified coordinators, and truthfully ends removed coordinator
responsibility. It never assigns the correction.

### Ownership map

| Fact                             | Authority                           | Derived/projection use            | Never authority                   |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Saved setting and cutover        | D35 recovery-policy head/receipt    | Preview/application target        | Client form, task, worker         |
| Needs-assignment occurrence/head | Website source                      | Cohort applicability/member fence | Application row, task status      |
| Cohort contract                  | D37 + Website application command   | Server member enumeration         | Client IDs, visible list, count   |
| Impact preview                   | Website policy-application reader   | Human consequence review          | Mutation authority, durable audit |
| Application header/member/result | Website operational recovery domain | Progress, audit, reconciliation   | Source state, policy head         |
| Routing generation               | Website recovery-routing contract   | Task recipient applicability      | Generic task assignee             |
| Shared task/recipient assignment | Tasks Hub                           | My tasks and coordination         | Correction ownership/completion   |
| Personal engagement              | Tasks/ADR-0027 model                | Read/unread presentation          | Routing/source completion         |
| Executor progress                | Dispatch ledger/optional Inngest    | Technical operations              | Application/member outcome        |

### Permanent invariants

1. Policy save and current-work application are separate commands, receipts,
   idempotency keys, authorization operations, and consequences.
2. Prospective-save success is never rolled back by preview/apply failure.
3. The cutover classifies pre-existing versus later occurrences unambiguously;
   recurrence is a successor occurrence.
4. Existing occurrences retain an explicit applied-policy/routing basis until
   an accepted current-work application changes it.
5. Current authorization still applies immediately; an old applied basis never
   preserves access.
6. Preview is current, permission-safe, non-authoritative, and recomputed at
   confirmation.
7. Client IDs, recipients, counts, or preview bodies never define the cohort.
8. One application targets one exact Tenant/environment/purpose/policy head and
   one D37 code-owned cohort contract.
9. Application membership is normalized, immutable, same-scope, and
   conservation-complete.
10. Every member commits atomically under its own expected heads; the entire
    cohort need not be one transaction.
11. One member has exactly one terminal outcome; progress is not applied.
12. Policy supersession stops every uncommitted member.
13. Already committed members are historical truth and are corrected only by a
    successor policy/application.
14. Source lane, source actionability, correction ownership, access, and
    public/Giving/financial state never change through D36.
15. D35 complete/zero/indeterminate resolution remains the release algebra.
16. Continuing recipients preserve one assignment/engagement lineage.
17. Newly admitted recipients alone receive fresh assignment/unread.
18. Removed recipients end as **Coordinator responsibility changed**, never
    completed/read/dismissed/source-resolved/access-revoked.
19. Applying lane-only ends personal applicability, not the source lane.
20. Work already using the target setting is `already_current` and never
    duplicates engagement.
21. Source change before commit releases no stale projection.
22. Bounded retries cannot outlive exact application/policy/source heads;
    terminal failure cannot release delayed work.
23. Exact semantic replay returns the receipt; changed meaning conflicts.
24. Protected content and hidden impact never enter executor/channel/log/
    analytics/search/AI/cache/export payloads.
25. Inngest is optional execution only; product records own truth.
26. D36 mechanics never become a generic workflow/bulk-task product or carry a
    Website roster into another source.

## Best staff and Tenant UX/UI

### Prospective save

Permanent helper copy:

```text
This setting applies to new returned work. Existing Needs assignment work and
coordinator tasks will not change until someone separately applies it to
current work.
```

After save:

```text
Returned Website work setting saved

Ana and Joel may receive tasks for new eligible returned work.
Existing work has not changed.

Update current work
Apply the saved setting to current Needs assignment work after reviewing
its effect.

[Review current-work impact]  [Done]
```

Save never auto-opens the review. If the actor leaves, the settings Card may
retain the calm **Update current work** follow-up only when exact authority and
permission-safe relevance are known. It is not an alert, error, recurring nag,
or unread item.

### Impact surface

Use one route-addressable responsive Base Maia Dialog or Sheet consistent with
the final implementation context; never nest it inside the settings editor:

```text
Apply this setting to current Needs assignment work?

The saved setting already applies to new returned work.
This separate action updates existing work.

Current Website items
4 will be checked

Personal coordinator assignments
3 will be added
2 will stay as they are — no new unread
1 will end — Coordinator responsibility changed

Core will recheck source state and authorization before every change.

Correction owners, Website content, publishing, and the public website will
not change. No emails, reminders, due dates, or target dates will be created.

[Not now]  [Apply setting to current work]
```

Units remain explicit: four Website items and six personal-assignment effects
are not “ten changes.” The label **4 will be checked** is used only when exact
and authorized. Per-person counts are omitted when they reveal Site/source
qualification.

For lane-only:

```text
Use Needs assignment only for current work?

6 personal coordinator assignments will end.
The shared Needs assignment lane and underlying correction work will remain.

[Not now]  [Use Needs assignment only for current work]
```

## Conceptual persistence, RLS, and authorization

### Smallest durable model

1. Existing immutable D35 policy revision/head/save receipt with monotonic
   cutover fence.
2. Immutable current-work policy-application header:
   Tenant/environment/purpose, target policy head, save receipt, cohort-contract
   version, expected impact digest, trusted actor/time, semantic command key,
   application state, safe totals, and audit linkage.
3. Normalized immutable occurrence members:
   source-work/recovery occurrence, pre-application routing head, source/auth
   fences, claim state, terminal outcome, successor routing receipt, and
   body-free failure/retry class.
4. Existing differential routing generations and normalized recipient
   membership/continuity.
5. Existing shared task identity, recipient assignment, personal engagement,
   source-end projection, and identifier-only outbox/dispatch records.

Preview rows need not become write authority. Confirmation recomputes impact and
persists the accepted application plus actual normalized membership/results.
Do not add a mutable `apply_pending` flag; divergence derives from immutable
applied-policy/routing receipts and the current policy head.

### Constraints

- Same-scope composite keys/FKs across Tenant, environment, purpose, policy,
  application, member, source occurrence, routing generation, Party, task, and
  engagement.
- One current policy head per Tenant/environment/purpose.
- One application semantic key per exact policy/cohort/impact meaning.
- One member per exact application/occurrence.
- One terminal outcome per member and exact result/header conservation.
- One successor routing effect per member/application.
- One active recipient assignment per occurrence/purpose/Party/surface.
- Immutable trusted actor/time/policy/source/routing/auth attribution.
- Check constraints for state/result combinations and no terminal-to-active
  transition.
- Restrictive delete/governed disposition preserving minimized business and
  security history.
- No protected bodies, roster snapshots, hidden impact, mutable authority, or
  arbitrary result JSON.

### Authorization boundary

Require separate code-registered operations to:

- save prospective D35 Tenant policy;
- determine whether **Update current work** may be offered;
- preview current-work impact;
- apply the target setting to D37’s current cohort;
- read authorized application progress/result/audit; and
- reconcile/repair identifier-only members.

Every operation derives active human, Tenant, environment, purpose, and time
server-side and re-proves its exact policy, source, task, and disclosure
predicates. Policy editing does not grant preview/apply/detail/audit authority.
D37 makes application a Tenant-wide governance action over the complete
compatible cohort and gives its distinct capability only the exact complete
aggregate impact required for consent—not restricted source detail.

Browser writes to policy heads, applications, members, routing generations,
source-backed tasks, recipient assignments, engagement continuity, receipts,
and audit are revoked. Privileged business logic lives in `packages/api` and app
handlers remain thin. RLS `USING` protects existing rows and `WITH CHECK`
protects inserted/resulting scope. Database owner, service role, SECURITY
DEFINER/RPC, worker, support, repair, import/export, Realtime, cache, and AI
paths repeat the same boundary.

## Lifecycle, concurrency, idempotency, and failure

```text
policy H1 current
    |
    | prospective save (receipt S2, cutover C2)
    v
policy H2 current -----------------> new occurrences use H2
    |
    | review current impact (read only)
    | explicit apply (application A2)
    v
captured pre-C2 members
    +-- applied / already_current
    +-- source_changed / not_current
    +-- target_policy_superseded
    +-- not_authorized / recipient_resolution_indeterminate
    +-- execution_failed
```

- **Stale impact:** no application; refresh; S2 remains saved.
- **Duplicate apply/lost response:** exact semantic replay returns A2.
- **Same key with changed target/cohort/digest:** hard conflict.
- **Two managers apply H2:** uniqueness/CAS creates one effective member
  successor; loser receives current application/result.
- **Manager saves H3 while A2 runs:** uncommitted A2 members stop; committed H2
  history remains until an explicit H3 application.
- **Source assigned while member waits:** source changed; no task.
- **New occurrence after C2:** H2 applies prospectively; A2 cannot duplicate.
- **Occurrence recurs:** successor identity uses current policy; old member
  cannot reopen.
- **Recipient unchanged:** engagement continuity; no unread reset.
- **Recipient removed:** typed route end; no fabricated completion/read.
- **Recipient newly admitted:** one fresh item after complete current auth.
- **Recipient loses access:** protected presentation disappears independently.
- **Resolver indeterminate:** no release; bounded retry under exact heads, then
  terminal safe result.
- **Task worker fails:** source lane remains; reconcile projection only.
- **Result-reader loses permission:** protected result disappears while
  body-free audit remains.

## Inngest boundary

Inngest may claim bounded pages of persisted application-member IDs, reload
current product state, invoke the same idempotent per-occurrence command, record
typed receipts, and reconcile projections. It receives no recipient list,
preview body, source detail, D30/D34 context, authorization claim, or trusted
result from an event.

It never decides cutover, cohort, policy, recipient, authorization, retry
terminality, application completeness, rollback, user status, source action, or
cross-source reuse. No provider function waits for the human; the human decision
is already persisted in Core.

## Normative requirements

1. **D36-R1 — Independent prospective save.** Policy save is complete,
   durable, and future-only regardless of the current-work path.
2. **D36-R2 — Exact cutover.** A trusted monotonic fence distinguishes current
   backlog from later occurrences and recurrence.
3. **D36-R3 — No silent current mutation.** Save, preview, navigation, or close
   changes no current routing or engagement.
4. **D36-R4 — Truthful save result.** Staff always learn that future changed and
   current work did not.
5. **D36-R5 — Optional follow-up.** Current review is secondary, non-blocking,
   non-nagging, and shown only under exact authority/relevance.
6. **D36-R6 — Separate capability.** Policy edit never implies current-work
   preview, application, detail, or audit authority.
7. **D36-R7 — Fresh preview.** Preview is server-computed, current,
   permission-safe, target-head bound, and non-authoritative.
8. **D36-R8 — Separate units.** Website items and personal assignments are
   never collapsed into one “changes” count.
9. **D36-R9 — No approximate action count.** Numeric action labels require exact
   complete disclosable proof.
10. **D36-R10 — Stable UX vocabulary.** Use **Update current work**, **Review
    current-work impact**, and **Apply setting to current work**, with truthful
    lane-only consequence copy.
11. **D36-R11 — Preview is confirmation.** No nested confirm, countdown,
    remembered consent, or type-to-confirm.
12. **D36-R12 — Server recompute.** Apply trusts no client cohort, count,
    recipient, impact, actor, or scope.
13. **D36-R13 — Stale means no effect.** Changed meaning blocks application
    without affecting the policy save.
14. **D36-R14 — Product-owned application.** Immutable normalized records own
    intent, progress, result, and audit.
15. **D36-R15 — D37 cohort gate.** Implementation cannot activate until D37’s
    complete compatible Tenant cohort, closed catalog/census, atomic seal,
    aggregate disclosure, and distinct capability are implemented and proved.
16. **D36-R16 — Per-occurrence atomicity.** Each member uses its own exact
    expected-head commit; no giant transaction.
17. **D36-R17 — Cohort conservation.** Every accepted member reaches one
    durable terminal result and totals reconcile.
18. **D36-R18 — Complete resolver.** D35 complete/zero/indeterminate auth
    applies independently to every member.
19. **D36-R19 — Differential continuity.** Continuing recipients preserve
    assignment and engagement without new unread.
20. **D36-R20 — Fresh new recipients.** Newly admitted authorized recipients
    alone receive one fresh assignment/engagement.
21. **D36-R21 — Truthful removed result.** Removed coordinator responsibility
    ends without completion/read/dismissal/access-loss fabrication.
22. **D36-R22 — Lane-only safety.** Applying lane-only ends personal
    coordinator assignments but never source Needs assignment.
23. **D36-R23 — No duplicate future work.** Occurrences already governed by the
    target setting are unchanged and never re-engaged.
24. **D36-R24 — Source-current fence.** Ended, assigned, superseded, or
    recurrent work receives no stale projection.
25. **D36-R25 — Policy-current fence.** Supersession stops uncommitted members;
    committed truth is never rolled back.
26. **D36-R26 — Durable idempotency.** Product keys/receipts own replay and
    changed-meaning conflict beyond transport windows.
27. **D36-R27 — Bounded retry.** Retry stays inside exact heads; terminal
    failure cannot create delayed work without fresh human action.
28. **D36-R28 — Durable accessible feedback.** Progress/results are
    route-addressable, resumable, in-page, accessible, and never toast-only.
29. **D36-R29 — Protected non-effect.** D36 reveals only safe facts and changes
    no source, access, public, Giving, finance, channel, or personnel meaning.
30. **D36-R30 — Optional executor and cross-source opt-in.** Inngest owns no
    fact; later sources require their own cohort/privacy/action decision.

## Acceptance criteria

### Prospective save and cutover

- **D36-AC001:** A valid D35 policy save appends one immutable revision, advances
  one expected head, writes one cutover and receipt, and succeeds independently.
- **D36-AC002:** Saving `named_coordinators` changes zero pre-existing
  occurrence routing generations, task assignments, or engagement rows.
- **D36-AC003:** Saving `source_lane_only` changes zero pre-existing personal
  coordinator assignments.
- **D36-AC004:** Save creates no current-work application, preview, member,
  task, notification, channel, or source mutation.
- **D36-AC005:** The save result states that future work changed and current work
  did not.
- **D36-AC006:** Preview/apply failure, cancel, close, staleness, or lost
  response never rolls back or ambiguously reports the saved policy.
- **D36-AC007:** An occurrence entering Needs assignment after the authoritative
  cutover uses the new policy prospectively.
- **D36-AC008:** An occurrence already in Needs assignment at cutover retains
  its exact applied-policy/routing basis.
- **D36-AC009:** An older occurrence that ends and later recurs creates a
  successor occurrence governed by the policy current at recurrence.
- **D36-AC010:** A cutover tie/order is resolved by one trusted monotonic
  sequence/fence, never client time or display timestamp.
- **D36-AC011:** Duplicate exact policy-save replay returns the original receipt.
- **D36-AC012:** Same save key with changed mode/members/predecessor conflicts.
- **D36-AC013:** Concurrent saves produce one expected-head winner; loser cannot
  silently rebase.
- **D36-AC014:** Unknown policy/cutover version releases no current mutation and
  preserves the source lane.
- **D36-AC015:** Current authorization loss takes effect immediately despite an
  older applied-policy basis.
- **D36-AC016:** Policy membership does not preserve source/task visibility
  after access loss.
- **D36-AC017:** A route manager without current-work capability can save policy
  prospectively and cannot preview/apply current work.
- **D36-AC018:** The settings helper explains prospective behavior before save.
- **D36-AC019:** Save never auto-opens or preselects current-work application.
- **D36-AC020:** Choosing **Not now** adds no nag, unread, task, channel,
  deadline, or setup-error state.

### Preview and cohort

- **D36-AC021:** **Update current work** is shown only under exact current
  capability and permission-safe relevance.
- **D36-AC022:** No-apply-authority presentation reveals no count or existence
  of hidden current work.
- **D36-AC023:** Preview is computed on the server from target policy, cutover,
  D37 cohort contract, current source/routing/auth heads, and resolver versions.
- **D36-AC024:** Client-supplied occurrence IDs, recipients, counts, actor,
  Tenant, or preview body are never authoritative.
- **D36-AC025:** Preview separates Website-item units from personal-assignment
  effects.
- **D36-AC026:** Preview shows newly added, continuing/no-new-unread, ended, and
  safe unchanged/changed categories only when authorized.
- **D36-AC027:** Preview contains no D30 explanation, D34 context, source body,
  hidden Site/locale, sibling read state, or unauthorized identity.
- **D36-AC028:** Per-person counts are omitted when they reveal source/Site
  qualification or restricted workload.
- **D36-AC029:** Hidden work cannot be inferred from totals, differences,
  timing, errors, pagination, action labels, result groups, or cache behavior.
- **D36-AC030:** Numeric primary-action copy appears only for an exact complete
  fully disclosable cohort count.

## D37 — Which current Needs-assignment work should one application cover?

### Context and concrete example

Hope Ministries has twelve current ownerless Website items across three Sites.
Ana can manage the Tenant’s recovery policy and inspect one ordinary Site; a
restricted Site’s source detail is not visible to her. D35 intentionally has
one Tenant policy and no Site overrides.

D36 now guarantees that saving is prospective and current adoption is a
separate operation. It does not yet decide whether that operation is
Tenant-wide governance or a selection of only work the initiating actor can
individually see. The choice affects policy coherence, authorization,
non-enumeration, operational completeness, accessibility, and whether settings
turn into a second task-management grid.

### Options

1. **Apply to the complete compatible current Tenant cohort under a distinct
   Tenant-wide application capability — recommended.** Core freezes the
   complete server-derived cohort, presents only authorized safe aggregates/
   details, re-proves every source and recipient, and starts nothing unless the
   full cohort can be determined. Policy editors without this additional
   capability can still save prospectively but cannot apply current work.
   This keeps one Tenant policy coherent without exposing restricted records.
2. **Apply only to work the initiating person can individually view.** This
   minimizes that actor’s immediate scope, but leaves hidden current work on old
   applied policy bases, may require several administrators to repeat the
   operation, and makes “current work updated” impossible to state completely.
3. **Let staff select Sites, filters, or individual rows.** This maximizes
   control but introduces stale selection, omission, hidden-work leakage,
   checkbox/bulk-grid accessibility, filter-policy drift, and a disguised Site
   override/rules product.

### Recommendation

Choose **Option 1**. A Tenant-wide setting should have one coherent, separately
authorized current-adoption operation. Permission-safe presentation and
fail-closed complete proof protect restricted work; policy editors without that
authority retain the safe prospective save. Exceptional individual assignment
continues through the source lane rather than policy exceptions.

### D37 resolution

**Founder direction:** Option 1 — complete compatible Tenant cohort under a
separate Tenant-wide application capability, provided it is modern, clear,
quiet, and repository-consistent.

**Disposition after adversarial review: Accept with required amendments.**

D37 defines the cohort as every registered-compatible pre-cutover Website
recovery occurrence current at one prepared authoritative seal for the exact
Tenant/environment/purpose. A closed producer/version catalog and source
occurrence/head census own membership. Unknown compatibility or incomplete
census blocks acceptance; visible rows, Sites, filters, tasks, recipient
qualification, and client IDs never narrow or expand it.

One separate Tenant-wide Website recovery current-work application capability
authorizes only this operation and the exact complete aggregate item/assignment
impact needed for informed consent. It grants no source detail, Site visibility,
policy edit, coordinator qualification, task administration, or correction
assignment. There is no partial visible-count mode or hidden/restricted split.

Preparation has no effect. One atomic seal binds normalized immutable
membership/count/digest and reviewed consequences before any D36 member claim.
Unreviewed widening requires fresh review; source/no-longer-current outcomes
may safely produce no effect. D36's per-occurrence expected-head,
complete/zero/indeterminate resolver, differential engagement, source-lane,
conservation, idempotency, failure, and projection boundaries remain unchanged.
