# Phase 24 D37 — Complete Tenant Current-Work Cohort

**Status:** Founder choice pressure-tested and recorded as Reserved  
**Decision:** Which already-current Website recovery occurrences one D36
current-work application covers, and which authority may apply one Tenant
policy across restricted work  
**Review date:** 2026-08-29  
**Scope:** D35 Returned Website work policy, D36 current-work application,
Website source occurrences, recipient routing, shared Tasks Hub projections,
Tenant authorization, privacy-safe aggregate disclosure, and optional
identifier-only execution

## Final disposition

**Accept Option 1 with required amendments.**

A complete compatible Tenant cohort is the strongest permanent fit for D35's
one Tenant policy and no-Site-override decision. A visible-only or selected
cohort would create hidden islands on older applied policy bases, invite
repeated manual work, and turn one settings decision into implicit temporary
Site exceptions.

The unamended phrase is not implementable. **Complete**, **compatible**,
**Tenant-wide**, **current**, and **separate capability** require closed
definitions. The accepted design therefore uses one code-owned cohort contract,
one narrow action capability, a complete authoritative census, a no-effect
prepared review, an atomic cohort seal, normalized immutable membership,
aggregate-only disclosure, and D36's per-occurrence atomic execution.

## Exact corrected decision

> A D36 application SHALL cover one **Website recovery current-work cohort**:
> every authoritative Website recovery occurrence in the same Tenant,
> environment, and **Assign returned Website work** purpose that entered Needs
> assignment before the target D35 policy's trusted cutover, remains current at
> the prepared cohort seal, and is classified compatible by the complete
> code-owned source-contract catalog.
>
> **Compatible** SHALL mean that the occurrence's registered producer and exact
> contract version implement the D35 current-policy-application contract. A
> known, explicitly versioned incompatible producer is outside this policy's
> scope. An unknown producer/version, missing registry entry, corrupt or
> duplicate occurrence identity, incomplete partition/page, contradictory
> head, stale census fence, truncation, timeout, or other inability to prove
> the full compatible universe makes cohort preparation `indeterminate`. Core
> SHALL offer no confirmation, accept no application, and change no current
> work. It never falls back to visible rows.
>
> Cohort membership SHALL come only from authoritative Website occurrence/head
> truth and the complete registered source-contract catalog. A Site, locale,
> current actor's source visibility, selected row, filter, page, lane result,
> Tasks Hub projection, notification, cache, search result, Payload document,
> analytics query, recipient qualification, or client-supplied identifier SHALL
> neither include nor exclude a member.
>
> The trusted cutover/source sequence—not display time, browser time, URL,
> task creation time, policy `updated_at`, or wall-clock comparison—SHALL decide
> pre-cutover membership. A post-cutover occurrence uses the target policy
> prospectively and is not a D37 member. Recurrence creates a new occurrence
> identity and never revives an older member. A pre-cutover occurrence already
> using the target policy remains in the complete cohort and ends
> `already_current`, preserving conservation and explainability.
>
> Core SHALL prepare the complete cohort and its differential impact without
> changing policy, routing, source work, Tasks Hub, engagement, or any external
> system. Preparation may be asynchronous and route-addressable, but the
> product-owned cohort evidence must be normalized, bounded to exact trusted
> heads, and marked complete before a confirmable review appears. There is no
> user-facing countdown or time-only expiry; changed authoritative heads, not
> pressure, make a review stale.
>
> Confirmation SHALL invoke one trusted server command and SHALL accept no
> caller Tenant, actor, Site, occurrence, member, recipient, count, filter,
> preview body, digest meaning, source result, or attribution. The command
> re-proves the active human, Tenant Assignment, distinct application
> capability and capability generation, target policy head/cutover, cohort
> contract/catalog, authoritative census proof, prepared member count/digest,
> and reviewed consequence bounds.
>
> Before any member may execute, one atomic seal SHALL bind the application,
> normalized immutable membership, exact Tenant/environment/purpose, target
> policy/cutover, cohort/catalog versions, source-census fence, count, digest,
> actor Party, capability generation, semantic command identity, and reviewed
> impact. Staging or seal failure produces no accepted application and no
> executable member. The seal may reference a fully prepared normalized
> manifest for scale; it SHALL NOT create one unbounded cross-backlog write
> transaction.
>
> A change between review and seal that could add a member, add or end a
> personal assignment, change a selected Party, change the policy, broaden
> disclosure, or otherwise exceed the reviewed consequences SHALL write
> nothing and require a fresh review. A member that merely ended, became
> already current, or otherwise can receive no effect beyond the reviewed bound
> may remain in the immutable cohort and later record D36's truthful no-effect
> outcome. Execution SHALL never substitute a newly widened recipient result.
>
> D37 establishes one separate **Tenant-wide Website recovery current-work
> application capability**. It authorizes only preparing, reviewing, accepting,
> and reading the safe result of this exact Tenant/environment/purpose policy-
> adoption operation. Managing the prospective D35 policy, being a Tenant or
> Site administrator, editing Website content, holding Tasks Hub access,
> appearing in the coordinator route, or holding ordinary source assignment
> authority SHALL NOT imply it. Application authority SHALL NOT imply any of
> those permissions.
>
> The application capability SHALL intentionally include the minimum complete
> Tenant-level aggregate disclosure necessary for informed consent: target
> policy mode and selected coordinator identities, exact Website-item count,
> and exact aggregate personal-assignment counts for add, preserve/no-new-
> unread, end, already-current, proved-zero, and currently unverifiable
> consequences. It SHALL NOT disclose member identity, Site, locale, title,
> source body, D30 explanation, D34 context, per-person workload, qualification
> reason, visible-versus-restricted split, sibling engagement, or any
> independently protected detail.
>
> This aggregate disclosure is part of the narrow high-impact operation—not an
> accidental read grant. If a Tenant does not trust a person with the complete
> aggregate blast radius, it SHALL NOT grant that person the application
> capability. Core SHALL NOT build a second partial-count mode: **work you can
> view**, visible/hidden subtraction, restricted placeholders, conditional
> totals, and timing/error differences can confuse consent and become
> enumeration oracles.
>
> The settings UI SHALL use ordinary staff language, not the technical cohort
> term. The stable section remains **Update current work** and the trigger
> remains **Review current-work impact**. The review title remains **Apply this
> setting to current Needs assignment work?** A scope block SHALL say **Across
> [organization] — all current Needs assignment work covered by this setting**.
> It SHALL explain that restricted source details are not shown and that
> selected coordinators receive tasks only for occurrences they are currently
> authorized to access and assign.
>
> The review SHALL keep Website-item and personal-assignment units separate and
> show exact aggregate add/preserve/end/already-current/proved-zero/unverified
> consequences in a compact semantic description list. The ordinary primary
> action remains **Apply setting to current work**; the D36 lane-only action
> remains **Use Needs assignment only for current work**. The review itself is
> the single confirmation. Core SHALL add no selection grid, Site filter,
> nested modal, type-to-confirm phrase, remembered consent, preselected
> checkbox, destructive styling by default, or approximate count.
>
> A policy editor without the application capability SHALL receive the quiet
> completed save truth—**The setting was saved for new returned work. Existing
> work has not changed.** Core SHALL show no disabled Tenant-wide button,
> backlog existence/count, hidden capability-holder roster, alert, badge,
> unread item, request-access dead end, or recurring nag. An application
> capability holder receives a calm secondary action; choosing **Not now**
> changes nothing and creates no engagement.
>
> If the complete census is empty or every member already uses the target,
> Core SHALL say **Current work already uses this setting** and offer no
> confirmation. If completeness cannot be proved, Core SHALL say **We couldn't
> check all current work. Nothing was started. The saved setting still applies
> to new work.** It may show a source-specific diagnostic only through an
> independently authorized source detail surface and SHALL never offer
> **Continue with visible work**.
>
> After the atomic seal, the persistent status SHALL say **Current-work update
> started**, show the exact authorized aggregate progress/result, state that
> the user may leave, and remain route-addressable after navigation or lost
> response. The terminal heading SHALL say **Current-work update finished**,
> not **Completed**, because no source item or personal task was completed.
> Toast, Realtime, and animation may supplement but never replace the durable
> surface or receipt-based refresh.
>
> Tenant-wide application authority SHALL never qualify a coordinator. Every
> sealed member still uses D35's complete/zero/indeterminate current recipient
> resolver over the configured one-to-three Parties and each Party's exact
> active Tenant Assignment, Site/source visibility, and **Assign returned
> Website work** authority. Proved zero is a valid target-policy basis with no
> personal task and the source lane intact. Indeterminate or materially changed
> recipient impact releases nobody and changes no prior routing basis.
>
> Each sealed member SHALL then execute through D36's per-occurrence expected-
> head source command and reach exactly one durable terminal outcome. Cohort
> acceptance is complete; cross-occurrence execution is safely resumable and
> not one giant transaction. One member's source change, authorization loss,
> recipient uncertainty, or execution failure cannot corrupt or roll back a
> committed sibling. Header/member/result totals SHALL conserve the sealed
> cohort exactly.
>
> The initiating actor's application capability generation and the target
> policy SHALL remain current before every uncommitted effect. Revocation or
> policy supersession stops remaining members under D36's typed outcomes.
> Already committed routing generations remain immutable history. Another
> authorized human must obtain a fresh review and invoke a successor
> application; a worker never impersonates the original actor or uses a broad
> service role as product authority.
>
> Browser writes SHALL be revoked. Same-scope composite relationships,
> immutable trusted attribution, unique application/occurrence membership,
> effect uniqueness, terminal-state checks, operation-correct RLS `USING` and
> `WITH CHECK`, `ENABLE` plus `FORCE RLS`, least grants, hardened functions,
> and equivalent owner/service/RPC/worker/support/repair/export/cache/Realtime/
> AI enforcement SHALL preserve the same Tenant and action boundary. An allowed
> update can never move a row to another Tenant, environment, purpose, policy,
> application, occurrence, actor, capability generation, or terminal result.
>
> Inngest, if used, may execute product-owned preparation or claim sealed
> identifier-only members through the same application service, bounded flow
> control, and durable claims. It SHALL NOT enumerate the cohort from event
> payloads, define compatibility, authorize the actor, seal membership, resolve
> recipients from copied data, own idempotency/results, or become required for
> the source lane. CMS, Tasks Hub, notifications, caches, and integrations
> likewise never own the cohort.
>
> D37 creates no Site/locale override, row selection, saved subset, policy
> exception, coordinator fallback, correction assignment, source transition,
> task completion, content/public/Giving/finance mutation, email, bell item,
> SMS, push, digest, reminder, due date, target date, SLA, escalation, employee
> score, generic bulk-task API, or automatic Website-to-Mobilize behavior.

## Problem validity, necessity, and strongest alternative

The root problem is not “staff need a bulk editor.” It is that one prospective
Tenant recovery policy can otherwise coexist indefinitely with older current
routing bases. D36 correctly makes current adoption optional; D37 keeps the
application coherent without granting restricted content access.

The strongest simpler alternative is **future-only**: never provide Tenant-wide
current application and leave source-authorized staff to recover individual
work. It removes the cohort/capability/ledger surface entirely and remains the
safe operational fallback. It is weaker as the permanent product because it
requires repeated manual glue and leaves hidden old-policy islands precisely
when a Tenant deliberately wants one recovery posture.

The selected application remains optional, separately authorized, and
measurable. A Tenant with no holder or no current need loses no source
functionality: prospective save and the complete Needs assignment lane still
work.

## Domain model, ownership, and invariants

### Canonical concepts

- **Website recovery current-work cohort:** the complete server-proved set of
  compatible pre-cutover Website recovery occurrences current at one prepared
  seal for an exact Tenant/environment/purpose.
- **Cohort contract:** the versioned code rule that classifies registered source
  producers/versions and defines membership from authoritative occurrence
  truth.
- **Prepared cohort evidence:** no-effect, immutable-enough evidence containing
  normalized identifiers, trusted heads, count, digest, and aggregate impact.
- **Cohort seal:** the atomic acceptance receipt that binds one complete
  prepared membership and reviewed meaning before execution.
- **Tenant-wide Website recovery current-work application capability:** the
  narrow operation authority plus minimum aggregate disclosure; never source
  detail or coordinator qualification.

### Ownership map

| Fact                             | Authority                                | Derived consumer                   | Never authority                      |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Target policy/cutover            | D35 policy head/receipt                  | D37 cohort predicate               | Settings form, task, worker          |
| Current recovery occurrence/head | Website source                           | Census/member fences               | Lane projection, Tasks Hub, CMS      |
| Producer compatibility           | Code-owned source-contract catalog       | Cohort classifier                  | Tenant setting, filename, event kind |
| Complete prepared membership     | Website current-work application service | Review/seal                        | Browser IDs, visible list, cache     |
| Safe aggregate impact            | Authorized D37 projection                | Human review                       | Mutation input, source detail grant  |
| Accepted application/seal        | Website operational recovery domain      | Claims/progress/audit              | Inngest run, toast, analytics        |
| Per-member routing result        | D36 source/routing command               | Tasks projection, aggregate result | Batch header guess, task mutation    |
| Personal engagement              | ADR-0027 recipient projection            | My tasks/read state                | Source completion, cohort membership |

### Permanent invariants

1. One D37 application targets one Tenant, environment, D35 purpose, target
   policy head/cutover, cohort contract/catalog, prepared seal, and capability
   generation.
2. Every compatible authoritative pre-cutover occurrence current at the
   prepared seal is included exactly once.
3. No post-cutover, known-incompatible, cross-scope, visible-only, selected, or
   client-injected occurrence is included.
4. Unknown compatibility or incomplete census yields `indeterminate`, never a
   smaller cohort.
5. Complete empty and indeterminate are distinct.
6. Prepared review causes no source, routing, task, engagement, channel, or
   external effect.
7. No member may be claimed before a valid atomic cohort seal.
8. Header count equals normalized member count; terminal totals conserve it.
9. One application/occurrence has one member and at most one target routing
   successor effect.
10. A reviewed consequence may stay equal or become a no-effect typed result;
    it can never widen without a fresh review.
11. Tenant-wide application authority grants no source visibility and never
    qualifies a coordinator.
12. Every recipient still requires complete current D35 authorization.
13. Continuing recipients preserve assignment/engagement/read state; only new
    recipients receive new unread.
14. Source Needs assignment and exact source actions remain authoritative and
    available through every application state.
15. Terminal members never reactivate; correction is a successor application.
16. No hidden detail or per-person workload enters aggregate projection,
    events, logs, caches, search, analytics, AI, export, or Inngest.

### Quiet settings state

For an authorized application holder, keep the existing D35 card and add one
secondary section rather than another dashboard:

```text
Update current work

Apply the saved setting to current Needs assignment work across the
organization after reviewing its effect.

[Review current-work impact]
```

It is not an alert, badge, unread item, reminder, recurring prompt, or automatic
modal. A policy editor without the capability sees only the successful
prospective-save truth. Core does not show a disabled action or permission
matrix inside the work settings card.

### Preparation

```text
Checking current work across the organization…

Nothing will change until you review and confirm.
You can leave this page.
```

Preparation is route-addressable when it outlives one request. It downloads no
backlog or source bodies. Realtime may enhance the result; receipt polling/
refresh remains sufficient.

## Conceptual persistence, RLS, and authorization

### Smallest durable extension to D36

- Application header: exact Tenant/environment/purpose, target policy/cutover,
  cohort-contract and source-catalog versions, census fence, prepared digest/
  count, reviewed aggregate digest, seal receipt/state, semantic key, actor
  Party, capability generation, and trusted times.
- Normalized member: same-scope application/occurrence identity, trusted
  pre-cutover entry sequence, expected source/applied-policy/routing heads,
  reviewed-effect bound, terminal outcome, and source receipt reference.
- Product claim/attempt history: identifier-only lease/attempt/result codes;
  no protected body or copied recipient roster.
- Safe aggregate projection: derived from the application/member ledger under
  the D37 capability; never a writable source or raw-member view.

### Constraints and indexes

- Composite same-Tenant/environment/purpose keys for every policy, application,
  occurrence, member, result, Party, routing, and task relation.
- Unique semantic application identity and unique
  `(application, occurrence)` member.
- One successful routing effect per exact occurrence/target policy/routing
  predecessor; exact replay returns the original receipt.
- Check constraints for prepared/sealed/executing/terminal combinations and
  append-only trusted attribution.
- Header/member/result conservation is server-derived and independently
  reconcilable; clients cannot write counters or terminality.
- No ordinary cascade deletes application history, stable Party attribution,
  routing receipts, or audit. Tenant disposal follows governing retention.
- Indexed authoritative census on exact scope/current state/cutover sequence;
  indexed member claims on exact scope/application/status/stable keyset.
- No arbitrary source body, D30/D34 context, coordinator roster, per-person
  analytics, hidden classification reason, client snapshot, or unbounded JSON.

### Authorization boundary

Preview, preparation, acceptance, progress, aggregate result, retry, and repair
are separate server operations. Every one derives active human, Tenant,
environment, purpose, time, and capability generation from trusted context.
The D37 capability is action-specific and default-deny. D38 now makes it one
zero-by-default Phase 12 `explicit_only` grant, administered separately by
`permissions.manage_grants` with complete provenance; D39 now permits typed
direct and protected flat Access-group surfaces through Phase 12 before
activation.

Browser roles receive no authoritative `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, or `DELETE`.
`USING` must protect existing rows and `WITH CHECK` must protect inserted/
resulting scope. Scope and trusted fields are immutable. `ENABLE` plus `FORCE
RLS`, column/table grants, pinned function `search_path`, least-privilege
execution roles, and explicit owner/service/RPC/worker/support/repair/export/
Realtime/cache/AI checks are all required. RLS is defense in depth, not the
only capability check.

## Lifecycle, concurrency, idempotency, and failure

```text
prospective policy saved
  |
  +-- prepare complete authoritative cohort
  |      complete empty/aligned -> no confirmation
  |      indeterminate         -> no application
  |      complete impact       -> review
  |
  +-- human with exact capability confirms
         stale/widened meaning -> no application; fresh review
         valid seal            -> immutable accepted cohort
                                   |
                                   +-- per-member D36 execution
                                         applied
                                         already_current
                                         source_changed
                                         target_policy_superseded
                                         not_current
                                         not_authorized
                                         recipient_resolution_indeterminate
                                         execution_failed
```

- Concurrent matching confirmations converge through the semantic application
  key and cohort digest. Changed meaning under the same key conflicts.
- One Tenant/purpose/target may have one active accepted application. A second
  actor resumes its status or prepares a successor only after current rules
  allow it; processing order never changes membership.
- Source, policy, capability, routing, and recipient heads are re-proved at
  each effect. A materially wider effect is never substituted.
- Transient infrastructure failure may retry only the same sealed member and
  unchanged semantic heads. Terminal members never release delayed work.
- Source/routing commit and identifier-only projection intent are atomic per
  occurrence. Projection failure is not source/routing failure.
- Lost-response handling looks up the receipt before resubmission. Closing,
  navigation, or browser loss does not cancel accepted work.
- A newer policy or capability revocation stops uncommitted work. Existing
  commits are immutable; correction rolls forward through a new policy/
  application, never bulk rollback or read-state reset.
- A rollout kill switch stops new preparation, seals, or claims while
  preserving source lane, prospective policy, ledgers, receipts, and repair.

## Inngest boundary

Inngest is a reasonable optional executor for durable preparation paging,
sealed-member claims, bounded concurrency, retry, and reconciliation only when
it invokes the same product-owned commands. Product Postgres owns catalog,
census, manifest, seal, claims, authorization generations, routing, results,
and idempotency. Inngest events carry schema-versioned identifiers, not
members, counts, recipients, source detail, D30/D34 bodies, or authorization
claims. A synchronous/internal worker path and source lane remain usable when
Inngest is unavailable or replaced.

## Normative requirements

1. **D37-R1 — Complete compatible cohort.** Include every compatible
   authoritative pre-cutover occurrence current at the prepared seal.
2. **D37-R2 — Closed compatibility catalog.** Known versions classify
   explicitly; unknown or incomplete catalog state blocks acceptance.
3. **D37-R3 — Authoritative census.** Source occurrence/head truth alone
   defines membership.
4. **D37-R4 — Trusted cutover.** Monotonic source/cutover sequence, not
   wall-clock presentation, decides membership.
5. **D37-R5 — Complete-zero algebra.** Complete empty, complete nonempty, and
   indeterminate remain distinct.
6. **D37-R6 — No visible subset.** Actor visibility, Sites, filters, pages,
   selections, task rows, and client IDs never narrow membership.
7. **D37-R7 — Prepared no-effect evidence.** Cohort preparation changes no
   operational or engagement truth.
8. **D37-R8 — Atomic seal before claim.** No member executes until exact
   normalized membership/count/digest is sealed.
9. **D37-R9 — Separate Tenant action capability.** Policy edit and every
   source/task/content/admin permission neither imply nor are implied by apply.
10. **D37-R10 — Minimum aggregate disclosure.** The capability includes exact
    complete aggregate impact required for consent and no source detail.
11. **D37-R11 — No partial disclosure mode.** No visible/hidden split,
    conditional total, subtotal, member list, or per-person breakdown.
12. **D37-R12 — Human confirmation.** A currently capable human explicitly
    accepts; AI may prepare but cannot cross the approval gate.
13. **D37-R13 — Trusted recomputation.** Server heads/evidence, never client
    meaning, authorize seal and effects.
14. **D37-R14 — No unreviewed widening.** Changed membership or differential
    effect beyond the reviewed bound requires fresh review.
15. **D37-R15 — Immutable normalized membership.** One same-scope member per
    application/occurrence; no mutable JSON selection.
16. **D37-R16 — Complete acceptance, per-member execution.** One seal records
    complete intent; each source effect remains independently atomic.
17. **D37-R17 — Recipient independence.** Application authority never
    qualifies or authorizes a coordinator.
18. **D37-R18 — D35 resolver fidelity.** Complete/zero/indeterminate current
    recipient resolution controls every member without fallback.
19. **D37-R19 — D36 engagement fidelity.** Continuing state is preserved, new
    recipients alone get unread, and removal uses the typed policy reason.
20. **D37-R20 — Source lane invariance.** Needs assignment and source actions
    survive preparation, application, failure, and repair.
21. **D37-R21 — Current capability fence.** Revocation stops every uncommitted
    member; workers do not impersonate or bypass.
22. **D37-R22 — Current policy/source fences.** Supersession or source change
    yields typed no-effect outcomes for uncommitted members.
23. **D37-R23 — Conservation and terminality.** Every sealed member has exactly
    one terminal outcome; totals reconcile and never regress.
24. **D37-R24 — Durable semantic idempotency.** Exact replay returns receipt;
    changed meaning conflicts; transport dedupe is insufficient.
25. **D37-R25 — Persistent accessible UX.** Review/progress/result are
    route-addressable, aggregate-only, text-first, and programmatically clear.
26. **D37-R26 — Database authorization.** Composite scope, constraints,
    immutable trusted fields, RLS/grants, and privileged parity are required.
27. **D37-R27 — Identifier-only orchestration.** Inngest is optional execution,
    never catalog/cohort/auth/routing/result/source truth.
28. **D37-R28 — Production-shaped scale.** Indexed census, bounded preparation,
    keyset claims, Tenant fairness, and measured budgets precede activation.
29. **D37-R29 — No policy expansion.** No Site/locale/row subset, exception,
    generic workflow/bulk-task engine, channel, date, or cross-source reuse.
30. **D37-R30 — Reserved until proof.** D37–D40, OpenSpec, implementation,
    tests, canary, monitors, and release evidence must agree before activation.

## Acceptance criteria

### Cohort universe and compatibility

- **D37-AC001:** One model-based oracle enumerates every authoritative current
  Website recovery occurrence for exact Tenant/environment/purpose.
- **D37-AC002:** Every producer and exact contract version classifies as
  compatible, known incompatible, or unknown.
- **D37-AC003:** Unknown producer/version prevents a confirmable review.
- **D37-AC004:** A missing partition, page, cursor, registry entry, or census
  fence prevents a confirmable review.
- **D37-AC005:** Timeout, truncation, overflow, corruption, duplicate current
  identity, or contradictory heads prevents acceptance.
- **D37-AC006:** Complete empty is returned distinctly from indeterminate.
- **D37-AC007:** Known incompatible occurrences are excluded only by the
  versioned code catalog, never a Tenant preference.
- **D37-AC008:** Restricted Site/source visibility does not exclude a compatible
  occurrence.
- **D37-AC009:** Missing/delayed Tasks Hub, notification, cache, search, or CMS
  projection does not exclude a compatible occurrence.
- **D37-AC010:** Complete zero recipient qualification does not exclude a
  compatible occurrence.
- **D37-AC011:** The browser cannot add or omit a member by IDs, Sites, locale,
  filter, sort, page, count, or preview body.
- **D37-AC012:** Source catalog and census proof are independently
  reconcilable against authoritative source history.

### Cutover, current state, and recurrence

- **D37-AC013:** Entry sequence strictly before target cutover is a candidate;
  at/after cutover is prospective.
- **D37-AC014:** Exact cutover-boundary tests contain no wall-clock tie.
- **D37-AC015:** Display time, `updated_at`, task time, URL, and policy form time
  do not affect membership.
- **D37-AC016:** A pre-cutover occurrence current at prepared seal appears
  exactly once.
- **D37-AC017:** A pre-cutover occurrence already using the target remains a
  member and results `already_current`.
- **D37-AC018:** A post-cutover occurrence is excluded and cannot receive a
  duplicate task/unread.
- **D37-AC019:** Recurrence creates a successor occurrence identity.
- **D37-AC020:** A terminal old occurrence never re-enters a new cohort.
- **D37-AC021:** Source end before seal excludes the no-longer-current
  occurrence through authoritative truth.
- **D37-AC022:** Source end after seal yields a typed no-effect member result.
- **D37-AC023:** Archived/transferred/merged Site cases follow exact source
  occurrence/head rules, not display status.
- **D37-AC024:** Cohort conservation can explain every pre-cutover compatible
  current occurrence.

### Preparation, review, and seal

- **D37-AC025:** Preparing cohort evidence changes no policy, source, routing,
  task, engagement, notification, public, Giving, finance, or integration
  state.
- **D37-AC026:** Long preparation is route-addressable and recoverable after
  navigation/lost response.
- **D37-AC027:** Prepared evidence stores normalized identifiers/heads/count/
  digest, not protected source bodies or recipient rosters.
- **D37-AC028:** A confirmable review appears only after completeness is proved.
- **D37-AC029:** Confirmation derives actor/Tenant/environment/purpose/time from
  trusted context.
- **D37-AC030:** Confirmation re-proves the application capability generation.
- **D37-AC031:** Confirmation re-proves target policy head/cutover and cohort/
  catalog versions.
- **D37-AC032:** Confirmation re-proves prepared count/digest/census evidence.
- **D37-AC033:** Any unreviewed widening writes nothing and returns fresh-review
  guidance.
- **D37-AC034:** A harmless member no-effect change cannot add a replacement
  member after seal.
- **D37-AC035:** No member claim succeeds before atomic valid seal.
- **D37-AC036:** Seal failure leaves no accepted/executable partial cohort.

### Capability and authorization

- **D37-AC037:** Policy edit alone cannot offer, preview, apply, read aggregate
  result, retry, or repair D37.
- **D37-AC038:** Tenant/Site admin label alone cannot imply D37 capability.
- **D37-AC039:** Coordinator membership, Tasks Hub access, source visibility,
  and assignment authority do not imply D37 capability.
- **D37-AC040:** D37 capability grants none of those permissions.
- **D37-AC041:** D37 requires an active same-Tenant human assignment and exact
  Tenant/environment/purpose capability.
- **D37-AC042:** AI can prepare/explain but cannot confirm without the capable
  human approval.
- **D37-AC043:** Actor Party and capability generation are immutable on the

## Ruthless synthesis

### Verified facts, judgments, assumptions, and unknowns

- **Verified repository fact:** D35 has one Tenant policy and no Site override;
  D36 separates prospective save from explicit current adoption.
- **Verified repository fact:** Website/source truth owns occurrence/action/

  or execution.
- **Verified repository fact:** current generic task and finance/support batch

  include exact aggregate blast-radius disclosure. A person trusted to mutate
  all compatible current work must be able to understand how much work and how

  separates `permissions.manage_grants` from possession, binds Active Tenant
  Assignment, requires complete provenance, and fences uncommitted effects.

## Migration, activation, and rollback

- Additive schema/catalog/auth changes only; old code must ignore new readers
  and new code must fail closed on old/unknown generations.
- Infer no cohort, member, application, capability holder, recipient, task, or
  engagement from legacy rows.
- Deploy catalog/census/schema/constraints/RLS/grants/denials/readers before

## D38 — Who should initially hold and grant this capability?

### Context and concrete example

Hope Ministries lets Ana configure **Returned Website work** for future
occurrences. Jordan is responsible for organization-wide operational changes
across restricted Sites. D37 now prevents Ana's policy-edit permission from
silently authorizing Tenant-wide current adoption, but the product must still
decide how Jordan receives that authority and whether a Tenant may leave it
unassigned.

This decision affects least privilege, setup friction, small-ministry
continuity, audit, revocation, and whether Core accidentally turns a narrow
capability back into a broad administrator role.

### Options

#### Option 1 — explicit Tenant governance grant; zero holders allowed — recommended

A separately authorized Tenant access administrator explicitly grants **Apply
Website recovery settings to current work** to a trusted active staff member
through the intended Phase 12 authorization system. The grant is Tenant-scoped, visible
to authorized access administrators, audited, revocable, and grants no source
detail, policy edit, coordinator qualification, or source assignment. A Tenant
may have zero holders; prospective save and the Needs assignment lane remain
fully usable.

**User/Tenant UX:** policy editors see a clean prospective save; capable
operators see the calm current-work action; access administrators manage one
plain-language permission in the future product-backed People & access UI. Nobody is inferred from a
title or asked to maintain a Website-specific roster.

**Impact:** strongest least privilege and separation of duties with modest
one-time setup. It does require a safe bootstrap authority and explicit
delegation/revocation evidence.

#### Option 2 — automatically grant every Tenant administrator

Every current Tenant administrator receives the capability by default.

**User/Tenant UX:** no setup, but people with unrelated administrative duties
quietly gain authority to fan out or end personal work across restricted
Sites.

**Impact:** lowest friction and broader blast radius; it makes current broad
role labels part of product authorization and complicates future narrowing.

#### Option 3 — automatically grant every recovery-policy editor

Anyone who can save the prospective D35 policy can apply it to current work.

**User/Tenant UX:** the setting and action always stay together, but staff
cannot delegate future configuration separately from Tenant-wide backlog
mutation.

**Impact:** directly collapses D36/D37's separate capability boundary and
should be rejected.

### Recommendation and exact question

**My recommendation is Option 1 — explicit Tenant governance grant with zero
holders allowed.** It follows current least-privilege practice, preserves small-
ministry safety because the lane needs no holder, avoids a new Website-specific
role/roster, and keeps the capability understandable and reversible.

Do you choose **Option 1 — explicit Tenant governance grant; zero holders
allowed**, **Option 2 — every Tenant administrator by default**, or **Option 3
— every recovery-policy editor by default**? You may amend any option.
