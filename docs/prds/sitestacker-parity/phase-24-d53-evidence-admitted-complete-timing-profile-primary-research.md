# Phase 24 D53 — Evidence-Qualified Complete Timing-Pair Primary Research

**Date:** 2026-08-29

**Founder answer:** **Option 1 — remain Off until each exact complete timing
profile independently passes the D47 evidence gate**

**Disposition:** **Accept with required amendments**

**Scope:** qualification of exact complete code-owned
`(wait_for_seconds, useful_for_seconds)` timing-pair candidates, D47 evidence
binding, versioning, retirement, failure, rollout, minimal future UX,
authorization, RLS, privacy, scale, proof, and one D54 question only; no numeric
pair, live production experiment, database-configured registry, approval/
research workflow engine, schema, OpenSpec, runtime, telemetry, feature flag,
message key, channel, provider, executor, or UI artifact

## Purpose and research standard

D52 makes source time one indivisible complete timing profile: positive finite
whole elapsed seconds `wait_for_seconds` determine inclusive `not_before`, and
positive finite whole elapsed seconds `useful_for_seconds` determine exclusive
`useful_until`. It deliberately supplies no values. D53 decides how any exact
pair may enter the future closed profile set.

The founder selected the evidence-first model. Core starts and remains Off with
no cadence UI until at least one exact complete pair independently passes D47's
preregistered representative evidence gate and every D48–D52 proof. This is not
permission to create a research-management product. Candidate protocols,
findings, reviews, and decisions remain governed documents and ordinary product
work. A future activated access-review timing profile is a reviewed version-
controlled code contract,
not a database-authored rule, remote-config value, plugin, feature flag, or
automatic result of a research status transition.

Evidence labels are strict:

- **Repository fact:** verified in the current Core repository.
- **Verified external fact:** verified in current official first-party material.
- **Requirement inference:** required by governing Core/platform behavior.
- **Product judgment:** Core deliberately chooses among multiple safe models.
- **Assumption:** plausible but not established by representative ministry data.
- **Unresolved unknown:** reserved for a later founder, research, or design gate.

Vendor values and workflows are comparative evidence only. Core imports no
numeric pair, campaign duration, provider TTL, approval engine, experiment
platform, remote registry, UI pattern, research sample size, retirement rule,
or retention period merely because another product uses one.

## Executive finding

**Accept Option 1 with required amendments.** It is the only D53 model
consistent with D47's governing gate and D52's complete-pair invariant:

- GOV.UK user-research guidance says to turn assumptions into questions, choose
  methods that yield reliable evidence, recruit actual/likely and disabled/
  support-needing users, conduct iterative rounds, and let method determine
  participant count. Its privacy guidance requires informed consent, minimum
  necessary collection, secure restricted storage, purpose limitation, and
  deletion when no longer needed.
- Cross-product timing values remain non-transferable: Entra access reviews,
  Okta certification campaigns, Contentful scheduled actions, and Stripe Prices
  encode different review, publication, or commerce meanings. D52 already
  proves the profile shape; none proves a Core pair.
- Stripe prevents in-place amount mutation, recommends a new Price for a new
  amount, and archives old Prices while existing subscriptions remain. Entra
  snapshots each review instance; Okta allows broad campaign edits only before
  launch; Contentful uses explicit versions/status. These support immutable
  profile revisions and prospective retirement without importing
  their business semantics.
- USWDS and WCAG support one clearly labelled mutually exclusive choice with a
  genuine none/Off option and enough instruction without clutter. Core still
  uses Base UI/base-maia, not vendor components.
- Core's own ADR-0026 and Phase 17 manifest already establish the permanent
  pattern: small named versioned code-owned choices, generated/validated
  contracts, immutable bindings, and database rows as references/projections—
  not Tenant-authored arbitrary automation.

The corrected model is deliberately modest. A D47 evidence pack evaluates one
exact pair as a whole against the complete D46 no-reminder baseline. Any
missing measure, material cohort, independent review, critical meaning
misread, guardrail failure, contradiction, or privacy breach rejects that pair.
Passing yields an **evidence-qualified timing-pair proposal** and founder/source
decision only. It creates no code,
schema, hidden value, UI, flag, production treatment, or automatic activation.

If later implementation is separately authorized, an **activated access-review
timing profile** receives one stable code-owned identity and immutable revision
containing the complete pair and approved display contract. Values are never
edited in place or mixed. Retirement removes the profile from every new choice
and API selection while preserving a Tenant selected policy head that already
references it as truthful read-only/current state, plus every D43 pinned source
tuple. No automatic replacement occurs. D54 decides local in-product reminder
presentation. A separate pre-activation decision must define urgent safety
withdrawal and current-head behavior; no profile activates until it does.

## Exact corrected D53 decision

1. D53 selects **evidence qualification per exact complete timing-pair
   candidate**.
2. D53 creates no non-Off profile now. The only current policy behavior remains
   absence/Off, and no Off-only placeholder control ships.
3. A prequalification study identifies its timing-pair candidate by immutable
   `research_candidate_id`, protocol version, exact pair, and research context.
   That identifier is not a product profile identity or revision. A code-owned
   profile identity/revision may be assigned only by a later complete activation
   package and must link back to the qualified candidate.
4. Neither value may be omitted, nullable, zero/negative, floating, inferred
   from the other, inherited from a provider, selected independently, or
   mutated in place.
5. D53 chooses no profile identity, label, number, ratio, maximum profile count,
   default non-Off value, evidence result, or implementation location.
6. Every timing-pair candidate independently passes the complete D47 evidence
   gate as a pair. Evidence for either duration alone, another pair, a vendor
   default, or a general desire for reminders cannot qualify it.
7. Before collection, the pair's evidence protocol preregisters the exact pair,
   observed problem, affected outcome, complete D46 no-reminder baseline,
   research questions, sampling frame, methods, primary success measure,
   fatigue/fairness/privacy/accessibility/low-bandwidth guardrails, exclusions,
   stopping rule, and qualification/rejection decision rule.
8. Thresholds, outcomes, exclusions, cohort definitions, and decision rules
   cannot be changed after results are observed to make a pair pass. A justified
   protocol revision creates a new preregistered study/version before new data.
9. The sampling frame derives materially distinct Tenant size/type, staffing,
   coordinator count, timezone/locale, device/connectivity, part-time/volunteer,
   disability/assistive-technology, support-needs, and access-sensitivity
   contexts from current authorized evidence—not invented ministry personas.
10. Research uses actual or likely source users, iterative rounds, appropriate
    methods, and method-justified sample sizes. D53 invents no universal count.
11. The complete D46 baseline includes the persistent Access requests lane,
    source-backed task, required in-product attention, and any separately
    governed D45 initial email. A pair must beat the predeclared attention/
    recovery outcome against that full baseline.
12. Preference statements, stakeholder votes, vendor parity, clicks, opens,
    provider acceptance, or worker success are insufficient. The pair must
    improve the predeclared user/domain outcome without failed guardrails.
13. Task-based final-round comprehension has zero tolerated critical
    interpretations that the reminder creates Due/Overdue, SLA, escalation,
    access change/removal, automatic decision, staff performance score, or
    guaranteed delivery. Any such interpretation rejects/redesigns/retests.
14. Fatigue/fairness proof covers perceived pressure/blame, duplicate attention,
    localization/timezones, part-time/volunteer work, mobile/low bandwidth,
    accessibility, and different Tenant/coordinator contexts.
15. Operational proof covers D48 new-request-only admission, D49 recipient
    states, D50/D52 arithmetic/boundaries, D51 edit/Off/re-enable, source/effect
    idempotency, authorization/RLS, migration, recovery, provider ambiguity,
    load, and no catch-up.
16. D47's six reviewer domains—Product, UX Research, IAM/Security, Privacy,
    Accessibility, and Architecture—review the exact evidence pack and stated
    limits, with no sole proposer self-approving. Founder/source approval may
    record the pair only as an evidence-qualified timing-pair proposal.
17. A failed, incomplete, stale-with-material-change, contradictory, privacy-
    contaminated, inaccessible, or unreviewed pack qualifies nothing. No
    product profile identity, disabled row, hidden option, experimental entry,
    beta, or “coming soon” placeholder is created.
18. No live production experiment may be used to discover whether a pair is
    beneficial or safe: no A/B/randomized Tenant/staff treatment, hidden
    reminder, experimental send, production cohort, silent profile, engagement
    optimization, or value tuning on live D43 work.
19. Permitted research uses consented prototypes/scenarios, non-production test
    environments/fixtures, interviews/usability studies, and authorized
    minimized historical/operational aggregates within their existing purpose.
    It never changes real access, tasks, requests, or sends a real reminder.
20. Research participation is voluntary and purpose-specific. Collect only the
    minimum data needed, protect sensitive access/ministry context, record
    informed consent, restrict access, predeclare retention/deletion, honor
    withdrawal, and anonymize reported findings where possible.
21. Individual staff response times, missed work, opens, or actions cannot become
    performance scoring, risk rankings, manager surveillance, or timing-pair-
    qualification optimization features.
22. Passing the gate creates an evidence-qualified timing-pair proposal only. It
    does
    not automatically create code, schema, configuration, registry entry,
    policy, message key, UI, feature flag, telemetry, experiment, or activation.
23. D53 does not create an evidence database, approval table, workflow status,
    low-code rules engine, admin console, research portal, or automated
    evidence-to-registry pipeline.
24. Evidence packs and approvals remain durable governed documentation using
    normal repository/product research processes; they are not executable or
    product artifacts. A future code-contract change is a separately reviewed
    version-controlled implementation change.
25. The future authoritative runtime registry is a closed code-owned build-
    validated contract following ADR-0026/Phase 17 patterns, not database-
    authored configuration, remote config, provider metadata, plugin data,
    Tenant JSON, environment variable, or analytics result.
26. Exact file/module/type names remain design work. D53 freezes behavior and
    ownership, not a TypeScript shape, generator, table, or package.
27. A future client may submit only a visible activated access-review timing
    profile identity/revision. The server resolves its exact pair from the
    compatible code contract and derives Tenant, actor, Tenant selected policy
    head,
    values, and audit evidence.
28. Profile identity and revision are globally unambiguous in their registered
    policy family, never reused, never repointed to another pair, and retained
    for every persisted reference/history.
29. Changing either second value, calculation/display meaning, or other
    source-semantic contract creates a new timing-pair candidate, new D47 proof,
    and—only in a later activation package—a new immutable profile revision or
    identity. It never edits an activated revision.
30. Unknown, partial, unversioned, mismatched, unavailable, corrupt, or
    unsupported current references fail closed to typed cadence safe non-
    admission and operations evidence. They never choose Off as if it were an
    intentional Tenant save, nearest pair, current default, or another profile.
31. Future persistence stores the selected profile identity/revision. Every D43
    pinned source tuple contains only that identity/revision, the exact pair,
    and derived source facts including `not_before`, `useful_until`, and the
    calculation evidence required to reproduce them. It never pins a research
    artifact or evidence pack, and a current registry lookup never reinterprets
    historical work.
32. A new activated access-review timing profile applies only through a later
    deliberate Tenant selected policy head publication and then only to genuine
    later D43 episodes under D48/D51. It creates no current-work migration or
    backfill.
33. **Retirement** removes an activated profile from every new Tenant choice,
    API enumeration for selection, and new policy publication. It does not
    mutate a Tenant selected policy head that already references it, D43 pinned
    source tuples, tasks, effects, or history; that Tenant selected policy head
    continues
    prospective D43 source admission until deliberate Tenant policy change.
34. A Tenant whose Tenant selected policy head references a retired profile
    retains
    truthful read-only/current visibility until an authorized deliberate
    change. Once it changes away, that retired profile cannot be selected again.
35. Every activated profile identity/revision remains decodable under governed
    retention and backups even after retirement or withdrawal. Failed research
    creates no product identity or placeholder; its research records follow the
    approved research retention/deletion policy.
36. Retirement never automatically substitutes a successor, changes either
    value, converts to Off, publishes a Tenant policy, or sends a message.
37. Material evidence of harm may require stronger product-wide safety
    withdrawal than retirement. A separate pre-activation decision—not D54—must
    define authority, threshold, sequencing, current-head/source-admission
    effects, and UX. Withdrawal must block new offering/selection/source
    admission while retaining required historical decoders, compatibility, and
    history. No non-Off profile activates before it is resolved.
38. Missing raw evidence, required reviewer input, or required source access may
    block initial qualification or a planned rereview. Lawful scheduled raw-data
    deletion, link rot, or vendor/tool outage never silently changes an already
    governed runtime state or makes release network-dependent. Activation and
    retirement require explicit governed documentation and code changes backed
    by durable minimized local decision/provenance.
39. No profile has an automatic evidence TTL. Material changes in user context,
    policy meaning, architecture, law/privacy, accessibility, provider/channel,
    or observed harm trigger documented re-review; urgent safety withdrawal is
    the separate pre-activation decision in item 37.
40. The first future policy UI may ship only after one exact non-Off profile,
    all D48–D54/later gates, implementation/security/accessibility proof, and
    explicit release authorization are complete. Before then there is no UI.
41. The first released selector presents Off plus at most the one first proven
    non-Off profile. Later profiles appear only after their own independent
    evidence and complete compatible release.
42. Each visible choice is one vertically listed labelled radio/card
    representing a complete profile. Users cannot combine durations, enter a
    number/date, open an advanced scheduler, or select a partial pair.
43. The activated profile display contract includes one approved outcome-
    meaningful label
    and concise helper that explains one courtesy reminder and skip-instead-of-
    late meaning without exposing internal field names or two controls.
44. Off remains explicit, valid, quiet, and the default/absence baseline. No
    profile is labelled Recommended, Popular, Smart, Optimal, or Experimental.
45. Candidate, rejected, evidence-pending, or implementation-pending profiles
    never appear in Tenant UX, API enumerations, exports, or ordinary docs.
46. For a Tenant currently on a retired profile, settings show the exact profile
    as current and no longer offered, read-only. The Tenant may deliberately
    choose Off or another activated profile, but cannot reselect the retired
    profile after moving away. There is no alarm, forced migration, or hidden
    substitution.
47. Policy changes retain D51's explicit Save/Cancel, prospective application,
    stale-head recovery, semantic receipt recovery, Off consequence review,
    accessibility, localization, mobile, and low-bandwidth contracts.
48. Future human publication uses the D44/Phase 12 purpose-bound
    `permissions.manage_grants` model. Profile activation/retirement is product
    governance/code authority, never a Tenant admin capability.
49. Future persistence must enforce same-Tenant policy/source relationships,
    immutable references/pinned values, valid states, restrictive deletion,
    least grants, application authorization, RLS `USING`/`WITH CHECK`, and owner/
    service/worker/support/BYPASSRLS parity.
50. D53 adds no profile, number, evidence workflow, registry code, schema,
    migration, OpenSpec, policy row, API, flag, event, job, telemetry, UI,
    dependency, experiment, or placeholder now.

## Current official primary evidence

### Research quality, inclusion, and privacy

- [GOV.UK plan user research](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/plan-user-research-for-your-service)
  says to turn assumptions into research questions, choose methods yielding
  reliable evidence, run iterative rounds, include disabled/support-needing
  users, and make participant count method-dependent.
- [GOV.UK user research introduction](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/how-user-research-improves-service-design)
  emphasizes actual user need and what works rather than popularity, inclusive
  research, and reducing unchallenged stakeholder bias.
- [GOV.UK participant guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/find-user-research-participants)
  requires actual/likely users, diverse access/support contexts, minimum
  recruitment data, limited sharing, and deletion when no longer needed.
- [GOV.UK informed consent](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/getting-users-consent-for-research)
  requires understandable voluntary consent, disclosed purpose/data/use/
  observers/retention/rights, and stopping/deletion on withdrawal.
- [GOV.UK research-data privacy](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/managing-user-research-data-participant-privacy)
  requires minimum necessary collection, secure/restricted storage, purpose-
  bound use, reasonable retention/deletion, and anonymized outputs where possible.
- [ICO purpose limitation](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/purpose-limitation)
  confirms specified legitimate purposes and constrained reuse of personal data.

These sources support D47's documented gate and research-data safeguards. They
do not prescribe Core thresholds, profile values, reviewers, or an approval
application.

### Immutable/versioned product configuration

- [Stripe Prices](https://docs.stripe.com/products-prices/manage-prices)
  cannot change amount in place; Stripe recommends a new Price, can archive an
  old one for new use, preserves existing subscriptions, and permits deletion
  only when never used.
- [Microsoft Entra access-review creation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/id-governance/create-access-review)
  snapshots each instance; changes during it appear in later recurrence.
- [Okta campaigns](https://help.okta.com/en-us/Content/Topics/identity-governance/access-certification/campaigns.htm)
  permit broad modification before launch but only reassignment/end after
  launch and no modification after end.
- [Contentful scheduled actions](https://www.contentful.com/developers/docs/references/content-management-api/scheduled-actions/)
  uses explicit statuses and version-conflict rejection for stale updates.

These are strong comparators for immutable revision, prospective change,
archive/retirement, and preserved history. None supplies profile values or
requires a runtime registry service.

### Minimal choice UX and accessibility

- [USWDS radio buttons](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/radio-buttons/)
  present exactly one labelled mutually exclusive choice and explain a none
  option where zero is valid.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) requires labels/instructions, error
  identification/suggestion, predictable input, focus/reflow, name/role/value,
  and programmatically determinable status.
- [W3C labels/instructions understanding](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/labels-or-instructions.html)
  warns both insufficient and excessive instruction can impede users.

Core imports no component. The evidence supports a short vertical complete-
choice selector with explicit Off, not two timing fields or evidence clutter.

## Evidence classification

### Verified repository facts

- No D47–D53 cadence runtime, profile, registry, value, evidence workflow, UI,
  experiment, or placeholder currently ships.
- D47 already defines the complete qualification gate and no-auto-activation
  rule.
- D52 defines one indivisible positive whole-second timing pair and no value.
- ADR-0026/Phase 17 provide a code-owned versioned contract pattern, while
  Phase 12 remains cadence/source authority.

### Verified external facts

- Current official research guidance favors explicit questions, suitable
  methods, iterative inclusive actual-user research, consent, minimization,
  purpose limitation, and deletion.
- Mature systems use immutable/snapshotted versions and prospective archive/
  retirement rather than rewriting active history.
- Accessible design guidance supports one clearly labelled choice and restrained
  instruction.

### Requirement inferences

- Evidence must bind the exact pair as a whole; otherwise untested combinations
  can appear through independent controls or inference.
- Candidate/research/reviewer state must not become runtime authorization; a
  normal deliberate governing-documentation/code decision separates evidence
  from execution.
- Stable identity/revision and retained decoder are necessary to replay pinned
  D48/D50/D52 history after retirement/upgrades.
- No UI/runtime artifact may precede one evidence-qualified timing-pair proposal
  and all remaining gates,
  or Off-only/experimental placeholders become accidental product contracts.

### Product judgments

- Zero critical deadline/access/performance misreads is proportionate because
  the feature concerns sensitive access and copy can be redesigned.
- No live production experiment is appropriate: experimental attention would
  affect real access-governance staff without a qualified pair.
- Retirement is prospective and non-destructive. Stronger safety withdrawal
  requires a separate pre-activation decision; D54 instead governs local in-
  product reminder presentation.
- First release should show Off plus only the first proven profile, not a catalog
  filled to look complete.

### Assumptions requiring evidence

- At least one exact pair may eventually outperform the full D46 baseline
  without unacceptable fatigue/fairness/privacy/accessibility harm.
- Representative ministry cohorts can be recruited without exposing sensitive
  request, role, location, or member-care details.
- One concise complete-profile label/helper will be understood without exposing
  separate wait/usefulness controls.

### Unresolved unknowns

- No pair, number, label, evidence outcome, implementation module, or profile
  count is known.
- D54 must decide local in-product reminder presentation. A separate pre-
  activation decision must define urgent product-wide safety withdrawal effects
  on current Tenant heads, new source admission, and compatibility history.
- Content, channels, preferences, provider adapters, retention, implementation,
  activation, and quantitative operational budgets remain later gates.

## Best UX and UI contract

### No surface now

No cadence page, Off-only control, disabled card, “coming soon,” evidence badge,
research status, wait/usefulness field, advanced scheduler, preview, API option,
or hidden feature flag is authorized.

### First future release after all gates

The future governed route-addressable **People & access → Access requests →
Settings** Base Maia form may show exactly Off plus the first activated access-
review timing profile.
Choices are vertical semantic radio/cards with full clickable labels. Off is a
real explicit choice and the safe default/absence baseline.

Each non-Off card is one indivisible outcome, not two fields. Its approved
semantic/interaction contract states the cadence in user language; one concise
helper states:

> Creates one courtesy reminder if the access request is still waiting. If Asym
> cannot create it soon enough, it skips it instead of sending it late.

No evidence/version/internal field names, profile IDs, research claims,
Recommended/Popular/Smart labels, countdowns, due/overdue/SLA/escalation, or
delivery guarantees appear. **Save changes** and **Cancel** retain D51's
prospective/stale/receipt behavior; Off uses D51's inline consequence review.

An available, collapsed-by-default **How timing works** disclosure renders the
selected profile's exact pair in localized plain-language elapsed units and
explains that eligibility begins after the wait period and creation is skipped
after the following usefulness window. Opening it is optional; its availability
and accurate content are required. It never exposes internal field names. The
tested semantic/interaction contract is frozen, not literal English strings:
meaning-preserving editorial, accessibility, and localization corrections use
normal content/accessibility review, while any material meaning or interaction
change that could affect comprehension, fatigue, fairness, or harm requires a
new timing-pair candidate and requalification.

### Retired current-profile journey

A Tenant whose Tenant selected policy head already references a retired profile
sees
its exact read-only current summary and a quiet **No longer offered for new
selections** note when editing. It may leave the Tenant selected policy head
unchanged or
deliberately choose Off/another activated profile. After changing away, the old
profile disappears from available choices and cannot be restored. No forced
modal, alarm, deadline, auto-migration, task, message, or provider effect occurs.
Urgent safety withdrawal remains a separate pre-activation decision.

### Accessibility and field conditions

Future UX uses shared `@asym/ui` Base UI/base-maia and Maia/Zinc tokens;
semantic fieldset/legend/radio labels/descriptions/errors/status; predictable
explicit submission; visible/unobscured focus; keyboard/screen-reader order;
non-color meaning; forced colors; touch targets; reduced motion; 320 CSS-pixel/
400-percent reflow; RTL/CJK/translation expansion; mobile touch; and low-
bandwidth draft/receipt recovery. It never relies on disabled controls/tooltips
to explain unavailable research candidates.

## Problem validity and strongest alternatives

D53 solves a real governance problem: D52's safe shape is incomplete until
exact values exist, but a plausible-looking value can become durable policy
without evidence. The decision is at the right level—product evidence
qualification
of a closed contract—not Tenant configuration, provider settings, or executor
rollout.

The strongest alternative is choosing one conventional pair now. It yields the
simplest apparent UI and fastest implementation, but current products encode
incompatible meanings and no representative ministry evidence supports any
number. “Validate in rollout” would make live sensitive access work the
experiment and anchor later evidence to a shipped default.

Tenant-configurable durations are the other strong alternative. They respect
local preference but create two-dimensional combinations, false expertise,
incomparable support, invalid-state/migration burden, and deadline confusion.
D47 explicitly chose a small code-owned set.

No-build/Off remains the strongest product alternative until evidence passes.
Option 1 preserves it exactly rather than presenting delay as inability to
finish the feature.

## D54 — Local in-product presentation of the courtesy occurrence

### Context and concrete user journey

A coordinator has already received and read the initial **Access review needs
attention** item, and the source-owned request is still waiting when the one
courtesy occurrence becomes eligible. D53 determines neither a new task nor a
new source request. D54 decides only whether and how this later occurrence gets
recipient-specific in-product attention. Tasks Hub, email, push, Slack, Teams,
Google Chat, digests, reminders, escalation, and other channels keep their own
governed projection/delivery contracts.

### Options

1. **Distinct, perceptually and programmatically grouped in-product reminder
   item — recommended.** Create one new recipient-specific item with its own
   stable meaning/key and unread state, grouped/correlated with the initial
   request-attention item through consistent visual layout, reading order,
   accessible naming/description, and programmatic relationships; color or
   proximity alone is insufficient. A quiet
   title such as **Access review is still waiting** opens the exact currently
   authorized request. The original item's engagement/history and the one
   source-backed task remain unchanged.
2. **No new in-product item.** Record only the source occurrence and permit
   separately governed external projections. This avoids duplicate in-product
   attention but can make the courtesy occurrence invisible to coordinators who
   rely on the product.
   **Ruled out by governing decisions:** Resurfacing/resetting the initial item or
   altering the task is not a viable D54 option. It destroys stable engagement/
   history or makes presentation mutate source-owned work, conflicting with
   [ADR-0027](../../adr/0027-one-notification-presentation-and-engagement-model.md)
   and [ADR-0183](../../adr/0183-source-owned-work-projects-into-one-shared-tasks-hub.md).

### Recommendation and adversarial check

Choose Option 1. A distinct item preserves immutable history and recipient-
specific unread semantics while perceptual and programmatic grouping prevents
two unrelated-looking alerts across visual and assistive-technology use. It must deduplicate by the durable courtesy occurrence, never reset the
original item, never create or modify the source task, and remain independently
authorized per recipient. Option 2 is safer than corrupting history but weakens
the recovery purpose; reset/resurface/task mutation remains rejected because
visual compactness cannot justify dual ownership or historical mutation.
