## Here is what we are deciding

We are not choosing another Web Studio feature. The coverage audit found that
D1–D35 resolve every substantive product area in the Phase 23 source prompt.
The decision is whether to formally close grooming, freeze those decisions as
the founder source of truth, carry the required testing and evidence into the
eventual specification, and stop until the founder explicitly invokes
`$to-spec`.

## Why this matters

Without an explicit closure, future agents can mistake prompt examples for
unresolved architecture, reopen settled decisions, invent generic integration
machinery, or silently omit the testing/evidence package. Closing too early
would be equally harmful if a real user outcome or ownership conflict were
still missing.

The audit found no such gap. The remaining downstream products already have
clear owner boundaries; Phase 23 needs no extra integration dashboard, event
platform, workflow engine, reporting layer, AI writer, or provider-facing staff
surface.

## Reasonable paths

### Option A-prime — Close and freeze now

Ratify D1–D35 as the complete founder product contract, confirm downstream
seams as owner-bounded and deferred to their named phases, carry the source
prompt's testing and evidence requirements into the future spec, record the
formal closure, and stop. No implementation is authorized.

**Benefit:** The future spec and tickets receive a complete, internally
consistent authority without reopening product design or prebuilding later
phases.

**Cost:** Any later discovery that truly contradicts the contract requires an
explicit numbered amendment rather than an informal edit. That is intentional.

### Option B — Hold closure for one named contradiction or omission

Keep grooming open only if the founder can identify one exact user outcome,
ownership conflict, or source-prompt requirement that D1–D35 contradict or do
not cover. Research and decide only that bounded gap.

**Benefit:** Protects against closing over a concrete known omission.

**Cost:** There is currently no evidence-backed gap to resolve.

### Option C — Reopen broad grooming

Revisit ratified decisions or search generally for additional adjacent CMS
features.

**Benefit:** Reopens prior decisions and permits more discovery before the
specification boundary is fixed.

**Cost:** High scope-creep and contradiction risk, with no identified user need
or architectural deficiency. This would make the phase less implementable, not
more complete.
