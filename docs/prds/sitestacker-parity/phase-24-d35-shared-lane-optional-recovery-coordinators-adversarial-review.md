# Phase 24 D35 — Shared Needs-Assignment Lane and Optional Recovery Coordinators

**Status:** Founder choice pressure-tested and recorded as Reserved  
**Decision:** How ownerless Website correction work remains discoverable and
when selected staff receive personal responsibility to assign it  
**Review date:** 2026-08-29  
**Scope:** D31–D34 Website correction responsibility recovery, the shared Tasks
Hub contract, and reusable mechanics for later source-backed work

## Final disposition

**Accept Option 1 with required amendments.**

The selected hybrid is the strongest permanent direction only when its two
parts remain different facts:

1. **Website always owns a permission-filtered Needs assignment lane.** It is a
   current source read model, not a Mission Control queue, task, assignee,
   shared notification, claim pool, or second lifecycle.
2. **A Tenant may optionally select one to three Website work-recovery
   coordinators.** They receive personal assignment projections for the
   separate source action **Assign returned Website work** only when currently
   authorized. They do not become correction owners or gain access.

The safe, no-setup default is `source_lane_only`. Optional personal
accountability is progressively disclosed. D35 does not add a Site override,
team/group destination, capability broadcast, primary/backup order, claim,
round robin, workload routing, SLA, due date, reminder, or email. The source
lane remains complete when configuration, qualification, Tasks Hub,
notifications, Realtime, a worker, or Inngest fails.

## Exact corrected decision

> Website SHALL expose one permission-filtered **Needs assignment lane** over
> current authoritative source-work heads. One row exists for each current
> compatible recovery occurrence in which one or more exact Website
> Source-action scopes remain required and have no responsible recipient. The
> lane is a Website source view—not a `mission_control_queue`, task assignee,
> shared notification/read state, claim, or second persisted business state.
>
> The lane cannot be disabled, dismissed, archived, snoozed, claimed,
> completed, reordered by a user-authored priority, or mutated through a task
> row. It remains available regardless of coordinator policy, route
> qualification, task/attention projection, channel delivery, Realtime,
> worker, or Inngest state. Independently authorized staff may assign work from
> the lane without being configured as coordinators.
>
> Website SHALL have one closed, versioned Tenant policy with two effective
> postures:
>
> - `source_lane_only` — the safe code-owned, zero-setup default; or
> - `named_coordinators` — exactly one to three distinct, unordered, co-equal
>   **Website work-recovery coordinators**.
>
> A missing policy row resolves to `source_lane_only@1`; it is not an ambiguous
> unconfigured state. `source_lane_only` is not “off.” A named policy with zero,
> duplicate, cross-Tenant, inactive, or more than three members is invalid and
> the prior policy head remains current. Removing the last member requires an
> explicit successor choice **Use Needs assignment only**. D35 adds no Site
> override; exact Site/source authorization at occurrence and action time
> narrows each Tenant coordinator. Per-Site coordinator policy requires later
> representative evidence and a separate decision.
>
> The D35 route SHALL have a distinct purpose, contract key, policy head,
> immutable revisions, member history, recipient role, action, audit meaning,
> presentation, and retention from D21 Website review responsibility and D29
> external-review follow-up. Core never copies, aliases, synchronizes, defaults
> from, or suggests using those rosters. The same Party may be selected for
> several purposes only through separate deliberate saves.
>
> Route membership records responsibility intent only. It grants no Active
> Tenant Assignment, Website capability, Site/source visibility, protected
> detail, Page/Navigation/Communications edit access, task authority,
> publication, Giving, finance, or employee status. Route-management authority,
> coordinator responsibility, source-lane visibility, protected-detail
> visibility, assignment-action authority, and eligibility to receive the
> underlying correction are separate current predicates.
>
> For each exact Needs-assignment recovery occurrence, the source resolver
> SHALL enumerate the complete configured one-to-three-person set and intersect
> it with current Tenant/environment, identity, Active Tenant Assignment,
> exact Site/source and recovery-detail visibility, authority to perform
> **Assign returned Website work**, Phase 12 floors/governance, current source
> policy, and every expected head. A complete result may release the complete
> currently qualified subset. Proved zero creates no personal projection.
> Partial, stale, contradictory, corrupt, timed-out, over-limit, or
> indeterminate proof releases nobody. It never broadcasts or falls back;
> the source lane remains.
>
> One Needs-assignment recovery occurrence has one stable source-work identity
> and at most one shared source-backed recovery-task identity. Tasks Hub may
> create one recipient-specific assignment and personal engagement projection
> for each admitted coordinator; it SHALL NOT create independent copied tasks,
> one group assignee, shared unread, or a generic queue row. Source/team counts
> count the occurrence once. Each coordinator sees at most one personal row and
> their read changes only their engagement.
>
> The exact coordinator action is **Assign returned Website work**. It assigns
> correction responsibility; it does not edit, approve, publish, complete,
> cancel, or otherwise satisfy the correction. The Tasks Hub row keeps this
> source action primary and exposes no generic Complete, Dismiss, Suppress,
> Delete, Reassign, drag-to-Done, bulk, comment, due-date, reminder, claim, or
> acceptance control.
>
> An authorized coordinator or independently authorized non-coordinator assigns
> exact ownerless scopes only through a D33-style Website source command. The
> command derives the actor, resolves only current eligible destinations,
> re-proves source, recovery, destination, scope, policy, route, identity, and
> authorization heads, and atomically appends the new performer responsibility
> generation, immutable source receipt, and identifier-only projection intent.
> Assignment grants the destination no access; current eligibility is required
> independently.
>
> The first successful expected-head source assignment wins. Its receipt ends
> the assigned scopes in the lane and every applicable coordinator projection.
> The actor may see **Assigned in Website**; siblings see **Owner assigned** or
> **Assigned elsewhere** unless actor/destination identity is independently
> visible. Assigning only some scopes keeps the remaining scopes and recovery
> action current. Reading, opening, route membership, task status, notification
> state, or worker success never ends the lane.
>
> A coordinator who is also a currently eligible correction performer may use
> an exact source **Assign to me** action. That appends a fresh performer
> responsibility generation; it never converts coordinator engagement into
> performer engagement or treats task possession as access. The ordinary D31
> correction task is then projected under its own action/engagement lineage.
>
> D30 explanations and D34 Return recovery context remain protected source
> references. They never appear in source-lane list facts, coordinator task
> rows, notifications, email, workflow events, logs, search, AI, generic
> exports, caches, analytics, or route policy. Protected recovery detail loads

>
> D35 creates no separate bell item, email, SMS, push, digest, recurring
> reminder, target date, age escalation, SLA, sound, acceptance workflow,
> manager/editor broadcast, Site override, team/group destination, automatic
> fallback, primary/backup order, round robin, schedule, workload score, AI

> claim/takeover, Site override, or automation requires its own evidence-backed
> decision.
>
> Inngest is optional short-lived projection/reconciliation execution after a
> product-owned source/outbox commit. Its event carries identifiers only. It
> never creates or clears the lane, selects coordinators or assignment targets,

## Problem validity, necessity, and strongest alternative

The source lane solves the root correctness problem: required work with no
responsible person remains discoverable and actionable without guessing an
audience. It is necessary once D33 permits a final-recipient return.

Personal coordinator tasks solve a narrower discoverability/accountability
problem. They are not necessary for correctness, and Core has no representative
Tenant evidence proving every ministry wants a third Website roster after D21
and D29. The strongest alternative is therefore **source lane only**. It has no
route configuration, fan-out, personal engagement, route drift, or duplicate-
work risk.

The selected hybrid is better only because:

- `source_lane_only` is the complete default and requires no setup;
- personal accountability is an explicit Tenant choice;
- the coordinator route is capped and source-authorized;
- no route failure can hide or block the lane; and
- the feature can be removed without changing source truth if evidence shows
  little benefit.

This decision is made at the correct level: Website owns ownerless work and the
assign-owner action; Tasks Hub owns only admitted presentation/coordination;
Tenant settings own responsibility intent, not authorization or routing truth.

## Current modern-practice review

- [Salesforce queue assignment for Action Plans](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=ind.fsc_action_plans_queues.htm&type=5)
  is available to Nonprofit Cloud and lets a team share work until a member
  takes ownership; queue email is optional. It supports an always-findable
  shared-work surface and optional attention, but Core rejects queue ownership
  as Website source truth.
- [Dynamics 365 Customer Service queues](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/use/work-with-queues)
  organize work, permit pick/release/manual route, and track queue state. Its
  administration guidance also warns that concurrent adds/assignments can
  create duplicate queue items. Core prevents this through one product source-
  work identity, database uniqueness, and source expected heads.
- [Dynamics assignment methods](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/assignment-methods)
  explicitly supports “no auto assignment” but warns that someone must monitor
  the queue; its capacity, presence, skill, operating-hours, round-robin, and
  employee-monitoring machinery demonstrate why D35 should not become an
  omnichannel routing product.
- [Jira Service Management queues](https://support.atlassian.com/jira-service-management-cloud/docs/how-are-queues-used-in-jira-service-management/)
  provide a high-level triage/assign view, bounded counts, filters, and
  performance warnings. Core adopts the find/triage/assign interaction while
  keeping the view a source projection rather than configurable JQL authority.
- [Zendesk shared and unassigned views](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408888828570-Creating-views-to-build-customized-lists-of-tickets)
  show that work can remain discoverable in a restricted shared view without a
  personal recipient. Zendesk also documents group reassignment appearing in
  an unassigned view without notifying the group, supporting Core's quiet
  lane-first default.
- [HubSpot shared task queues](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/tasks/use-task-queues)
  let several users view and complete one set of tasks, but queues are labels/
  views and carry generic task-completion semantics. Core keeps one shared task
  identity while source assignment—not a task checkbox—ends recovery.
- [Contentful Entry Tasks](https://www.contentful.com/developers/docs/references/content-management-api/entry-tasks/)
  support team assignment and send email to every team member, but explicitly
  do not verify that assignees can read the entry. That is the exact broadcast,
  notification, and access footgun Core must not import.
- [Blackbaud workflow tasks and inboxes](https://webfiles-sc1.blackbaud.com/files/support/helpfiles/infinitydevguide/content/workflow/coworkflowtasksandinboxes.html)
  provide nonprofit CRM precedent for workflow-owned human tasks assigned to
  specific users/roles. Core retains the useful source/work distinction while
  rejecting long-lived workflow-engine state as the product authority.
- [OWASP authorization guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html)
  recommends least privilege, deny by default, and permission validation on
  every request. Route membership can therefore never substitute for current
  source authorization.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) requires meaningful focus order,
  visible/unobscured focus, minimum target considerations, programmatic names/
  states, and accessible status messages. Core adds its 44-pixel product target
  and 320-pixel/400% reflow gates.
- [Inngest idempotency guidance](https://www.inngest.com/docs/guides/handling-idempotency)
  states application code should itself be idempotent and that provider event/
  function keys are bounded to 24 hours. Permanent task/recipient uniqueness
  therefore remains in Asym Postgres.

**Best-practice conclusion:** an authoritative shared lane, explicit small
responsibility set, current authorization intersection, quiet default, and one
source-controlled outcome combine well-established patterns. Generic queues,
team broadcasts, access-by-assignment, auto-routing, and provider-owned human
workflows conflict with Core's governing decisions and are rejected.

## Domain model, ownership, and invariants

### Canonical concepts

**Needs assignment lane** is the current, permission-filtered Website source
view of exact required Source-action scopes with no responsible recipient. It
is neither a task queue nor a lifecycle owner.

**Website work-recovery coordinator** is one of at most three equal staff
Parties deliberately selected by a Tenant to receive personal responsibility
for the source action **Assign returned Website work** when currently
authorized. The selection is responsibility intent only.

**Needs-assignment recovery occurrence** is one source-owned occurrence for a
compatible action/privacy grain while one or more exact scopes remain in
Needs assignment. It supplies the stable identity for the lane row and any
subordinate task/recipient projections. Once every scope leaves the state, the
occurrence is terminal; later recurrence creates a successor.

### Ownership map

| Fact                                                | Authority                                                      | Derived/read consumers                       | Never authority                                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Required ownerless scopes and current recovery head | Website operational source                                     | Needs assignment lane, task presentation     | Task status, notification, route, Inngest      |
| Safe lane facts and protected detail projection     | Website source contract                                        | Mission Control Website UI                   | Task body, CMS, cache, search                  |
| Tenant recovery posture and responsibility intent   | Immutable D35 policy/member revisions                          | Effective route resolver, settings summary   | Role name, capability, D29 roster              |
| Current coordinator qualification                   | Phase 12/source authorization over the exact occurrence/action | Routing generation and personal presentation | Saved membership or task possession            |
| Recovery occurrence/task identity                   | Website source-work contract                                   | Lane/task correlation and dedupe             | Display text, provider event, profile ID       |
| Personal task assignment/engagement                 | Tasks Hub                                                      | My tasks and personal unread                 | Source responsibility or correction completion |
| New correction owner and assignment result          | Website source command/receipt                                 | Lane/task terminal projection                | Generic task assignee/status                   |
| Executor outcome                                    | Product dispatch ledger and optional Inngest                   | Technical operations                         | Source, policy, assignment, or audit truth     |

### Permanent invariants

1. Every current qualifying source occurrence appears in exactly the currently
   authorized source lane even when no policy row exists.
2. The lane has no shared engagement, task state, claim, or completion.
3. `source_lane_only` has no coordinator members.
4. `named_coordinators` has one to three distinct same-Tenant stable Parties.
5. D35 is Tenant-only; it has no Site override, fallback, or union.
6. Route-management authority, responsibility intent, current qualification,
   lane visibility, detail visibility, and assignment authority are separate.
7. Membership grants no access and capability alone creates no personal task.
8. A complete resolver result releases the whole qualified subset or none;
   partial/unknown never releases a partial audience.
9. Proved zero produces no personal projection and never hides the lane.
10. One recovery occurrence has at most one shared recovery-task identity.
11. One exact coordinator Party/recipient role/surface has at most one active
    recipient assignment for an occurrence.
12. Team/source counts count the occurrence once; My tasks counts it once per
    admitted recipient.
13. Each recipient owns only personal engagement; one read affects no sibling.
14. Opening or reading does not claim or reserve the work.
15. Only an authoritative source assignment/end receipt changes lane/task
    applicability.
16. Partial owner assignment ends only the exact assigned scopes.
17. Coordinator responsibility and correction-performer responsibility have
    separate action and engagement lineages, even for the same Party.
18. Route changes never rewrite source history or silently transfer personal
    engagement; D36 permits only a separate reviewed current-work application.
19. Access loss is not completion; later access does not revive old engagement.
20. Recurrence creates a successor recovery occurrence/task generation.
21. D29 and D35 have different keys, heads, members, roles, actions, history,
    UI, and retention; no alias or copy exists.
22. D30/D34 protected data is absent from list/task/channel/executor surfaces.
23. No task/notification/provider state can create, clear, hide, or complete
    Needs assignment.
24. D35 has no public, donor, missionary, publication, Giving, or financial
    effect.

## Best staff and Tenant UX/UI

### Settings information architecture

Keep D35 on the accepted **Settings → Websites → Reviews** page because this
recovery arises from review-requested correction, but do not place it inside
**Review responsibilities**. Add one separate section:

```text
Website reviews

Review responsibilities
  Review notifications
  External review follow-up

Correction responsibilities
  Returned Website work
```

This preserves one coherent settings destination while distinguishing three
different jobs. D35 does not create a top-level settings page, generic People
role, Tasks queue setting, or Site-exceptions editor.

### Settings summary card

```text
Returned Website work

Returned correction work always stays available in
Website > Changes requested > Needs assignment.

Personal coordinator tasks
Needs assignment only                                      [Change]
```

The summary never says **Not configured**, **Off**, or **No recovery**, because
the source lane is always active.

### Settings editor

```text
When returned Website work has no responsible person

● Keep it in Needs assignment only
  Authorized Website staff can find and assign it there.

○ Also give specific people a task to assign it
  Choose 1–3 people. They receive a task only when they can currently
  view and assign that exact Website work.

Choosing people does not give them Website access.

[Save]
```

Selecting the second option progressively reveals a shared Base UI combobox
and one-to-three selected-person rows. The picker enumerates only visible,
active, same-Tenant staff Parties and separately displays current qualification
such as **Can currently assign returned Website work** or **Will not receive a
task until access is available**. Responsibility intent may be saved for an
active but currently unqualified person only after the warning; no task is
released until current exact qualification passes.

Members are unordered. There is no drag handle, primary/backup label, schedule,
weight, availability, workload, or email field. Duplicate selection and a
fourth person are blocked before confirmation and rejected at the server. The
final-member removal flow explicitly chooses **Use Needs assignment only**.
The same Party on D29 and D35 appears in both summaries but is never offered as
a copy/sync shortcut.

### Source lane

Location:

```text
Website
  Changes requested
    Needs assignment
```

Safe row:

```text
Needs assignment
Assign the French Page correction
hope.org · French (Canada)

Returned work still needs an owner.                  [Assign work]
```

The lane uses a text-first responsive list/table with filters for Site, locale,

but is display order, not source priority, SLA, or due date. Counts and rows use
the same authorization predicate. The empty state says **No Website work needs
assignment**. Loading, no-access, dependency failure, stale, and source-changed
states remain distinct.

The list never includes D30 explanation, D34 context, return actor, candidate
body, coordinator names, sibling read state, or target suggestions. Protected
source detail loads only after a current purpose-specific check.

The row has one primary source CTA and no checkbox or generic overflow actions.

## Conceptual persistence, RLS, and authorization

### Smallest durable model

1. **Website work-recovery policy revision**
   - exact Tenant/environment and route purpose;
   - immutable contract version, revision, predecessor/current-head lineage;
   - mode `source_lane_only | named_coordinators`;
   - trusted actor/time and safe impact evidence.
2. **Coordinator member revision**
   - same Tenant/environment/policy revision;
   - stable Party plus exact recipient purpose/role;
   - one-to-three unique rows only in named mode;
   - responsibility intent, not capability snapshot.
3. **Needs-assignment recovery occurrence/routing generation**
   - exact Tenant/environment/Site/source-work/action/privacy grain;
   - current source/recovery head and scope digest;
   - governing policy head and complete/zero/indeterminate resolver result;
   - admitted coordinator Party/role references without protected body.
4. **Shared source-backed task and recipient assignments**
   - one task identity tied to the recovery occurrence and exact coordinator
     action;
   - zero-to-three recipient assignment generations;
   - personal engagement and source-derived applicability/end.
5. **Source assignment receipt**
   - trusted actor, target, exact scopes, source/policy/authorization heads,
     semantic command identity, immutable result, and projection intent.

Do not create a CMS task, generic coordinator role/group, Mission Control queue,
shared notification/read row, mutable task assignee, route-member JSON array,
or Inngest-owned workflow record as business authority.

### Constraints and relationships

- One Tenant/environment/purpose has at most one current policy head.
- Named mode has one to three distinct same-Tenant stable Parties; lane-only has
  none. A CHECK alone is insufficient for cross-row cardinality, so the single
  server command and relational uniqueness/constraint trigger or equivalent
  transaction-safe mechanism must enforce the full invariant.
- Same-scope composite foreign keys prevent route, member, occurrence, task,
  recipient, and receipt attachment across Tenant/environment/Site/source.
- Route/member revisions and routing generations are append-only. Deletion of
  a current member creates a successor revision; profile deletion or Party
  disposition never erases trusted audit lineage.
- One source recovery occurrence/action kind has at most one shared task.
- One task/recipient Party/recipient-role/generation has at most one active
  assignment and engagement projection.
- Source terminal heads prevent late create/reopen. Recurrence uses a successor
  occurrence identity.
- No default, label, member list, protected body, or mutable authority hides in
  JSON.
- Index the source lane by same-scope state, entered-at, and stable identity;
  index task/recipient active lookup and policy current-head lookup. Add no
  context/actor analytics index without measured need and privacy approval.

### Authorization boundary

D35 requires separately registered operations for at least:

- view safe Needs-assignment lane;
- read protected recovery detail and D34 context;
- enumerate eligible destinations;
- assign returned Website work;
- manage the Tenant work-recovery policy; and
- view policy coverage and immutable audit.

At every list, count, cursor, detail, candidate, settings, preview, commit,
history, export, and realtime request, the server derives Tenant/environment,
actor/Party/role, Active Tenant Assignment, Site/source scope, route/source/
policy/authorization heads, and exact purpose from trusted context. Route
membership, task possession, broad staff/admin role, Page edit permission, or
capability alone is insufficient.

Browser roles receive no direct INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE grant on policy heads,
member revisions, routing generations, source state, source-backed task or
recipient assignments, receipts, or audit. `USING` protects existing rows and
current read purpose; `WITH CHECK` or a hardened command boundary protects the
resulting scope, mode, member cardinality, identity, operation, task policy,
recipient role, and immutable heads. Security-definer functions pin
`search_path` and derive actor/time. Service-role, owner, worker, support,
repair, import, export, cache, Realtime, and AI paths repeat the same boundary.

Money types and precision are unaffected; D35 is explicitly forbidden from
creating any Giving, contribution, settlement, ledger, accounting, or payment
effect.

| later genuine gap recurrence                      | successor occurrence/task identity              | fresh eligible engagement only                                            |

The source assignment command is a short transaction with no provider call
under its lock. It uses product-owned semantic idempotency over Tenant,
environment, source recovery occurrence, selected scopes, target, operation,
expected source/policy/authorization heads, and governing versions. Same key
and same meaning returns the existing receipt; changed target/scopes/meaning
conflicts. A lost response performs receipt lookup before retry.

Route settings changes do not silently rewrite current recovery occurrences or
personal engagement. D36 requires prospective save plus a separate reviewed,
per-occurrence current-work application; D37 defines its complete compatible
pre-cutover Tenant cohort, closed catalog/census, atomic seal, and distinct
Tenant-wide application capability. D38 grant governance and D39 protected
direct/group source governance are now fixed; implementation proof still
blocks activation.

## Inngest boundary

Inngest may execute short, replay-safe materialization/end/reconciliation after
the product stores a Needs-assignment occurrence, routing result/projection
intent, or source assignment receipt in its dispatch ledger. Events contain
only schema-versioned Tenant-safe identifiers; workers reload current product
state and apply monotonic idempotent projections.

Inngest never creates or clears the lane; owns a policy, member, source, task,
assignment, engagement, or audit fact; selects coordinators or performer
targets; interprets D34 context; carries D30/D34 bodies or recipient lists;
claims or waits for human work; sends default channels; defines permanent
dedupe; or applies Website routes to Mobilize. A simpler database outbox worker
is equally valid if it meets the same contract.

## Normative requirements

1. **D35-R1 — Lane always available.** Every current qualifying source
   occurrence appears in the exact authorized Website Needs assignment lane
   independent of policy and projections.
2. **D35-R2 — Lane is a source view.** The lane is not a Mission Control queue,
   task, assignee, shared notification, claim, or lifecycle owner.
3. **D35-R3 — No lane engagement.** Lane rows have no unread, dismiss, archive,
   snooze, claim, completion, or user-owned status.
4. **D35-R4 — Safe default.** Missing policy resolves to code-owned
   `source_lane_only@1` with no setup requirement or warning nag.
5. **D35-R5 — Two policy modes.** The only effective modes are
   `source_lane_only` and `named_coordinators`.
6. **D35-R6 — Tenant-only v1.** D35 has no Site override, implicit Site
   fallback, team/group destination, or route union.
7. **D35-R7 — Named cardinality.** Named mode contains one to three unique,
   unordered, co-equal same-Tenant stable Parties; lane-only contains none.
8. **D35-R8 — Distinct route purpose.** D35 has no roster/head/member/role/
   history/default/sync relationship with D21 or D29.
9. **D35-R9 — Responsibility not permission.** Route management, coordinator
   intent, source authorization, and correction eligibility remain separate.
10. **D35-R10 — Complete resolver.** The bounded resolver enumerates the full
    route and returns complete qualified subset, proved zero, or indeterminate;
    uncertainty releases nobody.
11. **D35-R11 — No broadcast/fallback.** Proved zero or failure creates no
    manager/editor/capability/admin fallback; the lane remains.
12. **D35-R12 — Stable occurrence identity.** One exact compatible Needs-
    assignment source generation owns one recovery occurrence identity.
13. **D35-R13 — One shared task.** One recovery occurrence/action has at most
    one shared source-backed task identity, never copied tasks per recipient.
14. **D35-R14 — Personal assignments.** Each admitted coordinator receives at
    most one recipient-specific assignment and engagement projection.
15. **D35-R15 — Honest counts.** Team/source counts count an occurrence once;
    My tasks counts once for the current recipient.
16. **D35-R16 — Personal engagement.** Reading changes only the current
    recipient's engagement and never claims or ends work.
17. **D35-R17 — Exact source action.** **Assign returned Website work** assigns
    responsibility only and cannot complete or edit the correction.
18. **D35-R18 — No generic task controls.** Source-backed recovery tasks reject
    generic completion, reassignment, deletion, dismissal, suppression,
    comments, dates, reminders, bulk, claim, and acceptance.
19. **D35-R19 — Current reproof.** List, detail, candidate enumeration,
    assignment, history, route management, and projection re-prove exact
    current authorization and heads.
20. **D35-R20 — First source winner.** Only an authoritative expected-head
    source receipt assigns scopes and ends corresponding lane/task projections.
21. **D35-R21 — Partial-scope truth.** Assignment ends only assigned scopes;
    every remaining gap stays current.
22. **D35-R22 — Separate performer lineage.** Coordinator and correction-owner
    assignments/engagement never merge or transfer, including self-assignment.
23. **D35-R23 — Protected reference-only.** D30 explanation, D34 context,
    candidate bodies, prior actors, and coordinator identities are absent from
    safe lane/task/channel/executor data.
24. **D35-R24 — No external attention.** D35 creates no separate bell item,
    email, SMS, push, digest, reminder, due date, SLA, or age escalation.
25. **D35-R25 — Atomic idempotency.** Product receipts and uniqueness—not
    transport windows—own command/task/recipient idempotency and concurrency.
26. **D35-R26 — Lane-safe failure.** Task, notification, Realtime, resolver,
    worker, or Inngest failure never removes or blocks the source lane.
27. **D35-R27 — Prospective save and explicit adoption.** Policy saves never
    affect current occurrences; only D36’s separately authorized, reviewed,
    per-occurrence application may change their applied routing basis.
28. **D35-R28 — Cross-domain opt-in.** Future sources reuse only mechanics
    after their own lane/action/audience/visibility/retention decision.
29. **D35-R29 — Humane-use boundary.** Coordinator selection, receipt, age, and
    action rates cannot score, rank, discipline, schedule, or infer staff
    workload/availability/performance.
30. **D35-R30 — Evidence-bound optionality.** If personal coordinator tasks do
    not materially improve recovery over lane-only, Core keeps the lane and
    removes/simplifies the optional route rather than adding automation.

## Acceptance criteria

### Source lane and policy

- **D35-AC001:** Given one authorized current compatible recovery occurrence,
  the Website Needs assignment lane shows it once.
- **D35-AC002:** Given two incompatible action/privacy grains for one source
  object, the lane preserves their distinct source identities and safe labels.
- **D35-AC003:** Given no current ownerless required scope, no lane row exists.
- **D35-AC004:** Given only some scopes assigned, the remaining exact gaps stay
  in the lane and assigned scopes leave.
- **D35-AC005:** Given a source terminal or superseded receipt, the applicable
  lane row ends as **No longer required**.
- **D35-AC006:** Given no D35 policy row, effective policy is
  `source_lane_only@1` and the lane remains fully usable.
- **D35-AC007:** Given lane-only policy, no personal coordinator assignment is
  created.
- **D35-AC008:** Named mode accepts one unique current same-Tenant Party.
- **D35-AC009:** Named mode accepts three unique current same-Tenant Parties.
- **D35-AC010:** Named mode rejects zero members and preserves the prior head.
- **D35-AC011:** Named mode rejects a fourth member and preserves the prior
  head.
- **D35-AC012:** Named mode rejects duplicate stable Parties even when display
  names or profiles differ.
- **D35-AC013:** Named mode rejects a cross-Tenant or cross-environment Party.
- **D35-AC014:** Lane-only mode rejects retained current member rows.
- **D35-AC015:** Removing the final coordinator requires the explicit
  **Use Needs assignment only** choice.
- **D35-AC016:** Member order does not change effective policy, recipient
  identity, counts, or audit meaning.
- **D35-AC017:** No D35 Site override, Site inheritance selector, or Site route
  record is available.
- **D35-AC018:** Cloning, importing, or transferring a Site copies no D35
  roster or history; destination Tenant policy applies independently.
- **D35-AC019:** An unknown future policy version releases no personal
  projection while preserving authorized source-lane behavior.
- **D35-AC020:** Policy revisions are immutable, current-headed, actor/time
  attributed from trusted context, and retain governed history.

  Giving, finance, or other Site capability.

  authority, and coordinator action authority does not imply route management.
- **D35-AC030:** An independently authorized non-coordinator can assign current

  visibility receives no personal item for that occurrence.
- **D35-AC032:** A current configured coordinator without the exact assignment

## Ruthless synthesis

### Verified facts, judgments, assumptions, and unknowns

- **Verified repository facts:** ADR-0183 makes source work authoritative and
  Tasks Hub subordinate; ADR-0027 makes engagement recipient-specific; D29's
  Review coordinators own decline/expiry follow-up, not correction assignment;
  D31–D34 establish source-owned correction responsibility, Needs assignment,
  return context, current authorization, and expected-head receipts. Current
  generic Mission Control tasks/attention rows cannot safely represent D35.
- **Verified external facts:** mature service/work products use shared filtered
  work views and optional assignment/routing; richer automatic routing adds
  presence, capacity, skill, monitoring, and operational complexity. Current
  OWASP guidance requires deny-by-default, least privilege, and authorization
  on every request. WCAG 2.2 governs focus, status, target, reflow, and
  non-color access. Inngest transport idempotency is bounded and cannot replace
  durable business-effect uniqueness.
- **Product judgments:** the safe default is a complete source lane; optional
  coordinators are Tenant-level only, one to three, unordered, co-equal, and
  progressively disclosed; no Site override is justified in D35; the route
  should be removed or simplified if it does not improve recovery.
- **Assumptions:** some missions ministries benefit from named accountability
  beyond routinely checking a shared source lane; authorized Website staff can
  understand three responsibility purposes when IA and consequence copy keep
  them separate. Representative moderated research and canary telemetry must
  verify both before Live activation.
- **Repository decision:** D38 resolves zero-by-default explicit-only
  holder/grant governance; D39 resolves assignment sources as typed direct and
  protected flat Access-group paths. D36 resolves operation mechanics and D37
  resolves cohort/action/disclosure scope.

### What must be resolved before this decision is recorded

Nothing further. The ambiguous parts of the founder wording are resolved in
the corrected decision: source lane versus task, safe default, Tenant-only
scope, separate purpose, current authorization, complete resolver, one shared
identity with personal engagement, source-controlled action/end, protected
reference-only context, no default channels, and optional Inngest.

### What must be captured in specification and design

1. Define the lane, coordinator, occurrence, route-purpose, shared task,
   recipient assignment, personal engagement, and source receipt vocabulary.
2. Specify the two policy modes, missing-row behavior, cardinality, immutable
   heads, same-scope relationships, complete/zero/indeterminate resolver, and
   no Site override.
3. Specify separate authorization operations for lane, detail, candidate,
   source assignment, policy management, coverage/audit, and every privileged
   path.
4. Specify the source-owned lifecycle, exact action scopes, first-wins CAS,
   idempotency conflict behavior, partial assignment, recurrence, access loss,

## D36 — How should a coordinator-policy save affect work already in Needs assignment?

### Context and concrete example

Hope Ministries currently has four authorized Website rows in **Needs
assignment**. Ana changes **Returned Website work** from **Needs assignment
only** to **Also give specific people a task** and selects Ana and Joel.

The ordinary save can safely govern future recovery occurrences. Current rows
are different: automatically applying the save could create an unexpected
burst of personal tasks or disclose work a selected person did not expect;
never applying it could leave the four current rows without the accountability
Ana was trying to add. The product must also preserve an unchanged recipient's
read state, re-prove every current source/policy/auth head, and avoid showing
counts for work the settings manager cannot inspect.

### Options

1. **Prospective save plus an explicit current-work action — recommended.**
   Save affects future occurrences. If current work exists, show a permission-
   safe impact summary and a separate **Apply coordinators to current work**
   action. That command reloads current heads, resolves the complete current
   route, preserves unchanged recipient engagement, creates fresh personal
   assignments only for newly admitted recipients, ends only explicitly
   removed recipient projections if authorized by the transition, and reports
   applied/skipped/changed outcomes without exposing hidden work. This adds one
   deliberate step only when current work exists and gives staff control over
   immediate fan-out.
2. **Automatically apply every save to all current work.** This is one click,
   but it couples configuration to potentially large private task fan-out,
   creates lost-response and rollback ambiguity, and makes a harmless roster
   correction capable of silently changing several people's workloads.
3. **Future occurrences only.** This has the simplest mutation and lowest
   surprise, but a Tenant configuring coordinators specifically to recover a
   current backlog must open and handle each current row separately.

### Recommendation

Choose **Option 1**. It is the clearest least-surprise UX, matches the accepted
Phase 24 pattern of prospective settings plus explicit differential impact,
and is the only option that safely combines one low-friction repair action with
current authorization, concurrency, privacy, and personal-engagement integrity.

## Subsequent D36 resolution

D36 accepts Option 1 with required amendments. A D35 policy save writes a
trusted cutover and affects later recovery occurrences only; every current
occurrence retains its applied policy/routing basis. **Update current work →
Review current-work impact** is a separate capability and source-owned product
operation. Its preview is permission-safe and recomputed at confirmation; its
normalized members append per-occurrence differential routing under exact
heads. Continuing recipients preserve engagement, new recipients alone receive
fresh unread, and removed recipients end as **Coordinator responsibility
changed**. Lane-only removes personal coordinator assignments but never the
source lane.

The historical D36 phrase **Apply coordinators to current work** is superseded
as universal UI copy because it is false for member removal or lane-only.

uses **Use Needs assignment only for current work**. D37 now chooses the
complete compatible Tenant cohort and separate Tenant-wide application
capability; D38 grant governance and D39 protected direct/group source
