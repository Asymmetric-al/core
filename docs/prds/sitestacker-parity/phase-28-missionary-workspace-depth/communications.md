# Phase 28 — Communications, newsletter signup and personal connections

This is the canonical normative communications requirement volume for Phase 28.
It defines intended behavior; ratification, publication and test descriptions do
not establish implementation, provider qualification or activation. Each REQ,
US and AC identifier is stable and independently traceable. All normative
conditions of a requirement apply together with its acceptance outcomes.

The decision basis is the selected native signup direction in D6, the ratified
D7, D14, D15 and D18 review packets, D42 and D43, and selected D44/D45. Later
rulings supersede earlier proposals within their exact scope: qualified current
account-mailbox proof may admit immediate Join under D42, and confirmation lasts
168 elapsed hours under D45. D6's earlier universal fresh-confirmation and
72-hour proposals are not requirements. Its separately proposed pending-data
24-hour purge was not adopted. A finite native pending/proof/minimal-evidence
retention profile remains an explicit owner qualification gate; the seven-day
confirmation period does not itself define storage retention.

Native membership, external Request newsletter, human-authored newsletter,
individual correspondence, calendar copy and optional AI editing are distinct
source operations. None acquires another's consent, permissions or effects.
The full native newsletter journey remains required; automatic standing digests
are an intentional difference from the comparator, not an implied Send authority.

### Common release and effect contract

Every confirmation, newsletter, real email test and personal message requires
an actual qualified communication producer, immutable binding,
purpose, recipient resolver, typed facts, current source fence, sender/reply
permission, retention class, exact dispatch profile and proof pack. A Reserved
catalog entry, generic helper, provider SDK, UI placeholder or published issue
is insufficient. The complete bounded plan is validated and committed before any
member is dispatchable; parent/member count and digest and immutable command
identity are checked with release last. A sealed large-issue audience may fan out
through bounded durable occurrences, each complete under that contract. It never
reruns a live expanding audience or creates a second communications ledger.

Source acceptance, preparation and provider-attempt admission are separate.
A permanent attempt fence commits before any external byte; no network call
occurs inside the source transaction. A queue lease or prepared artifact is not
submission. Source cancellation ordered before attempt admission stops the new
effect; admission ordered first preserves possible external truth. Current
source/recipient/contact/disclosure/consent/suppression/sender/grant/utility
checks apply at their owning effect boundary. Positive exact-purpose authority
is required in addition to the canonical contact restrictions; calling authored
mail transactional cannot bypass applicable do-not-contact, do-not-email or
suppression. Topic-only withdrawal retains its actual scope.

Frozen audiences may narrow but may not expand, substitute recipients or
silently follow a changed address. Phase 27 appeal closure or required review
invalidation fences dependent unadmitted solicitation; independently authorized
receipts, service and fulfillment keep their own authority. Resend team/account
suppression and source contact restrictions are distinct evidence. Changing
sender domain or provider cannot automatically clear either restriction.

Only the exact closed Phase 17 Resend profile permits its qualified identical
sealed-envelope retry. Gmail/Graph uncertainty does not inherit that guarantee.
Accepted or ambiguous non-idempotent personal-mail work is never replayed,
rekeyed, cloned or shifted to another account. Acceptance, delivery, engagement,
human exchange and business completion remain distinct. Durable communication
history contains permitted body-free identity/outcome evidence, never personal
subjects/bodies, raw tokens, URLs, credentials or provider payloads. Source drafts,
execution bytes, any qualified readable projection and external mailbox copies
have separate custody and current access.

### Exact activation gates

Calendar copy and AI generation keep their Phase 31/shared-AI operation and
recovery owners; neither becomes a Phase 6 message or new notification merely
because it has an external effect. Their explicit requirement-specific contracts
below govern the corresponding admission, uncertainty and retry boundaries.

The atomic compiler and the prepared-artifact/sealed-provider-submission producer
are separate dependencies. The latter's actual implementation allocation and
component proof remain unresolved in the current inspected owner packet; the
compiler cannot be credited with this missing transport capability. Affected
provider paths remain blocked until the exact source handoff, prepared/submission
producer, matching-owner outcomes and adapter qualify. Independent native work
remains usable, and unrelated completion of all Phase 6 is not required.

Required owner amendments include the bounded native subscription lane alongside
Phase 25 external Request and Phase 23's older Phase 32-only subscription wording;
the native confirmation's exact same-Tenant non-constituent recipient descriptor;
actual Phase 4 ordinary verified-mailbox proof and its acceptance-time ordering;
native authored content, media, reply and retention profiles; the personal
connected-account topology alongside tenant Resend; and the native Appointment
plus minimal calendar copy. No fake Party/Auth user, second sender pipeline,
unified personal inbox or blanket whole-phase dependency supplies these seams.

Before activation each affected source/provider profile declares and enforces
finite field/byte/member/admission/backlog/rate/concurrency/retry/fairness bounds,
retention/disposal classes, and measured capacity. Native newsletter scheduling
also requires a finite source horizon and absolute usefulness deadline. Their
unselected numeric values must be qualified by the existing owners, not guessed
from a provider window or borrowed from unrelated Request newsletter retention.
Ratified numeric values appear in the relevant requirements below and are not
provider maxima or measured capacity claims.

## REQ28-M01 — Control the exact public signup entry

**Story US28-M01:** As an authorized missionary, I want to show or hide native newsletter signup on my permitted Page, so that I control its public invitation without changing existing subscribers.

**Decision basis:** D6 selected native signup and exact-Page control; D43 C01–C05/C09; Phase 22/23 placement and release owners.

The following behavior is required.

- Use the current exact-Page contributor/source capability, P22/P23 typed
  placement and existing publication/review workflow. Subject/spouse/coauthor,
  Ministry/Support Assignment or CRM access alone grants no Page mutation.
- New entries are not inferred On from a published Page, Resend connection, old
  Request button or legacy contacts. On needs deliberate choice, current
  readiness and normal release. Show Saved/awaiting review separately from Shown.
- Page-entry Off immediately stops new source intake through that entry,
  including cached forms/direct requests and stale publication candidates. It
  does not publish/unpublish the Page, change reach, remove consent, block
  independently permitted issue sends or replace signup with external Request.
- Display: “Hides signup from this page. Earlier requests can still be confirmed
  before they expire. Existing subscribers are unchanged.”
- Prove both orders of intake/Off, cached form, stale release, wrong Page actor
  and provider outage; no save outcome falsely claims public visibility.

- **AC28-M01-01:** Given an actor whose only relationship is Page subject, spouse, coauthor or Ministry/Support Assignment, when that actor changes the signup entry without the current exact-Page capability, then the command is denied and no publication candidate or effective entry changes.
- **AC28-M01-02:** Given an accepted entry Off and a cached form or older release candidate, when either attempts new intake or publication, then the controlling source fence prevents a new accepted request or reenabled entry, while the existing Page and subscriptions remain unchanged.
- **AC28-M01-03:** Given a permitted On save awaiting normal review, when the save succeeds, then the interface distinguishes saved/awaiting review from shown publicly and releases the entry only after current readiness and publication authority pass.

## REQ28-M02 — Understand organization signup controls

**Story US28-M02:** As an authorized organization administrator, I want simple independent signup controls, so that hiding a Page and disabling admissions have predictable effects.

**Decision basis:** D42 C07; D43 C05/C09; Phase 3/12 source consent and settings authority.

The following behavior is required.

- The source-owned native signup capability can be disabled under existing
  settings/permission owners. Disabled blocks new and pending admissions and
  unstarted confirmation dispatch, while preserving existing subscriptions,
  unsubscribe, giving/Page reach and independently authorized newsletter delivery.
- Reenable permits a still-current unexpired original request to finish; it does
  not renew the clock, revive withdrawn/superseded proof or automatically replay
  a P6 intent already terminally suppressed.
- One organization option, **Let signed-in readers use their verified email**,
  governs D42. Its Off routes subsequent new/rejoin requests through confirmation;
  it does not change existing membership or automatically activate pending work.
- Default On applies only to newly deliberately enabled and qualified native
  capability. Preserve stricter policy and Page Off; missing/invalid policy is
  not On. No per-Page/provider/project/reader exceptions or duration editor.

- **AC28-M02-01:** Given an unexpired request accepted before Page hide, when organization native signup is disabled, then pending admission and not-yet-admitted confirmation dispatch are blocked without deleting existing membership, stopping unrelated authorized issues or disabling unsubscribe.
- **AC28-M02-02:** Given a newly deliberately enabled qualified native capability, when no stricter existing policy applies, then the verified-email option starts On; given missing or invalid policy, then immediate admission is unavailable rather than inferred On.
- **AC28-M02-03:** Given organization signup is reenabled or the verified-email shortcut changes, when an old request is reviewed, then its original expiry and revocation history remain binding and no terminally stopped delivery or pending admission restarts automatically.

## REQ28-M03 — Join as a guest

**Story US28-M03:** As a public reader, I want a short newsletter signup without donating or creating an account, so that I can request that newsletter directly.

**Decision basis:** D6 selected native public signup; D42 C09; D43 C08; Phase 3/4/6/17 source and proof owners.

The following behavior is required.

- Require Email, allow one optional Name, identify the public-safe newsletter and
  explain purpose/withdrawal. No required gift/login/phone/postal/split legal name,
  prechecked marketing bundle, forced popup or checkout diversion.
- Resolve operational Tenant/Page/newsletter via the qualified published target
  and CMS-to-operational mapping. Posted tenant/actor/template/sender/header
  values are not authority. Optional name cannot overwrite canonical identity.
- For an eligible guest instruction requiring fresh proof, atomically accept
  one pending occurrence, exact scope/destination, original deadline, minimal
  evidence, durable operation outcome and required confirmation delivery intent.
  Only a durably accepted intake outcome permits “Signup received.” An
  active-identical request stays unchanged without unnecessary confirmation;
  restricted/ineligible outcomes remain non-enumerating and cannot claim a
  pending request or queue forbidden confirmation.
- Public receipt does not disclose prior membership, donor/CRM match or a
  restriction. It is not proof of confirmed subscription or message delivery.
- Duplicate HTTP keys cannot create endless mail; unverified intake cannot
  reserve an address indefinitely against its legitimate reader.

- **AC28-M03-01:** Given a qualified published signup entry and an eligible guest instruction that requires fresh proof, when valid Email and optional Name are submitted without a gift or account, then one pending source occurrence and required confirmation intent commit together and only afterward does the reader receive the generic accepted receipt.
- **AC28-M03-02:** Given a fresh-proof instruction fails before its required source-and-intent commit, when the request returns, then no accepted receipt or partial membership is presented and retry uses the same operation rather than duplicating effects.
- **AC28-M03-03:** Given a forged Tenant, sender or template field, or an address matching an existing restricted or active record, when public intake is handled, then trusted source routing controls the target and the response reveals no hidden match or restriction.

## REQ28-M04 — Join with a current qualified verified account address

**Story US28-M04:** As a signed-in reader, I want one deliberate Join using my displayed verified account email, so that I need not repeat an already qualified mailbox proof.

**Decision basis:** D42 C01–C05/C15; Phase 4 accepted mailbox/authenticator proof and Phase 12 public-reader boundary.

The following behavior is required.

- A ready private view shows the exact current address, named newsletter, one
  Join/Rejoin action and Use another email; no repeated name/email or second
  consent dialog. Login alone never subscribes.
- Server validates current Supabase principal/session, accepted authenticator
  and P4 mailbox provenance for the exact subject/address revision. A profile
  email, cached JWT, cached session state, generic callback, administrative/autoconfirm
  timestamp or arbitrary linked identity is insufficient.
- Before immediate Join is enabled, the shared Auth owner must qualify an actual
  ordinary native mailbox-verification producer and its exact challenge,
  destination and redemption evidence. Keep only necessary proof/current-revision
  facts in application-owned private identity scope; do not add newsletter
  identity truth or custom objects to the managed Auth schema. Provider evidence
  qualifies only through its separately admitted P4 lane, with applicable
  A10/G01 gates preserved. Prior mailbox proof cannot admit an otherwise
  unqualified authenticator and is not fresh possession or donor-claim proof.
- Immediate Join is genuinely implemented for that ordinary qualified native
  proof lane; permanently forcing every reader through email fails D42.
- A qualified public reader needs no target-tenant staff/donor membership, fake
  profile or fifth P12 context. Impersonation/view-as cannot supply the reader's
  instruction. Public read projection remains read-only; the canonical source
  command admits the exact self action.
- Prove current-address/session/policy/restriction changes serialized at acceptance,
  including direct native Auth mutation. An identity read followed by an unrelated write
  or asynchronous invalidation does not prove this boundary.

- **AC28-M04-01:** Given the organization permits immediate Join and the displayed address has current qualified native mailbox proof, when its signed-in owner deliberately Joins, then that exact newsletter/address becomes active once without another confirmation email, target-tenant membership creation or a second consent dialog.
- **AC28-M04-02:** Given a cached token, administrative confirmation or unqualified historical proof, when immediate Join is attempted, then it cannot activate membership and the ordinary confirmation route remains available without fabricating provenance.
- **AC28-M04-03:** Given concurrent direct Auth address/session or controlling-policy mutation, when Join reaches its acceptance boundary, then one defensible currentness order decides the result; stale address or session authority cannot commit through a check-then-write gap.

- **AC28-M05-02:** Given an identity service or current-authority lookup fails, when the reader attempts immediate Join, then no automatic fallback mail or false active/pending state is created; the interface offers deliberate safe recovery.
- **AC28-M05-03:** Given account address A changes to B and later back to A, when old proof or an old Join is replayed, then retired revision authority is not reused and existing newsletter destinations are not moved by account changes.

## REQ28-M06 — See an existing membership or deliberately rejoin

**Story US28-M06:** As a reader, I want an identical active signup to stay unchanged and a deliberate rejoin to be simple, so that repeated actions do not duplicate or revive consent.

**Decision basis:** D42 C10/C12/C14; D43 C07; Phase 3 exact-purpose consent and suppression.

The following behavior is required.

- At most one current membership exists per exact tenant/newsletter/destination
  identity. Active-identical Join is no-op and sends no new confirmation.
- Fresh eligible Rejoin may replace only that newsletter/destination's ordinary
  withdrawal, preserving history. It cannot clear global objection, complaint,
  hard bounce, person restriction or another source safety rule.
- No staff ticket or extra dialog solely for rejoin. The operation identity
  distinguishes fresh intent from replay: a new HTTP key alone is not fresh human
  rejoin, while a permanent address-only key cannot block legitimate future intent.
- Join/confirm/withdraw serialize on one source consent boundary. An old accepted
  Join retry recovers its historical receipt without reversing later withdrawal;
  withdrawal invalidates older unfinished instructions.

- **AC28-M06-01:** Given one active membership for the exact tenant/newsletter/destination, when the same current Join is repeated, then membership remains unchanged and no duplicate membership, confirmation or welcome effect is produced.
- **AC28-M06-02:** Given an ordinary withdrawal and otherwise current eligibility, when a fresh deliberate Rejoin is accepted, then only that exact withdrawal is superseded; an independent global objection, complaint or hard bounce still blocks effective use.
- **AC28-M06-03:** Given Join was accepted before a later withdrawal, when the old command or a duplicate with a different transport key is retried, then its permitted historical outcome is recovered without restoring membership; a genuinely new eligible Rejoin remains possible.

## REQ28-M07 — Confirm through one protected deliberate action

**Story US28-M07:** As a reader needing confirmation, I want to confirm the exact newsletter/address without signing in, so that a forwarded/scanned email cannot silently enroll me.

**Decision basis:** D43 C06/C13; Phase 17 protected-action protocol with the bounded native-newsletter non-constituent descriptor amendment.

  establishes the bounded non-authorizing landing session; qualified review then
  exposes the exact Confirm subscription action under current source proof.
- GET/HEAD/prefetch/preview/scanner opening neither subscribes nor consumes proof.

- Apply existing CSP/cache/referrer/cookie/HMAC/origin/CSRF/key-rotation contracts.

  fails safely, not a redirect guessing new authority. Original Page/device is
  unnecessary.
- Confirmation is not Auth email verification/OTP sign-in, CRM claiming, protected
  reading or identity merge. Do not substitute Auth sign-in or verification operations.

- **AC28-M07-01:** Given a valid confirmation link, when a scanner, preview, GET or HEAD opens it, then membership and proof-use state remain unchanged and the selector-only response exposes no private purpose, address or deadline.
- **AC28-M07-02:** Given valid fragment proof and current source authority, when the protected landing protocol and deliberate confirmation action complete, then only the exact frozen newsletter/address may be admitted without login, Auth verification or CRM identity creation.
- **AC28-M07-03:** Given a stripped fragment, missing script, retired host, wrong origin or invalid verifier, when the link is used, then proof is not consumed by a weakened fallback and safe nonrevealing help is shown.

## REQ28-M09 — Know the seven-day deadline

**Story US28-M09:** As a reader needing confirmation, I want one unambiguous deadline, so that I know when I must finish and how to start again if it expires.

**Decision basis:** D44 A and D45 A: one Asym-controlled 168-hour period; D43 C03 strict expiry.

The following behavior is required.

- Set the source deadline to authoritative original acceptance plus 168 elapsed hours, once.
  Require trusted decision time **strictly before** expiry after lock waits.
  Equality is expired; DST/leap dates/local display zone cannot change validity.
- Render the actual source deadline in the permitted email/review as localized
  date, time and timezone. Generic selector doorway still reveals no deadline.
  No relative-only midnight, countdown or timing configuration.
- Read, resend, retries, provider delay, Page Off/On and organization Off/On never
  extend/pause/reset the clock. Future policy change is prospective; confirmed
  memberships do not expire at their old proof deadline.
- Expired link offers fresh Join only through a currently authorized entry.
  Old link never creates fresh intent automatically. Late delivery is honestly
  expired; no invented replacement validity.

- **AC28-M09-01:** Given an occurrence accepted at an authoritative instant, when its deadline is established, then it is exactly 168 elapsed hours later and the localized display identifies date, time and timezone without changing the source instant.
- **AC28-M09-02:** Given trusted decision time reaches the deadline during a lock wait, when confirmation attempts to commit, then equality or later is expired even if the request started earlier; DST, leap dates and display-zone changes do not change the result.
- **AC28-M09-03:** Given resend, provider delay, hide/reopen, disable/reenable or restoration, when the original request is recovered, then its deadline never extends; an expired link may offer fresh Join only through a currently authorized entry and never expires an already confirmed membership.
