# Phase 28 owner reconciliation

This normative companion records the narrow cross-owner amendments authorized by
the complete Phase 28 `/to-spec` instruction on 2026-10-18. It synthesizes the
selected D1–D50 decisions and ratified completeness C01–C20 without changing their
historical selection/ratification labels. The confirmed testing seams govern
implementation proof. Formal synthesis and specification publication do not mean
that a producer is implemented, qualified or activated.

The [main specification](../phase-28-missionary-workspace-depth.md) and its domain
volumes own Phase 28 behavior. This file identifies exactly where predecessor
contracts are extended or superseded. Governing ADR-0001, ADR-0009, ADR-0012/0013,
ADR-0206 and ADR-0207 remain intact: native CRM, risk-scaled ordinary posting,
stable recurring line identity, fully immutable posted financial facts and Site's
separate attribution axis. No second CRM, financial ledger, subscriber master,
authorization resolver or communication dispatcher is created.

## OA-01 — Relationship preparation, sharing, correction and referral work

**Owners:** P9 relationship/preparation source; P4 identity; P3/P10/P12 disclosure
and authority; P27 partnership/Request consumers. **Trace:** D1–D5, D17,
D27–D28, D34–D36, D38–D39; completeness C02/C12–C15.

personal CRM or supporter enrollment. Deliberate sharing admits only eligible
contact facts into ordinary authorized staff CRM access; private preparation,
notes and context do not silently accompany that action. Archiving, eligible

Departure creates no new reader or successor entitlement. D28 permits eligible

under normal current-work rules; it does not revive grants, messages or effects.

P9/P12 shall support separately qualified selected-field contact correction and
restricted self-import. Trusted tenant, actor, attribution and ownership are
server-derived; a caller-selected target remains a proposal. Old and resulting
state, source revision, field purpose and current authority are checked together.
Import cannot infer donor identity, staff visibility, verification or consent.

P30's closed D17 self-import profile admits the permitted missionary as reviewer
and committer only for their own restricted-preparation target, through P9/P4
commands and current P12 grants. P29 owns the private source file and parser
derivatives under the target-purpose access, retention, hold, disposal and
restore rules. The [P29/P30 owner contracts](../../program-roadmap/owner-constraints.md#phase-30)

stable-ID bulk-update rules cannot widen this profile. Qualify the actual target,
review/commit, custody, current authority, replay and per-row recovery seams
before activating self-import; no existing importer or stored file proves them.

Referral access, approved minimal Expired history and continued active work are
separate source purposes. Expired history contains only currently approved
identifying information, never contact details/private referral context.
Continuation is a new deliberate authorized action; the missionary control is
initially Off and staff retain their independent powers. New and Continued have
separate finite organization-set periods, which may be equal. New uses the
original given-to-Maya calendar date and the disclosed last usable day; Continued
uses its existing elapsed timing. Recording late never grants a fresh window.
The source acceptance rule, policy version, permitted disclosure and exact end
boundary must be proved without manufacturing an unknown original time. No
numeric referral duration or configurable timing-mode matrix is introduced.

The narrow preparation/history purposes extend permitted workspace scope; they
never permit general CRM enumeration, sensitive notes, recognition-contact
inference or new source access through a task, referral label or dashboard.
