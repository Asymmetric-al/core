## Validation Contract

- Decision IDs are exactly D1–D18 and D57–D84; D19–D55 and deferred D56 are intentionally absent.
- Story IDs are exactly US24-001 through US24-120 and map one-to-one to the PRD's numbered stories.
- OpenSpec requirement names must resolve within `add-multi-site-management` capability deltas.
- Task IDs must resolve in `openspec/changes/add-multi-site-management/tasks.md`.
- Test and evidence IDs below are stable proof obligations. Implementation may add cases but must not delete or remap an ID without updating the originating decision.
- Provider-proof IDs match `P24-[A-Z][A-Z0-9-]*`; published implementation tickets match `P24-[0-9]{2,3}` (`P24-01` through `P24-126`). The suffix grammars are disjoint even though both retain their already-published Phase 24 prefix.
- The planned CI validator must reject a provider proof or implementation ticket that does not match its exact grammar, as well as any token that could make the namespaces overlap.
- The planned CI validator must reject duplicate IDs, gaps, invalid paths/names/tasks, or normative rows without a test and release-evidence target.

## Release Closure Rule

A story or decision may be reported Built only after its implementation and all listed deterministic tests pass. It may be reported Live only after applicable migration/cohort and provider evidence passes. It may be reported Confirmed only after the bound release-evidence package, including manual accessibility/usability or qualified external review where required, is accepted. A passing unit test, provider status, schema row, or published document alone cannot advance those claims.
