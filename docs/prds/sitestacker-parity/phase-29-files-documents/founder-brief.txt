\#\# 7\. Authority model to resolve and preserve

Explain the difference between a business document, a version, the exact stored bytes, a rendition, a provider copy, and a use of that document.

Phase 29 owns generic custody: immutable byte identity and integrity, private intake, processing/quarantine, qualified reading renditions, physical-copy inventory, authorized delivery, access evidence, owner-directed holds, and physical disposition.

A consuming domain owns what its document means, how it relates to a business record, which version is current or accepted, who may use it for that purpose, and its retention/disposition authority. For general resources or documents not already owned elsewhere, explicitly define the narrow owner rather than leaving them ownerless or appropriating existing domains.

Preserve these contracts:

\- \*\*Phase 18:\*\* Logical Document and Generated Document Version identity, canonical accessible PDF, official currentness, issuance/correction, and evidence-only revisions. File Manager cannot expose old receipts as donor-selectable alternatives or regenerate/replace them through a generic upload.  
\- \*\*Phase 21:\*\* receipt/plan/travel/card/opening evidence, exact claim/item/version coverage, collaborator access, records-package content/completeness, retention, and financial decisions. Adopting custody preserves existing opaque byte IDs; storing or losing a file cannot certify an expense, settle a case, or prove payment.  
\- \*\*Phase 22:\*\* specialized ministry media identity, sanitized masters, placements, exact page-release references, and withdrawal. Preserve its discarded-source-name and restricted-worker rules; do not retain forbidden provenance just because a generic metadata field exists.  
\- \*\*Phase 23:\*\* logical tenant-wide Media catalog, revision lineage, Site qualification, rights/safety evidence, placements, and Used in. D1 alone activates a public generation. Phase 29 does not build a competing Media library or decide public eligibility.  
\- \*\*Phase 26:\*\* attachment occurrences, conversation restrictions, and exact reader purpose. Technical previews are not official Phase 18 artifacts, shared CRM copies, reusable email assets, or a new search corpus.

“One document across uses” must not become one universal mutable record that overwrites these identities. One byte object can support several typed source references without collapsing their distinct business meaning.

Keep Mission Control, Public Website, Donor Portal, and Missionary Workspace distinct. Storage administration, staff rank, folder membership, a document link, or a coaching relationship must not grant otherwise prohibited domain access.

\#\# 8\. Coverage: identity, organization, and discovery

\#\#\# Identity and links

Resolve stable opaque identities, append-only byte versions, source attribution, provider-copy identity, exact-version versus owner-qualified current-version references, and correction lineage.

Rename/move must preserve identity and references. A new storage provider or object key does not create a new business document. A newly uploaded file with the same name does not automatically replace another document.

A matching digest proves byte equality, not identical provenance, rights, purpose, authority, or business significance. Separate same-tenant duplicate suggestions from physical deduplication. Avoid cross-tenant discovery and ensure deduplication cannot erase independently required retention or consent evidence.

Link creation/removal must use valid source commands and same-scope references. Attaching a document to a public or less restricted record must not enlarge its audience. Seeing one permitted use must not expose every other use. Unlinking one use does not delete the bytes or discharge other obligations.

\#\#\# Organization and search

Provide folders, labels, typed metadata, sorting, saved views, filters, recent work, and practical list/grid selection. Separate uploader, document steward, business owner, and person described by the document.

Folder hierarchy and metadata are organization, not automatic authorization or retention. Preserve Phase 23's already-decided Media-only folder contract rather than replacing it with a generic inherited-permission tree.

Define filename normalization, long/international names, collisions, breadcrumbs, Unfiled, moves, and personal/shared saved views. Reuse the shared metadata/view capability where appropriate instead of inventing an unrestricted schema designer.

Enforce authorization before search, facets, counts, autocomplete, ordering, duplicate warnings, pagination, and serialization. Sensitive titles, thumbnails, protected associations, and the mere existence of a hold/case can be confidential.

Bulk actions must bind a finite selection, distinguish selected rows from all matching results, and recheck each item's current permission/version. Show an impact preview for consequential changes and report succeeded, blocked, failed, and not-started items honestly. Do not claim an atomic batch while some remote effects succeeded; resume only the unresolved intended work.

Full-text extraction is separately purpose-qualified. Bind each index entry to exact source/version/coverage and current access; remove stale or revoked entries without exposing cached snippets. A partially indexed library cannot claim a complete no-results answer. Restricted content does not enter a broad search or AI corpus just because a user may download it.

\#\# 9\. Coverage: reliable upload, processing, and previews

\#\#\# Upload and replacement

Define purpose-specific accepted formats and byte/count limits, camera sources, multi-file batches, progress, pause/resume/retry/cancel, abandonment, duplicate handling, and browser/navigation recovery.

Use server-authorized upload sessions bound to actor, tenant, purpose, expected document/version, immutable target key, size/type bounds, expiry, and semantic retry identity. Do not let the client choose classification, tenant, public visibility, readiness, or overwrite behavior.

Direct-to-storage transfer is permitted only through the qualified narrow grant; it is not blanket bucket access. Bind and verify completion independently. Provider callbacks and browser success are signals, not proof that correct bytes were received and accepted.

Finalize using exact object identity, verified size/type/integrity, and source permission. Reconcile incomplete transfers, unknown outcomes, duplicate callbacks, cancellation/finalization races, orphans, and expired grants. A successful transfer can still need processing or business review.

Replacement creates a candidate immutable version and preserves the current accepted version until its owner deliberately adopts the successor. Interrupted replacement must not remove the usable predecessor.

\#\#\# Safety and processing

Apply a small code-owned, versioned profile per purpose. Intake is private. Validate actual content, not extension or browser MIME alone; bound archive expansion, frame/pixel/page counts, nesting, memory, CPU, time, storage, and retries.

Handle infected, suspicious, unscannable, encrypted, unsupported, malformed, and timed-out input distinctly. Scanner unavailability does not mean clean. Define safe operator recovery without a generic override that exposes known malicious content.

Isolate parsers/converters with minimum privileges, no ambient credentials, and no unapproved network access. Prevent path traversal, archive/symlink escapes, external references, scripts/macros, embedded executables, decompression bombs, and remote-resource fetching. Do not submit private documents to public malware-analysis services.

Qualify OCR or other extraction separately, including permitted input, provider egress, retention, language/coverage, and correction. Extracted text or an AI suggestion is not verified evidence or financial truth. Treat embedded document instructions as untrusted data, never authority to invoke tools, change policy, or expose other records.

Keep custody state, processing state, usable preview, domain review, business acceptance, rights, and publication separate. A clean scan does not mean authentic evidence, a valid signature, financial acceptance, or permission to publish.

Preserve originals where the source requires evidence fidelity. Metadata stripping, cropping, OCR, compression, redaction, conversion, and accessibility repair create separately identified outputs; they must not silently mutate signed or evidentiary originals. Apply stricter source-discard rules where already required.

\#\#\# Read-only preview and comparison

Carry forward Phase 26 D32's qualified images, static PDF, DOCX/PPTX reading renditions, XLSX saved-value view, and literal TXT/CSV contract. Reuse the narrow capability with current source access, not the Support conversation authority itself.

Its Office reader does not execute macros, recalculate formulas, refresh external data, accept revisions, or show omitted content as though it were absent from the original. Missing saved spreadsheet results are unavailable, not zero. Keep its declared coverage, limits, expiry, concurrency, and qualification requirements unless explicitly amended.

Define additional Phase 29 format/compare scope deliberately. A side-by-side view, extracted-text difference, visual difference, and legally meaningful agreement comparison are different products. Do not promise every-format fidelity or full comparison from a partial conversion.

Preview and Download original are separate actions. Unsupported or failed preview does not automatically download the file, open an external viewer, or share a private URL with an office service.

Bind derivatives to exact input digest, processor/profile version, options, restrictions, and coverage. Promotion and cleanup must have one winner; stale cleanup cannot delete an accepted rendition, and deletion-claimed bytes cannot become Ready. Reads cannot silently extend source or derivative retention.

\#\# 10\. Coverage: reviews, comments, and document requests

\#\#\# Requests and fulfillment

Support replacement requests, multiple required items where justified, reminders, and visible missing work.

Applicants and other limited-access participants may submit only to their authorized request. Upload-only access is not folder browsing, access to other submissions, an applicant account, or permission to read existing evidence. Claimed names/email addresses and possession of a link do not prove a legal identity.

Resolve link/session assurance, expiry, revocation, forwarded links, replay, interrupted submission, recipient change, and recovery without requiring unnecessary full accounts or weakening sensitive-purpose assurance.

Keep upload received, safe to inspect, reviewer decision, and source requirement satisfaction separate. A requirement can be satisfied only by its owning domain's accepted command/evidence. Rejection, needs-information, waiver, or replacement must not be invented by file-state changes.

Capture request definitions/versioned instructions so an edit during upload cannot silently discard required metadata or reinterpret the submission. Preserve idempotent handoff and exact version reviewed.

\#\# 11\. Coverage: private retrieval, sharing, and access evidence

A share identifies exact tenant, document/version or specifically qualified current-version resolution, recipient/audience, purpose, allowed action, expiry, and revocation. Default to the narrowest practical audience; an ordinary Copy link action must not make a document public.

Distinguish a reference link that still requires authorization from a bearer link that itself carries authority. Short expiry is not immediate revocation. \#\# 14\. Coverage: retention, holds, usage, and disposition

Apply owner-supplied versioned retention/disposition rules with their actual start event, purpose, scope, date precision, and hold authority. Do not invent a universal seven-year policy, restart a retention clock on download, or treat indefinite retention as automatically safest.

Distinguish removal from a view, unlinking, withdrawal, Trash, recovery retention, expiry, deletion eligibility, deletion claimed/in progress, partial deletion, and verified physical disposition. These need not become one enormous status enum; keep independent facts and clear user labels.

“Used in” needs complete coverage of registered owner/version families: business records, issued artifacts, drafts, live releases, schedules, private previews, reusable content, retained-delivery/recovery references, shares, communication preparation, and in-progress processing where applicable.

An empty visible use list is not proof of no use. Validate completeness with registered owner coverage and reconciliation. Missing/stale adapters block destructive conclusions. Reveal only permitted use details; an authorized records operator may need a protected explanation while an ordinary user sees a safe cannot-delete reason.

Prevent races between deletion and a new reference, hold, share, publication, upload finalization, or rendition promotion. Re-prove current authority and reference/hold state at the destructive admission point, not only when the confirmation dialog opened. Outbox cleanup alone cannot repair bytes already wrongly destroyed.

Execute authorized disposition across originals, versions, renditions, staging, indexes, provider copies, and applicable backups under exact source policy. Use provider APIs and verified outcomes; deleting a metadata row or adding a storage delete marker is not proof all bytes disappeared.

Keep an actionable partial-failure record and retry the same intended operation safely. Do not show Deleted while one required copy remains unresolved. Define lawful audit/tombstone retention without preserving unnecessary sensitive content.

Holds preserve necessary evidence but do not grant ordinary viewing, revive public access, or authorize indefinite unrelated data retention. Releasing one hold cannot bypass another. Any legal-policy conflict requires the designated records/legal owner; the agent must not invent compliance guarantees.

Restore cannot revive withdrawn rights, disposed-source eligibility, public generations, old shares, or expired requests. Apply disposal/revocation history before restored bytes are served. A clean backup is not permission to restore old access.

\#\# 15\. Providers, portability, backup, and operations

Choose the smallest qualified adapter set that meets the accepted purpose contracts. Multiple storage providers may be justified; multiple competing access authorities are not.

Keep provider-specific details behind the shared custody interface, not throughout domain code.

Use current official documentation and isolated synthetic tests. Supabase, Vercel, or Cloudinary examples do not establish production compatibility with Core. Do not rely on a beta label from an older page when a newer release changes the state. No real credentials in examples, logs, URLs, or source control.

Plan backup and restore for actual bytes, metadata, reference manifests, integrity, configuration, holds, and current revocations. Test a recovery-point and recovery-time target with representative data; do not infer recovery from database backups alone.

Inventory all copies. Verify content with appropriate digests; provider ETags are not universally interchangeable with the chosen content hash. Provider exit must preserve product identities, version lineage, permissions, retention, and source references before old copies are removed.

Authorized bulk download/exit uses a finite selected scope, current per-item authority, exact versions, a manifest, completeness labels, integrity evidence, short-lived private staging, and truthful partial results.
