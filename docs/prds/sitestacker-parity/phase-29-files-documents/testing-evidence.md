## Confirmed minimal approach

Use **the source-owned command, query and file-serving interfaces as the primary behavior seam**, with three complementary test families. Prefer existing Vitest, real PostgreSQL/local Supabase and Playwright infrastructure. These are proof layers around real product interfaces, not three new business subsystems and not one new seam per helper or ratification.

## Current source limitation that the design must not hide

`packages/api/src/identity/tenant-guard.ts`, blob `06133e276392d54dd00f188ae26331f7637e1988`, was freshly read in full after one failed fetch. It allows only acquire/release workflow-claim RPCs, injects/echo-checks the Tenant, and calls the supplied callback. Its atomic unit is **one guarded RPC**, not the whole JavaScript callback. The complete matching `with-tenant.acceptance.test.ts` (`49b20ae7b44bdafdf7e3675bdbc2e2deedefda36`) verifies that bounded contract with a client spy. Multiple SDK requests wrapped by this helper must never be described as one Phase29 transaction. New source commands require an actually qualified atomic database/application boundary or proved equivalent relevant-state protocol and native tests; they must not widen the allowlist by documentation alone.

The same principle applies to source actor derivation, current session/membership and P12 authorization. A test can inject a trusted test actor to isolate source logic, but another required integration case must prove how the production actor and application/database Tenant context are actually established and reset. Old JWT metadata, client-supplied actor fields, profile/Party assumptions or service_role access are not that proof.

## Minimal implementation responsibilities for the formal prose

These are conceptual responsibilities, not newly frozen package paths, endpoint names, table schemas or a generic workflow engine:

1. **Source-owned application boundary.** Each actual owner admits its commands, queries and source uses with current P12/source authority and relevant expected basis, commits the required business result/history/control/follow-through coherently, and recovers original operations under current disclosure. Ordinary request behavior, resource publication, partner reference and records policy/events retain their distinct source meaning; common transport does not merge their powers. Test through this boundary rather than private helper internals.
2. **Shared custody and serving boundary with narrow adapters.** Exact byte/version/use/copy identities, verified intake/sealed outputs, current read/delivery, complete references/holds and disposal/restore provide the common file guarantees. Source owners supply purpose/current restrictions; actual provider/processor adapters supply qualified physical behavior. Reuse existing infrastructure where it meets the adopted contract; add only the actual missing source-facing interfaces. No new vendor, centralized legal-policy owner or universal replacement of specialist adapters is selected by the testing decision.
3. **Existing application surfaces and projections.** Staff, recipient, resource and public interfaces invoke qualified commands and current safe queries/serving. Reuse shared design primitives and declared projection/classification boundaries. Keep request-private cache/auth behavior and source-owned public release semantics distinct. The browser does not become the source of event truth, history, authority or upload integrity.

Tenant policy resolution, event impact, operation recovery and current adverse-state ordering belong within these responsibilities. They do not require a separate event registry, per-button ledger platform, generic graph or new test framework. Existing independently owned P6/P9/P12 and specialist consumers remain explicit dependencies, not duplicated services.

## Concrete acceptance inventory to carry into formal testing decisions

- **Command/result:** ordinary issue/intake/review/correction/selected acceptance and terminal remedy; immutable received facts and independent references; policy draft/activation/application/correction and external occurrence conflict/retraction; resources and personal preferences; exact source-specific success, refusal, unknown and no-change outcomes. Matching original replay precedes fresh eligibility, changed intent conflicts, stale/ABA/expired intent cannot revive effects, and real same-visible-value factual correction remains material.
- **Transaction/order:** two independent native sessions with controlled barriers for acceptance versus fulfillment/correction, source admission versus reference/hold/disposition, policy/event correction versus final destructive admission, and simultaneous semantic duplicates. Assert authoritative resulting rows, history, result and durable work, not merely which mock promise returned first. Test both relevant orders, rollback/crash/lost response and after-wait current checks.
- **Every-door authority:** actual anon/authenticated/source server/nonhuman/service/owner contexts; missing/mismatched/reused Tenant context; inactive memberships and revoked sessions where required; old/resulting rows and composite/null keys; SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/UPSERT/MERGE, trigger results, views/RPC/functions/overloads and direct/collection/service routes. Catalog and static guards supplement real calls. Preserve one P12 PDP, its coarse RLS and current field projection; do not grow a second policy engine just to satisfy a test.
- **Bytes/provider/processors:** private original and rendition/output identities; malicious/unsupported input and finite resource bounds; valid still-open grants/in-flight writers after cancellation; immutable sealed bytes; range/HEAD/conditional/current cache behavior; partial and unknown provider results; no blind delete/rekey/refund; exact-copy inventory, restore integrity and adverse controls before resumed serving. Real adapters require their own current contract qualification beyond fakes and local emulation.
- **Users:** small end-to-end request with receipt/checking/review/F, mistaken acceptance, lost response and access changes; independent resource/reference use, policy/event repair and Stop/possible-effect explanation; authorized public release/withdrawal. Include keyboard/focus/AT, non-drag equivalents, mobile, locale/RTL/IME and weak network. No fabricated participant studies or all-screen screenshot assertions.
- **Migration/rollout/performance:** complete reached-family census and compatible old/new versions; blocked unsupported profiles and capable repair; replay/retention/restore fences. Use declared per-source units for selected members, query/page count, bytes, CPU/memory, transaction/lock time, retry horizon and copies. Preserve the exact P12 R6 logical governance-read/decision-ledger bound and its actual exceptions; it is not a universal total-SQL limit. No unmeasured capacity or zero-cost claim.
