## Delivery boundaries

ID-DIRECTORY consumes ID-SIGNIN's qualified binding substrate, not necessarily its completed UI/checkpoint. Shared binding and revocation capabilities can qualify before a whole checkpoint closes; preserve the acyclic EID-W01–W12 graph. Required sign-in enforcement cannot precede the revocation and recovery proof it needs. ID-OPERATIONS consumes both qualified lanes. All three checkpoints are required for full completion.

Phase 12's negative deactivation adapter remains independent of Phase 44. Directory revocation cannot wait for Workflow Studio or a staffing task. Native group changes, directory changes and emergency changes converge through the same source owner under their distinct authority. No SMS, application acceptance, financial succession or sealed-care grant follows from enterprise sign-in.

Carry all 34 EID cases and applicable `EXT-INT-01`–`EXT-INT-12` and `IG-AT-*` cases into exact-profile implementation evidence. Do not reuse #686 as the full enterprise feature ticket, activate unrelated reserved seams, or remove the old-generation inertness/identity-safety tests. Publish bounded successor tickets only after overlap and affected owner-decision checks.
