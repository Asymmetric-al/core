## Concrete implementation choices, not invented product facts

- Rediscover the actual supported Payload-major-compatible Next/React/Puck/toolchain cohort. Puck 0.23.0 and Inngest 4.5.1 are dated source references, not approvals to edit current pins. Do not silently downgrade Payload, mix channels or force peers.
- Qualify the smallest isolated composer deployment that passes the browser contract, including blocked third-party cookies. The equally isolated full-page mode is allowed; a fourth product app, same-origin downgrade or raw Payload fallback is not implied.
- The launch binding is exact Tenant/environment/presentation project/package root, with one active binding per project/environment. Multiple authorized Sites may consume admitted versions independently. Record concrete repository/project/Site relationships; do not infer a tenant-wide one-repository quota. Cross-Tenant branch partition is not an admitted isolation strategy.
- Qualify the build provider/image, egress/dependency/cache controls, capacity and custody profiles against the specified numeric starting profile. Limits are engineering qualification targets, not a commercial tier or claim of current capacity.
- Resolve actual module exports and capability keys against the current owners. `packages/presentation-sdk` and CMS subtree examples are placement proposals; do not invent parallel authorities because a proposed name is absent.
- Source maintainer/support, license and retention evidence must come from the responsible parties and exact artifacts. A planning document cannot manufacture those facts.

These choices have falsifiable gates and an implementation owner. A demand for arbitrary untrusted production code, independently hosted frontends, expanded Article grammar, cross-Tenant source sharing or weaker safety requires a new explicit owner/product decision; none is a fallback when qualification fails.
