# ADR-0090: Finance-closed Field Account cycles and independent payment truth

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 21 grill session — D1)

Phase 21 uses tenant-scheduled, finance-closed support cycles rather than
treating newly recorded gifts as immediately available worker money. The
organization-owned Field Account remains an append-only operational allocation
subledger; QBO or Xero remains authoritative for the books; and the tenant's
external payroll or accounts-payable process remains authoritative for payment
execution. Asym may record exact plans, handoffs, evidence, and derived
progress, but it does not move money, run payroll, or let accounting-provider
balances overwrite Field Account truth.

One report-first experience preserves distinct Expense Claims, policy
decisions, Approved Expense Snapshots, Reimbursement Obligations, Field Account
Funding Coverage, External Payment Occurrences, and accounting-ready handoffs.
Approval is not payment, funding coverage is not liability, and an insufficient
Field Account balance cannot automatically erase or relabel an already
established obligation. When a current Support Workspace Publication Profile
Version authorizes missionary balance publication, the balance is
source-labelled and dated; omitting that publication never changes the
underlying finance truth, close, history, or finance access. `Paid` requires
payment evidence. Closed cycles, approved snapshots, obligations, payments,
and corrections are immutable and linked through append-only succession.

**Phase 21 D23 precision amendment (2026-08-01).** D23 expense-effect
candidates enter a Support Cycle only as pre-admitted normalized occurrences at
or below the captured close cursor. A first qualification observed after a
prior cycle closes is a new late-admitted occurrence in the next permitted
cycle, not a correction of an effect that never existed; only a later source
change to an admitted root is a correction. The exact approved item/split and
its balanced effect group is the atomic unit, so one blocked slice does not hold
unrelated work. Claim, obligation, payment, D23 support-balance inclusion,
Phase 20 accounting, and final provider truth remain independently labelled.
