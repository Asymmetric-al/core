# ADR-0123: Page-resolved, source-authoritative public support progress

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 22 grill session - D6)

## Context

Missionary support is often sustained monthly giving, while projects may be
finite, seasonal, recurring, or intentionally presented without public
financial progress. A universal meter, Page-Family assumption, or tenant-built
formula would conflate commitments, posted gifts, internal Field Account truth,
currencies, and periods. A live tenant default would also let one settings edit
silently change already released public claims.

## Decision

Every released Public Ministry Page pins one immutable, prospective, page-
resolved Public Support Progress Profile Version selecting either **Do not show
public progress** or one compatible code-certified metric. Tenant and Page-
Family defaults may seed new drafts only; they never remain in the live
resolution chain or mutate existing pages. Page Family guides compatible
presets without deciding whether a page shows progress or whether its
fundraising is recurring or finite.

Phase 22 owns the public binding and presentation, not the numbers. Phase 13
owns corrected posted-effective gross and typed-count projections, Phase 16
owns commitment and cadence-normalized support truth, Phase 28 owns missionary
Support-Raising Goal Versions, and Phase 15 offline gifts participate only
after becoming Phase 13 posted-effective facts. Phase 20 settlement/accounting,
Phase 21 Field Account/availability/payment facts, and Phase 33 reporting
conversion cannot become progress inputs or fallbacks. Every monetary metric is
single-currency and labels commitment, receipt, period, and goal meaning
exactly.

D2's Page Release Manifest pins the profile and metric-contract generation, not
a copied result. Source-owned changes advance a disposable, correction-aware,
source-watermarked public projection without editorial republishing. A change
to visibility, metric, goal, currency, period, or scope creates a prospective
successor page release. Hidden state is enforced at the server and egress
boundary; missing, stale, suppressed, or incompatible truth omits only the
widget and creates a cause-owned staff exception, never zero or automatic
fallback.

## Consequences

- Staff configure one page through one quiet card and exact visitor preview;
  contributors cannot alter protected financial meaning.
- Existing and unprovable pages migrate hidden, and mutable legacy funding
  counters are never authority.
- Existing anonymous raw-worker/fund access and browser Realtime exposure are
  removed through a forward migration and proved absent after the full migration
  chain; public rendering uses only the server-owned Phase 5 choke point.
- Cache identity includes exact release, D6 profile/projection, and safety scope.
  Ordinary positive refresh may be stale-while-revalidate, but hiding, adverse
  correction, retirement, containment, and Phase 10 narrowing expire the
  affected scope immediately while request-time gates remain authoritative.
- Public progress stays correction-aware, privacy-filtered, accessible,
  independently optional, and decoupled from Giving availability.
- New progress meanings require a certified source contract rather than tenant
  formulas or Page-Family inference.
