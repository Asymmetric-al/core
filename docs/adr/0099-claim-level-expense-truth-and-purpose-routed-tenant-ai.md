# ADR-0099: Claim-level expense truth and purpose-routed tenant AI

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 21 grill session — D10)

Phase 21 treats the **Expense Claim Version**, not an Expense Report, as the
smallest claimant-authored and independently dispositioned source fact. An
optional autosaved Expense Report Draft and immutable Expense Report Submission
organize exact Claim Versions for a familiar trip, project, month, or
single-expense review experience. They are review envelopes and derived
summaries, not aggregate approval, obligation, funding, payment, accounting, or
provider truth.

Every claim item or split receives one exact, policy-versioned disposition.
Clean claims may advance while selected claims receive a bounded
needs-information, rejection, or exclusion outcome. Returned work creates a
linked successor Claim Version; prior submissions and decisions remain
immutable. Each approved Claim Version produces at most one non-overlapping
Approved Expense Snapshot for its exact approved coverage. Later approved
coverage is a linked supplement or successor, and later financial change uses
the applicable cause-linked append-only correction. Reimbursement Obligation,
Field Account Funding Coverage, External Payment Occurrence, the Phase 20
Accounting-Ready Expense Handoff, Accounting Release, and final QBO/Xero truth
remain independently authoritative.

Receipt and substantiation evidence uses immutable private Receipt Evidence
Assets and versioned many-to-many Expense Evidence Links. Exact coverage,
evidence role, actor, and purpose are preserved; missing-receipt declarations
are a distinct governed evidence kind. Original bytes remain immutable while
crop, rotation, OCR, thumbnail, redaction, and accessible renditions remain
derived. The public `document-uploads` bucket and public media paths are
prohibited. Phase 21 cannot ship receipt evidence until a genuinely private
Phase-29-compatible byte seam provides opaque identity, upload finalization,
malware/file hygiene, short-lived authorized access, access audit, retention,
and hold behavior. Phase 29 later owns the common byte/access lifecycle without
changing Phase 21 evidence meaning or coverage.

D10 also establishes one minimal shared tenant-owned AI execution foundation
for this and later product domains. It separates stable AI Provider Connections
from write-only encrypted and revisioned Provider Credentials. A closed
code-owned AI Feature Purpose is bound prospectively through one immutable,
purpose-specific **AI Capability Binding Version** to an exact provider
connection, credential revision, capability-certified provider/model/region,
input/output contract, data-use posture, schema/prompt family, and budget/rate
envelope. The tenant may reuse one connection or choose different credentials,
models, regions, and budgets per purpose.

Every invocation requires an authorization- and classification-gated
minimum-data AI Egress Manifest and immutable AI Invocation Evidence. Receipt
pixels and text are untrusted data. Extraction receives no tools, browsing,
network retrieval, code execution, tenant search, or write authority; output
must satisfy a strict structured schema and deterministic money/date/currency
validation. Phase 21 stores only non-authoritative Receipt Extraction or
Expense Match Suggestion Versions. Material fields and evidence matches require
explicit authorized human confirmation before ordinary source facts change.

Tenant-supplied credentials do not weaken Phase 3/10 classification. Restricted,
high-risk, care, security, or otherwise prohibited receipt context cannot leave
through remote AI. Missing, revoked, invalid, rate-limited, unavailable,
unsupported, unsafe, or over-budget AI always leaves the original evidence
intact and the full manual path available. In-flight work pins its exact
Credential Revision and Binding Version. It cannot silently switch provider,
account, model, region, or purpose, and ambiguous provider outcomes are
inspected before any retry.

The ordinary admin surface is purpose-first (**Settings → AI features**) with
provider/model, data sent, region, data-use posture, billing owner, budget,
health, test evidence, and manual fallback. Secrets are never read back.
Separate Phase 12 capabilities govern connection management, credential
replacement/revocation, binding activation, usage/cost visibility, invocation,
suggestion acceptance, claim review, evidence access, and protected audit
retrieval. A broad role, possession of a key, or provider account access is not
product authorization.

Phase 21 owns receipt-extraction and expense-match suggestion semantics and
acceptance. Phase 22 D16 consumes the same foundation for Public Page writing
assistance but owns content acceptance and publication. Phase 40 later consumes
rather than replaces the shared foundation for the generalized AI Operator
Workbench.

**Phase 22 D16 consumption amendment (2026-08-06).** Phase 22 now consumes the
`public-profile drafting` purpose for one source-bounded Public Page Writing
Assistant, including an independently certified source-language and exact
English-locale pair for the **Translate to English** action. The generated text
is not a certified translation. This shared foundation continues to
own only connection, credential, binding, egress, invocation, adapter, usage,
cost, and revocation truth. Phase 22 exclusively owns exact Page sources,
suggestions, application into a working revision, the check-work warning, and
content acceptance; Phase 10 retains safety, Phase 12 retains current actor
authority, Phase 24 retains locale and translation-status truth, and D4/D5
retain submission and release. Provider translation success never means a
fluent, verified, bilingual-reviewed, official, certified, released, or
published Page.

**Phase 21 D24 precision amendment (2026-08-02).** A D10 Expense Claim may be
prepared under one exact D24 Expense Collaboration Assignment without changing
the claimant or making the helper the source of claimant truth. Claimant,
economic payer, evidence contributor, preparer, submitter,
confirmer/attestor, reviewer, approver, beneficiary/payee, and actual principal
remain separate typed facts. An immutable Claimant Confirmation Version pins
one complete exact Claim Version digest, material assertions, item/split facts,
ISO currencies, and evidence-link set before another actor may mechanically
submit it; any material successor stales that confirmation. A qualifying
external attestation must remain claimant-authored and meet the Asym minimum
proof floor. Helper action, sender identity, OCR/match/model output,
notification, silence, relationship, or prior claim never creates claimant
confirmation.

D24 preparation, invitation, confirmation, submission, and actor provenance
grant no review, approval, obligation, Field Account, payment, payroll, or
accounting authority. Helper evidence remains private, staged, scanned,
non-authoritative, and explicitly linked under current authorization. Submitted
correction creates a linked D10 successor and, where another actor submits,
fresh claimant confirmation and a fresh immutable Expense Report Submission;
no original actor or prior version is rewritten.

This decision rejects public receipt storage, destructive report reopening,
report-level paid/synced authority, a generic expense ledger, provider-native
fields as domain truth, one global tenant AI key, one credential column per
feature, arbitrary endpoints or models, tenant-authored system prompts, secret
readback, browser-side provider calls, silent provider fallback, AI-authored
claim/policy/financial/publication truth, and any dependency on AI for manual
expense completion.

**Phase 21 D25 precision amendment (2026-08-02).** D25 may request facts and
observe one exact D10 successor, withdrawal, or evidence response, but it never
edits a submitted Claim Version, authors claimant facts, treats comments,
email, OCR/match/AI output, silence, or a timer as substantiation, or reopens an
Expense Report Submission. Changed material facts use the ordinary linked D10
successor and any required fresh claimant confirmation/submission. The D25
Case, Occurrence, Projection, Downstream Impact Manifest, or **Complete** label
is coordination truth only and cannot create or mutate claim, evidence,
submission, approval, payment, Field Account, statement, or accounting truth.
