# Accounting-ready expense handoff

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 20 grill session — D18)

**Phase 21 D1/D4/D7/D10/D13/D14/D15/D16/D18/D22/D23/D24/D25 precision amendments:** accepted
through 2026-08-02.

Phase 21 owns the complete expense report, receipts, substantiation, Expense
Policy Decision, Approved Expense Snapshot, Reimbursement Obligation, Field
Account Funding Coverage, Field Account effect, and source-owned evidence of an
External Payment Occurrence. The tenant's payroll,
accounts-payable, or authorized manual payment process owns payment execution
and provider-native status. Phase 20 receives only one PII-minimized, Tenant-,
Legal-Entity-, source-version-, and posting-owner-pinned **Accounting-Ready
Expense Handoff**. The handoff may contain a closed catalog of Cleared
Organization-Paid Expense, Approved Reimbursement Obligation,
evidence-qualified external Reimbursement Payment, evidence-qualified Expense
Advance Issuance Occurrence, separately certified Expense Advance Application
typed accounting effect where applicable, source-qualified Claimant Repayment
Occurrence whose immutable `return_family` is exactly `cash_claimant_return` or
`expense_advance_return`, and cause-linked correction occurrences plus exact
**Reimbursement Payment Coverage**. Phase 20 validates those facts and compiles
the existing immutable Accounting Release; it does not own expense approval,
Field Account Funding Coverage, AP balances, payment execution, card-liability
settlement, payroll or tax adjudication, or final reconciliation.

Each expense or obligation occurrence references exactly one applicable
Approved Expense Snapshot lineage. A report remains a review workspace and may
yield zero, one, or many snapshots for exact approved Expense Claim Version,
item, or split coverage. Later approval of previously unapproved coverage
creates a non-overlapping supplement or successor; it never rewrites a prior
snapshot or repacks coverage already handed to accounting. A later External
Payment Occurrence is represented
by separate source-owned evidence, governed by Phase 20 D17 posting ownership, whose
coverage references every exact obligation and originating snapshot version.
Payment coverage is homogeneous for one Tenant, Legal Entity, payee,
disbursement currency, and posting owner. A reimbursement-only payment is
conserved through exact Reimbursement Payment Coverage plus signed
payment-side residual dispositions. When one atomic payment covers both
compensation and reimbursement, one complete typed payment manifest uses the
External Payment Occurrence's one payment currency and conserves the source
amount through exact Compensation Payment Coverage, Reimbursement Payment
Coverage, and one signed, typed, explicitly resolved residual disposition,
including zero. A covered source component in another currency carries
immutable source/payment amounts and exact conversion evidence; an unresolved
residual or FX ambiguity fails closed. Cross-payee batches are grouping
envelopes around separate atomic External Payment Occurrences.

Phase 20 D17 still assigns one posting owner to the complete payment. When external
payroll or accounts payable already owns accounting for a mixed payment, D18
may project its exact reimbursement slice and evidence for linkage and status
but MUST NOT create a standalone reimbursement-payment Accounting Release. An
Asym-owned mixed-payment release requires one certified complete compensation
source contract, accountant-confirmed posting semantics, and exact Phase 20 D17 posting
owner for the whole payment; otherwise the payment remains accounting-dark and
opens one exception. A Compensation Handoff Package cannot enter through the
D18 expense lane. Neither can a Compensation Draft Delivery Profile Version,
Provider Draft Operation, provider draft/input acceptance, or its
readback/confirmation evidence; Phase 21 D7 does not create a second accounting
source or QBO/Xero Accounting path.

Snapshot-rooted handoffs carry exactly one Approved Expense Snapshot identity;
payment handoffs instead carry one payment-source identity plus the complete
covered-obligation and originating-snapshot reference set. They never invent a
primary snapshot. Original payment applications are immutable. Later returns,
disputes, or corrections are new signed occurrences, and no application may
exceed the obligation's remaining approved amount.

One adaptive report-first experience remains the ordinary user experience, but
Expense Claim Version, claim/item disposition, Approved Expense Snapshot or
supplement, Reimbursement Obligation, Field Account Funding Coverage, External
Payment Occurrence, Accounting Release, provider delivery, Bank Match, and
final QBO/Xero truth remain independently authoritative. The D16 advance and
claimant-repayment families require their exact separately certified source
contracts. Per-diem, mileage, and other travel-allowance families remain
unavailable unless one Phase 21 D18 Travel Allowance Calculation Occurrence is
frozen through an eligible Approved Expense Snapshot and Phase 20 has separately
certified the closed occurrence discriminator and accountant-confirmed posting
recipe. Phase 20 receives only the minimum immutable result and lineage; it never
receives raw routes or GPS, Source Packages, cumulative-capacity internals,
tenant applicability decisions, tax treatment, or claimant workflow, and it
never calculates an allowance. Raw receipts, AI suggestions, and report status
are not copied into Phase 20; a purpose-scoped destination attachment requires a
separate policy and projection. Provider identifiers, mutable balances, inferred
payment, aggregate `Paid` authority, and warning-only duplicate re-export cannot
enter source truth.

Phase 21 D13 keeps Expense Program Activation Versions, Expense Policy Cohorts
and Membership Versions, Expense Governance Profile Versions and Assignments,
Expense Governance Resolutions, Expense Approval Route Versions, Approval
Assignment Snapshots, Expense Review Actions, Reviewer Exceptions, reviewer
identities, private Receipt Evidence, and mutable review work in Phase 21.
Phase 20 receives only the minimum PII-safe frozen Approved Expense Snapshot
and final policy-decision lineage required to prove the Accounting-Ready
Expense Handoff; it never copies or executes the governance or review workflow.

Phase 21 D14 keeps Organization Card Sources, Organization Card Import Profile
Versions, Organization Card Activity File Assets and Import Manifests, row
provenance, Organization Card Assignment Versions, Organization Card Evidence
Coverage, possible-overlap decisions, personal/non-business and unresolved
coverage, import exceptions, and private claimant tasks in Phase 21. Phase 20
may receive only a source-final posted organization-card occurrence whose exact
business coverage is frozen in an eligible Approved Expense Snapshot and
projected as the existing **Cleared Organization-Paid Expense** occurrence. A
pending authorization, file acceptance, row import, source assignment, receipt
attachment, fuzzy match, personal portion, refund/dispute evidence without its
typed source contract, issuer settlement, or card-liability payment cannot
create or advance an Accounting-Ready Expense Handoff.

Phase 21 D15 keeps Reimbursement Handoff Packages, Reimbursement Delivery
Profile Versions, Reimbursement Execution Claims, Reimbursement Handoff
Coverage, Handoff Attestations, Reimbursement Handoff Operations, provider
draft/input acceptance and readback, operation ambiguity, and external-release
workflow in Phase 21. Those records prove only their exact package, access,
execution-ownership, handoff, or provider-operation facts. They cannot create
an External Payment Occurrence, Reimbursement Payment Coverage, Accounting
Posting Intent, or Accounting Release. Only an independently eligible Approved
Reimbursement Obligation or separately source-qualified External Payment
Occurrence may cross D18 through the Accounting-Ready Expense Handoff.
QBO/Xero Accounting objects remain Phase 20-only and cannot be substituted for
a D15 accounts-payable draft/input lane.

An Execution Claim may reference an already-applicable Phase 20 D17 source-family
posting-ownership contract but cannot assign or infer the posting owner of a
future payment. Phase 20 D17 assigns one owner when that source or atomic payment
occurrence exists. Provider draft readback proves only handoff-operation
outcome. A staff Handoff Attestation is not staff payment evidence, and
staff-attested payment evidence remains explicitly **Payment recorded by
finance** rather than silently upgraded to provider-confirmed payment.
Phase 21 D16 supplies the separately certified advance and claimant-repayment
source boundary. Only an evidence-qualified **Expense Advance Issuance
Occurrence**, a separately certified **Expense Advance Application** typed
accounting effect where applicable, a source-qualified **Claimant Repayment
Occurrence** whose immutable `return_family` is exactly
`cash_claimant_return` or `expense_advance_return`, and their cause-linked
corrections may cross. The two return families remain distinct typed
occurrences; neither is a negative ordinary expense or a mutable claimant
balance.

Expense Advance Policy Version, Claimant Repayment Policy Version, Expense
Advance Authorization Version, Expense Settlement Determination,
Repayment Subject Determination, Claimant Repayment Decision, uncertified
Claimant Repayment Requirement, Advance Residual Position, tasks, raw Advance
Evidence Observation or Repayment Evidence Observation, disputes, Repayment
Restitution Review, and Field Account Funding Coverage remain Phase 21 truth
and fail closed as accounting sources. A Claimant Repayment Requirement remains
accounting-dark unless a separately accountant-certified policy and source
contract recognizes the exact receivable. Such receivable recognition still
does not prove a return, payment, settlement, or reconciliation.

Every admitted D16 family receives its own independently applicable Phase 20 D17 posting
owner. It cannot inherit posting ownership from an authorization, policy,
requirement, Field Account effect, handoff package, or related source family.

Phase 21 D22 keeps every optional posture, Prospective Expense Request Version,
private plan-evidence reference, Prospective Expense Governance Resolution,
operation-scoped Approval Assignment Snapshot, Expense Review Action,
Prospective Expense Authorization Decision, compatible capacity reservation,
later-claim Authorization Coverage, unused-scope declaration, residual,
successor, and correction in Phase 21. Missing required prior authorization is
a typed D13 source exception, not a Phase 20 accounting exception. Exact D22
coverage may remain source-domain lineage on a later approved expense, but it
cannot determine posting, establish incurrence or substantiation, replace the
actual D10/D13 approval, or create a handoff source. D22 is also distinct from
D16 Expense Advance Authorization: neither authorization family proves issue,
expense, obligation, payment, or accounting.

Phase 21 D23 keeps every Expense Field Account Effect Recognition Profile,
Expense Field Account Effect Basis, Field Account Funding Coverage and
Disposition, Expense Field Account Effect Coverage, operational Expense Field
Account Effect, Support Cycle inclusion/through date, qualification exception,
and cause-linked correction in Phase 21. These objects govern only operational
support-balance inclusion timing. They cannot create, select, date, modify,
release, redeliver, or reconcile an Accounting Posting Intent, Accounting
Release, Delivery Package, provider operation, Expected Bank Arrival, Bank
Match, or QBO/Xero object. An independently certified Approved Expense
Snapshot, Reimbursement Obligation, source-final organization-paid occurrence,
External Payment Occurrence, or cause-linked correction may still qualify
through its own closed D18 discriminator and D17 posting owner; it does not
inherit D23 mode, profile, effect identity, close, or accounting date. QBO/Xero
bills, payments, home amounts, readback, drift, and reconciliation cannot
qualify or rewrite D23 truth. Minimum necessary D23 lineage may be retained as
non-authoritative operational provenance only. D23 Effect Coverage and Phase
20 Source Coverage occupy separate purpose namespaces; neither consumes,
fulfills, releases, dates, or gates the other, and the D23 operational effect
cannot become a second accounting source for the same economic occurrence.

Phase 21 D24 keeps every Expense Collaboration Assignment Version, invitation,
accepted helper principal, Evidence Access Projection Version, Claimant
Confirmation or admitted external attestation, helper action, and exact actor-
provenance record in Phase 21. These objects describe bounded collaboration on
one claim; they neither replace Phase 12 authorization nor prove incurrence,
substantiation, approval, obligation, payment, Field Account effect, posting,
or reconciliation. Phase 20 rejects the complete D24 family before D18 source
admission. Only an independently qualified D10/D13 Approved Expense Snapshot,
Reimbursement Obligation, source-final organization-paid occurrence, External
Payment Occurrence, or cause-linked correction may enter its existing closed
lane. Minimum necessary D24 provenance may accompany that source only as
non-authoritative lineage. Phase 20, QBO, and Xero cannot grant, revoke,
expand, satisfy, or rewrite D24 assignment, consent, evidence-access, or helper
authority.

Phase 21 D25 keeps every Expense Claim Resolution Cause Contract Version,
Case, Occurrence, Projection, Downstream Impact Manifest and disposition,
task/message/response, source-owner request, and case-completion proof in
Phase 21 coordination truth. Phase 20 rejects the complete D25 family before
D18 source admission: none creates an expense source, posting owner, Accounting
Posting Intent, Accounting Release, Delivery Package, provider operation,
Expected Bank Arrival, Bank Match, or reconciliation evidence.

If an independently authoritative source correction qualifies, only that
source enters its existing closed lane. Phase 20 alone appends any Compensating
Accounting Release, selects an accountant-permitted period and treatment,
delivers and reads back the provider result, and reports drift. D25 may retain
only opaque correlation and observe that result. A D25 **Complete** label,
Mission Control task, message delivery, silence, or timeout cannot complete an
Accounting Exception Case or prove accounting delivery, provider acceptance,
posting, or reconciliation.

The handoff carries source dates, source currencies, source FX evidence, and
approved expense line-disposition coverage. D4 and D11—not Phase 21—derive
accounting-effective dates, functional or accounting currency, and the
Canonical Accounting Effect. D4's Source Coverage Manifest is separately
derived and must reconcile to the handoff. D18 does not extend D10 Bank Match
to outbound reimbursement disbursements; that would require a separate
decision.
