# Workload-shaped certified accounting capacity

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 20 grill session — D15)

Phase 20 admits direct QuickBooks Online or Xero delivery by evaluating the
exact immutable **Provider Delivery Plan**, not by applying one source-item cap
or promising unlimited best-effort queueing. Existing destination Capability
Certificates prove current organization-specific capabilities; one
product-owned, versioned **Certified Execution Envelope** proves the operation,
line, byte, batch, latency, readback, and recovery shapes Asym has actually
tested for an exact provider contract, adapter, recipe, and environment. Live
quota, provider health, commercial headroom, and tenant-fair queue position
remain volatile observations rather than certificate truth.

Work inside the certified structural envelope may be queued under
provider-native, tenant-fair backpressure and receives a calibrated,
confidence-aware completion window. Work outside it cannot enter direct
delivery until evidence prospectively expands the envelope or staff make a
permitted pre-release choice through the authority that owns the Posting
Profile, grouping, or staff-mediated lane. A frozen release never changes
grain, destination, or delivery lane to fit capacity. New writes cannot consume
the scheduler budget protected for exact readback and ambiguous-outcome
recovery, and no provider HTTP success, batch response, or timing estimate
replaces operation-level provider evidence.

The ordinary experience extends the existing Release Horizon with one quiet
readiness state, truthful comparable-operation counts, range-based timing,
background execution, and exception-only notifications. Tenants control their
bounded accounting representation, D12 cadence, and permitted pre-freeze
delivery choice; they do not configure provider rate limits, queue priority,
concurrency, or safety overrides.
