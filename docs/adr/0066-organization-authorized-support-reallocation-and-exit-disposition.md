# Organization-authorized support reallocation and exit disposition

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 21 grill session — D5)

Phase 21 uses one bounded Support Reallocation Case to coordinate a nonbinding
worker preference, prospective tenant policy, exact accepted-source purpose and
Field Account capacity coverage, a conflict-aware organization Decision, and
independently authoritative financial, lifecycle, payment, accounting, and
communication outcomes. The case is not a mutable financial aggregate and
never makes support worker-owned.

An internal reallocation is an immutable same-Tenant, same-Legal-Entity,
same-currency balanced Field Account occurrence. The Decision and every side of
the pair append atomically against exact reviewed versions, and the complete
pair advances both Finance-confirmed balances only through one later Support
Cycle Close. Cross-entity, cross-currency, personal, and external destinations
are not internal reallocations. Earlier Phase 21 wording that says `internal
transfer` means only this D5-qualified internal Support Reallocation occurrence
and grants no worker execution authority.

External charitable succession remains covered until an exactly identified,
jurisdiction-appropriate Handoff and a matching authoritative Result are
proved. Payment evidence alone is insufficient. Its qualified Field Account
effect is one atomic balanced occurrence: the source debit and an exact typed
organization-control/disposition counter-entry enter the same governed close.
That counter-entry is not a recipient Field Account or general-ledger truth,
and a one-sided external debit cannot qualify.

A close-covered qualified internal or external occurrence is the sole eligible
future Phase 20 source root. The current Phase 20 generation deliberately keeps
this source family unsupported and accounting-dark until a separately approved
Phase 20 change certifies its schema, accountant semantics, Posting Profile
recipe, and Posting Ownership Cutover behavior. Phase 20 remains the sole
QBO/Xero accounting doorway; generic journal and artifact fallbacks are
prohibited.

Exit disposition begins only from an exact Worker Lifecycle Authority
Reference. One conserving Exit Disposition Manifest preserves every
purpose-and-currency amount, independently live obligation, and future writer;
source domains own their retirement or succession before read-only closure.
Late facts use cause-linked append-only recovery. Phase 21 does not erase donor
purpose, permit self-dealing approval, silently claw back a destination, move
money, issue donor refunds, write QBO/Xero Accounting directly, or provide a
generic workflow engine. A separately certified regional Xero Payroll
draft-input operation under D7 is a different capability and cannot carry a
Support Reallocation occurrence.

**Phase 21 D23 precision amendment (2026-08-01).** An exit or Support
Assignment/Field Account retirement inventories every D23 reservation,
qualified candidate, closed effect, correction, and unresolved slice exactly
once. The boundary stops new forward admission without suppressing source-owned
corrections against original immutable scope. D23 cannot auto-reallocate to
solve a shortfall or invoke D5 without a separately authorized purpose-
compatible Case.
