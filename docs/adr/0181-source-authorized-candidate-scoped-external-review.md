# ADR-0181: Source-authorized candidate-scoped external review

**Status:** Accepted (founder rulings, Phase 24 Grill session — D25–D26,
D30–D40, 2026-08-29)

## Context

Some exact protected Site, Page, Navigation, publication, or default-change
candidates require a human reviewer who did not materially participate. A
one-person ministry—or a small team in which every qualified person helped
prepare the candidate—can therefore have no internal reviewer. Requiring a
permanent Tenant member merely to review one candidate creates excess standing
access; permitting the same human or an administrator to bypass review destroys
the independence guarantee.

Phase 17 already accepts one source-specific candidate-scoped delegated review
pattern. Phase 24 D25 makes the cross-source boundary explicit and preserves
staff flexibility without turning that pattern into a generic guest, approval,
or workflow product.

D25 establishes the safe external-review capability for the no-internal-
reviewer dead end, but availability remains an independent Tenant governance
choice. Some organizations prohibit all external disclosure; others need only
the recovery path; and others deliberately use qualified outside language,
legal, accessibility, board, or denominational reviewers even when an internal
reviewer is eligible. Capability answers who may act. Availability policy
answers whether the organization permits that external action to be offered.

D25 also admitted source-owned **Request changes** with “structured,
non-public feedback” but did not define the minimum body, source reference,
privacy boundary, or reviewer/staff interaction. Without a precise rule, one
source could accept a bare result while another grows a mutable comment or task
system. D30 closes that ambiguity without expanding the external grant.

## Decision

Core permits **source-authorized candidate-scoped external review**. Every
applicable source must affirmatively allow external review and provide a
complete, purpose-limited review projection for the exact immutable candidate.
One nonmember human may hold one active invitation or accepted review grant for
that candidate at a time.

The authority boundaries are fixed:

- Phase 4 owns the authority-free invitation, intended-human binding,
  credential, issuance, expiry, replacement, revocation, deliberate redemption,
  and invitation audit.
- Phase 12 owns a distinct **Candidate Review Authorization Context**. It is
  created only after verified acceptance and authorizes only the exact review
  projection and source command. It is not an Active Tenant Assignment,
  membership, role, group, internal review route, or reusable capability.
- Each consequence-owning source owns delegation admissibility, the immutable
  candidate and participant lineage, the minimum sufficient external review
  projection, review eligibility, the final decision, and any explicitly
  combined publication or activation effect.
- Phase 17 and Phase 6 own governed invitation presentation, dispatch, provider
  outcome, and communication history. Delivery never proves acceptance,
  authority, review, or public effect.
- CRM/Party identity owns the stable human. A private Tenant **Saved reviewer
  contact** may reference that person to make future selection faster, but it
  grants no access, qualification, eligibility, route membership, or assurance.

Every candidate requires a fresh invitation, acceptance, D23/D24 independence
proof, exact grant, current assurance, and source command. The delegate cannot
edit, export, download through a product feature, browse unrelated records,
re-delegate, change routes, review a successor, or access Giving or financial
truth. Candidate, dependency, participant, identity, policy, projection,
authorization, revocation, expiry, or source-admissibility changes end favorable
access immediately.

Staff may save a reviewer contact, send again without extending authority,
cancel, revoke, replace the active reviewer immediately, or invite the same
person for a successor after reviewing the new impact. These actions preserve
source work and immutable history. They never create recurring reminders,
automatic disclosure, standing external access, or a reusable approval.

The invitation and every request are Tenant-, environment-, Site-, source-,
candidate-, participant-digest-, projection-, human-, assurance-, expiry-, and
revocation-bound. Pending, failed-delivery, declined, expired, canceled,
revoked, replaced, superseded, ambiguous, or indeterminate invitations grant
nothing. The final command re-proves all current facts and expected heads in one
short compare-and-swap transaction. Failure leaves the current public Site
unchanged.

D25 establishes the safe capability without making it universally available
when an eligible internal reviewer exists. D26 cannot weaken that capability's
scope, identity, projection, or lifecycle boundary.

Phase 24 D26 resolves that availability question with one closed, versioned
**Website external-review availability posture**. A Tenant chooses exactly one
of:

- `external_review_prohibited` — external review is unavailable even when no
  internal reviewer remains;
- `recovery_only` — the platform and migration default, offering D25 only after
  a complete current proof of zero eligible internal reviewers; or
- `source_permitted_choice` — authorized staff may deliberately choose D25 even
  while an eligible internal reviewer exists.

A Site inherits the current Tenant posture or explicitly narrows it to
`recovery_only` or `external_review_prohibited`; it can never widen the Tenant
ceiling. Every source prohibition, qualification, D23/D24 independence rule,
D25 identity/projection boundary, and indeterminate proof remains stricter and
wins. Saving the posture creates no invitation, notification, reviewer choice,
grant, route, review, publication, or public effect.

The posture is current authorization truth. Narrowing advances an immutable
policy head and epoch that immediately denies every pending or accepted D25
path that no longer qualifies; durable idempotent reconciliation records the
ended lifecycle afterward. Widening requires fresh current evaluation and
never resurrects an expired, canceled, replaced, revoked, superseded, ended, or
completed invitation, credential, grant, session, or link. Completed review
history remains immutable.

A source-admitted external `changes_requested` result requires exactly one
private Unicode plain-text **Request-changes explanation** answering **What
needs to change?** After versioned line-ending normalization, NFC, and trimming
of surrounding whitespace, it contains 1–1,000 Unicode code points. It may also
contain zero or one **Review source anchor** selected from the exact immutable
review projection. The anchor is a source-issued opaque identity bound to the
Tenant, environment, Site, source, candidate, review epoch, adapter version,
and projection digest; it is never caller text, a URL, DOM selector, array
position, copied content, permission, or inferred successor location.

The reviewer enters the explanation in a responsive in-flow Base Maia decision
panel that keeps the candidate evidence available. Core allows overtyping and
editing down, never silently truncates, retains an unsent draft only in page
memory, and gives one consequence-led final action rather than another
confirmation. The explanation is original-language untrusted data: it is
escaped as inert plain text with automatic direction, is not machine
translated, auto-linked, semantically scored, or blocked by brittle secret/
payment heuristics, and never becomes rich text, a comment, task, checklist,
attachment, mention, assignment, due date, or conversation.

One expected-head source command re-proves every D23–D27 and D25-context fact
and atomically appends the terminal result, protected readable-body reference,
optional verified anchor, immutable receipt, source/lane transition, trusted
attribution, and authorization-context end. Same-key/same-canonical-meaning
retry returns the receipt; changed meaning rejects; stale and concurrent losers
write nothing. The result never enters D28 reassignment. The current candidate,
Live Website, and Giving remain unchanged; any corrected successor is a new
candidate requiring fresh review.

Authorized staff read the immutable original explanation only on the exact
source repair/history surface and may open an anchor only in the retained
original projection under current authorization. The body never appears in a
notification/email preview, log, trace, metric, analytics property, cache key,
search/AI index, generic export, CMS field, task, public surface, Giving, or
finance. It follows the source's purpose-specific records schedule; body-free
result/audit history remains distinct. A narrowly authorized, audited privacy/
security command may quarantine and dispose of unsafe text without rewriting
the result, actor, protected keyed fingerprint, or history. Raw or unsalted
body hashes are forbidden and fingerprints never enter telemetry.

A `changes_requested` result also opens one source-owned **Correction attention
episode** while one or more registered correction actions remain required. A
finite, versioned source contract declares typed next actions, responsibility,
current action and visibility authorization, safe list facts, protected detail,
destination, source end, retention, recipient ceiling, and projection policy.
It never derives action or routing from the explanation, anchor, historical
author, capability alone, AI, task state, or workflow-provider state.

Only a complete bounded set of people who are both currently responsible and
currently authorized receives recipient-specific attention or future Tasks Hub
assignment projections. Proved zero guesses nobody; partial, indeterminate,
stale, contradictory, timed-out, or over-ceiling resolution releases nobody.
The source remains independently discoverable to authorized staff.

Notification and source-backed task presentations are separate subordinate
projections over the same source-work identity. Reading clears only personal
unread. Assignment grants no access, protected feedback remains referenced at
the source rather than copied, and no task, notification, timer, worker, or
provider state can clear source work. Source end makes the projection
inapplicable; ADR-0183 governs the shared Tasks Hub boundary.

D32 makes Website correction tasks source-controlled at the narrowest exact
source-action scope the Website source can prove. Tasks Hub exposes no generic
Complete, reopen, dismiss, delete, bulk, shortcut, AI, support, or worker
completion transition. A recipient's presentation closes when their final
assigned source-action predicate ends, even if another action or recipient
keeps the correction episode active. The authoritative source receipt and typed
reason alone distinguish **Completed in Website**, **No longer required**, and
**Reassigned**; access loss is never completion. A future Website human
follow-up may be independently completable only when it is a separately
defined deliverable rather than the Website correction action itself.

D33 makes correction-responsibility recovery an operational Website source
transition rather than a Tasks Hub or Payload assignee edit. **This needs
someone else** may hand selected exact correction scopes to one currently
source-eligible named person, or return them without naming a successor. The
source re-proves the actor, target, current authorization/policy/source heads,
and exact scope at commit and appends one complete immutable successor
generation and receipt. Continuing recipients keep engagement; new recipients
alone receive fresh projection. A no-successor return is **Returned** while
another responsible person remains and becomes **Returned for reassignment**
plus source **Needs assignment** only when nobody remains. Protected D30
explanation/anchor content is never copied into the picker, task, transition,
event, workflow payload, notification, or audit text.

D34 adds one **Return recovery context** only when the recipient deliberately
returns exact correction scopes and the authoritative post-state creates
**Needs assignment**. Named handoff and a return that preserves another
responsible recipient collect none. Website v1 accepts exactly one unselected
code—`responsibility_mismatch`, `cannot_take_current_work`, or `other`—with no
prose, note, default, Tenant customization, or copied D30 explanation/anchor.

The code records only the actor's selection and may change only permission-safe
guidance on the authorized Website recovery surface. It never proves or changes
cause, source action, eligibility, permission, destination, route, scope,
priority, time, notification, completion, publication, Giving, finance, or
personnel meaning. The operational source atomically owns the code/version,
exact gap scopes, transition receipt, purpose-specific visibility, and records
schedule. Tasks Hub references but never copies or interprets it; future
sources do not inherit Website labels by convention.

D35 makes the source-owned **Needs assignment lane** the complete zero-setup
recovery surface and permits one optional Tenant policy naming one to three
Website work-recovery coordinators. The policy has only
`source_lane_only@1` or one-to-three-member named mode and no Site override.
Current exact Site/source visibility and **Assign returned Website work**
authority narrow recipients at each occurrence; membership grants nothing,
and any incomplete/indeterminate proof releases nobody. This route is separate
from D21 review responsibility and D29 Review coordinators.

Personal coordinator work is a recipient-specific projection of one shared
source-backed recovery-task identity. It assigns a correction owner only
through the D33 expected-head source command and cannot itself edit, publish,
complete, dismiss, claim, or satisfy correction work. The source assignment/
end receipt alone clears applicable lane/task projections. D30/D34 protected
detail remains reference-only, and D35 creates no second bell item, email,
reminder, target date, SLA, escalation, or cross-source/Mobilize policy.

D36 makes an ordinary D35 policy save prospective from one trusted cutover and
changes current Needs-assignment coordinator routing only through a separately
authorized **Update current work** application. Preview is fresh,
permission-safe, non-authoritative, and recomputed before acceptance. One
accepted product-owned application has normalized occurrence members/results;
every occurrence re-proves source/policy/routing/authorization and appends one
atomic differential routing successor plus identifier-only projection intent.

Continuing recipients preserve assignment/engagement and read state; newly
admitted recipients alone get fresh unread; removed recipients end as
**Coordinator responsibility changed** without fabricated completion/read/
source/access meaning. Lane-only ends personal assignments while source Needs
assignment remains. Policy supersession stops uncommitted old-target members,
committed history is corrected only by a successor explicit application, and
Tasks Hub/Inngest never own the cohort, routing, result, or source truth. D37
now fixes that boundary: one application covers the complete compatible
pre-cutover Tenant cohort proved from authoritative Website occurrence heads
and a closed producer/version catalog. Unknown or incomplete census state
blocks acceptance; actor visibility, Sites, filters, task rows, recipient
qualification, and client IDs never narrow membership.

D37 uses a separate Tenant-wide Website recovery current-work application
capability. It authorizes the exact action and minimum complete aggregate
item/assignment consequences required for informed consent, but grants no
source detail, Site visibility, policy edit, coordinator qualification,
correction assignment, task administration, or other action. Prepared evidence
has no effect; one atomic seal binds normalized immutable membership before
D36's per-occurrence execution. No visible-subset mode, Site exception, hidden
count split, or unreviewed consequence widening is allowed.

D38 makes that application capability `explicit_only` in Phase 12 and permits
zero effective holders. It is seeded nowhere and never implied by Owner/Admin/
staff/Web Studio/policy editing/Site/source/coordinator/task/support state.
Only a current same-Tenant `permissions.manage_grants` holder within live
delegation scope/ceiling may deliberately grant or revoke it; possessing the
application capability is not grant authority. Human grants bind the exact
Active Tenant Assignment, and current EffectiveAccess deduplicates and explains
every surviving path.

Zero holders leaves prospective policy and the Needs assignment lane complete
and creates no warning, task, unread, email, reminder, or fallback holder.
Every source mutation advances the authorization epoch, but revocation,
expiry, assignment end, suspension, or applicable delegation end fences later
uncommitted D37 effects only when final post-change EffectiveAccess no longer
contains D38. Committed source history remains immutable, and regrant requires
a fresh application. The product-backed Phase 12 People & access/My Access
experience—not the seed-backed Teams demo or a Website roster—owns grant/
revoke UX.

D39 permits two central sources through that one model: a typed direct
assignment-capability grant to an exact Active Tenant Assignment, and a
capability grant to a governed flat **Access group** whose current memberships
also bind exact Active Tenant Assignments. Direct serves a specific person or
exception; a group serves a stable job function. Both are additive,
deduplicated, independently expiring/revocable provenance paths; neither is
seeded, inferred, required, or copied into per-member grants.

A D38-bearing Access group is protected authorization state, not an ordinary
Team. Attaching D38 requires `permissions.manage_grants` within the actor's live
assignable-capability ceiling. Adding or activating a member separately
requires `permissions.manage_membership` with exact group scope and a live
protected-group administration ceiling covering the complete current bundle
and bundle revision. Self-add, nested/dynamic/external/cross-Tenant/nonstaff
membership, label authority, current Teams reuse, and asynchronous
authorization are forbidden. Final post-change EffectiveAccess—not the source
row changed—determines whether D37 is fenced.

D40 permits one deliberate **separate direct grant** when an exact Active
Tenant Assignment currently receives D38 only through one or more group paths.
The secondary person-access flow shows every current group source first and
states that current abilities will not change but the direct source will
survive group loss. It requires a fresh privacy-safe reason and an explicit
unpreselected independent duration. It reuses the typed direct-grant relation,
records immutable overlap-creation provenance, and creates no new authorization
kind or resolver.

Review and commit bind the complete current group-source set and all relevant
heads, re-prove grant authority/ceiling and self/SoD/quorum rules, and require
no current direct source. Any relevant source or authority change conflicts;
the command never falls through into an ordinary restorative grant. Grant,
basis, audit, receipt, and one epoch advance commit atomically even though the
current capability set is unchanged. Later group loss does not terminate the
independent direct grant, and only final EffectiveAccess loss fences D37.

## Consequences

- Phase 12 must add the Candidate Review Authorization Context to its closed
  authorization-context union before implementation.
- Page, Navigation, Communications, and Site/default sources require explicit
  external-review adapter contracts; one unsupported or indeterminate source
  blocks the combined external review.
- External reviewers use a focused authenticated, no-store review surface—not
  Mission Control membership or the internal D19–D22 attention route.
- Saved reviewer contacts remain reference-only convenience data. Contact
  removal and active invitation revocation are separate explicit operations.
- Invitation, delivery, acceptance, projection access, review decision, and
  public effect remain distinct lifecycle facts and audit records.
- Existing Live content remains active while external review is pending or
  unavailable. D25 creates no Giving, Legal Entity, Stripe, settlement, bank,
  currency, contribution, receipt, ledger, or accounting authority.
- Tenant and Site posture management require separate registry-owned Website-
  policy capabilities, current trusted scope, fresh assurance, expected-head
  compare-and-swap, immutable history, permission-safe impact previews, and
  server-derived actor attribution. Broad administrator roles are not a
  substitute.
- Settings use three explicit radio choices with recovery-only visibly marked
  recommended, an explicit save, and distinct configured/effective/inherited
  labels. They do not use an ambiguous sharing switch or a custom policy
  builder.
- New, cloned, imported, or transferred Sites inherit the destination Tenant;
  they do not copy another Site's override or active external-review path.
- Every source that admits `changes_requested` must implement the same
  explanation contract and must either expose one bounded source-anchor
  manifest or explicitly support no anchor; a generic comments/tasks table is
  not a compatibility layer.
- The 1,000-code-point ceiling is a versioned Core product default. A named
  limit-pressure monitor and representative original-language research govern
  any increase; Core never teaches abbreviation or adds attachments/email as a
  workaround.
- D30 remains Reserved until its source transitions, protected body lifecycle,
  capabilities, database constraints, grants/RLS, mixed-version rollout,
  accessibility, localization, security, concurrency, and release evidence are
  implemented and proved.
- D31 remains Reserved until its finite source-work contract, exact action and
  responsibility resolvers, current authorization, shared Tasks Hub and Phase
  17 projection boundaries, durable idempotency, tenant-safe data model,
  accessibility, reconciliation, monitors, and release evidence are implemented
  and proved. It creates no CMS-specific task system.
- D33 remains Reserved until its source responsibility operations, exact action
  scopes, candidate resolver, Needs-assignment recovery, immutable generations/
  receipts, current authorization, RLS/grants, accessibility, migration,
  reconciliation, monitors, and release evidence are implemented and proved.
- D35 remains Reserved until its source lane, Tenant-only bounded policy,
  distinct route purpose, exact authorization resolver, shared-task/personal-
  engagement projection, generic-mutation denials, source assignment/end,
  privacy, accessibility, migration, reconciliation, monitors, and release
  evidence are implemented and proved. D36 must govern any effect on current
  Needs-assignment work before coordinator policy writers activate.
- D36–D40 remain Reserved until the protected direct/group assignment and
  continuity-source model,
  prospective cutover, closed source catalog, authoritative census, prepared
  evidence/atomic seal, preview/application commands, normalized member/result
  model, RLS/grants, differential engagement, per-occurrence atomicity,
  idempotency, privacy, accessibility, migration, reconciliation, monitors, and
  release evidence are implemented and proved.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Internal staff only:** rejected as the sole path because it deadlocks
  genuinely small ministries or pressures them to grant unnecessary permanent
  access.
- **Standing external reviewer role, pool, or guest membership:** rejected
  because contact reuse would become durable authority and unrelated-data
  exposure.
- **Several simultaneous external invitees by copying the D22 internal-route
  cap:** rejected because D22 governs attention, not external disclosure, and
  Phase 17's accepted precedent is one candidate-scoped delegate.
- **Administrator or same-human exception:** rejected because it invalidates
  D23/D24 independence. Emergency recovery may restore a compatible previously
  reviewed safe baseline, never approve novel protected work.
- **Only recovery-only and optional states:** rejected because capability
  removal is not a transparent, durable Tenant prohibition and a role change
  could silently alter disclosure policy.
- **Always offer source-permitted external review:** rejected because it makes
  a privacy-sensitive option available without Tenant opt-in.
- **Prospective-only narrowing or dormant reactivation:** rejected because the
  displayed current policy would not govern current access and a later widening
  could silently revive old credentials.
- **Source-, locale-, reviewer-, Page-, or domain-specific policy builder:**
  rejected because source owners already control admissibility and the extra
  rules would create contradictory state and brittle administration.
- **Optional explanation:** rejected for `changes_requested` because a terminal
  “repair this candidate” result without repair context is semantically
  incomplete; optional notes remain valid for other source outcomes whose
  contracts admit them.
- **Required checklist, several anchored comments, or rich feedback editor:**
  rejected because no stable cross-source taxonomy or collaboration need is
  proven and those models add disproportionate lifecycle, privacy,
  authorization, accessibility, migration, and operational burden.
