# Rail-qualified support-cycle admission without worker-availability claims

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 21 grill session — D2)

Phase 21 creates provisional monetary support activity only from exact source-
posted Phase 13 money-designation coverage or one exact source-final D21
Noncash Support Realization Manifest. An original noncash posting, FMV,
recognized value, appraisal, estimate, or uncovered disposition remains
activity only and is structurally ineligible for a monetary Support Allocation
Candidate. One prospective, tenant-owned **Support Allocation Readiness Policy**
then decides which positive allocations have the rail-specific evidence
required for a Support Cycle close. The guided policy uses exact provider
settlement evidence for Stripe, deposit or direct-credit evidence for offline
money, and the capability-certified source finality frozen by D21 for a noncash
realization. Bounded source-family settings may recognize a posted money gift
earlier or require bank evidence later, but cannot weaken D21's exact manifest
requirements. Known refunds, returns, disputes, voids, redesignations, and
corrections cannot be deferred to preserve a larger balance; negative effects
append promptly, and both sides of a redesignation or internal transfer enter
together. Phase 21 D17's one-time Field Account
Operational Cutover alone establishes the initial Finance-confirmed Field
Account Balance. Thereafter, only immutable close coverage advances ordinary
positive activity; mandatory adverse corrections remain continuous.

Readiness is not worker availability, spendability, compensation, ownership,
or permission to withdraw. The organization retains those decisions. Exact
provider terms such as Stripe Balance Transaction `available` remain
finance-only evidence and do not become missionary-facing language. Internally,
the disposable evaluation is **Support Close Readiness**; only immutable close
coverage has balance authority.

Missionaries receive one privacy-filtered **Missionary Support Activity
Projection** that feels like a small, calm CRM without creating a second donor
CRM. D19 scopes each projection to one exact Tenant, Legal Entity, Support
Assignment, purpose, and currently authorized Principal/Active Tenant
Assignment; a Party's several Support Assignments are never combined. It
composes Phase 13 effective contribution facts, Phase 14's
`getSupporterRoster`, Phase 16's safe recurring-support statements, and the
Phase 3/10/12 projection and authorization floor. Ordinary recorded gifts need
no status badge. Material corrections and reversals remain visible. An
accessible optional disclosure uses plain source language such as `Recorded`,
`Processing`, `Received`, `Not received`, `Returned`, `Refunded`, `Reversed`, or
`Corrected`; an exact `Declined` outcome may appear only when the source proves
it, never with a private provider reason. The projection never exposes raw
provider, deposit, payout, Bank Match, close-readiness, or accounting state.
When D21 applies, one quiet grouped lifecycle may relate the original noncash
gift to its later finance-closed realized support. It never creates or displays
a second gift, and it never presents valuation, disposition costs, or proceeds
as worker-available or paid.

Phase 21 does not own supporter contact fields, donor CRUD, notes, tasks,
appeals, newsletters, or coaching. Phase 14 remains the roster authority and
Phase 28 owns the support-raising CRM. Restricted and anonymous facts are
filtered before counts, totals, search, pagination, notifications, exports,
and caching.

This design rejects a universal settlement flag, a QBO/Xero prerequisite, a
rules DSL, mutable retroactive policy, half-admitted balanced movements,
deferrable adverse corrections, a Phase 21 donor/contact CRM, and any
missionary-facing claim that provider settlement makes funds available to the
worker. It also rejects valuation-as-support and any attempt to admit a noncash
source without the exact D21 realization manifest.
