# ADR-0098: Optional approved Support Plans and bounded workspace publication

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 21 grill session — D9)

Phase 21 defaults each Tenant, Legal Entity, Support Assignment,
charitable-purpose, ISO-currency, and applicable Field-Account scope to
**Support planning not managed in Asym**. A tenant may prospectively activate
one winning immutable **Approved Support Plan Version** containing bounded
recurring and dated organization-approved needs plus one optional
same-currency diagnostic reserve target. The Plan creates no gift, restriction,
balance, compensation, reimbursement, payroll, accounting, payment, or public
fundraising effect. Phase 13 and Phase 21 activity, Phase 16 commitments, D1
Finance-confirmed Field Account Balances, D4 compensation funding, and Phase 28
Support-Raising Goals remain independently authoritative.

One finite, prospective, tenant-owned **Support Workspace Publication Profile
Version** selects only independently authorized, source-backed modules for each
audience. Phase 12 capabilities govern who may administer it; job titles are
not hardcoded. The Profile controls presentation only and cannot create truth,
change a calculation, widen authorization, or substitute one source for
another. `Not managed`, `not configured`, `hidden`, `unauthorized`, `stale`,
`temporarily unavailable`, `no source data`, and an authoritative zero remain
different states. D1 and D6 finance truth continues whether or not a tenant
publishes balances to missionaries; a hidden balance and its balance-derived
projections are not queried, calculated, cached, exported, alerted on, or
otherwise leaked for that audience by default. Personal reorder and collapse
preferences remain harmless presentation state.

Phase 21 D17 adds one off-by-default **Imported reference history** presentation
option inside this same profile. It may expose only privacy-filtered,
source-labelled, structurally inert, bounded reference rows that the current
audience is independently authorized to see. It has no balance, Support
Activity, notification, document, external-feed, accounting, payroll/AP,
reimbursement, or payment authority. When the option is off, reference history
is not queried, cached, counted, exported, or leaked to that audience. Enabling
it never replays historical events or downstream effects.

**Balance Coverage**, **Reserve Position**, and any optional **Commitment
Forecast** are disposable, purpose-separated, source-versioned projections.
The Finance-confirmed Planning Coverage Base begins with the D1
Finance-confirmed Field Account Balance, subtracts qualified negative
open-cycle Field Account effects not yet in that close, active non-reusable
Field Account Funding Coverage, and active Support Reallocation Coverage not
yet replaced by its posted debit, and never adds provisional positive support.
Each amount is subtracted exactly once. Balance-derived projections additionally
require a compatible winning Plan, a positive approved recurring need, and the
same ISO currency. Missing prerequisites produce an absent or `Not calculated`
projection, never zero, infinity, an inferred value, or a converted aggregate.
Phase 16 commitments never increase the Finance-confirmed balance or its
coverage. Every displayed result preserves exact source versions, currency,
effective and through dates, and non-availability language.

Reserve Position has one signed meaning:

```text
Finance-confirmed Planning Coverage Base
− reserve_target_minor_units
= Reserve Position
```

A positive result means above target; a negative result means shortfall. It
does not reserve money or authorize spending, compensation, reimbursement, or
reallocation.

A Phase 28 fundraising goal may be created from a Plan only through an explicit
copy-with-provenance action; neither authority live-synchronizes or rewrites the
other. D8 and Phase 31 gain no D9 Plan or balance-derived feed fields without a
later explicit, provider-certified decision. Phase 21 may determine bounded
alert eligibility, while Phases 17 and 6 retain message-content and delivery
authority.

This decision rejects mandatory Plans, commitments, balances, reserves, or
alerts; false zeroes; arbitrary formulas or dashboard builders; UI hiding as a
security boundary; automatic goal synchronization; cross-currency totals;
retroactive mutation; hidden-source leakage; and any claim that support is
available, withdrawable, payroll-ready, payable, or paid.
