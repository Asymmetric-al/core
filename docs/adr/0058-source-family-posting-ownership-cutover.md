# Source-family-specific Posting Ownership Cutover

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 20 grill session — D17)

Exactly one system may own provider posting for one canonical source occurrence,
Legal Entity, accounting destination, source account or instrument, Accounting
Posting Intent family, and source-authoritative ownership interval. A
**Posting Ownership Cutover** transfers that authority prospectively at one
complete source-family boundary. It cannot split a payout, Deposit Group,
expense, payable, payment, or another source-owned atomic occurrence merely
because dates cross the selected handoff.

Clean forward-only activation at the next complete drained boundary is the
default. One bounded **Cutover Coverage Manifest** distinguishes
previous-owner coverage, exact previous-owner provider evidence, proved-unposted
gaps, prospective Asym ownership, intentional exclusion, and ambiguity.
Provider inspection states its exact or bounded capability; it never claims a
universal QBO/Xero historical-absence proof. Optional historical backfill is
off by default and may cover only a frozen population positively proved
unposted.

Asym serializes its own ownership fence but cannot atomically lock an
independent connector or manual bookkeeper. A final bounded inspection and
post-activation overlap detection therefore remain active. Conflicts quarantine
only affected unreleased work through the existing accounting-exception
contract. Previous-owner posting evidence keeps its provenance, and each late
correction receives one current execution owner under the append-only
correction contract. Neither direct delivery nor a staff-mediated package may
bypass the ownership fence.
