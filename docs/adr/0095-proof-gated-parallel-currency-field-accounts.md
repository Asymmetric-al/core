# ADR-0095: Proof-gated parallel currency-scoped Field Accounts

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 21 grill session — D6)

Phase 21 uses one explicit, quiet, Legal-Entity-owned default Field Account
currency and permits tenant-authorized parallel currency-scoped Field Accounts
only through prospective, source-family-specific proof. Every Field Account has
one immutable currency; entries, assessments, reservations, capacity, Support
Cycle closes, admission coverage, corrections, statements, reallocations, and
retirement remain exact and independently balanced per currency. Related
accounts may appear together as one Support Assignment's **Support balances**
workspace, but that grouping has no writable aggregate, converted balance, or
financial authority.

The default is an immutable prospective **Default Field Account Currency
Version**. Every additional path uses an immutable prospective **Field Account
Currency Activation Version** scoped to the exact Tenant, Legal Entity,
Support Assignment, approved purpose, destination Field Account identity or
atomic creation intent, currency, source family, source binding, environment,
and half-open interval. Atomic creation enforces exactly one Field Account per
Tenant, Legal Entity, Support Assignment, and ISO currency.

When the Phase 13 source header and organization-controlled Field Account
allocation basis use different currencies, one immutable
admission-contract-owned **Support Currency Allocation Manifest** preserves the
complete effective hard-tender source line set—including fee-cover or other
non-support lines—and one exact typed target basis such as
`provider_balance_gross`, `bank_credited_amount`, or another closed
D2-qualified exact basis. It freezes provider or bank evidence, typed
conversion provenance, separately observed costs, the existing deterministic
largest-remainder minor-unit allocation, residual disposition, unique
coverage, and independent source/target conservation. Only eligible
non-fee-cover designation target portions may create Gross Support
Allocations; `gross` here means before separate Phase 21 assessment and cost
effects, not that every source exposed processor gross.

Every cross-currency refund, return, dispute, or source correction uses a new
immutable successor/correction manifest with that adverse occurrence's exact
source and target evidence and allocation bounded by remaining original
coverage. Phase 21 never mechanically reverses the original target amount or
rate. Neither manifest rewrites gift or receipt truth, supplies accounting
translation, or creates an Asym exchange rate.

Currency activation, source readiness, Support Cycle admission and close,
Phase 20 accounting delivery, and external payroll or accounts-payable payment
remain independently authoritative. A failed or expired source capability may
quarantine only affected new positive work. It cannot invalidate a prior
close, hide history, block another currency, suppress a known adverse
correction, or make QBO/Xero a Field Account authority. Retained Stripe
settlement reuses Phase 20 D20 evidence only for the exact capabilities that
evidence proves; offline and direct-credit sources may instead use their
qualified same-currency evidence under Phase 21 D2.

Activation and default changes are atomic and prospective. Retirement stops
ordinary new positive admission only after every open cycle, reservation,
obligation, correction writer, and residual has an explicit owner; history and
mandatory adverse corrections remain live. Internal D5 reallocations remain
same-Tenant, same-Legal-Entity, and same-currency. External conversion is
represented only by exact externally owned source and result evidence, while
QBO or Xero owns translation, revaluation, and foreign-exchange accounting.

Finance always retains every exact per-currency balance. When a current Support
Workspace Publication Profile Version authorizes missionary balance
publication, an ordinary single-currency tenant sees one Finance-confirmed
support balance with its ISO currency and through date and no multicurrency
controls. When parallel accounts exist, every balance authorized for that
audience remains simultaneously discoverable, ISO-labelled, and independently
through-dated; the Profile cannot selectively conceal a sibling currency while
claiming to publish that balance family. If balance publication is off, the
missionary sees no balance or balance-derived placeholder, while finance truth
continues unchanged. There is no authoritative converted grand total,
selector-only discovery, mutable currency, destructive merge, or Phase 21 FX
engine.

**Phase 21 D23 precision amendment (2026-08-01).** Currency-lane retirement
inventories every D23 reservation, candidate, effect, correction, and
unresolved slice in that ISO currency exactly once. It stops new forward
admission while source-owned corrections remain tied to the original scope.
D23 expense conversion evidence is distinct from the D6 Support Currency
Allocation Manifest; neither substitutes for the other, creates a currency
lane, converts an account, or permits cross-currency internal reallocation.
