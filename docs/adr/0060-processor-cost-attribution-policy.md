# Processor-cost attribution policy

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 20 grill session — D19)

Payment-processing costs are organization-borne by default. A tenant may
prospectively activate one fee-cover-first mode in which exact, source-linked
ordinary processor costs that remain after associated donor fee-cover are
attributed across the original supported Designations. The gross
supported-gift amount, separate fee-cover contribution, complete processor
expense, and provider settlement remain independent facts; together the first
two preserve complete gross gift truth. The mode changes only the expense's
internal organization-versus-Designation attribution. The frozen Phase 20 D6
mapping then resolves each resulting share to an Accounting Reporting Target.

The policy is pinned to one Tenant, Legal Entity, Settlement Account Binding,
source family, and effective half-open policy interval; a new version activates
only at a complete settlement boundary. It uses integer minor units,
deterministic largest-remainder allocation, one immutable Processor Cost
Attribution Manifest, and one bounded prospective exception that keeps a
policy-exempt Designation's calculated share organization-borne rather than
shifting it to another fund. Dispute, FX, payout, reserve, platform, tax,
standalone, unknown, and source-unlinked costs never enter the allocator.

Direct QBO/Xero delivery must preserve gross revenue, complete expense, and
required reporting dimensions or remain not ready. The artifact lane may
proceed only when its exact import surface is independently certified to
preserve the same effect; it cannot cure a provider capability gap. The policy
never creates a net gift, changes receipts, rewrites released accounting, or
claims legal, GAAP, or CRA certification. Phase 20 does not itself debit a
Phase 21 Field Account; any later Phase 21 effect must consume the same proved
occurrence exactly once under Phase 21 authority.

Phase 21 D21 asset-sale, brokerage, liquidation, valuation, disposition, and
proceeds costs are outside this processor-cost family even when a provider calls
one of them a processing fee. D21 alone may use exact source evidence for those
costs when freezing a Realized Support Basis; neither Phase 20 D19 nor Phase 21
D20 may relabel or apply them as processor or residual organization support
costs.
