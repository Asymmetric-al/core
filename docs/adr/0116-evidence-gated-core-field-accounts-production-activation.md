# ADR-0116: Evidence-gated Core Field Accounts production activation

**Status:** Accepted (founder ruling, Phase 21 grill session — D27)

## Context

Phase 21 D1-D26 define Field Account, source-admission, close, assessment,
expense, compensation, access, statement, provider, opening-position, records,
and recovery truth. They do not by themselves define how one exact tenant scope
may safely move from shipped but non-authoritative software through production
proof into D17 authority, how optional capabilities become executable later, or
how a post-start defect is contained without erasing financial history.

One tenant-wide enable switch would couple unrelated entities, currencies,
providers, and capabilities into one blast radius. Independent feature flags
would create an unreviewable dependency graph and several contradictory meanings
of `live`. A second Phase 21 activation state would duplicate D17's sole Field
Account Operational Cutover, while a second readiness or close calculation
would duplicate D11.

## Decision

> **C-prime-amended-and-hardened (C-prime-R) — one quiet, evidence-gated Core
> Field Accounts Production Activation Contract composed through D17's sole
> Operational Cutover, with proof-gated tenant-selected optional capability
> bindings and cause-owned live containment; using one immutable Phase 21
> Release Generation, one prospective Field Accounts Adoption Plan Version,
> and one content-addressed machine-prepared Go-Live Readiness Manifest bound to
> the exact Tenant, Legal Entity, ISO currency, complete Support Assignment and
> source-family census, environment, code/schema generation, and D17 half-open
> authority boundary, while referencing and never recreating, weakening,
> waiving, or reinterpreting every applicable D1-D26 and owning-phase fact;
> separating fully synthetic demonstration and provider sandbox evidence from
> a production-authorized, complete-cohort, structurally side-effect-dark,
> non-authoritative shadow; giving finance one accessible exception-first
> consequence review and literal start action; and performing final actor,
> permission, source, cohort, policy, mapping, manifest, revocation, and
> generation reproof inside D17's idempotent CAS-guarded cutover rather than a
> second activation state. D17 authority, first and later D11 close/integrity
> proof, D9/D12 publication, Phase 20 accounting delivery, compensation and
> reimbursement handoffs, notifications, external feeds, provider outcomes,
> reconciliation, payroll completion, and payment remain independently
> authoritative; a named missionary pilot scopes only exact D19/Phase 12
> publication, never financial rows. One disposable through-dated Operational
> Readiness Projection keeps healthy tenants quiet and opens only cause-owned
> exceptions; smallest-scope prospective containment stops affected new
> positive or discretionary behavior while preserving immutable history,
> authorized reads, D26 custody export, established obligations, mandatory
> adverse corrections, artifact/manual continuity, and append-only recovery —
> without a tenant-global enable bit, arbitrary flag or workflow matrix,
> random-row financial canary, mutable readiness truth, shadow side effect,
> repeated manual certification, sandbox-as-production proof, generic
> compliance badge, blind retry, implicit downstream success, force-close,
> destructive rollback, or any claim that configured, connected, checked,
> active, closed, published, delivered, posted, reconciled, payroll-complete,
> payable, or paid are the same fact.**

### Authority and evidence model

D27 is a thin evidence-composition and consequence-review contract. It creates
no financial calculation, activation ledger, close authority, provider result,
permission grant, or general release-management platform.

The durable model contains:

1. one platform-owned immutable **Phase 21 Release Generation** identifying the
   exact production generation and its security, isolation, invariant,
   workload, accessibility, observability, restore, and recovery evidence;
2. one tenant-owned prospective **Field Accounts Adoption Plan Version**
   selecting a complete core cohort and only the optional capabilities the
   tenant uses, from a finite server-owned catalog rather than a custom rules
   graph; and
3. one immutable content-addressed **Field Accounts Go-Live Readiness Manifest**
   referencing the current D1-D26 and owning-phase proofs for that exact scope,
   boundary, actor, authorization epoch, source cursor, policy/mapping version,
   D17 opening-preparation/shadow result, and consequence review.

A rebuildable, through-dated **Field Accounts Operational Readiness
Projection** composes current owner facts for staff presentation. It is neither
durable authority nor valid command input. Readiness is recomputed from the
manifest and current revocation/freshness state; it is not a mutable Boolean.

D17 remains the only financial activation transition. Its short transaction
reauthorizes the actor, re-proves every bound input, verifies the manifest
digest and semantic operation identity, and performs the sole CAS-guarded Field
Account Operational Cutover. A changed input returns to consequence review.
A lost client response is inspected by operation identity rather than blindly
retried. D11 remains the sole close and integrity authority before and after
cutover.

### Proof classes and cohort closure

Synthetic demonstration, provider sandbox evidence, D17 production-shaped
opening preparation/shadow evidence, and production authority are distinct
proof classes. No record changes class by flipping a flag.

D27 references and invokes D17's existing production-shaped opening preparation
and shadow-reconciliation contract; it owns no second balance calculation,
mapping-admissibility decision, source disposition, reconciliation result, or
financial shadow. D17 may use production-authorized reads and the live
deterministic compilers over the complete activation cohort, but output ports
are structurally unable to create Field Account truth, reservations,
obligations, statements, messages, accounting releases, provider operations,
payroll or reimbursement handoffs, notifications, or external feeds. Its
private evidence is resumable, bounded, content-addressed, and non-authoritative.

One financial activation cohort is the complete D17 census for one Tenant,
Legal Entity, and ISO currency, including all in-scope Support Assignments,
source families, splits, corrections, reallocations, reservations, obligations,
and atomic currency/source groups. Random percentages and named-worker
financial canaries are prohibited. A named missionary pilot is only a separate
D9/D19/Phase 12 publication audience over the same production read model.

### Experience and optional capabilities

The one resumable **Set up Field Accounts** workspace extends D17's existing
**Start Field Accounts** journey. It has four calm sections: **Your setup**,
**Checks**, **Try and compare**, and **Review and start**. It begins with the
quiet one-Legal-Entity, one-currency core and progressively reveals only
tenant-selected optional capabilities. Checks are grouped by **Asym checks
automatically**, **Your organization**, or **Qualified review**; healthy
evidence collapses and staff resolve only genuine choices or exceptions.

Sample and production comparison lanes are unmistakably labelled. One
check-answers page explains the exact scope and boundary, what becomes
authoritative or visible, what becomes executable, what remains off, and what
containment can and cannot stop. A literal action such as **Start USD Field
Accounts on 31 August 2026** invokes D17 after final reproof. There is no
percentage-complete score, force-pass, `Mark complete`, recurring certification
ceremony, or generic `Enable Phase 21` action.

Publication, statements, expenses, cards, travel, tenant AI, compensation,
reimbursement, accounting, notifications, external feeds, additional
currencies, and provider operations remain separately versioned prospective
bindings under their owning decisions. Adding one later never reopens core
activation or recomputes prior history. A failed optional capability never
makes the core inactive.

After cutover, D9 alone determines whether the D17 Finance-confirmed opening
position may be published, and D12 governs closed-cycle statement publication.
First-D11-close verification is shown to finance as an independent through-dated
fact; it neither grants nor revokes publication.

### Existing D26 records-family resolution

D27 creates no seventh Phase 21 records family. Durable Release Generation,
Adoption Plan, Go-Live Readiness Manifest, consequence-review,
activation-operation, and containment evidence resolve under D26's existing
authorization/audit/evidence family. Replaceable shadow working data and
diagnostics resolve under D26's transient staging/preview/diagnostic family;
any sealed proof referenced by a readiness manifest is durable audit evidence.

### Containment and recovery

After D17 there is no destructive rollback. Current owner facts are composed at
each positive or discretionary effect port. A cause-owned failure stops only
the smallest proved future admission, publication, invitation, or executable
delivery scope. Immutable history, current authorized reads, D26 custody
exports, established compensation and reimbursement obligations, mandatory
refunds and adverse corrections, source-owned repair, artifact/manual
continuity, and append-only recovery continue unless a separately authoritative
legal or security restriction forbids the access itself.

Provider, publication, accounting, storage, notification, or feed failures
remain local to their owner. Clearing a platform emergency control does not
grant or automatically resume a tenant capability; prospective reactivation
requires current owner evidence. No status implies an external operation was
undone or that accounting reconciliation, payroll completion, or payment
occurred.

## Consequences

- Simple tenants receive one quiet guided core path without configuring unused
  capabilities; complex tenants retain bounded prospective flexibility.
- Finance reviews one exact consequence summary rather than assembling or
  repeatedly attesting to a dependency graph.
- D17's production-shaped opening preparation/shadow can exercise real data
  shape without acquiring authority or producing downstream effects; D27 does
  not create another financial shadow.
- Financial and publication pilots can no longer be confused: financial
  cohorts are complete, while exact authorized audiences may be introduced
  progressively.
- Every `active`, `closed`, `published`, `delivered`, `posted`, `reconciled`,
  `payroll-complete`, `payable`, and `paid` fact keeps its own owner and evidence.
- Implementation must prove exact tenant isolation, same-scope relational
  integrity, forced coarse RLS plus Phase 12 authorization, current-version CAS,
  concurrency and lost-response recovery, production-shaped load, private
  evidence, accessible setup, and asymmetric containment before activation.
- D1-D26 remain binding and unchanged.

## Phase 21 D28 precision amendment

D27 may reference a current D28 Travel Allowance Cumulative Admission when a
tenant selects cumulative travel calculation during setup or later adds it as
an optional capability. That reference cannot create, waive, repair, or
reinterpret D28 opening or prospective-completeness proof, cannot make an
incomplete travel pool block already-safe Core Field Accounts, and cannot
reopen D17's sole monetary cutover. See
[ADR-0117](./0117-proof-gated-opening-cumulative-travel-allowance-admission.md).

## Alternatives rejected

- **One tenant-wide Phase 21 switch.** Rejected because unrelated Legal
  Entities, currencies, providers, and optional capabilities have independent
  readiness and failure scopes.
- **Independent feature flags everywhere.** Rejected because they create
  unsupported combinations, contradictory meanings of `live`, operational
  flag debt, and a tenant-authored dependency graph.
- **Second activation or close state.** Rejected because D17 and D11 already own
  those irreversible facts.
- **A D27-owned second financial shadow.** Rejected because D17 already owns
  production-shaped opening preparation, mapping admissibility, source
  disposition, and shadow reconciliation.
- **Random-row or named-worker financial canary.** Rejected because it can split
  source coverage and atomic financial groups.
- **Sandbox or OAuth as production proof.** Rejected because provider
  environments, products, regions, scopes, plans, and operations differ.
- **Rollback after authoritative posting.** Rejected because it would erase or
  contradict immutable history, obligations, and mandatory adverse correction.
